Recently, there was a great earthquake in the capital, and the shaking did not cease for several days. The master of Tōrōan came carrying a small record in his sleeve and requested that I write a preface for it. Upon reading his account, I found that commentary and illustrations were already included as described to the right. I therefore set down records of the signs (of earthquakes) from the Ninna era as a substitute for a formal preface, so as to barely fulfill his request.
Seventh month of autumn, Bunsei 13, the year of Kanoe-Tora【Note 1】
　　　　　　Takudō Gantai　(seal)(seal)

　(Red square seal) Considerations on Earthquakes

On the second day of the seventh month of Bunsei 13, the year of Kanoe-Tora, around the Hour of the Monkey (申の刻)【Note 2】, a great earthquake struck, shaking and trembling violently, so that earthen storehouses and mud-plastered walls (tsuiji)【Note 3】 throughout the capital (Rakuchū)【Note 4】 were greatly damaged; some buildings completely collapsed. Numerous earthen storehouses crumbled, and nearly all mud-plastered walls and tall fences fell, with many people suffering injuries. Although people had heard that such things had happened in the distant past, there had been no earthquake this violent in the capital in recent memory, so everyone was struck with shock and fear. People rushed out of their homes into the main streets, laid out mats, and busied themselves arranging makeshift shelters.

【Note 1: 1830; the era name was changed to Tenpō in December of the same year.】
