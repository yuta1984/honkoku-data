【Upper section】
Bunsei 11, the Year of the Rat (1828),
On the 12th day of the 11th month, likewise
beginning from the fifth hour of the morning (around 8 AM),
a great earthquake began to shake,
and for three days until the 14th,
it did not cease shaking, day or night.
The coastal areas of Tōfuri
and Itsumo-zaki [place names]

【Lower section】
The mountain of the Yahiko Myōjin shrine
collapsed greatly
and was pushed out into the sea.
In the same area,
Sanjō-machi (San-jo town),
Tsubame-machi (Tsubame town),
and also
the East Gate ruins, the temple hall, and the great gate—
all were shaken down without exception. Beyond this, fields, mountains, and rivers collapsed and crumbled, overflowing onto the land. The number of people and horses injured was beyond counting.
Approximately more than 8,000 houses
collapsed and crumbled, and more than 3,000 head of cattle and horses
were killed.
This great earthquake, rare in all ages past and present,
its general account
is here recorded.
