### Living Creatures (Fauna)

○ Among waterfowl, there are small birds that form flocks. Russian hunters load their guns with over fifty pellets of scatter shot and fire. The birds are by nature sharp and alert; at the sound of the gun, they dive underwater. Therefore, the foreigners calculate in advance the depth to which the birds dive and shoot into the water. A flock numbers around fourteen or fifteen birds, and with one shot, three or four birds can be taken. There is also another species, extremely small—about the size of a cicada—with brilliantly beautiful plumage. The foreigners shoot these with guns, remove the entrails, preserve them as dried specimens, and present them as tribute to the imperial capital. In the capital city, there is a great repository (natural history storehouse) where the fauna and flora of the realm are classified as useful or useless and stored. Illustrations of every species must be drawn and submitted. This is because the vast expanse of the territory makes it impossible for even the Emperor himself to personally inspect it all, and so local products and regional specialties are examined and documented in this manner.
