【Right folio】
〇 "Hedo" is the harbor entrance of "Owahe" Island. The size of the island is estimated to be approximately forty to fifty ri. The traveler stayed only four days and was therefore unable to obtain detailed information. Nevertheless, the desolate character of the place can be surmised. The port faces northeast and is backed by a range of steep, towering mountains. In the hollow depressions of these mountains, sparse small trees grow. From one precipitous peak, fire spews forth. Ships at sea use this volcano as a landmark to identify the port. Its sharp, steep peak surpasses all the scenery observed by travelers among the "San'ichi" archipelago. To climb it would be a distance of over one ri. The width of the harbor mouth is over twenty chō; on both sides, massive rocks cluster and protrude five to six shaku above the water surface. The interior of the harbor is flat and sandy, and where sand and soil combine, the shore rises three to four shaku high, winding like a belt across the harbor.

【Left folio】
It runs horizontally across the harbor. Sappanwood and sugarcane grow luxuriantly everywhere, forming forests. The brothels are situated behind the forest. On the sand there are settlements of island inhabitants and large trading houses. Coconut palms grow in clusters. At the water's edge, vessels called "Keno" are arranged on the sand to serve as anchors — this is to prevent them from being carried away by the current. The left side of the harbor also forms a small bay, where "Keno" vessels should be moored.
