Calculating the growth of the inland population: in 1790 《marginal note: Kansei 2, year of kanoe-inu》 there were 3,929,000 people; in 1800 《marginal note: Kansei 12, year of kanoe-saru》 there were 5,281,000 people; in 1817 《marginal note: Bunka 14, year of hinoto-ushi》 there were 10,250,000 people. Upon Spain's cession of Florida ("Furōrida"), it reached 20,000,000.

In the heartland of America, many tribes indigenous to that land from ancient times dwell there. To the west of the Mississippi River, even now there remain wilderness areas, great marshes, and vast swamps covered with dense grass. In "Savannah(?)" there are regions overgrown with wild grasses where the wild people known as "Indigenen" (Indians) come and settle. Therefore, counting all the states of America together, there are 50,000,000 people.

Each of the united states independently administers its own governance and issues decrees. The highest official is called the "Government" 《marginal note: that is, "President"》. Among the various states, there is only one "President" appointed. In some cases, a council official 《marginal note: "Senate", also called "Lord"》 countersigns. In order to deliberate on matters of great importance common to all states — military affairs, peace negotiations, the dispatch of envoys, trade, naval forces, taxation, and the minting of currency — two "Lords" from each state are pre-established in the Grand Assembly Hall. "Lords" are also sent to the offices of local officials. The number is not fixed, being proportional to the size of the population of each area. The assembly of these two offices — local and deliberative — is presided over by "Congress" 《marginal note: alliance leader》.
