When the men stated that they wished to find appropriate work, the magistrate kindly told them that since they were in his custody, there was no need for such a thing, and that they should live without worry. Nevertheless, after yet another year or so had passed, they again earnestly expressed their wish to perform some suitable work. They also went to the "Ryochi" and stated their case, whereupon he said that in the meantime he would employ them as servants, and they were assigned tasks such as splitting firewood, drawing water, cleaning, and going out carrying packed lunches.

One entry: Denzo — whose name in Japan had been "Fudenojo" — noticed that the local people, whenever they called to him, always seemed to be saying "Tenzo" or "Tenzou," and so he changed his name to Denzo accordingly.

One entry: Toraemon was hired by a local carpenter and gradually became his apprentice.

One entry: Jusuke, through the care of the "Ryochi," traveled to a place called "Yuuroo," located about three ri from the harbor, where he lodged at the home of a farmer named "Tetsuhani" and called upon a doctor to tend to his health. However, there was no improvement; he developed something resembling dysentery, and died of illness at the end of the first month of the following year.
