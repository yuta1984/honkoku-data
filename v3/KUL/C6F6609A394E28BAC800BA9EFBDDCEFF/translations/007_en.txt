(Continued from previous page) When they swam up to them, the foreigners made gestures as if to ask "Are there others with you?" So the castaways pointed toward the cave and gestured to indicate that two people remained there. Meanwhile, another boat was rowed around toward the cave with very dark-skinned people aboard. At this time, Denzō was inside the cave nursing Juske, when a figure appeared at the cave entrance — black as the bottom of a pot, with only eyes and teeth standing out — and tried somehow to drag Denzō out. Terrified, Denzō struggled and shook him off. Then a fair-skinned foreigner came and gestured to indicate that the other three were already aboard the ship, so Denzō followed along and got into their boat. Juske was carried by one of the dark-skinned men and placed aboard the boat. Both boats then rowed back to the large ship, where the castaways were brought aboard. The ship was approximately thirty ken in length and six ken in width, with three masts, and had about ten foreigners aboard. They were treated with exceptional kindness, each receiving medicine and a garment with tubular sleeves. They were cared for in various ways. Furthermore, when the foreigners made gestures as if asking "Is there anything left behind on the island?", the castaways gestured to indicate they had left clothes behind.
