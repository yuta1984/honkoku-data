Copy of the Official Communication to Kyoto and Osaka

　　I humbly write to inform you of the following.
Item: On the morning of the last day of the twelfth month of the previous year, yet another foreign vessel was reported drifting off the coast of Kanezaki. Ships were promptly dispatched from Kanezaki-ura and rowed out to the location. Tokitoki Shinuemon, the resident official (jōban) of Jishima Island, immediately went out to inspect the vessel and determined that it appeared to be the same Korean fishing vessel that had previously drifted ashore off Genkai Island, with six persons aboard. In response, the ashigaru-gashira (foot soldier commanders) Furuta Heibei, Ōoka Jintayū, and Iwata Magozaemon, along with other officials, were dispatched as per customary procedure, and proceeded to Nagasaki.
