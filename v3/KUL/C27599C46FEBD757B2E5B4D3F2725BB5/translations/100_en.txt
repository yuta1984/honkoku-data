Ninth month, fifth day　　　　Ōoto Rokuzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kōri Heima
　　　　　To: Lord Nomura Tarōbei
　　　　　To: Lord Kuroda Yukinori
　　　　　To: Lord Yoshida Shikibu
　　　　　To: Lord Urakami Saburōbei

(Regarding affairs in Kyoto and Osaka)

We write to inform you of the following. On the eighth of last month, one Chinese vessel ran aground and drifted ashore at Kishiura within our domain. The permanent garrison of Himeshima [定番] was dispatched to stand guard, and from the castle town as well, guards and various officials were promptly sent out in the customary manner. Upon communicating with [the crew] by written exchange [筆談], it was ascertained that the vessel was a ship from Batavia [咬𠺕吧], bound for Nagasaki for the purpose of trade, and that it had been driven ashore by unfavorable winds and waves on the open sea. They reported a crew of sixty-five persons. This matter has been reported to the Nagasaki Magistrate's Office [長崎御奉行所], and upon receiving instructions therefrom, the plan is to escort the vessel to Nagasaki with guards accompanying them. We ask that you please formally notify the officials on your end of this matter, following established precedent as communicated from here. The details of the oral statement [口上] have been sent under separate cover.

　　　Respectfully submitted,
　　　Eighth month, twelfth day　　　The Council of Chief Retainers
　　To the Accounts Magistrate [御勘定奉行]:
　　　　Lord Takaya Kahei
　To the Osaka Warehouse Agent [大坂御蔵本]:
　　　　Lord Ogawa Danemon

Regarding the above matter, it is noted [in a communication dated the twentieth of the eighth month] that the notification was duly made to the Town Magistrate of the Castle Guard's Jurisdiction [御城番領御町奉行].
