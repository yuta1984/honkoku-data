(Damage to the water channel/inlet,) matters concerning the fields of the bettō (別当, shrine administrator) household, and the new land reclamation/construction project of Ujiari.

Item: For the general cleaning at Kibune and the removal of old timber, the Hoshu ujizamurai (保守氏侍, retainer of the Hoshu clan) is to go up (to Kibune) on the twentieth.

20th: In the evening, went up to the area of the inner shrine (奥社) at Kibune for a general cleaning.

21st: Fourteen people in total went up — people from Shichiku Village (紫竹村) and people from this place. They rebuilt the damaged sections of the stone wall, and entirely redressed the foundation stones and rubble stones.
Tonight the rain and wind are fierce.

22nd: Same as before. Construction was completed, and the magistrates (奉行衆) returned to Kyoto. Due to last night's rain and wind,
the embankment at the rear of Hijiri Jinja (聖神寺) suffered flood damage.

23rd: Through today, three days of construction work at Kibune were carried out.

24th: People came to visit for the Jizō pilgrimage (地蔵参り).

25th: Around this time, regarding absences from the kobан (小番, a scheduled duty rotation), four members of the court nobility (公家衆) are under house confinement (閉門). They are: Lord Takeya (竹屋殿), Lord Washio (鷲尾殿), Lord Yamashina (山科殿), and one more (name not recorded).

26th: At the lecture hall (講所), the old sanctuary building of the temporary shrine (権社) was moved to the lecture hall site and rebuilt as it originally stood. Bids were invited for the chindan (鎮壇, ritual ground-pacification) ceremony. The contract was awarded to carpenter Chōzaemon (長左衛門) for 580 monme (匁).
