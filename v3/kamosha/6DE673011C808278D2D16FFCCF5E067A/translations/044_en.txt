Four people went out and called upon Yorihisa to discuss the matter. It was an undeniable fact that this location had originally been encroached upon—taken illegally from shrine grounds—by shifting more than one ken westward from the east. However, the current master was unaware of this. It was agreed that it would be questionable to raise objections at this late stage over something that had been allowed to continue as it was, and so even if a fence covering that extended just over one shaku eastward were to be constructed, it was proposed that it should be accommodated by the shrine community, and that this consideration would be appropriate. The other party consented, and it was agreed that this arrangement would suffice.

The 6th Day: At the assembly, it was decided that beginning today, the bathhouse (yuya), which had been closed since the 6th year of Kanbun, would be reopened. The duty Shōjin-gashira (head of ritual abstinence) and those observing purification were each required to perform ritual ablutions (gyōzui), as declared by the Okina-shi (elder official) in a posted notice.
