The matter of court ranks (位階) was reported to the Tensō, who gave their agreement and attended. As evening came,
there was a meeting, but I did not attend.

6th Day: Note that today, copies of the aforementioned written documents were brought along.

7th Day / 8th Day: Wind blowing. A meeting was held. Yesterday, the manservant (chūgen) Yazō was sent to Ōtsu. The Gondaibu (senior official) handled the matter: a donation of 20 koku worth of rice for horse-racing (競馬) expenses, contributed by Lord Ii Genba, was converted into silver — amounting to over 1 kan and 50 monme — and was collected and brought back. Regarding the above sum, the share was 50 monme and 2 bu per person.

Today's meeting: I did not attend. Regarding the matter submitted to the authorities (公儀) concerning the claims of Hoka-kane and Yori, it is said that the manner of handling was reported.

9th Day: At today's meeting, due to the date of the legal proceedings (公事), it is said that a lawsuit was also filed by Kaneyori. At the lecture hall (講所), accounts were settled for the ■ construction (rebuilding/repair) of the shrine, totaling 1 kan, 830 monme, 6 bu, 7 rin, and 2 mō in expenses. The remainder was 70 monme, 7 bu, 9 rin, and 8 mō. Regarding rice: from the previous year's remaining stock of 1 koku, 2 to, 7 shō, 8 gō, and 8 shaku, a quantity of 9 to, 3 shō, 9 gō, and 6 shaku was distributed. The further remaining amount is 3 to, 3 shō, 9 gō, and 2 shaku.
