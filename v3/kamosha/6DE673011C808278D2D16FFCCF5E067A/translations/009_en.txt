It was stated that: "In any case, the seven families should convene an assembly and provide a unified reply. Since this matter has not yet been communicated, for today it should not be carried out." Therefore, that evening, the newly appointed shrine officials (shinpo shashi) held another meeting, and sent Yasie Sanao as a messenger to the shrine superintendent's residence to inquire, "Is there anything amiss? How do matters stand?" The shrine superintendent replied: "In accordance with what has been said, the affair of the eight shrines should proceed with the eight shrines' shrine officials performing the Sengū rite together. There is absolutely no difference in this regard."

5th day: clear, slightly cloudy, slight rain. At the Hour of the Horse (noon), there was a combined assembly of both senior and junior members, and the three groups of junior members all gathered together. The administrator (satanin) made the following announcement: "Regarding the ceremony of the formal Sengū, each of the eight shrine officials shall conduct the Sengū at their respective shrines. The members of the seven families have given their consent. We are deeply grateful for your discreet efforts on this matter—this is the message conveyed to the newly appointed officials."

Next, concerning the layout of the shrine precinct: the small steps (kobashi) facing the Tanao Shrine at the shrine grounds were to be removed, and henceforth a kichō (hanging curtain) was to be placed at the middle gate (chūmon). Those worshippers who wished to approach the divine presence directly should proceed by going around in front of the New Shrine (Shingū-sha). Visitors from other places should offer their worship from in front of the Tanao Shrine before departing. Furthermore, should anyone wish to approach the vicinity of the sacred presence, the shrine official on duty was instructed to guide them to proceed around from the east.

Next, it was stated that the rumor that the precedent references (kanrei) produced on this occasion came from the Mori family is entirely uncalled for (i.e., such rumors should not be spread).
