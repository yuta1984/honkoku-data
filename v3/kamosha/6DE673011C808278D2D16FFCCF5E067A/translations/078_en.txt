The ujibito (shrine people/hereditary shrine members) include Kura-no-jō Yasumori and two others — three persons in total. It is noted that they are to visit on the first day of the month to receive a reply.

In the evening, a deliberation was held regarding how to respond when calling upon the sainin (shrine administrator) the following day, specifically concerning the question of whether an audience (omemie) had been granted during the Kan'ei era, and whether there had been any gifts bestowed (hairyōmono). The household diaries were consulted but yielded no clear answer; it appears the occasion had been combined with the Aoi Ceremony (Aoi-rei).
