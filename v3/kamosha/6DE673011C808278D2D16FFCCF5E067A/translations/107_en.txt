Regarding a matter related to the origin paintings (engi-e) of Ei-zōtani (Shadow Image Valley), a request came from the Kanō Geki's residence for a brief consultation. It was decided that tomorrow the shrine administrator (zōshō) Tsushima-no-kami should proceed to Gojō for this purpose.

Concerning the dispute between Hokka and Kanei, a bound document (kakitsuke) containing precedents of cases in which property transferred to a daughter of Kanei had not been returned to the main family (honke) when there were no heirs was submitted. However, it was concluded that, since no formal deliberation (hihan) had been conducted within the shrine organization (shachū), the matter should be returned to Kanei's jurisdiction.
