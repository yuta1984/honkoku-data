22nd Day: A meeting was held. I arrived late.
　Regarding the claims from Hiiragino, a statement was made by Ogata Heizaemon.
　The long chest (nagamochi) containing the official offerings (kanpei), along with other items, were to be put up for auction.

23rd Day: To the "totte" [a designated place], ancient御几帳 (御几帳, ceremonial curtain stands), white silk cords for the borders of御簾 (sudare, blinds), thin silk oil covers (aburatan), and other ancient articles from the Tenshō, Kōji, and Kan'ei eras were all wrapped in oil covers and distributed to the "totte." They were then allocated by lottery to the households of shrine priests (shashi) and ujibito (clan members associated with the shrine). Some distributions were deferred to a later date.

One item: What is a "kaketsuno" (掛角, hanging horn)? Answer: Long ago, in the sea of Ise (perhaps), a mysterious light appeared every night. At that time, the Emperor held a deliberation, and a divination was performed at the nokiro (a covered corridor of the palace). The oracle indicated that an ancient sword from time immemorial lay at the bottom of the sea. Divers (ama) were sent to investigate, and sure enough, a sacred sword in the shape of this "kaketsuno" was found. This shape was split in two to create a sword (ken), and then the sword was split in two to produce a tachi (long sword). From this point forward, spears (hoko), naginata (halberd), yari (lance), and other weapons came into being. Today, among all shrines in the land, it is perhaps only our shrine (Kamo Shrine) that makes use of the three sacred treasures (sanshu no shinpō).

25th Day: The rain that had continued through last night cleared today. A wind blew in the early hours of this morning.
