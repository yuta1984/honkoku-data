Using copies of the previous talismans (maefuda), the draft documents were checked one by one and scrutinized.

The 25th Day (Clear): From before dawn today, the rewriting of the sacred talismans (ofuda) before the shrine deity was carried out. The portion for the shrine families (shake) was written by Moku-no-Gonnosuke and Suemichi, while the portion for the tōne (shrine servants/officials) was written by the ujizamurai (clan warrior retainer).
