26th Day: Regarding the western and eastern shrines of ■ki (a place name, partially illegible), the western shrine has long been managed by the Suruga-no-kami family, and the eastern shrine by the Kai-no-kami family. However, as both had fallen into serious disrepair, it was decided to reconstruct both shrines using the old timber from the current construction project (gozōei). Accordingly, a temporary enshrinement ceremony (kari-sengū) was held this evening. All necessary expenses and materials were provided from within the shrine community.

27th Day: The formal enshrinement ceremony (shō-sengū) of the same shrine was conducted. Regarding the officiant of the enshrinement ceremony (sengū-shi), at the meeting of the 22nd, opinions were divided—some held that the kannagi (shrine priest) should appropriately perform the duties, while others suggested that someone suitable from the local townspeople should be chosen—and no resolution was reached at that time. In the end, it was decided that Ho (保), the shrine priest, would perform the duties.
　At the time of the temporary enshrinement, sacred sake, washed rice, and fish were offered. At the formal enshrinement, the morning offerings consisted of sacred sake and washed rice; the evening offerings, prepared and presented by the appointed officials (satanin), consisted of white rice, red rice (sekihan), side dishes, and small sea bream, served at the lecture hall (kōjo).

28th Day: The regular meeting was postponed to today due to yesterday's enshrinement ceremony. After the meeting concluded, everyone partook of sake and offerings, followed by an evening meal. At today's meeting, there were various deliberations regarding the claims of Ho Kakane (保嘉兼) concerning a certain dependency matter. The son of Yamanao (山直) received a grant of the hamatoko (浜床, a shoreside ceremonial space) at Kibune (貴船). As a result of this, a request was made for a御几帳 (ceremonial curtain stand), and one was duly sent.
　It was reported that the dispute between Genzaemon and Bingo had been resolved.
