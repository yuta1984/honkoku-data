　　Copy of the Royal Proclamation (Senmyō) from the Formal Shrine Relocation (Sengū) ceremony of the sixteenth day of last ninth month

By the Emperor's proclamation, spoken with reverence before the august presence of the Great Deity of Kamo, it is humbly declared: The construction and maintenance of a splendid shrine is an auspicious omen of a well-governed age. As the shrine had gradually fallen into disrepair, carved and jeweled ornaments have been set in place, pillars erected, and all has been made magnificent and beautiful, causing the great divine power of the Myōjin to shine brightly. Having constructed and prepared the main hall, we now conduct the ceremony of the shrine relocation (sengū). If this can be accomplished free from the disturbance of wind and rain, in peace and without incident, in accordance with the divine will, it shall be by virtue of the Great Deity's profound benevolence and boundless divine assistance...
