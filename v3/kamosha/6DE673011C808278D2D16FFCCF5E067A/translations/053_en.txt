The (御指貫, formal court trousers) have been changed to the "aoi-maru" (hollyhock roundel) pattern on this occasion.

Regarding the御薗 (misono, sacred garden/cloth): During the Tenshō and Kōji eras, small hollyhock (ko-aoi) woven fabric was used as the border, with the center featuring a white damask with a saki-aoi (hollyhock) ground pattern and a roundel as the upper design. The "ko-aoi" refers to the small hollyhock pattern used at the imperial court—a design combining diamond lattice with thistle-like flowers. In the fifth year of Kan'ei, this was used for the sword bag (御太刀袋) and bow bag (御弓袋). On this occasion, the ko-aoi pattern specific to this shrine is used, with the upper design being a tomoe (comma-shaped) roundel executed in red thread.

Kibune Shrine (貴布祢社) has traditionally used the tomoe roundel since ancient times, while this shrine (Kamo Shrine), so as not to alter what is placed at the sacred seat (玉座), has used either a floral roundel or a hollyhock flower roundel—and this occasion marks the first time (this particular design has been adopted).

The御茶碗 (ceremonial tea bowl) is silver-plated, as is its stand. The御盃 (ceremonial cup) is silver-plated, and its stand is gold-plated with carved peony designs, made in the "horse-head stand" (馬頭台) style.

The御箸 (ceremonial chopsticks) come with a silver-plated stand.

The御屏風 (folding screen), 御衣架 (clothes rack), 大床子 (large dais), 柳筥 (willow box), 楾 (water pitcher), and 角盥 (rectangular washbasin) are all as before (following precedent).

Today's preparations are for the Main Shrine (本宮) only.

The coverings (ゆたん) for the辛櫃 (karabitu, storage chests) are all made of Yamato brocade in the five-color aoi (hollyhock) pattern.
