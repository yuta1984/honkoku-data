Yesterday, Lord Echizen, Lord Aki, the sub-magistrates (Muhei-dono and Kanemon-dono), Nakai Mondo, the same Chiyo, and Mokubei appeared before the Imperial Liaison (Gidensō) to pay their respects — eight persons in total.

Today, a messenger arrived at the Shin'in Gosho (the retired emperor's palace) from Kaga, and the following matter was raised in private: The kasen (poetry immortals painting / poem card compilation) is expected to be completed around midday tomorrow, and the relevant personnel (yakusha-shū) should come to receive it. The calligraphers are said to be three persons: the Shin'in Gosho, Ryōnin, Lord Konoe, and the Lord of Shōren'in. (Kamo / Kifune) Accordingly, it was decided that [the kasen] should be hung at the ofudaya (talisman hall), the Kifune worship hall, and other places for regular veneration.

◦ The dispute between the master carpenter (sei-daiku) and the master builder (sei-tōryō) concerning the matter of bowstring [materials/fees] was announced. It was decided to defer the matter pending further investigation.

◦ Regarding the matter of the permanent kagura rice fields (永代神楽田) for Princess Onna Sannomiya: [the area being] six tan and seven se [approximately], with a fixed annual yield of three koku and five to, the receipt (ukejō) from the guarantor Tonodō Michinao was announced, dated the fourth day of the eighth month.

◦ It was stated that the matter of proceeding to Edo to pay respects should be privately inquired of His Excellency's wishes.

◦ The residents of Minamizushi-chō have requested to receive the bridge timber from the riverbank at the exit [of the town].
