There was a deliberation as to whether it would be appropriate to use the remnants as covers for the harae (purification ritual) documents of each household.

Regarding the sacred robes (go-shinpuku) of the Main Shrine, it appears that there had previously been an established custom for their distribution.

13th: The neighbor Ichirō'emon passed away, reportedly at the age of fifty-four.

[Interlinear note]: Today it was rumored that two men from the Kibune valley had set out for Edo — said to be a needless matter. Later it was heard that the two who went down were Sakon'emon and Gen'nojō.

Today, at the Kannushi's residence, the five senior attendants (jōrō-shū) of the sacred robes received three hundred mon as payment in lieu of a kosode (short-sleeved kimono), and two hundred mon in lieu of a chihaya (a type of ceremonial garment). Furthermore, the cut fabric remnants (hashire no kiregire) from this occasion were distributed and handed over to them.

[Inserted note]: "In the Kan'ei era, [the sacred robes] were placed in a nagamochi (long chest) and sealed with the stamps of five bugyō (overseers); however, they were stolen during the night, and various investigations were conducted. Nevertheless, because [the investigator] was on bad terms with [the suspect], the matter of the theft was concluded, and we did not hear that [the items] were not handed over to the five bugyō this time around. There is a further entry on the 22nd — see the section for the 22nd."
The above was inserted as a note.

Now, the tō-bettō (head administrator), uta-yaku (poetry officer), shoshi (administrator), and mokudai (deputy) were called to the bugyō instead of taking their turn among the attendants, and were not even given a share of the fabric remnants — what is this about? In the Kan'ei 5th year, the Kannushi was Yōkyū. At that time, the cut silk pieces placed in the nagamochi had been sealed, but upon the petition of the chūgen (low-ranking servant), the seal was broken, and in a single night Yōkyū stole everything. He was quite the accomplished thief, but because [those involved] were on bad terms with him, matters came to this pass. Furthermore, no lawsuit came of it, and though various explanations were offered...
