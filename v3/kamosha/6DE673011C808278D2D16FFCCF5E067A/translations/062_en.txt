　　There are two earthenware vessels (土器) each, and there are also pot-shaped earthenwares, so the medicine is placed in these and presented. However, only one cup is used; three remain unused. There are two rod-shaped implements with pouches attached; sake is poured into just one cup, earthenwares are stacked beneath it, and it is placed on a round tray (丸盆) and presented. First, medicine from the Niedo (贄殿, the shrine kitchen/food-preparation hall) is placed in a sake-warming vessel (銚子) and steeped in sake.

　Next, the sacred offerings (神供) of the inner and outer shrines are withdrawn. One person on daily duty remains behind and presents the evening offerings (夕御料). They proceed around the shrine precincts, take their seats at the administrative hall (庁屋), and attend the presentation of the sacred offerings.
　　The subordinate magistrates (下奉行) of the shogunate spread mats in the long gallery (長床) on the west, south, and north sides of the worship hall (拝殿). Mounted officers (与力) and foot soldiers (同心), some twenty to thirty in total, and lower-ranking officials (雑色) make their rounds of the gates. This was not something requested from our side; it was on the order of the magistrate (御奉行).

The 17th Day: There are tasks of storage and cleaning. Beginning today, ritual seclusion (参籠) at Kifune Shrine commences.
　Shrine officials: the Kifune Negi (祢宜, senior priest), the Hafuri (祝, ritualist priest), Narihisa, and a substitute for Yasutaka — [name unclear due to erasure, possibly "Yasunori"]. Yasutaka is eleven years old, hence the substitution due to his young age.
　As for the ujibito (氏人, clan members/subordinate shrine officials): for the First Group, Keiken substitutes for Kiyonori; Second Group, Oribe no Suke Yasuka; Third Group, a substitute for Yukikazu; Fourth Group, Kiyomura; Fifth Group, Saemon no Suke Yasutoshi.
　Today, visits are made to various parties to pay respects. Nothing whatsoever is given to the warrior (武家) side. To the Tenso (伝奏, court liaison to the shogunate): one load of a to-barrel (斗樽), kombu seaweed, sea bream, and dried squid (するめ). To the Ben (弁殿, a court official): one load of a seven-sho barrel (七升樽).
