Ninth Month (a long month of 30 days)

1st Day: Regarding the pine branches from the sacred mountain (Kōyama), eight overseers (bugyō) were privately commissioned, with each person providing two hundred (workers?) as daily wages. Today, 37 bundles each were distributed among approximately 155 persons.

2nd Day: Rainy weather. A gathering was held. I (the author) did not attend.

3rd Day: Rain. The company of attendant monks (gusō) submitted a petition to the imperial intermediaries (tensō), stating their wish to hold a relic veneration ceremony (Shari-kuyō) without fail.
At today's gathering, it was decided that two persons would visit the tensō the following morning.

5th Day: Rain. A gathering was held. The discussion concerned Chikurin-in ■'s visit to the authorities (kōgi) the previous day, along with the notice that if the matter could not be resolved within the shrine community, they were to appear before the authorities on the 7th. Regarding the matters of Kogen, the head official's (bantō) travel land (ōraiden), and the claims of the tenant farmer (gesaku), I did not attend. This was because I serve as the bugyō of the lecture hall (kōsho), and as the monthly duty officer (tsuki-bugyō), I submitted my regrets.
It was decided that the gate of Jūrakuji Temple should be roofed with wooden planks. Furthermore, it was resolved that a large screen wall (ōbyō) of three to four bays (ken) should be erected to the north. However, since there are boundary stones from when the land held by the Matsushita family (the landowner) was purchased from Ujifuku, it was agreed that the parties should gather on-site to verify the boundaries — with Korehisa, Nobunao, Shigetoshi, and Ujisaburai designated to attend.
