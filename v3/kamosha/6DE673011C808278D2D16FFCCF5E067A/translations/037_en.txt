Today, at half past the seventh hour (approximately 5:00 p.m.), the writing was completed. At that moment, light rain fell. [This was taken as] a rain of divine acceptance (go-nōju), and there was boundless joy.

27th Day: A meeting (yoriai) was held, and an inspection (nairan) and accounting (kanjō) were conducted.
　The roofing of the chidan (ritual foundation platform) at the kōsho (lecture hall) was completed. Furthermore, the ridgepole plaque (munafuda) was written by the ujizamurai (clan warrior retainer).

　　　　　Enpō 7th Year (1679), Auspicious Day of the 8th Month

On the 25th of last month, the written reply regarding the sacred relics (shari) was submitted to the Tensō (court liaison officer). [The content was as follows:] This time, in light of comprehensive retrenchment (go-kanryaku), the sacred treasures (go-shinpō) and the various revenues and articles pertaining to the shrine families (shake) are to be curtailed. Therefore, on this occasion, the matter of the sacred relics (shari) is also to be suspended. The precise wording differs [from this summary], but this is the gist of it.
