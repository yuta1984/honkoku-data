Around the 3rd of the month, there was an inquiry from the Densō (Imperial Liaison Officer) regarding matters related to Kibune Shrine, asking how many precedents there were of court nobles (kugyō) making official visits there. In reply: during the Tenshō era, Lord Nakayama the Great Counselor (Dainagon); during the Kan'ei era, Lord Yanagiwara. Since it is a Grand Shrine (taisha) among the Twenty-Two Shrines and has been graced with imperial proclamations (senmyō), it was reported that a petition would be submitted requesting (the continuation of such visits).

7th Day: At the evening assembly, a report was made. Since the two individuals, Hachinojō and Kume, had captured small birds and left them in the custody of the shrine, as an act of apology (wagikoto), Korehisa and Norikisa went today to visit the two magistrates (bugyō). The response was that the matter would also be reported to the Lord of Echizen, and they were told to return home for now.

- Regarding the current shrine reconstruction (gozōei): it is considered a great honor, and it was decided that after covering the expenses for those traveling to Edo, the matter would be reported to the Bakufu (御公儀).
