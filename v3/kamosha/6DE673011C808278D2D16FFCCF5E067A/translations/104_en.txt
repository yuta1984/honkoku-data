7th day: No gathering (yoriai) today.

8th day: Light rain. Today is the day of the kanjō (initiation/consecration ceremony) for Yasuyō, Keiken, and Kyoka. However, since the following day marks the twenty-first day (from the previous ceremony), it was decided that the initiation should be conferred after midnight. Everyone assembled at the lecture hall (kōsho). I (the author) presided over the goma (fire ritual). Hoshinshin conferred (the initiation) upon Yasuyō, and Suenori (季周) conferred it upon Keiken. The three-subject ceremony (sanka gyōji) was conducted by Ujizumi; after the bell-ringing ritual concluded, the initiation was conferred. The proceedings of the kyoka ceremony and those that follow are listed separately.

　Today, the altar decoration and solemn adornments are shown in the figure on the left. Tonight, a wedding ceremony (muko-iri) took place for Gonno-Tayū Yasuke, son of Nishi-Ichinokamikō/Fukuhisa. Additionally, the daughter of Koyōken was married as the wife of Sakon-no-shōgen Ujimichi.
