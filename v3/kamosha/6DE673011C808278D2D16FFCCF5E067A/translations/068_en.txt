(Continued from the previous page) The curtains were shifted southward and stretched around to the east side of the temporary shrine (kariden). On top of these, five-colored decorative hangings (maku) were layered and hung. The temporary pavilion (akuya) was situated between Tama Bridge and Kataoka Bridge, extending two ken (approximately 3.6 meters) east from the river, and one and a half ken (approximately 2.7 meters) in length from north to south. As was done at the time of the main shrine's ceremony, four panels of white cloth were hung 《Interlinear note: at both ends and on both sides of the central ridgepole, totaling four positions》, with two panels of blue cloth among them 《Interlinear note: one panel on each side》. Fifteen reed mats (komo) for the mat pathway (endō), supplied by the Bureau of Cleaning and Furnishings (Kamon-ryō), were laid out.
