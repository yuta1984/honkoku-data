**14th Day:** The installation of the negi-ita (祢板, a type of ceremonial board) at the gatehouse of Jūrakuji Temple was completed.
From today, the chief carpenters (正大工) and blacksmiths entered into ritual confinement (参籠) at the administrative building (庁屋). During the Kan'ei era, this confinement lasted seven days, but on this occasion it is three days. This is because the ritual purification ceremony (清祓) and the御釪 [uncertain reading] are to be performed.

**15th Day:** Clear weather. Preparations for the following day (the Shōsenkū) were made, and two 肝煎 (kimoire, coordinators/supervisors) from each group of the visiting dignitaries' party convened to determine the administrators (奉行) for all matters.
The yin-yang master (陰陽師) Sōken performed the nighttime purification ritual (清祓).

**16th Day:** Clear skies. The formal shrine relocation ceremony (正遷宮) of the Main Shrine (本宮) was held at the Hour of the Dog (戌時, approximately 8 p.m.).
Around the Hour of the Ram (未刻, approximately 2 p.m.), the Imperial Liaison (御伝奏) Kanjūji Chūnagon Nagayoshi, Seikanji Ben-dono Hirosada, the Vice Official (次官) Karahashi Shikibu-no-shōjō-dono, the Daigeki (大外記), the Kanmu (官務), and various envoys (諸使) each visited the residences of the shrine families (社家). Around this time, written notices of lodging assignments (宿札) were quietly circulated to each lodging establishment.

Next, the officials assembled together to coordinate the proceedings for that evening, and then made a visit to the shrine (社参) to inspect the state of the shrine precincts (社頭). All were dressed in hunting robes (狩衣) and eboshi caps (烏帽子). At this time, the son of Seikanji Dainagon-dono accompanied the group as part of his official duties.
