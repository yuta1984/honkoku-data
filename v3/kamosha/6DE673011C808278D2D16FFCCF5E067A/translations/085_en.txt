10th (Clear) As part of the "chintan" ceremony at the lecture hall (kōsho), the purification and decoration of the Tsuchidono (土殿) and a general cleaning were carried out. Around this time, the role-players (yakusha) were going out to various places to pay respects (o-rei) for the shrine's relocation ceremony (o-sengū). During a gathering at Igarashi Sōbei's residence, it was conveyed that since the Shinke Bugyō (officials overseeing new households) were visiting various places, it would be appropriate to submit a written record of the number of houses within the Kamo precinct.

For the striking fee (uchichin) for two swords (odachi) and two halberds (hoko), approximately five silver coins (ginshi) were paid to the swordsmith Izumi.

To the Tenyaku no kami (Head of the Bureau of Medicine), one silver coin was given as a gift in return for three sets of toso (spiced sake for New Year) and byakusan (medicinal powder). To Kanō Nuinosuke, five silver coins were given. (Marginal note: Two coins were sent to his disciple Gonnoshin, as they were going up to Kibune Shrine.)

Regarding the temporary shrines (karidono) of the eight shrines, notices were sent out via the jitte (十手, administrative relay) that requests could be made at a rate of 500 people per main shrine and 100 people per subordinate shrine. However, the allocation for Kibune Shrine was 200 people — this pertains to the temporary shrine of the main shrine — due to the long distance. There were fourteen or fifteen people acting as intermediaries (kuchire). The temporary structure (kariya) for housing the sacred treasures of the provisional shrine (gondono) cost one kanmon.
