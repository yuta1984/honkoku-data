The 24th Day
Tonight, the Shōsengū (formal enshrinement ceremony) was performed at Tanao Shrine, Hajio Shrine, Sugio Shrine, and Hashifuno Shrine. The head priest oversaw the sacred transfer, with the following groups of ujibito (shrine community members) in attendance: the 6th group (Mondo no suke Hiroyoshi), the 7th group (Saemon no taifu Munemura), the 8th group (Sueyasu), the 9th group (Mikawa no suke Sueki, acting as deputy for Mondo no suke), and the 10th group (Kotosa, acting as deputy for Zusho no suke, without stipend/rank).

The 25th Day
Rain fell from the previous night, clearing around noon. Today, a gathering was held at the pilgrimage lodge (sankōya). An earnest invitation was extended to a certain □□ head priest who had come down from Edo, but it was declined on the grounds that there was no one to formally receive the role.
Tonight, the Shōsengū ceremony was performed at six shrines: Yamao Shrine, Fujio Shrine, Kawao Shrine, Suō Shrine, Kajita Shrine, and Iwamoto Shrine. The officiants were the same as before.

Arrangements regarding the lodging for the Imperial Messenger (the Counselor and various scribes) scheduled for the 27th were deliberated. It was also decided that Kyūma Hachijōjō's relatives would be asked to submit a petition, and that the appropriate lodging assignments would be determined and a written notice sent to the Imperial Liaison (Gidensō) the following day.
