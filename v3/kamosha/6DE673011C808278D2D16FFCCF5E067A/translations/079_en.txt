Jōe-shū (members of the white-robed attendants): thirty persons (it is noted that not a single one remains/is absent)

Next year will be a Sō-hanagaeshi (general reversal/full turnover), and the fact that not a single one remains is unprecedented.

Five Mountain Wardens (Gonin-yamamori)

Second Day, Clear: A meeting (yoriai) was held. All the furnishings of the inner sanctuary (naijin) of the Kifune Rear Shrine (Kibune Okumiya) — including the hamayuka (ceremonial platform/dais) and kichō (curtain stands), among other items — were found to be entirely missing. [This matter involves] Motome Hidenao, the fifth son of Yamajiki.

A letter was submitted requesting repairs to the inner sanctuary (naijin) of the Kamo Shrine in Mikawa Province, which has fallen into considerable disrepair.
Furthermore, regarding a matter that has been raised for some time concerning the hand-duties (teshiki): it was stated that the inner sanctuary of the Muro Myōjin in Banshu (Harima Province) is also in disrepair, and a request was made to apply for repairs under the allocation for the subordinate shrine (gonsha) as well.
It was determined that while these matters should be communicated to the respective parties, it would be proper to submit them once more to a council (shūhyō) for deliberation. It was also raised that, for a request of this magnitude, it would be appropriate to include a gift (reimotsu) when making the application.
