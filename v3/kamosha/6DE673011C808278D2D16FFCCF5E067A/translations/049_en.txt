(The old curtains,) only the replaced ones were taken out and sent to the Chief Priest's residence (神主亭) for storage. They were then promptly brought and handed over in the middle corridor area.

**Substitute attendants for shrine officials:** In place of Ōta Priest (太田禰宜) Suechika, his son Suezane attended; in place of the Shingū Celebrant (新宮祝), Norichisa attended; in place of the Kataoka Priest (片岡禰宜), Kumanosuke attended.

In the evening, a gathering (寄合) was held, at which the following matters were reported: First, an account of the proceedings at yesterday's meeting with the Imperial Messenger (御伝奏). Next, discussion about the person who was to travel to Edo to pay respects (御礼). Next, regarding the matter of the temporary pavilion (幄屋) to be erected at Kifune Shrine, a notice was given that an official (from the Bureau of Carpentry, 木工寮) would arrive on the eleventh day, and discussion was held about who would serve as guide (案内者).

Since the time of the enshrining ceremony (遷宮) coincided with the same hour (as another ceremony), this would cause inconvenience for the Taguchi party. Additionally, regarding whether to submit a request to the lower magistrate (下奉行) of the warrior class simultaneously — since there was no precedent, it was decided that no such request should be made. There was also the matter of the offerings (進上物) to be presented when the Imperial Envoy (勅使) arrives, and the matter of the order of entertainment (馳走) to be provided by boat (木船).

**Tenth Day.** In the morning, the thatched roof coverings at the gate of Jūrakuji Temple were peeled back (rolled up). Six people from the same neighborhood were dispatched to remove the old thatch.

In the afternoon, the sacred treasures (御神宝) were transported — by four local men (地下四人) — from the Chief Priest's residence to the Sacred Treasure House (神宝屋) at the shrine precincts. Furthermore, curtains, cloth, and other such items were brought into the administrative building (庁屋). (Continues on the next page.)
