[Continued from the previous page: The reception of the imperial decree at Kataoka Shrine]

Next, after placing one scroll of the imperial decree (senjī) into the lid of a traveling basket (kori), the ritual priest (hafuri-shoku) Shukkyū received it, had the attendant in plain silk robes (hōi) carry the basket, and [Shukkyū] inspected (hiken) the decree while standing.
(Marginal note: The wind was blowing and made it difficult to read. Not knowing the proper ancient precedent [for how to handle this], it was quite a sorry sight.)
After inspecting [the decree], he re-rolled it as before, had the attendant in hōi carry it, took out the ceremonial baton (shaku) he had been keeping in his breast fold, and once again both parties bowed simultaneously (ichiyu).

Next, [the decree] was offered and presented before the temporary shrine hall (kari-dono) of Kataoka. After placing it there, [Shukkyū] turned and walked back westward along the eave of the bridge hall (hashidono), faced the imperial decree messenger (senjī-tsukai), and bowed once. The imperial decree messenger then returned the bow.

Next, the decree messenger, accompanied by Hoshu and Nobuaki, took their seats in the office chamber (chōya). Shukkyū retrieved (tetsu) the decree that had been presented and inspected before the deity, and at the ofuda-ya (御札屋, the talisman house) of the post-ceremony banquet hall (naoraisho) at the shrine precinct, the shrine officials (shashi) and clan members (ujibito) inspected it.

[Marginal annotation:]
Properly speaking, Kataoka Shrine should have been attended to first in this ceremony. Furthermore, [the decree] should have been received by the chief priest (kannushi) at the bridge hall (hashidono). However, returning [to attend to this] would cause undue delay. Privately, an instruction came from the Transmission Official (Tensō) to consider past precedents — both those in which Kataoka Shrine was attended to first, and those in which Kifune Shrine was attended to first — and to compile and submit a written report. Accordingly, a written memorandum was submitted [to the authorities]. Based on this, on the present occasion, the inner shrine of Kifune (Kifune Oku-sha) being a newly reconstructed shrine building, Kifune was attended to first today. This was done in accordance with the instruction [from above].
