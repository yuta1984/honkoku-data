Regarding the paddy fields (tanden) that have long been under the jurisdiction of the Genban-gashira (the head of a certain administrative post), in the current year both parties have cultivated the land and the proceeds have been divided between the two. It was decided that from next year onward, the current cultivator shall continue to work the land. It is said that this field, which normally yields a land tax (nengu-mai) of one to five shō, has produced approximately four to of surplus rice (jōmai).

3rd day, clear. This evening, a gathering of newly appointed shrine officials (shinpo no shashi) was held at the lecture hall (kōsho). The purpose was to hear the explanations of those among the seven families who had not attended the previous evening. The messengers sent to deliver replies to the four families — Mori, Tomino, Umetsuji, and Toriiooji — were two individuals: Kura no Gon'nosuke and Tsushima-no-Kami. The Senior Negi (shō-negi) Yorihisa and the Senior Hōri (shō-hōri) Shigetoshi were present at the assembly. The replies from the three families — Tomino, Umetsuji, and Toriiooji — stated that it was most auspicious and praiseworthy that the junior officials (matsushoku no shū) should duly fulfill their duties as shrine officers (tsukasakudomo) during the formal Sengū ceremony.

The reply from Mori Korihisa stated: "I consider it most proper and admirable that the shrine officers perform their duties at that shrine. Mori holds the same view. However, there is a rumor circulating that the precedent cited on this occasion was put forward by the Mori family, and of late this has caused us considerable distress. In light of this, it was suggested that the newly appointed members should submit a written oath (seijō), but this proposal was withdrawn by those present at the assembly."
