(Continued from previous page) On the 9th of last month, there was stone-laying work at the Kōsho (lecture hall) and the Chintanjo (a ritual purification hall).

12th day: Clear weather. Word arrived that, as a mark of gratitude for yesterday's ceremony, Lord Echizen-no-kami and Nakai Mondo would be coming up [to the shrine] the next day for an inspection (o-miwake). In response, the members of the Cleaning Confraternity (Sōji-kō) cleaned the shrine precincts through the night, laid the Misono Bridge, and constructed a road. Regarding the Horikawa area, word was sent as far as the Yatone party, and the reply was that a bridge would be laid. Instructions were sent that if it proved impossible to lay a bridge, the water channels should be cut off and the water allowed to dry up.

Today there was a meeting (yoriai), but I did not attend.

13th day: Clear weather. Lord Toda Echigo-no-kami Tadatsugu, Lord Makita Aki-no-kami, as well as members of the Inspector corps (yokome-shū)—or perhaps others, two additional persons—accompanied by Nakai Mondo, came to conduct an inspection of the construction works (go-zōei), making a thorough tour of Shimogamo Shrine and then of this shrine.
The manner of their reception was as follows: first, the two Zasshō (administrative officers) went out to meet them at the area of Odoro Pond (o-doro-ike) and led them forward. Outside the Okamoto Gate, seven or eight persons dressed in formal sleeveless jackets and hakama (katagimu-hakama) went out to receive them. At Ōta Shrine, two or three persons beyond the two senior officials (ryōkan) waited in the vicinity of Ōta. Next, the party proceeded from Yamamoto-chō to the Jingūji (shrine temple), where the head priest and others waited at the area near the archery range (iba) of the Jingūji.
