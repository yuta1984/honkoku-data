Kibune (Shrine assignment): Tsuchiya Jirōemon, Takahashi Seiuemon
Shimogamo (Shrine assignment): Katō Sobuemon, Okumura Jūzaemon
Two persons each were assigned, all under the jurisdiction of Lord Toda Echizen-no-kami.

The aforementioned persons were to receive a daily rice stipend of seven shō and five gō each, and were to attend daily service accompanied by:
one spear-bearer, one manservant (chūgen), one luggage-box carrier, and one lunchbox carrier—
four attendants each in total.
Furthermore, the doshin (lower-ranking officials) were to receive a daily allowance of two shō and five gō each.
