Regarding each shrine's rituals at the (Kamo) shrines, the intention is that the transfer of the shrine deity (sengū) should be duly performed.
The shrine chief priest (kannagi) stated that this is entirely appropriate, and that if past precedents are presented, there is nothing further to discuss — it is an auspicious matter. The precedents are recorded above (in the preceding entry).

Of the Seven Families (shichike-shū), those present were: Ho (Ka?), Nobuhisa, and Shigetomi; those absent were: Korehisa, Norihisa, Narihisa, and Junpei. The two individuals, Suemichi and ■Hisa, were consulted via messenger.

Next, using the surplus silver from the current construction project (zōei), the following items are to be attended to: the court robes (hō) for the costumes worn at the horse races (kōmabe) and below the steps (kaishita), the court robes for the costume worn by the kannagi while seated, the right-side sleeves (migi-kata no sode), as well as the hitoe-hakama (sashigama) and formal robes (daimon) of shizuori fabric for the first day of the month (tsuitachi), and the repair of the dengaku (ritual field music) equipment.

The assembly deliberated as follows:
Regarding the shizuori daimon and sashigama for the first day of the month, these have since ancient times been the financial responsibility (mōke no yaku) of the head priest. In that case, should the daimon for successive generations of the Shoshidai (Deputy) and Mōdai (Representative) also be commissioned? Furthermore, the other shrine officials (shashiku-shū) would also desire the sashigama.
