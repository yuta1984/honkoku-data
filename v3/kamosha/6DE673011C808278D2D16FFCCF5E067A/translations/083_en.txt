Based on a copy brought by Sueharu (季芳), transcribed from Kyoto.

Items prepared and presented (調進) by the Left Board Steward (左弁掌, i.e., the Imperial Treasury, Naizōryō) as offerings (幣物) from the Imperial Palace (禁中) at the time of the Grand Shrine Rebuilding Ceremony (正遷宮) of this shrine (Kamo Shrine):

- Five-colored twill silk (綾); pattern: standing hollyhock (立葵)　　- Five-colored silk (加賀silk from Kaga Province)
- Cotton: one kin [approx. 600g] ※Note: this refers to cotton thread; "cloth" here means raw cotton　　- Hemp: one kin
- White cloth: one tan [approx. 10 meters] ※bleached cloth (sarashi)　　- White thread: one kin ※refers to silk thread
- Gold wand offering (金幣): one

These six categories of items are placed in a willow box (柳筥), accompanied by a letter of transmittal.

The same applies to Kifune Shrine. However, the pattern is small hollyhock (小葵), and the wand offering is a silver wand (銀幣).
Items prepared by the Imperial Treasury (内蔵寮):
- Five-colored silk　- White cloth: one tan　- Hemp
- Cotton　- White thread
These items are wrapped in cloth, tied so that the contents are not visible, and placed in a willow box. They are assembled into three bundles.

In the Kan'ei 5th year (1628), as the Imperial Treasury (内蔵寮) may not have existed, the Superintendent of Accounts (出納) and the Bureau of the Treasury (大蔵) [handled this matter]. Because [a certain person] served as the annual administrator (年預) of the Imperial Treasury, the Superintendent of Accounts [presented offerings] to this shrine and Kifune [Shrine]...
