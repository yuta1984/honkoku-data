One: Tomorrow, Yasutsu will be leaving the capital on private business, so he left instructions with Harima-no-suke to handle any official matters during his absence. He also asked Hokaku to assist. Since Hokaku was away, he conveyed the message to the household and then departed. He also mentioned this matter at the meeting hall.

18th Day, Hour of the Dragon, Clear, with occasional light showers from time to time.

One: Today, the representatives Narikahisa and Hokaku brought the official rank petition (koorizami) to Lord Hino-Ben, where it was indicated that the precedent record (kanrei) for Nagasue was insufficient and should be rewritten and resubmitted. Furthermore, it was pointed out that the precedent record for Suenaga did not qualify for the Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade (Jushii-no-jō). Hokaku explained that among the shrine priests (shashi) from Shingū and below, there are no precedents stronger than fifty-two years of age for the Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade; for those without office (mushoku), there are precedents at fifty-one years of age; and among the seven-family shrine priests, there are precedents ranging from thirty to forty-four or forty-five years of age. However, in recent years, the seven families have submitted precedents according to the seven-family rules, the shrine priests from Shingū and below according to their own rules, and those without office according to their own rules, which is why the records stand as they are. This explanation was accepted and heard.

In that case, it was directed that the precedent record for Suenaga be left as it stands, and that all remaining examples within the shrine community of persons who had been recommended for the Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade while under the age of fifty be written out and submitted. This was acknowledged, and he withdrew. Upon returning home, those who attended the small assembly (ko-yoriai) were the head priests Narikahisa, Yoriak, and Hokaku. Yasutsu did not attend due to his departure from the capital. Regarding the matter, while all precedents of the Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade for those under fifty should be written out, at Hokaku's discretion the following eight persons were selected, and after consultation among the head priests, a decision was reached.

　Conferred Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade (No Office, 7 years in rank): Kanenari (age 51)
　Conferred Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade (Head Priest, 3 years in rank): Yasunori (age 31)
　Conferred Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade (Senior Celebrant, 4 years in rank): Shigeto (age 30)
　Conferred Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade (Senior Negi, 4 years in rank): Tomohisa (age 31)
　Conferred Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade (No Office, 9 years in rank): Suminao (age 52)
　Conferred Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade (No Office, 10 years in rank): Suetomo (age 51)
　Conferred Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade (Head Priest, 6 years in rank): Masahisa (age 42)
　Conferred Junior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade (Wakamiya Celebrant, 5 years in rank): Kunishi (age 53)

　Precedent Record for Nagasue:
　Conferred Junior Fifth Rank, Upper Grade (12 years in rank): Narimori (age 56)
　Conferred Junior Fifth Rank, Upper Grade (14 years in rank): Suekiyo (age 54)

19th Day, Hour of the Snake, Clear.
