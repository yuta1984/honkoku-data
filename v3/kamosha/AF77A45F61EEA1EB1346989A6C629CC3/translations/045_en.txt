As the exchanges of questions and answers continued in this exhaustive manner one by one, night had fallen. In the end, it was directed that [the parties] should submit a single written document and then depart.

Item: A copy of the document submitted by the *shashi* party

　　　　　　Memorandum (*Oboegaki*)

We are most grateful to learn that the *kuzen* (imperial oral decree) for the position of *gon-hafuri* (*gon-kōhaku*) has been received. However, [the person in question] performed no *haiga* (formal worship) before the deity, made no public announcement (*hirō*) within the shrine, made no renewal of the sacred amulets (*ofuda*) before the deity, performed none of the required ritual protocols (*shahō*) toward the *kannagi*, performed none of the proper divine duties (*kamiyaku*), and only served as a substitute (*daiyaku*)—and that person died eight years ago. Moreover, despite being granted *ōrai-den* (transit lands) as an *ujibito* and performing *ujibito* duties, and despite having received the *kuzen*, [the family] did not succeed to the professional duties of a *shashi*, omitted the prescribed rites (*shahō*), and now invokes shrine precedents (*shareí*)—all of this amounts to a disregard for the divine will (*shinryo*) and is the height of impropriety. In particular, contrary to what was entrusted by the *shō-hafuri* (chief *hafuri*), they have unlawfully appropriated (*ōryō*) the *hafuri*'s stipend (*hafurishiro*). We respectfully request that an inquiry be made as to what precedent was cited in submitting the petition for this *gon-hafuri kuzen*. Furthermore, the fact that [this matter] has deceived the divine will and been concealed until now is utterly beyond the pale. There has never been any instance of appointing an *ujibito* to the position of *shashi*. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　Twelfth month, 18th day　　　　The *shashi* of Kamo Shrine [collective]
