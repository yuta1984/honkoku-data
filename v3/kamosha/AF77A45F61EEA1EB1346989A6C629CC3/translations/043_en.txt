○ The *ujibito* stated: "It was submitted on the basis of the precedent of the *kuzen* for the office of Shigéaki (*Jūshū*), which Kaan the Elder just presented for [the Kampaku's] inspection. Namely, Jūshū was Jūsetsu's great-grandfather."

○ The *shashi* stated: "Did Jūsetsu perform the *haiga* (formal congratulatory audience) for the office of *gongohaku*?"

○ The *ujibito* stated: "He did perform the *haiga*. Shall we not object to the *shashi* side's claim that they performed the *haiga*? [Jūsetsu] received the *kuzen* and proceeded with the *haiga* as was proper. Yet in their earlier statement, [the *shashi* side] claimed that 'in recent times, a *kuzen* for the office has not been received.' In that case, when the *shashi* side speaks of '*haiga*,' are they referring to a *haiga* for the conferment of court rank (*joshaku*) and the like?"

○ The *shashi* stated: "There are precedents for the son of a *shashi* from another shrine coming and succeeding to the office. However, there is no precedent for an *ujibito* succeeding to the position."

○ The *ujibito* stated: "If even a person from another shrine may succeed by precedent, then all the more so should a member of the *ujibito* (*ujigami* people) of the Kamo *agataonushi* (district chieftains) be the one to succeed in order of precedence."

○ The *shashi* stated: "As a precedent for an *ujibito* not succeeding, there is the case of Matsushita Motohisa, who was appointed *kannagi* (*shinushi*, shrine priest) at the age of four."

○ The *ujibito* stated: "As Motohisa was the *sōryō* (legitimate heir) of the Matsushita [family], even if [the heir] were two years old, he should be made to succeed. As for other vacant offices, who other than an *ujibito* would——" [text continues on following page]
