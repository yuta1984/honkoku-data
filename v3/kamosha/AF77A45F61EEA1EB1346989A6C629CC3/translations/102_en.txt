One: Regarding those who have been confined (rōsha) as a penalty (katai), the council of adjudicators (hyōjōshū) shall deliberate and set a fixed number of days for their confinement; upon the expiration of that term, they shall be released from custody. Furthermore, any items held in custody shall not be retained for an extended period; they shall be promptly investigated and returned without delay.

One: Regarding those who have received an official endorsement (urajihan) or a summons (meshijō) but have appeared late, the distance of their place of residence shall be taken into consideration, the number of days calculated accordingly, and, depending on the gravity of their offense, they shall be subjected to either confinement (rōsha) or a monetary fine (kamoryō).

The articles set forth above are to be strictly observed.
