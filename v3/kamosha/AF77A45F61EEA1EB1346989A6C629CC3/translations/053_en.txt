One item: Regarding the matter of ascending to the inner sanctuary (Shōden)

There are many Shasha who ascend to the inner sanctuary in accordance with the scale of their positions. Furthermore, brothers of Shasha who hold no position also ascend to the inner sanctuary. This is precisely the customary practice by which Ujibito ascend to the inner sanctuary. As has been stated repeatedly, those who hold no shrine position are all Ujibito. Moreover, even among the brothers of Shasha, those who have no such aspiration do not ascend to the inner sanctuary even to this day.

　　　　　　The above concludes [this section].

One item: As [a document was] sent up to the Lady of the In'no Gosho [Imperial Consort's Residence] from the Shasha side, [a petition] was submitted with a letter from the Lady of the In, and a copy of [what was] presented to the Emperor is as follows:

　　　"Not even knowing whether I should be called a Negi [shrine priest],
　　　　how can I serve the mighty gods?"　　　　　Kamo no Chōmei

　　　(Written upon seeing the hollyhock [Aoi], having withdrawn from shrine society as his wishes had not been fulfilled, and living in seclusion)
　　　《Shin Kokinshū, Book 18》

　　　"When I see them, yet again my tears fall like the twin strands of Morokatsura—
　　　　what vow was made, that they should separate so?"

　　　　　19th day of the 11th month

[The oral explanation states:] It was submitted [to the Emperor] that Kamo no Chōmei had desired the position of Negi, but because he was merely an Ujibito, his wish could not be fulfilled, and thus he composed this poem.
