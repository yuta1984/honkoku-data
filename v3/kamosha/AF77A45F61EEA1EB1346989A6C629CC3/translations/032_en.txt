　　　　　　Memorandum

　　　　　　　　　《As written in the original (a frequently appearing phrase)》
　　　Ujibito Shigeto, a descendant (祖子) of the Sei-Hafuri of Kamo Shrine, was placed in custody as a deputy (代) of the Gon-Hafuri; however, it has been reported that (the Gon-Hafuri) is claiming full authority (正体) in the matter. There is absolutely no precedent for this. Since (Shigeto) has served until today as deputy of the Gon-Hafuri, we intend to present our argument in full accordance with shrine law. Should the matter we have presented prove to be false, we are prepared to accept whatever investigation or punishment may be ordered. Written accordingly.
