As they had stated their intentions, the written oath (okibumi) concerning the succession of the five families, which had been previously determined through consultation, was read aloud to all parties. Once everyone had given their consent, a petition for sequential transfer of positions (shidai tennin) and two types of written documents were submitted. These documents were then submitted to the magistrate's office (Bugyōsho) on this occasion, and they exist in sealed form with witnesses from the shrine community present. The details can be found in the shrine's daily records (nisshiki). The petition for sequential transfer of positions submitted at that time is recorded below.

　　　A respectfully submitted oral petition

　On this occasion, it has been ordered that written documents be submitted listing those persons eligible for succession of the five families — namely, those who have branched off from the five families and those who branched off long ago. The entire shrine community acknowledges and humbly accepts this order, and hereby submits the written documents listing the said individuals after due consideration. In this regard, the chief priest (kannushi) Shigetsuna states that, concerning the succession of the five families on this occasion, he wishes to petition on behalf of the entire shrine community that succession be entrusted to the following five individuals: his eldest son Shigeharu, his second son Jirōmaru, his two brothers Shigefuru and Jūyū, and Okamoto Kunai's younger brother Jimatsudayū Horei.
