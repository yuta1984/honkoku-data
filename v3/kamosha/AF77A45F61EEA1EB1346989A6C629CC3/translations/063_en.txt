Item: Regarding the claim that the jinryō (personnel stipends) for the 28th day of the 10th month should be performed in accordance with established precedents — as stated in my previous submission, if the shrine estates (sharyo) remain unchanged from before, then the matter should proceed according to precedent. However, given that the shrine estates are no longer in their former condition, to pursue litigation (訴詔) insisting that the jinryō for the 28th day of the 10th month alone should follow precedent, while the performance of shrine rituals throughout the year is affected, is not appropriate for the sake of prayers for the peace of the realm. Though [Shigenori] immediately wished to raise this matter, both parties were urged twice by the shrine administrator (shabu) to hold consultations and proceed peacefully — yet [the opposing party] ultimately refused to comply. Does this then mean that Shigenori caused the neglect (kettai) of the rituals?

Furthermore, the claim that revenues from the sacred estates (jinryō) were appropriated for private use is an extremely rash allegation. The annual tribute (tōkō) from the sacred estates, from the 1st through the 12th month, should be calculated as to how much is consumed in performing shrine rituals and whether any surplus remains — this is a matter requiring proper accounting. A petition (mōshijō) that fails to address this is so deficient that it is not even worth debating.

Item: Regarding the matter of neglected shrine duties (kettai) during the Ōei era — whether such negligence occurred over several hundred years — even if it spans more than a hundred years, how could neglect of shrine duties ever be concealed? Furthermore, regarding the claim that there was no negligence in Bunmei 1 (1469): the officials of the shrine administration, the Kibune [shrine], and the uji-bito community are all well aware of the many instances of shortfalls in the jinryō payments.

Item: Regarding the matter of there being a suitable person to be appointed to the position of Gon-hōri yet the appointment being unduly delayed and withheld — if this is denied, then this article has no meaning whatsoever.
