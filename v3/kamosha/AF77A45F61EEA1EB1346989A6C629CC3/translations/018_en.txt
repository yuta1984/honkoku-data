Now that the five families have been given these orders, we request that, with the exception of the Hayashi and Okamoto families, appointments and transfers of positions be made in accordance with shrine law, and that for the remaining six positions, as has been the custom, suitable individuals be selected from within the shrine community and newly appointed as directed. This would accord with the provisions of the Kōnin Code, uphold ancient laws and shrine precedents, prevent any disorder or disputes, and bring peace and stability to the entire shrine community. We have absolutely no intention of defying the current orders; however, we humbly petition that measures be taken so that, for years to come, no such grievances will arise, and that divine rites and festivals may be performed without incident and with sincerity. For this reason, we have made this appeal, and we humbly request that you understand the above and grant our petition as stated. Respectfully submitted.

Representative of the entire Kamo Shrine community
19th day of the 2nd month, Year of the Ox
Fujiki Daizen Gonnosuke
