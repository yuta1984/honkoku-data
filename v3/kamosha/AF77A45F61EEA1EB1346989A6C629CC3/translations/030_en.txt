　　　　General Genealogy — Records of Appointments from Ujibito to Negi and Hafuri

《First son of Shrine Head (Kannagi) Narisuke》
Entry: Sadanari — On the eleventh day of the fourth month of Eishō 5 (1050), when his father Narisuke was promoted from Kibune Negi to Gon-Negi, Sadanari was appointed from the status of ujibito to Kibune Negi.

《Fifth son of Shrine Head Narika》
Entry: Narinobu — On the twenty-seventh day of the twelfth month of Kyūju 2 (1155), appointed from ujibito to Ōta Hafuri. In Chōkan 2 (1164), promoted from Ōta Hafuri to Gon-Hafuri.

《First son of Shrine Head Narihira》
Entry: Iehira — On the second day of the fifth month of Hōen 2 (1136), appointed from ujibito to Kibune Negi, filling the vacancy left by his father Narihira. On the twelfth day of the tenth month of Jōan 4 (1174), appointed Shrine Head.

《First son of Shrine Head Narihisa》
Entry: Yasuhisa — On the ninth day of the eleventh month of Chōshō 1 (1132), appointed from ujibito to Kibune Negi. On the twenty-ninth day of the eleventh month of Ninpei 2 (1152), appointed Shrine Head.

《First son of Shrine Head Yasuhisa》
Entry: Hisanori — On the fourteenth day of the sixth month of Ninpei 4 (1154), appointed from ujibito to Kibune Negi. Later promoted to Gon-Negi.
　　　　　　[Personal note:] The Daiki records this as the fourteenth day of the sixth month of Kyūju 1 (1154).

《Second son of Shrine Head Yasuhisa》
Entry: Sukeyasu — On the twentieth day of the fourth month of Nin'an 2 (1167), appointed from ujibito to Ōta Hafuri. On the fifteenth day of the ninth month of Kenkyū 4 (1193), appointed Shrine Head.
