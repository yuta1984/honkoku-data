○ The *shashi* stated: "Even at times when the number of *shashi* personnel was small, there has been no instance of succession from among the many *ujibito*."

○ The *ujibito* stated: "The lapsing of *negi* and *hafuri* positions at branch shrines is something that both the *shashi* and the *ujibito* have mutually acknowledged over many years. Nevertheless, although the *negi* position at Kifune Shrine had long been vacant as well, there is the case of Matsushita Tonomo-no-kami who, at the age of thirty, served as *negi* for the first time this past third month of the present year."

○ The *shashi* stated: "Ōkura-shōyu Jūsetsu is definitively holding (*chigyō*) the *kyūden* (stipend lands) and *ōrai-den* (transit lands) of the *ujibito*."

○ The *ujibito* stated: "Not limited to Jūsetsu—whenever there is a *shashi* whose circumstances are poor, if he performs the role of *shōjin-gashira* (head of ritual abstinence), the *ōrai-den* should always be given to him."

○ The *shashi* stated: "Regarding the nomination of Jūsetsu as *gon-hafuri* (*gongohaku*), we have not received even a single word of notification."
