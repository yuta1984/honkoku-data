【Regarding the Ongoing Disputes Between Shrine Officials (Shashi) and Ujibito】

Item: In the first year of Kyōhō (1716), following customary practice, there were those among the ujibito who wished to petition for court ranks. Lord Konoe stated that he found it disorderly that the ujibito had been, in recent years, increasingly petitioning for court ranks at younger ages, and instructed that they should henceforth consult thoroughly with the chief priest (kannushi). In response, the representatives of our side stated that in matters pertaining to Kamo Shrine, whether great or small—even the most trivial matters—the chief priest and other shrine officials (shashi) and ujibito would convene meetings to deliberate. In particular, regarding court ranks, they would gather for consecutive days to research precedents (kan'rei) in every detail…
