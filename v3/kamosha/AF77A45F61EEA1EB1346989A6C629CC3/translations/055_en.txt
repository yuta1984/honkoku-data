Item: On the 21st day of the 11th month of Kanei 8 (1631), the shrine officials (社司) submitted a further petition (koorizami/小折紙). The contents were as follows:

　　Petition: Junior Fifth Rank, Lower Grade　　　　Shrine Chief (kannagi): Yukihisa
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　（age 25）
　　Petition: Shingū Negi (Associate Priest of the New Shrine)　　　Hirohumi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　（age 19）
　　Petition: Shingū Hafuri (Ritual Priest of the New Shrine)　　　Hirohisa
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　（age 13）
　　Petition: Ōta Negi (Associate Priest of Ōta Shrine)　　　　　　Sukehisa

　　Although the petition was submitted in this manner, the response from Lord Mitsuhiro stated that, given the disputes of previous years, the matter should be thoroughly investigated before submission, and thus no resolution was reached.

Item: Recorded as told by Kise Kichijūrō, a retainer of the Konoe household:

　　On the 17th day of the 12th month of Kanei 4 (1627), Shōhafuri Nyūdō Kean (the chief ritual priest who had taken Buddhist vows) visited the Kampaku (Imperial Regent), and presented the kuzen (official appointment document) for the position of Shigaki. Furthermore, a written document was previously submitted to the Kampaku stating that in Keichō 18 (1613), Shigetsugu was appointed as Gon-hafuri (Provisional Ritual Priest), and in Genna 1 (1615), was formally appointed as Shōhafuri (Chief Ritual Priest).

Item: After this, when rumors spread that the shrine officials' claims were unlikely to be accepted, the shrine officials, in deep dissatisfaction, reportedly expressed their intention to collectively submit letters of resignation for all the positions they currently held. Upon hearing this, the clan members (ujibito) again submitted a written statement of reasons (risho) to the Tensō (Imperial Liaison). The record of this submission follows:

　　　　　　Respectfully submitted:

Item: Regarding the sacred ceremony of the Otanagakai (御棚会) and the Hōhei (奉幣, offering of sacred cloth) on the night of the 14th of this month.

Item: Regarding the sacred ceremony, the opening of the sacred doors (御戸開), and the duties within the inner sanctuary (内陣) on the early morning of the 15th of the same month.
