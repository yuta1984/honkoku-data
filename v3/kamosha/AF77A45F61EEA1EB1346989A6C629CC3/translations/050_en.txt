Item: Regarding the succession to positions at other shrines, such cases may well exist. Nevertheless, given the numerous precedents of *ujibito* being appointed to such positions, it would be difficult to sustain [the *shashi*'s argument]. Furthermore, the practice of *ujibito* serving as *shashi* is not limited to this shrine; there are precedents at other shrines as well. Most notably, at Kasuga Shrine, it is a well-established fact that *ujibito* have been appointed as *kannagi* (chief priests).

Item: In Keichō 19 (1614), when we submitted a petition that the *ujibito* Jusetsu should be appointed to the office of *Gon-hafuri* (Provisional Chaplain), the *shashi* stated: "Since Jūji, the son of the former *Shō-hafuri*, was appointed *Gon-hafuri* in Keichō 18 (1613), Jusetsu cannot become *Gon-hafuri*, as there is no precedent for two persons holding the same office concurrently." In response to this, on the eighteenth day [of a certain month], both parties were summoned to the residence of His Highness, and an inquiry was conducted into the matter of the *Gon-hafuri* appointment. When it was commanded that the *kuzен* (imperial appointment document) by which Jūji had been appointed *Gon-hafuri* be produced for inspection, it became apparent that no such *kuzen* for Jūji's office existed. Nevertheless, Jūji presented a *kuzen* for a rank promotion (*kаkyū*) in place of an appointment document, claiming it was the document for the office. His Highness rejected this and expressed deep suspicion. Thereupon, when the *kuzen* appointing the *ujibito* Jusetsu to his office was summoned for inspection, it was declared to be genuine and proper. In order to escape this blunder, the *shashi* and others pleaded that "in recent times, we have not received *kuzen* for [shrine] offices." However, if they had indeed not received *kuzen* in recent times, why had they previously claimed that Jūji received a *kuzen* for the office in Keichō 18? Their self-contradiction within that very meeting was as plain as turning over one's hand. As has been pointed out, if no *kuzen* was received, what evidence can they produce for privately styling themselves *negi* and *hafuri*?
