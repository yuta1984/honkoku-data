　Regarding the above: documentary evidence (証文) has been submitted showing that ujibito have been appointed to the positions of Negi and Hafuri. In response, an order was issued stating that if there are any old records (旧記) indicating that ujibito do not become shrine administrators, those should be submitted. However, no such records were produced, and yet unfounded articles continue to be raised. In sum, given that no documents have been submitted to counter the appointment of ujibito as shrine administrators, it would be most gratefully received if a ruling were issued in accordance with precedent (先規), as requested in the ujibito petition. We respectfully request that the content of the above be duly conveyed [to the relevant authorities]. Respectfully submitted.
