Item: On the eighteenth day of the twelfth month, at the御所 (*gosho*, front?) of the Kake-Hakuden (*Kekuhakuden*?), there was a formal dispute (*shōron*) between both parties — the *shashi* and the *ujibito*.

[A record of the main points of the exchanges at this time:]

○ From the *ujibito* side: They present their case regarding Sukehisa and Nobusue, citing the contents of their genealogies.

○ The *shashi* state: "Sukehisa is a person who was appointed *kannagi* (*kannushi*) in the thirty-fourth year of Ōei (1427). To claim that he was an *ujibito* is entirely unreasonable."

○ The *ujibito* state: "We too are making our claim on the basis of our genealogy. We respectfully request that the genealogies of both parties be examined."

○ The *shashi* side state: "In the genealogy among the *ujibito*, taking the small notation (*kogaki*) concerning Sukehisa…" [text continues on next page]
