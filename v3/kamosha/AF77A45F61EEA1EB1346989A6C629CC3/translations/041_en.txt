**Copy of the Imperial Responses from the Various Noble Houses** *(Interlinear note in red: "Personal note by Naokazu: This is found in the Gongohaku-ki, and is therefore omitted here.")*

Item: On the 18th day of the 12th month, at the residence of the Kampaku (*Regent*) [written as "Kekkaku-dono"; a variant reading suggests "Kankaku"], a formal dispute (*sōron*) was held between the *shashi* and the *ujibito*.

**Summary record of the questions and answers at this time:**

○ The *ujibito* side submitted a claim regarding Sukehisa and Nobusue, based on the contents of their genealogy (*keizu*).

○ The *shashi* stated: "Sukehisa is a person who was appointed as *kannushi* (chief priest) in the 34th year of Ōei (1427). Despite this, to claim that he was an *ujibito* is utterly unreasonable (*hibun*)."

○ The *ujibito* stated: "Our side likewise makes its claim on the basis of our genealogy. We respectfully request that both parties' genealogies be examined."

○ The *shashi* side stated: "In the genealogy among the *ujibito*, the small marginal notation (*kogaki-tsuke*) of Sukehisa——" [text continues on following page]
