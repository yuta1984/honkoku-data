Article: Kamo Shrine Chief Priest (神主) Norihisa (矩久), Senior Ritualist (祢宜) Seihei (誠平), Hafuri (祝) Shigetsuke (重副), Provisional Senior Ritualist (権祢宜) Mitsuhisa (盈久), Kibune Senior Ritualist (貴布祢宜) Nobuhisa (宣久), and Kibune Hafuri Okihisa (起久)
　　　The above six individuals, having privately submitted letters of resignation from their shrine posts, were found guilty of the offense of negligence of shrine rites, and on the 21st day of the 1st month of Meireki 3 (1657), were dismissed from their current posts. Subsequently, an order was issued that several members of the uji (clan) should be appointed to fill the vacated positions from the Chief Priest downward. This was most welcome, but as the former post-holders repeatedly submitted petitions of lament, it was deemed appropriate that, in addition to the required ritual of apology, an act of special pardon (御宥助) be granted; and on the 2nd day of the 9th month of the same year, they were reinstated to their original posts.

　　　A copy of a written pledge (一札)

　On this occasion, following the Imperial sanction (勅許) granted for the post of Provisional Hafuri, [we] did submit a petition and tender our letters of resignation from our shrine posts, which was regarded as most presumptuous and caused [those above us] great distress. We humbly submitted our apologies, and have been graciously pardoned, for which we are deeply grateful. Henceforth, regarding the matter of shrine posts, now that Imperial sanction has been granted, we shall not raise any further objections or complaints whatsoever. Therefore —
