…was granted the Junior Fifth Rank, Upper Grade (*Jushii-no-jō*).

Furthermore, among the current shrine priests (*shashi*) of the Seven Houses, there are likewise many who received court rank (*joshaku*) only after their coming-of-age ceremony (*genpuku*):

　　Mori Hida-no-Kami Tsuruhisa — received court rank at age 20, after coming-of-age
　　Matsushita Minbu Tsunehisa — received court rank at age 18, after coming-of-age
　　Hayashi Kunai-no-Gonno-Taifu Shigetsugu — received court rank at age 19, after coming-of-age
　　Mori Ukyō Tsunehisa — received court rank at age 20, after coming-of-age

As the foregoing demonstrates, there has never been any established rule or precedent (*kakushiki*) in this matter from the outset.

**Index of Principal Documents (要文目録)**

**Kyōhō 4, 2nd Month, 11th Day**

One matter: The *Tensō* (court liaison officer) Lord Madenokōji had previously declined his duties on account of the death of his daughter, and accordingly the role of *Tensō* had been delegated to Lord Sono. However, on the 25th of the same month, Lord Sono also declined, and so today the appointment was once again directed to Lord Madenokōji.

---

**4th Month, 23rd Day**

One matter: The list of names of those who have served in the role of *daikan* (代官, shrine administrator/deputy) since the Eiroku era, as had been requested by the *Tensō* on the 20th, was today submitted.

---

**11th Month, 22nd Day**

One matter: The *kannagi* (神主, head priest) Yasutaka passed away and a resignation was submitted on his behalf. Inquiry was also made as to whether a transfer appointment (*tenpō*) from one shrine to Hayashi Shigetsugu should be recommended, to which the response was given that such a transfer appointment should not be made.
