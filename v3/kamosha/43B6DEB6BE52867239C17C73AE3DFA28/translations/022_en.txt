**[Continuation and conclusion of the petition]**

…However, the fact that we have been instructed not to submit petitions on behalf of persons under the age of twenty is a matter that diminishes the authority and prestige of this great shrine, and we find it most lamentable and exceedingly troubling. We humbly petition that, upon your deliberation, an order be issued that henceforth, in accordance with former precedent (senkи), all matters be submitted together as before.

　　Should the foregoing be graciously heard and understood, and an official ruling be issued, and should the order be given in accordance with our petition, all the ujibito of the shrine would receive this with the deepest gratitude and humble reverence. Respectfully submitted.

**The Second Day of the Fourth Month**

One matter: In response to a summons, Suekyo and Kiyoshige appeared. The document submitted the other day by members of the Five Houses was shown to them, and they were instructed to submit by the following day a written statement as to whether there are any precedents for the wearing of the court robe (chakuhō) after the tenth year of Genroku.
