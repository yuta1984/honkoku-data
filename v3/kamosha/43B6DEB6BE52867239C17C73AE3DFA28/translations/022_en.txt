…However, the instruction that petitions must not be submitted on behalf of persons under the age of twenty is a matter that diminishes the authority and dignity of so great a shrine, and we find it most lamentable and deeply distressing. We therefore humbly petition that, upon due deliberation, an order be issued henceforth in accordance with prior custom, permitting all parties to submit petitions equally, as has been the established practice.

　　Should the foregoing be heard and understood, and should
　　a directive be issued and the matter be settled in accordance
　　with our petition, all ujibito shall receive this with the
　　deepest gratitude and reverence. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　　　Representatives of the Kamo Ujibito
　　　　　　　　　　Okamoto Ukyo-no-Gonodaibu Kiyoshige

　　　　　　　　Same [co-representative]
　　　　　　　　　　Ikedono Sanuki-no-Kami Yasunori

　To: Lord Karasuma Ben

**4th Month, 2nd Day**

One matter: In response to a summons, Suehisa and Kiyoshige appeared. They were shown the document submitted by members of the Five Houses some days prior, and were instructed to submit, by the following day, a written statement regarding whether or not there are precedents for the wearing of the ceremonial robe (hō) after the tenth year of Genroku [1697].
