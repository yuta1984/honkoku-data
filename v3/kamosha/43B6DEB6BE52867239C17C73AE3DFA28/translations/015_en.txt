　　　Precedents of Kamo Shrine Ritual Officials Dismissed from Their Posts Due to Negligence of Shrine Rites

Article: Kamo Shrine Hafuri (祝) Mitsusada (光定)
　　　In the 1st month of Ōan 5 (1372), he was negligent (闕怠) in the various shrine rites at Kibune Shrine, and in the 8th month of the same year he was dismissed from his current post.

Article: Kamo Shrine Hafuri Shigemoto (重基)
　　　During the Ōei era (1394–1428), he was negligent in the shrine rites on the 3rd day of the 1st month, and was dismissed from his current post.

Article: Kamo Shrine Hafuri Hideshige (秀重)
　　　On the 20th day of the 1st month of Kakitsu 3 (1443), he was negligent in the rites for the pilgrimage to Kibune Shrine (貴布祢詣). Although he did attend and perform the duties on the following 21st day, he was nonetheless dismissed from his current post.

Article: Kamo Shrine Hafuri Shigenobu (重脩)
　　　In the 5th month of Entoku 3 (1491), he was dismissed from his current post by way of guilt by association (連坐), due to the offense of his son Shigenori's (重則) negligence of shrine duties.
