Item: Should there come a time in later years when, in accordance with ancient custom, a petition is made for the successive transfer of offices from the ujigami-priest (ujigami-no-hafuri) up through the chief priest (kannagi, shinshū), the entire body of shrine people shall jointly submit such a petition, setting aside the titles of the five families and reverting to the original former titles.

Item: When descendants of the undersigned are, in the future, recommended by the shrine community as a whole and appointed for the first time to a shrine office, the newly appointed individual shall first affix their seal to this document before the letter of recommendation (kyōjō) from the shrine community is submitted. Should there be any who cause disorder in the matter of affixing their seal, the body of shrine people shall remove such a person from their position. Furthermore, even if a person has already affixed their seal, should they act in violation of the terms of this document (okibumi), the body of shrine people shall promptly lodge a complaint with the Imperial Liaison (gidensō), and that person shall be dismissed from their office.

Item: Should the descendants of the undersigned naturally have no male heirs, they shall not take an adopted son from the descendants of the Seven Families (shichike). An adopted son must be chosen from among the shrine people (ujibito) outside the Seven Families. Conversely, should a family have many descendants, they shall not send any of them to another family, even if earnestly requested to do so by one of the Seven Families. Regarding the details of the above: concerning the individuals who are to succeed the five families on this occasion, the Imperial Liaison, Middle Counselor Madenokōji Naohusa, instructed that an investigation be conducted into the genealogical records (kyūbetsu) of each respective family, as well as the genealogical records within the Sixteen Lineages (jūroku-ryū), and that a written report be submitted.
