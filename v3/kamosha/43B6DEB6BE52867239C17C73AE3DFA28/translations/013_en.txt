Kyōhō 2nd year (1717), 4th month, 9th day

Article: Regarding the sequential transfer and appointment (転補) of the office of Shō-hafuri (正祝職), the Transmission Official (伝奏), Lord Madenokōji, conveyed the directive to the general representatives (惣代) of the entire shrine community and to the members of the Seven Families (七家).

Article: Regarding the matter of the principal offices (本職), since the directive was issued yesterday (the 8th), a petition (願書) was submitted by Shingū and the other shrines below it, concerning the handling of vacancies (闕職).

4th month, 11th day

Article: Concerning the directive conveyed on the 8th of last month, as the Hayashi Jūryū lineage (林重流) has a personal petition, they hereby decline the transfer appointment (転補) on this occasion. Accordingly, an inquiry in the form of a written oral statement (口状書) was submitted on behalf of the entire shrine community.

Article: The general representatives of the entire shrine and the members of the Seven Families presented themselves before the Transmission Official upon summons. Regarding the matter of the office of Shō-hafuri, while the petition from the Hayashi family's own faction (林家一分) was also brought to official deliberation (御沙汰), it was declared that on this occasion all were to undergo the transfer appointment together.

4th month, 20th day

Article: Imperial sanction (勅許) was granted for the transfer appointments from the office of Shō-hafuri downward to that of the Kataoka-hafuri. Regarding the office of Kibune (貴布祢職), it was stated that a brief informal document (小折紙) should be submitted after Tsunehisa (庸久) arrives from Edo.
