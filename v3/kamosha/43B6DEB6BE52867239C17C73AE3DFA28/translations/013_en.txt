Kyōhō 2nd Year (1717), 4th Month, 9th Day

Article: Regarding the procedures for the post of Senior Hafuri (正祝職) and the transfer-appointment posts (転補職), the Transmission Official (伝奏), Lord Madenokōji, conveyed instructions to the General Representative of the entire shrine (一社惣代) and the members of the Seven Families (七家).

Article: Following the instructions issued yesterday concerning the main shrine posts, a petition (願書) was submitted by Shingū shrine and others below it, regarding the handling of matters during vacancies (闕職).

4th Month, 11th Day

Article: Concerning the instructions issued on the 8th of last month, Hayashi Shigenaga (林重流) submitted a request stating that he had personal reasons to decline the transfer-appointment (転補) on this occasion. Accordingly, a written oral inquiry (口状書) was submitted on behalf of the entire shrine to the Transmission Official.

Article: The General Representative of the entire shrine and the members of the Seven Families appeared before the Transmission Official upon summons. It was indicated that the matter of the request from the Hayashi family's personal circumstances (林家一分之願) regarding the Senior Hafuri post should also receive an official ruling; however, the instruction given was that on this occasion, all should jointly proceed with the transfer-appointments as reported.

4th Month, 20th Day

Article: Imperial sanction (勅許) was granted for the transfer-appointment of posts from the Senior Hafuri (正祝職) down to the Kataoka Hafuri (片岡祝). Regarding the Kibune post (貴布祢職), it was conveyed that a small written note (小折紙) should be submitted after Yōkyū's return from Edo.
