**16th Day of the Same Month**

One matter: Summoned by the *bugyō*, Ben-dono, the general representative (*sōdai*) of the combined shrine appeared, whereupon it was directed that written documents be submitted by the following day, listing the collateral lines (*shoryū*) of the Five Houses, as well as the official titles (*kannoyaku*), court ranks (*ikai*), and ages of the current [members of those lines].

One matter: An inquiry was made as to how the vacancies (*kake*) in the rotation of daily offerings (*nikkū rinban*) should be managed. The response was that precedents, if any existed, should be submitted. Accordingly, a document recording past precedents — in which a *daikan* (deputy official) had served in attendance whenever a vacancy arose, regardless of which position was vacant — was submitted on the following day, the 18th.

---

**19th Day of the Same Month**

One matter: It was directed that for vacancies in the rotation of daily offerings (*nikkū rinban*), the *daikan* role should serve in attendance. This directive was thereupon communicated to the *daikan* members.
