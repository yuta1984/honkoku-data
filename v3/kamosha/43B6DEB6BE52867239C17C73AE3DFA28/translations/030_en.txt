**[Continuation from the 8th Day of the Fifth Month]**

[It was directed that] genealogical records of the founding ancestors of the Torii, Ōji, Matsushita, Mori, Hayashi, Okamoto, and other families should be submitted.

One matter: Hayashi and Okamoto were summoned, and various inquiries were made. It was decided that from the Okamoto family, documents should be submitted concerning the founding ancestors of the Seven Houses, as well as the written directives issued to the Seven Houses in the fourth year of Kanbun (1664).

---

**14th Day of the Same Month**

One matter: Summoned by the *bugyō*, the general representative (*sōdai*) of the combined shrine appeared, whereupon it was decided that written documents should be submitted stating the assessed yield (*kokudaka*) of shrine estate lands, as well as the assessed yields, provinces, districts, and villages of the stipends held in other provinces by shrine priests (*shashi*) and clan members (*ujibito*) who held separate vermillion-seal grants (*go-shuin*).

---

**11th Day of the Sixth Month**

One matter: Around the *inu-no-koku* (approximately 8 p.m.), a written notice arrived from Igarashi. It stated that the following six persons — Takehisa, Yoshihisa, Tsunehisa, Korihisa, Sahei, and Tomohisa — were needed for official business (*go-yō*), and that they were to present themselves at the office of the Lord of Awa-no-Kami (*Awa-no-Kami-dono*) the following morning of the 12th, after the fifth hour (approximately 8 a.m.).

---

**12th Day of the Same Month**

One matter: Pursuant to last night's summons, the said six persons appeared at the *bugyō*'s office. After the *uma-no-koku* (around noon), *yoriki*, *dōshin*, and *zōshiki* (official attendants of varying ranks) came to the shrine, and the shrine priests (*shashi*) and council members (*hyōjōshū*) went to meet them and accompanied them to their respective houses.

One matter: At the *saru-no-koku* (approximately 4 p.m.), the said six persons were escorted back to their homes from the *bugyō*'s office by officials (*yakunin*), and were handed over to the officials who had already been stationed at [their homes], after which the [escorting] officials departed.

One matter: A petition was submitted requesting that old records (*kyūki*) and secret documents (*shinpi no shomotsu*) be entrusted to the custody of both the Hayashi and Okamoto households.

One matter: It was directed that floor plans (*ezu*) of the residences of the Five Houses be drawn up.

One matter: At the *inu-no-koku* (approximately 8 p.m.), [the sacred object/authority represented by] the *In-dono* was transferred to the main shrine hall (*shaden*).

One matter: At the *i-no-koku* (approximately 10 p.m.), at the summons of the *Ben-dono* (the *bugyō*'s official), a formal proclamation (*senka*) was made to Shigetsuna [conferring upon him] the position of Head Priest (*kannushi-shoku*).
