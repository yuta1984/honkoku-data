**[Continued — Kyōhō 4 (1719), 11th Month]**

---

**Same Month, 25th Day**

One matter: The imperial sanction (*chokkyō*) for Yasutaka's resignation was conveyed. A small folded paper (*koorigami*) recording both the transfer appointment (*tenpō*) of Tsuruhisa and his promotion in court rank (*jōkai*) were submitted together.

---

**Same Month, 26th Day**

One matter: Written inquiries from the magistrate's office (*bugyō*), conveyed through the *Ben* (attendant official), regarding the following items:

- One matter: Examples of persons who advanced in precedence without an impediment (*koji shōgai*) and in violation of customary order (*chōetsu*).
- One matter: Precedents for a shrine priest's promotion in rank (*jōkai*) occurring on the same day as another appointment.

It was indicated that written statements on the above three items should be submitted.

---

**Same Month, 27th Day**

One matter: It was conveyed that there was no need to submit simultaneously the matter of the shrine priest appointment and the matter of court rank promotion (*jōkai*) on the same day.

---

**Same Month, 29th Day**

One matter: Regarding to which party the *naōrai* (ritual feast) portion belonging to the daily-offering priest (*nichiku kannagi*) should be directed during the vacancy of the post — this matter was settled in accordance with Tsuruhisa's memorandum (*oboegaki*).

---

**12th Month, 13th Day**

One matter: In response to yesterday's summons, the general representatives (*sōdai*) of the entire shrine — Shingū and those below — appeared, and were addressed at the residence (*otei*) of the Liaison Officer for the Military Houses (*Buke Tensō*), Lord Nakain.

One matter: The following matters were conveyed by written document (*gokaitsuke*): the imperial sanction for Tsuruhisa to serve as head priest (*kannagi*) and for Hisamasa to serve as senior priest (*negi*); and furthermore, matters concerning the manner of performing the *daikan* duties, the wearing of court robes (*chakuhō*), and the granting of court rank (*joshaku*). After the return of said general representatives to their home district, meetings and exchanges were held, and discussions took place back and forth with the members of the Five Houses (*goke*); in the end, they stated that they would not be able to submit a collective acceptance of the directive.

---

**Same Month, 14th Day** *(Two directives conveyed)*

One matter: Early in the morning, those from Shingū and below submitted a written expression of gratitude for the previous day's directives.

*(Record of two written directives issued the previous day)*

One matter: When a vacancy (*kesshoku*) arises among the Five Officers (*gokan*), shrine priests of the Seven Houses (*nanake*) shall assume the duties of the Kataoka shrine post. When a vacancy arises at Kataoka or the shrines below it, the duty shall be carried out by members of the fifteen *ujibito* (hereditary shrine attendants) who have traditionally been called upon to serve in the role of *daikan*.
