Kyōhō 1st Year (From the 22nd day of the 8th month to the 29th day of the 12th month)
Post-Administrative Record, Part 1 — Index of Important Documents

Kyōhō 1st Year, 10th Month, 13th Day

Item: Regarding the matter of performing duties in the role of代官 (daikan/deputy administrator), for the first time a written oral statement (口上書) and one volume of precedent examples (勘例) were submitted from the ujibito (shrine community members) to the Tendō (court liaison).

Item: It was decreed that when the position of Shōhafuri (Chief Ritualist) falls vacant, a son of a shrine official (shashi) who holds court rank shall be appointed to serve; and that when the position of Kataoka-hafuri falls vacant due to incapacity, a person from among the ujibito who holds court rank shall be appointed to serve.
