**28th Day of the Same Month**

One matter: Having been summoned by the *bugyō*'s representative, the *bendono*, the general representatives (*sōdai*) of the Five Houses (*go-ke*) and those below Shingū appeared. It was there declared that for tonight's sacred rites (*o-shinji*), since the position of the *Shōhafuri* (Chief Ritualist) was vacant, the duty should be performed by a shrine official from Kataoka; thereafter, it should be performed by the eldest sons of the Seven Houses (*shichike*). Since there were [particular] intentions (*omoimeshi*) in this matter, it was declared that for matters during the first month [the arrangements] should be as stated above. Beyond that, further instructions would be issued — this [was communicated] to both parties.

One matter: In response to the above directive, Kiyoshige and Ujimoto appeared [before the authorities] on behalf of Shingū and others to seek clarification. At the audience (*go-taimen*), it was stated that the declaration had been made because of [His/Her] particular intentions, and that this was the nature of the instruction.
