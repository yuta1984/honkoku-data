One matter: When a vacancy (*kesshoku*) arises among the Five Officers (*gokan*), and the *negi* (senior priests) and *hafuri* (ritualists) of Kataoka and Kibune shrines are, by circumstance, unable to serve due to impediment or illness, the duty shall be carried out by five *ujibito* (hereditary shrine attendants) serving in the *daikan* capacity. Since there are precedents from the past in which *ujibito* served as *daikan* during vacancies among the head priest and four officers (*shikan*), even when the vacancy falls within the Five Officers, the *ujibito* serving in the *daikan* role shall perform the duty.

---

***On the Matter of Wearing Court Robes (Chakuhō)***

Even those who are eldest sons (*chakudan*) of the Seven Houses (*nanake*) shrine priests but do not currently hold a post (*mushoku*) shall, on the day of their rank conferral (*joshaku*) and coming-of-age ceremony (*genpuku*), don their court robes (*ibō*) and attend the shrine. From the following day onward, the wearing of court robes shall be suspended. The same applies to *ujibito*: on the day of their rank conferral and coming-of-age ceremony, they shall don their court robes and attend the shrine, after which wearing of court robes shall be suspended from the following day.

> *Proviso*: Those who have no court rank on the day of their coming-of-age ceremony shall, as is proper, wear *jōe* (plain white purified robes).

---

***On the Matter of Rank Conferral (Joshaku)***

The eldest sons of the Seven Houses shrine priests shall, in accordance with recent precedent, petition for rank (*sōshaku*) from the age of nine.

As for *ujibito*, in previous years it was directed that petitions should be made from the age of twenty; however, henceforth petitions for rank shall be made from the age of fifteen.

However, although the imperial sanction (*chokkyō*) had already been conveyed the previous day, the written copy of the expression of gratitude was submitted only today — and this delay was reprimanded.
