**Extract from the aforementioned written response:**

Among the same articles, it was stated that the hereditary shrine priests (*sōden no shashi*) received court rank (*joshaku*) while still in the state of childhood (童形, *dōkei*, i.e., before coming-of-age), and thereafter underwent the coming-of-age ceremony (*genpuku*). At the same time, they were appointed to shrine positions (*shashoku*), and in accordance with their age, they were conferred the Third Rank (*Sanmi*).

One matter: The practice of receiving court rank while still in the state of childhood is by no means limited exclusively to the members of the Seven Houses. Among the general body of *ujibito* (clan members affiliated with the shrine), there are also many who received court rank in the state of childhood. A few examples are recorded below on the left:

- *Ujibito*, elder brother of Kiyozen, ancestor of the current Okamoto Ukyo-no-gonnokami Kiyoshige:
  - **Kiyou** — Received court rank at age 7 while in the state of childhood; underwent coming-of-age (*genpuku*) at age 8, and was thereupon appointed to the position of *Gyōbu-no-gon-no-shōsuke* (権刑部少輔, Junior Assistant Minister of Justice).

- Ancestor (grandfather) of the same Kiyoshige:
  - **Kiyozen** — Received court rank at age 9 while in the state of childhood.

- *Ujibito*, father of the current Nishiike Izumo-no-kami Hisao:
  - **Aihisa** — Received court rank at age 7 while in the state of childhood; underwent coming-of-age at age 8, and was appointed to the position of *Uemon-no-jō* (右衛門尉, Lieutenant of the Right Gate Guards).
