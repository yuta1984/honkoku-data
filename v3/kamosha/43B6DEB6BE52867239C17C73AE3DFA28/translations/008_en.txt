Item: The matter of shrine precedent regarding appointment and investiture to shrine positions and court ranks without distinction, as one line of the same surname of the Kamo Shrine families.

The Kamo no Agata-nushi, who serve as shrine families of the Wakeikazuchi Imperial Grand Shrine, are descendants of the deity Kamo Taketsunumi no Mikoto.
In the second year of the Taika era (646) under Emperor Kōtoku, Otokoyuka was appointed as negi (associate priest).
Since the appointment of Arizane in the ninth year of the Tenryaku era (955) under Emperor Murakami (referred to here as "Yūjō Tennō"), the entire shrine family community has regarded this Arizane as their founding ancestor. From that time forward, those deemed suitable were selected from among his descendants, without distinction between legitimate and illegitimate lineage, to be appointed as kannagi (chief priest), negi (associate priest), or hafuri (junior priest)—collectively referred to as "shrine officers" (shashi).
