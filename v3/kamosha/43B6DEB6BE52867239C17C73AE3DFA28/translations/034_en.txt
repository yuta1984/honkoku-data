The above are those who separated earliest from among the sixteen lineages of the Kamo ujibito.

The shrine families (*sha-ke*) of the Kamo Shrine all branched out from a single individual — the *Negi* (Senior Priest) Arisane — during the *Tenryaku* era (*c.* 947–957 CE), and there are absolutely no separately distinct lineages or clans (*tane-shō*) [among them]. Down to the present day, the bloodlines have continued in unbroken succession, and within that clan there is no distinction made between legitimate (*chaku*) and collateral (*sho*) [birth]. Rather, [individuals] are selected according to their ability and appointed to shrine offices (*sha-shoku*) accordingly.

This same principle can indeed be found in the *Kōnin no Kyaku* (Supplementary Regulations of the Kōnin Era).

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;That is all.
