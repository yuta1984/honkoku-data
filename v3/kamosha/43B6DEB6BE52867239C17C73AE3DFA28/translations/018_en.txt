[A record of] the roster (交名, kyōmyō) of those who served as deputy officials (代官役) at the monthly First-Day Renewal Festival (御更祭), submitted in response to an inquiry, covering the period from Eiroku 8 (1565) to Shōhō 3 (1646).

20th day of the 10th month

Article: Regarding the matter of Mizuno Izumino-kami, the Shrine Overseer (御所司), it was ordered by the Senior Councillors (老中) that his appointment be changed, and [the matter] was conveyed to Matsudaira Igano-kami.

4th day of the 1st month, Kyōhō 3 (1718)

Article: Today, upon the conclusion of the inaugural audience ceremony (披露始), at the small assembly (小寄合), deliberations were held concerning the resignation of Kiyomitsu. The view emerged that for members of the Five Houses (五家), [this would involve] promotion by transfer (転昇); however, as it had been strictly prohibited the year before last for those below the rank of Naka-Shingū to perform duties in the manner of promotion by transfer, they stated that it would be difficult [to proceed accordingly], and the parties' views did not align. It was therefore resolved that each side should separately submit their respective views to the Imperial Liaison Official (伝奏).

29th day of the same month

Article: Matsushita Teruhisa performed his coming-of-age ceremony (元服) today, and attended the shrine dressed in court cap-and-robe (衣冠) and layered robes (襲).
