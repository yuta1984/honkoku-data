Same Day
Item: It was decreed that when the Shōhafuri (Chief Ritualist) position is vacant at the ujigami festival, a horse shall be provided by one of the shrines.

5th Day of the Same Month
Item: It was decreed that on the days of the rotating duty (junban) when the Shōhafuri is vacant, the shrine community shall conduct the naorai (post-ritual communal meal/offering service) in place of the regular supply of offerings.

6th Day of the Same Month
Item: When the Shōhafuri position is vacant, the Gon-hafuri (Deputy Ritualist) shall perform those duties, doing so through mutual consultation among those of that office. If it is difficult under shrine regulations (shaḥō) for the Gon-hafuri to perform the duties of the vacant Shōhafuri position, the matter shall be reported and the Shō-gon-negi (Senior Deputy Ritualist) shall take on those duties, with the replacement to be arranged through mutual consultation. It is strictly prohibited to perform such duties in the manner of a promotion (tenShō). In any case, it was decreed that a formal acceptance (o-uke) shall be submitted the following day.

17th Day of the Same Month
Item: It was decreed that at the divine rites (goshinjī) of the Rinji-sai (Extraordinary Festival), when the Shōhafuri is vacant the Gon-hafuri shall perform the duties, and when the Gon-hafuri position is also vacant, an ujibito serving in the daikan (deputy administrator) role shall perform the duties. This was duly conveyed.

17th Day of the 11th Month
Item: Regarding the decree issued today, since the shrine officials from the Shō-gon-negi down to the Kifune shrine did not submit their formal acceptance, the divine rites of this evening were, following the precedent of the Meireki era, to be performed entirely by ujibito serving in the daikan role — from the Shō-gon-negi rank downward. This decree was issued, a formal acceptance was submitted, and the daikan personnel duly performed the duties.

23rd Day of the Same Month
Item: Regarding the matter of sending a substitute representative (daisan) on behalf of a shrine official (shikan) who is in a period of heavy mourning (jūfuku), upon inquiry it was decreed that this be managed through mutual consultation.
