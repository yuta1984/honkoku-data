Within the shrine community, the five families of Mori, Tomino, Matsushita, Umetsuji, and Toriiji-ōji at that time failed to submit their acceptance and acted in defiance of the Imperial Decree (chokujō). Consequently, on the twelfth day of the sixth month of the fifth year [of Kyōhō, i.e., 1720], by order of both the imperial court and the military government (kōbu), these parties were ordered into house confinement (heimon). Then, on the fifteenth day of the second month of the sixth year [1721], the aforementioned parties were subjected to severe punishment. This was indeed divine retribution, and must be regarded with the utmost reverence and fear. As for the continuation of these five families' lineages, this matter was entrusted to the shrine people (ujibito). Henceforth, the oath shall be observed with ever greater strictness and rigor. Should anyone violate the terms herein stated, they shall incur the divine punishment of all the great and small deities throughout Japan, as well as the deities of this shrine. Thus, this written oath of pledge (kishōmon) is as stated above.
