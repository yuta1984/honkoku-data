**[Continuation of the Account]**

When [the documents] were brought in person, there was a request (*o-konomi*) regarding the closing endorsement (*okugaki*) of the written statement (*kakitsuke*) of those belonging to the same lineage, and it was [accordingly] revised and submitted. Furthermore, concerning the death of Hakkyū, an inquiry was made as to whether he had left any children. It was reported — again by written document — that he had no natural children, that a man called Shigetake, the second son of Jūryū, [Hakkyū's] wife's father's relative (*togai*), had been adopted as heir and had succeeded to the household, but that the separate bloodline of the Matsushita [family] had thereby become extinct. Following the above proceedings, the *Tensō* (Imperial Liaison) withdrew, and the *Bugyō* (Magistrate/Administrator) likewise brought the same copies of the documents, reported the outcome of the proceedings at the *Tensō*, and also withdrew.
