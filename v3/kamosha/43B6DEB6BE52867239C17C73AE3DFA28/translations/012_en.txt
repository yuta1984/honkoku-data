In short, because the absence of職領 (office stipends/landholdings attached to shrine posts) had led to vacancies (闕職), and this dispute had arisen therefrom, on this occasion the職領 of twenty-one offices, from that of the chief priest (神主) down to the Ujigami-hafuri, were endowed (寄附), and it was declared that henceforth these offices should be dutifully maintained without lapse, using these landholdings.

Although the establishment of restrictions (限制) on shrine offices at this time was not in accordance with ancient custom, it was done in compliance with the command of the military government (武命), and a reconciliation was achieved. Matters pertaining to the shrine, great and small alike, were resolved through unified deliberation among all shrine families (社家).

21st day of the same month

Article: Upon being summoned by the Transmission Official (伝奏), Ujino-mune-kiyoshi (旨顕清) also presented himself, whereupon it was stated that the position of代官役 (deputy official) had been established since ancient times for the purpose of filling vacancies, and that the contents of the old records should be duly reported.

Article: Whether there had been any consultations (語合) with the clansmen (ujibito) serving as代官役 since ancient times.

Article: Whether, at times when duties pertaining to the inner sanctuary (御内陣) of the main shrine arose, the shrine officers (社司) of Kataoka and Kibune served in their stead during consultations with the clansmen.
