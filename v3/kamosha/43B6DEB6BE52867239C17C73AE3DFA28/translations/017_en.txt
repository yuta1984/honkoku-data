　　　　　　　　　Matsushita Hyōbu-no-taifu (seal)
　　　　To His Excellency, the Middle Counselor Shibayama

As described above, at both the Upper and Lower Kamo Shrines, it has been the practice since ancient times to dismiss from their current posts those who have been negligent in the performance of shrine rites; we have roughly compiled and submitted [herewith] what may be found in the old records to that effect. In particular, regarding the conduct of the five individuals on this occasion, whether one characterizes it as defiance of an Imperial command or as neglect of shrine rites, we believe their offense exceeds prior precedents in severity. Should matters be left as they stand, willful and self-serving conduct will only become more prevalent, the order within the shrine precincts will not be restored, and we find this situation to be a matter of the utmost concern. The foregoing is our respectful submission.

Note: The above cited precedents were submitted confidentially to the Imperial Liaison Official (御伝奏). Accordingly, this has been recorded in the yokoori-chō (横折帳, the bound record book).
