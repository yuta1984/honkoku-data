　　　To His Excellency the Middle Counselor Shibayama

As stated above, with regard to both the Upper and Lower Kamo Shrines, [we] have undertaken a rough investigation of the passages found in old records indicating that, since ancient times, those who have been negligent in the performance of shrine rites have been dismissed from their current posts, and hereby respectfully submit the results of said investigation. In particular, it is our humble view that the conduct of the five individuals on this occasion — whether characterized as defiance of Imperial command or as neglect of shrine rites — constitutes an offense that exceeds all precedent. Were this matter to be left as is, self-willful behavior would only become more prevalent, order within the shrine would be impossible to maintain, and we would find ourselves in the greatest of difficulties. Respectfully submitted.

Note: The above document of precedents was submitted in confidence (御内証) to the Imperial Liaison (御伝奏). It was therefore recorded in the yokooricho (横折帳, a folded ledger).
