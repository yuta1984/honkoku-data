(Continuing from the previous page, contents of the petition of the 11th day of the 4th month)

　　　Because [the proxy official] had continuously served without interruption in the role of proxy official (代官役) for many years during periods of vacancy, that practice became an established shrine precedent (社例), and to this day the proxy official serves every year. For this reason as well, during vacancies, the ujibito (氏人, hereditary shrine-affiliated person) serving as proxy official has been performing supplementary duties.

　　Excerpt from the 21st day of the 4th month:

　　Article: Today, Yasutake (保慷), the eldest son of shrine chief (神主) Hōsai (保斎), underwent the capping ceremony (元服, coming-of-age). His father Hōsai was seated in attendance; Shingū-hafuri Seika (清可) performed the hair-dressing; Sueshige (季荘) [annotation: at this time, the shrine chief of Nitama Shrine] served as the role-bearer (役送); and [someone] described as a carpenter of the establishment was also present. After the ceremony concluded, [the party] proceeded to the shrine. Accompanied by Yasutaka (保喬), Yasutake (保慷) wore a folded eboshi hat (折烏帽子), a plain white robe (浄衣), and purple divided skirts (紫指貫).

5th month, 4th day

Article: Imperial sanction (勅許) was granted: Yasuhisa (庸久) is transferred and appointed (転補) to the position of Negi (祢宜) of Kibune Shrine (貴布祢職); and Yasutsugu (保補) is newly appointed (新補) to the office of Hafuri (祝職).

Same month, 5th day

Article: Although Tsunehisa (庸久) has not yet performed the congratulatory audience (拝賀), he carries out the duties of Negi of Kibune Shrine.

Same month, 7th day

Article: A petition (願書) and written oral statement (口状書) regarding the proxy official role (代官役) were submitted.

Article: Due to negligence in shrine duties (神事闕怠), a notification was issued regarding the examination of precedents for punishment (勘例) of shrine officers (社職).
