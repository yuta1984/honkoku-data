(Continued from the previous page; from within the petition of the 11th day of the 4th month:)
　　　During periods of vacancy (闕職), the proxy duty (代官役) has been performed continuously over many years, and because this was deemed praiseworthy, it became an established shrine custom (社例). To this day, those serving as proxy duty officers perform this duty every year. Furthermore, the fact that shrine parishioners (氏人) in the proxy duty role have provided supplementary service during vacancies is likewise due to the same praiseworthy precedent.

Excerpt from the 21st Day of the 4th Month

Article: Today, Hokō Motoaki (保慷元昭), the eldest son of the shrine chief (神主) Hosai (保斎), performed his coming-of-age ceremony (元服/加冠). His father Hosai was seated in attendance; Shingū Hafuri Seika (新宮祝清可) performed the hair-dressing rite (理髪); and Kishō (季荘) [marginal note: who was at that time the shrine chief of Nitama (尓玉)] performed the sending of roles (役送). The carpenters who regularly attended also participated. After the ceremony concluded, they proceeded to worship at the shrine. Hokyō (保喬) accompanied them, and Hokō was dressed in a folded eboshi cap (折烏帽子), a white ceremonial robe (浄衣), and purple hakama trousers (紫指貫).

5th Month, 4th Day
Article: Imperial sanction (勅許) was granted for the transfer-appointment (転補) of Yōkyū (庸久) to the post of Negi (祢宜) at Kibune Shrine (貴布祢), and for the supplementary and new appointments (保補・新補) to the Hafuri posts.

Same Month, 5th Day
Article: Although Yōkyū had not yet performed the formal audience of gratitude (拝賀), he carried out the duties and services of the Kibune Negi.

Same Month, 7th Day
Article: A petition (願書) and a written oral inquiry (口状書) were submitted concerning the proxy duty (代官役).

Article: Due to negligence of shrine rites (神事闕怠), a precedent inquiry (勘例) was circulated to the shrine officers (社職).
