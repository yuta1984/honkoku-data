25th Day, Tsuchinoe, Cloudy — Large Evening Thunderstorm in the Afternoon

1. Today there is a small meeting. Attendees:

1. Regarding yesterday's petition — whether to appear again today for further inquiry, or to draft a written lament and submit it as a petition — deliberations covered various opinions. In the end, for this instance of water, the form of "grant received" has been maintained in terms of protocol even without actual water. If so, perhaps it would be appropriate to return the grant to Koyama village today; or alternatively, since Koyama village's allocation runs only through today, from tomorrow the allocation could be received by one [party], with six parts distributed to Nakamura and four parts to Koyama, and the return of the grant could follow after a day or two. Various deliberations were held but all had difficulties. Since attendance was thin and Yasushige, Kuniuji, and others were absent, it was unanimously decided that thorough consultation should continue until evening, with a complete return of the grant being acceptable.

2. According to Mikawa-no-kami's address, the planting work in Hongō district is mostly complete and should be finished within today. Furthermore, the water officials will be informed this evening. The water officials will withdraw from tonight, resting through the night and the following day and night. It is desired that sake be provided for them. Since they worked exceptionally hard, it was suggested that a small reward be given. In previous years, one tō [of sake] each was given. This year it seems that the same amount will not suffice. A request will be made later, but for now, one kanmon in cash should be given as sake money. This was communicated to the meeting hall.

3. Regarding the petition of Ōno farmers for lower water [rights].

4. Regarding the performance of a rain prayer ceremony: Although the paddies have been planted more broadly than usual, the sufficiency of irrigation water is uncertain. The precedent is to wait three days after the completion of large paddy work, then perform the rain prayer. The consensus was that it should be convened as soon as possible. Since the rain prayer matter was too significant to be decided at the small meeting, a large meeting was held after the midday meal.
