17th Day of the 8th Month
[Written in red ink] Concerning the Jūsan-koku Mountain — Matter to be handled within the shrine community (shachū)

One entry: Recently, peasants of the western side of Kawanobori Mountain have been working on a path through their own mountain holdings, with the intention of making it into a public thoroughfare extending from Hatake Village. They sent word requesting that Kamigamo also contribute laborers (ninsoku) to assist. The mountain village headman stated that this path is a road belonging to the Kamigamo mountain people (yamato), and that if it were developed into a public thoroughfare as described, it would cause inconvenience to the Kamigamo peasants; therefore, a refusal should be conveyed stating that this should not be done. While it would have been proper to consult with the peasants of the four villages (shi-chō), it was stated that obtaining their agreement would be exceedingly difficult, and after consultation, a refusal was promptly sent. However, today word came again from Hatake Village, saying that since this is the road of the Kamigamo mountain people, the request for assistance had been sent, but the reply that it should not be done was [unacceptable / unsatisfactory — text continues on next page]
