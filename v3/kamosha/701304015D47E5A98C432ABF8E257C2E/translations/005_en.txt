Item: Ceremonial-day assembly (式日寄会). Other matters are omitted.

[Written in red] Matter concerning the refusal of transfer for the Shōhōri (正祝)

Item: Regarding the resignation of the Yuki priest (就久神主), the Senior Negi (正祢宜), the associate Hōri (祝), and others who are due to be transferred should thereby be made aware of the situation. Since the Senior Negi and the Hōri were not present, this was conveyed to them, and they were instructed that if they had any objections, they should submit a written statement of refusal in their reply. The Senior Negi sent back a reply indicating that they understood. As for Hōri Shigetomi, he replied that he had no intention of accepting a transfer; upon being told that if he would not accept the transfer he should submit his objections in writing, he replied that since he was not accepting the transfer, he had no particular objection to state.

The assertion that those who belong to the Seven Families (七家) within the shrine community should not be subject to transfer reveals an underlying motive — namely, the conviction that the position of Shōhōri is a hereditary post (譜代) of their family.
