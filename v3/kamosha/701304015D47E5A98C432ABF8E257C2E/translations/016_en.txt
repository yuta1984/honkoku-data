The petitioners wished to receive [the land to the west of the First Torii gate] as a grant, and also to use a small portion as tobacco allowance. The evaluation states: This is a matter that cannot be permitted. Although it would be desirable to offer a small measure of relief, collecting ground fees (bajisen) is something the shrine (ichi-sha) does not authorize.

5th Day of the 5th Month
A letter arrived from the musicians' group (gakunin-chū). It was reported that on the 7th, the Grand Music Piece (taikyoku) for the sacred prayers (gokitō) of the Imperial Palace was performed at the Hashidono (Bridge Hall).

22nd Day of the 7th Month
