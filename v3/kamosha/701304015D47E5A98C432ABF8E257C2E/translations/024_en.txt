Concerning the repair costs for the flood-control embankment (kawayoke-tsutsumi) of Kamigamo: From the 10th year of Kanbun (1670), the Itakura Naizen-no-Shō ordered the construction of a new embankment, and new reclaimed land (shinkai) was designated as the source of repair funds. From the following year (the Year of the Boar), under the administration of Gomi Tōkurō, the income from the government reserve land (okura-ire) was assessed annually, and we received official receipts (kudafuda) each year, duly submitting payment. Thereafter, from the 1st year of Tenna (1681, the Year of the Rooster), under the administration of Kobori Jinuemon, we continued to make annual assessed payments up through the recent Year of the Ox. Respectfully submitted.

　　　1st day of the 12th month　　　　　Village headman of Kamigamo
