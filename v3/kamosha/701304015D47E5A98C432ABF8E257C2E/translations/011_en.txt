The request was made to this party that the person in question be approved within the shrine community and allowed to accompany [the procession] as an attendant. Likewise, His Highness Arisugawa made an identical request regarding the Umagonokami (Right Equerry), asking that he too be approved and included as an attendant.

Although it is not the place of the Imperial Liaison (Dendō) to forcibly request something that is subject to shrine law, since the request comes from the aforementioned distinguished persons, it is being conveyed. [The Dendō] asked that this matter be discussed among the shrine community and, if possible, that approval be granted in accordance with the request.

After considerable deliberation, the assessment was that, while this was an august command, it was desirable that approval be given this time in such a way as not to set a precedent for the future. However, there had been long-standing disputes regarding the "nissū" (the period of ritual abstinence/days of observance), and as a result there existed written regulations and formal procedures for the whole shrine. Indeed, there was already a precedent whereby those who had traveled to the Kantō region during the period of "nissū" were demoted to the next lower seat in the procession order, in accordance with the regulations. Given all these circumstances, it was impossible to reach an immediate decision on the spot. It was decided that the two persons in question would be invited the following evening, and all would gather for further deliberation. The attendees then withdrew.
