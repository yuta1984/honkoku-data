2nd day of the 12th month

Item: Yamamoto Kunai has reported that Genbannof Yamazaki has been falling behind year after year on his oil donations (kisin) to the shrine. Regarding this matter for which a petition had been submitted: he has at last managed to prepare five pieces of silver (ginshi), and in presenting these, he now petitions that henceforth he be excused from the oil donations. Genbannsubmitted this petition.

Commentary: Although there was a person who made donations in the time of the ancestors, when it came to the descendants, they became negligent, and despite repeated reminders being sent, the oil donations remained in arrears. Under these circumstances, Genban, feeling the grace of the divine (myōga), offers a modest amount of silver and requests to be excused from future oil donations. This is a reasonable request, and it is the opinion that permission should be granted.

Item: The following written document circulated to the village headman Shichibe'e was brought in for review:

　　Regarding the repair costs for the Kamo River embankment: Please send as soon as possible copies of the final tallies (shiri) from the old land survey register (kenchi-chō) and the new land survey register. Additionally, please indicate in writing whether the areas under each person's administration are within the embankment zone designated for repair costs, or whether there are also areas outside the embankment zone, and submit this explanation together with the above. We have been instructed to convey this message, and accordingly do so. Respectfully submitted.
