　　　　To: Uramatsu Ben-dono
　　　　　　　Zassho-chū (Administrative Staff)

　　　　　　　Oral Petition Humbly Submitted

One: In the second month of this year, we submitted a written notice that Tomino Sanmi (the head shrine priest) wished to resign from the position of head shrine priest. In response, we were instructed to delay the matter a little longer, and he has continued to serve until now. We were instructed that, in accordance with the shrine's established precedent, the sequential transfer of posts (jidai ten'nin) should proceed from the Kibune Shrine's hafuri (officiating priest). However, the Sei-hafuri (chief officiating priest) Hayashi Sanmi claims that his position is hereditary (fudai) and therefore not subject to transfer, causing the matter to stall. Regarding the sequential transfer system, when the position of Gon-hafuri (provisional officiating priest) fell vacant in the fifteenth year of Genroku [1702], a lawsuit was filed and after extensive deliberation, on the twenty-first day of the tenth month, it was decreed by Suruga-no-kami-dono, based on the articles of regulation, that the sequential transfer should be carried out. Thereafter, during the time when Lord Hiramatsu Chūnagon served as Tendō (the Court's liaison), Hayashi Kunai reported the position of Kibune Nigi (shrine official) to the Court, and the sequential transfer was duly executed. We humbly request that you issue the order for the sequential transfer to be carried out without delay this time as well. Respectfully submitted.
