2nd Day of the 4th Month
[Written in red ink] Concerning the Kawakamigō Villagers, the Haikai-hana Shinjō (festival flower ritual land) matter.

One entry: A formal written petition (kōjōgaki) was submitted by the peasants of Kawakami Village. It states that there had been land designated as Haikai-hana Shinjō (ritual land for the "wandering flower" festival ceremony), but since the cadastral survey conducted by Ai Settsu-no-kami, this land has been lost from the records. Of the land that had been in Kamo domain, after Jirōzaemon, a shrine attendant (jinin), was dismissed in a previous year, Jirōzaemon sold it to Genbanoske, and now there is no ritual land whatsoever, causing great hardship. Therefore, they petition that this land be requested by the shrine (ichi-sha) and granted as Haikai-hana Shinjō land. Furthermore, since the written document (origami) received from the shrine on the day in question is merely a formality, they wish to formally receive it per the official inventory (gomokuroku) and use it for the celebration of the ritual.
　Evaluation: It is said that this matter cannot be granted, etc.
