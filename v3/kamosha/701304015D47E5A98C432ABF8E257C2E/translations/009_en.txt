A message was sent to the Third Rank (Lord Lin/Hayashi) stating that if the transfer of position were not to proceed, the matter should be reported as a formal refusal to the Magistrate (御奉行). When this was conveyed through Fukken (福顕), the reply came back: "Whatever the circumstances may be, we shall act as it suits you. If we are summoned, we shall appear, present our refusal, and act in accordance with your instructions."

---

*Respectfully submitted, the following oral statement:*

On this occasion, as the Third Rank Tomino has stated his wish to resign from the position of Chief Priest (神主職), we duly notified all parties that we would report to the Imperial Liaison (御伝奏) regarding the matter of successive transfer of positions starting from the Kibune Priest (貴布祢祝). However, the Chief Priest (正祝), Third Rank Hayashi/Lin, has stated that the position is a hereditary one (譜代職) and therefore cannot be subject to a transfer. Regarding this matter of successive transfer, there is a precedent from the twenty-first day of the tenth month of Genroku 15 (1702), when, following a vacancy in the post of Provisional Priest (権祝) and after extensive deliberations, an order was issued for successive transfers to proceed. We would be most grateful if you would be so kind as to issue an order for this to proceed without delay on this occasion as well. Respectfully submitted.
