Twenty-eighth Day of the Ninth Month

Representatives: Korikyu, Shoken

One: Having been summoned, the two of us appeared yesterday morning at the residence of Settsu-no-Kami-dono. Hayashi Shigeyoshi came forward. The officials requested that Shigeyoshi submit a written statement of his intentions (口上書), but he indicated he had no such preparedness to do so. Shortly thereafter, the officials instructed that a written response to the shrine community's (社家中) oral statement should be submitted. After withdrawing briefly to compose their responses, all parties appeared again before the official. The oral statement from the head faction (頭方) was then read aloud, and Fukaya Heizaemon read aloud the litigation document (公事状) from that time.

Shigeyoshi's response was as follows: when the sequential transfer (次第転任) was ordered regarding the vacancy in the position of Gon-hafuri (provisional officiating priest), it had been stated that the matter of the Sei-hafuri (chief officiating priest) would be addressed at a later date. Subsequently, the Imperial Court Liaison (御伝奏), Lord Hiramatsu, conveyed this to the shrine representatives. Since no further instructions had been issued after that, he (Shigeyoshi) had not taken up the post of Sei-hafuri.

In response to this, the officials said: "Shall we inquire whether Lord Hiramatsu did indeed give such instructions? Representatives, is this truly the case?" At this, Korikyu answered "Ah—" and was about to explain the circumstances, when Settsu-no-Kami-dono said, "You should consider whether such matters were indeed the case before you speak." Korikyu then stated, "We have not received such instructions. The matter at that time concerned only the position of Gon-hafuri." However, the officials ordered them to "carefully examine the shrine's daily records (日記) and other documents, and submit a detailed written account," whereupon all parties withdrew. Thus ends the account.
