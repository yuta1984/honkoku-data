Hōei 7, Year of the Tiger — Second Month, Full (30-day month)

Monthly Duty Officers: Renkyū, Suene, Sueyuki, Seiken, and Kaisho-hoho (Assembly Hall Supplementary Officer)

First Day, Hinoe-Saru (丙申), Clear

[Written in red] Matters concerning the exclusion of those subject to ritual pollution from the lottery

1. Early in the morning, in accordance with established custom, prior to the lottery drawing for the six roles (六役) at the Council Hall (評議所), Seika [an official] announced at the Assembly Hall (会所) the following: Regarding the six roles, even if a person's lot is drawn, there are cases where the matter must be submitted to the shrine community for consideration. Furthermore, any person serving in official capacity (御奉公人) who is not present at the session, even if their lot is drawn, must surrender their lot and the draw shall be redone. Additionally, regarding the question of whether those who are in a state of ritual pollution (触穢) should be excluded from the lottery at this time — this matter was deliberated upon at a gathering of senior and junior officials yesterday morning, and it was unanimously decided that such persons shall indeed be excluded.
