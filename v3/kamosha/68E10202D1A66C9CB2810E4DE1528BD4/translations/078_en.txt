…Because the stipulated materials (ryō) had not been distributed, they attended in ordinary dress (jōtai) for the time being. In total, ten persons were present in the reception room (zashiki). The servers were shrine priests (kannushi) or samurai from within the shrine administration office (shamu).

【Regarding the Application of Gofun (White Pigment) to the Entire Ceiling of the Inner Sanctuary】 (Written in red)

Item: On the same day, the Old Gonsha (Ko-gonsha) was opened and everything was inspected. It was found that the coffered ceiling (oriageten'jō) had been entirely coated with gofun (white chalk/shell pigment). The shrine administrators (shashi) were surprised, as they had been unaware of this.
