Item: The 21st day, clear. Five individuals—Mori, Matsushita, Samanosuke, Nagato no Kami, and Mokuzō no Jō—visited Lord Gomi and earnestly petitioned that it would be improper and unreasonable for the work to be put out to competitive bidding (nyūsatsu), requesting instead that the shrine's portion be assigned on the basis of labor costs (kōsū). However, their request was not accepted.

Item: The 22nd day, clear.

Item: The 23rd day, clear, with occasional drizzle. The shrine administrator (zōshō) and Uemon no Taifu visited Lord Gomi and Lord Karasuma to report that the timber yard entry (kiyairi — the commencement of work at the timber yard) was scheduled for the 28th of the coming month.

Item: The 24th day, clear.

Item: The 25th day, clear. Nagato no Kami, Zushō no Kami, Awazu Shikibu, and Kyūgoto — these four individuals — visited Lord Karasuma and presented all of the documents (shōmon) of the general assembly (sōchū) for his inspection.

Item: The 27th day. Uemon no Taifu, Mokuzō no Jō, Mori, Matsushita, Mimasaka no Kami, and Nuino no Suke — these individuals — called upon Lord Gomi, requesting that he serve as overseer (bugyō) for the timber yard entry scheduled for the following day. They also reported having made the same request to Lord Itakura. Lord Gomi responded that he would inform the Lord of Shūshū (Suō no Kami) from his side, and that he would also send his own deputy (shitai) up to the shrine. With that, the group departed.
