Item: The eighth day of the twelfth month. Lord Kinemon and Lord Gomi came to pay a visit.
　　　　(What follows, up until the New Year, is not recorded here as the writer was in the capital. Inquire of Chūbei for details.)

Item: The twenty-first day of the same month. The various craftsmen had been working up until this day, but as the previous month (eleventh month) had been pressing, each of them ceased their work.


○ Kan'ei 5 (1628)

Item: On the eleventh day of the first month, for the first time, the carpenters, various craftsmen, and four members of the timber merchant's staff (irige-dai) came up. There were formal congratulatory exchanges and a round of drinks was held. However, upon selecting the timber and examining the quality of the wood, it was found that there were no suitable materials available, and so for the time being, the craftsmen and those below them did not carry out their previous work.

Item: On the twenty-first day of the same month, the junior commissioners (shita-bugyō), as before, each came up with four men, and summoned the timber merchants, instructing them to arrange for the timber without delay.

Item: On the twenty-third day of the same month, through an intermediary (satanin) from the entire shrine community (sōchū), a pressing request was made to the shrine priests (shashi) regarding the sacred treasures (shinpō). However, Lord Gomi was informed by Lord Mimasaka that they would "not engage in any deliberation on the matter."
