Item: The area behind the Wakamiya [Junior Shrine] should be opened up; likewise, the area to the west should be opened up.
〈Reply: If it is possible to construct an earthen wall (tsujiji) within the moat, it should be left as is for now, and the matter should be deliberated upon privately. It would be best to leave the cedar trees standing.〉

The above concludes [the formal items]. In addition, further replies from the shrine officials (shashi) were as follows:

Item: One of the Senior/Junior chief carpenters (gon-daiku) should wear formal court dress (sokutai), though the upper hakama need only extend to the hem; no under-robe (shitago) is required, and the remainder is long [in the style of a noshi]. The various craftsmen should wear court cap and robe (ikan), and it would seem appropriate to provide each with one outer garment.

Item: The twenty-second day, fine weather. Today, the trees on top of the earthen embankment (doi) belonging to the Wakamiya-den precinct were felled; the zelkova tree (keyaki) at the Takakura Gate was also felled.

Item: On the same day, the chief carpenters completed the written specifications (kakitate) for the ridgepole-raising ceremony. This document is contained in a separate sheet. A meeting was held at the Eidō [memorial hall], with assigned supervisors in attendance, and the document was then shown to the shrine officials, who found it to be entirely satisfactory. However, it was noted that should inquiries come from the public authorities (kōgi), the shrine officials should also be prepared to show the celebratory protocols from the ridgepole-raising of previous years.

Item: At the same meeting, the shrine officials stated that they wished to report the dimensions (shaku) of the sacred treasures (shinpō) [large type, in red]; accordingly, it was stated that a shrine administrator (zasshō) from the general assembly (sōchū) should accompany the deputy (shitodai) of Lord Gomi to show [the treasures]. The general assembly, however, held that [the sacred treasures] should first be shown to this side (the sōchū), and that an envoy should be attached after the viewing. Persons responsible for the matter (sadanin) were dispatched up to three times, but in the end no consent was obtained, and everyone dispersed for the time being.

Item: The twenty-third day, fine weather. Today, the chief carpenters petitioned that they intended to insert a foundation (dodai) beneath the Junior Shrine (gon-sha) and privately proceed with moving it (hiki-ya, i.e., relocating the building on rollers). However, the shrine officials stated that "the moat (hori) must come first…" (continues on next page)
