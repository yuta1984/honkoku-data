Item: The 16th day, clear.

Item: The 17th day, clear. Samanosuke, Mimasaka no Kami, (Left→Right) emon no Taifu, Shinkichi, Tanba, and Hanbei visited Lord Gomi, and showed him the shogunal vermilion-seal documents (goshuin) concerning the affairs of Kamo Shrine from previous years, along with other correspondence documents (uchigami).

Item: The 18th day, clear. Lord Gomi, accompanied by Furui Kihei, the head carpenter, Chikugo, Gon'emon, and others, arrived at around the Hour of the Snake (approximately 10 a.m.) to inspect the condition of the timber yard and other matters at this location. The shrine priests (shashi), shrine representatives (shatō), and assistant deputies (sashizoe) all came out accordingly. They consulted on all matters, and additionally were asked about the certificates (kitte) mentioned the previous day, as well as established customs and practices from before. The shrine priests and clan members all responded together on each point. It was decided that henceforth, certificates and the like would bear the seal (hangyō) of the shrine administrator (zōshō).

Item: The 19th day. Uemon no Taifu, Samanosuke, and Mimasaka no Kami visited Lord Gomi to offer thanks for the previous day and to follow up once more on the matter of the certificates. However, Furui Kihei said, "Today is an inauspicious day; please come again tomorrow morning," and so the group returned.

Item: The 20th day, clear. As promised the previous day, the aforementioned individuals came out, had the certificates rewritten and returned. A messenger was then sent to both the Mori and Matsushita households informing them of the above, the receipts (azukarijō) of the seven custodians (azukarishū) were retrieved, and additionally, to the public authorities (kōgi)...
