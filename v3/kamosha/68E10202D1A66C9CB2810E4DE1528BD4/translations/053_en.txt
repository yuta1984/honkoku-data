The ceremony was conducted in the imperial palace (kinchū). Accordingly, word was sent this morning from the Denso (court liaison/transmitter of imperial communications) to the effect that the imperial messenger (senjishi) bearing the decree would be visiting here today.

In ancient times, it is recorded in old documents that on the occasion of an imperial messenger, there were banquets (kyōzen) and various celebratory ceremonies (shūgi). However, as this was a sudden matter, the celebration was conducted in accordance with this memorandum as written. It was something that should have been reported in confidence beforehand, but since it was uncertain whether the imperial messenger would come or not, no prior report was made. For the time of the main shrine (shōden), a report shall be made in advance of the day, and I humbly ask that the appropriate preparations be made accordingly. Respectfully submitted.
