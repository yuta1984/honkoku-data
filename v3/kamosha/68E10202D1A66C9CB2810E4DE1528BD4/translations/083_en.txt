(Continued from the previous page) The number of dancers (maibito) remained the same as before. From today, all five of the senior ladies (jōrō) supervising the sacred garments had their eyebrows groomed/drawn. The wives of the shrine administrators (shashi), as on the previous day, came out and assisted. Tomotsugu [red vertical mark] recorded the measurements of the sacred robes (o-fuku), the sacred informal court robes (o-nōshi), the sacred formal robes (o-shōzoku), and other items; and regarding the cutting of the robes, so that the subsequent [work] would not be poorly done—the mother of Mori Mochiiku cut [the cloth], but this was a great error. Therefore, the daughter of the Governor of Tōtōmi (Tōtōmi-no-kami) [red vertical mark] [side note: Known as "Tayū," she was knowledgeable in the cutting and sewing of the Emperor's robes in the imperial palace (kinchū), and so she transmitted to Mochiiku's mother how the cutting and sewing of the robes on this occasion should be properly done. This matter was the greatest disgrace to all those assembled].

Item: On the 7th day, it rained. The sewing of the sacred robes continued, with the same number of people as before.

Item: On the same day, a record of the disbursement of a receipt (kitte).

　　　　Regarding the receipt of provisions expenses (makanai-ryō) for the period of sacred retreat (sanrō):

　　　　Total: 2 kan [side note: Out of the 16 kan for eight shrine administrators (shashi), including the share of one person, Gon-no-hafuri Ōkura [red vertical mark], this amount is now received.
