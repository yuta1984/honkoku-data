Having sent this letter as written above, a reply arrived from Kyūi-dono, who had been present (at the residence):

"I am deeply grateful for your kind letter. Regarding the details of today's events that you have informed me of — I had gone out intending to report them immediately to the danna (master/patron), but as he was away, I shall convey the contents of your written note to him upon his return. Moreover, had you informed us in advance, there may perhaps have been some adjustments or objections the danna might have wished to raise. As it has already concluded auspiciously and swiftly, and this now concerns future matters, I am at a loss as to what further to say. I look forward to the occasion of meeting you in person. Respectfully submitted."
