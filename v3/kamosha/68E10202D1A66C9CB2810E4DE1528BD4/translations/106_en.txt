(The message) reached (Lord Gomi's deputy and Lord Nakabō's deputy), and the honorable pillars were raised. The order was: first the east pillar, then the west, then the north, then the south(west) pillar. When the west pillar was being raised, the shrine priests (*shashi*) came forth and took their positions in the garden between the *Shūgen-ya* (ritual hall) and the *tsubone* (inner chamber). The Head Priest Takahisa, the Senior Priest (*Shō-negi*) Yoshihisa, the Junior Priest (*Gon-negi*) Mitsuhisa, the Kataoka Priest (*Kataoka-hafuri*) Yorihisa, and Yoshihisa's younger brother Suke(Yū)hisa *(marginal note: at this time an ujibito)* — each were seated on rush mats *(marginal note: one each)*, wearing hunting robes (*kariginu*), with west as the seat of honor, facing north. The Senior Ritualist (*Shō-hafuri*) Jūshō *(marginal note: on bereavement leave)*, the Former Head Priest Ikuhisa *(marginal note: indisposed)*, the Kataoka Negi Seihei *(marginal note: ill)*, and the Kifune Negi Tokuhisa *(marginal note: ill)* — these four did not attend. The ujibito were seated in the *tsubone*'s connecting corridor (*tōrō*), the *naoraijo* (post-ritual banquet hall), the west passageway (*nishi-watariya*), and the *tsubone*, *(marginal note: each wearing hunting robes, informal robes, or plain white robes as they saw fit, in no particular order of rank)*, and seated according to seniority of age.

Next, upon the completion of the pillar-raising, sacred sake was offered and presented. *(Marginal note: For details of the offerings, refer to the Sōchūki [General Records].)* Then the evening offerings were made *(marginal note: the Shūgen and the Shi [masters] same as before)*. After the ceremony of one round of the lower-ranking sake cup (*shita-kanbai*), all withdrew.
