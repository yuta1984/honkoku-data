Two officials of the Bureau of Carpentry (Moku no Jō) came and conveyed instructions.

Item: On the same day, through an intermediary (satanin), a request was made to the shrine priests (shashi) wishing to consult them regarding the sacred treasures (shinpō). The priests replied that they would hold a meeting and then give their response, and departed.

Item: On the twenty-fourth day, a meeting was held, and the response conveyed to the entire shrine community (sōchū) was that "as there are those assigned to palace duty at the Imperial Court, or those with other impediments (koshō), a reply will be given at a later date."

Item: On the twenty-fifth day, cloudy. Lord Gomi came around to inspect during the afternoon hours, also stopping by to worship. He donated one kan of coins (sankisen), and sake was offered to him in return from the entire shrine community. Lord Mori, Lord Matsushita-Bungo, the Ōinokami, the Iyonokami, Shinkichi, both shrine administrators (zasshō), and the carpenters all came out to receive him. It was said that from there he would proceed to pay his respects at Shimogamo Shrine.

Item: On the first day of the second month, the carpenters and other various craftsmen began their work for the current year starting from this day. During the intervening period, the work had been delayed due to the lack of available timber.

Item: On the eighth day of the same month, five persons — Matsumori, the Nuinosuké, the Zushōnokami, and the Uemon-no-Tayū — went to call upon Lord Gomi, stating that they wished to speak with him directly in person. However, he refused an audience, and they were turned away with the reply that all matters should be communicated through the deputy commissioners (tedai no bugyō).

Item: On the ninth day of the same month, at the residence of the head priest (kannushi-tei), Mori, Kean, the Mozunokami, and the Uemon-no-Suke and the others present conferred together, drew up plans and instructions for the interior of the middle gate (chūmon) and within the tower gate (rōmon), and each withdrew, with the understanding that these would be presented to Lord Gomi upon his visit.

Item: On the tenth day of the same month, Lord Gomi came up. The shrine community heads (shadō-shū), the Mozunokami, the Mimasaka-no-Kami, and the Iyo-no-Kami (were in attendance).
