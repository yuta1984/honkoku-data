　　　　　○Report submitted: Account of rice provisions for mourning-period meal service by the mokudai (deputy administrator), covering Kan'ei 8th and 9th years.

[Approval seal]
Item: 1 koku, 5 to, 8 shō, 4 gō of rice.
　　　For the period from the 18th to the 25th of the 11th month of Kan'ei 8 (1631): eight days of evening meals.
　　　Morning meals were to be provided by the sōmu (shrine administrator) separately.
　　　The recipients were: the sōmu (1 person),御服上臈 gofu-jōrō (5 persons), shoshi (officers), mokudai (deputy), azukari (custodian), bettō (administrator), uta-yaku (poetry official), and shakutori (sake pourers, 2 persons) — totaling 13 persons.
　　　The daily ration (gekō) amounts to 1 to, 9 shō, 8 gō: specifically, 1 shō, 6 gō per person for 11 persons, and 1 shō, 1 gō each for the 2 shakutori.
