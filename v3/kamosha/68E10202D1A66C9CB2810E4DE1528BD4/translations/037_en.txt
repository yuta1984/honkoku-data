Item: On the sixteenth day, clear. Today also, everyone came out to the shrine precinct, and construction work was carried out on various parts of the moat. From today, the cypress-bark craftsmen (hinoki-kawa daiku) entered the site, dressed in hakama and kataginu (formal surcoat).

Item: On the seventeenth day, cloudy in the morning, rainy in the afternoon. Today, the Head Priest, the Chief Priest, the shrine administrator (zasshō), the directing official (sashizoe), and the Bizen [official] — four persons in total — went out to the Kamo Liaison to the Court (Kamo-densō; Lord Karasuma Mitsuhiro [in red]). They went out together regarding the matter of requesting the formal assignment of an auspicious date for dismantling the old main shrine hall, as well as for the erection of the pillars of the temporary shrine hall.

Item: On the eighteenth day, clear. Lord Gomi arrived, and the shrine supervising officials (shadō sashizoe-shū) came out. 〈From today, large ropes and other cords were prepared at the administrative office (chōya).〉

Item: On the twentieth day, clear, with snow in the afternoon. The Head Priest, the Chief Priest, the Samanosuke, and, in place of Bichū, Tōtōmi — these officials went to the Court Liaison (densō) to inquire about the date for the announcement regarding the temporary shrine hall. While it is possible to hold the pillar-raising, ridgepole-raising, and temporary enshrinement ceremonies all at once (as a single formal ceremony, jin no gi), since the old main shrine hall must first be dismantled before anything else can proceed, and since there is precedent in old practice for arranging the dismantling on a privately determined date (uchi-hidon), the purpose of their visit was to convey that the formal grand ceremony (jin no gi) would not be necessary at this stage, and that the old shrine hall would simply be dismantled — and they asked that this be duly noted. The Court Liaison also expressed that this was entirely reasonable.

Item: On the twenty-first day, clear. It was the equinox period (higan). Today, a meeting of the deputy officials (sashizoe) was held at the Influence Hall (eidō).
