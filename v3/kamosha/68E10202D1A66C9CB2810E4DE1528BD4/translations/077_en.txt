It was indeed the case that in the architectural drawings as well, [it was shown as] having a double roof in the space (this transcribed as written in the original). The shrine administrators (shashi) stated that this arrangement seemed appropriate. Furthermore, a Yamato carpenter stated: "I am not aware of a double roof being used for what is called a rōmon (two-storied gate). 【Records for the 3rd Day of the 4th Month】

Item: The 3rd day. Cloudy skies. Today for the first time, the sewing of the divine robes (o-fuku) commenced at the shrine administration office (shamu). However, for today it was decided that the curtains (maku) would be more appropriate, and so the curtains were sewn. The morning meal was provided as a reception by the shrine administration office (shamu), while the evening meal was prepared by the Deputy Administrator-daifu (mokudai-daifu). Food allocations (gegyō) were sent from the general assembly (sōchū) to the deputy administrators (mokudai). The number of persons [receiving allocations] were:

　Five senior female attendants in charge of the divine robes (o-fuku-jōrō); the Bettō-daifu: Kaneshige; the Azukari-daifu: Ujimori;
　The Shoshi-daifu (Ichishō · Yasuyuki); acting for the Mokudai-daifu (Hida-no-kami · Seiken): Kii-no-kami · Hoyo (and others);
　The poetry official (uta-no-yaku) · Nuinodo-no-suke · acting for Nariak: Gyōbu-no-taifu · Tomonao. Total: ten persons.
　(Note: among the five-person group [gojin-shū], court robes [jōe] and plain robes [hōi] are mixed together.)

　Regarding the five senior attendants of the divine robes (o-fuku-jōrō), one portion each assigned as follows:
　　One portion: Tanba-no-kami · Kiyota
　　One portion: Uma-no-suke · Kiyochika
　　One portion: Kamechiyodaifu · Hirohisa
　　One portion: Hyūga-no-kami · Sueyoshi
　　One portion: Minbu-no-jō · Ujinori

　Persons who appeared today (shutsushū):
　　The wife (naigi) of Tanba-no-kami; the daughter of Hyūga-no-kami; on behalf of Kamechiyodaifu: the wife of Mimasaka-no-kami · Takatoshi; on behalf of Uma-no-suke: the daughter of Miya-no-shōhō · Nozomi (also described as the daughter of Naiki); on behalf of Minbu-no-jō: the wife of Bichū-no-kami · Yasuaki.
