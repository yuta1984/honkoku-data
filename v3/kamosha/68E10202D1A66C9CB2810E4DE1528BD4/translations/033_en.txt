Item: The sixth day, clear skies.

Item: The seventh day, clear skies. Lord Itakura Suruga and Lord Gomi both came to make an inspection visit. Thereupon, all shrine officials (shikan), including the elder Kaan, were present, and from among the shrine families (shake), the Miyashō and others including the members of the shrine assembly (shatō-kōshū) also attended. Various matters were inquired into before the deity's presence, and each party gave their responses. The two commissioners (bugyō) instructed the carpenters and other workers involved to apply themselves with due diligence to the affairs of the shrine's construction. This was a matter of great joy for the entire shrine.

Item: The eighth day, clear during the day, frequently cloudy and rainy at night.

Item: The twentieth day, Lord Kinemon came to inspect the construction works, and the members of the shrine's uji-people (ujibito-shū) all attended, holding deliberations on various matters. The shrine priests (shashi) did not attend, as they had all gone up to the capital in connection with affairs relating to the priestly positions (negi-shoku). On this day, a letter was received from the Imperial Palace (kinri) addressed to the Retired Emperor's residence (sen'in). The wording is omitted here.

Item: The twenty-fifth day. Upon the occasion of Lord Kinemon's visit, he inquired as to whether the temporary enshrinement ceremony (kari-sengū) could be completed within the current year. Yōkyū stepped forward and replied that it would not be possible to carry it out by the year's end. The members of the entire shrine community remarked among themselves: "Even during the Tenshō period construction, there was a temporary enshrinement on the twenty-first day of the fifth month of Tenshō 18 [1590], followed by the formal enshrinement (shō-sengū) on the second day of the seventh month of the following year. To reply without hesitation about such matters — matters of which he knows nothing whatsoever — surely amounts to deceiving the current commissioner."

Item: The twenty-ninth day, clear skies. Lord Itakura Suruga came to make an inspection visit.
