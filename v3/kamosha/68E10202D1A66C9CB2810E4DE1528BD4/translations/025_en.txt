Item: The 8th day, clear. A morning assembly was held at the Eido (shadow hall/memorial hall). A messenger was sent to the Mori household requesting that the old records be shown to the entire community (sōchū), and immediate consent was received.

Item: The 9th day, clear skies, rain in the evening. An assembly was held at the Eido. Regarding the upcoming reconstruction project, it was decided by deliberation that a written pledge (okibumi) would be established, stating that no improper conduct or self-serving behavior whatsoever would be permitted.

Item: The 10th day, clear. A messenger arrived from Lord Gomi Kin'emon, inquiring as a precautionary measure whether the arrival of carpenters in the near future might cause any difficulties with regard to lumber and other matters.

Item: The 11th day, clear. Five individuals—Mori, Matsushita, Minbu no Taifu, Kurando no Suke, Sabyōe, and Uemon no Taifu—appeared before Lord Gomi, where they were informed that carpenters would be entering the site around the 17th or 18th of the month, and that the carpenters assigned to this location should be made to calculate the number of workdays required.

Item: The 12th day, clear. At the Eido, the community's documents were sorted and examined.

Item: The 13th day, clear. At the Eido, the documents in the small chest (kobako) were inspected and reviewed.

Item: The 14th day, clear. Five individuals—Ōkinokami, Mimasaka no Kami, Zushono Kami, Tonomo no Kami, Jirōsaku, and Gyōbu no Taifu—went to the Mori household as messengers on behalf of the entire community, where they consulted on all matters and then visited to view the old records.

Item: The 15th day, clear. Rain fell repeatedly during the night.
