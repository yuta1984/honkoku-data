One entry: The 4th day of the same month, clear weather. In the evening, a meeting (yoriai) was held at the Eidō (shadow hall/memorial hall). This was the presentation of two written documents concerning the provisional shrine transfer (kari-sengū) and the ridge-raising ceremony (mune-age). The documents are found on a separate sheet.

One entry: The 5th day of the same month, clear weather. A messenger was sent from the meeting to the shrine officials (shashi). Those present were: Bungo, Ōi, Nuidono, Kawachi, Shuzen, Nagato, Ukon-no-shōgen, Ujiyuki, and Shumenosuke. A gathering was taking place at Matsushita at the time, and because Taiami was present, no deliberations could be held, and they returned. Thereupon, Taiami petitioned to be allowed to inspect the metal fittings of the sacred treasures (shinpō no kanamono). In response to this, the shrine officials (shashi) assigned were: Takahisa, Mochiohisa, and Jūfuku — three persons; and from the ujibito (clan members/shrine community): Bungo, Iyo, Bicchū, Nuidono, and Ōi.
