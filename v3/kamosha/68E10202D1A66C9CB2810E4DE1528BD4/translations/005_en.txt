Item: On the 19th day of the 1st month, six people from the general shrine community — Takahisa, Yoshihisa, Ōigashira (Ujishō), Nagato no Kami (corrected in red ink to: Genkyu), (in red ink: Shūken), and the two zōshō (Right Gate Lieutenant and Shinano no Kami) — called upon Lord Itakura Suō. They requested that he use his influence to bring about the favorable execution of the shrine reconstruction that had been conveyed to the head priest on the previous occasion. His reply was that since the matter stemmed from the Chūgū's (Imperial Consort's) personal vow, it was not within his discretion to alter it, and that all matters should be conveyed to Her Highness the Chūgū through appropriate channels.

Item: On the 23rd of the same month, a Senza-harae (a purification ceremony of one thousand ritual cleansings) was performed before the deity at the shrine, to pray for the successful completion of the reconstruction.

Item: [Date left blank] With the intention of paying their respects at both the residences of the Chūgū and the Chūwa Mon'in (Nakakazu Mon'in), three people — Takahisa, Yoshihisa, and Mimasaka no Kami — presented themselves for the ceremonial visit.
(In red ink: Takatoshi)

Item: [Date left blank] Three people — Takahisa, Yoshihisa, and Zushō no Kami — brought a plan (illustrated map) of the shrine to the Chūwa-in and requested that the reconstruction be carried out in its entirety according to the plan. At that moment, the Nyoin (the former Empress) happened to be visiting the Chūgū, and the reply was to leave the illustrated plan behind. The plan was accordingly left and they departed. On that same day, while the Chūwa Mon'in and the Chūgū were in the same place, they viewed the illustrated plan.

Item: On the 28th of the same month, Mimasaka was summoned by the Chūwa Mon'in, and through the medium-ranking lady-in-waiting (chūrō) Izumi-dono, he was conveyed the message that the complete reconstruction of the shrine would be carried out, and that he should be at ease with this knowledge.
