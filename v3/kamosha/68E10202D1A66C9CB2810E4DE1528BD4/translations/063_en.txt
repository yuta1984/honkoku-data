Furthermore, the sacred mirror (goshinkyo) and the kake-tsuno (hanging ceremonial horns), being of a sacred and secret nature, were viewed only briefly. The bugyō (administrative officials), as well as the shrine priests, ujibito (clan members affiliated with the shrine), carpenters, and indeed even the painters, craftsmen, and lacquerers of ancient lineage, all expressed admiration at the quality of the craftsmanship. However, upon cross-referencing with the mokuroku (inventory list) submitted earlier, it was found that more than ten items were absent. A new inventory of the missing items was then prepared and submitted to Lord Gomi.
