Ujiyuki's Record (Upper Volume)

Record of the Kan'ei Reconstruction and Shrine Transfer
(From the 14th day of the 1st month of Kan'ei 4 to the 25th day of the 9th month of Kan'ei 5)
[In red ink] Ujiyuki: In Kan'ei 4, aged 33, Junior Fifth Rank Lower Grade, Koto Tayū, not currently holding office.

[In red ink] Events prior to this should be investigated further.

Item: On the 14th day of the 1st month of Kan'ei 4 [1627], the shrine priest Takahisa called upon Lord Itakura Suō to pay his New Year's respects. Lord Suō (the Governor of Suō) then informed Takahisa that, by the vow of the Chūgū (the Imperial Consort), an order had been issued for the reconstruction of this shrine (Kamo Shrine), and that Lord Gomi had been appointed as the administrator (bugyō) in charge of the matter. He instructed that this should be conveyed to the members of the shrine community. Takahisa acknowledged this, returned home, and on the evening of the same day, reported these matters at the gathering at the御棚 (mitana) administration meeting in the shrine office (chōya).
