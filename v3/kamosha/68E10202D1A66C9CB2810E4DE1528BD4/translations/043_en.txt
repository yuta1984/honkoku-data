Furthermore, in the record of the sixth day of the third month, it is written: When the imperial messenger arrived, the Head Priest wore full court dress [sōtai] or lower-ranked court dress [ikan]; the monk Ke'an wore ritual robes [saifuku]; and Tomino Hirohisa wore a hunting robe [kariginu]. On the ninth day of the fourth month of the same [year], during the purification rite [kiyoharai] at the Junior Shrine, Tamotsu states that under the direction of Ke'an and Shigetaka, [participants] sat on round rush cushions [enza] below the steps of the Junior Shrine, dressed only in a large under-robe [ōkatahira], a cotton cap, and below the ritual robe [saifuku]—a modest appearance, it is said. Tamotsu reasons from this record that Ke'an had shaved his head and was indeed a Buddhist monk. If so, his service within the inner sanctuary would truly be a rare and extraordinary event, and one might well fear the displeasure of the deities.)

[Main text:]

In addition to the shrine community members, there were approximately thirty others; beyond these, various functionaries, the Deputy Head Administrator (bettō-azukari), the representative of the land steward (mokudai no dai), the Director of the Bureau of Books (Zusho-no-kami), and shrine administrators (shoshi), each attending in their capacity, took their seats in the open corridor (sukiro). Thereupon, Shigetsugu brought the sacred key (o-kagi) and opened the sacred doors. Next, the Junior Ritualist (gon-negi) attended, followed by the Head Priest and those below him. Ke'an proceeded into the inner sanctuary (go-nai). In the outer sanctuary (gejin), the Shrine Administrator Deputy (shamu-dai) headed [the assembly], and upon the steps above: Ujimasu, Ujiei (interlinear note: slightly late in arriving), the Deputy Head Administrator, the Senior Official (taifu), the shrine administrators, and the land steward, all in plain silk robes (hōi). Following these, Uemon-no-suke, Shimōsa, Bungo, and thereafter [furigana: thirty persons / as in the original text] approximately [thirty persons] entered within the curtains, and the sacred treasures were brought out one by one and passed along [in a relay]. At the temporary shrine (karidono) for the sacred treasures, the Monthly Administrator (tsuki-gyōji), Mizu-no-shō, Ukon-no-shōgen, Tonomo-no-kami, and Kawachi-no-kami and others entered to receive [the treasures] and stored them in their respective places (interlinear note: at this time Ke'an made an inspection round).

The manner of bringing out the sacred treasures was as follows: first came the inner sanctuary's offering tables (o-daiban) [interlinear note: lacquered in vermilion], two of them, and the eight-legged stands (hassoku) [interlinear note: lacquered in black], followed by the great paper offerings (ōnusa), sacred curtained screens (o-kichō), sacred floor mats (o-tatami), sacred folding screens (o-byōbu), sacred seat mats (o-za-datami), sacred beach-flooring platforms (o-hamadoko) [interlinear note: four of them], the canopies of the same beach-flooring platforms, sacred robes (o-fuku), two Chinese-style boxes (karabits), offering wands (nusa-gushi) [interlinear note: approximately one shaku five sun in length, octagonal in shape], sacred halberds (o-hoko), and various other kinds of sacred treasures—all were transferred in relay (tengu). In order to create a karmic bond (kechien), people were admitted within the curtains to receive [the treasures] with their hands before they were passed along.

After this, the Junior Ritualist Mitsuhisa and Chief Ritualist of Prayers Shigeyoshi each carried one Chinese-style box (karabits); next, the Head Priest (Takahisa) carried the reliquary stupa (sharītō), the Chief Ritualist (Mochiisa) carried the sutras placed on a willow-leaf [shaped stand], the Kibune Shrine Ritualist (Tokuhisa) carried the sacred sword (o-tachi), and Shigetsugu carried the sacred bow—following this order, they processed out and transferred [these items] to the temporary shrine of the treasure hall. And within the treasure hall [were placed the treasures] from the Junior Shrine up to that time——
