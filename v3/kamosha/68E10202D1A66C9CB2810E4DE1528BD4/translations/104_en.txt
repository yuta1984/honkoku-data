1. The 20th day. Clear skies. This morning at the Palace (Imperial Court), a formal ceremony (*jin'gi*) for the pillar-raising and ridge-beam raising (*jōtō*) of the main hall was conducted. Accordingly, the envoy bearing the Imperial Rescript (*senjishi*) arrived today at the Hour of the Snake (approximately 10:00 a.m.). He first stopped at the lodgings of the *zassho* (administrative officer) Suetsada, where he donned his formal court robes (*sokutai*). As the appointed time approached, the Imperial Rescript envoy

— *(furigana reading: Miyoshi no Zentaishi Hidekate)* — along with the deputy envoy Muneoka Ryōkō, and the bearer of the inspection box (*rankō*) *(marginal note: a functionary called Shissei, a samurai)*, each received a purification ritual (*harae*) performed by the Bureau of Onmyō (*Onmyōryō*) near the First Torii gate.

Next, the Head Priest (*kannagi*) Takahisa *(marginal note: formal court robes, sokutai)*, the Senior Negi Mochiisa, the Junior Negi Mitsuhisa, the Former Head Priest Ikuhisa, and the Honorary Hafuri Shigemura *(marginal note: each in court costume,ikan)* proceeded from the Narabashi Bridge to stand in line before the Rōmon (two-storied gate), awaiting the arrival of the Imperial Rescript. The Imperial Rescript envoy arrived at the Hashidono (bridge pavilion). All persons other than the Head Priest — shrine priests and ujibito — stood in line at the western waiting area (*tamari*) of the Hosodono (narrow hall) and observed the proceedings at the Hashidono. When the formalities there were concluded, as the rescript envoy rose from his seat, the procession passed over the stone bridge and through the East Hikawa Gate (*Higashi Hikawa-mon*), then lined up at the waiting area before the *tsubone* (inner chamber).
*(Marginal note: West is the position of honor; facing north.)*
