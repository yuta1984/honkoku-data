*(Red annotation — from the Shūtō Diary [Head of the Assembly's Diary]):*
*9th month, 2nd day. Year of the Monkey. Fair weather. The main shrine (*go-hongū*) ridgepole-raising (*jōtō*) ceremony was held. Fifty barrels were hoisted onto the ridgepole — these were the "dance-entry barrels" (*mai-iri-taru*). The sacred shimenawa rope, dried vine and cloth totaling six willow[-branch bundles], four gate-keepers (*kado-no-osa*), two bundles — sixty *kan* worth of materials were used. Seven *koku* of glutinous rice and rice cakes, and ten *koku* of sake were used.*

Item: On the 24th day of the 12th month, on the night of the *Shō-sengū* (Main Enshrinement), certain persons among the *jige-kannin* (lower-ranking court officials) carried five *sao* [carrying poles] of long chests (*nagamochi*) [and presented them as offerings].

*(Red annotation — from the Shūken Diary):*
*11th month, 12th day. Year of the Snake. Fair weather. Lists of shrine priests (*shashi*), *ujibito*, various officials, and *kyōsho-shū* (sutra hall staff), including their clothing allowances, were drawn up and submitted to Lord Kinemon. All complete.*
*Same [month], 27th day. Fair weather. Provisional enshrinement (*kari-sengū*) at Kataoka Shrine. At the Hour of the Rat (midnight), offerings of rice amounting to 1 *koku* 2 *to* were sent [to be received]. Three *koku* were allocated as a celebratory gift for lacquering the cauldrons of the sake hall and bathhouse; the *Moku-no-jō* (Director of Carpentry) provided [the funds].*

*(Red annotation):*
*12th month, 4th day. Lord Kinemon came up [to the capital]; Sōnyū accompanied him, carrying a plain white robe (*jōe*) and divided skirt (*sashinuki*). At Fujiki Bridge, the upper bridge of the bridge-hall was dismantled and rehung.*
*Same [month], 24th day. Rain fell from early evening (*shoya*). At the Hour of the Boar (*inu-no-koku*, approximately 10 p.m.), the *Shō-sengū* (Main Enshrinement) was performed. The *tendō* (imperial messenger) was the Urakawa [Karasuma] Major Counselor (*Dainagon*). The *hōhei* (offering of sacred branches) and *senmyō* (imperial proclamation) were [delivered] by Lord Karasuma. Lord Kinemon also lodged [at the shrine] through the night.*

[The long chests were] offered up. Inside these long chests there are six *me* (items/eyes [of netting?]). This is a record of the copy.

*(Red annotation):*
*26th day. A *nō* performance was held as a celebratory ceremony for the enshrinement in front of the government office (*chōya*). [The pieces performed were:] Takasago, Tamura, Shintanbōsen, Benkei, and Tatsuta.*
