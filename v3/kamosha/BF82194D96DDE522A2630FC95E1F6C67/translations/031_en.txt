Main Shrine (Honmiya)　　　Amatsu Hikohiko-ho-ninigi no Mikoto

Kataoka-sha　　　　　　Ōkuninushi no Mikoto　　*(Because the divine offerings number seven, and there are seven names for a single deity)*

**[Left folio]**

Kifune-sha　　　　　　Kuraokami (High Water Dragon Deity)
　　The inner deity: Susanoo no Mikoto　　※ Oral transmission
　　The chapter on the Age of the Gods states: "Drawing his sword and slaying Kagutuchi, he divided him into three parts. One part became the deity of thunder; one part became Ōyamatsumi no Kami; one part became Takaotsutsumi."

Shingū-sha　　　　　　*(Yamao)* 　Takaotsutsumi　*(Kawao-sha)* *(Located by the riverside)*
　　About five hundred years ago, there was traffic and communication between this place and Kurama, but when the road fell into disuse and was cut off, the deity was enshrined here by transfer (kanjō) and worshipped.

Ōta-sha　　　　　　　Sarutahiko no Mikoto
　　This is the deity of the arising of a single thought (*ichinen hasshō*). The small shrines to the left and right are of the same body; the Fukutoku-sha (Benzaiten) and the Chinjusha remain unclear (as to their enshrined deities).
