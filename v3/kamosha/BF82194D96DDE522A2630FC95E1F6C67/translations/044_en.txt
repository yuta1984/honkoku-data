Why was [the shrine] built in the uchi-dai (inner platform) style during the Kan'ei [reconstruction]? The Kan'ei [reconstruction] was carried out during the reign of Lord Daitoku-in (Tokugawa Hidetada), and all matters were constructed with great care and thoroughness. The constructions of the Kōji and Tenshō eras were simplified. Therefore, it was decided that [the shrine] should be built in the same manner as the Kan'ei reconstruction, etc.

Although [the chief carpenter Saemon] objected, saying it should perhaps follow the Tenshō style, it was again decided that the Kan'ei precedent should be followed, etc.
