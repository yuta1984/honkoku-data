Ōnamuchi no Kami (大己貴神)
　　This is the explanation of the Shamusoke (社務家, shrine administrative house). Ōnamuchi no Kami has seven names. This is a single oral tradition (kuden) connected to the Nihongi (日本紀). The seven shrines each separately enshrine and worship one of these seven names.
