Copy of a letter sent up from the Lower Shrine. Circulated to the Upper Shrine along with the draft; passed through to the administrative officer (zōshō) as customary.

Now, with regard to the matter of the origins (yuisho) that you had previously inquired about — you were so kind as to promptly circulate your draft to us. As for our shrine's version, though it largely follows your shrine's draft with only minor differences, we have at last completed our own draft following deliberation, and we are now sending it up to you. We humbly inquire: should the final document be written in formal vertical script (tategaki) on the principal paper, and should it be submitted folded into quarters? We also wish to ask whether, once both parties have completed their drafts, all parties should submit them together on the coming twenty-seventh day. We kindly request that you inform us regarding the matter of preparing the final document (honshi). We hereby return the draft that we had borrowed. We sincerely apologize for the considerable delay in doing so. We write thus in hopes of conveying our intentions.

Respectfully submitted.

Fifteenth day of the Eleventh Month

Upper Shrine　　　　　　　Lower Shrine
　Natō, Junior Fourth Rank (Shō-shii)　　　　Kamō-kyō, Third Rank (Sanmi)
　Nakaooji, Junior Fourth Rank (Ju-shii)　　　Tanaka, Junior Fourth Rank (Ju-shii)

We humbly await your reply.
