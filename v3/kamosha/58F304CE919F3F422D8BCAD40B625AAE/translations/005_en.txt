## Kamo no Agata-nushi: Register of Ages and Succession (continued)

**Born in An'ei 4th year (1775), year of Kinoto-Hitsuji (乙未)**

First son of the late Kiyoharu:
Removed from the daily attendance register (nicchichō); childhood name Motarō — **Funaki Kiyonobu** (舟木清延)
→ Removed from the list of shrine families on the 8th day of the 5th month of Kōka 4th year (1847), due to succession into another family.

First son of the late Tokukiyo:
Removed from the daily attendance register — childhood name Tokujirō — **Okamoto Kiyotomo** (岡本清類)

---

**Born in An'ei 5th year (1776), year of Hinoe-Saru (丙申); age 81 as of the year of U (卯)**

Third son of the late Yasusue:
Newly appointed on the 29th day of the 10th month of Tenpō 11th year (1840), at that time age 65.
Shingū Hafuri, Junior Fourth Rank Upper Grade (正四位上), Governor of Wakasa Province: **Nakaōji Yoshihaya** (中大路季快).

First son of the late Tsunesuke:
Died on the 7th day of the 10th month of Kaei 5th year (1852), at the age of 77.
Held the position of "First Elder" (ichi-rō) at that time.
Junior Fourth Rank Lower Grade (正四位下), Governor of Tanba Province: **Yamamoto Tsunatada** (山本経董).
Born on the 21st day of the 10th month.

Second son of the late Tokukiyo:
Removed from the daily attendance register — childhood name Kinojirō — **Okamoto Kiyotomo** (岡本清知).

---

**Born in An'ei 6th year (1777), year of Hinoto-Tori (丁酉); age 80**

First son of the late Kunikiyo:
Junior Fourth Rank Lower Grade (従四位下), Governor of Chikuzen Province: **Shiba Tomokiyo** (芝具清).
Born on the 10th day of the 6th month.

First son of the late Urakiyo:
Died on the 20th day of the 9th month of Kaei 5th year (1852), at the age of 76.
Held the position of "Second Elder" (ni-rō) at that time.
Junior Fourth Rank Lower Grade (正四位下), Governor of Iki Province: **Higashitsuji Kiyonobu** (東辻清矢).
Born on the 27th day of the 7th month.

---

**Born in An'ei 7th year (1778), year of Tsuchinoe-Inu (戊戌); age 79**

Second son of the late Takeaki:
Junior Fourth Rank Lower Grade (従四位下), Governor of Bungo Province: **Minamiōji Noriaki** (南大路式顕).
Born on the 8th day of the 10th month.

First son of the late Yasuaki:
Junior Fourth Rank Lower Grade (正四位下), Governor of Ōsumi Province: **Fujiki Sukeaki** (藤木扶顕).
Born on the 25th day of the 7th month.
