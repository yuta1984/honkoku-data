## Kamo no Agata-nushi: Register of Ages and Succession

**Born in Meiwa 1st year (1764), year of Kinoe-Saru (甲申)**
Resigned on the 12th day of the 4th month of Kaei 4th year (1851); died in the same year in the □th month, at the age of 88.
First son of the late Hisaaki (陳顕).
Former Shingū Negi, Junior Fourth Rank Upper Grade (正四位上), Governor of Ōsumi Province: **Fujiki Kazuaki** (藤木数顕).
Born on the 17th day of the 12th month.

**Born in An'ei 1st year (1772), year of Mizunoe-Tatsu (壬辰)**
Resigned on the 5th day of the 6th month of Kaei 4th year (1851); died in the same year in the □th month, at the age of 80.
Second son of the late Tsunehisa (経古).
Former Shingū Negi, Junior Fourth Rank Upper Grade (正四位上), Governor of Bitchū Province: **Okamoto Sadashi** (岡本経必).
Born on the 29th day of the 8th month.
