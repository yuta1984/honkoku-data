Hoshino Sōi Minamoto Sadayasu

○ Regarding the illness of the御所様 (the imperial or noble personage), the attending physician (御執匙) Kobayashi Bungo-no-suke declined due to his own illness, and the duty was assigned to Fukui Ōmi-no-kami, who accepted. However, whereas the prescribed medicine had previously been carp broth (kōgyo-tō), it was changed at this occasion to bunshō-tō (a different medicinal preparation).

22nd day (Kishi/己巳), Cloudy. Regarding the finalization of Chikoko's remarriage, [I] went to Seyakuin (the household of the施薬院), where the father and son met and the matter was duly conveyed. ○ [I] stopped by at Horikawa's residence and conveyed various greetings regarding yesterday's matters.

23rd day (Kōgo/庚午), Clear. Today, on the occasion of the Sanban-gakari御田楽 (sacred music performance) at Ujitetsu's residence, Kyōsen and Kyōkei attended for dinner. As this was Kyōkei's first time performing in a substitute capacity, everyone gathered. Kyōki was absent due to illness. Kyōsen and Kiyouo, both of them, were entrusted with and accepted the role of organizers from this year onward. Kyōko attended the possession ceremony after nightfall, and all matters proceeded as customary. ○ Regarding the successful arrangement of Yoshie's marriage, congratulatory gifts arrived from Hoshōin and two persons — Daigi and Horikawa Saemon-no-daishi — consisting of a box containing two ladies' fans and one tan of crimson silk. ○ Horikawa Ōmi-no-kami's petition to resign his office, return his post, and surrender his position certificate (位記) was heard and granted as requested.

24th day (Shinbi/辛未), Rain. Following Horikawa Saemon-no-daishi's succession to the headship of the household, scribes (史生) and officials were invited for the first time. Shinjo Iyo-no-jō, Shinoda Kazusa-no-jō, Fukunishi Ōsumi-no-sakan, and others attended. The rest did not attend. I attended in everyday dress to offer congratulations, and celebratory sake and food were served, which I partook of. Horikawa Hyōbu-no-chū also came and joined in the same manner.

25th day (Jinshin/壬申), Clear. [I] visited Seo Kishi-shirō's residence for the first time after the mourning period ended, conveyed various greetings about Kyoto matters, and also informed him that Yoshie's marriage arrangement with the Hoshino family had been concluded.

Regarding the following month's 1st day: a directive was issued for prayers to be offered simultaneously at all shrines. The proclamation text for the norito (ritual prayer) reads as follows:

"In recent times, the harvests have been poor, and last autumn's crop was especially sparse, with the people on the verge of starvation. To dispel this ill omen and welcome an abundant year, we rely entirely upon the divine grace and protection of the gods. Therefore, prayers for abundant harvests of the five grains, peace throughout the realm, the eternal prosperity of the imperial reign, and the joy and wellbeing of the common people shall be offered on the first day of next month, with all shrines united in their devotion, drawing forth their utmost sincerity and concentrating their fervent prayers."
