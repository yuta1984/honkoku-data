The chief priest (kannagi) and others proceeded together to the sanctuary and took their seats at the haiden (worship hall), performed the divine rites from beginning to end, and upon completion, all five persons withdrew to the duty station (tsume-bansho). The chief priest and both officials then withdrew directly to the imikoya (purification house), and each returned home before the Hour of the Rooster (approximately 6 p.m.).
