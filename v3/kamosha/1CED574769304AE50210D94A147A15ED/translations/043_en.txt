　　Record of Duties Performed by the Daikan (Land Steward)

Article 1: The following are strictly prohibited: absence from daily attendance (nissан), proxy transfers (dаiten), serving as a substitute attendant (tomosiro), acting as a proxy in one's official duties (shokudai), serving as a proxy family member/page (iegodai), and lodging elsewhere (taшuku).

Article 1: Serving as a proxy guard at the shrine precincts (shatō bandai) and proxy prayer attendance (gokitō-dai) are permissible.
　　On the days of sacred rites (shinjи), proxy guard duty (bandai) is not permitted.

　　　The above articles shall be strictly observed.
