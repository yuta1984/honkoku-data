One item: Three shō-measure casks of sake (this year: two shō); three varieties of side dishes (this year, suspended — therefore no side dishes; [originally:] mackerel in vinegar dressing (saba no nuta), octopus dressed in white sauce (tako shiramono), and simmered dishes [the fillings being small taro and grilled tofu, etc.]).
　　　　The laborers' fees (busen) are to be sent to the Hyōgijo (deliberation office) for collection.

One item: Camphor (twenty ryō-weight); one folded pack of hanshi paper; along with tea bags, split firewood, a tea kettle, an iron trivet, a hand bucket, and ladle.

One item: Taking advantage of today's occasion, two persons responsible for the equipment hand-over on the first day of the Tenth Month are to be designated and arranged.

　　　The First Day of the Tenth Month

One item: Today, the two persons in charge of the box hand-over to the current Daikan are to dress in formal kamishimo attire, proceed [to the location] around the Hour of the Horse (noon), carry out a thorough inspection and hand-over, and upon completion, the ceremonial gratuity (shūgi) is to follow the same precedent as last year.

One item: Approximately ten eggplants for the Eleventh Month's Aitsukasadori meeting (joint administrative gathering) are to be delivered from the Daikan to the Bettō Tayū (chief administrator).

　　　The Eighth Day of the Twelfth Month

One item: At someone's residence, the accounting of all miscellaneous expenses incurred during the assembly (san-chū) is to be conducted.
