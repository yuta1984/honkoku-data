　　　　Before the Bon Festival

One: The accounting (sanyō) of expenses (iriyō) for this April's Omigawari Festival and the Kurabeuma (horse racing) should be completed, but as everyone is occupied with various duties, it has been deferred until winter.

　　　　Second Day of the Ninth Month

One: This evening, all members shall gather at Kanken-tei to discuss the necessary supplies for the upcoming eighth and ninth days, and to determine in advance (uchitori) the persons assigned to attend duty on the morning of the ninth day (three persons on the eighth day, two persons on the ninth day).
