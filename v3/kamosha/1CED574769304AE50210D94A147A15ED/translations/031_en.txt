　　　　The Eighth Day

One: Prepare in advance for the following morning's use — 2 tachi (long swords), 2 heigushi (ritual wand skewers), and 2 shōgi (folding stools).
One: After breakfast, the daikan (administrative officers) shall each assemble at the mochi-pounding lodging (the residence of Keiji), and have the laborers pound the mochi. Notably, fifty hanahira-mochi (flat decorative rice cakes) shall be made (approximately 5 sun thick — as thin as paper).

　The remainder is to be prepared as red bean mochi (azuki-mochi) for entertaining the next day's party of Kanenori's attendants, the 2 fumi-sashi (document carriers), and the 2 tachi-obi (sword bearers). Additionally, prepare persimmons, chestnuts, and the like, and set out the 42 kamidachi (paper-and-straw decorative sticks). The method of preparation is recorded below. With these matters concluded, all parties dispersed.
　　Note: After the mochi-pounding is finished, the laborers shall be given sake, mochi, and the like before being sent home. No further provisions (gekō) shall be given beyond this.
