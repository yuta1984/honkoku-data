Items Required for the Ninth Day

One: Sashibu (official appointment documents; 6 sheets of hōsho paper required): 2 documents　　　　One: Fumi-sashi no tsue (document-carrying staffs): 2
One: Shōgi (folding stools): 2　　　　One: Tachi (long swords): 2

　　The above matters having been settled and recorded, all withdrew.

　　　　The Eighth Day

One: Prepare for the following morning: 2 tachi (long swords), 2 nusa-gushi (ritual wand skewers), and 2 shōgi (folding stools).
One: The daikan (administrative officers) shall each assemble after breakfast at the mochi-tsuki-yado (mochi-pounding lodgings; the residence of Keiji).
　　Have the laborers pound the mochi. In particular, fifty hana-hira mochi (flat decorative mochi, approximately 5 sun across, as thin as paper) are to be prepared.

　The remainder shall be for the Kaneyori group's use on the following day. Two persons assigned to carry documents (fumi-sashi) and two persons assigned to wear tachi swords shall be invited and entertained. Red bean mochi shall be prepared, along with persimmons, chestnuts, and the like. The forty-two kamidachi (paper-and-straw decorative sticks) shall be set up by skewering them in place. The method of preparation is recorded below. With this completed, all withdrew.

　　However: once the mochi-pounding is finished, the laborers shall be given sake and mochi, and then sent home. No other provisions (gekyō) are to be issued.
