**The 16th Day**

One item: A hospitality meal (kyōō-zen) for one person is to be presented by Tsunetoki.

**The 29th Day of the 3rd Month**

One item: The two persons designated for御料籠 (goryō-kago, the palanquin duty), Kangen and Onken, are to report for duty. The notification of the ritual three days prior, and the guidance on the day itself, will be issued by the monthly duty officer (tsukigyōji).

One item: Tomorrow, for the wooden boat ceremony, the following items are required: vine-hooks (tsurukake), two pieces of flat wood (katagi), and two pairs of straw sandals (zōri). For this month, sacred sake (omiki) is to be prepared: one shō of sake, two pieces of dried fish (hoshi-zakana), and a small amount of washed rice. However, this is subject to the agreement of the five persons concerned. The above preparations are to be made by Tsunetoki.

One item: Two laborers (ninpu) are to be assigned to Gorōtarō. (They are assigned at the instruction of Tsunetoki. Once the work is completed, the laborers are each to be given eight gō of rice as a meal allowance for lunch.)

One item: Tomorrow will be an independent meal arrangement (dokiben). However, the items brought are to include sandals (kutsura/zōri) received from others.

**The 1st Day of the 4th Month**

One item: For the Osaraimatsuri (Purification Festival), to be conducted in the same manner as the 11th month ceremony, the following persons are to report for duty:

　　Shrine Master (kannushi): Horei　　Priest (negi): Norihisa

　　Celebrant priest (hafuri): Tsuzuhei, Tsunetoki (acting for Tsunonae)　　Deputy officials (daikan): Kangen, Onken (acting for Kunikiyo), Ujito

　　Yin-Yang diviner (onmyō): Yoriken　　Sōjin Head (shōjin tōnin, ritual abstinence leader): Hōchō, Benseī, Hōyō, Takeken, Naonozomi, Misaodashi, Nukan, Tōzetsuken, Jinbutsu, Risan

　　(Note: Among the deputy officials, Bensei is assigned as the ritual abstinence leader; accordingly, four persons are to report for duty.)

One item: Today, sacred sake (omiki) is to be offered. It is to be prepared by entrusting Sōichi with the task. The inner sanctuary's portion is to be prepared by Kangen.
