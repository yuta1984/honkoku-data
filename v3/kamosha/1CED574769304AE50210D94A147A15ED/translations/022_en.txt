[Continuing from the previous page regarding the confirmation of robes by the Shoshidai and Mokudai:]
They inquire whether there are any discrepancies in the left-side ishiuchi (stone-strike pattern), and similarly for the right side; upon receiving the reply that there are no discrepancies, the matter is provisionally recorded and they withdraw. Next, two daikan wearing jōe step forward and announce that the ranpu (hair-setting water) and morobinaizu (sidelocks water) are to be distributed by Kyōji. After this announcement, the riding attendants (norizuri) withdraw.

One item: The robes for the head priests (kannushi), those at the foot of the staircases (kaidan-shimo), and the onmyōji shall be distributed to them at their own convenience. Once this is complete, the shōzoku-bugyō and daikan shall instruct the porters to close up the Chōya as it was at the start, and each person shall withdraw.

One item: The large drum (ōtsuzumi), gong drum (shōko), and buchi (two), tanpo (two), and tatami mats (two mats), etc., stored at the Takakura (high storehouse) shall be brought out and placed at the naorai-dokoro (post-ceremony banquet hall).
