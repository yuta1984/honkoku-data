　　　　22nd day

Item: Today, the ritual robes are brought out. The Shōzoku-bugyō (Robes Administrator), Waahei, Waakiyo, the current deputies Yoshitsune and Mitsuki, and one of the former deputies — all, promptly after the morning meal, have laborers carry the robes packed in long chests (nagabitsu) from the storehouse at Takakura, and transport them to the Imikoya (Ritual Purity House). On the first day of the month, the mounted attendants (noriziri) each come to collect [their robes].

　　　　Around the 28th–29th day

Item: Regarding the receiving of the assignment tallies (sashifu) on the coming first day of the month, one deputy is to call upon the mounted attendants of Shitsu and Kanatsu with due courtesy.
　　(Note: The deputy on duty at that time, wearing formal hemp formal wear (asa-kamishimo), is to receive the assignment tallies; Yoshitsune is to proceed to the area west of Narabashi.)

Item: On the same day, regarding the pouring of sake offerings and the tethering of horses at the official hall (chōya) on the coming 5th day, in recent years it has been customary to ask those wearing ritual white robes (jōe) from among the deputies — this too should be requested of them in advance.

Item: Forty strands of midareo (braided/tangled cords — 20 for the left rope, 20 for the right rope) are to be prepared. Two shō of payment rice (ryōhan) are to be distributed.
　　(This year it is assigned to Oka no Kyūzaemon.)
