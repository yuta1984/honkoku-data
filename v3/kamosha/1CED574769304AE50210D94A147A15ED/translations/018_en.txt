One item: From the Yin-Yang household (onmyō-ke), seven meals are sent to the Yatome (eight sacred maidens), and two meals to the deputy officials (daikan). The deputy officials for this year are Kangen and Onken. The items are recorded as follows:

　　Rice (placed in and covered with butterbur [fuki] leaves); Lettuce (blanched greens with bonito placed on top, served in an earthenware dish); Tofu, two pieces (in an earthenware dish); Eel (unagi), two fish (in a shallow bowl [kubatsuki]); Bracken (warabi) dressed greens (in a woven-pattern earthenware dish).

　The above is delivered after the morning meal. A return acknowledgment is to be sent, wrapped in half-paper (hanshi), expressing gratitude with the phrase "medetaku" (auspicious/with joy).

One item: To the two persons of Misaodashi and Tōri (Tsū no Hito), two shō each of rice (or sake) are to be sent.

**The 5th Day**

One item: After the morning meal, following guidance as directed, the two deputy officials (Tsunetoki and Ujito) shall proceed to the residence of whichever of the Yatome is designated, for the garlic sake (hiru-sake) ceremony.

**The 21st Day**

One item: The day before, it is instructed by the Ontō (senior official) to Gorōtarō that two laborers shall come the following day to bring out the ceremonial robes (shōzoku). Furthermore, the Shōzoku Bugyō (Costume Magistrate) has already notified via circular letter the shrine priests (shashi) and the Norijiri and those below them, that on a specified day at the Hour of the Snake (around 10 a.m.), they are to be assembled at the Imikoya (ritual purification house) for the handing over of the robes. The deputy officials are not involved in this matter. However, the two deputy officials who are to appear on the day should coordinate with each other in advance. Also, the current deputy officials are to notify the former deputy officials that one person bearing a sealed message should appear.

**The 22nd Day**

One item: Today, the ceremonial robes are brought out. The Shōzoku Bugyō (Costume Magistrates) are Norihisa and Toshiyasu; the current deputy officials are Onken and Ujito.
