One item: Yesterday, the Yin-Yang household (onmyō-ke) sent seven meal trays to the Eight Maidens (yatome) and two meal trays to the deputy officials (daikan). The deputy officials for the current year are Kangen and Onken. The items are recorded below:

　　Rice (placed in and covered with butterbur leaves) ／ Lettuce (as a dressed dish, topped with bonito, placed in an earthenware vessel) ／ Two pieces of tofu (placed in an earthenware vessel) ／ Two eels (placed in a hollow cup) ／ Bracken dressed dish (placed in a cloth-patterned earthenware vessel)

　The above arrives after the morning meal. They are returned wrapped in hanshi paper with a message of congratulations.

One item: Two shō of rice (in the form of stamped certificates/han) are sent to each of the two persons: Misaodashi and Tōzetsuken.

**The 5th Day**

One item: After the morning meal, depending on notification, the two deputy officials—Tsunetoki and Ujito—are to go to the residence of one of the Eight Maidens for the garlic sake (hiruzake) ceremony.

**The 21st Day**

One item: The day before, Ontō instructed Gorōtarō that two laborers should come the next day for the taking out of the ceremonial robes (shōzoku-dashi). Furthermore, the Ceremonial Robe Officials (shōzoku-bugyō) had previously sent a circular notice to the shrine priests (shashi) and those of the rank of noriiri and below, informing them on which day and at what hour of the mi (around 10 a.m.) they should assemble at the Imikoya (the ritual purity house) to receive and hand over the robes. The deputy officials are not involved in this matter. However, the two deputy officials who appear on the day are to coordinate with one another in advance. Furthermore, the current deputy officials are to notify the former deputy officials that one person bearing a sealed document should also appear.

**The 22nd Day**

One item: Today is the ceremonial robe distribution day (shōzoku-dashi). Ceremonial Robe Officials: Norihisa and Juho. Current deputy officials: Onken and Ujito.
