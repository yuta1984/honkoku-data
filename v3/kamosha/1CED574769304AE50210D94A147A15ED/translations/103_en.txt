All matters were agreed upon in consultation. The conditions were as follows: for the three seasonal ceremonies (kisshiki) of the Small Festival (shōsai), the porridge offering (kayu goryō), and the foot archery (hosha), attendance (sankin) should be made; for the three occasions of New Year's Day, the third day's rabbit-staff ceremony (uzutsue), and the seventh day's white horse ceremony (hakuba), Keisen should attend; and for the御棚 (sacred shelf ceremonies) as well as the sacred guard duty (gobiban), Yasuteru should attend. Additionally, regarding the御棚会 (sacred shelf assembly) for which the two individuals have reported obstacles (koshō), it was stated that a notification would be made in the near future to the assembly hall (kaisho), and that an official directive should be issued for the former daikan to attend.

**24th day (Tsuchinoe-Uma), Fine weather.** Keisen alone went to the Council Hall (hyōgisho) and made representations to Toshihira at the assembly hall, stating: since the two among the current daikan—Yasuyuki and Yasumasa—have reported obstacles, a directive should be issued for the former daikan to attend during the upcoming New Year's御棚会 ceremony. On this occasion, Toshihira informed him that since there was as yet no candidate for the newly appointed position regarding the vacancy mentioned, the former daikan should first attend during the Small Festival on the 29th. Since this matter had been raised informally in passing, there was no need to make a separate formal representation.

**27th day (Kanoto-Tori), Cloudy.** Today, an imperial appointment (senka) was issued for transfers across ten positions, from the Shinmiya hafuri down to the ujigami negi. Although this was to have followed the imperial court's assembly (goyoriai) at the imperial palace, since the Small Festival's sacred rites were approaching, a special petition was submitted and the appointment was granted. There are indeed many precedents for such a procedure. Today, I was summoned to the assembly hall and informed that since there was still no newly appointed person, the daikan officials should attend in place of the vacancy during the sacred rites throughout the coming first month (New Year period). I then went to Yasuteru's residence and reported the communications from the assembly hall on the 24th and today. Furthermore, regarding the matter of notifying the sōja dayū (superintendent of shrine affairs) of the names of the daikan officials and those attending in place of the vacancy of the ujigami hafuri, beginning from the Small Festival—as previously agreed—it was settled that Yasuteru would make this notification.

**29th day (Mizunoto-I), Light snow.** From among the eight court ladies (yaotome), the festival meat offering fees (sokonryō) for the two persons of the daikan and the U(ji) [clan representative] arrived today from Tekken among the yaotome: whereas the regular annual payment in recent years had been three shō and six gō, due to this year's drought damage (kandson), the amount was one shō, five gō, nine shaku, and eight sai.
