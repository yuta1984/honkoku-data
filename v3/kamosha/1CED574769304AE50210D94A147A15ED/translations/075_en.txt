**Items to be Distributed by the Sata-nin (Administrative Officials)**

One item: 25 pieces of ray/skate fish (鰩), with 4 pieces to be cut thin in advance; One item: 200 persimmons (called "Kubo")
One item: 2 shō of chestnuts; One item: 4 portions of sashинуки (ceremonial hakama) for child attendants
One item: 1 chōshi (ceremonial sake vessel with lid)

Of the above, the ray, chestnuts, and persimmons are to be brought from the assembly hall (kaisho). The sashинуки and chōshi are to be arranged in advance, and on the 8th day, persons shall be sent to collect them.

One item: Sake fee of 6 mon in silver (This is to be paid by Kōken from the "ōrai-den" rice field tax revenues for the current year. It should be arranged in advance that the daikan (administrative official) submits this by the 7th day.)

　　For years of Ne (Rat) and Uma (Horse): Kōken is responsible
　　For years of Ushi (Ox) and Hitsuji (Sheep): Shikkyū is responsible
　　For years of Tora (Tiger) and Saru (Monkey): Sueshoku is responsible
　　For years of U (Rabbit) and Tori (Rooster): Hoken is responsible
　　For years of Tatsu (Dragon) and Inu (Dog): Kyōji is responsible
　　For years of Mi (Snake) and I (Boar): Suenuki is responsible

**Items Required for the 9th Day**

One item: 2 sashifu (official written orders, requiring 6 sheets of hōsho paper); One item: 2 branches for document holders (fumi-sashi)
One item: 2 shōgi (folding stools); One item: 2 tachi (long swords)

The above matters have been decided and recorded. All parties then dispersed.
