Item: Today, sacred sake (omiki) is to be offered. It is to be prepared by entrusting [the task] to Sōichi, while the inner [portion] is to be prepared by Fueki.

Item: From the Onmyō (Yin-Yang) household, ritual food offerings (zō / osagari) are to be sent — seven portions to the Yatome (eight shrine maidens) and two portions to the deputies (daikan). This year [the recipients are] Fueki and Mitsuki. The items are recorded below on the left:
　　Rice (placed in a butterbur leaf and covered with the same)　Lettuce (placed in an earthenware dish as a dressed vegetable, placed on top)
　　(Two pieces of tofu in an earthenware dish; two eels in a dish with a hollow; bracken fern dressed vegetables in a cloth-textured earthenware dish)
　　The above items arrive after the morning meal. Two sheets of hōsho paper are enclosed, and a reply expressing congratulations is to be returned.

　　　　5th day

Item: After the morning meal, as directed, two deputies are to go to whichever of the Yatome's residence [is designated] for the garlic sake (ninniku-zake) ritual. [The two are] Yoshitsune and Mitsuki.

　　　　21st day

Item: For the issuing of ceremonial robes (shōzoku) tomorrow, the instruction that two laborers are to attend is to be conveyed to Gorōtarō the day before by the Soe clan [official]. Moreover, well in advance, the Shōzoku Bugyō (Ceremonial Robe Administrator) is to circulate a written notice to the shrine priests (shashi) and those ranked from Noriziri downward, indicating on what date and at the Hour of the Snake (approximately 10 a.m.) they are to be assembled at the Imiko-ya (Ritual Purity House) for the handover. The deputies are not involved in this procedure; however, the two attending deputies are to coordinate with each other in advance. Furthermore, one person of the "Fūji" rank from among the previous deputies is to appear.
