Item: After the morning meal, as soon as notice is received, two deputies will proceed to the residence of one of the eight young female ritualists (yaotome) for the garlic sake (hiruzake) [ceremony]. The assigned deputies are Yoshitsune and Mitsuki.

　　　　21st day

Item: Regarding the bringing out of the ritual robes tomorrow, two laborers are to attend; this is to be communicated to Gorōtarō by the Soe clan the day before. Moreover, well in advance, a circular notice (mawashibumi) is to be sent to the Shōzoku-bugyō (Robes Administrator), the shrine priests (shashi), mounted attendants (noriziri), and those below, specifying that on such-and-such a day at the Hour of the Snake (around 10 a.m.), [the robes] will be assembled and handed over at the Imikoya (Ritual Purity House). The deputies are not involved in this matter. Furthermore, the two deputies who are to appear [that day] are to confer with each other in advance.
