Same year (Kansei 4), 5th month, 27th day:

One set of sacred hemp offerings (ōnusa/taima, 21 shrines' worth); one御撫物 (o-nademon, a ritual rubbing object, replacement); one noshi, placed on an unpainted wooden stand.
　　The above were presented to the Imperial Palace (Kinri-Gosho). As an honorific first-fruits offering (o-hatsuho), one piece of white silver was graciously bestowed.

One set of sacred hemp offerings (12 shrines' worth); one set of five bundles of wheat rice dumplings (mugi chimaki), placed on an unpainted wooden stand — presented to the Lady of the Long Bridge (Nagahashi-dono).
One set of sacred hemp offerings (12 shrines' worth); one set of three bundles of the same dumplings each, placed on lacquered stands — presented to the Three Tayū (Sandayu).

One set of sacred hemp offerings (21 shrines' worth); one御撫物 (ritual rubbing object, replacement); one noshi, placed on an unpainted wooden stand.
　　The above were presented to the Retired Emperor's Palace (Sentō-Gosho). As an honorific first-fruits offering, 200 hiki in gold was graciously bestowed.

One set of sacred hemp offerings (12 shrines' worth); one set of three bundles of wheat rice dumplings each, placed on lacquered stands.
　　(To the new Middle Counselor [Shin-Chūnagon-dono]; to Lord Ōshima)

The representatives (shuttō) for the above presentations were: Funkyō, acting deputy for the Kifune Shrine Priest; and Kazuhira, acting as substitute for Norito (祝・続平代). Upon returning home, one piece of silver was exchanged for copper cash to pay for the various procured items (shiragemono), and the remaining cash together with the 200 hiki in gold were divided into two portions and collected; the share belonging to the deputy and intermediary staff was held in trust by Funkyō. The ritual rubbing objects (o-nademon) were also kept in Funkyō's custody.

Same year, 5th month, 24th day: Presentation of Rolled Offerings (Maki-rui Kenjō) — as it was the turn of the Kifune Shrine Priest
　　One sheet each was presented to: the Imperial Palace, the Retired Emperor's Palace, the Lady of the Long Bridge (Ukyō-no-daibu), the Sakyō-no-daibu, the Kodaibu, the new Middle Counselor, the Minamoto Middle Counselor, and others.
　　The above were, as in previous years, successfully presented through cordial relations (nyūkon) with the representatives of the entire shrine (isshamōshidai-chū).
