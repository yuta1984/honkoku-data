[Continuation of Annual Duty Assignments]

- Last day of the 10th month —御料籠 (goryōkago, ritual palanquin duty), two persons: Tsunetoki and Binsei
- 1st day of the 11th month — All five to attend official duties together
- 5th day of the same month — Two persons to appear before the Tai and Yaotome groups: Kanken and Onken

　　Note: Due to drought damage (kanson) in Meiwa 8 (1771), attendance gatherings were suspended for eight years from that year. A council (hyōjō) determined that the collected rice of three to one gō would be distributed among the Yaotome group members and both daikan officials, totaling ten persons, at a rate of three shō per person. Accordingly, the distribution is to be carried out once the three to one gō is collected, and the same arrangement applies to the current year.
