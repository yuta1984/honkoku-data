Article 1: Since Samanosuke Tsuneyori (Kyōyō) is to serve as Shōjin-gashira (Head of Ritual Abstinence) the following year, inquiry was made to the kimo-iri (mediating elders) as to how this should be handled. The response was that, since he has been appointed as daikan (deputy official), he should first fulfill that duty himself. Furthermore, regarding whether the person designated as Shōjin-gashira for the following year should be excluded from the daikan lottery (kuji-fuda), or left in as usual, the senior members (rōjin-shū) deliberated at length, but no clear resolution was immediately forthcoming. This matter relates to the deliberations at the time when, in the second year of Kansei (1790), Tajima-no-suke and Bensei were appointed as daikan. It was determined that when Tsuneyori (Kyōyō) is appointed as daikan, no objection shall be raised.

　In the eighth year of Meiwa (1771), due to damage from drought, and with great reluctance, it was decided that for a period of eight years the assemblies would be suspended, and that the collected rice of three to one gō would be distributed among the ten persons comprising the Inu-otome-chū (戌乙女中) and the two daikan—three shō per person, as determined by council deliberation. Accordingly, distribution was to be made upon receipt of the three to one gō. The same arrangement applies to the current year as well.
