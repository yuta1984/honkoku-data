Article 1: Since Samanosuke Tsuneyori (Kyōyō) is to serve as Shōjin-gashira (Head of Ritual Abstinence) the following year, inquiry was made to the kimo-iri (mediating officials) as to how this should be handled. The response was that, as it is a matter to which one has been appointed, one should first fulfill the duties oneself. Furthermore, regarding whether the lottery tickets (kujifuda) for daikan (local official) assignments should be excluded or included as before in the year prior to one's service as Shōjin-gashira, the elder members discussed the matter at length but could not readily reach a conclusion. This is based on the deliberations conducted at the time when Tajima-no-suke Binsei was appointed as daikan in the second year of Kansei (1790). When Kyōyō was appointed as daikan, there had been no particular issue.

Regarding the drought damage of Meiwa 8 (1771): with great reluctance, for a period of eight years, the gatherings were suspended, and a rice payment of three to and one gō (収納米 3斗1合) was to be distributed among ten persons in total — comprising the Itsushi and Otome-chū members and the two daikan — at a rate of three shō per person, as determined by council (hyōjō). Accordingly, the distribution is to take place as soon as the three to and one gō has been collected. The same arrangement applies to the current year as well.
