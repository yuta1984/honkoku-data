One entry: The shrine offering money (heisho-sen) was collected from the year-duty room, with five people's shares received and distributed to each person. This year, the amount was thirty-one mon per person.

One entry: A meeting was held with the people responsible for decorating the sacred shelf (o-tana), followed by an evening meal served to them. The menu, served around the Hour of the Horse (noon), was as follows:
　　Grilled dish (furi-style) / Simmered dish (taro, burdock root, skewered shellfish) / Soup (yuba [tofu skin], sesame) / Namasu (vinegared dish) / Rice / Chopsticks
　　Three types of sake accompaniments (octopus, burdock root, herring roe)
