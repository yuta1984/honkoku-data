With the above concluded, the Chōya (administrative hall) was closed as before, and everyone withdrew. The time was around the half of the Inokoku (approximately 11:00 p.m.).

　　　　The Ninth Day　Clear

One entry: The items brought in advance for the Chōyō (Double Ninth Festival) divine rites were as follows:
　　Tachi swords (two), hei-gushi (offering wands/staffs, two), shōgi/tokigi (folding stools, two), sashifu (credential documents, two)

After the morning meal, the two individuals Tsunetoki (acting as Daiei/Nae) and Onken (each dressed in jōe [white purification robes] and sashinu [formal divided skirts]) proceeded promptly to the shrine precinct. Four individuals carrying bunsō (document holders) and wearing swords (also in jōe and sashinu) accompanied them, and they waited for the divine rites to conclude. As customary, after the rites before the deity were completed, sumo wrestling took place in the south garden of the Hosodono (a narrow hall/corridor building), and all procedures and protocols were carried out as per precedent. The two sashifu (credential documents) were both received and taken back by the Daikan (deputy official). The bunsō duties were carried out by Keiken and Edakiyo; the sword-bearing duties by Oaken and Shōmarudayū.
