For the Mikawari-sai: All five daikan together proceed at the Hour of the Hare (approx. 6 a.m.) to worship at this shrine and at Ōta Shrine, then proceed to worship at Kifune Shrine, Okuhana, and other shrines. Upon completing these visits, they rest at the duty station (tsume-bansho). Shortly thereafter, the御料 (goryo — sacred palanquin/sacred objects) arrives. Before the Hour of the Ram (approx. 2 p.m.), all five persons don their sashimki (formal court trousers).
