Item: The four individuals responsible for fumiさし (document holders) and tachi-obi (sword-wearing duties) should confer and arrange matters in advance.　　Onrui

Item: All annual miscellaneous expenses are to be recorded in the account ledger one by one. (One fold of hanshi paper to be prepared for the use of the colleagues.)

Item: Since Hōreki 5 (1755), the order of duty for the deputies (daikan) has been established. The current order of deputies is as follows:
　　Shōshiinoge　Kyōji　　Jūshiinojō　Bensei　　Jūshinoge　Kanken
　　Jūshinoge　Onken　　Jūshinoge　Ujito

Furthermore, throughout the year, substitution at another person's lodging, substitution for daily shrine visits (nichi-san), substitution for attendance duties, substitution for official roles, and substitution for household retainer duties are strictly prohibited. However, substitution for shrine duty rotations (sha-tō) and prayer duties (go-kitō) is permissible.

Item: On the first day of the 10th month, each of the five members of the kago-gumi should be informed and asked to prepare accordingly.

Item: Personnel to handle the preparation and presentation of divine offerings (shinku) and those passing through the courtyard should be arranged in advance. This year, Kanken and Zetsuken were asked.
