　　　　　13th Day

Article 1: Early in the morning after breakfast, one servant (shimobe) was sent to the ko-tsubone (small office/room) at the shrine head to obtain one set of kago-ke (wicker baskets) and one andon (paper lantern). Note: the kago-ke should have its shimenawa (sacred rope) re-strung, and if the andon is damaged, it should be re-papered and made ready.

Article 1: Items to be brought to the chōya (administrative building): two nomi (chisels), two nokogiri (saws), and one small knife (kozugatana) each per person.

Article 1: Approximately 25 strips of ne-fuji (root wisteria binding material) — [to be] of about three to four shaku in length, well-beaten and split finely — and three stalks of shino-dake (thin bamboo) for use as square-cut material (sunpō). The pillar of the Kawakami-gō section of the chōya has a square notch cut into it; vertical bamboo (tatedake): 2 shaku 5 sun; surrounding bamboo (mawari-dake): 4 shaku 8 sun; bow bamboo (yumidake): 1 shaku 5 sun.

Article 1: It would perhaps be appropriate to prepare one tall lantern (taka-chōchin).
