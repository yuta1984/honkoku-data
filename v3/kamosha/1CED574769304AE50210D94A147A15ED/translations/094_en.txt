## Required Items for Basket Arrangement (籠組) and Shelf Decoration (棚飾り)

**【Basket Arrangement (籠組)】**
- Three varieties of side dishes (肴): octopus (tako), herring roe (kazunoko), burdock root (gobō)
  - Additionally: furikko (seasoning powder/sprinkled ingredient), 1 monme 7 bu 5 rin (in silver currency)

**【Shelf Decoration (棚kazari)】**

| Course | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| Rice (飯) | |
| Grilled dish (焼物) | furi (sprinkled salt preparation) |
| Soup (汁) | cod (tara) as available, clear broth (sumashi) |
| Simmered dish (煮物) | taro, with added carrot, burdock root, dried cod (bōtara) |
| Vinegared dish (鱠/namasu) | daikon radish, bonito (katsuo) |
| Late-night meal (夜食) | Simmered dish: with added carrot, tofu, kelp, dried small fish (iriko) |
| Soup for late-night meal | kakinefuri (a type of seasoning or preparation) |
| Side dish (肴) | sakoshi (small portions in a basket?) |
