Ninth Month (a "large" month of thirty days)

1st day (Kanoe-Tora, 庚寅): Light rain falling from time to time. Regarding the Uchitori ceremony on the 8th and the Sekku (seasonal festival) divine rites and related matters, a general consultation was held today. The district officials, Yasuteru (acting for Chōken), Suenori, and Yasunori gathered at Yasuyuki's residence (the mochi-pounding lodge, 餅搗宿) for a midday meal, after which they conferred on various items. Keisen was occupied with attendances at the Rotationally-scheduled Prayers (旬御祈祷) and separate prayers for the Imperial Court (禁中別御祈祷), and so the district official side recorded his absence.

7th day (Hinoe-Saru, 丙申): Cloudy, strong wind. From Tosa-no-suke Suetsura, the sake-fee silver of six momme converted into cash—six hundred and sixty mon—arrived today as provisions, delivered to Keisen. This was in accordance with a prior request that had been submitted in advance.

8th day (Hinoto-Tori, 丁酉): Clear. After breakfast, the district officials, Yasuteru (acting for Chōken), Keisen, Suenori, and Yasunori each assembled at Yasuyuki's residence (the mochi-pounding lodge). All provisions for hosting the shrine priests and those below them at tonight's Uchitori (内取り) divine rite were fully prepared.
