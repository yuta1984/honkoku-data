(Kaika Memorandum)
Item: The taiko (large drum) and shōko (gong) for the kaika (steps area) are to be prepared by the kaika kimoiri (manager/chief of the kaika officials).

Item: The tatami mats for the kaika area are to be set in place by the jinnin (shrine servants) the following day.

　　　　　The Fifth Day

Item: From the kaika left group, one katariai (liaison/negotiator) accompanied by a shimobe (attendant/servant) is to be dispatched to the head priest (kannagi) to collect the roku-kinu (gift silk stipends) for both left and right groups. The return acknowledgment (henben) for both left and right is to be made from the right group.

Item: Immediately after the morning meal, eight hoko (ceremonial halberds/spears) are to be erected by the jinnin under the direction of the right group's kaika officials. This year, the fukichiri are to be brought with due courtesy (nyūkon) to the catering staff (makanai) of the chōya.
　Locations for erection:
　　One cherry tree at the horse departure point (umaoshi no sakura); one at the whipping cherry tree (sakuuchi-zakura); one at the maple tree (kaede);
　　Five within the lawn of the tongu (temporary imperial/deity rest pavilion): the upper three and the lower two are to be spaced slightly apart from each other.

Item: After the morning meal, one jinnin is to be sent to summon (someone) at the sakadono (sake/wine hall).
