○ Regarding the divine rites of the sacred shelf assembly (御棚会): among the current district officials (代官) of the assembly, since both Yasuyuki and Yasunori were absent due to incapacity (故障), the former district officials Yasuki and Yasutomo served in their place over both days of attendance. As compensation for this service, the two absent officials each passed on their 御結鎮銭 of 240 mon directly to the substitutes. In addition, 300 mon in cash per person was reportedly sent as an undetermined supplementary portion. Furthermore, it is said that the consultation participants (語合の輩) for both days were also arranged through the former district officials.

15th day (己卯), clear. At the Hour of the Hare (around 6 a.m.), the divine rites for the porridge offering (粥御料) were held. The absent-post official (闕職), the district deity celebrant (氏神祝), the district official (代官), and Suenori of provisional Senior Fourth Rank Lower Grade attended in court dress (衣冠). ○ To the five members of the basket group (籠組) — Ujihiko, Yasufumi, Ujifusa, Suenori, and Kiyoyasu — were sent two shō of payment rice per person, one booklet of thin paper (薄様), and one booklet of miscellaneous paper (雑紙) each. Also, to the consultation participant (語合の仁) and Hikkei, who was responsible for decorating the sacred shelf, were sent two shō of payment rice, three booklets of thin paper, one booklet of miscellaneous paper, one awl (錐), a scraping board (掻板), and other items. Note: the items sent to the basket group members were established in the twelfth year of Hōreki (1762) as a rule decided by the deliberation (評定) of the Shingū and other parties, involving both senior and junior members.

Furthermore, the sending of payment rice to the shelf-decorator was not recorded in the official duty records; however, since it had become recent practice to send it, it was sent in accordance with that recent precedent.

16th day (庚辰), clear. This evening, the divine rites for the mounted archery ceremony (歩射) were held. Suenori of Senior Fourth Rank Lower Grade, serving as absent-post official, district deity celebrant, and district official, attended in full court dress (束帯). ○ One portion of the formal banquet meal (饗応膳) for the district officials was collected from Keisen.

24th day (戊子), clear. On the lower Day of the Rat (下子日), around the Hour of the Horse (around noon), the Lantern Lighting Festival (燃燈祭) was held. Suenori of Senior Fourth Rank Lower Grade, serving as absent-post official, shrine official, and district official, attended in court dress (衣冠). Note: the Lantern Lighting Festival is a ceremony in which only the deputy celebrant (権祝) and the lower-ranking celebrant staff (祝方) participate.

29th day (癸巳), clear. Awaji Tokikiyo (age 26) was newly appointed as the district deity celebrant (氏神祝), and the formal appointment decree (宣下) was issued today.

Third Month (a short month)

29th day (癸巳), clear. The offering basket (御料籠) for the 御更祭 (御更 Festival). District officials Yasuteru and Yasunori attended, each dressed in ritual robes (浄衣) and gathered trousers (指貫). ○ The set of three-sun offerings (三寸の具) to be presented at Kibune Shrine on the following first day of the month is to be prepared and kept at Keisen's residence, as in the eleventh month. Also, the vine-hanging (蔓掛け) is to be prepared at Yasuyuki's residence, again as in winter.
