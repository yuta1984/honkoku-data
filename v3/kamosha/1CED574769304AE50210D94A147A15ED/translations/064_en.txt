　　　　　First Day of the Fifth Month

Item: The assignment tallies (sashifu) are to be received from the Shoshidai (Deputy of the Imperial Deputy), and the parties are to line up and read them aloud at the area west of Narabashi. In recent years, this has been arranged courteously with the mounted attendants of Shitsu and Kanatsu. This year, whoever represents Shitsu and whoever represents Kanatsu will perform the duty accordingly. At that time, one deputy dressed in formal hemp formal wear (asa-kamishimo) proceeds to the area of the Goshonya (御所屋, the official residence building) and collects the old assignment tallies before returning.

Item: In the evening, the instruction is to be conveyed to Gorōtarō that four laborers for the robing/robe-lifting duties (shōzoku-age) on the coming 3rd day are to arrive promptly after the morning meal.
