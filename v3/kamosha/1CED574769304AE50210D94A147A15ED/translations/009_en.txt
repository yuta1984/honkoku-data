　　　　11th Day

Item: Today's chōhan (meal duty at the administrative office) was handled by Onken.

Item: At today's chōhan, the banners/notice boards (haribumi) assigned to each of the various gō (district/village associations) were taken home, one copy per district. Since I (the record-keeper) happened to have occasion to meet with Onken at a convenient juncture, I asked him to convey mine together with his. Within today or tomorrow, one copy of the aforementioned haribumi is to be written out in kana script. As paper stock, hōsho paper is to be used, and the labels (fuda) indicating the gō affiliations to be attached to the baskets (kago) are also to be written out and prepared. At the time of the kagori-gumi (enclosure/retreat group) ceremony, all of these items are to be brought to the chōya (administrative building).

Item: Notice of the shrine ceremony (shinjи fure) is to be given to Jinshi and Kyō, as the persons responsible for the御棚錺 (o-tana-kazari, the decorating of the sacred shelves/altar display) on the coming 14th day.
