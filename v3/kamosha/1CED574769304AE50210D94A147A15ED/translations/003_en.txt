The first day of the tenth month, Tenmei 5 (1785), the year of Kinoto-Mi (乙巳).

The five daikan (deputy officials) are as listed below:

- Tajima-no-suke: Binsei
- Yamato-no-Gonkami: Yasuyа　　[marginal note: transferred to Onken]
- Kai-no-suke: Shigenobu　　　[marginal note: transferred to Kanken]
- Tōtōmi-no-suke: Ujito
- Minbu-no-jō: Yukitsune　　　[marginal note: transferred to Tsunetoki]

The aforementioned individuals gathered at Yukitsune's residence after the midday meal, each dressed in formal kamishimo attire.
