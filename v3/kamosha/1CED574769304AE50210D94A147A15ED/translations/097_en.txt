Junior Fourth Rank, Lower Grade — Yasuyuki
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Junior Fourth Rank, Lower Grade — Suenori
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Senior Fourth Rank, Upper Grade — Yasunaga

Item: Throughout the year, other lodgings (ta-yado), proxy daily visits (nichi-san-dai), proxy ritual transfer attendances (ten-ku-dai), proxy duty holders (shoku-dai), and proxy family retainers (keishi-dai) are strictly prohibited.

Item: Proxy shrine watch duties (sha-tō ban-dai) and proxy prayer services (go-kitō-dai) are permissible.

　　The above articles shall be strictly observed.

Item: As this current Year of the Ram (Bunsei 6, 1823) has seen damage from drought, a notice (te-bure) was issued in the past ninth month, mandating that all members of the shrine community observe frugality throughout the year, from the ninth month of this Year of the Ram through the eighth month of the coming Year of the Monkey.

Item: In the first month of the coming year, five advisors/coordinators (kago-gumi no katawase) are to be arranged without delay.

Tenth Month — Short month (29 days)
　First day (cyclical day: Hinoe-Saru / 丙申) — Rain. After the morning meal, the current daikan (shrine deputies) each assembled at the residence of Yasuyuki, dressed in their chakuji-kamishimo (formal attire). The two former daikan, Furuaki and Ujinaga, arrived dressed likewise in chakuji-kamishimo. The costume box (shōzoku-bako) was handed over, containing the right-side yellow robe (ki-hō), the rules document (okite-bumi), one storage box (iri-bako), and one key box (kagi-bako). Thereafter, the former daikan withdrew. Note that in normal years, celebratory sake is served at the time of the box-handover ceremony; however, as frugality is being observed this year, that ritual was omitted.
