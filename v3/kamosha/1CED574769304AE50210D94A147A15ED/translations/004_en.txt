Shortly thereafter, two former daikan (deputy officials) arrived, bringing with them an old costume box containing a box of regulations [okite-bumi] (one box) and a key box (one box), which they handed over. Upon their departure, the following refreshments were offered as a celebratory gesture:

- Soup: [tofu and bonito]
- Sake: [refined sake, two shō]
- Three types of side dishes: [herring roe, octopus, and boiled greens]

Once this concluded, everyone withdrew. It should be noted that Shigenobu was present at this gathering, but I (the author) did not attend.

One item: After dusk (lit. after lamps were lit), all five gathered at Yukitsune's residence, wearing only hakama. That evening, Kanetori, members of the daikan kimoiri (supervisory officials), Chikaken, Kiyotachi, and Kiyotari, both senior and junior members, assembled. At this time, it was proposed that "such-and-such a person wishes to transfer [a post] to a certain individual," and it was requested that "regarding matters of subordinate positions, please provide direction as appropriate." After these decisions were made, the remaining sake and food from the midday meal were brought out and served. This was done at the discretion of the five daikan.

One item: Since Binsei is scheduled to serve as shōjin-gashira (head of ritual abstinence duties) next year, the kimoiri were consulted as to how this should be handled. The response was that since he has been assigned to this role, he should fulfill it himself, and so Binsei assumed this duty.

One item: After the kimoiri withdrew, the annual duties were assigned as follows:

- Annual guidance for daily offerings (nichiku) at Kifune Shrine: Onken
- Same — custody of the nakamanoma box: Tsunetoki
- Same — provisioning lodgings for miscellaneous expenses: the same person (Tsunetoki)
