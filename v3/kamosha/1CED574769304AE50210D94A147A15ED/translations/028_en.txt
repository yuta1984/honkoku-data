One: After the shinjin (shrine servants) finished storing the hoko (halberds/ritual spears), when they came to offer congratulations, they were given sake (served in a teishi [ladle-vessel] with approximately five earthenware dishes) and side dishes (dried cod and wakame seaweed).

One: One daikan (administrative official) shall proceed to the shrine precincts to retrieve the fukichirashi (decorative streamers/wind-blown ornaments) used during the hoko storage ceremony and bring them back.
　　(Note: Once the tenth watch is completed, one daikan shall promptly proceed to retrieve the five fukichirashi in front of the Tongu [temporary shrine] and bring them back.)
　　The said fukichirashi shall be returned to the shoku-naka [administrative body of the shrine] the following day.

One: Three curtains (with two hand-washing tubs) shall be carried by lower-ranking servants and sent to the shrine precincts.

One: At the chōya (administrative hall), lower the shitomi [latticed shutters] as they were originally, confirm fire safety precautions are in order, and then withdraw.

　　　　Seventh Day

One: Instruct Gorōtarō that four laborers for raising/storing the ceremonial robes on the following eighth day shall arrive promptly after their morning meal.

　　　　Eighth Day

One: Due to rain today, the raising of the ceremonial robes is postponed to the ninth day.

　　　　Ninth Day

One: As on the third day prior, open the chōya, and once the shōzoku bugyō (robes administrators: Norihisa and Toshiyasu) and four daikan have assembled, instruct a messenger to ring the bell at the Jingūji (shrine temple).
