One entry: For the portion belonging to the eight shrines (hachi-sha): eight gō of naka-ori (medium-sized folded cypress-wood containers), eight sets of go-ryō go-hei (sacred wands for the sacred portion), and go-ryō kago (sacred baskets) are handed over to the sha-shi (shrine functionaries), with the toneri (attendants) coming to receive them.

One entry: The ō-nusa (large sacred purification wand) is handed over to the toneri.

One entry: Items such as birds attached to heishi (sacred sake flasks/bottles) are handed over to the heishi-mochi (those who carry/handle the sacred flasks).

One entry: To the bettō-dayū (the chief administrator/superintendent), one jō of usu-yō (thin ceremonial paper) and one jō of zō-shi (miscellaneous paper) are to be given. These should be collected and delivered beforehand.

One entry: Upon completion of the mono-mori no gi (ceremony of arranging/piling offerings on trays), one jō of usu-yō, one jō of zō-shi, and one jō of nusa-tori-sen (monetary offerings for sacred wand recipients) [large and small] are to be sent to Taguchi.

One entry: Once the o-tana-kago (sacred shelf/basket ceremony) is concluded, the kago (baskets), ke (food containers/boxes), and andon (paper lanterns) are returned, and the covers of the ko-tsubone (small compartment/side chamber) are laid out. The hinoki-ba (cypress leaves) and matsu-ba (pine needles) are cleared away and dispersed to the respective daikan's (local administrator's) sato-tei (residential quarters/lodgings in the village).

　　　　　　The Fifteenth Day

One entry: As a reward for the hardship and labor of the kago-gumi (basket-carrying group), in accordance with the resolution made on the ninth day of the twelfth month of Hōreki 12 (1762) at a council (hyōjō) of the sha-shi from the Shin-gū and below, and as established among the rō-nyaku-chū (elders and youth group) of Kanetori: two shō of bei-gen (rice strings/rice stipend) [米弦], one jō of usu-yō, and one jō of zō-shi, among other items, are to be sent.

One entry: To the person(s) who arranged and coordinated (katariai/go-ai) the o-tana-kazari (decorating of the sacred shelf): three jō of usu-yō, one jō of zō-shi, one awl (kiri), hatsuri-ita (trimming/shaving board), and other items are to be sent.
