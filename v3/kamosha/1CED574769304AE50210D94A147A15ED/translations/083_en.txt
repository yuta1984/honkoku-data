The annual expenses for communal payments (nakama-harai) amount to approximately 5 kanmon in total. This is to be handed directly to the階下 (subordinate staff). Approximately 20 monme per person. This is separate from the above.

Item: Previously, per the regulations established by the Kaneyor elder-and-youth group (老若中), the following matters — basket assignments (kago-gumi), bamboo receipt affairs, thin paper (usuyō), sumo authorization passes (sumō sashifu), name-writing formats (shōmyō kakikata), etc. — have been examined and written up; the letter of transmittal is as follows.

Item: Where there is a person subject to the granting or revoking of the daikan (代官) post, and it is difficult to grant or revoke [the post] for a single individual, and when there is no suitably qualified person available, the situation is to be reported to the Kaneyori elder-and-youth group for their consideration and instruction.

Item: Regarding the matter of persons at the階下 (subordinate) level, [appointments] shall not be decided by lottery (kuji-tori); rather, a suitably qualified person shall be selected from among the current daikan and recommended to Kaneyori.
