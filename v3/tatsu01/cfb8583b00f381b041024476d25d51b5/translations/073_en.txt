**Chinese-style couplet (with Japanese phonetic readings):**
The golden line hangs down, fine and delicate, baited with fragrant lure;
The golden carp, unhurried and serene, plays idly with the straight-set hook.

**Haiku (by Buson):**
*The fishing line—*
*swayed by the autumn wind*
*blowing through.*
