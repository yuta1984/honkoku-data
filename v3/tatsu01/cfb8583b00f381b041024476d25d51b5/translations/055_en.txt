## Takigi Nō (Firewood Nō)

According to the records:
The Takigi Nō performed at the South Great Gate (Nandaimon) of Kōfukuji temple originated from the temple's second-month (February) religious ceremonies, which were held at night. The temple monks, unable to endure the cold of early spring, would burn firewood in front of the gate and, by the light of the flames, perform various entertainments to pass the long winter nights. Subsequently, the four troupes of sarugaku began performing in this tradition. From the seventh to the fourteenth day of the second month, paper is scattered on the grass to test whether rain is falling; if the paper does not become wet, the performances begin. During this time, Nō is also performed at the Wakamiya Shrine, and on both the ninth and tenth days, the four troupes take turns in their duties.
