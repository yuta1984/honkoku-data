**From the *Shin Senzaishū* (New Collection of a Thousand Years):**

*Saho sugite / Nara no tamuke ni / oku nusa wa / imo ni aimiru / shirushi □ keri*

(The sacred offerings — the *nusa* — that I place at the wayside shrine of Nara, having passed through Saho, are a token of my prayer that I might meet my beloved.)

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Emperor Shōmu
