【Right Page】

The Kasuga Festival is a sacred ceremony of the four shrines of the Great Shrine. It is held twice a year: on the Day of the Monkey in the second month, and on the Day of the Monkey in the eleventh month. This ceremonial form was first established after Nakatomi no Hidemoto submitted a formal petition to the court in the ninth month of Kashō 3 (850), during the reign of Emperor Nimmei. Thereafter, it is said that the festival was first performed beginning on the night of Kōshin (the 57th day of the sexagenary cycle), the 9th day of the 11th month of Jōgan 11 (869), during the reign of Emperor Seiwa.
