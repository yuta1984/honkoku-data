【Gyokuyō (Imperial Anthology)】

*Kasugano ni / mata wakaki sa / i tatsu mama / tsuma komoru tomo / iu hito ya naki*

(Composed by: Tokiwa-i Nyūdō, former Grand Minister of State)

---

**Modern English rendering:**

At Kasuga Plain, even as the fresh young grasses rise again in spring, is there truly no one left to call out lovingly — even though my beloved remains secluded within?
