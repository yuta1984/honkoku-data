**Poem: From the "Gyokuyō" (Jeweled Leaves) imperial anthology**

*Kasugano ni / mata urawakaki / saita tsuma /
tsuma komoru tomo / iu hito ya naki*

On the fields of Kasuga,
The young shoots bloom anew—
Tender tips of grass.
Though I linger at the verge,
There is no one to call "my love."

　　　　　　By Tokiwai Nyūdō, Former Grand Minister of State (Saionji Kintsune)
