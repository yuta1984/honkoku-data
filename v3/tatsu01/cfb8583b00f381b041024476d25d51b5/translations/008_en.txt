The *Kuji Kongen* states:
"What is today called the *Kuzu no Sō* — the singing of songs and the playing of flutes — is understood to mean that [the Kuzu people] have come from Yoshino at the beginning of the new year." (And so forth.)

The *Gōke Shidai* states:
"The Kuzu perform their songs and flutes outside the Eimeimon Gate."
