## Narasaka (奈良坂) · Hannya Road (般若路) · Sakano Zaike (酒野在家)

From the *Man'yōshū*:

> *Aoniyoshi / Nara no ōchi wa / yuki yokedo / kono yamaji wa / yukiahi ni keri*
> 　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Yakamori

(Though the great road of Nara — beautiful Nara — is broad and easy to travel, on this mountain path we have chanced to meet one another.)
