**Sahogawa (Saho River)**

From the Gokyogoku Hyakuban Utaawase (Poetry Contest in One Hundred Rounds of the Latter Kyogoku):

*Mizu kami ni / tanomi wa kakeki / Sahogawa no / sue no fujinami / tayuta su na*

Upon the upper stream have I placed my hopes — O Saho River, let not the waves of wisteria at your far reaches waver and fade away.
