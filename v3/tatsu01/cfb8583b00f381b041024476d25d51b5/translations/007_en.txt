**Editorial Notes (Hanrei)**

· The fifteen districts (gun) within the domain vary in size and extent. Larger districts are spread across two volumes per district, while smaller districts may have as many as five districts consolidated into a single volume. The boundaries of each district are noted in fine writing above the enclosing frame, serving as a guide for the reader.

· Many of the great temples depicted in this work have stood for over a thousand years since their founding. As times have changed and provinces and districts have fallen into turmoil, many have fallen into ruin or been destroyed by fire. Therefore, the illustrations depict the present-day scenery, while the historical background is described based on old records. Kōfukuji and Yakushiji temples are examples of this.

· Among the illustrations, there are large paintings featuring human figures. Those depicting the spirit of classical poems are intended to convey the scenic character of the locality. Those depicting actual events are meant to serve as an aid so that beginners may find them easy to understand. The illustrations of Kasugano and the Ryōben Cedar are examples of this.

· Newly constructed halls and newly erected inscribed monuments and the like have been omitted here; only those things of refined elegance and aesthetic distinction have been selected for inclusion.
