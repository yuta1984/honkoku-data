【Right Page】
**Editorial Notes (Hanrei)**

1. Among the fifteen districts (gun) within the province, there are differences in the size of territorial boundaries. The larger districts span two volumes each, while the smaller ones may have five districts consolidated into a single volume. The boundary of each district is noted in small characters above the border frame, serving as a guide.

2. Many of the great temple complexes depicted in these illustrations have a history of over a thousand years since their founding. As times have changed, and during periods of unrest across the provinces, many have fallen into ruin or been destroyed by fire. Therefore, the illustrations depict the scenery as it appears today, while the historical background is recorded based on old chronicles. Kōfuku-ji Temple and Yakushi-ji Temple are examples of this.

3. Among the illustrations, there are large-format figure paintings inserted at intervals. Those depicting the spirit of classical poems are intended to evoke the character and atmosphere of each locale, while those depicting actual historical events are meant to make the content easily accessible to young and inexperienced readers. The illustrations of Kasugano and the Rōben Cedar are examples of this.

4. Newly constructed halls and newly erected stone monuments and the like have been omitted here; only those things of refined elegance and artistic charm have been selected and included.

*(Chinese-style poem)*

How vast is this land of great and numinous spirit —
A thousand years have passed, yet the fragrant grasses are ever new.
Mist and haze preserve these scenes of surpassing beauty,
Drawing wandering poets here, now and always.

　　　　　　　Ōe Sukehira
