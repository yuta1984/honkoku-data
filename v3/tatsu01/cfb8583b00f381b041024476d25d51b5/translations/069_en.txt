**Source: Goshūi Wakashū (Later Collection of Gleanings)**

**Waka Poem:**

*Kasugano no*
*kusaba wa yake to*
*mie naku ni*
*shita moe wataru*
*haru no sawarabi*

**By: Dainagon Kinsane**

---

**（Modern English rendering）**

Though the grasses and leaves of Kasugano
show no sign of burning in the spring fires,
beneath the surface, smoldering and spreading—
the young bracken ferns of spring
are quietly pushing through.
