*From the Yoshino Travel Journal,*
*composed at Tōnomine:*

Seeking and finding—
here too the mountain peaks
run on with cherry blossoms,
Yoshino flowing into Hatsuse,
a lodging amid the flowers.

— Asukai Masaaki
