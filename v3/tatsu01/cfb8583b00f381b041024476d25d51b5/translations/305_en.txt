**Mount Ama no Kaguyama (Heavenly Kagu Mountain)**

*[From the "Congratulatory" section of the Senzaishū]*

"As long as the moon that rises
over Ama no Kaguyama
continues to shine,
so too shall your reign
never cease."

— The Former Grand Minister of State (Ōmiya Saki no Daijō-daijin)
