...is here. The center of this province is mountainous, opening into plains within. The areas of Yoshino and Uda are, in particular, lands of outstanding persons and divine spirit. Here there is one called Akisato Shōseki, who has thoroughly sought out famous places, historical sites, old houses, and the customs of both clergy and laity, as well as prevailing trends and manners, transcribing them all into illustrations,

【Left Page】

and carefully adding written descriptions, compiling them into a number of volumes, to be named *Yamato Zue* (Illustrated Guide to Yamato). This work shall not merely delight the eyes of young children and women, but shall surely move even the most refined and cultivated gentlemen of taste to drop their jaws in admiration. After it is committed to woodblock, may the *masaki* vine grow long...
