…This province is mountainous at its heart, with a plain spread across its center. The areas around Yoshino and Uda are, in particular, lands of outstanding people and sacred spirit. Here there was one called Akisato Shōseki, who exhaustively sought out famous places, historic sites, old residences, and the ways of both clergy and laity, as well as local customs and manners, painting each one in pictures,

【Left page】
…and composing vivid accompanying text, compiling the whole into a work of several volumes, which he named the *Yamato Zue*. It is not merely something to delight the eyes of women and children — truly, it ought to make even those gentlemen of refined taste and elegant pursuits break into an involuntary smile. After it is committed to the woodblock press, may it endure as long as the creeping *masaki* vine…
