[From the Kasuga Koki (ancient records of Kasuga):]
　　*The left and right of the torii gate are indeed Takama-no-hara — come gather here, O deities of all four directions!*

**The Haraito-no-Yashiro** (Shrine of the Purification Deity) enshrines Seoritsu-hime. [According to the Hōei Records, she is also identified with the Kumano Gongen deity.]
　[The stone lantern before the Haraito shrine is renowned throughout the land. Total height: 6 shaku 1 sun 5 bu; the fire-box is hexagonal.] [Illustration of the stone lantern]

**Kamigaki-no-Mori** (Sacred Fence Forest) and **Kamigaki-yama** (Sacred Fence Mountain) lie to the north of the Haraito shrine.
　[From the Fūga Wakashū anthology:]
　　*The grasses and leaves of Kamigaki-no-Mori have scattered and lie strewn about; only the pampas grass remains on the plain of Kasugano.* — Inno Hyōe no Kami
　[From the Gyokugin anthology:]
　　*Will the day I cross the awe-inspiring Kamigaki-yama be the limit of my love-tormented thoughts?* — Ietaka

**Chakutō-den** (the Hall of Arrival Registration) stands to the right of Kamigaki-no-Mori. It was established in Engi 16 (916 CE). [Those who accompany the Imperial envoy to the Kasuga Festival register their arrival here before proceeding to worship at the shrine.]

**Jigoku-dani** (Hell Valley) lies to the south of this. [Among the disciples of the Gedatsu Shōnin (Saint of Liberation) was a scholar monk named Sōzu Shōen, who was renowned for his learning. After his death, he reportedly possessed the body of a woman and spoke many things, including: "By the compassionate means of our Great Deity, anyone who has merely encountered this place — no matter how grave their sins — shall not be cast into the hells of other realms. A hell has been prepared beneath Kasugano; sinners are gathered there, purified with lustral water (shōsui), and made to hear sūtras and dhāraṇīs, and are thus saved. Though I too had sunk into the realm of demons, the lustral water of compassionate means entered my mouth, freeing me from the three torments of heat, and hearing the teachings of the 'harmonious-light' manifestation of the deity, I came to know the pleasures of the afterlife — is this not a thing of profound gratitude?" All who heard this were moved to deep admiration, as recorded in the Shasekishū.]

**The Enomoto Shrine** (榎本祠) lies to the east of this. It is said to be the shrine of the local tutelary deity of Kasuga-yama. The enshrined deity is Saruta-hiko-no-Kami.

　[From the Kasuga Koki:]
　　*Receiving the water of the deity who passed over Seiryū Bridge — how joyful! Immediately this body, immediately Buddhahood.* — Gedatsu Shōnin
　[From the same source:]
　　*All wishes are fulfilled on the Middle Road of Mikasa; O cedar boughs, O Bridge of Conversation!*

**Kensaki-ishi** (Sword-Tip Stone) is in Kamigaki-no-Mori. It is said to be transmitted that "if one forgetfully steps upon this stone, misfortune will follow," though the reason for this is unknown. To the east of this stands **the Wisteria Torii Gate**. [In former times, wisteria grew around this torii, blooming luxuriantly every spring — hence the name.]
　[From the Shin-Senzai Wakashū: composed in connection with a folding screen depicting the Kasuga Festival ceremony, made on the occasion of the enthronement of the Empress Consort in Genkō 3 (1333):]
　　*If you stop to approach, let each of the officials mind themselves — beneath the shade of the blossoms of the Wisteria Torii Gate.* — Emperor Go-Daigo
　[From the Hōei Records, composed in reference to the naming of this torii as the "Bodhi Gate" (Bodaimon):]
　　*Passing through the awe-inspiring torii of the gods, all manner of sins vanish and disappear.*

**The Eight Small Shrines of the Outer Precinct** (外院) [the area outside the covered gallery is called the "Outer Precinct"]:
　**Tadataka Kongō Dōji Shrine** [located to the north inside the Naishi Gate; enshrines Izanagi-no-Mikoto]
　**Tsubakimoto Shrine** (椿本祠) [located to the east of the Tadataka Shrine; enshrines Mimusubi-no-Sukune-no-Mikoto]
　**Fūjin Shrine** (Wind God Shrine) [located to the south of Tsubakimoto Shrine; enshrines the Tatsuta Myōjin deity]
　**Sugimoto Shrine** (椙本祠) [located at the base of the inner sacred fence; enshrines Ōyama-Uigami-no-Mikoto]
　**Sakun Shrine** (佐軍祠) [located to the east of Sugimoto Shrine; enshrines Tagorihime]
　**Kurikara Shrine** (栗辛祠) [located in the same area; enshrines Hosuserino-Mikoto]
　**Umimoto Shrine** (海本祠) [located to the south of the Kurikara Shrine; enshrines Ōmononushi-no-Mikoto]
　**Hachirai-jin Shrine** (八雷神祠, Shrine of the Eight Thunder Deities) [located to the east of the Umimoto Shrine; all of these are recorded in the Kasuga Koki]
