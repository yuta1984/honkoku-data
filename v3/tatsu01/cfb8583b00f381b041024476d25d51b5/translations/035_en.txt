## [Double-Page Spread Illustration — Right Page Text]

The childhood name of Bishop Ryōben was Konjū Sennin ("The Golden Eagle Hermit"). Taking an image of Shukongōshin (Vajrapāṇi) as his principal object of veneration, he recited the *Avatamsaka Sūtra* (*Kegon-kyō*). He climbed upon a stone nearby and, facing toward the imperial capital, chanted: "*Konrin Shōō, tenchō chikyū*" ("May the Wheel-Turning Sage King reign as long as heaven and earth endure"). His voice reached all the way to the distant imperial palace, purple clouds rose up into the sky and illuminated the imperial residence. The Emperor, finding this most wondrous, dispatched an imperial envoy to that place, and summoned Konjū Sennin to the court.
