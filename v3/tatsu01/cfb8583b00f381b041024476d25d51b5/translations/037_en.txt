**[Left Page]** *(Waka poem)*

> *Zoku Sha-ken* (Sequel/Continuation Offering)

**Tadesan** (手向山, "Mountain of Offering")

Though the *nusa* offerings at this mountain
have now become a thing of the distant past—
still, scatter and linger, do not fall away,
O crimson leaves upon the peak.

— *Nakahara no Moromitsu no Ason*
