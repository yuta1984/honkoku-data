　　　　　　　Sakai Village　　　　　　　Village Head: Taemon (of the Konno clan)

This village is a post-station (umaya). It is called thus because it sits on the boundary between Kawabe District to the north and Senboku District to the south. The Wakan Shiori-bumi states: "'Sakai' [境/堺] is likely derived from 'sakagai' (坂合), meaning 'the meeting of slopes.' In the Nihon Shoki, the word 'Sakaibe' (境部) is also written as 'Sakaibe' (坂合部)." Hence the place is also called "Sakahime." In the Man'yōshū, the characters "三中" are read as "sakai," because Mount Fuji (Fujisan) straddles the three provinces of Kai, Shinano, and Suruga—though it is said that this should be read "minaka," meaning "the very center" (真中). The correct character for "sakai" (境) is "畍." The colloquial expression "nannja sakai" and the like is a compressed form of speech arising from this same reasoning. The boundary between Ōmi and Yamashiro provinces lies at Oiwake, and this is said to be the dividing line between the thirty-three provinces of the west and the thirty-three provinces of the east—so it appears.

The Kyōhō Gun'yūki states: "Sakai Village: 85 households, of which one is a temple. Post-station (umaya): 8 chō and 32 ken to the Yodogawa; 3 ri, 25 chō, and 22 ken to Toyoshima Motomachi. Borders Funaoka Village in Kawabe District; includes Aikaino (or Awasekaino), Kariyasawa, and Mizuochi; delimited by the highway bridge." Upon reflection, this area was likely once the place known as Karishiba Barrier (苅柴関, Karishiba no Seki).
