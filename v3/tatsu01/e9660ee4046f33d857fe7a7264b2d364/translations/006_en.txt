## Pilgrimage Songs (Goeika)

**Station 9 – Nara, Nan'en-dō (Kōfuku-ji Temple)**
On a spring day, the Southern Circular Hall gleams with radiance,
As thin clouds clear away above Mount Mikasa.

**Station 10 – Yamashiro Province, Uji, Mimuroto-ji Temple**
Through the long night, as the moon fades away at Mimuroto,
Pillar-like waves rise upon the shoals of the Uji River.

**Station 11 – Same Province (Yamashiro), Kami-Daigo-ji (Juntei-dō)**
Even those of contrary conditions are saved without exception in this sacred precinct —
How worthy it is to walk the path of pilgrimage here!

**Station 12 – Ōmi Province, Iwama-dera (Shōhō-ji Temple)**
Where might the headwaters be? — Here at Iwama-dera,
Is that the sound of waves striking the shore, or the wind in the pines?

**Station 13 – Ōmi Province, Ishiyama-dera Temple**
Though the heart that prays for the world to come may be light,
The Buddha's vow is heavy as the stones of Ishiyama.

**Station 14 – Same Province (Ōmi), Nagara no Mii-dera (Onjō-ji Temple)**
The moon drifting in and out among the waves — at Mii-dera,
The lake brightens at dawn to the resonance of the temple bell.

**Station 15 – Kyoto, Ima-Kumano Kannon-ji Temple**
Since ancient times it has stood, though none may know it — Ima-Kumano:
The Buddha's vow remains ever fresh and wondrous.

**Station 16 – Same (Kyoto), Kiyomizu-dera Temple**
The pine-scented wind, and Otowa's waterfall at Kiyomizu —
The heart that cups and drinks that water must feel cool and clear.
