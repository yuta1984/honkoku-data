【Pilgrim's Songs (continued)】

Temple Nine — Nanen-dō (Kōfukuji), Nara
　The spring sunlight shines upon the Southern Circular Hall —
　　Above Mount Mikasa, the thin clouds begin to clear

Temple Ten — Mimurodo-dera, Uji, Yamashiro Province
　All through the night, pressing on to find the moon at Mimuroto —
　　White-crested waves rise upon the shoals of the Uji River

Temple Eleven — Kami-Daigoji (Juntei-dō), Yamashiro Province
　Because this vow saves all beings without exception, even those of contrary karma —
　　The road of pilgrimage is filled with reassurance and hope

Temple Twelve — Iwama-dera (Shōbōji), Ōmi Province
　Where might be the source of these waters? — at Iwama-dera
　　The waves that strike the bank sound like wind through the pines

Temple Thirteen — Ishiyama-dera, Ōmi Province
　Though the heart that prays for the world to come may be light and faint —
　　The Buddha's vow is as heavy and steadfast as the mountain of stone

Temple Fourteen — Mii-dera (Nagara-yama Onjōji), Ōtsu, Ōmi Province
　The moon rises and sets over the waves — at Mii-dera
　　The lake dawns anew with the resonance of the temple bell

Temple Fifteen — Ima-Kumano (Kannon-ji), Kyoto
　Though none may know since ancient times when first it was established — Ima-Kumano:
　　The Buddha's vow reveals itself ever fresh and new

Temple Sixteen — Kiyomizudera, Kyoto
　The wind through the pines, the falls of Otowa at Kiyomizu —
　　The heart that cups these sacred waters must feel cool and serene
