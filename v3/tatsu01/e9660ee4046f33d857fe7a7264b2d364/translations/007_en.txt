【Pilgrim's Songs (continued)】

Temple Seventeen — Rokuhara-dō (Rokuharamitsuji), Kyoto
　However heavy they may be, the five great sins shall surely not remain —
　　For I am one who makes pilgrimage to Rokuhara-dō

Temple Eighteen — Rokkaku-dō (Chōhōji), Kyoto
　The inside of this heart of mine has six corners —
　　I pray only that they may become round and smooth

Temple Nineteen — Kōdō-ji (Kōdōji / Gyōganji), Kyoto
　Gazing upon the flowers, hopes now stir at Kōdō —
　　Even the many grasses of the garden are in full bloom

Temple Twenty — Yoshimine-dera, Yamashiro Province
　Past the fields and facing the mountain path, a sky full of rain —
　　Yet even beyond Yoshimine, the evening shower clears

Temple Twenty-One — Anaoji, Tamba Province
　Be not troubled that you were born into such a world as this —
　　Simply trust and call upon the name, ten times, or even once

Temple Twenty-Two — Sōjiji, Settsu Province
　High and low alike, without distinction — at Sōjiji
　　There is none who does not rely upon the Buddha's vow

Temple Twenty-Three — Katsuo-dera, Settsu Province
　However heavy your sins may be, the teaching of Katsuo-dera bears them up —
　　How peaceful is the one who places trust in the Buddha

Temple Twenty-Four — Nakayama-dera, Settsu Province
　Passing through the fields, passing through the village, on to Nakayama —
　　We make our pilgrimage to this temple for the sake of the world to come
