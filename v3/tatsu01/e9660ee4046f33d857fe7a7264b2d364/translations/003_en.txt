　　The Origins of the Pilgrimage

To trace the origins of the pilgrimage: although the deity Kumano Gongen first began to make rounds through this world, for a long time this practice had fallen into abeyance. However, the sixty-fifth emperor of Japan, known as the Cloistered Emperor Kazan, ascended to the throne at the age of seventeen on the tenth day of the tenth month of Eikan 2 (984). Nevertheless, having a deep desire to renounce the world, on the night of the twenty-third day of the sixth month of Kanna 3 (987), he secretly slipped out of the imperial palace and had his head shaved at Kazan-ji temple, thereafter being reverently referred to as the Nyūgaku Hōō (Retired Emperor Nyūgaku).
