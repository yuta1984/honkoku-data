【Pilgrim's Songs (continued)】

Temple Twenty-Five — Kiyomizudera, Harima Province
　What compassion — extending to all who pass through its gates, of every station —
　　What more need one seek? The pure waters are here, at Kiyomizu

Temple Twenty-Six — Hokkeji (Ichijōji), Harima Province
　In spring the cherry blossoms, in summer the mandarin orange, in autumn the chrysanthemum —
　　The flower mountain of the Dharma, ever blooming, never ceasing

Temple Twenty-Seven — Shoshazan-dera (Enkyōji), Harima Province
　Climbing all the long way up to Shosha, the mountain wind descends —
　　Even the sound of the pines must surely be the voice of the Law

Temple Twenty-Eight — Nariaidera, Tango Province
　The sound of the waves, the resonance of the pines — these are the sounds of Nariai —
　　The wind blows across to Amanohashidate

Temple Twenty-Nine — Matsu-no-o-dera, Tango Province
　How many ages must have passed since ancient times — and yet the tidings come —
　　For a thousand years the temple of Matsu-no-o has stood here waiting

Temple Thirty — Chikubushima (Hōgon-ji), Ōmi Province
　The moon, the sun — floating there between the waves — Chikubushima —
　　It feels as though one's boat is laden with treasure

Temple Thirty-One — Chōmei-ji, Ōmi Province
　Eight thousand years of life, long as the trailing willow, flows forth —
　　The steps we take toward the temple shall be our crown of grace

Temple Thirty-Two — Kannon-ji, Ōmi Province
　Oh, how sacred! Guide us onward, Kannon-ji —
　　These steps we carry from distant lands toward you
