## Pilgrimage Songs (Goeika)

**Station 1 – Kii Province, Nachi-san (Seiganto-ji Temple)**
Fudaraku ya — the sacred waves of Kumano's trees:
Upon Mount Nachi's heights, the waterfall resounds.

**Station 2 – Same Province (Kii), Kimii-dera Temple**
From my distant hometown I have come here to Kimii-dera;
Even the flower-filled capital of Nara feels close now.

**Station 3 – Same Province (Kii), Kokawa-dera Temple**
As deep as a father's and mother's grace is Kokawa-dera;
A shrine where the Buddha's vow fills us with trust.

**Station 4 – Izumi Province, Makino-o-dera (施福寺, Seifuku-ji)**
Along the mountain path through cypress groves and pine forests I press on,
And at Makino-o Temple, even my horse grows spirited.

**Station 5 – Kawachi Province, Fujii-dera (Katsui-dera)**
From the moment one visits, trust is placed in Fujii-dera;
Above the lotus throne, purple clouds gather.

**Station 6 – Yamato Province, Tsubosaka-dera (Minami Hokke-ji)**
Taking up stone, holding water — at Tsubosaka,
Even the sand of the garden must be of the Pure Land.

**Station 7 – Same Province (Yamato), Oka-dera (Ryūgai-ji)**
This morning I behold the moss in the garden of Tsunu Oka-dera —
It shines just like the radiance of Nara itself.

**Station 8 – Same Province (Yamato), Hase-dera Temple**
No matter how many times I make the pilgrimage, my heart is drawn to Hase-dera;
Both the mountains and the Buddha's vow run deep as the valley stream.
