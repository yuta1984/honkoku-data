## Pilgrimage Poems

**First Temple: Nachi-san, Kii Province**
Oh, Fudaraku! —
The waves breaking on the shore
of the three Kumano shrines;
At Nachi's holy mountain,
the sacred waterfall roars and resounds.

**Second Temple: Kimii-dera, Kii Province**
From distant homelands,
travelers come from afar
to Kimii-dera;
Even the flower-filled capital
must now be drawing near.

**Third Temple: Kokawa-dera, Kii Province**
Deep as the grace
of one's own father and mother —
so is Kokawa-dera;
The Buddha's sacred vow
— how reassuring is this shrine!

**Fourth Temple: Makino-o-dera, Izumi Province**
Along mountain paths,
through cypress groves and pine forests
I make my way;
At Makino-o Temple,
my horse lifts its spirit and steps lively.

**Fifth Temple: Fujii-dera, Kawachi Province**
From the moment I set out
I place all my hopes and trust
in Fujii-dera;
On the lotus throne adorned with blossoms,
purple clouds of grace gather.

**Sixth Temple: Tsubosaka-dera, Yamato Province**
Drawing water from the rocks,
the pond brims with sacred water
at Tsubosaka;
Even the sands of the temple garden
must surely be the Pure Land.

**Seventh Temple: Oka-dera, Yamato Province**
Looking out this morning,
the moss in the garden
of Tsunu-oka Temple —
It seems to shine and glow,
reflecting the light of Nara.

**Eighth Temple: Hase-dera, Yamato Province**
No matter how many times
I make the pilgrimage,
at Hase-dera
the mountains and Buddha's vows alike
run deep as the valley stream.
