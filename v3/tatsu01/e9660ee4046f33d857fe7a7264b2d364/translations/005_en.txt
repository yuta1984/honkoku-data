【Pilgrim's Songs】

Temple One — Nachisan (Seigantoji), Kii Province
　At Fudaraku — the waves that strike the shore at Mikumano's
　　Nachi's sacred mountain resound with the rush of cascading falls

Temple Two — Kimiidera, Kii Province
　From my distant hometown, I have come all this way to Kimiidera —
　　Even the flowery capital of Kyoto must now be drawing near

Temple Three — Kokawdera, Kii Province
　Deep as the blessings of father and mother is Kokawdera —
　　A shrine where the Buddha's vow fills one with trust and hope

Temple Four — Makinoosan Sefukuji (Makinonotera), Izumi Province
　Along the deep mountain path, through groves of cypress and pine I press on —
　　At Makinodera, even my horse lifts its spirits

Temple Five — Fujiidera, Kawachi Province
　From the very moment of my arrival, I place all my faith in Fujiidera —
　　Upon a lotus dais, purple clouds gather

Temple Six — Tsubosakadera (Minamihokkeji), Yamato Province
　Standing rocks and gathered waters at Tsubosaka —
　　Even the garden's sand must be as the Pure Land

Temple Seven — Okaji, Yamato Province
　This morning I see: the dew on the mossy garden of Okadera —
　　It shines as though it were the light of lapis lazuli itself

Temple Eight — Hasedera, Yamato Province
　No matter how many times I come to worship, my heart turns to Hasedera —
　　The mountain and its vow are both as deep as the valley stream
