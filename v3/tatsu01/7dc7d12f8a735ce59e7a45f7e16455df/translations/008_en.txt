(Continued from the previous page) ...for some reason, there was a time when he had temporarily neglected his practice. Then one day, a miraculous bird came flying toward the Venerable and cried out to him, saying: "Venerable One, why have you been neglecting your recitations? To the east of this place, there is a sacred site known as Chichibu District in Musashi Province. The Buddhist Dharma shall flourish there for eternity. O great virtuous one, you must, for the sake of protecting the Dharma in that land, recite the wondrous sutras as before, and furthermore make plans for the restoration of the temple there. I am a messenger of Kanzeon (Avalokiteśvara)." With that, the bird spread its golden wings, shining brilliantly in all four directions, and flew off toward the east.

[Left page]

By nature, this Venerable had attained the divine ear (tenni-tsū), as recorded in his biography, and was thus able to understand the song of the miraculous bird. He commanded the assembled congregation to pray, and for the flourishing of the Buddhist Dharma in that land, caused the wondrous sutras to be recited forty thousand times. Thereafter, on the thirteenth day of the third month of the fourth year of Kankō (1007 CE), as the Venerable faced his passing, he issued a final instruction to his disciple, the monk Genzū, saying: "After my death, go to Chichibu in the Eastern Provinces, and make that place increasingly a sacred site of Kanzeon. So that the traces of the karmic bonds that the Bodhisattva Gyōki had bestowed upon that place shall never decline..."
