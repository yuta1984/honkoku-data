## A Record of Miraculous Manifestations of Kannon at the Thirty-Four Sacred Sites of Chichibu, Volume Two

### Site Seven: Ushifushi — Hōchō-ji Temple on Mount Seitai
※Main Hall: Four ken on each of four sides, facing east

Principal Image: Eleven-Faced Kannon (standing figure, height: 1 shaku 2 sun 3 bu), carved by the Bodhisattva Gyōki

Regarding the origins of the principal image of this temple: In the second year of Jōhei, during the reign of the sixty-first human sovereign, Emperor Suzaku, in the village of Sueno in this district, at the castle on Mount Hanazono, there was a high-ranking retainer of a certain lord — a man known by some name such as "Saemon no Kami" — who was a chief vassal of notorious ill repute among warriors.
