...this is a hymn of praise for the origins of this temple's founding. How fitting indeed — the recitation of Mahāyāna (sutras) is the primary cause for rebirth in the highest grade (jōbon ōjō), and is one of the Three Meritorious Acts (sanpuku) as expounded in the Contemplation Sutra (Kangyō). For those who make a pilgrimage to this place even once, how could the benefits gained from the Wondrous Sutra (Myōkyō/Lotus Sutra) be in vain? That at the end of one's life one immediately proceeds to the golden gate of the world of peace and bliss (the Pure Land) — is that not something most reassuring? That this temple has been designated the foremost of the (Chichibu) pilgrimage sites — had it not been by the determined judgment of a manifestation of the Buddha (gonge), who could possibly have reached such a conclusion? Let no one view this with carelessness or indifference.

○ Second Sacred Site — Ōodana — Daihōzan Shinpukuji Temple 【Side note: Main hall five ken, facing south on all four sides】
　Principal image: Shō Kannon 【Side note: Standing image, height one shaku five sun one bu】　Carved by Bodhisattva Gyōki

Inquiring into the origins of the principal image, the Shō Kannon of this temple: the founding master of this temple, the Great Hermit Daihō Zenji, was a person who possessed both wisdom and practice in equal measure. He concealed his brilliance and hid his virtue, maintaining a brisk and pure manner (like a clear wind) and an upright and lofty integrity (like towering peaks). He abstained from food, avoided grain, and never for even a moment removed his dharma robes from his body.
