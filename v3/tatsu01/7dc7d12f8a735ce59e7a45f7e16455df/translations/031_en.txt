(Continued from previous page) …reciting sutras, and repented the deep and grievous sins her husband had committed during his lifetime. She expressed her gratitude to the monk dwelling in the mountains, then departed from before the principal image, and took up lodging at a farmhouse in the vicinity. From time to time she would offer incense and flowers at the grave mound, devoting herself entirely to performing memorial services for her husband's soul. It was around this time that the cow belonging to the farmhouse where the woman was staying gave birth to a single calf.

This calf yearned after the woman just as a young child yearns after its mother. There was not a single person who witnessed this and did not find it strange. The woman and her daughter together grew fond of the cow, and whenever they went to pay their respects at the grave mound of the husband, they would always lead the cow along with them. Then one day, when this cow came near the grave mound, it suddenly lay down and would not move at all. When the mother and daughter, thinking this strange, tried to pull it back to its feet, the cow raised its head, shed tears, and suddenly spoke in a human voice, saying: "Do not be startled, you two. I am none other than the final form taken by your husband, so-and-so. Though through the evil and wickedness of my former life I have wandered round and round through the three evil realms of existence, and now find myself in such a form as this, I count it a blessing that I was born in this place and am able to meet you once more — this is my only consolation. Both of you, mother and daughter, awaken your hearts in faith, and for the sake of extinguishing my karmic hindrances, make daily offerings morning and evening to the Kannon who resides in this place. Furthermore, a monk who has attained the samādhi of nenbutsu…"
