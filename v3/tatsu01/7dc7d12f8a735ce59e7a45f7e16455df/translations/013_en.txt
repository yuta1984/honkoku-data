The votive verse (eika) says:
　*Megurikite / tanomi wo kakeshi / Ōtana no / chikai mo fukaki / tanikawa no mizu*
　("Having wandered and come here at last, placing my trust in Ōtana — the vow, like the waters of the valley stream, runs deep.")

The votive verse of this temple expresses the meaning that the demon woman who roamed as a night-wandering spirit, seeking karmic connection with the Buddha by going here and there, at last came to this place, and through the profound power of the principal image's vow, was immediately released from her body as a demon-beast. It likens the deep grace of the Bodhisattva

Since this verse should also resonate with the meaning of the boundless depth and breadth of the Original Teacher Tathāgata's ocean of wisdom and vows, as well as with the spirit of the next-in-succession Bodhisattva's great vow "as deep as the ocean" (*jin nyo kai*), I offer here only a brief note on its meaning.
