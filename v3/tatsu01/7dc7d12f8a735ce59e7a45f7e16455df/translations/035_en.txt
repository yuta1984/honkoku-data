**Station Eight: Seitaisan Saizenji (青苔山西善寺)**
*(The hall measures four *ken* on each side, facing south)*
**Principal Image: Eleven-Faced Kannon** *(Standing image, height: 7 *sun* 1 *bu*)* **Carved by the Venerable Eshin (恵心僧都)**

The principal image of this temple was given, together with a triad of Amida (Amitābha) Buddha also carved by the Venerable Eshin, to a devout lay practitioner known as Ushin no Koji (有信居士). It was enshrined in this place, while the Amida triad was installed in a separate sub-temple, where it was venerated in worship morning and evening for many years. This sub-temple is today called the *Oku-no-in* (奥の院, "Inner Sanctuary").

In due course, however, during the middle period, warfare (兵革) broke out repeatedly, and all the shrines and temples within the entire precinct and surrounding region fell into ruin.
