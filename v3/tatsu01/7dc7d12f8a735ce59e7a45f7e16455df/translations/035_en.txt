**The Eighth Temple: Seizan (青苔山, Mount Seitai), Saizenji (西善寺)**
*The main hall is four *ken* square, facing south on all four sides.*
**Principal image: Eleven-Faced Kannon (十一面観音)** — standing figure, height seven *sun* one *bu* — carved by the Sōzu Eshin (恵心僧都).

The principal image of this temple, together with the Amida Triad (阿弥陀三尊) carved by the Sōzu (i.e., Eshin Sōzu, Genshin), was presented to a lay Buddhist devotee named Ushin (有信居士). These were enshrined in this place, with the Amida Triad installed in a separate sub-temple (別院), where they were venerated morning and evening over many years. This sub-temple is today known as the Oku-no-in (奥の院, "Inner Sanctuary"). However, in the middle period, wars and armed conflicts broke out repeatedly, and all the shrines and temples throughout the entire district fell into ruin and desolation.
