**Editorial Notes for the Kannon Entsū-den**

○ The engi (origin stories/legends) recorded in this compilation were gathered through my long-held aspiration. Having traveled from distant Musashi Province to Chichibu and resided there for several years, I made a thorough circuit of all the sacred sites throughout the district. With the permission of the head priest of each temple, I was granted access to the secret archive boxes (hikyō) in which the engi documents are preserved. I then cross-referenced these with the private draft manuscripts compiled by various scholars and gentlemen, corrected certain points against the testimonies of elderly local residents, and consulted classical texts wherever doubts remained. Although this undertaking may be considered largely complete, I fear there may still be not a few omissions and errors.

○ The reason this compilation does not record the meaning and significance of the name "Kanzeon" (Avalokiteśvara) is that it is treated in full detail in Shōyo's *Reigen Shinshō*.
