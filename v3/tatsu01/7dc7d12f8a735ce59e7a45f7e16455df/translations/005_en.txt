Although there are matters that ought to be discussed, the reason they are left without debate is that one must consider how forcibly searching and probing might become an obstacle to faith. Wise persons must surely be broad-minded and forgiving about this.

○As for the devotional poems (eika) of the pilgrimage circuit: some originate from the manifestations (jigen) of the Bodhisattva; some were composed by later generations; and some contain errors in grammatical particles (te-ni-o-ha). Without equally distinguishing between the elegant and the vulgar among them, the reason for setting forth explanatory notes is solely to serve as an aid to spreading the faith (kanka). I do not in the least regard the ridicule or mockery of onlookers.
However, for those whose meaning is difficult for me to fathom with my limited abilities, I shall for the time being await the learned annotations of those who come after.

○As for the pilgrimage circuit: the roads going and coming, near and far, have been recorded in detail as a convenience for travelers. Furthermore, the shrines and temples in those vicinities have all been recorded, and are included together in the appendix. Those who journey to these places should without fail carry this work with them. However, can one still avoid all errors and

○Within this compilation, at the twenty-ninth site, Sasa no To, the text becomes quite lengthy; however, the informal conversations (kandan) of the Venerable Elder (sonshuku) have been set down just as they were heard, out of fear that even the slightest detail of those events and traces might be omitted. Moreover, the reason for recording in colloquial language (rigo) is that the Venerable Elder, being deeply committed to the salvation of beings, did not favor ornamentation in prose, and would not permit even a single character of embellishment or polishing.

○As for the karmic origins (innen) recorded in this compilation: some matters are described in detail and the text is lengthy; others are summarized briefly with abbreviated text. This does not at all reflect any difference in the relative superiority of the miraculous efficacies (reiken).
