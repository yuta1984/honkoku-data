The following is a genealogical chart of successive Japanese emperors.

|--------|---------|---------|---------------|

| 5th | Emperor Kōshō | Kōshō | 83 years |
| 6th | Emperor Kōan | Kōan | 102 years |
| 7th | Emperor Kōrei | Kōrei | 76 years |
| 8th | Emperor Kōgen | Kōgen | 57 years |
| 9th | Emperor Kaika | Kaika | 60 years |
| 10th | Emperor Sujin | Sujin | 68 years |
| 11th | Emperor Suinin | Suinin | 90 years |

| 13th | Emperor Seimu | Seimu | 61 years |

| 15th | Empress Jingū (consort of Emperor Chūai) | Jingū | 69 years |

| 17th | Emperor Nintoku | Nintoku | 87 years |
| 18th | Emperor Richū | Richū | 6 years |

| 22nd | Emperor Yūryaku | Yūryaku | 23 years |

The chart also notes Yamato Takeru no Mikoto as the father of Emperor Chūai (14th), and Empress Jingū (15th) is explicitly noted as the consort of Emperor Chūai. The lineage continues beyond Emperor Seinei (23rd).
