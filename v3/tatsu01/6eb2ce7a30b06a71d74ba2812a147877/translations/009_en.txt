### On the Five Provinces of Kinai

Yamashiro, Yamato, Kawachi, Izumi, and Settsu — these are the five provinces.

The term "Kinai" (Home Provinces) refers to the fact that the capital lies at the center of the realm; thus, these five provinces are called the center of Japan. Even in the "Jitō" section of the *Nihon Shoki* (Chronicles of Japan), the character "畿 (ki)" is read as "chikashi" (meaning "near"). Because these are provinces close to the capital, they are called "Kinai." Furthermore, the character "畿" can also be read as "meguru" (meaning "to surround" or "to encircle"), as these are provinces that encircle the capital on all four sides.

**[Question]** When Japan is divided into eight regions, seven of them have the character "dō" (道, meaning "road" or "circuit") in their names. Why is it that Kinai alone does not have the character "dō"?

**[Answer]** Because Kinai lies at the very center of Japan as the provinces closest to the capital, the character "dō" is not attached to it.
