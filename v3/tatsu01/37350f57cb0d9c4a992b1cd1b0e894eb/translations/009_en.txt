【Right folio】

It is also called "Shiwakami Hotsuma Kuni" (Land of Excellence Rising Above the Rocky Shores), because it surpasses all other lands. When Amaterasu Ōtaijin (the Great Deity of the Sun) was born, the affairs of heaven were entrusted to her. The Great Deity appointed the Imperial Grandchild Ninigi no Mikoto as the lord of this land, designating it "Toyoashihara no Nakatsukuni" (The Fertile Land of Reed Plains in the Middle). "Toyoashihara" refers to the vigorous sprouting and growth of reed shoots, while "Nakatsukuni" means that it lies at the center of heaven and earth, at the apex where the sun and moon shine directly and righteously.

【Left folio】

It is also called "Chiio Aki Mizuho no Kuni" (Land of Abundant Autumn Rice Ears for a Thousand Five Hundred Autumns). "Mizuho" (ripening rice ears) is that which nourishes people, and "Thousand Five Hundred Autumns" is an auspicious celebratory expression for it. Emperor Jinmu established his capital in Yamato Province, and "Yamato" came to represent the whole realm. When the Emperor viewed the shape of the land from his imperial palanquin, it resembled a dragonfly (tombo), and so it was called "Akitsushima" (Dragonfly Island).
