Aizu Fudoki (Records of Aizu)

Territorial Boundaries (Hōiki)

Aizu lies within the domain of Ōshū (Mutsu Province), situated in the southwest of the two provinces. Aizu, Yama, Ōnuma, and Kawanuma together are collectively called the "Four Districts of Aizu."

　[Note: Originally, the four districts formed a single district called Aizu, which was then divided to create Yama District, and further split into Ōnuma and Kawanuma. In recent times, the name of Aizu District was lost and Inagawa was added in its place — this is an error. The nine characters in the Wamyō Ruijushō reading "now divided into the two districts of Ōnuma and Kawanuma," found under the entry for Shirakawa, ought properly to be placed under the entry for Aizu.]
