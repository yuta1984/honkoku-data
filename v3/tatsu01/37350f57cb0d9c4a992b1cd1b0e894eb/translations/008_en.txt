Preface to the Aizu Fudoki

Since heaven and earth came into being, our divine land (shinkoku) has existed. Izanagi no Mikoto and Izanami no Mikoto, inheriting the work of the gods, established the central pillar of the land and created the Great Eight Islands (Ōyashima). The various divine children were each entrusted with their own domains. This land is called "Uruyasukuni" (Land of Tranquil Shores), because the four seas are at peace and calm. It is also called "Hokochitataru-kuni" (Land Replete with Fine Spears), because military implements are abundantly provided.
