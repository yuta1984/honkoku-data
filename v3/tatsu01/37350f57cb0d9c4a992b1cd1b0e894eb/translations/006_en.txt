...travel on the same roads and write in the same script (that is, as the unification of the realm is now complete), the edicts and instructions of the sovereign spread universally throughout the land, and the topographical records (fudoki) of the sixty-odd provinces can be compiled and presented forthwith. The Lord of the Junior Fourth Rank, Left Middle Captain (Sahyōe no kami), of the Minamoto clan, is an illustrious scion of noble lineage and a model and exemplar of the warrior class. Enfeoffed at the castle of Aizu district in Ōshū (Mutsu Province), he also exercises concurrent governance over the three districts of Yama, Ōnuma, and Kawanuma.
