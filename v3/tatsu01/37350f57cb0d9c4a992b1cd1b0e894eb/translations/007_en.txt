Verifying and determining (the contents), the fair copy was gradually completed before long. One day, (the Lord) summoned me and requested that I compose a preface for this work. Reflecting upon the nature of this book, I find that the breadth of four districts has been compressed into a single volume, suspended before both eyes (for ready reference). Indeed, without the toil of Mencius's cart-tracks (his travels through the states), one may attain the essence of Zichang's (Sima Qian's) journeys — is this not a most fortunate thing? I have privately heard that the benevolent governance of the state of Teng is something the whole world

【Left folio】
...may take as a model, and that Héngqú's (Zhang Zai's) experiment in a single district may likewise be extended to all under heaven. Can it then be said that this fudoki of the jurisdiction cannot be emulated and relied upon across the Five Home Provinces and the Seven Circuits (i.e., the entire realm)? Can it be said that it cannot serve as a standard and guide? Though the lord's profound intentions cannot be lightly fathomed, I have no wish to conceal my humble sentiments. Thus I write as above. Mid-decade of the middle winter month (eleventh month), the year Kanoto-i of the Kanbun era (1671). Kōbun'in Taishi, Lin Shu (Hayashi Jo), reverently composed this preface.
