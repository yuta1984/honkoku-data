Furthermore, making use of this location guide (*tokorodzuke*) as reference, (I ask that you) kindly compile and publish a detailed account of the distances along the roads through the various provinces, so that it may serve as an aid to those who undertake the circuit of the provinces. This prefatory note (*kuchigaki*) to the *Engi* is set down here in broad outline, primarily in order to make known the locations (*tokorodzuke*) (of the Kokubunji temples).

　　　Ninth month, the fifth year of Hōei (1708), Year of the Rat

　Regarding the above: although the main text was to contain location descriptions (*tokorogaki*) of the Kokubunji temples at its conclusion, as these are already included in a separate edition of the *Engi-kyō*, they have been omitted here.
