## Diagram of Kamigoryō Shrine (*Kamigoryō no Yashiro*)

### Record of the Goryō Shrine (*Goryōsha Ki*)

**【Waka Poem】**

> To protect the reign of our sovereign,
> how fitting that eight deities
> have come to dwell
> so close to the ninefold palace
> of the imperial court.

*(Composed by: Kōsei / Takakiyo)*

*[Meaning: The eight enshrined deities reside near the imperial palace (the "ninefold" court) in order to guard and protect the Emperor's reign.]*
