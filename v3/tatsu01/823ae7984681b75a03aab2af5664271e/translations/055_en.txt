The "Tearai-mizu" (hand-washing water) is located to the north of the intersection of Karasuma Street and Nishiki-koji. Long ago, when the Gion Mikoshi's otabisho (temporary resting place for the portable shrine) was situated in Ōmandokoro-machi, those who came to worship would purify their hands with water here.

Following this tradition, even today, from the 7th to the 14th day of the sixth month, the well is opened and made available as a place for ritual hand-washing (temizu).

The spring water is remarkably cold and pure, without equal. It is said that drinking this water will protect one from pestilence and disease.
