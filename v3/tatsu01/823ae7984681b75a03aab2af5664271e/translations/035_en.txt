The Gosho Hachiman Shrine is located to the west of Takakura on Hachimanchō (also known as Oike-dōri). It was established by Ashikaga Takauji during the Kōei era. It bears the name Hōōzan Tōji-ji Temple, and it is said that the National Zen Master Musō Soseki also resided here for a time.

The name "Oike" (Honorable Pond) derives from the fact that in former times there was a residence of the Lord of Fukōen, and in its garden there was a pond. Even today, a small remnant of the site of that old pond survives behind the residences to the north of Oike in Ryōgaemachi. The Kasasagi Bridge (Magpie Bridge) refers to the stone bridge to the west of Karasuma on Oike-dōri. It is the old name of the bridge that once spanned this waterside villa.
