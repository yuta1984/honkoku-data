## Editorial Notes (Continued)

Item 4: Within the illustrations, there are scenes featuring large-scale depictions of human figures. These are intended to convey the beauty of the four seasons and to indicate places of leisure and recreation — for example, cherry blossom viewing in the eastern part of Kyoto (Rakutō) and firefly catching at Uji.

Item 5: Where sites listed in the illustrations occupy continuous, connected stretches of land, they are linked together by means of a circular mark (○) placed above the border lines. Examples include the connected areas from Yawata Jingūji to Shukuin and Iwashimizu, or the linked sites of Hashidera, Eshin'in, and Kōshōji in Uji.

Item 6: Although an illustration of Mount Hiei is included, the areas of Tōtō (East Pagoda precinct) and Sakamoto all fall within Ōmi Province. Nevertheless, as they form a continuous and inseparable landscape from the West Pagoda (Saitō) precinct, they cannot be excluded. Similarly, the Kannon of Yamazaki Valley lies within the borders of Settsu Province, but since it forms part of a continuous landscape, it is included in the illustrations.
