## Chinese Poem (Kanshi)

The artist has mastered the secret art of compressing vast distances onto the page;
Surpassing immortal sages, he spares the viewer even the smallest step of travel,
For the imperial capital lies entire within the eye.

　　　 Wa Katsumei [Seal]
