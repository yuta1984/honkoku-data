## Ikesu (Live Fish Preserve/Restaurant)

The establishment known as "ikesu" is located along the Takase River, to the north of Sanjō. A pavilion has been erected on the riverbank, where all manner of fish and fowl are prepared and served to guests, with sake and delicacies offered for sale. The carp that once moved Wang Xiang with his filial piety, and the sea bass that Zhang Han longed for from a thousand leagues away — all are now kept abundantly in the riverside ikesu, awaiting guests from both the capital and the provinces. However, it is said that the gathering of women and the playing of musical instruments such as the koto and shamisen have been prohibited here since ancient times, a rule that has been passed down as the longstanding custom of this establishment.
