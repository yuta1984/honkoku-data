## Illustrated Map of Hanshū-in and Its Surroundings

This page is an illustrated diagram (map) identifying notable landmarks in and around **Hanshū-in** temple and its vicinity.

- **Hanshū-in** (般舟院) — the temple itself
- **Former Site of Shigure-tei** (時雨亭旧地) — the location where the Shigure-tei pavilion once stood
- **Memorial Pagoda of Princess Shikishi** (式子内親王塔) — a commemorative stupa for Imperial Princess Shikishi
- **Main Hall** (本堂) — the main hall of Hanshū-in
- **Goma Hall** (護摩堂) — the hall for *goma* (sacred fire ritual)
- **Teika-no-zushi** (定家辻子) — a small lane (*zushi*) associated with Fujiwara no Teika
