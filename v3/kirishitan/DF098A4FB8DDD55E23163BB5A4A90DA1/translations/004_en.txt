There had been a secret report that the rebels had entrenched themselves in the old castle of Hara within the Arima domain. Unusually, there were also reports that several hundred boats were being assembled and that they appeared to be preparing to leave the castle. It was surmised that this was in preparation for another attack on Shimabara Castle. Therefore, before the enemy could advance, the forces of the Matsukura and Tachibana houses were to enter Shimabara Castle and defend it firmly, while the forces of the Nabeshima and Arima houses were to quietly station themselves near the castle, so that when the rebels came ashore from their boats and approached the castle, they could be surrounded from both inside and outside and captured without a single one escaping. In accordance with this command, Hayakawa came to Tahira village at around the seventh hour of that night (approximately 4 a.m.) and met with Inatsugu to convey the orders of the senior envoy.

He told Iki (Heizaemon): "It stands to reason that when peasants rebel against the country, they do not prefer open battle in the field but rather take refuge in fortifications. Having heard that all forces are advancing today, there is no reason for them to leave the castle now. The report must be an error on the part of the informant." Heizaemon then proceeded to the encampment of Arima Naiki to explain the matter, asking what Iki had to say. When (Iki) explained the situation in detail, Naiki and Heizaemon went together to Iki's encampment. After the two consulted, they said to Heizaemon: "You should hurry back ahead of us and report to Lord Naizen and Lord Jūzō that although we intend to relay the instructions given yesterday to the chief retainers, along the way…"
