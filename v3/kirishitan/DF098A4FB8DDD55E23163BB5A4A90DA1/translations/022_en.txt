(Continued from the previous page) The men were made to carry the stones, and Saadayu placed his own stone below (in his garment) and returned. The full account of the mission was reported to both lords (Naizen-dono and Jūzō-dono), who instructed everyone to rest. Saadayu stepped forward and reported: "I went on the mission, but could not confirm anything with certainty. I returned from the beach in this manner." When he repeated his report, Naizen-dono and Jūzō-dono appeared pleased.

According to the Ōmura Kansuke Family Chronicle: On the eighth day of the twelfth month, the forces of Arima Genba-dono took up positions at Mie Village, and likewise the forces of Tachibana Sakon-dono took up positions from Anotoku Village to Shimabara.

According to the Rissai Kyūbunki: On the eighth day of the twelfth month, Lord Tadashige pressed his forces close to the castle at Hara.
