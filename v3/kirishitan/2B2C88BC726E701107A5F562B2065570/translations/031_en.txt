Seeing that the various forces appeared to be withdrawing, just as he was about to charge forward at the vanguard, a messenger of Date Tōtōmi-no-kami Hidemune named Kajita Gonbei arrived. (Note: the original manuscript has missing characters at this point.) In reply to Naizen-no-sho, he said: "You speak most admirably. I myself do not favor being the first to charge — rather, I intend to tour the various encampments and then give the order for the vanguard assault. You, stay here and handle matters as the situation demands." With that, he left and went to Matsukura's encampment to give his instructions: "Since the Arima vanguard forces did not charge, you should quickly ride in at the very front."

Nagato-no-kami replied: "Since early this morning before dawn we have been positioned at the base of the walls, but the musket fire is so fierce that we simply cannot break through. At present we are also watching for an opportunity, so it would be best for you as well to observe the situation." With this reply, he did not withdraw. Naizen-no-sho grew angry and said: "Since Nagato [Matsukura] is a principal party to this rebellion's resolution, how can he sit and watch like the other encampments? It will be shameful to face criticism later, so it would be proper to exert yourselves today, distinguish yourselves above the other forces, and charge at the vanguard." However, despite these orders, Nagato-no-kami would not advance.
