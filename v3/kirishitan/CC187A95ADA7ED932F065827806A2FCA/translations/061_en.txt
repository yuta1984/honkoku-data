These are the individuals noted. Furthermore, at the breach point of Mutō Kudayū's [assault], there was a wounded enemy who could neither advance nor retreat. This person had a shaved head, with [the remaining hair] bound with a golden cord (motoyu​i), and was wearing a thin silk lined robe (awase) over a kusazuri-style armored coat (kiko​mi), lying prostrate before a folding camp stool (shōgi). Mutō's retainer, Tatsuta Rokuemon, stripped the armored coat from this wounded man, and upon examining his waist, found that he was wearing a [charm or amulet associated with] evil spirits (ma).
