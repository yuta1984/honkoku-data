As troops continued to pour in one after another, someone called out to Yoda Uemon, saying, "Surely now is the time to bring the honorable banner into the main encampment!" Yoda laughed scornfully and replied, "The signal for bringing in the banner is not something for the likes of you to know. Your concern is unnecessary." After a short while, as dawn broke white and pale, Yoda shouldered the banner, climbed along the stone wall, and planted the flag inside the main citadel (honmaru). Yamamura Hikoshiro and Ikoma Heiemon were also with Yoda at this time. When this banner became visible from Lord Tadazato's quarters, as clear as if within arm's reach, the lord's joy was immeasurable. Now, as friendly forces poured into the honmaru, the enemy withdrew five or six ken and formed a battle line in front of their quarters, raining down arrows and gunfire, and hurling stones and timber. Yet even this could not hold back the attack, so thinking they could defend no longer, they set fire to the straw mats spread in their quarters and threw them into the space between the enemy and friendly forces. Our forces were troubled by this and for a time could not advance. Ikeda Moemon turned to his comrades and said, "Even if the enemy charges at us, do not retreat a single step. If we do fall back, behind us are tall stone walls — we would all fall upon one another in a most unseemly death. Once the fierce flames ahead have subsided, we shall all charge together as one."
