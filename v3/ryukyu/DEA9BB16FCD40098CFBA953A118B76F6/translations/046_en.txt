## Sheet 3 (Mutsu Province, Eastern Provinces Section, Map Sheet 3)

This sheet covers the northern portions of Mutsu Province (陸奥国), broadly corresponding to the Oshika and Kesen coastal areas of Miyagi Prefecture, the interior highlands of Iwate Prefecture, and the northernmost districts reaching into the border regions with Dewa Province and Tsugaru/Nanbu domains.
