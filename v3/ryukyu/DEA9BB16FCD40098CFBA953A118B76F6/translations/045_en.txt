## Sheet 2 (Mutsu Province, Eastern Provinces Section, Map Sheet 2)

This sheet continues the geographical index of Mutsu Province (陸奥国), covering the coastal and central-eastern districts — broadly corresponding to modern southern Fukushima Prefecture (the Iwaki/Hamadōri coastal region) and Miyagi Prefecture (the Sendai region).
