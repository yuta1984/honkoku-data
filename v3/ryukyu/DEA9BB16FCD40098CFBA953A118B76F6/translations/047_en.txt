## Sheet 4 (Mutsu Province / Tsugaru / Matsumae — Eastern Provinces Section, Map Sheet 4)

This sheet covers the northernmost areas of Mutsu Province (陸奥国), including Kita District (北郡) and Tsugaru District (津軽郡), and extends northward into the Tsugaru Peninsula, the Shimokita Peninsula, the Oshima Peninsula, and the southern coastal areas of Ezo (蝦夷地, present-day Hokkaido), including the Matsumae domain.
