【Mutsu Province (Ōshū / "Province of the Interior")】

This page of the map depicts Mutsu Province (陸奥国, broadly corresponding to present-day Fukushima, Miyagi, Iwate, Aomori, and Akita prefectures — though this section focuses on the southern portion, roughly modern Fukushima Prefecture), with its districts (郡) and place names arranged geographically.
