Item: On the 7th day of the 8th month, regarding a marriage negotiation (endan), the household official (goyōnin) Daidōji Magokurō Shigeyuki went to call upon the Chamberlain (sobashū) Ōoka Izumo-no-kami Tadamitsu, and they conducted a private consultation directly. Tadamitsu expressed his approval, and further indicated that it would also be appropriate to make a private inquiry to the Senior Councilor (rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke. Accordingly, on the 11th of the same month, Magokurō went to call upon Sagami-no-kami and submitted the following communication:

　　　"Previously, by private instruction, a marriage negotiation was arranged between one of the daughters of His Lordship the Owari Chūnagon and Ogimaru. In the spring of the past Year of the Snake [1749], through Kuze Chūemon on the part of the Chūnagon, and through Ogasawara Nuido-no-suke on the part of our Hyōbu-no-taifu [the late lord], a mutual private agreement (naiyaku) was concluded between both parties. It was agreed that, upon the Chūnagon's next visit to Edo (sanpu), a formal official inquiry would be made. However, as the Hyōbu-no-taifu passed away last winter, the matter was postponed, and we have privately discussed that the formal inquiry would be made during this coming winter. Although Ogimaru is still of a young age, as this marriage negotiation was concluded with the knowledge (naibun) of the previous lord, we inquire as to whether there would be any obstacle to proceeding with a formal inquiry during this coming winter. We humbly seek your private opinion on this matter. Respectfully submitted.
　　　　　8th Month"

Item: On the 12th day of the same month, through the messenger and resident agent (rusuī) Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi, a domain population register (okuni ninbetsu aratamechō) was submitted to the Senior Inspector (Ōmetsuke) Ishikawa Tosa-no-kami Masatomo, in accordance with the notice issued on the 25th day of the 2nd month.
