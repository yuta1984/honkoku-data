　　　　　　First Month

Item: On the nineteenth day of the first month, as it had been decreed by written order from the previous winter that matters which could not be resolved unilaterally should be referred to Tokugawa Gyōbukyō Munetada, the chief elder (karō) Honda Yagara Masa■ arrived from the home domain at the Tokiwabashi residence on the eighteenth (yesterday), and today, accompanied by chief elder Okabe Sakanosuké Omotosada, appeared before Senior Councillor (rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masaaki to present the following expression of gratitude:

　　　　　Last winter, the elders who were present here in Edo were summoned and received instructions by written order. We have duly conveyed the contents of this communication to the elders at our home domain, and they are fully apprised of and duly respectful of the matter. We are most grateful for this gracious consideration. Accordingly, the elders at the home domain wish to express their gratitude for the above, and we have come to Edo as their representative on this occasion, and have thus come to call upon you.
　　　　　　First Month　　　　　Chief Elder of Matsudaira Oginomaru
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Honda Yagara

Item: On the twenty-third day of the same month, a reply was received from Senior Councillor Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masaaki, and the chief elder Okabe Sakanosuké Omotosada, the middle elder (chūrō) Akashi Shūzen Norihusa, and the household official (go-yōnin) Daidōji Magokurō Shigetsura — three persons in total — called upon Lord Sagami-no-kami, each bearing one silver coin in lieu of a horse (uma-dashi). They were granted an audience. This audience with Lord Sagami-no-kami had been requested on the nineteenth of the preceding days.

Item: On the twenty-fourth day of the same month, [a communication was issued] to the senior inspectors (ō-metsuke) regarding the matter of peasants in the private domains of various provinces gathering in large numbers at the residences of their lords, castle towns, or jinya (administrative outposts) to petition regarding the collection of annual taxes (nengu), or to request emergency food loans (fujiki) and seed loans (tanegashi).
