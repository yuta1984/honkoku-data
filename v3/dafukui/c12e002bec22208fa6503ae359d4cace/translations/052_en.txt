【Official Reply Note (responding to the inquiry on the previous page)】
Sixth month — To Matsudaira Ogimaru
Official Reply Note:
The matter may proceed as inquired.

Additionally:
One copy of a precedent document regarding an offering from the private purse of Matsudaira Mutsu-no-kami was submitted, but it is not recorded here.

Item: On the 6th day of the 7th month, as a congratulatory gift for the Oikedama ceremony, the resident envoy (留守居) Tsuda Kuemon Morihiro was dispatched as messenger — in the same manner as the previous lord — and ten ryō of gold was presented to the Kubō-sama (the Shogun). At the Yanagi-no-ma chamber of the Main Keep, the duty Senior Councilor Honda Hōki-no-kami Masayoshi presided, and the Sōja-ban (announcer of gifts) Naitō Yamato-no-kami Yoriyoshi announced the presentation.
To the Dainagon-sama (the heir), the resident envoy Katō Chōemon Masatoshi was dispatched as messenger, and the same amount was presented. At the West Keep, the attendant Senior Councilor Akimoto Tajima-no-kami (Suzutomo) presided, and the Sōja-ban Inoue Tōtōmi-no-kami Masaatsu announced the presentation.

Item: On the 15th day of the 7th month, as a congratulatory gift for the Obon mid-year festival (Chūgen), for the first time a letter from the private purse was sent, and lotus rice (hasu-meshi) in one decorative setting and a box of sliced mackerel (sashisaba) containing twenty pieces each were presented to the Kubō-sama, the Ōgosho-sama, and the Dainagon-sama. This had been approved by inquiry on the 21st of the previous month (sixth month).
From the Kubō-sama, three pieces of silver were granted to the messenger, and one thousand hiki in gold was granted to the escort and carriers.
