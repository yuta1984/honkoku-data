**[Response (*tsukifuda*) from the Bakufu]**

　　　　Sixth Month　　　　　[Addressed to] Matsudaira Ōgimaru

　　Attached Memorandum (*Tsukifuda*):
　　　The offering may be made as inquired.

Additionally, one document of precedents (*reisho*) relating to the private offerings made by Lord Matsudaira Mutsu-no-Kami was separately submitted, but this is not recorded here.

---

One: On the sixth day of the Seventh Month, as a congratulatory gift for *Oimi-dama* [a celebratory occasion], the Resident Official (*rusui*) Tsuda Kuemon (Morihiro) was dispatched as envoy, and in accordance with the precedent set by the late lord, ten ryō of gold were presented to the Kubō-sama [the Shogun]. The presentation was made at the Yanagi-no-Ma of the Hon-maru [Main Keep], where the Duty Councillor (*Goyōban*) Lord Honda Hōki-no-Kami (Masayoshi) was in attendance, and the announcement was made by the Chief of Ceremonies (*Sōjaban*) Lord Naitō Yamato-no-Kami (Yoriyoshi).

　To the Dainagon-sama [the heir/Great Counselor], the Resident Official Katō Chōemon (Masatoshi) was dispatched as envoy, and the same gift was presented in like manner. At the corresponding reception room in the Nishi-maru [West Keep], Lord Akimoto Tajima (Suzutomo), the attending Senior Councillor (*rōjū*), was in attendance, and the announcement was made by the Chief of Ceremonies Lord Inoue Tōtōmi-no-Kami (Masaatsu).

---

One: On the fifteenth day of the Seventh Month, as congratulatory gifts for *Chūgen* [the mid-year gift-giving season], for the first time an accompanying letter (*ofumi*) from private [internal domain] funds was included, and the following were presented to the Kubō-sama, the Ōgosho-sama [the retired Shogun], and the Dainagon-sama:

　　Lotus rice (*ren-han*) — one ceremonial arrangement (*kazari*) each
　　Mackerel (*sashisaba*, twenty skewers) — one box each

　(Approved by inquiry on the twenty-first day of the preceding Sixth Month.)

　From the Kubō-sama: three pieces of silver (*hakugin*) were bestowed upon the envoy, and one thousand *hiki* in gold were granted to the escort and bearers (*sairyō* and *mochinin*).
