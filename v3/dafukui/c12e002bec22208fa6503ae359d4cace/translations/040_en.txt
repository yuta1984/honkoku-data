Item: Those residing in their home provinces or domains are to send a written messenger (tsukai-fuda).
　　　　　　Note: Heirs and retired members who are likewise residing in their home provinces or domains are to do the same.
　　　　Item: Construction work and music (narumono) are to be suspended for five days, from today through the 2nd of next month.
　　　　　The above is to be disseminated as a notification.
　　　　　　27th day of the fourth month

Item: On the same day, in accordance with the same notification (regarding the passing of the retired sovereign), it was ordered that music (narumono) be suspended at the Edo residence for five days — from today through the 2nd of the fifth month — and in the home domain for three days — from today through the 29th.

Item: On the same day, at Tentoku-ji Temple, a memorial service for the twenty-seventh anniversary of the death of Lady Hōsen-in was conducted, beginning from the evening of the 26th through this morning. The same ceremony was likewise conducted at Unshō-ji Temple, and a tea offering ceremony (o-chanoyu) was also held at Matsuoka Tenryū-ji Temple.

Item: On the same day, as the domain lord was of young age, the Chūrō (Middle Elders), Yōnin (Administrators), Soba-bugyō (Chamberlain Officials), Metsuke (Inspectors), and others were ordered to submit oaths of loyalty (seishi) at the main residence (o-yashiki).

Item: 28th day of the fourth month:
In connection with the passing of the Sentō Sakuramachi-in (the retired sovereign Sakuramachi), a messenger was dispatched to inquire after the well-being of the relevant parties: the duty official (go-yōban) Lord Sakai Uemon-no-jō Tadayori; the Senior Councilor attending the O-gosho-sama (the retired shogun) Lord Nishio Oki-no-kami Tadanao; and the Senior Councilor attending the Dainagon-sama, Lord Akimoto Tajima-no-kami Suzutomo. The castle keeper (go-rusui) Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi was dispatched as the messenger, in accordance with the notification issued on the 27th, yesterday.
