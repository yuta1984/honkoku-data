Item: Those residing in their home domain or village shall dispatch a written message.
　　　　　　Note: The heirs and retired members of those residing in the home domain or village shall do likewise.
　　　　Item: Construction work and musical performances (nari-mono) are to be suspended for five days, from today through the second day of the coming month.
　　　　　　The foregoing is to be duly circulated.
　　　　　　　　Twenty-seventh day of the fourth month.

Item: On the same day, in accordance with the foregoing, it was decreed that musical performances be suspended for five days at the Edo residence (from today through the second day of the fifth month), and for three days in the home domain (from today through the twenty-ninth of the same month).

Item: On the same day, at Tentoku-ji temple, a memorial Buddhist service (hōji) marking the twenty-seventh anniversary of the death of Lady Hōsen'in was conducted from the evening of the twenty-sixth through the morning of this day. A similar service was conducted at Unshō-ji temple, and a tea ceremony (o-chanoyu) was also held at the Matsugaoka Tenryū-ji temple.

Item: On the same day, as the lord was of young age, oaths of allegiance (seishi) were administered to the middle elders (chūrō), chamberlains (yōnin), attendants (osoba), magistrates (o-bugyō), and inspectors (o-metsuke) and others at the lord's residence.

Item: On the twenty-eighth day of the fourth month, on account of the passing (hōgyō) of the Retired Sovereign (Sentō, the Sakuramachi-in), envoys were dispatched to inquire after the well-being of the Duty Commissioner Sakai Uemon-no-jō (Tadayori), the Senior Counselor attached to the Ōgosho (Retired Shogun) Lord Nishio Oki-no-kami (Tadanao), and the Senior Counselor attached to the Lord Dainagon, Lord Akimoto Tajima-no-kami (Suzutomo), in the person of the Resident Official Yokota Sakudayū (Naomichi). (Notice had been received on the twenty-seventh, yesterday.)
