Item: On the same day, the Senior Councillor (rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-Kami Masasuke received a visit from the house's steward (goyōnin) Daidōji Magokurō Shigeyoshi.
　The previous day, steward Kurahashi Jintayū had sent word to appear today. Upon appearing, he was told that regarding the marriage proposal (endan) that had been presented for private inspection the previous day, there should be no objections to it. Furthermore, he was informed through steward Kurahashi Jintayū that the matter should be privately inquired about again during the coming winter. Magokurō further stated that, as he understood it, in matters concerning the Three Gosanke Houses, it was sufficient for one party to make the request. He noted that Owari Domain's chief retainer Takenokosha Yamashiro-no-Kami had previously mentioned that since their house was of distinguished lineage, he would try to arrange for Ogimaru-dono to make his own inquiry, alongside the inquiry that the Lord of Owari would make to the public authorities (kōgi). He conveyed that, since this matter could result in their house being disregarded, they wished to make inquiries through the advance guard units (sakite-shū) and others employed for such requests. Upon hearing this, Jintayū informed him that the full details would be communicated to Sagami-no-Kami. Therefore, on the following day, the 13th, Magokurō appeared before the Chamberlain (sobashū) Ōoka Izumo-no-Kami Tadamitsu to convey the above matter.

Item: On the 13th day of the 8th month, through Mochizuki San'ei the Elder, the Chamberlain Ogasawara Iwami-no-Kami Masato inquired about the progress of cultivating the seeds of Korean ginseng (chōsenjin'nin) that had previously been bestowed as a gift. Following investigation at the home domain, a letter was attached from the house steward Daidōji Magokurō Shigeyoshi to San'ei the Elder, and the matter was forwarded through a foot attendant (okachimonono).
