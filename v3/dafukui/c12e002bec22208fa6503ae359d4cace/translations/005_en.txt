One kago (palanquin load) was received for the first time as a gift. On that occasion, three hundred hiki in gold were sent to the messenger; one hundred hiki to the decorator (kazari-kata); two hundred hiki to the inventory-list escort (mokurokusairyō) and two inventory-list box carriers; three hundred hiki to the five senior escorts (kami-sairyō); two hundred hiki to the five junior escorts (shimo-sairyō); and three hundred hiki to the twenty-two carriers (mochi-nin). In addition, ozōni (rice cake soup), sake, and clear soup (suimono) were also bestowed.

　Note: Regarding the gifts bestowed upon the messengers and others in connection with the receipt of the kagami-mochi, as this subsequently became standard practice, it will not be recorded hereafter.

Regarding the expression of gratitude for the receipt of these gifts: as (Lord Shigemasa) was still of young age, Shikibu-no-Shōyū Chikateру-sama served as his representative (proxy) and paid courtesy calls to the Goyōban (Duty Rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masaaki, to the Rōjū attending the Ōgosho-sama, Nishio Oki-no-kami Tadahisa (corrected from "Suketomo" by strikethrough), and to the Rōjū attending the Dainagon-sama, Akimoto Tajima-no-kami Suketomo. To the remaining Rōjū (Senior Councilors), a messenger was dispatched in the form of the acting guard captain and side attendant-in-training, Daidōji Tonomo Shigenaga.

　Note: Regarding the dispatching of courtesy messengers to the remaining Rōjū in connection with the receipt of these gifts, as this subsequently became standard practice, it will not be recorded hereafter.

Item: On the same day, in connection with the same matter, as a courtesy, a female messenger named Sakio was dispatched to the Hon-maru (Main Keep), and a female messenger named Yasoda was dispatched to the Nishi-maru (West Keep).

　Note: Regarding the dispatching of female courtesy messengers in connection with the receipt of these gifts, as this subsequently became standard practice, it will not be recorded hereafter.
