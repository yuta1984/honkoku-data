　　　Note: A seal certificate (判鑑) of the chief retainers (家老) was also submitted to Lord Itakura Sado-no-Kami, who is in charge of the Usui Checkpoint; however, as the content of that certificate is identical to the one above, it has not been recorded here.

　　　Item: Regarding the matter of the seal certificate, it has been the practice to exchange it each time there is a change among the chief retainers; however, this shall not be recorded hereafter.
