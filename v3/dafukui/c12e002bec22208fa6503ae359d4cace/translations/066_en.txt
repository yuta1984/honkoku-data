Item: On the 23rd day of the 11th month, it is reported that through his chief elder (karō) Takenokoši Yamashiro-no-Kami, the Owari Middle Counselor (Chūnagon) (Munekatsu)-sama submitted the following marriage inquiry (okotowari-ukagai) to the duty elder (goyōban) Honda Hōki-no-Kami (Masayoshi)-dono:

　　　　　We wish to request that, should there be a
　　　　　suitable match, a marriage alliance be arranged
　　　　　for the daughter of Owari-dono. We humbly
　　　　　ask that this matter be received favorably.
　　　　　　23rd day of the 11th month

　　　　　As was already conveyed informally on a
　　　　　previous occasion, it is understood that there
　　　　　is no private objection whatsoever to a
　　　　　betrothal between the daughter of Owari-dono
　　　　　and Matsudaira Yoshimaru.
　　　　　　23rd day of the 11th month

Item: On the same day, through the vanguard commander (osaki-te) Ogasawara Nuinosuke-dono, the following formal marriage notification document (odashifumi) was submitted to the duty elder Honda Hōki-no-Kami (Masayoshi)-dono:

　　　　　Regarding the marriage arrangement between
　　　　　the daughter of Owari Chūnagon-dono and
　　　　　myself: during the lifetime of my adoptive
　　　　　father Hyōbu-no-Taifu, this matter had already
　　　　　been discussed privately and informally at an
　　　　　earlier date. I hereby respectfully bring this
　　　　　to your attention for the record.
　　　　　　11th month　　　　　　Matsudaira Yoshimaru
