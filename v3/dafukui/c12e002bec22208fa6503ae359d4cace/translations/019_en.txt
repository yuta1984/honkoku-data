Note: As it has henceforth become standard practice to dispatch a messenger to inquire after the wellbeing of the Lord Dainagon each and every time he visits either Tōeizan (Kan'eiji Temple) or Zōjōji Temple, this shall no longer be recorded here.

Item: On the last day of the 12th month, the resident keeper Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi was dispatched as messenger to the Senior Resident Keeper (御留守居年寄) Tsuchiya Hyōbu-no-shō Hidenao-dono, to request that, regarding the matter of affixing seals (判形) to the pass-request acknowledgment documents (過書御請状), while three retainers — Tsuda Kyūuemon, Katō Chōuemon, and Yokota Sakudayū — had been assigned this duty, in the event that any one of them was unavailable, the document could be settled with the seal of just one person.

Item: On the 6th day of the 3rd month, having been summoned by the Duty Elder (御用番) Honda Hōki-no-Kami Masayoshi-dono, the resident keeper Katō Chōuemon Masatoshi appeared before him and received the following written notice:

　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Ogimaru
　　　On the coming 15th day, you are to proceed to the Inner Palace (大奥) to make a formal visit to the castle.
　　　　　　　　　　　3rd Month

Item: On the same day, regarding the same matter, word was received via a formal letter (奉文) from the senior ladies-in-waiting (老女中) of the Main Keep (御本丸). In acknowledgment thereof, letters (御文) were dispatched to the senior ladies-in-waiting attached to the Shōgun (公方様), the Retired Shōgun (大御所様), and the Lord Dainagon respectively.

Item: On the 7th day of the same month, the resident keeper Katō Chōuemon Masatoshi communicated the following written notice to the Senior Resident Keeper Tsuchiya Hyōbu-no-shō Hidenao-dono and the Head of the Inner Enclosure Guard (御広敷番之頭) Yokoyama Den'uemon-dono.
