Item: On the same day, in connection with the same matter, through a letter forwarded by the senior ladies-in-waiting (rōjochū), one decorative setting of lotus rice, one box of mackerel, and one cask of sake were received for the first time as gifts. On that occasion, the following amounts were sent: three hundred hiki in gold as a gift list (mokurok) to the messenger; three hundred hiki each to the three senior escorts (kami-sairyō); two hundred hiki each to the four junior escorts (shimo-sairyō); one hundred hiki to the one decorator (kazarikata); and three hundred hiki to the fifteen carriers (mochinin). In addition, sake and clear soup were served.
　　Note: Regarding the granting of gifts to the messengers in connection with receiving the lotus rice, these matters shall henceforth follow established precedent and will not be recorded here.

Regarding the expression of thanks for the aforementioned received gifts: as the lord was still of a young age, Fukujirō Katafusa-sama served as his representative (proxy), attending upon the duty Senior Councilor Honda Hōki-no-kami Masayoshi, the Senior Councilor attached to the Ōgosho-sama, Nishio Oki-no-kami Tadanao, and the Senior Councilor attached to the Dainagon-sama, Akimoto Tajima-no-kami Suzutomo. To the remaining Senior Councilors, the Guard Commander (Gosho-inban-gashira) Shimada Seizaemon Shigeyuki was dispatched as messenger. To Senior Councilor Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke, the house elder (karō) Ashida Zushō Kenno was separately dispatched as a special messenger of thanks. To the Chamberlains (Sobashū) Takai Hyōbu-no-Shōyū Nobufusa, Ōoka Izumo-no-kami Tadamitsu, and Ogasawara Iwami-no-kami Masato, messengers (otsukaiban) were dispatched.
　　Note: Regarding the dispatching of messengers to the remaining Senior Councilors as an expression of thanks for the aforementioned received gifts, these matters shall henceforth follow established precedent and will not be recorded here.
