When the written inquiry (oukagaisho) was submitted, it was communicated that, regarding the standing of Ogimaru-sama, if there were precedents for formally presenting tribute offerings to the Ōgosho-sama, such offerings should be submitted. In response, the following records of formal precedents for tribute offerings were submitted:

　　Item: On the 28th day of the 7th month of Enkyō 4, the year of the Hare (1747), on the occasion of the formal adoption ceremony conducted through a representative (nadai), one document recording precedents for tribute offerings to the Three Honorable Residences (Mio-sho-sama).

　　Item: On the 23rd day of the 12th month of the same year, upon completion of the residential transfer (ohikiwatashi), and on the 25th of the same month, one document recording precedents for tribute offerings to the Three Honorable Residences.

　　Item: On the 15th day of the 4th month of Kan'en 1, the year of the Dragon (1748), following the residential transfer, on the occasion of the first attendance at the inner chambers (ōoku) of the Main Keep (honmaru), one document recording precedents for tribute offerings.

　　Item: In the 6th month of the current year of the Horse (1750), during the memorial Buddhist services for the 25th death anniversary of Jōen-in-sama, one document recording precedents for tribute offerings.

　　The above four precedent documents were submitted, but are omitted here [from the record].

　　Note: Regarding the formal tribute offerings to Ōgosho-sama, on this occasion from Owari-sama:
　　　　To the Kubō-sama: two varieties, one load (ikka)
　　　　To the Dainagon-sama: one variety, one load (ikka)
　　were presented through the chief retainer acting as messenger.
