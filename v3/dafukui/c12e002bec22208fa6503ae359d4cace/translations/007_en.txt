Item: On the seventh day of the first month, on the occasion of the Young Herbs Festival (wakana no o-shūgi), the caretaker official (o-rusuī) Katō Chōuemon Masatoshi was dispatched as messenger, and, following the precedent set by the previous domain lord, one box of fresh sea bream (sōtai) was presented to the Kubō-sama. The Chamberlain (o-sobashū) Mizuno Kawachi-no-kami Tadatomi informed the messenger that the presentation would be conveyed to the Kubō-sama in due course.
　For the Dainagon-sama, the caretaker official (o-rusuī) Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi was dispatched as messenger to the Nishi-maru (Western Keep), where the same gift was presented. The Chamberlain (o-sobashū) Toda Tosa-no-kami Ujiaki informed the messenger that the presentation would be conveyed in due course.

Item: On the eighth day of the same month, the domain's financial officer (o-katte-yaku) Hayashi Shinsuke [remainder illegible] appeared at the Finance Office (o-kanjōsho) and reported that the handover of the formerly-administered domain lands (moto o-azukari-sho) to the local magistrate (o-daikan) Naitō Jūuemon in the home domain had been completed on the twenty-third day of the twelfth month of the previous year. This report was subsequently also conveyed to the Finance Magistrate (o-kanjō-bugyō) Endō Ise-no-kami Yasutsugi.

Item: On the tenth day of the same month, as the Kubō-sama had made a visit to Tōei-zan (Kan'ei-ji Temple), a messenger — the caretaker official (o-rusuī) Katō Chōuemon Masatoshi — was dispatched to the castle upon the Kubō-sama's return, to inquire after his health and well-being. The Inspector (metsuke) Wakisaka Kazue informed the messenger that the Kubō-sama was in increasingly fine health and spirits.

　　Note: As the dispatch of a messenger to inquire after the Kubō-sama's well-being following each visit to Tōei-zan subsequently became standard practice, it will not be recorded hereafter.
