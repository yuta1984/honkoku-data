Item: On the seventh day of the first month, on the occasion of the Young Herbs (Wakana) celebration, the Rusuii Katō Chōuemon (Masatoshi) was dispatched as messenger, and in accordance with the precedent of the previous lord, one box of fresh sea bream (sen-tai) was presented to the Kubō-sama. The Sobashū (Senior Attendant) Mizuno Kawachi-no-kami (Tadatomi) conveyed that the presentation would be duly reported at a later time.
　To the Dainagon-sama, the Rusuii Yokota Sakudayū (Naomichi) was dispatched as messenger to the Nishi-maru (Western Keep), and the same gift was presented. The Sobashū Toda Tosa-no-kami (Ujiaki) conveyed that the presentation would likewise be duly reported at a later time.

Item: On the eighth day of the same month, the Okatte-yaku (Financial Official) Hayashi Shinsuke (■■) appeared at the Gokanjōsho (Financial Magistrate's Office) to report that, concerning the matter of the former Oazukari-sho (entrusted territory), the transfer to the Daikan (Local Deputy Administrator) Naitō Jūemon at the domain had been completed on the twenty-third day of the twelfth month of the previous year. Following this, the same report was conveyed to the Kanjō-bugyō (Financial Magistrate) Endō Ise-no-kami (Yakitsugu).

Item: On the tenth day of the same month, on the occasion of the Kubō-sama's visit to Tōeizan (Kan'ei-ji Temple), after his return, the Rusuii Katō Chōuemon (Masatoshi) was dispatched as messenger to the castle to inquire after his well-being. The Metsuke (Inspector) Wakisaka Kazue conveyed that his well-being was exceedingly good.
　　　Note: Regarding the dispatch of a messenger to inquire after the Kubō-sama's well-being each time he visits Tōeizan, since this will henceforth become a regular established custom, it will not be recorded again.
