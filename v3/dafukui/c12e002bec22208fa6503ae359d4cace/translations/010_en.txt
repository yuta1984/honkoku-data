Item: On the twenty-seventh day of the first month, at Tentokuji temple, the hundredth-day memorial service (hyakkanichi hōji) for Lady Tokushōin was conducted in advance, beginning from the evening of the twenty-sixth day (yesterday) through this morning.
　　Note: The proper date would have been the second day of the second month; however, as the Shogun's New Year's celebratory ceremonies were scheduled for the first day of that month, the service was moved forward (otokoshi).

Item: On the twenty-eighth day of the same month, following the precedent of the previous generation, five salted codfish (shiotara) were offered in private to the Ōgosho (the Retired Shogun). In connection with this, a letter from the domain's Edo representative (rusui) Tsuda Kuuemon Morihiro was attached and the gift was sent via a foot attendant (okachi) to the household retainer of the Shogun's attendant (sobashū) Ogasawara Iwami-no-kami Masanori.

Item: On the twenty-ninth day of the same month, as the Shogun made a visit to Zōjōji temple, after his return, the Edo domain representative Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi was dispatched to Edo Castle as a messenger to inquire after the Shogun's well-being, and was received by the Inspector (metsuke) Kamio Ichibei.
　　Note: The practice of dispatching a messenger to inquire after the Shogun's well-being each time he visits Zōjōji temple shall henceforth be considered a standing custom and shall not be recorded each time.

Item: On the same day, also for the same reason, a letter was sent for the first time from the inner household (go-naishō) to the senior lady attendants (rōjochū) of the Main Keep (Honmaru) to inquire after the Shogun's well-being.
　　Note: Both for visits to Tōeizan (Kan'eiji) and Zōjōji, after the Shogun's return, [the practice of sending a letter to inquire after his well-being shall also be considered a standing custom and shall not be recorded each time].
