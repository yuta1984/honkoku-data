Item: To the Great Counselor (Dainagon-sama) as well, the same person [Katō Chōuemon] was dispatched to present the same [gift]. The Shōgun's Attendant Councilor, Toda Tosa-no-Kami (Ujimei), informed [the envoy] that the matter would be reported at a later hour.

Item: On the 25th day of the 12th month, on the occasion of the celebration of [the new lord's] succession to the headship of the domain, a general amnesty was proclaimed throughout the domain.

Item: On the same day, a circular was received from the Senior Inspector (ōmetsuke), Ishikawa Tosa-no-Kami (Masatomo), stating that the population registers (ninbetsu-aratame-chōmen) of the various provinces for the current Year of the Horse were to be submitted to Tosa-no-Kami by around the 8th or 9th month, and that all matters of inspection and documentation were to follow the established precedents — this notification having been circulated through the castle liaison officers (rusui) of Matsudaira Daizenno-daibu-sama and Hosokawa Etchū-no-Kami.

Item: On the same day, as a memorial service (hōji) was held at Tōeizan [Kan'eiji Temple] for the third anniversary of the death of Shishin'in-sama (the biological mother of the Great Counselor), an envoy — the castle liaison officer Yokota Sakudayū (Naomichi) — was dispatched to the Nishi-no-Maru [the Great Counselor's residence] to inquire after the Great Counselor's well-being, presenting [as a gift] yams (yamaimo; thirty roots, arranged on a stand in a green basket) [prior approval having been obtained on the 20th]. The Attendant Councilor, Toda Tosa-no-Kami (Ujimei), informed [the envoy] that the matter would be reported at a later hour.

Item: On the 26th day of the same month, regarding the same matter [the memorial service], an envoy — the unit commander and bow-bearer captain Kaga Kurōuemon (personal name illegible) — was dispatched to present one piece of white silver as incense offering money (kōden) to the main temple precinct (honbō) of Tōeizan [prior approval having been obtained on the 19th].

Item: On the 29th day of the same month, as the Great Counselor paid a visit to Tōeizan to pray before the spirit of Shishin'in-sama, after the Great Counselor's return, an envoy — the castle liaison officer Yokota Sakudayū (Naomichi) — was dispatched to the castle to inquire after [the Great Counselor's] well-being, and was received by the Inspector (metsuke), Makino Oribe.
