　　　　　　Eighth Month

Item: On the 19th day of the 8th month, a written notice was issued by the Inspectors (metsuke) Kamio Ichizaemon and Tsuchiya Chōsaburō, regarding the dismounting area (gejosha) at the main gate of the Western Citadel (Nishinomaru) during attendance for the New Year's greetings, the Five Annual Festivals, monthly ceremonies, and other occasions. Henceforth, the dismounting area is to be designated up to the seventh pillar from the corner of the covered waiting area (koshikake) at the main gate of the Western Citadel. Accordingly, upon proceeding to the castle, palanquins, hasami-bako (luggage boxes carried on poles), and the like are to be moved directly to the side of the waiting area and along the moat embankment, kept within the limits of the standing guard posts, and must not protrude beyond. Upon the lord's departure, attendants are to present themselves as soon as they catch sight of their master. Nothing is to be left outside the designated area. Since inspectors of foot soldiers (o-kachi-metsuke) and inspectors of minor attendants (o-kōnin-metsuke) will be deployed to manage the designated areas, the head attendants among the retinue and all others down to the lowest ranks are to be duly informed that they must strictly follow instructions. This circular notice was received from the留守居 (castle keepers in Edo) and供頭 (attendant chiefs) of Matsudaira Mutsu-no-Kami and Matsudaira Aki-no-Kami.

Item: On the same day, at the Third Enclosure (Sannomaru), from today through the 28th of this month, members of the various units (shogumi) were ordered to perform guard duty (mokuchū). An attendant officer (sobayaku) was dispatched as a proxy representative (o-nadai), and prizes/rewards were distributed to those who had performed well.
　　　Note: Henceforth, orders for guard duty to members of the various units will not be recorded.

Item: On the 25th of the same month, the members of the Jōfu-gumi (the unit of retainers permanently stationed in Edo) were similarly ordered to perform the same duties. The deputy attendant officer (sobayaku-dai) and chief attendant (goyōnin) Mōuke Denzaburō (■Fuku) was dispatched as a proxy representative, and prizes/rewards were distributed to those who had performed well.
