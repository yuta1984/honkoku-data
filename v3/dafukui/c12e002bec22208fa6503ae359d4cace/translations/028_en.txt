Item: On the 24th day of the 3rd month, a summons was issued by Honda Hōki-no-Kami (Masayoshi), who was serving as the duty senior councillor (goyōban). The domain's resident agent (ōrusuī), Yokota Sakudayū (Naomichi), attended and received the following written notice:

　　　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Yoshimaru

　　　　On the 27th of this month,
　　　　you are to proceed to the Inner Chambers (Ōoku) of the West Citadel (Nishinomaru).
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　3rd Month

Item: On the 25th day of the same month, as an expression of gratitude for the above,
letters were sent to the senior ladies-in-waiting (rōjochū) attending the Shōgun's consort (Kubō-sama), the Retired Shōgun (Ōgosho-sama), and the Grand Counselor (Dainagon-sama).

　　　Note: Although the letters of thanks should have been sent the previous day, they were delayed,
　　　　　　and were therefore sent this morning instead.

Item: On the same day, the domain's resident agent, Tsuda Kurōemon (Moriyuki), conveyed the following written notice to Tsuchiya Hyōbu-no-Shōyū (Hidenao), the senior official overseeing resident agents (ōrusuī-toshiyori):

　　　　Those to accompany Yoshimaru upon his visit to the Inner Chambers of the West Citadel:

　　　　　　　　　　　House Elder (karō): Ashida Zusho
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　His retainers: 5 persons
　　　　　　　　　　　Steward (yōnin): Shimada Seizaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same rank: 4 persons
