Item: On the 24th day of the 3rd month, a summons was received from Hon'da Hoki-no-kami Masayoshi, the duty senior councillor (go-yōban). The domain's Edo representative (o-rusui) Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi attended, whereupon the following was conveyed by written notice:

　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira O-Yoshimaru

　　　On the coming 27th day,
　　　You are to proceed to the Inner Chambers (Ōoku) of the Western Citadel (Nishi-no-maru).
　　　　　　　　3rd Month

Item: On the 25th day of the same month, as a gesture of thanks for the above,
letters were dispatched to the senior ladies-in-waiting (rōjochū) attendant upon the Kubō-sama, the Ōgosho-sama, and the Dainagon-sama.

　　　Note: The said letters of thanks should have been dispatched the previous day, but as they were delayed, they were sent out this morning instead.

Item: On the same day, the domain's Edo representative Tsuda Kuemon Moruyuki conveyed the following by written notice to Tsuchiya Hyōbu-no-shōyu Hidenao, the senior Edo representative (o-rusui toshiyori):

　　　Those who will accompany Yoshimaru upon his entry into the Inner Chambers of the Western Citadel:

　　　　　　　　　　　　　House Elder (karō): Ashida Zusho
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　His retainers: 5 persons
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Steward (yōnin): Shimada Seiemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same rank: 4 persons
