— Castle (wearing noshime formal robes and hemp kamishimo).

　Note: At the time of the visit to the castle, two o-komono (attendant footmen) came to the residence to serve as guides. From the Hirakawa Gate to within the Kami-bairin (Upper Plum Grove), the palanquin was used; dismounting outside the kitte gate. The sword was carried from that point up to the Ohiroshiki Genkan Ue-no-ma (the upper room of the broad-entry vestibule of the Ōoku) by the two men Akita Sangozaemon (Katsushizu) and Shimoyama Shichirōzaemon (Naosada).

　　Note: Henceforth, one side attendant accompanying as escort shall carry the sword.

Entering the rest chamber (go-kyūsokusho) of the Ōoku, [Shigemasa] changed into long trailing hakama, and in the reception hall (go-taimenjo), at the lower dais (ge-dan), he expressed his respects to the Kubō (the Shōgun). An august reply (jōi) was received. At the upper dais (jō-dan), he received the honorable gift of nagashi-awabi (long dried abalone). On that occasion, from the private household (go-naishō), the following were presented as tribute: one box of kombu (kelp), one box of dried sea bream, one cask of sake, and additionally one box containing five inrō (medicine cases). Having concluded the presentation, the following were received as gifts: ten rolls of white crepe silk (shiro-chirimen), and additionally three tan of satin (donsu).

The Kubō retired. The Dainagon then appeared at the same place. In the same manner as above, respects were expressed, and the nagashi-awabi was received. On that occasion, from the private household, the following were presented: one box of dried sea bream, one cask of sake, and additionally one box containing ten purses (kinchaku). Having concluded the presentation, the following were received: five tan of white crepe silk, and additionally three tan of satin.

To Manjirō-sama (the room of the audience is not known), the following were presented from the private household: one set of sea bream, and additionally one box of toy dolls (o-teasobi ningyō).
