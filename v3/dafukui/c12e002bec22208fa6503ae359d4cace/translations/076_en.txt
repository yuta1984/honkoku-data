To the attached Senior Councillor Akimoto Tajima-no-Kami (Suzutomo) of the Lord Great Counselor's household, given his young age [i.e., the Lord Great Counselor's youth], the residential caretaker (rusuí) Katō Chōemon (Masatoshi) was dispatched as envoy.

　　　Note: Regarding the above midwinter inquiry after the Lord Great Counselor's wellbeing, the dispatch of an envoy during the period of his youth is henceforth established as standard practice and will not be recorded separately.

1. On the 12th day of the 12th month, the duty councillor Matsudaira Ukon-shōgen (Takemoto) summoned the residential caretaker Yokota Sakudayū (Naomichi), who duly appeared. Through an attached note (tsukefuda) appended to the letter of inquiry (ukagaisho) submitted on the 5th of the previous month, the following instructions were conveyed:

　　　　　Letter of Inquiry
　　　　　　　　Memorandum

　　　　　To the Three Households (san-gosho):
　　　　　　Dried cod (hidara) — one box each

　　　　　The above items are to be presented via a messenger from the inner household (gonaishō) as a midwinter inquiry after their wellbeing. We humbly request your guidance on this matter.
　　　　　　12th month, 5th day　　　　　Matsudaira Ogimaru

　　　　　Attached Note (Tsukefuda):
　　　　　　You may proceed as inquired.
