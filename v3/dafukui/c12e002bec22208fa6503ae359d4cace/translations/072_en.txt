11th month, 28th day　　　　　Matsudaira Okimaru

Attached Note (Otsukefuda):
　This matter shall be as inquired.

Written Inquiry (Oukagaisho):
　On the occasion of the marriage arrangement (engumi) having been proclaimed, should it be so ordered in accordance with the inquiry regarding the tribute offerings to the Three Honorable Residences, we wish to present these through the chief retainers (karō). We humbly submit this inquiry. Respectfully submitted.
　　11th month, 28th day　　　　　Matsudaira Okimaru

Attached Note (Otsukefuda):
　This matter shall be as inquired.

Written Inquiry (Oukagaisho):
　　　Memorandum

　To the Three Honorable Residences:
　　Fresh sea bream (namatai)　— one folded box each

　The above is submitted as follows: on the occasion of the marriage arrangement having been proclaimed, we humbly wish to present the above through a female messenger (onnazukai) from the inner household (onaisho). We humbly submit this inquiry on this occasion. Respectfully submitted.
　　11th month, 28th day　　　　　Matsudaira Okimaru
