A messenger was sent to the Ōgosho-sama (Retired Shogun), with Rusui (domain representative in Edo) Katō Chōuemon Masatoshi serving as the envoy, presenting the same gifts as described above. At the Sotetsu-no-ma (Sago Palm Room) in the same location (Edo Castle), the gifts and inventory list were handed over to the retainers of Sōjaban (Master of Ceremonies) Nagai Iga-no-kami Naonobu.

To the Dainagon-sama (Senior Counselor/Great Counselor), the same person (Katō Chōuemon Masatoshi) was sent as messenger, presenting the same gifts as above. At the same location, the gifts and inventory list were handed over to the retainers of Sōjaban Inoue Tōtōmi-no-kami Masaatsu.

Item: On the same day, in connection with the same matter, for the first time a private letter (御内証より) was sent, and a decorated kagami-mochi (mirror rice cake) was presented to each of the Kubō-sama (Shogun), Ōgosho-sama (Retired Shogun), and Dainagon-sama. This had already been approved on the twenty-sixth day of the twelfth month of the previous year.

From the Kubō-sama, three pieces of silver were bestowed upon the messenger, and one thousand hiki in gold were bestowed upon the escort and bearers.

From the Ōgosho-sama and Dainagon-sama, two pieces of silver each were bestowed upon the messengers, and five hundred hiki in gold each were bestowed upon the escorts and bearers.

As an expression of gratitude for the gifts bestowed upon the messengers,
letters of thanks were sent for the first time to the senior ladies-in-waiting (rōjochū) attending upon the Kubō-sama, Ōgosho-sama, and Dainagon-sama.

　Note: Although gifts were bestowed upon the messengers in connection with this kagami-mochi presentation, and letters of thanks were sent, as this subsequently became standard practice, it will not be recorded hereafter.
