Item: On the same day, in connection with the foregoing, female envoys (onna-tsukai) and Yasoda were dispatched from the inner quarters (go-naishō) to the Main Keep (Hon-maru) and the West Keep (Nishi-maru) as a gesture of thanks.

　　　　　Item: Hereafter, the dispatch of female envoys in connection with the said gift-receipt will not be recorded, as it has become standard practice.

On this occasion, as both the Senior Lady (o-toshiyori) Sakio and Yasoda had repeatedly served as envoys on behalf of the Shogun, they were each granted two scrolls (makimono) as a reward.

Item: On the seventh day of the fifth month, at the Main Keep, the lord's representative (myōdai) was assigned to the Senior Counselors' martial arts review (bugei go-ran) in place of the lord, with the Senior Retainer (o-karō) appointed as said representative. Swordsmanship (kenjutsu) was assigned from today through the tenth, and archery (yumi) and firearms (teppō) from the seventeenth through the twenty-third of the same month.

　　　Note: The practice of attending the martial arts review by proxy (myōdai) during residence in Edo has hereafter become standard and will not be recorded.

Item: On the eighth day of the same month, a circular (mawashijō) was received from the Senior Inspector-Generals (ō-metsuke), relayed by the residential agents (rusuii) of Lord Matsudaira Aki-no-kami (Yoshinaga) and Lord Matsudaira Ōi-no-kami, as follows:

　　　　　Regarding the demise of the Retired Emperor (Sentō Hōgō崩御): Lords of Fourth Rank and holders of domains of 100,000 koku or more are to send envoys to Kyoto to present incense offerings (kōden), in accordance with the precedent set at the time of the death of the Nakamikado-in in the second year of Genbun (1737).
