The above persons are respectfully requested to be granted permission to enter and exit from the Sakashita Gate through the Nishi-no-Maru Rear Gate, the same Kitte Gate, and as far as the O-hiroshiki. Respectfully submitted.

　Third month　　　　　　Retainer of Matsudaira Oyoshimaru:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Tsuda Kuemon

　Note: Although this written notice was also submitted to the Head of the O-hiroshiki Guard, Andō Tarōemon, it is not recorded here.

Item: On the twenty-seventh day of the third month, at the Hour of the Sheep [approximately 2:00 p.m.], Oyoshimaru made his first visit to the Great Inner Chambers (Ō-ooku) of the Nishi-no-Maru following the assumption of family headship.
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　(Dressed in noshime and hemp-cloth formal hakama [asa-kamishimo])

He dismounted from his palanquin outside the Kitte Gate, having traveled from the Sakashita Gate through the Rear Gate.

　Note: The sword-box (hasami-bako), the three ceremonial standing umbrellas, and the sunshade were all conveyed to the inner chambers. Tea and lunch provisions were carried up to the top of the main entrance (genkan); the palanquin was placed inside the shikidai (entry landing).

The Head of the O-hiroshiki Guard, Andō Tarōemon, came out to receive him at the shikidai. In the Ō-ooku rest room, Oyoshimaru changed into formal long hakama (naga-bakama). At the lower dais (ge-dan) of the audience chamber (taimenjo), he paid his respects to the Ō-gosho-sama [the retired lord/great retired one]. An imperial/senior command was bestowed. At the upper dais (jō-dan), he received hand-length abalone (te-naga awabi) as a gift.
