　Third month　　　　　　Retainer of Matsudaira Oyoshimaru:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Tsuda Kuemon

Note: The above written notice was also submitted to the Head of the O-hiroshiki Guard, Lord Andō Tarōemon, but this is not recorded here.

Item: On the twenty-seventh day of the third month, at the Hour of the Boar (around 10 a.m.), Oyoshimaru made his first visit to the Great Inner Chambers (Ō-ooku) of the Nishi-no-Maru after the succession to the headship of the family. He wore formal noshime robes with hempen ceremonial hakama (asa-kamishimo). He dismounted outside the Kitte Gate, having entered from the Sakashita Gate through the Rear Gate.

　　Note: The personal chest (hasami-bako), three standing umbrellas, and the sun parasol were all sent around to the inner quarters. Tea and lunch were placed on the raised platform above the main entrance. The palanquin was stored inside the ceremonial entrance porch (shikidai).

The Head of the O-hiroshiki Guard, Lord Andō Tarōemon, came out to receive him at the ceremonial entrance porch (shikidai). In the Ō-ooku resting room, Oyoshimaru changed into formal long hakama (naga-hakama). At the audience hall, from the lower dais (ge-dan), he formally presented his greetings to the Ōgosho-sama (the Retired Lord). A pronouncement (jōi) was received in return. On the upper dais (jō-dan), he received dried abalone (te-naga awabi) as a gift. On that occasion, from the inner private quarters, a box of dried sea bream, one load of ceremonial sake (o-taru), and additionally five sets of sashes (o-kondō) were presented.
