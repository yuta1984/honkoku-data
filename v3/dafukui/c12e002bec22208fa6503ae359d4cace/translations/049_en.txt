**Official Records (continued)**

Item: On the first day of the sixth month, in the same manner as above, the messenger Azuma Shingorōemon Sukeharu was sent together with the Kyoto caretaker (御留守居) Arakawa Sukeemon to Sennyū-ji Temple, where ten pieces of white silver were offered as condolence money (御香奠). This was carried out in response to a notice issued on the eighth day of the previous month (fifth month), and Shingorōemon departed Kyoto on the following second day.
　　Note: Shingorōemon arrived back in Fukui on the sixth day of the same month.

Item: On the sixth day of the same month, as a memorial service (二十五回忌) for Lady Jōen-in — the biological mother of Lord Yūtoku-in — was to be held at Tōeizan (Kan'ei-ji Temple), a messenger was dispatched to inquire after the wellbeing of the Kubō-sama (the Shōgun) and the Ōgosho-sama (the Retired Shōgun). The caretaker (御留守居) Katō Chōemon Masatoshi was sent as messenger, bearing one box containing fifteen bags of arrowroot starch (葛粉) as a gift. The formal inquiry had already been completed on the second day.

Item: On the ninth day of the same month, for the same reason as above, the messenger — the Senior Guard Commander (御先物頭) Sakakibara Sukeemon Narifusa, wearing formal long hakama (長袴) — accompanied by the caretaker (御留守居) Yokota Sakudayū, proceeded to the main temple hall (御本坊) at Tōeizan, where one piece of white silver was offered as condolence incense money (御香奠) before the sacred altar (御霊前). The formal inquiry had already been completed on the second day. After the announcement was made to the temple administrator (執当中), Sukeemon completed his ritual bow and withdrew.
　　Note: For messengers of the Three Gosanke Families, affiliated domain families (御家), and Senior Councilors (御老中), offerings were made and bows were performed before the altar (御霊前) as described in the main text. The messengers of the Senior Councilors wore half-hakama (半袴).
