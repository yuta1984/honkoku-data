　　Item: The practice of dispatching a letter to inquire after the Shogun's well-being each time he visits Zōjōji temple shall henceforth be considered a standing custom and shall not be recorded each time.
　　Item: Even when the Ōgosho (Retired Shogun) or the Dainagon make visits [to temples], no such letter of inquiry shall be dispatched.

Item: On the first day of the second month, in celebration of the Shogun's fortieth birthday, the domain's Edo representative (rusui) Tsuda Kuuemon Morihiro was dispatched as a messenger bearing a box of fresh sea bream (sōdai) as a gift. In accordance with the notice issued on the twenty-fourth day of the previous month (first month), the presentation was received by the Shogun's protocol officer (sōjaban) Kutsuki Tosa-no-kami Haratsuna at the Hinoki-no-ma (Hall of Cypress) in Edo Castle.

Item: On the second day of the same month, having been summoned by the Senior Councillor (Rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masaaki, the domain's Edo representative Katō Chōemon Masatoshi attended the meeting. Regarding the Shogun's birthday celebrations, a petition (oukagai-sho) had been submitted requesting permission to conduct prayers (kitō) at Sannō Shrine, and to offer talismans (ofuda), protective charms (omamori), and one variety of boxed provisions (hakosakana) from the inner household (go-naishō). Today, via an attached note (tsukefuda), an instruction was issued approving the petition as submitted.
　　Note: Following this instruction, the prayer ritual, talismans, and protective charms were requested from Kannriin at Sannō Shrine, and the items were duly presented on the thirteenth day of the same month.
