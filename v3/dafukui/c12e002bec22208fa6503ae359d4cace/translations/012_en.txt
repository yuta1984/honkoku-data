Item: On the fifth day of the second month, at the Hyōjōsho (the Judicial Council chambers), the domain's Senior Councillor (go-karō) Matsudaira Shume Masaaki conveyed the following instruction to Amagata Kazuma Tomoyoshi, the attendant official (goyōnin) of the Head of the Shogunal Guard (goshoinhanbantō): Given that since last year, excessive expenditures have placed severe strain on the domain's finances (okatate), potentially affecting the conduct of governance itself, Amagata Kazuma has been appointed to oversee the reduction of all manner of expenditures for the benefit of the domain. He is therefore directed to consult openly and without concealment with all officials, from the magistrates (bugyō) on down.

Item: On the same day, it was decreed that at the annual festivals of Shinmei-gū Shrine, Kimachi Hachiman-gū Shrine, and Matsumoto Tennō Shrine, a deputy representative (odaisan) and a prayer superintendent (okitō bugyō) shall be sent to offer sacred first-fruits (ohatsuhо̄).

Item: On the ninth day of the same month, having been summoned by the Senior Councillor (Rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masaaki, the domain's Edo representative (rusui) Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi attended the meeting, whereupon an instruction was issued via an attached note (tsukefuda) in response to the petition (oukagai-sho) that had been submitted on the twenty-sixth day of the previous month (first month).

　　　　Petition:
　　　　　It has previously been decreed that while I [Matsudaira Oyoshimaru] am still of young age, I should attend the New Year's greetings by proceeding to the Main Keep (Honmaru), the West Keep (Nishinomaru), and the Great Interior (Ōoku) of Edo Castle. I hereby humbly inquire as to the arrangements for my attendance at the castle at this time.
　　　　Item: On the occasion of the above castle attendance, I wish to present gifts to the Three Gosho [residences of retired Shogunal figures] and to Manjirō-sama, as described in the attached document. Furthermore, I believe it proper to offer gifts to the lady attendants (jochū) as described in the attached document. I humbly submit this inquiry for consideration.
　　　　　　First month　　　　　　Matsudaira Oyoshimaru
