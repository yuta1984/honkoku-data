The hundredth-day memorial service (hyakkanichi hōji) for Lady Tokushōin was conducted.

Item: On the fifth day of the second month, at the Hyōjōsho (Deliberation Hall), the Senior Chamberlain's aide (goyōnin) Amagata Kazuma (Tomoyoshi) was informed that, given the excessive expenditures since the previous year which had severely strained the domain's finances and were likely to impede the conduct of governance, he had been appointed to the post of financial supervisor (goyōgakari) to identify ways to reduce expenditures across all matters for the benefit of the domain. The domain Elder (karō) Matsudaira Shume (Masamitsu) conveyed the instruction that all officials, including magistrates and other functionaries, were to discuss this matter without concealment.

Item: On the same day, it was decreed that at the annual festivals of Shinmei-gū (Kicho), Hachiman-gū (Matsumoto), and Tennō Shrine, an honorary offering (hatsuhо) was to be presented via a representative (odaisan) and the prayer magistrate (okitō bugyō).

Item: On the ninth day of the same month, having been summoned by the Senior Councilor (Rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami (Masasuke), the Edo caretaker (rusuiyaku) Yokota Sakudayū (Naomichi) appeared before him, whereupon instructions were issued via an attached reply slip (tsukefuda) in response to the petition (ukagaisho) that had been submitted on the twenty-sixth day of the previous month.

　　　　(Contents of the petition:)

　　　　　It was previously decreed that while I am yet of young age, I should attend the castle (登城) to pay New Year's congratulatory respects (nengashi) to the Main Keep (Honmaru), the West Keep (Nishinomaru), and the Inner Quarters (Ōoku). I hereby respectfully inquire regarding the matter of attending the castle on this occasion.

　　　　Item: On the occasion of the said castle attendance, I wish to present offerings to the Three Gosho (Three Honored Ladies) and to Manjirō-sama, as described in the accompanying document. Furthermore, I intend to send gifts to the ladies-in-waiting (jochū) as described in the accompanying document. I humbly inquire regarding these matters. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　First Month　　　　　　Matsudaira Oyamaru
