(Continued from previous page) In order to report to the Duty Senior Councilor (御用番), Lord Matsudaira Ukonshōgen Takemoto, the domain's caretaker (御留守居) Katō Chōemon Masatoshi was dispatched as messenger.

Item: On the eighteenth day of the sixth month, as a summer greeting (暑中御機嫌伺い), messengers were dispatched to: the Duty Senior Councilor Lord Matsudaira Ukonshōgen Takemoto; the Senior Councilor attached to the Ōgosho-sama, Lord Nishio Okino-kami Tadanao; and the Senior Councilor attached to the Dainagon-sama, Lord Akimoto Tajima-no-kami Suzutomo — in recognition of the domain lord's young age (御幼年), the domain's caretaker (御留守居) Tsuda Kyūemon Morinaga was dispatched as messenger.
　　Note: Regarding the aforementioned summer greetings, as it is established practice to dispatch a messenger while the domain lord is of young age, this matter will henceforth not be recorded.

Item: On the nineteenth day of the same month, on account of the summer season, the domain's caretaker (御留守居) Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi was dispatched as messenger, following the precedent set by the previous domain lord (御先代):

　To the Kubō-sama (the Shōgun), one box containing one thousand bundles of locally produced sōmen noodles (索麺) was presented as a gift. The Shōgun's chamberlain (御側衆) Lord Mizuno Kawachi-no-kami conveyed that the gift would be formally announced at a later time.

　To the Ōgosho-sama (the Retired Shōgun), the same messenger presented the same gift. The Retired Shōgun's chamberlain (御側衆) Lord Kobori Tosa-no-kami conveyed that the gift would be formally announced at a later time.

　To the Dainagon-sama, the same gift was presented in the same manner. The chamberlain (御側衆) Lord Mizuno Tamba-no-kami conveyed that the gift would be formally announced at a later time.
