Kan'en 3rd Year, Kanoe-Uma (1750)

Item: On the first day of the first month (New Year's Day), the New Year's ceremonial observances were held at the residence (o-yashiro). However, due to the Lord's young age, an abbreviated ceremony was conducted.

Item: On the same day, regarding the New Year's congratulatory gifts — as the Lord was of young age — the Head of the Gosho'in Guard (御書院番頭), Sasaji Gonuemon (Takatsune; wearing a suo [formal divided skirt and jacket]), was dispatched as envoy, following the precedent set by the previous lord. He presented to the Kubō-sama (the Shogun) one sword and one horse (substituted with ten ryō of gold). The Senior Councilor (Rōjū) appeared in the second room of the Great Hall (Ōhiroma) of the castle, and in the third room, the sword registry (目録) was presented to the Announcement Officer (Sōja-ban), Lord Honda Nagato-no-kami (Tadahide).
