The Third Year of Kan'en (1750), the Year of the Horse (庚午)

Item: On the first day of the first month (New Year's Day), the New Year's ceremonial rites were held at the御屋形 (the lord's residence).
　　Note: Due to the lord's young age, a simplified ceremony was conducted.

Item: On the same day, regarding the New Year's congratulatory gifts, due to the lord's young age, the messenger appointed was the Head of the御書院番 (shogunal guard unit), Sasaji Gonuemon Takatsune, who wore a ceremonial suo (素袍) robe, and following the precedent set by the previous lord, he presented to the Shogun one sword and one horse (substituted by ten ryō of gold). The Senior Councilor (老中) appeared in the second chamber (二之間) of the Great Hall (大広間) of Edo Castle, and in the third chamber (三之間), the official sword register (目録) was presented to the Sōjaban (奏者番, master of ceremonies), Honda Nagato-no-kami Tadahide.
