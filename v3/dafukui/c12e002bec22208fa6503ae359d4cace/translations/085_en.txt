　Item: As it was the private intention of Tanuma, Lord of Tonomo-no-Kami, that the second of the two copies be withheld, only the first copy was submitted to the Lord of Sagami-no-Kami.

Item: On the twenty-sixth day of the twelfth month, as the second-batch fresh cod — a regional product of the domain — could not be presented as an offering, a written notification was submitted to the duty Senior Councilor Sakai Uemon-no-Jō [Tadayori], through the agency of the official castle representative Yokota Sakudayū [Naomichi].

　　　As the fresh cod from this domain's territory was to be offered twice during the cold season, it was dispatched from the home domain on the nineteenth of this month; however, due to deep snow in the mountains, its progress was delayed, and although it arrived this evening, the fish had spoiled during the journey, and accordingly it was not presented. This notification is hereby conveyed through a messenger. Respectfully submitted.

　　　Twenty-sixth day of the twelfth month　　　　Matsudaira Ogimaru
