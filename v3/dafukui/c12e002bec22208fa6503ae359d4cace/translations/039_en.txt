　　　　　From this point forward, a letter was to be sent on every occasion of a visit to Tōei-zan, but as this became standard practice, it is not recorded further here.

Item: On the same day, at Manpuku-zan,
a tea offering ceremony (o-chanoyu) was conducted in commemoration of the hundredth memorial of Lord Taiyū-in (Tokugawa Iemitsu).

Item: On the 21st day of the fourth month, as a memorial service was held at Tōei-zan for the same reason, a messenger — the Guard Commander (go-shoin-bangashira) Nakane Yukinojo Shūkan — was dispatched in formal attire (noshime-nagabakama) to present five sheets of white silver as a condolence offering (kōden) to the head temple (go-honbō) at that location. This was in accordance with instructions issued by written document at the residence of Lord Hotta Sagami-no-kami on the 20th day of the eleventh month of the previous year.

Item: On the same day, as the aforementioned memorial service had concluded,
in order to inquire after the well-being of the Kubō-sama (the Shogun),
a messenger — the castle keeper (go-rusui) Tsuda Kyūuemon Morihiro — was dispatched to the duty official (go-yōban) Lord Sakai Uemon-no-jō Tadayori. This was in accordance with a notification issued on the 19th.

Item: On the 27th day of the same month, a notification from among the Senior Inspectors (ō-metsuke) was circulated through the castle keepers of Lord Matsudaira Aki-no-kami Yoshinaga and Lord Matsudaira Ōi-no-kami.

　　　Item: The Sentō [the retired sovereign residing at the Sentō Palace] had fallen ill and, being unable to recover, passed away on the 23rd of this month. In order to pay respects and inquire after the Imperial household's well-being, all [retainers] are to attend [the castle] on the 28th, tomorrow.
　　　　　Note: All are also to attend at the Nishi-no-maru (West Keep).
　　　Item: The regular monthly ceremonial salutations (tsuki-nami o-rei) are to be suspended.
　　　Item: Those who are ill, young, or retired are to send a messenger to the residence of the Senior Councilors serving as monthly duty officers — Oki-no-kami (Nishio Tadanao) and Tajima-no-kami (Akimoto Suzutomo).
