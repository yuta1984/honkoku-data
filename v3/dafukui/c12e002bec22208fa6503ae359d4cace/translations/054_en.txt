Item: On the same day, in connection with the same matter, as an expression of thanks from the inner household (ōnaisho), a female messenger (onna-tsukai) named Sakio was dispatched to the Hon-maru, and a female messenger named Yasoda was dispatched to the Nishi-maru.
　　Note: Regarding the dispatching of female messengers as an expression of thanks in connection with the aforementioned received gifts, these matters shall henceforth follow established precedent and will not be recorded here.

Item: On the 28th day of the 7th month, a circular letter (mawashijō) was received from the Senior Inspectors (Ōmetsuke), stating: "Among the footmen (kachi-no-mono) in the procession escorts and the manservants (chūgen) of poor conduct, there are those who have become particularly coarse and uncouth (kasatsu). Those who employ persons from rural areas (zaishō-mono) must ensure that such individuals are not in their service. Henceforth, this shall be strictly prohibited. Servants (hōkōnin) should also be informed of this matter accordingly." This circular notice was received from the resident agents (rusuī) of Matsudaira Aki-no-kami Yoshinaga and Matsudaira Ōi-no-kami (name not recorded).

Item: On the 1st day of the 8th month, for the celebratory occasion (shūgi), as the lord was still of a young age, the vanguard unit commander (osaki-monotō-gashira) Sakakibara Sukizaemon Narufusa was dispatched as messenger, wearing white formal robes and long hakama (hakama), following the precedent established by the previous lord:
　To the Kubō-sama: a single sword (tachi) and, in lieu of one horse, ten ryō in gold were presented. At the third chamber of the Grand Hall (Ōhiroma) of the castle, the Senior Councilors (rōjū) were present, and the Sōja-ban (chamberlain of reception) Naitō Yamato-no-kami Yoriyoshi presided over the formal announcement.
　To the Dainagon-sama: the same messenger presented the same gifts at the same location. The gifts and their inventory list (mokurok) were handed over to a retainer of the Sōja-ban Inoue Tōtōmi-no-kami Masaatsu.

Item: On the 6th day of the same month, through the messenger and resident agent (rusuī) Tsuda Kyūemon Morihiro, following the precedent of the previous lord:
　To the Kubō-sama: three locally produced first-catch cod (hatsutara) were presented. The Chamberlain (Sobashū) Miura Higo-no-kami (name not recorded) stated that the formal announcement would be made at a later time.
　To the Dainagon-sama: the same messenger presented the same gifts. The Chamberlain Mizuno Tamba-no-kami (name not recorded) stated that the formal announcement would be made at a later time.
