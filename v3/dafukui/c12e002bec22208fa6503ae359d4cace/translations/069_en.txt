The Senior Guards Captain (goshoinhangashira) Inaba Kaname (Masamitsu) was dispatched.

Item: On the same day, in connection with the marriage arrangement having been ordered, the nadai Dewa-no-Kami (Muneyuki)-sama proceeded to the residence of Owari-sama, where Matsudaira Nakatsukasa-no-Taifu (Yoshitoshi)-dono, Matsudaira Kambe-no-Kami (Katsunaga)-dono, and Matsudaira Danjō-no-Daihi (Katsumasa)-dono were waiting to receive him. He met in person with Chūnagon (Munekatsu)-sama and Chūjō (Munemichi)-sama, and after various hospitalities were offered, upon conclusion Dewa-no-Kami-sama withdrew.

From Chūnagon-sama, as a return courtesy visit to this residence, the chief retainer (karō) Takekoshi Yamashiro-no-Kami was dispatched; from Chūjō-sama, the guardian-tutor (ofuyaku) Takagi Hachirōzaemon was dispatched. They were guided to the reception room (omimaijo), where the business manager (oyōnin) appeared and received their verbal messages. They were then guided to the Third Room (San-no-Ma) of the Audience Chamber (shoin), where the chief retainer (okarō) and the middle elders (ochūrō) appeared and exchanged greetings, and hospitality was offered. Upon its conclusion, [the lord] took his seat in the upper dais room (jōdan-no-ma) of the Great Audience Chamber, with members of the same clan (godōsei) seated in row before the second alcove (Ni-no-toko), and Ōtawara Izumo-no-Kami-dono, Mori Tsushima-no-Kami-dono, and Ogasawara Iyo-no-Kami-dono seated in the adjoining corridor (ochinhoro). Yamashiro-no-Kami and Hachirōzaemon were summoned, and the middle elder Nakane Yumino (Shūkan) made the formal introductions. (As Chūnagon-sama's messenger: Takekoshi Yamashiro-no-Kami) The business manager Shimada Seizaemon (Shigeyuki) made the formal introductions. (As Chūjō-sama's messenger: Takagi Hachirōzaemon.) Upon conclusion, a direct reply (jikitō) was given, and as Yamashiro-no-Kami and Hachirōzaemon withdrew, the members of the same clan escorted them as far as the Second Room (Ni-no-Ma). The chief retainer, middle elders, and business managers saw them off as far as the mirror panel (kagamiita) of the entry vestibule (shikidai). As a further return courtesy to Owari-sama, the attendant unit captain (osoba monotogashira) Oka Saburōemon (Tsunetada) was dispatched as a messenger.

Item: On the 29th day of the 11th month, in connection with the same matter, the courier on foot (otomo hikyaku) Yano Sandayū (Masafusa) was dispatched to the home domain (okuni-moto) with a five-day journey. He departed from the Tokiwa-bashi residence this evening.
