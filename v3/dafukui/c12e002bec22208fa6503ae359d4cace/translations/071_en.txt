(Continued from previous page) As it was desired to present tribute offerings to the Three Honorable Residences through both formal and informal channels, prior consultations were held with Lord Hotta Sagami-no-kami and Lord Ōoka Izumo-no-kami, after which a written inquiry (oukagaisho) was submitted to the duty senior councillor (goyōban) on the 28th of last month. This written inquiry is recorded under the entry for the 6th day [of the following month].

Item: On the 6th day of the 12th month, as a summons was received from Senior Councillor (Rōjū) Honda Hōki-no-kami (Masayoshi), the domain's Edo residential representative (orusuii), Katō Chōuemon (Masatoshi), appeared before him. There, regarding the written inquiry submitted on the 28th of the previous month, instructions were conveyed by means of an attached note (otsukefuda).

　　　Written Inquiry (Oukagaisho)

　　　　　Memorandum

　　To the Kubō-sama:
　　　Two varieties, one load (ikka)

　　To the Ōgosho-sama:
　　　One variety, one load (ikka)

　　To the Dainagon-sama:
　　　One variety, one load (ikka)

　　On the occasion of the marriage arrangement (engumi) having been proclaimed, we humbly wish to present the above-stated offerings through a messenger, and respectfully submit this inquiry. Respectfully submitted.
