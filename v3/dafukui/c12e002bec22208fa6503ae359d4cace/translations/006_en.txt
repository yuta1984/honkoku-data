...to Izumo-no-kami Tadamitsu and Ogasawara Iwami-no-kami Masanori, a messenger-escort officer (o-tsukaiban) was dispatched as messenger.

Item: On the same day, on the occasion of the first snowfall, the domain's caretaker official (o-rusuī) Katō Chōuemon Masatoshi was dispatched to the castle as a messenger to inquire after the health and well-being of the Kubō-sama, the Ōgosho-sama, and the Dainagon-sama. At the Hon-maru (Main Keep), the inspector (metsuke) Mizuno Seizoku received the messenger, and at the Nishi-maru (Western Keep), the inspector (metsuke) Hagiwara Mondono-shō received the messenger.

　　Note: As snow had not fallen since the previous winter and it snowed for the first time today, a messenger was dispatched to inquire after their well-being.
　　Item: As the dispatch of the above messenger subsequently became standard practice, it will not be recorded hereafter.

Item: On the third day of the first month, on the occasion of the ceremonial New Year's first chanting of utai (o-utai-hajime), the domain's caretaker official (o-rusuī) Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi was dispatched as messenger, and, following the precedent set by the previous domain lord, two ceremonial cups (o-sakazuki-dai, o-kawarake) and one piece of silver as sake money were presented to the Kubō-sama.

Item: On the fourth day of the same month, as a congratulatory gesture marking the conclusion of the three-day New Year ceremonial formalities (sanganichi no go-kishiki), the caretaker official (o-rusuī) Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi was dispatched as messenger to: the Rōjū on duty (goyōban) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke, the Senior Councillor (Rōjū) attached to the Ōgosho-sama Nishio Oki-no-kami Tadanao, and the Senior Councillor (Rōjū) attached to the Dainagon-sama Akimoto Tajima-no-kami Tomoaki. Subsequently, the domain's Kyoto caretaker official (dōsho o-rusuī) Arakawa Sukeemon Katsushige was dispatched as messenger to the Kyoto Deputy (Kyōto Shoshidai) Matsudaira Bungo-no-kami Sukekuni.
