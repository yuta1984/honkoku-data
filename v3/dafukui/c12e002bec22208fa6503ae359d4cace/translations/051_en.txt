**[Continued from previous page: Instructions regarding the partial remission of borrowed rice for domain retainers]**

Regarding the matter of the borrowed rice (*shakumai*) owed by members of the domain household: the late lord [our predecessor] had repeatedly expressed his deep concern over this burden, and word has reached us that the retainers are indeed in a state of severe hardship. Accordingly, it has been decided that a portion of the outstanding borrowed rice shall be forgiven [i.e., its repayment waived]. Given the current state of domain finances, this kind of treatment would ordinarily be quite beyond what could be managed; nevertheless, in consideration of the late lord's profound wishes in this matter, and as this is also the beginning of the new lord's reign, it has been declared that — for all these reasons — retainers shall continue to observe frugality as before, and shall endeavor with ever-greater diligence and careful management to sustain the domain's affairs. Thus it is decreed. The foregoing is hereby communicated.

　　Note: At the Edo residence, the same instructions were conveyed on the twenty-eighth day of the same month by the chief retainer (*karō*) Ashida Zusho.
