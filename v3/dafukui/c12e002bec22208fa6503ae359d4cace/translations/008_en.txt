Item: On the eleventh day of the first month, regarding the completion of the transfer of the former entrusted territory (Oazukari-sho), a formal notification document (Otodoke-sho) was submitted to the Goyōban (Duty Magistrate) Hotta Sagami-no-kami (Masaaki) through the Rusuii (Edo Resident Representative) Yokota Sakudayū (Naomichi) as messenger.

　　　　As the entrusted territory (Azukari-chi) in Echizen Province has been relinquished, the transfer to the Daikan (Local Deputy Administrator) Naitō Jūuemon was completed on the twenty-third day of the twelfth month. We hereby respectfully notify you of this matter.
　　　　Respectfully submitted,
　　　　　Eleventh day of the first month　　　　　Matsudaira Ogimaru

Item: On the seventeenth day of the same month, at Sannō (Shrine), in accordance with the precedent of the previous lord, prayers for the safety and well-being of the Bakufu (public authority) were conducted. A messenger in the form of an Otsukaiban (Messenger Official) was dispatched, and ten pieces of silver were offered.

　　　Note: Although these prayers are conducted annually in the first, fifth, and ninth months, since this will henceforth become a regular established custom, it will not be recorded again.

Item: On the eighteenth day of the same month, as there was a private inquiry from the Yashiki-aratame (Residence Inspector) Suzuki Ihei to the attached official Nakajima Shōzō (Masahira), the following was sent from Shōzō in reply:

　　　　Within the Honjo Sunamura Shinden (reclaimed land):
　　　　　　Kakae-chi (privately held land)　　Tax-exempt land
　　　　　　　Twenty thousand tsubo
　　　The above land was purchased as water land (Mizuchi) from Sunamura Shinzaemon on the twenty-ninth day of the seventh month of Kanbun 3 (1663), the year of the Rabbit, and has been held in possession until the present time.
