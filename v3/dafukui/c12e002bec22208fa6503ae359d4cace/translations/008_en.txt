Item: On the eleventh day of the first month, as the handover of the formerly-administered domain lands had been completed, the caretaker official (o-rusuī) Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi was dispatched as messenger to the Duty Councillor (go-yōban) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masaaki, delivering the following written notification:

　　　　As the domain has been relieved of its administration of the entrusted lands (o-azukari-chi) in Echizen Province, we hereby notify you that the handover to the magistrate (o-daikan) Naitō Jūuemon was completed on the twenty-third day of the twelfth month. We respectfully submit this notification.
　　　　　Eleventh day of the first month　　　　　Matsudaira Oginomaru

Item: On the seventeenth day of the same month, as prayers for the safety and peace of the realm (kōgi go-anzen no go-kitō) were conducted at Sannō (Hie Shrine), following the precedent of the previous domain lord, ten pieces of silver were offered via a messenger dispatched through the domain's official messenger (o-tsukaiban).

　　Note: Although these prayers are conducted annually in the first, fifth, and ninth months, as this subsequently became standard practice, it will not be recorded hereafter.

Item: On the eighteenth day of the same month, as the residence inspector (yashiki-aratame) Suzuki Ihei made a private inquiry to the attached official (o-tsuki) Nakajima Shōzō Masahira, the following reply was sent from Shōzō:

　　　　Within the Honjō Sunamura Shinden:
　　　　　　Kakaechи (privately-held land)　　　Tax-exempt land
　　　　　　　20,000 tsubo
　　　The above land was purchased as wetland (mizuchi) from Sunamura Shinzaemon on the twenty-ninth day of the seventh month of the third year of Kanbun (1663), the Year of the Rabbit, and has been held in possession ever since.
