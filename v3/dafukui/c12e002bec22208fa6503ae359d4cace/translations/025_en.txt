One: 14th day of the Third Month

Regarding the matter of gifts to the ladies-in-waiting attending the Lord Great Counselor (Dainagon-sama) — whereas until last year gifts were presented on the occasion of visits to the Nishinomaru (Western Citadel), starting this year they are to be presented on the occasion of visits to the Hon-maru (Main Citadel). Notification of this was conveyed via messenger — the castle caretaker (o-rusuii) Katō Chōuemon Masatoshi — to the duty senior councillor (go-yōban) Matsudaira Ukon no Shōgen Takemoto, as well as to the Senior Councillor attached to the Lord Great Counselor, Akimoto Tajima no Kami Suzutomo.

Note: As prior private notice had been received from the senior ladies (o-toshiyori) to the effect that it was desired that, from this occasion onward, gifts to the ladies-in-waiting attending the Lord Great Counselor be presented at the time of visits to the Hon-maru, a private inquiry was made to Senior Councillor Hotta Sagami no Kami. Having received his assent, it was communicated that Lord Sagami no Kami had directed that notification be delivered to the duty senior councillor and to the Senior Councillors of the Nishinomaru.
