Hakone Checkpoint (Gosekisho)
　　　　　　　　To the Guard Officials

　　Note: Regarding travel permits (tegata) for passage through the Yokogawa Checkpoint, a seal (inkan) of the mansion administrator identical to the above was dispatched; however, the exact date is not known.

Item: On the last day of the 9th month, the duty magistrate (goyōban) Hotta Sagami-no-Kami (Masasuke)-dono summoned the resident representatives (rusuī) of Matsudaira Kaga-no-Kami-dono, Matsudaira Chikuzen-no-Kami-dono, and Matsudaira Daizenno-daibu-sama, and issued the following written directive, which was subsequently circulated to said resident representatives:

　　　　Written Directive

　　　　　Regarding letters that were previously sent by persons currently in a state of ritual impurity due to childbirth (san'e) or mourning (imchū):

　　　　　Until the mourning period has concluded, such correspondence is to be held back; regardless of the matter at hand, the resident representative (rusuī) is to notify the relevant parties that, due to the sender's state of ritual impurity, the letter will be submitted in due course.

　　　　　Note: Regarding urgent dispatches (chūshin-jō) and similar communications, those which, by established precedent, have been permitted to be sent even during periods of ritual impurity shall continue to be handled in the same manner as before.

Item: On the 4th day of the 10th month:
　As a celebration marking the completion of the 100th memorial service (hyakkaki) for Lord Taiyūin (the third Tokugawa Shogun, Iemitsu), a Noh performance was held. The resident representative Katō Chōzaemon (Masatoshi) was dispatched as envoy to present one box of fresh sea bream (senzai hitoori) as an offering. (Notice was issued on the 2nd of the preceding month.)
　The presentation was made at Hinoki-no-Ma (the Cypress Room) in the castle, with the shogunal announcer (sōjaban) Ōta Settsu-no-Kami (Suketoshi)-dono presiding over the formal reception.
