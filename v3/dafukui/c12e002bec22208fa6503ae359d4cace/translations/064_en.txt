The resident representative presented one box of locally produced salted sweetfish roe baskets (fifty in number) to the Kubō-sama (the Shogun). The senior chamberlain (sobashū) Kose Yamato-no-Kami (Toshihiro)-dono conveyed that the presentation would be formally received at a later time.

To the Dainagon-sama (the Senior Counselor / Lord of high court rank), the same envoy presented an identical gift. The senior chamberlain Mizuno Tamba-no-Kami-dono conveyed that the presentation would likewise be formally received at a later time.

Item: On the 15th day of the 10th month, as the Senior Elder (rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-Kami (Masasuke)-dono was to make a pilgrimage (tossan) to Nikkō the following spring, a horse was presented to him discreetly through the castle monk (gojōbōzu) Kitagawa Hokyūsen, along with a gift inventory (meiroku).

Item: On the 21st day of the same month, at Unshōji temple, the first anniversary memorial service (ichishūki hōji) for Tokushōin-sama was held from the evening of the 19th through the morning of the 21st (spanning two nights and three days). A similar service was also conducted at Tentokuji temple.

Item: On the 29th day of the same month, the household administrator (goyōnin) Daidōji Magokurō (Shigenori) called upon the senior chamberlain Ōoka Izumo-no-Kami (Tadamitsu)-dono and, presenting a formal marriage proposal document (odashifumi), entered into a private consultation. He was told that the proposal seemed quite favorable, and the document was retained by the chamberlain.

　　Note: The aforementioned formal proposal document is recorded in the entry for the 23rd day of the coming 11th month.

Item: On the 1st day of the 11th month, the following directive was issued and conveyed by the inspectors (metsuke) to the members of the guard units (obangumi) stationed at the Edo mansion:

　　　　　As has been repeatedly decreed previously, regarding clothing: even during guard duty or when serving as envoys, the use of plain, simple garments (sōfuku) is entirely permissible. Furthermore, on occasions of gatherings and meetings, matters should be conducted as simply as possible. This applies even when persons from other establishments attend such gatherings...
