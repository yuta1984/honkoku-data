**Record Concerning Memorial Tablets (Part 1)**
Having arranged the ritual vessels and conducted the ceremonies for making offerings, the rites for the enshrinement of the memorial tablet (霊牌) were performed. The memorial tablet was entrusted to Tenyō-in temple for perpetual safekeeping and protection.
Recorded with reverence, Summer, Fifth Month, Kan'en 3, Year of Kanoe-Uma (1750).

**Record Concerning Memorial Tablets (Part 2)**
Posthumous Buddhist name (法謚): Keiju-in-dono. She was the daughter of Lord Hirohashi Fujikimi and the consort of Lord Takayoshi (隆芳公). She gave birth to Lord Taian (大安公). She passed away on the seventeenth day of the eighth month of autumn, in the first year of Manji, Year of Tsuchinoe-Inu (1658), and was interred at Denzū-in Temple, north of Edo Castle. As Lord Takayoshi's memorial tablet was already enshrined at Zōjō-ji Temple, her tablet was placed alongside it. This year, Kanoe-Uma (1750), the memorial tablet was restored and enshrined in a miniature shrine cabinet (龕). Its guardianship was assigned to Tenyō-in.
Recorded with reverence, Summer, Fifth Month, Kan'en 3, Year of Kanoe-Uma (1750).

**Official Correspondence Records**
Item: On the twenty-fourth day of the fifth month, a letter of condolence inquiring after the wellbeing [of the relevant parties] was dispatched to the Imperial Liaison Officers (伝奏) — Lord Koga, Minister of the Right Udaishō Michie, and Lord Yanagiwara, Minister of the Great Counselor Mitsufusa — upon the occasion of the death of the Sentō [the Retired Emperor]. The messenger Azuma Shingorōemon Sukeharu was sent accompanied by the Kyoto caretaker (御留守居) Arakawa Sukeemon.

Item: On the last day of the same month, as a summons was issued to attend at the residence of the Imperial Liaison Officer Lord Yanagiwara, Minister of the Great Counselor Mitsufusa, the messenger Azuma Shingorōemon Sukeharu duly appeared, whereupon a written reply was handed over.

(Content of the reply)
Upon the demise of the Sentō [Retired Emperor], [we] humbly dispatched a messenger to inquire after your wellbeing and respectfully conveyed the contents of the written communication. Submitted with deepest reverence.
