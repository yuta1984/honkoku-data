**[Continuation of Memorial Tablet Records]**

The ritual vessels shall be prepared, offerings shall be presented, and the ceremony of enshrining the memorial tablet shall be conducted. The memorial tablet is to be entrusted to Ten'yōin Temple for perpetual care and preservation.
Recorded with reverence, Fifth Month of Kanen 3, the year Kanoe-Uma (1750).

The posthumous Buddhist title "Keijuin-dono" belonged to the daughter of the Hirohashi Fujiwara family and wife of Lord Ryūhō (Matsudaira Tadamasa). She gave birth to Lord Taian (Matsudaira Mitsumichi) and passed away on the seventeenth day of the Eighth Month of Manji 1 (1658), the year Tsuchinoe-Inu. She was interred at Dentsu'in Temple, north of Edo Castle. Since Lord Ryūhō's memorial tablet was already at Zōjōji Temple, hers was enshrined alongside it. In the present year Kanoe-Uma (Kanen 3, 1750), the memorial tablet was renovated and placed in a miniature shrine. Its care is to be entrusted to Ten'yōin Temple.
Recorded with reverence, Fifth Month of Kanen 3, the year Kanoe-Uma (1750).

**[Diplomatic and Ceremonial Records]**

One: On the twenty-fourth day of the Fifth Month, an inquiry letter expressing condolences was sent to Denso [imperial court liaisons] Lord Kuga-Udasho (Michie) and Lord Yanagiwara-Dainagon (Mitsutuna) regarding the demise of the Sentō [the retired Emperor]. The letter was dispatched via messenger Higashi Shingorōemon (Sukeharu), accompanied by Arakawa Sukeemon, the Kyoto Resident Official.

One: On the last day of the same month, upon being summoned to the residence of Denso Lord Yanagiwara-Dainagon (Mitsutuna), the messenger Higashi Shingorōemon (Sukeharu) presented himself and received a reply letter.

**[Contents of the Reply Letter]**
Regarding the demise of the Sentō, we have duly received and conveyed to the appropriate parties the contents of your letter, in which you dispatched a messenger to express your condolences. Respectfully submitted.
