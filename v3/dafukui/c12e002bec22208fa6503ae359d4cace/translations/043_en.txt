　　　　　　5th month

One entry: On the 13th day of the 5th month, through the messenger and caretaker (rusui) Tsuda Kūemon Morihiro, and following the precedent established by the previous domain lord, a box of thirty skewers of first-catch mackerel (hatsusaba), a local product of the domain, was presented to the Shogun. The Shogun's chamberlain (sobashū), Lord Mizuno Kawachi-no-kami Tadatomi, conveyed that the presentation would be duly reported at a later time.

　To the Lord Great Counsellor (Dainagon), the same person (Tsuda Kūemon Morihiro) was dispatched as messenger, and an identical presentation was made. The chamberlain (sobashū), Lord Mizuno Tamba-no-kami, conveyed that the presentation would be duly reported at a later time.

One entry: On the same day, regarding the repair of the stone walls surrounding the castle in the domain (Fukui), and the dredging of the castle moat, a written petition along with a map was submitted to the duty councillor (goyōban), Lord Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke, through the caretaker Tsuda Kūemon Morihiro.

(Contents of the petition)
　　　　　As one section of the stone walls of Fukui Castle in Echizen Province has in places collapsed and in places begun to bulge outward,
　　　　　we humbly request permission to rebuild the said walls, and also to dredge the silted-up moat, in accordance with the red-line markings on the accompanying map,
　　　　　and to carry out the construction works gradually, restoring them to their original condition. Respectfully submitted.
　　　　　　5th month　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Ogimaru
