Third Month　　　　　Retainer of Matsudaira Ogimaru
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Katō Chōuemon

Concerning Ogimaru's visit to the castle on the coming 15th:
　　One item: Boxed meals (bentō), under our management — three sets
　　One item: Hot water tub (yuboke) — one load
　　　　　　　Supervisor (sairyō) for the above: one person
　　　　　　　Porters (mochippu): eleven persons

Please be so good as to arrange that the above persons may pass through without hindrance, coming and going via the Hirakawaguchi, the Jōge Bairin o-kitte gate, and the o-hiroshiki (outer quarters). Respectfully submitted.

　　　　Third Month　　　　　Retainer of Matsudaira Ogimaru
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Katō Chōuemon

Note: Although a written notice of the above was submitted on the 25th of last month, as the date was officially announced only yesterday, this document is hereby resubmitted on this 7th day with the corrected details.

One item: On the occasion of the visit to the Ōoku (Great Interior) on the 13th day of the Third Month — whereas until now the senior ladies-in-waiting (otoshiyori) would accompany [Ogimaru's] palanquin as escorts as far as the o-hiroshiki, from this year there will be no such escort, and it has been indicated by way of private communication (go-naigi) that [Ogimaru] should ride in the palanquin only as far as the outside of the kitte gate within the Kami-Bairin.
