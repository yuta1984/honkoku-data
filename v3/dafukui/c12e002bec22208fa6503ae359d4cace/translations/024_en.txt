Third Month　　　　　Retainer of Matsudaira Ogimaru
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Katō Chōuemon

Regarding Ogimaru's visit to the castle on the coming 15th:

　　One: Bento (meal boxes) — management thereof:　three [units]
　　One: Hot water tubs (yu-oke):　　　　　　　　　one load
　　　　　Supervisor (sairyō):　　one person
　　　　　Porters (mochippu):　　eleven persons

Regarding the above, please be so good as to arrange that they may pass through without hindrance, going in and out via the Hirakawaguchi, the Jōge Bairin o-kitte gate, and through to the o-hiroshiki (outer quarters). Respectfully submitted.

　　　　Third Month　　　　　Retainer of Matsudaira Ogimaru
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Katō Chōuemon

Note: Although a written notice of the above was submitted on the 25th of last month, as the date was announced only yesterday, this notice is hereby resubmitted on this 7th day in the same form.

One: On the occasion of the visit to the Ōoku (inner chambers) on the 13th day of the Third Month — whereas until now the senior ladies (o-toshiyori) had accompanied [Her Ladyship] by palanquin as far as the o-hiroshiki, it has been privately communicated that, starting this year, as there will be no accompanying escorts, [Her Ladyship] is to ride by palanquin only as far as outside the kitte gate within the Upper Bairin. This private instruction was conveyed from the castle caretaker senior official (o-rusuigachi toshiyori), Tsuchiya Hyōbu no Shōyū Hidenao, to the head of the o-hiroshiki guard (o-hiroshiki-ban no kashira), Yokoyama…
