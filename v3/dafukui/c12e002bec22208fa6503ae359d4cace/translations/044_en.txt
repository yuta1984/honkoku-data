　　　　　As the aforementioned stone walls have in some places collapsed and in other places begun to bulge outward, we respectfully request that reconstruction works be ordered, and furthermore that dredging of the silted moat be carried out, in accordance with the red markings on the attached map, on a continuing basis to restore these to their original state.

　　　　　Respectfully submitted.
　　　　　　Kan'en 3rd year, Kanoe-uma [1750], Month and Day　　　　　Matsudaira Ogimaru　[Official Seal]

　　　　　Note: The preliminary map (shitaezu) referred to above had been previously submitted for Lord Sagami-no-kami's private review, and his instructions having been received, it was formally submitted on this day. Beneath the lord's name, his personal signature (kakihan) ought normally to appear; however, as the lord is still of young age, both his personal signature and official seal have not yet been formally established. It was therefore privately inquired of Lord Sagami-no-kami whether a submission without such a signature might be acceptable.
