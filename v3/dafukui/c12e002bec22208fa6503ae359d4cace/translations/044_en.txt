　Length: 25 ken 1 shaku; height: 2 ken; collapse and outward bulging have occurred.

One entry: Similarly, one section of the moat to the north of the outer enclosure (sotokuruwa), at a corner turn; width 14 ken, length 175 ken and a half; silted up.

One entry: One section of the moat from outside the Takegahana Gate of the outer enclosure to outside the Jizō-waki Gate, at a corner turn; width 7 ken, length 437 ken; silted up.

One entry: From outside the Jizō-waki Gate at the same location to the north of outside the Koumadashi Gate, a continuous corner-turn moat; width 10 ken, length 900 ken 3 shaku; silted up.

　As the aforementioned stone walls have in places collapsed and in places begun to bulge outward, we humbly request permission to rebuild them, and also to dredge the silted-up moats, in accordance with the red-line markings on the accompanying map, carrying out the construction works gradually and restoring them to their original condition.

　Respectfully submitted.
　　Kan'en 3rd year, Kanoe-uma [1750], month and day　　　　Matsudaira Ogimaru　[Official seal]

　　　Note: The preliminary sketch (draft map) mentioned above was submitted in advance to Lord Sagami-no-kami for his private review, whereupon he provided his guidance, and it is being formally submitted today. In the space below the name of the lord, there ought to be an official signature (kakihan/monogram), but as the lord is still of young age, neither a formal signature nor a seal has yet been established. A private inquiry was therefore made to Lord Sagami-no-kami as to whether submission without a seal or signature would be acceptable. His response was that, as the matter of petitioning for castle repairs is not a trivial affair, it should be submitted by means of a joint written petition (renmei no hōsho)...
