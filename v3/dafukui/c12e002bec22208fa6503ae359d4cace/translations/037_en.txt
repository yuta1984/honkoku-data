Accordingly, the steward(s) (go-yōnin), the commissioners (bugyō), and the resident officials (rusui) were to consult together and handle matters in such a way as to reduce expenditures for the benefit of the domain. Furthermore, regarding the fact that the senior guard-captain and steward Shimada Seizaemon (Shigeyoshi) and the personal attendant (o-soba) Inaba Kaname (Masaaki) also had a great many matters to attend to in their service to the lord's person, they were likewise commanded to serve as officials in charge of economization (go-kenkyaku go-yōkakari). This was communicated to them by the duty-month chief retainer (tsukiban karō) Ashida Zushō (Kenno).

　　Note: In the home domain as well, an order of economization was issued on the twenty-ninth day of last month.

Item: On the eleventh day of the fourth month, summoned from the Hitotsubashi residence, the chief retainers Ashida Zushō Kenno and Okabe Mikinosuke (Nusada), and the middle elder (chūrō) Akashi Nuido (Kifusa), presented themselves, whereupon His Lordship the Gyōbukyō (Munetada) granted them an audience in the reception room (o-za-no-ma), and conveyed the following intentions:

　　　　　Summary of His Lordship's Words:
　　　　　　As Yoshimaru is of a young age, and apart from matters relating to the Shogunate, you must attend with great care to matters of domain governance.

　The Hitotsubashi chief retainer relayed this communication on that occasion.

Item: On the thirteenth day of the same month, the steward Daidōji Magokurō (Shigeyoshi) presented himself at the Hitotsubashi residence, and received word from the steward Komiyama Risuke. It was conveyed that a written inquiry (o-ukagaisho) concerning the damaged sections of the stone walls around the castle in the home domain and the dredging of the castle moats, which had been submitted for the private review of the Gyōbukyō (Munetada) on the eleventh, had been reviewed, and since the Gyōbukyō had no particular objections or comments, it was commanded through the steward Komiyama Risuke that the written inquiry should be formally submitted to the duty commissioner (go-yōban).
