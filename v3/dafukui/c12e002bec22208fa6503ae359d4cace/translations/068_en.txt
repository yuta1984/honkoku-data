Item: On the 28th day of the 11th month, the representative (nadai) Dewa-no-Kami (Muneyuki)-sama proceeded to the castle (dressed in fusa-ginu small robes [fukusa-kosode] and formal hemp kamishimo). At the veranda of the White Audience Chamber (Shiroshoin), the Senior Councilors were assembled in attendance, and the duty elder Honda Hōki-no-Kami (Masayoshi)-dono delivered the following official notification:

　　　　It has been ordered that the daughter of Owari-dono
　　　　be given in marriage to Yoshimaru.

Upon the conclusion of the above, Dewa-no-Kami-sama proceeded directly to this residence (oyakata), and at the Great Audience Chamber (Ōshoin) met in person with [the lord], conveying the purport of the above pronouncement. After the celebratory offering of long abalone (oshinoshi) [a traditional betrothal gift] was completed, Dewa-no-Kami-sama changed into formal noshime robes and, as an expression of gratitude, made a round of visits (mawaridome) to the Senior Councilors, the Senior Councilors attached to the Ōgosho-sama and the Dainagon-sama, and dispatched the chief retainer (okaro) Ashida Zushо (Kenno) as a messenger to the Junior Councilors (wakadoshiyori) and the Junior Councilors attached to both [the Ōgosho and Dainagon]. To the lord's attendants (gosokushū), the audience-ceremony officials (gosōjaban), the Commissioner of Temples and Shrines (jisha-bugyō), the Edo resident deputies (orusui), the senior inspectors (ōmetsuke), and the town magistrates (machi-bugyō), messenger-officials (otsukai-ban) and senior guardsmen (ōgobanshi) were dispatched as messengers. Subsequently, the Edo resident deputy of that post, Yagi Kōriuemon (Mutsumasa), was dispatched as a messenger to the Kyoto Deputy (Kyoto Shoshidai), Matsudaira Bungo-no-Kami (Sukenori)-dono.

Item: On the same day, in connection with the above matter, a female messenger (onnazukai) was dispatched from the inner household (onaishō) [i.e., the ladies' quarters] to offer formal thanks to the Kubō-sama, the Ōgosho-sama, and the Dainagon-sama. (The courtesy call [oukagai] for today was completed.)

Item: On the same day, regarding the fact that the marriage arrangement had been announced to Owari Chūnagon (Munekatsu)-sama this morning by means of the shogunal messengers (kōshi) — Senior Councilor Matsudaira Ukon-no-Shōgen (Takemoto)-dono and Nishimaru Senior Councilor Akimoto Tajima-no-Kami (Suzutomo)-dono — Chūnagon-sama sent Ōbangashira (senior guard captain) Ueno Kōzaemon as a courtesy envoy, while Chūjō (Munemichi)-sama sent Sawai Sanzaemon of equivalent rank; the nadai Shikibu-no-Shōyū (Chikатeru)-sama received them. As a greeting in return for the above, a messenger was immediately dispatched to Owari-sama.
