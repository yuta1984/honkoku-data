Regarding the matter of performing the formal greeting (礼/rei) by proxy, the resident keeper (留守居/rusuii) Katō Chōuemon Masatoshi called upon Senior Councilor (老中/rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-Kami Masasuke to discreetly inquire about the proper protocol. He was informed that, given that the visit to the castle concerns a young lord still in his youth, it would not be necessary to send a proxy for the formal greeting; instead, a messenger should be dispatched to the Senior Councilors directly. As for the appropriate standing of the messenger, it was indicated that this should be determined in accordance with the standing of the family, and that proceeding accordingly would be proper. This communication was conveyed through the chamberlain (用人/yōnin) of Sagami-no-Kami.

Item: On the 13th day of the 12th month, as a New Year's greeting to the Shōgun (公方様/Kubō-sama) from the inner household (御内証/go-naishō), a female messenger was dispatched to present one Sannō amulet (山王御札守) and one box of dried sea bream (干鯛). This matter had been approved upon inquiry on the 2nd of the same month. On that occasion, the female messenger was granted gift items in return, and a letter of thanks for those received gifts was sent to the Senior Lady-in-Waiting (老女中/rōjochū) of the Main Keep (本丸/Honmaru).

Item: On the 15th day of the same month, Fukujirō Katafusa-sama made his first formal audience (御目見え/o-memie) at Itoigawa in Echigo Province. As a formal expression of gratitude for this, messengers—the resident keeper and an escort messenger—were dispatched to: the Duty Councilor (御用番/go-yōban) Matsudaira Ukon-shōgen Takemoto; the Senior Councilor assigned to the Retired Shōgun's household (大御所様御付御老中), Nishio Oki-no-Kami Tadanao; and the Senior Councilor assigned to the Dainagon's household (大納言様御付御老中), Akimoto Tajima Ryōchō.

Item: On the 18th day of the same month, in connection with the recent change of domain lord (御代替わり/go-daitagawari), the resident keeper Yokota Sakudayū Naomichi was dispatched with instructions to exchange the official clan seal certificate (判鑑/hankan) with Matsudaira Izu-no-Kami Nobunao, who was in charge of the Arai Checkpoint (新居御関所) in Tōtōmi Province, as set out in the document shown below.
