Regarding the matter of performing the formal salutation (o-rei) by proxy (o-nadai), the castle liaison officer (o-rusuigashira) Katō Chōuemon (Masatoshi) visited Senior Councilor (Rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-Kami (Masaaki) to discreetly inquire about the proper procedure. He was informed, through the Senior Councilor's attendant (yōnin), that since it was the case of a young lord making his castle visit, there was no need for a formal proxy salutation; rather, it would be sufficient to dispatch an envoy to the Senior Councilors, and that the appropriate rank and standing of such an envoy should be in accordance with the family's status — this was the response conveyed.

Item: On the 13th day of the 12th month, from the Inner Household (o-naishō), in observance of the New Year's greetings to the Shōgun (Kubō-sama), a female messenger was dispatched to present one Sannō protective talisman (o-fuda) and one box of dried sea bream (hoshidai). (This had been duly approved on the 2nd of the month.) On this occasion, a gift of appreciation (hairyōmono) was bestowed upon the female messenger, and a letter of thanks for said gift was sent to the Senior Ladies-in-Waiting of the Hon-maru.

Item: On the 15th of the same month, Fukujirō (Katafusa)-sama (of Itoigawa, Echigo Province) made his first formal audience (o-memie). To express gratitude for this occasion, envoys — the castle liaison officer's deputy (o-rusuigashira-kai) and the messenger officer (o-tsukaiban) — were dispatched to: the Duty Senior Councilor (Goyōban) Matsudaira Ukon-shōgen (Takemoto); the Senior Councilor attached to the Ōgosho-sama (Retired Shōgun), Nishio Oki-no-Kami (Tadanao); and the Senior Councilor attached to the Dainagon-sama, Akimoto Tajima (Suzutomo).
