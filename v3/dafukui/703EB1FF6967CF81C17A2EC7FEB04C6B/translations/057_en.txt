One entry: On the 22nd day of the 3rd month, in celebration of the lord's appointment to office, members of the lord's extended family (go-ichimon) and retainers of the hatamoto class were invited and entertained.

One entry: On the 25th day of the 3rd month, for the same reason, hatamoto retainers who had regular dealings with the domain were invited and entertained.

One entry: On the same day, as the lord's residence grounds were found to be too cramped, the lord wished to receive an additional grant of adjacent land. To this end, the following message was conveyed through the intermediary of the keeper of the residence (rusuiyaku), Ōzeki Shingoзаemon (Naomi), to the Senior Councilor (Rōjū), Lord Inoue Kawachi-no-kami (Masamine):

　　　　Oral Statement

　　My residential estate has become too cramped, causing considerable inconvenience. I have heard that on this occasion, the residence of Matsudaira Kai-no-kami is to be exchanged with that of Sakai Saemon-no-suke. Therefore, regarding the congested area at the front of the estate, I humbly request that some additional land be granted to me as part of this redistribution arrangement. Should this be difficult to arrange, I would also be content if the land could be made available as a loan (azukarichi). I must emphasize that this is not an urgent request. As I felt at ease raising this matter, I wished to discuss it in a private and informal manner. I humbly request your favorable consideration.

　　　　　　3rd Month　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Iyo-no-kami

One entry: On the 26th day of the 3rd month, the lord paid a visit to Senior Councilor Inoue Kawachi-no-kami (Masamine) and performed his duties of courtesy.

One entry: On the 29th day of the 3rd month, due to an earthquake, a messenger was dispatched to the duty councilor (go-yōban) and the lord's personal chamberlains (go-soba go-yōnin) to inquire after the Shogun's wellbeing.
