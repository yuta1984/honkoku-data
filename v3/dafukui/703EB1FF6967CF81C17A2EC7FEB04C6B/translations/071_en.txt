...Regarding the inspection of the [Fukagawa] estate, we plan to submit notification to the relevant officials and to Lord Ina Hanzaemon on the 22nd of this month. As we were instructed to inform each of you of this matter in advance, we have come to report it to you. We humbly request that you raise this matter appropriately at your next meeting. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Resident Representative of Matsudaira Iyo-no-kami,
　　　　21st day of the 8th month　　　Ōzeki Shingorōzaemon

Item, 3rd day of the 9th month: Concerning the inspection of the Fukagawa estate, word came from Lord Iida Shirōzaemon, and the resident representative Ōzeki Shingorōzaemon (Naomi) appeared before him. He was informed that, regarding the Fukagawa estate about which notification had previously been submitted, inquiry had been made to the duty senior councilor (goyōban), and the instruction received was that the matter should proceed as the domain sees fit.

Item, 4th day of the 9th month: As a celebration gift for the Chōyō Festival (9th day of the 9th month), a messenger was sent presenting, as customary, a kosode robe and silver as offerings.

Item, 6th day of the 9th month: The private estate (kakae-yashiki) in Fukagawa was formally transferred to Lord Matsudaira Iga-no-kami.

Item, 7th day of the 9th month: Via written correspondence, the season's first cod (hatsutara) from the domain's local products were presented, as is customary.

Item, 15th day of the 9th month: From Lord Karasuma Uchūben (Mitsuyoshi [Kōei]), to celebrate the successful arrangement of the recent marriage, a messenger in the form of the domain agent (zōshō) Araki Daigaku was sent bearing the following gifts: a ceremonial sword, gold in lieu of a horse, one box of kelp (konbu), one box of dried sea bream (hoshidai), and one cask of sake (otaru). From Lady Yōrin'in: one box of kelp, one box of dried squid (surume), and one thousand hiki in lieu of a cask of sake. To Lady Umehime: two rolls of sha-aya silk fabric.
