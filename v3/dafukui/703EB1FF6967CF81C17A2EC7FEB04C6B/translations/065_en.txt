Note: Although it was arranged to request prayers at Nezu Shōsen'in, the exact date is unknown.

1. On the 25th day of the 6th month: An inner letter (go-naisho) from the Shogun was delivered at the castle, in recognition of the domain's offering presented for the Boys' Festival (Tango) celebration. Gifts bestowed upon the retainers followed precedent.

　　　　"As a celebration of the Boys' Festival, five
　　　　items of summer garments (katabira and hitoe)
　　　　have arrived; we are most pleased to receive them.
　　　　The details shall be conveyed by Matsudaira
　　　　Kii-no-kami."
　　　　　　2nd day of the 5th month — [Shogunal] Black Seal
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Fukui Shōshō-dono

　　　　Note: As the inner letter issued in connection with this celebratory offering has been the same on subsequent occasions, it will not be recorded hereafter.

2. On the 28th day of the 6th month: As it was the height of summer, following precedent, the lord sent a letter together with locally produced sōmen (thin wheat noodles) and dried sea bream as offerings.

3. On the 3rd day of the 7th month: The envoy sent to inquire after the Shogun's health during the summer heat, Kamisangawa Tahē, arrived in Edo and called upon the duty official (go-yōban), Inoue Kawachi-no-kami (Masamine), delivering the lord's letter.

4. On the 4th day of the 7th month: As Lady Toyohime was soon to depart for Kyoto, she invited the lord to her chambers as a farewell, where a banquet was held in his honor.

5. On the 6th day of the 7th month: As a congratulatory gift for the Namazakami celebration, an envoy was dispatched presenting gold as per precedent…
