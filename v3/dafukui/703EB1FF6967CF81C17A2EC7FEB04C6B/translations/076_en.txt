　　　　　Tenth month of the Year of the Ram [1715]

Item: On the 25th day of the tenth month, regarding the presentation of congratulatory gifts for the Chrysanthemum Festival (Chōyō), an inner letter (o-naibumi) was delivered at the castle, and the gifts bestowed upon retainers were as per established custom.

　　In celebration of the Chrysanthemum Festival,
　　five kosode robes have arrived; we are most pleased.
　　The details shall be conveyed by Toda
　　Yamashiro-no-kami.
　　　　　Seventh day of the ninth month, [bearing the] black seal
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　To the Lord of Fukui, Junior Governor

　　　Note: As the inner letters (o-naibumi) regarding subsequent such congratulatory presentations follow the same form, they will not be recorded hereafter.

Item: On the first day of the eleventh month, sumo wrestling was ordered to be held at the Kawabata teahouse. From the senior retainers (kōchi) downward — including the ban-gashira (guard commanders), yoriai-bangai (irregular attendants), shomotsu-gashira (various unit heads), o-tsukaiban (messengers/inspectors), as well as their children and the official domain physicians — all were commanded to attend as spectators.

Item: On the fourth day of the eleventh month, in order to inquire about the timing of the upcoming sankin (alternate attendance) journey in the coming Year of the Monkey, a messenger — Ōtani [name] — was dispatched (to Edo).
