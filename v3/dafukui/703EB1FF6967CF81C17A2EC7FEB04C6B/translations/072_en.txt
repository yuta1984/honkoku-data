In the Mujina-no-ma (Badger Chamber) of the Main Keep (Hon-maru), Daigaku (Araki Daigaku) was granted an audience (omemie), and afterward was served a meal in the Teppō-no-ma (Musket Chamber).

Item, 17th day of the 9th month:
　　The Shōgun (Kubō-sama) had been indisposed (御不例), but upon his recovery (御快然), and his having appeared in the outer quarters (omote) on the 6th of this month, a messenger was dispatched to Edo to express the domain's congratulations on this happy occasion. The envoy appointed was the Senior Guard Commander (osaki-monogashira), Honda Jūrōbei (Shigeyasu), who departed Fukui today. (Word of the Shōgun's appearance on the 6th had been communicated via notification from the Senior Inspector [ōmetsuke].)

Item, 21st day of the 9th month: Word came from Senior Inspector (ōmetsuke) Yokota Bichū-no-kami (Yoshimatsu), and the resident representative Ōzeki Shingorōzaemon (Naomi) appeared before him. He was informed that he should make a copy of the following written notice (okaki-tsuke), which had been passed down from the duty senior councilor (goyōban) Lord Kuze Yamato-no-kami (Shigeyuki):

　　　　It has come to our attention that in various localities in recent times,
　　　　a practice called "mikasatsuke" has become widespread.
　　　　As this is equivalent to gambling, it shall be
　　　　strictly prohibited in both shogunal and private domains.
　　　　In general, regardless of the circumstances, should anyone
　　　　be found to be organizing or facilitating such activities,
　　　　the ringleaders as well as those who assist them shall be
　　　　apprehended immediately and the matter reported without delay.
　　　　　　　　　　　　9th month, year of the Ram (Hitsuji)
