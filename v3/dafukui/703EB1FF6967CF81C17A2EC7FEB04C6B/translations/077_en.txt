Item: (On the fourth day of the eleventh month,) Gizaemon (Kōcho) departed from Fukui today, having been dispatched to Edo.

Item: On the fifth day of the eleventh month, sumo wrestling was ordered to be held at the Kawabata teahouse. All were commanded to attend as spectators, from the o-shoinban (inner guard), o-goban (great guard), and shinban (new guard), along with their children, down to the yoriki (mounted retainers), and all other officials and their attendants.

Item: On the fifteenth day of the eleventh month, the messenger sent to inquire about the timing of the lord's sankin (alternate attendance) journey, Ōtani Gizaemon (Kōcho), arrived in Edo and presented himself to the rōjū on duty (go-yōban), Abe Bungo-no-kami (Masataka), accompanied by the domain's Edo resident agent (o-rusui), Miyakita Chōzaemon, and submitted the written inquiry.

Item: On the eighteenth day of the eleventh month, having been summoned by the rōjū on duty, Abe Bungo-no-kami (Masataka), the messenger Ōtani Gizaemon (Kōcho) presented himself and received an official directive (o-bōsho) stating that [the lord] should proceed to make his attendance at Edo (go-sanpu) during the fourth month of the coming year.

Item: On the second day of the twelfth month, a communication arrived from the Commissioner of Finance (o-kanjō bugyō), Mizuno Hōki-no-kami, and the domain's Edo resident agent (o-rusui) presented himself, whereupon a written directive was delivered along with a draft document, the contents of which are as follows:

　　Furthermore, if there are no sake breweries [in a given area], a written statement to that effect must also be submitted. The above.

　　Regarding the matter of sake-brewing rice that was recently ordered [to be investigated]: for those breweries found to exist within your domain's territory, conduct a thorough investigation and record full details in a ledger in accordance with the draft document enclosed herewith, and submit [said ledger] to the Finance Office (o-kanjō-sho) located behind the Ōte Guard Station no later than during the first month of the coming year. The above.

　　　Twelfth month　　　　　　　Ōkubo Ōsumi-no-kami
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Mizuno Inaba-no-kami
