Item, 29th day of the 7th month:
Due to the Shogun's illness, prayers were requested at Kanri-in temple.

Item, same day: For the same reason as above, the resident representative Ōzeki Shingorōzaemon (Naomi) appeared before the duty senior councilor Inoue Kawachi-no-kami (Masamine) to inform him that the wedding procession (go-nyūkoshi) of Lady Toyohime had been postponed.

Item, 1st day of the 8th month: As a celebratory greeting, a ceremonial sword and horse-money offering were presented through a messenger, as per custom.

Item, 2nd day of the 8th month: Called upon by the duty senior councilor Inoue Kawachi-no-kami (Masamine), the resident representative Ōzeki Shingorōzaemon (Naomi) appeared before him. He was informed through an attendant (yōnin) that, while the postponement of Lady Toyohime's marriage had previously been communicated due to the Shogun's illness, since the Shogun had continued to recover, the marriage could proceed at the domain's convenience.

Item, 3rd day of the 8th month: Lady Toyohime arrived in Kyoto and entered the household of Umarusan'i (Karasumaru) Udaiben (Mitsuyoshi), completing the marriage ceremony.

Item, 15th day of the 8th month: Through the resident representative Ōzeki Shingorōzaemon (Naomi) as messenger, the duty senior councilor Abe Bungo-no-kami (Masataka) was formally notified that Lady Toyohime had departed from the home domain on the 28th of last month (7th month), arrived in Kyoto on the 3rd of this month, and that the marriage had been completed.

Item, same day: Regarding the same matter, the resident representative Ōzeki Shingorōzaemon (Naomi) appeared before duty senior councilor Abe Bungo-no-kami (Masataka) carrying a written oral statement (kōjōgaki) as follows, and was instructed to submit it as an official letter (o-hirefuda):

　　"The niece of the Lord of Iyo-no-kami arrived in Kyoto on the 3rd of this month and on that same day entered the household of Udaiben Karasumaru..."
