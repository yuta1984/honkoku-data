Item: A townhouse property belonging to our retainer — frontage: 7 shaku 8 sun; depth: 19 ken — totaling 40 tsubo, 5 gō, 3 shaku.
　　(Located in Tōrishiomachi, under the jurisdiction of the town magistrate [machi-bugyō].)
　　　　Additionally, 41 tsubo 7 gō 3 shaku facing the riverside embankment (mukōgashi), with a frontage of 16 ken 4 shaku 5 sun and a depth of 2 ken and a half. Note: In accordance with the conditions of the surrounding lots, this portion has been converted into warehouse land (kurachi) by petition.

Item: A townhouse property belonging to our retainer — frontage: 4 ken 2 shaku 7 sun; depth: 19 ken 3 shaku 6 sun — totaling 86 tsubo 3 gō.
　　(Located in Nagatomicho, under the jurisdiction of the town magistrate.)
　　　　Note: This property is part of the Tōrishiomachi townhouse plot that has become a broad avenue (hirōkōji). A substitute plot (daichi) has been granted in its place.

　　The above two properties have been in the possession of our retainer Tashiro Dōhaku.

The above two townhouse properties, as recorded by the household of Inaba Iyono-kami (Masamichi), were desired by a townsman by the name of Ichiemon of Kubomachi, and have accordingly been transferred to him. This matter was reported to Lord Kuze Yamato-no-kami, and the transfer was completed on the 7th of this month. As Iyono-kami is currently residing in his home domain (zaishō) and the formal notification (otodoke) has consequently been delayed, I am writing in advance on his behalf to inform you. The formal notification from Iyono-kami's household will be exchanged with this letter in due course. Respectfully submitted.
