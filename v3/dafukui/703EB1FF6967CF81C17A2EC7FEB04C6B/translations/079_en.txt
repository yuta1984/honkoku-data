Item: On the fourteenth day of the twelfth month, the following written order was conveyed by the Senior Councilor (Rōjū), Lord Toda Yamashiro-no-kami (Tadamasa):

　　　A circular letter arrived from the Grand Inspectors (Ōmetsuke):

　　　　　It has come to our attention that, at the joint (yoriai) street-watch
　　　　　posts (tsuji-bansho), persons of low standing are being
　　　　　stationed in addition to the regular guards, or
　　　　　that gatherings of people are being held there.
　　　　　Henceforth, should such conduct occur and
　　　　　a transgression result, strict and severe
　　　　　punishment shall be meted out.
　　　　　　　　Twelfth month of the Year of the Ram [1715]

Item: On the nineteenth day of the twelfth month, on account of the cold season, a letter was sent and fresh cod (ikitara), a local domain product (the first presentation), was offered as customary.

Item: On the twenty-first day of the twelfth month, as a year-end congratulatory gift, a messenger was sent and a kosode (short-sleeved kimono) was presented as customary, along with an offering of silver.

Item: On the twenty-second day of the twelfth month, on account of the cold season, a letter was sent and fresh cod (ikitara), a local domain product (the second presentation), was offered as customary.

Item: On the same day, Kōsaka Buemon (Kōri), who had been dispatched as a messenger to inquire after [the Shogun's] well-being during the cold season, arrived in Edo and appeared before the Duty Councilor (Goyōban), Lord Toda Yamashiro-no-kami (Tadamasa), to whom he presented the letter.

　　　　Note: On the following day, the twenty-third, Buemon proceeded to the castle and duly delivered the tribute offerings.
