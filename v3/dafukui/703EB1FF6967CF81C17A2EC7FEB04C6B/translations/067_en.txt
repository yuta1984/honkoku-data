Note: Messengers were dispatched daily up until the 17th. From the 18th through the 4th day of the 8th month, messengers were dispatched every other day, but these are not recorded in detail.

1. On the 16th day of the 7th month: In connection with the same matter (the Shogun's illness), prayers were requested at Nezu Shōsen'in.
　　　　Note: As the ceremony was to be conducted by the Three Senior Tokugawa Houses (Go-sanke), the request was directed to them.

2. On the same day: In connection with the same matter, the domain's resident official (o-rusui), Murakami Sandayū (Masamine), called upon the duty official (go-yōban) and the Shogun's personal chamberlains (o-soba go-yōnin) to inquire after his condition. The chamberlain relayed that the Shogun's condition was gradually improving.

3. On the 17th day of the 7th month: In connection with the same matter, the resident official Ōseki Shingozaemon (Naomi) called upon the same offices to make inquiry, and received the same report.

4. On the 19th day of the 7th month, at the hour of five and a half (approximately 9 a.m.): Lady Toyohime departed from Fukui on her way to wed Lord Karasuma Uchuben (Mitsue). The procession and its accoutrements were as follows:
　　Lead escort: Negoro Seudayū (Harufusa)
　　Ceremonial spears (2), carrying boxes (2), a halberd, rear boxes (2), tea lunch box, water bucket, tea bowl box, confectionery lunch box (one load), change of clothing, palanquin (o-koshi)
　　Rear escort: Ogino Magonoemon (Masafū)

5. On the 20th day of the 7th month:
　　As word arrived from Edo that the Shogun remained ill, Lady Toyohime turned back from her lodging at Imajoand returned home.

6. On the 21st day of the 7th month: In connection with the same matter, in order to report the situation, a messenger — Honda Takebei (Takemasa) — was dispatched to the Kyoto Deputy (Kyōto Shoshidai), Lord Mizuno Izumi-no-kami (Tadayuki). Accordingly, on this day a foot courier (o-kachi hikyaku), Asakura Genzaemon, was sent to Kyoto…
