(Continued from the entry of the 28th day of the 9th month on the previous page)

　　　　Honda Jūrōbei (Shigeyasu), dispatched as a congratulatory envoy upon the Shōgun's (Kubō-sama's) recovery from illness, arrived in Edo and appeared before the duty senior councilor (goyōban), accompanied by the resident representative (rusui) Ōzeki Shingorōzaemon. The letter (gosho) was duly presented.

Item, 29th day of the 9th month, at the general attendance (sō-shusshi):
　　It was decreed that Princess Yasonomiya (an Imperial princess of the Retired Emperor's household) would be wed to the Shōgun (i.e., enter his household in marriage).

Item, 5th day of the 10th month: Word came from Lord Nakayama Izumo-no-kami, and the resident representative Miyanokita Chōzaemon appeared before him. He was informed by written notice that the Sanjūsangendō hall in Edo, which had been destroyed in a fire some years prior, was now to be rebuilt by order of the authorities; that the shogunate (kōgi) would also contribute; and that lords of ten thousand koku and above were likewise expected to make monetary contributions.

　　　　Note: Our domain (the Fukui domain) is expected to contribute fifty ryō.

Item, 10th month (date unknown): Lady Toyohime passed away (御袖を留められた).

Item, 11th day of the 10th month: As a congratulatory envoy for the recently announced marriage of Princess Yasonomiya to the Shōgun, Misawa Jinzaemon (Tomihisa) was dispatched to Edo. He departed Fukui today.

Item, 22nd day of the 10th month: As Tashiro Dōhaku (Yōkei) was to sell his townhouse property (machiyashiki) in Edo, the following written memorandum was submitted by the senior retainer (karō) Inaba Uneme (Masamichi) to the construction magistrate (ofushin bugyō), Lord Shimada Sado-no-kami (Masanobu):
