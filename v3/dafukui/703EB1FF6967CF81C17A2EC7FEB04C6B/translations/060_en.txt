Item: On the same day, [the lord's] caretaker official (rusuii), Murakami Sandayū (Masamine), was summoned by the Duty Senior Councilor Kuze Yamato-no-kami (Shigeyuki) and appeared before him, whereupon the following written notice was conveyed:

　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Iyo-no-kami

As the residential estate is cramped, from the remaining land of Matsudaira Kai-no-kami's surrendered estate designated for official use, a plot of over 860 tsubo on the rear side of the estate shall be additionally granted. For further details, please consult with the Construction Magistrate (Fushin-bugyō).

Item: On the same day, as an expression of gratitude for the above, [the lord] made the rounds to the Senior Councilors and Chamberlains in person, and dispatched messenger officials (otsukai-ban) as envoys to the Junior Councilors.

Item: On the nineteenth day of the fourth month, the granted horse (a bay, five years old) was led [to the residence] by a messenger sent from the Duty Senior Councilor Kuze Yamato-no-kami (Shigeyuki).

Item: On the same day, [the lord] paid a formal courtesy visit to the Duty Senior Councilor Kuze Yamato-no-kami (Shigeyuki).

Item: On the twenty-first day of the fourth month, to celebrate the successful conclusion of the memorial ceremony at Nikkōzan, [the lord] dispatched his caretaker official (rusui), Murakami Sandayū (Masamine), as an envoy, presenting one box of dried sea bream (hoshidai), one box of dried squid (surume), and one cask of sake (otaru). (The written notice for this had been conveyed on the sixth day of the previous month.)
