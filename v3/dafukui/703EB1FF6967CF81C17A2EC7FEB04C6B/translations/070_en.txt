Regarding the matter of gifts to be presented when offering thanks for this, we had wished to make preparations and inquired of Lord Inoue Kawachi-no-kami last month (the 7th month), whereupon we received instruction that, as she is a niece, it is not necessary to offer formal thanks, and moreover that gifts are also unnecessary. Item, 21st day of the 8th month: Concerning the transfer of the private estate (kakae-yashiki) in Fukagawa to Lord Matsudaira Iga-no-kami on this occasion, the resident representative Ōzeki Shingorōzaemon (Naomi) appeared before duty senior councilor Abe Bungo-no-kami (Masataka) and submitted the following written statement:

　　　　　　Within the reclaimed coastal lands of Fukagawa (Fukagawa kaibe shinden), there is a private estate (kakae-yashiki) within the territory administered by Lord Ina Hanzaemon. This estate had previously been the private estate of Lord Makino Inaba-no-kami, and after mutual agreement, it was acquired from him some years ago. Regarding this private estate, we have now made a request to transfer it to Lord Matsudaira Iga-no-kami, having discussed the matter privately in advance. [Regarding the Fukagawa estate...]
