【List of gifts to be presented upon visiting the Ōoku (inner chambers of Edo Castle)】

To His Lordship the Shōgun (Kubō-sama):
　　　One ceremonial sword and horse-substitute gold coin
　　　Five rolls of silk/scroll

To His Lordship the Dainagon (Great Counselor):
　　　One ceremonial sword and horse-substitute gold coin
　　　Three rolls of silk/scroll

To Her Ladyship the Misuchū (the Dainagon's consort):
　　　Three rolls of silk/scroll
　　　One box of dried sea bream

The above items are to be presented from the front (omote) side of the castle through the accompanying retainers.
As for the gifts to the Dainagon and his consort, these too are to be presented to the Hon-maru (main keep).
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Total: 4 items
