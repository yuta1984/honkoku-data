A carpenter builds houses, and a plasterer coats walls. For a house, the pillars are first erected, and then the roof is thatched/tiled; for a wall, the base layer is first prepared, and then the mud/plaster is applied. Eaves are attached to the roof, and a finish coat is applied over the base coat. Shelves and closets are fitted, and tatami and sliding fixtures are installed.
