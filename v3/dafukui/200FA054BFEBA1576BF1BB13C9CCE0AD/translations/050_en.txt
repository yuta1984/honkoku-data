○ For volume measurements (masuме): ten *sai* make one *shaku*, ○ ten *shaku* make one *gō*, ○ ten *gō* make one *shō*, ○ ten *shō* make one *to*, ○ and ten *to* make one *koku*. ○ For land division (jiwari): a square of six *shaku* on each side is called one *tsubo*, also known as one *bu*. ○ Thirty *bu* make one *se*, ten *se* make one *tan*, and ten *tan* make one *chō*. For road distance (michinori): sixty *ken* make one *chō*, and thirty-six *chō* make one *ri*.
