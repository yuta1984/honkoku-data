Regarding the adjudication of lawsuits, light punishments such as handcuffing as an immediate penalty are acceptable as they are, but cases involving capital punishment should be reported to the Finance Office. This was communicated by His Lordship Izumi-no-kami.
However, regarding this matter:
Based on the legal codes established by Lord Hideyasu, and the punishments ordered since the time of Lord Tadamasa, punishments shall be administered accordingly, and greetings were exchanged to this effect.

1. On the 16th day of the 6th month, regarding the orders recently issued to the domain territories, letters were sent to Honda Ōkura (Naganori) and other senior retainers (karō) and castle stewards (jōdai).

1. On the 18th day of the same month, as it was the height of summer, in accordance with custom, sōmen noodles and dried sea bream were presented as gifts via a messenger.

1. On the 19th day of the same month, the administrator Kanie Gyōbu-emon (Shigutaka), the resident representative Miyakita Jūrōzaemon (Sadayoshi), and the household finance officer Hatanaka Gihee (Kagahide) appeared at the Finance Office at the main gate (Ōte). There (as had been communicated the previous day, the 18th, by the Finance Office group leader and inspector), Finance Office group leaders Kimura Shirōbee and Koide Kahee were seated in attendance, and the village registry for the domain territories was handed over.

1. On the 21st day of the same month, as it was the height of summer, an official visit was made to the duty official (goyōban) Lord Toda Yamashiro-no-kami (Tadamasa) to inquire after his health.
