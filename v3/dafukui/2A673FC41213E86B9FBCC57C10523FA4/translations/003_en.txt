Kyōhō 5, Year of the Metal Rat (1720)

Item: On the first day of the first month (New Year's Day), the New Year's ceremonial observances were held at the Main Keep (Hon-maru).

Item: On the same day, as a New Year's congratulatory gift, Inagaki Yasaemon (Shigenobu), of the yoriai rank, was dispatched as an envoy, and, as per custom, a ceremonial sword (odachi) and a sum of money in lieu of a horse (umashiro) were presented.
　　Note: Yasaemon departed from Fukui on the fifth day of the twelfth month of the previous year.

Item: On the third day of the same month, as a congratulatory gift for the first noh chanting of the New Year (utaizone), an envoy was dispatched and, as per custom, a sake cup stand (sakazukidai) and a sum of money for sake (sakeshiro) were presented.

Item: On the seventh day of the same month, as a congratulatory gift for the Young Greens ceremony (wakana), an envoy was dispatched and, as per custom, sea bream (tai) was presented as a ceremonial dish.
