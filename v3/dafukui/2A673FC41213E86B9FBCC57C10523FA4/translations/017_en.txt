Item: On the same day, regarding the same matter, the resident deputy Ōseki Shingorōzaemon (Naomi) brought and submitted a written statement as follows:

　　　　　　　　　Memorandum of Oral Statement

　　　　　　The Kogitsune sword that was previously submitted has been inspected, and we have now received it back upon its return.

Item: Regarding the Anaga Shrine in Echizen Province — namely, the history and traditional records of its establishment through the enshrining of the Kasuga deity (Kasuga Myōjin), whether there is any particular reason for devotion to this shrine by anyone of the Fujiwara clan among the court nobles or samurai families, and whether any objects or items had been sent from Daitō-an, a sub-temple of Kenninji in Kyoto, to the said shrine — none of these matters are known to us here [in Edo].

Item: As the said shrine is located within Anaga Village, the local people have come to refer to it customarily as the Anaga Shrine.
