There was also a gracious conveyance of the Shōgun's will (jōi) from the Dainagon-sama [the Lord of Owari].
　　　Note: The procedures for handling the above special envoy (jōshi) are unclear.

In gratitude for the above, [the lord] proceeded to the Main Citadel (Hon-maru) and expressed his thanks in the Sakura Room (Sakura-no-ma), where he was received by the Announcements Official (o-sōja-ban) Doi Noto-no-kami [personal name: Toshisada]. This concluded, he then proceeded to the Western Citadel (Nishi-maru) and expressed his thanks in the Sakura Room, where he was received by the Announcements Official Doi Ōi-no-kami [personal name: Toshinori (利里)]. Following this, he made courtesy visits (o-mawari-kin) to the Senior Councillors (go-rōjū) and the Personal Attendant Officials (o-soba go-yōnin) of both citadels.

One: On the same day, regarding the same matter, a female messenger (onna-tsukai) was sent as a formal expression of gratitude from the Inner Chambers (go-naishō).

One: On the same day, regarding the same matter, letters were sent to Hon'da Kuranosuke [personal name: Fuku-Shō] and to the Senior Councillors and Castle Deputies (go-jōdai).

One: On the same day, regarding the celebration of the marriage engagement of Princess Manju-hime to the Lord of Owari, Chūjō [personal name: Haru-■]—while a general attendance (sō-dejishi) had previously been ordered for this occasion, the visit to the castle had been declined due to the confinement period (o-sanroku)—now that the confinement had ended, courtesy calls were made today to the Senior Councillors and Personal Attendant Officials of both citadels [Note in the original: The written notification was delivered by the Duty Official on the 29th of last month].

One: On the same day, regarding the same matter, as the confinement period had ended, a female messenger was dispatched today from the Inner Chambers to convey celebratory greetings.

One: On the same day, the seventh-night celebration (o-shichiya) for Yoshimaru-sama was held.
　　　Note: While this ought to have been conducted on the previous second day, it was held today because that day coincided with a major memorial observance of the Lord of Kii (Kii-sama).

One: On the fifth day of the fourth month, in order to relieve the extreme poverty of the most destitute residents of the town, one hundred bales of unhulled rice (籾/momi) were distributed.

One: On the same day, as the small peasants (ko-hyakushō) of the various villages had fallen into a state of near starvation, two hundred bales of unhulled rice and one hundred bales of hulled rice per unit (ichi-ryō) under the jurisdiction of the Three-District Magistrate (san-gun bugyō) were distributed as relief. In addition, one hundred and fifty bales of unhulled rice and seventy-five bales of hulled rice were distributed to the Kanazu domain (Kanazu-ryō).
