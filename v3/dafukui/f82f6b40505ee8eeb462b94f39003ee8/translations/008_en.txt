　　　　　　Separate Document
　　　　　　　Memorandum of Instructions

　　　　The domain's finances have been strained for many years, and this year unavoidable increases in expenditure have occurred. It is well known to His Lordship that the townspeople's districts have also fallen into particular hardship in recent years; however, as there is no other recourse, an emergency levy of funds (o-saikaku-kin) is hereby ordered, as set forth in the attached written notice. In connection with this matter, Shimada Seizaemon is dispatched on this occasion to deliver the written notice conveying His Lordship's intentions. Although it would ordinarily be appropriate to impose this obligation uniformly upon all parties, doing so in these times of hardship would cause particular distress to those of lower station and the common people. Therefore, prominent and leading individuals within each administrative jurisdiction are to deliberate and apportion the required sum appropriately among those under their charge, and issue instructions accordingly. Furthermore, the substance of the aforementioned written notice is to be explained to the relevant parties, and as this is a matter of exceptional urgency, they are to be instructed to exert every effort and render assistance as fully as possible. In this instance, the matter is to be handled without distinction, applying equally to group leaders (kumigashira), domain purveyors (go-yōtashi), exchange offices (fudasho), and chief administrators (motojime). Regarding repayment: beginning from the end of this year, a modest amount of interest will be added, and repayment will be made steadily in due course……
