One: On the twentieth day of the third month, the grandmother of a man called Sagobyōe of Kitaimaizumi Village in Yoshida District having reached the age of one hundred, she was granted a stipend of two-person rations (futari-fuchi).

One: On the twenty-third day of the same month, approximately one hundred townspeople and farmland workers under the ward headman (kumigashira) Niiya Yozaemon, citing destitution, had gathered at Anyōji Temple on the previous twenty-second day, and from there proceeded to the residences of ward headmen Maruya Kyūzaemon and Hyōzaemon to petition for relief rice (o-sukuimai). Around midday, approximately two hundred townspeople under ward headmen Nijūmoniya Sōzaemon and Kukutsu Matauemon gathered beneath the great bridge, and from there proceeded to the residences of Sōzaemon and Matauemon to make the same petition. The townspeople under ward headman Akao Kanemon likewise submitted a petition. Accordingly, on the following twenty-fourth day, the monthly duty senior retainer (tsukiban go-karō) Sakai Geki (Tomotaka) issued the following directive to the Temple and Shrine Magistrates (jisha-bugyō) and others:

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　To the Temple and Shrine Magistrates:

　　　　Regarding the fact that the lower-class townspeople, citing destitution, have of late been appearing in large numbers at the ward headmen's residences to petition for relief — while it would have been proper procedure for such petitions to be directed to the town headmen (machi-shōya), instead a large group conspired together and went directly to the ward headmen. This is an act showing no fear of authority and is an extreme breach of conduct. As for the headmen (shōya), allowing such a situation to pass without action was itself a failure of duty and a grave negligence. Accordingly, the town headmen of the districts from which these large groups came forth, as well as those identified as ringleaders among those who appeared and who are deemed particularly problematic, are to be investigated immediately, and approximately three persons per town are to be ordered into imprisonment. As for the headmen (shōya), following investigation to determine the degree of their culpability, they are to be ordered either into house confinement (oshikome) or into imprisonment.
