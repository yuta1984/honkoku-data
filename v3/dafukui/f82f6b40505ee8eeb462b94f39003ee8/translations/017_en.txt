Instructions to this effect are to be issued to the relevant officials. Furthermore, as the magistrates and inspectors are to confer on this matter, and there is precedent for such measures, arrangements are to be made so that no impediment arises.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　To the Magistrates (Go-bugyō):

　　　　　As it has come to our attention that lower-class townspeople are suffering due to the bad times, with difficulties in rice trading and other hardships leaving some in genuine distress, retail outlets for government relief rice are to be established and ordered to sell at prices reduced below the current market rate. Note that the above has already been communicated to Suzuki Chūuemon; further consultations are to be held in accordance with established precedent, and appropriate instructions are to be issued to each party.
　　　　　　　　　　〆 [End]

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　To the Inspectors (Go-metsuke):

　　　　　The substance of the above has been communicated to the Magistrates. Note also that instructions have been issued to Suzuki Chūuemon; further consultations are to be held and the matter handled accordingly.
