One: On the sixth day of the fourth month, as the Dainagon-sama [the Lord of Owari] had made his first visit (o-nari) to the vicinity of Iidamachi, the domain's resident official in Edo (o-rusuī), Kobayashi Mataemon [personal name: Masa-kata], was dispatched to the castle to inquire after his health upon the lord's return. There, he was received by the duty Inspector (o-metsuke), Lord Matsudaira Toneri.

One: On the same day, regarding the same matter, letters were sent to the Senior Lady Attendants (rōjo-chū) attached to the Shōgun (Kubō-sama), the Dainagon-sama, and the Midaidokoro-sama [the Shōgun's principal consort].

One: On the same day, an official messenger from Edo arrived in Fukui: the Inspector (o-metsuke) Hitoyanagi Shinku-rō [personal name: Hideo-katsu], accompanied by the Deputy Envoy, Grand Guard (ō-o-ban) Sakurai Ichinojō [personal name: Ō-koshi]. In the Tatar Room (Dattan-no-ma) of the Main Citadel (Hon-maru), with Honda Kuranosuke [personal name: Fuku-Shō] and the Senior Councillors (go-karō) seated in formal array, Shinkurō conveyed the following message [from the Shōgun]:

　　　"His Lordship and Her Ladyship are in excellent health and spirits. Above all, the safe delivery on the fifth of last month, and in particular the auspicious birth of the Young Lord (waka-tono-sama), have brought [the Shōgun] great joy and satisfaction. It is for this reason that Hitoyanagi Shinkurō and Sakurai Ichinojō have been dispatched. Each of you should receive this news with due reverence and gratitude."

After the above message was delivered, Honda Kuranosuke and the Senior Councillors were seated in their respective places, with Shinkurō and Ichinojō attending, while the Monthly Duty Senior Councillor (tsuki-ban go-karō), Okabe Mikino-suke [personal name: Nushi-sada], conveyed the instructions to the assembled retainers. As a formal response to this [the Shōgun's message], the Messenger Official (o-tsukai-ban) Atsumi Shin'emon [personal name: Tomo-kazu] was dispatched to Edo, departing Fukui today.
　　　Note: Shin'emon arrived at the Edo residence on the thirteenth [of the month].

One: On the same day, regarding the disturbances that had recently occurred in the castle town, the upper retainers (kōchi) Ōtani Sukeroku [personal name: Masa-nori] and Ogino Magonemon [personal name: Masa-sai], who had gone to the scene at the time of the incident, departed Fukui today to proceed to Edo to report on the nature of the said disturbance.
