(Continued from the previous page) ...having grown to a large crowd, they proceeded once again to the residence of the Master Carpenter (daiku-gashira) Fujima Matasaburō [personal name: Takenaka], where they smashed the household goods that had been left remaining from before. They then went on to destroy the household goods of Ikada-ya Kiemon of Ikada-machi, Okiya Chōemon of Myōkokuji, Fushimi-ya Jihei of Hachiman-machi, and Toriya Kahei of Kida-machi. The violence and disturbances directed against these wealthy merchants and others were extreme. The rioters refused to heed the words of the officials who had come out to suppress the unrest over the preceding days, and when the Messenger Officials (o-tsukai-ban) questioned them, they presented petitions and grievances, apparently intending to communicate their intentions to the officials. Accordingly, the Messenger Officials were each assigned to accompany one of the three high-ranking retainers (kōchi): Ōtani Sukeroku [personal name: Masanori], Hagino Magozaemon [personal name: Masanari], and Habakaribe Yukinojō [personal name: Yorihiro], to proceed to the scene and restore order. The Boatmen's Captain (o-kako-gashira) Tochiya Hachizaemon [personal name: Seishi] was ordered to secure the Akasato Rice Storehouse (o-kurashō); the Construction Magistrate (o-sakuji bugyō) Oka Saburōemon [personal name: Tsuneyuki] was ordered to secure the Mokuzō Kawaraguchi. Furthermore, orders were conveyed through the Monthly Duty Elder (tsuki-ban go-karō) Sakai Geki [personal name: Tomotaka] to all the monthly duty officials to be prepared, as the situation might require each of them to be ordered to take up guard positions accordingly.

　　　Note: At this time, peasants also gathered in Fuchū and Kanazu and committed acts of violence in various places.

One: On the same day, regarding the same matter, the Foot Courier (o-kachi hikyaku) Kai Handayū was dispatched to Edo on a schedule of three and a half days via the Doi highway. The Inspector (o-metsuke) Imamura Danemon [personal name: Yukinori] and the Messenger Official (o-tsukai-ban) Matsubara Zenzaemon [personal name: Nariyoshi] were dispatched on a five-day travel schedule.
