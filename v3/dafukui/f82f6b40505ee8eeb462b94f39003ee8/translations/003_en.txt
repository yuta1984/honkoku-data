Meiwa 5, the Year of Tsuchinoe-Ne (1768)

Item: On the first day of the First Month (New Year's Day), [Lord Shigetomi] attended the castle to offer New Year's congratulations. In accordance with custom, he presented a ceremonial sword and a horse stipend, and received crepe silk (gofuku) in return. Additionally, through a messenger, he presented silver and dried sea bream (hoshi-tai).

Item: On the same day, for the same reason, he presented ceremonial New Year's rice cakes (kagami-mochi) in accordance with custom, conveyed via a formal letter.

Item: On the same day, for the same reason, he received ceremonial rice cakes, dried sea bream, and a cask of sake in accordance with custom, conveyed through a letter from the senior lady-in-waiting (rōjochū). As a gesture of thanks for these gifts, [Lord Shigetomi] paid courtesy calls on the Goyōban (duty senior councilor), Lord Matsudaira Ukon-no-shōgen (Takemoto), and the Nishimaru Senior Councilor (rōjū), Lord Itakura Sado-no-kami (Katsukiyo).

Item: On the same day, a female messenger was sent from the private inner quarters (go-naishō) to present, in accordance with custom, locally produced tori-no-ko paper, small paper, and dried sea bream.
