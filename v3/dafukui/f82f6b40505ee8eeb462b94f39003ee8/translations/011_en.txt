One: On the 24th day of the 12th month, on the occasion of the 21st memorial service (death anniversary) for the Lady Shishin'in (the biological mother of Lord Ieharu), held at Tōeizan,
　in order to pay respects and inquire after the well-being of the Kubō-sama (the Shōgun), a messenger was dispatched — the resident keeper (o-rusuī) Yokota Sakudayū (Buddhist name: Shōdō) — who presented one box of year-end delicacies (having already received prior approval for this).

One: On the 26th day of the same month, for the same reason as above, a messenger — the head of the bow-bearers (o-mochi-yumi-gashira), Mochizuki Hachirōemon (Nobu■) — was dispatched to the main temple hall (go-honbō) of Tōeizan, where one piece of silver incense money (o-kōden) was presented (upon prior approval).

One: On the 27th day of the same month, to offer congratulations for the recently completed ceremony of the Appointment of the Crown Prince (Rippō, ■■■-in), all retainers were ordered to attend in full formal dress, and His Lordship proceeded to Edo Castle. At the Sakura no Ma (Cherry Blossom Chamber), the Senior Councillors (Rōjū) were seated in a row, and His Lordship conveyed his congratulations there, meeting with the Duty Councillor (go-yōban), Lord Abe Iyonokami (Masasuke). Thereafter, His Lordship proceeded to the West Citadel (Nishi-maru) and, at the veranda of the White Audience Chamber (Shiro-shoin), conveyed his congratulations and met with the Announcer of Visits (Sōja-ban), Lord Ōi Ōinokami (Tosato/Riritsu).
