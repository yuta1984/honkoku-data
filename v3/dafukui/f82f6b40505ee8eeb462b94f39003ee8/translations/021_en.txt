One: On the same day, peasants from the various villages once again came out to the castle town, and on the following day, the twenty-eighth, a large crowd assembled and approximately three hundred people descended upon the residence of the Master Carpenter (daiku-gashira) Fujima Matasaburō [personal name: Takenaka], smashing and destroying clothing, fixtures, and other contents of his residence and storehouse. They then proceeded to the establishments of Gokuin-ya Shōzaemon and Minō-ya Kizaemon, where they destroyed the remaining household goods that had been left from before. As the situation showed no sign of calming, orders were issued for the Inspector (o-metsuke) Imamura Danemon [personal name: Yukinori], the Vanguard Unit Commanders (o-saki mono-gashira) Miyakita Jūrozaemon [personal name: Sadayoshi] and Hagiwara Kinbei [personal name: Suehayashi], and the Messengers (o-tsukai-sha) Atsumi Shinemon [personal name: Tomokazu] and Sakai Jūronosuke [personal name: Motomi] to proceed to the scene and restore order. The Flag Magistrate (o-hata bugyō) Senboku Chōemon [personal name: Hisaoki], the Construction Magistrate (o-fushin bugyō) Kaifuku Seizaemon [personal name: Bannori], the Vanguard Unit Commander Amamori Tarōbei [personal name: Atsuoki], the Boatmen's Captain (o-kako-gashira) Tochiya Hachizaemon [personal name: Seishi] Sugigata, the Spear Magistrate (o-yari bugyō) Nakane Kuemon [personal name: Tokitomo], and the Long-handled Spear Magistrate (o-nagae bugyō) Kawai Takiemon [personal name: Tsugukiyo] were ordered to secure the various castle gates. Orders were also issued to the Side-Unit Commander (o-soba mono-gashira) to dispatch undercover agents (shinobi no mono) to the villages. Furthermore, as an urgent express courier (hayagake) to report to Edo, the Foot Inspector (o-kachi metsuke) Suganuma Gorozaemon [personal name: Sukehisa] was dispatched.

One: On the twenty-eighth day of the third month, it having been announced that Lady Manju-hime was to be betrothed to the Lord of Owari Middle Counselor [personal name: Harunori], all retainers were summoned to attend [the castle in celebration]; however, as [the lord's household] was in confinement following childbirth (sanjoku), a messenger was sent to the Senior Councillor (rōjū) Matsudaira Suō-no-kami [personal name: Yasutomi] with apologies, declining attendance at the castle, through the Rusui (caretaker official) Yokota Sakudayū [personal name: Naomichi].

One: On the twenty-ninth day of the same month, regarding the same matter, a summons was received from the Duty Councillor (go-yōban) Matsudaira Ukyō-no-daibu [personal name: Terutaka], and the Rusui Yokota Sakudayū [personal name: Naomichi] duly appeared. He was informed through a secretary (yōnin) that once the confinement period (sanjoku) had ended, [the lord] should pay courtesy calls (mawari-kin) to the Senior Councillors and the Side Chamberlain (sobayōnin) Tanuma Tonomo-no-kami [personal name: Okitsugu] at the Ryō-omaru [the two inner citadels], and that attendance at the castle proper at the Ryō-omaru was not required.
