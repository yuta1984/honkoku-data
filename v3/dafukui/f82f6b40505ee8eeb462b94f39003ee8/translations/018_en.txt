(Continued from the previous page) [Two of the headmen] were placed under house confinement (oshikomi); thirty-one others, who despite ordinarily living in a fitting manner had made inappropriate petitions, were ordered imprisoned (nyūrō).

One: On the same day, over one hundred peasants from villages under the authority of the Kanazu Magistrate (bugyō) Nara Chūzaemon [personal name: Takamasa] and those under the Nakakyō domain authority gathered at the Hamachō riverbank, then proceeded to the establishment of Minoya Kizaemon, a merchant in service to the domain (go-yōtashi chōnin), where they committed acts of violence (rōzeki) against sake barrels and the like, before withdrawing as night fell.

One: On the twenty-fifth day of the third month, at the middle of the Hour of the Snake (approximately 10 a.m.), at the Tokiwabashi residence, Lady Itashi gave birth safely, and the Young Lord was born. In consequence, Inspector (metsuke) Ichiyanagi Shinkunirō [personal name: Eishō] and Senior Guard (ō-goban) Sakurai Ichinoshin [personal name: Ōkyo] were dispatched to the home domain, the journey estimated at five days.

One: On the same day, in connection with the above, the Duty Senior Councillor (go-yōban) Matsudaira Ukyō-no-Daibu [personal name: Terutaka] submitted the following notification letter to the Senior Councillor of the Western Enclosure (Nishi-maru go-rōjū), Lord Itakura Sado-no-Kami [personal name: Katsukiyo], through the Resident Agent (go-rusuoi) Kobayashi Matauemon [personal name: Masamichi]:

　　My wife gave birth on this twenty-fifth day, at the middle of the Hour of the Snake, delivering a boy.
　　Accordingly, she will remain in confinement until the second day of the fourth month. I hereby submit this notification. Respectfully,
　　　　　　Twenty-fifth day of the third month　　　　Matsudaira Echizen-no-Kami

One: On the same day, in connection with the above,
　　His Excellency the Shōgun and the Lady of the Palace (Midaidokoro) sent their inquiries to Lady Itashi through a message conveyed by a senior lady-in-waiting (rōjo).
