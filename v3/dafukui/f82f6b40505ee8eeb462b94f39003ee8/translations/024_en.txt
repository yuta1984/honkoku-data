　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Yoshimaru
　　　The male child born on this occasion has been given the name as stated above. We hereby notify you of this. Respectfully submitted.
　　　　4th month, 2nd day　　　　　　Matsumoto Echizen-no-kami

One: On the same day, the Edo Resident Agent Kobayashi Mataemon proceeded to the Domain Duty Official Matsudaira Suō-no-kami and the Senior Councillor of the Western Citadel Itakura Sado-no-kami to convey that the confinement period (o-sanroku) would end from the following day, the third.

One: On the same day, since the peasants who had gathered in the castle town had gradually withdrawn and the streets of the town had finally become calm, the Foot Courier (o-kachi hikyaku) Nishimura Kōemon [personal name: ■■] was dispatched to Edo on a four-day travel schedule.

One: On the third day of the fourth month, regarding the recent safe delivery—while the expression of gratitude for the gifts received through the Senior Lady-in-Waiting's formal petition on the previous second day ought to have been made, it had been postponed due to the confinement—the gratitude was paid today to the Domain Duty Official Matsudaira Suō-no-kami and the Senior Councillor of the Western Citadel Itakura Sado-no-kami.

One: Regarding the same matter, through the Special Envoy (jōshi) and Announcements Official (o-sōja-ban) Ōoka Hyōgo-no-kami [personal name: Tadayoshi], one box of dried sea bream (hoshi-tai) was received from the Shōgun (kubō-sama).
