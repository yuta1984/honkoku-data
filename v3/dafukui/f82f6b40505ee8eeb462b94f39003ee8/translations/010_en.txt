　　　　　Although it would be appropriate to impose this obligation uniformly upon all parties, were it to be done in such a manner, it would cause particular hardship to the lesser people of lower standing during these times of poverty and distress. Therefore, the leading and influential persons of the town households are to deliberate and allocate the said sum of money proportionally among themselves, and the levy is to be so instructed. Furthermore, they are to be informed of the purport of the said written notice, and since this is a matter of exceptional importance to His Lordship's service, they should be told to exert their utmost efforts in fulfilling this obligation. As for repayment, beginning from this year-end, with a modest amount of interest added, repayment will certainly be made in due course; please convey this point as well in your instructions.
