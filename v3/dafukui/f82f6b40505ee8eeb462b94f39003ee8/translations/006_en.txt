　　　　It is His Lord's intention that simplification and economizing in [retinue arrangements] need not cause concern. With this understanding, although circumstances are exceedingly difficult, all parties are ordered to exercise careful consideration and frugality in all matters, and to continue carrying out their duties of service without interruption. The above is hereby issued.

　　　　　Second Month

　　　　　Note: The same directive was conveyed at the Edo residence on the sixth day of the Third Month.

Item: On the same day, the Senior Councilor (Karō) Sakai Geki (Tomo■) conveyed the following to the Kanazu magistrate, the town magistrate, the inspector (metsuke), the district magistrate (kōri bugyō), and others:

　　　　**Official Written Notice (Goshotsuke)**

　　　　While the domain's finances are already severely strained, additional extraordinary expenditures have now arisen in connection with the Lady's [the domain lord's consort's] departure/affairs, making the situation unavoidable. Furthermore, the costs of transport and related expenses during this period have increased considerably beyond expectations. Moreover, as this comes just before the lord's return to the domain, the shortfall in necessary funds has grown, raising concern that it may impede the lord's return journey — a matter of deep anxiety. The lord's attendance at Edo (sanpu) and subsequent return to the domain constitute the foremost obligation vis-à-vis the Shogunate, and are also in the interest of the domain as a whole. Therefore, His Lordship earnestly wishes to return to the domain without impediment. Accordingly, although it is a matter of considerable reluctance, as there is no other means available, an emergency levy of funds (o-saikaku-kin, a discretionary loan/forced contribution) is to be imposed upon both the towns and rural villages within the domain's territory.
