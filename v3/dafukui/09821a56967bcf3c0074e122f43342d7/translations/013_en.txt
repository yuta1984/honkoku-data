**〇 Notice delivered by letter regarding the New Year greetings of the coming year:**

The senior members (*oyabun no menmen*) shall, as in previous years, receive their New Year greetings on the 1st day of the new year. The junior members (*kodomo-bun*) shall receive their greetings on the 3rd day. Please be informed accordingly. That is all.

> 23rd day of the 12th month　　　Hatano Bunemon — Sasa Jiken'emon
