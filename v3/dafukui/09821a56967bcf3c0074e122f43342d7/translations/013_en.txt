**○ Communicated by letter. Regarding the New Year's greetings (*nenshirei*) for the coming year:** Those of the status of *oyabun* (senior/principal members) shall receive their New Year's greetings, as in previous years, on the first day of the New Year. Those of *kodomo-bun* (junior members) shall receive their greetings on the third day. Please be duly informed of this arrangement. Respectfully.

　　23rd day of the 12th month　　　Hatano Bun'emon　　　Sasaji Gonuemon

**Item — Hōreki 5th year, Year of the Boar (1755)**
　In Edo　　　**Memorandum (*Oboegaki*)**

○ Regarding the letters dispatched at the time of the couriers (*hikyaku*) sent to the home domain: it has been communicated on repeated occasions that, in accordance with long-established custom, letters should be written so as not to be [excessively] heavy [in weight]. Nevertheless, in recent years this has again become disorderly, and with each courier dispatch an excessive number of letters accumulate and are left [undelivered in a timely manner].
