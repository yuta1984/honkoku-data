○ This is to inform you by letter: Regarding next year's New Year's greeting ceremonies (*nenshirei*), those of the senior/principal rank (*oyabun*) shall receive greetings as usual on New Year's Day. Those of the junior/subordinate rank (*kodomo-bun*) shall receive greetings on the third day. Please take note of this accordingly. That is all.

　　　　23rd day of the 12th month　　　To: Hatano Bunuemon　　　Sasaji Gonuemon
