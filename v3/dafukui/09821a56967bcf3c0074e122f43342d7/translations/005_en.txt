　　　6th day of the 7th month　　Masugi Shozaemon (addressee)　　Hohokabe Hachizaemon

　　　　　Memorandum

Item, New Year greetings: Within the two days of the 1st and 2nd of the New Year, proceed to the Nanatsu-gura guardhouse and present yourself to the on-duty sōja-ban to offer your New Year's congratulations.

Item, Summer heat inquiry: Within five days of the beginning of the *doyo* (midsummer season), proceed to the Nanatsu-gura guardhouse to make your seasonal inquiry. Note: Any days of abstinence (*shōjin-bi*) falling within the said period are to be excluded.

Item, Midwinter cold inquiry: Upon the beginning of the cold season (*kan*), the same procedure as above applies.

Item, On occasions of congratulatory events: Once notification has been circulated by the senior councillors (*karō*), the same procedure as above is to be carried out within two days.

　　All of the above are to take place between the hour of five in the morning (approximately 8 a.m.) and before the hour of eight (approximately 2 p.m.).

Item, Personal ceremonial greetings: Present yourself at the residence of the month-duty *sōja-ban* to make your address.
　　Note: As it is difficult to know in advance whether they may be absent due to official duties or other engagements, you are to inquire beforehand as to whether they are at home.

【Left folio】

Item: The fourteenth year of Kyōhō (the year of the Rooster, 1729)

○ Regarding the offering of congratulations on the Five Annual Festivals (*gosekku*) and other celebratory occasions to the First Princess, it has been the practice hitherto to proceed to the residence of the monthly-duty senior councillor (*karō*); however, henceforth you are to proceed to the Nanatsu-gura to offer your congratulations. One of us shall be in attendance there at all times. This matter is communicated as above. That is all.

　　　　1st day of the 3rd month　　Ōtani Gizaemon (addressee)　　Isono Tamiya

Item: The fifteenth year of Kyōhō (the year of the Dog, 1730)

○ Regardless of the occasion, for both Edo and Kyoto, congratulatory letters to the ladies-in-waiting (*onnachu*) are to be submitted to their attendants, and each of you is to send letters as has been done previously. Although it had been the practice to proceed to the Nanatsu-gura guardhouse, that procedure is now discontinued, so please take note accordingly. That is all.

　　　　3rd day of the 4th month　　Kaifuku Seizaemon (addressee)　　Shibuya Yazei

　　Addendum: Regarding congratulations on the occasion of the lord's arrival at the domain (*nyūbu*), this too applies in the same manner as stated above, so please take note. That is all.
