7th day of the 7th month　　Masugi Shoザemon (addressee)　　Hahakabe Hachizaemon

**Memorandum (おぼえ)**

Item — New Year's Greetings: Within the two days of the 1st and 2nd of the New Year, you are to present yourself at the Nanatsu-gura Guard Post and pay your respects to the duty Sōjaban (Master of Ceremonies officer), offering your congratulatory New Year's greetings.

Item — Summer Seasonal Greetings: Within five days of the commencement of the *doyō* (the midsummer period), you are to present yourself at the Nanatsu-gura Guard Post and make your inquiry after one's health. Should any days of Buddhist abstinence (*shōjin-bi*) fall within that period, those days are to be excluded.

Item — Winter Seasonal Greetings: Upon the commencement of the midwinter period (*kan*), the same procedure as above applies.

Item — On occasions of celebratory events and the like: Upon receipt of a circular notice from the Senior Elders (go-karō-chū), you are to present yourself within two days, following the same procedure as above.
　　For all of the above, presentations shall be made between the hour of 5 o'clock and before the hour of 8 o'clock in the morning.

Item — Personal ceremonial greetings: These are to be paid in person at the residence of the monthly-duty Sōjaban officer.
　　Note: As it is difficult to predict whether the officer may be absent on official business or other matters, you should first make inquiries in advance as to whether he is at home.

　　　　7th day of the 7th month

**Item — The fourteenth year of Kyōhō (the year of the Rooster, 1729)**

○ Regarding the matter of presenting congratulatory greetings to Lady Hatsu-hime on the Five Seasonal Festivals (*go-sekku*) and other celebratory occasions: although it has been the practice to present oneself at the residences of the monthly-duty Senior Elders, henceforth you are to present yourself at the Nanatsu-gura and offer your congratulations there. One of us will be in attendance there at all times. This notice is issued to inform you of the above. That is all.

　　　　1st day of the 3rd month　　Ōtani Gizaemon (addressee)　　Isono Tamiya

**Item — The fifteenth year of Kyōhō (the year of the Dog, 1730)**

○ Regardless of the nature of the occasion, whether in Edo or Kyoto, congratulatory letters addressed to the ladies-in-waiting (*onna-chū*) are to be submitted to their respective attendants (*otsukinokata*), and each person is to continue to submit letters as has been done previously. Although it has been the practice up to now to present oneself at the Nanatsu-gura Guard Post, that practice is hereby discontinued. Please be informed accordingly. That is all.

　　　　3rd day of the 4th month　　　Kaifuku Seizaemon (addressee)

　　Addendum: In connection with the lord's formal entry into the domain (*go-nyūbu*), the same procedure as above shall apply for celebratory greetings. Please be informed accordingly. That is all.
