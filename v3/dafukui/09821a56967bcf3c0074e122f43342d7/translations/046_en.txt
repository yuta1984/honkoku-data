1. Bunsei 10, Year of the Boar (1827)

〇 Regarding the schedule for monthly attendance at the castle, apart from the New Year ceremonies and the Five Seasonal Festivals:

　　The 1st day of the month, the 15th day of the month, and — for the 12th month only — the 28th day.
　　On these days, attendance at the castle is to be made at the hour of Go-han (approximately 9:00 a.m.) without awaiting a separate notification. Should attendance be cancelled, a notification will be issued.

　　Provision: Attendance at the castle for New Year ceremonies and the Five Seasonal Festivals remains as per previous custom.

　　The 7th day of the 1st month, the 1st day of the 3rd month, and the 15th day of the 7th month:
　　Attendance is not required on these days.
　　　　　　　27th day of the 5th month
　　The foregoing was conveyed by Akita Michinosuke.

2. Bunsei 11, Year of the Rat (1828) — Issued in Edo

〇 Regarding the matter of the bulk of correspondence sent via courier (hikyaku): this has been ordered on numerous previous occasions, and was strictly ordered in the Year of the Dog (1826), at which time it was also communicated that official goods (go-yōmono) were to be submitted directly to the Finance Office (go-kanjōsho) by each respective party, with the nature of the goods clearly indicated. However, it has come to attention that recently, official correspondence (go-yōjō) as well as lodging letters (yado-jō) from various quarters have been growing bulky again. Therefore, all parties are to take note and comply with the previously issued orders. Should there be any correspondence that becomes bulky, it is to be held back (not sent). Please take note accordingly.

　　Provision: In the case of official correspondence, it is to be submitted with a covering letter from the unit head (yakugashira).

As the foregoing has been communicated, should there be any relevant parties, this is to be duly conveyed to them. The above.
