**Item — The third year of Enkyō (the year of the Tiger, 1746)**

*(Interlinear note: Item — On the 13th day of the 5th month, Amagata Kazuma distributed a written notice as follows:*
*O-ura-dōri [the rear passage/route]: the 17th day of each month.*
*Regarding the inspection [of the gate/passage] from the preceding evening, the matter of observing mourning prohibitions [hiki] is to be reviewed.)*

Item — On the 12th day of the 10th month, Amagata Kazuma conveyed that the following dates are to be communicated to all officials (*go-yakunin-domo*) as days on which they should exercise restraint (*enryo*) at the御坂 (*o-saka*, the lord's gate or official checkpoint):

　　　　　　　Total


**Rules governing the presentation of formal greetings (*go-rei*) during the five-year period beginning from the coming year of the Rabbit (卯年)**

**[Left folio]**

Item — The New Year's greetings (*nenshi go-rei*) shall be conducted in the same manner as heretofore.
　　*However*: the horse-tribute payment (*o-umashiro*) shall be reduced by half compared to the previous custom.

Item — On each of the Five Seasonal Festivals (*go-sekku*), as well as on the 1st day of the 8th month and the 28th day of the last month (*gokugetsu*, the 12th month), each individual (*meimei*) is to personally receive the formal greeting (*go-rei*).
　　*However*: there shall be *o-tsumami mochi* (small rice cakes offered as refreshment).

Item — The monthly regular greetings (*tsukiji no go-rei*) shall be conducted as a collective ceremony (*sō go-rei*), as shall be decreed.
　　*However*: even on a regular monthly occasion, if there is a special circumstance, it shall be received by each person individually (*menmen*).

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Nakane Yukinoi
　　　Tiger year, last month, [date]　　　Amagata Kazuma
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Namikawa Ichikurō

　The above was communicated by the *go-sōja-ban* (official managing formal audiences) on the 21st day of the 12th month.

**Item — The fourth year of Enkyō (the year of the Rabbit, 1747)**

**Verbal Instruction (*kōtatsu*)**

○ Regarding the matter of *sō-shusshi* (collective attendance/appearance): there are those who refer to this practice as "*sō-rei*" (collective greeting/ceremony). Henceforth, it is to be handled and referred to as "*sō-shusshi*" or "*ichidō no go-rei*" (collective formal greeting).
