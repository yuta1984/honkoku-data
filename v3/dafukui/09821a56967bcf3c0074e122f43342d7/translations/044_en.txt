As it appears that some individuals have been directly submitting their residence letters (yado-jō) and similar documents without going through the proper channels, henceforth no such submissions will be accepted unless they are made under the name of the unit sub-chief (kogashira). The relevant parties are to communicate this to the sub-chiefs of the twelve units accordingly.

1. In the 4th month of Bunsei 7, Year of the Monkey (1824): As an exceptional austerity order (kenkaku) has been issued on this occasion, and although strict instructions regarding correspondence sent to various parties had already been given previously, the following was further communicated to the unit sub-chiefs and disseminated from the monthly duty officer to all twelve units.

When official couriers (o-hikyaku) are dispatched, instructions had previously been given that official business correspondence for the sub-chiefs of the Mochi-zutsugumi (the musket-bearing unit) and residence letters from unit members must be made extremely compact, and submitted with no more than five bu (approx. 15mm) of thickness per unit. However, it has come to light that some have been submitting items in separate sealed packets through intermediaries. Henceforth, it has been strictly communicated to the clerks (monokaki) that not only separate sealed packets, but even letters from sub-chiefs themselves will be firmly refused if their sealed bulk exceeds the permitted thickness. In particular, given that an austerity order has now been issued across all domains, sub-chiefs are to exercise due diligence to ensure there are no further misunderstandings.
