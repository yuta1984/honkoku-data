**○ 25th day of the 6th month — Written notice delivered by the Chief Steward (*go-yōnin*), as follows:**

Regarding the practice, hitherto conducted by correspondence (*shochū*), of presenting New Year's greetings, inquiries after the Young Lord's health during the hot and cold seasons, congratulatory messages on occasions of joy, and other such matters: while the lord is in residence [in the domain], such greetings are to be conveyed together at the same time as the formal congratulations are presented to the Lord himself, and should be directed to the Chief Stewards as well. Note that it is not necessary to send written correspondence to the Edo office on these occasions.

**○ 26th day of the 9th month — Upon being summoned to the administrative office at the Seventh Storehouse (*Nanatsu-gura go-yōjo*) by Sasa Jigoemon and Shimada Seizaemon, the following written notice was received. It was explained that this notice was being delivered because there are now new arrangements (*Shinban-gumi*) in place in Edo as well.**

　　　　　**Memorandum (*Oboegaki*)**

Item — Those persons who have served ten years after being raised to the rank of *Shinban* (new guard unit) from the position of minor officials (*ko-yakunin*) or foot soldiers (*o-kachi*), or from among those specially promoted to *Shinban* rank, shall be admitted to the *Shinban-gumi* (new guard corps).

Item — Those who have been admitted to the *Shinban-gumi* and served forty years shall be admitted to the *Ō-go-ban-gumi* (senior guard corps).
　　　　　**Total: fifty years.**

　　Provided, however, that this applies regardless of whether the person is a second-generation descendant from a grandfather, or the individual himself (*genshin*), based on the accumulation of years of service. Furthermore, those who have served in an official capacity (*yakukata*) or have rendered particularly distinguished service may, at the discretion of the authorities at that time, receive a ruling (*o-sata*) irrespective of the number of years. The same applies to those who have been appointed to the *Shinban-gumi* from the outset.

Item — Those who have served twenty-five years from the year of their promotion to the *Ō-go-ban-gumi* shall attain the status of *Aimishōtai-sue* (latter stage of middle-rank status).

Item — Those who have served twenty-five years from *Aimishōtai-sue* shall, likewise, be admitted to the *Ō-go-ban-gumi* from the first position in order of seniority (*junseki*).
　　　　　**Total: fifty years.**

　　Provided, however, that this applies regardless of whether the person is a second- or third-generation descendant from a grandfather, or the individual himself, based on the accumulation of years of service. Those who have been appointed to a position of seniority (*junseki*) by virtue of their office shall continue as before. Those who, even in their own person, have served with distinguished merit for special reasons are not bound by the above number of years, and a ruling shall be issued at the discretion of the authorities at that time.

The above provisions shall henceforth be examined and applied at the time of the guard-duty roster assignments (*o-banwari*).

**○ 1st day of the 10th month — Nakane Yukinojō delivered one written notice as follows, with instructions that it be communicated to the foot-soldier inspectors (*o-kachi metsuke*):**

On the 12th day of the 9th month, Ogasawara Nuinosuke and Kuze Chūemon were summoned before Hotta Sagami-no-Kami, who informed them of the following: Two years ago, when Senior Inspector Hotta Kaga-no-Kami was dispatched as a shogunal envoy (*jōshi*) to inquire after the health of Matsudaira Noto-no-Kami during his illness, there arose a dispute (*mōshiawase*) between the retinue of Sakai Yamato-no-Kami and the retinue of Kaga-no-Kami [regarding precedence and conduct]. It has since come to the attention [of the Senior Councilors] that the retinues of various daimyo lords have continued to mix and jostle (*iritsuni*). The Senior Councilors (*go-rōjū*) have duly issued instructions on the matter, and [the retinues] are particularly enjoined to maintain good order and avoid any confusion in crowded places. Furthermore, it is His Excellency's intention that this be enforced with even greater strictness with regard to outside lords (*tozama*). Sagami-no-Kami stated that [the parties] should be informed of this matter each in their respective [capacities].
