**〇 25th day of the 6th month — A written notice delivered by the *go-yōnin* (senior administrators) as follows:**

Regarding the customary inquiries after the Young Lord's (*Wakatono-sama*'s) well-being at New Year, midsummer, and midwinter, as well as congratulatory greetings and other such communications that have hitherto been submitted in writing: during the lord's residence in the domain (*go-zaikoku-chū*), such greetings are to be conveyed together with those presented to the lord (*Tono-sama*), and shall also be communicated to the *go-yōnin*. Note that it is not necessary to send written correspondence to the Edo office in this regard.

**〇 26th day of the 9th month — Upon being summoned to the *go-yōsho* (administrative office) at the Shichitsugura by Sasa Jiken'emon and Shimada Seizaemon, the following written notice was received. It was explained that, in Edo, this matter was to be handled by the *shinbangumi* (new guard unit).**

　　　　　　　***Memorandum***

Item — Those among the minor officials (*ko-yakunin*), foot soldiers (*o-kachi*), and newly appointed *shinban*-rank personnel who have served for ten years shall be admitted to the *Shinbangumi* (New Guard Unit).

Item — Those who have been admitted to the *Shinbangumi* and have served for forty years shall be admitted to the *Ō-o-bangumi* (Great Guard Unit).
　　　　　*Combined total: fifty years.*
　　　*Note: This applies regardless of whether it is the grandfather's generation, the second generation, or the present person themselves, according to the number of years served. However, those who have served in an official capacity or have distinguished themselves in service may, at the discretion of the authorities at that time, receive a ruling without regard to the number of years. The same applies to those who were assigned to the Shinbangumi from the outset.*

Item — Those who have served for twenty-five years from the year of their appointment to the *Ō-o-bangumi* shall advance to *ai-mibue-sue* (an intermediate status designation).

Item — Those who have served for twenty-five years beyond *ai-mibue-sue* shall likewise be admitted to the *Ō-o-bangumi* from the beginning of the seniority (*junsheki*) ranking.
　　　　　*Combined total: fifty years.*
　　　*Note: This applies regardless of whether it is the second or third generation from the grandfather, or the present person, according to the number of years served. However, those who have been assigned seniority ranking (*junsheki*) by virtue of their post shall continue as before. Those who, even in the present generation, have special circumstances or distinguished service shall, without being bound by the aforementioned number of years, receive a ruling at the discretion of the authorities at that time.*

The above provisions are to be examined and communicated at the time of future guard-duty roster assignments (*o-banzuke*).

**〇 1st day of the 10th month — A written notice delivered by Nakane Yukinojō, to be communicated to the *o-kachi-metsuke* (inspectors of the foot soldiers):**

On the 12th day of the 9th month, Ogasawara Nuinosuke and Kuze ■■emon were summoned before Lord Hotta Sagami-no-kami and were informed as follows:

In the year before last, when Lord Matsudaira Noto-no-kami was ill, Lord Hotta Kaga-no-kami was sent as a special envoy (*jōshi*) to inquire after his health. At that time, a dispute or mix-up (*mōshiawase*) arose between the attendants of Lord Sakai Yamato-no-kami and those of Lord Kaga-no-kami.

It has since been heard that the attendant processions (*o-tomogawari*) of various *daimyō* lords have continued to intermingle in a disorderly manner (*iritsu ni*). The Senior Councillors (*go-rōjū*) have repeatedly issued instructions that in crowded locations especially, there should be no such confusion. Nevertheless, it is the considered view [of the Senior Councillors] that in the case of *tozama* (outside lords) in particular, strict orders (*kitto*) should be issued henceforth. Lord Sagami-no-kami instructed that this matter be communicated accordingly to each party concerned.
