Item: Bunka 11th year, Year of the Dog (1814)
　Item: On the 3rd day of the 10th month, the go-yōnin (chamberlain) communicated the following per the attached separate document, and notice was accordingly given to each party.
　　　　　　　　《Supplementary note: This concerns Tsunogorō-sama.》
　Item: On this occasion, a male child (go-danshi-sama) has been born in the O-oku (the Inner Chambers) in Edo. Those who have personnel under their unit command (kumi-shihai) are to inform said personnel of this matter, and arrangements are to be made for them to offer their respectful congratulations (kyōetsu) to their commanding officer (kashira).
　Item: The formal official notification (kōhen o-todoke) to the public authorities shall be postponed for the time being. All parties are to take note of this, and this point is likewise to be communicated.
　　　　　　End

Item: Bunka 12th year, Year of the Boar (1815)
　Item: On the 18th day of the 1st month, the go-yōnin communicated that, as decreed per the attached separate document, notice was accordingly given to each party.
　　　　　　　　Separate Document
　○ Concerning the presentation of formal salutations (o-rei) by the o-kaireki (shell-game attendants/pages) at the time of the lord's departure from the castle (go-shūden).
　Item: Those serving as o-kaireki in the shin-ban-gumi (new guard unit) who are assigned to the o-shoin-ban (senior guard corps) and continue to serve as o-kaireki shall, upon their first attendance in the lord's retinue at the time of the lord's departure from the castle, present their formal salutation through the introduction of the o-kachi-gashira (commander of foot soldiers).
　Item: Even if the aforementioned individuals received their appointments during the lord's absence from the castle, they shall likewise present their formal salutation upon first attending in the lord's retinue after his return to the castle.
　Item: Previously, when o-kaireki received stipend increases (o-kuwaemashi) or other such honorable appointments, and thereafter first attended in the lord's retinue at the time of his departure from the castle, they presented their formal salutation through the introduction of the o-kachi-gashira. Henceforth, said formal salutation is to be discontinued.
　　　Proviso: Even if persons from the ō-go-ban (senior guards), the o-rusui-ban murole (non-stipendiary castle-keepers), or the o-rusui-ban-gumi (castle-keeper unit) are appointed as o-kaireki and assigned to the o-shoin-ban, no formal salutation is required.
　　　Item: When o-kaireki are appointed to the o-shoin-ban-gumi while remaining in their current post (igata), no formal salutation is required.
　　　Item: When a member of the o-shoin-ban serving as o-kaireki is granted retirement (kyūsoku), and that person's son is subsequently appointed as o-kaireki and assigned to the o-shoin-ban-gumi, no formal salutation is required.
　　　　　　End
