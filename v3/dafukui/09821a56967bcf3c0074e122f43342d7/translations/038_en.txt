Item: Bunka 8th year, Year of the Sheep (1811)
　Item: On the 19th day of the 2nd month, the following was communicated by the o-yōnin (senior household official) in accordance with the attached separate document, and notice was given to each party accordingly.
　　○ On this occasion, a male heir (go-danshi-sama) has been born in the O-oku (the inner residential quarters of the domain lord's palace). Regarding this matter, Kasuya Naiki, the o-kachi-metsuke (inspectors of foot soldiers), and all other personnel under unit command are to be informed, and arrangements are to be made for them to offer their respectful congratulations (kyōetsu).
　　Item: As for the matter of official notification to the public authorities (kōhen), it is to be deferred for the time being. All parties are to take note of this accordingly, and this point is also to be communicated.
　　　　　End

　Item: On the 11th day of the 10th month, Shibuya Gonzaemon communicated the following: As decreed per the attached separate document, the days of abstention from taking life (sesshō-yoke-bi) have been proclaimed.
