1. Bunsei 12, Year of the Ox (1829)

〇 Regarding the matter of sending correspondence to Edo, all parties are to take note as per the attached separate sheet, as conveyed by Takada Hamon.

　　　　　　Separate Sheet

1. On the occasions when the Senior Elders (go-karōchū) send an official letter (hōsho) conveying expressions of reverent congratulation (kyōetsu), the practice of sending a written letter to His Lordship (uebue-sama) to convey congratulations shall henceforth be discontinued.

2. Greetings for the New Year, midsummer, and midwinter shall continue as before; for all other occasions, it shall not be necessary to send letters during the lord's term of residence in Edo (go-nengen-chū).
　　Provision: Should there be an occasion where it becomes necessary to send an unexpected (extraordinary) letter, a notification shall be issued to that effect.
　　Addendum: During the lord's term of residence in Edo, letters are to be sent under a joint signature (renmei).

3. Letters of formal congratulation pertaining to one's personal rank and status (mibun o-rei) shall continue as before.
　　　　　　26th day of the 10th month

---

〇 Regarding the regular couriers (tsugi-hikyaku) dispatched to Edo during the lord's residence there (go-zaifuchū): henceforth, these couriers are to depart by dusk (evening). Therefore, official correspondence (go-yōjō) is to be submitted before that deadline. Should there be urgent business and correspondence cannot be submitted by the said deadline, notification shall be given on each such occasion. Furthermore, as the following has been circulated to all relevant parties via the attached separate sheet, please take note accordingly. The above.
　　　　　　28th day of the 10th month　　　　Takada Hamon

　　　　　　Separate Sheet

1. Even in the case of official correspondence, care is to be taken to ensure that the bulk remains low.

2. For lodging letters (yado-jō), only the necessary business is to be written, and the bulk is to be kept as small as possible.
　　Provision: Bulky sealed packets will not be accepted even if submitted. Strict orders to this effect have been issued on this occasion; please take note accordingly.

3. Letters passed between those outside the circle of relatives and those of particularly close personal connection (enko kankei) are to be considered unnecessary and shall not be permitted.
