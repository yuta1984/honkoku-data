*(Continued from previous page)* — 3rd month

The above was communicated by Watanabe Sanaka via written notice on the 8th day of the 3rd month.

○ On this occasion, as the *Kōgi* (the Shogunate) has graciously announced the appointment of an heir (*go-yōkun-sama*), officials are to submit a letter of congratulation to the Young Lord (*Wakadono-sama*). Regarding the addressee of said letter, it is to be directed to colleagues (*dōyaku-chū*) stationed in Edo.

Item — Regarding matters already established as occasions for presenting formal greetings — namely, the New Year, summer and winter seasons, the Lord's departure for Edo (*go-sankin*), and return to the domain (*go-kikoku*) — as well as the receipt of hawks and skylarks as gifts from the Shogunate, and matters for which notifications are issued by the Senior Councillors (*go-karō-chū*): for all such occasions, letters of congratulation and the like are to be submitted in the same manner as to both Lords (*ryō-tonosama*). Beyond the above, letters shall be submitted as directed by subsequent notifications.

**Item — First year of Kan'en (the year of the Dragon, 1748)**

○ Henceforth, in the event of thunder, earthquakes, and the like, officials need not go out to inquire after the Lord's wellbeing (*go-kigen ukagai*) when the event is generally of moderate intensity. However, when thunder or earthquakes are exceptionally severe, the matter is treated differently. During the nighttime, officials need not go out. Should the event be severe enough to warrant going out, [the inquiry shall be made] on the following day.
