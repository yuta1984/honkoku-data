**Item — 5th Year of An'ei, Year of the Monkey (1776)**

Item — On the 7th day of the 10th month, Suganuma Shichirōzaemon conveyed the following: it had been decreed that on the following day (the 8th), at the hour of Go-hantime (approximately 9:00 a.m.), an escort assembly (*o-tomo-zoroi*) would be formed for a visit to Kōkenji Temple.

Furthermore, as the Senior Councillors (*go-karōchū*) were scheduled to serve as the lord's representatives (*o-naidai*) at Unshōji Temple the following day, he communicated that all matters should be understood in accordance with the separately attached written notice. This was accordingly relayed to colleagues of the same rank, and the *o-kachi-metsuke* (inspectors of foot soldiers) were also notified that henceforth they should perform their duties in accordance with the written notice.

**Item — Procedures when the Senior Councillors visit the temples as the lord's representatives:**

| Position | Location to Proceed To |
|---|---|
| *Jūjō* (Head Priest/Abbot) | The room adjacent to the reception chamber (*o-chaku-za-no-ma*) |
| *Goyōnin* | The outer veranda at the latticed door of the main hall (*hondō*) |
| *O-metsuke* | Same as above |
| *O-kachi-metsuke* | The extended step/threshold of the main hall (*hondō no shikidashi*) |
| *O-bōzu* (domain chaplain attendants) | The middle of the veranda beside the memorial tablet hall (*o-ihaisho*) |

Ritual water for hand-washing (*temizu*) shall be set out and attended to accordingly.

Item — When serving as the lord's representative, an *o-tsukibito* (attending escort) shall be posted along the route to guide the party to the temple; this shall be communicated to the *o-metsuke*.

Item — Regarding temple visits: should a postponement occur after notification has already been issued,

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Senior Councillors (*go-karōchū*) | Shall serve as the lord's representatives in the role of advance guard (*o-senbан*) |
| *Goyōnin* of the advance guard (*o-senban*) | Shall notify the Senior Councillors at the temple of the postponement |
| *Goyōnin* | Shall themselves proceed directly to the temple in the capacity of the advance guard |
