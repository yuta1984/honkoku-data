As for letters of substance, they are exceptional; routine duty letters (*kinjo*) of any kind are strictly unnecessary. Furthermore, regarding lodging letters (*yadojo*), they should be written to be as light in weight as possible, and the word "yadojo" (lodging letter) should be written at the top. For any other unavoidable miscellaneous lodging-related matters, those too should be written and submitted in as compact a form as possible. In the end, if letters pile up and remain undelivered, each person's business will be delayed, and this will on the contrary cause difficulties. It has also been heard that some individuals are requesting favors from officials through personal connections (*mukayori*). Regarding this matter as well, as was established in previous years, it is absolutely forbidden to request either intermediary services or any such favors.

Item — Regarding duty letters (*kinjo*) for New Year's greetings, midsummer and midwinter greetings, and congratulatory or condolence communications: as previously proclaimed, these are strictly unnecessary.

The foregoing matters were proclaimed in previous years as well, but recently they have again fallen into disorder; therefore, this proclamation is hereby reissued.

　　　　　12th month

*The above was communicated on the 16th day of the 12th month at the Edo residence by Inagaki Yasuemon by means of a written notice.*

**Item — Hōreki 6th year, Year of the Rat (1756)**

○ Communicated by means of a receipt document (*tegata*): Since the numerous accompanying letters (*yorijo*) dispatched with the couriers (*ohikyaku*) have been causing difficulties, detailed instructions were previously issued at the Edo residence. Accordingly, the instructions are hereby issued this time as stated on the attached separate sheet. Please be duly informed. Respectfully.

　　　　2nd day of the 2nd month　　Suzuki Genbei　　Sasaji Gonuemon

　　　　　　**Memorandum (*Oboegaki*)**

○ On the occasion of the courier's (*ohikyaku*) coming and going, accompanying letters (*yorijo*) in large numbers have repeatedly caused difficulties and remained undelivered, both at the home domain and in Edo. Accordingly, since detailed instructions have now been issued at the Edo residence, henceforth it is strictly forbidden to submit letters that are heavy or bulky, as well as letters requesting favors through personal connections. Should a letter happen to be heavy or bulky, it will be returned after investigation on that occasion. Moreover, since proclamations regarding this matter were also issued in previous years, it is to be understood by all that, apart from matters of genuine business, it is entirely unnecessary and forbidden to send personal duty letters (*kinjo*) for New Year's greetings, midsummer and midwinter greetings, or congratulatory and condolence occasions.

　　　　　2nd month, Year of the Rat

**Item — Meiwa 1st year, Year of the Monkey (1764)**

Item — On the 12th day of the 5th month, Sasaji Gonuemon communicated that, since the monthly ceremony of greetings (*tsukigoto no rei*) will be received on the coming 15th day, each person should proceed to the castle at the hour of *gohanjitoki* (approximately 5 o'clock in the early morning, i.e., around 8 a.m.). As for those of junior status (*kodomo*), those who had attended the castle on the occasion of the lord's arrival (*gonyūbu*) are to attend at their own discretion.
