**Kansei 1st Year (1789), Year of the Rooster**

**23rd day of the 5th month:** Mizutani Jirōsuke communicated the following regarding the proper manner for submitting letters of inquiry after one's lord's health and congratulatory correspondence going forward:

At the New Year, mid-summer, mid-winter, and other occasions of celebration or respectful acknowledgment, separate letters had previously been submitted to each of the ladies of the household (okatakata-sama). However, given the current season of retrenchment and economy, submitting individual letters to each lady would result in a great many letters. Therefore, henceforth, a single letter (ichishi) addressed collectively to all the ladies of the household is to be composed and submitted.

Furthermore, even when submitting a letter solely to the principal consort (go-mae-sama), it shall be written in the form addressed to the Edo administrators (go-yōnin).

**Model opening phrase when writing to all five ladies (go-go-kata-sama) on a single sheet:**

> "Ippitsu kei-jō itashisōrō. Uebito-sama masumasu go-kigen yoku (etc.). Migi o-kan no tame mōshi-agetaku (etc.)."
> ("I humbly write this letter. I trust that the ladies are in ever-excellent health [etc.]. I write to offer my congratulations [etc.].")

**Model opening phrase when writing to both His Lordship and the Young Lord:**

> "Ippitsu kei-jō itashisōrō. Tono-sama, Waka-tono-sama masumasu go-kigen yoku (etc.). Migi o-kan no tame mōshi-agetaku (etc.)."

　　Proviso: When submitting to only one person, follow the above forms accordingly.

　　　　〆 (End of notice)

---

**22nd day of the 7th month:** Mizutani Jirōsuke communicated the following — that the contents of the attached document are to be understood and followed going forward — and accordingly these instructions were conveyed to the foot inspectors (okachi-metsuke) and the members of their units:

1. When dispatching express couriers (hikyaku), all official correspondence to various destinations shall be kept as compact and unbulky as possible.

2. Concerning letters submitted through personal connections or intermediaries requesting favors from officials: as has been broadly communicated previously, under no circumstances shall such letters be forwarded or acted upon as intermediary.

3. Letters addressed to the residences of those on leave from Edo attendance (Edo-rusu no menmen) shall likewise be kept as compact as possible. In any case, if letters are allowed to accumulate, it will create difficulties in attending to each person's own affairs.

4. Concerning those in permanent Edo residence (Edo-jōfu), their relatives, and those acting as intermediaries for matters such as stipend grants and supernumerary quotas (gigi-saku): truly unavoidable business and correspondence with other locations are excepted; however, all other routine correspondence — regardless of occasion, whether New Year greetings, summer or winter salutations, or matters of good or ill fortune — is absolutely prohibited, even for unit commanders (shihai-gashira).

The above has been communicated previously, but as observance has grown lax and disorderly, this notice is issued anew. Ensure that there are no misunderstandings.

　　　　　(7th month)

---

○ This is communicated by letter. Hitherto, when express couriers (hikyaku) were dispatched or arrived from other provinces, those days falling on inauspicious calendar dates (omoi o-higara) were avoided. However, going forward, no such consideration of auspicious dates need be taken. Please understand this accordingly. That is all.
