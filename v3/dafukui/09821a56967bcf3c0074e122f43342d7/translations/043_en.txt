Item: Bunsei 4th year, Year of the Snake (1821)
　Item: On the 15th day of the 10th month, Mōke Sakuma communicated the following: since the matter in the attached document has been circulated to all relevant parties, they are to understand and act accordingly.
　　　　　　Separate Document
〇Regarding the dispatch of post messengers (hikyaku): even in the case of official correspondence (goyōjō) sent to various quarters, it shall not be made bulky; likewise, letters sent to those on standby duty in Edo (rūsho no menmen) shall, as much as possible, be made compact in size. Furthermore, routine correspondence shall henceforth be considered unnecessary. As there has been a directive from the Road Magistrates (Dōchū Bugyō) on this matter, the above is hereby notified. Take care that there are no misunderstandings.
　　　　　　[End]

〇On this occasion, an agreement has been reached with Lord Matsudaira Hizen-no-kami, and the matter shall be handled with mutual respect (Ryōkei). Be aware of this accordingly.
　　　　　　16th day of the 11th month

Item: Bunsei 5th year, Year of the Horse (1822)
　Item: On the 15th day of the 4th month, the Goyōnin (official intermediary) communicated the following, which was duly passed on to each party:
　〇On the 29th day of last month, Lady Osuma safely delivered a baby girl (known as Lady Kin-hime). Those with organizational responsibilities (kumi shihai) shall communicate this news and convey congratulations (kyōetsu) to their respective superiors. Furthermore, the formal notification to the public authorities (kōben) shall be deferred for the time being. Be aware of this, and communicate it accordingly.
　　　　　[End]

〇As previously circulated, even in the case of official correspondence sent via post messenger to various quarters, it shall be made as compact as possible. However, there may be cases where official items (goyōmono) cannot be made compact. In such cases, the relevant party shall go in person to the residence of the Goyōnin on monthly duty, bringing the item in question to present it. Any item submitted without following this procedure shall be returned, even if it bears the official seal of goyōmono. Since there has been a communication from Edo on this matter, this is hereby formally notified. Please be duly informed. The above.
　　　　4th month, 18th day

As the above was communicated by Shibuya Gonzaemon, a covering note was written and the monthly duty (gumi kōtō) squad leader was instructed to pass the following along to the twelve squad leaders (jūni-gumi kogashira):
〇As the Goyōnin has issued the attached notification, while this has already been instructed previously, once again, when submitting residential letters (yadojō) from the squad, they shall be limited to a thickness of three bu (approximately 9mm), and shall be submitted to the monthly duty residence by the hour of the Eight (around 2:00 PM) on the day of the post messenger's departure.
