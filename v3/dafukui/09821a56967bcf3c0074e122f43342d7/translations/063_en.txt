## Kaei 2nd Year (1849), Year of the Rooster

### Restrictions on Courier Parcel Size

Regarding the enforcement of regulations along the highways, repeated orders have been issued by the public roads (i.e., the Shogunate), and instructions have previously been given to ensure that courier mail boxes and similar parcels do not exceed the permitted volume and weight. Therefore, please make every effort to keep official correspondence (goyōjō) as well as lodging communications (yadojō) as compact as possible. Furthermore, letters related to routine duty inspections must not be submitted at all. Should it be unavoidable that an official letter becomes bulky, please go to the Office of Secretaries (oyūhitsu-beya) and notify the current month-duty officer in advance. Instructions have been given not to accept parcels without prior authorization. If lodging communications are excessively bulky, they may be left without being handled. Regarding this matter, strict notices were already issued in Tenpō 7 (1836) and Tenpō 13 (1842), but it has come to our attention that compliance has gradually slackened, and therefore this new order has been issued. Please be aware of and act accordingly.

1st day of the 7th month — Inoko Jōuemon (addressee) / Tominaga Shinzaemon (issuer)

Having informed the relevant parties by means of the ten-tori (circular confirmation) method, we attached the following separate notice within the main document and instructed the small unit commanders (kogashira) to communicate this to all twelve unit leaders.

> This time, a notice has been transmitted from the Senior Officials (Goyōnin) as per the attached document. Although strict instructions have been given on numerous previous occasions, compliance has recently slackened and letters have become bulky. Henceforth, any parcels found to be oversized will not be accepted. From now on, if letters are submitted that exceed three bu in thickness/volume, they will be left behind. Each unit should ensure that lodging communications and similar documents are kept as compact as possible, and they must be submitted to the month-duty officer's residence by the Hour of the Monkey (approximately 2:00 PM) on the day the courier departs.
>
> End.
