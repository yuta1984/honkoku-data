## Kaei 2nd Year (1849, Year of the Rooster)

### Regulations on the Volume of Baggage Along the Road

Regarding the enforcement of controls along roads, as there have been repeated directives from the public highways (i.e., the shogunate), it has been previously ordered that courier pouches and letter boxes should not become excessively heavy or bulky. Therefore, official correspondence as well as lodging letters (*shuku-jō*) should be made as compact as possible. Routine duty inspection letters and the like shall not be submitted at all. If, however, official correspondence unavoidably becomes bulky, one should go to the Scribes' Office (*o-yuhitsu-beya*) and report the matter to the current monthly duty officer. Until instructions are given, the correspondence will not be accepted. In cases where lodging letters are too bulky, they may be left behind without further negotiation. On this matter, strict directives were issued in Tenpō 7 (1836) and Tenpō 13 (1842), but it has been heard that compliance has gradually slackened. Therefore, a renewed directive has been issued at this time. Please take note accordingly.

1st day of the 7th month　　To: Inoko Jōemon　　From: Tominaga Shinzaemon

As the above was communicated through a designated officer (*ten-tori*), the following separate note was attached to the above text and the twelve-unit squad leaders (*kumi-kogashira*) were instructed to pass it along:

> Regarding the separate document recently communicated from the Chief Steward (*go-yōnin*), despite repeated strict instructions issued previously, it has recently become slack and letter bulk has increased. Henceforth, oversized items will not be accepted. From now on, if correspondence exceeding three *bu* in thickness is submitted, it will be left behind. Therefore, each unit is again reminded to make lodging letters and similar correspondence as compact as possible, and to submit them to the monthly duty officer's residence by the Hour of the Rooster (*yatsu-doki*, approximately 2:00 p.m.) on the day the courier departs.
>
> End.
