**[Regulations Regarding Honorific Titles]**

○ Tono-sama (the Lord), Waka-tono-sama (the Young Lord), Ogimaru-sama, Omae-sama, Sadahime-sama, Hirohime-sama, and Go-shoshi-sama (children born of concubines).

1. When persons of the lord's family have been adopted out of the domain household into another house, male members are to continue to be referred to using the honorific suffix "sama," and female members likewise are to continue to be addressed using "sama."

2. Members of the affiliated branch families (go-ichimон) and persons accorded bilateral respect (go-ryōkei) are all to be addressed using "sama."
　　Note: Members of the Three Tokugawa Branch Houses (go-sanke), the Three Tokugawa Cadet Houses (go-sankyo), and all other such persons are also to be addressed using "sama."

　　　　(End of notice)　17th day of the 8th month

The foregoing was communicated as having been proclaimed, by the household official (go-yōnin) Watanabe Jūrozaemon.

1. On the 19th day of the 10th month of the same year, in Edo, a written notice to the same effect was delivered by Nakane Yukinojō. At the end of that notice, the following was appended:

　　**Regarding posthumous titles for those who have passed away:**
　　　Go-daidai-sama (successive lords), Omae-sama, Go-shoshi-sama

　　Among members of the lord's family who have been adopted out into other households: male members are to be addressed using "sama," and female members are likewise to be addressed using "sama."
　　　Note: If a person has been adopted into the shogunal household (kōgi go-yō), "sama" is to be used.

　　Members of affiliated branch families and persons accorded bilateral respect are all to be addressed using "sama."
　　　Note: Seiganin-sama, Tensōin-sama, and Kakuryōin-sama are to be addressed using "sama."

　　All parties are to take note of the foregoing and to use this form of address in writing from this point forward, as has been proclaimed.

**Kansei 6th Year (1794), Year of the Tiger**

○ Hitherto, when the lord's party crossed at Keya using sculled boats (kuribune) or ferryboats, all other ferryboats in the vicinity were halted and held back. However, it is hereby proclaimed that henceforth, even when the lord's ferry crossing is taking place, other boats before and after are not required to be stopped, and are to be allowed to continue ferrying passengers back and forth as normal. All relevant parties are to handle this accordingly.

　　　　　6th day of the 10th month

The foregoing was conveyed by Matsudaira Kanameto (Kanamebito); accordingly, it was communicated to the Keya retainer corps (侍中, samurai-jū) by means of a verbal notice in writing.
