Item: Kyōhō 13, Year of the Monkey (1728)

○ This is to inform you by letter. Following the recent order for simplification of procedures, the practice of sending letters to Edo regarding New Year's greetings, summer and winter seasonal salutations, congratulatory occasions, and personal formal greetings shall henceforth be discontinued. Furthermore, formal correspondence (hōsho) shall also not be submitted. From this point forward, on such occasions, you are to appear at the Nanatsu-gura guard post between the fourth hour of the morning and before the eighth hour, and present yourself to the duty officer on call. This matter is hereby communicated in advance as directed. The above.

　　　　Sixth month, thirteenth day

Postscript: Regarding summer and winter seasonal salutations, please appear and pay your respects after receiving word from Edo. The above.

　　　　　Lord Tsuchiya Jūrōemon　　　Utsunomiya Kageyu
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Suganuma Ichizaemon

　　Note: From the sixteenth day of the same month, as official business will be conducted at the Nanatsu-gura guard post, a note of cancellation from Kageyu is recorded here.

○ This is to inform you by letter. Following the recent order for simplification of procedures, the submission of letters to Edo for New Year's greetings, summer and winter seasonal salutations, congratulatory occasions, and personal formal greetings shall all be discontinued. It was communicated that henceforth, on such occasions, you should present yourselves at the Nanatsu-gura guard post. However, as there are other matters that would be inconvenienced by such an arrangement, from this point forward, a fixed schedule shall be established so that members may appear together. Therefore, regarding future procedures, please be guided by the attached written notice. The above.
