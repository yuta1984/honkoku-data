Item: The thirteenth year of Kyōhō (the year of the Monkey, 1728)

○ This is to inform you by letter: In connection with the recent order for simplification of ceremonies, the practice of sending letters to Edo for year-beginning greetings, summer and winter seasonal inquiries after one's lord's health, congratulatory occasions, and personal ceremonial greetings is hereby discontinued. Furthermore, formal letters written on hōsho paper are also not to be submitted. Henceforth, on such occasions, you are to proceed to the Nanatsu-gura guardhouse between the hour of four in the morning (approximately 10 a.m.) and before the hour of eight (approximately 2 p.m.), and present yourself to the on-duty colleagues to make your address. This matter is communicated in advance as required. That is all.

　　　13th day of the 6th month

Addendum: Regarding the summer and winter seasonal inquiries after the lord's health, please attend and make your inquiry after receiving word from Edo. That is all.

　　　　Tsuchiya Jūrōemon (addressee)　　　Utsunomiya Kageyu
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Suganuma Ichizaemon

　　Note: As official business at the Nanatsu-gura guardhouse commenced from the 16th of the same month, there is a recorded note of cancellation from Kageyu.

○ This is to inform you by letter: Regarding the recent order for simplification of ceremonies, the practice of sending letters to Edo for year-beginning greetings, summer and winter seasonal inquiries, congratulatory occasions, and personal ceremonial greetings has been discontinued. While it was communicated that henceforth on such occasions you should proceed to the Nanatsu-gura guardhouse, as doing so would cause inconveniences in other matters, from this point forward a fixed date shall be established and colleagues shall attend accordingly. Therefore, please take note that future arrangements will be as described in the attached written notice. That is all.
