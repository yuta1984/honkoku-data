Item: On the 19th day of the 6th month, regarding the matter of personnel name records (naamoto) for the御持筒組 (O-mochizutsu-gumi, the musket-bearer unit): until now, such records were submitted via the御用人 (goyōnin, senior administrator) and verified by the御目付 (o-metsuke, inspector/overseer) before being communicated. However, from this year onward, the inspection of name records for all units (shokumi) and palanquin-bearers (o-kago no mono) has been placed under the jurisdiction of the御目付. Accordingly, nine volumes of registers (o-chō-ai kusetsu) were circulated from the御用人. In connection with this, each party was summoned to the residence of the monthly duty officer (tsukiban) Miyakita Chōzaemon, and a written notice was issued requesting the submission of name records. Additionally, at that time, instructions regarding the procedures for hiring new members of the units were set out in a single document, and parties were instructed to submit these by the 26th of the coming month. As submissions were made one after another, corrections were added to the discrepancies found in the official registers, and the updated records were forwarded to Ōmiya Hikoemon.
Item: Henceforth, when hiring members for the units, not only those who have not yet completed the prescribed years of service, but also those who have completed the required years, must in all cases be hired only after making an official inquiry.
Item: The name records (naamoto-gaki) for unit members were previously to be submitted to the御用人, but henceforth they are to be submitted to the御目付. The method of recording remains the same as that previously submitted to the御用人.
