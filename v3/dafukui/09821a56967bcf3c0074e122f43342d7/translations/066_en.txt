**[Right folio]**

(Order in which letters are to be submitted:)
　　　　His Lordship (the Domain Lord)
　　　　Lady Shōei-in
　　　　Her Ladyship (the Lord's consort)
　　　　Items to be addressed in the above order.

Item: Regarding the matter of sending letters to Edo to offer congratulations and inquiries after one's health on the same day, from henceforth the matter is to be handled as per the attached separate document, as communicated by the said person.

　Sub-item: On occasions when congratulations (kyōetsu) are to be offered in response to a notice from the Senior Councillors (Karō), it is no longer necessary to submit a letter to His Lordship as was previously done. Instead, a letter of congratulation is to be submitted to Her Ladyship. It shall not be necessary to issue a notice for this on each subsequent occasion.

　Sub-item: At New Year, during the height of summer (shochū), and during the cold season (kanchū), letters are to be submitted to both His Lordship and Her Ladyship.

　Sub-item: Regarding the submission of letters of formal gratitude relating to one's status, this shall continue as before.
　Regarding all other occasions requiring the ad hoc submission of letters beyond the above, notification shall be issued each time as has been done previously.

Item: The format for writing letters is as follows:
　　　When addressing His Lordship and Her Ladyship, on a single sheet of paper:
　　　　"I humbly take up my brush to write…"
　　　　"I trust that your most esteemed self continues to enjoy the best of health…"
