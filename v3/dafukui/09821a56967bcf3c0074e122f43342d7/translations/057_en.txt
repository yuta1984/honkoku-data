Item: 14th year of Tenpō, Year of the Hare (1843)
　　　Shibuya Gonzaemon communicated the following:

〇 Attendance at castle (tōjō) for the New Year and the Five Seasonal Festivals (gosekku) is to proceed as in previous years.
　　　　　　　(12th month only)
　1st day, 15th day, and 28th day:
　For the above dates, one should proceed to the castle by the half-past-fifth-hour (approximately 9:00 a.m.) without waiting for a formal notice (ofure). If attendance is to be cancelled, a formal notice will be issued.

　7th day of the 1st month, 1st day of the 3rd month, 15th day of the 7th month:
　For the above dates, attendance at court is not required.

Item: Regarding attendance at the castle by children and grandchildren who have completed their formal audience (omemie) and are of the rank of sodome and above — this is left to individual discretion.

　　　　　13th day of the 6th month

In Edo:
〇 Regarding Lady Shōei-in: in the event of an emergency, it has already been discussed and approved that she should prepare to withdraw to the Main Keep (Hon-maru). 

　However, depending on the circumstances, it is thought that she may also withdraw to the Middle Residence (nakayashiki) on Reigan-jima, or to the residence at Honjo Nakanogō.

　　　　　21st day of the 11th month
