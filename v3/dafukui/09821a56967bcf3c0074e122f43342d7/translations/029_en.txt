**Kansei 7th Year (1795), Year of the Rabbit**

○ Regarding the death anniversary (忌日, ki-nichi) of Lady Kōan-in: it has been communicated that henceforth the observance date is to fall on the 8th day of the month.

　　　　　　3rd day of the 2nd month

The above was communicated by Matsudaira Kaname.

**Kansei 8th Year (1796), Year of the Dragon**

1. Regarding the purification rituals (o-kiyome): there have been pronouncements issued since the change of the Shogunal administration (go-daitai), as well as further pronouncements made in accordance with those. However, as there have been many errors in practice, new pronouncements have been issued today; henceforth, all are instructed to ensure there are no misunderstandings in this regard.

1. When making a visit to the Tōshō-gū Shrine, purification (o-kiyome) is to begin from dusk (sunset), and persons in mourning attire (kibuku) within the domain residence (o-yashiki) are to be inspected and accounted for.

1. The same applies during the prayer ceremonies (go-kitō) of the 1st, 5th, and 9th months.

1. Similarly, for visits to other shrines, regarding purification procedures: from dusk onward, those involved in handling offerings (meshi-age-mono) who are in mourning are to be identified and checked; those otherwise in a mourning period (fuku-kakari) may remain within the domain residence without issue. However, those who are to join the procession (o-tomo) must all be inspected regardless of whether they are in mourning or not.

The above has been proclaimed for both Edo and the home domain.

　　　　　　28th day of the 4th month

　　*(Note by the compiler: The above is a written notice (kakitsuke) that was handed by the senior elders (go-karō-chū) to the inspectors (go-metsuke) at the Edo establishment. No record of this being communicated via the Go-yōnin for either the eastern or northern districts appears to remain; however, it is recorded here as related information.)* 《Eight hundred ri》

1. On the 21st day of the 7th month, there was a communication by Mōri Denzaburō by means of a written notice regarding the procedures for the lord's procession (o-tomo gyōretsu); however, as this appears in the section on orders and commands (meirei no bu), it is omitted here.
