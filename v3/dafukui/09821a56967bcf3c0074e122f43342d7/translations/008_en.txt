**Item — The second year of Enkyō (the year of the Ox, 1745)**

○ On this occasion, it has been decreed that *yoriki* (mounted constables) and all lower-ranking officials (*ko-yakunin*) are to be permitted to attend and view the Noh performance (*go-nō haiken*). In connection with this, an inquiry was made by the group leaders (*kumigashira-chū*) as to whether those individuals should be permitted to carry their swords (*taitō*). In response, it was communicated through Amagata Kazuma that: there is no need to remove the short sword (*wakizashi*) [for ordinary occasions]; however, at the time when the Noh performance is performed in the lord's presence (*go-mae*), it has been decreed that the short sword is to be removed from the waist. Accordingly, all persons are to be aware of and comply with this instruction. Furthermore, regarding the *o-kachi-metsuke* (inspectors of foot soldiers), an inquiry was likewise made, and it was determined that they should follow the same protocol as the *yoriki*. However, it was communicated by Akashi Shōgen that those *o-kachi-metsuke* who are on duty, or who appear in the performance of their official responsibilities, need not remove their short swords. This matter was then conveyed to Matsubara Jirōzaemon.
