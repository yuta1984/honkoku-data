　　　　　　　(1st Month)

　Addendum: As for letters written by retainers, these are strictly prohibited without exception. On this point, masters (i.e., employers of such retainers) are to issue firm instructions to their subordinates.

　The above written notice was handed over by the Sōja-ban officers at the Nanatsu-gura guard post.

　　　　　　　16th day of the 1st month


【Pasted note】

Item — Enkyō 2nd year, Year of the Ox (1745):

　○ On this occasion, it has been commanded that yoriki (mounted police deputies) and petty officials (ko-yakunin), as a whole body, be permitted to view the Nō performance. In connection with this, the group leaders (kumigashira) have submitted an inquiry as to whether swords should be removed. The response given is that there is no need to remove the short sword (wakizashi); however, it has been decreed that when the Nō performance is held in the lord's presence (go-zen), the wakizashi is to be removed from the hip cord (koshi) and set aside. All parties are to acknowledge and comply with this decree. So reported Amagata Kazuma. Regarding the same matter as it pertains to the o-kachi-metsuke (inspectors of foot soldiers), upon making inquiry, it was determined that they are to do the same as the yoriki. However, it has been further communicated by Akashi Shōgen that when the o-kachi-metsuke are on duty roster, or when appearing in an official capacity, they need not remove their wakizashi. This ruling has been conveyed to Matsubara Jirōzaemon.
