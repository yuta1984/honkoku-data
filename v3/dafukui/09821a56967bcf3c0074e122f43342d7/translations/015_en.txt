**Memorandum of Verbal Instructions (*Kōtatsu no Oboegaki*)**

Item — Regarding all celebratory visits (*go-kan*), as well as visits to inquire after the lord's health (*go-kigen ukagai*) on occasions such as severe thunderstorms, earthquakes, and other such events: since these will be communicated through the *go-yōnin* (chamberlains/stewards), those for whom no such notification has been given need not present themselves.

　　*However, regarding inquiries after the lord's health during midsummer and midwinter, this shall be conducted as before.*

Item — During the lord's residence in Edo (*go-zaifuchū*), the practice of submitting celebratory addresses and inquiries after the lord's health by letter (*shojo*) shall also be conducted as described above.

　　*However, regarding New Year's congratulations, and celebratory addresses on occasions when a circular notice has been issued by the senior councilors (*go-karōchū*), as well as midsummer and midwinter health inquiries, these shall be conducted as before.*

Item — Regarding New Year's congratulations and midsummer/midwinter health inquiries addressed to the *go-mae-sama* (the lord's consort/lady): these shall be conducted in the same manner as during the lord's residence in Edo. As for unexpected celebratory addresses and letters of health inquiry beyond the above, these too shall be communicated through the *go-yōnin*.

　　*However, while the lord is residing in the home domain (*go-zaigokuchū*), when a circular notice is issued by the senior councilors, celebratory addresses shall be made. Matters other than these shall be conducted as described in the main text.*

　　　　　1st day of the 8th month

**Procedure for the General Ceremony of Greetings on the 15th Day of the 8th Month**

Item — When the lord appears in the outer hall (*omote*), those who still wear their forelocks (*maegami*) — including the physicians — shall line up together in the *Tokei-no-ma* (the "Clock Room") and present their greetings as one group, which the *go-yōnin* shall announce.

　　*However, those with forelocks and the physicians shall be seated in separate sections in their line. The physicians shall be arranged in their usual place (behind the *tokonoma* alcove in the great hall, *ohiroma*).*

Item — *Kōchi* (high-stipend retainers) and their sons; *go-bangashira* (troop commanders); *yoriai* (assembled retainers) and their sons; *bangai* (supernumerary) sons; down to the lowest *bangai*.

　　*However, the *yoriai bangai* and the *yoriai* sons' *bangai* are to present themselves as such.*

The above shall line up from approximately the second mat (*nijo-me*) from the far end of the great hall (*ōhiroma*).
