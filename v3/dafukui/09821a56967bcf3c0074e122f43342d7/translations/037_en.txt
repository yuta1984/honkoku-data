Item: Bunka 6th year, Year of the Snake (1809)
　Item: On the 14th day of the 9th month, Tagaya Gonnosuke communicated the following: His Lordship Matsudaira Sakonoe-no-kami has changed his title to "Shuri-no-daibu." Henceforth, the honorific dual-respect protocol (ryōkei) is to be applied in addressing him. This matter is to be communicated to all officials so that they are duly informed, and accordingly, notice was given to each party.
　　　　　End

Item: On the same day, Matsudaira Yōnin communicated the following: Upon the passing of Lady Ryūtokuin, a horse bit (kutsuwa) was discreetly bestowed upon each of the officials as a personal bequest (imotsu). Regarding the expression of gratitude for this gift, it was communicated that a letter of thanks should be submitted to the Senior Councillor (chūrō) in Edo.
　　　　　End

Bunka 7th year, Year of the Horse (1810)
　Item: On the 11th day of the 1st month, Amemori Genshirō communicated the following: As decreed per the attached separate document, all parties are to take note accordingly.
　Item: His Lordship Saheiza-no-suke has changed his title to "Saheiza-no-kami."
　Item: Regarding the internal honorary protocol (go-naibu) for His Lordship Kuki Izumi-no-kami, the dual-respect honorific (ryōkei) has been mutually agreed upon. For matters pertaining to the inner quarters (naka-mukai) and official public affairs (kōhen no tsuku), the previous one-sided protocol (kataage) continues as before; all other matters are to employ the full dual-respect protocol (ryōkei).
　　　　　End

Item: On the 26th day of the 10th month, Nagami Taemon communicated the following: In accordance with the attached separate document, and with the intention of informing Kasuya Naiki and all persons of equivalent or higher standing under his jurisdiction, notice was also given to Naiki and the O-kachi-metsuke (inspectors of foot soldiers).
　Item: On the occasion of the martial arts review (bugei o-ran), officials are to attend as much as possible without claiming exemption from duty (yōhiki). However, in cases of unavoidable official duties, one should consult with colleagues of the same post (dōyaku-chū), and arrangements for duty exemption are to be settled and established in advance.
　　　　　End
