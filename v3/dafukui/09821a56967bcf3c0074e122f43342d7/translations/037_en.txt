Item: Bunka 6, Year of the Snake (1809)
　Item: On the 14th day of the 9th month, Tagaya Gonnosuke reported the following: Lord Matsudaira Sakon'e-no-kami has changed his name to Shuridaifu. Henceforth, he is to be accorded the honorific treatment of "Ryō-keii" (dual-direction salutation). It was communicated that this matter should be relayed to the relevant officials as guidance, and accordingly it was conveyed to each of them.
　　　　　End

　Item: On the same day, Matsudaira Yōnin reported the following: Upon the passing of the Ryūtoku-in (a deceased person of high standing), as a bequest, each of the officials was privately granted one horse bridle (kutsuwa). Regarding the expression of gratitude for this gift, it was communicated that a letter should be sent to the O-chūrō (Senior Councillor) in Edo.
　　　　　End

Bunka 7, Year of the Horse (1810)
　Item: On the 11th day of the 1st month, Amemori Genshirō reported the following: As set forth in the attached document, this is to be understood accordingly.
　Item: Lord Hyōe-no-suke has changed his name to Lord Hyōe-no-kami.
　Item: It has been agreed in private with Lord Kuki Izumi-no-kami regarding the "Ryō-keii" (dual-direction salutation). For matters concerning the inner household (O-naka-muki) and official public-facing affairs (kōhen), the previous single-direction salutation (kata-umai) is to continue as before; for all other matters, the dual-direction salutation (ryō-keii) is to apply.
　　　　　End

Item: On the 26th day of the 10th month, Nagami Taemon reported the following: In accordance with the attached document, the relevant understanding is to be conveyed to Kasuya Naiki and all those of equivalent rank and above under his jurisdiction. Accordingly, the matter was communicated to Naiki and also to the O-kachi-metsuke (Foot Inspector).
　Item: At the time of the martial arts review (Bugei O-ran), officials are to attend as much as possible without claiming exemption on grounds of duty. However, in cases where unavoidable official business arises, the matter is to be inquired of one's fellow duty-officers, and a prior determination is to be made as to what shall be classified as an excused duty absence.
　　　　　End
