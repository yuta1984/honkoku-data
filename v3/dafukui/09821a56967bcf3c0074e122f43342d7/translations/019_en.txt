## An'ei 6th Year (1777), Year of the Rooster

### 17th Day of the 3rd Month — Conveyance by Hayashi Hachiemon at the Edo Residence:

It was conveyed that in the event of a postponement (*en'in*) of the lord's fixed monthly visits to the temples, the Senior Elders (*go-karōchū*) shall perform the duties of proxy representative (*o-naidai*). On such occasions, one of our number (i.e., from among the *o-kachi-metsuke*) shall be in attendance, as has been decreed. Furthermore, all matters are to be understood in accordance with a separately attached written notice, which was duly conveyed. Said written notice is recorded below.

---

#### Monthly Temple Visits During the Lord's Residence in the Domain

| Posthumous Buddhist Name | Date | Temple |
|---|---|---|
| Jōkōin-sama | 8th day | Zōjōji |
| Genryūin-sama | 18th day | Tentokuji |
| Tokushōin-sama | 21st day | Same temple (Tentokuji) |
| Kakuryōin-sama | 22nd day | Ryōun'in |

The above are regular temple visits. *Proviso:* In the event of postponement, the *go-karōchū* (Senior Elders) shall serve as proxy.

---

- **Item —** New Year visits (*nenshigyō*) and Bon season visits (*bon-chū*) shall continue as before; in the event of postponement, the *go-karōchū* shall serve as proxy.

- **Item —** Should the *go-karōchū* have other official duties, the *go-chūrō* (Middle Elders) shall perform the duty; should those also have duties, the *o-shoin-bangashira* (Captain of the Shoin Guard) shall perform it; and should the *o-shoin-bangashira* be unable to perform it, the *Goyōnin* shall do so.

- **Item —** Personnel to proceed to the temple on occasions when the *go-karōchū* serves as proxy:

| Position | Duty / Notes |
|---|---|
| *O-metsuke* (Inspector) | Should they have other duties, the *o-soba-monogashira* shall serve in their place; should the *o-soba-monogashira* also have duties, Fujita Kazaemon and Dōji Shichizaemon shall perform the duty. |
| *O-kachi-metsuke* | To accompany |
| *O-bōzu* | To serve as attendant for ritual hand-washing (*temizu*) |

In accordance with the above, the above personnel shall proceed to the temple in advance and shall go out to receive the *go-karōchū* upon their arrival.
