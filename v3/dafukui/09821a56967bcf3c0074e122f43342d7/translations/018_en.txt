*(Continued from the previous page — procedures in the event of postponement:)*

| Position | Arrangement |
|---|---|
| *O-metsuke* (Inspector) | As there is no advance guard (*o-senban*), the *o-soba-monogashira* (captain of the lord's personal attendants) shall serve in place of the *o-metsuke* |
| *O-kachi-metsuke* | Shall not attend |
| *O-bōzu* (domain chaplain attendants) | As they shall not attend, the ritual hand-washing (*temizu*) shall be arranged by the resident monks of the temple |

As the above has been decreed, Suganuma Shichirōzaemon conveyed that henceforth, after consultation among colleagues of the same rank, duties shall be performed in accordance with the written notice.

**Item — 28th day of the 10th month:** Suganuma Shichirōzaemon conveyed the following: regarding the *o-naidai* (representative) and *o-daisan* (proxy worship) at the death anniversary observances (*kijitsu*) for the ladies of the inner quarters (*go-jochū-sama-gata*), it has been decreed on this occasion that the *go-chūrō* (Middle Elders) shall perform this duty. A detailed written notice was handed over to the effect that one *o-kachi-metsuke* shall also attend on such occasions, and that arrangements for *o-tsukibito* (accompanying escorts) shall likewise be made. The contents were accordingly conveyed to the *o-kachi-metsuke*. *Proviso:* Should the *go-chūrō* have other official duties, the *o-shoin-bangashira* (Captain of the Shoin Guard) shall perform the duty; should the *o-shoin-bangashira* also have other official duties, the *Goyōnin* shall perform it.

The above applies during years when the lord resides in the domain (*go-zaikoku*): proxy worship shall be assigned to the *go-chūrō*. In years of the lord's absence, the previous customs shall apply.

**Procedures at the temple when the *go-chūrō* performs proxy worship (*o-daibai*):**

| Position | Location to Proceed To |
|---|---|
| *Jūjō* (Head Priest/Abbot) | The middle of the veranda beside the memorial tablet hall (*o-ihaisho*) |
| *O-kachi-metsuke* | To proceed to the extended step/threshold of the main hall (*o-dō no shikidashi*) |
| *O-bōzu* | To remain outside the latticed door (*ando*) of the same hall and attend to the ritual hand-washing (*temizu*) |

**Item —** When performing proxy worship, an *o-tsukibito* (accompanying escort) shall be posted along the route to guide the party to the temple; this matter shall be directed by the *o-metsuke*.
