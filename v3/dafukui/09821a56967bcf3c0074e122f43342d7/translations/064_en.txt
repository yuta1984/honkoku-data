### In Edo: Renaming of the Temporary Audience Chamber

Regarding the renovation and extension of the main reception room (*omote-ozashiki*), it has been directed that it shall henceforth be referred to as the "Temporary Audience Chamber" (*kari-o-shoin*).

19th day of the 9th month

The above was communicated by the Chief Steward.
As the above was communicated by the Chief Steward, it was relayed to each relevant party.

> Note: On the 26th, a birth took place (a baby was born), but on the morning of the 27th, the child passed away. There were no official days of abstention (*go-chōji-bi*) declared, but the situation was as described.

### Same (In Edo): Exemption of the Inspector (*metsuke*) from Escort Duty During Nearby Fires

The Inspector (*o-metsuke*) had previously been assigned to accompany Lady Shōei-in during her outings in the event of a fire in the vicinity (*chika-bi*). This duty has now been rescinded.

7th day of the 11th month

The above should be communicated to Ōmiya Tōma.

---

### Same (In Edo): Postponement of the Bridegroom's Visit, Bride's Return Visit, and Father-in-Law's Visit

It has been directed that the bridegroom's visit (*muko-iri*), the bride's return visit and presentation (*sato-hirō*), and the father-in-law's visit (*shūto-iri*) are all to be postponed for this year, and that these ceremonies shall be completed in early spring of the coming year.

14th day of the 11th month

As the above was communicated by the Chief Steward, it was conveyed to the Foot Inspectors (*o-kachi-metsuke*) accordingly.
