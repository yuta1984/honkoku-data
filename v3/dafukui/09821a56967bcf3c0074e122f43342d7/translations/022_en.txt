**Tenmei 2nd Year (1782), Year of the Tiger**

**9th Day of the 5th Month**
Isono Tamiya communicated the following decree, as stated in the attached document:

　　*Attached Document*

In cases where persons in mourning are present during purification ceremonies (o-kiyome), it is henceforth decreed that those in mourning shall be relieved of duty starting from the seventh hour (approx. 4:00 p.m.) of the preceding day.

〆

**9th Day of the 10th Month**
Isono Tamiya communicated the following: In accordance with the attached written directive, it is henceforth decreed that in both Edo and the home domain, all courier correspondence (o-okuri-gaki) and official dispatches (go-yōjō) shall be written using the terminology specified in the directive. Accordingly, copies of said directive were prepared and distributed to the foot inspectors (okachi-metsuke) and the unit clerks (kumi-monokaki), with instructions to ensure there are no errors henceforth. Furthermore, as it was decreed that the directive be forwarded to Edo with instructions to communicate this intent, it was sent via express courier on the 17th day of the same month. The directive reads as follows:

- For the lord's departure:　　　　　　→ write **"go-shutsu-den"** (御出殿, "Departed the residence")
- For the lord's return:　　　　　　　 → write **"go-ki-den"** (御帰殿, "Returned to the residence")
- For indicating the lord's destination: → write **"izukata e irasare"** (何方へ被為入, "Proceeded to [such-and-such place]")
- For the lord's stopover location:　　→ write **"go-zensho, izukata e irasare"** (御膳所何方へ被為入, "Proceeded to [such-and-such place] for a meal stop")

The above instructions for how to understand and write these terms were already decreed in the previous Year of the Rat (Tenmei 1, 1780), but as they had not been properly observed, the decree is issued once again. Should there be any errors henceforth, a stern reproof will be issued. The directive shall be conveyed in writing to the inspectors (metsuke).

　　　　10th Month, 9th Day

**Tenmei 4th Year (1784), Year of the Dragon**

**8th Day of the 5th Month**
Hashiba (Inaba) Oribu communicated the following: It was decreed as stated in the attached document, and all parties shall take note accordingly:

- What was formerly called **"o-ima"** (御居間, "private sitting room") shall henceforth be referred to as **"o-za-no-ma"** (御座之間, "audience chamber / sitting room").
- What was formerly called **"o-nema"** (御寝間, "bedchamber") shall henceforth be referred to as **"o-kyūsoku"** (御休息, "resting room").

〆

**24th Day of the 11th Month**
The following was communicated by Makino Shūzen via attached document, and was accordingly forwarded to Edo:

Regarding the tea-serving attendant detail (o-cha bentō): although previously one foot soldier (okachi) was assigned to accompany this duty, in other domain households no such arrangement exists, and it is an established practice that a Buddhist attendant (bōzu) must be assigned. Therefore, it is henceforth decreed that, in both Edo and the home domain, only the tea-ceremony Buddhist attendant (o-cha-kata go-bōzu) shall accompany the tea duty, and the foot soldier (okachi) shall no longer be assigned. As regards Edo, this arrangement shall take effect from the New Year's Day visit to Edo Castle by the Young Lord; as regards the home domain, it shall take effect from the lord's next imminent departure from the residence. That is all.

　　　　11th Month, 24th Day
