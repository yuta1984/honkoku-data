Item: Tenpō 13, Year of the Tiger (1842)

〇 Regarding Lady Teishōin: since last autumn she has been unwell and has not recovered. As she will be moving from her current residence, she is expected to remain in Edo for the time being. Please be aware of this. The above is for your information.

　　21st day of the 2nd month　　To: Tanabe Gotayū　　Takada Hamon

Addendum: The following is communicated by Senior Steward Hiramoto Sakunoemon. The separate document reads as follows.

〇 Regarding the regulation of traffic along the highways: as the bakufu (public authorities) have issued repeated directives on this matter, instructions have been given that the boxes carrying official correspondence by express messengers (hikyaku) must not exceed the prescribed weight limit (kanme). Although a notice was circulated in the Year of the Monkey (申年) some years ago, it has come to our attention that of late this has been treated with carelessness, which is a matter of concern. On this particular occasion, the bakufu is enforcing especially strict controls, and any cargo that exceeds the prescribed weight limit, even by a small amount, will not be permitted to pass. Therefore, as per the notice issued in previous years, all official correspondence (goyōjō) as well as accommodation letters (yadojō) should be kept as compact and low in bulk as possible. Furthermore, routine duty inspection letters need not be submitted at all. Should an official letter unavoidably become bulky, please come to us — those of us on monthly duty rotation (tsukiban) — and report the matter. Until such instructions are given, even official letters that are oversized will be refused receipt. Regarding accommodation letters as well, should a bulky one be submitted, if one were to negotiate the matter at the time with the scribes' room (goyūhitsu-beya) or the lay monks' room (gobōzu), it would cause delays and obstruct official business; and there may even be cases where letters are left behind without any negotiation at all. Please bear this in mind. The above is for your information.

　　　　　4th day of the 9th month
