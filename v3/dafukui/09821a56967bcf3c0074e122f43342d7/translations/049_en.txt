　　　　　　4th day of the 4th month　　Yokota Tayū-dono　　Akita Michinosuke

　　　　　　　Separate Document (Betsushi)

〇 As was communicated to all relevant parties on the 28th day of the 10th month of the previous Year of the Ox: all letters — whether lodging letters (yado-jō) or official correspondence (go-yōjō) — are to be made as compact and lightweight as possible and submitted before dusk; furthermore, bulky sealed items (kasadaka no fūmotsu) shall not be accepted, as has already been notified. Should bulky letters of either public or private nature be dispatched and then returned on account of their size, this would in turn cause unnecessary inconvenience; therefore, all parties are once again reminded to take care that no such bulky items are sent.

　　　　　〆

Appendix

1. Tenpō 4, Year of the Snake (1833), 4th month: The following was ordered to the heads (kogashira) of the twelve [units/groups] of御■:

〇 When official couriers (o-hikyaku) are dispatched, it has been repeatedly ordered that the official correspondence of members of the O-mochizutsu-gumi (the musket-bearer unit) and their kogashira (unit heads), as well as the lodging letters (yado-jō) of those on duty assignment (tsume no mono), are to be made as compact and lightweight as possible, and submitted from each unit in bundles of no more than five (go-bu bakari). However, it has come to attention that items are being submitted separately under separate covers via intermediaries. Henceforth, not only separate covers, but even letters from kogashira, if submitted in bulky sealed form, shall without exception be refused. Therefore, kogashira are once again reminded to take it upon themselves to ensure that no excess bulk accumulates before submission.
