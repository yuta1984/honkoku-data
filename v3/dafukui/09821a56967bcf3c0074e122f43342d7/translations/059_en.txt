Item: 2nd year of Kōka, Year of the Snake (1845)

〇 Regarding Lord Kagamimaru: on the 25th of last month, it was announced that he had been adopted as heir by the Lord of Owari Dainagon. Accordingly, a letter is to be sent to express congratulations to His Lordship (the lord of the domain).

　　　8th day of the 4th month

The foregoing was communicated by Mizutani Iki via written note (tentori), and congratulations were duly conveyed to His Lordship.

〇 In accordance with last year's directive, the conferral of the honorable sake cup (o-sakazuki) had been suspended; however, from this year onward, it has been ordered that the conferral shall resume as before. Please be informed accordingly. The above.

　　　5th day of the 9th month　　　　　　Shibuya Gonzaemon

The foregoing was communicated via written note (tentori).
