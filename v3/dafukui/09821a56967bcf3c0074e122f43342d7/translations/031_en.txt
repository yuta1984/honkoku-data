**Notification to Gate Duty Officers and Arquebus Unit Commanders**

To the gate duty officers (御門当番) and arquebus unit commanders (御先筒頭): Although the lord's intentions have not been directly conveyed, when he passes through the gate, the name of the officer on duty at the checkpoint gate (切手御門) is to be written down and submitted. It is to be understood henceforth as such, as conveyed from Utsunomiya Yasaburō to Nara Tarōemon.

　　　10th day of the 10th month

Item: On the 24th day of the 11th month, as set forth in the attached document, Watanabe Jūrozaemon conveyed that the relevant parties should each be notified accordingly, and the matter is to be handled by each party as appropriate.

Item: On the 3rd day of the 3rd month of Kansei 3 (Year of the Boar), when passing through the Seven-Man Guardhouse Gate (七人番所御門), Yamazato Gate (山里御門), and the Second Bailey Wicket Gate (二之丸木戸御門), because the escort was required to go outside these gates, the front escort was ordered to proceed outside the wicket gate at the garden entrance (御庭口外木戸御門).

　　However, from this time forward, as passage is again to be made through the garden (御庭内) as it was previously, this should be understood accordingly and communicated to all relevant parties.

The above was communicated by summoning the following individuals: Shin-ban (new guard unit) Imagawa Kichibei, Atsuharu Kinpei, Inspector (御見役) Nakazawa Jinbei, and Footmen Commander (御小人支配) Ban Jingozaemon. In addition, the unit commanders (組頭), monthly duty officers (割月番), and section leaders (小頭) were summoned and notified.

**Communicated by letter:**

It is hereby conveyed that the assignment of members of the Arquebus Unit (御先筒組) to guard the garden wicket gate outside the lord's quarters (御座所御裏御庭口外木戸御門), as ordered in the aforementioned Year of the Boar, is henceforth to be discontinued. Accordingly, the guards of the Inner Yamazato Gate (奥山里御門) are to revert to being staffed by members of the Arquebus Unit as before. Therefore, starting the morning of the 25th, they are to replace the members of the Sugikata unit (御杉形). Please ensure the relevant parties are so informed. Respectfully.
