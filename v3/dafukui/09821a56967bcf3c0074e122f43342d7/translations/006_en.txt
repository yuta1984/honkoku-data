**Item — The second year of Kanpō (the year of the Dog, 1742)**

**Memorandum**

○ Regarding letters dispatched by the domain courier (*go-hikyaku*): it has long been established by regulation that such letters should not be made excessively bulky or heavy. However, in recent years, a great many letters that are indeed heavy and bulky have been observed. Therefore, letters of a merely routine or ceremonial nature shall henceforth be strictly discontinued; only letters containing actual business matters are to be sent. If this is observed, letters to the lodgings of each individual will not be left behind, and ultimately this will be to the benefit of all concerned. Bear this in mind and take great care to ensure that letters are written so as not to be excessively bulky. Furthermore, regarding letters sent to domain officials (*go-yakunin*) through personal connections and intermediaries, requesting favours—

**[Left folio]**

—even if such requests have hitherto been made through personal connections and acquaintances, from this point forward, such practices are strictly prohibited. Furthermore, domain officials have been ordered on this occasion not to act as intermediaries in such matters in the future under any circumstances. With this in mind, letters are to be submitted to the *go-yuhitsu* room (the scribes' office) as has been the established practice.

As the above has been newly and formally ordered on this occasion, each of you is to be informed accordingly.
