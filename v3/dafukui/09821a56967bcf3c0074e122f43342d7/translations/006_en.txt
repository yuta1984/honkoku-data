**Item — The second year of Kanpō (the year of the Dog, 1742)**

**Memorandum**

○ Regarding letters sent by way of the domain's express courier (*o-hikyaku*): it has long been established by regulation that such letters should not be lengthy or redundant, yet in recent years a great many excessively long letters have been observed. Henceforth, purely routine and ceremonial letters (*hitōri no tsutome no jōsho*) are to be discontinued entirely; only letters containing actual business matters are to be sent. If this is done, letters to each person's lodgings (in Edo or elsewhere) will not accumulate, and ultimately this will be to each person's own benefit. With this understanding, please take care to write letters that are concise and not unnecessarily lengthy.

Furthermore, regarding letters sent through personal connections (*tezuru*) soliciting favors from domain officials (*o-yakunin*): although it has been the practice until now to make such requests through acquaintances,

**[Left folio]**

henceforth you are strictly to refrain from such practices. Moreover, the domain officials themselves have been instructed on this occasion that they are to refuse to act as intermediaries in such matters without exception. Please bear this in mind, and, as previously, submit such documents directly to the *o-yūhitsu* room (the domain's secretariat/scribes' office).

As the above has been formally re-issued on this occasion, each of you is to be informed accordingly.

As an *o-fure* (notice) has been issued in Edo as described in the attached document, you are hereby notified that an order has been issued to circulate the notice to all monthly-duty officers in this domain as well. Please be informed accordingly. That is all.
