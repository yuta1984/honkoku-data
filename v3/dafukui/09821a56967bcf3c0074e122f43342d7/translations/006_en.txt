Item — Kanpō 2nd year, the Year of the Dog (1742):

　　　　　Memorandum

○ Regarding letters sent via the official courier (go-hikyaku): It has long been established by regulation that such letters should not be excessive in bulk or repetitive in content. However, in recent years, many overly lengthy and repetitive letters have been observed. Therefore, routine letters of mere courtesy (relating to ordinary duties) are henceforth strictly prohibited; only letters containing actual business matters are to be sent. If this is followed, letters to the various lodgings of each party will not be left undelivered, and this will ultimately be to each individual's own benefit. With this understanding, one should take care to compose letters that are not excessive or redundant.

Furthermore, regarding letters addressed to official staff (yakinin) requesting favors through personal connections: although some individuals have heretofore relied on personal acquaintances and connections for such requests, from this point forward, such practices are strictly to be discontinued.

【Left folio】

Moreover, the official staff (yakinin) have also been instructed on this occasion that they are henceforth absolutely not to act as intermediaries for such requests. With this in mind, please submit correspondence as before to the Scribes' Room (go-yūhitsu-beya).

As the foregoing has been newly and formally decreed on this occasion, each party is hereby requested to take note accordingly.
