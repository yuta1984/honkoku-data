Item: 3rd year of Kōka, Year of the Horse (1846)

(In Edo)

〇 It has been announced that Lord Matsudaira Ukon-no-shōgen shall, from this 9th day onward, be accorded the honorific treatment known as "ryōkei." Please be informed of this accordingly, and communicate it to each person within your jurisdiction.

　　　9th day of the 5th month

The foregoing was communicated by Sano Naizen, and notification was duly sent to each relevant party.

　《Marginal note: This item was omitted from the general record (shoji-dome). Found in the room-posted copy (heya haridashi hikae) and the Happyakuri-te retained copy.》

〇 Regarding the inspection of ritual impurity (fuku-e aratame) at the time of worship visits to shrines and temples, a revised regulation has now been issued, as follows:

　　Tōshōgū　　　　Ankoku-den
At the time of worship visits to the above, all persons within the main residence (o-yakata) shall be subject to the fuku-e inspection from the preceding evening.
Item: Members of the escort and advance guard shall also be subject to the fuku-e inspection.

　　Shinmei-gū and others　　　Daichō-in　　　Anwaga
At the time of shrine visits to the above, ladies-in-waiting and other inner-quarters personnel who appear before His Lordship, as well as those involved in the preparation and handling of food and drink, shall be subject to the fuku-e inspection from the preceding evening.
Item: Members of the escort and advance guard shall also be subject to the fuku-e inspection.

　　　9th day of the 8th month

The foregoing was communicated in writing by Shimada Seizaemon, the duty goyōnin (administrator) for this month. It was accordingly conveyed to the o-kachome-tsuke (inspectors of foot soldiers) and posted in the administrative room.
