…one should go to inquire after [the lord's] wellbeing.

Item — Regarding fires: even if a fire breaks out within the castle town, if the wind direction is favorable and it is a minor fire (*ko-bi*), it shall not be necessary to go out [to attend upon the lord]. However, if there is a prospect that it may develop into a major conflagration (*taika*), one must go out. If the wind direction is unfavorable, one must go out even if the fire is minor.

　　　　　3rd day of the 6th month. Communicated in writing by Matsudaira Gentayū.

**[Pasted note]**

On the 28th day of the 5th month of the first year of Kan'en (year of the Dragon, 1748), Osamugon'emon communicated the following in writing:

　　　On this occasion, it has been conveyed that the Young Lord (*Wakadono-sama*) is greatly pleased (*go-ietsu*). As celebratory red rice (*o-sekihan*) has been bestowed [upon the retainers] in honor of this joyful occasion, [all relevant persons] are to present themselves at the lord's reception room (*go-zashō*) at half past the fifth hour (*go-ji han-doki*) on the first day of the coming month. Furthermore, those children who have already completed their formal audience (*o-mie*) are likewise to accompany [the group] at the ■■■■ interval, on the third day, after the fourth hour (*yo-ji sugi*), at the seventh [watch] (*nanatsu*) storage/warehouse (*kura*) [location], and are to proceed to the reception room as directed.

　　　　　　　　　Provided, however, that children are not required to present formal greetings (*go-rei*).
