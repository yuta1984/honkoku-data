**[Left folio]**

…[one should go] on the following day to inquire after [the lord's] well-being.

Item — Regarding fires: even if a fire breaks out within the castle town, if the wind direction is favorable and it is a minor fire, it is not necessary to go [to inquire after the lord's well-being]. However, if it appears that the fire may develop into a major conflagration, one should go. If the wind direction is unfavorable, one should go even if it is a minor fire.

　　　　　*3rd day of the 6th month — Matsudaira Gentayū communicated this in writing.*

**[Pasted note]**

On the 28th day of the 5th month of the 1st year of Kan'en (year of the Dragon, 1748), Jiken'emon communicated the following in writing:

　　　As the Young Lord (*Wakatono-sama*) is understood to be greatly pleased (*go-ietsu*), and as a celebratory gift of red rice (*o-sekihan*) has been bestowed, [all are to] proceed to the Presence Chamber (*go-zashō*) at the half of the fifth hour (approximately 9:00 a.m.) on the first day of next month. Furthermore, those children who have already completed their audience (*o-mie*) are also to attend — on the 3rd day, after the fourth hour, [they are to] assemble at the Shichitsugura [storehouse] and, as guided, proceed to the Presence Chamber as directed.

　　　　　*Note: Children need not offer formal salutations (*go-rei*).*
