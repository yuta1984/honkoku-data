**Kansei 2nd Year (1790), Year of the Dog**

**17th day of the 2nd month:** Nakane Yukinori presented the following written notice and instructed that it be communicated to those under the supervision of the relevant offices; accordingly, each was duly notified:

1. When a Senior Councillor (*karō*) attends a temple as a proxy representative (*nadai*), the practice of each official attending and standing by shall be discontinued henceforth.

2. Regarding the foot inspectors (*okachi-metsuke*): they shall continue to proceed one at a time, as before.

3. The use of the ferryboat at Moya (*Moya no watashi-bune*) and the official ferry shall be discontinued henceforth; passage is to be made via the Great Bridge (*Ōhashi*).

4. During pilgrimages to Daianji, Tenryūji, Anwaga, and Daichōin, the practice of providing boxed lunches (*bentō*) to those in the accompanying escort retinue shall be suspended for the duration of the period of retrenchment (*ken'yaku no nengen*).

5. During falconry excursions (*o-takano*), those accompanying as escorts shall wear straw rain capes (*mino*) as before; each party shall be so informed.

　　　　〆 (End of notice)

Addendum: Regarding the foot inspectors (*okachi-metsuke*) proceeding to the temple when a Middle Councillor (*chūrō*) attends in place of another as proxy worshipper (*daihai*): this too shall be discontinued henceforth. This was communicated by the same person on the 20th of the same month, and accordingly the foot inspectors were so notified.

---

**21st day of the 4th month:** The same person (Nakane Yukinori) communicated the following: Regarding the wearing of *noshime* (a formal patterned garment) during New Year prayers, it has been decreed — in accordance with His Lordship's expressed wishes — that this practice be discontinued. Please understand this accordingly, and furthermore communicate this to those within one's supervisory charge who wear *noshime*, as instructed. Each was so notified.

　　　　〆 (End of notice)
