1. Tenpō 6, Year of the Sheep (1835):

〇 It has been decreed that the memorial day (kini-chi) of Lady Tenryō-in, previously observed on the 12th day [of the relevant month], shall henceforth be changed to the 2nd day.
　　　　　11th month

In accordance with the above decree, Arakawa Jūuemon has communicated the following.

〇 On the occasion of the change of domain lordship (go-daigawari), the following has been decreed as per the attached separate document. Please take note accordingly and convey this to the relevant officials.
　　　　　26th day of the 12th month　　　　Amagata Magohachi

　　　　　Separate Document

　　Days Prohibited from Hunting on Granted Lands (Hairyōba Sesshō-yoke-bi):

　　Every month: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 8th, 17th, and 18th days

　　2nd month, 11th day　　[abbreviated notation]
　　3rd month, 18th day
　　3rd month, 24th day
　　4th month, 27th day
　　7th month, 27th day
　　9th month, 12th day
　　10th month, 21st day
　　12th month, 4th day
　　12th month, 26th day

The above days prohibited from hunting on granted lands are hereby revised.

2. Tenpō 7, Year of the Monkey (1836):
　　(In Edo)

〇 It has been decreed that on this 15th day [of the 4th month], Lord Matsudaira Awa-no-kami has received the honor of go-ryōkei (formal dual salutation of respect). Please take note accordingly and convey this to those under respective jurisdictions.
　　　　　　15th day of the 4th month

The above having been communicated by Arakawa Jūuemon, it has been duly conveyed to all relevant parties.
