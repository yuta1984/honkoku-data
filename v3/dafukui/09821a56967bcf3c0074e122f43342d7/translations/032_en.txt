**Kōwa 2, Year of the Dog (1802)**

○ Regarding Hon-no-jō-sama, who has been adopted as heir on this occasion, henceforth he is to be addressed and referred to as "Wakatono-sama" (Young Lord). Furthermore, as the order of address is Wakatono-sama followed by Omae-sama, this is to be conveyed to all those under authority, down to the lowest ranks.

○ Regarding the formal congratulatory greetings (恐悦, kyōetsu) on castle-attendance days and personal celebratory occasions: whereas heretofore one would make a separate appearance on the day of the O-zashō (御座所), this is no longer necessary; henceforth such matters are to be presented at the O-Honmaru.
　However, when a colleague of the same post withdraws (i.e., is relieved), one is to present oneself at the O-zashō.

　　　1st day of the 8th month

The above is to be understood accordingly, as conveyed by Tagaya Gonbē in a written notice.
