Item — [This applies to] all troop unit members (*shobangumi*), sons of the various unit commanders (*shomotogashira*), and sons of the new guard (*shinban*).

　The above shall line up from one mat below the head seat of the Tsuru-no-ma ("Crane Room"), continuing from there all the way to the Great Guard Room (*Ō-gobansho*).

Having lined up as described above, when the lord makes his appearance, the *go-yōnin* shall announce that those present are offering their congratulations for the day.

　　*However, the senior councilors (*go-karōchū*) shall advance their seats and take their places at each occasion. Furthermore, the *go-yōnin* shall take their position near the alcove pillar (*tokobashira*) in the Second Room (*ni-no-ma*). All sliding doors (*karakami*) are to be removed entirely.*

Item — Regarding the general reception of greetings today: the *go-yōnin* shall deliver verbal instructions (*kōtatsu*) to all, down to the supernumerary (*bangai*) retainers; from the duty supernumeraries (*yaku-bangai*) down to the lowest supernumeraries of the various unit commanders (*shomotogashira*), the *go-metsuke* (inspectors) shall deliver the verbal instructions.

　　　　　Supplementary Note (*Beppi*)

Item — The wearing of *noshime* (formal patterned robes) for New Year's is hereby exempted.

Item — Those who attend for the monthly ceremonial greetings (*tsukigime go-rei*) may wear either *asa-kamishimo* (formal hempen divided skirt and overvest) or *tsugi-kamishimo* (patched *kamishimo*) as they see fit; no particular requirement is imposed.

Item — For the Five Annual Festivals (*gosekku*), *asa-kamishimo* shall be worn as before.

The above has been ordered. Should any revisions be made hereafter, a further notice will be issued accordingly.
