Item: Genroku 16th year, the Year of the Sheep (1703)

○ Henceforth, when there are honorific tablets (o-fuda) for the Five Seasonal Festivals (go-sekku) and the monthly observances (tsukiji/tskinami), notice shall be given [to attend the castle], and all are to proceed to the castle to pay their respects. When such ceremonies of paying respects are to be cancelled, no notice will be sent out, and therefore each person should refrain from reporting to duty. The above is hereby communicated.

　　　　29th day of the 5th month　　Monthly duty officer: To Messrs. Iinuma Kanbei and Dedebuchi Heibei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　From: Shimada Seizaemon and Hayashi Hachiemon
