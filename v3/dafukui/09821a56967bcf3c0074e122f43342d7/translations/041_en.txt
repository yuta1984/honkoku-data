　Item: Regarding correspondence bearing the honorific "sama" (様) addressed to Tsunogorō-sama, as well as the dispatching of official business, these shall continue as before. For all other matters bearing the honorific "sama," parties are to take note accordingly.
　　　　29th day of the 5th month
　The go-yōnin communicated that the above is to be understood and that the officials (o-yakunin-domo) are likewise to be notified. Notice was accordingly given to each party.
　○ Matsudaira Inaba-no-kami-sama shall henceforth be addressed with the dual honorific (go-ryōkei). This is communicated for the purposes of general awareness. The substance of the above is to be communicated to the officials as well.
　　　　16th day of the 12th month
　The go-yōnin communicated the above, and notice was accordingly given to each party.

Item: Bunsei 1st year, Year of the Tiger (1818)
　○ Concerning the manner of addressing and writing the names of the daimyō lords, it has been decided that henceforth, beginning with those of the same clan name (go-dōsei-sama), family surnames (o-myōji) shall be appended. This is to be communicated to the officials.
　　　　11th day of the 1st month
　The go-yōnin communicated that the above has been decreed accordingly.
