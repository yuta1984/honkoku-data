Item: Bunka 9th year, Year of the Monkey (1812)
　Item: On the 13th day of the 4th month, Sengoku Manuemon communicated the following: As decreed per the attached separate document, all parties are to take note accordingly. Furthermore, regarding the matter of Ninnosuke-sama: on the 3rd day of this month, he was designated as the official heir (chakushi). Therefore, letters of congratulation are to be sent to the higher lords (jōjō-sama).
　　　Regarding Ninnosuke-sama: from the 3rd day of this month, he is henceforth to be respectfully addressed as "Waka-tono-sama" (the Young Lord).
　　　Item: The order of address shall be "Waka-tono-sama" followed by "O-mae-sama."
　　　　　End

Item: Bunka 10th year, Year of the Rooster (1813)
　Item: On the 24th day of the 1st month, Nakane Yukinojo communicated the following: The memorial anniversary (kijitsu) of Reishōin-sama had previously fallen on the 29th day, but henceforth it is to fall on the 26th day. In accordance with the instruction to inform those under one's supervision of this matter, notice was given to each party accordingly.
　　　　　End

Item: On the last day of the 3rd month, the following was communicated by the o-yōnin in accordance with the attached separate document, and notice was given to each party accordingly.
　　　　　　《Marginal note: This refers to a princess (hime-sama).》
　○ On this occasion, a female child (go-joshi-sama) has been born in the O-oku (the inner residential quarters of the domain lord's palace). Regarding this matter, Kasuya Naiki, the o-kachi-metsuke (inspectors of foot soldiers), and all other personnel under unit command are to be informed, and arrangements are to be made for them to offer their respectful congratulations (kyōetsu).
　Item: As for the matter of official notification to the public authorities (kōhen), it is to be deferred for the time being. All parties are to take note of this accordingly, and this point is also to be communicated.
