Item: Bunka 9th year, Year of the Monkey (1812)
　Item: On the 13th day of the 4th month, Sengoku Manemon communicated the following: As decreed per the attached separate document, all parties are to take note accordingly. Furthermore, regarding Jinnosuke-sama, as he has been established as the legitimate heir (chakushi) on the 3rd day of this month, a letter of congratulations is to be submitted to the senior lords (ue-ue-sama). This was duly communicated.
　　Regarding Jinnosuke-sama: from the 3rd day of this month, he is henceforth to be addressed as "Waka-tono-sama" (Young Lord).
　　Item: The order of address shall be "Waka-tono-sama" followed by "O-mae-sama."
　　　　　End

Item: Bunka 10th year, Year of the Rooster (1813)
　Item: On the 24th day of the 1st month, Nakane Yukinojo communicated the following: The memorial anniversary (kijitsu) of Reishōin-sama, previously observed on the 29th day, is henceforth to be held on the 26th day. As this matter was communicated with instruction to inform those under one's supervision, notice was duly given to each party.
　　　　　End

Item: On the last day of the 3rd month, the following was communicated by the o-yōnin in accordance with the attached separate document, and notice was given to each party accordingly.
　　　　　　　　《Note: This refers to a princess (hime-sama).》
　○ On this occasion, a female child (go-joshi-sama) has been born in the O-oku (the inner residential quarters of the domain lord's palace). Regarding this matter, Kasuya Naiki, the o-kachi-metsuke, and all other personnel under unit command are to be informed, and arrangements are to be made for them to offer their respectful congratulations (kyōetsu).
　Item: As for the matter of official notification to the public authorities (kōhen), it is to be deferred for the time being. All parties are to take note of this accordingly, and this point is also to be communicated.
