1. Bunsei 7, Year of the Monkey (1824):
○ As a general reduction by half (han-gen) has been ordered on this occasion, the礼銭 (reisen, ceremonial fees) for tachi-umashiro (sword and horse allowances) and tori-me (copper cash payments) are all to be reduced by half during the designated period.

　Provisos:
　· Those receiving stipends of 200 koku or more: all tachi-umashiro and tori-me to be halved until the coming Year of the Dog (1826).
　· Those receiving chigyo (land stipends) below 200 koku: all tachi-umashiro and tori-me to be halved during the current period of the lord's residence in the domain.
　· Those receiving kiri-mai (rice stipends paid in installments): likewise, all tori-me to be halved.
　　　　　15th day of the 8th month

As the above has been ordered, all are to be so informed, and the same is to be conveyed to those under one's command, as communicated by Nagami Kohachirō, and has accordingly been passed on to the relevant parties.

2. Bunsei 9, Year of the Dog (1826):
　· When dispatching couriers (hikyaku) to Edo, in cases where official goods (goyō-mono) are to be sent in addition to official correspondence (goyō-jō), henceforth the packing (tsutsumi-date) is to be completed at the Office of Accounts (go-kanjō-sho) before forwarding to Edo. As this arrangement has now been established, all official items are to be submitted to the Office of Accounts on the morning of dispatch. Since the packing will be handled at the Office of Accounts, official goods may be submitted as-is.
　　Proviso: Goods intended for the御膳所 (go-zensho, kitchen/dining office) are to be submitted to the御坐所役所 (go-zasho yaku-sho, the lord's residential office).

　· Even for official correspondence, all efforts should be made to keep the bulk to a minimum.

　· For residence letters (yado-jō), henceforth, when composing them, every effort should be made to keep them as compact as possible.
　　Proviso: Should the paper happen to be left over due to being kept too compact, this may cause inconvenience for individuals' own business.

　· Outside of close relatives or those with special connections, ordinary letters are not to be submitted.
　　Proviso: Even for business correspondence, having excessive bulk is strictly prohibited.

The foregoing has been communicated to all relevant parties as per the attached document; all are to govern themselves accordingly. That is all.
　　　　　3rd day of the 5th month　　　　　Akita Michinosuke

In Edo:
○ It has been announced that the memorial death anniversary (ki-nichi) of Lady Seitoku-in, previously held on the 8th day, has henceforth been changed to the 1st day (tsuitachi) of the month. However, regarding the New Year's Day (Gantan) ceremonies, as they commence with the receipt of official gifts from the shogunate (kōgi go-chōdai-mono) and all ritual procedures (o-shiki) are conducted accordingly, that matter is to be treated separately.
