1. Bunsei 7, Year of the Monkey (1824):
○ In light of the austerity reduction order issued on this occasion, it is hereby decreed that ceremonial fees (o-reisen), including tachi-umashiro (sword and horse stipend payments) and tori-me (cash gifts in coin), shall all be reduced by half during the period of the lord's residence in the domain.

　　Provisos:
　　· For those receiving 200 koku or more: tachi-umashiro and tori-me shall all be reduced by half until the coming Year of the Dog (1826).
　　· For those receiving less than 200 koku who receive stipends in land (chigyo-dori): tachi-umashiro and tori-me shall all be reduced by half for the duration of the current residence in the domain.
　　· For those receiving stipends in rice rations (o-kirimai-dori): the same as above — tori-me shall all be reduced by half.
　　　　　　　15th day of the 8th month

The above having been announced, Nagami Kohachirō conveyed this message with the instruction that all parties should take note accordingly and communicate the same to those under their supervision, which was then duly relayed to each party.

2. Bunsei 9, Year of the Dog (1826):
· When sending couriers (hikyaku) to Edo, if official goods (go-yōmono) are to be submitted in addition to official communications (go-yōjō), it has now been declared that henceforth all such items shall be packaged at the domain accounting office (go-kanjōsho) before being forwarded to Edo. Therefore, such items should be delivered to the accounting office on the morning of dispatch. Since the packaging will be handled at the accounting office, official items may be submitted as-is.
　　Proviso: Items for the use of the御膳所 (o-zenshō, kitchen/dining office) are to be submitted to the御坐所役所 (o-zasshosho, lord's residential office).

· Even in the case of official correspondence, all parties should take care to keep the bulk of their letters as low as possible.

· Regarding residence letters (yado-jō), henceforth these should be written and prepared in as compact a form as possible.
　　Proviso: Should there be cases where the bulk naturally cannot be reduced, this may cause inconvenience to individuals in carrying out their business.

· Except for correspondence with close family members (格別の続き), ordinary letters are not to be submitted.
　　Proviso: Even for business matters, bulky correspondence is strictly prohibited.

The above has been communicated to all relevant parties as per the attached document; please take note accordingly.
　　　　　　3rd day of the 5th month　　　　　Akita Michinosuke

From Edo:
○ It has been announced that the memorial day (kijitsu) of Lady Itokuin, which had been observed on the 8th, shall henceforth be changed to the 1st day of the month. Note that for New Year's Day (ganjitsu), the proceedings shall commence with the receipt of gifts from the shogunate (kōgi o-chōdaimono), and all ceremonies are to be conducted in full formal observance.
