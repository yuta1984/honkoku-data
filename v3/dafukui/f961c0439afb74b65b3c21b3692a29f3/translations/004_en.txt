(Continued from previous page) ...Iga-no-kami, Marugame Hyōgo-no-kami, and Ichihashi Kurōzaemon-no-jō were given orders, and troops were dispatched in separate directions to search for and bring down Yoshikage. They searched throughout the province. From the sixth temple of Yamada-shō in Ono, they received Yoshikage's head from Asakura Shikibu-tayū (Asakura Kagekiyo), and reported this to Lord Nobunaga.

Regarding the distribution of the entire province of Echizen among the three lords who had betrayed Yoshikage: Maeha Uemon-no-jō took the name Sakurada Harima-no-kami and was appointed to oversee the provincial governance (shugo-sho) of Echizen, with his castle placed at Toyomi (castle). Ono District was granted to Asakura Shikibu-tayū, who resided there as his castle seat. Kakuta Hikouemon-no-jō was granted the Fuchū district, where he established his castle. Lord Nobunaga's orders stated: "There will surely be uprisings (ikki). The three of you must consult with one another and defend your positions firmly and properly." On the same twenty-fifth day, he withdrew to the Azai District of Ōmi Province, toward Odani.

From around late the eighth month of Tenshō 1 (1573), Asakura Shikibu-tayū, Sakurada Harima-no-kami, and Kakuta Hikouemon-no-jō — each having destroyed their own lord and made themselves lords in his place — grew increasingly arrogant with each passing month and day.
