Regarding the retreat of Asakura Yoshikage to Kimotoda and Kamiyama in Ōmi Province (Gōshū), as it is described in the *Asai-ki* (Chronicles of the Asai clan), there is nothing further to add by way of introduction. This occurred in the eighth month of Tenshō 1 (1573).

The elevated area of Mount Odani, the castle residence of Asai Nagamasa, is called "Ōtake" (Great Peak). Within this elevated area, there is also a place called "Yakiomaru." Asai Mitsushima-no-kami [Asami Tsushima-no-kami] was stationed there by the Asai forces to defend it. Furthermore, at the very summit of Ōtake, Saitō Gyōbu, Kobayashi Rokuzaemon-no-jō, and Saihōin were placed under siege as reinforcements sent by the Asakura clan.
