Regarding Asakura Yoshikage's retreat to Kibomota and Kamiyama in Ōmi Province (Gōshū), as it is described in the "Asai-ki" (Chronicles of the Asai clan), there is nothing particular to add in the header notes. This occurred in the eighth month of Tenshō 1 (1573).

The high ground of Odani Castle, the stronghold of Asai Nagamasa, is called "Ōkasa." Among this elevated area there is also a place called "Yakiomaru." In this Yakiomaru, a man called Asami Tsushima-no-kami was stationed by the Asai forces. Furthermore, at the summit of Ōkasa, Saitō Gyōbu, Kobayashi Rokuza'emon-no-jō, and Saihōin were placed by the Asakura clan as reinforcements in garrison.
