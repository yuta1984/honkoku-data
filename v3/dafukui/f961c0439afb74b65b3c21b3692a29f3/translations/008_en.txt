(Continued from the previous page) ...while they were in a state of panic and confusion, attackers broke in from one direction and set fire to the castle. Within the castle, there were also those who had secretly marked themselves as allies [side note: "of the attacking force"], and they too set fires; many fled. As Hikouemonojō attempted to flee, Keya Shichirō rushed upon him, ran him through with a single spear thrust, and effortlessly struck off his head. Truly, it was by the disloyalty of these three villains that Yoshikage had been forced to take his own life on the twentieth day of the eighth month of this very year. Only some ninety days had passed since then, and now, without even a single proper battle, they had been cut down by the ikki—how pitiful a fate. Thus the men of the ikki rejoiced greatly, and offered their loyalty as a memorial tribute to their late lord.
