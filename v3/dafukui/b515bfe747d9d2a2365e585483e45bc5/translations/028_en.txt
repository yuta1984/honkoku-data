　　　　　　The matter was reported to His Lordship, who ordered that it be communicated to Edo. Accordingly, a draft notice was prepared and
　　　　　　submitted to His Lordship for approval. Upon receiving the command that it should be sent as written, it was dispatched
　　　　　　via the express messenger of this 10th day to Yoemon's office, with the following content:

　Regarding Edo:
　○It has been heard that recently, within the mansion grounds, some persons have been engaging in gambling-like amusements. Gambling has long been
　　strictly prohibited, and this is an utterly outrageous matter. Hereafter, gambling-like amusements are strictly forbidden.
　　Furthermore, the inspectors have been ordered to conduct a thorough investigation, and should any person, down to the lowest ranks, be found
　　to have violated this even slightly, they will be strictly punished. All persons should henceforth conduct themselves with due caution. The above.
　　　　　　　Second month

　　　　　　　A memorandum written subsequently regarding the reassignment of night patrol duties at that time
　○Additionally, regarding fire prevention — given the season, and especially since recently there have been persons throwing fire into the townspeople's areas at night —
　　during patrols, one should take extra care. The senior councilors have instructed that any suspicious persons should be investigated,
　　so take heed accordingly. The above.
　　　　　　　Third month, 25th day

　○Regarding fire prevention, although this has always been a matter for each person to observe with care, since this is also a period of His Lordship's absence, fire prevention matters must be

　　strictly enforced down to the lowest-ranking persons. Furthermore, it is prohibited to carry a lit lantern while walking about before the hour of Itsutsu (approximately 8 p.m.),
　　but it has been heard that this has recently been growing lax. Hereafter, whether when dispatching servants or when lower-ranking persons go about on their own business,
　　they should be instructed to carry lanterns before the hour of Itsutsu as was the previous practice. Should there be any disorderly conduct, strict corrections will be made,
　　so take heed accordingly. The above.
　　　　　　　Third month, 26th day　　To all　　　Additionally, to group supervisors～　　To the inspectors

　○When express messengers depart for Edo, the private letters from domain retainers are often bulky, causing delays on every occasion.
　　When the volume becomes large, express messengers must be dispatched even at times when there is no particular official business. Therefore, hereafter, bulky letters
　　will strictly not be forwarded. Bear this in mind, and when there is business to conduct, compose letters as compactly as possible
　　before submitting them. The above.
　　　　　　　Third month, 26th day　　　　　　　　　　　　Hori Jūbei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ōno Sanzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Iida Jizaemon

　○The daughter of Lord Matsudaira Kaga-no-kami will be passing through here on the 17th of the coming month, and therefore both men and women should refrain from going out to view the procession.
　　Everyone should take heed of the above, and strictly instruct group supervisors, servants, and all others accordingly.
