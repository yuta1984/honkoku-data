## An'ei 7 (1778), Year of the Dog

### Verbal Communication Issued in Edo

◦ Among the retainers of the domain's samurai households, when someone claims illness or injury, seeks out a townsperson relative in Edo to take them in, and recovers, they are supposed to be sent back to the home domain immediately. Their master was to obtain a written pledge and submit a verbal report detailing the nature of the illness or injury, and until now such requests had been handled accordingly.

However, in recent years, cases have been coming to light of individuals who claim illness or injury, go down to the Edo townspeople's quarters, and even after recovering, remain in Edo performing service, or go down to the townspeople's quarters and take up service there without even feigning illness. This is an outrageous matter.

Those who wish to take up service should, as has always been the rule, petition their supervising head official in the home domain first, and only proceed if circumstances are deemed unavoidable. Even those serving at the Edo residence who wish to seek service elsewhere must return once to the home domain before making such a request. Yet in recent years there have been those who do not follow these procedures, which is the height of impropriety.

Henceforth, each master shall conduct a thorough investigation, and for those who have falsely claimed illness or injury, the details shall be documented and reported. Those in the town quarters who have taken in such individuals shall also correspond in writing with the guarantors in the home domain. Upon recovery and return home, this must be reported to the supervising head official. Should delays occur, it will be ordered that the guarantors in the home domain be summoned. Therefore, masters are to strictly remind their retainers of these rules in advance. The above is hereby communicated.

　　　　　1st day of the 2nd month　　　　　Inspector (Metsuke)

---

◦ Fire prevention has been ordered on numerous occasions, but particularly at this time of year, the weather has been unusually dry for the season. Therefore, all are ordered, down to the lowest ranks, to remain vigilant without lapse and to be mindful of fire hazards. Let this be understood. The above is hereby communicated.

　　　　　5th day of the 12th month　　　　　Inspector (Metsuke)
　　　　　To all concerned　　Also to group supervisors

---

### Verbal Communication Issued in Edo

◦ Last night, a section of the tenement quarters (nagaya) of the residence on Reiganjima partially burned down. As a result, a gesture of self-restraint and inquiry is being made, and everyone is to conduct themselves with due decorum and restraint in all matters. Furthermore, it has been ordered that the shutters on the windows of the front tenement quarters are to be kept closed. Let this be understood. The above is hereby communicated.

　　　　　4th day of the 12th month　　　　　Inspector (Metsuke)

## An'ei 8 (1779), Year of the Pig

◦ I hereby inform you by letter: as the dry weather has continued and conditions have become particularly arid at this time, special care must be taken regarding fire hazards. In particular, regarding the Sagicho (New Year's bonfire ceremony) planned for tomorrow and the day after, care should be taken to conduct it in nearby open fields or riverbanks as soon as conditions allow. Please issue instructions accordingly to the members of your group. The above is hereby communicated.

　　　　　13th day of the 1st month　　　　　Kawai Zenjūrō
　　　　　To the Monthly Duty Commander of Various Unit Heads (Tsukiban Shomotsu-gashira)

※Note: The same instructions were communicated to the town magistrate (machi-bugyō) regarding the townspeople's quarters. However, as it rained on the 14th and conditions became sufficiently damp, things proceeded as before (text continues on next page).
