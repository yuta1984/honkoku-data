23rd day of the 4th month
　　　　　　　　To: All Unit Commanders (Mono-gashira)

(Note: The following concerns the fact that on this occasion [14th day of the 3rd month], a monetary grant was graciously bestowed, and a circular letter arrived from the Senior Councillors stating that expressions of gratitude should be conveyed. Accordingly, we have made our rounds to the Senior Councillors, and as we have also been instructed to ensure that this information reaches all subordinates without exception, we hereby communicate the following to all unit commanders.)

---

**Record of Verbal Notice**

Regarding this newly issued important directive: as was declared in the Year of the Tiger, everyone is to be fully aware, without any misunderstanding, of the need for frugality in all matters. Furthermore, since this relates also to the consideration of fabric quality for formal dress (kamishimo) and hakama as declared in the Year of the Boar, older items already in one's possession were to be overlooked (permitted). However, it has come to our attention that some individuals have construed this to mean that the regulations have been relaxed — this is a most grievous misunderstanding. Regarding attire and other matters where judgment is unclear, inquiries should be made to the Inspectors (Metsuke). Furthermore, it is directed that the purport of the Year of the Tiger declaration be communicated and instilled in all. Accordingly, please take note of the following. The above.

1. Regarding silk formal dress (kamishimo) and Gunnai-hira hakama and the like: as declared in the Year of the Boar, older items already in possession are permissible.

2. Regarding clothing: cotton garments are to be used as the primary choice. Coarse woven fabrics (futaori) and tsumugi (pongee silk) remain as before. However, tsumugi that is difficult to distinguish from refined silk in terms of fabric quality is strictly prohibited.

3. Regarding women's clothing: cotton garments are to be used; however, silk items already in one's possession are permissible. Katabira (summer robes) and silk-crepe garments are prohibited. Belts (obi) made of chirimen (crepe), shusu (satin), sha-aya (gauze weave), and the like are permissible. Waist ties (koshi-karage) are to follow the same rules.

4. On the day of a wedding, the bride's ceremonial dress is to be treated as an exception; all other attire is to follow the preceding articles.

5. Regarding gatherings (social meetings and banquets) and the exchange of gifts, the rules previously declared are to be strictly observed.

6. Among group deputies (kumi-tedai) and those below them, some individuals have been observed wearing clothing inappropriate to their means and status. Superiors and group supervisors are to issue strict instructions on this matter. Underkimonos (shita-katabira) or silk sleeveless jackets (kinu-kataginu) and the like are henceforth absolutely prohibited. All seasonal attire is to follow the above rules in every respect.
　　Note: When on duty in Edo, this is to be applied according to the circumstances of the time.

　　　　　4th day of the 6th month　　[Also to the groups]　　Inspector (Metsuke)

---

Although there have been repeated directives regarding fire safety, as fine weather has continued, winds have been rising, and vegetation is particularly dry, each person is to ensure that fire safety at their premises is strictly enforced without any negligence. This matter is to be firmly communicated to all subordinates as well. Should any suspicious individuals be spotted, they are to be apprehended on the spot and the Inspectors (Metsuke) are to be notified immediately. Even if there are errors in apprehension, this will not be held against you. As the Edo residence is currently without its lord and thus short of personnel, neighboring households are to be vigilant and mindful. As the foregoing has been newly declared, ensure that all retainers, down to the lowest rank, are strictly instructed accordingly. The above.

　　　　21st day of the 7th month　　To All　　[Also to the groups]　　Inspector (Metsuke)

**Verbal Notice**

1. For all domain members, both in Edo and in the home domain, from samurai retainers down to the lowest ranks: regarding petitions for supplementary payments (shikkaku-kin / failure-to-perform allowances), emergency allowances (o-teate-kin), and commendation payments (o-hobi-kin) granted beyond the standard stipend (gigi-saku); this now also applies to payments granted to Middle Elders (Chūrō), Councillors (Yōnin), personal attendants (o-soba-hajime), manservants' salaries (komono-kyū), and pages (o-koshō). Henceforth, all such payments shall be reduced to one-half of the regular stipend level. A strict order for reductions (sashiryaku) has been issued in accordance with the above; please take note accordingly.
