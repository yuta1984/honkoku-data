**[Concerning the performance of hayashi music within the town]**

**Verbal Notice from Edo**

Regarding the disposal of rubbish and refuse within the estate premises: although there has been a standing notice ordering that rubbish not be carelessly discarded outside, it has recently come to our attention that this is again being done in a disorderly manner. Henceforth, this rule is to be strictly observed. Ensure that refuse does not go beyond the designated notice-board areas, and that it is disposed of from the rear. This is to be firmly enforced. The above.
　　　　Last day of the 8th month　　[Also to the group supervisors]　　Inspector (Metsuke)

---

**Verbal Notice from Edo**

Regarding the members stationed at the estate rushing to the forefront of a fire (i.e., the advancing fire line): although there has long been a standing regulation on this matter, it has recently come to our attention that people are doing so without proper authorization. Be aware that going up to the fire site privately, outside of official duty, is not permitted. The above.
　　　　12th month　　Inspector (Metsuke)

---

**Verbal Notice from Edo**

Whenever a fire breaks out anywhere, when the designated firefighting units — including the御手当 (fire response teams), the Jintsū unit, or the Sanchō fire brigades — are assembling at the site, it has come to our attention that many unnecessary persons are mingling in the crowd and causing confusion by spectating, thereby obstructing the assembly of the required personnel. This is an unacceptable situation. Above all, during such tumultuous times, any negligence in attending to one's own post (御小屋) would be a most serious matter. On such occasions, particular care must be taken regarding fire prevention at one's own station. Needless to say, creating disturbances by loitering in unnecessary locations is absolutely prohibited. Ensure that those of lower rank are strictly instructed accordingly. The above.
　　　　19th day of the 12th month　　Inspector (Metsuke)

---

**An'ei 9th Year (Year of the Rat)**

On the 13th day of the 1st month, regarding the performance of Sagicho hayashi music on the following two days, as is customary each year: since this year is a year when the domain lord is away (on alternate-attendance duty in Edo), and furthermore as the snow is not particularly deep, letters were sent, as in the previous year, to the castle deputy (御城代) and those in charge of groups (組預かり), notifying each of them accordingly.
