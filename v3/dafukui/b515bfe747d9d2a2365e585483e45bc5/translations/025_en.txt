　○On this occasion, due to the memorial service for Tangen-ji Temple, a general pardon has been granted to the domain retainers, townspeople, and villagers alike. Accordingly, regarding those under each person's command, as well as retainers and servants, both for matters related to superiors and personal matters, those who were previously ordered into exile, as well as those subject to lighter punishments, are to be pardoned without further investigation, as has been instructed. Please be informed accordingly. The above.
　　　　　　　　10th month, 5th day　　All parties　　Additional note for unit commanders　　　　Inspectors

　　　Following the above notice, some individuals reported to the Inspectors that they had pardoned those they had ordered into exile. However, as the notice states, this is a matter to be handled at each person's own discretion following investigation, and there is no need for us to receive such reports — this response was conveyed to them. The senior elders (家老) also confirmed that this was the appropriate approach.

【Left page】
One. Shōtoku 2nd year, Year of the Dragon [1712]
　　　　　　On the 22nd day of the 3rd month, Inspector Inoue Handayū and Ogawa Yoemon called upon the town magistrate Kaifuku Ihē and discussed: gambling and related activities have long been strictly prohibited, yet card (karuta) selling in the town has still not ceased. An official notice is to be issued within the next day or two, at which time all commercial karuta cards found in the town are to be confiscated without exception. Furthermore, karuta selling is to be prohibited going forward. They also agreed to investigate what had become of the karuta cards sold since this past spring. The following day, the 23rd, the official notice was issued as follows:
【Marginal note】"Verified against the record kept by the Senior Elders"

　○As gambling has long been strictly prohibited, all people are expected to refrain from it. Nevertheless, it has been heard that even among the lower classes, gambling-like activities are being conducted, which is utterly unacceptable. From now on, those found engaging in such activities will be strictly punished. Each person must take note of this and firmly instruct those below them as well. Furthermore, as it is also the season for fires, please instruct everyone down to the lowest ranks not to be negligent regarding fire prevention. The above.　　　3rd month, 23rd day　　All parties　　Additional note for unit commanders　　　　Inspectors

　In Edo:
　○As each person's quarters in the tenement lodgings are cramped and unlikely to have bathing facilities, permission has been granted to use the bathhouses (public baths) in the vicinity of the residence. Since these are shared washing areas where people mix together, you must conduct yourself without roughness and avoid quarrels. Furthermore, on days of strong wind, going out is considered unnecessary, so please be informed accordingly. The above.
