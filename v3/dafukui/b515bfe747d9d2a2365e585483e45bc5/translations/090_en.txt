◦ It has come to our attention that among those in the domain who keep horses, some have been leaving their stables vacant for extended periods under the pretense of switching horses, which is an unacceptable situation. Those currently covered by this notice who do not have horses [stabled] are to stable them without delay. Please be accordingly informed. The above.
　　6th day of the 10th month　　Inspector (Ometsuke)
　　To all concerned　　[Note:] To those in the domain who keep horses

Note: On the 6th day of the 11th month, Komaki Takunosuke communicated that, since the previous notice regarding the stabling of horses within the domain, it appears that each person has been diligently stabling their horses; however, as there are some who have been negligent, this remains an unacceptable situation, and he requested that a further private investigation be conducted. Accordingly, Katsumura Santazaemon communicated this to Karata Kihei, and upon inquiry, it was determined that a uniform standard should be met. Therefore, it was directed that a written report be submitted listing those who have stabled their horses by this time, as well as those who have not yet done so, following a thorough investigation.

**Tenmei 2nd Year (Year of the Tiger, 1782)**

20th day of the 1st month:
1. Regarding Tada (多吉), the adopted son of Yoshiki Tanai: Although he was the second son of Morita Yahei, he has fallen ill. While it is hoped that he may recover, given his youth, Tanai himself is advancing in age, and therefore, under unavoidable circumstances and with the mutual consent of both parties, a request was made to return him. Approved on the 20th day of the 2nd month.

◦ Regarding petitions to return an adopted son due to illness, by mutual agreement between the parties: whereas such requests have hitherto been granted as petitioned, henceforth, even if by mutual agreement, the decision shall be made in accordance with the nature of the illness. Should there be those who make careless or improper petitions in this matter, both parties shall be subject to investigation and appropriate measures shall be taken. Please take note of this in advance and conduct yourselves accordingly. The above.
　　27th day of the 2nd month
