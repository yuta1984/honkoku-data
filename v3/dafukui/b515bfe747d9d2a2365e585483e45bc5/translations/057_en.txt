　　　　　22nd day of the 5th month　　　　　　　　　　　　The Inspectors (Metsuke)

Item, Kan'en 3, Year of the Horse:
　　　　　On the occasion of the change of domain lordship, the previously issued ordinance regarding commoners (townspeople and farmers) showing proper respect toward domain officials and retainers is hereby re-promulgated and communicated to the towns, the four districts (gun), Fuchū, and other areas.

○ It has been communicated on repeated occasions in prior years that farmers and townspeople must not behave disrespectfully toward samurai and other domain retainers. However, it has come to our attention that in recent years improper conduct has again been occurring. Therefore, this directive is hereby issued once more. A copy is to be kept with the village headman (shōya) for the farmers, with the town headman and officials for the townspeople, and with the transport agents (toiya) at post stations along the highways. It is to be strictly communicated so that all people of lower station will habitually understand and observe this regulation.

Item: Farmers who enter the castle town, upon encountering samurai or other domain retainers on the road, shall step aside and ensure they conduct themselves with all due respect.

Item: Farmers who lead cattle or horses into the residences of samurai or into the town shall take hold of the animals' reins/halters and ensure they do not obstruct those passing along the road.
　　Addendum: When cattle or horses are left tied up, they shall be secured in such a way as not to obstruct those passing along the road.

Item: Farmers and townspeople travelling along the roads who encounter samurai setting out for falconry or river hunting shall conduct themselves with all due respect.
　　Addendum: On narrow roads and lanes, upon encountering samurai, one shall take hold of one's cattle or horse by the halter and move to the side of the road in a respectful manner.

Item: Farmers who enter the castle town while carrying night soil or other unclean materials, upon encountering samurai or other domain retainers, shall

**[Left Page]**
　　move as far to the side as possible and pass by in a respectful manner. Particularly on back streets and narrow lanes, they shall take extra care not to obstruct those passing along the road.

Item: One shall not casually sit or lounge in front of the shops in the castle town's merchant quarters. Particularly when samurai are passing by, all conduct shall be orderly and proper.

Item: On the highways throughout the domain, not only toward samurai from other domains and territories, but even toward those of lower servant status, farmers, townspeople, transport agents, horse handlers, and all others shall show proper respect and conduct themselves without rudeness.

Item: Among those from shogunal lands (goryō) and other domains who enter the castle town and engage in commerce — visiting townspeople's establishments with familiarity and maintaining regular lodging houses — the rules and regulations are to be explained to them thoroughly in advance so that they conduct themselves without rudeness. Should any person violate these rules, even if they are from shogunal lands or another domain, they shall be subject to punishment, and the operators of their regular lodgings shall likewise bear responsibility and be penalized.

The above articles have been issued once more by renewed directive. Should any person be found in violation thereof, they shall be subject to strict punishment.
　　　　　　Kan'en 3 (1750), 6th month

The foregoing should be duly communicated.
　　　　　　6th month, Year of the Horse　　　　　　　　　The Inspectors (Metsuke)
