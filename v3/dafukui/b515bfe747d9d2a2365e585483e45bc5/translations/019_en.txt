First year of Hōei, Year of the Rooster (1705)
　○Regarding the waterways (upper waterways/aqueducts), notices have been issued from before to ensure there is no neglect, and furthermore, inspectors are dispatched daily to conduct checks. However, it has been heard that recently filthy matter has been carelessly discharged into the waterways and left as is. Henceforth, there must be no such incidents, and each estate should be ordered to clean thoroughly with renewed diligence. If anyone is found dumping or washing filthy things, a fine shall be immediately imposed upon discovery in accordance with the regulations. The above should be understood and conveyed to all, down to the lowest ranks. Furthermore, Yanagihara Seisuke and Imagawa Sekiemon are to make their rounds and conduct a thorough investigation. The above.
　　　　　　6th month, 12th day　　To all　　　　　　　　From the Inspectors
　　　Note: Atsumi Shinemon has been instructed to issue notices to the town districts. Notice was sent to the Honda Magotarō and Honda Shirōemon households via the foot-soldier inspectors (okachi metsuke).

Hōei 4th year, Year of the Boar (1707)
　　　　　1st day of the 2nd month. Remaining at the Edo estate, at the time of servant rotation, in recent years there has been a shortage of people, causing everyone difficulty. It appears that this year in particular, many of the lower servants intend to go out into the town districts of Edo to seek extra work. The senior retainers (omote-samurai) reported this concern to Inoue Handayu, suggesting some consideration be given to the matter. Handayu reported the situation to the senior retainers (go-karō-chū), and it was directed that a new ordinance be issued. Therefore, the following ordinance is hereby issued.

　1. Since servants have been scarce in recent years, all should exercise good judgment at the time of servant rotation. The level of wages for lower servants is as has been customarily announced in the home domain each year. Those kept on continuously into the following year, as well as those engaged mid-term, should all receive wages appropriate to their station as servants. Unreasonable treatment, however, must not occur. If there are those who seek wages beyond their station, or who feign illness and otherwise fail to meet their master's expectations, the matter should be carefully considered and reported to the inspectors. Upon investigation, appropriate measures will be ordered.
