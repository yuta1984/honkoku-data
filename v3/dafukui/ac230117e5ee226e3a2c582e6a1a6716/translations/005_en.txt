…regarding Ōsaka: on the nineteenth of the coming month, when [Hori Iga-no-kami] and Yamashiro-no-kami would make an inspection tour together through the residential quarters of the yoriki and dōshin, the plan was to shoot them both down with firearms and then storm into the castle. It was only at that moment that he fully realized, beyond any doubt, the truly grave nature of the conspiracy, and he urgently wished to report the matter to Yamashiro-no-kami. However, when he went to call on him, yoriki from other units were stationed in the waiting room, and since they were all disciples of Heihachirō and members of the same faction, he judged that exposure would be certain [if he spoke there]. He therefore resolved to find a way to meet secretly with Yamashiro-no-kami at night. While he was waiting for such an opportunity, on the night of the following day, the sixteenth, Ryōzaemon came again, saying that he would hand over the spears and other weapons in his possession, and that he should accompany him to Heihachirō's place.

[Left folio]

Yielding to this, he went along, whereupon [Heihachirō] urged him once more to commit fully to the cause, just as the plan described, and showed him the firearms and gunpowder he had already prepared. He said that, given the shortage of rice transport and the scarcity of grain, they would put out an announcement in the nearby villages that Heihachirō would distribute provisions from his residence, and that once all had coordinated and the announcement boards were set up, they would all rise up simultaneously — such was the plot he was told about. He was greatly alarmed, gave a suitably measured response, and then on the night of the following day, the seventeenth, secretly reported the matter to Yamashiro-no-kami. He was told that the inspection tour would be postponed, and that Sukejirou should come down to the [present] location and report to him in person the full details of the affair as described above…
