A written report concerning Hirayama Sukejirou, a doshin serving under Atobe Yamashiro-no-kami, who was dispatched to Osaka in connection with the disturbance there.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Yabe Suruga-no-kami

The day before yesterday, the 29th, just past the sixth hour, Hirayama Sukejirou — a doshin under Atobe Yamashiro-no-kami — arrived bearing a letter addressed to me from Yamashiro-no-kami himself, sent in accordance with Yamashiro-no-kami's instructions regarding the disturbance in Osaka. Upon reading it, I learned that Oshio Heihachiro, father of Oshio Kakuonosuke, a yoriki under Yamashiro-no-kami's command, had been plotting a grave and serious conspiracy.
