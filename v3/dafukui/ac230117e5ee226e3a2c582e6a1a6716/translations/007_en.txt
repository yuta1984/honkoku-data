【Right folio】
It is thought appropriate that all three persons be placed in the custody of a daimyo. We wish to receive your instructions regarding this custodial arrangement at the earliest opportunity. Respectfully submitted.

　　The first day of the third month

Regarding the written communication: I have received and duly acknowledged the instruction that Hirayama Sukenojirō and the two others are to be placed in the custody of Ōoka Kii-no-kami.

　　The first day of the third month, Year of the Rooster　　　　　Yabe Suruga-no-kami

As there is a matter to be communicated, please come to my residence immediately. Respectfully.

【Left folio】
　　　The first day of the third month　　　　　Yabe Suruga-no-kami

　　To: Lord Ōoka Kii-no-kami
　　　　　[At] Ryūzan-kyō (Ryūzankyō residence)

I write to inform you of the following. Herewith, I am sending, via messenger, one copy of a directive from Suruga-no-kami, enclosed as a separate document. Furthermore, as it has been decided that one yoriki and doshin of the Osaka Town Magistrate's office, along with two servants, are to be placed in your custody, please be informed of this matter accordingly.
