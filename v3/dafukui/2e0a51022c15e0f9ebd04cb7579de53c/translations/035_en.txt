Munenori-sama made the rounds of courtesy visits to the senior councillors (rōjū).

Item: Same day.
　On the occasion of O-Yoshimaru-sama's imminent relocation, as a farewell gift (senbetsu), from Tokugawa Gyōbukyō (Muneyori)-sama:
　To O-Yoshimaru-sama: one sword (o-katana; Nagamitsu, valued at twenty gold pieces), one short sword (o-wakizashi; [maker's name unclear], valued at fifteen gold pieces), and one hanging scroll set (o-kakemono; a triptych of three panels).
　From the O-Renjū-sama [the wife/consort of Gyōbukyō-sama]: a writing stand (o-bundai), an inkstone box (o-suzuribako), an incense box (o-kōbako), and one folding screen panel (o-byōbu).
　From Yasu-hime-sama: a book/reading stand (o-mitsudai; lacquered in black with family crest in maki-e decoration).
　From Sennnosuke-sama (the second son of Gyōbukyō-sama): one box of medicine cases/ornamental containers (o-inrō; a set of five) were presented.

Item: Same day.
　Regarding the matter of the audience granted to the messenger (o-shisha o-etsu) at the time when Munenori-sama and O-Yoshimaru-sama would present offerings of foodstuffs (o-sakana), to take place after O-Yoshimaru-sama's relocation had been completed:
　Munenori-sama had submitted a formal inquiry (o-ukagai-sho) on the seventeenth of last month, via the residential caretaker (o-rusui) Ōtani Ichiemon (Yoshitsugu), addressed to the supervising senior councillor (go-yōgakari rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-Kami (Masasuke)-dono. In reply, it was communicated today through a household official (yōnin) that the senior councillor would grant an audience at the time of the said presentation.
