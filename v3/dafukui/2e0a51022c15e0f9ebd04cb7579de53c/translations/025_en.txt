Separate Enclosure (beppi)

　　　　　　　　　　　　　Two retainers
　As you have been granted an audience (omemie), please proceed bringing them along. Respectfully submitted.

　The above instructions were conveyed from Munenori-sama to O-Yoshimaru-sama via messenger, the household official (goyōnin) Shimada Seizaemon (Shigeyuki).

Item: Twenty-eighth day of the seventh month (at the half-hour of the Sixth, approximately 7:00 a.m.)
　O-Yoshimaru-sama's proxy, Echigo-no-kami (Nagayoshi)-sama, wearing formal long hakama (nagabakama), proceeded to the castle. In the Black Audience Chamber (Kuro Shoin), congratulations were presented for the pronouncement of adoption.

From O-Yoshimaru-sama:
　To the Kubō-sama (the Shogun): One sword, one horse (in lieu thereof, ten ryō of gold), one hundred bundles of cotton wadding, one box of kombu kelp, one box of dried sea bream, and one cask of sake were presented.
　To the Ōgosho-sama (the Retired Shogun) and the Dainagon-sama: One sword, one horse (in lieu thereof, ten ryō of gold), fifty bundles of cotton wadding, one box of dried sea bream, and one cask of sake each were presented.
　To Manjirō-sama: One box of dried sea bream, and one thousand hiki in lieu of a cask of sake.
　To the O-Heya-sama: Ten rolls of crepe silk (crimson and white), and one box of dried squid (surume).
　To Gekkōin-sama: Five rolls of crepe silk (crimson and white), and one box of dried squid were presented.
　Gifts were also given to the ladies-in-waiting (o-tsuki jochu) of both the Main Castle (Honmaru) and the West Enclosure (Nishinomaru). After these formalities were concluded, he proceeded to the West Enclosure (Nishinomaru) to present congratulations, was received by the Sōja-ban (the official in charge of formal audiences), and thereafter made rounds of courtesy calls (mawarikin) to the senior councillors (rōjū) and junior councillors (wakadoshiyori).
