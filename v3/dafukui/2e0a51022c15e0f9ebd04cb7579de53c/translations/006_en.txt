Enkyō 2nd Year, Kinoto-Ushi (Year of the Ox)

1. The 29th day of the 1st month:
　Kogorō-sama made a visit to the Main Castle (Hon-maru) from the Hitotsubashi residence to offer New Year's greetings.
　　　Note: There should be records of gifts presented and received on this occasion; however, as the Hitotsubashi residence subsequently suffered a fire and was destroyed, the records from that time are unclear, and therefore they are not recorded here.

[The reign of Ieshige-sama] In this year, on the 1st day of the 9th month, Yoshimune-sama transferred governance to him.

1. The 25th day of the 11th month, at the Hitotsubashi residence:
　The ceremony of Kogorō-sama's Okami-oki (the coming-of-age hair-setting ceremony) was held. On this occasion,

　To Kogorō-sama,
　From the Kubō-sama: through the envoy, the attendant-chamberlain Matsudaira Suruga-no-kami, two kinds of food and one carrying-load were bestowed.
　From the Ōgosho-sama: through the envoy, the attendant-chamberlain Kose Hyūga-no-kami, one ornamental white hairpiece, twenty bundles of cotton, and two kinds of food and one carrying-load were bestowed.
　From the Dainagon-sama: through the envoy, the attendant-chamberlain Matsudaira Uneme-no-shō, one box of fresh sea bream was bestowed.
　On this occasion,
　Through Kogorō-sama's envoy, the Hitotsubashi house elder Sano Buzen-no-kami:
　To the Kubō-sama: one kind of food and one carrying-load
　To the Ōgosho-sama: gold in lieu of a horse
