Echizen-no-kami, Lord Shigemasa

During the reign of Lord Yoshimune

Kanpō 3, the year Mizunoto-I (1743)

1. On the 22nd day of the 8th month, born at the Hitotsubashi residence in Edo, Musashi Province. His original hexagram (honke) is Ken (☰, Heaven/Qian), and his elemental nature is Water.
　He is addressed as Kogorō-sama.

　　He is the legitimate heir of Lord Tokugawa Gyōbukyō Munetada (the fourth son of Lord Yoshimune, great-great-grandson of Tōshōgū).
　　His mother is Toshigimi-sama, the consort (misu-no-uchi) of Lord Gyōbukyō, and the daughter of Lord Ichijō Jungo-sama.
　　He is adopted as the heir of Lord Munenori.
