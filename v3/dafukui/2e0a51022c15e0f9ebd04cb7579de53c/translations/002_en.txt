Echizen-no-Kami (Lord of Echizen Province)
Lord Shigemasa

During the reign of Lord Yoshimune

Kanpō 3, the year of Mizunoto-I (Gui-Hai)

1. On the 22nd day of the 8th month, born at the Hitotsubashi residence in Edo, Musashi Province.
(His horoscope trigram: Qian [Heaven/乾]; his elemental nature: Water)

Known by the childhood name "Kōgorō."

He is the legitimate heir of Lord Tokugawa Gyōbukyō (Muneyori)—the great-great-grandson of Tōshō-gū [Tokugawa Ieyasu] and the fourth son of Lord Yoshimune—His mother is the consort of Lord Gyōbukyō, Lady Toshi-no-Kimi (a daughter of Lord Ichijō Juigō).

He was adopted as the heir of Lord Munenori.
