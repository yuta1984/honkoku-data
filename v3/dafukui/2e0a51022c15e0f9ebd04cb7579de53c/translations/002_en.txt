Lord Shigemasa, Governor of Echizen

During the reign of Lord Yoshimune

Kanpō 3rd year (the Year of the Water Boar, 1743)

Item: Born on the 22nd day of the 8th month at the Hitotsubashi residence in Edo, Musashi Province. His natal hexagram is Qian (Heaven/乾), and his elemental nature is Water.
He is called Lord Kogorō.

He is the legitimate heir of Lord Tokugawa Munetada, Minister of Justice (Gyōbukyō)—a great-great-grandson of Tōshōgū [Tokugawa Ieyasu] and the fourth son of Lord Yoshimune.
His mother is Lady Toshitoshi-gimi, the principal consort (御簾中) of Lord Gyōbukyō, and the daughter of Lady Ichijō Jungo.
He is adopted as the heir of Lord Munenori.
