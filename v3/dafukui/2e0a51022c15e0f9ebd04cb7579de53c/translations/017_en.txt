From O-Yoshimaru-sama to Sōnori-sama: As a summer seasonal greeting (doyo no mimai), a messenger — the personal attendant Tani Yagouemon — was dispatched, and a gift of fish was presented. In the Small Study (Shōshoin), Yagouemon was granted an audience as directed, and a direct reply was received (from Sōnori-sama).

　　Note: Matters of this kind continued to occur after this point, but will not be recorded hereafter.

Item: On the same day, regarding the formalities of expressing gratitude for the recently announced adoption, the resident keeper (rusuī) Ōtani Ichiuemon Yoshitsugu submitted the following inquiry (ukagai-sho) to the rōjū in charge of the matter, Lord Hotta Sagami-no-Kami Masasuke:

　　　　　　Memorandum

　　Item: Now that the adoption has been officially announced, at the time when Hyōbu-no-Taifu presents his formal thanks, should O-Yoshimaru also present thanks by proxy and offer a gift?

　　Item: After O-Yoshimaru's moving-in ceremony (watamashi), should we inquire about the occasion for father and son to together present their formal thanks? Furthermore, on both such occasions of thanks, should it be arranged for their retainers to be granted audiences as well?

　　Item: Regarding the senior councilors (rōjū) and other officials: should gifts be presented in accordance with the established protocol used when presenting thanks upon a formal attendance visit (sankin-rei)?

　　　　　　The above.
