Sixth Month

The following written instructions were delivered by Senior Councilor (Rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke, who was in charge of matters relating to the adoption:

· Regarding the name of Kōgorō, you are hereby instructed to change it as you see fit; please carry out accordingly.

· Item: You should understand that Kōgorō is to be moved to your residence once the construction of your mansion has been completed.

· Item: All matters are to be conducted in accordance with the family's status and rank, and everything is to be arranged accordingly.

Sixth Month

· Item: As Kōgorō has been appointed as an adopted son, both parties shall exchange one box of side dishes (sakana) each and one letter of congratulation. The date for this exchange shall be mutually agreed upon, to be held between the 13th and 15th of the coming month.

· Item: Both parties shall also exchange one box of side dishes each and one letter of congratulation with the Lord Gyōbu-kyō and his consort (御簾中). The date for this shall be separately agreed upon between the parties.
