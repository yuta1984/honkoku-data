6th Month

At this occasion, the Senior Councilor serving as the official in charge of the adoption matter (Oyōshi Goyōgakari), Hotta Sagami-no-Kami 《marginal note: Masasuke》, presented the following written instructions:

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　To Matsudaira Hyōbudaifu

　　　　　　Regarding the name of Kogorō, you are hereby directed to change it as you see fit. You are accordingly to act in accordance with this intent.
　　　　　1. You are to understand that Kogorō is to be moved [to your residence] once the construction of your residence is completed.
　　　　　1. In all matters, you are to conduct affairs in accordance with your family's rank (kakaku), and are to manage everything accordingly.

　　　　　　　6th Month

　　　　　1. Since Kogorō has been appointed as [your] adopted son, an exchange of congratulatory gifts — one box of side dishes (sakana) each — in the customary manner is to be made between [the parties]. Regarding the date, it shall be as agreed upon within the 13th or 15th of the coming days.
　　　　　1. To Gyōbukyō-dono and to the Lady [his consort] as well, an exchange of congratulatory gifts — one box of side dishes each — in the customary manner is to be made. Regarding the date, it shall be as agreed upon on a separate day.

　　　　　　　6th Month
