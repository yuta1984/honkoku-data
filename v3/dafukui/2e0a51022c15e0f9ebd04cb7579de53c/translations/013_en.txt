...presenting one arrangement of fresh sea bream, delivered to the Tsukiji residence (the Hitotsubashi secondary residence).

　　Note: As the main Hitotsubashi residence had burned down earlier that spring, Lord Kōgorō was at this time residing at the Tsukiji residence.

The Hitotsubashi chief retainer (karō) Hosoi Hida-no-kami came out to receive the messenger at the entry step (shikidai) and the anteroom (itanoma), and guided him into the residence. Thereupon, in the inner reception chamber, Lord Kōgorō was granted an audience, and personally presented the gift of long abalone (nagaawabi).

　　Note: At this time, the wet nurse was in attendance at his side and handled the proceedings on his behalf.

When the above was concluded, in the aforementioned reception room, Hida-no-kami conveyed the formal reply, and Kageyu withdrew from the residence.

　　Note: While a direct reply from Lord Kōgorō himself would ordinarily have been proper, owing to his young age, the reply was conveyed through Hida-no-kami.

Item: On the same day, regarding the matter of Lord Kōgorō's personal name, Lord Munenori submitted a formal inquiry (ukagai-sho) through the residential caretaker (rusui) Tsuda Kuemon Kanjo to the supervising Senior Councilor (yōgakari rōjū) Lord Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masaaki, as follows:

　　　　　As it has been ordered that Kōgorō's name be changed, we wish to change his name as described on the accompanying separate sheet. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　13th day of the 6th month　　　Matsudaira Hyōbu-no-tayū
