...one box of fresh sea bream was presented and delivered to the Tsukiji residence (the Hitotsubashi lower residence).

　　　Note: As the Hitotsubashi main residence had burned down during the previous spring, Lord Kogorō was at this time residing at the Tsukiji residence.

The Hitotsubashi chief retainer (okarō), Hosoi Hidano-Kami, came out to meet the messenger at the entry step (shikidai) and the anteroom (itanoma), guided him through the residence, and thereafter in the inner reception room (oku-ozashiki), Lord Kogorō was made to receive him in audience, and with his own hands bestowed a large dried abalone (onagaawabi) upon him.

　　　Note: At this time, the wet nurse (onme) was at his side and attended to the proceedings.

When the above was concluded, in the front reception room (saizen no ozashiki), Hidano-Kami conveyed the reply, and Kageyu withdrew.

　　　Note: Although a direct reply (jikitō) would have been appropriate, owing to Lord Kogorō's young age, the reply was delivered through Hidano-Kami on his behalf.
