Sixth Month　　　　　Retainer of Matsudaira Hyōbu-no-Taifu
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ōtani Ichiuemon

The foregoing matters were inquired of Lord Sagami-no-Kami through the household manager (yōnin) Kasugai Shōbei, and the responses were communicated to Ichiuemon as follows:

　　Item: At the time of presenting the formal thanks on this occasion, there is no need to make a separate inquiry regarding O-Yoshimaru-sama's expression of thanks.

　　Item: After O-Yoshimaru-sama's moving-in ceremony (watamashi), there is no need to make a separate inquiry regarding the occasion for father and son to present their formal thanks.

　　　Note: For further details, see the genealogical record (gofu) of Sōnori-sama.
