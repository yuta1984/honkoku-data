One item: Lady Ogimaru is to present one gift each to the Lord Kubō (Shogun) and the Lord Dainagon, to be offered through the Ōoku.
　　　　　Three ryō (silver) each — to the Senior Ladies (rōjo-shū) of the Gohonmaru (Main Keep)
　　　　　Two ryō (silver) each — to the Omotetsukai (messenger officials) at the guest reception
　　　　　Thirty ryō (silver) — to all ladies-in-waiting (sō-jochū)

　　　The above gifts are to be arranged as coming from Lady Ogimaru. As for Hyōbu-no-taifu (the lord/official), no gifts from him are necessary.

　　　Separate document:
　　　　　Regarding Lady Ogimaru's visit to the Ōoku — a written list of names and official titles of the accompanying retainers and ladies-in-waiting is to be submitted.

　　One: The castle visit is expected to take place around the 15th of the month. In due course, notification will be issued by the monthly duty official (tsuki-ban).
　　One: The formal greetings (o-rei) by Hyōbu-no-taifu are also to take place. This matter too shall be communicated through the monthly duty official.
　　One: Regarding any inquiries about the documents handed over today, prior to notification from the monthly duty official, such matters are to be reported to this office.
　　One: As for the gifts to be presented to the Ōoku on behalf of the Lord Kubō and the Lord Dainagon, items such as inkstone screens (suzuri-byōbu) and the like would be appropriate.

One: On the 14th day of the 4th month, the Senior Councilor (Rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke issued the above instructions, and accordingly...
