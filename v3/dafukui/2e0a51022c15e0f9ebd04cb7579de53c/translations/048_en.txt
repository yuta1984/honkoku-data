Item: Lady Ogimaru is to present one gift each to the Lord Kubō (Shogun) and the Lord Dainagon, to be offered from the Ōoku, as follows:

　　　　Three silver coins each　　　　Ō-omote (Main Keep): Senior Ladies-in-waiting (rōjo-shū)
　　　　Two silver coins each　　　　　Guest Reception (o-kyaku-ōsetsu)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　 Omote-tsukai (Messenger Officials)
　　　　Thirty silver coins (total)　　 All Ladies-in-waiting (sō-jochū)

　　　The above are to be arranged as gifts from Lady Ogimaru. No gifts are required from the Hyōbu-no-taifu (the accompanying senior official).

　　　Separate document:
　　　　　　Regarding Lady Ogimaru's visit to the Ōoku: a written list of names and official titles of the accompanying retainers and ladies-in-waiting is to be submitted.

　　　Item: The castle visit (登城) is expected to take place around the 15th of the month. Notice will be given through the monthly duty official (tsuki-ban).
　　　Item: The Hyōbu-no-taifu shall also make a formal courtesy call (o-rei). This too shall be communicated through the monthly duty official.
　　　Item: Regarding any inquiries about the documents handed over today, prior to any formal notification from the monthly duty official, such inquiries are to be directed to this office.
　　　Item: As for the one gift each to be presented to the Lord Kubō and the Lord Dainagon at the Ōoku, items such as inkstone screens (suzuri-byōbu) and the like are considered appropriate.
