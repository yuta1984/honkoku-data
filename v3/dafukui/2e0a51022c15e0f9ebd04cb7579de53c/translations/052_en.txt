In the lower seat (ge-dan) of the audience chamber (o-taimenjo) of the Ōoku, Lord Yoshimaru presented himself before the Kubō (the Shogun). The Shogun's address (jōi) was lengthy, and in the upper seat (jō-dan), Lord Yoshimaru received long abalone (naga-awabi) directly from the Shogun's hand.

　　Note: The aforementioned audience chamber of the Ōoku is said to be the room where the princesses and the ladies-in-waiting (misuchū-sama) of the Two Lords (go-ryōkyō) meet with the Shogun when they visit the castle.

The following were presented to the Shogun: one long sword (tachi), one gold coin in lieu of a horse, five rolls of crepe silk (chirimen); and from private funds (go-naishō): one box of kelp (konbu), one box of dried sea bream (hoshi-tai), one cask of sake (o-taru), and additionally one box containing five inrō (medicine cases).

From the Shogun, Lord Yoshimaru received directly: one short sword (wakizashi) made by Nobukuni, valued at fifteen gold coins, and additionally one folding screen with brushwork (o-fude byōbu). He put on the received wakizashi and once again offered his formal thanks.

The Shogun then withdrew to his inner chambers (nyūgo).

　　Note: The matter of receiving the wakizashi follows the precedent established for Lord Mito (Mito-sama), as relayed by Hōda, a senior attendant (otoshiyori) of the Hitotsubashi household.

To the Ōgosho (the Retired Shogun), the following were presented via a messenger: one long sword, one gold coin in lieu of a horse, and three rolls of crepe silk. This was done in accordance with instructions given on the 13th of this month.

　　Note: The presentation from private funds (go-naishō) is to be made when visiting the Ōoku of the Nishi-maru (Western Citadel) on the 15th of the coming 5th month, in accordance with the previously confirmed arrangements.

Before the Grand Counselor (Dainagon-sama), Lord Yoshimaru presented himself in the lower seat of the aforementioned audience chamber, and received long abalone from his hand (the record continues on the following page).
