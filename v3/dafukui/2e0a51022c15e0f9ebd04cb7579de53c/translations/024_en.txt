Item: On the same day (the twenty-fifth day of the seventh month),
　As it had previously been ordered that the congratulatory ceremony should be performed on behalf of O-Yoshimaru-sama as his proxy (mydai), Munenori-sama dispatched the resident steward (o-rusui) Ōtani Ichiemon (Yoshitsugu) as messenger to the duty official (Goyōban) Hotta Sagami-no-kami (Masasuke), submitting a written statement naming the proxy representative.

　　　　　On the occasion of presenting congratulations for the adoption,
　　　　　　　　　　　　Proxy for O-Yoshimaru:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Echigo-no-kami
　　　　　The above is as stated. Respectfully submitted.
　　　　　　　Twenty-fifth day of the seventh month　　　Matsudaira Hyōbu-no-taifu

Item: Twenty-seventh day of the seventh month.
　A written notice (kirigami) bearing the joint signatures of the senior councillors (rōjū) arrived for Munenori-sama.
　　　　　On the twenty-eighth day of tomorrow, at the Hour of the Fifth (approximately 8 o'clock in the morning), you are to proceed to the castle and present your congratulations for the pronouncement of the adoption.
　　　　　Moreover, formal long hakama (nagabakama) are to be worn. Furthermore, one proxy representative for O-Yoshimaru is likewise to proceed to the castle wearing formal long hakama.
　　　　　Respectfully submitted.
　　　　　　　Twenty-seventh day of the seventh month　　　Honda Hōki-no-kami
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Hotta Sagami-no-kami
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sakai Utano-kami
　　　　　　　To: Matsudaira Hyōbu-no-taifu
