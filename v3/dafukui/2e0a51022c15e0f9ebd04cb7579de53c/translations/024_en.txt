1. On the same day:

As there had previously been an order that the formal greetings (*o-rei*) were to be presented with Lord Yoshimaru's proxy (*omyōdai*), Lord Munenori dispatched the *o-rusui* (caretaker official) Ōtani Ichiemon Yoshitsugu as messenger to the Duty Elder (*goyōban*), Lord Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke, delivering a written document (*kakitsuke*) bearing the name of the designated proxy.

　　　　　On the occasion of presenting formal greetings for the adoption,
　　　　　　　　　　　　Acting as proxy for Yoshimaru:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Echigo-no-kami
　　　　　The above is as stated. Respectfully.
　　　　　　　25th day of the 7th month　　　Matsudaira Hyōbu-no-taifu

1. 27th day of the 7th month:

A *kirigami* (official note) bearing the joint signatures of the Senior Councilors (*Rōjū*) arrived for Lord Munenori.

　　　　　On the 28th, tomorrow, you are to proceed to the castle at the Hour of the Five (*itsutsu-doki*, approximately 8:00 a.m.) and present your formal greetings for the order regarding the adoption. You are to wear *nagabakama* (formal long trailing hakama) for this occasion. Furthermore, one person acting as proxy for Yoshimaru is likewise to proceed to the castle wearing *nagabakama*. Respectfully.
　　　　　　　27th day of the 7th month　　　Honda Hōki-no-kami
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Hotta Sagami-no-kami
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sakai Utano-kami
　　　　　To: Matsudaira Hyōbu-no-taifu
