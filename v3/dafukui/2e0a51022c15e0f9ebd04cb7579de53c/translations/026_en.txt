Note: On this occasion, Munenori-sama also proceeded to the castle to present congratulations; details of the gifts presented are recorded in Munenori-sama's genealogical record (go-fu).

Item: Twenty-ninth day of the seventh month. In connection with the adoption ceremony having been concluded successfully,
　From O-Yoshimaru-sama to Munenori-sama, as a congratulatory gift, via messenger, the Hitotsubashi household's chief elder (karō) Itami Hyōgo (Naomasa):
　One sword, one gold piece in lieu of a horse, one blade (Nagamitsu, valued at twenty gold pieces, with a three-piece set of fittings [mitokomono] in shakudō nanakoji with the family crest), one blade, five summer robes (katabira), five unlined robes (hitoemono), one box of kombu kelp, one box of dried sea bream, and one cask of sake were presented.
　　Note: As the mounting (koshirae) for the above blade could not be completed in time, it was handled by written inventory (mokuroku) for the time being.
　　Subsequently, on the twenty-first day of the ninth month, the mounting having been completed, it was sent along with a letter from the Hitotsubashi household official (goyōnin) via a foot inspector (okachi metsuke).

Item: The last day of the same month. For the same reason as above,
　From Munenori-sama to O-Yoshimaru-sama, via messenger, the chief elder (karō) Sakai Geki (Tomotaka):
　One sword, one gold piece in lieu of a horse, one blade (Moriye), one short sword (tantō/wakizashi; Kanemitsu), five summer robes (katabira), five unlined robes (hitoemono), one box of kombu kelp, one box of dried sea bream, and one cask of sake were presented.
