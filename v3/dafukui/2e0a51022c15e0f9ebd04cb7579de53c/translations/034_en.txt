(Continued from the previous page: from the O-Heya-sama, one folded arrangement of fresh sea bream, and) additionally one box of o-kinchaku (purses/pouches) was received.

From the Kubō-sama [the Shogun]: the two senior ladies-in-waiting (o-toshiyori) who had accompanied O-Yoshimaru-sama were each granted three rolls of cloth; the junior senior ladies (wakadoshiyori), the middle-ranking ladies-in-waiting (o-chūrō), the keepers of the inner gate (o-jōguchi), and the outer attendants (o-omotesukai) were each granted two rolls; and thirty pieces of silver were bestowed upon all the ladies of the household, from the head secretary (o-yūhitsu) downward.
　　Note: In addition to the above, gifts were also distributed to those who had remained on duty at the residence in the absence of the party.

At around seven-and-a-half hours (approximately 5 p.m.), O-Yoshimaru-sama withdrew from the castle. On this occasion, one set of stacked lacquer picnic boxes (o-sagejū) was received as a gift.

Item: Sixteenth day of the twelfth month. In connection with the same occasion as above, and by way of courtesy calls (o-rei),
　Munenori-sama made the rounds to visit the senior councillors (rōjū).

Item: Eighteenth day of the same month, at four-hour time (approximately 10 a.m.). In connection with the same occasion as above,
　O-Yoshimaru-sama proceeded from the Hitotsubashi residence to the Nishi-maru Inner Chambers (Ōoku) for an audience at the castle. (This had been announced in advance as far as the Hitotsubashi residence.)
　To the Ōgosho-sama [the Retired Shogun]: five rolls of crepe silk (chirimen) and one cask of assorted goods (isshu ichika) were presented.
　From the Ōgosho-sama: one sword (o-katana) personally bestowed by hand (Hisakuni, valued at 250 gold pieces, with fittings of three settings bearing the Aoi family crest, [further details unclear]), one box containing a picture scroll (emakimono; depicting wild horses, painted by Eisen [Kano Eisenin]), and additionally, as a special personal gesture, toys (o-teasobi: two bamboo hobby-horses and seven masks/face pieces) were received as gifts.

From the Ōgosho-sama: the two senior ladies-in-waiting who had accompanied O-Yoshimaru-sama were each granted three rolls of cloth; the junior senior ladies, the middle-ranking ladies-in-waiting, the keepers of the inner gate, and the outer attendants were each granted two rolls; and thirty pieces of silver were bestowed upon all the ladies of the household, from the head secretary downward. O-Yoshimaru-sama withdrew from the castle before dusk.
　　Note: The details of the gifts received at the time of withdrawal are not known.
