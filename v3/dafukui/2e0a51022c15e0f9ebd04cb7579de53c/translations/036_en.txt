Item: The 21st day of the 12th month.
　The furnishings and personal effects (o-dōgu) of O-Yoshimaru-sama were conveyed one after another from the Hitotsubashi residence (o-yashiki). The Hitotsubashi inner household supply official (o-hiroshiki go-yōtashi), Gamō Denjirō, and the other accompanying persons were provided with sake, soup (suimono), and gifts (kudashimono).
　　Note: The said furnishings were conveyed over the course of two days, from today the 21st through the following day the 22nd.
　　　The first consignment of furnishings entered through the main front gate (omote o-mon); the second consignment entered through the western gate (nishi o-mon).

Item: Same day.
　Regarding the senior lady attendant (o-toshiyori) Sakio, who is attached to O-Yoshimaru-sama's service (from the Hitotsubashi household), the household elder (o-karō) Sakai Geki (Tomotaka) conveyed the directive that she is to receive an allocation of thirty silver pieces (o-ateyuki gin) and a stipend for two persons (futari-fuchi). The other lady attendants in O-Yoshimaru-sama's service were also each granted their respective stipends in rice (o-kirimai).
　　Note: More than half of the lady attendants attached to O-Yoshimaru-sama arrived today from the Hitotsubashi household.

Item: The 22nd day of the same month, at the household elder's chamber (o-karō-beya) of the residence.
　To the members of O-Yoshimaru-sama's attendant staff, regarding her adoption into the household on this occasion: they were instructed to serve dutifully in all matters, just as they would in the main official household (o-omote); and, given that she is of tender years, they were to avoid frivolous or imprudent conduct (hanaji), and to refrain from allowing matters of a base or unseemly nature to reach her attention.
