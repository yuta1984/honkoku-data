1. On the 1st day of the 9th month, the seventh-night celebration (o-shichiya) for Lord Kōgorō was held at the Hitotsubashi residence. On this occasion, the following gifts were bestowed upon Lord Kōgorō:

From the Kubō-sama [the Shōgun], conveyed through the special envoy Senior Councilor (Rōjū) Matsudaira Sakon-shōgen (Noritsugu): one long sword (Bizen-no-kuni Iemori, valued at 20 gold pieces), one short sword (Nakajima Rai, valued at 10 gold pieces), three layers of infant's ceremonial robes (ubagi), and two varieties of provisions in two hampers, all received as gifts.

From the Udaishō-sama [the Heir Apparent / Lord of the Right], conveyed through the special envoy Senior Councilor Matsudaira Noto-no-kami (Noriyoshi): one short sword (Nagashū Yasuyoshi, valued at 20 gold pieces), two layers of infant's ceremonial robes, and two varieties of provisions in one hamper, all received as gifts.

From the Dainagon-sama [the Middle Counselor], conveyed through the same envoy: one short sword (Hasebe Kunihira, valued at 10 gold pieces), two layers of infant's ceremonial robes, and one variety of provisions in one hamper, all received as gifts.

1. On the 1st day of the 11th month, on the occasion of the o-irōnashi [the first formal change of clothing] celebration for Lord Kōgorō held at the Hitotsubashi residence, through the Hitotsubashi senior retainer (o-karō) Hirai Kunai-shōyū serving as messenger, the following were presented:

To the Kubō-sama: one long sword, gold in lieu of a horse, five rolls of silk, and two varieties of provisions in one hamper.
To the Udaishō-sama: one long sword, gold in lieu of a horse, five rolls of silk, and one variety of provisions in one hamper.
To the Dainagon-sama: one long sword, gold in lieu of a horse, and one variety of provisions in one hamper.
