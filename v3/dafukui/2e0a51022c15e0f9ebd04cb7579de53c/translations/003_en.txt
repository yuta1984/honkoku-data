On the first day of the ninth month of Kanpō 3 (1743), at the Hitotsubashi residence,
a celebration was held for Kogoro-sama's Oshichiya (seventh-night ceremony following birth). On this occasion,
the following gifts were bestowed upon Kogoro-sama:

From the Kubō-sama (the Shogun), through the envoy Senior Councilor Matsudaira Sakon-shōgen Norimurajō, the following were received: one sword (Bizen no Kuni Kashu, valued at twenty pieces of gold), one short sword (Nakajima Rai, valued at ten pieces of gold), three layers of birth robes, and two kinds of goods in two lots.

From the Udaishō-sama (the Lord Great General, i.e., the heir), through the envoy Senior Councilor Matsudaira Noto-no-kami Noriyori, the following were received: one short sword (Nagashu Yasuyoshi, valued at twenty pieces of gold), two layers of birth robes, and two kinds of goods in one lot.

From the Dainagon-sama (the Major Counselor), through the same envoy (Matsudaira Noto-no-kami Noriyori), the following were received: one short sword (Hasebe Kunihira, valued at ten pieces of gold), two layers of birth robes, and one kind of goods in one lot.

On the first day of the eleventh month of the same year, at the Hitotsubashi residence,
on the occasion of the Oironaoshi (the ceremonial first meal) celebration for Kogoro-sama, through the envoy Hitotsubashi Chief Retainer Hirai Kunai-no-shō, the following were presented:

To the Kubō-sama: one sword, gold horse substitute, five scrolls, and two kinds of goods in one lot.

To the Udaishō-sama: one sword, gold horse substitute, five scrolls, and one kind of goods in one lot.

To the Dainagon-sama: one sword, gold horse substitute, and one kind of goods in one lot.
