**[Regarding Gifts to the Ladies-in-Waiting and Table Attendants (*omotekai*) Attached to the Ōgosho-sama and the Dainagon-sama]**

(Continued from the preceding section) ...on the occasion of presenting formal greetings for the adoption, we wish to send gifts from myself as well as from Yoshimaru in the same manner as those sent to the ladies-in-waiting of the Main Keep (Honmaru). We humbly submit this inquiry. Respectfully.

**[Attached Response Note (*tsukefuda*)]**
You may send [the gifts] as inquired.

1. On the 25th day of the 7th month, a summons was received from the Duty Elder (*goyōban*), Lord Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke. The *o-rusui* (caretaker official) Ōtani Ichiemon Yoshitsugu was dispatched by Lord Munenori. A directive was issued via an attached note (*tsukefuda*) in response to the inquiry submitted on the 21st of the previous month.

**[Content of the Formal Inquiry]**

　To Lord Manjirō: One ceremonial sword, gold (horse substitute gift), one box of dried sea bream, 1,000 *mon* in lieu of a cask of sake
　To Gekkōin-sama: Ten rolls of silk, one box of dried squid
　To the Lady Takehime-no-kata: Same as above

On this occasion of presenting formal greetings for the adoption, we humbly inquire whether we should offer the above gifts from myself as well as from Yoshimaru.

**[Attached Response Note (*tsukefuda*)]**
　For Lord Manjirō: The ceremonial sword and horse substitute gift are to be discontinued; [send] one box of dried sea bream and 1,000 *mon* in lieu of a cask of sake.
　For Gekkōin-sama: Five rolls of silk and one box of dried squid.
