Attached document

　　　　　　　　　　　Formerly known as Kogorō,
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Yoshimaru

The above document having been submitted, on the same day at the eighth hour of the evening [approximately 10 p.m.], a summons was received from Lord Sagami-no-Kami, and the resident keeper (orusui) Ōtani Ichiemon 《marginal note: Yoshitsugu》 went to attend. He was informed that the name change of Lord Kogorō had been approved as requested. In order to express gratitude for this, Lord Munenori personally called upon the duty Senior Councilor (goyōban), Lord Sakai Uta-no-Kami 《marginal note: Tadatomo》, as well as Lord Sagami-no-Kami, and dispatched the resident keeper as a messenger to the remaining Senior Councilors.

1. On the same day, as Lord Kogorō had been renamed Yoshimaru, Lord Munenori sent his middle elder (ochūrō), Akashi Shōgen 《marginal note: Norihusa》, as a messenger, delivering the name register (omyō mokuroku) together with one box of fresh sea bream.

　　　　　　Oral message (okōjō):

　　　Regarding the matter of his name: having submitted an inquiry to the Senior Councilors, and having received approval as requested, a formal document (origami) has been prepared and is hereby presented. As this is a name that belonged to an ancestor, this is presented as a celebratory offering, with one item of accompanying food (osakana) added.
