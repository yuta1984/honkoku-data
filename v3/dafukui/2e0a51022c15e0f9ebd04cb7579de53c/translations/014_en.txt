**Separate Document (Attached)**

Regarding Kogoro:
Matsudaira Yoshimaru

Having submitted the above (request for the name change) as stated, on the same day at the eighth evening hour (approximately 2:00 PM), a summons arrived from the Lord of Sagami, and the domain's resident representative (rusui) Ōtani Ichiemon Yoshitsugu went to attend. He was informed that the renaming of Lord Kogoro had been approved exactly as petitioned.

As an expression of gratitude for this approval, visits were made to Lord Munénori's duty elder (goyōban), Sakai Utanokami Tadatomo, as well as to the Lord of Sagami. For the remaining Senior Councillors (rōjū), the domain's resident representative was dispatched as a messenger.

One entry, same day:

Upon Lord Kogoro's renaming to Yoshimaru,

Lord Munénori sent his Middle Elder (chūrō) Akashi Shōgen Norihusa as a messenger, delivering the written record of the new name along with one box of fresh sea bream.

**Verbal Message:**
Regarding the name, a petition was submitted to the Senior Councillors, and approval was granted exactly as requested; therefore, this written acknowledgment (orikami) has been prepared and presented.
As this name belonged to an honored ancestor, congratulations are offered in celebration, and one portion of accompaniment (fish) has been added and presented.
