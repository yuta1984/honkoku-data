One entry, same day:

From Lord Munénori, to the duty elder (goyōban) Sakai Utanokami Tadatomo, as well as to the assigned Senior Councillor (goyōgakari rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke,

Notification was delivered via the domain's resident representative (rusui) as messenger, informing them that Lord Kogoro's name had been changed from this day forward to Yoshimaru.

One entry, same day:

From Lord Yoshimaru,

A letter of inquiry regarding Lord Munénori's health and well-being was sent to his household officials (yōnin), accompanied by a covering letter from the Hitotsubashi household official.

　　　Note: Until Lord Yoshimaru's transfer of residence, such morning greetings and inquiries were made every day as described above.
　　　Lord Munénori also made inquiries regarding health and well-being every other day through letters from his household officials; however, these will not be recorded hereafter.
