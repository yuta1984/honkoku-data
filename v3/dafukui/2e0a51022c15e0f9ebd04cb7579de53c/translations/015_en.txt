1. On the same day,
　Lord Munenori dispatched the resident keeper (orusui) as a messenger to notify the duty Senior Councilor (goyōban), Lord Sakai Uta-no-Kami 《marginal note: Tadatomo》, and the assigned Senior Councilor (goyōgakari rōjū), Lord Hotta Sagami-no-Kami 《marginal note: Masasuke》, that Lord Kogorō's name had been changed to Yoshimaru effective that day.

1. On the same day,
　A letter arrived from the Hitotsubashi household's steward (goyōnin), addressed to Lord Munenori's stewards, on behalf of Lord Yoshimaru, to inquire after Lord Munenori's health and well-being.

　　Note: Until Lord Yoshimaru's relocation, such morning inquiries after Lord Munenori's health were made daily in this manner.
　　　　　Lord Munenori also, on alternating days, sent inquiries after Lord Yoshimaru's health and well-being via letter through his stewards; however, these will not be recorded hereafter.
