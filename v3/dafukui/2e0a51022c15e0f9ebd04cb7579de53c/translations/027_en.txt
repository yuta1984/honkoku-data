Item: Tenth day of the eighth month. For the same reason as above,
　On the occasion of Munenori-sama's visit to Tōei-zan (Kan'ei-ji Temple),
　From O-Yoshimaru-sama as well,
　To the shrine (go-miya) and to the memorial shrines of Taiyū-in-sama, Gen'yū-in-sama, and Jōken-in-sama, one sword and two pieces of silver in lieu of a horse each were offered.
　　Note: To the Nikkō go-monzeki as well, one sword and one piece of silver in lieu of a horse were presented.

Item: Nineteenth day of the same month. The chief elder (karō) Sakai Geki (Tomotaka) and all household officials (goyōnin) down to the lowest ranks were each assigned their respective quarters (o-heya-tsuki) on a provisional basis.

Item: Twenty-third day of the same month.
　O-Yoshimaru-sama moved from the Tsukiji residence to the Hitotsubashi mansion (at the Hour of the Ox [around 10 a.m.]), in celebration of the completion of the construction (go-fushin go-jōju) of the Hitotsubashi mansion.

Item: Twenty-fifth day of the same month. In connection with Tokugawa Gyōbukyō (Muneyori)-sama having moved from the Tsukiji residence to the Hitotsubashi mansion on the twenty-first of the said month, as a congratulatory gift, a female messenger (onnatsukai) was sent from the Hon-maru with gifts.
