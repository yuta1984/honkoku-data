Enkyō 3rd Year, Hinoe-Tora (Year of the Tiger)

1. The 25th day of the 9th month:
　Kogorō-sama visited the Main Castle (Hon-maru) from the Hitotsubashi residence to offer New Year's greetings.
　　　Note: There should be records of gifts presented and received on this occasion; however, as the Hitotsubashi residence subsequently suffered a fire and was destroyed, the records from that time are unclear, and therefore they are not recorded here.

1. The 1st day of the 10th month:
　Kogorō-sama, as above, visited the Nishi-maru (West Castle).
　　　Note: Same as above.
