Item: On the occasion of New Year's greetings (nen-rei) upon attending the West Keep (Nishinomaru),
　　　　To the Ōgosho-sama: one box of assorted fish (hako-zakana)
　　　　To the Dainagon-sama: same as above

　　Apart from the above, as regards the regular customary gifts (jōshiki o-agimono), there are none, as O-Yoshimaru-sama is still of a young age.
　　　Note: However, on occasions when O-Yoshimaru-sama receives a celebratory gift (shūgi), if there is a gift received (go-hairyō-mono), formal thanks are presented along with a return gift (o-agimono).

　　Item: As for other various persons of rank, no routine gifts have yet been presented; however, on occasions when O-Yoshimaru-sama receives celebratory gifts from various persons, return gifts (o-kaeshi-mono) were presented from this side to the following:

　　　　Gekkōin-sama　　　　　　Take-hime-sama
　　　　The Two Persons of Tayasu-sama　　Mito Saisho-sama
　　　　Mito Go-misu-naka Chiyohime-sama
　　　　Kyoto Ichijō Taikō-sama　　　　　Same, Kampaku-sama

　　As listed above.
