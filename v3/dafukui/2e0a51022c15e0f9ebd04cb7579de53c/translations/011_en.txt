Once the above matters were concluded, he proceeded to the Nishi-no-maru (West Citadel) to pay his respects, doing so through the Sōjaban (Master of Ceremonies). Thereafter, he made rounds of courtesy visits to the Senior Councilors (Rōjū) and Junior Councilors (Wakadoshiyori).

Item: On the same day, Senior Councilor Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke, who was in charge of the matter, delivered the following written instructions to the chief retainer (karō) of the Hitotsubashi household:

　　　Regarding Lord Kōgorō, as he has been appointed as the adopted son of Matsudaira Hyōbu-no-taifu, you are to inform [the Lord of Hitotsubashi] of this matter.

Item: On the same day, a written instruction delivered by Senior Councilor Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke to the Grand Inspector (Ōmetsuke) Ishikawa Tosa-no-kami Masatomo was circulated by the resident agents (rusuii) of Matsudaira Mutsu-no-kami Munemura and Matsudaira Sagami-no-kami Muneyasu:

　　　As Kōgorō has been appointed as the adopted son of Matsudaira Hyōbu-no-taifu, on the morning of the 13th, at the Hour of the Ox (approximately 10:00 a.m.), the following persons shall dress in formal hemp kamishimo and proceed to the Main Citadel (Hon-maru) and Nishi-no-maru (West Citadel) to offer congratulations to the Kubō-sama (the Shōgun) and the Ōgosho-sama (the Retired Shōgun): hereditary vassals (fudai), high-ranking ceremonial officials (kōke), those assigned to the Kari-no-ma (Wild Goose Chamber) and their eldest sons, Sōjaban (Masters of Ceremonies) and their eldest sons, those assigned to the Kiku-no-ma Fuchidome (Chrysanthemum Chamber gallery seats), various unit commanders (Shobangashira), various corps commanders (Shomotogashira), and officials of the rank of Hōi (wearing court robes) and above.

　　　Note: Those who are ill or of young age shall send a messenger to the residence of the Senior Councilor, the Lord of Oki-no-kami, to offer their congratulations.
