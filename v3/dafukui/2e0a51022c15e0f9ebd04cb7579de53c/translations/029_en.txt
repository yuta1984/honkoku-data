1. On the 21st day of the 9th month, at the Hitotsubashi residence, the following persons were appointed as attendants to Ogimaru-sama:
　　　　　Page (okoshō): Uehara Kyūzō　　　Same: Iwamoto Kinai
　　　　　Close attendant (okinju): Hashimoto Sahyōe　Same: Nakashima Shōzō
　　　　　Same: Iizuka Gohyōe　　　Same: Namikawa Seijirō

1. On the 25th day of the same month,
　Lord Munenori visited the Hitotsubashi residence,
　and had his first meeting (taigen) with Ogimaru-sama.
　On this occasion, three bolts of colored habutai silk, five dolls, and two pieces of gofunsha were presented to Ogimaru-sama.

1. On the 25th day of the 10th month,
　Ogimaru-sama departed from the Hitotsubashi residence accompanied by Tokugawa Gyōbukyō Munetada-sama,
　and made his first visit to the castle (登城) to the inner quarters (Ōoku) of the Main Keep (Honmaru) following his adoption.
　Prior notification had been sent to the Hitotsubashi residence in advance, and on that occasion,
　from the Shōgun (Kubō-sama): ten bolts of crepe (chirimen), one kind of appetizer (sakana), one scroll hand-rolled by the Shōgun himself, and additionally two kosode robes were received as gifts.
