Item: Twenty-first day of the ninth month. At the Hitotsubashi mansion, the following persons were appointed as O-Yoshimaru-sama's attendants (o-tsukininto):
　　　　　O-koshō [page]: Uehara Kyūzō　　　Same: Iwamoto Kinai
　　　　　O-kinshū [close attendant]: Hashimoto Sahyōe　　　Same: Nakajima Shōzō
　　　　　Same: Iizuka Gohei　　　Same: Namiwa Seijirō

Item: Twenty-fifth day of the same month.
　Munenori-sama visited the Hitotsubashi mansion,
　and had his first audience (go-taigan) with O-Yoshimaru-sama on this occasion.
　To O-Yoshimaru-sama, the following were presented: three bolts of colored habutae silk, five dolls (ningyō), and two pieces of御服紗 (go-fukusa, ceremonial wrapping cloth).

Item: Twenty-fifth day of the tenth month.
　O-Yoshimaru-sama, accompanied by Tokugawa Gyōbukyō (Munetada)-sama, departed from the Hitotsubashi mansion
　and entered the Ōoku of the Main Keep (o-honmaru) for the first time since being adopted — this entry into the castle having been announced in advance as far as the Hitotsubashi mansion.
　On this occasion, from the Kubō-sama:
　ten rolls of chirimen (figured crepe silk), one kind of o-sakana (ceremonial food offering), one scroll handwritten by the Kubō-sama himself (o-te-mizukara maki-zōshi),
　and additionally two kosode (short-sleeved kimono) were received as gifts.
