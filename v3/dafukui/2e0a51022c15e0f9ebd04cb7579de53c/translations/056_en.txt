Item: On the second day of the fifth month, at Zōjōji Temple,
　As the thirty-third anniversary memorial service for Lord Yūshōin was to be conducted,
　Lord Gijimaru sent as his envoy the head of the guard (goshoingumbantō), Amakata Kazuma Tomoyoshi, to the temple's abbot's quarters, where two pieces of white silver were offered as incense money (kōden). This had already been arranged following a formal inquiry on the previous day, the first of the month.
　　Note: Regarding the matter of this incense offering, an inquiry was made to the caretaker (rusui) of Lord Satsuma-no-Kami regarding Lord Matsudaira Masunosuke's duties; the reply came on the twenty-seventh of the previous month that Lord Masunosuke had no such duties until his formal audience (omemie) was completed. However, as Lord Gijimaru had previously offered incense money at the memorial service for Lord Shishin'in following a formal inquiry, a similar inquiry was made on this occasion as well, and having received instructions on the previous day (the first of the month), the offering was duly presented.

Item: On the eleventh day of the same month, a summons was received from the Duty Senior Councilor (goyōban), Lord Hotta Sagami-no-Kami Masasuke,
　and Lord Munenori dispatched the residential caretaker (rusui), Ōtani Ichiemon Yoshitsugu, whereupon the following was announced by written notice:

　　　　　Regarding Gijimaru: arrangements shall be made for
　　　　　him to proceed to the inner chambers of the Western
　　　　　Citadel (Nishinomaru Ōoku) on the fifteenth of this month.
　　　　　That is all.
　　　　　　　Fifth Month
