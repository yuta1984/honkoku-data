Kan'en 1st Year, Tsuchinoe-Tatsu (1748) — (This year, the era name was changed on the 18th day of the 7th month from Enkyō 5.)

Item: 22nd day of the 2nd month.
Regarding the matter of Yoshimaru-sama making a New Year's courtesy visit (o-rei) to the Main Keep (Honmaru), the Western Keep (Nishinomaru), and the Inner Chambers (Ōoku), in accordance with the precedents established by the domain, Munenori-sama previously submitted a letter of inquiry (ukagaisho) to the Official Duty Elder (goyōban) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke. In response, a directive was issued today via an attached slip (tsukefuda), stating that during Yoshimaru-sama's years of minority, he should make the New Year's courtesy visit to the Honmaru, Nishinomaru, and Ōoku, and that the specific date would be communicated at a later time.

Item: 19th day of the 3rd month.
The Shishinin-sama of Tōeizan (the biological mother of the Dainagon-sama) passed away on the 16th day of the 2nd month, and a memorial Buddhist service was held at that time. On behalf of Yoshimaru-sama, a messenger — the unit commander (omotogashira) and spear magistrate (yaribugyō) Sugigata, Mochizuki Hachiemon Nobuari — was dispatched to the main temple hall (honbō) of the said location, presenting one piece of silver as a condolence offering (kōden). This was done after proper inquiry.

Item: 29th day of the same month.
On behalf of Yoshimaru-sama, in order to inquire after the Dainagon-sama's well-being during the mourning period, a messenger — the resident official (rusuii) Katō Chōemon Masatoshi — was dispatched, presenting one set of cedar-box tiered food containers (sugijū) to the Nishinomaru. This was done after proper inquiry. However, the chamberlain (sobashū) Mizuno Tamba-no-kami informed the messenger that the gift would be duly presented at a later time.
