Item: On the twentieth day of the Seventh Month, a summons was issued by the Duty Councillor (go-yōban) Hotta Sagami-no-kami (Masasuke), and Sōnori-sama dispatched his Chief Resident Representative (o-rusuigashi) Ōtani Ichiuemon (Yoshitsugu). Regarding the written inquiry (go-ukagai-sho) submitted on the sixteenth of last month, instructions were conveyed via an attached note (go-futsuke-fuda).

　　　　　Written Inquiry
　　　　　　　　Memorandum

　　　　　　　Manjirō-sama
　　　　　　　Gekkōin-sama
　　　　　　　Lady Take-himegimi
　　　　　　　Lady Chiyo-hime

　　　　　Until now, on the occasion of O-Yoshimaru's celebratory events, the above-mentioned persons have exchanged gift offerings (o-otorimono) with him. Now that formal congratulations regarding the adoption have been presented, should O-Yoshimaru's household make presentation gifts (shinjōmono) to these persons henceforth? And if so, should our household likewise make presentation gifts?

　　　　　　Attached Note (go-futsuke-fuda)
　　　　　　　On this occasion, presentation gifts are to be made as inquired. Regarding future occasions,
　　　　　　　the matter is to be arranged through consultation with the house elders (karō) of the Gyōbukyō-dono's household.
