Item: On the twentieth day of the Seventh Month, a summons was received from the Go-yōban Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke-dono. Sōnori-sama dispatched the o-rusui (caretaker official) Ōtani Ichiemon Yoshitsugu. In response to the written inquiry (o-ukagai-sho) submitted on the sixteenth day of last month, an instruction was issued by means of an attached note (o-tsuke-fuda).

　　　　　　Written Inquiry (O-Ukagai-sho)
　　　　　　　　Memorandum

　　　　　　　Manjirō-sama
　　　　　　　Kōkōin-sama
　　　　　　　Takehime-no-Kata
　　　　　　　Chiyohime-no-Kata

　　　　It is understood that, up until now, the above-named persons have exchanged gifts (o-oto-mono) with O-Yoshimaru on the occasion of celebratory events. Following the formal expression of gratitude for the adoption that has now been presented, should O-Yoshimaru's household present offerings (shinjo-mono) to the above-named persons? And if so, should our household likewise present offerings to them?

　　　　　　　Attached Note (O-Tsuke-fuda)
　　　　　　　　Regarding the present occasion, you should proceed with gift offerings as described in the inquiry. As for future occasions, you should consult with the karō (senior retainer) of Gyōbukyō-dono and arrange matters accordingly.
