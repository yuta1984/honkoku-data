　　　Written Inquiry
　　　Item: Regarding the ladies behind the blinds (御簾中), as well as Lady Makihime and Lady Yoshihime — it was previously conveyed that there would be no need for gift exchanges with them. After the formal congratulations have been presented on this occasion, should O-Yoshimaru and our household make gift exchanges with these ladies?
　　　Attached Note
　　　　　Regarding this matter and future occasions, consult with the house elders (karō) of Gyōbukyō-dono's household and also with those of Uemon-no-kami-dono's household, and proceed as mutually agreed.

　　　Written Inquiry
　　　Item: O-Yoshimaru's household is to exchange gifts with Ichijō Kampaku-dono and the same household's Taikō-dono. Should our household also make gift exchanges with them henceforth?
　　　Attached Note
　　　　　The matter is to be arranged through consultation with the house elders of Gyōbukyō-dono's household.
