**Modern English Translation:**

　　Inner Physician: Yamawaki Zen'an
　　留守居 (Estate Manager): Ōtani Ichiuemon　(Proceeded ahead as advance guide)

When the aforementioned party entered, the *rusui* (estate manager) came out beyond the main gate to greet them, and the accompanying *goyōnin* (administrative officer) made the formal introductions. From there, they led the procession to the front of the private quarters gate, where the *bugyō* (commissioner), Maenami Chūbei Takayoshi (attached to Chikōin-sama), the inspector (*metsuke*), the side-captain (*okawa mono-gashira*), the foot-soldier captain (*okachi-gashira*), the room attendants (*oyabeya-tsuki*), and the personal guard Abe Yogouemon came out, with the accompanying *goyōnin* performing the formal introductions. Likewise, at the main entrance (*genkan shirazasu*), the senior councillors (*karō*), middle councillors (*chūrō*), administrative officers (*goyōnin*), and side-attendant trainees (*okawa-dō minarai*) came out, with the accompanying *goyōnin* performing the formal introductions.

In Yoshimaru-sama's private chambers (the sitting room), the party was seated, and the celebratory *naga-awabi* (long abalone) ceremony was performed immediately.

They then retired to the rest room, where celebratory *zōni* (rice cake soup) and *suimono* (clear soup) were served, attended to by the ladies-in-waiting. The formal long *hakama* was changed into, and they proceeded to the front sitting room. A meeting with Munenori-sama took place. The sword brought as a gift by Yoshimaru-sama was formally presented by the *karō* (senior councillor), dressed in long *hakama*, and in addition, two varieties of gifts in one load had already been presented in advance. Following this, the *naga-awabi* ceremony and the three-round sake (*sankon*) celebration were conducted. Cups of sake and accompanying food were offered to Yoshimaru-sama, and at this occasion, a sword and short sword (*wakizashi*) were also presented. The advance officer (*osakite*), Lord Iwamoto Naizen-no-shō, brought them forward and presented them to Yoshimaru-sama, and the formal receipt was completed. Yoshimaru-sama then offered cups of sake and food in return. Munenori-sama formally accepted the cup, and the sake-cup ceremony (*sakazuki-goto*) was handled by the advance officer Ogasawara Nuido-no-suke Tayū.
