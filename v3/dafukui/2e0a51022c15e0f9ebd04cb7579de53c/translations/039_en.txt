Inner Physician (Oku-o-ishi): Yamawaki Zen'an
　　　　Castle Keeper (O-rusuī): Ōtani Ichizaemon　　(Proceeds ahead to serve as guide)

At the time of the above party's arrival, the Castle Keeper comes out beyond the main front gate to receive them (the accompanying Chamberlain makes formal introductions). From there, proceeding ahead, at the gate before the private chambers, the Magistrate (o-bugyō), and Maenami Chūbei Katsu (Yukikatsu), attendant to Lady Chikōin, along with the Inspector (o-metsuke), Side-troop commander (o-soba-monotogashira), Foot-soldier captain (o-kachigashira), Chamber attendant (o-heya-tsuki), and Abe Yogozaemon of the personal guard (o-temahari) come out (the accompanying Chamberlain makes formal introductions). Likewise, at the main entrance graveled area (genkan-shirasu), the Household Elder, Middle Elder, Chamberlain, and Side-attendant trainees come out (the accompanying Chamberlain makes formal introductions).

In the private chambers and sitting room of Lord Ogimaru, he takes his seat, and a celebration with dried abalone (o-naga-awabi) is held immediately.

Entering the rest room (o-kyūsoku-jo), the ceremonial zōni soup and clear soup (o-suimono) for the handover are served (handled by the ladies-in-waiting).
His long formal hakama (o-nagabakama) is changed, and he proceeds to the front sitting room.
A face-to-face meeting with Lord Munenori.

The long sword (o-tachi) brought by Lord Ogimaru is formally presented by the Household Elder (wearing long formal hakama) (two additional items and one load had been presented in advance).
Following this, a celebration with dried abalone and the formal three-round saké ceremony (shiki-sankon) is held.
Saké cup and accompanying food (o-sakana) are presented to Lord Ogimaru, and at that time a sword and short sword (o-katana, o-wakizashi) are presented (carried out by the Advance Guard, Lord Iwamoto Naizen-no-shō, and presented to Lord Ogimaru). The reception is completed.
Lord Ogimaru presents saké cup and accompanying food in return.
