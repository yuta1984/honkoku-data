[From the inquiry document, continued:] Regarding the matter of Ogimaru-sama being received at the residence of the Hyōbu-no-taifu, it has been indicated that this is to take place within the current year. In connection with this, the Hyōbu-no-taifu has expressed his intention that the escort procession (tomōmawari) from the Hitotsubashi residence should be carried out in the same manner as it has been conducted until now. We humbly inquire as to how this matter should be handled. Respectfully submitted.
　　　　　　11th month　　　　　Itami Hyōgo-no-kami

　　　　[Appended note (ofuda):]
　　　　　Regarding the escort procession at the time of Ogimaru-sama's move, it should be arranged with the Hyōbu-no-taifu's residence that a formal reception escort be sent in accordance with the appropriate family status (iekaku).

1. 18th day of the 11th month:

　In connection with Ogimaru-sama's imminent move,
　[He] received from the Kubō-sama: one long sword (katana) made by Mihara, valued at 20 gold pieces (mai), and one short sword (wakizashi) made by Nobufusa, valued at 15 gold pieces.

1. 23rd day of the same month, at the Hitotsubashi residence:
　As there was a hakama-wearing ceremony (hakamagi) celebration for Ogimaru-sama, a female messenger was sent to present one box of dried sea bream (hoshidai) each to the Kubō-sama, the Ōgosho-sama, and the Dainagon-sama.
　On that occasion,
　To Ogimaru-sama, conveyed through a formal written message (hōbun) by the senior ladies-in-waiting (rōjūchū) of the Honmaru:
　From the Kubō-sama: one box of dried sea bream,
　From the Ōgosho-sama: five bolts of silk (makimono), one box of dried sea bream, and one set of ceremonial sake barrels (otaru),
