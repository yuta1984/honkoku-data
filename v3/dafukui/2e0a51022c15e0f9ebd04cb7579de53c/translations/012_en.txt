On the 13th day of the 6th month, upon the announcement of the adoption, Lord Kogorō sent congratulations to Lord Munenori through the chief retainer (okarō) of the Hitotsubashi household, Itami Hyōgono-Kami 《marginal note: Naokata》, who delivered one box of fresh sea bream on his behalf. On that occasion, a household official (yōnin) guided the messenger to the audience room (otaimensho), and in the Third Room of the Shoin (formal reception hall), the chief retainer Honda Kageyu 《marginal note: Masaki》 received the verbal message and conveyed it to Lord Munenori. Thereafter, Lord Munenori took his seat in the Great Shoin, Hyōgono-Kami was summoned before him, and the Middle Elder (chūrō) Akashi Shōgen 《marginal note: Norihusa》 formally presented the message. Once the direct reply (jikitō) was delivered, a dried abalone of great length (onagaawabi) was bestowed upon him. Hyōgono-Kami then renewed his expressions of felicitation, withdrew to the Third Room, and was served sake and soup. Subsequently, Lord Munenori sent a messenger to Hyōgono-Kami conveying three rolls of figured satin (sayā).
