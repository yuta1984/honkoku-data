Item: In addition to the above, those with domains of ten thousand koku or more shall likewise send a messenger to the residence of the monthly-duty Senior Councilor, the Lord of Oki-no-kami, to offer their congratulations.

　　　　Item: Those who are currently residing in their home provinces or villages shall send an express letter (tobisatsu) to the Senior Councilor, the Lord of Oki-no-kami, to offer their congratulations.

　　　　The foregoing shall be duly communicated to all concerned.

Item: On the 13th day of the 6th month, as Kōgorō had been appointed as an adopted son, Lord Kōgorō sent Itami Hyōgo-no-kami Naoyoshi, the chief retainer (karō) of the Hitotsubashi household, as a messenger to Lord Munenori to offer congratulations, presenting a gift of one arrangement of fresh sea bream. On that occasion, a household official (yōnin) guided the messenger to the reception hall (taimensho), and in the Third Chamber of the Study (Shoin no San-no-ma), the chief retainer Honda Kageyu Masaki received the messenger's formal greeting and conveyed it to Lord Munenori. When this was concluded, Lord Munenori took his seat in the Great Study (Ō-shoin) and summoned Hyōgo-no-kami, whereupon the Middle Elder (chūrō) Akashi Shōgen Norifusa made the formal announcement. After the direct reply was given, a gift of long abalone (nagaawabi) was presented. Hyōgo-no-kami once again offered his congratulations and withdrew to the Third Chamber, where he was served sake and soup (suimono). Thereafter, Lord Munenori sent a messenger to Hyōgo-no-kami with three rolls of sha-aya (thin twill silk) as a gift.

Item: On the same day, regarding the same matter, Lord Munenori sent the chief retainer Honda Kageyu Masaki as a messenger to Lord Kōgorō to offer congratulations on his behalf. (Continues on the next page)
