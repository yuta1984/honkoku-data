Item: On the 18th day of the 7th month, it was proclaimed that the era name would be changed to "Kan'en."

Item: On the 16th day of the 11th month, at the Hitotsubashi residence, Sen-no-suke (the third son of His Lordship Gyōbukyō) was born.

Item: On the 17th day of the same month, upon the passing of Yamato-no-kami Akinori (elder brother of Munenori), Ogimaru sent a messenger to their home domain to inquire after the wellbeing of Munenori. The attendant and meal-service officer (Gotsuki Ozenbanyaku) Yamazaki Shichirōemon Hidesada was dispatched for this purpose.
