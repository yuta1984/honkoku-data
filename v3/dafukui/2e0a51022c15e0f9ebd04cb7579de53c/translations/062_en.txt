One: On the eighteenth day of the seventh month, it was proclaimed that the era name be changed to Kan'en.

One: On the sixteenth day of the eleventh month, Sen-no-suke (the third son of Lord Gyōbukyō) was born at the Hitotsubashi residence.

One: On the seventeenth day of the same month, Lord Yamato-no-kami (Akinori) — elder brother of Lord Munenori — passed away. Accordingly, on behalf of Lord Yoshimaru, the attached dining-service attendant (go-tsuke-gozen-ban) Yamazaki Shichirōemon (Hideyoshi/Fusasada) was dispatched as a messenger to the home domain, to inquire after Lord Munenori's well-being.
