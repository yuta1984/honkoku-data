Item: On the 12th day of the 4th month,
The Senior Councilor (rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami (Masasuke) came as a shogunal envoy (jōshi) on the occasion of granting Lord Munenori leave (o-itoma) to return to his home domain. As there was a direct conversation (jikidan) at this time, the resident ambassador (rusui), Ōtani Ichiuemon (Yoshitsugu), was dispatched that evening to call upon Sagami-no-kami. The latter's steward (yōnin), Kasugai Shōbē, came out to receive him and conveyed the following: regarding the forthcoming visit of Lord Ogimaru to the Inner Chambers (Ō-oku), and the matter of presentation gifts for the Shogun and gift items for the ladies-in-waiting, he advised that preparations should generally follow the precedent set some years earlier when Masunosuke, the eldest son of Matsudaira Ōsumi-no-kami (Yoshitaka), had visited the Inner Chambers accompanied by Lady Takehime. A written note detailing that precedent was then conveyed privately through Shōbē, as follows:

　　On the occasion of Masunosuke's visit to the Inner Chambers:

　　　　　　　　　　　　From Masunosuke:
　　To the Shōgun (Kubō-sama):
　　　　　　A sword and gold coin in lieu of a horse — 1 piece
　　　　　　Scrolls — 5
　　To the Lord Dainagon:
　　　　　　A sword and gold coin in lieu of a horse — 1 piece
　　　　　　Scrolls — 3
　　　　　　　　　　　　From Matsudaira Ōsumi-no-kami:
　　To the Shōgun (Kubō-sama):
