Item: 12th day of the 4th month.

In connection with Munenori-sama's departure for his home domain, Senior Councilor (rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke came as an official envoy (jōshi) and a direct discussion (jikidan) took place. Accordingly, that evening, the resident official (rusuii) Ōtani Ichiemon Yoshitsugu was dispatched to call upon Sagami-no-kami's residence, whereupon the household official (yōnin) Kasugai Shōbei appeared and conveyed the following: In connection with the forthcoming directive (o-sata) for Yoshimaru-sama to make a visit to the Ōoku (Inner Chambers) of the castle, regarding the matter of presentation gifts (kenjōmono) and gifts to the ladies-in-waiting (jochū), it would be appropriate to use as a general model the gifts presented on the occasion when Masunosuke-dono, heir of Matsudaira Ōsumi-no-kami Yoshitaka, accompanied Takehime-no-kimi-sama to the Ōoku in a previous year. To this end, a written memorandum was discreetly passed through Shōbei, the contents of which were as follows:

On the Occasion of Masunosuke's Visit to the Ōoku

　　　　　　　　　　　　　From Masunosuke:
　　To the Kubō-sama (Shogun):
　　　　Honorific sword (o-tachi) and horse-price gold: 1 piece (kin)
　　　　Scrolls (makimono): 5
　　To the Dainagon-sama:
　　　　Honorific sword (o-tachi) and horse-price gold: 1 piece (kin)
　　　　Scrolls (makimono): 3
　　　　　　　　　　　　　From Matsudaira Ōsumi-no-kami:
　　To the Kubō-sama:
