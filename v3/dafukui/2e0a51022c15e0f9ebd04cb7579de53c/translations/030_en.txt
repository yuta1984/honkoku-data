From the Dainagon-sama: five bolts of crepe (chirimen), one kind of appetizer (sakana), and one box of hand-crafted toy dogs made by his own hands were received as gifts.
From Manjirō-sama: one box of fresh sea bream (tai) and additionally one box of toy dolls were received, and from Oheya-sama, seven decorative paper bags with floral designs were received.

　　　Note: No formal expression of gratitude was performed by Lord Munenori.

1. On the 3rd day of the 11th month, from the Hitotsubashi residence:
　The six pages (okoshō) and close attendants (okinju) who had been appointed to serve Ogimaru-sama were ordered to present themselves before Lord Munenori (omemie). Upon completion of this audience, the chief elder (karō) Sakai Geki Tomotaka announced that each of the six would be granted an allowance of 200 bales of rice (gōryōmai). Regarding their seating rank, the chief attendant (goyōnin) announced that they were to be placed in the rank of nagabakama-kaku (formal long-hakama rank), immediately after the shimbangashira (head of the new guard unit).

1. On the same day, within the Tokiwabashi residence:
　As the construction of Ogimaru-sama's chambers had been completed, a roof-raising ceremony (munage) was held. As a celebration of this occasion,
　Lord Munenori sent a congratulatory gift to Ogimaru-sama via his personal attendant unit commander (osoba monogashira), consisting of one box of fresh sea bream (tai) and one carrying-box set (hōkai) of food.

1. On the 7th day of the same month:
　Regarding the arrangements for the escort procession (oともまわり) during Ogimaru-sama's move to his new quarters, an inquiry document was submitted from the Hitotsubashi residence, to which instructions were given.
