Note: This is recorded here because it was the first visit.

Item: On the 26th day of the 12th month, a summons was issued by the Official Duty Elder (goyōban) Honda Hōki-no-kami Masayoshi, and by the Senior Councilor (rōjū) attached to the Dainagon-sama, Akimoto Tajima-no-kami Suketomo. In response, Munenori-sama dispatched his resident official (rusuii).

Regarding the matter of New Year's congratulatory gifts to be presented on behalf of Yoshimaru-sama, a directive was issued via an attached slip (tsukefuda) in response to the letter of inquiry (ukagaisho) that had previously been submitted.

　　[Contents of the Inquiry Letter (Ukagaisho):]
　　　To the Kubō-sama (the Shōgun),
　　　To the Ōgosho-sama (the Retired Shōgun),
　　　To the Dainagon-sama:
　　　　　A ceremonial sword and a gift of gold in lieu of horses (kinbadai)
　　　To Manjirō-sama,
　　　To the Oheya-sama:
　　　　　One box of assorted fish delicacies (hako-sakana) each
　　　　　　　The above

　　[Contents of the Attached Directive Slip (Tsukefuda):]
　　　Presentation of gifts is not required (i.e., gifts need not be presented).
