Item: On the 26th day of the 12th month, a summons was received from the Duty Senior Councilor (Goyōban), Honda Hōki-no-kami (Masayoshi), and from the Senior Councilor attached to the Lord Dainagon, Akimoto Tajima-no-kami (Ryōchō). In response, Lord Munenori dispatched the resident ambassador (rusui).

Regarding the matter of New Year's congratulatory offerings to be presented on behalf of Lord Ogimaru, instructions were conveyed via a written note (tsukefuda) affixed to the inquiry document (ukagai-sho) that had previously been submitted.

　　Inquiry Document:
　　　To the Kubō-sama (Shōgun)
　　　To the Ōgosho-sama (Retired Shōgun)
　　　To the Lord Dainagon:
　　　　　A ceremonial sword and gold (in lieu of a horse)
　　　To Manjirō-sama
　　　To the御部屋様 (O-heya-sama):
　　　　　One box of assorted delicacies each
　　　　　　　The above

　　Written Note in Response:
　　　There is no need to make these offerings.
