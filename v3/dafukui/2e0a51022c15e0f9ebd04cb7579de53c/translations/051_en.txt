Through a written notice submitted by Ōtani Ichiemon Yoshitsugu, the castle caretaker (rusui) acting on behalf of Lord Munenori, the following was announced:

　　　Tomorrow, the 15th, Lord Yoshimaru is to proceed to the Ōoku (the inner chambers) of the castle. You (Lord Yoshimaru) are also to offer your formal greetings tomorrow. Court robes (noshime) and formal long hakama are to be worn.

1. On the 15th day of the 4th month, at the Hour of the Snake (approximately 10 a.m.)

Following Lord Yoshimaru's relocation, this was his first visit to the Ōoku of the Hon-maru (main keep) to offer New Year's greetings, accompanied by a senior lady-in-waiting (otoshiyori). He wore noshime robes and formal hemp kamishimo.

　　Note: Two attendant footmen (okobito) and a personal attendant (otsukinbito) from the Hon-maru's Hiroshiki (service quarters) came to receive him at his departure, so the party proceeded ahead.

　　　1. At the bridge near Hirakawa Gate, two attendant footmen and a personal attendant were present; from that gate, a footman inspector (okobito metsuke) lifted his hakama hem and guided the procession ahead of the palanquin.
　　　1. From Hirakawa Gate to the Hiroshiki Gate, all those passing by prostrated themselves.

The gate before the Hiroshiki entrance was opened on one side, and the palanquin was carried in as far as the stone step of the formal entrance (shikidai). One head of the Hiroshiki guard came out to receive him at the lower landing of the shikidai. From there, male servants received the palanquin, and from the inner gate (jōguchi), under the care of the junior female attendants (osue jochū), he was carried in his palanquin all the way to the wide inner reception room (ohiroza shiki) beyond the inner gate.

　　Note: It had been planned that he would change into formal long hakama in the Karako Room, but as he was still of young age, the senior ladies-in-waiting announced that he should wear short hakama (han-bakama) instead.
