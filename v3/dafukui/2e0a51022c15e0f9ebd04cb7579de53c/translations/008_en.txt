Enkyō 4th Year, Hinoto-U (Year of the Rabbit)

1. The 27th day of the 4th month:
　To Kogorō-sama: From the Kubō-sama (Shōgun), the Ōgosho-sama (Retired Shōgun), and the Dainagon-sama, through an official messenger in the capacity of O-konando-gashira (Head Chamberlain), one iris helmet decoration each was bestowed as a gift.

1. The 29th day of the 5th month, to Senior Councilor Hotta Sagami-no-Kami Masasuke:
　Munenori-sama made a visit. The previous day, a summons had been conveyed through the domain's Rusuigashira (caretaker official) from Sagami-no-Kami, instructing that he should come today. Upon his arrival, a confidential communication was delivered.

　　　Confidential Written Communication

　　　　The contents of the document recently submitted, along with the memorandum of verbal communications, have been duly noted in their entirety.

　　　1. Regarding the matter of the provisional adoption discussed in a previous confidential communication: it had been considered that, should any unforeseen circumstances arise, Gyōbukyō-dono might be appointed as successor to the domain headship — this being the intention of the Ōgosho-sama — however, as (Gyōbukyō-dono's) age was deemed inappropriate, that plan was abandoned. In connection with this, the confidential communication concerns whether it would be appropriate to decree that Kogorō-dono, the eldest son of Gyōbukyō-dono, be adopted as heir by Hyōbu-no-taifu.
