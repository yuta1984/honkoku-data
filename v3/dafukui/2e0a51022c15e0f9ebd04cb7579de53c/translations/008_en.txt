Enkyō 4th Year, Hinoto-U (Year of the Rabbit)

1. The 27th day of the 4th month:
　To Kogorō-sama,
　From the Kubō-sama, the Ōgosho-sama, and the Dainagon-sama, through the intermediary of the Senior Page Chamberlain (O-konadō Tōdori), each presented one decorative iris helmet (shōbu kabuto) as a gift.

1. The 29th day of the 5th month: Sōnori-sama visited Senior Council Member (Rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-Kami Masasuke.《Marginal note: The previous day, Sagami-no-Kami had summoned the caretaker official (rusuī), with a message that [Sōnori-sama] should call upon him today.》Upon visiting, a confidential communication was conveyed.

　　　Confidential Written Communication

　　　　The contents of the document submitted some time ago, as well as the memorandum of the verbal communication, have been duly understood in full detail.
　　　1. At the time of the previous confidential communication regarding the provisional adoption, it was considered that should any unforeseen circumstances arise, Gyōbukyō-dono might be appointed as heir;
　　　　however, as the Ōgosho-sama had also given this consideration, it was discontinued on account of his age being deemed unsuitable.
　　　　In relation to this, it is now conveyed as a confidential matter whether Kogorō-dono, the legitimate son of Gyōbukyō-dono, should be appointed as the adopted son of Hyōbudaifu.
