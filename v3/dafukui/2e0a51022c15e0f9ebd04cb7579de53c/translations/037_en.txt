The members of the attendant staff from the Hitotsubashi household were likewise instructed to harbor no ill feeling toward one another, to maintain mutual harmony, and to ensure that each person conducted themselves without rudeness or coarseness. In particular, it was emphasized that those in the close personal service (o-kinshū-muki) should comport themselves with proper decorum and etiquette when dealing with those from outside the household (tozama), and should do so with ever greater cordiality. This was conveyed by the household elder (o-karō) Sakai Geki (Tomotaka).
　　　Note: On the 28th of the same month, the household elder Honda Yagara conveyed instructions to the Hitotsubashi-attached members of staff, directing them to continue to observe strictly the provisions set forth in the written directive (o-kakitsuke) that had previously been issued to them at the Hitotsubashi household, and informing them that they would be made aware of the household rules (o-kahō) and related matters on a continuing basis, and were therefore to carry out their duties with diligence.
　　　　　The content of the said written directive issued at the Hitotsubashi household is not known (details unknown).
