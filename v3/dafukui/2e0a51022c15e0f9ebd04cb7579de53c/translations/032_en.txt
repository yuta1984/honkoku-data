From the Dainagon-sama: one box of dried sea bream was received as a gift.

Item: Same day, in connection with the same occasion as above,
　From O-Yoshimaru-sama, through a messenger — the Hitotsubashi household's secretary (go-yōnin), Kawachi Hayato —
　to Munenori-sama: one ceremonial sword (o-tachi), one gold piece in lieu of a horse, one box of kelp, one box of dried sea bream, and one cask of sake were presented.
　To Lord Tokugawa Gyōbu-kyō (Munetada): through a messenger with an accompanying attendant — one sword (o-tachi), one gold piece in lieu of a horse, one box of kelp, one box of dried sea bream, and one cask of sake.
　To the Misu-no-naka-sama [the consort/lady of the household]: five rolls of crepe silk (chirimen) and one folded arrangement of sea bream were presented.

Item: Same day, in connection with the same occasion as above,
　From Munenori-sama to O-Yoshimaru-sama, through a messenger — the household secretary (go-yōnin) Amataka Kazuma Tomoyoshi —
　three layered kosode robes, two sets of formal hakama and jacket (kamishimo), one box of kelp, one box of dried sea bream, and one cask of sake were presented.
　From Lord Tokugawa Gyōbu-kyō (Munetada)
　to O-Yoshimaru-sama: five rolls of crepe silk, two sets of formal kamishimo, and one load of two kinds of gifts.
　From the Misu-no-naka-sama: two kosode robes and one load of two kinds of gifts were presented.

Item: Fifth day of the twelfth month.
　From Munenori-sama, to the rōjū (senior councillor) in charge of the matter, Lord Hotta Sagami-no-kami (Masasuke), a formal notice (o-todoke-sho) was submitted through a messenger and a resident caretaker (o-rusui), stating that O-Yoshimaru-sama's transfer of custody was to take place on the coming twenty-third day.

Item: Twelfth day of the same month.
