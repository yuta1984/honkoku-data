From the Dainagon-sama: one box of dried sea bream was received.

1. On the same day, in connection with the same matter:
　From Ogimaru-sama, via messenger Hitotsubashi-go-yōnin (household official) Kawachi Hayato,
　the following were presented to Munenori-sama: one ceremonial sword (tachi), horse-money (uma-dai) of one gold piece, one box of kelp, one box of dried sea bream, and one set of ceremonial sake barrels.
　To Tokugawa Gyōbu-kyō Munetane-sama, via messenger and attendant: one long sword (katana), horse-money of one gold piece, one box of kelp, one box of dried sea bream, one set of ceremonial sake barrels.
　To the Go-renchū-sama [the lady of the household]: five bolts of crepe silk (chirimen) and one arrangement of sea bream.

1. On the same day, in connection with the same matter:
　From Munenori-sama to Ogimaru-sama, via messenger and household official Amagata Kazuma Tomoyoshi:
　Three sets of lined robes (kosode), two sets of formal divided skirt and jacket (kamishimo), one box of kelp, one box of dried sea bream, and one set of ceremonial sake barrels were presented.
　From Tokugawa Gyōbu-kyō Munetane-sama
　to Ogimaru-sama: five bolts of crepe silk, two sets of formal kamishimo, and one set of two ceremonial gift items (nishu).
　From the Go-renchū-sama: two robes (kosode) and one set of two ceremonial gift items were presented.

1. 5th day of the 12th month:
　From Munenori-sama, via messenger and resident caretaker (rusui), a formal notification document (otodoke-sho) was submitted to the Senior Councilor (Rōjū) in charge, Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke, regarding the matter of Ogimaru-sama's transfer being set to take place on the 23rd of the coming month.
