Enkyō 1st Year, Kinoe-Ne (Year of the Rat) [This year, the era name was changed on the 29th day of the 2nd month of Kanpō 4th year]

1. The 23rd day of the 1st month:
　Kogorō-sama made a visit to the Main Castle (Hon-maru) from the Hitotsubashi residence to offer New Year's greetings.
　　　Note: There should be records of gifts presented and received on this occasion; however, as the Hitotsubashi residence subsequently suffered a fire and was destroyed, the records from that time are unclear, and therefore they are not recorded here.

1. The 23rd day of the 5th month:
　Kogorō-sama made his first visit to the West Citadel (Nishi-maru) from the Hitotsubashi residence.

　He had an audience with the Right General (Udaishō-sama) and the Grand Counselor (Dainagon-sama), and on this occasion,
　He received from the Right General a sword — in lieu of [the sword by] Sōshū Hiratsugu — ten gold coins, one sword (one piece).
