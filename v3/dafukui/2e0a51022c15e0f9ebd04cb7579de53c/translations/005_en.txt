Enkyō 1st year, Kinoe-Ne [1744] (This year, the era name was changed on the 29th day of the 2nd month from Kampō 4th year.)

1. On the 23rd day of the 1st month,
Lord Kōgorō proceeded to the Hon-maru [the Shōgun's main keep] from the Hitotsubashi residence for the New Year's congratulatory visit (nenshi no shūgi).

Note: There should have been gifts presented and gifts received on this occasion; however, as the Hitotsubashi residence subsequently burned down, the records from that time are unclear and are therefore not recorded here.

1. On the 23rd day of the 5th month,
Lord Kōgorō, for the first time, entered the Nishi-maru [the Western Keep] from the Hitotsubashi residence,

and had a formal audience (o-taigan) with the Udaishō-sama [the Lord of the Right] and the Dainagon-sama. On this occasion,

From the Udaishō-sama: one sword (Sōshū Hiroji, valued at 10 gold pieces) was received as a gift.
