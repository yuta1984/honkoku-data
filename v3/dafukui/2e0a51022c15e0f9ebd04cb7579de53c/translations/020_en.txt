Item: On the twelfth day of the Seventh Month, a summons was issued by the Duty Councillor (go-yōban) Hotta Sagami-no-kami (Masasuke), and Sōnori-sama dispatched his Chief Resident Representative (o-rusuigashi) Ōtani Ichiuemon (Yoshitsugu). On the first day of last month, when Ichiuemon had been dispatched to Sagami-no-kami's residence to formally present congratulations regarding the adoption (go-yōshi o-rei), a note (go-futsuke-fuda) was attached to the written inquiry (go-ukagai-sho) concerning the offerings (go-kenjōmono), stating:

　　O-Yoshimaru-sama is likewise to present:
　　　To the Kubō-sama (Shogun): a sword (o-tachi), gold in lieu of a horse (kin-umashiro), and one hundred bundles of cotton — two items, one load
　　　To the Ōgosho-sama and the Dainagon-sama: a sword (o-tachi), gold in lieu of a horse (kin-umashiro), and fifty bundles of cotton — one item, one load each

These offerings were to be presented in the same manner as Sōnori-sama's, as instructed. At that time, this was communicated via a separately written document (betsushi o-kakitsuke):

　　　　　Separate Document
　　　　　On the occasion of presenting formal congratulations (o-rei) upon the issuance of the order of adoption (yōshi o-ōse-idasa-re-sōrō), O-Yoshimaru is likewise to present [his congratulations] through a proxy (myōdai). Said proxy is to be dispatched from within the Echizen household. This matter is hereby conveyed for your understanding and guidance.

In accordance with the above instructions,
Sōnori-sama conveyed the matter to O-Yoshimaru-sama through a messenger, namely the Senior House Elder (go-karō) Sakai Geki (Tomotaka).
