[The matter] was handled by the go-yōnin (chief administrative official), and from this point forward, it is not recorded here as it falls under the regular ceremonial schedule (shikijitsu jōrei).

Item: On the twelfth day of the Seventh Month, a summons was received from the Go-yōban Hotta Sagami-no-kami Masasuke-dono. Sōnori-sama dispatched the o-rusuí (caretaker official) Ōtani Ichiemon Yoshitsugu. On the occasion of the formal expression of gratitude for the adoption (go-yōshi go-rei) that had been presented to Sagami-no-kami-dono via Ichiemon on the first day of last month, a written inquiry (o-ukagai-sho) regarding the gift offerings was responded to with an attached note (o-tsuke-fuda), indicating that:

　From O-Yoshimaru-sama as well:
　　To the Kubō-sama: one consignment (hito-ni) of two items — a ceremonial sword (o-tachi), gold in lieu of a horse (kin-umashiro), and one hundred bundles of cotton
　　To the Ōgosho-sama and Dainagon-sama: one consignment each of one item — a ceremonial sword (o-tachi), gold in lieu of a horse (kin-umashiro), and fifty bundles of cotton

　An instruction was given that the offerings should be presented in the same manner as those of Sōnori-sama, and on that occasion, the following was declared by means of a separately attached written document:

　　　　Separate Document
　　On the occasion of presenting formal thanks for the declaration of adoption, O-Yoshimaru should also submit the expression of thanks through a proxy (nadai/myōdai). Said proxy is to be dispatched from within the Echizen domain household. This has been communicated herewith for your information and compliance.

　The content of the above declaration was conveyed from Sōnori-sama to O-Yoshimaru-sama via a messenger, the go-karō (senior retainer) Sakai Geki Tomotaka.
