1. On the 5th day of the 6th month, to the Tsukiji residence 《marginal note: the Hitotsubashi lower residence》, Senior Council Member (Rōjū) Hotta Sagami-no-Kami 《marginal note: Masasuke》 was dispatched as a shogunal messenger, and the following confidential communication was conveyed:

　　　　Confidential Communication:
　　　　　In view of the birth of a male child [to Hyōbudaifu], Kogorō-dono is to be sent as the adopted son of Matsudaira Hyōbudaifu.

　　　　Note: On the preceding 4th day, Yabu Kazue-no-Kami, the Senior Attendant and Intermediary (O-soba Goyō Toritsugі), had informed Iтami Hyōgo-no-Kami, the chief retainer of Hitotsubashi, that the matter which had been privately communicated some days prior would be formally announced shortly, and that Hotta Sagami-no-Kami would deliver the message as a shogunal envoy on the following 5th day.

1. On the 12th day of the same month,
　Sōnori-sama proceeded to the castle 《marginal note: Yesterday, a written notice (kirigami) jointly signed by the Senior Councilors arrived》, whereupon in the Tamari-no-Ma of the Black Audience Chamber (Kuro Shoin), with the Senior Councilors seated in a row, the Goyōban (duty councilor) Sakai Uta-no-Kami 《marginal note: Tadatomo》 conveyed the content of the shogunal directive.

　　　　Shogunal Directive (Jōi):
　　　　　Kogorō-dono, the legitimate son of Gyōbukyō-dono, is hereby appointed as the adopted son of Matsudaira Hyōbudaifu.
