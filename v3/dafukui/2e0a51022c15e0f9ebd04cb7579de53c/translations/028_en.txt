To O-Yoshimaru-sama as well,
　From the Kubō-sama: one album of calligraphy samples (o-tegami) [poems, written by the Konoe Jūsangō] was received as a gift.
　From the Ōgosho-sama and the Dainagon-sama: two rolls of satin (shusu) and three rolls of figured satin (donsu) each were received.
　From the O-heya-sama: three bolts of colored habutae silk were received.

Item: Twenty-sixth day of the eighth month. For the same reason as above, to the duty official (goyōban) Honda Hōki-no-kami (Masayoshi), Munenori-sama went to call (in the manner of a personal meeting) and (after making inquiries) conveyed his thanks.

Item: Third day of the ninth month.
　From O-Yoshimaru-sama,
　To Munenori-sama, as a congratulatory gift for the Obon seasonal greeting (o-mimitama), one box of fresh sea bream was presented.
　　　Note: The customary official congratulatory gift for the o-mimitama is not presented before Obon; it is customarily handled at this time of year.

Item: The same day. As the Hitotsubashi household officials (goyōnin) had earlier inquired about the names of those appointed as goyō-gakari [liaison officials], a written list was sent to them. Thereupon, O-Yoshimaru-sama ordered that gifts be presented to the following persons as listed below:
　　　Chief elders (karō): Sakai Geki and Honda Yazuka — three rolls of sha-aya silk and 500 mon in lieu of a ceremonial cask (o-taru-dai) each.
　　　Middle elders (chūrō): Akashi Shōgen and Nagami Umon — two rolls of sha-aya silk and 300 mon in lieu of a ceremonial cask each. [Also:] Goshoin-bangashira, goyōnin, o-heya-tsuki: Amagata Kazuma.
