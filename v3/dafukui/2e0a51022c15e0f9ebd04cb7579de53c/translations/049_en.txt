Yesterday, through the留守居 (castle keeper/liaison official) Tsuda Kūemon Morichika, Lord Munenori submitted a formal inquiry (o-ukagai-sho). Today, a response was issued by way of an attached note (o-tsuke-fuda).

　　　Inquiry Document (O-ukagai-sho):
　　　　Regarding Lady Ogimaru — as it has previously been ordered that she is to visit the Ōoku of both castle keeps (ryō-omaru),
　　　　on the day of her visit to the Gohonmaru (Main Keep),
　　　　formal tribute offerings are to be presented to the Lord Kubō (Shogun) from the outer (public) quarters (omotemuki),
　　　　and at the time of her visit to the Nishi-no-maru (West Keep),

　　　　is it correct that tribute offerings from the outer quarters are to be presented to the Lord Ōgosho (Retired Shogun) and the Lord Dainagon as well?

　　　Attached Note (O-tsuke-fuda) [Response]:
　　　　The tribute offerings to the Lord Ōgosho and the Lord Dainagon are also to be presented
　　　　at the outer quarters (omotemuki) of the Gohonmaru on the 15th of tomorrow's date.

　　　Inquiry Document (O-ukagai-sho):
　　　One: Regarding the formal tribute offerings from the outer quarters on behalf of Lady Ogimaru — I had understood these would be presented through the Omotenaka-no-kuchi (the middle gate of the outer quarters), in the same manner as my own (the lord's) tribute offerings.
