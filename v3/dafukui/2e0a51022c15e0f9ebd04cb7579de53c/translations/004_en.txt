From the Udaishō-sama [the Heir Apparent / Lord of the Right], conveyed through the special envoy o-sobashū [Personal Attendant] Toda Tosa-no-kami: one variety of provisions in one hamper received as a gift.

Note: No gifts were received from the Dainagon-sama on this occasion.

1. On the 23rd day of the 11th month, departing from the Hitotsubashi residence, Lord Kōgorō visited the Sannō Shrine for his first shrine visit (o-miyamairi) — his riding escort and nurse-bearer being the Hitotsubashi senior attendant (o-toshiyori) Sakino — and thereafter stopped at the Hon-maru [the Shōgun's main keep].

To the Kubō-sama [the Shōgun], on the occasion of this formal audience (o-taigan), the following were presented: ten rolls of silk, one hamper of lacquered food containers (gyōki), and one hamper of two varieties of provisions.

On this occasion, the following were received as gifts from the Kubō-sama: one sword (Tomimitsu, valued at 20 gold pieces), 20 pieces of silver, 20 bundles of cotton wadding, and one hamper of one variety of provisions.

Upon the return, two hampers of lacquered food containers (gyōki) and one set of stacked lacquer boxes (soejū) were received as gifts.
