**7th day of the 8th month**

As successor to Hara Iyo-no-kami, Kyoto Town Magistrate (*Kyōto Machi-bugyō*):

Appointed to the post of *Ozama-kinshi* (chamberlain duty) concurrently with *Yoriai* (senior retainer assembly member): Nagai Mizu-no-shō, adopted son of Noto-no-kami.
Granted a stipend of 300 *hyō* (bales) of rice. — The same person as above.

**14th day of the same month**

Ordered to be relieved of duties (*o-yaku go-men*):
　　*Okanjō Ginmiyaku* (Accounts Inspector): Okada Awa-no-kami
　　Acting representative (*Mydai*): Tatsuta Rokusuke

**15th day of the same month**

The above individuals are summoned to Edo Castle for an audience (*go-mie*):
　　Matsudaira Yōdō — retired, adoptive father of Tosa-no-kami
