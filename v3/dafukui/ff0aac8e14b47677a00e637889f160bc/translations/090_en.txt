Tada Tatemaki, Shimada Sakon, Kano Shigezaburo, and Nagano Shuzen conspired together in treacherous plots. Most notably, in the year of Tsuchinoe-Uma (Ansei 5, 1858), they caused the unsealing of letters written by men of patriotic resolve and passed them to Watanabe Kinzaburo. This caused affairs to be thrown into chaos, ultimately resulting in the complete elimination at one stroke of those who were loyal patriots anxious for the nation's welfare. Their crimes are truly unpardonable under Heaven and Earth. Furthermore, each and every additional offense has been confessed in detail, too numerous to enumerate fully. Therefore, citing this as but one example of their crimes, heavenly punishment (tenchū) is hereby administered.

　　　　26th day of the 11th month

　　　　　　　　　　　　Commissioner of Finance (Kanjō-bugyō): Negishi Hizen-no-kami

On this occasion, when traveling to inspect the route of the (Shogun's) procession, although aware of the intent and purpose of the matter, unnecessary days were spent, causing hardship to those below. This was an imprudent matter. Therefore, he has been ordered to remain in confinement (sashihikae).

　　　　1st day of the 12th month

　　　　　　　　　　　　Lord Owari Dainagon

Regarding the above (Lord Owari Dainagon), it has been decreed that he should proceed ahead of the Shogun's journey to Kyoto, prior to the audience (taigan) scheduled for the coming second month.

　　　　　　　　　　　　Retired lord of Hyogo: Nakajō Gaōu (Elder)

Granted one thousand koku as a Kōke (High Steward).
