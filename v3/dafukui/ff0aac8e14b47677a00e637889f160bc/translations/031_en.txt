**The Last Day of the Sixth Month (Misoka, 6th Month)**

(Regarding the matter of Shimazu Saburō) As he had taken great pains to arrange for the dispatch of forces to bring about pacification in accordance with a private imperial instruction, a reward was granted to him.
The above was announced by Buzen-no-Kami (Buzen Governor) in the presence of the assembled Senior Councilors (Rōjū) at the veranda of the White Audience Chamber (Shiroshoin).

**Personnel Changes on the Last Day of the Sixth Month:**

- **Kyoto Deputy (Kyoto Shoshidai):** Sakai Wakasa-no-Kami
  → By the Shogun's will, relieved of his post. Assigned to the Teikan-no-Ma (a ceremonial hall rank).
  (His deputy, Morikawa Dewa-no-Kami, attended in his place.)

- **Osaka Castle Keeper (Osaka Jōdai):** Matsudaira Hōki-no-Kami
  → Appointed as Kyoto Deputy (Shoshidai); conferred the rank of Jijū (Chamberlain).

- **Commissioner of Temples and Shrines (Jisha Bugyō):** Matsudaira Izu-no-Kami
  → Appointed as Osaka Castle Keeper; elevated to the Fourth Court Rank (Shinbon).

- **Announcer (Sōjaban):** Arima Saemon-no-Suke
  → Assigned as additional duty to Commissioner of Temples and Shrines.

**Personnel Changes on the Third Day of the Seventh Month:**

- **Inspector General (Ōmetsuke) and concurrently Foreign Affairs Commissioner:** Ōkubo Etchū-no-Kami
  → Appointed as attendant to the Shogun's side (Sobashū Goyō Otoritsugi); stipend increased by 1,000 koku.

- **Finance Inspector (Kanjō Ginmiyaku):** Kikuchi Daisuke
  → Appointed as Foreign Affairs Commissioner; conferred the rank of Shotsaibū (諸大夫).
  Also changed his name/title to Iyo-no-Kami.

**Seventh Day of the Seventh Month (7th Month, 6th Day):**

By the Shogun's will, ten thousand koku (一〇万石) were granted as a provision for the continuation of the Hitotsubashi household, and a senior councillor's messenger was dispatched (to Lord Hitotsubashi Gyōbukyō).

Upon proceeding to Edo Castle for an audience with the Shogun, it was announced—in accordance with the Emperor's wishes (Eiryo)—that the same person (Hitotsubashi Gyōbukyō) was appointed as the Shogun's guardian (Kōken).

**The Ninth Day of the Same Month (7th Month, 9th Day):**

In accordance with the Emperor's wishes, Matsudaira Shungaku was formally informed of his appointment as **Seiji Sōsaishoku** (政事総裁職, President of Political Affairs / Chief Political Overseer).
