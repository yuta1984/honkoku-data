　　Regarding the directive (official notice), the following circular was distributed by the Onaramikata Rusuい:

　　　　　　　　　Notice

　　　　Item 1: Although a provincial inspection tour has been ordered this time, the submission of provincial maps (kuni-ezu) and castle maps (shiro-ezu) is unnecessary.
　　　　Item 2: No census of people, horses, or households shall be conducted.
　　　　Item 3: For horses and porters beyond those covered by the official shogunal seal (goshuin), the standard transport fees (packhorse charges) shall be paid promptly without delay.
　　　　Item 4: Regardless of the location being inspected, messengers, couriers, and gifts of any kind shall be entirely unnecessary. However, where a guide is brought in, prior notice of this shall be given.
　　　　Item 5: Cleaning and similar preparations are unnecessary. However, existing roads and bridges that present difficulty for passage are

(Left folio)
　　　　　　exempt from this rule.
　　　　Item 6: Construction or repair work at lodging places along the route is unnecessary, and new teahouses shall not be built.
　　　　Item 7: Members of the inspection tour, at their lodgings, shall sell their allotted rice and soybeans at the local market rate. All other goods for sale shall also be sold at the usual local prices.
　　　　　　　　　The above.
　　　　　　　　Intercalary 12th month, Year of the Ox
