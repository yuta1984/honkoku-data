Enkyō 1st Year, Kinoe-Ne (Year of the Rat) [Note: This year was originally Kanpō 4th year, but the era name was changed in the 2nd month.]

　・ On the 1st day of the 1st month (New Year's Day), the New Year ceremonies were held at the御屋形 (the lord's residence/palace).
　・ On the same day, as a New Year's congratulatory offering, the lord proceeded to Edo Castle as customary, presented a ceremonial sword and a monetary gift in lieu of a horse, and received rolls of silk fabric (呉服) in return.
　・ On the 3rd day of the same month, as a celebration for the First Noh Recitation of the New Year (御謡初), a messenger was dispatched to present, as customary, a sake cup stand (盃台) and a monetary gift in lieu of sake.
　・ On the 7th day of the same month, as a celebration for the Young Herbs Festival (若菜), a messenger was dispatched to present, as customary, a side dish of fish (sea bream).
