**Kanpō 2nd Year, Year of the Dog (1742)**

　Copy of a circular (mawashijō) from the Senior Inspectors (Ō-metsuke).

●〇 Regarding the wandering clergy and temple and shrine representatives (*jisha no tomogara*) who have received permission to conduct fundraising (*kange*) for the repair of temples and shrines throughout the provinces, and who are carrying fundraising certificates bearing the co-signed seal of the Commissioner of Temples and Shrines (*Jisha-bugyō*), and are touring the territories (*goryo*), private domains (*shiry*ō), temple and shrine lands, and post-towns: it has come to our attention that in some places, orders had previously been transmitted from local lords (*jitō*) to village communities to suspend such fundraising activities, with the result that the solicitors were turned away and told that fundraising could not be permitted. While it is at the discretion of the domain lord to prohibit private fundraising, the present tours are conducted with official authorization (*kōgi no men*) from the Shogunate. Accordingly, instructions are to be conveyed in advance by the Shogunate's agents (*go-daikan*) in the case of Shogunate territories, and by lords and their deputies (*jitō*) in the case of private domains, to the effect that those carrying a fundraising certificate bearing the co-signed seal of the Jisha-bugyō should be permitted to receive donations according to the donors' wishes. The above is to be circulated accordingly.

〇 As the foregoing has been transmitted by circular from the Senior Inspectors, take heed of its contents. The long-standing prohibition on participation in various donation solicitations (*shohōka*), as repeatedly ordered by this domain across successive generations, remains in full force as a standing regulation and is to be strictly observed. Matters ordered by the Shogunate are, however, a separate matter. On this occasion, in connection with the repair of the Konda Hachiman Shrine in Kawachi Province, the Shogunate has authorized a fundraising campaign; accordingly, a donation offering (*go-kishin-ryō*) has been put forward on behalf of the domain lord as well as the domain's retainers and residents of the post-towns. This matter has already been settled. As this is a time of hardship for the retainers and townspeople alike, the domain lord has settled the matter on their behalf; take heed of this accordingly. That is all. 29th day of the sixth month.

〇 As it has been ordered that the two accompanying written notices are to be circulated among the domain's retainers, each person is to take note accordingly. That is all.
　30th day of the sixth month. To all members. To the rotation group (*mawari-gumi*). Inspectors (*metsuke*).

【Attached slip (harigami)】

　Document transmitted by Lord Ogasawara Nuido-no-suke.

〇 It has been reported that in recent years, the caretaker officials (*rusuigachi*) of various domain lords have been gathering at teahouses in various places and engaging in dissolute entertainments. Henceforth, such gatherings at teahouses and similar establishments are to be prohibited. When it is necessary to meet, they should do so at their lord's reception room (*zashiki*) or in the row-house quarters (*nagaya*). Furthermore, there are reports of false rumors and troublesome matters being spread. This is a particularly improper state of affairs; strict orders are to be issued to ensure that no such conduct occurs. Moreover, any suggestion that the group of associates is becoming so unified as to make it difficult for their lords to manage them is even more unacceptable and must not be permitted. Strict and thorough orders are to be issued to prevent the recurrence of all the matters described above. Should any such incident occur hereafter, it shall be deemed the fault and responsibility of the lord concerned. 2nd day of the sixth month.

　Copy of a circular from the Senior Inspectors (*Ō-metsuke*).

　　【Text in the lower section】

　　　　The instructions conveyed by Lord Honda Chūsho.

〇 As previously notified, there have been reports of improper conduct on the part of the caretaker officials (*rusuigachi*) of various domain lords when overseeing the construction and repair of bridges, waterways, and other public works managed by their associations. If these reports are true, they are an extremely serious matter; orders are to be issued immediately to put a stop to such behavior. That is all. Sixth month.

▲〇 Regarding the inspection of weights and measures (*shohakari*): from ancient times, officials of Morizui Hikotarō have made rounds to inspect all weighing instruments. In recent years, however, it appears that some have come to regard this as a personal matter; it has been reported that even those who possess many weighing instruments produce only a few for inspection, conceal any defective ones, or claim not to possess any and refuse to submit to inspection. As has been previously announced, when officials from the Morizui office make their rounds for inspection, all weighing instruments must be produced without exception and without concealment. It is to be understood that any suspicious instruments will be confiscated. This instruction is to be strictly observed.
　The above is to be communicated throughout the thirty-three provinces of the Tōkaidō, Tōsandō (Nakasendō), Hokkuridō (Hokuriku), as well as Tanba, Tango, and Tajima, with Shogunate territories notified through the Shogunate's agents (*go-daikan*) and private domains through the local lords (*jitō*). The above is to be circulated accordingly. Eighth month.
