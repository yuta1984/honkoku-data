〇 In view of the hardship suffered by many people in managing their livelihoods in recent years, an order has been issued to reduce interest rates on loans and the like. In connection with this, the annual repayment installments for government loans (shohaisyaku) due this year shall be returned as an act of consideration (go-yōsha); accordingly, those portions are to be repaid beginning from next year. Take heed of this notice. Tenth month, Year of the Rooster.

**Genbun 2nd Year, Year of the Snake (1737)**

●〇 Concerning the lodging fees (hatagosen) and related charges established at the honjin (official post-station inn) during the sankin-kōtai (alternate attendance) processions and other journeys along the roads. For example, within a fee established at one hundred mon, those staying at secondary lodgings (shimojin) apply a deduction called "hikizeni" (引銭, a discount fee), reducing the payment by approximately thirty or forty mon. When the innkeepers (hatagoya) refuse to accept this reduction, they are threatened with the party transferring to an alternate lodging (wakishuku), which would entail preparation expenses for the innkeepers; thus the innkeepers have no choice but to accept the discount, and it has come to our attention that they are suffering great hardship as a result. This is ultimately believed to be the conduct of lower-ranking retainers and attendants. Henceforth, to prevent such occurrences, strict orders are to be issued to all lower-ranking attendants accompanying processions along the roads. It is our view that the substance of this notice should be regularly circulated among the caretaker officials (rusuigata). Eighth month.
