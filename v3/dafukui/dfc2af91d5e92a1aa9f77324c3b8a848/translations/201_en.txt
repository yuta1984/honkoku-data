Regarding the Occasion of the Formal Betrothal Ceremony (O-Yuinō)

Only those serving as留守居 (rusuigashira, officials managing domain affairs in the lord's absence) and other appointed officials shall wear plain (solid-colored) kamishimo in either hana-iro (light blue/lavender) or kane-iro (golden/brass color). The same applies to noshime (a type of formal robe). For all others, including those dispatched as messengers for the celebratory occasion, ordinary attire is to be worn.

The above instructions are to be communicated to all relevant parties. Tenth Month.
