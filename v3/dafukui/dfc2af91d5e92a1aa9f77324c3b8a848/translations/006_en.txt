**Kyōhō 15th Year, Year of the Dog (1730)　Entry dated the nineteenth day of the first month**

●1. Since the recoining of gold and silver during the Genroku era, the price of rice and grain had been high, but in recent years it has fallen. Yet the interest on loans and pledged goods remains at the former rate, and it is reported that people are suffering as a result. Therefore, concerning loans contracted since Genroku 15th Year, the Year of the Horse (1702), the interest rate shall henceforth be set at five percent or below. Furthermore, for existing loans that are renegotiated and re-documented before the end of this year, the same interest rate shall apply.

1. For amounts of principal and interest already outstanding and unsettled up to this point, the reduction in interest rates stipulated in this notice shall not apply.

1. Both parties are to strictly observe the above provisions without deviation. If repayment falls into arrears beyond this, the lender is to report the matter to the magistrate's office (bugyōsho). Conversely, if the lender refuses to reduce the interest rate below the stipulated level, the borrower may file a complaint.

1. For new loans, terms shall be determined by mutual agreement between the parties. Excessive interest rates (kōri) must not be charged arbitrarily.

The above is to be strictly observed.　Tenth month, Year of the Rooster.

As this written notice was issued, the village headmen (nanushi) and annual officials (nenban) made inquiries, in response to which further provisions were proclaimed in the form of posted notices. These supplementary items are recorded here as addenda.

【Section from the paper slip affixed to the left folio】

●1. Regarding Inuijikin (coins bearing the character 乾 ["inui"/northwest"]): although their circulation was halted at the end of the Year of the Tiger, from that point forward they are to circulate at a rate of two Inuijikin per one ryō of new gold or Keichō gold.

1. This same rate is to apply to annual tribute (nengu) and various taxes (shounjō), and so forth. Notice issued from the Finance Office (Gokanjōsho) in the first month of the Year of the Dog.

〇　Since rice prices have fallen in recent years while the wages of servants (hōkōnin) remain unchanged at the former high rate, a notice was issued at the end of last year directing that wages be reduced. When employing servants, each party is to be informed of these provisions and act accordingly. The above. Second month, Year of the Dog.

〇1. In recent years, there have been many cases of servants absconding or disappearing (toriige/kakuochi), which is ultimately attributable to the improper conduct of their guarantors. Accordingly, following an investigation, two hundred and two employment agents (hitonjuku/kuchireya) have been officially designated and formed into a guild (kumiai). It has been ordered by the Town Magistrate's Office (Machi-bugyōsho) that wages are also to be reduced accordingly.

1. In connection with the above, when someone comes to the employment agent's office to verify the seal on a servant's letter of guarantee (ukejō), the employment agents are strictly forbidden from entertaining such persons with hospitality of any kind. Furthermore, the practice of holding welcome banquets when servants move into lodgings (heyairi) is to be firmly discontinued. The employment agents have been so instructed. Heads of lodging houses (heyagashira) are likewise to be informed of this by their respective landlords.

1. Since employment agents are organized into guilds in each town district, inquiries are to be made at each respective location. The above. Second month, Year of the Dog.
