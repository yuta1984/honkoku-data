A written report submitted on the 6th day of the 12th month of Tenpō 10 (1839, year of己亥 tsuchinoto-i), in response to instructions from Lord Mizuno Echizen-no-kami.

One: This evening, having been summoned by Lord Mizuno Echizen-no-kami, Tanabe Shūkichi presented himself and received the following written directive through the Lord's personal attendant (goyōnin):

　　Regarding Mikuni Harbor in Echizen Province: in years of poor harvest, exporting rice and grain to other provinces would reduce the food supply within the province, potentially causing those within the country to fall into famine. It has thus been the practice to close the harbor depending on the abundance or scarcity of that year's harvest. It goes without saying that, in order to prevent the aforementioned villagers from falling into famine, the affairs of shogunal territories shall be managed by the district magistrate (gundai), and those of private domains by each respective domain lord, each considering the safety and wellbeing of their territories. If you wish to control the movement of rice, grain, and products strictly within your own domain, that shall be permitted. However, it has come to our attention that in recent times, you have been halting the sale of rice, grain, and various products from shogunal villages, as well as blocking the transport of rice from other domains, causing even the overland transport of locally produced goods to be delayed, while raising harbor fees (kuchi-sen) far beyond the normal rate — treating all this as the rules of your own domain and extending these measures even to shogunal and other domains' territories. It has also been reported that you have been manipulating the exchange rate of silver notes (ginsa) at your own discretion. This is highly objectionable conduct. Should such matters continue, a formal and strict inquiry and reprimand shall be issued. Therefore, henceforth you shall ensure that the transport of rice and other products outside your domain is not impeded, and that overland transport proceeds without obstruction; that harbor fees (kuchi-sen) are restored to their previous levels; that the exchange rate of silver notes is not arbitrarily lowered; and in all matters, you shall be firmly directed to ensure that no inconvenience is caused to shogunal territories or other domains.
