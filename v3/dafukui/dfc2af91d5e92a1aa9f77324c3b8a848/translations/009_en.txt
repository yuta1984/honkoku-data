　　　　　　Entry recorded on the 3rd day of the 10th month

●▲〇Since a proclamation has already been issued prohibiting the use of silver and gold fittings on combs (kushi) and hairpins (kōgai), such metal fittings will henceforth become "tsubushi-gin" (melted-down silver). Therefore, these items must not be hoarded, and must be sold to the Silver Mint (Ginza). In general, "haifuki-gin" (cupellation silver) and tsubushi-gin have never been articles that could freely be bought and sold privately. Tsubushi-gin is to be purchased by the Silver Mint. In recent years, it has come to our attention that due to misunderstanding, such transactions have been conducted freely and improperly. As has been the custom since ancient times, buying and selling of silver outside of the Silver Mint is strictly prohibited. Those who require silver goods or base silver (shita-gin) should purchase them at the Silver Mint. This directive is to be strictly observed.
　　The above is to be proclaimed accordingly.　　9th month of the year of Boar (亥)
