【Right folio】Red seal: Office of Inspection (監察府)

【Left folio】
　　　　　Editorial Notes (Preface)

Item 1: The number of omissions in the records of official government directives (御触留) is beyond counting, and this is truly a most regrettable state of negligence. These gaps are to be supplemented in due course by consulting the various records (諸事留) and the council records (評定留).

Item 2: Official directives concerning wanted persons (尋ね者), as well as those concerning increases in road transport fees (道中賃銭増), are omitted and not recorded here. However, it should be noted that regarding the directives on road transport fee increases, from the first month of the second year of Bunsei (1819, Year of the Rabbit), following deliberation, the practice of circulating such notices to all relevant parties was discontinued. This applies only up to the point such notices were forwarded to the magistrates (御奉行) and to Fuchū (the domain capital, present-day Fukui city).

Item 3: The ● symbol indicates a directive circulated to various parties within the domain (御国). The 〇 symbol indicates a directive issued only to the Edo office. The ▲ symbol indicates a directive circulated within the domain to those under the jurisdiction of the towns and four districts (町・四郡), or a notice intended for the awareness of supervisory heads (支配頭). Nevertheless, there are likely still many omissions. These too should be verified and corrected by carefully examining the various records (諸事留), council records (評定留), and bundles of official correspondence (御用状括).
　　Note: Items without any symbol are those for which no directive was issued either to the east (Edo) or to the north (the home domain).

Item 4: Official directives concerning fundraising solicitations (勧化) by various temples and shrines that are unrelated to the domain are largely omitted and not recorded.

Item 5: As this book prioritizes conciseness, some entries where the text could reasonably be abbreviated have been recorded with only their main points, with the detailed text omitted. For such matters, the original documents should be consulted directly.
　　Addendum: A certain number of ad hoc directives (当座の御触) are also not recorded.
