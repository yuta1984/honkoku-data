**[Pasted Notice]**

**Hōei 5th Year (1708), Year of the Rat**

**Memorandum**

Item: Large coins (*ōsen*) have been newly minted at the Kyoto mint (*zenigoza*) for the purpose of facilitating free circulation in society. Therefore, throughout all provinces, without exception, these new large coins shall be mixed and circulated alongside the current new coins without delay in commercial transactions of receiving and paying. Note: One large coin (*ōsen*) shall be equivalent to ten of the current coins.

Item: The exchange rate of the current new coins is fixed at between 3,900 and 4,000 *mon* per *ryō* of gold. This rate shall be strictly maintained without deviation, and large coins shall be mixed in and circulated accordingly.
　　Addendum: Large coins shall begin circulation from the fourth month of this year.

Item: Anyone found to have counterfeited large coins elsewhere shall be severely punished. The above.
　　　　Intercalary First Month

**Same Year — Directive from the Senior Inspectors (*Ō-metsuke*)**

Item: The practice of causing horses to shake their manes has no precedent in earlier times, and furthermore serves no useful purpose in the event of fire. Therefore, this practice shall henceforth be entirely prohibited.
　　　　Fourteenth Day of the Eighth Month

**Same Sixth Year, Year of the Ox (1709)**
**Copy of a Written Notice from the Senior Inspectors**

〇 Item: Regarding the shaking of horses' manes, notice was previously issued that this was unnecessary; however, as this practice causes no harm to the horses, it shall henceforth be permitted as before. The above.
　　　　Nineteenth Day of the First Month　　Senior Inspectors

　　When the previous directive was issued, we judged that it was not necessary to issue a formal notice here, and accordingly, no formal notice need be issued this time either; however, this copy has been sent for your information and awareness.

〇 Item: Notice has been received from the Senior Inspectors that individual inquiries directly to members of the Senior Council (*Rōjū*) shall cease, and all inquiries shall henceforth be directed to the *Rōjū* serving as the current monthly duty officer (*tsuki-ban*).

〇 On the occasion of the current memorial Buddhist service (*hōji*) for the shogunal house, a general amnesty (*taisha*) has been granted. In connection with this, for all members of the various households as well as the peasants of their domains, those who had matters pending before the authorities (*kōgi*) as well as those who had been subjected to punishments by their own lords' discretion — to the extent that pardoning them would not create any impediment to proper justice — shall be granted amnesty or permitted to return to service (*kizan*), as appropriate to the circumstances, at the discretion of their lords. This is considered proper. Please be made aware of the above. The above.
　　　　　　Twenty-First Day of the Second Month　　Senior Inspectors

**Memorandum**

　The levy (*unjo*) on sake shall henceforth be abolished. Both public (*goryō*) and private domains (*shiryō*) shall be made aware of this. The above.
　　　　　　Third Month

　　In connection with the above, all quotas and allotments (*kazu*) previously granted to sake brewers (*sakaya*) shall be abolished entirely. The fifteen foot soldiers (*ashigaru*) responsible for sake inspection, who had been placed under the authority of the three magistrates (*bugyō*), shall for the time being remain in their positions.
