On the 18th day, the local official Takaishi Mosaburō, while observing the foreign ships from approximately six or seven ri offshore from a place called Mihama, approached by small boat, received a letter, and confirmed that the vessel was indeed a Russian ship. The said ship had fire arrows (rockets/incendiary weapons) deployed, and appeared by no means to be a drifting vessel, but was clearly identified as a warship. This matter was immediately

【Left folio】
reported to the main vessel (this "main vessel" refers to the Nagasaki military ship called the Ōkusa-maru). Upon receiving orders from the Magistrate, retainers Takabayashi Chūdayū and Takama Ōsuke were dispatched as inspection envoys to verify the foreign vessel. With the Ōkusa-maru decorated with crested curtains, banners, and ship insignia raised, and accompanied by numerous tow-boats, they set out to display Japan's military prestige, heading three ri from Nagasaki.
