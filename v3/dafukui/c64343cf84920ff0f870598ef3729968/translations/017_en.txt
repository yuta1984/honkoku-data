At the Magistrate's Office, prayers for the subjugation of evil spirits are being conducted day and night at the Suwa Daimyōjin, the general guardian deity of Nagasaki. We presume that similar occurrences may be taking place at the various domain residences as well, and it would be advisable to conduct your own prayers at your discretion.
