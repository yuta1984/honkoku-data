The ship displayed a streamer with the words "Oroshiya-koku (Russia)" written in Japanese kana characters, and flew a ship's pennant [drawing of flag] as depicted here from the side of the vessel. Without any fear of the four cannon emplacements, the ship dropped anchor at a place called Shimazaki. Upon observing this ship, Baba Gorōemon and Takama Ōsuke, along with their attendants, boarded a tatami-floored vessel and, as an escort party,

[Left folio]
first approached the foreign ship from the main vessel by small boat, and through an interpreter, conveyed the following: "At this moment, two officials have been dispatched from the Chinzai [regional headquarters] as inspectors." However, the foreigners (Russians) replied to our party, saying: "As we have just arrived, the ship is still in disarray and in an unsightly state—please wait a short while, and we will then guide you aboard."
