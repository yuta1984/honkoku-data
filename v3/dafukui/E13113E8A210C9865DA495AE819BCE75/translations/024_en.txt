Item: On the 7th day of the 8th month, in connection with the same matter as above (the arrival of the Korean delegation), horses were dispatched to Maisaka for official use. Tōgō Saburōemon (Yasusada) and Sakai Rokurōemon (Motomi) coordinated the arrangement of men and horses, and departed from Fukui on this day.

Item: On the 9th day of the 8th month, as His Lordship the Nakatsukasa-no-taifu (Shōhei) had, for the first time, received a skylark caught by falcon on the 16th of the previous month, the resident keeper (rusuい) Miyakita Jūrozaemon (Sadayoshi) was dispatched as a messenger to the Senior Councillors (Rōjū) to express gratitude.

Item: On the 10th day of the 8th month, the Duty Councillor Inoue Kawachi-no-kami (Masamine) sent the following written note (kirigami) to the domain's Edo resident keeper:

[Note in original: "It appears the character 'tera' (temple) is missing before 'sha' (shrine)."]

　　Regarding the vermillion-seal documents (goshuinjō) for the (temple and) shrine landholdings within the domain, please send one person to my private residence after the eighth hour (approx. 2 a.m.) on the evening of the 11th. Respectfully.

　　　　8th month, 10th day　　　　Inoue, Kawachi (no kami)
　　　　To: Matsudaira Iyo-no-kami's
　　　　　　　　retainer

Item: On the 11th day of the 8th month, in accordance with the above matter, the resident keeper Miyakita Jūrozaemon (Sadayoshi) called upon the Duty Councillor Inoue Kawachi-no-kami (Masamine), whereupon the vermillion-seal document for the Fukui Shinmei Shrine landholdings was handed over with the following memorandum attached:

　　　　Memorandum
　　The vermillion-seal documents (goshuinjō) for the temple and shrine landholdings within the domain are herewith conveyed; please deliver them accordingly. As for the expression of gratitude, the temple and shrine representatives (are to attend)…
