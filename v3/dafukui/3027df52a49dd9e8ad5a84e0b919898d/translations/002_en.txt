The Toki Clan

　　In the middle period, the ancestor of the Toki clan, lords of Mino Province, was Toki Mino-no-kami Mitsuhira, the son of Iga-no-kami Mitsumoto, who was himself the seventh-generation descendant of a retainer of Minamoto Settsu-no-kami Yorimitsu. During the Bunji era (1185–1190), Mitsuhira was granted the post of shugo (military governor) of Mino Province (Nōshū) by Lord Yoritomo. Toki Hōki-no-kami Yorikiyo, the fifth-generation descendant of Mitsuhira, governed Mino Province during the time of Lord Takauji (Ashikaga Takauji). His legitimate heir was Daizen-no-daibu Yoriyasu; his second son was Akechi Shimotsuke-no-kami Yorikane; his third son was Ibi Dewa-no-kami Yorikatu; and his fourth son was Toki Mino-no-kami Yorimasa — all of whom continued their respective family lines. In the generation of Toki Mino-no-jirō Noriyori, the sixth-generation grandson of Yorimasa, the era was one of civil war (Sengoku), and neighboring provinces ceaselessly sought opportunities to encroach upon Mino.
