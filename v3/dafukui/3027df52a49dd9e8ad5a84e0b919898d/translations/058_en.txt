(Continued from previous page) …he was granted one hundred thousand *koku* in Wakasa, and Awakura, Kumagai, and Matsumiya were assigned as his *yoriki* (supporting retainers), and Obama (Castle) was bestowed upon him. From this point, Wakasa Province was pacified.

On the twenty-sixth day of the fourth month of Tenshō 5 (1577), Shibata (Katsuie) dispatched Mōri Shōsuke Yoshichika as messenger (to Azuchi), reporting that the Kaga ikki, having repeatedly fought against the vanguard forces of Sassa, Kanamori, and Maeda since the spring of the previous year, had now been met with the full exertion of Sakuma Gemba's efforts: on the twenty-second, Oyama Castle had been stormed and taken, and the heads of nineteen commanders — including Tsubosaka Hōki, the same Shingorō, Akatsuka Jinzaemon, Kaburagi Uemon, Matsunaga Tamba, Kongōji Saburōemon, and Kurose Tōzaemon — were being presented (to Nobunaga).

Shibata Shūriyō's (Katsuie's) wife had been the daughter of Io Ōmi-no-kami, but as she had died the previous winter, Nobunaga's younger sister — the Lady of Odani, who had formerly been given in marriage to Azai Bizen-no-kami Nagamasa — was now wed to Katsuie. The three daughters born of the Azai, aged nine, seven, and five years, accompanied her as well. On the twenty-fifth day of the sixth month she departed Azuchi by palanquin, and on the twenty-ninth of the same month she arrived at Kitanosho.

**[Left folio]**

At this time, Sakuma Gemba-no-jō Morimasa was garrisoned at Oyama Castle in Kaga; Tokuyama Gohyōenojō at Miyukizuka in the same province; Haigō Gozaemon at Daishōji in the same province; Shibata Iga-no-kami Katsutoyo at Maruoka in Echizen; Shibata Sanzaemon Katsumasa at Katsuyama in the same province; Kanamori Gorōhachi Narichika at Ōno; and Yasui Sakon Ieyoshi at Tōgō in the same province.

It is said that from around this time, a pontoon bridge was constructed over the Kuzuryū (Nine-Headed Dragon) River.

On the twenty-sixth day of the fourth month of Tenshō 5, Shibata Shūriyō Katsuie of Echizen sent Mōri Shōsuke Yoshichika as messenger to Azuchi to report as follows: the Kaga ikki had not been pacified in recent years, and since the spring of the previous year, forces led by the vanguard of Shibata Sanzaemon, Sakuma Gemba, Sassa Kuranousuke, Kanamori Gorōhachi, Maeda Matasaemon, and others — including Yasui Sakon, Haigō Gozaemon, and Tokuyama Gohyōe — had advanced into Kaga, attacking and demolishing enemy strongholds at Hinoya, Ataka, Nata, Komatsu, Teramatsuō, Kurahashi, Sasatani, Tsurugi, Kuragadake, Matsune, Torikoe, Kameyama, and others; yet the two districts of Nomi and Ishikawa had still not been pacified— (continues on next page)
