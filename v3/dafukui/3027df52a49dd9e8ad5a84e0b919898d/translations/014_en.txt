　　As [Yoshiaki's] situation was dangerous, he proceeded from there to Obama in Wakasa Province and came to rely upon Takeda Daizenno-taifu Yoshimune. Since Yoshimune had no natural heir, he had adopted Ukyō-no-suke Yoshiyori, the younger brother of Sasaki Yoshihide. As [Yoshiyori] was Yoshiaki's nephew, the Takeda father and son showed great respect toward Yoshiaki. However, as it was a small domain, [the Takeda] concluded that they would ultimately be unable to fulfill [their obligations], and so they sent a request to Asakura Yoshikage—upon whom they relied as their overlord [hatajirushi]—through Mutō Kōzuke-no-suke of Ishiyama in the Saburitan Valley. From Yoshiaki's side as well, Ōdachi Jibu-no-taifu Harutada was dispatched as a deputy envoy. Yoshikage humbly accepted, and promptly sent his houseman Asakura Shikibu-no-taifu Kagekiyo to Obama in Wakasa as a welcoming escort. On the last day of the ninth month of Eiroku 9 [1566], [Yoshiaki] was first moved to the castle of Tsuruga, which was within [the Asakura's] domain. To receive him there, Asakura Kingo-nyūdō Sōteki's son Kurōzaemon Kagetada and likewise his son Nakatsukasa-no-taifu Kagetsune, father and son together, welcomed [Yoshiaki] and entertained him with a hospitality that was unrivaled. He was then to be brought to Yoshikage's seat of power, the Ichijōdani, but around that time, one of Yoshikage's retainers, a man called Horie Nakatsukasa-no-sanrō Toshishige,

　　had an incident of turning against the Asakura, and had his landholdings in Sakanakita District confiscated and was driven out toward Kaga. Owing to this disturbance, [Yoshiaki] remained in Tsuruga for a time, and thereafter made his entry into Ichijō[dani]. His escort included: Niki Iga-no-kami Yoshimasa, Ōdachi Jibu-no-taifu Harutada, the same [Ōdachi] Iyo-no-kami Nobukata, Ueno Mutsu-no-kami Nobunaga [Nobutada], the same Nakatsukasa-no-taizen Harunobu, Isshiki Harima-no-kami Haruie, the same Shikibu-no-taifu Fujinaga, Ise Shimōsa-no-kami Sadataka, the same Ukyō-no-suke, Takeda Jibu-no-shō Nobumasa, Mibuchi Yamato-no-kami Akiie, the same Nagaoka Hyōbu-no-taifu Fujitaka, Iikawa Yamashiro-no-kami Nobumichi, the same Higo-no-kami, Andō Kurōdo Yasunori, Sugihara Hyōgo-no-suke Nagamori, Niwa Tango-no-kami, Ōkusa Jibu-no-shō, Soga Hyōgo-no-suke, Nose Tamba-no-kami, Numata Yajūrō, and Makishima Magokurorō at the fore—in all, some one hundred and twenty persons of all ranks. Yoshikage held [Yoshiaki] in extraordinary reverence and esteem, and devised stratagems for subduing the rebel Miyoshi Sakyō-no-taifu.
