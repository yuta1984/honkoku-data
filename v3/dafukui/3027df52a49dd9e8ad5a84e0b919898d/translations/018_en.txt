[Marginal note in vermillion, upper margin:]
Death of Takeda Yoshimune

　　Around this time, Hemi Suruga-no-kami of Takahama in Ōi District of Wakasa Province submitted [to Nobunaga]. Takeda Yoshimune of Obama in the same province was an enemy, but he had recently died suddenly, and since his adopted son, Ukyō-no-suke Yoshiyori, was of dim-witted character, the province turned against him, and Mitsuhide informed [Nobunaga] that retainers such as Awaya, Yamagata, Kumagai, Matsumiya, and Naitō were deeply committed to joining [Nobunaga's] side (and so forth).

[Marginal note in vermillion, upper margin:]
Nobunaga's First Advance to Tsuruga

Also, Kyōgoku Nagato-no-kami Takayoshi and Gotō Kisaburō Yorimoto, with over a thousand cavalry, established a camp to the west of Imazu in Ōmi Province and contained the enemy castles. Nobunaga changed his route from Kumakawa, passed by Obama Castle from a distance, took the Awagara mountain pass, arrived at Shinjo, took Kumagai Naizen along, and then, using Awaya Etchū-no-kami of Sagaki as his guide, advanced to the port of Tsuruga in Echizen Province. Upon hearing of this, Asakura dispatched Yamazaki Nagato-no-kami, Takugi Echigo-no-kami, Kawai Aki-no-kami, Kobayashi Bitchū-no-kami, Uozumi Bingo-no-kami, and Toda Shimōsa-no-kami with several thousand cavalry to Tsuruga, with the intention of reinforcing Asakura Nakatsukasa-no-taifu Kagehira, the commander of Kanagasaki Castle. Yoshikage also established his main camp at Kinome Pass. Nobunaga, declaring that he should attack the stronghold of Tezutsu Mountain—a subsidiary castle of Kanagasaki—climbed up to assault it.
