...as the vanguard, with Awaya Etchū-no-kami and Kumagai Denzaemon from Wakasa, Akechi Jūrozaemon as the deputy (*myōdai*) of Akechi Hyūga-no-kami (Mitsuhide) from western Ōmi, Bitō Hikobei as the deputy of Niwa Gorōzaemon from Sawayama, and the lord of Nagahama Castle, Kinoshita Tōkichirō Hideyoshi — who had changed his name the previous winter to Hashiba Chikuzen-no-kami Hideyoshi — appointed as generals, and they were dispatched to the port of Tsuruga, firmly securing the pass at Kinome-guchi.

As an aside inserted here: Eishū Risshi, the youngest son of Kusunoki Hyōenojō Tachibana Masatō, a resident of Kawachi Province, had become a resident monk (*jūryo*) at Heisenji in Echizen. The original ground manifestation (*honji*) of the Hakusan Gongen deity is the Eleven-faced Kannon. While Eishū was engaged in his religious practice before this sacred presence, his deceased elder brother, Hankan Masashige, appeared and met with Eishū. A grave marker has been erected at the place where this encounter occurred.

When Tanba Province was granted to Mitsuhide, the latter presented to Nobunaga a sword of two shaku and seven sun made by Rai Kunitsugu — a tachi called *Yumekiri* ("Dream Cutter") — which the Akechi clan had possessed for several generations, along with twenty volumes of the *Kokin Wakashū* belonging to the scholar Oe no Masafusa.

First, as it was reported that Nobunaga would attack from the port of Mikuni, Sugiura Iki Hōkyō, Wada Honkakuji, Morimoto Shōgoji, Hishikawa Kōgyōji, and Uzaka Honkōji were appointed generals, with Shōkun and Kyōjun of Mikuni, Shōkō of Kawajiri, Yūgi of Morita, Jōkō of Takagi, and Saikō of Oguro as captains. The combined ikki forces of Asuwa, Yoshida, Sakakita, and Sakaminami, totaling more than thirty thousand men, repaired and fortified ancient castles around Natsume-gō, Sanri-hama, and the port of Mikuni. The distance to the port of Yoshizaki was seven *ri*. The forces from Mikawa, Fujishima Chōshōji, Hisamatsu Shōganji, and others — including those from Sakakita, Ikegami, and the men of Enuma District in Kaga Province — amounting to more than twenty thousand men, constructed fortifications at Kitagata, Namimatsu, and Hamazaka.
