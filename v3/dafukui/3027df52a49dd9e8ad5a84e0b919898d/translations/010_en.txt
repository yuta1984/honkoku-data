Norinob's son, Ise-no-kami Tsunenobu, established his castle at Iwakura in Owari, while Norihiro's son, Yamato-no-kami Tsunehide, resided in the second enclosure [ni-no-maru] of Kiyosu Castle and revered their lord, the Buei [the head of the Shiba clan]. Thereafter, as Buei had no natural heir, he adopted Yoshitoshi, Saemon-no-suke of Ōno, a member of the clan. However, the retainers of Echizen and Owari disliked this, and appealed to the Shogun [Yoshimasa] to instead install Shibukawa Jibu-no-taifu Yoshikado as the new Buei. From this point on, the power of the Shiba clan declined, and the vassals of both provinces acted as they pleased, and conflicts did not cease. Around this time, Ise-no-kami Nobuyasu, son of Samasuke Toshinobu—six generations descended from Ise-no-kami Tsunenobu—rose up from Iwakura and attempted to dominate Owari. Meanwhile, Yamato-no-kami Munenobu—five generations descended from Tsunehide—sought to subjugate the entire province from his base at Kiyosu. Yamato-no-kami's younger brother, the lay monk Danjō-no-jō Nobusada, [known by his Buddhist name] Gatsugen, lived at a place called Shōbata and

[Marginal note in red ink, upper portion]
Dōsan's granddaughter

　　[Nobunaga] took Hirate Nakatsukasa-no-taifu Kiyohide as his senior retainer. Thereafter, the daughter of Saitō Yamashiro-no-kami Yoshitatsu, the lord of Mino, was presented as the daughter of his grandfather, the lay monk Dōsan, and given in marriage to Nobunaga. In the eighteenth year of the same era [Tenbun 18, 1549], his father Bingo-no-kami Nobuhide died.

　　In the first year of Kōji [1555], Nobunaga consulted with his uncle Magosaburō Nobumitsu, and together they defeated and destroyed Hikogoro Hironobu—son of Sadanobu, grandson of Oda Yamato-no-kami Munenobu, lord of Kiyosu Castle. At the age of twenty-two, Nobunaga moved into Kiyosu Castle. His uncle Nobumitsu was installed at Nagoya. However, an unexpected turn of events occurred: Magosaburō Nobumitsu was killed by his own retainer. Thereafter, Nobunaga's younger brother, Musashi-no-kami Nobuyuki, was installed at Nagoya Castle with Hayashi Sado-no-kami assigned to attend him.
