　　[Saitō] Tatsuoki was placed in command of the second column, and with over five thousand cavalry, [they] departed from the port of Sakai, passed through Hiragata, and advanced toward the capital to attack (and so forth). On the fifth day of the first month, [they] pressed upon the Shogun's residence at Honkokuji Temple in Rokujō (and so forth). The Shogun's forces were divided into three detachments. The first was headed by Hosokawa Uma-no-kami Harukane, Ise Shimōsa-no-kami, and Ueno Sado-no-kami, with Akechi Jūbei Mitsuhide, Nikaido Suruga-no-kami, and Iikawa Yamashiro-no-kami serving as military commanders. The second had Niki Iga-no-kami Yoshimasa, Nose Tamba-no-kami, and Iikawa Higo-no-kami in the vanguard, with Tsuda Samanosuke Toshihisa, the same Sakon, and Naitō Bitchū-no-kami serving as military commissioners. The third comprised Ōdate Jibu-no-taifu Harutada, Mitsubuchi Yamato-no-kami, Makishima Magokurō, and Soga Hyōgo-no-suke, with Nomura Etchū-no-kami Takakatsu, Yamagata Gen'nai, and Uno Yahashichirō handling tactical operations (and so forth). From within the defenses, Nomura Etchū, Yamagata Gen'nai, Uno Yahachi, Akaza Shichirōemon, and others sortied and captured Iwanari Yasuke, Ichida Kaname-no-suke, and Nara Sakon.
