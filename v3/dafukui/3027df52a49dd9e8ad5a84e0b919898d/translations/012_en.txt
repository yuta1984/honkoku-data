[Marginal note in red ink, upper portion]
The Asai clan's origins

　　The ancestor of the Asai family was Shikibu-no-taifu Kimikore [Kincore], the son of Dainagon Kimitsuna, who was himself the grandson of Sanjō Naidaijin Sanetsura, of the Kain branch of the Fujiwara clan. In the Eishō era [1504–1521], Kimikore first took up residence in Odani-no-shō in Asai District of Ōmi Province, departed from court aristocracy, and established himself as a warrior house. Sukemasa was four generations removed from Kimikore—he was the son of Hitachi-no-suke Kimimasa, grandson of Hyōgo-no-kami Masatsuna. Bizen-no-kami Nagamasa was the son of Sukemasa.

[Marginal note in red ink, upper portion]
Mino retainers submit to Nobunaga

　　Among the warriors of Mino Province who despised Tatsuoki's unrighteous conduct and came over to Nobunaga were: Inaba Iyo-no-kami, Ujike Hitachi-no-suke, Andō Iga-no-kami, Hachiya Hyōgo-no-suke, Fuwa Kawachi-no-kami, Marumo Saburōbe, Endō Samanosuke, Tōyama Kyūbe, Hara Hikojirō, Kanamori Gorōhachi, Nishio Goemon, Katō Saemon, Takenaka Gensuke, Itō Hikobe, and others. Besides these, there were also [the lords of] Ōgaki's Ujike, Sone's Inaba, and Kawado's Andō, and so forth.

[Marginal note in red ink, upper portion]
Mitsuhide departs from Echizen

　　Akechi Jūbe Mitsuhide had been in the service of Asakura Yoshikage, but from around the winter of Eiroku 8 [1565], owing to the slanders of Kuratani Gyōbu-no-taifu Tsugutomo, he fell out of Yoshikage's favor.

[Marginal note in red ink, upper portion]
The ancestor of the Kuratani

　　Tsugutomo was a fourth-generation descendant of Dainagon Yoshitsugu, the second son of Kubō Yoshimitsu; he was the son of Kamon-no-kami Tsugutoki, grandson of Uhyōe-no-suke Tsugutoshi. After Dainagon Yoshitsugu's lord, the Rokuonji-dono [Yoshimitsu], passed away in the summer of Ōei 15 [1408], [Yoshitsugu] lived close by [in relative obscurity]; in the spring of the twenty-fifth year of the same era [1418], the Dainagon himself passed away. His son Tsugutoshi was left an orphan, and following the connections available to him, went down to Echizen, settling in the vicinity of Ajimano. He was sheltered especially warmly by the Asakura clan as "the Kuratani御所 [lord]." In particular, the daughter of Gyōbu-no-taifu [Tsugutomo] became the wife [principal consort] of Yoshikage, it is said.
