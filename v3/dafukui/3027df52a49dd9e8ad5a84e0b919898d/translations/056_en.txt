**[Right folio]**

...they deployed at the post-town of Imajos and fortified Mount Ōuchi as their last redoubt (*tsume no shiro*), establishing their encampment there. At Kinome-guchi was Ishida's Saikōji; at Hachifuse was Hashidate's Shinshūji; at the Itatoriguchi pass was Shimoma Izumi; at the Sugitsuguchi pass was Ōmachi's Senjuji; at Chinōra was Ōshio's Enmiyaji; at Kōnoura were Wakabayashi Nagato and his son; and at Fuchū Ryūmonji was Miyake Gonnosuke. With these as their commanders, along with Ryōchin of Shidu, Yūkai of Hokogasaki, Zuishō of Makuri, Shōsen of Nago, Gangyō of Emori, Myōen of Kida, as well as rōnin of the former Asakura clan, and the ikki forces of Nyū District, Imakita, the Eastern District, Imaminami, the Western District, Imadate District, and Minakanamijō District — in total more than forty thousand men — each defended their respective passes.

In Nōshū (Mino Province), a council of war was held for the suppression of the northern Ikkō-ikki. Kanamori Gorōhachi and Hara Hikojirō, intending to advance from the valley of Neo into Echizen, passed through Itajō and Ōkawara, crossed Haiboshi Pass, and burst into Akiu and Sasamata. Endō Samanosuke and Tōyama Kyūbei came from Gujo, passed through Nagataki, crossed Aburazaka Pass, and attacked into Anamadani. Nobunaga departed Gifu Castle on the ninth day of the eighth month, and arrived at the port of Tsuruga on the twelfth.

**[Left folio]**

Akechi Mitsuhide and Shibata Katsuie attacked the Sugistu-guchi pass. The defenders of this sector were led by Ōmachi Senjuji, Shōsen of Nago, and Heijōshōen, among others. Senjuji's younger brother, Jibukyō Ryōzen, led his fellow temple brethren in resistance. Among the attacking forces, Shinji Sakuzaemon, Ise Yozaburō, and Shiouten Matabe launched their assault; Shimokōbe Tōuemon, Asaba Kozaemon, and Shindō Hikouemon fought targeting Ryōzen. From within the fortress, a warrior sallied forth calling out his name as "Kurodayu of Asamizu." On the fourteenth day of the eighth month, around the Hour of the Pig-Rat (approximately 11 p.m.), the moon shone as bright as midday. Ryōzen pressed too far into the enemy, and was cut down by Asaba (Kozaemon). Kurodayu was shot through his chest-plate (*munaita*) by the attacking forces' commander Okuda Shōdayu, and died in battle. Horie Nakatsukasa-no-Saburō, who had sided with the ikki and was present in this sector, led his men away and raised a battle cry from behind. Senjuji Ryōku, declaring "There is no escape now," fought fiercely and died in battle, and the fortifications at Sugistu were broken through. On the fifteenth day, the fortifications at Fuchū were surrounded. Hashiba Chikuzen-no-kami landed at Kōnoura on the fifteenth, routed the enemy, and rowed the captured boats back to Tsuruga.
