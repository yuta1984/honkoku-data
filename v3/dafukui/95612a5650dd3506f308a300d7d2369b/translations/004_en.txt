○ Note: Year of the Boar (Bunkyū 3, 1863)

On the 12th day of the 1st Month, a corpse dressed in the manner of a samurai was found wrapped in straw matting at the edge of the moat of the official residence (goyō yashiki) outside Shinbashi. On top of it was placed a board approximately three shaku wide and one shaku tall, bearing the following inscription:

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　(Addressed to:) Uno Hachirō

> "This man went by the name 'Tōō' [Eastern Cherry Blossom], and for many years mingled among the men of loyal ambition [shishi], while secretly gathering intelligence and leaking it to corrupt officials [kanri], thereby bringing harm to numerous men of sincere and loyal purpose. This conduct — treachery and betrayal — constitutes a grave crime that Heaven and Earth cannot tolerate. Therefore, divine punishment (tenchū) has been carried out upon him as described above."

　　1st Month — The aforementioned individual is said to have been a retainer of Nagai Hida-no-Kami.

---

○ 18th day of the 1st Month

　　Envoys dispatched:《Mizuno Izumi-no-Kami and Inoue Kawachi-no-Kami》→ To Lord Tayasu Dainagon

As per his petition, it has been decreed that he is to retire from his official rank by one grade and withdraw into retirement (inkyo). Regarding the stipend of 100,000 koku attached to his office, it has been conveyed (via the said envoys) that this is to be granted to his designated heir, Juchiyō.
