　　　Erected in the middle of the riverbed
　　　between Sanjō Bridge and Shijō Bridge,
　　　as shown at right.
　　　These are the heads of old wooden effigies.
　　　What is hanging below appears to be
　　　memorial tablets (ihai). All are old objects.
　　　The eyes are damaged.

　　　At the foot of Sanjō Bridge, there was a placard
　　　recording the nature of the crimes, but
　　　the crowd was so great that it could not be copied down.
　　　However, the gist of the crimes listed was
　　　that they had "forgotten the two characters of loyalty
　　　and righteousness, the virtuous customs of the imperial land,
　　　and had troubled the imperial will"—and that
　　　"there are at present three such persons,"
　　　and therefore "such measures will be carried out
　　　one after another"—or words to that effect were written,
　　　but the characters were small, and being viewed
　　　from a distance, it was impossible to read and copy them in detail.

　　　This wooden effigy is said to be one that
　　　was kept at Tōji-in temple.

　　　　　Tōji-in is said to be the mortuary temple of the Kamakura御坊 [honorific].
