　　"The sign that all hearts under heaven are united in harmony is this gathering of today."　— Lord Konoe
　　"In this peaceful and prosperous reign, even this assembly brings tranquility — such is today's banquet."　— Shungaku

　　　　4th day of the 12th month of the Year of the Boar
　　Ordered to guard the inner sea (Edo Bay), and entrusted with the coastal battery below Gotenyama. Defensive arrangements shall be strictly ordered in advance. Moreover, those stationed at Shinagawa post as guards — matters to be coordinated with those responsible for inner sea defense — are hereby relieved of their duties.

　　Regarding the instruction to serve as caretaker during the lord's journey to Kyoto,　　— Senior Retainer of Mito Domain
　　the custody of the coastal battery below Gotenyama at Shinagawa is hereby rescinded. However, during off-duty periods, troops shall be dispatched within the enclosure, as shall be reported accordingly. Details regarding locations shall be communicated at the appropriate time.　　— Okitsu Kurando
