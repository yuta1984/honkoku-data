**4th day of the 4th Month — Transmitted by order of the Lord Inaba-no-kami**
**To the Metsuke (inspectors):**
- Abe Harima-no-kami
- Sakai Shigenojō
- Ōkubo Kaga-no-kami
- Matsudaira Ukyō-no-suke
- Sōma Daizen-no-tayū

As has already been declared, British naval vessels have arrived, and this being a time of no small gravity, it is impossible to predict what disorders may arise as malicious elements take advantage of unrest in the city and surrounding areas, engaging in rioting and lawless violence. Accordingly, you are hereby ordered to maintain order. Each of you shall dispatch a contingent of men and, without regard to day or night, conduct patrols throughout the city. Upon encountering any acts of lawlessness, you are to arrest the offenders without mercy, and depending on the circumstances, it shall be permissible even to cut them down. For further details, consult with the Town Magistrate (Machi-bugyō).
The foregoing has been communicated to each of you; take due note accordingly.

---

**Declaration issued regarding what the Regent (Kampaku) communicated to the Lord of Mito on the 13th day of the 3rd Month:**

Since the Shogun has accepted the request to remain in Kyoto, [the Lord of Mito] has been ordered to proceed to the capital area to serve as guardian of the Kantō region. [He is] to proceed to Edo promptly, make thorough preparations for defense, and should the English barbarians happen to initiate hostilities, [he is] to exert every effort and engage them in decisive battle — such is the directive that has been issued.

---

**Written Notice transmitted by the Lord Makino Bizen-no-kami　　※ [He] being the Kyoto Shoshidai**

Inasmuch as the reply has been settled that a refusal shall be issued regarding the matter of expulsion of foreigners (jōi) on the 10th day of the 5th Month, each domain is to, with this in mind, reinforce the defenses along its own coastal areas with ever greater strictness, and in the event of an enemy assault, to sweep and repel [them]. You are requested to carry this out accordingly.
