【Left folio】
A poetry gathering (renga session) at the Shogunate (Ryūei), held on the 11th day of the 1st month of the 3rd year of Bunkyū (1863), Year of the Boar.

　　　　Opening verse (theme: morning)

May your fragrance reach even to the clouds, O blossoms of the ever-constant pine!　　　　　(Shōdō)
　　The voices of cranes rising in graceful dance, so serene and clear　　　(Lord Inner Minister)
On the spring sea, the waves are gentle and the tide comes in full　　　(Shōzon)
　　Around the island shores, the snow has melted away　　　(Shōshō)
The reflection pure — the moon's sacred vessel must have set forth　　　(Shin■)
　　The sounds have quieted; the autumn wind across the sky　　　(Yukikawa)
Colors appear: the maples of Hinohara forest, scattered here and there　　　(Mitsutomo)
　　The raining shower drips along the path beneath the cryptomeria　　　(Chi■)
Having waited in hope, at last the hototogisu has come to sing　　　(Jakukai)
　　Gazing at the summer mountains, so near to the eaves　　　(Nichijō)
The fishing pavilion grows ever cooler as the waterfall cascades　　　(Toyohide)
　　The courtiers who delight in the elegant and refined spirit　　　(Masatomi)
The melody of the instruments — how supremely exquisite it must be　　　(Michiyoshi)
