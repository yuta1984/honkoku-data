One item: Letter received from Tōbu (Edo)
Approximately 300 men under the command of Udono Hatsuō, Takahashi Ise-no-kami, and Chūjō Kin'nosuke are stationed at the Honjō-Ogasawara residence. It has been reported that they recently demanded military funds in a manner tantamount to forced borrowing, and foot soldiers and their subordinates recklessly caused disturbances at Ryōgoku, apparently beheading [someone] (the character here is unclear). Although there may be some legitimate grounds for their actions, there are unruly individuals among them who frequent brothels, run up tabs at restaurants without paying, and engage in petty forced borrowing. As a result, the following officials have been ordered to suppress the rōnin: Lord Sakai Saemon-no-jō, Lord Ōkubo Kaga-no-kami, Lord Abe Harima-no-kami, Lord Soma Daizennosuke, and Lord Matsudaira Ukyō-no-suke. These officials have been patrolling the city day and night. The rōnin are not confined only to Honjō; they have also taken up lodgings in the Koishikawa and Yushima areas, and some are staying at inns in Bakurochō. On the 14th day of the 4th month, something occurred when Lord Sakai's patrol officers were near Dentsu-in Temple in Koishikawa, resulting in a confrontation with rōnin, during which Lord Sakai's retainers were cut down. From that point, around the eighth hour (approx. 2 a.m.) the previous night, the situation became chaotic.
