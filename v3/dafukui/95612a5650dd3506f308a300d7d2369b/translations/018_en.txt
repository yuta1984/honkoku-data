**4th Month (date unspecified)**

As the Imperial Palace has sent word that the directive came from Lord Sanjō Chūnagon, who serves as the official in charge of guarding the capital (Kyōshi), this is hereby communicated for your information. Furthermore, regarding the number of personnel for the guard duties, you are requested to arrange for them to be reported to the said Lord Sanjō Chūnagon.
The foregoing is to be circulated among all lords holding fiefs of 100,000 koku or more.

**4th Month (date unspecified)**

As all lords holding fiefs of 100,000 koku or more have now been ordered to remain in Kyoto for the purpose of guarding the imperial capital, for the present year of Inoko (i.e., Bunkyū 3, 1863), you are to follow the schedule as laid out in the attached document, proceed to Kyoto from your respective domains, and make strict arrangements for the guard duties. It should be understood that this arrangement is to be carried out on a rotating basis.
The foregoing is to be circulated among all lords holding fiefs of 100,000 koku or more.

**4th Day of the 4th Month**

As British naval vessels have arrived and this is a time of no small gravity, it is impossible to predict what disorders may ensue as malicious elements take advantage of unrest in the city and surrounding areas, engaging in rioting and lawless violence. Accordingly, as a measure of control, each [official] has been ordered to dispatch a contingent of men, conduct patrols throughout the city day and night, and arrest any persons committing acts of lawlessness on the spot.

(Assigned Metsuke / Inspectors):
- Sakai Shigenojō
- Ōkubo Kaga-no-kami
- Abe Harima-no-kami
- Matsudaira Ukyō-no-suke
- Sōma Daizen-no-suke

Additionally assigned to this urgent matter:
- Commissioner of Finance (Kanjō-bugyō): Oguri Bungo-no-kami
- Inspector (Metsuke): Sugiura Shōichirō, and one other
- Auditor of Accounts (Kanjō-ginmiyaku): Suzuki Dainojin
