**22nd Day of the 1st Month**

Appointed to the position of Wakadoshiyori (Junior Council of Elders):　　Jisha-bugyō　Arima Tōtōmi-no-Kami

Matsudaira Awa-no-Kami

As there were also matters raised through private petition, he was relieved of both his concurrent posts as Director of the Land Forces (Rikugun Sōsai) and his additional charge overseeing naval affairs. Henceforth, he was instructed to attend the castle frequently and to bring to attention any matters he noticed, consulting on all affairs as needed.

---

　　　　　　　　　Sleeve-lining document (sode-ura)　　Matsudaira Shuri-no-Tayū

This time, British naval vessels arrived at the port of Yokohama and presented three difficult demands (nandai) in connection with the Namamugi Incident:

- **First**: That indemnity (compensation money) be paid;
- **Second**: That the head of Saburō (the perpetrator) be surrendered;
- **Third**: That warships be dispatched against Satsuma Domain.

As none of these demands could be readily acceded to, a response was delivered to that effect. Given that, depending on how matters unfold, it cannot be ruled out that warships may ultimately be directed toward Satsuma, this notice is hereby conveyed so that all parties may be duly informed and prepared.

　　2nd Month, Year of the Boar (Bunkyū 3, 1863)
