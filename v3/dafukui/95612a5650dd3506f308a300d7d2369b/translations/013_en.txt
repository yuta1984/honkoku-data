On the 21st day of the 3rd month of the year of the Boar (Bunkyu 3, 1863), at Nijō Castle in Kyoto, with the Senior Councillors (Rōjū) assembled, the following written notice was handed down to the various domain lords:

Regarding the Shogun's return to Edo, this has been repeatedly stayed by imperial edicts. As announced in the recent imperial communication, all matters pertaining to the office of Shogun are to continue to be entrusted as before. Accordingly, if the various domain lords and those below them carry out the direction of all matters of guard duty and defense, this will put [the Imperial Court] at ease. Depending on circumstances, [His Majesty] is so inclined as to personally lead an imperial expedition [if necessary].

　　　　[2]Xth day of the 3rd month　　　Humbly acknowledged and accepted.
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　[Shogun's personal name]

Item: The Kanagawa Magistrate, Asano Ise-no-kami, has reported that one British warship has set sail [and departed].

Item: On the 19th day of the 3rd month, the Grand Inspector (Ō-metsuke) issued the following notice in Kyoto:
　　Expel the barbarians (Jōi)
