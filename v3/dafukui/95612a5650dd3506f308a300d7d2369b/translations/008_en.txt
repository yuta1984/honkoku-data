**[Orders Regarding Princess Yō's Return to Her Home Domain]**

Seventh day of the third month

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Chamberlain to Princess Yō: Hatano Nabenosuke

Princess Yō is to return to the home domain of the Kaga Chūnagon (Middle Counselor of Kaga); he has been ordered to accompany her in attendance.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Inner Physician: Tsuda Ryōshunin

By the same order, he has been commanded to return and accompany her in attendance.

**[Order for Military Preparedness Against British Naval Vessels]**

Third month

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ii Kamon-no-Kami

British naval vessels have been directed toward Edo, and it has been declared that if no reply is received by the eighth day of the third month, they will proceed to war. As there is no basis upon which this demand can be accepted, (the authorities) are resolved to give battle. Accordingly, you are commanded to guard the area from Yokohama to Kawasaki. Orders have been issued that troops be dispatched immediately and that every effort be devoted to defense.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Yamashiro-no-Kami
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kuze Kenkichi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Iwaki Sakyō-no-Daibu
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Tsugaru Shikibu-no-Tayū
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Tamegoro
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Yamauchi Tōtōmi-no-Kami
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Manabe Awaji-no-Kami
