One: (The 11th day of the 4th Month)
　At this time of the imperial procession, there is a rumor that some unspeakable incident may occur — an unforeseeable matter. This is fundamentally a most vexing affair. Throughout all the lands under Heaven, there is no one who is not a subject of the King [i.e., the Emperor]. Not only warrior retainers, but also the common people and townspeople who come to observe [the procession] must carry out their duty to guard and protect [His Majesty].

---

　　　　　　Memorandum of Oral Statement

Among the various officials, beginning with Oguri Bungo-no-kami, there are many who, even at this critical juncture, continue to carry on the intentions of Ii [Naosuke] and Andō [Nobumasa]. They ought to be thoroughly investigated and put to death without exception; however, at this very moment when the foreign English barbarians are making impertinent demands, and especially since the Shogun's intentions remain unclear during his absence from Edo, [we] have held back for the time being. If the Shogun ultimately fails to make a decisive resolution, we shall have no choice but for us, the assembled rōnin, to take the lead and simultaneously put to death both the internal traitors and the foreign barbarians. We ask that you take note of this. Respectfully submitted.

　　29th day of the 3rd Month, Year of the Boar　　　　　Those Resolved to Die

The above is a copy of a notice posted at Nihonbashi.
