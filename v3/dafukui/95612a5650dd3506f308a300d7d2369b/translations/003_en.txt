**[Record of the 7th day of the 1st Month]**

○ 7th day, 1st Month
(In the御座間 [Ozama, the Shogun's private chamber]) — Matsudaira Kansō received an audience.

The Shogun's words were conveyed:
"As the Kubō-sama [the Shogun] is at an age when the cultivation of both literary and military arts is of the utmost importance, it has been decreed that he shall consult you on these matters. Henceforth, you are to attend from time to time and render your guidance and assistance."
The above was announced by the Rōjū (Senior Councillor) Kawachi-no-Kami [Lord of Kawachi] before an assembled group of Rōjū, at the black goose cedar sliding door of the Shiroshoin [White Study].

---

○ (Same day) — To Matsudaira Awa-no-Kami [Lord of Awa]:

"Although you were appointed as Commander of the Land Forces (Rikugun Sōsai) only recently at the end of last month, and there are many pressing duties, permission to return to your domain (ohima) has been granted. However, regarding your departure, you are to understand that it may be delayed until the latter part of this month."
The above was announced in the same location, by the same official (Kawachi-no-Kami), with the Rōjū assembled as before.

---

**[Record of the 12th day of the 1st Month]**

○ 12th day, 1st Month
Appointed as Jisha-bugyō (Commissioner of Temples and Shrines): Doi Ōsumi-no-Kami [Lord of Ōsumi]

---

**[Record of the 8th day of the 1st Month]**

○ 8th day, 1st Month
(In the御座間) — To Matsudaira Yōdō:

A sword: Made in Yamato Province, by Kanemasa (包利)
　　　　　Value: 50 gold coins (koban)
The above was personally bestowed by the Shogun's own hand, with gracious words.

---

○ Note: On the 10th day of the 1st Month, Matsudaira Yōdō departed for Kyoto by ship. On the same day that the retired lord of the Nabeshima domain also set out, there was a formal visit to Edo Castle.
