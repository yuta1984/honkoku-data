**[Left Page]**

Regarding the above: On the morning of the 19th of this month, just past the Hour of the Rooster (approximately 8 a.m.), the agents were summoned by Atobe Yamashiro-no-Kami, and word was received that an armed group had gathered at the Tenma unit compound, that people from various rural areas had also joined them, and that peasants were massing to converge on the area, with instructions given to make arrangements to suppress the situation. Almost immediately, a fire broke out at Tenma, and I promptly assembled the personnel listed by name above and deployed to the scene. At Tenma Bridge, yoriki and doshin of the town's administration had taken up defensive positions armed with spears and firearms. Furthermore, having received rumors that the rebel band was approaching from Higashinari District and the Kawachi Province area, I judged it advisable to circle around to the rear approach and cut off the back route, and so I deployed as far as the Nakano Village riverside embankment along the Yodo River and held that position. As there were no further reports of peasants massing from the villages or of uprisings and rampages in the rural areas, and as the fire in Osaka appeared to be growing ever larger, I dispatched my agents in separate directions to the various villages, and I myself first withdrew from that position.
