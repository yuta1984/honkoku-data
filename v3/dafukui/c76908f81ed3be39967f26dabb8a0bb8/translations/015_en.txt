　　　　　　　　　　Sōhachi　　Sōzaemon

The above individuals were arrested one after another between the 20th and the 24th of this month, and upon general interrogation, they stated that they had gone to Ōshio Heihachirō's residence because there was sand-carrying labor [to be performed there], whereupon they were shut inside the gate and instructed to carry out luggage — [packed] in wicker trunks, carrying boxes, or bags — [from the premises]. They stated that after carrying [the goods] out through the gate, they had fled one after another at various points between [the gate] and the vicinity of Namba Bridge, and had then returned home. They were accordingly ordered to be committed to prison.
