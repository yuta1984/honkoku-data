Regarding the incident in which Ōshio Heihachirō, a yoriki (mounted officer) of the Osaka Town Magistrate's office, conspired with a group of followers and set fire to the city of Osaka on the nineteenth day of the second month of Tenpō 8, Year of the Rooster [March 25, 1837]:

　　　　　Preliminary Report Concerning the Existence of the Conspirators

Upon hearing reports that Ōshio Kakunosuke, a yoriki under the command of Atobe, Lord of Yamashiro, together with his adoptive father Heihachirō — currently retired — had formed a conspiratorial band and were planning something of no ordinary gravity, instructions regarding the enforcement of controls in various areas were issued by Lord Yamashiro on the morning of yesterday, the nineteenth. However, shortly thereafter — past the hour of five [around 8 a.m.] — a fire broke out in the vicinity of the Osaka Town Magistrate Tenkai's compound. Accordingly, I mobilized my men and deployed to the scene, dispatched my deputies and agents to various locations to maintain order, and made arrangements to arrest suspicious individuals. Although I shall report the full details at a later time, I hereby submit this preliminary notification.

Respectfully submitted.

　　　　Twentieth day, second month, Year of the Rooster　　　　Nemoto Zenzaemon
