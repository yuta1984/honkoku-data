[Ōshio] Heihachirō had told [Matsunosuke] that if there were any further destitute people [in need], he should investigate and report back. Matsunosuke replied that he would continue to look after those beyond the ones who had already received charity as much as possible, and then returned home. He stated that on that occasion, a certain Yoichiemon from the village had been present at Sawaue. He was accordingly ordered to be prohibited from leaving [his residence].

The foregoing concerns the fact that on the 19th of this month, Ōshio Heihachirō, a former kumigashira yoriki [senior mounted constable] of the Osaka Town Magistracy, and his confederates conspired together, formed a faction, set fire to the residences of the Town Magistracy's yoriki and dōshin, the Tenma compound, and residences throughout the city, committed acts of violence, and fled. Accordingly, a manhunt was conducted, and the confederates and those implicated have been arrested and dealt with as described in this document. Arrangements will be made to confer with the Town Magistracy here and hand over all [the prisoners]. It is reported that there are those who have since been arrested or who have killed themselves, but a manhunt for remaining members of the faction is currently underway. This matter is hereby reported. Respectfully submitted.

**A written report regarding the riot committed by the conspirators in Osaka**

Ōshio Heihachirō, a former kumigashira yoriki of the Osaka Town Magistracy, formed a band of conspirators under his leadership, and on the morning of the 19th of this month, past the Hour of the Ox [approximately 6 a.m.], launched fire arrows and fired guns at the residences of the Town Magistracy's yoriki and dōshin, the Tenma compound, and throughout the city, committing acts of violence. The full account of these events is set out below.
