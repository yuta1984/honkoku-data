　　　　　　　　　　　　Jihēe, Otojirō, Sōjirō

Upon investigation of the above-named persons, it was found that on the morning of the 19th of last month [2nd month], Kōtarō, the son of Yoēmon of Sawakami-e Village, told them along the Yodogawa embankment that there was sand-carrying work (sunamochi) available at Heihachirō's residence and that they should come along. Thinking it was merely another occasion to receive charity relief, they made their way there one after another. Once inside the gate, however, they were locked in, and gunfire was discharged. In a state of fear, they were given instructions to carry out items that had fallen [from the premises]. Kasuke shouldered items such as a wicker trunk (tsuzura), a double-sided carrying load, a sandal bag, and a small long chest (ko-nagamochi). Among them, Kumaijirō, Ihachi, and Otojirō proceeded empty-handed as far as Koraibashi. There, the laborers scattered and fled, whereupon they abandoned the items they had been carrying and all fled and returned home. This is what they stated.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　(His) mother
