　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　[Under the jurisdiction of] my daikan office
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Settsu Province, Nishinari District, Sonezaki Village
　　　　Ordered to refrain from travel on the 23rd day of the 2nd month of the Rooster year　　Son of village elder Gorōemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Matsunosuke

The above-named individual is a disciple of Heihachirō. He states that when he visited [Heihachirō's residence] on the 1st of last month, he was given one hundred food-distribution tickets (segyōfuda) to distribute to the destitute people of his village. He further states that when he visited again on the 18th of the same month, Heihachirō told him that if there were any destitute people other than those who had already received the previous distribution, he should investigate and report them. He replied that there were no further connections beyond those who had already received assistance, then returned home. He also states that at that time, Yoichiemon of Sawakamie Village was present.
