The foregoing concerns those arrested and brought before me in connection with the conspiracy case. As stated in the document, [the report] was submitted to Atobe Yamashiro-no-kami on the 7th of this month. Accordingly, I hereby submit this copy for your information. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　20th Day, Third Month, Year of the Rooster　　　　Nemoto Zenzaemon

　　　One copy to Lord Naitō　　　　One copy to Lord Yabe

　　A written report submitted to the Osaka Town Magistrate's Office concerning persons implicated in the conspiracy case

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　[Under the jurisdiction of] my daikan office
　　Ordered into confinement on the 21st day of the 2nd month of the Rooster year　　Settsu Province, Higashinari District, Zengōji Village
　　Ordered released from confinement and permitted to travel elsewhere on the 2nd day of the 3rd month of the same year　　Landless peasant
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ubee

The above-named individual states that on the morning of the 9th of last month, while on the bank of the Yodo River, he was dispatched by Yoichiemon's son Kōtarō of Sawakamie Village to go to Ōshio Heihachirō's residence; however, upon seeing the state of the premises, he immediately fled and returned home.
