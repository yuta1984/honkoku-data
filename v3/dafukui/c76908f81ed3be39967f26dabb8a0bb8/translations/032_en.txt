　　　　　　**Report regarding the discovery of handbills (*sutebumi*)**

**[Left folio]**

Printed notices (*hangyō no kakitsuke*), enclosed in yellow silk bags and bearing inscriptions to the effect that "blessings bestowed by Heaven shall be distributed to all, down to the common farmers (*komai*) of each village," were found abandoned along the highways and at ferry landings within the villages under my magistracy — namely: Higashikawa Village in Higashinari District, Settsu Province; and Kajishima Village, Kamifukushima Village, Narikōji Village, Ōwada Village, Kōryūji Village, Kamitsuya Village, Shimosanbан Village, Kashima Village, and Eboe Village in Tennōji-shō, all in Nishinari District, Settsu Province — or were thrown into the homes of farmers by persons unknown. Reports of this were made to me, and I have confiscated these handbills at my magistracy. I hereby respectfully report this matter. As above.
