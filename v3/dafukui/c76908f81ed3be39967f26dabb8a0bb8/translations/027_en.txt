　　　　A written report submitted regarding the handover from the Osaka Town Magistrate's Office of persons arrested in connection with the conspiracy case.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　[Formerly under the jurisdiction of] Ukyō Seizaemon [as] daikan
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　[Currently] this village [is] under this official's daikan jurisdiction

Arrested and imprisoned on the 1st day of the 3rd month, Year of the Rooster:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Settsu Province, Higashinari District, Ikaino Village
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Peasant Rennnosuke's father,
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Shiba-no-suke

Upon conducting a full investigation of the above-named person, the following was found: Kumaijirō, the son of Gonzaemon of the main family (honke), had previously boarded at the residence of Ōshio Heihachirō, and through this connection had been visiting on occasions such as the New Year, midsummer, and midwinter greetings. On the 14th of last month [2nd month], he was summoned to come by Heihachirō, who told him: "I am distributing money to those in poverty; please distribute it among the needy in your village as you see fit," and handed over thirty certificates (fuda) worth one shū each, along with the money itself. Shibanosuke accepted these, returned to his village, and asked a fellow peasant named Shichibee to help distribute the funds among the poor as appropriate. To report back on this matter, he returned [to Heihachirō's residence] on the following day, the 15th, whereupon he received a further twenty-five certificates. He distributed these as payments of 200 mon each to fifty persons and accepted their receipts.
