**Report Regarding Individuals Connected to the Conspiracy Case**

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Formerly under the magistracy of Ishihara Seizaemon;
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Currently under my magistracy:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ikaino Village, Higashinari District, Settsu Province
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Farmer
　　　　Placed under village custody, Twenty-Fourth Day, Second Month, Year of the Rooster:　　　Shichibe

The above individual reportedly received a request from Shima-no-suke — the father of Rennojō, a farmer in the same village — and, in cooperation with Shima-no-suke, distributed relief funds (*segyōkin*) received from Ōshio Heihachirō to impoverished people within the village as they saw fit. On account of this report, (Ishihara) Seizaemon had him placed under village custody, and we were so informed. He is therefore submitted [for your consideration] while remaining under village custody. As above.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same village as above
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Farmer with no assessed landholding (*mukō hyakushō*)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kuzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　And seventy-nine others

The above individuals hold small assessed landholdings or none at all, and make their living through tenant farming, single-trade work, or day labor;

**[Left folio]**

all of them are persons in poverty. They reportedly each received relief funds of one *shū* in coin, or two hundred *mon* in copper cash, from Ōshio Heihachirō through the intermediary of Shima-no-suke within the village. On account of this report, Seizaemon had them placed under village custody and so informed us. However, since their involvement amounted to no more than receiving the relief distribution, their village custody has been lifted (*sashi-men*) and they have been so notified.

The individuals placed under village custody or subsequently released therefrom are as listed in the document. I hereby respectfully report this matter. As above.

　　　　Third Month, Year of the Rooster　　　　　Nemoto Zenzaemon

The foregoing concerns matters connected to the conspiracy case; as described in the document, this has already been reported to Atobe Yamashiro-no-Kami, and I hereby respectfully submit a copy of that report. Furthermore, the substance of the above has also been reported to the Public Affairs Magistrate's Office (*Kujigata Bugyōsho*). As above.

　　　　Twenty-Eighth Day, Third Month, Year of the Rooster　　　　　Nemoto Zenzaemon
