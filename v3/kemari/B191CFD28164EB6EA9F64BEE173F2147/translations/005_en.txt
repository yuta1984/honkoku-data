Item: Whether the garden faces south, west, north, or east, the placement of the trees should be replanted accordingly as follows.
　　[A note added personally by Masamori:] No matter which direction the garden faces, to the right of the eaves should be the pine (*matsu*), to the left should be the tachibana (橘, mandarin orange), facing the pine should be the maple (*kaede*), and facing the cherry (*sakura*) should be the willow (*yanagi*).
