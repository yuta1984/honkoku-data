　　　Preface

It is said that as the cycles of Heaven revolve and times change, it becomes easier to achieve great deeds. In the year Keichō 14 (1609), the year of Tsuchinoto-Tori (己酉), the heavens suddenly stirred and trembled, and moonlight shone from all four corners of the sky in a manner unlike ordinary times.
