**Hiyoku no Kadonami (The Gate District of Ill-Fated Love)**
**Tamaya Jishinbei: The Tub-Overturning Scene**

〽Shaken awake at the first moment of sleep by the writhing catfish-earthquake,
The fury of fire spreads, and all are
Lost in confusion over their children.
Where can anyone go? There is no word —
In the foremost brothel quarter of the northern provinces, the cries of the night,
The moored boats, there is not a single guest who fails to be roused from sleep.
Among them, the top of the great house
Keeps rumbling and groaning — what terrible fate,
The hardships of this cruel floating world,
And then, the final aftershock —
In place of the turtle, the guilt of the hand-cuffs
Now, drunk and in the bath,
〽How wretched, to find oneself turned upside down under a tub!
Jishinbei stumbles about, his eyes filling with tears...
