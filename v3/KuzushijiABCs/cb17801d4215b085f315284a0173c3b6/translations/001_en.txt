**"Umegoyomi Mitate Hasshoujin" — The Dashing Man: Tsurnosuke of the Takarabune (Treasure Ship)**

*(Kyōka — Comic Poem)*

Asked where I'm headed — to Naniwa Bay, of all places — walking together with a turtle that won't let go, stretching out like the crane of Jurōjin all at once; to be "hoteied" (tossed about) by Ebisu, Daikoku, and Ten [Benzaiten] at this very moment would be unbearable — what a funny state of affairs! Thanks to Edo's blessings, even Fukurokuju's great head cannot be pushed down; with the fierce spirit of a tiger leaping out the gate, in a temporary lodging within Benzaiten's Majima, listening to the sound of waves lapping against the boat — it sounds like lovesick boasting, but whether for just one night or more, this dream-laden treasure ship of sleep has never once left my pillow.
