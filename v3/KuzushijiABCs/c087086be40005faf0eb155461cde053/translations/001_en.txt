**"Eight Champions as Seen Through the Plum Almanac"**
Otokodate (Chivalrous Street Champion)
Ko-no-suke of the Komatsu-hiki

In the spring fields where haze drifts and hangs,
A goshoguruma (court carriage) is pulled out sideways—
Though the small pine (komatsu) is dragged along,
The swagger is too fierce to follow the proper way of the trade;
Even the abuse hurled is mild, raised as he was in the capital,
Like a bush warbler's uncertain "hō-hoke-kyō."

The purple of standing grasses, the tightly clenched fist of young bracken—
Striking and shattering like blazing ice newly thawed,
All at once bringing laughter in the green mountains;
Make peace now, and the roots and leaves of old grudges—
Leave them all, together with Komatsu, in the care of Ko-no-suke.
