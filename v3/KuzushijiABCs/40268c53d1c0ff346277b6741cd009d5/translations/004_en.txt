## Publisher's Foreword

The *jōruri* puppet play *Kanadehon Chūshingura* is celebrated throughout the land, so widely known that even children of three years of age are familiar with it. For this very reason, there will surely be those who point out errors and omissions (in works based upon it). Building upon that great popularity, works dramatizing the biographies of the loyal retainers—including musical pieces, stage performances, picture books, and popular illustrated novels—number in the hundreds, each varying in approach and highlighting its own peculiarities to convey the retainers' courage and steadfast loyalty. These works, praising men of loyalty unparalleled in Japan and China alike, have not faded from the world even as the years have passed. Nevertheless, there is no small number of works that, out of fondness for fictional embellishment, have transmitted inaccurate accounts of what actually occurred.

Therefore, at the opening of each volume, portraits of the loyal retainers have been modeled and drawn by Keisai (Eisen), and brief biographies of over forty men have been recorded by Elder Shunsui, based upon reliable historical sources. In depicting the scenes in which the retainers, sparing no effort of body or bone in their quest for vengeance, disguised their appearances and changed their names before ultimately achieving their long-cherished goal, the related facts concerning faithful wives and heroic women have also been included. To caution against wickedness and cowardice is, without doubt, one facet of the principle of rewarding good and punishing evil.

Readers, do not be puzzled that the frontispiece illustrations do not directly correspond to the main text. When the series is complete in its entirety, the brief biographies of the loyal retainers will be fully assembled. The main narrative has been written in the manner of popular *ninjōbon* (sentimental fiction), so that it may be easy for women and children to read, recording the principles of karmic retribution for good and evil. Written in the distinctive style of Kyōkunte-no-Shujin, it is crafted so as never to grow tedious.

Do not regard this work as being on the same level as the baseless rumors and idle talk that float about the world. There are very many unusual accounts and rare anecdotes concerning the loyal retainers that are unfamiliar to the ear. This is a tale through which, by revisiting the old, one may come to know the true history anew. We hope that as further volumes are published, the story will reach its finest passages, and we humbly request your esteemed readership and valued judgment.
