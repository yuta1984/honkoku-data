"From the moment you came in, I felt a fondness for you. And then the master and the mistress of this house, in their tasteful way, said: 'Go ahead and ask Shō-san for his help — it would be good to be in his care. That way, you won't have to go out to the parlor and wear yourself out pleasing customers. You seem to be in poor health, after all — drinking too much or staying up late wouldn't do you any good.' They showed me such kindness, one consideration after another. And that is why I felt all the more drawn to you. Even if I am in the way, I dearly wish to attend to you for at least this one night. Are you feeling unwell from the sake? Here now, let me gently rub your chest for you.

【Left page】
I will rub it gently. Please forgive my forwardness. Do you find it unpleasant?" Shōzaemon: "No, no — there's nothing in the least unwelcome about it, nothing to complain of — but, tell me, what is your situation in life, exactly?" O-Yasu: "You must think me terribly forward, I know. But this household belongs to your friend, so there is really no need to be on guard. I have come to this house as an apprentice geisha on trial. My father has had bad luck, and my mother is constantly ill, so we are always short of things. I thought perhaps if I could at least serve as a sake pourer at banquets…"
