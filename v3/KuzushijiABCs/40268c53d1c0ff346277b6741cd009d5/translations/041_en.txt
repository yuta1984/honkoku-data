Heibē: "Now now, there's no need to be so startled. If one were to sell it to those loyal rōnin of Enya's band, it would fetch a fine price——"

Sanjūrō: "What a remarkable uncle you are——"

Heibē: "Ah ha ha ha ha! Once you take a merchant for a son-in-law, you lose the craftsman's spirit, and every last thing gets turned to coin in your mind—I only said it in jest. Nothing to take to heart. Take it with you before anyone sees."

Sanjūrō: "Why, thank you very much."

He receives it reverently, holding it above his head. Heibē gazes steadily at his face——

Heibē: "Dear, dear—so young, and yet what a sorry state. And all the while Oshizu hasn't the faintest dream of it——"

Sanjūrō: "What do you mean—that Oshizu hasn't the faintest dream of it——?"

Heibē: "Now then, do be on your way for today——"

【Left folio】

Sanjūrō: "Very well then—until we meet again soon."

And so, taking his leave, he departs—his heart half filled with joy, half shadowed with doubt. From that time on, he grew ever closer to Oshizu, deepening their bond. And having confirmed beyond doubt that the floor plan he had obtained was indeed that of Kōno's residence under construction, he swiftly dispatched the plan to Ōboshi at Yamashina. Yuranosuke was greatly pleased, and quickly set those who would travel east on their way—the planning for the night raid was carried out by means of this very floor plan.
