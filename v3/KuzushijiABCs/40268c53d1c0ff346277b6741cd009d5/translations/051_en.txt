**[Right folio]**

...it is said to be a secret letter. Upon reading it thus, they found that if even a tenth of the Enya clan's honor and lineage were to be lost, the most loyal retainers—rather than seeking to avenge their lord—wished instead to find some means of restoring their master's house. Caring nothing for personal fame, even if they were to be scorned by the world as cowardly samurai who failed to avenge their lord, they had hoped to devote their loyalty by raising their late lord's younger brother to a position in the world. This may be inferred from the letter set out on the left.

---

**[Left folio]**

I have had the honor of reading your esteemed letter. I have received word from the theater manager (*za-moto*) that in Kyoto (the capital region), the actors are assembled to a satisfactory degree.

That being so, as the second theater in Kobikicho in your city has not been faring well, I should like to consult with you further regarding your intention to stage a *kiri-kyōgen* (closing play). In any case, I shall look forward to meeting you in person in the near future, at which time I shall speak with you directly. That is all.

　　　Month　Day　　　　　　Kogetsudō

　　　*Reply to the above*
