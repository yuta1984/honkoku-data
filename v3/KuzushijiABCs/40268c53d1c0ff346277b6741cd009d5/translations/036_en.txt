**[Right page]**

She seemed unlike a lowly woman, being honest and demure, with no fickle or frivolous heart in matters of love. She did not think ill of Sanjuro's manly appearance, yet at first she was not at all convinced. However, she said: "If we can truly become husband and wife in the future, I will not shrink from any hardship now. Even if we cannot be together for a year or two, let us swear to each other that our hearts will never change, and pledge our love on that promise." Her words were as beautiful as her face and heart, and Okano was truly overjoyed, continuing to devote himself to her with patience and sincerity. Though they were now deeply in love with each other, their rare meetings required crossing the barrier of prying eyes — and so their promise to meet secretly today was...

**[Left page]**

...at the home of her uncle in Mikoyama-chō. Since Oshizu had already confided in her uncle beforehand, she felt no hesitation and called out from the gate:

Oshizu: "Uncle, hasn't San-san (Mr. Sanjuro) come yet?"

Uncle: "Oh, Oshizu! Come on, come inside. No matter how much you're eager to see your handsome fellow, it's a bit much to be standing outside asking before you've even stepped in. Ha ha ha ha ha!"

He laughed good-naturedly. Oshizu smiled and blushed:

Oshizu: "Oh, Uncle, that's not it at all! Today I came early because I had all sorts of things to discuss, and while I was waiting, it got late before I knew it, and San-san had already come..."
