## Enya Hangan Takasada (塩谷判官高貞)

Gentle and temperate by nature, refined and modest in conduct, he loved and pitied his retainers as though they were his own children. His retainers, in turn, cherished their lord as a child cherishes a parent. Just as a crossbow worth a thousand gold pieces would not be discharged merely to shoot a small mouse, so too did Takasada endure with patience when dealing with those beneath his dignity. Yet when confronted with the moment of losing his domain and his life, he rose like a crouching dragon turning to face a fierce tiger, and could restrain himself no longer. The crime of causing a disturbance within the palace precincts was one that the law could not overlook or forgive. How lamentable — that a commander so accomplished in both the literary and martial arts should meet such a fate.

## Kō no Moronao (高野師直)

Desire conquered his benevolence, overpowered his righteousness, overwhelmed his sense of propriety, and defeated his wisdom. For this reason, driven by greed and wicked intent, he gloried in his authority and power, made light of his lord, and conducted himself with base villainy. He brought ruin upon himself, left behind a disgraced name, and destroyed his house. The shame of it spread and was transmitted even to foreign lands. Such is the extreme depth of foolishness. It may truly be said of him: he was a traitor who consumed his stipend without merit — a man with a human face but the heart of a beast.
