【Right page】
"Sleep here and take care of Shō-san for me. He must be quite drunk. We can't leave him sleeping alone, and besides, we don't have enough bedding for tonight, so please sleep beside Shō-san." At these words, O-Yasu seemed bound by the ill-fated karma of a previous life — she made no refusal, like a rice-straw boat steered by O-Fuyu's matchmaking, and showed a happy, smiling face, as beautiful as the glow of autumn leaves. Shōzaemon pretended not to hear, pretended not to see, yet his heart was pounding. The two temptations — drink and desire — were wrapped together under a single futon, and at the bell signaling bedtime, O-Fuyu rose from her seat. "Oh my, it's already the Hour of the Boar [around 10 p.m.]. Come now, I'll go downstairs and get to bed quickly," she said,
