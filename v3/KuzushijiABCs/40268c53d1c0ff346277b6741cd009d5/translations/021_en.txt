It was, in a word, an enviable residence. The young woman opened the latticed gate and went inside. Shōzaemon, who had followed all this way with longing in his heart, stood rooted to the spot, as though something he had been holding had been snatched from his hands. Just then, from the second floor came the sound of strings and flute — a gentle ensemble drifting down to him. Someone was playing a *tsuma-goto*, its pitch well-tuned, and between the strains of the melody came a woman's voice, more captivating than the song of the bush warbler. As he stood there, rapt and listening, Shōzaemon's heart fell into turmoil, and he turned his thoughts inward in private reverie:

"Ah, what a strange thing the world is. In the same world, among the same human beings — some live without a care for season or circumstance, playing the *koto* and *shamisen*, amusing themselves as they please, dwelling in what might as well be the Palace of Heavenly Pleasures, filled with beautiful women. And yet here am I, on the other side of all that — enduring hardship upon hardship, day after day and month after month. And when the day of fulfilling my great vow finally comes,

**[Left Page]**

it will mean dying in battle or cutting open my belly — my life to be decided by tomorrow or the day after. They say a man's whole life is like a dream, but mine is a particularly fleeting dream indeed."

As he murmured these words to himself at the gate, the latticed door swung open from within, and out stepped a woman of about thirty — fair-skinned and graceful in bearing — in the midst of having her *yukata* changed by a servant girl. She came face to face with Shōzaemon, and their eyes met.

**Woman:** "Why — Shō-san!"

she called out to him. Shōzaemon replied:

**Shō:** "Well, well — what a rare surprise. But first — how have you been since —"

**Woman:** "Oh my, goodness! Do come in, come in."

So saying, she led the way from the latticed gate toward the back of the house, then called out:

**Woman:** "Hello! Could you come here a moment?"

**Husband:** "Hey, hey — what is it?"
