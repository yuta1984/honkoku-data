## Preface to the Iroha Bunko

The *Jinlouz*i (*Kinrōshi*) states: "There are people who, upon taking up a scroll to read, immediately fall asleep. For this reason, books (scrolls) are called *kōtai* ('yellow nurse'). This is because they gladden the spirit and nurture one's nature, just as a wet nurse does."

Thus, reading a book came to be called *soheji* ("nursing alongside"). However, there must be two kinds of books that invite sleep. And likewise, there must be two different states of mind in those who fall asleep while reading.
