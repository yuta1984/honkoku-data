## Hayano Kanpei (早野勘平)　Personal name: Yoshitoshi (義利)

---

**[Right folio]**

Hayano Kanpei Yoshitoshi had made a solemn pledge of loyalty unto death — a vow to give his life in the cause of avenging his lord. His parents, however, were farming folk; beyond the gains and losses of trade, they had no understanding whatsoever of the obligations and principles of the warrior's way. Far from lamenting their son's fall into rōnin status, they were glad of it, and had already arranged for him to be adopted as a son-in-law into the household of a certain local gōshi (a rural samurai of lower rank) in Itami, intending to see him settled there in peace.

Yoshitoshi, unable to conceal the truth any longer, revealed everything to his parents and remonstrated with them, expressing his wish to fulfill both his duty of loyalty to his lord and his duty of filial piety — to be faithful in both obligations at once.

---

**[Left folio]**

His father, however, pressed by a sense of obligation toward the gōshi, went and openly disclosed his son's secret circumstances to that man. Finding himself in an impossible situation with no way out, Yoshitoshi wrote a farewell letter addressed to Ōboshi (大星) — that is, Ōishi Yoshio — and then stabbed the gōshi to death before taking his own life, thus fulfilling his vow of loyalty through death.

Yoshio (Ōishi Kuranosuke), at his retreat in Yamashina, heard of this and is said to have grieved deeply.

---

*Mononofu no michi to bakari wo*
*hitosuji ni omoitatekeri*
*shide no tabiji ni*

*(He resolved upon the warrior's way and nothing else, setting his heart upon that single path without wavering — and so he departed upon the journey of death.)*
