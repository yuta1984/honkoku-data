【Right folio】

(Continued from previous page) Heibē: "——and so there are strict orders to keep it from getting out."

Sanjūrō: "Oh, is that so—but surely it cannot be as serious as all that. At the time of the incident, perhaps, there may have been a few souls inclined toward such things—but it has now been the better part of two years. Seeing that no one has yet raised a hand, it seems unlikely that the spirit of sacrificing one's life out of loyalty to a former lord could so easily arise. And least of all from a merchant like myself—even if there were money to be made, upon hearing that it might cost me my life, I'd decline on the spot—there's nothing to worry about on that score. And besides, if one were to cut away the part where the name appears, no one would be able to tell whose residence this plan belongs to."

【Left folio】

Heibē: "Well now, you're right—if one were to cut away the part that reads 'Kōno Moronao,' it would be easy enough. It would be a shame to waste as scrap paper this plan of mine, into which I put more care than any other, made so clearly that even an amateur could read it—with the garden pond, the shrine of Inari, all recorded without omission—my pride and joy. Even as a wainscot lining for the wall, it would be a pleasure to the eye. Since you want it, I'll give it to you—just be sure not to forget to cut away the part that says 'Kōno.'"

Sanjūrō: "Why, I'll cut it away without fail."

Heibē: "If that's your intention, then as a token of Oshizu's bond, in pledge of my trust—I shall send it to you as a betrothal gift for the bridegroom——"

Sanjūrō: "Oh——"
