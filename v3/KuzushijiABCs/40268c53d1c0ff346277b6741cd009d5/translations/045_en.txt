She had given her heart without knowing what fate lay in store — and yet, even now, her longing for him only deepened.

Her uncle Heibei turned to Oshizu and said:

Heibei: "Now, Oshizu. You've been wearing that same gloomy face day after day, and I'm worried you'll make yourself ill. Come now — try to keep your spirits up a little. I understand, of course, that you have every reason to be downcast. But you must think of everything that happens in this world as something fixed by fate. It is precisely *because* Sanjūrō is a man of such deep sincerity and honor that he has set out on this vendetta with his life resolved. By rights, you could have been left behind without a word — cast aside as a passing flower, a fleeting affair he need not concern himself with afterward — and there would have been nothing you could do about it. And yet he went out of his way to deliver a great sum of money

**[Left folio]**

to you, and made arrangements throughout for your security and peace of mind. When he has done so much for you, there is no room left for resentment or tears."

Oshizu: "Indeed — that he should think of me so carefully, even for what comes after... how kind he has been. And above all, he is a man praised and celebrated by everyone in the world, by those who know him and those who do not alike. How could I ever forget him? Should it come to pass that we can never meet again, I will not go on living — that is my resolve."

Tears fell drop by drop onto her lap, and dabbing her face with a tissue paper, she continued:

Oshizu: "Uncle, please, when that time comes — forgive this unfilial child who goes before you..."
