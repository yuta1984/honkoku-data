## Hara Gōemon (原郷右衛門)　Personal name: Mototatsu (元辰)

---

**[Right folio]**

The loyal retainers of Enya took refuge within the castle, intending to follow their lord in death. That they bowed to their lord's benevolence and held their lives lightly was precisely because their lord was a true lord — and so they, in turn, were true retainers.

The full details of the loyal men's deeds are recorded at length in the main text of this work.

---

**[Left folio — Waka poem, attributed to Hara Mototatsu]**

*Kururu toshi*
*kuru toshi*
*wanu*
*mi ni areba*
*kokoro ni kakaru*
*kasumi to mo nashi*

(Since the passing year and the coming year are alike things that no longer concern this body of mine — set apart from the world as I am — there is nothing that hangs upon my heart like a veil of spring haze.)

*(The poem conveys a state of serene detachment from the passage of time and worldly affairs, suggesting that a man wholly devoted to the cause of revenge has freed his heart from all other anxieties.)*
