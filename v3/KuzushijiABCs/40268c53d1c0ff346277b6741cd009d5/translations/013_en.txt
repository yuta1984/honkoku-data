## [Right Page]

**Kohei**〽 "…What a joy this must be for you — I have been saying as much, and thinking how lonely it must feel at your age, I have been speaking of it with others too. But tell me — which name on this list is your son's? And while we're at it, what is his full formal name?"

**Jūbei**〽 "Well, that's just it — I had been so looking forward to pointing to it and saying 'there, that's him,' and yet no matter how many times I look through it, my son's name is nowhere to be found…"

**Kohei**〽 "What? Surely it must be there. How is his name written? What is your young master's full name?"

**Jūbei**〽 "His formal name is Oyamada Shōzaemon."

**Kohei**〽 "What?! *That* Oyamada Shōzaemon?!"

**Jūbei**〽 "My, what an extraordinary reaction. What is the matter with my son's name? He may be left off this list, but — could it be that there is news of him dying in battle?"

## [Left Page]

**Kohei**〽 "No, no — it is nothing of the sort, but…"

**Jūbei**〽 "No, no — clearly something is the matter. If you have heard any rumor about my son, whether good or ill, please — tell this old father of his."

**Kohei**〽 "I have heard nothing of any particular account, I assure you—"

Just at that moment, the back door slid quietly open, and in slipped a candy seller. It was Gosuke, a manservant who had formerly been in service to Jūbei and his son.

**Gosuke**〽 "Master, how are you keeping today? Your illness — has it improved?"

**Jūbei**〽 "Oh, Gosuke! Hard at work even in this cold, I see. Though I imagine the cold makes it hard to eke out a living as you'd like. But never mind that — this business that's been going around lately: have you heard what people are saying about it?"
