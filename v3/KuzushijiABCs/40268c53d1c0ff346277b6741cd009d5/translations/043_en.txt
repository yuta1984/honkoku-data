Having sought out (the floor plan) and sent it over, they cross-referenced it with the diagram for verification, and only after confirming there were no errors did they make use of it. Thus Ōboshi traveled east (to Edo) and issued instructions for the arrangements in various quarters. One day, addressing Okano Sanjūrō, he said:

Ōboshi: "Now then, Okano, what arrangements have you made for the settlement of your wife?"

Okano Sanjūrō: "Well, no — as you know, I have not yet taken a wife. Why do you ask such a thing?"

Ōboshi: "My, my — is that so? The detailed intelligence you provided concerning Moronao's residence — through whose connections did you obtain it? Even if you did not live together with her,

**[Left folio]**

you made a vow and promised to eventually become husband and wife — that lie told for the sake of our lord, one cannot call it a grave sin, yet she, with a woman's heart, has brooded deeply over it. At the appointed place, he will face either ritual suicide or death in battle — one of two fates — and if she were to reach that moment without knowing, what a shock it would be for her. It is not something that should be done by paying for a person's sincerity with money, but at least for the sake of that woman's peace of mind —"

And with that, he handed over twenty ryō in gold. Okano was greatly pleased, and using this as an allowance for the woman, (Ōboshi) said that until she departed from her home province next spring, whatever expenses arose, she was welcome to spend every last coin without worry.
