Ahead of him walked a girl of seventeen or eighteen, her hair done up in a *shimada* coiffure, her whole bearing stylish and graceful. Shōzaemon followed behind her, and the more he looked, the more it came back to him — it was not so long ago, in the early days of the third month, that a go-between had approached him about taking her as his wife. The arrangements had all but been settled, and they were in the midst of choosing an auspicious day for the wedding, when the catastrophe that befell the household scattered everything to the winds, and since then not a word had passed between them — that girl with whom fate had thus severed him. To say she merely *resembled* her seemed too weak — could it really be her? He quickened his pace, overtook her, and turned back to look at her face. It was indeed the very likeness of the woman he had promised to make his wife, and she was more beautiful than ever. The young woman, too, happened to glance up and meet his eyes, then turned her face away in bashful embarrassment. The hand that lifted her *kozuma* was delicate and graceful; her legs — glimpsed beneath her skirts — were whiter than the flurrying snowflakes, and about them was wrapped a *yumaki*

of crimson *chirimen* crepe. Was it a burning passion, or the wind of love, that sent a shiver through him at the sight of her bare feet in their *koma-geta*? The reins of Shōzaemon's heart — was it karma, was it an ill-fated bond, that made them slacken so? Released from his restraint, he followed the same road for some three *chō*, until, somewhere in the middle of an ordinary row of townhouses, he came upon a residence — not exactly grand, yet not entirely ordinary either. Its frontage spanned three *ken*, with a black wall of two *ken*, and within one *ken* a latticed wicket gate. A stone step of four *shaku* led to a box stair of two *shaku*. Inside the wall, a small garden was pleasingly laid out with a *mikoshi* pine arching overhead; a second-floor room had a verandah on two sides, its railing fashioned in decorative bamboo work.
