【Left folio】

Sanjūrō: "Yes, I humbly ask that you see to everything on my behalf."

Heibē: "Here, have a look at this—I have this many architectural drawings from jobs I took on since I was young."

Sanjūrō: "My, indeed—quite a collection! Ah, a good many of these are for samurai residences, aren't they."

Heibē: "That's right. With residences for warrior households, there are all sorts of exacting specifications quite unlike those for townspeople's buildings, you see—"

While talking thus, Sanjūrō notices one particular drawing and picks it up.

Sanjūrō: "Well now—this one here is Kōno Moronao's——"

Heibē: "That's a preliminary plan I drew at a colleague's request when his current residence was being built."

Sanjūrō: "Is that so? So even now it is built exactly according to this plan——"

Heibē: "Starting with the entrance hall, the inner chambers, the front reception rooms, the main palace——"
