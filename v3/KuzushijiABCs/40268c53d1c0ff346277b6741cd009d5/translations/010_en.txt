## *Iroha Bunko*, Volume One — A True and Authentic Account

*Composed and edited by Kyōkuntei Shujin of the Eastern Capital (Edo)*

**Chapter One: The Account of Oyamada Shōzaemon, together with Naosuke and Gonbei**

---

🎵 *(The cry of a yomiuri broadsheet seller)*

"Come and read the full account of the vendetta — come and read! A vendetta the like of which has rarely been seen in all of history! Read here the full account of how, on this recent occasion, the forty-seven rōnin of the House of En'ya launched a night raid upon the House of Kō and struck down the enemy of their lord!"

— No sooner did people hear the voice of the broadsheet seller crying out these details, going from street to street, high district and low, than figures came stepping out from beneath the eaves of this house and that, and the would-be buyers, jostling and competing with one another, rushed forward each trying to be first —
