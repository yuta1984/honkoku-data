　　　　　Chapter Four

Oshizu's uncle in Mikoyama-chō was a carpenter by the name of Heibē. Having recently passed on his guild membership to an apprentice, he now enjoyed the leisurely work of a retired craftsman—his semi-recreational approach to the trade being well known to others. A veteran carpenter skilled in architectural drafting, he had long been a master builder renowned for his expertise in floor plans and spatial layouts. Having returned from the worksite, he appeared to have something requiring attention, and was spreading out numerous architectural drawings, comparing one against another. Just then, Sanjūrō descended from the upstairs and turned to Heibē:

Sanjūrō: "My goodness—I dozed right off up there. Has Oshizu already——"

Heibē: "Why, she just got back while you were—my, you certainly——"
