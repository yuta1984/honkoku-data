"Don't try to sneak away — come here," he insists, forcing (Shōzaemon) to wash his feet. Just then, the young woman he had been taken with arrives carrying a hand towel.

Girl: "Here, please dry yourself with this. Here, let me wipe them for you."

Shōzaemon: "No, no, not at all — I couldn't possibly impose..."

Girl: "Would it bring divine punishment upon me to touch your honorable feet?" she says with a smile.

At this moment, Shōzaemon's heart goes pit-a-pat.

*(His wife urges Shōzaemon along and sends him to the reception room.)*

Wife: "Well then, Shō-san, enjoy yourself. I'll just pop over to the bathhouse."

Shōzaemon: "Yes, yes, please take your time."

*(Saying this, he proceeds to the reception room together with the host.)*

Ei(jirō?): "Right then! Someone bring plenty of charcoal. And right away, put the sake on to warm, and prepare some *nabeyaki* or a flat-dish meal. Come now, come now, over to the brazier —"
