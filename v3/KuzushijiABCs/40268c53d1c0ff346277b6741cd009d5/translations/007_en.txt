## Ōboshi Rikiya (大星力弥)　Personal name: Yoshikane (良金)

**[Right folio]**

Yoshikane followed his father Yoshio down to Kamakura, keeping his resolve as unyielding as gold and iron, and at last fulfilled the long-cherished wish of revenge. He was sixteen years of age at the time. His wisdom and courage surpassed those of the men around him, and his reputation for handsome looks was widely known.

Together with his comrade-in-arms Yato Norikane, he chastised the greed, misconduct, and villainy of Ono Kudayū and his son — those father and son who had served the enemy — and rectified their disloyalty and faithlessness. Was it Heaven's will that this deed should be accomplished by the hands of these two fine young men?

---

**[Left folio — Waka poem, attributed to Ōboshi Yoshikane]**

*Gokuraku no*
*michi wa hitosuji*
*kimi to tomo*
*Amida wo soete*
*shijūhachi-nin*

(The path to paradise is one and singular — together with our lord, joining our prayers to Amida Buddha's forty-eight vows, we forty-eight men walk it as one.)
