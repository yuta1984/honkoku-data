(Continued from previous page) Heibē: "——naturally, right down to the construction of the entire tenement row, in almost every respect it follows that very plan. The builder was my fellow apprentice from way back."

Sanjūrō: "Then this is truly a gift bestowed by Heaven——"

Heibē: "Eh? What do you mean?"

Sanjūrō: "Ahahaha—that was just me mimicking a line from the theatre!"

He laughs it off to cover himself; but in truth, in the days prior, using the pretense of a love affair, he had already gathered from that woman Oshizu a rough account of the layout of Kōno's residence, and had committed it to memory. Now, comparing what he remembered against this floor plan and working out the method of construction in his mind, he had formed a complete scheme—and, turning to Heibē, said:

Sanjūrō: "Excuse me, Uncle—might you give me two or three of these drawings?"

Heibē: "What do you intend to do with them?"

Sanjūrō: "Oh, you see, since I was a child I have always had a fondness for pictures and drawings of all kinds——

【Left folio】

——and so I have amassed quite a collection of Hishikawa's prints as well."

Heibē: "Oh, is that so? In that case I'll gladly give you plenty—help yourself to whichever ones you like. However, please leave this particular drawing behind. This is a plan that really ought not to be shown to others—truth be told, I had intended to burn it. It is not something to be shown around."

Sanjūrō: "Yes, this one, you say? Why, this is surely the residence of Lord Kōno, right across from where I live——"

Heibē: "Well, I suppose there's no great harm in that—but the rōnin of the Enyа household, bearing a grudge over their lord's death, are on the lookout for vengeance, and there are fears they may come seeking revenge. It's out of caution against that—that the layout of the residence should not be let out to the world——"
