"Others also urged me that it would be better to go out to work, and thinking it would help my parents, I came here to live."

Shō: "Oh my, oh my, is that so? How pitiful. So then, have you borrowed money from this household?"

Yasu: "Yes, well, that's true, but they do treat me with such kindness——"

As they talked, the cold pierced the skin like needles, and O-Yasu shivered involuntarily.

Yasu: "Oh, it's gotten cold."

She pulled a hand-warming brazier close.

Shō: "Indeed, it's grown cold enough to chill you to the bone. I'm warm from the sake, but you must be terribly——"

【Left page】

"——cold. I'll move over to one end, so even if you're reluctant, it'll help keep out the cold. Come, lie down here."

Yasu: "Thank you kindly. But I do feel bad imposing on you."

Shō: "It is I who should feel bad, but if you were to catch a cold, I'd feel all the more unable to face O-Fuyu. Now, now then——"

He reached for her hand, but O-Yasu was in no mood for such flirtation. Still innocent of men, she fidgeted shyly at a man's side — appearing naïve and endearing. Shōzaemon's heart was carried away, and in the end he forgot his solemn oath, sworn as firm as gold and iron, and gave in to his carnal desires. And so the next day, after the sake's intoxication had worn off, his conscience was filled with regret——
