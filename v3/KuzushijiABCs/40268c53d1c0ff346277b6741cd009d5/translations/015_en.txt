A slow-witted but honest fellow, with no forethought or consideration whatsoever.

Go: "Well now! In order to avenge their lord, forty-seven men assembled together and launched a night raid on the mansion of Kō no Moronao — this is no mere rumor of a great deed! It just so happened that I had been asked to carry a letter all the way to Kanasogi, and upon my return, coming from the other direction, I witnessed the splendid sight of them marching away, having surrounded and taken the head of their enemy. 'Loyal retainers!' 'Men of righteousness!' 'Heroes!' — the likes of which have never been heard of since Japan's beginning — people even stopped in the streets. Oh my, what an extraordinary reputation they have earned!"

Shige: "Well, well! So you happened to be heading that way and saw the men of the household one by one — how fortunate. There may well be accounts of each one's deeds, yet...

Go: "Indeed, that is just the matter. As you know, I am not exactly a brave man, but the moment I heard what was happening, I realized the young master must surely be among the party, and so, trembling with fear, I hurried close to get a look.
