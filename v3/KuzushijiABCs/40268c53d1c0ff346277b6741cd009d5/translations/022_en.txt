Out stepped the master of the house — himself a rōnin of the Enya clan. In former days, he had served as the treasurer (*kane-yaku*) at the Kamakura residence, and when the calamity befell the house, he had seized the opportunity to embezzle a considerable sum of money. Using that as his starting capital, he now kept a stable of women trained in the arts of entertainment, sending them to work as serving girls at restaurants and pocketing their earnings as his trade. He also lent money in secret, accumulating heavy interest, and had come to live quite comfortably. His true name was Tamamushi Ben'emon, but he had taken the alias Tamamura Eiji and was living here in hiding.

**Eiji:** "Well, well — what a rare sight! Shō-san, what brings you here? Come, come — get yourself in here!"

**Shō:** "My, my — Tamamushi-shi, it has been quite some time since we last parted. I trust you are in good health —"

**Woman:** "Oh, goodness, so formal!"

**Eiji:** "Come now, come now, step on in.

What on earth are you being so shy about? This isn't some stranger's house — it's mine! Now then — straw sandals, chest guard, leg wrappings, sword-hilt cover — you look just like a courier, I'll say!"

So saying, he called out toward the back of the house:

**Eiji:** "Hey! Hey! Bring some hot water from the copper boiler over to that basin, would you?"

**Shō:** "No, no, I really cannot stay. I have a rather urgent matter today and must call on several places."

**Eiji:** "Oh, don't be so tedious! In this cold, after all this time apart — sit down and have a bite of whatever's on hand, have a little drink to warm yourself up before you go.
