## Society Magic Lantern: One Hundred Selections, One Hundred Laughs
### A Swindler's Comic Tale — by Kotsukawa Dōjin

**[Guest]** Now then, Mr. Yokubari, I hear that you have some grand business venture planned regarding Taiwan, our newly acquired territory. As it happens, I myself am not entirely without similar ideas — in fact, I have recently been in discussion with Mr. Kanetsuruji about the matter. However, rather than acting independently, it would surely be more effective to work together. If you have no objection, I'd very much like you to let me in for a half-share.

**[Mr. Yokubari]** Well, since you've seen straight through me like that, there's no use hiding it, so I shall tell you. When it comes to making money, there's nothing like undeveloped land, I say. The opportunities now coming my way are, first of all, the manufacture of camphor and sugar; then the improvement of roads and the laying of railways. There are various other things besides, but the four major enterprises I intend to get started on right away are these. Since you are fortunately on good terms with Mr. Kanetsuruji, by all means take this map along and discuss the matter thoroughly with him. Of course, should you be in agreement, I would be happy to show you the plans and budget estimates as well.

**[Guest]** How gratifying that you've agreed so promptly! Still, whatever else one might say, the first thing needed is capital — as for that side of things...

**[Mr. Yokubari]** Ha! If you're going to come at me with that amateur approach, there's no money to be had!
