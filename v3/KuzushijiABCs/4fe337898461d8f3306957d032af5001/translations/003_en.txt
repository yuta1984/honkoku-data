【Title: A Picture of the Battle of Tabaruzaka】

Since the rebels from Kagoshima Prefecture invaded Kumamoto Prefecture, there have been battles in various places; however, these amounted to nothing more than minor skirmishes — pursuing and retreating, striking and being struck in return — and the reason there had been no decisive large-scale engagement was that the Imperial forces had not yet assembled in sufficient numbers. Since the 25th of last month, the Imperial troops have advanced steadily, with the rebel forces retreating day by day. However, as Tabaruzaka is the key route leading to Kumamoto, the rebel soldiers gathered and massed here, constructing breastworks at this strategically difficult terrain to mount their defense. Consequently, as the Imperial forces sought to seize this position in one stroke, the battle is said to have become extraordinarily fierce.
