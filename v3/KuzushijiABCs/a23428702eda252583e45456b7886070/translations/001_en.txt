**Shin-Yoshiwara-chō: Temporary Lodging Locations — Asakusa Section**

【Upper Section】

*Title: O-Namazu (The Great Catfish)*　　　Transmitted directly by Tokiwa Ju-Muji-tayū

〽︎ Now then, the rampage of the catfish, crushed under the weight of ten-thousand debts, brought disaster in every direction, leaving thousands to perish unsaved, adding sorrow upon sorrow beyond all precedent in history. In the season of spring's fury, the sky suddenly darkened, and the earth shook again and again. People rushed to shelter behind small thickets to protect themselves from collapsing storehouses and walls. At that time, the streets of each neighborhood were reduced to ruin — floor joists snapped, doors piled upon one another, the eaves of houses blocked — and when even the roof beams could no longer be supported, people met their wretched end, dying meaningless deaths. It is said that this is why the catfish is so feared. And thus, the people who lament their misfortune, blaming themselves even for such misdirected writings, receive only five *gō* of rice as relief from the lord's benevolence, while the dust of crumbling old walls never ceases to fall. The relentless upheaval of heaven and earth — boom, boom, boom — and how pitiful are those struck down by the earth of the lord's granary.

**2nd day of the 10th month, Ansei 2 (1855), year of the Kinoto-U (乙卯)**
