**Kettai (Obi-binding Ceremony)**  《This refers to the binding of the stomach band (haraоbi) in the fifth month of pregnancy. Also called "Chakutai."》

First, when a woman becomes pregnant, an auspicious day in the fifth month is chosen for the binding of the sash (obi). 【Note: The character for "懐" (pregnancy) is written with the female radical (女偏).】
At that time, the hikime and no (ceremonial arrows) are to be raised up,
and an elder female attendant (rōjo) is to be appointed.
Furthermore, the sash should be requested from a woman who has been blessed with many descendants, and that woman is designated the "obi-oya" (sash-parent/godmother).
