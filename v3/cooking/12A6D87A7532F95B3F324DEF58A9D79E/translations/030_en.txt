【Recipe 50】Method for Dressed Dried Daikon Strips

Cut dried daikon as-is into thin round slices, place them in a stacked container or a bowl, pour boiling water over them, and after a while, squeeze them well. If they are still tough, blanch briefly in hot water. Then dress them with sansho (Japanese pepper) miso mixed with sesame. If fish ingredients are to be included, add herring roe and black sesame for a "black dressing." For the black dressing, pot-soot is used.
