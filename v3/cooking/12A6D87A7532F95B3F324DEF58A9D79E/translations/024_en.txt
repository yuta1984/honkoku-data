【Entry 39】How to Make Daikon Moromi-zuke (Daikon Pickled in Sake Lees)
First, this is not limited to thin daikon; any daikon can be used for moromi-zuke when you are in a hurry. Cut the daikon to an appropriate size — whether into small pieces or any other shape — briefly dip in lukewarm water, quickly remove it, sprinkle generously with salt, place in a donburi bowl, sprinkle a little sake over it, place a small stone on top as a weight, leave it until properly balanced, then dress immediately with moromi and serve. Even eaten right away, the flavor is exceptional. This method is suitable not only in winter but at any time of year.
