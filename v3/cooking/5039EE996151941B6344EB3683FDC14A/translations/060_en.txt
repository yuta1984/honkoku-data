　　　　Notes on How to Make Surin-shu (Black Soybean Sake)

1.　To 1 shō of sake, add 2 gō and 9 shaku of black soybeans, half of which have been
　　decocted. Place in either a jar or a flask and leave to infuse for about 20 days, after
　　which it will be well made. When the time seems right, remove the beans and draw off
　　the sake. That is all.

　　　　On the Method for Making Amazake (Sweet Fermented Rice Drink)

1.　Split and steam glutinous rice (mochigome), then allow to cool.
1.　Walnut tree bark: one handful
1.　Lacquer tree bark: one handful
【Left page】
　　　　On How to Decoct the Above Two Ingredients

1.　For 1 shō of split rice, add 3 shō of water and decoct down to 2 shō,
　　then allow the liquid to cool.
　　　　On the Method of Preparation

1.　For 1 shō of split rice, use 4 shō of kōji (rice malt). Combine the kōji with the
　　decocted liquid made from the walnut bark and kokashi (roasted) bark, add salt as
　　well, and then add the split rice into that liquid to begin the fermentation.
1.　In winter, if it is needed in the morning, prepare and pack it in the evening.
1.　In summer, if it is needed in the morning, prepare and pack it at the time of the
　　first cock's crow (just before dawn).
