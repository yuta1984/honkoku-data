　　　　Method for Kokashi — transmitted by Yamamoto Ichiunin □

1.　Toshi-hoshii (year-dried cooked rice): 100 monme. Regular rice or black rice both work well.
　　On the method of roasting the above rice: Roast it so that not a single grain is scorched.
　　The right degree is when each grain has turned dark and glistens with a sweating sheen —
　　that is the proper measure.
1.　Chinpi (dried citrus peel): 20 monme.
　　　Roast to the degree that it is gently baked through.
1.　Yokuinin (Job's tears / Coix seed): Same as above.
1.　Sansho (Japanese pepper): 15 monme. A little □□□□□
　　　The above: 22nd day of the 2nd month (U/Rabbit month), Tenna 3rd year (1683), year of the Boar.

　　　Received from Raihokuji (temple).
　　　　On the Method of Making Nintozu-shu (Honeysuckle Sake)

1.　Honeysuckle (nindō) flowers: 5 shō
　　　Fresh (unprocessed)
1.　Imushi flowers: 5 shō
　　　Fresh (unprocessed)
1.　Kōji made from glutinous rice, prepared as white rice — also 5 shō
1.　Superior white glutinous rice: 1 tō, steamed
1.　Shōchū (distilled spirits): 2 tō
　　　Steam the honeysuckle, imushi, and kōji together, then sprinkle/mix into the cooked rice—
