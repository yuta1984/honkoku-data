　　　　Recipe for Yōei-shu (Nourishing Medicinal Wine) — received in writing from Matsuda I[...]:
1.　High-quality aged sake
1.　Angelica root (tōki), cinnamon bark (nikkei), corydalis tuber (engosaku): 1 ryō each
1.　Sichuan chinaberry (senrenshi), pitted: 2 monme; clove (chōji): 5 bu
　　Chop all of the above as one would prepare decocted medicine, place in the sake, and let
　　stand for 3 toki (approx. 6 hours) over 10 days. Then remove, press and discard the lees,
　　filter, and use.
　Indications: stroke (chūfū), beriberi (kakke), numbness of the feet, □ hernia (senki),
　various parasitic conditions, and swellings that do not come to a head.


　　　　Method for Morning Pickles — received in writing from Nakamura Shinzaemon
【Left page】
1.　Daikon radishes, mixed large and small: 150
1.　Salt: from a measure of 3 tō
1.　Rice malt (kōji): approximately 5 shō
　　Pickle the above daikon in one barrel. Sprinkle salt and kōji over each layer as you
　　arrange them.


　　　　Method for Kokashi (Spiced Pickling Powder)
1.　Rice: 2 monme　　Sansho pepper: 1 monme　　Dried citrus peel (chinpi): 5 bu
　　　Roast all three of the above.
1.　Fennel (uikiyo): 3 bu — as is (unroasted)
1.　Coix seed / Job's tears (yokuinin): 3 bu — lightly roasted
