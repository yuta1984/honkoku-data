The Fundamentals of Western Cuisine:
　　　An Illustration of the Three Grades of Stock

【Left Page】

First, divide one kin [approx. 600g] of sōmen noodles into four portions, cut to a length of about two sun [approx. 6 cm], and boil in hot water for half an hour [approx. one hour]. Add butter to the size of a thumb, and one handful of salt, and cook together until well done. Remove the liquid, then place the sōmen as-is into the third-grade white stock, bring to a boil, and simmer for about four and a half hours.
(Note: This quantity is sufficient to serve six persons.)

○ Eighth-Grade Soup ["Green Pea Soup" — meaning soup of podded peas]

Ingredients:
- Green peas (peas only, shelled): 9 gō [approx. 1.62 litres by volume]
- Butter: one quarter of one kin [approx. 150g]
- Salt-cured pork (salt pork)
- Spring onion/leek: 3
- Lettuce: 4
- Spinach: 1 handful
- Sugar: 1 lump
- First-grade dark stock: 2 quarts
- Additionally: 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres]
