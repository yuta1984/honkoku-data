This map has been compiled based upon the survey baselines of the late Master Inō [Tadataka], correcting the positions of provincial and district boundaries as well as mountains, rivers, and roads. Furthermore, maps from the various domains and prefectures collected since the Meiji Restoration have been gathered, and scholars of geography assembled at the Jishū Gijuku [academy] have followed Western cartographic methods to produce one sheet per province. Alongside these, maps of major cities and notable towns are included, with no detail omitted down to the finest subdivisions. The result admirably captures the essential information required. Among maps of our country, none more detailed than this has yet been seen. We humbly request that all learned persons who wish to deepen their knowledge of geography purchase and enjoy this work.
