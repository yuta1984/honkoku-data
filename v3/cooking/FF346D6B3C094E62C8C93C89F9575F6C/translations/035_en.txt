　　　**Chapter 2: Fish**

○ Item No. 21 — ["Boiled Cod Fish" — a method for cooking cod]

Soak the cod thoroughly in water; salt: one quarter of one *kin* (approx. 150g); water: one *shō* eight *gō* (approx. 1.8 litres)

　　　**Method of Preparation**

First, wash the cod thoroughly, using salt to wash away all contents from inside the belly, then leave it soaking in water for approximately one hour (*hitotoki*). Next, fill a pot sufficiently with water, place the cod into it, and simmer over a low flame for half [an hour]……
