**[Right folio]**
(Continued from previous page) Discard the egg whites and use only the yolks. First-grade black stock: 6 gō.

The above-listed ingredients are to be prepared in the following manner.

First, peel the cucumber, cut it into quarters lengthwise, remove the pulp and seeds, slice it thinly, and arrange on a plate. Sprinkle a little salt over it and squeeze out the moisture, then place it together with butter into a pot and warm over a gentle flame until steam begins to rise. After that, add one quart of first-grade stock and simmer over a low flame for one hour. Once it is well cooked, transfer to a separate vessel. At this point, beat the egg yolks and pour them over the top.
*(This recipe serves eight people.)*

○ **Sixth-grade soup** ["Shurien Sūpu" — a French term (i.e., *Julienne*)]

**[Left folio]**
Carrot, turnip, spring onion, and lettuce (*chisa*) — one of each; first-grade black stock.

The above ingredients are to be prepared as follows.

First, shave the carrot diagonally as one would shave burdock root, yielding about half a pint. Shave the spring onion diagonally to yield about one-quarter of a pint. Shave the lettuce in the same manner. Then bring two quarts of the first-grade stock to a boil, add all of the above ingredients, and simmer for one and a half hours. Skim off the scum and thereafter serve at the table.
*(This recipe serves six people.)*
