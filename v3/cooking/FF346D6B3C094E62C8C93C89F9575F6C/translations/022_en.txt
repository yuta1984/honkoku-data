(Continued from previous page: Fifth-Grade Soup — "Cucumber Soup")

Discard the egg whites and use only the yolks. First-grade dark stock: 6 gō [approx. 1.08 litres].

　　　The above various ingredients are to be prepared in the following manner:

First, peel the cucumber, quarter it lengthwise, remove the flesh and seeds, and slice thinly. Arrange on a plate, sprinkle with a little salt to draw out the moisture, then squeeze out the liquid. Place in a pot together with the butter and warm over a gentle flame until steam begins to rise. Then add one quart of the first-grade stock and simmer for one hour. Once well cooked over a low flame, transfer to a separate vessel. At this point, beat the egg yolks and pour them over the soup.
(Note: This quantity is sufficient to serve eight persons.)

○ Sixth-Grade Soup ["Julienne Soup" — a French term]

【Left Page】

Ingredients:
- Carrot: 1
- Turnip: 1
- Spring onion/leek: 1
- Lettuce: 1
One of each. First-grade dark stock.

　　　The above various ingredients are to be prepared in the following manner:

First, shave the carrot into thin diagonal slices as one would shave burdock root, to a quantity of about half a pint. Shave the onion into thin diagonal slices, to about one-eighth of a pint [lit. "half of a quarter of a pint"]. Shave the lettuce in the same manner as above. Then, bring two quarts of the first-grade dark stock to a boil, add all the aforementioned ingredients, and simmer for one and a half hours. Skim off the scum, and thereafter serve at table.
(Note: This quantity is sufficient to serve six persons.)
