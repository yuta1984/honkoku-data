　The above various ingredients are then prepared in the following manner:

First, peel the cucumber, cut it into quarters lengthwise, remove the inner flesh and seeds, slice it thinly, and arrange it on a plate. Sprinkle a little salt over it and squeeze out the liquid, then place it together with the butter into a pot and warm it over a low flame, taking the rising of steam as your cue. Thereafter, add one cup (quart measure) of the first-grade stock, and simmer over a low flame for one hour until well cooked, then transfer to a separate vessel. At this point, beat the egg yolks and pour them over the top. (This serves eight persons.)

○ Sixth-Grade Soup ["Julienne Soup" — a French term]

【Left page】
・Carrot, turnip, leek, and lettuce — one of each　　First-grade dark stock

　The above various ingredients are prepared in the following manner:

First, shave the carrot diagonally into thin slices, as one would shave burdock root, to make approximately half a pint in volume. Shave the leek diagonally to make approximately one quarter of a pint in volume. One head of lettuce, shaved in the same manner as above. Then take two cups (quart measure) of first-grade stock; first bring the dark stock to a boil, add all of the foregoing ingredients, and simmer for one and a half hours. Skim off the scum, and then serve. (This serves six persons.)
