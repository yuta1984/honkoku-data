○ Soup No. 19 [Eel Soup — meaning eel broth]

Eel: 3 kin; spring onion: 1 stalk; butter: 8/10 of a kin (approx. 800 g); powdered cinnamon: 1/64 of a kin; nutmeg, pepper, and salt: all to taste; wheat flour: 1/4 of a gō-masu; No. 17 fish stock: 1 shō 2 gō.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

Wash the eel and cut into pieces of just over 2 sun (approximately 7 cm), place in a pot together with the butter, simmer for ten minutes, then add the No. 17 fish stock and the spring onion—

[Left folio]

—finely chopped, along with the remaining ingredients, into the pot and mix together, simmering for approximately one and a half hours.

○ Soup No. 20 [Oyster Soup — meaning oyster broth]

Shucked oysters: 70 in number; No. 3 white soup stock: 1 shō 2 gō; salt, red pepper, and cinnamon: all to taste.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

Remove the shells from the oysters and place the meat and its liquor as-is into the pot and bring to a boil. In a separate vessel, bring the No. 3 white stock to a boil, then squeeze in only the oyster liquor. Simmer over a low flame for approximately half an hour.
