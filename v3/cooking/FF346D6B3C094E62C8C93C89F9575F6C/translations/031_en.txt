○ Nineteenth-Grade Soup ["Eel Soup" — meaning eel broth]

3 kin [approx. 1.8 kg] of eel, 1 stalk of spring onion [negi], 7/8 of 1 kin [approx. 525 g] of butter, 1/64 of 1 kin [approx. 10 g] of powdered cinnamon [keishi], nutmeg, pepper, and salt each to taste, 1/4 of 1 gō-masu [approx. 45 ml] of wheat flour, and 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres] of the seventeenth-grade fish stock.

Prepare all of the above ingredients as follows:

Wash the eel thoroughly, cut into pieces slightly over 2 sun [approx. 6 cm] in length, place together with butter into a pot, and simmer for ten minutes, then add the seventeenth-grade broth and the spring onion...

...finely chopped, together with all other ingredients, into the pot, mix well, and simmer for approximately one and a half hitotoki [about three hours].

○ Twentieth-Grade Soup ["Oyster Soup" — meaning oyster broth]

70 shucked oysters, 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres] of third-grade white stock, salt, red pepper, and cinnamon each to taste.

Prepare all of the above ingredients as follows:

Remove the shells from the oysters and place the flesh and juices as they are into a pot and bring to a boil. In a separate vessel, bring the third-grade white stock to a boil, then squeeze in only the oyster liquor. Simmer over a low flame for approximately half a hitotoki [about one hour].
