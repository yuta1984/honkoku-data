○ Nineteenth-Grade Soup ["Eel Soup" — meaning eel broth]

Eel: 3 kin [approx. 1.8 kg]; spring onion: 1 stalk; butter: eight-tenths of 1 kin [approx. 600 g]; powdered cinnamon: one sixty-fourth of 1 kin [approx. 9.4 g]; nutmeg, pepper, and salt: all to taste; wheat flour: one quarter of 1 gō-masu [approx. 45 ml]; Seventeenth-Grade fish stock: 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres].

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

Wash the eel, cut into pieces slightly over 2 sun [approx. 6 cm] in length, place together with the butter into a pot, boil for ten minutes [minutt], then add the Seventeenth-Grade broth and the spring onion —

— finely chopped, along with the remaining ingredients, into the pot. Mix together and simmer for approximately one and a half hitotoki [about three hours].

○ Twentieth-Grade Soup ["Oyster Soup" — meaning oyster broth]

Oysters, shelled: 70 pieces; Third-Grade white stock: 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres]; salt, red pepper, and cinnamon: all to taste.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

Remove the shells from the oysters, place only the meat and their natural liquid as-is into a pot and bring to a boil. In a separate vessel, bring the Third-Grade white stock to a boil, then squeeze in only the oyster liquid. Simmer over a low flame for approximately half a hitotoki [about one hour].
