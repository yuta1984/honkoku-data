○Recipe No. 26　"Baked Mackerel" (meaning: broiled/baked mackerel)

　Ground pepper, salt, a little oil, "parsley" (a variety of *seri*/water parsley), yuzu juice, butter

　　　Method of Preparation:

Remove the innards of the mackerel, wipe the belly and back well with a cloth, split open the back, and pour in ground pepper, salt, and oil. Then, broil for 10 minutes (*miniūto*), turning repeatedly throughout. Finely chop the "parsley," mix it with butter, add a little pepper, yuzu juice, and salt, mix together, and place back into the split back of the fish. Additionally, prepare melted butter in a separate vessel to serve alongside. Whether to use it or not is left to each person's preference.

○Recipe No. 29　"Boiled Salmon" (meaning: simmered fresh salmon)

　4.5 *kin* of salt, 1 *shō* 8 *gō* of water. The amounts of water and salt should be adjusted according to the size of the salmon; however, the proportions should be maintained at a ratio of 4.5 *kin* to 1 *shō* 8 *gō*.
