○Recipe No. 22　"Baked Ут Soup" (meaning: baked cod)

Leftover cold cod from the previous day, oysters — 12 of each; melted butter; mashed potatoes — one plateful

　　　Method of Preparation:

Layer the cod, oysters, butter, and mashed potatoes in order into a baking dish. Then place into a baking pan and bake for approximately half a *toki* (about one hour). Note that beforehand, the fish must have its innards carefully removed along with the bones, the oysters must be shelled, and the cod must be thinly sliced.
