○Recipe No. 22　"Baked Ut[ter] Soup" (Method for Baked Cod)

　Leftover cooked cod and oysters from the previous day, 12 each; melted butter; one serving of mashed potato on a plate.

　　　Method of Preparation for the Above

1. Fill a baking dish with the cod, oysters, butter, and potato in order, then place into a baking pan and roast for approximately half an hour [one hour]. Note that at the outset, the fish should have its entrails carefully removed together with the bones, the oysters should be shelled, and the cod should be sliced thinly.
