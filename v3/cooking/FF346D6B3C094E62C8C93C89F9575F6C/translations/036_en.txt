**No. 22 — "Baked Fish Soup" (meaning: baked cod)**

**Ingredients:**
Leftover cold cod and oysters from the previous day, twelve of each; melted butter; one serving of mashed potatoes (Java potatoes) on a plate.

**Method:**
Layer the cod, oysters, butter, and potatoes in order into a baking dish. Then place everything into a roasting pan and bake for approximately thirty minutes. Note that at the outset, the fish should be carefully cleaned of its entrails together with the bones; the oysters should be shelled; and the cod should be sliced thinly.
