○Recipe No. 24　"Boiled Flounders" (Method for Boiled Sole/Flounder)

　Soak the sole in water, with one quarter kin of salt, 1 shō 8 gō of water, and vinegar to taste, for approximately three days.

　　　Method of Preparation for the Above

1. Add enough water to submerge the fish completely, and simmer over a low flame for four and a half hours. Melted butter should be placed in a dish and served alongside. Whether to use the butter or not is left to the individual's preference.

○Recipe No. 25　"Fried Flounders" (Method for Fried Sole/Flounder)

　Egg, pork lard, sole/flounder.

　　　Method of Preparation for the Above

1. Remove the entrails of the sole, wipe the inside of the belly with salt, wash with cold water, and wipe thoroughly dry with cotton cloth or linen. Beat an egg in a pot, briefly soak the sole in water, then immediately remove it, crumble and sprinkle breadcrumbs over the fish, and place it into boiling lard. For approximately ten minutes, repeatedly turn the fish in the pan, browning both belly and back without interruption. When ready to serve, arrange "parsley" around the rim of the plate——
