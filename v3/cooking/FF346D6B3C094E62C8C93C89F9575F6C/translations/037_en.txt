**No. 24 — "Boiled Plaundrous" (meaning: boiled sole/flounder)**

**Ingredients:**
Sole, soaked in water; one quarter of one kin of salt (approx. 60 g); 1 shō 8 gō of water (approx. 3.24 litres); vinegar to taste (approximately 3 shaku).

**Method:**
Add enough water to submerge the fish and simmer over a low flame for approximately four and a half hours. Melted butter should be placed in a dish and served alongside. Whether or not to use the butter is left to each person's preference.

**No. 25 — "Fried Plaundrous" (meaning: fried sole/flounder)**

**Ingredients:**
Eggs, lard (pork fat), sole.

**Method:**
Remove the entrails of the sole, wipe the inside of the belly with salt, wash with cold water, and pat dry thoroughly with cotton cloth or linen. Beat an egg in a pan, briefly soak the sole in water, immediately remove it, coat it with breadcrumbs (bread crumbs made by crumbling the crumb of a loaf), then drop it into boiling lard and fry for approximately ten minutes, turning it continuously in the pan so that both the belly and back sides are evenly cooked.
