○Recipe No. 24　"Boiled Flounders" (meaning: boiled flatfish/sole)

Soak the sole in water with one quarter *kin* of salt, 1 *shō* 8 *gō* (approximately 3.24 liters) of water, and a measured amount of vinegar, for approximately 3 days.

　　　Method of Preparation:

Add enough water to submerge the fish, and simmer over a low flame for four and a half hours. Melted butter should be placed in a dish and served alongside. Whether to use it or not is left to each person's preference.

○Recipe No. 25　"Fried Flounders" (meaning: fried flatfish/sole)

Eggs, lard (pork fat), sole

　　　Method of Preparation:

Remove the innards from the sole, wipe the inside of the belly with salt, wash with cold water, and dry thoroughly with cotton cloth or linen. Beat the eggs well in a pan, briefly soak the sole in water, immediately remove it, crumble and sprinkle breadcrumbs (the crumb of bread) over it, and drop it into boiling lard. Fry for approximately 10 minutes (*miniūto*), turning it continuously in the pan, roasting and browning both the belly and back sides. When ready to serve, arrange "parsley" around the rim of the plate——
