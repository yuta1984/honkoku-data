○ Eighteenth-Grade Soup ["Crayfish/Lobster Brawn Soup" — meaning shrimp/lobster broth]

Ise lobster or kuruma prawn (when using either of these two varieties, fix the quantity at fifty pieces respectively). When preparing with kuruma prawns, use 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres] of the seventeenth-grade broth, breadcrumbs, soy sauce, cinnamon [keishi], and 1 gō [approx. 180 ml] of vinegar.

Prepare all of the above ingredients as follows:

Boil the prawns, remove the shells, add cinnamon, and simmer for half a hitotoki [approximately one hour]. Strain the cooking liquid, grind and pound [the shells and flesh] thoroughly in a mortar, add the seventeenth-grade broth... (continues on the next page)
