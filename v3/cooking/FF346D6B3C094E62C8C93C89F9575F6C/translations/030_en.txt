○ Eighteenth-Grade Soup ["Crawfish Brown Soup" — meaning prawn/lobster broth]

Ise lobster or kuruma prawn (when using either of the two, the quantity is fixed at fifty pieces each). However, when preparing with kuruma prawns, add 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres] of the Seventeenth-Grade broth, breadcrumbs, soy sauce, cinnamon, and 1 gō [approx. 180 ml] of vinegar.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

Boil the prawns, remove the shells, add cinnamon, and simmer for half a hitotoki [approximately one hour]. Strain off the cooking liquid, grind and pound [the prawn flesh] thoroughly in a mortar, and [add] the Seventeenth-Grade broth —
