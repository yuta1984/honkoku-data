○ Soup No. 18 ["Crayfish" Loan Soup — meaning prawn broth]

Use either spiny lobster (ise-ebi) or Japanese tiger prawns (kuruma-ebi) — when using either of these two varieties, set the quantity at fifty pieces. However, when preparing this dish with tiger prawns, use 1 shō 2 gō of the No. 17 broth, along with breadcrumbs, soy sauce, cinnamon, and 1 gō of vinegar for seasoning.
