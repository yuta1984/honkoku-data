(Continued from previous page: Preparation of Soup No. 13 — Turnip Soup)

Chop the leeks and place them into the boiling black stock, then add the butter and ground pepper, and boil for approximately one hour. Do not use a strong flame.

In a separate vessel, bring the remaining 1 shō of black stock to a boil, combine it with the previously cooked turnips and leeks, and simmer for approximately four hours. It is best to use a low flame throughout. (Serves six)
