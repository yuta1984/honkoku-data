Mince the spring onions and add them to the boiling dark stock, then mix in the butter and ground pepper and simmer for half a hitotoki [approximately one hour]. Do not use a strong flame. In a separate vessel, bring the remaining 1 shō [approx. 1.8 litres] of dark stock to a boil, combine it with the previously cooked turnips and spring onions, and simmer for approximately two hitotoki [approximately four hours]. It is advisable throughout to use a low flame. (Serves six persons.)
