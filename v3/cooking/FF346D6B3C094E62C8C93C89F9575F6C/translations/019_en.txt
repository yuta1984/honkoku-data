○The method for producing first-grade stock is as follows:

One: Beef shank — 4 kin [approx. 2.4 kg]; leftover meats from the previous day's cooking; all manner of fowl and game — goose, duck, chicken, domestic duck, etc. — using bones and sinew without sorting; also the salted ham (pig's leg meat) with fat removed — 1/4 kin; butter (made from cow's milk) — 1/8 kin; one stalk of leek; three cloves [to be inserted into the leek]; one turnip (daikon?); three carrots [Westerners call this "carrot"]; three small lumps of white sugar; ground pepper — approximately half a teaspoon; one nutmeg [ground into powder]; powdered cinnamon — 1/32 kin; salt — 1/8 kin; cold water — four quarts ["quart" is the name of a vessel holding slightly more than six gō]; additionally, cold water — two pints

　　　The above ingredients are then prepared in the following manner:

One: Cut the pork leg meat into pieces approximately three sun [approx. 9 cm] on each side, then boil together with the beef shank and all manner of fowl and game such as goose and duck, without sorting bones and sinew. For the cooking method: first, drop butter into the pot, add □ cups of water per pint, then combine all the aforementioned ingredients at once, mixing thoroughly inside the pot. Using the gradual browning and caramelization of the bottom of the pot as a guide, add four quarts of water and simmer over a low flame for five hours [note: one Western hour equals one Japanese half-hour; apply this standard hereafter].
