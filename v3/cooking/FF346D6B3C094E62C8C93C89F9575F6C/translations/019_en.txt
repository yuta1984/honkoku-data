・Beef shin (the lower leg facing forward)　　4 kin [approx. 2.4 kg] in weight

　　　The above ingredients are then prepared in the following manner:

・Cut the pork leg meat into pieces approximately 3 sun [approx. 9 cm] on each side. Then simmer the beef shin along with the flesh of geese, ducks, and other fowl and game, without separating out the bones or sinew. As for the method of simmering: first, drip butter into the pot, add □ cups of water in a pint measure, then combine all of the foregoing ingredients at once and place them in the pot. Stir the contents of the pot well, and taking as one's guide the point at which the bottom of the pot begins to thicken to a syrupy consistency, add 4 cups of water in a quart measure, and simmer over a low flame for 5 hours (note: one Western hour equals half a Japanese hour [ichitoki]; the same conversion applies hereafter).
