**○ Fourth-Grade Soup: "Betokido Soup"** (meaning: roasted broth)

Beef: 1 kin; green onions: 2 stalks; rice: 1/8 kin; Western peas of the yellow-podded variety: 1 pint; ground pepper and salt to taste; cold water: 4 quarts.
*(Note: In the West, yellow peas are called "Elrotohito" [Erbsensuppe/split pea].)*

**[Left folio]**
The above various ingredients are to be prepared as follows:

1. Slice the carrots, onions, and beef thinly. Place the rice, peas, ground pepper, and salt together with four quarts of cold water into a pot, seal the lid tightly so that no steam escapes, place it inside a roasting pan, and roast over a strong flame for four hours.
*(This amount is sufficient for eight servings.)*
