○ Fourth-Grade Soup ["Baked Soup" — meaning a roasted/baked broth]

Ingredients:
- Beef: 1 kin [approx. 600 g]
- Spring onions/leeks: 2 stalks
- Rice: ⅛ kin
- Yellow Western peas: 1 pint
- Ground pepper and salt: to taste
- Cold water: 4 quarts
(Note: Yellow peas are called "Yellow Peas" [*Erō Hī*] in the West)

【Left Page】

The above various ingredients are to be prepared in the following manner:

First, thinly slice the carrots, onions, and beef, then place together with the rice, peas, ground pepper, salt, and 4 quarts of cold water into a pot. Seal the lid tightly so that no steam escapes, then place inside a roasting pan and roast over a strong flame for as long as four hours.
(Note: This quantity is sufficient to serve eight persons.)

○ Fifth-Grade Soup ["Cucumber Soup" — meaning a cucumber broth]

Ingredients:
- Large cucumber: 1
- Butter: the size of a thumb
- Red eggplant [tomatoes], skin removed: 2
- Ground pepper and salt: to taste
- Hen's eggs: 2 (whites only)
