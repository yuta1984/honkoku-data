**[Right folio]**
(Continued from previous page) After that, add the carrots, ground pepper, and other ingredients together with four quarts of water all at once, and boil again for five hours. Skim off the scum as before, then strain well through a colander (suinō), using only the broth.

　　　This concludes the preparation method for the third-grade stock.

○ **Fourth-grade soup** ["Betokido Sūfu" — meaning "roasted broth"]

Beef: 1 kin; spring onions: 2 stalks; rice: 1/8 kin; yellow Western peas: 1 pint; ground pepper and salt to taste; cold water: 4 quarts.
*(Note: In the West, yellow peas are called "Ertlochit" [Erbsensuppe/Ertlocht].)*

**[Left folio]**
The above-listed ingredients are to be prepared in the following manner.

First, slice the carrots, spring onions, and beef thinly, then place together with the rice, peas, ground pepper, salt, and four cups of cold water into a pot. Seal the lid firmly so that no steam escapes, then place it inside a roasting pan and roast over a strong flame for four hours.
*(This recipe serves eight people.)*
