○ Fourth-Grade Soup ["Baked Soup" ※1 — meaning a roasted/baked broth]

・Beef　　1 kin [approx. 600 g]
・Leeks/spring onions　　2 stalks
・Rice　　⅛ kin [approx. 75 g]
・Yellow Western peas　　1 pint measure
・Ground pepper and salt　　to taste
・Cold water　　4 cups (quart measure)
　(In the West, yellow peas are called "yellow peas" [エルロー・ピー] ※2)

【Left page】
　The above various ingredients are then prepared in the following manner:

・Thinly slice the carrots, leeks, and beef, then place them together with the rice, peas, ground pepper, and salt into a pot along with four cups of cold water. Seal the lid tightly so that no steam escapes, then place it inside a roasting pan and roast over a strong flame for as long as four hours. (This is sufficient to serve eight persons.)
