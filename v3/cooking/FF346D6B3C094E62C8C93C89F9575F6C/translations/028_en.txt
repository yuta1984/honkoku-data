○ Fifteenth-Grade Soup ["Vegetable Marrow Soup" — meaning white gourd broth]

Six fresh white gourds [vegetable marrows], 1 gō 5 shaku [approx. 270 ml] of cow's milk, 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres] of third-grade white stock, ground pepper, and salt to taste.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

Slice the white gourd thinly, and place it together with the ground pepper, salt, and the boiling white stock into the pot; simmer over a low flame for approximately one hitotoki [about two hours]. Afterwards, add 1 gō 5 shaku [approx. 270 ml] of cow's milk and serve. (Serves six persons.)

○ Sixteenth-Grade Soup ["Mock Turtle Soup" — meaning snapping turtle broth (a substitute for real turtle)]

One half-head of veal, one-quarter kin [approx. 150 g] of butter, one-quarter kin [approx. 150 g] of salt-cured pork (lean part only), finely minced parsley (a type of herb with a strong, pleasant fragrance) filling approximately one-third of a gō-masu measure, the peeled and minced rind of a ripe yuzu citrus, a small amount of ground cinnamon [keishi], two stalks of spring onion, finely minced shiitake mushrooms filling approximately one-third of a gō-masu measure, wheat flour, 1 gō 5 shaku [approx. 270 ml] of Chablis [a type of wine], minced veal formed into balls (prepared in the manner of tsumire [fish dumplings]), seasoned with red pepper and nutmeg to taste, the juice squeezed from one mandarin orange and one yuzu citrus, and 1 shō 8 gō [approx. 3.24 litres] of first-grade dark stock.
