○ Soup No. 15 ["Vegetable Marrow" Soup — meaning white gourd/marrow broth]

6 fresh white gourds (vegetable marrow), 1 gō 5 shaku of cow's milk, 1 shō 2 gō of No. 3 white stock, ground pepper, and salt to taste.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows.

Slice the white gourd thinly, and add it together with the ground pepper and salt into the boiling white stock. Simmer over a low flame for approximately two hours. After that, add 1 gō 5 shaku of cow's milk and serve. (Serves six)

○ Soup No. 16 ["Mock Turtle" Soup — meaning turtle(-style) broth (lit. "shell-horned tortoise broth")]

Cut half of a calf's head/side, one-quarter kin of butter, one-quarter kin of salted pork (from the lean, fat-free portion), finely chopped parsley (a variety of parsley-like herb, highly fragrant) filling about one-third of a gō-masu measure, peeled and chopped ripe yuzu citrus peel, a small amount of cinnamon powder, 2 stalks of leek, finely chopped shiitake mushrooms filling about one-third of a gō-masu measure, wheat flour, 1 gō 5 shaku of Sherry wine (noted as a type of wine), minced calf meat (prepared in the manner of fish dumplings) shaped into balls, seasoned with red pepper and nutmeg, the juice squeezed from one mandarin orange and one yuzu citrus, and 1 shō 8 gō of No. 1 black stock.
