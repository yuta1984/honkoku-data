First, pour boiling water over the "civette" (meaning liver), chop it finely into pieces roughly the size of a thumb. Add beef, spring onion, nutmeg, cinnamon, pepper, and 1 shō 8 gō [approx. 3.24 litres] of salted water, and simmer all these ingredients together until the liver is thoroughly cooked. Remove the scum, and once fully cooked, lift out the liver and strain through a sieve (suinō). Thereafter, add butter and wheat flour into the broth and simmer for four and a half hours. After this, pour the simmered liquid over the liver. Add two spoonfuls of "Port wine" (a type of red wine) and serve. (Serves six persons.)

○ Tenth-Grade Soup ["Mutton" Soup — meaning mutton broth]

5 kin [approx. 3 kg] of mutton neck, 3 carrots, 2 turnips, "Parsley"

【Left Page】

— a type of herb (similar to Japanese parsley/seri) — 1 stalk, ground pepper, salt to taste, and 1 shō 8 gō [approx. 3.24 litres] of water.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

First, place 5 kin of mutton neck, the carrots, and all other ingredients into a pot together with 1 shō 8 gō of water, and simmer over a low flame for approximately three hours. Once fully cooked, remove the neck meat, carrots, and turnips, and arrange them in a clean serving vessel.

○ Eleventh-Grade Soup ["Rice Soup" — meaning rice broth]

One quarter kin [approx. 150 g] of rice, salt, red pepper (chilli), cinnamon, and nutmeg — a small amount of each — and 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres] of third-grade white stock.
