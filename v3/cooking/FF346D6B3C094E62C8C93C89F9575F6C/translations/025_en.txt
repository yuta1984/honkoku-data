First, pour boiling water over the "civet" (liver), chop it finely into pieces roughly the size of a thumb. Simmer together the beef, spring onion, nutmeg, cinnamon, pepper, and 1 shō 8 gō of salted water along with the above ingredients until the liver is thoroughly cooked, skimming off any scum. Once cooked, remove the liver and strain the liquid through a sieve (suinō). Then add butter and wheat flour into the broth and continue to simmer for four and a half hours. After this, pour the broth back over the liver. Add two spoonfuls of "Port Wine" (a name for red grape wine) and serve. (Serves 6 people)

○ Tenth-Grade Soup ["Mutton Soup" — meaning a soup made from mutton/sheep]

　Ingredients:
- Neck of mutton: 5 kin [approx. 3 kg]
- Carrot: 3
- Turnip: 2
- "Parsley" (a herb of the parsley/celery family): 1 sprig
- Ground pepper: to taste
- Salt: to taste
- Water: 1 shō 8 gō [approx. 3.24 litres]

【Left Page】

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

First, place the 5 kin of mutton neck, carrots, and all other ingredients into a pot together with 1 shō 8 gō of water and simmer over a low flame for three hours. Once cooked, remove the neck meat, carrots, and turnips and arrange them in a clean dish.

○ Eleventh-Grade Soup ["Rice Soup" — meaning a soup made with rice]

　Ingredients:
- Rice: one-quarter kin [approx. 150g]
- Salt, red pepper, cinnamon, and nutmeg: a small amount of each
- Third-grade white stock: 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres]
