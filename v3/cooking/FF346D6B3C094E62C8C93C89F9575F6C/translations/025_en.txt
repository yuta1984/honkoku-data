Pour boiling water over the "civit"《Note: meaning liver》, chop it finely into pieces roughly the size of a thumb, then simmer together with the beef, green onion, nutmeg, cinnamon, pepper, and one shō eight gō (approx. 3.2L) of salt water until the liver is fully cooked. Skim off the scum, and once cooked through, remove the liver and strain through a colander. Afterward, add butter and wheat flour into the liquid and simmer for four and a half hours.
