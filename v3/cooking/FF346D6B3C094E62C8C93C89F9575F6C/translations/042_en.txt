The best fish and seafood to accompany curry are: salmon, red stingray, spiny lobster (Ise ebi), mantis shrimp (shako), oysters, and so forth. Oyster broth should be boiled for approximately one hour (four quarter-hours). The other five ingredients should be boiled for approximately thirty minutes (half an hour). When serving, the dish should be presented with rice added to the plate.

　　　Chapter Three: Sauces (Tsuyu-shiru)

○Item No. 35　【Apple Sauce / Meaning: Apple Sauce (Ringo-tsuyu)】

Ingredients: 12 apples, fine sugar (to taste), butter (one piece the size of a walnut), water: 2 gō (approx. 360 ml)

　　　Method

First, peel the apples, cut them into quarters, soak them briefly in cold water, then immediately remove them and place them in a pot. Add approximately 2 gō of water and bring to a boil, heating until the apples break down. Using the point at which the apple flesh is fully cooked and collapsed as your guide, add the butter and sugar, mixing and mashing well. Then simmer over a low flame for approximately thirty minutes. This sauce is to be used by serving it alongside roasted pork or roasted goose.
