○ No. 52 《Marginal note: Côtelettes de Mouton Froid (Cold Mutton Cutlets). Meaning sliced cold mutton.》

Leftover cold cooked mutton, sliced; 1 egg; breadcrumbs; and

**[Left Page]**

Brown gravy from No. 37; a small amount of tomato (red eggplant/aubergine).

**Method:**

First, cut the mutton into thick slices of about 5 bu (approximately 1.5 cm) in thickness, stir together with the egg, dip the slices in the mixture, immediately remove them, coat with breadcrumbs, and afterwards, place the drippings (fat that dripped during roasting) into a pan, add the thick-cut meat into the pan, and fry 《Marginal note: until they turn brown (kite-color) is the standard》. Pour the cooking liquid into the tomato and gravy.
