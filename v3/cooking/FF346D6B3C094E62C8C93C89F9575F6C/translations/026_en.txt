○ Soup No. 12 ["Spinach Soup" / meaning: spinach broth]
　One serving of spinach, 1 shō 2 gō of No. 2 dark stock (bouillon)
　　　Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

**[Left folio]**
First, boil the spinach, drain off the water through a strainer, and then wring out the boiled spinach firmly with both hands, shaping it into a ball roughly the size of an egg. Place this into a pot of boiling dark stock (bouillon) and simmer over a low flame for approximately half an hour. (Serves 6)
