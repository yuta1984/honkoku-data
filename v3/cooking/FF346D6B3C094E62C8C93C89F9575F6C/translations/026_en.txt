Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

First, place the rice into boiling water and simmer for five minutes (in the Western system, one hour is divided into sixty minutes, and each such unit is called one "minute"), then strain through a sieve (suinō) and set aside. In a separate vessel, bring the white stock to a boil, then add all the previously cooked rice along with the red pepper and other ingredients at once, and simmer over a low flame for approximately one hour [hitotoki]. (Serves six persons.)

○ Twelfth-Grade Soup ["Spinach Soup" — meaning spinach broth]

One plateful of spinach, and 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres] of second-grade dark stock.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

【Left Page】

First, boil the spinach, then strain off the liquid through a sieve (suinō). Wring the cooked spinach firmly with both hands and press it into a ball approximately the size of an egg. Drop this into the dark stock that has been brought to a boil, and simmer over a low flame for approximately half an hour [half hitotoki, approx. one hour]. (Serves six persons.)
