(Soup No. 16 — Mock Turtle Soup, preparation method continued)

Prepare all of the above ingredients as follows:

Take the half-divided calf's head, pour boiling water over it, and then skim off the surface stock. Wrap it in cloth and boil for approximately one hour. After that, remove the bones and sinew, cut into pieces approximately two sun (about 6 cm) square, plunge briefly into cold water, and immediately remove. Then bring 1 shō 8 gō of No. 1 black stock to a boil, and add all at once the following: the ground calf's meat, the salt-cured pork, the parsley, the finely chopped yuzu zest, two leeks, the shiitake mushrooms cut in one-sun pieces, red pepper, and nutmeg. Simmer over a low flame for approximately four hours. Then mix in butter and flour to thicken, followed by the juice of yuzu and mandarin orange, and sherry wine (parsley wine)—

[Left folio]

—1 gō 5 shaku, along with the meat dumplings, and continue to boil for ten minutes. (Serves ten)
