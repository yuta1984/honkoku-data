Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

Scald the halved calf's head with boiling water, then skim off the surface broth, wrap [the head] in cloth, and boil for approximately one hitotoki [about two hours]. Thereafter, remove the bones and sinew, cut into approximately two-sun [approx. 6 cm] cubes, plunge briefly into cold water, and immediately remove. Then bring 1 shō 8 gō [approx. 3.24 litres] of first-grade dark stock to a boil, and add all at once: the veal, salt-cured pork, parsley, minced yuzu rind, two stalks of spring onion, shiitake mushrooms cut into one-sun [approx. 3 cm] pieces, red pepper, and nutmeg. Simmer over a low flame for four hitotoki [about eight hours]. Then mix in butter and wheat flour to thicken, and add the juice of yuzu and mandarin orange, Chablis wine [parsley wine],

○ Seventeenth-Grade Soup ["Fish Soup" — meaning fish broth]

Two kin [approx. 1.2 kg] of white-fleshed fish, bones removed; two stalks of spring onion; half a yuzu rind; two carrots; a small amount of ground cinnamon; and 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres] of water.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

Cut the fish flesh into approximately two-sun [approx. 6 cm] pieces and simmer together with the remaining ingredients in 1 shō [approx. 1.8 litres] of water for approximately two hitotoki [about four hours].
