Prepare all of the above ingredients as follows:

Pour boiling water over the halved calf's head, then collect the surface broth, wrap [the head] in cloth, and simmer for one hitotoki [approximately two hours]. After that, remove the bones and sinew, cut into pieces approximately two sun [approx. 6 cm] square, plunge into cold water, and immediately remove. Next, bring 1 shō 8 gō [approx. 3.24 litres] of first-grade dark stock to a boil, and add at once: the minced calf's meat, salt-cured pork, parsley, minced yuzu peel, two spring onions, shiitake mushrooms cut into one-sun [approx. 3 cm] pieces, red pepper, and nutmeg. Simmer over a low flame for four hitotoki [approximately eight hours]. Then mix in butter and flour to thicken, and add the juice of yuzu and mandarin orange, Sherry wine [lit. "parsley wine"],

○ Seventeenth-Grade Soup ["Fish Soup" — meaning fish broth]

Two kin [approx. 1.2 kg] of white-fleshed fish, bones removed; two spring onions; half a yuzu peel; two carrots; a small amount of ground cinnamon; 1 shō 2 gō [approx. 2.16 litres] of water.

Prepare the above ingredients as follows:

Cut the fish flesh into pieces of approximately two sun [approx. 6 cm], and simmer together with all the other ingredients in 1 shō [approx. 1.8 litres] of water for two hitotoki [approximately four hours].
