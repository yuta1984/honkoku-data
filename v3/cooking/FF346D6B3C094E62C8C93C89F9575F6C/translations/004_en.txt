How timely indeed! The dietary rules set by the Buddha have crumbled, and the hollow practice of vegetarian abstinence has been swept away entirely. Even if octopus-monks and potato-eating bishops preach outside of Kyoto, there is no need to worry about the smell of fish and meat any longer. Henceforth...
