**Chapter Four: Stewing and Roasting**

○ Recipe No. 49 [Cold Meat Curry. Refers to a dish made from leftover cold beef, mutton, and the like.]

A small amount of the brown meat sauce from Recipe No. 41, cold leftover roasted meat

**[Left Page]**

2 red eggplants (tomatoes*), 1 tablespoonful of soy sauce, 1 gō of Port wine (a type of wine), 1 gō and 5 shaku of ale (beer) measured as a quarter-masu. Warm these together, add a small amount of flour [to thicken the sauce], salt and pepper to taste.

**Method for the above:**

First, set aside the meat only, and put all the other ingredients into the pot. Simmer over a low flame for ten minutes. Leave it as-is until it cools, then skim off the scum, and boil again. At this point, add the meat to the pot and simmer for a further ten minutes or so.
