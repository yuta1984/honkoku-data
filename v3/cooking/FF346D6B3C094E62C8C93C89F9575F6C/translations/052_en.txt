*(Continued from previous page: Recipe No. 53)*

…one tablespoon of curry powder; one tablespoon (and a little more) of wheat flour; salt to taste; water and broth or liquid seasonings of any kind: approximately one gō (about 180 ml).

**Method:**

First, slice the onions thinly and place them together with the butter into a pan, cooking until they turn grey (mouse-colored). Then mix the curry powder and wheat flour together with the salt, and combine thoroughly. Afterwards, add the thinly sliced, butter-softened and grey-colored onions into the curry powder, wheat flour, and salt mixture; slice the meat thinly or mince it, place it into the pan, and mix it together with the foregoing ingredients.

**[Left Page]**

Cook over a low flame for approximately ten minutes. Then add water or broth, and cook again over a low flame for about half a *ji* (approximately one hour). Afterwards, dish it out onto a plate, arrange rice in a ring around the edge of the plate, and serve.
