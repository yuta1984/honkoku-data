○ Recipe No. 50 [Baked Beef. Meaning: roasted beef. Note: there is a roasting method; in this section, it is roasted in a pot/oven.]

Pre-roasted beef: 2 kin; Scallions (long onions): 2 stalks; Carrot: 1 root [select a large one]; Turnip: 1 root; Pepper and salt: to taste; Meat sauce from Recipe No. 30: 12 tablespoonfuls; Ale (beer): 3 tablespoonfuls (measured in the same spoon); Mashed potato (Jāgatara potato散らし): a small amount

**Method for the above:**

First, slice the beef thinly and place a piece of fat upon the flesh of each slice. Arrange the meat in a baking dish [meaning a baking tray], taking care that the slices do not overlap. Slice the scallions, turnip, and carrot thinly,

**[Left Page]**

and arrange them on top of the meat. Then sprinkle salt and pepper over them. Place another layer of meat on top, and arrange the scallions, turnip, and carrot once again over this layer, sprinkling salt and pepper as before. Continue in this fashion, layering the 2 kin of sliced meat alternately with the turnip, scallions, and the like, interleaving them until all the sliced meat is used up. After that, pour the meat sauce over it, pour in the ale, coat and seal the top of the dish with the mashed potato, and bake in a pot (oven) for approximately half a shichi [about one hour].
