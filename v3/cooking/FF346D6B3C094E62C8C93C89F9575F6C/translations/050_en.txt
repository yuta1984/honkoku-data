○ No. 50 《Marginal note: Hashed Beef. Meaning roasted/grilled beef. Note: The cooking method here involves roasting in a pot/oven.》

Previously roasted beef: 2 kin; Welsh onions (negi): 2 stalks; Carrot: 1 piece 《Marginal note: choose a large one》; Turnip: 1 root; Pepper and salt: to taste; Meat gravy from No. 30: 12 tablespoons; Ale (beer): 3 tablespoons (measured with the same spoon); Mashed potato (scattered): a small amount.

**Method:**

First, slice the beef thinly, and for each slice, place a piece of fat alongside the lean meat. Arrange the meat in a baking dish 《Marginal note: refers to a baking tray》, taking care that the pieces of meat do not overlap. Slice the onions, turnips, and carrots thinly,

**[Left Page]**

and arrange them on top of the meat. After that, sprinkle salt and pepper over them, then place another layer of meat on top, followed again by onion, turnip, and carrot, with salt and pepper sprinkled as before. Continue layering the 2 kin of sliced meat with the turnip, onion, and other vegetables, alternating layers, and packing them together until all the meat is used. After this, pour the meat gravy over the layers, pour in the ale, spread mashed potato over the top of the dish to cover it, and bake in the oven for approximately half a day (one hour).
