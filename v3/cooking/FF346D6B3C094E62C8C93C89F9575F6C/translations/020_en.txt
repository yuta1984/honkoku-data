○The method for producing second-grade stock is recorded below.

・Leftover cooking liquid　　4 cups in a quart measure
・Leftover meat from the previous day — regardless of whether it is fowl or game, used in its entirety without separating bones, skin, or sinew. Then, using the same weights and quantities as those employed in the first-grade section, add half a spoonful of ground pepper and a little salt to taste, and simmer for up to six hours.
・Skimming off the scum is to be done as before. When ready to serve, transfer to a separate vessel.
