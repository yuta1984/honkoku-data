Using a tablespoon (meaning a dining spoon), skim off the scum [lit. "mica"].

○ The method for producing second-grade stock is recorded below:

First, use 4 quarts of leftover stock, and leftover meat from the previous day — regardless of whether it is from birds or beasts, and without distinction as to bones, skin, or sinew — using all of it entirely. Then, using the same ingredients, quantities, and amounts as specified in the first-grade section, add half a spoon of ground pepper and a little salt to taste, and simmer for up to six hours. Skim off the scum in the same manner as before. When ready to serve, transfer to a separate vessel.

Ingredients:
- Calf's ankle (front legs only): 4 kin [approx. 2.4 kg]
- Leftover cold meat, without distinction as to skin or bone
- Salt-cured pork leg meat, fatty parts removed, thinly sliced: 4 kin
- Carrots: 3
- Spring onions/leeks: 2 stalks
- Ground white pepper: half a teaspoon
- Salt: ⅛ kin
- Nutmeg: 1 nut (ground into powder)
- Ground cinnamon: 1/64 kin
- Butter: 1/16 kin
- Cold water: 4 quarts
