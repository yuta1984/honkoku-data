Prepare the above-mentioned ingredients as follows:

First, take three pieces of thinly sliced salt-cured pork, three spring onions cut on the diagonal, and four heads of lettuce (chisa) cut on the diagonal. Bring the first-grade dark stock to a boil, and mix in six gō of peas (setting aside three gō for later), butter, the thinly sliced salt pork, spring onions, and lettuce together (at this point, set aside the spinach separately). Bring to a boil in a separate pot, then strain the liquid through a sieve (suinō), and pour only that strained liquid back over the other ingredients, simmering everything thoroughly. Pass the cooked salt pork, the six gō of peas, spring onions, and lettuce through the top of the sieve, pressing them down, and combine with the previously strained liquid.
