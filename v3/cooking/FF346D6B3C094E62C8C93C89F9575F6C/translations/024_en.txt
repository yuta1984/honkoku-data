Prepare the above-mentioned ingredients as follows:

First, take three pieces of thinly sliced salt-cured pork, three stalks of spring onion cut on the diagonal, and four heads of lettuce cut on the diagonal. Bring the first-grade dark stock to a boil, then mix in 6 gō [approx. 1.08 litres] of peas (setting aside 3 gō [approx. 0.54 litres] beforehand), butter, the thinly sliced salt pork, the spring onion, and the lettuce together. (At this point, set aside the spinach separately.) In a separate pot, bring to a boil and strain the liquid through a sieve (suinō). Pour only that strained liquid over the previously combined ingredients and simmer thoroughly. Pass the cooked salt pork, the 6 gō of peas, the spring onion, and the lettuce through the sieve from above, pressing them down into the previously strained liquid below.

Next, add the reserved 3 gō of peas and a small lump of sugar, simmer again until fully cooked, and prepare for serving.

○ Ninth-Grade Soup ["Sibbut" Soup — meaning liver soup]

Take the liver of wild goose or domestic duck, together with the feet and neck, as well as beef shank, totalling 2 kin [approx. 1.2 kg] in weight; 1 ox tail; 2 stalks of spring onion; 2 carrots; something called "Holf"; cinnamon at one hundred-and-eighth part of one kin [a very small quantity]; half of one nutmeg; salt and pepper to taste; butter at one-sixteenth of one kin [approx. 37g]; one heaped "Sitort spoonful" of wheat flour; and 1 shō [approx. 1.8 litres] of cold water.
