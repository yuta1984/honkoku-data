**Method (continued from No. 54):**

First, slice the leftover cold meat thinly and arrange the meat on the bottom of a baking dish. Finely mince salt, pepper, ground nutmeg, parsley, and onion together, and sprinkle them evenly over the meat. Place mashed potatoes on top of this, then lay a small additional amount of meat on top of that. Continue layering the meat and various ingredients alternately until the dish is completely full. Afterwards, spread pastry crust (crust) over the top without leaving any gaps, and bake in a baking oven for approximately one and a half *ji* (about three hours).
