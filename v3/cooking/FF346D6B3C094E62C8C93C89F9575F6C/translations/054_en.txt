**Ingredients (No. 55 — Mutton Cutlets, continued):**

Thinly sliced leftover cold mutton — a small amount; salt, pepper, and nutmeg to taste; finely chopped yuzu peel; a little ground cinnamon (桂子, *keishi*); three stalks of onion (spring onion/leek); butter — four-fifths of one *kin* (approx. 480g); one dessertspoonful of wheat flour; one *gō* and five *shaku* of No. 38 Gravy; one tablespoonful of yuzu juice.

**Method:**

First, sprinkle pepper, salt, nutmeg, cinnamon, and chopped yuzu peel over the mutton. Melt the butter in the pan called a "frying pan" (*Furaingu-han*), bring it to a boil, add the meat, and add one dessertspoon of flour (Dessert Spoon
