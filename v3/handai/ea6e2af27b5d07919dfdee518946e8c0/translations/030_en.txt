▲ This "Haru-yama" (Spring Mountain) has no particular landscaping or artifice, and presents an unremarkable mountain scene; yet it is said that this district thrives because people come to gaze upon this mountain. It is like the "laughing spring mountain" — while it is said that a mountain "laughs" when tree buds begin to unfurl, this mountain laughs in all four seasons alike, and is a mountain beloved by people.

　　*New Year's Day — how the name of this Spring Mountain shines with glory*

▲ As for Tamazusa Hill, because the local villagers are skilled at tending and landscaping it — building up this area, trimming that area — it has taken on a scenic aspect and become a renowned place. One should say this is an invention of the local villagers.
