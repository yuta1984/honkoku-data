▲ Himeogi — A famous place of recent renown. Though "the breeze over the reeds" (萩の上風) has been sung in poetry, it is the deeper, more genuine quality of the breeze here — no mere surface wind — that is celebrated. This truly deserves to be called a fine achievement.
