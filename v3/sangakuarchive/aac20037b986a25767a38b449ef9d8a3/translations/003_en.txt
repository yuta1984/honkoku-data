## [Problem: Finding the Annual Interest Rate from Principal and Total Amount]

Suppose there is a principal sum lent at annual interest, to be settled in full after a fixed number of years, with both principal and interest collected together at the end.
Given: principal = [some amount], total of principal and interest combined = [some amount], number of years = [some amount]; what is the annual interest rate?

**Answer: As given in the method below.**

**Method:**
Divide the principal by the total principal-plus-interest amount, then subtract one; call the remainder the "ratio" (*ritsu*). Divide this ratio by the number of years; call the result "Kō" (甲, "A"). Subtract Kō from the ratio; call the remainder "Otsu" (乙, "B"). Add the ratio to Otsu; call the result "Hei" (丙, "C"). Add the ratio to Hei; call the result "Tei" (丁, "D"). Add the ratio to Tei; call the result "Bo" (戊, "E"). Continue naming in this manner to obtain the "trunk" (*miki*, 幹).

Place one as the "original number" (*gensū*). Multiply by Kō and divide by one to obtain the "first difference" (*ichi-sa*). Multiply by Otsu and divide by two to obtain the "second difference" (*ni-sa*). Multiply by Hei and divide by three to obtain the "third difference" (*san-sa*). Continue obtaining each successive difference in this manner, subtract them cumulatively from the "original number," divide one by the remainder, then subtract one from the result; the annual interest rate is obtained. This agrees with the problem.
