**Problem:**
As shown in the figure, when ten chords are inscribed within a circle (though the number of chords does not matter), and each chord has a length of 3 sun, find the diameter of the circle such that the total area of the outer segments (the regions between the chords and the arc) is minimized.

**Answer:**
The diameter of the circle is a little more than 10 sun.

**Method (Solution):**
Place the number of chords; divide by the value of π (using the true value); add 1 to the quotient; take the square root; multiply by the length of the chord — this gives the diameter of the circle. This satisfies the problem.
