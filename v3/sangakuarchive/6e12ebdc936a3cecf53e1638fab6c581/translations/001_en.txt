**Title: Votive Offering (Hōnō)**

Suppose there is a belt-shaped (annular) circle as shown in the figure.
Inside it, there are four "ko-circles" (larger circles, 甲円),
and within the successive stacked circles (累円), there are
two "otsu-circles" (smaller circles, 乙円).
Given that the number of stacked circles equals the number, and the diameter of the final (smallest) circle equals the diameter of the ko-circles, what is the diameter of the otsu-circles?

**Answer:** Find the diameter of the otsu-circle using the method below.

**Method:**
Subtract the diameter of the final circle from the diameter of the ko-circle and call this value "ko" (子).
Multiply "ko" by the diameter of the ko-circle, take the square root, then subtract twice "ko" — the result is the diameter of the otsu-circle.
This satisfies the problem.

Formally expressed:
Let *d* = diameter of ko-circle, *m* = diameter of final (smallest) circle.
Let *s* = *d* − *m* (called "ko/子").
Then: diameter of otsu-circle = √(*s* · *d*) − 2*s*
