[Title: Votive Offering]

Suppose, as shown in the figure, within a circumscribed right-angle configuration (belt-circle), there are four circles of type "ko" (甲, larger circles) and a series of nested/separated circles ("rui-en"), which contain two circles of type "otsu" (乙, smaller circles). Given that the number of the nested circles and the diameter of the final (smallest) circle equal the diameter of the ko circle, find the diameter of the otsu circle.

Answer: The diameter of the otsu circle is found by the following method.

Method: Subtract the diameter of the final circle from the diameter of the ko circle, and call this value "ko" (子, "child"). Multiply this by the diameter of the ko circle. Take the square root, then subtract twice the value of "ko" (子). The result is the diameter of the otsu circle. This satisfies the problem.
