**[Title: Votive Offering (Hōnō)]**

Suppose, as shown in the figure, there is a straight (outer) circle containing four *kō*-circles (甲円) and a layered partition circle (*ruien*), within which two *otsu*-circles (乙円) are inscribed. Given that the diameter of the final (*sue*) circle equals the diameter of the *kō*-circle, find the diameter of the *otsu*-circle.

**Answer:** The diameter of the *otsu*-circle is found by the following method.

**Method:** Subtract the diameter of the final circle from the diameter of the *kō*-circle and call this value *ko* (子). Multiply this by the diameter of the *kō*-circle, take the square root, then subtract twice *ko*. The result is the diameter of the *otsu*-circle. This satisfies the problem.
