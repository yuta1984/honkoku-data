**[Title: Votive Offering]**

Suppose, as shown in the figure, there is a rectangle (belt/band shape) containing an outer circle, within which four *kō* circles (甲円) are inscribed, and within the spaces formed by the separating circles (*rui-en*), two *otsu* circles (乙円) are contained. Given only that the number of *rui* circles and the diameter of the final circle (*matsu-en*) equal the diameter of the *kō* circle, find the diameter of the *otsu* circle.

**Answer:** The diameter of the *otsu* circle is found by the following method (stated at left).

**Method:** Subtract the diameter of the final circle (*matsu-en*) from the diameter of the *kō* circle, and call this value *ko* (子, "child value"). Multiply this by the diameter of the *kō* circle. Take the square root of the result, then subtract twice *ko*. The result is the diameter of the *otsu* circle.
This satisfies the problem.
