**[Right folio]**

1. In the 10th month of Kan'en 3, the Year of the Horse [1750], he traveled up to Edo for *kinban* (alternate duty service), and returned in the 4th month of the following Year of the Sheep [1751].

2. In the 2nd month of Kan'en 4, the Year of the Sheep [1751], at the time of the succession of the domain headship (*go-katoku*), although there were many duties to attend to, he was commended (*go-shōbi*) for performing his service diligently and with great effort.

3. In the 11th month of the same year, his commanding officer (*kashira*) Miyake Magobei was appointed as *wakadoshiyori* (junior elder), and he was consequently transferred to the unit of Yanase Kyūma as his successor post.

4. In the 6th month of Hōreki 5, the Year of the Boar [1755], he traveled up to Edo for *kinban*, and returned in the 10th month of the same year.

5. In the 2nd month of Hōreki 12, the Year of the Horse [1762], his commanding officer Yanase Kyūma was appointed as *wakadoshiyori*, and he was consequently transferred to the unit of Yamazaki Sasuke as his successor post.

**[Left folio]**

　　*(continued from right folio)*

6. On the 15th day of the 10th month of Hōreki 13, the Year of the Sheep [1763], he died of illness at the age of sixty. Posthumous Buddhist name: Hōzenji. Officiating priest and place of burial: same as above.
