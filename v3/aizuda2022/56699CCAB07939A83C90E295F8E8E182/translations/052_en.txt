1. In the 10th month of Kan'en 3, the Year of the Horse [1750], he traveled up to Edo for rotational duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 4th month of the following Year of the Sheep [1751].

2. In the 2nd month of Kan'en 4, the Year of the Sheep [1751], at the time of the succession of the domain headship (*go-katoku*), amid a great number of duties and errands, he was commended and praised for performing his service diligently.

3. In the 11th month of the same year, the unit commander (*kashira*) Miyake Magobei was appointed as *wakadoshiyori* (junior elder), and he was consequently reassigned to the unit of Yanase Kyūma.

4. In the 6th month of Hōreki 5, the Year of the Boar [1755], he traveled up to Edo for rotational duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 10th month of the same year.

5. In the 2nd month of Hōreki 12, the Year of the Horse [1762], the unit commander Yanase Kyūma was appointed as *wakadoshiyori* (junior elder), and he was consequently reassigned to the unit of Yamazaki Sasuke (*struck through: "ai-" / corrected to "maka-ri"*).

6. On the 15th day of the 10th month of Hōreki 13, the Year of the Sheep [1763], he died of illness at the age of 60. Posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*): Hōzen-in. Officiant (*dōshi*). Place of burial: same as above.
