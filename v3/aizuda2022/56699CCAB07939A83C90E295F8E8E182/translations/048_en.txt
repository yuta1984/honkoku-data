**[Right folio]**

1. In the 3rd month of Genroku 1, the Year of the Dragon [1688], he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (*kinban*). In the 7th month of the same year, he served as an attendant for the lord's entry into the domain (*gonyūbu*) and descended [to Aizu].

2. In the 2nd month of Genroku 2, the Year of the Snake [1689], he served as an attendant for the return journey on the occasion of the lord's alternate attendance (*sankinkōtai*) in Edo.

3. In the 3rd month of Genroku 3, the Year of the Horse [1690], he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation. In the 5th month of the same year, he descended [to Aizu] due to illness.

4. In the 3rd month of Genroku 4, the Year of the Sheep [1691], he was appointed to the *tozama*-gumi (*tozamagumitsuki*) assignment, and served castle guard duty (*gojōgoban*) under the unit of Sugita Gorōbei.

5. On the 3rd day of the 7th month of Genroku 7, the Year of the Dog [1694], he died of illness at the age of twenty-eight. The family line was discontinued (*danzetsu*). His Buddhist posthumous name (*hōmyō*) and place of burial (*sōsho*) are unknown.
