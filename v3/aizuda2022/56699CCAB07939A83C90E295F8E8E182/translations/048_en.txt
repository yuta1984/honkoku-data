1. In the 3rd month of Genroku 1, the Year of the Dragon [1688], he traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*); in the 7th month of the same year, he served in attendance during the lord's entry into the domain (*go-nyūbu*), and then returned home.

2. In the 2nd month of Genroku 2, the Year of the Snake [1689], in connection with the lord's alternate-attendance journey (*sankин*), he served in the escort for the return trip.

3. In the 3rd month of Genroku 3, the Year of the Horse [1690], he traveled up to Edo for guard duty; in the 5th month of the same year, he returned home due to illness.

4. In the 3rd month of Genroku 4, the Year of the Sheep [1691], he was appointed to the *tozama-gumi* (outer group/formation), and served in castle guard duty (*oshiro go-ban*) under the unit of Sugita Gorōbei.

5. On the 3rd day of the 7th month of Genroku 7, the Year of the Dog [1694], he died of illness at the age of twenty-eight. The family line was discontinued (*danzetsu*). His Buddhist posthumous name (*hōmyō*) and burial site are unknown.
