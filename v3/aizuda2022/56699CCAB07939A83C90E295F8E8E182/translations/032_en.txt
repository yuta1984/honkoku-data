- In the fourth month of the same year (Bunka 8, 1811): in connection with construction work [go-fushin] on the coastal battery [o-daiba] and the garrison encampment [o-jinya] in Sagami Province, he was ordered to depart for Edo by the eighteenth day of the coming month, and, depending on circumstances, to proceed directly from there to Sagami Province. In the same month, he duly traveled up to Edo.

- In the same month: as his eldest son [せがれ] Emon had previously absconded [shuppon], and his second son Naojirō had been sent as an adopted son to his blood-brother Netsū Tōjirō, he petitioned for permission to designate his third son Tōnoshin as heir [chakushi]. His petition was granted as requested.

**[Left folio]**

- In the fifth month of the same year: although he had been appointed instructor [shihan] in swordsmanship [kenjutsu] in Sagami Province, as he had numerous concurrent duties [kenmu] beyond his primary responsibilities, and affairs were pressing and numerous, he was relieved of said instructor appointment, as was announced.

- In the sixth month of the same year: in connection with the acceptance and takeover of the new domain territory [go-shin-ryō] in Sagami Province, he departed in the same month and arrived at Kannonzaki in Sagami Province.
