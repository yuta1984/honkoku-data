Item: In the same year, having been ordered to perform the duties of copying official documents (御写物) and having faithfully carried them out, as a reward, one kosode (short-sleeved kimono) of recalled/old-stock crêpe silk (縮緬) bearing the lord's family crest was granted.

Item: In the same year, the 13th year, the year of the Dog (戌): Having been ordered to perform document-copying duties and having faithfully carried them out, as a reward, three pieces of silver (銀子三枚) were granted.

Item: In the 16th year, the year of the Boar (亥), 4th month: On the day before His Lordship's departure on his journey (御下向・御発駕), in a private audience, various items including a tobacco pipe (御煙筒) and hand towel (御手拭) were privately bestowed.

Item: In the 2nd month of the 4th year of Enkyō (延享四卯年, 1747): Documents required for the official oath ceremony (御誓詞)

Item: In the 4th month of the 2nd year of Kan'en (寛延二巳年, 1749): He was appointed to the position of Ō-nandokoyaku (大納戸役, Chief Storeroom Officer). It was proclaimed that until a successor was appointed, he should continue his duties as before, and that his official stipend (御役料) of fifty koku would remain unchanged.

Item: In the 10th month of the 2nd year of Hōreki (宝暦二申年, 1752): He was appointed as an attendant (御附) to Kansōin-sama and Rikōin-sama.
