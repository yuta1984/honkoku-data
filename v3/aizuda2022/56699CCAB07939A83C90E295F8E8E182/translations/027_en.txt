- In the second month of the same year [Bunka 12, 1815], from the preceding spring through until the tenth month, he continuously and diligently served as duty officer [yōban]; and even prior to this period, whenever there were difficulties with duty officer assignments, he had repeatedly stepped in to serve. For his exceptional dedication and exertion in these duties, formal commendation was issued.

- In the twelfth month of the same year, having diligently served in duties for Lord Teisho as well, he was granted two bundles of cotton as a mark of commendation.
　　　　(Note: After this, the same grant was bestowed upon him annually in the years that followed.)

- In the fourth month of Kyōwa 1, the Year of the Rooster (Note #1), upon his petition, he was ordered to accompany [Lord Teisho] on his journey down to the domain [Aizu], and duly departed; then in the same

**[Left folio]**

　　fifth month, he returned [to Edo].

- In the sixth month of the same year, having diligently served not only in his primary duties but also in various ad hoc assignments, he was granted one set of formal hemp ceremonial garments [o-asa kamishimo] as a mark of commendation.

- In the fifth month of the third year of the Boar [Kyōwa 3, 1803], during Lord Teisho's journey down to the domain, while those of the return-escort group did not proceed to Edo, he served in duties related to the inner attendant group [uchi-oともgata]; and as the allotment of porters and horses along the route had been reduced, he took on the ad hoc duty of distributing and coordinating the porters and horses among the members of the escort party. For this service, he was granted one bundle of cotton as a mark of commendation.
