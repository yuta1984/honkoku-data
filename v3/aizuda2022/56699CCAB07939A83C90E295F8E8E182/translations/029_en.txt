- In the seventh month of the fifth year, the Year of the Dragon (Bunka 5, 1808), it was announced that he had diligently served the previous year in supervising construction work [ofushin], including the repair of the fence along the south-facing street of the upper residence [kami-oyashiki] and the extension of the pleasure pavilion [oyuijo], among other projects.

- In the eighth month of the same year, in connection with the re-thatching/re-tiling of the roofs of the palace buildings and repairs at various locations, he was ordered to serve as supervisor in accordance with his previous petition [maemae no negai].

- In the tenth month of the same year, beginning with the enforcement of controls [oshikiri] over the human and horse advance-notice registers [hitoba sakifure] and transport-fee ledgers [chinchō] for those traveling the Aizu road, he was charged with overseeing and managing all such

**[Left folio]**

　　matters of strict control [shimari-kata], as was announced by order.

- In the eleventh month of the same year, he was additionally appointed to concurrently serve as Commissioner of Horse Equipment [Bagu Bugyō].
　　Note: Upon being assigned this concurrent duty, he was ordered to consult and coordinate with the Commissioner of Horse Equipment in Aizu, to conduct thorough examination of all newly produced items here in Edo as well as matters of maintenance and various articles, and to manage affairs so that expenditures would be reduced and strict oversight maintained throughout.

- In the second month of the sixth year, the Year of the Snake (Bunka 6, 1809), he was ordered to serve concurrently also as the head official [shuyaku] of the Military Studies Academy [Bugakuryō].
