**[Right folio]**

- In the same month, having diligently served as the receiving-sword partner during Lady Ryōtokuin's spear-arts practice sessions, he was privately granted one bundle of cotton as a reward.
　　　　(Note: After this, the same grant was bestowed upon him annually in the years that followed.)

- In the same month, he was appointed to the permanent post of messenger officer [otsukaiban sadaka], and duly served in that capacity.
　　　　(Note: After this, he was appointed to this duty on numerous occasions and served accordingly.)

- In the third month of the tenth year of the Horse [Bunka 10, 1813], he succeeded to the headship of his household, and was granted an estate of one hundred and fifty koku without any discrepancy.

- In the fifth month of the eleventh year of the Sheep [Bunka 11, 1814], as the senior retainer among the tozama shi [outside retainers] permanently stationed in Edo with a stipend of one hundred and fifty koku, he was ordered to serve on every occasion when the duty officer [yōban] and elevated service [takatsutome] were required. Every

**[Left folio]**

　　time he was to serve as ordered, he was granted nine portions of stipend rice [kunin fuchi] and an annual supplementary cash allowance of three ryō.

- In the sixth month of the same year, during the Hanada Memorial Service and the memorial rites for Lady Anshōin, he was ordered to serve as proxy worshipper on behalf of Lady Ryōtokuin.

- In the eleventh month of the same year, he was appointed as permanent inspector [jōdzume metsuke] and was ordered to be stationed at the upper residence [kami-o-yashiki].

- In the first month of the twelfth year of the Monkey [Bunka 12, 1815], his adoptive father Minamoto Ukiyū's legitimate grandson, Tomonosuke, was granted permission to be formally designated as legitimate heir, in accordance with a petition submitted to that effect.
