Heihachi Tomotake (first name: Tetsunoshin; born on the 25th day of the 12th month of Meiwa 5, the year of the Rat [1768])

　　Biological father: Second son of Netsu Zenbei Shigenori

　　Wife: Adopted daughter of foster father Jūemon Chōrin

Item: In the 3rd month of Kansei 2, the year of the Dog (1790), he was appointed as the kendō supervisor (shimeri-kata) at the Bugakuryō (the domain's martial arts academy).

Item: In the 7th month of the same 5th year, the year of the Ox (1793), a grand spear-fighting tournament (ōjiai) of the Ōuchi school of spearmanship was held for the lord's inspection (o-jōran). He performed admirably on that occasion, and as a result,

[Left folio]

　　he was privately presented with sake (as a reward from the lord).

Item: In the 3rd month of the same 7th year, the year of the Tiger (1795), upon the occasion of a fire spreading to domain buildings (gorui-shō), he contributed with particular dedication to the transport of timber. Recognizing this as a commendable act, he was treated to a meal at the domain school.

Item: In the 7th month of the same year, at the request of his (foster) father, he was for the first time granted an audience (o-memie) with the lord.

Item: In the same month, at the request of his (foster) father, he was appointed as a replacement guard (o-bandai) and served in the First Company (ichiban-gumi).
