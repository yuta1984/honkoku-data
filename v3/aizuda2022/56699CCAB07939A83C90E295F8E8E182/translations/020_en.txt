　　Mother: daughter of the Kurose clan

　　Wife: daughter of Aga Kyūzaemon Mitsutomo; [marriage ended by] her death


Item:　　In the 4th month of Meiwa 2, the year of the Rooster (1765), in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience [with the lord] (o-memie).

Item:　　In the intercalary 5th month of Tenmei 1, the year of the Ox (1781), he was appointed as a hired attendant in the o-tomo-ban-sada [escort guard unit], and having served diligently, he was awarded five pieces of silver as a mark of commendation.

Item:　　In the 9th month of Kansei 1, the year of the Rooster (1789), in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted permission to change his name to Jūzō.
