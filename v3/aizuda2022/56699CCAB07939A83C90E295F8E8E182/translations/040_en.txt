　　...and when [foreign ships] came to shore for the first time, preparations were swiftly made, a vessel was dispatched, and [he] performed guard duty for several days. In recognition of this service, he was awarded two bundles of cotton as a reward.

- In the 5th month of the same year [Bunsei 2, 1819], he applied himself diligently and without negligence to the Military Studies Academy (*Bugakuryō*), and his studies advanced beyond what was expected for his age. In recognition of this, he was awarded one piece of silver (*ginsu ichi-mai*) as a reward.

- In the 9th month of the 4th year [of Bunsei] (1821), the Year of the Snake, he was released from his duty as a hired retainer for outside domain warriors (*tozama-shi*) in Sagami Province, and having performed service at the garrison (*sonaeba*), he was awarded one bundle of cotton.

- In the 4th month of the 5th year [of Bunsei] (1822), the Year of the Horse, he submitted the customary courtesy call upon succession to the household headship (*okadoku o-rei*).

**[Left folio]**
　　On that same day, due to the reception of guests, he was engaged and entrusted with service as a hired page (*okoshō o-kyūnin o-yatoi*).
　　　　Note: In connection with the above, he was granted three *ryō* in gold as clothing expenses.

- In the 9th month of the same year, as he was known to be practiced in the martial arts, he was ordered to serve as a middle-ranking soldier (*chūshi*) within the company of a *bantō* (troop commander) during times of emergency, and it was declared that he was to carry out his duties in such a manner as not to be impeded by lack of resources.
