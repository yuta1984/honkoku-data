Item:　　In the 5th month of the same year [Tenmei 1, 1781], his request to change his name to Jūuemon (重右衛門) was granted.

Item:　　In the intercalary 5th month of the same year, as recognition that he had served diligently during his previous term of duty [as acting inspector (o-metsuke kari-yaku)], he was presented with three bundles of cotton as a reward.

Item:　　In the 2nd month of the same [Tenmei] 2, the year of the Tiger (1782), he was appointed as acting inspector (o-metsuke kari-yaku).

Item:　　In the 12th month of the same year, he was appointed as permanent resident inspector (jō-zume o-metsuke).

Item:　　In the 8th month of the same [Tenmei] 5, the year of the Snake (1785), [it was officially noted that] during the fire that broke out on the 26th day of the previous 12th month,

Item:　　In the 1st month of the same [Tenmei] 7, the year of the Horse (1787), during the memorial service (o-hōji) held at Jisshō-ji Temple in Mita on the 28th [of the previous month], he had been on duty; however, it was brought to [the lord's] attention that after the memorial service concluded, he had been treated to a banquet by the resident monk and had consumed sake, and that while this circumstance in itself might be understandable, he had become disorderly in his drinking and conducted himself in a highly imprudent manner. [The lord] considered it, that despite being in a position requiring strict discipline and constant attendance, and despite the gravity of the times, he had allowed himself to fall into such behavior, as entirely unbecoming of his office.
