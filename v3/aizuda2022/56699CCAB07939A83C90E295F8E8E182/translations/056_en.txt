**[Right folio]**

(Continued from the previous page) — this was officially declared.

1. In the 12th month of Kansei 7, the Year of the Rabbit [1795], for his diligent service as the person in charge of procuring timber (*mawari-zai*) for the construction works at the Shiba residence (*o-yashiro*), his proper management thereof, and for having worked single-handedly with great effort as the sole remaining member among his colleagues during the absence of the others (*tomari-jū*), he was awarded one secondhand *koshode* garment (*o-furu o-kosode hito-tsu*) as a reward.

2. In the same month, he departed for and ascended to Edo on rotation duty (*kinban*).

3. In the 6th month of Kansei 8, the Year of the Dragon [1796], for his effective management of supervisory affairs and his many years of capable and diligent service, it was officially declared in Edo that he would be granted an additional stipend (*o-yaku-ryō*) of thirty *koku*.

**[Left folio]**

4. In the 7th month of the same year, as he was due to rotate out the following month, it was declared that, since having him replaced at this particular juncture might cause difficulties, he was to be kept at his post (*o-tome*) until the completion of the palace construction works (*go-ten go-fushin*).

5. In the 5th month of Kansei 9, the Year of the Snake [1797], during the lord's journey down to the domain (*o-gekō*), he was appointed to the temporary post of *Metsuke* (Inspector/Censor) and descended to the domain.

6.
