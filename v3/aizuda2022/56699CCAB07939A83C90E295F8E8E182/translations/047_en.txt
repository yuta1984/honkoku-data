**Orinojō** (personal name unknown; initial name: Gonnosuke)
　　Mother: same as above

1. In the 3rd month of Enpō 8, the Year of the Monkey [1680], he was granted the standard stipend (*narabi no gisaku*) of four persons' rations (*yonin-fuchi*), and was summoned into service as a page (*okoshō*); however, due to his young age, he was excused from his regular guard duty (*kinban*) that year.

2. In the 3rd month of Tenna 1, the Year of the Rooster [1681], in connection with the succession to the domain headship (*okato*), he traveled up to Edo.

**[Left folio]**

3. In the 4th month of Tenna 2, the Year of the Dog [1682], he was granted leave (*暇*) due to his mother's illness, and returned home; in the 9th month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo again.

4. In the 7th month of Tenna 3, the Year of the Boar [1683], he was granted leave due to his father's illness, and in the 9th month of the same year traveled up to Edo; in the 4th month of the following Year of the Rat [1684], he returned home.

5. In the 9th month of Jōkyō 1, the Year of the Rat [1684], he traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*), and while in Edo was appointed to serve as a dining-table attendant (*gozenbан*).

6. In the 4th month of Jōkyō 4, the Year of the Rabbit [1687], he served in attendance during the lord's journey to the capital (Kyoto).

7. In the 6th month of the same year, in accordance with his petition, he was granted permission to undergo his coming-of-age ceremony (*genpuku*), and was appointed as an escort guard (*otomobан*); in the 7th month of the same year, he returned home.
