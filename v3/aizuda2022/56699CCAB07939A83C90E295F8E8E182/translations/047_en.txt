Daughter: wife of Teshirogi Taibei Katsukage
　　Mother: same as above

**Orinojō** (personal name unknown; childhood name: Gonsuke)
　　Mother: same as above

1. In the 3rd month of Enpō 8, the Year of the Monkey [1680], he was granted a stipend of eight *ryō* in pseudo-crop allocation (*gigi-saku*) and four persons' worth of provisions (*ninbuchi*), and was summoned to service as a page (*okoshō*). On account of his young age, he was excused from that year's duty rotation (*kinban*).

2. In the 3rd month of Tenna 1, the Year of the Rooster [1681], owing to the succession of the domain headship (*gokadoku*), he traveled up to Edo.

**[Left folio]**

3. In the 4th month of Tenna 2, the Year of the Dog [1682], he was granted leave (*ohima*) due to his mother's illness, and descended [from Edo to Aizu]. In the 9th month of the same year, he traveled back up to Edo.

4. In the 7th month of Tenna 3, the Year of the Boar [1683], he was granted leave due to his father's illness, and in the 9th month of the same year ascended [to Edo]. He descended [to Aizu] in the 4th month of the following Year of the Rat [1684].

5. In the 9th month of Jōkyō 1, the Year of the Rat [1684], he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (*kinban*), and while in Edo was appointed as a meal-service attendant (*gozenban*).

6. In the 4th month of Jōkyō 4, the Year of the Hare [1687], he served as an attendant on the occasion of the lord's visit to Kyoto (*gojokyō*).

7. In the 6th month of the same year, in accordance with his petition, he was granted the coming-of-age ceremony (*genpuku*), and was appointed as a companion guard (*otomiban*). He descended [to Aizu] in the 7th month of the same year.
