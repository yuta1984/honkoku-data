- In the first month of the eighth year, the Year of the Sheep (Bunka 8, 1811): he had previously been assigned to enter and participate in duties [tachiri tachiai-kin] at the Accounting Office [go-kantō-sho], but as there were matters causing difficulty [sashitsukae], he was relieved of the entering duty [tachiri-kin]. In connection with this, he was assigned to the regular attending duty [sadame-tachiai] at the Accounting Office, and was ordered to observe and become informed of all matters — including those related to revenues and expenditures [suiitō], as well as various audits [shō-ginmi], various estimates [shō-tsumori], and various official notices [shō-tatsu] — reviewing them and taking care to ensure they were handled in a manner beneficial to the domain.
