**[Right folio]**

(Continued from previous page) …for the effort he exerted [during the fire], he was bestowed with words of praise (*shōbi*) by the lord.

1. In Kyōhō 8, the Year of the Rabbit [1723], his petition to arrange a mutual exchange (*aiai-gae*) of the residence with Takemura Shinzaemon was granted in accordance with his request.

2. In the 1st month of Kyōhō 9, the Year of the Dragon [1724], his petition to take Tetsuemon, the fourth son of Ichinose Jinzaemon, as a son-in-law and adoptive heir (*muko-yōshi*), was granted in accordance with his request.

3. In the 3rd month of Kyōhō 10, the Year of the Snake [1725], in accordance with his petition, his son Magosaburō was appointed as his replacement guard (*ban-dai*).

4. On the 6th day of the 8th month of Genbun 1, the Year of the Dragon [1736], he died of illness at the age of sixty-two. His posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*) was Jizennin Dōshi. His place of burial is the same as noted above.
