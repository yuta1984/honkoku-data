　　He was relieved of his duties as castle guard (o-shiro o-ban) and resident official (i-yaku).

Item: In the 3rd month of the same 8th year, the year of the Dragon (1796), he was appointed as a permanently stationed outside retainer (jōzume-tozama-shi), and in the 5th month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo accompanied by his attendants.

Item: In the 7th month of the same year, he was appointed as the spearmanship supervisor (yarijutsu shimeri-kata).

Item: In the 8th month of the same year, he was appointed as acting unit commander (kari-mono-gashira) and served in that capacity until the 9th month of the same year.
　　　Note: After this, he was repeatedly appointed as acting unit commander and continued to serve in that role.

[Left folio]

Item: In the 10th month of the same year, during Lord Sadaaki's spear practice sessions, he was ordered to appear and, in consultation with Ishizuka Ichirōemon, was instructed to serve as his sparring partner. He attended every practice session and served as the practice partner (o-uke-tachi).
　　　Note: In recognition of his service in this duty, he was privately presented each year with one bundle of cotton (wadded cotton).

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, it was proclaimed that he had served diligently as Lord Sadaaki's messenger.
