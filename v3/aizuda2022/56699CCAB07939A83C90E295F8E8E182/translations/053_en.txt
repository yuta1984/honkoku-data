1. In the 8th month of Hōreki 5, the Year of the Boar [1755], in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (*o-memie*) with the lord.

**[Left folio]**

2. In the 12th month of Hōreki 13, the Year of the Sheep [1763], he succeeded to the family's hereditary stipend (*atoshiki*) of 250 *koku* of rice land without dispute, and assumed duty as a castle guard (*go-jō go-ban*).

3. In the 3rd month of Meiwa 2, the Year of the Rooster [1765], he traveled to Yamagata for rotational duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 9th month of the same year.

4. In the 10th month of Meiwa 3, the Year of the Dog [1766], he traveled up to Edo for rotational duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 4th month of the following Year of the Boar [1767], accompanying the lord's party.

5. In the 3rd month of Meiwa 8, the Year of the Hare [1771], the unit commander (*kashira*) Yamazaki Sasuke was relieved of his post, and he was consequently reassigned to the unit of Tanaka Kahei.
