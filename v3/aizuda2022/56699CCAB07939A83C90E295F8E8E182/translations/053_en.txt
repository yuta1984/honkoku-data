**Jirōzaemon Toshika** *(childhood/first name: Kingo)*

　　**First wife**: daughter of Machida Jikidaiyū Mitsutada　　*(Side note: Died of illness on the 29th day of the 9th month of Kansei 9, the Year of the Snake [1797]. Posthumous Buddhist name: Jikyōin Myōkai Nichiriō Daishi. Officiating priest and place of burial: same as above.)*

　　**Second wife**: daughter of Nakabayashi Saburōzaemon Naonori　*(Side note: Died of illness on the 10th day of the 1st month of Bunka 12, the Year of the Boar [1815]. Posthumous Buddhist name: Seishin'in Myōren Nikkaku Daishi. Officiating priest and place of burial: same as above.)*

1. In the 8th month of Hōreki 5, the Year of the Boar [1755], in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first audience (*o-memie*) with the domain lord.

**[Left folio]**

2. In the 12th month of Hōreki 13, the Year of the Sheep [1763], his inherited stipend (*atoshiki go-chigyō*) of 250 *koku* was confirmed without alteration, and he was assigned to the *tozama* unit, serving as castle guard (*go-shiro go-ban*) in the unit of Yamazaki Sasuke.

3. In the 3rd month of Meiwa 2, the Year of the Rooster [1765], he traveled to Yamagata for *kinban* (alternate duty service), and returned in the 9th month of the same year.

4. In the 10th month of Meiwa 3, the Year of the Dog [1766], he traveled up to Edo for *kinban*, and returned in the 4th month of the following Year of the Boar [1767], in attendance (*o-tomo*) upon the domain lord.

5. In the 3rd month of Meiwa 8, the Year of the Hare [1771], his commanding officer (*kashira*) Yamazaki Sasuke was relieved of his post, and he was consequently transferred to the unit of Tanaka Kahei as his successor post.
