Item:　　In the 3rd month of the same year [Tenmei 7, 1787], he was assigned to the unit of Takekawa Gorō and served as castle guard (o-shiro o-ban).

Item:　　In the 12th month of the same year, his request to change his name to Junsuke (醇助) was granted.

Item:　　In the 3rd month of [Tenmei] 8, the year of the Sheep (1788), his unit commander (kashira) Takekawa Gorō was appointed as Junior Elder (wakadoshiyori), and he was subsequently transferred to the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon, taking the place left by Takekawa's promotion.

Item:　　In the intercalary 6th month of Kansei 1, the year of the Rooster (1789), he was appointed as an tozama-shi attached to the chief elder (o-karō-tsuki tozama-shi) and entered the First Unit (ichibangumi).

Item:　　In the 11th month of [Kansei] 6, the year of the Tiger (1794), he was granted the residence plot (yashiki) that had previously belonged to Hanabatake Kurokōchi Jidayū.

Item:　　In the same month, his request to change his name back to Jūuemon (重右衛門) was granted.

Item:　　In the 12th month of the same year, his request to take as his son-in-law and adopted heir (muko-yōshi) Tetsunoshин, the second son of Netsу Zenbei, was granted.

Item:　　In the 7th month of [Kansei] 7, the year of the Rabbit (1795), in accordance with his petition, his son Heihachi was appointed to serve in his place (o-bandai).

Item:　　In the 3rd month of [Kansei] 10, the year of the Horse (1798), in accordance with his petition, he was granted permission to retire (inkyo).
