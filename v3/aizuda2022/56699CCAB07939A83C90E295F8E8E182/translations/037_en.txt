**[Right folio]**

- In the third month of the fourth year of the same era [Bunsei 4], the Year of the Snake [1821]: he was appointed as *jō-zume o-metsuke* [permanent-duty inspector] at the *mochiseki* [standing rank/allocated seat].

- In the same month: he was ordered to serve as instructor in gunnery arts [*hōjutsu shihan*] in Edo, and accordingly was ordered to be stationed at the Shiba domain residence [*Shiba o-yashiki*].

- In the same month: it was proclaimed that, with regard to his concurrent duties and investigative official duties [*tadashi go-yō*] and the like, he was to continue to perform them generally as before, until the withdrawal from Sagami Province [*Sōshū-omote o-haraiage*].

- In the fourth month of the same year: at the time of the formal handover of the fortified gun emplacements [*o-daiba*] and garrison quarters [*jinya*] at Kannonzaki and Hiraneyama in Sagami Province, he was ordered to present himself;

**[Left folio]**

...and the items that had been placed in readiness—including large and small firearms [*daishō-jū*] and other essential equipment—were all handed over directly by Ishizawa Tajirōemon and the entire party to the Uraga Magistrate [*Uraga go-bugyō*], Lord Naitō Izunokami and Lord Tsukushi Sadonokami, without any impediment or delay, and all matters were conducted smoothly.

- In the fifth month of the same year: he was ordered to withdraw from the domain garrison quarters in Sagami Province [*Sōshū o-jinya*], and on that same day he departed and arrived at the Shiba domain residence.

- In the same month: as the archive library materials [*o-bunko o-shomotsu*] were placed in his custody, he was ordered to consult with Takazu Heizō and attend to the matter jointly.
