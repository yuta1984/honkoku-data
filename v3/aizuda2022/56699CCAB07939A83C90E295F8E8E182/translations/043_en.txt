**[Right folio]**

1. [In the same year], he was appointed as an attendant (*o-tsuki*) to Lord Iwahiko, and was ordered to serve in close attendance at his side.
1. In the 11th month of the same year, at the time of the transfer of domain to Aizu, he was additionally granted an increase in stipend of 50 koku.
1. In Keian 4, the Year of the Hare [1651] *(month unknown)*, acting as the representative (*omyōdai*) of Lord Iwahiko, he traveled to Nikkō and made an offering of condolence money (*kōden*).
1. In the 9th month of Jōō 2, the Year of the Snake [1653], he was granted a further increase in stipend of 50 koku.
1. In the summer of Meireki 1, the Year of the Sheep [1655], he accompanied Lord Iwahiko on his journey down [to Aizu domain].

**[Left folio]**

1. In the 11th month of the same year, he accompanied Lord Iwahiko on his *sankin* [alternate attendance] journey up [to Edo].
1. In the 11th month of [Meireki] 3, the Year of the Rooster [1657], due to the passing of Lord Iwahiko, he was assigned to the *tozama-gumi* [outside retainer unit] and entered the unit of Wakisaka Uemon, where he served as a castle guard (*o-shiro go-ban*).
1. In Kanbun 6, the Year of the Horse [1666] *(month unknown)*, as his unit commander (*kashira*) Wakisaka Uemon was relieved of his duties, he was thereafter transferred into the unit of Sugita Gorōbei.
1. In the 3rd month of Enpō 2, the Year of the Tiger [1674], as requested, his son Magonosaburō was appointed to succeed him in the guard duty (*go-ban-gawari*).
