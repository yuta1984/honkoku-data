**[Right folio]**

1. In the 2nd month of Kansei 4, the Year of the Rat [1792], as his son Etsunojō had previously died of illness, his request to designate his third son Isaburo as heir was granted in accordance with his petition.

2. In the 3rd month of Kansei 5, the Year of the Ox [1793], he was appointed as *Go-fushin Bugyō* (Magistrate of Construction and Public Works).

3. In the 4th month of the same year, it was officially declared that, as he had served for many years as *Yama Bugyō* and had become a man of great expertise and experience, during periods when official duties permitted, he should also attend at the *Yama Yakusho* (Forestry Office), and that any persons with opinions or proposals regarding timber for construction (*mawari-zai*), stockpiled timber (*kakoi-zai*), revenue funds (*o-eki-kin*), and related matters should consult directly with the *Yama Bugyō* and also report to the magistrate's office (*bugyōsho*).

**[Left folio]**

4. In the 5th month of the same year, for having performed his previous duties with great diligence, properly managing matters of financial benefit to the domain, and for having effectively directed those under his supervision in their accounting and management, he was awarded three pieces of silver (*ginshi san-mai*) as a reward.

5. In the 9th month of the same year, during the construction works at Yugawa, which were extraordinary compared to those of ordinary years, he provided appropriate and effective direction [one character unclear], and was privately presented with sake (*o-sake*) as a mark of informal appreciation.
