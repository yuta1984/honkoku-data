**[Right folio]**

...(continued) he was ordered to serve as a messenger for the coastal defense [*o-katame shisha*], and as compensation for expenses incurred thereby, he was granted two *ryō* in gold.

- In the ninth month of the same year: having served diligently from the outset in the Sagami Province defense post [*Sōshū o-bibagjō*], fulfilling both his primary and concurrent duties without interruption, and having rendered many years of dedicated service, he was granted one set of formal ceremonial *kamishimo* [formal upper and lower garment ensemble worn by samurai] drawn from the lord's own used garments [*o-meshifuru*], as a mark of commendation.

- In the third month of the sixth year [of Bunsei], the Year of the Ram [1823]: on the grounds that the occasion corresponded to the anniversary memorial service [*nenki*] for his ancestors, he petitioned that he be permitted to travel down to Aizu to visit the graves, requesting thirty days of leave in addition to the travel time, and furthermore requesting that, at the time of the lord's own journey down [to Aizu], he be assigned appropriate duties in attendance;

**[Left folio]**

...and in response, he was appointed to the acting post of *michū monogashira* [provisional road-march unit commander], served in attendance accompanying the lord's procession down to Aizu, and returned [to Edo] in the sixth month of the same year.
