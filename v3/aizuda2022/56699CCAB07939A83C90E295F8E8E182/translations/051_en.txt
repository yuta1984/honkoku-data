《Marginal note: 4th generation; adoptive heir》
**Magosaburō Toshinobu** *(initially known as Tetsuemon)* 《Marginal note: Born in Genroku 16, the Year of the Sheep [1703]》
　Biological (father): Fourth son of Ichinose Jingozaemon Naonori
　Wife / Adoptive father: Daughter of Jirōzaemon Toshitake 《Marginal note: Died of illness on the 10th day of the 2nd month of Kansei 2, the Year of the Dog [1790]. Posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*): Hōjuin Myōzen Nichihō Daishi. Officiant (*dōshi*). Place of burial: same as above.》

1. In the 12th month of Genroku 4, the Year of the Sheep [1691], in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (*o-memie*) with the lord.

2. In the 1st month of Kyōhō 9, the Year of the Dragon [1724], in accordance with his parent's petition, his wish to change his name to Magosaburō was granted in accordance with the request.

3. In the 4th month of Kyōhō 10, the Year of the Snake [1725], in accordance with his parent's petition, he was appointed as his parent's replacement guard (*ban-dai*),

**[Left folio]**

　　and he served as castle guard (*o-shiro go-ban*) in the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon.

4. In the 3rd month of Kyōhō 15, the Year of the Dog [1730], the unit commander (*kashira*) Hyūga Saburōemon was ordered to retire, and he was subsequently reassigned to the unit of Bukawa Gensuke.

5. In the 2nd month of Kyōhō 17, the Year of the Rat [1732], the unit commander Bukawa Gensuke was appointed as *wakadoshiyori* (junior elder), and he was consequently reassigned to the unit of Miyake Magobei.

6. In the 10th month of Kyōhō 19, the Year of the Tiger [1734], he traveled up to Edo for rotational duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 4th month of the following Year of the Rabbit [1735].

7. In the 9th month of Genbun 1, the Year of the Dragon [1736], the hereditary stipend (*ato-shiki go-chigyō*) of 250 koku was conferred upon him without any discrepancy or reduction.
