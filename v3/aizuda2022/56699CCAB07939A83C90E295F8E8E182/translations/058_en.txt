**Hanzō Toshinori**　*(initial name: Isasaburō; born in the 9th month of An'ei 5, the Year of the Monkey [1776])*

> Mother: Same as above [i.e., same as the previous entry's mother].

> Wife: An adopted daughter of Saigō Heizō Chikasato.
> *(Side note: Died on the 13th day of the 12th month of Bunsei 4, the Year of the Snake [1821]. Posthumous Buddhist name: Chien'in Myōri Nichishō Daishi. Officiating priest and place of burial are the same as previously recorded.)*
> — Her birth mother was a daughter of Suwa Kingo Mitsuyoshi.

> [Second wife:] A daughter of Tanaka Saburōbei Gensai.
> *(Side note: Born on the 6th day of the 9th month of Kansei 1, the Year of the Rooster [1789].)*

1. In the intercalary 2nd month of Kansei 4, the Year of the Rat [1792], in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (*o-mie*) with the lord.

**[Left folio]**

1. In the 5th month of Kansei 12, the Year of the Monkey [1800], his inherited stipend (*atoshiki go-chigyō*) of 250 *koku* was confirmed and granted to him without alteration; he was appointed as an *ometsuke*-affiliated *tozama* retainer (*go-karō tsuki tozama-shi*), assigned to the 3rd unit (*sanbangumi*), and assumed duty as castle guard (*go-jō go-ban*).

1. In the 3rd month of Kyōwa 1, the Year of the Rooster [1801], at the occasion of the school's poetry gathering (*waka-kai*), he was ordered to assist the instructors (*shihan*) in all related matters.

1. In the 8th month of Kyōwa 2, the Year of the Dog [1802], having long applied himself diligently to the composition of *waka* poetry, and having composed over one hundred verses (*hyakushu*) within a set time at the domain school (*daigakkō*), and as his skill and execution were reported to be admirable, he was awarded as a reward thirty sheets of *kotaka-gami* (a type of fine paper).
