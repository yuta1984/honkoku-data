**[Right folio]**

　　　　(Note: After this, he received commendations on numerous occasions.)

- In the second month of the same ninth year of the Snake [Bunka 9, 1812], on the occasion of Lord Teisho's wedding ceremony, he was ordered to attend at the great hall of the Shiba Palace, and having served throughout the ceremony, he was granted two ryō and two bu as a clothing allowance.

- In the third month of the same year, he was provisionally appointed to the combined duties of guardian of the Mita residence and permanent fire watch officer.

- In the fourth month of the same year, as there were new construction works on the tenement buildings at the Mita residence, along with other repairs and renovations, he was ordered to serve as requested through a petition for the attendance of a inspector [metsuke].

**[Left folio]**

- In the same month, having served in duties related to the wedding ceremony, he was granted sake.

- In the fifth month of the same year, he was ordered to present himself at every occasion of Lady Ryōtokuin's spear-arts practice sessions, to serve as her practice partner.

- In the twelfth month of the same year, having diligently served as supervisor of swordsmanship and spear-arts both in Edo and Aizu, and having successfully cultivated the various students, he was granted two pieces of silver as a reward for his meritorious service.
