**[Right folio]**

1. In the 2nd month of An'ei 3, the Year of the Horse [1774], his request to change his name to Jirōzaemon was granted in accordance with his petition.

2. In the 12th month of An'ei 5, the Year of the Monkey [1776], his commanding officer (*kashira*) Tanaka Kahei was reassigned to a different post, and he was consequently transferred to the unit of Kasuga Jinzaemon as his successor post.

3. In the 11th month of An'ei 9, the Year of the Rat [1780], he was appointed as *o-metsuke* (inspector/censor).

4. In the 4th month of Tenmei 1, the Year of the Ox [1781], he traveled up to Edo for *kinban* (alternate duty service), and returned in the 10th month of the same year.
　　　　*Note: Although there was also duty service in the military staff quarters (*jinchū o-heya*) during this period, it was declared that he had performed his duties with great diligence.*

5. In the 5th month of Tenmei 5, the Year of the Snake [1785], he was appointed as *Yama Bugyō* (Mountain Magistrate/Commissioner of Forestry).

**[Left folio]**

6. In the 2nd month of Tenmei 8, the Year of the Monkey [1788], in regard to the clearing and management of unwanted trees (*akugi*) across various domain forests, the securing of the *o-hiraki-kin* (development/operating funds), and the performance of extraordinary duties beyond his regular responsibilities, he was awarded one set of formal hemp clothing (*ko-o-asa kamishimo*) as a reward for his meritorious service.

7. In the 4th month of the same year, it was officially declared that he had provided appropriate and effective direction during the transport of ship planks (*funaita*).

8. In the 3rd month of Kansei 2, the Year of the Dog [1790], for having properly managed the procurement of *go-yōzai* (timber requisitioned for official use) during the previous year, resulting in benefit to the domain, and for having extended his guidance to those under his supervision in all related matters, performing his duties with great diligence, he was awarded five pieces of silver (*ginshi go-mai*) as a reward.
