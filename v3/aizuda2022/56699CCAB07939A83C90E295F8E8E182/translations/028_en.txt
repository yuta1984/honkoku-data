**[Right folio]**

- In the twelfth month of the same year [Kyōwa 3, 1803], during the examination of multiple cases of inquiry [suguchiの糺] and the occasion of the general amnesty [taisha], he diligently carried out the review of numerous petitions for pardon [shagan]; for this service, he was granted one bundle of cotton as a mark of commendation.

- In Bunka 1, the Year of the Ox (Note #1), having also diligently served in duties for Lord Teisho, he was granted two bundles of cotton as a mark of commendation.

- In the twelfth month of Bunka 2, the Year of the Ox (1805), upon the occasion of the conveyance of Lord Teisho's mortal remains [gosonkai] down to the domain, he was ordered to serve both in the duties of the escort and as a member of the escort itself.

- In the first month of the third year, the Year of the Tiger (Bunka 3, 1806), he served in the escort of Lord Teisho's mortal remains, duly departed [to Aizu], and returned [to Edo] in the second month of the same year.

- In the third month of the same year, he was ordered to serve concurrently as spearmanship instructor [sōjutsu-shi]

**[Left folio]**

　　for the domain retainers in Edo, in addition to his other duties.

- In the second month of the fourth year, the Year of the Rabbit (Bunka 4, 1807), having diligently served for many years, and as there was large-scale construction work [ofushin] at both main halls and the row-houses [nagaya] of the upper residence [kami-oyashiki], he assiduously supervised the work, making frequent rounds of the construction sites where large numbers of townspeople craftsmen and workers had gathered, ensuring strict oversight and proper management; and furthermore, from the autumn of two years prior through the spring of the preceding year, as there had been a great volume of duties with insufficient personnel, he had skillfully coordinated the allocation of tasks and given appropriate directions, thereby rendering especially distinguished service.
