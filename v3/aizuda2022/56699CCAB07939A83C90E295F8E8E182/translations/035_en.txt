- In the fourth month of the same year: in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his acting post as District Magistrate [kōri-bugyō kari-yaku].

- In the eighth month of the same year: it was proclaimed that he was to concurrently hold the acting post of District Magistrate [kōri-bugyō kari-yaku], limited to investigative duties [tadashi-kata go-yō].

- In the twelfth month of the same year: as it was reported that he had applied himself diligently to multiple investigative cases [kazu-kuchi no tadashi] and had handled them with resolute decisiveness, he was awarded three pieces of silver [ginshi san-mai] as a mark of commendation.

- In the first month of the thirteenth year, the Year of the Rat [Bunsei 13 / Tenpō 1, 1830]: as a foreign vessel [ikoku-sen] had drifted ashore at Shimoda Harbor in Izu Province [Zuishū Shimoda minato], and as it was necessary to dispatch a contingent of guards, he was informed that he should make preparations with all haste in accordance with the standing orders for deployment [shutsujin no o-sadame], and

**[Left folio]**

...that he should ensure, without any impediment, the arrangement of all matters pertaining to both general administrative control [shoshimari-kata] and his concurrent official duties [kenmu go-yō]. On the thirteenth day of that same month, past the Hour of the Rabbit [yotsu-doki, approximately 10 a.m.], the full contingent set sail for Shimoda in Izu Province; on the fourteenth day they arrived at that location, carried out their official duties without delay, and returned to their station [kijin] on the eleventh day of the third month of the same year.

- In the same year: in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his assignment to pyrotechnics and gunnery duties [kajutsu go-yō-kakari].
