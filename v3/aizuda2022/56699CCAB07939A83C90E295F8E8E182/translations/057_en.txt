**[Right folio]**

(Continued from previous page) — for his diligent efforts and proper management in matters pertaining to repairs (*go-shūfuku*) and the like, and on account of these various circumstances, he was awarded as a reward one secondhand *katabira* (an unlined summer garment) and three *mai* of silver.

1. In the 4th month of Kansei 10, the Year of the Horse [1798], he ascended to Edo on rotation duty (*kinban*), whereupon he was appointed as Permanent-Post Construction Commissioner (*jō-zume go-fushin bugyō*); accordingly, he descended from Edo in the 8th month of the same year.

2. In the 5th month of Kansei 11, the Year of the Sheep [1799], for his proper direction and supervision of administrative affairs, and for his wholehearted efforts to prevent any losses to the domain, he was awarded as a reward three *mai* of silver and two bundles of cotton (*wata*).

**[Left folio]**

1. On the 2nd day of the 4th month of Kansei 12, the Year of the Monkey [1800], he died of illness at the age of sixty. His posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*) was Enri-in Chitei Nichiryo Koji. His officiating priest (*dōshi*) and place of burial (*sōsho*) are the same as previously recorded.
