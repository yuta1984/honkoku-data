## Genealogy of Yoshimura Heihachi
- **Clan name (sei):** Minamoto (Gen)
- **Province of origin:** Ōmi
- **Standard family crest (jōmon):** Marunouchi Kuginuki (a nail-puller design within a circle)
- **Camp crest (makumon):** Same as above

---

**Fifth son of Yoshimura Kangayu Tomotsugu (朝親)**

### Yoshimura Jūemon Tomomitsu (朝実)
- Childhood name: Shichisaburō; also known as: Kaneya
- Born in the year of the Goat (未), Meireki 1 (1655)

---

**[Regarding his mother]**
- Daughter of Tsuzuki Jizaemon Yoshiyasu, senior retainer (karō) to Lord Itakura, Governor of Suō
- Died of illness on the 16th day of the 8th month, Genroku 11 (1698), at the age of 83
- Posthumous Buddhist name: Kagakuin-dono Ganyoju Teida-daishi
- Place of burial: Shōjuin Temple, Nishitani, Maruoka, Echizen Province

---

**[Regarding his wife]**
- Daughter of a certain Kanbe (神戸)
- Died of illness on the 29th day of the 11th month, Enkyō 3 (1746), at the age of 80
- Posthumous Buddhist name: Kenshōin Kankin Teishin Daishi
- Place of burial: Chōsenji Temple, Tameike, Edo, Musashi Province

---

**1. Twelfth month, Kanbun 9 (1669), Year of the Rooster**
- Summoned to serve as a page (okoshō) to Lord Dōshin (土津様), and granted a stipend of ten ryō and four (monme) in gold certificates (okitte-kin).
