　　He was appointed as a hired guard (o-yatoi) for the outer retainers' great hall watch (Tozama-shi Ohiroma-ban), and in connection with this, was granted an allowance for clothing (着服代).
　　　　Note: Thereafter, on the occasions of invitations extended by the Senior Councilors (Rōjū-sama) and when distinguished guests were present, he was repeatedly appointed as a page escort (o-koshō o-tomo-ban) and serving attendant (o-kyūji) on a hired basis, and on each occasion was granted a clothing allowance.

Item:　　In the 3rd month of Enkyō 4, the year of the Rabbit (1747), he was granted five ryō in gold and a stipend for three persons, and in the capacity of (treating him as) an heir (chakushi), he was appointed as a hired attendant of the personal guard (o-kinjo-ban) of Ganshōin-sama.
　　　　Note: Subsequently, his role allowance (o-yakuryō) and expenses were increased, and his allotment voucher (o-kippu)

[Left folio]
　　　　was raised to twelve ryō in gold.

Item:　　In the 7th month of Hōreki 4, the year of the Dog (1754), as requested, his appointment as a hired personal guard attendant (o-kinjo-ban o-yatoi) was rescinded.

Item:　　In the 7th month of the same [Hōreki] 7, the year of the Ox (1757), he was granted, without any discrepancy, the family headship (katoku) and a stipend of one hundred koku, and because his father had diligently served for several decades, he was appointed to the Jōzume Tozama-gumi (常詰外様組, the Permanent Outer Retainer Unit).

Item:　　In the same month, he was assigned to the unit of Takahashi Koemon.

Item:　　In the 10th month of the same year, on the occasion of the funeral procession of Jusen'in-sama, he was appointed as a hired escort guard (o-tomo-ban o-yatoi) and duly performed escort duty.
