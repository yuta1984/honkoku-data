- In the twelfth month of Kansei 8 (Year of the Dragon): He was appointed to the courtesy audience with the lord (O-tōri O-memie), assigned as a hire at the Seki Oyōsho (a designated administrative office), ranked just below Munekawa Naoki, and was appointed above the junior officials (ko-yakunin) of the Okanjōsho.

- In the third month of Kansei 12 (Year of the Monkey): In recognition of his diligent service during Lord Sadaaki's sojourn in the domain, he was granted three ryō of silver as a reward.

- In the twelfth month of Kyōwa 1 (Year of the Rooster): In recognition of his diligent service as an officer in charge of the general amnesty (taisha) duties, he was granted three hundred hiki in gold as a reward.
