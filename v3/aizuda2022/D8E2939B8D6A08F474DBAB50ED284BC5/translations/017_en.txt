(Continued from previous page) He was appointed as a temporary hire at the Oyōbeya (senior officials' administrative chamber).

- In the eleventh month of the same year (Kansei 2): He was ordered to additionally take on the duties of the Jōwa (regular reconciliation/settlement of accounts) at the Oyōbeya, alongside his existing responsibilities.

- In the twelfth month of Kansei 4 (Year of the Rat): He was granted three bu of gold as a reward for having carefully and properly compiled the Zenkōchō (a register of commendable conduct/virtuous deeds) that was submitted to the central government (the Tokugawa Shogunate).

- In the ninth month of Kansei 5 (Year of the Ox): In recognition of the meritorious service of both father and son, and on account of exceptional circumstances, one koku was reduced from the late father's Ogisaku (stipend assessed on the basis of estimated crop yield), and he was granted six koku and two-person rations (ninbuchi),
