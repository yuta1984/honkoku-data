- In the same year (Tenmei 8, Year of the Monkey), fourth month: He was appointed to assist the Hakama-shikikata (the office responsible for formal ceremonial dress procedures).

- In the first month of Kansei 1 (Year of the Rooster), sixth month: He was appointed to assist with the Sō-ichishi Okanjō Mokuroku Aratame (the comprehensive review of the single-sheet financial/inventory records).

- In the intercalary sixth month of the same year: As he had requested, he was relieved of his duties assisting the Reishikikata (the office responsible for ceremonial protocols).
　　Note: During the period he served in the above assistance role, he was granted two bu of gold at a time as compensation for expenses incurred.
