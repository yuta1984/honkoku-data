Item: In the tenth month of Enkyō 4 (Year of the Rabbit), in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his duties as an attendant (tsukibito), and was assigned to the position of muryaku (no stipendiary role) under the jurisdiction of the Ginjosho (Investigative Office).

Item: In the tenth month of Hōreki 3 (Year of the Rooster), he was appointed as a scribe (monokaki) at the Yama-yakusho (Mountain Administration Office).

Item: In the first month of Meiwa 5 (Year of the Rat), in accordance with his petition, his gisaku (fictitious/allotted crop yield assessment) stipend was rescinded; however, in recognition of his many years of faithful service, he was granted two ryō of gold annually and was instructed to continue serving for as long as he was able.
