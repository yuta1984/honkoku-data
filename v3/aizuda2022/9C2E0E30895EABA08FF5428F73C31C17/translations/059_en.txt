- In An'ei 4 (Hitsujidoshi, year of the Sheep; month unknown): He was appointed as御料理人 (culinary official), granted a stipend on an annual basis, and was additionally ordered to continue serving concurrently in the role of御穀類役 (grain/provisions officer).

- In the same [An'ei] 5 (Sarudoshi, year of the Monkey), eleventh month: Upon the passing of Lady Rikōin (wife of Lord Matsudaira Katanobu), he was relieved of his post under the吟味所 (investigative office) administration and placed in a non-official capacity, and was appointed to the Aizu domestic affairs (Katte) division.

- In the same [An'ei] 8 (Inoshishi, year of the Boar), tenth month: A petition to adopt Rihyōmon, the son of Ogawa Ryōho, as his heir was granted.

- In the same year, twelfth month: Due to illness, his御擬作 (stipend/rice allocation) was revoked in accordance with his petition.

**[Left Folio]**

**Rihyōemon Yasuchika** (adopted son) — Born on the 12th day of the 1st month of Hōreki 7 (Ushidoshi, year of the Ox) [1757]

- Biological father: Eldest son of Ogawa Ryōho Mitsuyoshi
- Wife: Daughter of Suzuki Kōsoemon Shigenaga — Born on the 5th day of the 1st month of Meiwa 3 (Sarudoshi, year of the Monkey) [1766]

- In An'ei 8 (Inoshishi, year of the Boar), twelfth month: From within his adoptive father's御擬作 (stipend), one ryō was reduced, and he was granted two ryō and stipend for two persons (ninin-fuchi); he was additionally appointed to a non-official post under the吟味所 (investigative office) administration.

- In the same [An'ei] 9 (Nedoshi, year of the Rat), twelfth month: He was appointed to the provisional post of会所定番 (Kaisho standing guard, temporary appointment).
