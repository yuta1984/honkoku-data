- In the same year (month unknown), one ryō of gold was granted as an allowance for formal hakama (ceremonial trousers).

- In the 2nd year of Genbun (Year of the Snake, 1737), 10th month: Two ryō per year was granted as a hardship/effort allowance, and this was subsequently increased to three ryō per year.

- In the 4th year of the same era (Year of the Ram, 1739), 7th month: He was appointed as a trainee (minarai) at the Office of Inquiry (Ginmisho).

- In the 5th year (Year of the Monkey, 1740), 11th month: He was appointed as a hired worker (o-yatoi) at the Office of Inquiry.

- In the 3rd year of Kanpō (Year of the Boar, 1743), 5th month: He was granted eight koku and provisions for two people, and was appointed as a minor official (ko-yakunin) at the Office of Inquiry.

- In the 1st year of Hōreki (Year of the Ram, 1751) (month unknown): He served in a delegated role for the Kowatagata (small ferry/transport office), and for his diligent efforts, was granted two bu of gold as a hardship allowance.

**[Left folio]**

- In the same year, 3rd month: He was appointed, by means of a personnel review (o-ninken), to serve in a minor official capacity for the domain inspectors (o-kuni metsuke-shū).

- In the 2nd year (Year of the Monkey, 1752), 11th month: In recognition of his diligent service, he was granted two koku as an office stipend (o-yakuryō).

- In the 3rd year (Year of the Rooster, 1753), 1st month: In recognition of his tireless and exemplary service for the domain inspectors, he was rewarded with one ryō and one bu of gold as a commendation.

- In the same year (month unknown): For his service in the delegated role of o-kane-barai (payments/disbursements officer), he was granted one ryō of gold as an office stipend.

- In the 6th year (Year of the Rat, 1756) (month unknown): He served in a delegated role for castle duties (o-shiro go-yō), and was granted one ryō and one bu per year as an ongoing hardship allowance.
