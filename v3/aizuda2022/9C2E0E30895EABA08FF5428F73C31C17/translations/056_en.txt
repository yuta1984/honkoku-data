**[Right folio]**

The request was granted as wished.

- In the fifth month of the same (Bunsei) 4th year (Year of the Snake), he was provisionally appointed to the temporary post of secret affairs (*mitsujigoto tōza kariyaku*).

- In the seventh month of the same 6th year (Year of the Sheep), he was appointed to the post of secret affairs service (*mitsujigoto tsutome*).

- In the tenth month of the same 7th year (Year of the Monkey), upon the re-establishment of the magistrate's office (*bugyōsho*), he diligently performed the numerous administrative duties throughout, and having established proper standards for clerical work, he appropriately investigated various difficult procurement matters with an eye to the future, arriving at a general plan. Furthermore, regarding the matter of increasing the community granary (*shakura*),

**[Left folio]**

he labored with particular distinction and achieved meritorious results. Consequently, in addition to an increase of three *koku* five *to* in his official stipend (*yakuryō*), he was additionally granted a stipend of one person's ration (*hitori fuchi*) in direct reward.
