Jirō (adopted son) — (Divorced/Separated)
　　Biological father: Second son of Tawara Asaburō Katsushige

Yashirō (某, personal name unknown)
　　Biological father: Second son of Kaneko Chūzaemon Naoharu
　　Wife: Daughter of adoptive father Hyōe Naoyori *(Note: Died on the 20th day of the 12th month of Hōreki 13 [1763]; posthumous Buddhist name: Chikō Kenryō Shinnyō; burial site: Shōunzan Zenryūji Temple)*

Item: In the 7th month of Enkyō 2 (Year of the Ox [1745]), he was granted eight koku and two-man-fuchi from within his father's *gisaku* (provisional stipend allocation), and was assigned to oversee the *muyaku gimmi-sho* (office of inspection for those without assigned duties).

**[Left folio]**

Item: In (month unknown) of the same 3rd year (Year of the Tiger [1746]), he was appointed to the rank of *okachi* (foot soldier/attendant), granted an additional four koku stipend, and in the same year traveled to Edo for *kinban* (rotational guard duty).

Item: In the 4th month of Kan'en 1 (Year of the Dragon [1748]), he was ordered to accompany the lord on his *sankin* (alternate attendance) procession, both departing and returning, and traveled to Edo.

Item: In (month unknown) of the same 2nd year (Year of the Snake [1749]), he was assigned to serve as an inspector/witness for the *zankok aratame* (inspection of remaining grain stocks).

Item: In (month unknown) of the same 4th year (Year of the Ram [1751]), he was assigned to serve as a witness for the *onōnō tatiai* (official rice revenue inspection), and was granted two *bu* in gold as a reward for his efforts.

Item: In (month unknown) of Hōreki 4 (Year of the Dog [1754]), he traveled to Edo for rotational guard duty (*kinban*), completed one tour of duty, and returned home.
