[Right folio]
　　(Continued from previous entry) For his diligent service, he was granted sake as a reward.

One item: The same month.
　　Regarding the funeral arrangements for the Two Lords (Lord Sadasada and Lord Sadaaki) and their mausoleum duties, he was informed that it had reached the attention of the Lord that he had worked tirelessly day and night in performing these duties.

One item: The sixth year of the same era (Year of the Snake), eighth month. As Ezo (Hokkaido) had come under the direct control of the domain's lord (the Shogunate), and duties were numerous, he was commended for his diligent service.

One item: The seventh year of the same era (Year of the Horse), second month. For overseeing the construction and repair of the two mausolea at Innai from the very beginning through to completion, and for his exceptional dedication and labor, he was appointed Inspector of Construction Works (Ofushinkata Gimmiyakunin), and granted a stipend of one koku five to of rice and one person's ration allowance.

[Left folio]
One item: The same year, twelfth month. Regarding the residential construction along Ōmachi street, which was an urgent construction project given the circumstances of the times, he thoroughly examined every detail and devoted himself wholeheartedly to completing the work. As a reward for his service, he was granted one hundred hiki in gold.

One item: The ninth year of the same era (Year of the Monkey), twelfth month. Regarding the renovation of the lord's quarters at Mineyama and the replacement of the foundation of the main shrine, which was the first undertaking of its kind, he was thorough in his inspection and diligent in his efforts. As a reward, he was granted three pieces of silver.

One item: The tenth year of the same era (Year of the Rooster), second month, ninth day. He died of illness at the age of fifty-five. Posthumous Buddhist name: Sokutoku-in Shaku Zenjō Dōshi. Place of burial: Shōkyōji Temple, Kashiwabarayama, Ōkuboyama.
