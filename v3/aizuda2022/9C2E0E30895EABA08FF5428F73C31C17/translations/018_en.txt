**[Right folio]**

- In the 11th year of the same era (Year of the Snake) (month unknown): He served in a delegated role for the castle's small ferry/transport office (o-shiro kowatagata), and was granted three bu of gold as a hardship allowance.

- In the 12th year (Year of the Horse) (month unknown): He was appointed to attend to the domain's stables (o-umaya tachi-iri-kin), and without fail each year was granted one ryō, or alternatively one ryō and two bu, as a hardship allowance.

- In the 13th year (Year of the Ram), 12th month: In recognition of his diligent practice and devoted service in his current duties, he was granted directly an increase of two koku in his office stipend (o-yakuryō).

- In the 2nd year of Meiwa (Year of the Rooster) (month unknown): He was assigned to the Yamagata garrison duty (Yamagata kinban) by means of a personnel appointment (o-hito-sashi), and at that location was appointed to duties overseeing the receipt and disbursement of funds (motojiме kinsen ukebara) as well as delegated roles managing supplies and the dismissal/management of attendants (o-chūgen). He was granted allowances and expense supplements amounting to approximately three ryō per year in total.

---

**[Left folio]**

- In the 3rd year of the same era (Year of the Dog), 5th month: He was commended (o-shōbi) for having completed the above duties without any delay or obstruction and having returned home.

- In the 4th year (Year of the Boar), 3rd month: He was appointed to the fixed post of the additional revenue office (zōka-kin-kata sadame-nin'yaku), and was granted three ryō of gold per year as a hardship allowance.

- In the 6th year of An'ei (Year of the Rooster), 4th month: In recognition of his diligent service and the fact that he was a person of standing within his unit (saki-gumi), he was granted the rank of independent ceremonial greeting (dokurei), and his seat was placed immediately after that of Kasuga Kibei.

- In the 7th year of the same era (Year of the Dog), 11th month, 6th day: He died of illness at the age of fifty-eight. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Kōjōin Shakushu Seimu. Place of burial: same as above.

**Daughter:** Married to Watanabe Rokurōbei Hisatoyo (渡部六郎兵衛久運).
