**[Right folio]**

One item: In the eighth month of the same year,
On the occasion of the distribution of the late Lord Teisho's (貞昭様) personal effects, one small robe (*kosode*) and one silk summer garment (*katabira*) were privately bestowed upon him.

One item: In the eleventh month of the same year, from the temporary autumn duties through to that spring, as he had served diligently day and night without any difficulties despite a heavy workload, two *bu* in gold were bestowed upon him.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year,
On the occasion of the lord's residence at his quarters, as he had served diligently in official duties, sake was privately bestowed upon him.
　　　　Note: This was also bestowed upon him in subsequent years.

One item: In the first month of the sixth year (Year of the *Mi*/Snake), he was ordered to serve as an envoy to Ise, and departed on the fourth day of that month.

**[Left folio]**

Note: As the men who had been dispatched to Ezo (Hokkaido) in the previous year (Year of the *Tatsu*/Dragon) had returned to camp without incident, he was ordered to conduct prayers of thanksgiving for their safe return (*jōfuku*), and after completing both ritual prayers without incident and returning bearing the protective amulets (*omamori fuda*), he received the following:
　　　　From Lady Juhōin: 200 *hiki* in gold bestowed upon him.
　　　　From Lady Renjuin and
　　　　From the Lord: 100 *hiki* in gold bestowed upon him.

One item: In the third month of the seventh year (Year of the *Uma*/Horse),
As the lord had relocated his residence, and as he had served diligently in official duties up to that point, 200 *hiki* in gold were privately bestowed upon him.
