【Right folio】
When the same lord (Hoshina Masayuki) grew older, (Nao) was summoned and served as his companion (the nature of "gigi-saku" is unknown).

In the 12th month of Genna 3 (1617), the year of the Cow,
When Lord Hoshina (Masayuki) had grown,
He accompanied Lord Dogishi (Hoshina Masamitsu) to his castle at Takato in the Inabu district of Shinano Province (present-day Nagano Prefecture),
serving as an attendant at the age of 11.

Note:
When Lord Hoshina (Masayuki) was in Edo, (Nao) also attended in the inner quarters,
and was employed day and night in the lord's close attendance (御渕廻り).

One entry, year and month unknown: Upon reaching adulthood, he was appointed to the position of御弓之者 (archer/bow attendant).

【Left folio】
One entry, 8th month of Kan'ei 13 (1636), year of the Rat: At the time of the domain transfer to Mogami in Dewa Province,
he accompanied the lord and received an increase of 10 bales (hyō), bringing his stipend to 18 koku and support for 2 persons.
He was appointed to the rank of御歩行 (foot soldier/retainer on foot), and was told that he would be promoted in the east,
receiving various gracious words of encouragement. He also received a formal haori coat from the lord, and thereafter was repeatedly summoned
and granted various gifts and appointments.

One entry, 12th month of the same year 18 (1641), year of the Snake: He received an increase of 10 bales (hyō).

One entry, 8th month of the same year 20 (1643), year of the Ram: At the time of the domain transfer to Aizu,
he accompanied the lord, but having fallen ill,
he petitioned and was assigned to serve as a hearth-side guard (御長囲炉裏御番).

One entry, 2nd month of Keian 4 (1651), year of the Rabbit: Due to excessive drinking, he was regarded as an unruly and unmanageable person,
but as various people interceded on his behalf,
he was (allowed to continue serving as) foot patrol guard within the castle (御城中之足御番)...
