(He was) ordered to take on the duties, and henceforth was ordered to serve in the role of managing the receipts and disbursements of the candle storehouse (rōsoku-gura) and paper storehouse (kami-gura).

Item: In the third month of the same eighth year (Year of the Sheep), he was ordered to complete both the receipts/disbursements of the three honorable storehouses and the final accounts (hon-chū kantei), then return home. He completed the final accounts on schedule, handed over to his successor, and on the fourteenth day of the fifth month of the same year, he was granted permission to depart.

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, until the personnel arrangements for Sōshū (Sagami Province) were settled, while continuing his primary duties, he was ordered to concurrently serve as

a minor official of the Sōshū accounting office (go-kantei-kata ko-yakunin).

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent service and his efforts in investigating and resolving the matter of outstanding provisional promissory notes (kari-tegata) in Edo: he traveled to Edo, consulted with various parties regarding the large-scale settlement of the previous year's outstanding provisional notes, properly investigated matters, fulfilled various assigned duties, established procedures and future regulations for accounting, and applied himself diligently to his work. In acknowledgment of these exceptional efforts, his stipend of twelve ryō was converted to four koku, and his office salary (yakuryō) of one ryō and three bu was converted to three koku and five to.
