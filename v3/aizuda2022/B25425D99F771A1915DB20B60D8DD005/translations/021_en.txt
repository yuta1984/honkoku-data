[Right folio]
　　On the 16th of the same month, he arrived in Edo and remained at the Wadakura residence, where he made the necessary arrangements for the Kyoto mission. He departed Edo on the 21st, and arrived in Kyoto on the 4th day of the 9th month. Having completed the required preparations, on the 4th day of the 10th month, a message arrived from the chief retainer (御用人) of the Yoshida house, stating that the posthumous name (御霊号) would be formally presented on the following 5th day, and that Sasahara Isazaburō and his party should appear at the Yoshida mansion at the hour of Five-and-a-half (around 9 a.m.). Accordingly, on the following 5th day, they duly appeared at the appointed hour. The posthumous name in the form of a Sōgen Senji (宗源宣旨) was presented to Isazaburō. On that occasion, he was summoned to the presence of Lord Yoshida himself, granted an audience, and received warm and gracious words from him. He was also treated to a formal meal in the antechamber (御次).

[Left folio]
　　On the 7th of the same month, the written communication (御書) addressed to Lord Teishō was handed over. On the 8th, the procession carrying the posthumous name departed Kyoto, and on the 28th, he duly accompanied the party to the Sannomaru Palace without incident.

Item: In the 11th month of the same 3rd year (1806), Year of the Tiger, having diligently performed his numerous duties from the previous autumn through that spring, and having also previously served in the provisional role of Inspector of Foot Soldiers (御徒目付仮役) during his journey to Kyoto, and having escorted the posthumous name back to the domain without incident, he was recognized for his exceptional dedication and hard work, and was granted 500 hiki in gold as a reward.
