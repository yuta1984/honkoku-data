(Continued from previous page) For diligently attending to duties with care toward economy of effort, a reward of two ryō of silver was granted.

One item: In the eleventh month of Bunka 2 (1805), the year of the Ox, regarding the preparation of maps of the horse-grazing grounds of the three villages of Ōtera, Honji, and Migami, as well as other tasks, it was noted that extraordinary services had been rendered; accordingly, two ryō of silver was granted as a reward.

One item: In the eighth month of the same sixth year (1809), the year of the Snake, regarding the compilation of the *Shinpen Aizu Fudoki* (New Edition of the Aizu Topography), there were locations at several temple sites where taxable land was intermingled within temple precincts; as these were carefully investigated so as to serve as future proof of the boundary distinctions,

One item: In the fifth month of the same eighth year (1811), the year of the Sheep, for diligently and capably managing various extraordinary duties including cadastral surveys and land measurement (*sao-ire*) of several villages, five ryō of silver was granted as a reward.

One item: In the third month of the same ninth year (1812), the year of the Monkey, for going out on duty and working diligently in the examination of the land exchange (*o-hikikaeri-chi*) at that time, sake was granted as a reward.
