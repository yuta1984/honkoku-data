Item: In the 6th month of Bunka 1 (1804), Year of the Rat, having served diligently in the duties relating to Lord Kinbun's shrine visit (御宮参り), he was granted sake in recognition of his service.

Item: In the 6th month of the same year, he was instructed to henceforth take charge of all duties relating to the ritual ceremonies (御祭式) of temples and shrines, and was to serve as the standing officer in charge thereof. He served at every ceremony held at the Mine-yama Shrine and its associated burial site (御墳), and naturally at every ceremony at the Toyooka Shrine and the In'nai burial site, as well as at all other temple and shrine ceremonies and

[Left folio]
　　religious rites (御法式). He was entrusted single-handedly with all ceremonial duties, and served concurrently as the official in charge of investigating and coordinating the ceremonial procedures (御式取調役詰).

Item: In the intercalary 8th month of the same 2nd year (1805), Year of the Ox, concerning the matter of conferring the posthumous Buddhist/Shinto name (御霊号) upon Lord Kyōtei, which as per precedent should have been requested of the Yoshida house, there were particular circumstances that necessitated direct negotiations with the Yoshida house. Accordingly, Sasahara Isazaburō was to be dispatched to Kyoto as an envoy for this purpose. He was ordered to serve in this capacity while continuing his regular duties, traveling in the provisional role of御徒目付 (inspector of foot soldiers), and departed on the 11th day of the same month [... continues on the next page].
