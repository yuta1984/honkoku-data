**[Right folio]**

Item: In the twelfth month of the fourth year of the same era (Year of the Rat), as he had performed duties beyond his regular assigned duties (*teireide yaku*) and incurred expenses in so doing, he was granted one *bu* of gold.
　　　Note: From this point forward, payment was to be made on a daily pro-rated basis (*hiwari*).

Item: In the eighth month of the seventh year of the same era (Year of the Rabbit), in connection with his village inspection rounds (*kaison*), he was appointed to serve in a full/principal capacity (*hon'yaku*) for the Land Survey Office (*kenchikata*).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was appointed as a Land Survey official (*kenchi yakunin*) and granted three *to* of official stipend (*oyakuryō*).

**[Left folio]**

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, during the fire that spread to and destroyed the domain's residence (*oyashiki*) at Shiba in Edo, he personally transported various materials and supplies, and was rewarded with sake.

Item: In the sixth month of the eleventh year of the same era (Year of the Sheep), it was officially announced that he had diligently attended to the reduction of scheduled days (*hinami no genkata*) at the upper lodging (*kamijuku*) in the country villages (*gōson*).

Item: In the third month of the second year of Kyōwa (Year of the Dog), he was appointed to the post of cash payment officer for charcoal and firewood (*sumi takigi genbara ninvaku*).
