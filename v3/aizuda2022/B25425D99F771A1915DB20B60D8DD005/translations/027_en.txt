**[Right folio]**
(Continued from previous page) ...an increase (*kamasú*) of two *koku* five *to* in official stipend (*yakuryō*) was directly granted and bestowed.

- Item: In the third month of the fourteenth year (Year of the Ox), due to the strict austerity measures (*shōryaku*) now being ordered, he was commanded to continue serving in the roles of the Ritual Affairs Office (*Saiji-kata*) and the Shrine Funds management (*Shinryō-gakari*) under the reduced/consolidated arrangement.

- Item: In the sixth month of the same year, having been appointed to garrison duty (*rusubí*), he was additionally ordered to serve as a guard of the Innai Mountain (*Innai-yama*).

- Item: In the tenth month of the same year, it was announced that his title was to be changed to the combined position of Inspector of the Ritual Affairs Office (*Saiji Yakusho Gimmiyaku*) and Inspector of the Public Works Office (*Osakuji-kata Gimmiyaku*).

**[Left folio]**

- Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, it was announced that the supplementary expense allowance (*hiho-kin*) would continue to be granted as before — three *bu* per year — without deduction (*go-kariagé nashi*).

- Item: In the twelfth month of the first year of Bunsei (Year of the Tiger), having diligently served for many years, and having devoted himself fully from the outset to the construction works (*fusin*) of the Sericulture Association (*Saiyōsha*), and having also served with great dedication in the new construction of the meeting hall (*kaisho*), in recognition of his especially outstanding conduct, it was proclaimed that he would be elevated in rank above the *Sekimawari-mai Yakunin* (席廻米役人, Officials of the Seat-Rotation Rice Stipend).

- Item: In the fifth month of the second year (Year of the Rabbit), he was appointed to serve in the Third Division garrison duty (*rusubi san-ban*), Twelfth Unit (*jūni-ban-gumi*), Yoriai-gumi (*yoriai-gumi*, an assembly/combined unit).
