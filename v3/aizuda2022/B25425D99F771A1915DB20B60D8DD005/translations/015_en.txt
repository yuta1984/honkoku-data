One item: In the eleventh month of the same eleventh year (1814), the year of the Dog, for diligently serving over a number of years, five hundred *hiki* in gold was granted as a reward.

One item: In the ninth month of the same twelfth year (1815), the year of the Boar, a petition to change one's name to Ichizaemon was granted as requested.

One item: In the seventh month of the same thirteenth year (1816), the year of the Rat, for diligently serving over a number of years, one was appointed to the provisional position of *Kendichi Yakunin Saotō* (head surveyor among the cadastral survey officials); and although this was a position of considerable weight and responsibility, one was graciously granted the rank of *dokurei* (independent ceremonial standing),
