**[Right folio]**
Kumeuemon Yoshieki (born in the 5th year of Genroku, Year of the Monkey [1692])

- Mother: Daughter of the Yoshiwara clan
- Wife: Daughter of Kizu Masuemon (died of illness on the 27th day of the 10th month of the 8th year of Hōreki, Year of the Tiger [1758]; posthumous Buddhist name: Jizan Myōshō Shinnyo; officiating priest from Ryūenzan Shiunji Temple; burial site: Ōkuboyama)

1. In the 5th month of the 9th year of Kyōhō, Year of the Dragon (1724), he was employed as a *kaimai sairyō* (transport rice supervisor). Details regarding his assigned rice yield (*gigigisaku*) and related matters are unclear.

2. In the 5th month of the 10th year (1725), having performed his duties aboard ship in an exemplary manner, he was awarded 1,000 *mon* in copper coins (*toriме*) as a commendation.

**[Left folio]**

3. In the 6th month of the 11th year, Year of the Horse (1726), he was appointed as the permanent cargo supervisor (*jō nori*) at Kubota riverside landing.

4. In the 13th year, Year of the Monkey (1728), month unknown: having performed well in his temporary post as *kuragata-yaku* (warehouse administrator), he was awarded one *bu* in gold as a commendation.

5. In the 11th month of the 20th year, Year of the Rabbit (1735): he was newly appointed to oversee the Shirakawa wholesale house (*toiya*) and was charged with making direct payments for transport fees. Having diligently performed his duties and demonstrated sound judgment in reducing transport fees, he was awarded one *bu* in gold as a commendation.
