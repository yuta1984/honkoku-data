- Item: In the fifth month of the twelfth year (Year of the Boar), due to the reorganization of the Ceremonial Protocol Office (*Oshiki-gakari*) at this time, [his] title was changed to *Saiji-kata Chōyaku* (祭事方調役, Records Officer for Ritual Affairs), and it was announced that he would be placed under the supervision of the *Saiji Bugyō* (御祭事奉行, Magistrate of Ritual Affairs).
