**[Right folio]**

- Item: In the ninth month of the same year, as there was said to be someone unsuitable for the post (*yakuba ni fuō*), he was relieved of his current duties (*tōmu go-men*) and was appointed to the Third *Yoriai-gumi* (assembly unit) attached to the Senior Councillor (*go-karō-tsuki san no yoriai-gumi*).

- Item: In the eleventh month of the third year (Year of the Dragon), he was appointed as an Investigative Official (*shirabekyaku*) for the Ceremonial Affairs section (*go-shiki*) and the Stipend Records section (*bungenkata*).
　　Note: Regarding the manner of his duties, it was announced that matters falling under the supervision (*sahai*) of the Inspector (*metsuke*) that pertained to individual cases (*ikko*) were to be reported through the Inspector.

- Item: In the fifth month of the fourth year (Year of the Snake), it was ordered that his seat ranking (*seki*) be placed above that of Ōba Shichirō, Heiji Sase, and Toyoki.

**[Left folio]**

- Item: In the twelfth month of the fifth year (Year of the Horse), having served diligently and capably during the busy period of official duties since that spring, he was awarded three hundred *hiki* (匹) in gold as a commendation prize (*go-hōbi*).

- Item: In the third month of the sixth year (Year of the Sheep), on the occasion of enshrining (*kanjō*) the divine spirit (*go-shintai*) of Lord Kinbun (*Kinbun-sama*) at the shrine on Mount Mine (*Mineyama no go-yashiro*), he traveled to that location for said official duty and performed the ceremonial rites (*go-saishiki no go-yō*).

- Item: In the tenth month of the same year, having diligently served in the investigation and execution of duties pertaining to the enshrinement of Lord Kinbun and the accompanying ceremonial rites, he was awarded two bundles of cotton (*wata niba*) as a commendation prize.
