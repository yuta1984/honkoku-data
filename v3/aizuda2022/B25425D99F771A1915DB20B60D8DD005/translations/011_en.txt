[Continued from previous page] …he was appointed as an official in charge of revising village land allocations (*gōwarimoto aratame yakunin*).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was appointed to the post for the *Ushū* [i.e., Dewa Province] *Shōsō* [granary/grain management] duties (*sic*) and traveled up to Edo.

Item: In the fourth year of the same era (Year of the Sheep), month unknown, it was officially proclaimed that he had served diligently in the *Shōsō* duties (*sic*).

Item: In the sixth month of the fifth year of the same era (Year of the Monkey), he purchased a residence beyond his authorized rank (*bunso no yashiki*), and as an official entrusted with *jikakata* (local district) matters, this was deemed a grave act of imprudence; accordingly, he was relieved of his official post.

Item: In the second month of the seventh year of the same era (Year of the Dog), he was appointed to the *gimmisho* (Deliberation Office) jurisdiction as a person without post (*muyaku*).

**[Left folio]**

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was appointed to the provisional post in the Temple and Shrine Affairs (*jishakata*) section of the Town Office (*machi yakusho*).

Item: In the third month of the ninth year of the same era (Year of the Rat), having applied himself diligently to procuring funds for the domain's revenue (*go-irikata*), he was granted two *bu* in gold as a reward.

Item: In the intercalary fifth month of the first year of the Tenmei era (Year of the Ox), he died of illness. Posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*): Daichū Renjō. Place of burial: same as above.
