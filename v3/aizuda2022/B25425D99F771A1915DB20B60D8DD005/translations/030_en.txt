**Genealogy of Okada Konomo**
*(Marginal note: Clan name [*honsei*]: Sugawara; Standard crest [*sadamon*]: corner-cut square with five leaves of *nandina* [heavenly bamboo]; Home province [*honkoku*]: Ōshū [written as "Ōriku," sic])*

**Okada Konomo** (eldest son of Okada Gengo Sadatoshi) — Personal name [*imina*]: Sadayoshi (定好)
*(Marginal note: Common names [*tsūshō*]: first Shotarōsuke, then Harunosu, then Sadamoto; after retirement [*inkyo*]: Yōzen)*

　　Mother: daughter of the Koike clan
　　Wife: daughter of Negishi Kihei-shi Aki
*(Marginal note: Died of illness on the seventeenth day of the twelfth month of Hōreki 8 [1758], Year of the Tiger; Confucian burial; posthumous title [*okuri-na*]: Teishō Reijin; burial site: Ōkuboyama)*

- Item: Born in the second year of Jōkyō (Year of the Ox, 1685), month unknown, in the Akaoka sub-district at the end of Keirinjicho.
