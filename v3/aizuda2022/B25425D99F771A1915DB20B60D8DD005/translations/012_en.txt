**[Right folio]**

Ichizaemon Takemasa　(earlier name: Kumeremon)
　　Mother: Daughter of the Yoshimura clan
　　Wife: Daughter of Tsuchiya Jūhachi #1

Item: In the sixth month of the first year of Tenmei (Year of the Ox), two *koku* were deducted from the *gisaku* [posthumous stipend allocation] of his late father, and he was granted six *koku* two *to* and a stipend for two persons (*ninin-fuchi*); he was summoned forth as a person without post (*muyaku*) under the jurisdiction of the *gimmisho* (Deliberation Office).

Item: In the tenth month of the third year of the same era (Year of the Rabbit), he was appointed as a temporary hire (*tōza oyatoi*) for the Land Survey Office (*kenchikata*), and served until the first month of the following Year of the Dragon.

**[Left folio]**

Item: In the seventh month of the sixth year of the same era (Year of the Horse), he was appointed as a temporary hire (*tōza oyatoi*) at the Accounts Office (*okanjōsho*), and served until the tenth month of the same year.

Item: In the fifth month of the eighth year of the same era (Year of the Monkey), he was appointed to the post of *Nakanokuchi* guard (*Nakanokuchi ban*).

Item: In the fifth month of the first year of Kansei (Year of the Rooster), he was appointed as a temporary hire (*tōza oyatoi*) for the Land Survey Office (*kenchikata*).
