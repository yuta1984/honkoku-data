Item: In the 12th month of the same year, he was instructed to serve concurrently as accountant (金銭受払役人) for the Shikikata Shinryō office and as junior official (小役人) for both offices. In recognition of his diligent service in handling the accounts amid the many demands placed upon him, he was informed that he would receive, as a supplementary expense allowance, one ryō in gold each year from the shinryō funds, without any deduction (御借上なし).

Item: In the 6th month of the 7th year (1810), Year of the Horse, he was provisionally appointed to serve as a supplementary inspector (吟味役補勤) for both the Shikikata and Shinryō offices.

[Left folio]

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, he was instructed to serve as a supplementary deputy (補勤) on occasions when the incumbent inspector (吟味役) was unable to report for duty due to illness.

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, he was appointed as a junior official (小役人) of the Finance Office (御勘定所). In addition to his existing stipend, he was granted an additional stipend of one koku five to, and was instructed to serve as an officer in charge of personnel and stipend records (分限方御用懸り).

Item: In the same month, while continuing to serve in his appointed role in the personnel and stipend section (分限方), he was additionally instructed to concurrently serve in the revenue collection section (御成ヶ方).

Item: In the same month, given that he had served for many years in the affairs of the Shikikata office and had become a person of proven expertise and experience, [he was instructed that] on occasions when his primary duties permitted—[he should assist with Shikikata affairs...]
