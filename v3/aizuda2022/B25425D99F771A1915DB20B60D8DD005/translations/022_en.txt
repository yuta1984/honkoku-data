Item: In the 4th year (1807), Year of the Rabbit, in the 6th month — having attended with great care and diligence to the investigation of the ceremonies and rites concerning the enshrinement (御勧請) of the two deities (御両神様), he was, in recognition of this special service, granted the honor of a formal audience (御通御目見) by special dispensation.

Item: In the 5th year (1808), Year of the Dragon, in the 12th month — in acknowledgment of his diligent service throughout the year despite its many demands, he was informed that he would receive two bu in gold annually as a supplementary expense allowance (費補), without deduction (御借上なく).

Item: In the 6th year (1809), Year of the Snake, in the 8th month — in connection with the assignment of roles for the New Year ceremonial rites (年始御規式), he diligently performed his numerous duties, submitted his own proposals, and arranged for the streamlining of outgoing procedures within the office.

[Left folio]
　　Furthermore, having devoted himself diligently to various ad hoc matters as well, he was granted five ryō in silver as a reward.

Item: In the 9th month of the same year — in recognition of his service in the provisional role of Inspector of Foot Soldiers (御徒目付仮役) during his journey to Kyoto five years prior, in the Year of the Ox, in connection with the posthumous name (御霊号) mission, he was granted one bu in gold.

Item: In the 11th month of the same year — he was appointed to the position of financial officer (金銭受払役人) at the Shikikata Shinryō office (御式方神料役所), with his seat ranked above that of the Kirishitan-ruizoku officer (切支丹類族役人). He was further instructed to concurrently serve as a minor official (小役人) at both offices. His stipend for the role was set at one koku of rice, effective immediately—
