Wife: Daughter of Yoshimura Sakuan
　　*(Died of illness on the 8th day of the 11th month of An'ei 1 [Year of the Dragon], at the age of 33. Posthumous Buddhist name: Sekkin Chijō Daishi. Officiating priest and place of burial: same as above.)*
　　Daughter of Tateoka Bunzaemon Yoshitaka
　　*(Died of illness on the 6th day of the 2nd month of An'ei 4 [Year of the Ram], at the age of 40. Posthumous Buddhist name: Baigan Chikō Shinnyo. Officiating priest and place of burial: same as above.)*
　　Daughter of Atsumi Ryōemon Toshikoko

One entry: In the third month of Hōreki 6 (Year of the Rat), two *bu* were deducted from the *gigi-saku* (simulated stipend) inherited from his late father, and he was granted a stipend of two *ryō* two *bu* and provisions for two persons, and was called into service under the Ginmijo (*Examination Office*) in a non-designated role (*mureki*).

One entry: In the second month of the same year 10 (Year of the Dragon), he was ordered to serve as a temporary hired scribe (*tōza oyatoi*) at the *Gōri Yakusho* (District Office).

**[Left folio]**

One entry: In the tenth month of the same year 12 (Year of the Horse), he was formally appointed as a scribe (*monokaki*) at the District Office, and his salary of two *ryō* two *bu* was converted and raised to a fixed stipend of seven *koku* two *to*.

One entry: In the tenth month of the same year 13 (Year of the Ram), he was assigned to a duty post (*nin'yaku*) in connection with a boundary dispute involving Mashimomura, Yoshiimachi-gumi, Satorimura, and a mountain boundary, all within the jurisdiction of the Echigo Deputy Magistrate (*Echigo Odaikan-sho*).

One entry: In the third month of Meiwa 1 (Year of the Monkey), he traveled up to Edo in connection with the aforementioned boundary dispute. The following year, in the sixth month of the Year of the Rooster (Meiwa 2), the official adjudication (*go-saikyo*) was concluded and he returned.
