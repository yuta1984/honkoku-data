One entry: In the third month of Hōreki 6 (Year of the Rat), two *bu* were deducted from the late father's *ogisaku* (provisional stipend inherited from the deceased father), and he was granted two ryō two *bu* plus provisions for two persons, and was called into service at the Ginmisho (Deliberation Office) under its jurisdiction, without a specific assigned role.

One entry: In the second month of the same [Hōreki] 10 (Year of the Dragon), he was appointed as a temporary hired clerk (*oyatoi monokaki*) at the Kōri Yakusho (District Office).

**[Left folio]**

One entry: In the tenth month of the same [Hōreki] 12 (Year of the Horse), he was formally appointed as a clerk (*monokaki*) at the District Office, and his stipend of two ryō two *bu* was raised and converted to a fixed stipend of seven *koku* two *to* of rice.

One entry: In the tenth month of the same [Hōreki] 13 (Year of the Ram), he was appointed to a special duty regarding a boundary dispute between Mashimomura village and Sattorimura village of the Ishima-gumi district, both under the Echigo Deputy Magistrate's jurisdiction.

One entry: In the third month of Meiwa 1 (Year of the Monkey), he traveled up to Edo in connection with the aforementioned boundary dispute. The official judgment (*gosaikyо̄*) was concluded in the sixth month of the following Year of the Rooster (Meiwa 2), and he returned.
