One entry: In the first month of the same 4th year (Tenmei 4, Year of the Dragon), due to his diligent and uninterrupted service in the investigative duties (shirabe goyō), he was granted five ryō of silver as a reward.

One entry: In the first month of the same 5th year (Year of the Snake), due to his diligent service, he was granted an official stipend (o-yakuryō) of one koku and one person's ration (hitori-fuchi).

One entry: In the same month, he was appointed to secret official duties (mitsujigoyō), and while serving in the increasingly burdensome austerity measures office (o-shōryaku-kata), he was instructed that on occasions of meetings among various channels of authority (suji-suji no kaigō), he should, as circumstances required, communicate and consult directly with those relevant parties.

One entry: In the twelfth month of the same 7th year (Year of the Goat), in recognition of his diligent and meticulous service in the austerity measures office (o-shōryaku-kata) since the Year of the Snake two years prior, he was granted two silver coins (ginsu niimai) as a reward.

One entry: In the third month of the same 8th year (Year of the Monkey), he traveled up to Edo to accompany the lord on his official visit to the Shogunal capital (o-sanpu).
　　Note: It was proclaimed that he had served diligently and without interruption in his duties as escort, official duties, and along the road while in Edo.
