· (Continuing from the previous entry) He was ordered to conduct himself accordingly.

· In the ninth month of the same fourth year (Year of the Rabbit), he turned out for training exercises and spent one night in camp.

· In the first month of the same fifth year (Year of the Dragon), he departed for duty in the defense of Ezo (Hokkaido), and arrived and established camp on Karafuto Island (Sakhalin).
　　Note: At the time of departure, he was granted an audience (o-memie) in the Great Drawing Room (Ōshoin), and received a cup of sake from the lord in the Small Drawing Room (Koshoin).

· In the ninth month of the same year, upon returning after completing his duty in the defense of Ezo, he was granted an audience in the Great Drawing Room.

· In the twelfth month of the same year, having traveled to fulfill his duty in the defense of Ezo, and having labored for a number of days there, including overseeing travel by both sea and land routes, and having completed his duties without delay with all troops returned to camp, he was granted a formal banquet (o-ryōri) at the castle in recognition of this.

· In the eighth month of the same sixth year (Year of the Snake), for having performed and labored in his duty in the defense of Ezo, he was granted one set of seasonal clothing (jifuku).

· In the same month, for having supervised the packhorse transport (konida) along the route and performed administrative duties (saihan) in connection with his Ezo defense duties, he was granted one ryō of gold as reimbursement for expenses.
