· (Continued from the previous page) Among the various companies, his unit's formation and marching were especially outstanding, and their ranking advanced accordingly. As a result, the entire group was granted sake by the lord.

· In the eighth month of the same fourteenth year (Year of the Ox), he turned out for the Ōtorigari (large-scale military exercise in the form of a hunt).

· In the tenth month of the same year, during the Ōtorigari, as the exercise ground was shifted and the opening maneuvers commenced, his unit's formation and marching were found to be exceptionally fine and operations proceeded without delay. It was proclaimed by the lord that he had long been diligent in combined unit training exercises, and the entire group received commendation from the lord.

· In the same month, having diligently applied himself to military drills (sōren), he was awarded a new spearhead (shinmi no yari, a spear with a newly forged blade) as a reward.

· In the same month, he was appointed head supervisor of medicinal herb cultivation (yakusō uetate-kata tōdori), and was granted the status of O-kinjū Ichi-no-yoriai-seki (a seat in the first-rank assembly of close attendants to the lord). Furthermore, regarding his duties overseeing ginseng cultivation at Suwahara, he had repeatedly traveled to that location, consulted appropriately with those in charge there, managed no small amount of funds for the cultivation and restoration of the plantings, and had labored diligently in various matters. For these efforts, he was granted three silver coins (ginshi) and two bundles of cotton (wata futataba) as a reward.

· In the same month, he was instructed that in the event of an emergency (fuji no setsu), he was to join the reserve forces (o-sonae).

· In the second month of the second year of Bunsei (Year of the Rabbit, 1819), he was appointed to a permanent assignment (sadame-shutsuyaku) at Suwahara and was granted a stipend of two-person rations (ninin-fuchi).
