One entry: In the second month of the same 3rd year (Anei 3, Year of the Horse), in connection with an incident involving rice trading certificates (tegata) during his tenure in the austerity measures role (o-shōryaku-kata nin'yaku), his stipend grant (o-gisaku), half-salary (han-kyū), and official duties (o-yakugi) were all revoked, and he was placed in the Kobushin (reserve/inactive) status.

One entry: In the twelfth month of the same 4th year (Anei 4, Year of the Goat), in terms of seniority, he was appointed immediately below the chief of the cadastral survey officials (kenchi yakunin筆頭).

One entry: In the third month of the same 5th year (Anei 5, Year of the Monkey), his petition to change his name to Shichirōemon was granted.

One entry: In the tenth month of the same 7th year (Anei 7, Year of the Dog), he was appointed as a temporary hired official (tōza o-yatoi) of the Goyōsho (Administrative Office).

One entry: In the tenth month of the same 8th year (Anei 8, Year of the Boar), he was appointed as a full official (yakunin) of the Goyōsho.

One entry: In the sixth month of Tenmei 1 (Year of the Ox), he was ordered to investigate the personnel registers (ninbetsu), official titles (yakumei), and stipend grants (o-gisaku) of those who had been summoned at Takato and Mogami and had accompanied the lord to Aizu. Working alongside Iizasa Shichirō and others, they conducted the investigation, and in the eighth month of the following year (Year of the Tiger), submitted a total of ten volumes.

One entry: In the tenth month of the same year, due to an unexpected visit to Edo (府 = the Shogunal capital) by the lord, he traveled up to Edo to accompany the return party, and came back down in the eleventh month of the same year.
