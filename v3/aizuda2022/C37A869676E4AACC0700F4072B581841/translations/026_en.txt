· In the eighth month of the same year, he turned out for the Ōtorigari (a large-scale military exercise in the form of a hunt) and spent one night in camp.

· In the ninth month of the same year, he was appointed to the post of supervisor of medicinal herb cultivation (yakusō uetate-kata), and was granted the status of O-kinjū Ichi-no-yoriai-seki (a seat in the first-rank assembly of close attendants to the lord).

· In the eleventh month of the same year, during the period of preparatory duties (senmu), he turned out for the Ōtorigari, and it was proclaimed by the lord that his unit's formation and marching were in fine order, that operations proceeded without delay, and that he had long been diligent in combined unit training exercises.

· In the twelfth month of the same eighth year (Year of the Sheep), having begun from the surveying of the land for the cultivation of ginseng at Suwahara, and having diligently managed the site and its administration, he was granted three silver coins (ginshi) as a reward.

· In the twelfth month of the same eleventh year (Year of the Dog), regarding his appointment as supervisor of ginseng management at Suwahara, it was proclaimed that no further funding from the post's budget would be required, and he was additionally assigned to the Soto-Jūgumi (Outer Ten Companies). His diligent service up to this point was also commended.
　　Note: He entered the unit of Jinbo Kurannosuke and performed castle guard duties (o-shiro o-ban).

· In the first month of the same twelfth year (Year of the Boar), he was appointed to a concurrent temporary post (tōza kari-yaku) in addition to his existing appointment as supervisor of ginseng cultivation.
