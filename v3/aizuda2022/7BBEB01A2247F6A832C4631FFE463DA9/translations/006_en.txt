**[Right folio]**

1. In the 7th year of Kanbun, Year of the Ram (1667), month unknown: Following the dismissal of unit commander (*kashira*) Ibuka Shirōbei from his post, he came under the successor unit of Ikoma Gobei.

1. In the 3rd year of Enpō, Year of the Rabbit (1675), 10th month: In accordance with his request, his son Shirōzaemon was appointed as his replacement on castle guard duty (*oban*).

1. In the 1st year of Genroku, Year of the Dragon (1688), 6th month: In accordance with his request, he was granted permission to retire (*inkyo*).

1. In the same 4th year, Year of the Ram (1691), month unknown: His request to change his name to "Zenshin" was granted.

1. In the same 8th year, Year of the Boar (1695), 13th day of the 3rd month: He died of illness at the age of eighty-seven. Posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*): Honkō Zenshin Koji. Place of burial: Shōgonzan Shōhōji.

**[Left folio]**

**Second Generation**

Shirōemon Shigenao (first name: Denshirō; also known as Takeemon; born in the 15th year of Kan'ei, Year of the Tiger [1638])

　　Mother: Daughter of the Kōsaka clan
　　Wife: Daughter of Ariga Gonzaemon Mitsutada (died of illness on the 24th day of the 8th month of the 15th year of Genroku, Year of the Horse [1702]; posthumous Buddhist name: Gaigen Myōun Daishi; place of burial: same as above [Shōgonzan Shōhōji])

1. In the 3rd year of Enpō, Year of the Rabbit (1675), 10th month: In accordance with his father's request, he was appointed as his replacement on guard duty, and served in castle guard duty (*oshiro oban*) under the unit of Ikoma Gobei.

1. In the 2nd year of Tenna, Year of the Dog (1682), 12th month: Following the death of unit commander (*kashira*) Ikoma Gobei, he came under the successor unit of Ichinose Denbei.
