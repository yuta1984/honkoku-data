**Genealogy of Yokochi Chūemon**

- Clan name (sei): Fujiwara
- Standard family crest (jōmon): Three tortoiseshell patterns within a circle
- Alternate crest (kawamon): Three fans
- Province of origin (honnoku): Tōtōmi
- Curtain crest (makumon): Rising wisteria

**Yokochi Kinbei Shigetsugu** (childhood name: Kichisaburō; name in retirement: Zenshin)
Second son of Yokochi Chūemon Hideshige
Born in the second year of Keichō, Year of the Rooster (1597)

- Mother: Daughter of Kawajiri Higo-no-kami [name unknown]
　Died of illness on the 13th day of the 2nd month, Kanbun 11, Year of the Boar (1671). Posthumous Buddhist name: Teishōin Myōan Nichishō. Burial place: Jueizan Jōkōji Temple.

- Wife: Daughter of Kōsaka Yohei Munenao
　Died of illness on the 16th day of the 4th month, Kan'ei 3, Year of the Tiger (1650). Posthumous Buddhist name: Shakuni Myōken. Burial place: Muryōzan Chōmyōji Temple.
