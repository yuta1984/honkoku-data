1. In the 3rd month of Bunka 1, Year of the Rat (1804): He was relieved of his duties at the *Bugakuryō* (Military Studies Institute). In recognition of his diligent service up to that point, he was awarded one bundle of cotton (*wata hitotaba*) as a reward.

1. In the 4th month of the same year: He traveled up to Edo for his rotation of duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 10th month of the same year.
　　　　Note: Throughout the period of duty (*jinchū*), he served without a single absence and with great diligence. This was formally praised and commended.

1. In the 4th month of Bunka 2, Year of the Ox (1805): He traveled up to Edo for a return posting (*tachi-kaeri*).

1. In the 1st month of Bunka 4, Year of the Rabbit (1807): He was ordered to take on the concurrent post of *kaimai jōyaku tetsudai* (assistant to the senior officer in charge of rice transport).
　　　　Note: Regarding this concurrent appointment as *kaimai jōyaku tetsudai*: in recent years, rice prices have fallen (*ge-jiki*), causing hardship across all classes (*shimin*). Moreover, the domain's own financial management (*o-kuriawase*) has been particularly strained. Accordingly, from the current spring onward, he was ordered to significantly increase the volume of transported rice (*kaimai*) dispatched (to Edo and other destinations) so as to help stabilize rice prices within the domain. Although this represents an extraordinary burden of service, he was to proceed to the road routes as an officer on duty (*shutsuyaku*); and given the circumstances of the moment, he was directed not only to manage the transport arrangements but also to economize on all incidental expenses (*o-irikata*), and to personally oversee the unloading and handling of the goods in a manner most beneficial to the domain.
