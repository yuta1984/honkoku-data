**[Right folio]**

…(Due to his adoptive father's misconduct, Shigeyasu was dismissed from his post, but) the designated heir was subsequently recalled to service.

1. In the 7th month of Kansei 4, Year of the Rat (1792): Regarding the mounted retainers (*tozama-shi*) of the Eighth Unit attached to the Senior Councilor (*go-karō*) and the new guard unit (*shinban-gumi*), he was ordered to attend on the day of the spear-arts (*sōjutsu*) assembly, at which senior officers (*kashiragashira*) would observe a review, and to manage the proper and correct conduct of proceedings.

1. In the 4th month of Kansei 6, Year of the Tiger (1794): In recognition of his dedicated efforts in organizing and promoting spear-arts (*sōjutsu*) practice sessions among the domain's retainers on an ad hoc basis since the year before last, he was awarded one bundle of cotton (*wata hitotaba*) as a gift of commendation.

1. In the 7th month of the same year: He was appointed as the *shimari-kata* (supervisor/concluding instructor) of the Hōzō-in School of spear arts (*Hōzō-in-ryū sōjutsu*).
　　　　*Note: In connection with the above appointment, he was granted a stipend allowance (*hiho-kin*).*

1. In the 6th month of Kansei 7, Year of the Rabbit (1795): In accordance with his (adoptive) father's petition, he was appointed as a *bandai* (duty guard) and assigned to the mounted retainers (*tozama-shi*) attached to the Senior Councilor.

**[Left folio]**

　　　　*Note: Given his concurrent duties as* shimari-kata *of spear arts, he was exempted from the regular attendance obligations (*iryaku chakutō*) for castle guard duty.*

1. In the 11th month of Kansei 8, Year of the Dragon (1796): He was granted without question his family headship (*katoku*) and a stipend of two hundred *koku*.

1. In the 7th month of Kansei 10, Year of the Horse (1798): His sustained dedication and achievement of a skill level of seven-tenths (*shichibun*) or above in spear-arts practice, maintained continuously from the Year of the Rabbit (1795) through the previous year (1797), was praised and commended.

1. In the 10th month of Kansei 11, Year of the Sheep (1799): He attended the *oitorigari* (domain-wide bird-hunting exercise).
　　　　*Note: Of the five subsequent times he attended, on one occasion he went as an ordinary participant, and on three occasions he made kills, for which he received gifts in recognition.*
