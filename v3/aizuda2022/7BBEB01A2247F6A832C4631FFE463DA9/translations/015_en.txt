1. In the 2nd month of Genbun 5, Year of the Monkey (1740): In accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (*o-memie*) with the lord.

1. In the 2nd month of An'ei 6, Year of the Rooster (1777): He was granted the family headship (*katoku*) and a stipend of two hundred *koku* without alteration. He was assigned to the *tozama-gumi* (outer-retainer unit) and placed in Ikoma Kuranosuke's unit, where he performed castle guard duty (*go-jō go-ban*).

1. In the 3rd month of the same 7th year, Year of the Ram (1778)＃1: He was praised and commended for having performed his castle guard duty without negligence.

1. In the 8th month of the same 8th year, Year of the Monkey (1779)＃1: His request was granted to take Shigehachi, the second son of Shiga Magotarō, as his son-in-law and adopted son (*muko-yōshi*).

1. In the 6th month of Kansei 1, Year of the Rooster (1789): As the unit commander (*kashira*) Ikoma Kurannosuke was relieved of his duties, he was transferred to Ibuka Kenmotsu's unit as the successor assignment.

1. In the intercalary 6th month of the same year: He was reassigned to a unit attached to the *go-karō* (chief retainer/elder).
