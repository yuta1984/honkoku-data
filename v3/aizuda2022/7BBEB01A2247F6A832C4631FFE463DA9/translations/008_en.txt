　　(Second wife): Daughter of Nagata Tōbei Tadachika　(Died of illness on the 3rd day of the 7th month of the 8th year of Hōreki, Year of the Rabbit (1758); posthumous Buddhist name: Kessō Kōei Daishi; place of burial: same as above (Shōgonzan Shōhōji))

1. In the 3rd year of Hōei, Year of the Dog (1706), 7th month: In accordance with his father's request, he was appointed as his replacement on castle guard duty (*oban*), and served on castle guard (*gojō goban*) as part of the Harada Iyo unit.

1. In the same 7th year, Year of the Tiger (1710), 4th month: When he traveled up to Edo for attendance duty (*kinban*) as part of the lord's escort (*osupai*), he was appointed as a temporary escort guard (*karigobanbан*) during the journey, performed the duty, and returned in the 10th month of the same year.

1. In the 1st year of Shōtoku, Year of the Rabbit (1711), 3rd month: He was granted, without dispute, the hereditary stipend (*atoshiki gochigyō*) of two hundred *koku* of rice.
