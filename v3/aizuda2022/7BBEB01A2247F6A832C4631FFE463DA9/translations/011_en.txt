**[Right folio]**

1. In the 12th month of the same year (the 3rd year of Hōreki, Year of the Rooster, 1753): During the Lord Kyōtei's illness with measles (*hashika*), he performed overnight guard duty (*fushinban*), standing watch through both night-watches without sleep, and was commended for this service by official proclamation.

1. In the 3rd month of the same 4th year, Year of the Dog (1754): As he had applied himself diligently and performed his duties in the field encampment without any deficiency, he was awarded one *tan* of bleached cloth (*sarashi*) in lieu of hempen formal hakama and jacket (*asa no kamishimo*) as a reward.

1. In the 10th month of the same 5th year, Year of the Boar (1755): He traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 4th month of the following year of the Rat (1756).

1. In the 3rd month of the same 6th year, Year of the Rat (1756): As he had applied himself with care and diligence during his guard duty and performed his duties without any deficiency, he was awarded one used *kosode* (short-sleeved garment) as a reward.

**[Left folio]**

　　(Note: The item was presented to him in the presence of the lord.)

1. In the 10th month of the same 8th year, Year of the Tiger (1758): He traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*), and in the 3rd month of the following year of the Rabbit (1759), he returned accompanying the lord's procession back to the domain (*go-nyūbu*).

1. In the 3rd month of the same 9th year, Year of the Rabbit (1759): As he had applied himself diligently in the field encampment and performed his duties without any deficiency, he was awarded one set of used hempen formal hakama and jacket (*furumono asa no kamishimo*) as a reward.

1. In the 12th month of the same year: He was commended by official proclamation for diligently performing his duties as an on-site supervisor (*tachiai*) of the construction works at the inner-precinct mausoleum (*in'nai go-fun*).
