**[Right folio]**

1. In the 1st month of the 8th year of Kyōhō, Year of the Rabbit (1723): Permission was granted, in accordance with his request, for him to take Hyūga Yosōemon's younger brother, Kōta, as his son-in-law and adopted heir.

1. In the 4th month of the same year: Following the death of the unit commander (*kumigashira*) Harada Iyo, he was reassigned to the unit of Kajiwara Heima as the successor post.

1. In the 4th month of the same 11th year, Year of the Horse (1726): He traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 10th month of the same year.

1. In the 2nd month of the same 19th year, Year of the Tiger (1734): Following the death of the unit commander Kajiwara Heima, he was reassigned to the unit of Jinbō Kurannosuke as the successor post.

1. In the 5th month of the 3rd year of Genbun, Year of the Horse (1738): In accordance with his request, his son Kōta was appointed as his replacement on guard duty (*oban dairi*).
