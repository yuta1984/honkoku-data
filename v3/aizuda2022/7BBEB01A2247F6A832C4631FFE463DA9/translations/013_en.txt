**[Right folio]**

*(Continued from previous page)* …he traveled there for official duty, and was additionally appointed to serve concurrently as District Magistrate (*Kōri Bugyō*), which he duly performed.

1. In the 10th month of the same year: He traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 5th month of the following year of the Ox (1769).

1. In the 4th month of the same 6th year, Year of the Ox (1769): As he had applied himself diligently and performed his duties in the field encampment without any deficiency, he was awarded one set of hempen formal hakama and jacket (*asa no kamishimo*) as a reward.

1. In the 7th month of the same year: His wish to change his given name to Sukukyūrō (*Sukekurō*) was granted as requested.

1. In the 10th month of the same 7th year, Year of the Tiger (1770): He traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 4th month of the following year of the Rabbit (1771).

**[Left folio]**

1. In the 4th month of the same 8th year, Year of the Rabbit (1771): As he had applied himself diligently and performed his duties in the field encampment without any deficiency, he was awarded one set of used hempen formal hakama and jacket (*o-furu asa no kamishimo*) as a reward.

1. In the 5th month of the 2nd year of An'ei, Year of the Snake (1773): He traveled up to Edo for guard duty during the lord's absence (*otomari kinban*), but in the 10th month of the same year, he submitted a petition to be relieved of his post (*jieki no negai*) on account of illness. His petition was granted, he was relieved of his post, and he was assigned to the no-duty unit (*muryaku-gumi*). Furthermore, as he had applied himself with diligence over many years, he was awarded three *monme* of silver as a reward.
　　　*Note: The above includes his service in attending to field construction works (*jinchū go-fushin tachiai*).*

1. In the 12th month of the same year: He was praised and commended for having applied himself diligently and without deficiency during his guard duty that summer, during his previous tour of duty.
