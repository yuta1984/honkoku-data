**[Right folio]**

　　Wife (first): Daughter of his adoptive father Kinbei Shigenori. (Died of illness on the 24th day of the 8th month of the 4th year of Kan'en, Year of the Sheep (1751). Posthumous Buddhist name: Shunchōin Jikan Myōshō Daishi; place of burial: same as above (Shōgonzan Shōhōji).)

　　Wife (second): Daughter of Sakurai Yaichirōemon Masayoshi. (Died of illness on the 4th day of the 3rd month of the 3rd year of Kansei, Year of the Boar (1791). Posthumous Buddhist name: Shinkyōin Juhō Myōshin Daishi; place of burial: same as above (Shōgonzan Shōhōji).)

1. In the 6th month of the 8th year of Kyōhō, Year of the Rabbit (1723): In accordance with his father's request, he was granted his first audience (*omemie*) with the lord.

1. In the 5th month of the 3rd year of Genbun, Year of the Horse (1738): In accordance with his father's request, he was appointed as his father's replacement on guard duty (*oban dairi*), and served castle guard (*oshiro goban*) in the unit of Jinbō Kurannosuke.

1. In the 11th month of the same 4th year, Year of the Sheep (1739): He was granted without question the hereditary stipend (*atoshiki gochigyō*) of two hundred *koku*.

**[Left folio]**

1. In the 10th month of the 4th year of Enkyō, Year of the Rabbit (1747): As the unit commander (*kumigashira*) Jinbō Kurannosuke was relieved of his post in accordance with his own request, he was reassigned to the unit of Kohara Gorōemon as the successor post.

1. In the 6th month of the 1st year of Hōreki, Year of the Sheep (1751): He was appointed as inspector (*ometsuke*).

1. In the 9th month of the same year: He traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 3rd month of the following year of the Monkey (1752).

1. In the 2nd month of the same 2nd year, Year of the Monkey (1752): Although it was his first campaign (*shojin*), he was commended for his diligence and for performing his duties without any deficiency.

1. In the 10th month of the same 3rd year, Year of the Rooster (1753): He traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 4th month of the following year of the Dog (1754).
