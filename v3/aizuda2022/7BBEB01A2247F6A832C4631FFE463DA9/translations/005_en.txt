**[Right folio]**

1. [Continued from previous page] Through this intercession, he was summoned into service as a page (*okoshō*) to Lord Dōgi (Hoshina Masayuki). (The details of the provisional stipend (*gigigisaku*) are unclear.)

1. [Year and month unknown]
　He was appointed as a page (*okoshō*) to Lord Tsuchitsu (Hoshina Masayuki).

1. In the same 10th year, Year of the Rooster (Kan'ei 10, 1633), month unknown: In accordance with his request, he was relieved of his post and assigned to the *tozama gumi* (outer retainer group).

1. In the same 13th year, Year of the Rat (Kan'ei 13, 1636), 8th month: On the occasion of the domain's relocation to Mogami in Dewa Province, he was granted one hundred *koku* and assigned to the unit under Ibuka Kenmotsu.

1. In the same 19th year, Year of the Horse (Kan'ei 19, 1642), 3rd month: He was granted an additional stipend of fifty *koku*.

**[Left folio]**

1. In the same 20th year, Year of the Ram (Kan'ei 20, 1643), 8th month: On the occasion of the domain's relocation to Aizu, he was granted a standard increase of fifty *koku*.

1. In the 4th year of Keian, Year of the Rabbit (1651), 7th month: Following the death of unit commander (*kashira*) Ibuka Kenmotsu, he came under the successor unit of Ibuka Moemon.

1. In the 2nd year of Jōō, Year of the Snake (1653), 10th month: He traveled to Edo for garrison duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 4th month of the following Year of the Horse (1654).

1. In the 2nd year of Manji, Year of the Boar (1659), 8th month: He traveled to Edo for garrison duty, and returned in the 4th month of the following Year of the Rat (1660).

1. In the 10th month of the same year: Following the reassignment of unit commander Ibuka Moemon, he came under the successor unit of Ibuka Shirōbei.
