**[Right folio]**

1. In the 1st year of Genroku, Year of the Dragon (1688), 6th month: He was granted, without dispute, a hereditary stipend (*katoku*) of two hundred *koku* of rice.

1. In the same 8th year, Year of the Boar (1695), winter: Following the dismissal of unit commander (*kashira*) Ichinose Denbei from his post, he came under the successor unit of Harada Iyo.

1. Date unknown: His request to take Kinbei, the second son of Matsunaga Shōemon, as his son-in-law and adopted heir (*muko yōshi*), was granted.

1. In the 2nd year of Hōei, Year of the Dog (1705), 7th month: In accordance with his request, his son Kinbei was appointed as his replacement on castle guard duty (*oban*).

1. In the same 7th year, Year of the Tiger (1710), 12th month: He died of illness at the age of seventy-three. Posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*): Setsuo Jōsei Koji. Place of burial: same as above (Shōgonzan Shōhōji).
