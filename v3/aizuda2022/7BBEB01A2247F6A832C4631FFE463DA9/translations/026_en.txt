**[Right folio]**

- In the 9th month of Shōtoku 4, Year of the Horse (1714), he was granted ten *nin-fuchi* (a stipend of ten persons' worth of rice rations) and summoned into service as a domain physician (*o-ishi*).

- In the 8th month of the same 6th year, Year of the Monkey (1716), he was granted an additional ten *nin-fuchi*, making his total stipend twenty *nin-fuchi*.

- In the 3rd month of Kyōhō 8, Year of the Rabbit (1723), he was appointed as a personal/attending physician (*o-soba ishi*).

- On the 21st day of the 10th month of the same 11th year, Year of the Horse (1726), he died of illness at the age of sixty-one. Posthumous Buddhist name: *Jōshi-in Tōgan Koji*. Place of burial: Yakuōji Temple of Mount Myōshō, Mita, Edo.

**[Left folio]**

**Shōitsu Nagahayashi** (first called *Hanzō*; later also known as *Shōsen*)

　　Mother: Daughter of the Yoshida clan
　　Wife: Daughter of Ōtani Kindayū (given name unknown) — (Died of illness on the 23rd day of the 11th month of Tenmei 5, Year of the Snake \[1785\]. Posthumous Buddhist name: *Jōze-in Myōyū Nichikō Daishi*. Place of burial: Same as above.)

- In the 2nd month of Kyōhō 12, Year of the Sheep (1727), out of his late father's stipend, he was granted ten *nin-fuchi* and summoned into service as a domain physician.

- In the 12th month of Kanhō 3, Year of the Boar (1743), he was granted an additional five *nin-fuchi*.

- In the 6th month of Kan'en 2, Year of the Snake (1749), he was granted an additional five *nin-fuchi* (entry continues on the following page).
