**[Right folio]**

1. In the 12th month of the same year (Kansei 11, 1799): During that year's *oitori-gari* (ritual hunting drive), the order and alignment of the formation (*retsugo*), the uniformity of step and bearing, and the overall smoothness of proceedings were particularly commended. The authorities deemed this to reflect his longstanding diligence in private training (*uchi-narashi*), and accordingly, he and all members of his unit (*ai-gumi*) together received formal praise and commendation from the lord.
　　　　Note: From this point forward, he received such commendation on every subsequent occasion.

1. In the 1st month of Kansei 12, Year of the Monkey (1800): In recognition of his diligent and exemplary service as *shimari-kata* (supervisor/concluding instructor) of spear arts, and his success in cultivating and training the students (*shosei*) in his charge, he was awarded two *mai* of silver (*ginshi niimai*) as a reward.

1. In the 5th month of the same year: He was appointed to the role of *o-tomo-ban* (escort guard duty).
　　　　Note: In connection with this appointment, in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his duties as *shimari-kata* of the spear arts.

**[Left folio]**

1. In the 10th month of the same year: He traveled up to Edo to perform his *kinban* (rotation of duty service), and returned in the 5th month of the following Year of the Dog (Kyōwa 1, 1801).
　　　　Note: Throughout the period of duty (*jinchū*), he served without a single absence and with great diligence. This was formally praised and commended.

1. In the 9th month of Kyōwa 2, Year of the Dog (1802): It had long been recognized that he was well accomplished in the spear arts of Shiga Yozanbei's school. On this occasion, however, Yozanbei died of illness, and as his son, Koshirō, was still young and inexperienced, Shigeyasu was ordered to stay by Koshirō's side for the time being (*tōbun*), to assist with and oversee matters relating to practice (*keikōsuji*), and to ensure that the training of the disciples (*mondetomo*) would proceed smoothly and effectively.

1. In the 10th month of the same year: Shiga Yozanbei's (son Koshirō) made his way to Inawashiro.
