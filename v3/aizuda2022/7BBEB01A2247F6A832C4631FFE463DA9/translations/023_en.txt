1. In the 9th month of Bunka 5, Year of the Dragon (1808): Having served his concurrent post diligently, having undertaken numerous dispatch duties, having ensured that the management of rice transport and other related operations proceeded smoothly and properly, and having labored through various hardships and difficulties, he was awarded, as a mark of recognition and reward, one *tan* (bolt) of bleached cloth (*sarashi*) as a substitute gift in lieu of a formal *kamishimo* ceremonial garment.

1. In the same month (9th month of Bunka 5): He was dispatched on duty to Shirakawa, and returned in the 3rd month of the following Year of the Snake (Bunka 6, 1809).

1. In the 3rd month of Bunka 6, Year of the Snake (1809): As the rice transport operations (*mawarimai*) had been transferred (to other personnel/management), he was relieved of his concurrent post as assistant to the senior rice transport official (*kaimai kamiyaku tetsudai*).
