1. In the 8th month of the same 3rd year, Year of the Horse (1774): He was praised and commended for having applied himself diligently in attending to construction works (*go-fushin tachiai*) during his previous tour of guard duty.

1. In the 1st month of the same 6th year, Year of the Rooster (1777): He was granted permission to retire (*inkyo*) as he had requested.

1. In the 12th month of the 3rd year of Tenmei, Year of the Rabbit (1783): He died of illness at the age of seventy-seven. His posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*) was Kikyūin Chūzan Dōsetsu Koji. His place of burial is the same as previously recorded.
