1. In the 4th month of the same 12th year, Year of the Horse (1762): During his guard duty in the lord's absence (*otomari kinban*), he applied himself diligently in performing his duties and was praised and commended for this.

1. In the 9th month of the 1st year of Meiwa, Year of the Monkey (1764): He traveled to Yamagata in Uzen Province (*Ushū Yamagata*) for guard duty (*kinban*), and while stationed there (*zaiban*), he was additionally appointed to serve concurrently as Construction Magistrate (*go-fushin bugyō*).

1. From the 6th month of the same 2nd year, Year of the Rooster (1765): Beginning from the time of the formal receiving (*uketori*) of Yamagata Castle, throughout his guard duty, he attended carefully to all matters, applied himself diligently to new undertakings, and managed affairs capably. For this, he was awarded one set of used hempen formal hakama and jacket (*omeshi-furu asa no kamishimo*) as a reward. Furthermore, he had concurrently served as Construction Magistrate (*go-fushin bugyō*) with exceptional dedication and effort,

　　and was commended for this by official proclamation.

1. In the 10th month of the same 3rd year, Year of the Dog (1766): He traveled up to Edo for guard duty (*kinban*), and returned in the 4th month of the following year of the Boar (1767).

1. In the 12th month of the same year: As he had applied himself with diligence over many years in the Edo residence, he was awarded one used *kosode* (short-sleeved garment) as a reward.

1. In the 3rd month of the same 4th year, Year of the Boar (1767): He was praised and commended for applying himself diligently and performing his duties in the field encampment without any deficiency.

1. In the 5th month of the same year: He was ordered to conduct an inspection of actual goods held in the rural granaries (*gōzō genbutsu aratame*) and carried out his rounds accordingly.
