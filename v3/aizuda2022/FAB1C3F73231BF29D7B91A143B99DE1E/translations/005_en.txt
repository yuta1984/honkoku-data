【Right folio】
Gorōemon Shigehusa　(formerly known as: Tokubē)

- Mother: Daughter of the Machida clan
- Wife: Daughter of Ōno Gen'emon (a retainer of Lord Abe Ise-no-Kami)
　《Died of illness on the 2nd day of the 4th month of Kyōhō 10 (1725). Posthumous Buddhist name: Jōsei-in Myōyū Shinnyо. Place of burial: [remainder missing]》

1. In the 1st month of Kanbun 8 (申, 1668), as per his father's petition, he was appointed to serve as Edo Ohiroma Goban (Edo Great Hall Guard) and Otsukai (Messenger/Envoy).

2. In the 10th month of the same 9th year (酉, 1669), as per his father's petition, he was appointed as a substitute guard (Bandai), and served as castle guard in Sugita Gorōbē's unit.

【Left folio】
3. In the 8th month of the same 11th year (亥, 1671), he received his family inheritance of 150 koku in stipend without dispute.

4. In the 4th month of Enpō 3 (卯, 1675), he traveled to Edo for guard duty (Kinban), returning in the 10th month of the same year.

5. In the 8th month of Jōkyō 3 (寅, 1686), he was appointed to the position of Odaikan-yaku (Deputy Magistrate/Land Administrator).

6. In the 6th month of Genroku 1 (辰, 1688), he was appointed to the position of Jōzume Ō-onando-yaku (Permanent Resident Great Household Treasury Official).

7. In the 11th month of the same 2nd year (巳, 1689), he was appointed as Jōzume Ometsuke (Permanent Resident Inspector/Censor) at the Hama residence.

8. In the 8th month of the same 6th year (酉, 1694), he was appointed as Jōzume Ofushin-bugyō (Permanent Resident Construction Magistrate)— [entry continues on next page]
