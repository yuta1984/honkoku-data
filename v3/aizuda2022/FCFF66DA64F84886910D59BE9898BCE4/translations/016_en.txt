His biological father was the third son of Satō Heizaemon Takaniwa.
His wife was the daughter of his adoptive father Kuzaemon Mitsuhisa (known in earlier life as Yae, and later as Yaeno; born on the 26th day of the 8th month of the 5th year of Meiwa (1768), the year of the Rat).

One item: On the 5th day of the 7th month of the 3rd year of Kyōwa (1803), the year of the Boar, by means of the standard appointment document (nami no okitte-fu), she was summoned into service as an attendant lady-in-waiting (o-tsuki o-tsugi jochū) to the newborn princess (go-shussei-sama). On the 2nd day of the 8th month of the same year, she appeared at the castle, and on the 8th day of the same month, she was ordered to have an audience with Lady Teishō.

One item: In the 9th month of the same year, Lord Kinbun was born, and from that very night she entered into service.

One item: In the same month, as a congratulatory gift for the Seventh Night celebration (o-shichiya), she received a sum of two hundred hiki (in lieu of celebratory fish) from Lady Teishō, and a similar gift (in lieu of celebratory fish) was also bestowed by Lady Juhō.

One item: In the 3rd month of the 1st year of Bunka (1804), the year of the Rat, at the time of the Shrine Visit (o-miyamairi), she served in attendance at the Toyooka Shrine visit, and for this celebratory occasion received a gift of fish money (the record appears to continue on the following page).
