1. In the 8th month of the same year [Tenmei 4]: The unit commander (kashira) Takahashi Koemon was appointed to the position of Elder (karō), and as a result, the successor unit assignment became that of Kayano Gondayū.

2. In the 9th month of Kansei 1, the year of the Rooster: As the unit commander Kayano Gondayū was relieved of his post, the successor unit assignment became that of Issei Dengoro.

3. In the 7th month of Kansei 2, the year of the Dog: As the unit commander Issei Dengoro was relieved of his post, the successor unit assignment became that of Sugita Gorōbei.

4. In the 3rd month of Kansei 4, the year of the Rat: He participated in the Oitorigari (the domain's large-scale military review and hunting exercise).
　　Note: He participated three more times after this.

5. In the same month: As this was the first occasion of the Oitorigari, it was observed that all had applied themselves diligently; the formations were well-ordered, and while one would not ordinarily expect such drill in foot-marching and the like — especially given the short time available for preparation — the units were seen to be well-coordinated, and this favorable assessment was conveyed by official proclamation to all unit members assembled together.
　　Note: On every subsequent occasion, he continued to receive commendation.
