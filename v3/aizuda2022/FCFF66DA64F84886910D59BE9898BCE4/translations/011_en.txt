...served in castle guard duty under the (Sakai/Ohara) Emon unit.

One item: In the 4th month of the 15th year of Kyōhō (1730), the year of the Dog, he was dispatched to Edo as an escort guard (otomo kinban), serving in the temporary role of escort attendant along the road, and returned in the 10th month of the same year.
　　Note: He was commended for having fulfilled his duties without a single absence throughout the journey and during his time in Edo.

One item: In the 1st month of the 18th year of Kyōhō (1733), the year of the Ox, following the reassignment of his unit commander Obara Tsuneuemon, he was subsequently transferred to the unit under Tanaka Kahyōe.

One item: In the 2nd month of the same year, he received a commendation for serving his castle guard duties without negligence.

One item: In the 7th month of the 1st year of Kanpō (1741), the year of the Rooster, he was additionally appointed as a district magistrate (daikan).

One item: In the 6th month of the 2nd year of Kanpō (1742), the year of the Dog, he reported that he was unable to properly handle the financial accounting duties and found it difficult to continue as district magistrate; his request to be relieved of the position was granted.

One item: In the 12th month of the 3rd year of Kanpō (1743), the year of the Boar, when Higuchi Tanzaemon was to present archery arts to Lord Tsuchitoko-sama (Matsudaira Katasada), he was ordered through the lord's chamberlain (goyōnin) to prepare and submit written records, and he duly composed and submitted seven volumes.
