One item: In the 8th month of the same year (Bunka 1, 1804), she was given worn articles of clothing previously used by Lord Kinbun.
　　Note: From this point forward, she received such gifts annually.

One item: In the 12th month of the same year, owing to her diligent service during a period of reduced staffing, she was granted one ryō in gold through an internal discretionary stipend (goyō-nai).

One item: In the same month, as a year-end congratulatory gift (o-seibo), she was given one bundle of cotton wadding.
　　Note: From this point forward, she received the same gift annually.

One item: In the 1st month of the following year of the Ox (Bunka 2, 1805), having performed sewing duties (o-shitate), she was granted one hundred hiki in gold as a sewing fee (o-hari-dai).
　　Note: From this point forward, she received the same gift annually.

One item: In the 5th month of the same year, owing to her diligent service during a period of reduced staffing, she was ordered to have an audience (o-mie) upon the occasion of Lord Kyōjō's arrival at the castle, by means of an internal discretionary arrangement (o-yō).

One item: In the 7th month of the same year, at the time of Lord Kyōjō's passing, she served in attendance at the worship at the temporary mortuary (o-kariya o-hai), as well as at the funeral procession (o-sōsō o-mi-...) (the record continues on the following page).
