One item: In the tenth month of the fifth year [of Bunka], the year of the Dragon (1808), he was appointed to serve concurrently as assistant to the horse inspector (koma-aratame-yaku tetsudai).

One item: In the fifth month of the [seventh] year [of Bunka], the year of the Horse (1810), because he administered the villages under his jurisdiction with the utmost care and diligence, and managed affairs in an exemplary manner, he was awarded three pieces of silver (ginsu san-mai) as a reward.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, because the arrangements at the horse office (koma-yakusho) were reorganized, it was directed that his concurrent assignment be changed to that of horse affairs officer (koma-kata-kakari).

One item: In the seventh month of the eighth year [of Bunka], the year of the Goat (1811), he was appointed to serve concurrently as assistant magistrate for the domain's direct landholdings (o-kura-iri go-daikan minarai).

One item: In the fifth month of the ninth year [of Bunka], the year of the Monkey (1812), because he managed the arrangements for the childrearing subsidy fund (ubako yōiku-kin), he was awarded two bundles of cotton (wata futataba) as a reward.

One item: In the fourth month of the tenth year [of Bunka], the year of the Rooster (1813), because he was transferred to the jige-kata post, he was relieved of his concurrent appointment as assistant magistrate; and because he had served diligently up to that point, he was awarded two bundles of cotton (wata futataba) as a reward.

One item: In the eleventh month of the same year, in accordance with his petition, his son Shōemon was appointed to serve in his stead as guard (o-ban-dai).
