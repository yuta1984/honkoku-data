At the time when the Gongen-sama [Tokugawa Ieyasu] was in residence, there was construction work on the castle, and since Dōgi-sama performed service for this purpose, Shōgen also rendered his service accordingly. For this, he was granted seasonal robes [jifuku] from Gongen-sama.

One entry: Genna 4, the Year of the Horse (1618), month unknown: He was granted retirement [inkyo] in accordance with his petition.

One entry: The year and month of his death from illness are unknown. Posthumous Buddhist name: Gyokuhōin-dono Tenshu Jōshin Koji. Place of burial: Kōrenzi [Lotus Fragrance Temple], Bukkōzan, Jōina District, Takato.
