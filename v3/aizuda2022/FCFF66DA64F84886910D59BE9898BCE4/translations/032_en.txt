Note: On subsequent occasions as well, commendation (o-shōbi) was similarly bestowed.

One item: In the same month, having shown diligent effort in military drill (sōren), he was granted one new spear (shin-mi no yari, hitotsuho).

One item: In the eleventh month of the same year, as head of household (katoku), he was granted a stipend of one hundred koku of assessed rice land (go-chigyō hyaku-koku) without any discrepancy.

One item: In the first month of the first year of Bunsei, the year of the Tiger (1818), his request to wear the ko-sode (short-sleeved garment), noshi-me (formal patterned kimono), awase (lined garment), katabira (unlined garment), and kamishimo (formal divided skirt and surcoat) that had been granted to his grandfather and were in his possession was approved.

One item: In the twelfth month of the third year of Bunsei, the year of the Dragon (1820), he was appointed on a temporary basis to the concurrent post of apprentice district magistrate (o-daikan minarai kenmu), as a provisional assignment.

One item: In the third month of the fourth year of Bunsei, the year of the Snake (1821), he was ordered to manage the accounting for the charcoal (okibi-tan) required by the domain school (gakkō).

One item: In the fourth month of the same year, he was again appointed to the concurrent post of apprentice district magistrate (o-daikan minarai kenmu).

One item: In the seventh month of the fifth year of Bunsei, the year of the Horse (1822), in recognition of his diligent service in various extraordinary duties during the year before last, he was granted one bundle of cotton (wata hitotsuwa) as a reward.
