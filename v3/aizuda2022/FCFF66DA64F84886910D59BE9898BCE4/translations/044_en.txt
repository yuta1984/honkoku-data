Item: In the third month of the same year, the residential plot that had formerly belonged to Karasawa Heiemon in Shinmachi was granted to him.

Item: In the fourth month of the same year, as the lord departed on his journey (gohassha), and the two ladies relocated to their new quarters (o-watamashi), four colleagues (nakama) shared the duty of attendance day and night.

Item: In the seventh month of Shōtoku 5 (1715), the Year of the Sheep, he was bestowed by the two ladies with a ko-sode (short-sleeved inner robe) and a katabira (unlined summer robe).

Item: In the seventh month of Kyōhō 1 (1716), the Year of the Monkey, in recognition of his diligent service, he was granted a written document stating that he would receive one bolt of silk (hiki) each year.

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, as memorial gifts (go-ibutsu) from Lady Kōmyō-in, he received four katabira (unlined robes) and one hitoe (single-layered garment) each; and again in the twelfth month of the same year, he was given one additional ko-sode (short-sleeved robe).

Item: In the eighth month of Kyōhō 2 (1717), the Year of the Rooster, his post was relinquished as requested, and he was ordered to return to his former position of ko-ban (junior guard).

Item: In the same month, he was ordered to present himself at the ladies' chambers from time to time to pay his respects and inquire after their well-being.
