Item: In the third month of Kyōhō 3 (1718), the Year of the Dog, when Lady Setsusō-in departed for Edo, he was ordered to accompany her on the return journey; he traveled up to Edo and returned in the fourth month of the same year.
　　Note: During the journey, he served as guard at the main official lodgings (honjin-ban) along the road.

Item: In the eighth month of Kyōhō 5 (1720), the Year of the Rat, he traveled up to Edo as part of the alternate-duty garrison (kinban), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.

Item: In the intercalary seventh month of Kyōhō 6 (1721), the Year of the Ox, the residential plot that had formerly belonged to Nakagawa Jihee was reassigned and granted to him.

Item: In the third month of Kyōhō 9 (1724), the Year of the Dragon, he was appointed as a daikan (magistrate/land administrator), and his seating rank was assigned immediately after that of Shiga Zenbee.

Item: In the first month of Kyōhō 12 (1727), the Year of the Sheep, the upper residence (uwayashiki) that had formerly belonged to Ueshima Rihee was reassigned and granted to him.

Item: In the eleventh month of Kyōhō 13 (1728), the Year of the Monkey, in recognition of his capable service in his current duties, he was granted an increase in stipend of five koku and one person's rations (hitori-fuchi), and was appointed as head of the investigative office (ginmi-gashira).

Item: In the eleventh month of Kyōhō 14 (1729), the Year of the Rooster, his post was relinquished as requested, and he was ordered into a position without official duties (muyaku).
