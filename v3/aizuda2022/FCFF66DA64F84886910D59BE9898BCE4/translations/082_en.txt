1. In the 10th month of An'ei 7, the year of the Dog: He traveled to Edo for garrison duty, and returned home in the 5th month of the following year of the Boar, as part of the escort retinue.

2. In the 9th month of An'ei 8, the year of the Boar: He was granted the inherited stipend of 100 koku without alteration, and was commanded to continue performing escort guard duty as before.

3. In the 10th month of Tenmei 1, the year of the Ox: On the occasion of an unexpected/unscheduled visit to Edo by the lord, he traveled to Edo as part of the escort and return party.

4. In the 4th month of Tenmei 2, the year of the Tiger: He traveled to Edo for garrison duty and returned home in the 10th month of the same year.

5. In the 12th month of the same year: Ukisu Shōdayū's second son, Shichikurō, was appointed as his son-in-law and adopted heir, in accordance with his petition.

6. In the 8th month of Tenmei 3, the year of the Rabbit: It was officially proclaimed that, having considered the state of the duty roster and having previously submitted his proposal — namely, that the number of personnel on garrison duty had been reduced while the frequency of the lord's outings had increased, making matters especially demanding — he and his colleagues cooperated with one another, set their minds to the task, and performed their duties with great diligence, and this was regarded as particularly admirable.

7. In the 3rd month of Tenmei 4, the year of the Dragon: In accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his post (correction visible in original: "escort" struck through, replaced with "post/role"), assigned to the outside retainer unit (tozama-gumi), placed under the unit of Takahashi Koemon, and performed duty as castle guard.
