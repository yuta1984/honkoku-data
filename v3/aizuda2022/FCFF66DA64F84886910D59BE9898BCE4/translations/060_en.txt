(Continued from previous page: granted an inherited stipend of one hundred koku,) he was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit) and placed in the unit of Kajiwara Heima, and was assigned to ko-fushin (reserve/training status) for the purpose of training in martial arts.

Item: (Struck-through entry: Assigned to training ko-fushin — deleted by strikethrough)

Item: In the ninth month of Bunka 4, Year of the Rabbit (1807), in connection with the duty of guarding Ezo (Hokkaido), he appeared at the Hongōhara training grounds during the exercise of men and horses.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, in connection with the Ezo guard duty, he was released from training ko-fushin status, and from that same month began serving as a castle guard (go-jōban).

Item: In the first month of Bunka 5, Year of the Dragon (1808), as he was dispatched for the lord's service in Ezo, he was granted an audience (o-memie) with the lord and received the honor of o-nagare (a ceremonial serving of sake from the lord's own cup).
　　Note: He arrived at Rishiri Island in the sixth month and performed his assigned duties there.

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, the aforementioned duties having been completed, he returned to his post (home domain).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he had been dispatched for the duty of guarding Ezo, and — having applied himself diligently over many days both on sea and land routes and in that region, completing his duties without delay, and then returning home — he and all members of his unit (ai-gumi) were collectively presented with a formal meal (go-ryōri) at the castle in recognition of their service.
