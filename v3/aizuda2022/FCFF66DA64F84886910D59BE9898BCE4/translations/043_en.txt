(Continued from the previous page) He was summoned to serve as a ko-ban (junior guard).

Item: In the second month of Shōtoku 2 (1712), the Year of the Dragon, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned in the ninth month of the same year.

Item: In the ninth month of the same year, he was appointed as a hired escort for Lady Gyokurin-in and Lady Kōmyō-in, and fulfilled this duty from the beginning of the tenth month through the eleventh month, at which point the guard duty was suspended due to snowfall.

Item: In the third month of Shōtoku 3 (1713), the Year of the Snake, he was again appointed to escort both ladies in the same manner as the previous year; however, in the fifth month, the escort duty was suspended due to the arrival of Lord Tokuō on his journey down (to the domain).

Item: In the intercalary fifth month of the same year, groups of eight men from among the colleagues (nakama) were ordered to rush to Lady Gyokurin-in and Lady Kōmyō-in in the event of a fire.

Item: In the first month of Shōtoku 4 (1714), the Year of the Horse, he was appointed to serve as a personal protective guard (futokoro-mori) for Lady Gyokurin-in and Lady Kōmyō-in.

Item: In the second month of the same year, since there was no guard duty while (the lord) was residing in the domain, in order to maintain familiarity, two men at a time from among the colleagues were ordered to attend upon the ladies' chambers every day. (Continues on next page)
