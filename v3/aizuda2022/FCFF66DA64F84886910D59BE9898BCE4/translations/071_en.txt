One entry: In the 10th month of Meireki 2, the year of the Monkey (1656), he went up to Edo for duty rotation, served one tour, and returned home.

One entry: In the 6th month of Manji 2, the year of the Boar (1659), as he had petitioned, he was relieved of his official post and assigned to the Yoriai-gumi (a reserve/retired samurai group).

One entry: In the 4th month of the 3rd year of the same era, the year of the Rat (1660), he submitted a petition for a duty rotation replacement, upon which he was immediately ordered to retire.

One entry: On the 21st day of the 9th month of Kanbun 5, the year of the Snake (1665), he died of illness at the age of seventy-five. His posthumous Buddhist name was Gangō Sōden Koji. His burial place is Tenninji Temple at Banshōzan.
