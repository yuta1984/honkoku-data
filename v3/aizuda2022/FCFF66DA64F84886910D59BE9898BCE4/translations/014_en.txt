One item: In the 3rd month of the 5th year of Meiwa (1768), the year of the Rat, in connection with the handover of Yamagata Castle in Ushū (present-day Yamagata Prefecture), he traveled to that location and carried out the said official duty.

One item: In the 7th month of the 6th year of Meiwa (1769), the year of the Ox, he was appointed as a Inspector (Ometsuke).

One item: In the 9th month of the same year, he was granted a role stipend (oyakuryō) of fifty koku.

One item: In the 3rd month of the 7th year of Meiwa (1770), the year of the Tiger, he traveled up to Edo as an attendant on guard duty (otomo kinban).

One item: In the 4th month of the same year, it was officially proclaimed that he had served diligently in his duties both as an attendant and throughout the journey.

One item: In the 6th month of the same year, he submitted a petition for leave of absence (ohima) due to illness. In response, a replacement was ordered to travel up [to Edo] in his stead. However, as his condition made travel extremely difficult, his request to lodge at the residence of his kinsman (dōbyō) Kanji until he was well enough to travel was granted in accordance with his petition.

One item: In the 7th month of the same year, his petition to take as a son-in-law adoptee (muko yōshi) Heisaburō, the third son of Satō Heizaemon, was granted in accordance with his wishes.
