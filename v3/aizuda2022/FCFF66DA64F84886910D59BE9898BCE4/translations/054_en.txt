Biological origin: Third son of Azumi Kanzaemon Moriyuki
　Wife: Adopted daughter of his adoptive father Koemon Toshiyasu
　　（Her biological origin: Daughter of Kujūrō Toshinao）

Item: In the ninth month of An'ei 8, Year of the Boar (1779), in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (o-memie) with the lord.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, in accordance with his father's petition, he was appointed to castle guard duty (o-bandai), and served as castle guard in the unit of Harada Ihee.

Item: In the second month of Tenmei 1, Year of the Ox (1781), he was granted the inherited stipend of one hundred koku without any discrepancy.

Item: In the fourth month of the same year, as the unit commander (kumi-gashira) Harada Ihee had died of illness, he was transferred to the unit of Takahashi Ichinojō to succeed to that post.

Item: In the fourth month of Tenmei 3, Year of the Rabbit (1783), he was appointed to escort guard duty (o-tomo-ban).

Item: In the fourth month of Tenmei 6, Year of the Horse (1786), he traveled up to Edo for rotation duty (kinban), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: It was officially declared that throughout this period of Edo service, he had performed his duties diligently without any absences.
