(Continued from previous page) ...she served as an attendant at the memorial visit to the temple precinct (in-nai). Furthermore, as a bequest (o-katami), she was given one ceramic flower vase and one fukusa (a ceremonial cloth used for wrapping gifts).

One item: In the 12th month of the same year (Bunka 2, 1805), owing to her diligent service during a period of reduced staffing, she was specially granted two ryō in gold out of personal consideration (o-omoimeshi).

One item: In the same month, at the time of Lord Teishō's passing, she served in attendance at the worship at the temporary mortuary (o-kariya o-hai), as well as in the funeral procession (o-sōsō o-miokuri). Furthermore, during the memorial visit to the temple precinct (in-nai), she was added to the main attendant group (go-hondate) to serve as an escort.

One item: In the 3rd month of the third year of the Tiger (Bunka 3, 1806), she was appointed as a lady-in-waiting (o-soba jochū).

One item: In the 4th month of the same year, until Lord Kinbun's departure for Edo (o-sanpu), she served equally in attendance to Lord Shōshō (the Young Lord).

One item: In the 12th month of the same year, from Lord Kinbun and the Lord of the Domain (o-tōkun-sama), one bundle each of cotton wadding was granted (to her)... (the record continues on the following page).
