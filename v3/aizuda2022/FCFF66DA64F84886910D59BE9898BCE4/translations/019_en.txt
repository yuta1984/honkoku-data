　　Note: From this point forward, the same grant was to be given every year.

One item: In the 6th month of the fourth year of the Rabbit (Bunka 4, 1807), she was granted a used small robe (o-meshifuru o-kosode) that had been worn by the Lord of the Domain (o-tōkun-sama).

One item: In the 3rd month of the seventh year of the Horse (Bunka 7, 1810), in connection with Lord Kinbun's departure for Edo (o-sanpu), she traveled ahead to the capital and was commanded to have an audience (o-mie) with Lady Juhōin, during which she received dried abalone (o-te-noshi-awabi) from her hands.

One item: In the same month, when Lady Renjuin came to stay at the upper residence (o-kami-yashiki), she was commanded to have an audience (o-mie) with her.

One item: In the 6th month of the ninth year of the Monkey (Bunka 9, 1812), she was appointed as o-soba omotetsūkai (a senior female messenger/attendant role), and was granted the name "Yaeno."
