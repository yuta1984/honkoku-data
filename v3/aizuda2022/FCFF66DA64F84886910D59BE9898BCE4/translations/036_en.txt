## Genealogical Record of Hinata Miki — Clan: Minamoto (Gen) — Home Province: Shinano — Primary Family Crest: Hawk Feathers (Chigai Takaha) within a Circle — Alternate Crest: Sword-and-Diamond (Kenbishi) within a Circle

Third son of Hinata Hizaemon Chikashige

**Hinata Miki Shigekari** (Childhood name: Matasaburō; born on the 2nd day of the 10th month of the 3rd year of An'ei [1774])

- Mother: Daughter of Rusui [Domain representative in Edo] Ishida Jūbei Narumasa
- Wife: Adopted daughter of Ichiyanagi Shinzaburō Naoaki (born on the 9th day of the 1st month of the 9th year of An'ei [1780])
  - Biological father: Daughter of Tanaka Jirōdayū Tsugutomo
