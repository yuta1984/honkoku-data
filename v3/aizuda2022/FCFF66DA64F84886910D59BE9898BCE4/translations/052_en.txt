(Continued from previous page) ...traveled up to Edo, and returned in the fourth month of the following year, the Year of the Rooster (1777), as part of the lord's entourage.

Item: In the fourth month of the same sixth year, the Year of the Rooster (1777), as he had applied himself diligently and performed his duties admirably during his current posting (kinban), he was granted a set of formal hemp ceremonial garments (asa o-kamishimo ikku) as a reward.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, as he was not proficient in his current duties, he was relieved of his post and assigned to the tozama-gumi (outer group), placed under Kayano Gonbei's unit, and took up duty as a castle guard (o-shiro o-ban).

Item: In the first month of the same seventh year, the Year of the Dog (1778), as the unit commander (kumigashira) Kayano Gonbei was ordered to retire, he was transferred to Harada Iyo's unit as the successor arrangement.

Item: In the eighth month of the same eighth year, the Year of the Boar (1779), his request to take Sanchū, the third son of Azumi Kanemon, as his son-in-law and adopted heir (muko-yōshi), was granted.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, in accordance with his request, his son Sanchū was appointed as his duty replacement (o-bandai).

Item: On the ninth day of the twelfth month of Anei 9 (1780), the Year of the Rat, he died of illness at the age of sixty-four. His posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō) was Hōmyōin Gyōshū Nichian Koji. His place of burial is the same as previously recorded.
