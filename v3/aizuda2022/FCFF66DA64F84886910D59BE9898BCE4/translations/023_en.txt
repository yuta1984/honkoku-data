One item: In the same month, due to an unscheduled departure for Edo (sankō), the retainer proceeded to Edo as a vanguard attendant (saki-ban).

One item: In the fourth month of Tenmei 2, the year of the Tiger (1782), one ryō of gold was privately bestowed upon him.

One item: In the same month, the retainer was appointed as a trainee of the o-zen-ban (dining/meal service duty).
　　Note: After this, the retainer was appointed on multiple occasions to temporary and fixed-temporary positions within the o-zen-ban and performed those duties accordingly.

One item: In the seventh month of the same year, as there were items from the lord's personal wardrobe stored in the o-tamari (storage), three items — one lined garment (awase), one unlined summer garment (katabira), and one hakama — were unexpectedly bestowed as an expression of the lord's personal consideration, as conveyed through the o-yōnin.

One item: In the first month of Tenmei 3, the year of the Rabbit (1783), having been recognized for diligent service on ordinary days and for four consecutive years of perfect attendance (kaikkin), the retainer was, in recognition of this achievement, bestowed with one set (one top and bottom) of o-meshi-asa (hemp garments of the lord's own selection) as a reward, as conveyed through the o-yōnin.

One item: In the fifth month of the same year, upon the lord's return to the domain (o-gekō), the retainer accompanied the procession back to the domain as a rear-guard attendant (ato-ban).
