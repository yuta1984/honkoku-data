Shichirōemon Mitsunori　(Born in the Year of the Rabbit, Tenshō 19 [1591])
　Mother: same as above
　Wife: Daughter of Shibata Hyōe Moritsugu (Died of illness on the 21st day of the 4th month, Enpō 3, Year of the Rabbit [1675]. Posthumous Buddhist name: Shōokyaboku Daishi. Place of burial: Tenneiji Temple, Banshōzan)

One entry: In the 5th month of Genna 1 [1615], at the time of the Osaka Campaign [Siege of Osaka], he sortied into battle. However, as his father Shōgen had sustained serious wounds, he was granted leave and withdrew [from the battlefield] to tend to him.

One entry: Genna 4, the Year of the Horse [1618], month unknown: He was granted the family headship [katoku] and a stipend [chigyō] of 200 koku without any discrepancy, and was ordered to have this communicated [by notification] to the retainers of the three mounted warriors [sankiの士] of Kami-Ina.

One entry: In the 3rd month of Kan'ei 9, the Year of the Monkey [1632], at the time of construction work on the main Buddha hall of Zōjōji Temple, he was appointed as superintendent of construction [fuсhin bugyō].

One entry: In the 4th month of the same 10th year [Kan'ei 10, Year of the Rooster, 1633], as Jōkōin-sama traveled to the hot springs at Asama in Shinshū [Shinano Province] for a medicinal bath, he served as her escort.
