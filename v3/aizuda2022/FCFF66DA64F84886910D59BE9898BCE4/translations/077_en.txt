Item: In the 2nd month of Genroku 9, the year of the Rat (1696), in recognition of his ancestors' distinguished past service, and as he was among the hereditary retainers (fudai), he was graciously granted a new stipend of 100 koku and was summoned to serve as an escort guard (otomo-ban).

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, he traveled to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned in the 3rd month of the following year of the Ox (1697).

Item: In the 1st month of the same 10th year, the year of the Ox (1697), he was granted the former residence of Koizumi Tsunaemon.

Item: In the 5th month of the same year, he traveled to and returned from Edo to escort the lord on his return journey, and then accompanied the lord back.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 12th year, the year of the Rabbit (1699), he traveled to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the 6th month of the same year accompanying the lord.

Item: In the 5th month of the 15th year, the year of the Horse (1702), he traveled for the lord's escort on the return journey, and returned in the same month.
