One item: In the ninth month of the same year, the hereditary stipend of one hundred koku was granted without any discrepancy, and the retainer was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit). He was placed in the unit of Kasuya Gozaemon and performed castle guard duty.

One item: In the twelfth month of An'ei 3, the year of the Horse (1774), the retainer's wish to change his name to "Shōzō" was granted in accordance with his petition.

One item: In the first month of An'ei 9, the year of the Rat (1780), the retainer was appointed as an o-koshō (page/personal attendant to the lord).

One item: In the same month, the retainer's wish to change his name to "Shōkurō" was granted in accordance with his petition.

One item: In the third month of the same year, as the lord departed for his required attendance in Edo (sankō), the retainer proceeded to Edo as a rear-guard attendant (ato-ban).

One item: In the third month of Tenmei 1, the year of the Ox (1781), as there were items from the lord's old personal wardrobe, one ko-sode (short-sleeved kimono) was bestowed as an expression of the lord's personal consideration.

One item: In the fourth month of the same year, as the lord returned to the domain (o-gekō), the retainer preceded him back to the domain as a vanguard attendant (saki-ban).
