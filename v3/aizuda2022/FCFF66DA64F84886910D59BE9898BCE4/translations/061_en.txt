Item: In the eighth month of Bunka 6, Year of the Snake (1809), in recognition of his diligent service in the Ezo guard duties, he was awarded one set of御時服 (go-jifuku, a formal garment presented as a gift from the lord) as a mark of commendation.

Item: In the same month, he went to Hongōhara and lodged there for one night in connection with the tori-kari (falconry hunt/military exercise).
　　Note: Of the two subsequent occasions on which he participated in the exercise, on one occasion he received game as a gift.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, during the tori-kari exercise, the formation and marching were well ordered and operations proceeded without hindrance. As it was deemed that this was owing to their diligent practice of combined firearms drill (ai-tetsu keiko), all members of his unit (ai-gumi) were collectively praised and commended by the lord.
　　Note: Thereafter, they received such commendation on every subsequent occasion.

Item: In the twelfth month of Bunka 9, Year of the Monkey (1812), the training methods for the combined unit drills at the Sannomaru training hall and at Hongōhara were reformed beginning that spring. On the occasion of its inauguration, as the formation and marching were well ordered, it was deemed that this was owing to their diligent efforts, and all members of his unit were collectively praised and commended by the lord.
