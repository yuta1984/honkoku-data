(Continued from previous page) He was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outside retainer unit) and placed under the command of Ueda Hachirozaemon's unit, where he served as castle guard (o-shiro o-ban).

Item: In the 4th month of Shōtoku 3, the year of the Snake (1713), he traveled to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the 10th month of the same year.

Item: In the 10th month of Kyōhō 17, the year of the Rat (1732), he was appointed as Commissioner of the Gold Mines (Kinzan-bugyō).

Item: In the 12th month of the same 18th year, the year of the Ox (1733), it was officially declared that he had diligently performed his duties in the matters entrusted to him by the domain inspectors (o-kuni-metsuke-shū).

Item: In the 12th month of Genbun 4, the year of the Ram (1739), his request to take Shōda Matabei's younger brother, Shichirozaemon, as a son-in-law and adopted heir was granted.

Item: In the 3rd month of the same 5th year, the year of the Monkey (1740), as he had requested, he was relieved of his official duties and assigned to the Tozama-gumi, placed under the command of Jinbo Kuranoshuke's unit.
　　Note: As his financial accounts had not yet been settled, he did not actually enter into guard service.

Item: On the 6th day of the 6th month of the same year, he died of illness at the age of 73.
　Posthumous Buddhist name: Kenzan Dōen Koji　Place of burial: Same as above (as previously recorded)
