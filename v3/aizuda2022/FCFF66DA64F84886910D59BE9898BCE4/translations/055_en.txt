Item: In the sixth month of Kansei 1, Year of the Rooster (1789), during the lord's journey down from Edo (go-gekō), he returned (to his domain), traveled up (to Edo) to serve as an escort, accompanied the lord, and returned (to his domain).

Item: In the third month of Kansei 2, Year of the Dog (1790), he traveled up to Edo for rotation duty (kinban), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: It was officially declared that throughout the journey he had performed his duties without any absences; and in the ninth month of the same year, it was officially declared that throughout his rotation duty he had performed his duties diligently without any absences.

Item: In the third month of Kansei 6, Year of the Tiger (1794), he returned (to his domain) and traveled up to Edo to accompany Lord Sadaaki on his sankin-kōtai procession to Edo.
　　Note: It was officially declared that throughout the journey, he had performed his duties diligently without any absences.

Item: In the fourth month of Kansei 8, Year of the Dragon (1796), he traveled up to Edo for rotation duty (kinban), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: It was officially declared that throughout his period of service, he had performed his duties without any absences.
