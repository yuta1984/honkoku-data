(Continued from previous page) ...he was summoned and placed in the unit of Jinbo Kurannosuke, and served as a guard at the castle.

Item: In the third month of Enkyo 4 (1747), the Year of the Hare, he was granted a standard gisaku stipend of twenty-five koku with an allowance for four attendants (yoninbuchi), and was appointed to the role of Otomo-ban (escort guard duty).

Item: In the tenth month of Kan'en 1 (1748), the Year of the Dragon, he traveled up to Edo for alternate attendance (kinban) duty, and returned in the company of the lord in the fourth month of the following Year of the Snake (1749).
　　Note: It was announced that he had served without a single absence throughout the campaign/journey.

Item: In the third month of the same third year (1750), the Year of the Horse, he traveled up to Edo again for escort and alternate attendance duty, and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: It was announced that he had served without a single absence throughout the journey.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, upon the arrival of the honored remains of Lord Shijo (士常様), he served as a guard at the temporary mortuary chapel (o-kariden).

Item: In the first month of Hōreki 3 (1753), the Year of the Rooster, as he had performed various duties during the residence of the domain inspector officials (o-kuni-metsuke-shū), he was granted a commendation (o-shōbi).
