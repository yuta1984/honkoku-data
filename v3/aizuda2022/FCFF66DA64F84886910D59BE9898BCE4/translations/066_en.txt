[Mitsumori] was summoned into service with our domain and was granted a stipend of 200 koku in Higuchi Village, where his ancestors had resided for generations. Within the Takato domain's territory, there was a place of 50 koku bordering Kiso, Matsumoto, Suwa, and Miwa, and [the following persons] were stationed there: Shōgen, as well as Shibata Hyōe, Higuchi Sakuzaemon, Higuchi Gorōzaemon, Higuchi Matasaemon, Yajima Tamba, Tatsuno Genta, Ariga Tarōzaemon, Hiraide Sanuki, Hiraide Kohyōe, Iga Gōzaemon, Akabane Hida, and Urushido Nuido-u-emon, along with a large number of archers and gunners. Accordingly, both Shōgen and Tahei were ordered to oversee the management of these soldiers and troops.

One entry: The twelfth month of the same Tenshō 12, the Year of the Monkey (1584): At the time of Ogasawara Sadayoshi's attack, and also at the time of the Ueda campaign, Shōgen, Tahei, and eleven other warriors exerted themselves to the utmost. Sakuzaemon and Gorōzaemon were killed in battle, and accordingly their sons were each granted succession to their family estates. At the time of the Osaka campaign, Shōgen and Tahei served as spear magistrates in attendance; Tahei and Seizaemon were killed in battle.
