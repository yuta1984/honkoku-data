　　Note: On subsequent occasions, being the same circumstances, he was each time presented with sake (rice wine) by the lord.

Item: In the tenth month of Bunka 14, Year of the Ox (1817), in recognition of his diligent and exemplary performance in military drills (sōren), he was awarded one new spearhead (atarashimi no yari, issui) as a mark of commendation.

Item: In the same month, during the repair works on the castle stone walls, he voluntarily presented himself and performed manual labor as an act of personal devotion. This was declared by the lord to be a matter of particularly notable conduct, and he was presented with sake as a reward.
