**Matabō [Matazo], Shigehiro** (childhood name: Kumeno-suke; born on the 3rd day of the 12th month of Kansei 11 [1799], Year of the Sheep)
　Mother: Adopted daughter of the Ichiyanagi clan
　Wife: Daughter of Uchida Denzaemon Chikayoshi (born on the 11th day of the 6th month of Kyōwa 2 [1802], Year of the Dog)

- Item 1: In the 3rd month of Bunka 7 [1810], Year of the Horse, in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first audience [o-memie] with the lord.

**Kiyojirō** (born on the 8th day of the 1st month of Kyōwa 2 [1802], Year of the Dog; died of illness on the 13th day of the 11th month of Kyōwa 3 [1803], Year of the Boar; posthumous Buddhist name [hōmyō]: Sōkō Dōji; place of burial: Hōshōzan Jōkōji)
　Mother: Same as above

**Daughter** (born on the 11th day of the 11th month of Bunka 1 [1804], Year of the Rat; died on the 10th day of the 8th month of Bunka 9 [1812], Year of the Monkey; posthumous Buddhist name: Shūgetsu Myōki Dōjo; place of burial: same as above)
　Mother: Same as above
