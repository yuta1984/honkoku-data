1. In the 7th month of Bunka 2, the year of the Ox [1805]: As his son Shichikurō died of illness, his second son Sajirō was, in accordance with his petition, designated as the heir (chakushi).

2. In the 8th month of Bunka 3, the year of the Tiger [1806]: Due to illness, he was permitted, in accordance with his petition, to surrender his kofushin stipend and retire to recuperate at his leisure.

3. On the 9th day of the 10th month of the same year: He died of illness at the age of sixty-eight. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Ichiyū Gентай Koji. Place of burial: same as above.

**Kakehi Rokudayū Mitsutatsu** — Adopted son of the senior lady-in-waiting (rōjo) Fujii
　His mother was a daughter of his grandfather Iemon Mitsuchika.

1. In the 1st month of Hōreki 8, the year of the Pig [1758]: He was granted a stipend of eight ryō and four-person rice allowance (yoninbuchi), and was called into service as an o-koshō (page/attendant).

2. In the 1st month of Meiwa 5, the year of the Rat [1768]: By private/informal directive (go-naī), he was appointed as the adopted son of the senior lady-in-waiting Fujii.
