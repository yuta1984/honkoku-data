One item: In the 8th month of the 2nd year of Enkyō (1745), the year of the Ox, he was commended for having mastered the martial arts.

One item: In the 11th month of the same year, he was commended for having served twenty years of castle guard duty without negligence.

One item: In the 6th month of the 1st year of Hōreki (1751), the year of the Sheep, following the appointment of his unit commander Tanaka Kahyōe as a junior elder (wakadoshiyori), he was transferred to the unit under Hori Hanemon.

One item: In the 1st month of the 3rd year of Hōreki (1753), the year of the Rooster, he was commended for having served thirty years of castle guard duty without negligence.

One item: In the 11th month of the 10th year of Hōreki (1760), the year of the Dragon, he was granted a set of seasonal garments (jifuku) in recognition of thirty-eight years of perfect attendance in service.

One item: In the 1st month of the 12th year of Hōreki (1762), the year of the Horse, following the dismissal of his unit commander Hori Hanemon from his post, he was subsequently transferred to the unit under Ueda Hachirōuemon.

One item: On the 2nd day of the 2nd month of the 13th year of Hōreki (1763), the year of the Sheep, he died of illness at the age of sixty-one. His posthumous Buddhist name was Denshuinin Rinhōsoyu Koji. His place of burial is the same as stated above.
