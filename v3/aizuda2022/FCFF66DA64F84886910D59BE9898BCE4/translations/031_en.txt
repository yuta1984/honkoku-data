One item: In the twelfth month of the thirteenth year [of Bunka], the year of the Rat (1816), during training at the Sannomaru (third enclosure) drill ground, his formation alignment and related conduct were consistently excellent, and he advanced in rank, having attained the top grade for three consecutive years. Recognizing this as the result of his diligent effort, it was announced that the entire unit would be granted sake (o-sakazuki) by the lord.

One item: In the second month of the fourteenth year [of Bunka], the year of the Ox (1817), as the unit commander (gashira), Jinbo Kurannosuke, was appointed to the post of Junior Councilor (wakadoshiyori), he was consequently reassigned as a supplementary appointment to the unit of Suka Sakon, filling the vacancy left behind.

One item: In the third month of the same year, in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his duty as assistant instructor of ceremonial etiquette (reishiki-kata shinan tetsudai).

One item: In the eighth month of the same year, having gone out for the bird-hunting exercise (oitori-gari) and made a catch, he was granted an audience with the lord at the hunting ground (o-ba). The following day, at the castle, he received the hunted birds as a gift from the lord.
　　Note: On one further occasion after this, he again went out, made a catch, and similarly received a gift in the same manner.

One item: In the tenth month of the same year, as the hunting ground for the current bird-hunting exercise had been changed and it was the first time [using the new location], his formation, marching order, and related conduct were well-coordinated, the units were efficiently linked together, and operations proceeded without impediment. It was decreed that this was a reflection of his longstanding diligence in combined unit training (gōtai keiko), and the entire unit received commendation (o-shōbi) from the lord.
