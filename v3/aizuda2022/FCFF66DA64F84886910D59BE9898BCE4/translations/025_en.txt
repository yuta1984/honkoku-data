(Continued from previous page) ...a petition was submitted requesting permission to adopt Kichigoro, the younger brother of Maruyama Shirobei, as an heir. However, before the lord's authorization (o-gechi) could be granted, the retainer died of illness. After his death, the family was informed that the request would be honored posthumously, as communicated to all the relatives concerned.

One item: On the second day of the same month [fourth month of Kansei 4, 1792], he died of illness at the age of thirty-three. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Onryōin Kyōgaku Jōken Koji. Officiating monk (dōshi): from Shōunzan Zenryūji temple. Place of burial: Ōkuboyama.

[One] daughter: Wife of the adopted heir Shōkurō Mitsutomi.
　　Mother: same as above [i.e., same mother as the deceased]

Shōtarō: Died of illness on the twenty-sixth day of the fifth month of Tenmei 8, the year of the Monkey (1788), at the age of three. Posthumous Buddhist name: Ryōun Zendōji. Place of burial: Shōunzan Zenryūji.
　　Mother: daughter of his grandfather Kyūzaemon Mitsuhisa

Kannojō (adopted heir of the sixth generation): Mitsoon [childhood name: Kichitarō]
　　Biological origin: third son of Maruyama Saheiji Tanetada
