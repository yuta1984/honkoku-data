Accordingly, (the administration of the Asaka-gumi was) handed over to Sonemon.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, at the kaisho (assembly hall), it was officially decreed that, as Uemon had been subjected to official punishment (o-shioki) this time, he was notified that he was to be placed under house confinement (heimon).

Item: On the twenty-third day of the eleventh month of the same year, the house confinement was graciously pardoned.

Item: In the same month, (the administration of) the Asaka-gumi was received back from Shimizu Sonemon, and he resumed his duties as daikan as before.

Item: In the tenth month of Genbun 2 (1737), the Year of the Snake, as Asai Yajiuma had fallen ill, he was appointed as acting official (kari-yaku) for the Takaku-gumi, and served in this capacity until the twelfth month, at which point Sakai Uhee was appointed as daikan, and he accordingly handed over his duties to Uhee.

Item: In the third month of Genbun 4 (1739), the Year of the Goat, in recognition of his capable service in collecting revenues (o-shūnō-kata) and in instructing and admonishing the local populace (jige), he was granted two bundles of cotton (wata) as a reward.

Item: On the second day of the fourth month of the same year, he died of illness at the age of seventy-one.
　Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Senryōin Tsūen Nichiyō Koji　Place of burial: Hōshōzan Jōkōji Temple
