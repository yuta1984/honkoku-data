One: In the ninth month of the same year, at the rank of mochi-seki, he was appointed as close-attendant guard [o-kinjo-ban] to Lord Sadaaki, and at that time was ordered to serve as a regular attendant escort [hira-ban] for Lord Kyōsada.

One: In the third month of the sixth year [of Tenmei, 1786, the year of the Horse], it was officially declared that, since the previous ninth month, he had served diligently without a single absence.

One: In the twelfth month of the same year, given that he had served diligently in a posting with few personnel and considerable expenditure of effort, he was granted two bundles of cotton [as a reward].

One: In the first month of the seventh year [of Tenmei, 1787, the year of the Sheep], owing to his diligent service, it was declared that the allowance in lieu of his Edo stipend would not be subject to the loan-levy [o-kari-age], and that he would receive one ryō in gold per year.

One: In the same month, he was relieved of his post as close-attendant guard and reassigned to the role of attendant escort for Lord Sadaaki.

One: In the first month of the eighth year [of Tenmei, 1788, the year of the Monkey], the request that Tetsunojō, the biological son of his adoptive father Jinuemon, be made the legitimate heir, was granted as petitioned.

One: In the twelfth month of the same year, as petitioned, he was relieved of his current duties, appointed to the Aizu katte-tozama-gumi [the Aizu domain's outer-retainer unit], entered the unit of Harada Gorouemon, and served as castle guard.
