One item: In the tenth month of the same year, regarding the said combined unit training (gōtai keiko): since the year of the Rabbit (Bōnen), the bird-hunt chase (oi-torikari) had been suspended, and a lighter form of drill (sōren) had been ordered in its place in the aforementioned style. It was thereupon declared that the formation of ranks, keeping of pace, and manner of deployment had all proceeded without impediment, and that it was evident he had long applied himself with diligence and care to his training.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, on the occasion of the lord's pilgrimage to In'nai, he performed official duty (shutsuyaku) as a supplementary assistant to the district magistrate (o-daikan hōkin), going out to attend upon the procession en route, and was graciously acknowledged by the lord's words (o-i o komutte sōrō koto).

Tomogoro — (Died of illness on the sixteenth day of the eleventh month of the eighth year of Kansei, the year of the Dragon [1796], at the age of twenty. Posthumous Buddhist name [hōmyō]: Yōkō Zendōji. Place of burial: Shōunzan Zenryūji.)
Mother: daughter of the Hara clan.

Daughter — (Born on the twentieth day of the eighth month of the ninth year of Kansei, the year of the Snake [1797].) Adopted as the daughter of Aga Yasojirō Yoshimasa, but subsequently separated. Later became the wife of Satō Hyakutarō Nobukata.
Mother: same as above.
