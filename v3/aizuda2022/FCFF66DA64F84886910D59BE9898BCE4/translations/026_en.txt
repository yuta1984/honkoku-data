Wife: daughter of Hara Sagouemon Toshimine. [Died of illness on the second day of the second month of Bunsei 3, the year of the Dragon (1820), at the age of forty-eight. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Kakumeiin Tōhō Shōka Daishi. Officiating monk (dōshi): from Shōunzan Zenryūji temple. Place of burial: Ōkuboyama.]

One item: In the sixth month of Kansei 4, the year of the Rat (1792), he was granted the hereditary stipend (atoshiki go-chigyō) of one hundred koku without any change, was assigned to the Tozama-gumi unit, placed in the unit of Jinbo Kuranosuke, and duly performed duties as castle guard (o-shiro o-ban).

One item: In the same month, he was directed to continue serving as a school recitation instructor (judoshi) as before; accordingly, he was relieved of the duty of attending the castle guard muster roll (chakutō) for both castle guard and residential duty (iryaku).

One item: In the ninth month of the fifth year, the year of the Ox (1793), he turned out [for service] on the occasion of the falconry hunt (oitori-gari).

One item: In the first month of the seventh year, the year of the Rabbit (1795), as the school [system] was reorganized, he was relieved of his duties as recitation instructor. In recognition of his diligent service up to that point, he was awarded one bundle of cotton (wata hitotaba) as a reward.

One item: In the eighth month of the ninth year, the year of the Snake (1797), he turned out [for service] on the occasion of the falconry hunt.
　　Note: After this, he turned out on six occasions in total, and on one occasion, as a preliminary exercise (narashi), he went out to Hongōhara and lodged there for one night.
