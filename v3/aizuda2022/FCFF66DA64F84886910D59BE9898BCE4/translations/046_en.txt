Note: When he petitioned to return the stipend increase that had been granted to him the previous year, he was informed by official decree that it should remain with him as before.

Item: In the eighth month of Kyōhō 16 (1731), the Year of the Boar, he was appointed as head of the accounts office (o-kanjō-gashira).

Item: In the eighth month of Kyōhō 17 (1732), the Year of the Rat, in recognition of his capable prior service, he was appointed as daikan (magistrate/land administrator), and his seating rank was assigned above that of Nomura Kōji.

Item: In the tenth month of Kyōhō 20 (1735), the Year of the Rabbit, in recognition of his capable service in various duties, he was assigned to the tozama-gumi (outer retainer group), placed under the command of Obara Gorōemon's unit, and took up castle guard duty (o-shiro o-ban).

Item: In the third month of Genbun 1 (1736), the Year of the Dragon, he was ordered to transfer to the unit under Jinbō Kurannosuke's command.

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, as the daikan Ōtake Kakubee had ceased to perform his duties, he was ordered to administer the Asaka-gumi (Asaka district unit) in Ōtake's place.
