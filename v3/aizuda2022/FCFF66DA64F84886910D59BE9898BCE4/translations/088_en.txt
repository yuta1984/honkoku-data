1. In the 11th month of the same year [Bunsei 1, year of the Ox, 1818]: As he had no biological children, he submitted a petition requesting to take Chūshirō, the second son of Machino Shōzaburō, as his son-in-law and adopted heir. Before an official directive was issued in response to this petition, he died of illness on the 21st day of the same month at the age of thirty-two. Thereafter, it was announced to his relatives that his petition would be granted posthumously. Funeral rites were performed according to Shinto custom; his posthumous spiritual name (*reigō*) was Tamahiko-rei; burial place: [remainder missing]

**Hayato Mitsuharu**　《Originally named Chūshirō; born on the 29th day of the 7th month of Kansei 12, year of the Monkey [1800]》

　Biological father: Machino Shōzaburō Shigeyoshi, second son
　Wife: Daughter of adoptive father Iemon Mitsunaga

1. In the 3rd month of the 9th year of Bunka [year of the Horse, 1812]: As it became known that he had been diligently devoted to his studies and showed a level of skill beyond his years, he was awarded the texts *Kōkyō Taigi* (Great Meaning of the Classic of Filial Piety) and *Shisho Shūchū* (Collected Commentaries on the Four Books) as a mark of commendation.
