Biological father: Second son of Shōda Kazubei Tadakuzu.
Wife: Daughter of adoptive father Iemon Mitsuchika (died of illness on the 20th day of the 9th month of Meiwa 4, the year of the Boar; posthumous Buddhist name: Jikian Myōkū Daishi; burial place: same as above).

1. In the intercalary 7th month of Genbunn 5, the year of the Monkey: He was granted, without dispute, the inherited stipend of 100 koku; assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer group); placed under the command of Jimbo Kurannosuke's unit; and performed duty as castle guard.

2. In the 3rd month of Kan'en 3, the year of the Horse: He traveled to Edo [the shogunal capital] to serve in the escort party, performed duty as guard at the official lodging (honjin) along the road, and returned home upon rotation in the 10th month of the same year.

3. In the 3rd month of Hōreki 3, the year of the Rooster: His nephew Iemon was, in accordance with his petition, appointed as his adopted son.

4. In the 7th month of Meiwa 5, the year of the Rat: His son Iemon was, in accordance with his petition, appointed as his replacement in guard duty.

5. On the 3rd day of the 8th month of Meiwa 8, the year of the Boar: He died of illness at the age of 66. Posthumous Buddhist name: Ichigen Sōkaku Koji; burial place: same as above.

Daughter: Wife of adopted son Shichirozaemon Mitsunao.
