One item: In the third month of the same year [the tenth year of Bunka, the year of the Rooster, 1813], he was provisionally appointed on a temporary basis to the additional post of school recitation instructor (gakkō judoshi soegkin).

One item: In the fifth month of the same year, he was formally appointed to the additional post of school recitation instructor.

One item: In the intercalary eleventh month of the same year, in accordance with his father's petition, he was appointed as a guard replacement (o-bandai), assigned to the unit of Jinbo Kurannosuke, and was instructed to continue serving concurrently as school recitation instructor as before. In connection with this appointment, it was officially announced that he was exempted from castle guard duty (o-shiro o-ban) as well as from the attendance requirement for the residential post (iryaku chakutō).
