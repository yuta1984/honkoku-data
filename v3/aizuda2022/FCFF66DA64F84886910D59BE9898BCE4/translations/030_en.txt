One item: In the third month of the same year [Bunka 10, year of the Rooster, 1813], he was provisionally appointed on a temporary basis to assist the school recitation instructor (jūshi soekkin).

One item: In the fifth month of the same year, he was formally appointed to assist the school recitation instructor (jūshi soekkin).

One item: In the intercalary eleventh month of the same year, in accordance with his father's petition, he was appointed as a guard replacement (o-bandai), assigned to the unit of Jinbo Kurannosuke, and was directed to continue serving as assistant to the school recitation instructor as before. In connection with this, it was officially announced that he was excused from castle guard duty (o-shiro o-ban) and from the requirement to present himself for residential duty attendance (iryaku chakutō).

One item: In the same month, as the school's organizational arrangements were revised, he was relieved of his duty as assistant recitation instructor. In recognition of his diligent service up to that point, he was awarded one bundle of cotton (wata hitotaba) as a commendation gift.

One item: In the twelfth month of the eleventh year [of Bunka], the year of the Dog (1814), during combined unit training (gōtai keiko), his formation alignment, marching order, and related conduct were found to be excellent, and he was advanced to the top rank for that year. It was decreed that this was a reflection of his longstanding diligence, and the entire unit received commendation from the lord.

One item: In the eleventh month of the twelfth year [of Bunka], the year of the Boar (1815), he was appointed to assist in instructing ceremonial etiquette (reishiki-kata shinan tetsudai-kin). In connection with this, it was officially announced that he was excused from castle guard duty (o-shiro o-ban) and from the requirement to present himself for residential duty attendance (iryaku chakutō).
