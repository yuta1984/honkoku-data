Daughter of Shiba Gorōemon Yoshiki

Item: In the 9th month of Jōkyō 2 (1685), the year of the Ox, he was granted, without discrepancy, the inherited stipend (atoshiki go-chigyō) of 550 koku, and served as castle guard (go-shiro go-ban) in the unit of Kayano Gonbei.

Item: In the 10th month of the same 3rd year (1686), the year of the Tiger, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban). The following month, the 4th month of the year of the Rabbit (1687), he was ordered to accompany the lord on his journey to Kyoto, serving as escort directly from Edo, and returned in the 5th month of the same year.

Item: In the 10th month of the same 4th year (1687), the year of the Rabbit, he was appointed to the post of Monoganashira (unit commander), and was entrusted with command of the lord's ashigaru bowmen (go-yumi ashigaru).

Item: In the 6th month of Genroku 1 (1688), the year of the Dragon, upon the lord's entry into the domain (go-nyūbu), he was ordered to go out to meet the lord's lodging and serve as escort. He led his unit and went out as far as Fukura to serve as escort.

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the 2nd month of the following year of the Snake (1689).

Item: In the 9th month of the same 6th year (1693), the year of the Rooster, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the 3rd month of the following year of the Dog (1694).
