**Iemon Mitsunaga** — (Marginal note: childhood name Sajirō; born in the 7th month of Tenmei 6, the year of the Horse [1786])
　　Mother: same as above
　　Wife: a daughter of Machida Mosuke Takakane

1. In the 5th month of Bunka 3, the year of the Tiger [1806]: In accordance with his parent's petition, was granted an audience with the lord for the first time [御目見].

1. In the 12th month of the same year: The inherited stipend of one hundred koku was granted without alteration. As he was to undergo training in the arts [of military accomplishment], he was not assigned to regular duties, and was instructed to submit the kofu-shinryō (the fee paid by those placed in the kofushin reserve status).
