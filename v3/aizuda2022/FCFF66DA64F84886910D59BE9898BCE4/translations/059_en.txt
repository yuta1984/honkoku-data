**Genzō Toshinaga**

　Actual birth: second son of Iida Kujūrō (Funoki/Nuno-kyū)
　Wife: daughter of his adoptive father Sanai Toshikata

Item: In the seventh month of Kōwa 3, Year of the Boar (1803), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted his first audience (o-memie) with the lord.

Item: In the ninth month of the same year, in accordance with his parent's petition, his request to change his name to Genzō was granted.

Item: In the eleventh month of Bunka 3, Year of the Tiger (1806), he was granted, without discrepancy, an inherited stipend (atoshiki go-chigyō) of one hundred koku.
