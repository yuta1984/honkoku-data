One: In the twelfth month of the ninth year of An'ei [1780, the year of the Rat], a petition to adopt Gonji-yoshi, the fourth son of Tōyama Tōemon, as an heir was granted.

One: On the fourth day of the fourth month of the first year of Tenmei [1781, the year of the Ox], he died of illness at the age of forty-nine. Posthumous Buddhist name [hōmyō]: Shinkaku Dōi Shinji. Place of burial: Tokusui-in, within the precincts of Zōjō-ji Temple in Edo.

【Kichitarō】
　Mother: a daughter of the Shibuya clan.

Seidayū Tsugitomo (adopted son)　　[Original name: Gonji; born on the fifth day of the eleventh month of the sixth year of Hōreki (1756, the year of the Snake)]
　Biological father: Tōyama Tōemon Kagetoshi [景珍], fourth son.
　First wife: a daughter of Fujisawa Sagenji Chikatsune. Born on the third day of the tenth month of the twelfth year of Hōreki [1762, the year of the Sheep]. Died of illness on the eleventh day of the eighth month of the fourth year of Kansei [1792, the year of the Rat]. Posthumous Buddhist name: Raien Jōsei Daifu. Place of burial: Shōgakuzan Jōganji Temple.
　　Second wife: a daughter of Murakami Shōemon Yoshifusa. Born on the seventh day of the ninth month of the fourth year of Meiwa [1767, the year of the Boar]. Died of illness on the ninth day of the tenth month of the eleventh year of Bunka [1814, the year of the Dog]. Posthumous Buddhist name: Chiken Kakushō Daishi. Place of burial: Hōshōzan Jōkōji Temple.

One: In the second month of the first year of Tenmei [1781, the year of the Ox], five koku was deducted from the stipend [gigi-saku] inherited from his late father, and he was granted twenty koku with a ration of four persons' provisions [yonin-fuuchi], and was called into service as a tōzama-shi [outside retainer] stationed permanently in Edo [Edo jōzume].
