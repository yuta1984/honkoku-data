Note: It was officially declared that even though the number of attendants had been considerably reduced during the journey, he had performed his duties diligently without any absences.

Item: In the twelfth month of Kansei 12, Year of the Monkey (1800), his request to take Kingo, the second son of Iida Kyūjūrō, as a son-in-law and adoptive heir (muko-yōshi) was approved.

Item: In the fourth month of Kyōwa 1, Year of the Rooster (1801), he returned (to his domain) and traveled up to Edo to serve as an escort for the lord's return procession, accompanied the lord, and returned (to his domain).
　　Note: It was officially declared that even though the number of attendants had been considerably reduced during the journey, he had performed his duties diligently.

Item: In the fifth month of the same year, he was appointed to the position of o-metsuke (inspector/censor).

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, he was additionally appointed to serve as kōji-bugyō (magistrate of public affairs/lawsuits), and served in that capacity until the eighth month of the same year.

Item: In the same month, he was granted an office stipend (yakuryō) of thirty koku.

Item: Since the lord's journey down from Edo (go-gekō) in the sixth month of Bunka 1, Year of the Rat (1804), as there had been a shortage of staff among his colleagues and matters had been numerous and demanding, he had continued to serve diligently throughout the lord's residence in the domain; and for this, he was granted two bundles of cotton as a reward.
