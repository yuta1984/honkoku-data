One: In the third month of the ninth year of Bunka [1812, the year of the Monkey], as he had applied himself diligently to his studies and was said to demonstrate skill beyond his years, he was awarded, as a commendation, copies of the *Kōkyō Daigi* [Great Meaning of the Classic of Filial Piety], the *Shisho Shūchū* [Collected Annotations on the Four Books], and the *Shōgaku* [Elementary Learning].

One: In the seventh month of the tenth year [of Bunka, 1813, the year of the Rooster], as he had continued to apply himself diligently to his studies and had advanced one rank for demonstrating skill beyond his years, he was awarded, as a commendation, a copy of the *Sanrei Kochū* [Ancient Annotations on the Three Rites].
