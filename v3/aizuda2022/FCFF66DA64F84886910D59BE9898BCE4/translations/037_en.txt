- Item 1: In the 9th month of the same year [Kansei 5, 1793], he was called out to participate in the Otoritogari [a military field exercise involving bird hunting].
  - Note: After this, he participated seven more times.

- Item 1: In the 4th month of the 6th year of the same era [Kansei 6, 1794, Year of the Tiger], it was proclaimed that during the previous autumn's Otoritogari exercises, his footwork, movement, and general conduct were well-ordered and coordinated, and this was deemed to be the result of his diligent private training.
  - Note: After this, he was commended on every occasion.

- Item 1: In the 9th month of the 8th year of the same era [Kansei 8, 1796, Year of the Dragon], having shown diligence in the martial arts and achieved mastery in a recognized discipline, he was released from the Kobushin fee obligation, ordered to resume active duty, and assigned to castle guard service.

- Item 1: In the same month, it was noted that he had consistently practiced spearmanship without neglect and with particular diligence. During a prior group demonstration, his performance was also excellent and was regarded as admirable. Accordingly, he was granted a gift of sake. It was further proclaimed that he should continue henceforth without any slackening of effort, and continue to train diligently in his art.
