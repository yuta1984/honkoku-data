One item: In the eleventh month of the same year, a request for leave of absence was submitted due to illness, and the request was granted as submitted. In recognition of many years of diligent service, a reward of one ryō in gold was bestowed.

One item: In the first month of Bunsei 5, the year of the Horse (1822), upon attending the New Year's ceremonial gathering (Oshōzome), it was found that Lord Kinbun was unwell (御不例). Nevertheless, out of deep consideration, attendance and service at the御座 (lord's sitting chamber) was ordered even during his illness. The retainer attended day and night without interruption at the lord's side, providing nursing care, and received repeated expressions of warm personal regard from the lord.

One item: In the second month of the same year, upon Lord Kinbun's passing, in the third month, during the funeral procession, the retainer was ordered to see off the procession and proceeded to the San-no-Maru (third enclosure of the castle).

One item: In the same year (month unknown), one tan of ryōryō (fabric; transcribed as in the original) was bestowed as a memento from the deceased lord's estate.
