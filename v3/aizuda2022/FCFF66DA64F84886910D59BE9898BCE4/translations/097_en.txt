One: In the same [ninth month of the first year of Kansei, 1789, the year of the Rooster], he was placed into the unit of Yokoyama Takiuemon.

One: In the seventh month of the second year [of Kansei, 1790, the year of the Dog], the upper residence [kami-yashiki] of Akabane Tarōzaemon was granted to him.

One: In the third month of the fourth year [of Kansei, 1792, the year of the Rat], he turned out for the tori-oi-gari [the domain's ceremonial hunt].
　　Note: After this, he turned out for the hunt six times in total, and on one of those occasions received a game animal as a grant.

One: In the same month, as this was the first time [he participated in] the tori-oi-gari, he appeared to have served diligently, and each unit assembled in fine order. As for the unit members, it was all the more remarkable that they had drilled their marching and formation even before the order was issued, with little time available; it was noted that they appeared to have assembled in fine order. The entire unit received commendation together.
　　Note: After this, they received commendation on every subsequent occasion.

One: In the ninth month of the seventh year [of Kansei, 1795, the year of the Rabbit], as he had continuously pursued spear [sōjutsu] training from the year of the Rat [1792] through the previous year without interruption and with great diligence, he was granted one bundle of cotton as a reward.

One: In the seventh month of the tenth year [of Kansei, 1798, the year of the Horse], it was officially declared that it was considered admirable that, from the year of the Rabbit [1795] through the previous year, he had continuously and diligently pursued spear training and attained a level of skill of seven-tenths proficiency or above.
