Item: In the 4th month of Manji 3, the year of the Rat (1660), he inherited the family headship and was granted a stipend of 550 koku without discrepancy. He was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outside vassals unit) and placed in the unit of Akabane Jinemon, where he performed castle guard duties.

Item: In the 8th month of Kanbun 2, the year of the Tiger (1662), he traveled to Edo for rotation duty (kinban), and returned home in the 2nd month of the following year of the Rabbit (1663).

Item: In the 5th month of the same 6th year, the year of the Horse (1666), he was appointed as a Monohead (物頭, unit commander).

Item: In the 3rd month of the same 8th year, the year of the Monkey (1668), he traveled to Edo for rotation duty, and returned in the 9th month of the same year.

Item: In the 8th month of the same 12th year, the year of the Rat (1672), he traveled to Edo as part of the lord's entourage for rotation duty, completed one tour of service, and returned home.

Item: In the 4th month of Enpō 6, the year of the Horse (1678), he traveled to Edo as part of the lord's entourage for rotation duty, completed one tour of service, and returned home.

Item: In the 4th month of Tenna 2, the year of the Dog (1682), he traveled to Edo as part of the lord's entourage for rotation duty, completed one tour of service, and returned home.

Item: In the intercalary 5th month of the same 3rd year, the year of the Boar (1683), he was appointed as the castellan (御城代) of Inawashiro Castle, on account of his distinguished lineage.

Item: In the 6th month of Jōkyō 2, the year of the Ox (1685), he was relieved of his official post in accordance with his petition.
