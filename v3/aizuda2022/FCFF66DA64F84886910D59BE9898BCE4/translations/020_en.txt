On the occasion of Lord Kinbun's ceremonial departure (o-nori-dashi), she was granted a celebratory gift of 200 hiki in gold, and one used small robe (o-meshifuru o-kosode) from Lady Juhōin.

One item: In the 12th month of the same year, as a celebratory gift for his receiving an official court rank/appointment (go-ninkan), she was granted 300 hiki in gold.
　　Note: As she had made a considerable number of tailored articles (o-shitatemono) for the occasion, she was additionally granted 2 ryō in gold.

One item: In the 10th month of the eleventh year of the Dog (Bunka 11, 1814), on the occasion of the sleeve-trimming ceremony (o-sodedome), she was granted a congratulatory gift consisting of a formal sleeve ornament, a formal undersash (o-shitajime), and a gift inventory (o-mokuroku).

One item: In the 2nd month of the twelfth year of the Boar (Bunka 12, 1815), as a celebratory gift for the coming-of-age ceremony (o-genpuku), she was granted a gift inventory (o-mokuroku).

One item: In the 5th month of the same year, on the occasion of departing to accompany the lord's entry into the domain (o-nyūbu), she had an audience with Lady Renjuin and was commanded to receive various gifts; she also had an audience with Lady Juhōin and received one tan of shima-chirimen (striped crepe silk), along with various other gifts...
