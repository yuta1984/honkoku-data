　　Note: After this, he was commended on every subsequent occasion [of the hunt].

One item: In the seventh month of the tenth year, the year of the Horse (1798) [strikethrough: seven, corrected to seven], he practiced spearmanship (sōjutsu) continuously throughout, and the fact that he performed with ease (yawara) to a degree of nine-tenths or better was considered admirable; he was accordingly awarded one bundle of cotton (wata hitotaba) as a reward.

One item: In the ninth month of the same year, his request to designate the biological son of his adoptive father, Takamaro, as his heir (chakushi) was granted in accordance with his petition.

One item: In the twelfth month of the twelfth year, the year of the Monkey (1800), his request to change his name to Kanonojō was granted in accordance with his petition.

One item: In the third month of Kyōwa 2, the year of the Dog (1802), he was appointed to serve as school recitation instructor (judoshi); accordingly, he was relieved of the duty of attending the muster roll (chakutō) for both castle guard (o-shiro o-ban) and residential duty (iryaku), as directed.

One item: In the third month of Bunka 2, the year of the Ox (1805), he was appointed to the temporary post of assistant district magistrate (go-daikan minarai), concurrently held on a provisional basis.

One item: In the seventh month of the third year [of Bunka], the year of the Tiger (1806), he was appointed to serve concurrently as assistant district magistrate (go-daikan minarai).
