Iemon Mitsumura (childhood/first name: Shichiroku; born on the 27th day of the 7th month of Genbunn 4, the year of the Sheep).

Biological father: Eldest son of Shichirozaemon Mitsuhiro.
Wife: Daughter of Uwazaki Jūbei Hiroki (died of illness on the 11th day of the 4th month of Bunka 9, the year of the Monkey; posthumous Buddhist name: Reikō Moshō Daishi; burial place: same as above).

1. In the 7th month of Meiwa 5, the year of the Rat: In accordance with his parent's petition, he was appointed as replacement guard (bандай), placed under the unit of Hinata Saburōemon, and performed duty as castle guard.

2. In the 5th month of An'ei 4, the year of the Sheep: He was appointed to escort guard duty (otomo-ban).

3. In the 3rd month of An'ei 5, the year of the Monkey: He traveled to Edo to serve in the escort party, and returned home in the 10th month of the same year.
However, it was officially proclaimed that he had performed his duties without a single absence, both along the road and while stationed. Furthermore, thereafter, on every occasion of both departures for duty and returns, he received official commendation.
