(Continued from previous page) ...the lord proceeded to Edo (sankō), and the retainer was left behind to remain at Aizu, with instructions that he would be summoned to Edo at a later date, as conveyed through the o-yōnin (chamberlain/official intermediary).

One item: In the third month of Tenmei 6, the year of the Horse (1786), as an invitation (o-shōsei) had been extended, the retainer was ordered to proceed to Edo during the fourth month of that year, and duly did so.

One item: In the sixth month of Tenmei 7, the year of the Sheep (1787), as the lord returned to the domain (o-gekō), the retainer accompanied the procession back to the domain as a rear-guard attendant (ato-ban).

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, having maintained perfect attendance (kaikkin) for four years, the retainer was, in recognition of this achievement, bestowed with one set (one top and bottom) of o-meshi-asa (hemp garments of the lord's own selection) as a reward, as conveyed through the o-yōnin.

One item: In the third month of Tenmei 8, the year of the Monkey (1788), the retainer proceeded to Edo as an attendant accompanying the lord (o-tomo) for the lord's alternate attendance (sankō), and in the sixth month of the following year of the Rooster (Tenmei 9 / Kansei 1, 1789), the retainer returned to the domain as an attendant accompanying the lord (o-tomo) on the lord's return journey (o-gekō).

One item: In the third month of Kansei 2, the year of the Dog (1790), the retainer proceeded to Edo as an attendant accompanying the lord (o-tomo) for the lord's alternate attendance (sankō).

One item: In the tenth month of the same year, while serving on duty rotation in Edo (kinban), the retainer was assigned to Aizu domain business (Aizu o-yō) and returned to the domain.
