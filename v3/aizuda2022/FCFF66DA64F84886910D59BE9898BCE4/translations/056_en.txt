Item: In the fourth month of Kansei 9, Year of the Snake (1797), he returned (to his domain) and traveled up to Edo to serve as an escort for the lord's return procession, accompanied the lord, and returned (to his domain).
　　Note: It was officially declared that even though the number of attendants had been considerably reduced during the journey, he had performed his duties diligently without any absences.

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, he was appointed to the position of go-toban (gate/door guard duty).

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo as part of the escort rotation duty (kinban), and returned in the fourth month of the following Year of the Horse (1798).
　　Note: It was officially declared that throughout the aforementioned period of service and the journey, even though the number of attendants had been considerably reduced, he had performed his duties diligently without any absences.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, in lieu of a monetary labor stipend (rōgin), he was granted one set of formal hakama (go-meshi-furuasa no go-kamishimo) made of old hemp cloth.

Item: In the second month of Kansei 10, Year of the Horse (1798), when he traveled up to Edo for rotation duty, he was appointed to the provisional post of inspector (o-metsuke kari-yaku) at the Edo residence, served in that capacity until the fourth month, and then returned (to his domain).

Item: In the fourth month of Kansei 11, Year of the Goat (1799), on the occasion of the lord's journey down from Edo (go-gekō), he returned (to his domain) and traveled up to Edo to serve as an escort, accompanied the lord, and returned (to his domain).
