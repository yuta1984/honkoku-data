Shōemon (seventh generation) Mitsuaki: *[marginal note: first known as Ukon, later also called Takamaro | born on the twenty-sixth day of the second month of Kansei 2, the year of the Dog (1790)]*
Real father: second son of Shōkurō Mitsutomi.
Wife: daughter of Ibuka Mokusamitsu Shigemi (as in the original manuscript).

One item: In the eighth month of the third year of Bunka, the year of the Tiger (1806), in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first audience with the lord (o-mie).

One item: In the second month of the eighth year [of Bunka], the year of the Goat (1811), it was officially recognized that he had served as school section chief (gakkō jūchō) for a number of years without any lapse and with great diligence.

One item: In the second month of the tenth year [of Bunka], the year of the Rooster (1813), his request to change his name to Shōemon was granted in accordance with his petition.
