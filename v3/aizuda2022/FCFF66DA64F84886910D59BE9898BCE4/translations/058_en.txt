Item: In the twelfth month of the same year (Bunka 1, 1804), as he had diligently performed his duties as overseer of the construction works (go-fushin) at the domain school (gakkō), he was granted two bundles of cotton as a reward.

Item: In the third month of Bunka 2, Year of the Ox (1805), he was appointed to additionally serve concurrently as chief officer (shuyaku) of both Aizu domain treasury offices (go-nandо̄). He was ordered to attend regularly at the accounts office (go-kanjōsho) disbursement section (haraigata) and the same office's purchasing section (o-kaimonojo). Regarding the duties of these offices, he was ordered to consult with the chief officer, the investigative officer (ginmigakari), and all members of the accounts office, and not only to devise proper methods, but also to diligently oversee the receipts and disbursements of money, to keep careful and detailed attention to measures for reducing expenditures, to follow through with thoroughness, and to ensure that the management of affairs was carried out with complete attentiveness.

Item: In the ninth month of the same year, he was ordered to succeed Yokoyama Kazuma in his duties at the post-station closing office (ekisho shimari-kata yakuba).

Item: In the sixth month of Bunka 3, Year of the Tiger (1806), as he had diligently performed his duties on duty rotation at the go-shitateсho (clothing/equipment preparation office), he was granted two bundles of cotton as a reward.

Item: On the fifth day of the tenth month of the same year, he died of illness at the age of fifty-six.
