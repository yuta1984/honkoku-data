　　Note: Thereafter, they received one further commendation.

One: In the twelfth month of the same year [i.e., the twelfth year of Bunka, 1815], at the training ground of the *Sannomaru* [the third citadel] and at Hongōhara, combined-unit drills were conducted, and the alignment of the ranks and marching steps were well-ordered, with the unit advancing to the upper grade for that year. This was regarded as the result of sustained diligence, and the entire unit was commended accordingly.

One: In the tenth month of the fourteenth year [of Bunka, 1817], for his zealous application to military exercises (*sōren*), he was awarded, as a commendation, one new spear (*atarashimi no yari, issui*).

One: In the eleventh month of the second year of Bunsei [1819, the year of the Hare], his unit commander (*kashira*), Naitō Suke'emon, was appointed to the position of *Karō* [Chief Elder/Senior Councillor], and [Tetsunojō] was consequently transferred to the unit of Obara Tsunegorō, who assumed the role of the succeeding officer.
