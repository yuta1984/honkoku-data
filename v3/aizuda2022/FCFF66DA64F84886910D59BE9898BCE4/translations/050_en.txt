Item: In the seventh month of the same fourth year, the Year of the Dog (1754), he was appointed to the concurrent post of o-daikan (district administrator/magistrate) attached to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit).
　　Note: He was placed in the unit of Yanase Kyūma.

Item: In the sixth month of the same sixth year, the Year of the Rat (1756), he was ordered to transfer to the unit of Obara Gorōbei.

Item: In the ninth month of the same tenth year, the Year of the Dragon (1760), as he had satisfactorily carried out various duties—more numerous than in ordinary years—from the previous winter through the current spring, he was awarded two silver coins (ginshi niimai) as a reward.

Item: In the fifth month of the same twelfth year, the Year of the Horse (1762), as it was reported that he had applied himself diligently on a daily basis and had handled the instruction and guidance of the local people (jige) in an exemplary manner, he was awarded two bolts of silk (kinu nihiki) as a reward.

Item: In the third month of Meiwa 1 (1764), the Year of the Monkey, as he had served well over a number of years, he was granted three silver coins (ginshi sanmai).

Item: In the fifth month of the same fourth year, the Year of the Boar (1767), his request to be relieved of the post of o-daikan was granted.

Item: In the ninth month of the same year, he was again ordered to return to the post of o-daikan.
