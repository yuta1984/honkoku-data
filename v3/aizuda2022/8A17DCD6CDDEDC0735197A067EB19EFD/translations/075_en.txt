Item: In the second month of the seventh year of Bunka (1810), the Year of the Horse, in accordance with the petition of his adoptive father Ikuemon, he was appointed as a castle guard substitute (o-ban-dai), and served guard duty at the castle in the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon.

Item: In the same month, he was appointed to serve as the instructor/coordinator (shimeri-kata) of the Taishi-ryū school of swordsmanship, and was consequently exempted from castle guard duty and attendance roll call (iyaku-chakutō).

Item: In the same month, he had in his possession an honorific kosode (short-sleeved kimono bearing the lord's crest) and formal hakama ensemble (kamishimo) that his biological father Sagoemon had previously received as gifts from the lord. He petitioned to be permitted to wear these out of piety and gratitude (myōga), and his petition was granted accordingly.

Item: In the third month of the same year, in accordance with the petition of his unit commander (kashira) Hyūga Saburōemon, the said commander was granted retirement, and the subsequent post was transferred to the unit of Obara Tachi.

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, he was appointed as an outside retainer (tozama-shi) of the Sōshū unit.

Item: In the ninth month of the eighth year of Bunka (1811), the Year of the Sheep, he was ordered to travel to Sōshū (Sagami Province) for duty rotation (kinban), and was instructed to travel up to the capital while attending to the needs of the official horse(s) along the way.
