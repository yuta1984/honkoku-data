Item: In the 1st month of the same year (Bunka 5, 1808) [continued from the previous page], having been ordered to serve in the defense of Ezo, he departed for his assignment. Prior to his departure, he was granted an audience (御目見) in the Great Audience Chamber (大書院), and in the Small Audience Chamber (小書院) he received the lord's sake cup (御流頂戴).
　　Note: On this occasion, he was also ordered to oversee the pack-horse train (小荷駄) during the journey, and was granted one ryō of gold as a supplement for expenses.

Item: In the 6th month of the same year (1808), his entire unit was ordered to proceed to Rishiri Island (リイシリ島). In connection with this, as the inspector (御目付) Itō Nanigaemon had already proceeded to Rishiri Island for advance duties, he was appointed to serve as acting inspector (御目付仮役) and proceeded to that location.

Item: In the 7th month of the same year (1808), when the order was issued to withdraw from that location, he was assigned to serve as rear guard (後殿). As the inspector Itō Nanigaemon had withdrawn ahead of him, he was once again appointed as acting inspector, and carried out his duties throughout the journey back.

Item: In the 9th month of the same year (1808), upon returning to the domain (帰陣), he was granted an audience in the Great Audience Chamber.
