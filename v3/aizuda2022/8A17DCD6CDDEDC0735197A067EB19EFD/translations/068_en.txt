　　　joined Harada Matasuke's unit, serving as a castle guard.

One entry: In the third month of An'ei 2, the year of the Snake (1773), the unit commander (頭) Harada Matasuke
　　　died of illness, and he was consequently assigned to
　　　the unit of Ikoma Kurannosuke as the successor post.

One entry: In the tenth month of the same sixth year, the year of the Boar (1777), he was granted without alteration
　　　one hundred koku of stipend as the inheritance
　　　from his late father's estate.

One entry: In the fourth month of Kansei 1, the year of the Rooster (1789), as the unit commander
　　　Ikoma Kurannosuke was relieved of his post, he was consequently
　　　assigned to the unit of Ibuka Kenmotsu as the successor post.

One entry: In the ninth month of the same fifth year, the year of the Ox (1793), he participated in the falconry hunt (追鳥狩).

One entry: In the twelfth month of the same year, his younger brother Mitsugu, while still alive,
　　　had diligently saved and set aside 231 ryō in gold,
　　　which he offered to submit [to the domain].
　　　This act was regarded as admirable and praiseworthy,
　　　and it was decreed that 42 bales of rice per year would be
　　　granted to him in perpetuity without any reduction (御借上).
