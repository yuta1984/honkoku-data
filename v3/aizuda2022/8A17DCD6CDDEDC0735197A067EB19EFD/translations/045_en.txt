(Continued from previous page) ...for having diligently served as the 御普請御用懸 (official overseer of construction works) for the new construction at the Ōkawabata residence, he was granted seven ryō of silver as a reward.

One entry: In the sixth month of the same year:
　　　Having been appointed to supplementary duty (補勤) in the 御用部屋 since the year of the Tiger (寅年), six years prior, and having diligently fulfilled both his regular duties amid a demanding workload and additional temporary assignments, he was granted two pieces of silver (銀子二枚) as a reward.

One entry: In the tenth month of the same year:
　　　It was proclaimed that, in addition to his concurrent role as overseer (御用懸) for the engraving of the "Sehon Kogi" (世本古義), he had diligently consulted with the various relevant parties and applied himself with great effort.

One entry: In the same month:
　　　Upon receipt of orders to dispatch personnel to Mount Nikkō (日光山) for fire-watch duty (火の番) in connection with the memorial service (御法事) for Lord Daiyūin [Tokugawa Iemitsu], he was ordered to serve as the overseer (御用懸) for said duty.

One entry: In the second month of the twelfth year of Bunsei [1829], the year of the Monkey (申年):
　　　In connection with the dispatch of personnel to Mount Nikkō, as it was arranged that the lodging temple Myōdōin (妙道院) at that location would be handed over [for use], he was ordered to proceed to the site prior to the departure of the personnel, to negotiate with Nanshōin (南昭院), take receipt of Myōdōin, arrange the seating layout and orientation, and investigate any other matters that might cause difficulties, so as to manage everything appropriately, to report to the relevant local authorities, and to serve as official envoy...
