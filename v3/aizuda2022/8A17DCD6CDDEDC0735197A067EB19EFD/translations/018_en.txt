Item: In the 1st month of Kyōwa 2, the Year of the Dog (1802), he was appointed as Jūchō (什長, Section Head/Group Leader) of the domain school.

Item: In the 2nd month of Bunka 1, the Year of the Rat (1804), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (御目見) with the lord.

Item: In the 5th month of the 3rd year of the same era, Bunka 3, the Year of the Tiger (1806), as the organization of the domain school was being restructured and the number of Jūchō positions was being reduced, he was relieved of said duties. As he had performed his role with diligence and had devoted considerable effort to the teaching and instruction of the Dōjikun (童子訓, a moral education text for children), attending to every detail with care, he was commended and granted one bundle of raw cotton (古綿一把) as a reward, as formally proclaimed.

Item: In the 1st month of the 5th year of the same era, Bunka 5, the Year of the Dragon (1808), having traveled to assist with the Ezo garrison duty (蝦夷地御固), he was granted a formal audience in the Great Audience Chamber (大書院), received a serving of the lord's meal (御流), and departed on his mission.

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, having returned from the campaign (帰陣), he was granted a formal audience.

Item: In the 11th month of the same year, he was appointed to the position of overseer/administrator of the domain school (塾締め方).
