[Continued from previous page] Having devoted himself to his duties for many days and completed all official business, with the entire unit having returned from the campaign (帰陣), he was granted a formal meal (御料理) at the castle.

Item: In the 8th month of the 6th year of the same era, Bunka 6 (1809), the Year of the Snake (巳), having diligently performed his duties in guarding Ezo (蝦夷地), he was granted one set of seasonal garments (時服). Furthermore, it was formally proclaimed that he had performed with distinction his duties of managing the pack-horse transport (小荷駄差配) during the journey, as well as his role as Acting Inspector (御目付仮役).

Item: In the 5th month of the 8th year, Bunka 8 (1811), the Year of the Sheep (未), in accordance with his petition, his son Sōkichi was appointed to succeed him in the guard position (御番代).
