(Continued from previous page) …he traveled up to Edo, and returned in the tenth month of the same year.

Item: In the ninth month of the same year (Bunsei 13), it was declared that he had served diligently without absence during the field encampment (*jinchū*).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, at the combined drill (*gattai keiko*) held at the Sannomaru training ground (*kyōjō*), the formation and marching were well-ordered, and the unit had advanced to the upper ranking (*jōtō*) for that year. It was declared that this was owing to diligent effort, and the entire group of comrades was commended.

Item: In the eighth month of Bunsei 14 (Year of the Ox), he turned out for the falconry hunt (*oidorikari*).
　　Note: After this, he also turned out on occasions of combined unit drills (*gōtai keiko*), for a total of two occasions.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, at the said falconry hunt, although the hunting ground had been changed and it was the first time operations were held there, the formation and marching were exceptionally well-ordered, the units maintained close coordination, and operations proceeded without hindrance. It was declared that this was owing to the group's diligent practice of combined unit drills, and the entire group of comrades was commended.
　　Note: Thereafter, the group received commendations on every occasion.

Item: In the same month, in recognition of his diligent and devoted efforts in military drills (*sōren*), he was awarded one new spear blade (*shinmi no yari ippō*) as a reward.
