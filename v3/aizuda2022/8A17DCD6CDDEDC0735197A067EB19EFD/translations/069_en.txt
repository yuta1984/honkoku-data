One entry: In the fourth month of Kansei 9, the year of the Snake (1797), regarding the matter of borrowing a domain loan (御貸金), the circumstances of his negotiations with Takagi Gen'emon, as well as the circumstances of his negotiations with Misaka Kuemon and Shimojo Tomoya regarding the same matter, were all contrary to what those persons had stated in their accounts. However, upon investigation, the record of said negotiations and your [i.e., the subject's] account were found to be reasonable and truthful.

　　　Nevertheless, concerning Gen'emon: even if one were to argue that [his conduct] was unbecoming of a unit commander (組頭), the matter of completely severing all public and private relations—and his statement that he would not seek an audience [with his superior] regarding either public or private affairs with the unit commander who had been given instructions from above—constitutes an insubordinate manner of speech. Furthermore, although it is said that he did not come forward [with his complaint] due to an excess of anger, the fact that after refusing [to deal with the matter], he subsequently came forward independently regarding the domain loan matter amounts to gross negligence. As this is altogether inappropriate, he was dismissed from his unit assignment, and it was decreed that twenty koku were to be confiscated from his stipend—
