One item: In the first month of the sixth year (Bunka 6, 1809, the year of the Snake), having diligently served over many years, with both regular internal duties (go-naiyō) and numerous ad hoc duties being extremely demanding, and having served with particular diligence despite being a one-man post, while also attending carefully to minute details in keeping with the ongoing austerity measures, he was granted, as a reward, one bolt of bleached cloth (sarashi) in lieu of a ceremonial hemp *kamishimo* ensemble, along with three pieces of silver.

One item: In the third month of the same year, as the rotation (※) of the Group Leader of the Permanent Duty Office was being carried out, he was assigned to the Aizu domestic service (Aizu katte), and it was declared that he should immediately begin his duty rotation (kinban) from that spring, and return after being rotated out (※) in the autumn.

One item: In the ninth month of the same year, having diligently served over many years, having competently managed the internal affairs duties (go-naiyō-muki) as well as the confidential financial matters (go-naishō-kata) and various large-scale assigned duties, and having fulfilled his rotation duties without delay, it was declared that, in recognition of all of this and by special consideration, he was raised to the rank of *jō-kamishimo* (ordinary formal dress rank).

One item: In the third month of the seventh year (Bunka 7, 1810, the year of the Horse), in accordance with his petition, his son Niwakichi was appointed as his duty replacement (o-bandai).

One item: In the third month of the eighth year (Bunka 8, 1811, the year of the Sheep/Goat), in accordance with his petition, he was granted permission to retire (inkyo).
