Item: In the twelfth month of the same year (Bunsei 8), during the falconry hunt (*oidorikari*), the formation and marching were well-ordered and operations proceeded without hindrance. It was declared that this was owing to the group's diligent practice of combined drills (*gattai keiko*), and the entire unit was commended together.
　　Note: The unit received commendations on subsequent occasions as well.

Item: In the seventh month of Bunsei 9 (Year of the Monkey), he was ordered to serve as an assistant instructor (*minamitetsudan*) in the *Ichishi-ryū* school of spear (*sōjutsu*). In connection with this, it was declared that he would be relieved of his duties as castle guard (*gojō goban*) and resident duty officer (*iryaku*).

Item: In the seventh month of Bunsei 12 (Year of the Boar), he was appointed to serve as an escort guard (*otomobан*).

Item: In the same month, as requested, he was relieved of his duties as head instructor (*shimeri-kata*) of spear techniques.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, due to an unscheduled visit to the castle (*fuji ononobori*), he returned and served as an escort for the procession.

Item: In the first month of Bunsei 13 (Year of the Rat), it was declared that he had served diligently without absence, even though the number of personnel for the road procession (*odōchū*) had been reduced.
