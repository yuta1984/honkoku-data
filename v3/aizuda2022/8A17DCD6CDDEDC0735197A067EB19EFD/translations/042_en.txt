[Continued from previous page:] From the very beginning, the negotiations and communications were extremely numerous and busy, yet he diligently managed them appropriately and performed extraordinary services; as a reward, he was granted two pieces of silver.

One entry: In the seventh month of the eighth year of the same [Bunsei] era, the Year of the Dragon (Bunsei 8, 1825):
　　　He was ordered to serve as a person in charge of affairs (御用懸) for Lord Teisho's wedding ceremony.

One entry: In the second month of the ninth year of the same era, the Year of the Snake (Bunsei 9, 1826):
　　　On the occasion of the formal betrothal gifts (御結納) being sent from Lord Teisho to Lady Juhōin, he was appointed to the role of Daidzumiyaku (台積役, the official overseeing the stacking/arranging of ceremonial goods). He joined the procession carrying the betrothal gifts and proceeded to the residence of Lord Ii Kamon-no-kami, where he performed his duties. Lord Kamon-no-kami granted him two pieces of silver.

One entry: In the second month of the same year:
　　　Having served as the person in charge of the wedding ceremony affairs, he was granted two pieces of silver from Lady Juhōin.

One entry: In the third month of the same year:
　　　On the occasion of Lord Ii Kamon-no-kami's ceremonial visit as the bride's father (御舅入り), he was granted two lengths of white silk from the same lord.
