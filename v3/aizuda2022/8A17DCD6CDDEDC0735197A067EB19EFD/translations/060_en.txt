[Continued from previous page] — Yanase Sanzaemon was appointed as wakadoshiyori (junior elder), and consequently the subsequent post assignment was transferred to the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon.

One entry: In the fifth month of the same fourth year (1707), the Year of the Boar, due to illness, his request to be relieved of his concurrent post as daikan (local magistrate) was granted.

One entry: In the eighth month of the same year, in accordance with his petition, his son Ikuemon was appointed as his replacement in guard duty (obandai).

One entry: On the last day of the third month of Shōtoku 2 (1712), the Year of the Dragon, he died of illness. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Yūyo Jugen Shinji. Officiating monk (dōshi): same as above. Place of burial: Ōkuboyama.


Ikuemon Katsutoshi

Mother: Daughter of the Horiuchi clan

Wife: Daughter of Tōyama Toneri Tameyuki

One entry: In the eighth month of Hōei 4 (1707), the Year of the Boar, in accordance with his father's petition, he was appointed as a replacement in guard duty (obandai), and served in castle guard duty (o-shiro o-ban) in the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon.
