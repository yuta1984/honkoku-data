Item: In the second month of the sixth year of Kansei (1794), the Year of the Tiger, by petition of his father, he was granted his first audience with the lord.

Item: In the eighth month of the first year of Kyōwa (1801), the Year of the Rooster, by petition of his father, he was appointed to castle guard duty (go-bandai) and served as castle guard in the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon.

Item: In the ninth month of the fourth year of Bunka (1807), the Year of the Rabbit, he appeared at the horse-breaking parade (naramazoroe).

Item: In the first month of the fifth year (1808), the Year of the Dragon, as he was dispatched for the duty of defending Ezo (Hokkaido), he was granted an audience in the Great Drawing Room (Ō-shoin), and was granted a cup of sake (o-nagare) in the Small Drawing Room (Ko-shoin), after which he proceeded to Karafuto (Sakhalin).

Item: On the twenty-eighth day of the fourth month of the same year, on Karafuto Island (Sakhalin), he sustained wounds inflicted by Kataoka Nakazō and died.
　　Buddhist posthumous name: Shōseiin Shin'yo Jōzen Koji
　　Place of burial: Karafuto Island (Sakhalin)
