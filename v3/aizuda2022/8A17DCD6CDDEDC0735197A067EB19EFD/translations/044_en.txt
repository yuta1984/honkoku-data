Naturally, since personnel here in Edo rotate on a revolving basis, it is imperative that annual receipts, disbursements, and discrepancies be made fully comprehensible [to incoming staff]. Regarding this matter, therefore, whether large or small, all issues shall be discussed and managed jointly among the 御勘定頭 (chief accounting officer), Riemon, and Sanzō. However, given that the 御用所 and 御用部屋 also have their own official duties, in order to avoid interference with those duties, the 御勘定頭 shall also make visits to the 御用所 and 御用部屋 to consult with them. This was the message conveyed.

One entry: In the first month of the tenth year of Bunsei [1827], the year of the Horse (午年):
　　　As there were likely areas where reductions could be made regarding the management of the 夫丸 (laborers/porters), he was ordered to consult with Uchimura Sanzō and periodically present himself at the 人割方 (labor allocation office) to conduct inspections. The two men conferred and presented themselves at the 人割方 to carry out the necessary reductions.

One entry: In the twelfth month of the same year:
　　　Having diligently performed his duties and also rendered service in temporary assignments, he was directly granted an increase (御加増) of one koku and stipend for two persons (二人扶持) added to his existing official stipend (御役料).
