One: In the fourth month of the same year (Meiwa 1, 1764), for having devoted himself wholeheartedly and diligently to the various duties related to the sacred palanquin (mikoshi) and sacred implements (shinki) of Lord Haniwa-sama (Tsuchitsu-sama), he was granted a reward of two bu in gold.

One: In the twelfth month of Meiwa 2, the Year of the Rooster (1765), he was appointed to the permanent post of receipts-and-disbursements officer (ukebara-sadanin-yaku) for the supplementary funds (zōkkin) of the Gimmisho (Investigative Office). He was also instructed to carry out routine administrative duties (sadawa) during any idle periods, and was granted two ryō in gold annually from within the supplementary funds.

One: In the first month of Meiwa 8, the Year of the Rabbit (1771), on account of his excellence in arithmetic (sanjutsu) and his distinguished ancestral lineage, and considering both as weighty grounds, he was granted the rank of Dokurei (the privilege of making solo formal salutations to the lord), and was informed by proclamation that his seat position was to be ranked immediately after that of Kobayashi Kinzaemon.

One: In the third month of the same year, he was appointed to the temporary post of Kaisho (reform officer) at the Gimmisho on an interim basis, serving until the eleventh month of the same year, and was granted payment at the fixed rate of three monme of chōgin silver.
　　Note: He was instructed to continue performing his permanent post duties regarding the supplementary funds as before.
