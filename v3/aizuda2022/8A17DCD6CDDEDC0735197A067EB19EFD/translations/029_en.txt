Item: In the eighth month of the same twentieth year of Kan'ei [the year of the Sheep, 1643], he accompanied [the lord] on the relocation to Aizu. The fifty bales of rice that had been granted to him until that time were converted and newly granted as eighteen koku in official stipend certificates [go-kippū]. As there was no official for collecting the dye-house [kōya] guild silver tax in Aizu, he was ordered to take charge of supervising the dye-houses.

Item: On the fifth day of the ninth month of the second year of Meireki [the year of the Monkey, 1656], he died of illness. His posthumous Buddhist name was Chōun-in Kiyo Jōetsu Koji. His place of burial was Jōan-ji temple at Furudate-yama.
