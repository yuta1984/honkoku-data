(Continued from previous page) ...he served as castle guard (御城御番).

Note: He was to continue his duties as the administrator of Ōuchi-ryū spear techniques (大内流槍術締め方) as before; however, given that his daily duties had become extremely numerous and demanding, he was relieved of his castle guard duties (御城御番) and his residential role attendance (居役着倒し).

Item: In the 12th month of the same year [Bunka 8, 1811], as the method of joint unit drills (合隊稽古) at the Sannomaru training hall (三ノ丸教場) and at Hongōhara had been reformed since the spring of that year and the exercises had commenced, it was recognized that the proper formation, alignment of ranks, and coordination of steps were achieved due to his diligent efforts, and accordingly, his entire unit was formally commended.

Item: In the 12th month of the 9th year [Bunka 9, the Year of the Monkey, 1812], he was appointed as an escort guard (御供番).

Item: In the 1st month of the 10th year [Bunka 10, the Year of the Rooster, 1813], in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his duties as administrator of spear techniques (槍術締め方) at the Bugakuryō (武学寮).

Item: In the 4th month of the same year, in connection with Lord Kinbun's (欽文様) procession/departure (御乗出), he was appointed to emergency guard duty (不時勤番), traveled up to Edo, and returned in the 10th month of the same year.

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, it was formally proclaimed that he had served with perfect attendance (無不参) and with great diligence throughout the encampment period.
