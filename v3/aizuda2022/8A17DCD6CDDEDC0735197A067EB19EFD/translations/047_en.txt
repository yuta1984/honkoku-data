One entry: In the eighth month of the same year, during a Buddhist memorial service (御法事) at Mount Nikkō, personnel were dispatched for fire-watch duty at the same location. He was assigned the post-duty administrative duties (御跡御用) from the very beginning, traveled there as well, and served diligently. In recognition of this, he was awarded two silver coins (銀子二枚) as a reward.

One entry: In the eleventh month of the same year, regarding the affairs of the御部屋御用所 (the lord's private quarters administrative office), it was determined that, following the precedent of the御表 (the outer/official quarters), the handling of memorial services could not be conducted without proper arrangement. Accordingly, officials of the御表御用所 were ordered to rotate — one at a time — traveling to Shiba (芝) to assist in the duties of said administrative office, and the members of the 仲ヶ間 (intermediate-ranking staff) went to Shiba on alternating shifts.

One entry: In the first month of the first year of Kyōwa (享和元年, the year of the Rooster, 1801), in recognition of years of diligent service, including repeated fulfillment of extraordinary duties (臨時之御用), and for his distinguished and meritorious service, he was informed by special order that he was to be appointed to the御近習一ノ寄合席 (a senior retainer seat within the lord's personal attendants).

One entry: In the fourth month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent service in the temporary supplementary duties of the御部屋御用所 since the previous year, he was bestowed one bundle of cotton (綿一把) as a reward from Lord Teishō (貞昭様).
