(Continued from previous page) ...he traveled up to Edo, and in the 5th month of the following Year of the Boar [Bunka 12, 1815], he returned as part of the lord's escort (御供).

Item: In the 4th month of the 12th year [Bunka 12, the Year of the Boar, 1815], it was formally proclaimed that he had served with perfect attendance (無不参) and with great diligence throughout the encampment period.

Item: In the 6th month of the same year, it was formally proclaimed that he had served the lord's procession (御道中) with perfect attendance and with great diligence.

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, he participated in the falcon hunt / bird-driving hunt (追鳥狩), made a catch, and received a reward gift (拝領).
Note: After this, he participated three more times, and on one of those occasions he made a catch and received a reward gift.

Item: In the 11th month of the same year, during the bird-driving hunt, the formation, alignment of ranks, and coordination of steps were exceptionally well-ordered, and the units maintained cohesive connection and operated without any hindrance. This was recognized as being due to his especially diligent efforts in joint training exercises (合体稽古) conducted beforehand, and accordingly, his entire barracks companions (仲ヶ間) were formally commended.
Note: After this, he was commended on every occasion (of the hunt).

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, he was appointed to the return escort duty (立帰り御供) and traveled up to Edo.

Item: In the 1st month of the 13th year [Bunka 13, the Year of the Rat, 1816], it was formally proclaimed that he had served the lord's procession (御道中) with perfect attendance.
