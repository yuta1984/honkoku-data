One entry: In the fifth month of the same year:
　　　Upon [the lord's] departure from Edo to the domain (御下向), he accompanied the party and returned to Aizu.

One entry: In the intercalary seventh month of the same year:
　　　He was informed through the duty officer of the御用所 (administrative office) that he should consult well with Tsuchiya Chōzaburō, as the following message had been conveyed:

　　　"Here in Edo, everyone rotates on a half-year basis, making it difficult to fully grasp the details of the 御内証方 (financial/accounting division). Therefore, regarding matters both routine and current, it is essential that the personnel of the 御用所 and 御用部屋 (attendants' offices) be well-informed. Accordingly, Tsuchiya Chōzaburō is now traveling up to Edo [from Aizu] to consult with the 御勘定頭 (chief accounting officer) regarding the thorough and careful examination of all account books relating to both the domain's debt management (御借金方) and its regular expenditures (御常用). In this regard, Nakayama Riemon, who is in charge of financial affairs at the 御用所, and Uchimura Sanzō, stationed at the 御用部屋, should, both when consulting with Tsuchiya Chōzaburō and in their daily duties, present themselves at the 御勘定所 (accounting office) and, regarding matters such as the receipts and disbursements of rice and money as well as dealings with various creditors, if they have any knowledge or views on the matter, they should report them to the 御勘定頭 without acting unilaterally.
