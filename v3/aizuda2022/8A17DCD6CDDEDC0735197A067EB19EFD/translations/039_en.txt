Item: In the twelfth month of the same eighth year of the Pig (Tenmei 8, 1788), he was appointed as a minor official (ko-yakunin) of the Ginmisho (Investigation Office).

Item: In the second month of Tenmei 5, Year of the Snake (1785), he was provisionally appointed as a temporary Gosho official (court/domain office official).

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, he was appointed as a Goyosho official (official of the domain administrative office).

Item: In the twelfth month of Tenmei 7, Year of the Sheep (1787), he was appointed to serve as an official in charge of training and administrative duties related to the townspeople's financial matters (Jikamachi-kata go-katte-ban kakari-goyō-kakari) (※1).

　　Note: Regarding the kakari-goyō duties, he was also ordered to serve concurrently in secret/confidential matters (mitsujigoyō).

Item: In the second month of Tenmei 8, Year of the Monkey (1788), upon the departure of the Kaikoku-shi (Circuit Inspector) (※2), he was appointed to serve in the relevant official capacity and carried out those duties.

Item: In the twelfth month of Kansei 1, Year of the Rooster (1789), due to his diligent and dedicated service in his assigned duties, he was specially granted a stipend of one koku of rice and provisions for one person (※3).

Item: In the third month of Kansei 2, Year of the Dog (1790), on the occasion of the lord's sankin (alternate attendance journey to Edo) (※4), he traveled to Edo as part of the escort and guard detail, returning in the tenth month of the same year.
