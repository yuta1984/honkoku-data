A request was submitted to be relieved of the duties of the deputy magistrate (daikan-yaku). In response, an order was issued to continue serving while taking care of one's health.

One entry: In the eighth month of the same twentieth year of the Rabbit (1735), the unit head (kumigashira) Sugita Gorōbei was granted retirement as requested, and [the subject] was transferred to the unit of Ikoma Gobei as successor to the post.

One entry: In the eighth month of Genbun 2 (1737), the year of the Snake, permission was granted as requested to be relieved of the concurrent duties as deputy magistrate (daikan-yaku).

One entry: In the third month of the same fifth year, the year of the Monkey (1740), as requested, [the subject's] son Jūji was appointed as a replacement guard (o-bandai).

One entry: On the seventeenth day of the twelfth month of Kanpō 2 (1742), the year of the Dog, died of illness at the age of sixty-seven. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Hōdō-in Shōyo Kōkan Koji. Place of burial: same as above.
