He was ordered to continue serving as an escort guard (otomo-ban).

Item: In the third month of Enkyō 1 [the year of the Rooster; possibly an error for the year of the Rat], having fallen ill, he submitted a request to resign from his post. He was, however, ordered to take care of his health and continue in his duties.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, he again submitted a request to resign from his post. His request was granted, and he was relieved of his escort guard duties. He was then assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit), placed under the command of Kayano Gonbei's unit, and served as a castle guard (oshiro o-ban).

Item: In the twelfth month of Hōreki 3 [the year of the Rooster], his request to change his name to Rokuemon was approved.

Item: In the third month of the same sixth year [the year of the Rat], he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban). However, in the seventh month of the same year, due to illness, he submitted a request and was permitted to return home.

Item: In the first month of the same seventh year [the year of the Ox], his unit commander Kayano Gonbei was granted his wish to retire. The subsequent unit command passed to the unit of Kasuga Jinzaemon.

Item: In the ninth month of the same ninth year [the year of the Rabbit], his unit commander Kasuga Jinzaemon was relieved of his post as requested. The subsequent unit command passed to the unit of Mukawa Sukeemon.
