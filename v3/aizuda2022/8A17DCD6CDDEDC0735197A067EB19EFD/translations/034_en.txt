(In the 7th month of Kyōhō 2, the Year of the Rooster, he was appointed as a trainee at the Gimisho [Inquiry Office].)

Item: In the same 3rd year, the Year of the Dog (Kyōhō 3 [1718]), it was decreed that he would receive an annual allowance of one ryō for hakama (formal pleated trousers).

Item: In the same 5th year, the Year of the Rat (Kyōhō 5 [1720]), in the 12th month, he was appointed as a temporary hire (o-yatoi) at the Gimisho (Inquiry Office), and was granted an annual expense allowance of two ryō.

Item: In the same 9th year, the Year of the Dragon (Kyōhō 9 [1724]), in the 2nd month, on account of his diligent and dedicated service, he was granted eight koku and a stipend for two people (futari-fuchi), and was appointed as a minor official (ko-yakunin) at the Gimisho.

Item: In the same 11th year, the Year of the Horse (Kyōhō 11 [1726]), in the 1st month, because he was a person whose ancestors had a certain matter (of concern), his father's provisional stipend (gisaku) of eighteen koku was reduced to thirteen koku and a stipend for two people. Furthermore, the provisional stipend (gisaku) that had been granted up to that time was rescinded.

Item: In the same 20th year, the Year of the Rabbit (Kyōhō 20 [1735]), in the 3rd month, he was ordered to serve in Edo (Edo-kinban) through the o-hitosashi (official dispatch to the shogunate), traveled up to Edo, participated in inquiries at both the upper residence (kami-yashiki) and the Shiba meeting hall (Shiba kaisho), and served in rotation every fifteen days alongside Kawashima Chūtarō, a minor official of the Go-kanjōsho (Shogunal Finance Office).

Item: In Genbun 2, the Year of the Snake (1737), in the 6th month, he was granted the former residence (ato-yashiki) of Arai Yaemon.
