Wife: Daughter of Horiuchi Sakuemon Moritoshi

One entry: In Manji 1 (1658), the Year of the Dog, in accordance with his father's petition, he was appointed as a replacement in guard duty (obandai), and was further ordered to continue serving as a land survey official (kenchi yakunin).

One entry: In the ninth month of the same third year (1660), the Year of the Rat, in accordance with his father's petition, fifty koku of newly developed land was granted to him in the form of gokuramoto-washi (rice disbursed directly from the domain's storehouses).

One entry: In the eleventh month of Enpō 7 (1679), the Year of the Sheep, he was appointed to additionally serve concurrently as a gōchūma-sashihiki-yaku (rural district accounting/settlement officer).

One entry: In the seventh month of Jōkyō 1 (1684), the Year of the Rat, he was granted his inherited stipend (atoshiki) of one hundred koku without dispute, and was ordered to continue serving as before in both his roles as land survey official and rural district accounting officer.

One entry: In the seventh month of the same fourth year (1687), the Year of the Rabbit, he was appointed to the minor post of o-nando-yaku (keeper of the lord's personal storeroom/chamberlain's office).

One entry: In the intercalary fourth month of Hōei 2 (1705), the Year of the Rooster, he was appointed to additionally serve concurrently as a daikan (local magistrate) attached to the tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit), and was assigned to the unit of Yanase Sanzaemon.
