Item: In the twelfth month of the tenth year of Bunka (1813), the Year of the Rooster, he diligently carried out his duties at the newly established defensive post (o-sonae-ba) in Sōshū (Sagami Province), and additionally performed temporary official duties during the journey up to Edo. In recognition of these efforts, he was awarded two bundles of cotton as a commendation.

Item: In the eighth month of the thirteenth year of Bunka (1816), the Year of the Rat, he was appointed as an escort guard for the Aizu lord's private household (Aizu-katte o-tomo-ban).

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for rotational duty (kinban) and served in the first unit (ichijin). In the fifth month of the following Year of the Ox (1817), he served as an escort for the lord's return journey (o-gekō) back to the domain.

Item: In the fifth month of the fourteenth year of Bunka (1817), the Year of the Ox, it was formally declared that he had diligently performed his duties throughout the encampment period without a single absence.

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, it was formally declared that, even though the number of personnel in the traveling party had been reduced, he had performed his duties with particular diligence and dedication.

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, he went out for the bird-hunt (o-tori-gari) and made a catch, for which he received a reward from the lord.
　　Note: After this, on the occasion of combined unit training exercises, he went out twice, and on one of those occasions made a catch, for which he again received a reward from the lord.
