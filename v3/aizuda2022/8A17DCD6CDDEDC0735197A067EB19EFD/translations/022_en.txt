Item: In the 4th month of the same year [Bunka 13, 1816], upon the occasion of the lord's journey to Kyoto (御上京), he served as escort (御供) for both the outward and return journeys.

Item: In the 10th month of the 14th year of Bunka, the Year of the Ox [1817], because he had applied himself diligently to military drills (操練), it was proclaimed that he would be awarded one new spearhead (新身の槍一穂) as a reward for his meritorious conduct.

Item: In the 9th month of the 1st year of Bunsei, the Year of the Tiger [1818], on the occasion of the lord's alternate-attendance journey (御参勤), he was temporarily appointed to the role of gate guard (御戸番仮役) as far as Akai Village (赤井村) for the purpose of seeing off the procession.

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, regarding the training exercises at the Third Citadel Training Hall (三ノ丸教場), the formation, alignment of ranks, and coordination of steps were well-ordered, and it was proclaimed that because he had applied himself with great diligence in advancing to the highest rank over a period of three years, his entire barracks companions (仲ヶ間) were commended and were to be presented with ceremonial sake (御酒).

Item: In the 3rd month of the 2nd year of Bunsei, the Year of the Rabbit [1819], he traveled up to Edo to serve in the welcoming escort party (御迎え) for the lord's return, and then returned as part of the lord's escort.

Item: In the 4th month of the same year, it was formally proclaimed that he had served the lord's procession (御道中) with perfect attendance (無不参).

Item: In the 4th month of the 3rd year of Bunsei, the Year of the Dragon [1820], he traveled up to Edo to serve in the lord's escort as a regular duty retainer (勤番), and returned in the 10th month of the same year.
