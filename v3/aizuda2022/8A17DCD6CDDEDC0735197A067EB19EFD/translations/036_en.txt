- In the 12th month of the 5th year of Genbun (申年, 1740), it was decreed that one ryō of gold per year be granted as hakama-dai (a stipend for formal hakama clothing).

- In the 12th month of the 1st year of Kanpō (酉年, 1741), he was appointed as a hired official (go-yatoi) at the Ginmisho (the domain's investigative/judicial office), and two ryō of gold per year were granted as a labor reward (hone-ori-ryō).

- In the intercalary 4th month of the 3rd year of Kanpō (亥年, 1743), as he was a person whose ancestors had been subject to some disciplinary circumstance, the full amount of his late father's gigi-saku stipend of 13 koku and 2 nin-fuchi was granted to him, and he was summoned to serve as a minor official (ko-yakunin) at the Ginmisho.

- In the 5th month of the 1st year of Kan'en (辰年, 1748), upon the occasion of Lord Kyōjō's (松平容頌, Matsudaira Katanobu) first attendance at the Shogun's court in Edo, various duties and assigned roles were entrusted to him; as he diligently performed these duties, he was rewarded with two bu of gold as a commendation.

- In the 12th month of the 1st year of Hōreki (未年, 1751), for his diligent service, a role stipend (yaku-ryō) of 2 koku was granted.

- In the 2nd month of the 13th year of Hōreki (未年, 1763), for his diligent service, an increase and adjustment (加増・直し) was made to his existing role stipend of 2 koku. [Note: There appears to be a missing character after "御直" in the original manuscript.]
