One entry: On the last day of the third month of the same year, he departed Edo, arriving at Mount Nikkō on the second day of the fourth month. On the third day, he negotiated with Nanshōin (南昭院), took receipt of the seating arrangements at the lodging temple Myōdōin (妙道院), consulted with the relevant officials regarding the seating allocation (座割り) of the various duty stations and the readiness of the necessary equipment and furnishings for the same location, managed matters accordingly, and reported the receipt of the above to the main temple (御本坊) and the Deputy Inspector (御目代). He duly served as official envoy in this matter.

One entry: In the same month, upon completion of the proxy worship (御代拝) at the shrine at Nikkō, at the residence of Tanaka Saburōbei (田中三郎兵衛), he was ordered, upon the petition of the 御聞番 (liaison officer), to serve as envoy to Lord Matsudaira Izu-no-kami (松平伊豆守) to report this, and he duly fulfilled said mission.

One entry: In the same month, upon completion of the fire-watch duty, he was ordered, upon the petition of the 御聞番, to serve as the envoy for the delivery of gifts (御進物) to the following five temples: Shūgakuin Gon-Sōjō (修学院権僧正), Dairakuin (大楽院), Ryūkōin (龍光院), Jōdoin (浄土院), and Ryōgain (楞伽院). He duly fulfilled said assignment.
