One entry: He was granted fifty koku, summoned into service, and appointed as a land survey official (kenchi yakunin).

One entry: In the year Shōhō 2 (1645), the Year of the Rooster, as he had developed fifty koku of new farmland (shinden), he was granted an increase bringing his total stipend to one hundred koku.

One entry: In the year Keian 1 (1648), the Year of the Rooster [possibly an error for the Year of the Rat], in the ninth month, he was appointed as a daikan (local magistrate/land steward).

One entry: In the autumn of Manji 1 (1658), the Year of the Dog, as he had requested, his son Rokudayū was appointed as his replacement in guard duty (obandai).

One entry: On the twenty-fifth day of the fourth month of Jōkyō 1 (1684), the Year of the Rat, he died of illness at the age of eighty-five. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Seiyо Yūshin Koji.

　　Place of burial: Bodaisan Gokurakuji Temple
