Item: In the tenth month of the same year (Bunka 14, 1817), during the bird-hunt, the hunting ground was relocated and it was the very first occasion at the new location. Nevertheless, the formation (reigo), pace, and coordination of the unit were well-ordered and operations proceeded without any hindrance. Recognizing that this was owing to the diligent training the unit had previously undertaken together, all members of the group received a commendation from the lord.
　　Note: After this, commendations were received on every subsequent occasion.

Item: In the same month, as he had diligently and commendably applied himself to drills and training exercises (sōren), he was awarded a new spear blade (one spear head, shin-mi no yari) as a mark of honor.

Item: In the fifth month of the third year of Bunsei (1820), the Year of the Dragon, his inherited stipend (atoshiki go-chigyō) of eighty koku was granted to him without alteration, and he was instructed to continue serving as an escort guard (o-tomo-ban) as before.

Item: In the fifth month of the fourth year of Bunsei (1821), the Year of the Snake, he traveled up to Edo in order to return to the domain and serve as part of the welcoming escort for the lord's journey down to the domain (o-gekō).

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, even though the number of personnel in the traveling party had been reduced, it was formally declared that he had performed his duties without a single absence and with great diligence.
