One item: In the eleventh month of the same year (Bunka 2, 1805), having diligently served over the years in busy and demanding posts, it was declared through a special intermediary arrangement that, in accordance with his petition, his son Niwakichi would be appointed as Inspector (metsuke) at a suitable opportunity.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, having devoted himself over many years to mastering and refining his official duties, and especially since the time when the magistrate's office (bugyōsho) was abolished two years prior and consolidated into the Administrative Office (goyō-beya), the workload had become particularly heavy; and moreover, having served diligently and laboriously in official matters over many years with commendable results, it was declared that, in recognition of his altogether outstanding meritorious service and by special consideration, his previous provisional stipend (gigi-saku go-chigyō) of one hundred koku was hereby confirmed and granted as his formal stipend (o-naoshi).

One item: In the first month of Bunka 3 (1806, the year of the Tiger), he was appointed as Group Leader (kumigashira) of the Permanent Duty Office (jōzume goyōsho), his rank was raised to the equivalent of District Magistrate (kōri bugyō), and it was declared that he should carry out his duties in keeping with the ongoing austerity measures.

One item: In the second month of the same year, he was granted a stipend supplement (yakuryō) of thirty koku.
