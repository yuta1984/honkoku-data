One item: In the fifth month of the same year (Kyōwa 1, 1801), he accompanied the lord on his journey down to Edo, and upon returning, he traveled down to Aizu.

One item: In the eighth month of Kyōwa 2 (1802, the year of the Dog), he diligently served in the repair and renovation works of the long tenement row (nagaya) of the upper residence (kami-o-yashiki) during the previous year, and as a reward he was granted one piece of silver.

One item: In the ninth month of the same year, since being appointed to the supplementary duty (hokkin) of the office of the御用部屋 (御用部屋, goyō-beya, the administrative office) from the year of the Monkey (Kyōwa 1), he had diligently carried out his duties, and as a reward he was granted two pieces of silver.

One item: In the eleventh month of Kyōwa 3 (1803, the year of the Pig), he was instructed that, during the period of prescribed cost-reduction measures, he should carry out his duties with streamlining as his guiding principle, and that depending on circumstances, he should go directly to the various relevant parties and engage in direct negotiations.

One item: In the fifth month of Bunka 2 (1805, the year of the Ox), he accompanied the lord on his journey and, upon returning, traveled down to Aizu.

One item: In the sixth month of the same year, the matter of the outstanding debt owed by Ōmiya Kihēji proved to be a difficult and protracted affair; he diligently served in this extraordinary duty, and as a reward he was granted one bundle of cotton.
