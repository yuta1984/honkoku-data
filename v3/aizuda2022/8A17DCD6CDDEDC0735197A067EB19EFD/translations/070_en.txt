- In the 12th month of Kansei 10 (1798), the Year of the Horse, he was assigned to the tōzama-gumi (outer retainer group) and placed in the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon, where he served as castle guard (oshiro oban).

- In the 8th month of Kyōwa 1 (1801), the Year of the Rooster, as requested, his son Seigo was appointed as his substitute guard (oban-dai).

- In the 7th month of Bunka 5 (1808), the Year of the Dragon, his son Seigōrō, having traveled to Ezo (Hokkaido) on official domain business, was wounded by one Kataoka Nakazō at that location and died (jimetsu). As a result, as requested, he (the father) was permitted to return to active duty (saikin).

- In the 1st month of Bunka 6 (1809), the Year of the Snake, his request to take Sakuma, the younger brother of Hara Sagoemon, as a son-in-law and adopted son (muko-yōshi), was granted.

- In the 8th month of the same year, during the toidori-gari (a domain hunting exercise), as requested, his son Sakuma was appointed as his proxy for the military muster (jindai).

- In the 2nd month of Bunka 7 (1810), the Year of the Horse, as requested, his son Sakuma was appointed as his substitute guard (oban-dai).
