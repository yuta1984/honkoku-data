**Isuke Shigeyori** 　(childhood name: Momotanosuke)

Mother: Concubine; clan unknown.

Wife: Daughter of Takahashi Kouemon Toshitsugu (died of illness on the thirteenth day of the fifth month of Tenmei 4 [1784], year of the Dragon, at the age of seventy. Posthumous Buddhist name: Sōgon-in Myōjō Nichishō Daimyō [#1]. Officiating priest: Myōryōzan Daihōji Temple. Burial site: Ōkuboyama).

1. In the first month of Kyōhō 13 (1728), year of the Monkey, in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first audience (o-mie) with the lord.

1. In the second month of the eighteenth year of the same era (Kyōhō 18, 1733), year of the Ox, his father Banzō was engaged in the duty of assisting with the castle moat dredging, and upon submitting a petition, he accompanied his father to Edo, where he was assigned to serve as a guard in the Great Hall (o-hirōma ban) [#2], and was further ordered to assist at the construction site, serving until the completion of the construction work.
