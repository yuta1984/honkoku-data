Gizaemon Masakata 《Interlinear note: Formerly known as Uzō | Born on the 15th day of the 10th month of Meiwa 8, year of the Rabbit (1771)》
　Biological father: Eldest son of Gihachi Moriyori
　Wife: Daughter of Hattori Zenji Yoshinori

1. In the 11th month of An'ei 8, year of the Boar (1779): Of his late father's stipend (gigi-saku), three koku were reduced, and he was granted four koku and a ration for two persons (ninin-fuchi), and was called into service as a Nakanokuchi-ban (Middle Gate Guard).

1. In the 6th month of Tenmei 6, year of the Horse (1786): He was assigned to the duty of airing books (mushibo-shi go-yō) at the domain library (go-bunko).
　　Note: After this, he was repeatedly assigned to said duty and continued to perform it.

1. In the 7th month of the same 8th year, year of the Monkey (1788): He was temporarily employed (tōza go-yatoi) at the domain accounting office (go-kanjōsho).
