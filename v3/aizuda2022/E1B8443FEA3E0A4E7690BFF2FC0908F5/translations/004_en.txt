**Genealogy of Hattori Tōkurō**　　(Family crest: Two arrows within a Genji wheel / Alternate crest: Three oak leaves within a circle)

Second son of Hattori Jihei
　**Hattori Jihei Hisakazu**　　(Childhood name: Jirō / Born in the Year of the Dragon, Enpō 4 [1676])

　　Mother's family lineage: unknown

One entry: Though the exact date is unknown, he was appointed as a clerk (*monokaki*) at the Public Affairs Office (*Kujisho*). It is recorded by oral tradition that in the 2nd year of Jōkyō (1685, Year of the Ox) and again in the 4th year of the same era (1687, Year of the Rabbit), he traveled twice to Edo in connection with duties related to the suppression of Christians (*Kirishitan*), and served for a number of years. However, as no official service records (*kinshō*) have been passed down, the details remain unclear.
