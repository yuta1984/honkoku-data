Item: In the sixth month of Keian 4 (1651), the Year of the Rabbit, there was a shortage of officials at Karusawa, and Hattori Giuemon was appointed from among the御歩行衆 (o-hokoishū, foot soldiers/attendants).

Item: In the tenth month of Jōō 1 (1652), the Year of the Dragon, Hattori Giuemon was granted a stipend of twelve koku.
　　Note: The above was found in the official records (kanki) and was therefore recorded here, but since the details of his service are not found at all in the family records (iemoto), it has been transcribed as-is.

Item: On the third day of the ninth month of Meireki 3 (1657), the Year of the Rooster, he died of illness. Posthumous Buddhist name: Shōjuin Seigi Shinji. Place of burial: Chōshōzan Kufukuji Temple.

　Gihei [personal name unknown]
　　Mother: Unknown
　　Wife: Daughter of Higuchi Hikosō [personal name unknown]

　Item: In the Year of the Rooster, Meireki 3 (1657) (month unknown), he was granted twelve koku and a stipend for two attendants, and it is said by oral tradition that he was summoned into service as an o-kachi (foot soldier).
