**Tōkurō Hijokata** (childhood name: Gennosuke; born the 26th day of the 9th month of Anei 6 \[1777\])

- **Mother**: Daughter of the Furuzawa clan
- **First wife**: Daughter of Hirano Gonrokushigeno
　　(Born the 15th day of the 10th month of Tenmei 1 \[1781\]; died of illness the 26th day of the 2nd month of Bunka 5 \[1808\]; Buddhist posthumous name: Teiyō Myōhō Daishi; place of burial: same as above)
- **Second wife**: Daughter of Masuda Genshirō Muneoki
　　(Born the 5th day of the 5th month of Tenmei 5 \[1785\]; died of illness the 12th day of the 7th month of Bunsei 5 \[1822\]; Buddhist posthumous name: Shinpō Myōshō Daishi; place of burial: Unryūin within Tōkaiji Temple, Shinagawa, Edo)
- **Third wife**: Daughter of Minawo Shichirōemon Naruhigashi (born the 8th day of the 4th month of Kansei 4 \[1792\])

---

Item: In the 12th month of Kansei 8 \[1796\], he was appointed as a temporary hire (*tōza oyatoi*) at the domain's warehouse office (*okura-iri yakusho*).

Item: In the 5th month of Kansei 11 \[1799\], he was granted a stipend of 5 koku 5 to and food allowance for 2 persons—reduced by 1 koku from his father's *ogisaku* (stipend)—was summoned into service, and was appointed as a permanent hire (*jōyatoi*) to the post of revenue assessment clerk (*okura-iri otorigashira-shirabe yakunin*).

Item: In the 6th month of the same year, his seating rank was set just below that of the *oheya-osha hikatakata* (attendants responsible for fire duties in the lord's chambers).

Item: In the 3rd month of Kyōwa 1 \[1801\], he was appointed as an escort (*uwanori*) for the wax cargo from the domain's Echigo holdings. He traveled from there through Shinshū (Nagano) to Edo, completed the duty of delivering goods to the Asakusa warehouse, and was released from duty in the 5th month.
　　Note: After this, from the 3rd month of Bunka 10 \[1813\], he was appointed to permanent duty in Edo (*Edo jōzume*), and thereafter was assigned on multiple occasions to garrison duties (*kinban*), emergency garrison duties (*fujikiinban*), or dispatch missions (*shutsuyaku*) to Edo and the Nanzan domain holdings in Echigo entrusted to the domain.
