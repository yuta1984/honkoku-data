In the same 8th year, year of the Boar (An'ei 8, 1779), 9th month: Regarding Uzō, the biological son of his adoptive father, who had been in poor health since childhood — as he had now recovered from his illness, [Moriaki] petitioned to have Uzō designated as the legitimate heir, and this request was granted.

1. In the same year, 9th month: He died of illness at the age of twenty-seven. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Honshōin Ryōgaku Shinshi. Place of burial: same as above.
