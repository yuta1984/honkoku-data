- In December of the same year, he was appointed to the temporary position (*zariza kake*) of *Ryō-Onando* (dual treasury/wardrobe office).

- In the third month of the eleventh year of the Dog (*Inu*) [Bunka 11, 1814], he was additionally appointed to serve concurrently as an assistant (*tetsudai*) for the *Ryō-Onando* role.

- In the sixth month of the same year, regarding the provisions (rice rations) for the personnel stationed and dispatched to the Echigo region: since it was heard that purchasing rice in areas where supplies were short would leave provisions thin, he was ordered to arrange without negligence the transportation of rice to the stationed personnel; and since it was also difficult to foresee how many additional personnel would be dispatched in the future, he was ordered to make arrangements with all speed.

- In the same month, due to an unforeseen situation (*furyo no gi*) arising in the Echigo region, personnel were dispatched; the *kaimai* (rice transport) officers assigned to the domain border conducted themselves commendably, mobilizing promptly and working diligently through the heat of summer, and it was therefore announced that they would be rewarded with sake.

- In the third month of the twelfth year of the Boar (*I*) [Bunka 12, 1815], he was appointed to the *Ryō-Onando* role, with his stipend of 3 *koku* and 5 *to* remaining as previously granted.
