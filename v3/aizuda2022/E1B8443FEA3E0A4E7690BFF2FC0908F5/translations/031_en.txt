- In the tenth month of the fourteenth year of the Ox (*Ushi*) [Bunka 14, 1817], he and the *Okanjō Kumigashira* (accounting section heads) were ordered to divide their responsibilities between the *Gojōyō-kata* (routine/current expenditure section) and the *Oshakkin* (loan/debt) matters. Since at that time the receipt and disbursement of actual cash (*genkin-sen*) was handled by the *Ryō-Onando*, and the *Gojōyō* affairs were already placed under the separate charge of the *Ryō-Onando* officials, it was ordered that, as before, both *Ryō-Onando* officials, with regard to the management and coordination of said monetary funds, should join and work together with the *Gojōyō*-assigned *Okanjō Kumigashira*.

- In the third month of the first year of Bunsei, the Year of the Tiger (*Tora*) [1818], in recognition of his diligent service since his earlier duties, he was granted an additional stipend of 1 *koku* and 1 *ninfuchi* (one person's ration allowance) on top of his existing role stipend (*yakuryō*).

- In the twelfth month of the same year, regarding the new construction (*shinken go-fushin*) of the *Kaisho* (meeting/administrative hall), it was announced that he would be rewarded with sake, as it was felt graciously (*oboshimeshare*) that he had not spared himself from working with his own hands (*jishin hataraku*).

- In the fourth month of the second year of Bunsei, the Year of the Rabbit (*U*) [1819], for his diligent and extraordinary service (*rinji no kinrō*) in handling the processing of refined gold and silver (*yakane-gin*, i.e., assaying/smelting of precious metals), he was granted one *taba* of silver (*ginsu hitotaba*) as a reward.
