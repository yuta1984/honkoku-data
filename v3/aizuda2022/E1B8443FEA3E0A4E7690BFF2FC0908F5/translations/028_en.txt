One entry: In the twelfth month of the fourth year of the same era [Bunka 4, 1807], he was ordered to serve as an inspector/liaison to handle the backlog of accounts in the district silk (郡絹方) accounting office.

One entry: In the sixth month of the fifth year [Bunka 5, 1808], he submitted a detailed report stating that there was no longer any remaining business for him to handle regarding the said silk accounting matters, and inquiring whether he should return to his primary duties. However, it was communicated that the situation appeared to be such that the assigned officers alone could not resolve the matters quickly, and therefore he was ordered to also involve himself with the accounting equipment procurement office (勘定道具取揃方) and continue handling the backlog.

One entry: In the eleventh month of the same year, since the said accounts had still not been resolved, he reported again, and was ordered to attend to his primary duties while also appearing at the silk office during breaks in official business, to continue processing the remaining backlog.

One entry: In the second month of the sixth year [Bunka 6, 1809], he was ordered to serve as a temporary acting officer (当座仮役) in the Minor Role Treasury Office (小役御納戸), also concurrently serving in the Principal Treasury Office (本途御納戸).

One entry: In the fifth month of the same year, he was informed that it was no longer necessary for him to hold a concurrent appointment in the Principal Treasury Office.
