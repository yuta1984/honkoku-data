1. In Manji 2 (1659), the Year of the Boar, 4th month: He was appointed as head of the firearms unit (oteppō-gashira)

1. In the 10th month of the same year: His father Mōemon, having rendered great meritorious service to Lord Dōgi, was appointed as a unit commander (kumigashira)

1. In Kanbun 1 (1661), the Year of the Ox, 4th month: He was granted permission to use a palanquin (norimono) in Aizu

1. In the intercalary 8th month of the same year: He traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned in the 2nd month of the following Year of the Tiger

1. In the 5th year of the same era (1665), the Year of the Snake, 8th month: He traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the 3rd month of the following Year of the Horse

1. In the 7th year of the same era (1667), the Year of the Sheep, 6th month: He died of illness at the age of 62. Posthumous Buddhist name: Hōshōin-dono Shin'ō Dōtetsu Koji. Place of burial: Kongōzan Erin-ji Temple. In later years, as his posthumous name was found to be similar to the name used for Lady Hōshōin, it was changed to Eisshōin.
　　Note: During his illness and after his death, he received a gracious inquiry from his lord, and a helmet (kabuto) was offered as a posthumous gift

Daughter　Name: Ritsu　Born on the 10th day of the 8th month, Kan'ei 13 (1636), the Year of the Rooster　Wife of Kimoto Gonnosuke Nariyori
