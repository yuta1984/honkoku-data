One item: On the same day, he served as an escort when Lady Teikanin proceeded to the castle, and presented himself at the Ohiromashiki (the formal reception area of the Shogun's consort's quarters). Because he had served as escort for Lady Teikanin, he was notified by the Grand Resident Official (Ō-otomari), Lord Hōga Yamashiro-no-kami, that he was to receive the following gifts: two rolls of crêpe (chirimen) (with a plain wood stand) from the Shogun (Kubō-sama), and two rolls of red-and-white crêpe (kōhaku chirimen) (with a plain wood stand) from the Midaidokoro (the Shogun's principal consort).
　　Note: While stationed in the Ohiromashiki, he was ordered by the Midaidokoro to receive a meal (o-shita). Ladies-in-waiting brought out food, including dishes, sake, and side dishes, which he partook of. Beyond this, he received full meals on two further occasions, and additionally partook on numerous occasions of red rice (sekihan), sake, side dishes, rice cakes (mochi), confections, and other such items.

One item: On the same day, as recognition for having performed the escort duty during the celebratory occasion, upon Lady Teikanin's return to her residence, three rolls of red-and-white glossed silk (shaya) (with a plain wood stand [remainder missing]) were bestowed.

One item: On the same day, from Lady Teikanin herself, one box of fresh sea bream (sen-tai), along with red rice (sekihan) and various side dishes packed in tiered boxes (jūzume), were bestowed.
