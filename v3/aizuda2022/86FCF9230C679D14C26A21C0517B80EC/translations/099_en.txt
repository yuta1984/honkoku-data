There being no other location that could serve as a substitute, it would be appropriate to have each of these locations transferred (as domain territory) and to order the construction of the respective facilities accordingly. Furthermore, since it is impossible to predict which stretch of coastline a foreign vessel might approach, preparations must be made — not only for the coastal villages but also for nearby villages — so that laborers can be mobilized in an emergency, with signals established in advance so that people can rush to their posts and fulfill their duties without delay. However, the majority of the people in the coastal villages are fishermen or seafarers, who spend their ordinary days out fishing, or who have gone to other provinces for their livelihoods; there are not a few such persons, making it difficult to count on them being available at the critical moment. Accordingly, unless not only the coastal villages but also the nearby villages within earshot of the signal are transferred as domain territory, there will be impediments in urgent and unforeseen situations. As Miura District is not divided into scattered enclaves but forms contiguous land, it goes without saying; and even with regard to Kamakura District, we earnestly wish that it be transferred as a single, consolidated block. If only the residential plot of the guard post is transferred for both Nishi-Uraga and Misaki-machi, and the surrounding areas remain as御料 (Tenryō — direct Tokugawa shogunate territory), then in times of emergency, given that there are also duties at the guardhouse, the matter of mobilizing personnel would be particularly problematic. It is therefore appropriate to submit a petition requesting that, with due and generous consideration of these circumstances, the aforementioned areas also be transferred as domain territory. These points were all reported in detail one by one, and even at the inspection tour (junken) destination, the same matters were explained to Sen'hachi in an appropriate manner, following the form established previously. Based on the views set forth in the preceding articles, the formal recommendation (shintatsu) was submitted on the twenty-first day of the ninth month.
