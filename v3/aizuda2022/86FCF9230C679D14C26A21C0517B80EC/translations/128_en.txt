[Continued from the previous page: the wedding ceremonies were successfully concluded,] and as a congratulatory gift for this occasion, a box of dried sea bream (hoshi-tai) was presented via messenger to the residence of Lady Teikanin.

One item: On the first day of the third month of the same year, as a congratulatory gift for the wedding, three rolls of red-and-white (kōhaku) glossed silk (shaya) (with a plain wood stand) were bestowed by Lady Teikanin.

One item: On the same day, in return for the congratulatory gifts presented [to Lady Teikanin] for the wedding, three rolls of red-and-white crêpe (chirimen) (with a plain wood stand) were bestowed [upon him].

One item: On the same day, confections were privately received from Lady Teikanin.
　　Note: From this time until her passing, various items were bestowed on numerous occasions.

One item: On the third day of the same month, he was ordered to view Lady Teikanin's hina dolls. Sake and clear soup (suimono) were served to all the attached household officials (go-yōnin). The ladies-in-waiting who had been stationed in the Main Keep (Honmaru) came forward and exchanged greetings. As private gifts, various items were bestowed, including wrapped gifts, a tobacco pouch, a sake cup, steamed confections (mushigashi), wrapped side dishes, and tiered box dishes (jūkō), among other things.

One item: On the ninth day of the same month, on the occasion of a formal visit to the castle (hizanoshi go-tōjō), Senior Councillor (rōjū) Lord Aoyama Shimotsuke-no-kami attended as the Shogun's envoy (ue-tsukai). After the presentation of celebratory gifts was completed, [the subject] was summoned and informed by Lord Shimotsuke-no-kami that he was to receive gifts.
