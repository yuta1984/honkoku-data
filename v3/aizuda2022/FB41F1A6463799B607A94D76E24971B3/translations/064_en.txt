Item: In the fourth month of the same [Kansei] 8, the year of the Dragon (1796), on account of having diligently performed his duties over many years, granted an allowance of one person's stipend (*fuchi*) and an official salary (*yakuryō*).

Item: In the tenth month of the same [Kansei] 10, the year of the Horse (1798), having diligently performed his duties in connection with Lady Ryōtoku-in's personal excursion affairs (*onoridade go-yō*), granted two *bu* of gold as a reward.

Item: In the fifth month of the same [Kansei] 11, the year of the Sheep (1799), having diligently performed his duties over many years, handled various matters to the benefit of the domain, and in particular having rendered especially distinguished service in his duties as *hitowari-kata* (personnel allocation officer), the official salary (*yakuryō*) he had been receiving up to that point was increased by one person's stipend (*fuchi*) and adjusted accordingly.

Item: In the fifth month of Kyōwa 2, the year of the Dog (1802), having served in the *go-bunki-kata* (domain financial allocation) duties and having contributed to the domain's benefit, granted one *ryō* and three *bu* of gold as a reward.
