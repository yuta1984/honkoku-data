Wife: A woman of the Komatsu clan [personal name unknown]　　(Died of illness on the seventeenth day of the ninth month of the seventh year of Genroku [*tsuchi-no-e inu*, 1694]; Posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*): Tsūshin Myōshō Shinnyo; Place of burial: Hōshōzan Jōkōji Temple)

Item: In a year and month not recorded, he was granted eighteen *koku* and a maintenance allowance for two persons (*futari fuchi*) at Takatō, served as a foot soldier (*okachi*) under Lord Dōgi, performed escort duty (*tomo*) on two occasions for the Osaka campaigns, and thereafter continuously served Lord Hiwatari [Hoshina].

Item: In the eighth month of the thirteenth year of Kan'ei (*ne*, 1636), at the time of the lord's transfer (*oshogarae*) to Mogami, he accompanied [the lord] and served as head of the street patrol (*tsujiban no kashira*) in that locality.

Item: In the eighth month of the twentieth year of the same era (*hitsuji*, 1643), at the time of the lord's transfer to Aizu, he accompanied [the lord] and continued in his duties as before.

Item: During the Keian era (1648–1652), he was granted ten *koku* and eight *to* plus a maintenance allowance for two persons, and was appointed as a guard of the treasury (*o-kinzō ban*).

Item: In the tenth month of the third year of Jōō (*uma*, 1654), he was appointed on permanent assignment (*jōzume*) in Edo as an attendant to the chambers of Lord Seikōin (*Seikōin-sama*).

Item: On the seventeenth day of the first month of the second year of Manji (*i*, 1659), he died of illness. Posthumous Buddhist name: Kan'eki Shusen. Place of burial: Nandaiji Temple at Kōhōzan, Edo.

Daughter:　　Wife of Ishikawa Iemon [personal name unknown] (a retainer of Lord Suwa Inaba no Kami)
