**Katsushirō Mitsutoki** 《Born on the 5th day of the intercalary 12th month of Enkyō 2 (1745), Year of the Ox》
Mother: Same as above (as previously noted).

One: In the 3rd month of Kansei 1 (1789), Year of the Rooster, it had long been heard that his conduct and behavior were exceedingly improper. Finding this to be an outrage, [the domain authorities] ordered that he be banished to another domain.

One: In the 7th month of Kansei 2 (1790), Year of the Dog, in accordance with his petition, he was graciously permitted to re-enter the domain.

One: In the 3rd month of Bunka 10 (1813), Year of the Rooster, he was appointed to serve as a hired retainer (o-yatoi) of the tozama [outside] domain at Misaki in Sagami Province (present-day Miura City, Kanagawa Prefecture), was granted a stipend of three persons' worth of rice rations (san-nin fuchi), and served guard duty at the gun battery (odaiba) under the command of Harada Matasuke's unit.

One: In the 10th month of Bunka 13 (1816), Year of the Rat, it was proclaimed that during the previous spring, when a foreign ship drifted ashore at Shimoda in Izu Province (present-day Shimoda City, Shizuoka Prefecture), all personnel showed admirable diligence and prepared swiftly and effectively.
　　Note: After this, each time a foreign ship drifted into the area, he served in the coastal defense and as a large-cannon gunner at the gun battery, and was commended accordingly on each occasion.
