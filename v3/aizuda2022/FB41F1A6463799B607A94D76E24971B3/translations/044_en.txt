Seizaemon Mitsuyasu　《Initial name: Kakuzō | Born on the 4th day of the 8th month of An'ei 5 (1776), Year of the Monkey》
　Mother: Same as above
　Wife: Daughter of Kimura Genjirō Tadahiro (later divorced)
　　　　Daughter of Shimizu Yūhachi Takemoto (likewise divorced)
　　　　Daughter of Iikawa Tetsuzō Mitsusada 《Born on the 21st day of the 6th month of Tenmei 2 (1782), Year of the Tiger》

One: In the 2nd month of Tenmei 8 (1788), Year of the Monkey, in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience with the lord (o-mie).

One: In the 3rd month of Kansei 8 (1796), Year of the Dragon, he was appointed as Nenchō Jūchō (senior youth group leader).

One: In the 11th month of the same year, he was appointed to the role of Haizen Shimar-kata (supervisor of food/meal service).

One: In the 4th month of Kansei 9 (1797), Year of the Snake, in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his duties as Nenchō Jūchō.

One: In the 8th month of the same year, in accordance with his father's petition, he was appointed as O-bandai (castle guard substitute), and served in castle guard duty in the unit of Obara Naidoki.

One: In the same month, he appeared at the Oitorigari (the lord's hunting drive) and received game as a gift.
