1. The 5th month of the same year [Hōei 5, 1708]:
　As he had served admirably as a study companion to Lady Shinshō-in, he was granted an increase of five *koku* and provisions for two persons.

1. The 1st month of the 6th year of the same era, Year of the Ox [Hōei 6, 1709]:
　Upon the passing of Lady Shinshō-in, he was appointed as personal guardian (*Okaidanmori*) to Lady Shōsen-in.

1. The intercalary 5th month of Shōtoku 3, Year of the Snake [1713]:
　He was appointed to the role of *Onando* (keeper of the inner storehouse/chamberlain) for Lady Shōsen-in.

1. The 8th month of Kyōhō 5, Year of the Rat [1720]:
　His stipend, which had until now been in the form of *gisaku* (a provisional or assessed land grant), was converted to a full *chigyō* (enfeoffed domain) of one hundred *koku*.

1. The 6th month of the 11th year of the same era, Year of the Horse [Kyōhō 11, 1726]:
　During the period when their colleague Iguchi Sakichi was serving in the temporary role of *Ometsuke* (inspector general), it was proclaimed that Hitotsuyanagi Iemon and Kawase Gennai had both worked diligently together in his absence.

1. The 4th month of the 12th year of the same era, Year of the Sheep [Kyōhō 12, 1727]:
　Upon the passing of Lady Shōsen-in, he was assigned to the *Tozama-gumi* (outer group) for permanent Edo duty, and entered the unit commanded by Yamazaki Sasuke.
