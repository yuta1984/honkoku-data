[On Katsunoshин Naokeі (continued)]

- (Continued from previous page) Having diligently performed his duties continuously for several months as a *ban-oyatoi* (temporary guard hire), he was granted two bundles of cotton.

- In the seventh month of Bunka 5, the Year of the Dragon (1808), because he had applied himself with diligence to instruction and had cultivated his disciples well, he was granted an additional stipend of two-person rations (*ninin-fuchi*) from the school fund (*gakkō-ryō*) for the duration of his service as a master instructor (*shihan*).

- In the sixth month of Bunka 7, the Year of the Horse (1810), he was appointed as an armored soldier (*kōshi*) for the emergency reserve unit (*fuji o-sonae*).

- In the fifth month of Bunka 8, the Year of the Goat (1811), it was proclaimed that he had applied himself with great diligence in appearing at the formation drill (*ashinami-keiko*).
　　Note: After this occasion, he received commendation repeatedly on numerous occasions.

- In the eleventh month of Bunka 13, the Year of the Rat (1816), during the lord's journey to Kyoto and his absence, when there was a shortage of personnel, he diligently performed his duties as a designated hire from among the outside retainers (*tozama-shi-sadame-oyatoi*), and in recognition thereof he was granted one bundle of cotton as a reward.

- In the third month of Bunsei 1, the Year of the Tiger (1818), having devoted himself with diligence to archery instruction for many years and having cultivated his disciples well, he was granted an additional stipend of two-person rations (*ninin-fuchi*) from the school fund (*gakkō-ryō*).
