One: In the third month of An'ei 7, Year of the Dog (1778), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (*o-mie*) with the lord.

One: In the twelfth month of Kansei 3, Year of the Boar (1791), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was appointed to castle guard duty (*o-ban-dai*), and served in castle guard duty (*o-shiro o-ban*) under the unit of Yokoyama Takiemon.

One: In the third month of the fourth year of the same era, Year of the Rat (Kansei 4, 1792), he turned out for the *oitori-gari* (a large-scale bird hunt conducted as a military exercise).

One: In the same month, regarding this *oitori-gari*, as it was the inaugural event, there had been little opportunity for drill and formation practice (*ashinami-geiko*) prior to the announcement, and moreover there had been very little time to prepare; nevertheless, the formations were well assembled and presented for inspection, and it was decreed that the unit as a whole was commended (*go-shōbi*).

One: In the seventh month of the fifth year of the same era, Year of the Ox (Kansei 5, 1793), he was appointed to escort duty (*o-tomo-ban*).

One: In the third month of the sixth year of the same era, Year of the Tiger (Kansei 6, 1794), he traveled up to Edo (*maka-nobori*) as part of the escort (*o-tomo*) for Lady Teishō's attendance at the shogunal capital (*go-sanpu*), and returned (*maka-kudari*) in the fourth month of the same year.
　　Note: It was decreed that throughout the journey, he had diligently and devotedly performed his duties without any neglect.
