Item: [Continued from the previous page] In the twelfth month of Kansei 1, the year of the Rooster (1789), appointed to serve in the role of attendant official (*ninryaku*) for Lord Sadaaki's (*Teishō-sama*) personal excursion duties (*onoridade go-yō*), and having duly performed such service, granted three *bu* of gold as a reward; furthermore, privately granted five *ryō* of silver from Lord Sadaaki himself.

Item: In the fourth month of the same [Kansei] 3, the year of the Boar (1791), on account of having diligently performed his duties on ordinary days, it was declared that, although this was a matter of considerable weight and distinction, he was to be granted the privilege of *dokurei* (the right to an individual audience with the lord).

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, on account of having attended carefully and in detail to the matters of the inspection (*go-chōgi*) of the previous year of the Dog (1790), and having handled them in a manner beneficial to the domain, as a reward — [the remainder of the text is missing].

Item: In the tenth month of the same [Kansei] 4, the year of the Rat (1792), appointed to the provisional post (*kariyaku*) of *Go-Kanjō Aratame-yaku* (Accounts Inspection Officer).

Item: In the fifth month of the same [Kansei] 5, the year of the Ox (1793), appointed to the provisional post (*kariyaku*) of Ladies' Quarters Attendant (*jochū-tsuki*), accompanying [the party] on the journey down to Aizu, having performed his duties along the road without incident and completed the accounting duties (*go-kanjō-yaku*), granted one *chōgin* (silver ingot) as a reward.

Item: In the tenth month of the same [Kansei] 6, the year of the Tiger (1794), appointed to the permanent post (*jōzume*) of *Go-Kanjō Aratame-yaku* (Accounts Inspection Officer), and instructed to perform duties relating to the *bunki* (domain budget/financial allocation) affairs.
