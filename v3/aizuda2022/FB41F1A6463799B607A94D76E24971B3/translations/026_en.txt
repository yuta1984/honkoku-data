　　(Second wife) Daughter of Kurogouchi Yūemon Tokikata

Item: In the eighth month of Kōwa 3, the Year of the Boar (1803), in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (*omemie*) with the lord.

Item: In the eighth month of Bunka 2, the Year of the Ox (1805), on the occasion of Lord Teishow (Matsudaira Katahiro)'s succession to the domain headship, he was appointed as a page (*okoshō*) in the service of the lord, on a hired basis.

Item: In the second month of Bunka 3, the Year of the Tiger (1806), on the very day of Lord Kinbun (Matsudaira Katazumi)'s succession to the domain headship, he was appointed to escort duty (*otomobан*), on a hired basis, for that day only.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was appointed to the *tozama-shi* (outside retainer) hired service.
　　　Note: He was appointed to this role repeatedly thereafter.

Item: In the fourth month of Bunka 4, the Year of the Rabbit (1807), he was appointed to the role of *kenjutsu shimari-kata* (supervisor/overseer of swordsmanship).

Item: In the sixth month of Bunka 7, the Year of the Horse (1810), his inherited stipend (*atoshiki gochigyō*) of one hundred and fifty *koku* was confirmed without dispute and granted to him. He was assigned to the Aizu *katte-tozama-gumi* (outside retainer unit under the domain's household affairs). Since he was not assigned to general duties, being expected to devote himself to the training of martial and other arts, he was instructed to pay the *kobushin-ryō* (a levy in lieu of active service).
