Toyokichi　　　Born on the 24th day of the 11th month of Kansei 3 (1791), the Year of the Boar; died of illness on the 7th day of the 8th month of Kyōwa 2 (1802), the Year of the Dog. Posthumous Buddhist name: Gendatsuin Jōkan Koji. Place of burial: Ōkuboyama.

　Mother: Same as above (daughter of the Kayano clan)

Daughter　Born on the 12th day of the 2nd month of Kansei 9 (1797), the Year of the Snake　　Wife of Shinoda Hyōzaemon Takamine; separated before the marriage was consummated (婚以前離別). 
　Mother: Same as above (daughter of the Kayano clan)　　Subsequently wife of Kōno Ukon Koretaka (later separated).

Ichinoshinn　　Born on the 2nd day of the 9th month of Kyōwa 2 (1802), the Year of the Dog.

　Mother: Same as above (daughter of the Kayano clan)

Daughter　　　Born on the 19th day of the 5th month of Bunka 9 (1812), the Year of the Monkey.

　Mother: Daughter of the Komatsu clan
