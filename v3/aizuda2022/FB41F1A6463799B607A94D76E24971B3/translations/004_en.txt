(Continued from previous page) By [the lord's] benevolence, a stipend of twenty *koku* and provisions for three persons was granted, and he was called into service; as for the nature of his duties, it was declared that further instructions would be issued in due course.

1. The 12th month of the same year [Genroku 10, 1697]:
　He was appointed to the role of *Okaidanmori* (personal attendant/guardian) to Lady Shinshō-in, and was assigned to permanent duty in Edo.

1. The 2nd month of the 11th year of the same era, Year of the Tiger [Genroku 11, 1698]:
　Upon Lady Shōsen-in's journey to [Edo], he was ordered to accompany her, carrying the guardian sword, as part of her entourage for the journey.

1. The 26th day of the 4th month of the same year:
　Lady Shōsen-in departed from Aizu, arrived in Edo on the 4th day of the 5th month, and from the following 5th day, he entered into service attending Lady Shinshō-in.

1. The 2nd month of Hōei 4, Year of the Boar [1707]:
　He was appointed to the additional role of *Onandо* (keeper of the inner storehouse/chamberlain) for Lady Shinshō-in.
　　*Note: He served in this combined role for two years, until the following Year of the Rat.*

1. The 1st month of the 5th year of the same era, Year of the Rat [Hōei 5, 1708]:
　As he had not received the general clemency/relief [granted to others], he was awarded two bolts of silk as a reward.
