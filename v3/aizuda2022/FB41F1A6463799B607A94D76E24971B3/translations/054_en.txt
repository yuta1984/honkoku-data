One entry: Tenshō 13, Year of the Rooster (1585), seventh month — Upon the occasion of his father Gorōzaemon's death in battle, [Genbei] was granted the headship of the household. However, he subsequently died young, and the family line was extinguished. His posthumous Buddhist name and place of burial are unknown.
　　Note: Based on what is recorded in the genealogy of his son Genbei, it appears that [this Genbei] died of illness in the eighth month of Jōō 1 (1652).
