One: In the ninth month of the same year (Kansei 7, Year of the Rabbit, 1795), he turned out as part of the escort (*o-tomo*) for the *oitori-gari* (bird hunt military exercise), and participated in the mounted hunt (*kiba-gari*).

One: In the third month of the eighth year of the same era, Year of the Dragon (Kansei 8, 1796), he traveled up to Edo (*maka-nobori*) in preparation for the return escort, and returned (*maka-kudari*) in the fourth month of the same year.
　　Note: It was decreed that throughout the journey, he had diligently and devotedly performed his duties without any neglect.

One: In the twelfth month of the same year (1796), he traveled up to Edo (*maka-nobori*) for an unscheduled/extraordinary tour of duty (*fuji kinban*), and returned (*maka-kudari*) in the fifth month of the following Year of the Snake (1797).

One: In the second month of the ninth year of the same era, Year of the Snake (Kansei 9, 1797), he was appointed to the position of permanent Edo-based escort guard (*Edo jōzume o-tomo-ban*).

One: In the third month of the same year, on the occasion of the lord's return journey to the domain (*go-gekō*), he petitioned to make the descent as part of the escort (*o-tomo*), and was granted permission to travel down together with his household (*ka-nai dōdō*).
　　Note: It was decreed that throughout the journey, he had diligently and devotedly performed his duties without any neglect.

One: In the fourth month of the same year, it was decreed that since the previous twelfth month, he had diligently and devotedly performed his duties without any neglect.
　　Note: From this point forward, it was decreed that whenever he achieved a perfect attendance record (*kaikin*) for each successive half-year period, the same proclamation as stated in the main text would be issued.
