One: In the twelfth month of the same ninth year of the Kan/Hōreki era, Year of the Rabbit (Hōreki 9, 1759), he was appointed to the provisional post of construction magistrate (*go-fushin bugyō kari-yaku*).

One: In the third month of the same eleventh year, Year of the Snake (Hōreki 11, 1761), as he had served diligently day and night despite an extremely busy workload as a single-person post, he was granted one set of formal hemp ceremonial garments (*asa no o-kamishimo*).

One: In the first month of the same fourteenth year, Year of the Ox (Hōreki 14 / Meiwa 1, 1764), in recognition of his diligent service, his existing stipend of fifty *koku* was increased, and he received the grant directly from above.

One: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was ordered to investigate missing items from the great treasury storehouse (*ō-o-nando kura*), and in recognition of his labors in inspecting the domain's implements and utensils, he was granted two pieces of silver (*ginsu nikai*).

One: In the twelfth month of Meiwa 3, Year of the Dog (1766), his request to have Tazen, the younger brother of Saka Watari, become his son-in-law through adoption, was granted as petitioned.

One: In the third month of the same fourth year, Year of the Boar (Meiwa 4, 1767), as the accounting management of the Shiba estate had long been carried out negligently, he was relieved of his official duties and assigned to the Aizu *katte-tozama* group, entering the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon.

One: In the fourth month of the same year, he led his retainers (*rōtō*) to Aizu and took up castle guard duty.
