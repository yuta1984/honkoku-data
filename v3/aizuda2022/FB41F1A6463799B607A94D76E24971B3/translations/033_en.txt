**Genealogy of Higuchi Seizaemon**　《Interlinear note: Clan surname: Nakahara | Province of origin: Shinano》　《Interlinear note: Primary family crest: Migi-Mitsudomoe (three comma shapes, right-turning) | Secondary crest: Mizu-Omodaka (water plantain) | Camp curtain crest: Gunbai (war fan)》

【Subject of Entry】Higuchi Seizaemon　Mitsuyasu (given name)
　　　　Third son of Higuchi Seizaemon Mitsuhisa
　　　　(Common name / earlier name): Higuchi Seizaemon Tameji
　Mother: A daughter of Kodaira Shikibu-zaemon Nobumoro
　Wife: A daughter of Kurasawa Koemon Shigenao

Item: In the 6th month of Meireki 2, the Year of the Monkey (1656), he was granted a stipend of fifteen koku and a ration for three persons, and was appointed to the position of御右筆 (go-yūhitsu, official scribe/secretary).
