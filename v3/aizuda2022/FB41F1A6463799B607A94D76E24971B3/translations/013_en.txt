One: In the fifth month of Kansei 1, Year of the Rooster (1789), he was granted the falconry house (*o-taka beya*) residence, as had previously been the case.

One: In the tenth month of the same year, upon the dismissal of his unit commander (*kashira*) Harada Gorōemon from his post, he was transferred to the unit of Yokoyama Takiemon as his new assignment.

One: In the twelfth month of the third year of the same era, Year of the Boar (Kansei 3, 1791), in accordance with his petition, his son Tōtarō was appointed to serve as his guard duty replacement (*go-bandai*).

One: In the third month of the seventh year of the same era, Year of the Rabbit (Kansei 7, 1795), in accordance with his petition, he was granted permission to retire (*inkyo*).

One: On the second day of the seventh month of Bunka 1, Year of the Rat (1804), he died of illness at the age of seventy-five. His posthumous Buddhist name was Zenryōin Tesshin Juseiki-koji. Place of burial: Chōkokuji Temple (*Hasé-dera*) in Shibuya, Edo.

Daughter — Wife of a certain Notsu Orie (a retainer of Lord Matsudaira Awa-no-kami). 《Divorced.》
　　Mother: same as above. Wife of adopted son Iemon Naoyoshi.
Daughter 《Makino Hatsutani》 — Born on the twentieth day of the eighth month of Kan'en 3, Year of the Dragon (1750).
