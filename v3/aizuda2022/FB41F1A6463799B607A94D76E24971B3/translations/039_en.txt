…However, as he had long been regarded as someone poor at accounting (fu-kanjō-mono), he submitted a request stating that he found it difficult to continue serving as acting magistrate indefinitely, and he was relieved of the post upon serving until the 4th month of the following Year of the Boar.

Item: At the beginning of the 4th month of Kan'en 1, the Year of the Dragon (1748), as Ikoma Gobei was assigned to Edo duty (kinban), [Mitsunaka] served as his administrative deputy until the 10th month of the same year.

Item: In the 10th month of Hōreki 1, the Year of the Ram (1751), for the same reason [i.e., Ikoma Gobei's Edo duty], he served [as deputy] until the 4th month of the following Year of the Dog.

Item: At the beginning of the 4th month of the 5th year of the same era, the Year of the Boar (1755), Ikoma Gobei was, as requested, relieved of his post (o-yaku o-men), and [Mitsunaka's] subsequent post became that of the unit of Takahashi Koemon.

Item: At the beginning of the 7th month of Meiwa 2, the Year of the Rooster (1765), as Takahashi Koemon was appointed to the post of Junior Elder (wakadoshiyori), [Mitsunaka's] subsequent post became that of the unit of Kayano Gonbei.

Item: In the 9th month of the 5th year of the same era, the Year of the Rat (1768), as requested, his son Tomoe was appointed as guard replacement (oban-dai).

Item: On the last day of the 10th month of the 9th year of the same era, the Year of the Dragon (1772), he died of illness at the age of sixty-four. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Ittō Ryōkan Koji. Place of burial: same as above.
