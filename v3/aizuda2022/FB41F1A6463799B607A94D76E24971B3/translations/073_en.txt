One: In the ninth month of Kyōhō 1, the year of Saru (1716), due to his many years of diligent service, he was granted an increase of three koku.

One: In the fourth month of Kyōhō 5, the year of Ne (1720), he traveled to Edo for alternate attendance duty (勤番) accompanying the lord, and upon his return in the tenth month of the same year, for his diligent service during his time in Edo, he was granted one piece of silver.

One: In the tenth month of Kyōhō 9, the year of Tatsu (1724), he traveled to Edo for alternate attendance duty, and upon his return in the fifth month of the following year of Mi (1725), accompanying the lord, for his diligent service during his time in Edo, he was granted two pieces of silver.

One: In the eleventh month of Kyōhō 12, the year of Hitsuji (1727), for his many years of diligent and honest service, he was granted an increase of three koku.

One: In the fourth month of Kyōhō 15, the year of Inu (1730), he traveled to Edo for alternate attendance duty accompanying the lord, and upon his return in the tenth month of the same year, for his unfailingly diligent service, he was granted two pieces of silver.

One: In the ninth month of Kyōhō 16, the year of I (1731), he was appointed to the duty of overseeing the funeral procession of Lord Tokuō (徳翁様).
