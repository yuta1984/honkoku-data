Gorōzaemon Mitsuyuki　(Initial name: Sehei; born in Shinshū in the fifth year of Kan'ei, *tatsu* [1628])

　Mother:　[Missing] (not recorded)
　Wife: A daughter of Akaha Genbei Tsugumitsu　(Died of illness on the seventh day of the third month of the first year of Tenna, *tori* [1681]; Posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*): Chōshun'in Kagetsu Myōsei Shinnyo; Place of burial: Kanōzan Ganjōjuji Temple)
　　　　A daughter of Suzuki Rokubei Shigechika

Item: In the seventh month of the second year of Meireki (*saru*, 1656), he was granted nine *koku* and a maintenance allowance for two persons (*futari fuchi*), and was summoned into service as a foot soldier (*okachi*) in the chambers of Lord Hōshōin. As the chamber was short-staffed, however, he was additionally assigned duties including: escort service, document handling in the secretariat (*oyō-beya*), register-keeping in the great hall (*ohiroma*), and document handling in the storeroom (*onando*). Furthermore, as the young lord was still of tender age, he was directed to serve in a manner equivalent to that of a page (*okoshō*), including serving as a companion for play and as a personal body attendant (*ofutokoro-mori*).

Item: In the fourth month of the first year of Manji (*inu*, 1658), he was granted an additional stipend of three *koku* and one person's maintenance allowance, bringing his total to twelve *koku* and a maintenance allowance for three persons. He was relieved of his duties as a foot soldier (*okachi*), and was directed to handle documents in the secretariat (*oyō-beya*), to maintain registers in the great hall (*ohiroma*), and to handle documents in the storeroom (*onando*).
