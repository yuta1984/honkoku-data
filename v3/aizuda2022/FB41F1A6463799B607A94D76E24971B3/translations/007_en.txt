1. The 8th month of Kyōhō 17, Year of the Rat [1732]:
　The hereditary stipend of one hundred *koku* was granted without dispute, and he was assigned to the *Edo jōzume* (permanent Edo duty) *tozama* unit, serving as a *hirōma* guard in the unit of Yamazaki Sasuke.
