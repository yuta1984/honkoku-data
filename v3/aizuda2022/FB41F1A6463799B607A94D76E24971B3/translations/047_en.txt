- In the first month of the tenth year (Year of the Rooster): He was appointed to concurrently serve in artillery (hōjutsu) duties.

- In the intercalary eleventh month of the same year: He was appointed as instructor in artillery (hōjutsu shihan).

- In the twelfth month of the same year: Having devoted himself diligently to his duties at the newly established coastal defense positions in Sagami Province, and having labored in the organization of defensive preparations as well as fire-artillery duties, he was awarded two silver coins (ginshi niimai) as a commendation.

- In the second month of the eleventh year (Year of the Dog): He was provisionally appointed on a temporary basis to the firefighting supervisory post (hikeshi-gakari); however, in the ninth month of the same year, his request was granted and he was relieved of the firefighting supervisory duties.

- In the second month of the twelfth year (Year of the Boar): His superior, Nagasaka Heidayū, was reassigned, and consequently he was transferred to Ibuka Denjirō's unit.

- In the twelfth month of the same year: He was appointed as artillery instructor (hōjutsu shihan); being newly installed in this role, he applied himself with care to the training of the students, diligently bringing them along well, and was therefore awarded one silver coin (ginshi ichimai) as a commendation.

- In the first month of the thirteenth year (Year of the Rat): When a foreign vessel was sighted in the harbor of Shimoda in Izu Province, he served continuously as a gunner at the coastal battery from New Year's Day through the eleventh day of the same month.
