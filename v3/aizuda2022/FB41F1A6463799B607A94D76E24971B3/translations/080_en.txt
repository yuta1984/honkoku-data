Gorōzaemon Mitsura (childhood names: first Yoshinosuke, then Tsunehachi; born on the 29th day of the 1st month of Meiwa 1, the Year of the Monkey)
　Biological father: eldest son of Satō Kakuemon Nobuyasu
　Wife: daughter of Kayano Uhyōe Nagahide

1. In the 3rd month of Anei 3 (1774), the Year of the Horse, in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience with the lord.

1. In the 3rd month of Tenmei 2 (1782), the Year of the Tiger, he was granted, without any discrepancy, an inherited stipend of 100 koku, and served as castle guard within Yamazaki Sasuke's unit.

1. In the 9th month of Kansei 1 (1789), the Year of the Rooster, as the unit commander Yamazaki Sasuke was relieved of his post, he was consequently transferred to Kajiwara Heima's unit as its successor.

1. In the 9th month of Kansei 5 (1793), the Year of the Ox, he participated in the falconry hunt (Oitorigari).
　　Note: After this occasion, he participated a total of seven times, including the mounted troop reviews (Umazoroi).

1. In the 4th month of Kansei 6 (1794), the Year of the Tiger, it was recognized that during the previous autumn's falconry hunt, his unit's marching formation and ranks were well-ordered, which was deemed to be the result of his diligent private training and preparation beforehand; accordingly, the entire unit received commendation.
