His posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*) was Kanchiin Tessō Gikan Koji. His place of burial is not precisely known, but is recorded as Unzan Zenryūji temple.

Adopted Daughter — Wife of adopted son Iemon Naoyoshi
　Biological father: A daughter of Muramatsu Yasubyōe (a retainer serving in the capacity of *kōgi o-kachi* [foot soldier in service of the shogunate]), family name unknown.
Daughter — 《Born in the second year of Genbun, Year of the Ox (1737). Died of illness on the second day of the ninth month of Hōreki 3, Year of the Rooster (1753), at the age of twenty-seven. Posthumous Buddhist name: Kōsō Shūrin Shinnyō. Place of burial: Chōkokuji temple [Hasederа], Shibuya, Edo.》
　　Mother: a daughter of the Nakamura clan.

Toyonosuke Naoi — 《Born in the third year of Kanpō, Year of the Boar (1743). Died of illness on the seventh day of the twelfth month of Meiwa 2, Year of the Rooster (1765), at the age of twenty-three. Posthumous Buddhist name: Shinreiin Hakusei Teisetsu Koji. Place of burial: same as above.》
　　Mother: a daughter of the Ogino clan.

Iemon Naoyoshi — 《First called Tazen, then Hachibe; after retirement, known as Ikkyū. Born on the first day of the second month of Kyōhō 15, Year of the Dog (1730).》
　　Biological fourth son of Saka Seizaemon Naokazu.
