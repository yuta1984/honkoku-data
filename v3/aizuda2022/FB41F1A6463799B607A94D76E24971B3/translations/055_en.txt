Genbei (personal name unknown)
　　Mother: unknown

One entry: Jōō 1, Year of the Dragon (1652), ninth month, tenth day — A document of intercession (torinaoshi-sho) was found stating that a man called Higuchi Genbei had died of illness the previous month (eighth month), that his son had reached the age of thirteen, and that as [Genbei] was a hereditary retainer (fudai no mono), [the son] was requesting an official allowance certificate (kirifu-tori; note: the original text writes 府 but this appears to be a transcription error). Furthermore, in the same month, it was reported that Higuchi Genbei had been granted 18 koku and a stipend for two persons (ninin-fuchi), and that his son was thirteen years of age that year. It appears that, of the aforementioned stipend rice (okirimai), twenty bales of Aizu tawara [rice] and a stipend for two persons were granted; however, no family tradition (kagen-den) has been passed down regarding this matter.
