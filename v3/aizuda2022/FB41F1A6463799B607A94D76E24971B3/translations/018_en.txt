One: In the eleventh month of Kyōwa 2, Year of the Dog (1802), as he had achieved a perfect attendance record (*kaikin*) of a full three years throughout the lord's residence in Edo (*go-zaifu*), from the sixth month of the previous Year of the Sheep (1799) through the tenth month of the current year, he was awarded two pieces of silver (*ginshi nima*) as a reward for his meritorious service.

One: In the fourth month of Bunka 1, Year of the Rat (1804), he was appointed to the provisional post of *o-meshiban* (personal attendant guard).

One: In the tenth month of the same year, it was decreed that he had continuously performed his duties as provisional *o-toba-n* (gate guard) from the fourth month of the current year onward.

One: In the third month of the second year of the same era, Year of the Ox (Bunka 2, 1805), he was ordered to serve in attendance (*o-tomo*) to Lord Teisho (*Teishō-sama*) while Lord Kyōtei (*Kyōtei-sama*) was residing in the domain. Upon his petition, he performed this duty through the seventh month of the same year without receiving reimbursement for various expenses (*shohiryō*).

One: In the eighth month of the same year, as Lord Kyōtei (*Kyōtei-sama*) had fallen ill, Lord Teishō (*Teishō-sama*) descended to Aizu to attend to him, and he was ordered to serve as escort (*o-tomo*).

One: In the intercalary eighth month of the same year, upon Lord Teishō's (*Teishō-sama*) succession as head of the household (*okato*), he was appointed to serve as attendant (*o-kyūji*) in the guest reception chamber (*o-kyaku-zashiki*).
