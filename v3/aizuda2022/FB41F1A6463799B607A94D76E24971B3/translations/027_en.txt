[Iemon Naosaku (continued)]

...he was entered into the unit of [Takahashi Yogo]rō.
　　Note: Upon his petition, he was stationed in Edo.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year (Bunka 7, Year of the Horse, 1810), as it was reported that he had applied himself diligently to the martial arts and had attained a level of mastery, he was exempted from the *kobushin-ryō* levy and was ordered to resume active duty.

Item: In the second month of Bunka 8, the Year of the Boar (1811), in the event of an emergency, he was appointed as an armored soldier (*kōshi*) of the reserve unit (*sonaegumi*).

Item: In the same month, in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his appointment as *kenjutsu shimari-kata* (supervisor of swordsmanship).

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, he was appointed to the permanent-posting outside retainer unit (*jōzume tozama-shi*).

Item: In the same month, in the event of an emergency, he was appointed as *onagae-gashira* (pike/spear unit commander) of the lord's personal reserve unit (*otegoto sonae*).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was appointed as *otsukaiban* (messenger/liaison officer).
　　Note: He was appointed to this role repeatedly thereafter.

Item: In the ninth month of Bunka 10, the Year of the Rooster (1813), he was appointed as *kari-mononogashira* (acting unit commander).
　　Note: He was appointed to this role repeatedly thereafter.

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, he was ordered to perform augmented duty (*kōka-kin*) based on his previous stipend level (*mae-mae-daka*), in the manner used when there were no outside retainers of that stipend rank.
