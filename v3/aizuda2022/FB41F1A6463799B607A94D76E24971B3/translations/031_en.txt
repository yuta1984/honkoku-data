　Wife of Kawamura Kurōzō (a retainer of Matsudaira Ukon-no-shōgen-sama) 《Divorced》
　Mother: Same as above

Item: From the age of three, in the third year of Kyōwa, the Year of the Pig (Kyōwa 3, 1803), she was repeatedly summoned to attend upon Juhōin-sama and Renjuin-sama, and received gifts on numerous occasions.

Item: On the 11th day of the seventh month of Bunka 14, the Year of the Ox (1817), she died of illness at the age of seventeen. Posthumous Buddhist name: Gogan Myōyō Daishi. Place of burial: Same as above (Chōkokuji Temple, Shibuya, Edo).
