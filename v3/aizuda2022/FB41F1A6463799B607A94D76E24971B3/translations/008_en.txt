1. The 4th month of the same year [Genbun 1, 1736]:
　On the occasion of the celebratory gathering (*go-shūgi*) for [the lord's] succession to the family headship (*go-katoku*), when the Senior Councillors (*o-rōjū*) were invited as guests, he was appointed to temporary service as a page (*o-koshō o-yatoi*). Having fulfilled his duties without any deficiency, he was rewarded with a formal meal (*go-ryōri*).

1. The 9th month of Kanhō 1, Year of the Rooster [1741]:
　He served in the escort accompanying [the lord] on his journey to Kyoto (*go-jōkyō*).

1. The 2nd month of the 2nd year of the same era, Year of the Dog [Kanhō 2, 1742]:
　He was appointed to the role of gate guard (*o-toban*).

1. The 1st month of the 3rd year of the same era, Year of the Boar [Kanhō 3, 1743]:
　In lieu of a labor stipend (*rōgin*), he was granted one set of formal *kamishimo* court dress made of old hemp cloth (*o-furu-asa*).

1. The 11th month of Enkyō 1, Year of the Rat [1744]:
　A fire broke out from the residence of Lord Matsudaira Sakon-no-Shōgen, and [the lord] made an unscheduled visit to Edo Castle (*fuji go-tōjō*). [This retainer] promptly arrived and served in the escort as [the lord] departed from the castle (*go-gejō*). He was commended for this service, and was rewarded with clear soup (*o-suimono*) and sake.

1. The same month [11th month of Enkyō 1, 1744]:
　On the occasion of the wedding ceremony of Lord Dojō (*Dojō-sama*), he performed the duty of *o-koshiyori* (attending to the palanquin approach).
