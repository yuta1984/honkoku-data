Accordingly, during the period of the aforementioned service, his stipend was increased by two persons' rations (*ninin-fuchi*), bringing it to nine persons' rations (*kunin-fuchi*).

Item: In the first month of the eleventh year, the Year of the Dog (Bunka 11, 1814), he was appointed to the provisional role of *sōjaban* (master of ceremonies/official presenter).
　　Note: He was appointed to this role repeatedly thereafter.

Item: In the third month of the thirteenth year, the Year of the Rat (Bunka 13, 1816), as a memorial Buddhist service (*hōji*) was to be held at Jisshōji temple, he was appointed to attend in proxy on behalf of the lord (*oshiro daisan*).

Item: In the fourth month of the same year, he accompanied the lord on his journey to Kyoto.

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, as he had diligently performed the provisional additional duties of *otsukaiban* (messenger/liaison officer) for several months from before the lord's departure for Kyoto through after his return to Edo, he was awarded a set of formal hemp ceremonial clothing (*asa okamishimo hitokumi*) as a reward.

Item: In the fourth month of the fourteenth year, the Year of the Ox (Bunka 14, 1817), he was additionally appointed as *okikiban* (intelligence/information officer).
　　Note: He was appointed to this role repeatedly thereafter.

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, as he had achieved perfect attendance (*kakin*) throughout three years of various guard duties (*obangata*), he was awarded two pieces of silver (*ginshi nimai*).
