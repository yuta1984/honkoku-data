Item: In the 10th month of the 8th year of the same era, the Year of the Boar (1695), he went up to Edo for a tour of duty (kinban), and returned in the 4th month of the following Year of the Rat (1696).

Item: In the 7th month of the 11th year of the same era, the Year of the Tiger (1698), he was granted the inherited stipend (atoshiki gochigyō) of one hundred koku without dispute.

Item: In the 2nd month of the 15th year of the same era, the Year of the Horse (1702), he was appointed as a domain intendant (odaikan).

Item: In the 8th month of Hōei 6, the Year of the Ox (1709), having performed his official duties admirably, he was rewarded with two pieces of silver (ginshi nimai) as a commendation.

Item: In the 4th month of Shōtoku 1, the Year of the Rabbit (1711), he was appointed as domain intendant (odaikan) for the Minami-yama, Inan, and Ihoku districts.

Item: In the 7th month of the 3rd year of the same era, the Year of the Snake (1713), he was appointed as Inspector (ometsuke) and granted an official stipend supplement (oyakuryō) of fifty koku.

Item: In the 2nd month of the 4th year of the same era, the Year of the Horse (1714), it being deemed that he was not well-suited to his official duties, he was relieved of his post and assigned to the tozama-gumitsuke (outside retainer unit). He was placed in the unit of Jinbo Mozaemon and served as castle guard (o-shiro o-ban).

Item: In the 4th month of Kyōhō 1, the Year of the Monkey (1716), he went up to Edo for a tour of duty (kinban), and returned in the 10th month of the same year.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 5th year of the same era, the Year of the Rat (1720), he was appointed as domain intendant (odaikan).

Item: In the 7th month of the 7th year of the same era, the Year of the Tiger (1722), he was appointed as domain intendant (odaikan) for the Minami-yama district.

Item: In the 5th month of the 9th year of the same era, the Year of the Dragon (1724), as requested, he was relieved of his official duties.
