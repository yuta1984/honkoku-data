Item: In the intercalary fourth month of the second year of Bunsei, the Year of the Rabbit (Bunsei 2, 1819), as he had diligently continued the provisional duties of *otsukaiban* (messenger/liaison officer) without interruption for several months, he was awarded a set of formal hemp ceremonial clothing (*asa okamishimo hitokumi*).

Item: In the third month of the fourth year of the same era, the Year of the Snake (Bunsei 4, 1821), he was appointed as the designated fire warden (*jōhikeshi*) at the Shiba residence.

Item: In the fifth month of the same year, as Tanaka Tetsujiō was appointed as permanent duty unit commander (*jōzume bantō*), [he] was placed into that unit.

Item: In the fifth month of the fifth year of the same era, the Year of the Horse (Bunsei 5, 1822), during the fire that broke out in Kobikicho, he led his men out and directed the firefighting efforts effectively, successfully extinguishing the fire, and this was formally commended.
