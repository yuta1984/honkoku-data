Saigū Mitsunaka　　(originally named Tetsuzō; born on the 11th day of the 7th month of Hōei 6, the Year of the Ox [1709])
　Mother: adopted daughter of the Shirai clan.
　Wife: daughter of Yamadera Gonnosuke Yasuchika. (Died of illness on the 18th day of the 4th month of An'ei 4, the Year of the Ram [1775]. Posthumous Buddhist name: Eirinshōhan Daishi. Place of burial: same as above.)

Item: In the 10th month of Kyōhō 11, the Year of the Horse (1726), as his parent requested, he was appointed as guard replacement (oban-dai), and served as castle guard (o-shiro o-ban) in the unit of Sugita Gorōbei.

Item: At the beginning of the 7th month of the 20th year of the same era, the Year of the Rabbit (1735), Sugita Gorōbei was, as requested, relieved of his post (o-yaku o-men), and the subsequent post became that of the unit of Ikoma Gobei.

Item: In the 4th month of Genbun 1, the Year of the Dragon (1736), the inherited stipend (atoshiki o-chigyō) of one hundred koku was granted without any discrepancy.

Item: In the 10th month of Kanpō 2, the Year of the Dog (1742), he was appointed as acting magistrate (o-daikan kari-yaku)…
