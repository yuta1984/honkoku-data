[On Katsunoshин Naokeі]
(First name: Kojirō; born on the third day of the ninth month of Anei 3, the Year of the Horse [1774])
　Mother: same as previously stated.
　Wife: younger sister of Ogino Jōuemon Sadanao (a retainer of Lord Mori Higo-no-kami).

- In the seventh month of Kansei 9, the Year of the Snake (1797), he was appointed as the supervisor (shimari-kata) of spear arts (sōjutsu).

- In the eleventh month of the same year, he was appointed as the supervisor of Inzai-school archery (kyūjutsu).

- In the eighth month of Kansei 10, the Year of the Horse (1798), he was appointed as the supervisor of Taishi-ryū swordsmanship (kenjutsu).

- In the second month of Kansei 11, the Year of the Sheep (1799), he was appointed on a hired basis as an attendant (otomo-kata) to Lord Teishō.
　　Note: He was appointed to this role repeatedly thereafter.

- In the third month of the same year, he was appointed on a hired basis as a close attendant (gokinju-ban) to Lady Ryōtoku-in.
　　Note: He was appointed to this role repeatedly thereafter.
