**Seiemon Mitsutoyo** 《First name: Kitarō, later Seizaemon; born in Enpō 3, the Year of the Tiger (1675)》
　Mother: A daughter of the Kurasawa clan.
　First wife: An adopted daughter of Higuchi Sakuzaemon Mitsuharu. 《She died on the 2nd day of the 7th month of Hōei 1, the Year of the Monkey (1704). Posthumous Buddhist name: Shōya Ryōton Shinnyо. Place of burial: Same as above.》
　　　In actuality, a daughter of Yamadera Sosuke Yasusada.
　Second wife: An adopted daughter of Shirai Gorōdayū Tanenori. 《She died of illness on the 13th day of the 8th month of Kan'en 1, the Year of the Dragon (1748). Posthumous Buddhist name: Jōgon'in Myōshū Shinnyо. Place of burial: Same as above.》
　　　In actuality, a daughter of Ichinose Kanbei (given name unknown).

Item: In the 2nd month of Genroku 3, the Year of the Horse (1690), as his father had requested, he was appointed as duty successor (obandai), and he served as castle guard (o-shiro o-ban) in the unit of Ichinose Denbei.
