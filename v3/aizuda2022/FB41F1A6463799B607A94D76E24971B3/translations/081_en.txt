Note: After this, he received commendation on every occasion.

1. In the 1st month of Bunka 5 (1808), the Year of the Dragon, having been appointed to duty for the defense of Ezo (Hokkaido), he was granted a formal audience with the lord, given leave of absence, and was also bestowed a meal (御流) at the Shōni-in.

1. In the same month, he departed from this place for the said duty, traveled to Sōya in Ezo, and subsequently relocated to Rishiri, where he performed garrison duties.

1. In the 8th month of the same year, his assigned duty having been completed, he set sail from that location and returned to his post.

1. In the 12th month of the same year, on account of the said duty — which involved travel by both sea and land — and having diligently carried out his service for several days in that region, returning without any difficulties, he was bestowed a formal meal (御料理).

1. In the 8th month of Bunka 6 (1809), the Year of the Snake, having fulfilled his duties in the defense of Ezo with diligence, he was bestowed one set of seasonal clothing (時服).

1. In the 12th month of Bunka 9 (1812), the Year of the Monkey, as the methods for joint unit drills at the Sannomaru training ground and at Hongōhara had been reformed from that spring, and as this was the inaugural occasion, the unit's formation and marching steps were well-ordered and executed with diligence, which was recognized and commended; accordingly, the entire unit received commendation.
