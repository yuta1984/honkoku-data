Item: In the 3rd month of Manji 2, the Year of the Boar (1659), he went up to Edo.
　　Note: It would appear this was for a tour of duty (kinban), but there is no record of this in the family's own documents; it is found in the official domain records (kanki).

Item: In the 8th month of the same year, he was appointed as a document writer (monokaki) in the御次 (o-tsugi) office.

Item: In the 9th month of the 3rd year of the same era, the Year of the Rat (1660), upon being granted leave (o-itoma), he was granted an audience (o-mie).
　　Note: There is no record of this in the family's documents; it appears as stated in the official domain records. It would appear that while he was in residence in Edo, upon being granted leave to return to Aizu, he was accorded an audience.

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, he was granted an increase of five koku to his stipend.

Item: In the 12th month of Kanbun 3, the Year of the Rabbit (1663), he was granted an increase of five koku to his stipend.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 5th year of the same era, the Year of the Snake (1665), his stipend, which had previously been designated as gisaku (a provisional/equivalent stipend), was formally converted to a landed stipend (chigyō) of one hundred koku.
