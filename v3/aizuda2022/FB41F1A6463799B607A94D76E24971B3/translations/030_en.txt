Item: In the eighth month of the second year of Bunsei, the Year of the Rabbit (Bunsei 2, 1819), he was ordered to assist Ōtake Isamu in performing the *meigen* (bowstring-twanging) ritual and the fire-quelling (*hishizume*) Shinto ceremony at the Juhōin-sama's palace. Upon completion, he was awarded two hundred *hiki* in gold as a mark of commendation.

Item: In the twelfth month of the third year of the same era, the Year of the Dragon (Bunsei 3, 1820), upon the successful completion of construction works (*gofushin*) at the palace, he was ordered to assist his uncle Katsu-no-shin with the *hikime* ceremonial arrow duties.

Item: In the first month of the fourth year of the same era, the Year of the Snake (Bunsei 4, 1821), on an auspicious day, he fulfilled without delay the duty of assisting with the *hikime* ceremonial arrow ritual.

Item: In the ninth month of the fifth year of the same era, the Year of the Horse (Bunsei 5, 1822), in an emergency situation, he was appointed as a *kōshi* (armored warrior/cavalryman) in Tanaka Tetsujiō's unit.
