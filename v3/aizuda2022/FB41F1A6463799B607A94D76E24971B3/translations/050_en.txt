- In the tenth month of the same year: Regarding practice drills at the training grounds, as the formation, foot alignment, and other elements had been brought into order and had reached the highest grade over a period of three years, he was awarded ceremonial sake.

- In the eleventh month of the third year (Year of the Dragon): The unit commander (kashira) Nagai Sakyō died of illness, and until a successor could be appointed to the post, Tanaka Tetsujirō was appointed to serve as acting supervisor (torishimariyaku).

- In the eleventh month of the same year: He was ordered to withdraw from the Sagami Province (Sōshū) station.

- In the fifth month of the fourth year (Year of the Snake): As a foreign vessel was reported sighted off the coast of Mito, he took up his station at the coastal battery (odaiba) as an artillery crew member before dawn, and withdrew during the same night.

- On the fifth day of the same month: As the formal handover of the coastal battery was completed, he departed from that location on the following sixth day, taking Rōji with him, and arrived in Aizu on the seventeenth day.

- In the eighth month of the same year: Having been reclassified as an tozama-shi (outside retainer) here, and as he had served continuously and with many years of diligent effort at the Sagami Province defense station from its very beginning, he was granted one set of seasonal clothing (toki-fuku). It was also formally declared that he had served with great diligence as an instructor in the established martial arts at that location.

　　Note: He was placed in the Obara Mino unit and performed castle guard duties.

- In the twelfth month of the same year: At the time of the handover of the Sagami Province station, as a foreign vessel was sighted and he served with diligence, he was awarded ceremonial sake.

- In the eighth month of the sixth year (Year of the Sheep): He attended the combined unit drill exercises.
