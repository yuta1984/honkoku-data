Mother: same as above

Although there is no original record (moto-den) for this woman's family line, a written note (kakitsuke) by Higuchi Hikobei states: "Since the son-in-law Arimochi Gentayū absconded (chikuden), I have raised both my daughter and grandchild together. The daughter has since been married off. The grandchild Senzuke has reached the age of eleven, and as he is the sole child of that man [Arimochi Gentayū], there is a wish for him to enter religious life (become a Buddhist monk). It is accordingly desired that he be made a disciple of Zenryūji temple." This is found recorded in the official records (kanki).
