- In the second month of the sixth year of the Snake (Bunsei 9, 1826), he was provisionally appointed as Ometsuke (inspector/censor) to succeed Kamiyama Kandayū.

- In the third month of the same year, in recognition of his perfect attendance record in his duties as Ohiroma-ban (Great Hall Guard) and various other posts over a period of three years, from the Year of the Tiger (Bunsei 5) through the Year of the Dragon (Bunsei 7), he was awarded two silver coins as a mark of commendation.

- In the second month of the seventh year of the Horse (Bunsei 10, 1827), he was appointed to the post of Kiyoto-ban (Kiyoto Guard duty).

- On the fifteenth day of the fourth month of the same year, he died of illness at the age of forty-eight. His posthumous Buddhist name was Shōzen-in Ippō Dōkan Koji. His place of burial is the same as stated previously.
