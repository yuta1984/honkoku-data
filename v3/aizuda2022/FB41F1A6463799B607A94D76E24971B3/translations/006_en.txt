1. The 10th month of the 13th year of the same era, Year of the Monkey [Kyōhō 13, 1728]:
　As he had reached an advanced age, he was informed that he was relieved of his duties accompanying [his lord] on various occasions.

1. The 11th month of the 16th year of the same era, Year of the Boar [Kyōhō 16, 1731]:
　His petition to take Gunji, the third son of Higuchi Yahyōbei, as his son-in-law and adopted heir, was granted as requested.

1. The 12th day of the 6th month of the 17th year of the same era, Year of the Rat [Kyōhō 17, 1732]:
　He died of illness at the age of sixty-nine. Posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*): Jikishin Jun'kō Koji. Place of burial: Chōkoku-ji [Hasederai] Temple, Shibuya, Edo.

Hikojirō (common name: Tsugutomo) — separated [from the family register].
　Biological father: Hitotsuyanagi Uzaemon Naoshige, second son.

Iemon Naoyoshi (first name: Gunji; born the 5th month of Kanbun 5, Year of the Snake [1665]):
　Biological father: third son of Higuchi Yahyōbei Mitsutake.
　Wife: adopted daughter of his adoptive father Iemon Naoyuki. (Born in Shōtoku 3, Year of the Snake [1713]. Died of illness on the 26th day of the 10th month of Kyōhō 16, Year of the Boar [1731], at the age of nineteen. Posthumous Buddhist name: Tei■ Hekikan Shinnyo. Place of burial: same as above.)
