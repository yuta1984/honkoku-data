One: In the fifth month of Genbun 3, the year of Uma (1738), he traveled to Edo on unscheduled (emergency) alternate attendance duty, and upon his return in the eighth month of the same year, for having traveled to Edo on short notice and serving with diligence, he was granted three pieces of silver.

One: In the sixth month of Genbun 4, the year of Hitsuji (1739), for his thorough knowledge and disciplined training in his current duties, and for his many years of excellent service, he was appointed to the Tozama-gumi (外様組) post, and was additionally ordered to concurrently serve at the御用所 (administrative office). He was assigned to the unit of Numazawa Gonbei (沼沢権兵衛).

One: In the fourth month of Genbun 5, the year of Saru (1740), on the occasion of the lord's sankin-kōtai (alternate attendance), he was appointed via the personnel officer (御人指) to serve in the escort attendance duty. He traveled to Edo, and it was acknowledged that he had served with diligence both in his escort duties and during the journey. Upon his return in the tenth month of the same year, for his diligent service, he was granted three pieces of silver.

One: In the first month of Kanpō 1, the year of Tori (1741), he was notified via the personnel officer that he was to be appointed to escort the lord on his journey to Kyoto, and was instructed to travel up to Kyoto as soon as his preparations were complete. He departed in the same month. However, the lord's journey to Kyoto was postponed and extended into the autumn, and as a result, the entire party was ordered to rotate and return home.
