Wife — Daughter of adoptive father Iemon Naoyoshi. 《Born on the eleventh day of the sixth month of Enkyō 3, Year of the Rat (1746). Died of illness on the twenty-ninth day of the tenth month of Bunka 11, Year of the Dog (1814). Posthumous Buddhist name: Zenmyōin Hon'ō Teishin Daishi. Place of burial: same as above.》

One: In the first month of Meiwa 4, Year of the Boar (1767), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (*o-memie*) with the lord.

One: In the eleventh month of the same year, he was granted the inherited stipend (*atoshiki go-chigyō*) of one hundred and fifty *koku* without dispute, was assigned to the *tozama-gumi* (outer retainer unit), and served as castle guard (*go-shiro go-ban*) in the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon.

One: In the third month of An'ei 3, Year of the Horse (1774), his petition to take Tōtarō, the youngest son of Tatsuno Genzaemon, as his son-in-law and adopted heir (*muko yōshi*), was granted.

One: In the same month, he traveled up to Edo to perform his duty of attendance (*kinban*) as part of the lord's escort (*o-tomo*), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: He was appointed as a temporary escort guard (*kari o-tomo-ban*) during the journey.

One: In the twelfth month of the fourth year of the same era, Year of the Sheep (An'ei 4, 1775), his petition to change his name to Iemon was granted.

One: In the fourth month of the ninth year of the same era, Year of the Rat (An'ei 9, 1780), upon the retirement of his unit commander (*kashira*) Hyūga Saburōemon, he was transferred to the unit of Harada Gorōemon as his new assignment.
