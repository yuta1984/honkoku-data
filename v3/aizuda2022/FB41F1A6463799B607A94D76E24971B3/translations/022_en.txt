[On Katsunoshин Naokeі (continued)]

- (Continued from previous page) He was appointed to assist as an instructor of bow-relinquishment (yumi-dan shinan-kata tetsudai-kin).

- In the seventh month of Kōwa 1, the Year of the Rooster (1801), he was appointed as an assistant for the *hikime* ritual at the time of Lady Juhō-in's safe delivery.

- In the tenth month of the same year, having carried out the *hikime* ceremonial duties at the birth without any delay or incident, he was granted one piece of silver by Lord Teishō.

- In the same month, he was granted three bundles of cotton by Lady Juhō-in and Lady Genkō-in.

- In the twelfth month of Kōwa 2, the Year of the Dog (1802), because he had long devoted himself wholeheartedly to archery, diligently instructed members of the domain, and cultivated many students, he was granted a stipend of three-person rations (*sannin-fuchi*) from the school fund (*gakkō-ryō*).

- In the ninth month of Kōwa 3, the Year of the Boar (1803), at the request of Lord Kanō Yamato-no-kami to this domain (Aizu), he was appointed to provide instruction in archery to Lord Eitarō and Lord Rokurō.
　　Note: He was similarly appointed to instruct under Lord Honda Inaba-no-kami as well.
