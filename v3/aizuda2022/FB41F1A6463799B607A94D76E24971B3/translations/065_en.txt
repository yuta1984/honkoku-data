Item: In that same year (Bunka 3, year of the Tiger, 1806), he was due to proceed down to Aizu; however, since he had served as the annual duty officer (*nenban*) for the candle storehouse (*rōsoku go-kura*) and the costume loan storehouse (*o-kashi-shōzoku go-kura*) in the preceding year of the Ox (Bunka 2, 1805) and had completed the associated accounting (*go-kanjō*), he remained in place as an official-in-charge (*go-yō-gakari*). During this time, in the seventh month of that year, he was provisionally appointed to the position of minor official (*ko-yakunin kari-yaku*) of the Accounts Office (*go-kanjō-sho*), continuing in this role through to the third month of the year of the Rabbit [i.e., Bunka 4, 1807].

Item: In the seventh month of the same [Bunka] 4, year of the Rabbit (1807), he was provisionally appointed to the position of *o-hiroshiki o-kachi metsuke* (Foot Inspector of the Grand Ceremonial Office) on a temporary basis (*tōza kari-yaku*), serving through to the eighth month of the same year.

Item: In the sixth month of the same [Bunka] 5, year of the Dragon (1808), he was tasked with copying Japanese and Chinese military treatises (*heisho*) and legal codes (*ritsusho*), as well as copying old records of the administrative office (*go-yōsho kyūki*), continuing in this task through to the first month of the following year of the Snake (Bunka 6, 1809).

Item: In the first month of the same [Bunka] 6, year of the Snake (1809), he was provisionally appointed on a temporary basis (*tōza kari-yaku*) as a treasury disbursement officer (*o-kane-harai yakunin*).

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, he was appointed as a permanent Edo-based treasury disbursement officer (*Edo jōzume o-kane-harai yakunin*). In terms of seniority (*seki*), he was placed above Andō Etsu-no-suke, who held the positions of *aratame-yaku* (inspection officer), *bugu yakunin* (armaments officer), and *kinsen ukeharai ginmi-yaku* (monetary receipts and payments auditor), ranking just below the *aratame-yaku* position.
