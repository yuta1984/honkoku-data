One: In the eighth month of the same year, due to the arrival of the domain inspector (御国目付), he was appointed to serve in the related duties, and continued in this role until the spring of the following year of Ushi (1733).

One: In the twelfth month of the same year, for his many decades of honest and diligent service, and furthermore for his smooth and unimpeded execution of duties related to the domain inspector's visit, he was granted an increase of three koku and one person's stipend (一人扶持).

One: In the fourth month of Kyōhō 18, the year of Ushi (1733), he traveled to Edo for alternate attendance duty, and upon his return in the tenth month of the same year, for his diligent service during the duty period, he was granted two pieces of silver as a reward.

One: In the fourth month of Kyōhō 19, the year of Tora (1734), he was appointed to oversee the construction and repair works (御普請) at the lord's mausoleum (院内御墳), and for this he was granted one piece of silver as a reward.

One: In the eleventh month of the same year, he was granted the former residence plot of Hara Yasuemon (原安右衛門) as a reassignment of property.
