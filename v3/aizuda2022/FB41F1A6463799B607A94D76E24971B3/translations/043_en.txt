One: In the 2nd month of Bunka [sic; Bunsei] 1 (1818), Year of the Tiger, because he had applied himself diligently to military drills and training, he was granted one spear with a new blade.

One: In the 2nd month of Bunsei 2 (1819), Year of the Rabbit, in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his duties as a hired retainer (o-yatoi) of the tozama [outside] domain.

One: In the 3rd month of the same year, while serving as a hired retainer of the tozama domain in Sagami Province (Sōshū), after having taken charge of the defensive position (o-sonaeba), foreign ships drifted into the area on multiple occasions. During the previous summer, [a foreign ship] came close to the waters off the domain's territory, and he was stationed at the gun battery as a large-cannon gunner and rendered distinguished service. For this, he was granted one bundle of cotton as a reward.

One: On the 2nd day of the 11th month of the same year [Bunsei 2, 1819], he died of illness at the age of 75. His posthumous Buddhist name was Shōsō Sōshu Koji. His place of burial was Okamura, Misaki, Sagami Province.
