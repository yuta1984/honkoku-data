Item: In the seventh month of the second year of the same era [Genroku 2, 1689], he was appointed to the position of Ohirishiki Metsuke (inspector of the inner palace).

Item: In the twelfth month of the eighth year of the same era [Genroku 8, 1695], he was awarded one ryō of gold as a reward for his dedicated efforts carried out single-handedly.

Item: On the fifth day of the fifth month of the tenth year of the same era [Genroku 10, 1697], he died of illness at the age of seventy. His posthumous Buddhist name was Sōen Nisshin; his burial place was Jōkōji Temple at Hōshōzan.
