[Daughter]　　　　　Wife of Suwa Yaemon Mitsatsugu (divorced).
　Mother: daughter of the Yamadera clan.　　　[Subsequently became] wife of Waragai Kichinosuke Naganori.

Tomoe Mitsuchin　　　　(Childhood name: Tsurukichi. Born on the 20th day of the 12th month of Genmon 3, the Year of the Horse [1738].)
　Mother: same as above.
　Wife: daughter of Kubota Yaheiji Yoshitaka. (Born on the 21st day of the 3rd month of Enkyō 2, the Year of the Ox [1745]. Died of illness on the 19th day of the 2nd month of Kyōwa 1, the Year of the Rooster [1801], at the age of fifty-seven. Posthumous Buddhist name: Shisō Myōshin Daishi. Place of burial: same as above.)

Item: In the 3rd month of Kan'en 1, the Year of the Dragon (1748), as requested by his father, he was, for the first time, granted an audience [with the lord] (o-memie).

Item: In the 9th month of Meiwa 5, the Year of the Rat (1768), as requested by his father, he was appointed as guard replacement (oban-dai) and served as castle guard (o-shiro o-ban) in the unit of Kayano Gonbei.

Item: In the 12th month of An'ei 1, the Year of the Dragon (1772), he was granted, without any discrepancy, the inherited stipend (atoshiki go-chigyō) of one hundred koku.

Item: At the beginning of the 2nd month of the 7th year of the same era, the Year of the Dog (1778), as Kayano Gonbei retired (inkyo), his subsequent post became that of the unit of Harada Ihei.

Item: At the beginning of the 11th month of the 9th year of the same era, the Year of the Rat (1780), as Harada Ihei died of illness, his subsequent post became that of the unit of Takahashi Koemon.
