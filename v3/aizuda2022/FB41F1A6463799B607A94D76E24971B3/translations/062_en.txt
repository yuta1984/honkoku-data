Item: Granted ten *koku* and provisions for two servants (*ninin-fuchi*), and summoned to serve as a *Nakanokuchi-ban* (Middle Gate Guard).

Item: In the fifth month of Meiwa 3, the year of the Dog (1766), appointed to the position of *Hiroji Yakunin* (land surveyor/local official) on a temporary hired basis (*tōza oyatoi*).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same [Meiwa] 7, the year of the Tiger (1770), appointed to the position of *Hiroji Yakunin* on a permanent hired basis (*sadame yatoi*).

Item: In the same month, at the time of the conversion of tax exemptions (*menshō kirikae*), on account of having handled the assessment of the crop yield (*sakamo no mikakari*) appropriately and satisfactorily, granted one *bu* of gold as a reward.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same [Meiwa] 9, the year of the Dragon (1772), on the same grounds as above, granted one *bu* of gold.

Item: In the twelfth month of Anei 7, the year of the Dog (1778), appointed to the position of *Hiroji Yakunin*.

Item: In the sixth month of Tenmei 1, the year of the Ox (1781), appointed to the position of minor official (*ko-yakunin*) at the *Edo jōzume* Accounts Office (*Go-Kanjōsho*) [i.e., the permanent Edo posting of the domain's financial office].

Item: In the third month of the same [Tenmei] 8, the year of the Monkey (1788), appointed to the provisional post (*kariyaku*) of *Go-Kanjō Aratame-yaku* (Accounts Inspection Officer).

Item: In the fifth month of the same year, on account of having diligently and capably performed his duties on ordinary days, granted one *ryō* of gold as a reward.
