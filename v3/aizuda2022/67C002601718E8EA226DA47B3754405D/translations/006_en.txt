Upon the passing of Lord Taiyūin [Tokugawa Iemitsu],
　　　when Lord Hoshina [Masayuki] made a pilgrimage to Nikkō,
　　　[the retainer] was ordered to accompany him and duly performed his duties.

One entry: Eighth month of the first year of Jōō, Year of the Dragon (1652)
　　　[The retainer] was summoned before Lord Hoshina, granted a stipend of 200 koku,
　　　performed his coming-of-age ceremony (genpuku),
　　　and was ordered to enter into service under Lord Iwahiko [Hoshina Masamoto].
　　　He duly performed close attendance service to Lord Iwahiko.

One entry: Ninth month of the third year of the same era [Jōō], Year of the Horse (1654)
　　　In the presence of Lord Hoshina, he was granted an additional stipend of 50 koku.

One entry: Sixth month of the first year of Meireki, Year of the Sheep (1655)
　　　When Lord Iwahiko made his first visit down to Aizu,
　　　[the retainer] accompanied him to Aizu, and in the tenth month of the same year,
　　　upon Lord Iwahiko's attendance at the Shogunal court [in Edo],
　　　[the retainer] accompanied him back to Edo.

One entry: Third month of the third year of the same era [Meireki], Year of the Rooster (1657)
　　　Lord Iwahiko passed away, and as his remains were
　　　conveyed down to Aizu, [the retainer] accompanied [the procession] to Aizu.
　　　After the funeral was completed, upon returning to Edo,
　　　[the retainer] was assigned to the Permanent Garrison Tozama Unit (常詰外様組), [stationed at] Seki...
