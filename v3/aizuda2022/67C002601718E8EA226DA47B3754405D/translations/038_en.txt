He was assigned to the unit of Yamazaki Sasuke and performed guard duty at the castle.

One entry: In the twelfth month of the same seventh year (1787), the year of the Goat, as his younger brother had presented game birds caught by falconry, he was granted three hiki of cotton as a reward.

One entry: In the tenth month of Kansei 1 (1789), the year of the Rooster, his unit commander Yamazaki Sasuke was relieved of his post, and he was consequently reassigned to the unit of Kajiwara Samon.

One entry: In the ninth month of the same fifth year (1793), the year of the Ox, he went out for the "otoridgari" (a large-scale organized bird hunt) and presented his catch as an offering, for which he received a gift in return.

　　　Note: After this, he participated six more times.

One entry: In the fourth month of the same sixth year (1794), the year of the Tiger, regarding the previous autumn's otoridgari, it was noted that the formation and ranks of his unit were well-coordinated, and this was deemed to be a result of diligent private practice beforehand; accordingly, the entire unit was commended.

　　　Note: After this, the unit received commendations on every occasion.

One entry: In the ninth month of the same seventh year (1795), the year of the Rabbit, as he had consistently dedicated himself to spear (yari) technique practice from the year of the Rat through the year of the Tiger, he was granted one bundle (taba) of cotton as a reward...
