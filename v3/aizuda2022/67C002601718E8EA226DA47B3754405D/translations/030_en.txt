**Jūrōemon Yasukimi** (first name: Gensaburō; born in the seventh month of the sixth year of Kyōhō, the year of the Ox [1721])

Mother: Same as above (as previously noted)

Wife: Daughter of Narita Tamon Tadakiyo

Item: In the second month of the fourth year of Hōreki, the year of the Dog (1754), having previously been adopted into the household of Narita Tamon, [his elder brother] Gennosuke died of illness. In accordance with his father's petition, he was permitted to return to his birth family and was designated as the legitimate heir.

Item: In the intercalary second month of the same year, in accordance with his father's petition, he traveled up to Edo and was granted his first formal audience [with the lord].

Item: In the fourth month of the same year, he was granted the inherited stipend of 600 koku without any reduction, was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit), placed under the command of Hyūga Gen'emon's unit, and took up duties as a castle guard.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for rotational duty (kinban), and returned in the fourth month of the following year of the Boar (1755).

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, he changed his name to Jūrōemon.
