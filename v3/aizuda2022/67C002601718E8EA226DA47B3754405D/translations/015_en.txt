[Continued from the previous page] —having performed said duties, and having performed them with distinction in the lord's presence, in warm personal regard (gokon), he was presented with one御羽織 (formal jacket), one御帷子 (summer ceremonial robe), and additionally, on an informal occasion, a rinzu (figured satin) 御肩衣 (sleeveless formal vest) and a single-layer hakama (formal divided skirt); beyond these, he was also appointed to receive the regular ceremonial gifts (jōwa no hairyōmono), and he returned to the domain in the ninth month of the same year.

Item: In the ninth month of the fifth year of Shōtoku (1715, Year of the Sheep), he traveled up to Edo as a resident duty guard during the lord's absence (orusu kinban), and returned in the third month of the following Year of the Monkey (1716, first year of Kyōhō).
　　　However, he was appointed to receive the regular ceremonial gifts (jōwa no hairyōmono).

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, he was granted exemption from restrictions on the use of a palanquin (kago) in Edo.

Item: In the eighth month of the second year of Kyōhō (1717, Year of the Rooster), he was appointed General Superintendent (sōji) for the festival ceremonies at Minesan (Mineya-san).

Item: In the ninth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo as a resident duty guard during the lord's absence (orusu kinban), and returned in the third month of the same [following] year.
　　　However, he was appointed to receive the regular ceremonial gifts (jōwa no hairyōmono).

Item: In the sixth month of the third year of Kyōhō (1718, Year of the Dog), he submitted a petition to resign from office on account of his advanced age; [in response, it was noted that] there had been no delays whatsoever in his official duties, that he had attended to matters with great care and diligence, and that he had served well— [continued on the next page].
