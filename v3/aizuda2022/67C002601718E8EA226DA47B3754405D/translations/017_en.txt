He was granted an increase of one hundred koku, and since he had reached old age, it was proclaimed in the lord's presence that he should take good care of himself while continuing his duties.

Item: In the third month of the eleventh year of Bunsei (1828), the Year of the Horse, his request to be relieved of his current post was granted, and in accordance with his private petition, he was immediately ordered into retirement. The family stipend of six hundred koku of assessed land was duly transferred without change to his son Jūrōemon. Furthermore, it was proclaimed that, in recognition of years of diligent and exemplary service, and given that Jūrōemon himself had served since his time as a page (o-koshō), out of paternal consideration for father and son, Jūrōemon was appointed to the post of o-tsukaiban (messenger official).

Item: On the sixth day of the twelfth month of the following year, the twelfth year of Bunsei (1829), the Year of the Ram, he died of illness at the age of seventy-two. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Chisei-in Kōyo Kaien Koji. Place of burial: same as above (as previously recorded).
