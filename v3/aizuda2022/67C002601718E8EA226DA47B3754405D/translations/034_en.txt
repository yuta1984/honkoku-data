One: In the intercalary second month of the fourth year of Hōreki (1754), the year of the Dog, [he] was granted a provisional stipend (gigi-saku) of eight ryō and four-man rations at the standard level, and was summoned into service as an o-koshō (page/attendant).
　　　Note: He served as an escort on each occasion of the lord's journey to and from Edo (sankin-kōtai).

One: In the tenth month of the ninth year of the same era (1759), the year of the Rabbit, he was granted half-coming-of-age (han-genpuku) as requested.

One: In the third month of the twelfth year of the same era (1762), the year of the Horse, he was granted full coming-of-age (hon-genpuku) as requested, and was directed to continue serving as an o-koshō as before.

One: In the eighth month of the seventh year of Meiwa (1770), the year of the Tiger, he was appointed as the adopted son of his elder brother Jūrōemon, in accordance with the elder brother's petition.

One: In the ninth month of the same year, he was granted his first formal audience with the lord (o-memie), in accordance with the petition of his [adoptive] parent.

One: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was granted the inherited stipend of 600 koku without alteration, was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit), was placed in the unit of Kasuya Gozaemon, and took up duty as a castle guard (o-shiro o-ban).

One: In the ninth month of the eighth year of the same era (1771), the year of the Rabbit, he traveled up to Edo as an escort for the lord's sankin-kōtai, serving as a kinban (rotating duty attendant).
