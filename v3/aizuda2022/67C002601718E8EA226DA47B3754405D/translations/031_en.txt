Item: In the ninth month of the ninth year of the same era, the year of the Rabbit (1759), he was appointed as a *monomgashira* (unit commander).

Item: In the first month of the tenth year of the same era, the year of the Dragon (1760), he took custody of the detained person Kajiwara Denkurō from Hyūga Saburōemon, and in the second month of the same year, transferred him to the next duty officer, Baba Zuisho.

Item: In the fifth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for an unscheduled rotational duty (*fuji kinban*), and in the eleventh month of the same year, was assigned to escort duty for the shogunal document (*gohanmotsu keigo*), after which he returned.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, it was officially declared that he had served with great diligence and hardship in the escort duty for the shogunal document.

Item: In the fourth month of the twelfth year of the same era, the year of the Horse (1762), he took custody of the detained person Yoshikawa Takiemon from Andō Kuzaemon, and in the fifth month of the same year, as the person was ordered to be banished to another domain, he was handed over to the *kōjisho* (judicial office).

Item: In the ninth month of the first year of Meiwa, the year of the Monkey (1764), he was appointed to the duty of receiving Yamagata Castle in Ushū (Dewa Province).
