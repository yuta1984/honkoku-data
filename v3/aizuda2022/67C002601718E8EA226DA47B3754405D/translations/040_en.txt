...(dispatched for Ezochi garrison duty during the previous spring,) he traveled by both sea and land routes, and diligently performed his duties for several days at that location. As the entire contingent of personnel returned from the campaign without exception, (his lord) was greatly pleased, and he was treated to a formal meal at the castle.

One entry: In the eighth month of the same sixth year (1809), the year of the Snake, as he had rendered distinguished service in the Ezochi garrison duty, he was granted one set of seasonal garments (jifuku) as a reward.

One entry: On the twenty-seventh day of the seventh month of the same twelfth year (1815), the year of the Boar, he died of illness at the age of fifty-two. His posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō) was Jikōin Gyōgen Ryōi Koji. His place of burial is the same as above.

　Daughter: Died of illness on the twenty-eighth day of the ninth month of Kansei 1 (1789), the year of the Rooster. Posthumous Buddhist name: Sōgen Dōjo. Place of burial: Ōkuboyama.

　　Mother: A woman of the Machida clan
