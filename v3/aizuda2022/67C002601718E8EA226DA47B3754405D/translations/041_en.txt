Kichijirō Yasuchika　　　Born on the fifth day of the fourth month of Kansei 6 (1794), the year of the Tiger.

　　　Mother: Same as above

　　　Wife: Daughter of Higuchi Kannojō Mitsoon　　Born on the fifth day of the third month of Kansei 11 (1799), the year of the Sheep.

One entry: In the eleventh month of Bunka 10 (1813), the year of the Rooster, it was officially proclaimed that he had attended the Bugakuryō (Military Studies Institute) for five years without a single absence, and had applied himself diligently to the martial arts.

One entry: In the second month of the same eleventh year (1814), the year of the Dog, he was appointed to the provisional position of assistant to the archery instructor (kyūjutsu shihan tedai-kin tōza kari-yaku), and served in this capacity until the ninth month of the same year.

One entry: In the ninth month of the same twelfth year (1815), the year of the Boar, as it was recognized that his ancestors had served (the domain) well, and that his late father Jūrōemon had never been remiss in his martial preparedness and was noted as a person of admirable character, on account of these various exceptional circumstances, the full stipend of twenty persons' worth (ninjin-bun) that had been granted to his late father was bestowed upon him without reduction. He was then summoned to serve in the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit) and performed castle guard duty (gojō goban) in the unit of Kajiwara Heima.
