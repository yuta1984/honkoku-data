[He] returned [to the domain] in the ninth month of the same year.
　　　However, he was presented with jōwa (regular ceremonial) gifts, though the specific items are unknown.

Item: In the tenth month of the second year of Shōtoku (1712, Year of the Dragon), when the lord's representative (ondairi) performed his duties at Nikkō, [the subject] was designated by personal appointment (ohito-sashi) to serve as an escort and was additionally appointed to concurrently serve as御用人 (personal secretary/chamberlain), which duties he duly performed.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, in the lord's presence, in gracious recognition of his many years of distinguished service, and with warm personal regard (gokon), an increase of one hundred koku was bestowed upon him.

Item: In the eleventh month of the third year of Shōtoku (1713, Year of the Snake), in the lord's presence, in recognition of his many years of distinguished service, he was appointed to the position of bugyō (commissioner/magistrate); furthermore, he was subsequently appointed to countersign official documents in the御次 (anteroom/waiting room), and it was decreed that he would receive dōshin-ryō (a stipend for subordinate staff).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, it was decreed that he was granted exemption from the restrictions on carrying a matoi (standard) and uma-jirushi (horse insignia), and furthermore was granted permission to use a palanquin (norimono) within Aizu.

Item: In the fifth month of the fourth year of Shōtoku (1714, Year of the Horse), in connection with the celebratory banquet for the Shōgun's investiture (senge), he was appointed General Superintendent (sōji) and traveled up to Edo, where he undertook said duties [continued on the next page].
