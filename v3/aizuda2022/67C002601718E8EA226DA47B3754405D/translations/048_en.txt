**[Left folio]**

One entry: In the twelfth month of the seventh year of Kanbun, the Year of the Sheep (1667), in accordance with his father's petition, he was appointed as a guard replacement (o-ban-dai) and assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit), entering the unit of Segi Saburōzaemon, and performed castle guard duty.

One entry: In the third month of the eighth year of the same era, the Year of the Monkey (1668), he was granted the hereditary stipend (atoshiki go-chigyō) of 450 koku without any discrepancy.

One entry: In the ninth month of the same year, he went up to Edo for his rotation of duty (kinban), and returned [to Aizu] in the third month of the following Year of the Rooster (1669).

One entry: At the beginning of the seventh month of the tenth year of the same era, the Year of the Dog (1670), Segi Saburōzaemon was relieved of his post in accordance with his petition, and the successor unit came under the command of Jinbō Mozaemon.
