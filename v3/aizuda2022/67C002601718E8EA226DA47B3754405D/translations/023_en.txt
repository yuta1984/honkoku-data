　One entry: In the 3rd month of the 11th year [of Kyōhō], the Year of the Horse, he was granted his family headship (katoku) with an inherited stipend of 600 koku, without any reduction. This was because his father Genbei had served admirably over many years, and Jūrōemon himself had also served well during his time as o-koshō (page/attendant). In recognition of the merit of both father and son, it was declared that he was to be appointed as o-tsukai-ban (messenger/envoy official).

　One entry: In the 10th month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for a period of duty rotation (kinban); and in the 4th month of the following Year of the Goat, on the occasion of the lord's return to the domain, he served in attendance and traveled back [to Aizu].

　One entry: In the 4th month of the 12th year [of Kyōhō], the Year of the Goat, it was declared that, this being his first campaign/deployment (shojin), he had applied himself diligently and served admirably.

　One entry: In the 4th month of the 14th year [of Kyōhō], the Year of the Rooster, he traveled up to Edo for a period of duty rotation, and returned [to Aizu] in the 10th month of the same year.

　One entry: In the 5th month of the 16th year [of Kyōhō], the Year of the Boar, he was ordered to serve as an envoy (o-tsukai) to the Shogunate and traveled up to Edo. However, the roads along the way were struck by severe flooding, making it extremely difficult to arrive within the scheduled number of days. Nevertheless, exerting every effort, he managed to arrive within the prescribed schedule...
