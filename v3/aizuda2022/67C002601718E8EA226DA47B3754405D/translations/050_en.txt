**[Left folio]**

- In the eleventh month of Jōkyō 4 (1687), the year of the Rabbit, he was granted the inherited stipend of 450 koku of territory without dispute, was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit), placed in the unit of Hori Han'emon, and served as castle guard.

- In the ninth month of Genroku 1 (1688), the year of the Dragon, the unit commander Hori Han'emon was granted retirement as requested, and consequently the unit was reorganized under the command of Jinbō Mozaemon.

- In the ninth month of Genroku 2 (1689), the year of the Snake, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned in the third month of the following year of the Horse (1690).
