One: In the same month, he went up to Edo as part of the regular duty rotation (kinban), performed one tour of duty, and returned.

One: In the second month of Jōkyō 2 (the Year of the Ox, 1685), in connection with the confinement/custody of Lord Matsudaira Shuri, he went up to Edo as an escort party. In the same month, he served as a guard for Lord Shuri's journey and returned.

One: In the eighth month of the same year, following the death of Lord Shuri, he went up to Edo, was immediately summoned before [his lord], who inquired about the circumstances [of the death]. That same night, Lord Abe Bungo-no-Kami was sent [to him], and he reported in full detail the circumstances of Lord Shuri's death. In the same month, he was granted leave and returned. Regarding the management of Lord Shuri's remaining affairs, instructions in the form of a written directive were conveyed to Yanase Sanzaemon.

One: In the tenth month of the same year, he went up to Edo as part of the regular duty rotation, performed one tour of duty, and returned.

One: In the first month of Jōkyō 3 (the Year of the Tiger, 1686), he was appointed as Junior Group Leader (ko-bangashira).

One: In the seventh month of the fourth year [of Jōkyō] (the Year of the Rabbit, 1687), his petition was granted and he was relieved of his official post.
