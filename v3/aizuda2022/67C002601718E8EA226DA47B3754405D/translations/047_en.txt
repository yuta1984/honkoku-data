(Continued from previous page) He served as the first unit (ichijin) and upon descending [to Aizu] in the eighth month of the same year, upon his petition, he was relieved of his post and was appointed Head of the Rifle Corps (Teppō-gashira). He was then immediately dispatched as a messenger to Yonezawa, carried out the assignment, and submitted the reply in writing from Aizu.

One entry: In the second year of Kanbun, the Year of the Tiger (1662), when he went up to Edo for his rotation of duty (kinban), he was appointed by Lady Hōshō-in as a messenger to convey New Year's greetings for the Hassaku festival. He went up [to Edo], directly served in the first unit, and then returned [to Aizu].

One entry: In the sixth year of the same era, the Year of the Horse (1666), in the sixth month, in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his post and assigned to the Yoriai-gumi (reserve/retired retainer group).

One entry: In the seventh year of the same era, the Year of the Sheep (1667), in the eleventh month, in accordance with his petition, his son Tahei was appointed in his place for guard duty (o-ban-dai).

One entry: On the third day of the twelfth month of the same year, he died of illness at the age of sixty-five. Posthumous Buddhist name: Chūdō-in Sessō Nisshin Koji. Place of burial: Ryūkoku-zan Hokke-ji Temple.

　Daughter: Wife of Maruyama Yajirōemon Shigenori

　　　Mother: Daughter of the Obara clan
