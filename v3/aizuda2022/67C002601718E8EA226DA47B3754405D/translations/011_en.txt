One: In the fifth month of the fourth year [of Enpō] (the Year of the Dragon, 1676), he was granted his coming-of-age ceremony (genpuku), and was appointed to the post of Tsunezume Otomo-ban (permanent-duty escort guard).

One: In the tenth month of the seventh year [of Enpō] (the Year of the Sheep, 1679), upon his father's petition, his wife and children relocated and traveled down to Aizu, whereupon he also traveled down to Aizu together with his father, and alternately served in the escort guard (otomo-ban) duty.

One: In the third month of Tenna 1 (the Year of the Rooster, 1681), in connection with the celebratory banquet for the shogunal investiture, he was designated by name [for service] and was appointed to accompany an okoshō (page) along the road as he traveled up to Edo. Upon arriving, he immediately commenced his duty rotation (kinban), and traveled back down [to Aizu] in the tenth month of the same year.

One: In the tenth month of Jōkyō 2 (the Year of the Ox, 1685), he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), whereupon he was appointed to the post of Otodaban (gate guard), and traveled back down [to Aizu] in the fourth month of the following Year of the Tiger (Jōkyō 3, 1686).
　　Note: He was granted one set of formal ceremonial garments (kamishimo) for service in the field/on duty.
