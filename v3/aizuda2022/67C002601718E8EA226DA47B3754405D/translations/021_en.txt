　One entry: In the 4th month of the 5th year [of Hōei], the Year of the Rat, on the occasion of the lord's sankin-kōtai journey, he traveled up [to Edo] as part of the advance guard; however, in the 1st month of the following Year of the Ox, he fell ill with smallpox, lost his hair, and found it difficult to continue his duties; therefore, upon his petition, he was permitted to return to Aizu.

　One entry: In the 4th month of the 6th year [of Hōei], the Year of the Ox, in accordance with his petition, he was granted a half-genpuku ceremony as ordered.
