Genbei Yasumitsu 〔First known as Kajinojō, then as Jūrōemon. Born in the seventh month of Meireki 2 (the Year of the Monkey, 1656), in Edo.〕

　　　Mother: Daughter of the Miyaji clan

　　　Wife: Daughter of Miyake Kuemon Munetika

One: In the fourth month of Kanbun 11 (the Year of the Tiger, 1671), he was granted eight ryō in gold and a stipend for four retainers, and was summoned into service as a page (okoshō).

One: In the fifth month of the twelfth year [of Kanbun] (the Year of the Rat, 1672), in connection with Lord Hoshina's journey down to Aizu, he accompanied him and traveled down [to Aizu], and in the ninth month of the same year, he accompanied him and traveled back up [to Edo].
