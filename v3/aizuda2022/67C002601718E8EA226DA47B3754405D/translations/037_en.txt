Biological father: Eldest son of Jūroemon Yasukimi

　　　　　Wife: Daughter of Machida Shichirojūemon Takakatsu

　　　　　　　Daughter of Ariga Hachirōdayū Mitsusato

One entry: In the first month of An'ei 1 (1772), the year of the Dragon, as his father Kanji had died young, his stipend of 600 koku was confiscated, and he was granted 20 persons' worth of rations (ninsoku fuchi) and called into service as an outside-rank retainer (kumagai no shi).

One entry: In the twelfth month of the same third year (1774), the year of the Horse, he was granted the residence previously held by Harada Ritetsu.

One entry: In the eighth month of Tenmei 3 (1783), the year of the Rabbit, his request was granted, and he was granted his first formal audience (omemie) with the lord.

One entry: In the third month of the same fourth year (1784), the year of the Dragon, as he expressed deep remorse for having performed no service of any kind, his petition requesting that he be assigned to suitable duties was granted.
