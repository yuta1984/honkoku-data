Item: In the second month of the fifteenth year of Genroku (1702), he was appointed Ō-metsuke (Senior Inspector) in the presence of the lord.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, he was summoned on official business, traveled up to Edo, completed his duties, and upon his return, was presented with one kosode (short-sleeved kimono).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was appointed to oversee the kogoshin (samurai on reserve/inactive duty).

Item: In the third month of the first year of Hōei (1704, Year of the Monkey), in the lord's presence, it was graciously acknowledged that his diligent and tireless daily attendance at the meeting hall (kaisho) had been a source of hardship; in recognition of this, he was graciously presented with one御召 (御召羽織, a formal haori jacket belonging to the lord).

Item: In the first month of the second year of Hōei (1705, Year of the Rooster), he was appointed Head of the Pages (Okoshō-gashira) and Supervisor of the Horse Affairs (Oumakatashahai).

Item: In the third month of the same year, he traveled up [to Edo] for his tour of duty (kinban), and returned in the ninth month of the same year.
　　　However, he was presented with jōwa (regular/standard ceremonial) gifts, though the specific items are unknown.
