(Continued from previous page) He traveled up to Edo and returned home in the ninth month of the same year.

One item: In the ninth month of the third year of the same era, the Year of the Boar (Enkyō 3, 1746), he traveled up to Edo for routine duty (kinban). In the eleventh month of the same year, he was ordered to serve in the role of official in charge of affairs related to the wedding ceremony of Lady Baien-in, scheduled for the fourth month of the following year. He was therefore ordered to remain in Edo through the following year and to return home after the wedding ceremony was completed.

One item: In the fifth month of the first year of Enkyō, the Year of the Rat (1744), having applied himself diligently and worked hard in all matters pertaining to Lady Baien-in's wedding ceremony and performed his duties admirably, he was rewarded with five pieces of silver and one box of ceremonial fish (o-sakana). Furthermore, he was summoned to the lord's presence, received gracious words of commendation, and was presented with a set of formal attire (kamishimo). Upon taking his leave, he was again graciously commended for his diligence, hard work, and excellent service in all matters, and he returned home in the same month.

One item: In the eleventh month of the same year, due to illness, his official duties were pardoned as requested, and he was assigned to a position without official role (muryaku).

One item: In the second month of the same year, his seat ranking was listed as next after Nagai Asaemon (record continues).
