The aforementioned matter was so decreed.

One: In September of the same year, he was dispatched to Edo as an extraordinary (futoji) duty rotation, completed one tour of service, and returned home.

One: In the second month of the eighteenth year, the Year of the Ox (Kyōhō 18, 1733), having performed his duties in the service of the Domain Inspector (Okuni-metsuke) group satisfactorily, he was granted a formal gift of upper and lower garments (kamishimo) and silver.

One: In the fifth month of the same year, he was appointed to the position of Sōja-ban (Master of Ceremonies/Announcer).

One: In the ninth month of the same year, he departed for Edo as part of the duty rotation; however, due to illness, he petitioned and was permitted to return home in the twelfth month of the same year.

One: In the twelfth month of the nineteenth year, the Year of the Tiger, he was appointed to the position of O-kachi Shihai (Supervisor of Foot Soldiers).

One: In the intercalary third month of the twentieth year, the Year of the Rabbit, he was dispatched to Edo as an extraordinary duty rotation, and returned home in the ninth month of the same year.

One: In the third month of Genbun 1, the Year of the Dragon (1736), he was appointed to the position of O-mochi-yumi-gashira (Head of the Bow Corps).

One: In the ninth month of the same year, he was appointed to the position of Ō-ometsuke (Senior Inspector/Chief Censor).
