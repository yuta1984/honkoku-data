[Missions were carried out as messenger to] the Sentō Palace (retired emperor's residence), the In'yō-sama (the retired empress/imperial consort), the princes and princesses, as well as the Osaka Castle Deputy (城代) and the castle garrison officers.

One: In the seventh year of the same era (Enpō 7, the Year of the Goat), in the tenth month, he petitioned to bring his wife and children to Aizu. The petition was granted, and he brought his wife and children down to Aizu. However, he was soon ordered to return to Edo, and did so in the same month.

One: In the eighth year (the Year of the Monkey), in the ninth month, while in Edo, he was granted an increase in stipend of fifty koku. In the tenth month of the same year, he was granted leave and returned to Aizu.

One: In the tenth year of the same era [Tenna 2] (the Year of the Dog), in the third month, in celebration of the Shogun's investiture proclamation, and in connection with an invitation extended to the Senior Councillors (rōjū), he was ordered to go up to Edo to perform said duty. He did so and returned in the tenth month of the same year.

One: In the twelfth month of the same year, having been assigned to fire-watch duty, he was promptly ordered to go up to Edo. He did so, and returned in the fourth month of the following Year of the Boar (Tenna 3).
