**Genealogy of Obara Kyūtarō**　[Surname: Kiyohara　Province of Origin: Shinano]　[Standard Family Crest: Suhama (sandbar design)　Alternate Crest: Ishimochi with Gosan-no-Kiri (paulownia)　Curtain Crest: Same as above]

Second son of Obara Gorōzaemon Toshinobu:
　Obara Kurōemon Toshiyasu　[Original childhood name: Kyūtarō; after retirement, took the Buddhist name Jishō. Born in the year of the Tiger, Keian 3 (1650).]

　　　Mother: Daughter of Miyake Magobei Shigenao
　　　First Wife: Second daughter of Miyake Magobei Shigenji　[Died of illness on the 9th day of the 10th month, Enpō 7 (1679), the year of the Sheep, at age 16. Posthumous Buddhist name: Shakuni Shōtei. Place of burial: A temple in Edo, details unknown.]
　　　Same person (second marriage): Third daughter of the same man　[Died of illness on the 14th day of the 1st month, Genroku 7 (1694), the year of the Dog, at age 26. Posthumous Buddhist name: Honshūin Shōyo Myoshin. Officiating priest: From Hōshōzan Jōkōji Temple. Place of burial: Ōkuboyama.]

One entry: In the 3rd year of Kanbun (1663), the year of the Rabbit [month unknown], he was granted a stipend of the rank of "Narai no Ogisaku" (a standard allotment of land/rice).
