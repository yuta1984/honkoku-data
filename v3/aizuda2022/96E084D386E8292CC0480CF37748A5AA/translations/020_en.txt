One item: In the eighth month of the same year (Hōreki 6, 1756), as requested, he was relieved of his duties and was ordered to be attached to the Aizu Katte-tozama-gumi (outside duty group). He returned [to Aizu] and was placed in the unit of Takahashi Koemon, where he performed castle guard duty.

One item: In the eighth month of Meiwa 2, the year of the Rooster (1765), the unit commander (kashira) Takahashi Koemon was appointed to the position of wakadoshiyori (junior elder), and [the subject] was consequently transferred to the unit of Kasano Gonbei.

One item: In the seventh month of the same seventh year, the year of the Tiger (1770), he was appointed to the post of onna-kitte-yaku (women's pass officer).

【Left page】

One item: In the sixth month of Anei 5, the year of the Monkey (1776), as requested, he was relieved of his post as onna-kitte-yaku, and in accordance with his private petition, he was immediately ordered to retire (inkyo).

One item: On the twenty-eighth day of the third month of the sixth year of the same era, the year of the Rooster (1777), he died of illness at the age of seventy-eight. His posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō) was Shōshin-in Henka Ichiyō Koji. The officiant monk (dōshi) was from Jūtokuzan Jōnen-ji temple. His place of burial was Ōkuboyama.
