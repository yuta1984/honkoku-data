When foreign ships sailed into the offshore area near the beach, he set out to perform guard duty. On the 21st day, as the foreign ships were ordered to return, he withdrew to Kannonsaki on the 22nd day.

One item: In June of the same year, regarding the swift preparations he had made when a foreign ship was sighted off the coast of Bōshū the previous autumn, he was informed that this matter would be duly reported to the higher authorities through the appropriate channel.

One item: In the third month of the second year of Bunsei (1819), the Year of the Rabbit, following the assumption of responsibility for Sagami Province, foreign ships drifted in on multiple occasions, and during the previous summer they came into the offshore waters of the domain territory for the first time. He promptly made preparations and

[Left folio]
set out by ship, performing guard duties for several days, and for his diligent service he was rewarded with two bundles of cotton.

One item: In the fourth month of the same year, given that the Sagami coastal area was an important defensive post, and since he was serving as a hired retainer (o-yatoi) among the samurai, ranked in the line of armored warriors (kōshi), it was ordered through a special arrangement that he be granted an audience (o-mie) at the appropriate occasion, and furthermore that henceforth, during the lord's residence in Edo, he should appear in rotation, one or two persons at a time, for the New Year's greetings.
