1. In the 3rd month of Bunsei 1 (Tora year, 1818), in recognition of his diligent and devoted efforts at military drills and training (*kurineri*), he was presented with a new spear (*mi no yari, issui*—one new spearhead) as a reward.

2. On the 13th day of the 5th month of the same year, while he was on duty at the Hiraneyama coastal battery (*o-daiba*), a foreign vessel intruded as far as the waters off Kurihama—within the domain's jurisdiction (*go-ryōbun*)—during the night of that same day. Accordingly, from his assigned post at Hiraneyama, he remained on site without delay, deployed vessels to guard against the foreign ship, and on the 21st day, upon being ordered to stand down following the foreign vessel's departure, he withdrew to the Hiraneyama garrison (*o-jinya*) and on the 26th of the same month vacated the post entirely.

**[Left folio]**

3. In the 6th month of the same year, he was informed that the prompt and well-prepared response by all personnel during the previous autumn's sighting of a foreign vessel off the coast of Awa Province (*Bōshū*) would be reported to the lord at a suitable opportunity.

4. On the 9th day of the 7th month of the same year, an urgent report (*chūshin*) arrived stating that a foreign vessel had been sighted off Shirahama in Awa Province (*Bōshū*). As the current year-duty officer (*nenban*) at Hiraneyama, he immediately rushed to the location, and on the 11th day withdrew from his post.
