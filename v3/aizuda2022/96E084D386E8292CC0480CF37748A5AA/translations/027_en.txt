【Right page】

　　Out of the lord's personal benevolence, he was granted one set of formal hakama and kamishimo made of old hemp cloth (go-koma).

One item: In the fourth month of Meiwa 4, the year of the Boar (1767), due to the lord's return journey to the domain (go-gekō), he served in attendance and traveled down ahead of the lord's procession from along the road.

One item: In the ninth month of the same year, regarding a petition (negai) submitted by peasants (hyakushō) that involved complicated matters, he served as intermediary (o-toritsugi) without any errors; the petition was referred to the relevant authority for investigation (ginmi), and it was approved as requested. Furthermore, it was declared [by the lord] that the contents of what he had reported and the findings of the investigation by the relevant authority were in complete agreement, and that he had carried out his role as intermediary in an exemplary manner.

One item: In the tenth month of the same year, regarding matters of internal official business (go-naiyō), on multiple occasions

【Left page】

　　he was dispatched to rural villages as well, staying overnight on two occasions and exerting great effort; for this, he was granted two bundles of cotton wadding.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was entrusted with various official duties and worked diligently despite the great volume of tasks, even serving through sleepless nights; as a reward, he was granted three silver coins (ginsu san-mai) and two lengths of silk fabric (kinu ni-hiki).

One item: In the same month, he was appointed to the provisional role of Oku-ban (inner guard duty).
　　　Note: After this, he was appointed to this role on multiple occasions and carried out his duties accordingly.

One item: In the same month, [it was determined that] one person from among the pages (o-koshō) should be posted during the lord's absence; accordingly, [beginning] during the following month, Sasahara Yoichirō [was to serve in this capacity—text continues on next page].
