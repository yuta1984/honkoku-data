Note: During the period of the aforementioned service, various items were granted on multiple occasions from the lord's private storehouse (o-nando-barai), including a formal haori (short coat) bearing the family crest, a formal kamishimo (ceremonial vest and hakama) with lining, and various other garments bearing the family crest, among other items.

Item: In the sixth month of the fifth year of An'ei, the Year of the Monkey (An'ei 5, 1776), he was granted without question one hundred koku of stipend as his inherited estate (katoku), and was left in a position without specific duties (muyaku) as before. The provisional stipend (gisaku) that had previously been granted to him was rescinded.

Item: In the eighth month of the ninth year of An'ei, the Year of the Rat (An'ei 9, 1780), he was appointed to the position of permanent attendant official (jōzume go-yōyaku) to Lady Gimyōin, and his rank was raised above that of the O-tsukaiban (Messenger/Inspector officials).

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, as the petition regarding Lady Gimyōin's adoption of an heir had been granted as requested, he was awarded one piece of silver (ginsu ichimai) from Lord Kyōtei as a congratulatory gift.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent service, he was granted a role stipend (yakuryō) of fifty koku.

Item: In the fifth month of the first year of Tenmei, the Year of the Ox (Tenmei 1, 1781), in recognition of his fulfillment of duties related to the lord's procession (o-nori-dashi) without delay, he was granted one set of formal kamishimo made of hemp (o-furu-asa o-kamishimo) by Lady Gimyōin.
