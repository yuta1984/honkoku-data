One item: In the fifth month of the same year (1765), when the lord made his return journey to the domain (go-gekō), [the lord] visited and paid respects at Nikkō [Tōshōgū Shrine], and [he] served in attendance and traveled down [from Edo].

One item: In the tenth month of the same year, due to an unscheduled (fūji) alternate attendance journey (go-sankin) [to Edo], he served in attendance and traveled up [to Edo].

One item: In the fifth month of Meiwa 3, the year of the Dog (1766), even during his scheduled duty shifts (tōban), he was frequently required to remain behind (inokori) due to official duties, and as he had worked diligently on ordinary days as well, out of the lord's personal benevolence, he was granted one summer unlined garment (katabira).

One item: In the sixth month of the same year, on the occasion of the reception hosted for the Senior Councillors (rōjū),
