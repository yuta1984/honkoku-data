【Right page】

　　(In conjunction with the lord's entry into the domain,) he was ordered to accompany the advance guard page (o-saki-ban no o-koshō), and from the very day of the lord's arrival, he was appointed to the provisional role (kari-yaku) of o-zen-ban (dining service duty), which he performed throughout the lord's stay in the domain.
　　　　Note: In this regard, he was appointed to the said provisional role on multiple occasions, both in Edo and in Aizu.

One item: In the second month of Hōreki 10, the year of the Dragon (1760), due to an unexpected visit to Edo (fuji go-sanpu), he traveled up [to Edo] in attendance [of the lord].

One item: In the third month of the same year, he was ordered to accompany the lord on his visit to Kyoto (go-jōkyō), and traveled up [to Kyoto] accordingly.

One item: In the second month of Hōreki 11, the year of the Snake (1761), on the occasion of the lord's wedding ceremony and the invitation of the Senior Councillors (rōjū),

【Left page】

　　he received words of praise and commendation for his admirable guidance and supervision of the meal service arrangements.

One item: In the fourth month of the same year, he traveled down [to the domain] in attendance [of the lord] during the lord's journey to the domain (go-gekō).
　　　　Note: Throughout the lord's stay in the domain, he was appointed to the fixed provisional role (sadame-kari-yaku) of o-zen-ban.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, as his personal duties and responsibilities had been numerous and he had diligently carried out his daily service, recognizing his devoted labor, he was granted two bolts of silk (kinu nippiki) as a reward.

One item: In the fourth month of Hōreki 12, the year of the Horse (1762), he traveled up [to Edo] in attendance [of the lord] for the lord's alternate attendance duty (go-sankin).
