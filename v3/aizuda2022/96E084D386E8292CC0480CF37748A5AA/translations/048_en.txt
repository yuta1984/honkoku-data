Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, during the period of his prior duties, he diligently performed his service at the newly established station upon returning to Sagami Province (Sōshū). He distinguished himself particularly in the handling of unit management affairs, and also managed matters relating to the local subordinate domain personnel (jika-suji) with great care. Since he had performed his various concurrent and additional duties all with meticulous attention, he was awarded, as a mark of commendation, one bolt of silk (in lieu of a formal short-sleeved garment, o-kosode), together with cotton padding as supplement, and five pieces of silver.

Item: In the fifth month of the same eleventh year of the Dog [Bunka 12, 1815], since he had applied himself diligently to training in the craft of the helmsman (tomote), he was presented with sake as a reward.

Item: On the first day of the first month of the same thirteenth year of the Rat [Bunka 14, 1817], upon news that a foreign vessel had been sighted off the coast of Shimoda in Izu Province (Zushū), the unit's combined corps (kumi-yoriai-gumi) — apart from those on regular annual duty — gathered in full force at the Hiraneyama battery (o-daiba), withdrawing on the ninth day of the same month.

Item: In the first month of the same fourteenth year of the Ox [Bunka 15, 1818], recalling that the previous spring a foreign vessel had been sighted off the coast of Shimoda in Izu Province, he had remained stationed at Hiraneyama for several days and had managed affairs with full diligence in terms of general estimation and coordination. On account of this service, he was presented with sake as a reward.
