One item: In the second month of the first year of Genbun, the year of the Dragon (1736), he was appointed as an escort guard (otomo-ban).

One item: In the third month of the second year of the same era, the year of the Snake (1737), he traveled up to Edo as part of the duty service rotation (kinban) and completed one tour of duty before returning home.
　　Note: During the tour, on the occasion of a reception hosted by the Senior Councillors (Rōjū), he was ordered to serve [the lord] in the Small Audience Chamber (Ko-shoin), and was given a gratuity for formal attire (fukudai). Upon his return, it was formally announced that he had performed his duties during the tour diligently and without any absences, and thereafter he received commendation (shōbi) on every subsequent occasion of duty service.


【Left page】

One item: In the fourth month of the fourth year of the same era, the year of the Ram (1739), on the occasion of [the lord's] visit to the domain (o-gekō), he traveled up to Edo to serve as part of the welcoming party, and returned home as part of the lord's escort.
　　Note: It was formally announced that he had served in the traveling escort without any absences and with great diligence, and thereafter the same commendation was issued on every subsequent occasion.

One item: In the third month of the fifth year of the same era, the year of the Monkey (1740), he traveled up to Edo as an escort for the lord's alternate attendance (sankinkōtai) and as part of the duty service rotation, completed one tour of duty, and returned home.
　　Note: During the tour, he was appointed to the provisional post of gate guard (o-toban kari-yaku), and was subsequently appointed to the same role on numerous further occasions.
