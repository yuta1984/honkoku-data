However, the private training hall (taku-keikoba) was designated only for the training of second sons and below; should a student become an heir (chakushi), or succeed to another family name, he was required to present himself anew at the training hall within the Military Studies Institute (Bugakuryō) for his practice. At my own residence, I have been consulting with students regardless of whether they are heirs or younger sons; yet since the majority of my current disciples are heirs (chakushi), after they succeed to the family headship, when they present themselves at the residences of their respective superiors or at the martial training hall, and attend lectures and instruction sessions in connection with official duties, (the question arises whether) for private home training,

...they would need to formally enroll with the Military Affairs Magistrate (Gunjishōgyō) by way of a petition for home training. If that were to be the case, there would inevitably be additional costs incurred during their training, and of course it would render my instruction unnecessary. Therefore, I submitted a request that, with the exception of those who wished of their own accord to change their teacher, those who did not wish to do so should be permitted to continue their training under me even after taking up official duties.
