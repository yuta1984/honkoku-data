Item: In the third month of the second year of Tenmei, the Year of the Tiger (Tenmei 2, 1782), he was granted one set of old hemp formal kamishimo (o-furu-asa o-kamishimo) corresponding to the summer allotment from the previous year, and one old kosode (short-sleeved robe) corresponding to the winter allotment from the previous year.

Note: It had been customary to grant one set of hemp kamishimo twice a year, once in summer and once in winter. However, given that he had taken on additional duties and had continued to serve without interruption as a permanent attendant (jōzume), the kamishimo that should have been granted the previous winter was converted and granted as a kosode instead. Furthermore, it was decreed that henceforth, one of the two annual grants would be converted and provided as a kosode and a hitoe-style robe (katabira).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, by the gracious intention of Lady Senmyōin, he was granted one set of hemp formal kamishimo and one unlined haori (short coat).

Item: In the seventh month of the fourth year of Tenmei, the Year of the Dragon (Tenmei 4, 1784), by the gracious intention of the Go-yōnin (Senior Administrative Official), he was granted one set of hemp formal kamishimo.

Item: In the first month of the fifth year of Tenmei, the Year of the Snake (Tenmei 5, 1785), as he had no male heir, his petition to take Nagakichi, the younger brother of Ōnuma Gosaburō, as his son-in-law and adopted heir (muko-yōshi) was granted as requested.
