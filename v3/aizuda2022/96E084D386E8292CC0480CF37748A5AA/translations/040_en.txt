Item: In the second month of the second year of Kyōwa (享和二年), the year of Inu (戌), he was granted the reassignment of the borrowed residence (o-kashiage yashiki) formerly held by Kōsaka Kinai, located in Yoneshiro Sanchō (米代三之丁).

Item: In the sixth month of that same year, owing to the great volume of his current duties, he was relieved of his post as instructor of military science (heigaku shinan), as he had requested.

Item: In the eighth month of that same year, he traveled up to Edo (江戸) for his tour of duty (kinban), and in the third month of the following year of I (亥), he returned in attendance upon his lord's return journey (o-gekō).

Item: In the fifth month of that same year (year of I), during his lord's return journey, the allotment of men and horses along the road was reduced, which caused considerable hardship. Nevertheless, he carried out his duties with great diligence, and additionally performed extraordinary service in the distribution and coordination of men and horses for those in the escort party. For this, as a reward,

[Left folio]

...he was granted two bundles of cotton (wata).

Item: In the eighth month of that same year, during the toritoi-gari (bird-hunt drive), he went out to participate and succeeded in shooting down a bird; however, his horse was difficult to handle and he was unable to dismount. Noticing this, Nomura Chūjirō called out to him, and after declining the offer, he dismounted and retrieved the bird and handed it over. He subsequently reported this to the o-yūhitsu-sho (the official secretarial office) as a case of a shared catch (ai-uchi). The office deemed it a reasonable matter and indicated it would be reported to their lord at an appropriate occasion. However, it was communicated to him that it was not in fact a shared catch, but rather a contest won by a single individual (i.e., his own achievement alone).
