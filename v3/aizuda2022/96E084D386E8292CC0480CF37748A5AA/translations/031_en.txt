(Continued from previous page) He was appointed to oversee the wedding ceremony preparations for Lady Shundōin.
　　Note: In connection with the above, it was declared that he would be excused from his regular guard duties.

Item: In the fourth month of the same year (Bunsei 7, 1824), it was declared that he had performed all duties without negligence during the lord's absence, and he was awarded two bundles of cotton wadding (wata).

Item: In the fifth month of the same year, having completed his assigned duties, he departed and returned home.

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, having traveled up to Edo, it was declared that he would be granted a labor allowance (rōgin) at the rate of one piece (ichimai) per year.

Item: In the fifth month of the eighth year of Bunsei, the Year of the Rabbit (Bunsei 8, 1825), he was assigned an official duty and traveled up to Edo; he returned home in the tenth month of the same year.

Item: In the fourth month of the first year of An'ei, the Year of the Dragon (An'ei 1, 1772), he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and during occasions of the lord's visits to Edo Castle and inspection rounds, he was appointed as Assistant Sword Bearer (o-katana-ban joseki).

Item: In the first month of the second year of An'ei, the Year of the Snake (An'ei 2, 1773), as his services were no longer required, he was assigned to a position without specific duties (muyaku), and his rank was placed immediately after the Muyaku-gumi and above Yokoyama Kūhyōe. In recognition of his diligent service to date, he was granted one set of formal kamishimo made of hemp (o-furu-asa o-kamishimo).
