Rokkaku Takamei — Adopted son of Nakazawa Chūjirō Takanao

　　Mother: Same as above (as previously noted)

- In the 12th month of Kōwa 3, the Year of the Boar (1803): Having applied himself diligently to his studies and being reputed to demonstrate skill beyond his years, he was awarded a copy of the *Liji Guzhu* (Ancient Commentary on the Book of Rites) as a commendation.

- In the 2nd month of Bunka 9, the Year of the Monkey (1812): He was appointed as a *kudoku-shi* (reading instructor) at the domain academy (*gakkan*).

- In the 12th month of the same 10th year, the Year of the Rooster (1813): He was appointed as a temporary hired retainer (*tōza o-yatoi*) among the *tozama-shi* (outside/non-regular retainers) at Kannonsaki.

**[Left page]**

- In the 12th month of the same year: As the new coastal defense post (*o-sonaeba*) in Sagami Province had been newly established, having served diligently as a temporary hired retainer among the *tozama-shi*, he was awarded one bundle of cotton (*wata hitotaba*) as a commendation.

- In the 12th month of Bunka 11, the Year of the Dog (1814): He was reappointed as a *kudoku-shi* at the domain academy, and having applied himself diligently from the very beginning of his appointment and handled the students (*shosē*) well, he was awarded one bundle of cotton as a commendation.
