One item: In the second month of the same year (Hōreki 5, 1755), as Lady Kansō-in had passed away, he was ordered to serve under Hara Yahei, performing night watch duties (fushine-ban) and other tasks, and at the time of the funeral procession (御出棺), he carried out various official duties.

One item: In the third month of the same year, he was given Lady Kansō-in's used small-sleeved robes (kosode) and summer robes (katabira).

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, from Lady Rankō-in, he was given used small-sleeved robes (kosode) and summer robes (katabira).
