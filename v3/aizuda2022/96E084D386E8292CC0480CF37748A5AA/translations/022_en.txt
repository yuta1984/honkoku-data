【Right page】

　　Wife: Daughter of Ōizumi Gorōzaemon Taka《interlinear note: Died of illness on the sixth day of the ninth month of Kansei 3, the year of the Boar (1791). Posthumous Buddhist name: Emeiin Kyōichi Jukōkōshi. Place of burial: Shōshūji Temple, Azabu, Edo》

One item: In the first month of Kan'en 1, the year of the Dragon (1748), he was granted a stipend of eight ryō with rations for four persons (as a standard allocation), and was summoned to serve as a page (o-koshō).

One item: In the fourth month of the same year, on the occasion of the lord's journey to Edo (go-sanpu), he traveled up [to Edo] serving as an advance courier (o-saki-ban). In the fourth month of the following year of the Snake (1749), on the occasion of the lord's return [to Aizu], he traveled back [to the domain] serving as an advance courier.

One item: In the fourth month of Kan'en 3, the year of the Horse (1750), on the occasion of the lord's journey to Edo, he traveled up [to Edo] serving as an advance courier.

One item: In the sixth month of the same year, in accordance with his petition, he was granted a half-coming-of-age ceremony (han-genpuku).

【Left page】

　　(In the sixth month of the same year,) [he was] granted [a half-coming-of-age ceremony].

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, upon the passing of Lord Dojō [Matsudaira Katanobu], he was granted various remaining personal effects as keepsakes (go-imotsu).

One item: In the same month, as [the lord] was to remain permanently in Edo (go-jōfu), all members of the Nakake-ma [attendant room] were ordered to serve on permanent duty; it was proclaimed that, on a rotation of every two to three years, each would be granted a leave of approximately one hundred days for rest.

One item: In the fifth month of Hōreki 1, the year of the Sheep (1751), in accordance with his petition, he was granted his coming-of-age ceremony (genpuku), and it was proclaimed that he should continue serving as a page (o-koshō) as before.
