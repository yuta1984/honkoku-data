1. In the 8th month of Bunka 1 (Tori year, 1804), he was appointed to the acting position of Daijūchō (Chief of the Great Group/Senior Section) in succession to Shinoda Genshichi.

2. In the 6th month of Bunka 3 (Tora year, 1806), he was newly assigned to oversee the domain school. In connection with the instruction of the *Dōjikun* (a Confucian educational text), he took charge of organizing various activities for students both inside and outside the school, and was recognized for his diligent efforts in numerous respects. As a special reward, he was granted three *bu* in gold annually from the school's budget, free from any obligation of repayment.

3. In the 1st month of Bunka 5 (Tatsu year, 1808), as the northern territories (Ezo) defense mission was being undertaken, he traveled to Karafuto (Sakhalin) as an attendant/assistant to his father. On this occasion,

**[Left folio]**

he was granted a formal audience with the lord and received the honor of a ceremonial cup of sake (*o-ryū*), departing on the 5th day of that same month.

　　*Note: On the 13th day of the 3rd month of that same year, they set sail from Matsumae, arriving at Karafuto Island on the 19th day. He served as attendant on every occasion of armored military drills (*katchū sōren*) conducted within the encampment.*

4. In the 7th month of the same year (Bunka 5, 1808), when the mission was completed and the troops were withdrawn, he traveled to Sōya together with his father.

5. In the 8th month of the same year, he departed Sōya and traveled overland through Ezo (Hokkaido), arriving at Ōterамура (Ōtera village) in the 11th month. There, he was assigned to attend to both the seriously and lightly [wounded or ill] among the troops that had been dispatched the previous spring for the defense of Ezo.
