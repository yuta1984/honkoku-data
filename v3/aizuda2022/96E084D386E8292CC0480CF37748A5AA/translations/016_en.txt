【Right page】

　　(Continued from previous page) He served as escort when [the lord] proceeded to Edo Castle; [the lord] was greatly pleased, and after graciously expressing his satisfaction, bestowed upon him sake [as a reward].
　　On the occasion of the wedding ceremony of Lady Baien-in, he performed the duty of presenting ceremonial gifts at the palanquin approach (o-koshiyori), and was given a grant of cloth allowance (fuku-dai).

One item: In the second month of the second year [of Enkyō], the year of the Ox (1745), it was proclaimed that he had served with diligence and performed his duties admirably during the execution of the Eight Lectures (Hakkō).

One item: In the same month, on the occasion of [the lord's] visit to the domain (o-gekō), he was ordered to perform advance lodging arrangements (o-yadowari) and departed [for the domain].

【Left page】

　　Note: While carrying out the lodging arrangements and having traveled as far as Oyama, word arrived that the Imperial Prince would be returning from Nikkō to Edo on the same day as [the lord's] overnight stop (o-haku) at Kasukabe. Therefore, he returned to Sugito and managed the overnight arrangements at Sugito. At Fukura, on the occasion when his lodging arrangement duties were officially concluded, he received the lord's acknowledgment that there had been no delays or problems up to that point.

One item: In the same year (month unknown), in recognition of his dedicated service in the lodging arrangements, he was awarded one piece of silver as a reward.

One item: In the eighth month of the same year, it was proclaimed that he had achieved proficiency in various arts (sho-gei).
