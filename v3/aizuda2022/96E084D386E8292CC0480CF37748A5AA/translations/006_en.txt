In the eleventh month of that same year, Shozaemon was granted ten koku and two-person rations, and was summoned into service as an o-kachi (foot soldier attendant). In the second year of Kan'ei [Keian] (1649), he was granted two koku in stipend for his duties. In the second year of Jōō (1653), he traveled up to Edo for rotation duty (kinban), where on the eighteenth day of the fifth month, during his period of service, he killed Kawahara Bun'zaemon in a quarrel.
