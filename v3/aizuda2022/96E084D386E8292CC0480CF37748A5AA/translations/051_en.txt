[Right folio]
Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, regarding the training exercises at the Kannonsaki training ground, the formations and ranks of the armored troops (kōshi) had been brought into full order and had achieved the highest rank for three consecutive years. In recognition of his diligent efforts in supervision and management, he was awarded a gift of sake.

Item: In the first month of the third year [of Bunsei], the year of the Dragon [1820], in connection with the investigation of a boundary dispute between Hōfukuji temple in Uragō Village, Miura District, and the village headman (nanushi) Jirōzaemon of the same village, he was provisionally appointed to the post of Kōji Bugyō (magistrate of civil litigation). As Higuchi Genta had been dispatched to Edo for this business, he was ordered to conduct a thorough joint investigation and to resolve the matter expeditiously.
　　Note: During the investigation, he was also ordered to investigate the matter of the murder of Onchō, a retired monk (inkyo) of Jōrakuji temple in Ashina Village, whom [the perpetrator] had encountered and killed.

[Left folio]
Item: In the third month of the same year, he had previously been assigned to oversee and assist with the principal arts of civil and military training (bunbu no gei); however, as the general arrangements had now been revised, he was relieved of that duty. It was announced that he had performed his service diligently up to that point.

Item: In the fifth month of the fourth year [of Bunsei], the year of the Snake [1821], he was ordered to conduct a general inspection and normalization (furunarashi) of the newly acquired domain territory in Sagami Province (Sōshū). Upon completion of the withdrawal of personnel, he descended [from Edo] after the outside retainers (tozama-shi) and the combined assembly unit (yoriai-gumi) had withdrawn, taking his attendants with him.
