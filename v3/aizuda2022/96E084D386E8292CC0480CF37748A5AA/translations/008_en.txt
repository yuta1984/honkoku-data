As he had been called into service only recently, and in order that he might become accustomed to his duties, it was arranged through petition and discussion that he would return [to Aizu] in the eighth month of the following year of the Rooster (1681).

One item: In the twelfth month of the first year of Tenna, the year of the Rooster (1681), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation (kinban), completed one tour of duty, and returned.
　　　Note: As he was charged with fire-warden duty (o-hikeshi go-yō), he performed said duty during his tour.

One item: In the first month of the third year of the same era, the year of the Boar (1683), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation, completed one tour of duty, and returned.
　　　Note: During his tour, as the lord performed a proxy visit to Nikkō (go-meidai), he served in attendance. As it was reported that he had served competently and without incident throughout the journey, he was granted a monetary reward.

【Left page】
One item: In the tenth month of the same year, he was appointed as a temporary inspector (kari-metsuke) at the rice transport office (kaimai-sho), and performed the role of liaison/inspector (tachiai) at various locations including Niigata and the Kantō region.

One item: In the second year of Jōkyō, the year of the Rabbit (1685) — month unknown — in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of said liaison duties.

One item: In the fourth month of the first year of Genroku, the year of the Dragon (1688), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation, and returned in the sixth month of the same year as an attendant accompanying the lord's journey back to the domain (go-gekō go-tomo).
