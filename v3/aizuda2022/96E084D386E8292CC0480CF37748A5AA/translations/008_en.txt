As he had only recently been called into service and needed to become accustomed to his duties, he petitioned to remain in Edo beyond his scheduled term, and descended [to Aizu] in the eighth month of the following year of the Rooster (1681).

One item: In the twelfth month of the first year of Tenna, the year of the Rooster (1681), he went up to Edo on duty rotation, completed one tour of duty, and returned.
　　Note: As he was commanded to serve in the fire-watch duty (okeshi goyō), he fulfilled said duty during his tour.

One item: In the first month of the third year of the same era, the year of the Boar (1683), he went up to Edo on duty rotation, completed one tour of duty, and returned.
　　Note: As the lord's representative duty at Nikkō (Nikkō omitai no otsutome) was being carried out during his tour, he served in the accompanying party. As the entire journey was carried out safely and without incident, this was formally acknowledged, and he was granted a monetary reward.

【Left page】

One item: In the tenth month of the same year, he was ordered to serve as acting inspector (kari-metsuke) at the rice transport office (kaimai-sho), performing inspection duties at Niigata and various locations in the Kantō region.

One item: In the second year of Jōkyō, the year of the Rabbit (1685) — month unknown — in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of said inspection duties.

One item: In the fourth month of the first year of Genroku, the year of the Dragon (1688), he went up to Edo on duty rotation, and in the sixth month he returned [to Aizu] in attendance upon the lord's journey down from Edo (gogeikō).
