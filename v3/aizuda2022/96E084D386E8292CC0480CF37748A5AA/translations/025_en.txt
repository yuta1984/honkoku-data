One item: In the twelfth month of the same year (1762), during the occasion of the lord's ceremonial spear display (o-yari) and tea ceremony (o-chanoyu), he worked diligently in his duties, and was therefore granted one bundle of cotton wadding (wata hito-taba).
　　　　Note: After this, on each occasion of the tea ceremony, musical accompaniment (o-hayashi), and similar events, it was declared that he had exerted himself on every occasion, and he was repeatedly granted cotton wadding and similar items.

One item: In the first month of Hōreki 13, the year of the Sheep (1763), he was appointed to the role of o-zen-ban (dining service duty).

One item: In the fourth month of the same year, due to the lord's return to the domain (go-gekō), he traveled down [from Edo] in attendance.

One item: In the eleventh month of the same year, despite the great volume of duties to attend to directly for the lord's personal needs (o-temoto go-yō),

　　　　Note: After this, on repeated occasions during the lord's residence in Edo (go-zaifu), the grants were made by virtue of the lord's personal benevolence.
