When (foreign ships appeared off the coast of Shirahama in Bōshū,) all personnel acted with commendable readiness and swiftly made their preparations, and it was ordered that this matter be duly reported to the higher authorities through the appropriate channel.

One item: In the ninth month of the same year, regarding the fact that the personnel of both garrison houses (jin'ya), both senior and junior, had continued to devote themselves diligently to training in stern-oarsmanship (tomote), while this discipline differs in nature from other supplementary arts, as it is a skill essential to coastal defense, it had been ordered (to be practiced); and in recognition of this, a special gift of sake was bestowed, with further instructions that they continue to apply themselves with even greater diligence.
