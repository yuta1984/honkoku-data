**[Right folio]**

[Regarding the fire at the Warijō mentioned on the previous page,] he was commended for his diligent and devoted efforts.

1. In the 11th month of the same year (Bunka 7, 1810), he was appointed to serve as a hired attendant (*o-yatoi-kin*) to the outside (*tozama*) retainers permanently stationed in Sagami Province (*Sōshū*), and it was announced that he would receive a stipend of three-person's provisions (*san-nin fuchi*) while stationed there.

2. In the 9th month of Bunka 8 (Hitsuji year, 1811), as there were regulations governing the use of horses when dispatching men and horses, and it was not possible for a large number of household members to travel together, he traveled to his post accompanied by his servants (*rōtō*).

3. In the 12th month of Bunka 10 (Tori year, 1813), in recognition of his diligent service at the newly established defense position (*o-sonaeba*) in Sagami Province, he was presented with two bundles of cotton (*wata futa-taba*) as a reward.

4. In the 9th month of Bunka 11 (Inu year, 1814), in recognition of his diligent practice of ■-hand techniques (*■te shugyō*, martial arts discipline; first character illegible), he was presented with sake.

5. In the 1st month of Bunka 13 (Ne year, 1816), when a foreign vessel was sighted off the coast of Shimoda in Izu Province (*Zushū*), he was assigned to the vanguard defense unit (*senpō o-sonae-gumi*) and ordered to remain vigilant and on guard.

6. In the 1st month of Bunka 14 (Ushi year, 1817), regarding the previous spring's sighting of a foreign vessel off Shimoda, he was informed that the prompt and well-prepared response of all personnel involved would be reported to the lord at a suitable opportunity.
