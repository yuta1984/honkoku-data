*(Continued from previous page)* …that he had applied himself diligently and performed admirably in that matter, and this was formally proclaimed.

1. In the 11th month of the same year (Bunka 7, 1810), he was appointed to the position of hired retainer on permanent duty in Sagami Province (*Sōshū jōzume tōzama-shi oyatoi-kin*), and it was formally proclaimed that he would receive a stipend of three-person rations (*sannin fuchi*) while stationed there.

2. In the 9th month of Bunka 8 (Hitsuji year, 1811), as there were regulations governing the horses used when dispatching men and horses, and it was not possible for a large household to attend together in full, he attended [the castle] accompanied by his retainer (*rōtō*).

3. In the 12th month of Bunka 10 (Tori year, 1813), as he had applied himself diligently at the newly established Sagami Province defense position (*Sōshū go-bibasho*), he was presented with two bundles of cotton (*wata futataba*) as a reward.

6.
