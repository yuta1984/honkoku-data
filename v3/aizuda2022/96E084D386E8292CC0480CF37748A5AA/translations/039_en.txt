...even if one were to take up official duties and pursue training under official assignments, there would be no need to re-enroll in another school — this was communicated to him by order.

Item: In the ninth month of that same year, he attended the toritoi-gari (bird-hunt drive), and was granted the birds that were caught.

Item: In the second month of the eleventh year, the year of Hitsuji (未), he was provisionally appointed as a go-daikan (deputy administrator), but was relieved of the post as requested due to illness.

Item: In the ninth month of that same year, he again attended the toritoi-gari, and was granted the birds that were caught.

Item: In the eleventh month of that same year, during the toritoi-gari, the movements and timing of the group were well coordinated and in unison, their advance and retreat handled with great skill;

[Left folio]

...everything was in good order throughout, and it was recognized that the members had also been diligently practicing in private training sessions (uchi-keiko). For this, the entire unit received a commendation from their lord.

Item: In the third month of the twelfth year, the year of Saru (申), he was appointed as go-metsuke (inspector/censor).

Item: In the fourth month of that same year, he was granted a role stipend (yakuryō) of thirty koku.

Item: In the fifth month of that same year, he was exempted from a certain extraordinary assignment that would have been issued in times of unexpected circumstances.

Item: In the fourth month of the first year of Kyōwa (享和元年), the year of Tori (酉), he was granted the residential plot (yashiki) formerly belonging to Warakiya (Waraki) Kichirōemon.
