One item: In the third month of the first year of Kyōhō, the year of the Monkey (1716), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation, completed one tour of duty, and returned.
　　　Note: During his tour, on the occasion when the Senior Councillors (rōjū) hosted a reception to celebrate the proclamation of a new shogun (shōgun senge), he performed his duties without any hindrance or obstruction, and accordingly he was granted a sum of silver.

One item: In the ninth month of the second year of the same era, the year of the Rooster (1717), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation, completed one tour of duty, and returned.
　　　Note: Upon his return, it was recognized that he had performed his service during the tour with distinction, and accordingly,

One item: In the third month of the fourth year of the same era, the year of the Boar (1719), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation, completed one tour of duty, and returned.

One item: In the third month of the fifth year of the same era, the year of the Rat (1720), in recognition of his many years of diligent service, his seat of precedence was raised above that of Hasunuma Gizaemon.

One item: In the intercalary seventh month of the sixth year of the same era, the year of the Ox (1721), in recognition of several years of diligent service, he was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit) and placed in the company of Obara Gorōemon, performing castle guard duty.

One item: In the second month of the seventh year of the same era, the year of the Tiger (1722), in accordance with his petition, his son Heiji was appointed as his replacement in the guard duties.
