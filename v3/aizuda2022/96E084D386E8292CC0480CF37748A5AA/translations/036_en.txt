Item: In the seventh month of the seventh year of the same era, the Year of the Rabbit (Kansei 7, 1795), he was granted the inherited stipend (atoshiki go-chigyō) of 100 koku without any reduction, and was appointed to the tozama-gumi-tsuki (attachment to the outer samurai corps). He was assigned to the unit of Kasuga Jinzaemon and performed guard duty at the castle (o-shiro go-ban).

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, it came to be known that he had previously been studying Naganuma-ryū military science (heigaku) in Edo but had returned (to Aizu) while his training was still only half complete, and that if he were to study for another half year or more, he might well attain full transmission (kaiden) of the school. At this time, he was ordered to go up to Edo, to remain there until he had completed and received the full transmission, and to apply himself diligently to his training.

[Left folio]
Furthermore, it was decreed that during his stay, he would be granted seven-person stipend (shichinin-fuchi) in Edo without any reduction (御借上なく).

　　Note: Accordingly, he departed for Edo in the twelfth month of the same year, completed his full transmission (kaiden) in the ninth month of the following year, the Year of the Dragon (Kansei 8, 1796), and returned (to Aizu).

Item: In the sixth month of the ninth year of the same era, the Year of the Snake (Kansei 9, 1797), having previously studied Naganuma-ryū military science in Edo, and in order to further strengthen the school's official role within the domain, he was once again ordered to undergo training. He again went up to Edo, applied himself diligently, obtained full transmission, and returned.
