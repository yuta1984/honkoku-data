**Motarō Yoshiharu**　（Born on the 29th day of the 12th month of Tenmei 7 [1787], the Year of the Goat）

　　　　Mother: Daughter of his grandfather, Gondayū Yoshitaka

**[Left folio]**

　　　　Wife: Daughter of Kōyama Kantayū Moritane　（Born on the 22nd day of the 12th month of Kansei 4 [1792], the Year of the Rat）

One entry: In the 10th month of Kōwa 2 [1802], the Year of the Dog, when the御用人所 (the administrative office) was destroyed by a lightning-caused fire, [Motarō Yoshiharu] rushed to the scene immediately and worked diligently in firefighting and fire prevention efforts. This was recognized and commended by the lord.

One entry: In the 4th month of the same year, he was appointed to oversee and manage the Shogakuryō (the domain's calligraphy/writing academy).

One entry: In the 8th month of Kōwa 3 [1803], the Year of the Boar, at his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (o-mimai) with the lord.
