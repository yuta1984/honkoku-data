**Kyūtarō Yoshiharu**: Born on the 29th day of the 12th month of Tenmei 7 (Hitsuji year, 1787)
　　　　Mother: Daughter of his grandfather, Gondayū Yoshitaka

**[Left folio]**

Wife: Daughter of Kōyama Kantayū Moritane; born on the 22nd day of the 12th month of Kansei 4 (Ne year, 1792)

1. In the 10th month of Kōwa 2 (Inu year, 1802), when the御用人所 (the senior retainers' office) was destroyed by a lightning strike and fire, [Kyūtarō Yoshiharu] promptly rushed to the scene and worked to suppress the flames. This action was officially commended and recognized.

2. In the 4th month of the same year (Kōwa 2, 1802), he was appointed to the position of overseer/administrator (締まり方) of the Shogakuryō (the domain's calligraphy/writing academy).

3. In the 8th month of Kōwa 3 (Inoshishi year, 1803), in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (初御目見え) with the lord.
