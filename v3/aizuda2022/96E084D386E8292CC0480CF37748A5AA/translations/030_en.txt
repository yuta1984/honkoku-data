(Continued from previous page) ...he had applied himself diligently to all matters related to his assigned duties, requiring little guidance and proving of great assistance; accordingly, it was declared that this was a source of considerable satisfaction, and he received a personal commendation to that effect.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year (Bunsei 6, 1823), he was appointed as Gokinjū Bantōdori (Head of the Close-Attendance Guard), and his rank was raised to the equivalent of Kinzan Bugyō (Gold Mine Magistrate). He was to continue performing his assigned duties as before, to serve as intermediary for general business matters, and to report all official communications (shōtatsu) directly to the Senior Elders (o-karō-chū). Furthermore, it was declared that his stipend and allowances would remain as previously established.

Item: In the same month, he was assigned an official duty and departed for Edo.

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year (Bunsei 6, 1823), he was additionally appointed as Ofune Bugyō (Ship Magistrate) and was declared to be responsible for the supervision of the sailors (kako).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent service, he was granted the right to wear the formal kamishimo on all regular occasions, and his rank was placed immediately after that of Higuchi Kanjи.

Item: In the first month of the seventh year of Bunsei, the Year of the Tiger (Bunsei 7, 1824), in recognition of his diligent work and the successful completion of the road construction project at Go-no-chō, he was awarded one bundle of cotton wadding (wata) as a reward.
