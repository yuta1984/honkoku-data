*(Continued from previous page)* …that those men had completed their duties and returned without incident, which greatly pleased [the lord]. Considering the hardships of traveling by sea and land to such distant places, it had been arranged that sake would be presented to them at a lodging on the way before arriving at Wakamatsu. However, as he had returned ahead of the rest of the troops, he was informed that the gift would instead be presented at the castle, and it was duly presented to him thereafter.

1. In the 11th month of the same year (Bunka 5, 1808), all personnel stationed at Sōya were granted a formal audience [with the lord].

2. In the 12th month of the same year (Bunka 5, 1808), as he had traveled [to Ezo] as an attendant during the Ezo defense mission, he was presented with a formal meal at the castle.

**[Left folio]**

3. In the 8th month of Bunka 6 (Mi year, 1809), as he had traveled [to Ezo] as an attendant during the Ezo defense mission, he was presented with one bundle of cotton (*wata hitotaba*).

4. In the 9th month of the same year (Bunka 6, 1809), he was appointed to the acting position of assistant to the school recitation instructor (*shōshi soekingakari* kanyaku).

5. In the 11th month of the same year (Bunka 6, 1809), he was formally appointed as assistant to the school recitation instructor (*shōshi soekin*) and was granted a stipend of two-person rations (*ninin fuchi*).

6. In the 5th month of Bunka 7 (Uma year, 1810), when a fire broke out at the Warigba (*warigba*, the domain's administrative office handling stipends and accounting), he promptly rushed to the scene, taking charge of firefighting efforts and providing various necessary supplies.
