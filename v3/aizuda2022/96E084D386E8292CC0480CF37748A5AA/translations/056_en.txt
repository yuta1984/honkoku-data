**[Right folio]**

[The troops engaged in the Ezo defense mission] completed their duties and returned without incident, which greatly pleased [the lord]. Recognizing that the travel to and from such distant lands by both sea and land had been an extreme hardship, it was intended that sake be presented to him at the last post station before arriving at Wakamatsu. However, since he had returned ahead of the main body of troops, he was informed that the sake would instead be presented to him at the castle, and it was duly bestowed upon him at a later date.

1. In the 11th month of the same year (Bunka 5, 1808), all of the troops that had been stationed at Sōya were granted a formal audience with the lord.

2. In the 12th month of the same year, in recognition of his travels to Ezo as an attendant during the northern defense mission, he was presented with a formal meal (*o-ryōri*) at the castle.

**[Left folio]**

3. In the 8th month of Bunka 6 (Mi year, 1809), in recognition of his travels to Ezo as an attendant during the northern defense mission, he was presented with one bundle of cotton (*wata hitotaba*).

4. In the 9th month of the same year, he was appointed to the acting position of assistant to the school recitation teacher (*juku-shi soe-kin kari-yaku*).

5. In the 11th month of the same year, he was formally appointed as assistant to the school recitation teacher (*juku-shi soe-kin*) and granted a stipend of two-person's provisions (*futari fuchi*).

6. In the 5th month of Bunka 7 (Uma year, 1810), when a fire broke out at the *Warijō* (a domain administrative office) some time before, he rushed to the scene promptly, beginning firefighting efforts and contributing various goods and supplies.
