Note: Regarding the dispatch of personnel, he was instructed to liaise with Nagai Uemon of the Sendai domain's retainer corps.

Item: In the first month of the same fifth year of the Dragon [Bunka 5, 1808], as he was to proceed to Ezo (Hokkaido) for the duty of coastal defense, he was granted an audience (o-mimai) [with the domain lord], received the ceremonial cup (o-nagare), and departed on the fifth day of that month.

Item: In the third month of that same year, he was instructed to survey and lay out (nawabari) the overall encampment (sō-jin'ya) at the domain's designated post in Karafuto (Sakhalin), and further that there might also be occasion to investigate the strategic advantages and disadvantages of offensive and defensive positions at said post.

[Left folio]

Item: On the thirteenth day of that same month, he set sail from Matsumae, and on the nineteenth arrived by ship at the domain's designated post in Karafuto. There, he was additionally instructed to carry out the encampment survey (nawabari) for the Ruuta post as well, and accordingly crossed the sea to that location and completed the survey.

Note: In the seventh month of that same year, upon the completion of his duties and withdrawal from the post, he became the last to leave (atoki), crossed the sea to Sōya, and was instructed to arrange billetings (yadowari) for the personnel stationed at Sōya, as well as those stationed at Rishiri and Matsumae.
