Item: In the first month of that same year [the fourth year of Bunka, year of U], he was appointed as Road Magistrate (Dōchū Bugyō), his court rank was raised to the equivalent of District Magistrate (Kōri Bugyō-kaku), and his official stipend (oyakuryō) of thirty koku was continued as before.
