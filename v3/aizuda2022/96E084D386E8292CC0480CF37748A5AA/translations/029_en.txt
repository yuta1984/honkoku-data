　　Note: It was officially declared that he had served without fail in his role as assistant escort.

Item: In the seventh month of the same year (Bunsei 6, 1823), he was appointed to the position of Gokinjkata Goyōgakari (Officer in Charge of Service Arrangements).

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, he was relieved of his guard (o-ban) duties, and was to continue performing his various assigned tasks as before. In addition, he would be assigned further duties as circumstances required. In connection with this, he was instructed to mingle broadly with those undergoing training as permanent Edo residents (jōfu), as well as with those of the heavy and light retainer households (jūkei yashiki) stationed permanently in Edo, and also with the various officials of the heavy and light ranks stationed in Edo, so as to become well-versed in matters across all domains. It was further declared that in the event of a shortage of personnel, he might again be assigned to guard duty.

Item: In the same month (eighth month), it was officially declared that, from the tenth month of the previous year through the fifth month of the current year (Bunsei 6), he had served as an on-duty guard during the lord's visits to the castle, and had performed the role of assistant escort on multiple occasions.

Item: In the same month (eighth month), it was officially declared that he had maintained perfect attendance over a number of years, and that the construction site at Roku-no-chō had been completed to a satisfactory standard; accordingly, he was awarded two lengths of silk (the character 両 appears to be as written in the original manuscript).
