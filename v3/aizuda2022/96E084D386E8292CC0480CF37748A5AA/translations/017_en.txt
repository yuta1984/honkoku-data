One item: In the ninth month of the same year (1745), he was ordered to hunt with a firearm. Having shot and brought down three pheasants, it was noted that his marksmanship was skillful, and as a private token of appreciation, he was given one bolt of silk.

One item: In the fourth month of the fourth year of Enkyō, the year of the Rabbit (1747), on the occasion of [the lord's] visit to the domain, he returned [to Edo] to serve as escort, traveled up [to Edo], and then accompanied [the lord] back down [to the domain].

One item: In the fourth month of the second year of Kan'en, the year of the Snake (1749), on the occasion of [the lord's] visit to the domain, he returned [to Edo] to serve as escort, traveled up [to Edo], and then accompanied [the lord] back down [to the domain].

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, during the forceful petition (gōso) by the peasants, he was ordered to guard the gate in the vicinity of Odagaki (the location of the unidentified gate).

One item: In the tenth month of the same year, upon the passing of Lord Dojō [Matsudaira Tadatomi] and the conveyance of his coffin [to the domain], he returned [to Edo] to serve as escort, traveled up [to Edo], and then accompanied [the procession] down [to the domain].

One item: In the sixth month of the second year of Hōreki, the year of the Monkey (1752), as his son Heikichi had died of illness the previous winter, he submitted a request to designate his third son Kazuma as heir, and this request was granted.

One item: In the ninth month of the same year, he was appointed to the role of permanent attendant (jōzume goyō-yaku) to Lady Rin'kō-in, and [began serving] from the same [year].
