　　[Sasahara Yoichirō] was to be dispatched [to the castle town]; accordingly, [the subject] was ordered to rise immediately upon [Sasahara's] arrival and proceed up [to the castle].

One item: In the third month of Meiwa 5, the year of the Rat (1768), due to his diligent service, he was granted an increase in stipend (go-kasan) of three ryō in gold.

One item: In the fifth month of the same year, regarding the handling of the lord's personal affairs (o-temoto go-yō) on this occasion, the superintendent (go-yōnin) was ordered to manage matters using surplus funds; however, as matters could not be properly concluded without someone at the lord's side to be entrusted with these affairs, he was ordered to serve as intermediary (o-toritsugi) for said business.

One item: In the eighth month of the same year, beginning with [duties assigned at] the castle,

　　he diligently and capably managed all the various matters he had been assigned at [those] locations and elsewhere. Furthermore, on this occasion, regarding flood-damaged areas along the Ōkawa river, he made repeated visits to rural villages and incurred personal expenses in doing so; for these reasons, he was privately granted two silver coins (ginsu ni-mai).

One item: In the tenth month of the same year, as Sasahara Yoichirō had gone down [to the domain], he went up [to the castle] in rotation to replace him.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was appointed to Oku-ban (inner guard duty).
