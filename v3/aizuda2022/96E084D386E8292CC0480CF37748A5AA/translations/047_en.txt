Item: In the eighth month of the same ninth year of the Monkey [Bunka 10, 1813], he was appointed to serve as an inspector (tachi'iri-tsutome) at the Accounts Office (o-kanjō-dokoro). He was ordered to manage not only general accounting controls (shime-kata), but also the overall affairs of the Accounts Office, consulting with the Accounts Chief (o-kanjō-gashira) on matters both great and small, and taking measures to ensure that business was conducted smoothly and efficiently.

Item: In the fourth month of the same tenth year of the Rooster [Bunka 11, 1814], he was appointed as head of a unit within the Sagami Province Guard Commander's regiment (Sōshū-bangashira-kumi-kashira). Upon taking up this post, he was relieved of his baton of command (saihai), his seat ranking was placed within the bangashira-kumi-kashira uchikomi class in order of seniority among his peers, and his official stipend supplement of 200 koku was to continue as before...
