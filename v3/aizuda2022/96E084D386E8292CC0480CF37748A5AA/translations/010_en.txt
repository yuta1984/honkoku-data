One item: In the twelfth month of the fourteenth year of the same era, the year of the Snake (1701), he was granted an additional stipend of five koku.

One item: In the twelfth month of the fifteenth year of the same era, the year of the Horse (1702), he was granted one set of formal hemp ceremonial vestments (ori-ire go-mon asa o-kamishimo) bearing the lord's crest.

One item: In the third month of the third year of Hōei, the year of the Dog (1706), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation (kinban), completed one tour of duty, and returned.
　　　Note: During his tour, it was recognized that he had performed his duties diligently over many years, and accordingly he was granted a sum of silver.

One item: In the third month of the fifth year of the same era, the year of the Rat (1708), he was granted an additional stipend of three koku.

【Left page】
One item: In the ninth month of the fourth year of Shōtoku, the year of the Horse (1714), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, it was proclaimed that, having served with sincerity and diligence for several decades, and having in particular applied himself with great industry to the provisioning and catering duties (makanai-kata), attending carefully to matters of even the smallest detail, and managing affairs with exceptional skill and judgment, he was, despite the straitened circumstances of the times, raised in stipend to one hundred koku of assessed landholding (chigyo) in recognition of his longstanding meritorious service. He was further instructed to continue to apply himself with ever greater diligence in the performance of his duties.

One item: In the third month of the fifth year of the same era, the year of the Goat (1715), upon the completion of his duty rotation and his return [to Aizu], it was proclaimed that he had performed his service during his tour with distinction, and he was granted a sum of silver [in recognition thereof].
