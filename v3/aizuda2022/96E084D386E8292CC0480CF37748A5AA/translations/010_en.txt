One item: In the twelfth month of the fourteenth year of the same era, the year of the Snake (1701), he was granted an additional stipend of five koku.

One item: In the twelfth month of the fifteenth year of the same era, the year of the Horse (1702), he was granted one set of formal hempen ceremonial dress (ori-ire go-mon asa-kamishimo) bearing the lord's crest.

One item: In the third month of the third year of Hōei, the year of the Dog (1706), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation (kinban), completed one tour of duty, and returned [to Aizu].
　　　Note: On account of his many years of commendable service during the tour, he was granted a sum of silver.

One item: In the third month of the fifth year of the same era, the year of the Rat (1708), he was granted an additional stipend of three koku.

【Left page】
One item: In the ninth month of the fourth year of Shōtoku, the year of the Horse (1714), he was sent to Edo on duty rotation.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, [it was proclaimed that] over the course of several decades he had served with sincerity and diligence, and in particular had devoted himself to the domain's provisioning duties (o-makanai-kata), attending carefully to even the smallest matters and handling affairs with consistently sound judgment. On account of this, though the financial circumstances of the domain being what they were, in recognition of his long years of meritorious service, his stipend (o-chigyō) was raised and rounded up to one hundred koku. It was further proclaimed that he should continue to apply himself diligently and carry on accordingly in his duties.

One item: In the third month of the fifth year of the same era, the year of the Goat (1715), upon completing his duty rotation and returning [to Aizu], it was proclaimed that he had served commendably during his tour, and he was granted a sum of silver.
