One item: In the fifth month of Kyōhō 20, the year of the Rabbit (1735), in accordance with his father's petition, he traveled up to Edo and was granted his first audience (o-memie) [with the lord].

One item: In the twelfth month of Kan'en 2, the year of the Snake (1749), when the peasants staged a forcible petition (gōso), he was ordered to be attached to Nezū Shinkurō and to coordinate with the Suwa shrine community (Suwa-shachū).

One item: In the second month of the third year of the same era, the year of the Horse (1750), it was officially proclaimed that he had served diligently during the peasants' forcible petition.

One item: On the fourteenth day of the eleventh month of Hōreki 1, the year of the Sheep (1751), he died of illness at the age of twenty-seven. His posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō) was Shōjuin Mōa Yūshoku Koji.
