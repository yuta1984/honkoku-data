Item: In that same month, regarding the general matter of personnel tallies (hito-daka) and expenditure tallies (tsukaidaka), there being also a public-side ordinance (kōhen o-sadame; struck through: "residence"), these matters were to be officially reported. Accordingly, he was ordered to take charge of investigating and managing the personnel tallies, expenditure tallies, and the drafting of advance notices (sakifure) for those stationed in Sagami Province, as well as the procedures for official reporting to the public authorities.

Item: In the first month of the same seventh year of the Sheep [Bunka 8, 1811], he was appointed to oversee the organization of foot troops (ashitsugi meshishi-kata) in Sagami Province, and all those serving in the allocation division (warikata-tsutome no mono) were ordered to report to the office, to consult with the Construction Magistrate (o-fushin-bugyō), and to take measures to ensure that

Item: In the eighth month of that same year, he departed and traveled up to Sagami Province.
　　Note: This was in connection with affairs pertaining to the newly acquired domain (shin go-ryō) and its administration. He brought with him copies of various registers from the Aizu-side District Office (gun-yakusho), the civil litigation office (kuji-dokoro), as well as the town offices, demesne (go-kura'iri) offices, and mountain offices (yama-yakusho).

Item: In the seventh month of that same year, he was appointed to the concurrent post of Ship Magistrate (fune-bugyō), and was ordered to fit out sixteen warships of the domain fleet.
