Item: In that same month, since matters of personnel numbers (hito-daka) and expenditure allocations (tsukaidaka) generally also concern the official shogunal residence (the character "yaku/tei" corrected from "sada/jō" by strikethrough), and must be duly reported, he was ordered to take charge of investigating and managing the personnel numbers, expenditure allocations, advance notice documents (sakifure), and the preparation of official reports to the shogunal authorities on behalf of those on permanent assignment in Sagami Province.

Item: In the first month of the same seventh year of the Sheep [Bunka 7, 1810], he was ordered to oversee the management of foot inspections/surveys (ashikiwa) in Sagami Province. He was ordered to have all those assigned to the allocation duties (warigata-tsutome) report to the office, consult with the Construction Magistrate (go-fushin-bugyō), and arrange matters so that official business would proceed without hindrance.

Item: In the eighth month of that same year, he set out and traveled up to Sagami Province.
　　　　Note: This was in connection with the settlement arrangements for the newly acquired domain territories; he copied and brought with him the various registers (shochōki) from the Aizu district offices (kōri-yakusho), litigation offices (kujisho), and also the town demesne and mountain offices.

Item: In the seventh month of that same year, he was appointed to the concurrent post of Ship Magistrate (fune-bugyō), and was ordered to strip and refit sixteen warships (go-gunsen).
