Item: In the second month of the seventh year of the same era, the Year of the Rabbit (Kansei 7, 1795), he was appointed to the Aizu Katte Muyaku-gumi (the non-duty unit under the household affairs administration). As he had diligently served in various official duties up to this time, it was decreed that the special stipend of 30 koku would continue to be granted to him as before, whereupon he returned to Aizu.

Item: On the first day of the sixth month of the same year, he died of illness at the age of sixty-one. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Kōun-in In'a Gonssetsu Koji. The officiant monk (dōshi) and place of burial are the same as above.

(Adopted son)

Kazuma Yoshitake　　(First name: Chōkichi; born on the 26th day of the first month of the first year of Meiwa, the Year of the Rooster [Meiwa 1, 1764])

Biological lineage: Fifth son of Ōnuma Kutarō Chikanori

Wife: Daughter of his adoptive father, Gondaibu Yoshitaka

Item: In the fifth month of the fifth year of Tenmei, the Year of the Snake (Tenmei 5, 1785), in accordance with the petition of his (adoptive) parent, he was granted his first formal audience (o-mie).

Item: In the twelfth month of the first year of Kansei, the Year of the Rooster (Kansei 1, 1789), while in Edo, he was appointed as a tozama-shi-jō o-yatoi (a retained irregular post in the outer samurai corps).
　　Note: After this, he was repeatedly appointed to retained service and continued to serve.
