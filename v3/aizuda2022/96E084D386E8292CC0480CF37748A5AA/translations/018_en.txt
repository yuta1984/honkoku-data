【Right page】

　　In the tenth month [of the same year (Hōreki 2, 1752)], he traveled up to Edo with his attendants (rōtō).

One item: In the sixth month of the third year of Hōreki, the year of the Rooster (1753), he was given a used formal summer robe (katabira) [that had belonged to Lady Rankō-in].

One item: In the eleventh month of the same year, on the occasion of Lady Rankō-in's Ken'ō [celebration], he was given cotton wadding (the exact quantity unrecorded) as a celebratory gift.

One item: In the fourth month of the fourth year of Hōreki, the year of the Dog (1754), he was given a used lined garment (awase) from Lady Kansō-in, and a used small-sleeved robe (kosode) and summer robe (katabira) from Lady Rankō-in.

【Left page】

One item: In the sixth month of the same year, as both noble ladies had taken ill with measles (hashika), he served with particular diligence, and in recognition of this, he was awarded gold coins and paper tissues [used by the ladies].

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was appointed as the New Year's attendant (toshi-otoko) for Lady Rankō-in, and was privately awarded silver coins.

One item: In the first month of the fifth year of Hōreki, the year of the Boar (1755), having served as the New Year's attendant (toshi-otoko), he was awarded a fan and paper [called] hatake-tomokagami in the presence of both noble ladies.
