　　(Continued from previous page) He was ordered to serve as escort for Lord Tojō-sama's [the Aizu domain lord's] journey to Kyoto, and traveled up [to Kyoto] while performing the provisional post of gate guard (o-toban kari-yaku).
　　Note: While in Kyoto, he was appointed to the provisional post of anteroom duty officer (o-tsugiban kari-yaku), and on the occasion of a reception hosted by His Lordship Tanba-no-kami [Shichiyaku], he was appointed to the role of supervisor of the meal service (o-zenbu saihan nin-yaku).

One item: In the eighth month of the second year of the same era, the year of the Dog (1742), he was appointed as gate guard (o-toban).

One item: In the same month, he traveled up [to Edo] as part of the return escort for the lord's alternate attendance (sankinkōtai).

One item: In the fourth month of the third year of the same era, the year of the Boar (1743), on the occasion of [the lord's] visit to the domain (o-gekō), he traveled up [to Edo] to serve as part of the welcoming party,


【Left page】

　　and returned home as part of the lord's escort.

One item: In the seventh month of the same year, he was appointed to the provisional post of anteroom duty officer (o-tsugiban kari-yaku).

One item: In the tenth month of the first year of Enkyō, the year of the Rat (1744), he traveled up to Edo as part of the duty service rotation (kinban).
　　Note: During the tour, on the occasion when [the lord's] horse was called for, he was ordered to perform the duty of arranging the lord's hakama (ceremonial trousers) [and attend to his dress], and in lieu of a labor stipend (rōgin), he was given one set of formal hemp garments (asa-kamishimo). When a fire broke out at the residence of Matsudaira Sakon-shōgen-sama, and [the lord] proceeded to Edo Castle, he promptly turned out and served as escort — not only was his diligence and attentiveness commendable, but he acted with great swiftness —
