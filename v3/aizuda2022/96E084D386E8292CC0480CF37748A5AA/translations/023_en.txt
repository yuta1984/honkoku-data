One item: In the eighth month of the same year (Hōreki 1, 1751), he was granted leave for rest and recuperation, and returned [to the domain] in the ninth month of the same year.
　　　　Note: Due to illness, upon petition, he traveled back up [to Edo] in the fifth month of the following year of the Monkey (1752).

One item: In the sixth month of Hōreki 2, the year of the Monkey (1752), as his elder brother Heikichi had died of illness, he was appointed heir (chakushi) in accordance with his father's petition.

One item: In the sixth month of Hōreki 4, the year of the Dog (1754), he was appointed as a trainee (minarai) for the o-zen-ban (dining service duty).

One item: In the first month of Hōreki 5, the year of the Boar (1755), he was ordered to participate for the first time in the official ceremonies (go-kishiki) of the current lord [of the domain], and having performed his duties without any difficulties in matters that had previously lapsed,

One item: In the fourth month of the same year, due to his diligent service and the fact that he had incurred costs and expenses, he was granted five silver coins (gin-ko) per year during the period of the lord's permanent residence in Edo (o-jōfu).

One item: In the eighth month of Hōreki 6, the year of the Rat (1756), during the occasion of the invitation of the Senior Councillors (rōjū), he served the meal arrangements admirably, receiving praise from the o-sakite-shū (vanguard retainers); it was conveyed that this was regarded as the result of his long-dedicated practice and training, and he received words of praise and commendation.
