【Right page】
One item: In the second month of the second year of the same era, the year of the Serpent (1689), he returned and went up to Edo in attendance upon the lord's sankin (alternate attendance journey to Edo).
　　Note: During the journey, he was ordered to serve as acting inspector (kari metsuke) and carried out these duties.

One item: In the sixth month of the same year, he went up to Edo on duty rotation, completed one tour of duty, and returned.
　　Note: During his tour of duty, he served continuously as acting inspector (hikitōshi kari metsuke).

One item: In the seventh month of the third year of the same era, the year of the Horse (1690), he went up to Edo on duty rotation, completed one tour of duty, and returned.
　　Note: During his tour of duty, he served continuously as acting inspector (hikitōshi kari metsuke).

【Left page】
One item: In the first month of the fourth year of the same era, the year of the Goat (1691), he was appointed as permanent-duty foot inspector of the inner palace (jōzume ohirosiki okachi metsuke), was granted an increase in stipend of three koku and one person's rations (hitori fuchi), and went up to Edo in the same month.

One item: In the third month of the sixth year of the same era, the year of the Rooster (1693), Lady Enyūin passed away; accordingly, in the fifth month of the same year, he was appointed as permanent-duty foot inspector at the upper residence (kami oyashiki jōzume okachi metsuke).

【Written in red】One item: In the first month of the eighth year of the same era, the year of the Boar (1695), he was appointed as permanent-duty foot inspector at the Shiba residence (Shiba oyashiki jōzume kachi metsuke).

One item: In the fifth month of the eighth year of the same era, the year of the Boar (1695), he was appointed as chief of provisions for the alternate-attendance group (kōtai omakanaigashira), immediately undertook duty rotation service, and returned in the ninth month of the same year.
