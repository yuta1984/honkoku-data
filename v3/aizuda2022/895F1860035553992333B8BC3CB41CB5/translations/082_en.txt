**[Right Side]**

However, in conjunction with his reassignment to *Misaki-zume* and his additional appointment to *Zokuji* duties, he was further instructed to also take charge of the receipt and disbursement management (*ukebara*i) of the storehouse at the Misaki garrison (*Ojinya*). In the ninth month of the same year, he relocated to Misaki.

- In the first month of the tenth year (Year of the Rooster), he was relieved of his guard duties at the *Odaiba* (coastal battery/gun emplacement).

- In the twelfth month of the same year, although the *Osobaba* (defensive position/fortification site) was newly established, he applied himself diligently in his duties, attending to various assigned tasks including those of the *Bugugata* (arms and equipment management). In recognition of this, he was awarded one *ryō* of gold as a reward.

**[Left Side]**

- In the first month of the eleventh year (Year of the Dog), he was appointed as *Okachi-metsuke* (inspector of foot soldiers) for the *Sōshū-zume* (Sagami Province garrison), and was granted one *koku* of rice as his official stipend (*yakuryō*). He was then instructed to be reassigned to the *Kannonzaki-zume* (Kannonzaki garrison duty).

- In the second month of the same year, his seat rank was appointed to be next after *Muramatsu Kakushirō*, who served as *Tōzume Gōheki Yakunin* (distant-garrison weapons officer).

- In the seventh month of the same year, he escorted a prisoner named Ichibē from Nishi-Uraga to the residence of Lord Ibuchi Kai-no-kami, where he handed the prisoner over.

- In the first month of the thirteenth year (Year of the Rat), a foreign vessel (*ibune*) drifted near the waters off Shimoda in Izu Province (*Zushū*).
