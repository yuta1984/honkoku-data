**[Right folio]**
　　[In addition to the stipend of three ryō and rations for two men,] he was granted a servant's ration (kozōmono-fuchi) for one person and a duty allowance (o-yakunryō) of two ryō in gold.

　　　Note: From the same day, he was also ordered to continue performing his entertainment duties (o-nagusami go-yō) as before.

- In the 4th month of the 2nd year of the same era (mi year), having diligently performed his entertainment duties, he was awarded two bu in gold as a reward.

- In the 12th month of the same year, he performed guard duty at the castle (o-shiro iriban).

- In the 7th month of the 3rd year (uma year), the castle guard duty was abolished, and he was relieved of that obligation.
　　　Note: In connection with this, he was granted a guard allowance (iriban-ryō) at the rate of two bu per year.

**[Left folio]**
　　[disbursed] on a prorated basis.

- In the 12th month of the same year, he was ordered to make the cord for the ceremonial court cap (eboshi no o), and presented the completed item.
　　　Note: He thereafter presented completed items on three further occasions.

- In the 4th month of the 2nd year [of the next era] (saru year), he was appointed as a provisional inspector (sadame kari-metsuke), and served until the 25th of the same month.

- In the 12th month of the same year, having repeatedly presented completed cords for the ceremonial court cap (eboshi no o), he was awarded one hundred hiki in gold as a reward.
