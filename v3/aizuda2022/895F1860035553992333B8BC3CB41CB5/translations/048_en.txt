- In the 3rd month of the same 8th year (Tsuchinoe-saru, 1788), he was appointed as a bannō-tsuki yoriki (attached mounted retainer to a troop commander), and as he held the senior position (筆頭), he served as goyōban (duty officer for official business).
- In the 3rd month of the 1st year of Kansei (Tsuchinoto-tori, 1789), in recognition of his continuous and diligent service as goyōban from the 3rd month through the 12th month of that year, he received a commendation (shōbi).
- In the 3rd month of the 4th year of Kansei (Mizunoe-ne, 1792), he turned out for the oitori-gari (the domain's ceremonial bird hunt).
　　　Note: He turned out for this event two more times thereafter.
- In the 9th month of the 9th year of Kansei (Tsuchinoto-mi, 1797), as a daikumi yoriki (mounted retainer of the great unit) had performed duty as a備 (reserve/formation) force, he was appointed to serve as guard at the Sesakusho (manufacturing/production office) that had been vacated by the former.

- In the 12th month of the 10th year of Kansei (Kanoe-uma, 1798), he received a commendation for not neglecting his spear arts (sōjutsu) despite his advanced age, and for his continued diligent service, which was deemed admirable and praiseworthy.
- On the 13th day of the 8th month of the 12th year of Kansei (Kanoe-saru, 1800), he died of illness at the age of seventy. His posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō) was Tonyoshukyō (頓誉秋教). His place of burial is the same as previously noted.
