- In the 12th month of the 13th year (hitsuji/sheep year), he was appointed as a foot-soldier inspector for the grand ceremonial chamber (o-hiroshiki o-kachi-metsuke), and served until the 5th month of the following year (saru/monkey year).

- In the 5th month of Meiwa 2 (tori/rooster year, 1765), he was appointed as a regular/provisional inspector (jō-kari-metsuke), relocated to the upper residence (kami-o-yashiki), and served until the 10th month of the same year.

- In the 1st month of the 3rd year of the same era (inu/dog year, 1766), he was appointed as an additional guard for the grand ceremonial chamber (o-hiroshiki kaban), and served until the 2nd month of the same year.

- In the 5th month of the 6th year of the same era (ushi/ox year, 1769), he was appointed as a regular/provisional inspector (jō-kari-metsuke) at the Shiba residence, and served until the 4th month of the following tora/tiger year (1770).
