[Right folio]
　　He served in attendance during the lord's return to the domain (go-gekō) and returned [to the domain].
　　　Note: As he was appointed as an extraordinary provisional inspector (fuji kari-metsuke) during the campaign, a compensation of one ryō and one bu (gold) was granted.

Item: In the fifth month of the same thirteenth year [of Hōreki, 1763] (Year of the Sheep), as he had fulfilled perfect attendance through all three rotations of guard duty (kinban san-jin), he was granted two bu (gold) as a commendation.

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, he was appointed as a one-year inspector at the Rice Stipend Storehouse (go-fuchi-gura ichinen tachiai), serving until the second month of the following year of the Monkey [1764].
　　　Note: For this service, a compensation of two bu (gold) was granted.

Item: In the tenth month of the first year of Meiwa [1764] (Year of the Monkey), he was appointed as inspector at the Revenue Collection Office (go-shūnō-kata tachiai).
　　　Note: For this service, a compensation of two bu (gold) was granted.

[Left folio]
Item: In the seventh month of the same second year [of Meiwa, 1765] (Year of the Rooster), he was appointed as a provisional inspector (kari-metsuke) equivalent to the full position (honroku dōzen), serving until the tenth month of the same year.
　　　Note: For this service, a compensation of one bu (gold) was granted.

Item: In the first month of the same third year [of Meiwa, 1766] (Year of the Dog), he was appointed as a foot inspector (o-kachi-metsuke), and was granted an increase in stipend of one person's rations (hitori fuchi), along with a duty allowance (yakuryō) of three koku of rice.

Item: In the same month, he was appointed as a one-year inspector at the Construction Office (go-fushin-kata ichinen tachiai).

Item: In the fourth month of the same year, he traveled to Edo for his rotation of guard duty (kinban), and returned [to the domain] in the tenth month of the same year.

Item: From the second month to the fourth month of the same fifth year [of Meiwa, 1768] (Year of the Rat), he was appointed and served as assistant inspector at the Examination Office (ginmi-sho-ho tachiai).
