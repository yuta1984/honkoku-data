- In the 12th month of the 6th year of Bunsei (Hitsuji, 1823): While receiving a stipend for provisions (o-makanai-fuchi), he was left free to attend the academy for literary and martial arts studies (bunbu no gakuryō) at his own discretion. However, having set his resolve with particular determination, it was reported that he attended the martial arts practice hall and the calligraphy practice hall without fail and applied himself diligently. Accordingly, he was presented with sake (o-sake).
