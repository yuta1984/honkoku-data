One: In the eighth month of the same year, in anticipation of an emergency (fuji), within the provisional outer guard (kari-omote-sonae), he was ordered to serve within the Elder's Guard (go-karō-sonae) and the Lord's Guard (go-tōshu-sonae), specifically in the duties of Upper Residence guard (kami-o-yashiki-dzume), Junior Elder's Guard (wakadoshiyori-sonae), and Assembly Unit (yoriai-gumi). It was further declared that he need not perform the duties of the Lord's Guard at Mita (Mita-dzume) or the duties of the Foot Inspectors' unit (o-kachi-metsuke).

One: In the seventh month of Bunsei 4 (Year of the Snake), as he had rendered especially distinguished service in the urgent repairs and restoration (shūfuku) of the residence of the Renjuin-sama, attending even to matters of expenditure and finances (o-iri-kata), he was granted a reward of three ryō of silver.

One: In the tenth month of the same year, having applied himself diligently to his current duties and exhausted every effort in the construction and repair works (go-fushin) at several residences, performing his duties admirably, his existing official stipend (o-yakuryō) of two koku was increased and revised accordingly.
