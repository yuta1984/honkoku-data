[Right folio]
One: In the eighth month of Kyōhō 1 (Year of the Monkey), when Lord Tokuō and Lady Shōsen'in graced the residence of Miyake Magobei with their visit, [the retainer] was called into their presence and received sake.

One: In the ninth month of that same year, for his diligent service as a single-person duty assignment, it was decreed that his service allowance (rōgin) would be increased by one piece, to be distributed at three pieces per occasion.

One: In the eleventh month of that same year, in connection with the Shōgun's investiture proclamation and the Senior Councillors' banquet, [the retainer] was appointed to a designated role and granted a clothing allowance.

One: In the fourth month of Kyōhō 2 (Year of the Rooster), [the retainer] was commended and praised for his diligent service.

[Left folio]
One: In the tenth month of Kyōhō 4 (Year of the Boar), in connection with the duties surrounding the Korean diplomatic mission's visit, [the retainer] was commended and praised for his diligent efforts.

One: In the ninth month of Kyōhō 5 (Year of the Rat), an increase in stipend of two koku was granted.

One: In the second month of Kyōhō 6 (Year of the Ox), in connection with the wedding ceremony of Lady Senmyōin, [the retainer] was appointed to a designated role and granted a clothing allowance.

One: In the seventh month of Kyōhō 7 (Year of the Tiger), in accordance with the wish of [the retainer's] father/parent, the nominal allotment (gisaku) was rescinded, and in its place three koku plus one person's Edo ration (Edo-fuchi) were granted, amounting in total to fifteen koku and five persons' rations.
