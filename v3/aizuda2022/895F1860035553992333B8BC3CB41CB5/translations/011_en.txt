**[Right folio]**
　　[The men under review] had diligently performed their duties; those with small stipends would naturally have had considerable private expenses, yet all of them had maintained their commitment and served faithfully. As a reward, each was granted two hundred hiki in gold, distributed to the group of colleagues (nakama) as a whole.

- In the intercalary 11th month of the same year, having worked diligently at the time of a fire that broke out at the residence of Lord Hayashi Daigaku-no-kami, he was granted sake as a reward.

- In the 6th month of the same year, in accordance with his father's petition, the allotted stipend land (gisaku) was reclaimed, and out of a stipend of twelve koku and rations for three men, one portion of

**[Left folio]**
　　the servant's rations (kozōmono-fuchi) was granted to him, and he was called up to serve as a foot soldier (o-omote o-kachi).

- In the 3rd month of the 9th year (u year), due to the illness of Kobayashi Genshirō, he was ordered to take over the latter's castle guard duty (o-shiro iriban), serving until the 21st of the same month, and was granted a guard allowance (iriban-ryō) on a prorated basis.

- In the 5th month of the same year, he was appointed as an additional guard for the grand ceremonial chamber (o-hiroshiki-ban kaban), and served until the 6th month of the same year.

- In the 12th month of the same year, he was ordered to perform castle guard duty, and on the 29th of the same month, he attended upon [his lord] during the journey to the capital (Kyoto).
