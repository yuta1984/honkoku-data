【Right Page】
One: In the intercalary sixth month of the same fifth year (Year of the Dragon), regarding new arrangements for village administration: having worked diligently and faithfully from the outset, sincerely pursuing the revitalization of village conditions, also contributing to reducing the burdens on the commoners (farmers), and properly managing both emergency grain reserves and ration collections — for these reasons, he was granted the exceptional honor of dokurei (the privilege of a private audience with the lord).

One: In the tenth month of the same year, he was additionally appointed to serve concurrently as overseer of the horse division (komagata-gakari).

【Left Page】
One: In the twelfth month of the same seventh year (Year of the Horse), he was appointed to the Sagami Province garrison's bannerman-attached yoriai-gumi (assembly group). His seat was incorporated into the go-karō-attached third yoriai-gumi in order of seniority, and he was additionally appointed to serve concurrently as overseer of military equipment (bugugata).

One: In the same month, regarding his duties as zokuji (clan/family administrator), since the collection of outstanding payments was still in progress, he was instructed to continue in his role as before.

One: In the eighth month of the same eighth year (Year of the Sheep), he departed, arriving at the Kannonsaki御陣屋 (garrison outpost at Kannonsaki) on the eighth day of the ninth month.

One: In the eighth month of the same ninth year (Year of the Monkey), he was reassigned to the Misaki garrison, was additionally appointed to serve concurrently as zokuji (clan administrator), and was relieved of his concurrent duties as overseer of military equipment (bugugata).
