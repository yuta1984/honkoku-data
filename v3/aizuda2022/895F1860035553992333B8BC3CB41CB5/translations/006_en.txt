One entry: 1st month of the same 13th year [Kyōhō 13, 1728], the year of the Monkey, he was appointed as *Okachi* (foot attendant) for the chambers of Lord Dojō-sama.

One entry: 4th month of the same 14th year [Kyōhō 14, 1729], the year of the Rooster, during the lord's residence in the domain (*go-zaikoku*), he was appointed as *Sadame Kari-metsuke* (standing temporary inspector) at the Upper Residence (*Kami Oyashiki*), and carried out his duties through the 4th month of the following year [year of the Dog].

One entry: 10th month of the same 15th year [Kyōhō 15, 1730], the year of the Dog, he was appointed to *Oshiro Iriban* (castle entry guard duty), and carried out his duties through the 4th month of the following year [year of the Boar].

One entry: 7th month of the same 16th year [Kyōhō 16, 1731], the year of the Boar, he was appointed to the duty of overseeing the preparation of the lord's swords (*go-koshimono go-koshirae go-yō*), and carried out his duties through the 12th month of the same year.
