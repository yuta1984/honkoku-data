- In the twelfth month of the second year of Bunka (Year of the Ox [1805]), as the mortal remains (*gosonkai*) of Lady Teishō were to be conveyed down to Aizu, he was appointed to accompany the procession by order of *go-hitosashi* (personal designation/direct appointment).
　　*Note: In connection with the above, it was formally declared that he had applied himself with great diligence in this service.*

- In the second month of the third year of the same era (Year of the Tiger [1806]), as he had been managing the paper storehouse, the candle storehouse, and the formal attire lending storehouse, and as official business at those facilities had at that time become less frequent, he submitted a petition requesting that he be exempted from his duties of attending and witnessing inspections (*tachiri-tachiai*). In response, he was appointed to conduct inspections of those storehouses henceforth on a rotating basis,

- In the third month of the same year, as he had rendered particularly distinguished service during a fire that broke out along the road in front of the Shiba residence (*Shiba o-yashiki*), he was granted sake as a reward.
