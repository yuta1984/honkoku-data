**[Right folio]**

- In the eighth month of the same year [Kansei 4, 1792], as Lord Teishō performed the role of representative (代参) at Nikkōsan, [the retainer] accompanied him in attendance.

- In the tenth month of the same year, [he] was appointed to castle guard duty (御城入番).

- In the eleventh month of the same year, having applied himself diligently to the arts of both civil and military learning, it was decreed that on the 17th of the following day he would be dispatched to Kanagawa, and that sake would be bestowed upon him at Suzugamori, at the establishment of Denkichi.

*(Continued from the previous folio)*: For having achieved perfect attendance throughout the year, a reward of 2 *bu* in gold was bestowed upon him.

**[Left folio]**

- In the tenth month of the sixth year [Kansei 6, 1794, year of the Tiger]: For having served without interruption in the inner castle guard from the fourth month of that year to the present time, 2 *bu* in gold was bestowed as compensation for his efforts.

- In the fifth month of the seventh year [Kansei 7, 1795, year of the Rabbit]: For having served without interruption in castle guard duty from the previous tenth month to the present time, 2 *bu* in gold was bestowed as compensation for his efforts.

- In the ninth month of the ninth year [Kansei 9, 1797, year of the Snake]: For having achieved perfect attendance without interruption from the eighth month of the Tiger year, four years prior, through the eighth month of the current year — a full three years — a reward of 200 *hiki* in gold was bestowed.

- In the tenth month of the same year, [regarding] castle guard duty from the fourth month of the current year onward… *(continues on the next folio)*
