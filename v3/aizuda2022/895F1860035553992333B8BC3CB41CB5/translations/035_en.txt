[Marginal note in red ink]

On the 6th day of the 1st month of Hōei 5, the Year of the Rat (1708): It was reported that Ikari Kisōemon, the elder brother (義兄, brother-in-law or sworn brother) of Shirato Muemon, had committed some impropriety (fuchōhō) and received a reprimand from the domain. As a senior retainer of long standing, [Kisōemon] submitted a petition stating that he ought to refrain [from public activities] out of deference. In response, [the domain] conveyed to him that while official duties were a separate matter, he should conduct himself with restraint in private affairs. This account is found in the official diary (nikki). As a result of this record, it appears that Kasuke Mitsushige had an elder brother; however, since no such information has been transmitted within the family, this note has been appended here for reference.
