However, in the third month of the same year, as the foreign vessel had departed, he was ordered to withdraw, and accordingly vacated his post that same month.

- In the third month of the second year of Bunsei (Year of the Rabbit, 1819), following the assumption of responsibility for Sagami Province (*Sōshū*), foreign ships (*ikokubune*) had drifted into the area on repeated occasions, and during the preceding summer, one had for the first time approached the waters off the domain's coastal territory. He worked diligently in the tightening of security and management at the garrison (*Ojinya*) and in attending to the aforementioned duties. In recognition of this, he was awarded one hundred *hiki* of gold as a reward.

- In the first month of the fourth year (Year of the Snake, 1821), while retaining his current seat rank (*mochiseki*), he was appointed to the post of *Jōzume Gojō Kōjōban* (permanent-garrison official message/oral-report officer).

**[Left Side]**

However, as there was official business to attend to in Edo, he was instructed to make preparations and travel down to Edo as soon as he was ready. He departed on the eighth day of the second month of the same year, and arrived at the domain residence in Shiba, Edo, on the ninth day.

- In the ninth month of the same year, in recognition of his continuous and devoted service at the Sagami defensive post (*Osobaba*) from its very beginning over many years, he was granted three hundred *hiki* of gold.

- In the eleventh month of the same year, he was appointed as caretaker (*yakatamori*) of the Fukagawa residence.
