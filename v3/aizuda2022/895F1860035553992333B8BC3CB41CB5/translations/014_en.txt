One entry: In the twelfth month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent service, and though it was a matter of great significance, he was granted the rank of *dokurei* (the privilege of a private audience with the lord).

One entry: In the twelfth month of Tenmei 2 (Year of the Tiger), in recognition of his unimpeded service in private duties for the lord, he was privately awarded a bonus of 200 *hiki* in gold as a mark of commendation.

One entry: On the sixteenth day of the tenth month of the following year (Year of the Hare, Tenmei 3), he died of illness at the age of fifty-six.
Posthumous Buddhist name: Yūgen-in Ryōtatsu Shinji. Burial place: same as above (as previously noted).

**[Daughter]**
Mother: Same as above.

One entry: Year and month unknown.
She was summoned to serve as a lady-in-waiting (*osoba jochū*) to Rin'kō-in-sama, and accompanied the lady as an attendant (*o-tsuke*) for the wedding to Lord Inaba Tango-no-kami-sama. When Lord Inaba was appointed *wakadoshiyori* (Junior Elder), she changed her name to "Okitsu." After the lord's passing, she was assigned as an attendant to the lord's adopted daughter, was appointed *rōjo* (senior lady-in-waiting), and accompanied that lady as an attendant for her wedding to Lord Arima Saheiji-no-suke-sama.
