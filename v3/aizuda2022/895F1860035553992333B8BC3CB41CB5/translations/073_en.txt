【Right Folio】
Item: In the fifth month of the same year (Meiwa 1 [1764]):
　　On the occasion of the Lord Young Master's birth celebration, and the invitation of the Senior Councilors (Rōjū), he was appointed to a duty role and received a clothing stipend.

Item: In the third month of the second year of the same era (Meiwa 2 [1765], Year of the Rooster):
　　On the occasion of Lady Rikōin's wedding ceremony, he was appointed to a duty role and received a clothing stipend.

Item: In the third month of the third year (Meiwa 3 [1766], Year of the Dog):
　　On the occasion of the Lord's coming-of-age hair-cutting ceremony (gorihatsu), a saddled horse was presented as a gift. It was announced that he had applied great effort and produced excellent results with regard to the decorative tassels (atsufusa) and the horse equipment.

【Left Folio】
　　　Note: In the same month, as the presentation was completed successfully, sake was bestowed upon him.

Item: In the fifth month of the same year:
　　On the occasion of the young lord's coming-of-age hair-cutting ceremony celebration, and the invitation of the Senior Councilors, he was appointed to a duty role and received a clothing stipend of three bu (金三分).

Item: In the second month of the eighth year of the same era (Meiwa 8 [1771], Year of the Rabbit):
　　He was relieved of his concurrent post as Officer of Military Weapons (gohyōki-yaku).

Item: In the ninth month of the fifth year of An'ei (1776, Year of the Monkey):
　　He was appointed once again to the concurrent post of Officer of Military Weapons.
