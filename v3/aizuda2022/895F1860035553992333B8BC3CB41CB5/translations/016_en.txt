**[Left folio]**
- In the 12th month of Tenmei 3 (1783, Year of the Rabbit): Following the death of his father Sagenjii, four *koku* were deducted from his father's *gisaku* (provisional stipend), and he was granted eight *koku* and a stipend for two persons, and was called into service under the Aizu *Katte-ginmisho* (Office of Financial Affairs) without a specific post.

- In the 2nd month of Tenmei 4 (1784, Year of the Dragon): Due to illness, his petition to remain in Edo as before, until his recovery, was granted.

- In Tenmei 6 (1786, Year of the Horse), month unknown: On the occasion of a formal banquet (*shōsei*), all officials of the *Kananjosho* (Accounting Office) were ordered to perform serving duties, and they practiced the proper serving of meals (*haizen*).
