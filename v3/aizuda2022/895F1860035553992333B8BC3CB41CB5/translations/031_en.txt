**[Right folio]**

One: In the fifth month of the same year, in connection with the lord's return journey (go-gekō) from Edo, he was ordered to accompany the procession and return with it.

One: In the third month of Bunka 15 (Year of the Tiger), as he had applied himself diligently over a number of years, and had rendered especially distinguished service in his additional concurrent duties (kaku-yaku), by special consideration his seat-rank (seki) was raised to the position immediately below that of Akiyama Ryōsuke.

One: In the fourth month of the same year, he was appointed to the post of go-shiki-gakari (officer in charge of ceremonies and formal protocol).

One: In the third month of Bunka 2 (Year of the Rabbit, 1819), as Sakazaki Tono's son Sagoro, who had been disinherited (taicha-ku), was to be sent down to Aizu, he was ordered to accompany and escort him, traveled down to Aizu, and handed him over to the Kōjisho (the office handling legal and judicial matters).

**[Left folio]**

One: In the (intercalary) fourth month of the same year, he was appointed to concurrently serve as an examiner (ginmi-yaku) for the permanent construction office (jōdzume go-fushin-kata) and as manager of receipts and disbursements of labor and materials (hitodemon ukebari). His official stipend (o-yakuryō) of two koku was to continue to be granted as before.

One: In connection with the same month's appointment to the concurrent duties of examiner for the permanent construction contracting office (jōdzume go-fushin-ukekata) and manager of receipts and disbursements of labor and materials, he was ordered that whenever construction works, repairs, or maintenance were carried out at any of the three residences (the upper, middle, and lower residences — kami, naka, and shimo o-yashiki), he was to proceed there and conduct his inspection and examination duties.

One: In the same month, he was ordered to continue performing his duties as officer in charge of the inquiry/investigation department (go-tadashi-kata) and the ceremonial office (go-shiki-kata), as he had done hitherto.
