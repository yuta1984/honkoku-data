- In the second month of the same year, he was appointed to the role of *Shōnin-warigata* (person-allocation officer for upper-rank personnel).
　　*Note: Since there were expenses involved in carrying out this appointed role, he was granted two *bu* in gold annually as a subsidy for those costs.*

- In the twelfth month of the same year, he was instructed that in the event of a fire breaking out, he was to immediately proceed to a place from which the fire could be seen, assess the direction and distance, and, should the deployment of personnel become necessary, enter the area in his capacity as officer in charge of the allocation division and arrange the deployment of men.

- On the same day, as fires had broken out frequently during this period and he had worked diligently in deploying personnel on each occasion, he was granted rice wine (*sake*) as a reward.

- In the fourth month of the third year (Year of the Boar),

**[Left folio]**

he served at the temple on each occasion of the funeral procession and memorial services for Lady Shōju-in (*Shōju-in-sama*), rendering particularly dedicated service, and was therefore granted rice wine as a reward.

- In the seventh month of the same year, it was noted that when there were persons to be investigated, the procedures had previously fallen behind, and there were instances in which the investigation had not been carried out thoroughly. He was therefore instructed to cultivate an amicable relationship in advance with the town constables (*dōshin*) who regularly interacted with the domain, and was ordered through the *okikiban* (duty officer for receiving information) to arrange such introductions.
