**[Right folio]**
Sagenji Naotsugi (childhood name: Jinroku)

Mother: Daughter of the Yoshimura clan
Wife: Daughter of Taniai (Hachiōji dōshin) Kanroku Korihisa
　　[She died of illness on the 24th day of the 9th month of Tenmei 3 (1783), hare year. Posthumous Buddhist name: Shūgetsu Myōshō Shinnyo. Place of burial: same as above.]

- On New Year's Day of Enkyō 4 (1747, hare year), he was appointed as a temporary hired foot soldier (o-kachi o-yatoi) to assist with the presentation of ceremonial offerings.
　　Note: He performed this duty once thereafter as well.

- In the 4th month of the same year, in connection with the lord's attendance at Edo (go-sanpu), he was appointed to assist with the

**[Left folio]**
　　presentation of ceremonial offerings.

- In the 9th month of the same year, he was appointed as a temporary hired foot soldier (tōza o-yatoi) and served until the 10th month of the same year.

- In the 3rd month of Kan'en 1 (1748, dragon year), he was appointed as a temporary hired foot soldier for Lady Geneiin, and served until the 4th month of the same year.

- In the 5th month of the same year, he was appointed as a temporary hired foot soldier, and served until the 10th month of the same year.

- In the 10th month of the same year, he was formally summoned as a foot soldier (o-kachi) in service to Lady Ganshōin, with a stipend of three ryō and a ration for two men (san-ryō futari-fuchi).
