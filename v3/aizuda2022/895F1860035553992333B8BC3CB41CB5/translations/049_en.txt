　　Wife: Daughter of Furukawa Wanai Katsuoki

- In the 3rd month of the 6th year of Kansei (Kinoe-tora, 1794), having applied himself diligently to the martial arts in his daily life and having performed his skills admirably on the occasion of a review before the lord, he received a commendation (shōbi) and was granted sake.
- In the 5th month of the 7th year of Kansei (Kinoto-u, 1795), having diligently served in the supervision and management of the Bugakuryō (Military Studies Academy) and the private training hall (yaka-keikoba), performed his skills admirably, and also served in the instruction and correction of students (shōsei), he was granted the freedom to attend the literary and military academies at his own discretion (i.e., as he saw fit).
- In the 10th month of the 12th year of Kansei (Kanoe-saru, 1800), three koku and one person's ration (hitori-fuchi) were deducted from his late father's allotted stipend (gizaku), and he was granted twelve koku and two persons' rations, and was called into service as an o-kachi (foot soldier/infantry retainer).
