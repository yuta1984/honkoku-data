Item: In the first month of the second year (Year of the Snake), he was appointed as a one-year inspector at the Rice Transport Office (Mawarimai-dokoro).
　　Note: While serving in this appointed capacity at the Rice Transport Office, in the twelfth month of the same year, when the peasants mounted a forcible petition (強訴, gōso), he was sent by official designation to Shiokawa Village, where he carried out his assigned duties.

Item: In the second month of the third year (Year of the Horse), when the peasants of Inawashiro were arrested, he was dispatched by official designation and carried out his duties, for which he was commended for his dedicated service.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, he traveled to Edo for his rotation of guard duty (kinban). However, as the sacred remains (go-sonkotsu) of Lord Hani (Tsuchimoto-sama) were being brought down [to the domain], he was appointed to serve in all matters during the journey,

[Left folio]
　　including attending to the accompanying envoys and other such duties, and he returned [to the domain] as part of the escort party.

Item: In the eighth month of the first year of Hōreki (Year of the Ram), as the domain inspector officials (go-kuni-metsuke-shū) were to descend [to the domain from Edo], he was appointed to travel to Edo to receive them. He traveled up to Edo and returned together with the party. During their inspection tour of villages (go-junson), he was further appointed to serve as an escort, attending to lodging arrangements and other duties along the way, and also accompanied them on their return journey to Edo.
　　Note: He was commended for his dedicated service in these matters, and was granted one ryō of gold as a reward.

Item: In the eighth month of the third year (Year of the Rooster), he died of illness at the age of fifty-three. His posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō) is Tan'yo...
