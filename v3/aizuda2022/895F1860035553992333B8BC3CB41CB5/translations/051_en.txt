**[Right folio]**
　　He was additionally appointed to serve concurrently as a writing clerk (fude-yaku) for the Tokusazu [planning/drafting role] under Yokoyama Kazuma. In connection with this, an expense supplement of one ryō and two bu per year was granted without deduction (mu-okariage).

- In the 2nd month of the 3rd year of the same era (Tora, 1806), he was appointed to attend the post-town wholesale agent (eki-tonya) of the local town, and was instructed to handle matters through consultation with the town office as well. Furthermore, when goods belonging to the Lord's kitchen (o-daidokoro) and the meeting hall (kaisho) were to be dispatched, he was additionally appointed to attend to the packing of cargo and to oversee the horse-transit passes (uma-kitte), and handled matters pertaining to both the official requisition orders for persons and horses (kōben no jinba o-kakidashi).

- In the 4th month of the 4th year of the same era (U, 1807), he was appointed concurrently to the Office of Retrenchment (o-shōryaku-kata). Regarding his duties as foot-soldier (o-kachi), his attendance-verification duties (tachiai) were discontinued in the same month; instead, he served the Office of Retrenchment by attending and performing the duties of a liaison inspector (tachiai-yakunin) across the various offices. His concurrent post at the post-station office (eki-kata) was thereupon relieved.

**[Left folio]**

- From the same month, he had been reporting for duty to the Construction Office (o-fushin-kata) and the Kowateri Office (kowateri-kata). However, since it was found that the post-station regulatory office (ekisho shimari-kata yakuba) could not manage official business with only the currently assigned staff, he was appointed to take over that post; and until he had become accustomed to the role, he was instructed to continue reporting to the post-station regulatory office as before.
　　　Note: During slack periods at the regulatory office, he was instructed to make rotating attendance rounds (kaikin) at the Kowateri Office and any other office as needed.
