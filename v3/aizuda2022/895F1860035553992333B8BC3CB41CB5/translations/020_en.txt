**[Right folio]**

- In the third month of the same year, he was appointed as a permanent temporary inspector (*jō-kari metsuke*) at the Shiba residence, and served until the seventh month of the same year.

- In the fifth month of the same year, it was noted that while previously serving as a permanent temporary inspector, during the night of the second day of the eighth month, when tools belonging to a town carpenter went missing at the carpenter's workshop (*warijoba daiku-goya*) at the Shiba residence, he exerted every effort and succeeded in locating them. As a reward, he was granted one hundred *hiki* in gold.

- In the tenth month of the same year, he was appointed to the duty of overseeing the preparation of the lord's swords (*okoshimono ogoshirae*).

- In the second month of the thirteenth year (Year of the Rooster), he was appointed as a permanent temporary inspector at the Shiba residence.

**[Left folio]**

He served in this role until the fourth month.

- In the sixth month of the same year, he was again appointed as a permanent temporary inspector at the Shiba residence.

- In the first month of the second year of Kyōwa (Year of the Dog), he was appointed as a permanent (*tsunezume*) foot-soldier inspector (*okachi metsuke*), and was granted a stipend of three *koku* and one person's ration (*hitori fuchi*) as his official salary, along with one person's Edo ration (*Edo fuchi*). His previous servant's ration (*komono fuchi*) of one person was rescinded. He was also appointed to the roles of investigator (*tadashi-kata*) and ceremonial affairs officer (*oshikikata*).

　　*Note: On account of the above, he relocated to the upper residence (kamiyashiki).*
