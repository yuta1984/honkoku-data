[Right folio]
　　　(continuing from previous page) He was appointed to serve as an escort accompanying the procession of ceremonial implements (o-dōgu), and having fulfilled his duties, was granted silver coins as a congratulatory gift from Lord Matsudaira Sadanokami, after which he returned upon completion of his assignment.

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, he was appointed as a one-year inspector at the Shinryō Office (Shinryō Yakusho), serving until the twelfth month of the same year.

Item: In the third month of the third year (Year of the Tiger), he was appointed as an inspector for the domain's loan matters (go-shakkin-kata tachiai) in Kyoto and Osaka, traveling up to the capital and returning in the first month of the following year (Year of the Rabbit).
　　Note: He was commended for having conducted the domain's loan negotiations, transport rice payments, and disbursement matters (go-shakkin, go-mawarimai, o-harai-kata) all satisfactorily in Kyoto and Osaka, and was granted three ryō of gold as a reward.

[Left folio]
Item: In the fourth month of the fourth year (Year of the Rabbit), he was appointed to duties at the Sewing/Preparation Room (o-shitatesho), serving until the fourth month of the following year (Year of the Dragon).

Item: In the first month of the first year of Kan'en (Year of the Dragon), in connection with the visit of a Korean delegation that summer and the provision of relay horses (okuri-uma) from Edo to Maisaka for their return journey, he was appointed by official designation (o-hitosashi) to travel to Edo. He departed in the fifth month and returned in the seventh month of the same year.
　　Note: Prior to his departure for Maisaka, he was granted three ryō and one bu of gold; upon his return, he was commended for having carried out his duties without delay.
