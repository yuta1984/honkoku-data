**[Right folio]**
Sukezaemon Kagenaga (childhood/earlier names: Kumenosuke, then Kihachi, then Kinrokuró; born on the 2nd day of the 12th month of Kyóhó 10 [1725]):
　　Mother: Same as above (i.e., the same mother as the previous entry)
　　Wife: Daughter of Higuchi Yahei Yasukatsu (born on the 25th day of the 3rd month of Kyóhó 17 [1732]; died of illness on the 8th day of the 3rd month of Bunka 2 [1805], at the age of 74; posthumous Buddhist name: Tokuyо Myóhó Shinnyо; burial place: Kodate-yama Jóanji Temple)

One entry: In the 7th month of the 1st year of Kan'en [1748], the decades of meritorious service rendered by his father Rokubei, who was on duty as a guard at the Fukagawa residence, were acknowledged, and [Kagenaga] was ordered to serve as a substitute during periods when his father was hindered by illness or similar circumstances.

**[Left folio]**
　　　Note: Even when not due to illness, it was ordered that when multiple concurrent duties at the residence arose simultaneously and required attendance, he was to fulfill one of those duties.

One entry: In the 1st month of the 1st year of Hóreki [1751], as a congratulatory gift upon [his father's] succession of the household headship, a meal was bestowed upon [Kagenaga] in the Room of the Clock (Tokei no Ma).
　　　Note: This was bestowed on account of his father Rokubei's illness.

One entry: In the same 2nd year [1752], in the 1st month of the year of the Monkey, his father Rokubei died of illness, and while [Kagenaga] was in mourning, he was ordered to continue his duties as before until further official appointments were made, and he was granted an exemption from the full observance of mourning restrictions.
