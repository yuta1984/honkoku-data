- In the same month, he was appointed to attend and witness (*tachiri-tachiai*) the inspection of the *ofuchi-gura* (stipend rice storehouse), the paper storehouse, and the candle storehouse regarding the lending of formal attire (*okashi-shōzoku*). Regarding the *ofuchi-gura*, he was further instructed to carry out the accounting (*okanjō*) as well.

- In the same month, he was appointed to attend and witness inspections of storehouses at various locations. However, as this made it difficult to keep up with his primary duties, he was granted exemption (*goyōsha*) from his *onagi tōban* (duty rotation serving in the anteroom/attendant corps).

- In the twelfth month of the same year, as he had applied himself diligently during the recent internal investigation (*otadashi-kata nai-yō*),

**[Left folio]**

he was granted one hundred *hiki* in gold as a reward.

- In the first month of the fourth year (Year of the Rat), while continuing his duties of attending and witnessing inspections, he was additionally appointed to concurrently serve as *ō-makanai-yaku* (chief provisioning officer).

- On the same day, he was additionally appointed to concurrently oversee the *mawarimai-kata* (rice transportation management), and was instructed to carry out the accounting of *demokoku* (surplus grain yielded beyond the stipulated amount).

- In the third month of the same year, as his concurrent duties of *ō-makanai* and various other roles — including attending and witnessing storehouse inspections — had become exceedingly busy, he was granted exemption from accompanying Lady Teishō (*Teishō-sama*).
　　*Note: He was also granted exemption from his regular duty-rotation attendance in his primary post.*
