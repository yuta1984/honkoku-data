Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, as Nezу Mohei was sent down to Aizu by palanquin (ami-kago), he accompanied him on the journey back [to Aizu], and in the first month of the following year of the Monkey, traveled up to Edo once again. In the fifth month of the same year, he served in attendance during the lord's return to the domain and returned [to the domain].

Item: In the first month of the same sixth year [of An'ei, 1777] (Year of the Rooster), he had been appointed as a one-year inspector at the Rice Distribution Office (kaimai-sho ichinen tachiai). As it was found that there was substandard rice (aku-mai) at the Akutsu riverbank landing (Akutsu-gashi), he was ordered to proceed there and personally verify the situation. Having traveled there and completed his assigned duties, he returned [to the domain]. Then, in the twelfth month of the same year, as sake brewing was suspended, he was appointed by designation (go-hitosashi) simultaneously as inspector of the sake merchant barrel-volume duty (sakaya okudaka-yaku tachiai) and as inspector of the closing and accounting office (shimeri-kata tachiai). During intervals between these duties, he was ordered to attend and serve as inspector at the Rice Distribution Office (kaimai-kata).
