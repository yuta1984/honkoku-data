Item: In the tenth month of the same seventh year [of Hōreki, 1757] (Year of the Ox), he traveled to Edo for his rotation of guard duty (kinban), whereupon he was appointed as an extraordinary provisional inspector (fuji kari-metsuke). From the fourth month of the following year of the Tiger, he served as castle entry guard (o-shiro nyūban), and returned in the same month.
　　Note: As he was appointed and served as an extraordinary provisional inspector, a compensation of two bu (gold) was granted.

Item: In the ninth month of the same eighth year [of Hōreki, 1758] (Year of the Tiger), he was appointed as a temporary inspector at the Rice Transport Office (Mawarimai-kata tōza tachiai), serving until the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: For this service, a compensation was granted in accordance with established precedent.

Item: In the fourth month of the same ninth year [of Hōreki, 1759] (Year of the Rabbit), on the occasion of the lord's entry into the domain (go-nyūbu), he returned [to the domain] to serve as part of the welcoming party, traveled to [Edo], served in attendance, and returned [to the domain].

[Left folio]
Item: In the first month of the same tenth year [of Hōreki, 1760] (Year of the Dragon), he returned and traveled to [Edo] to serve in attendance during the lord's journey to Kyoto (go-jōkyō go-tomo). During the journey, he was appointed through the designation of personnel for boat assignments (go-funahai go-hitosashi) for both the outward and return trips. During the lord's stay in Kyoto, he served as a hired ledger keeper at the official lodging (go-ryokan o-chōtsuke o-yatoi).

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, he was appointed as a provisional inspector (kari-metsuke) equivalent to the full position (honroku dōzen).

Item: In the first month of the same twelfth year [of Hōreki, 1762] (Year of the Horse), it was proclaimed that he had promptly rushed to attend to the fire that broke out at the residence of Nomura Unji during the previous winter.

Item: In the third month of the same year, he was appointed as acting group leader (kumigashira-dai), serving until the ninth month of the same year.
