**[Right folio]**

- In the 8th month of the 7th year of the same era (tora/tiger year, 1770), as a result of his distinguished service during the fire that broke out in front of the Shiba residence on the 19th of the preceding month, he was granted two bu in gold.

- In the 2nd month of the 8th year (u/rabbit year, 1771), he was appointed as a sub-inspector accompanying [the lord's] retinue (o-tomo-saki shita-metsuke).

- In the 9th month of the same year, he was appointed as a permanent/standing foot-soldier inspector (jō-zume o-kachi-metsuke), and was granted a salary stipend (o-yaku-ryō) of three koku and one-person ration, plus two-person rations for Edo subsistence allowance; however, the one-person ration for a manservant (kozō-fuchi) that he had previously received was rescinded.

- In the 5th month of An'ei 2 (mi/snake year, 1773), during [the period of] the current domain lord, he was appointed as a regular supervisor at the Shiba meeting hall (Shiba kaijo jō-tachiai), and served until the 4th month of the following uma/horse year (1774).

**[Left folio]**

- In the 5th month of the 4th year of the same era (hitsuji/sheep year, 1775), he was appointed as a regular supervisor at the Shiba meeting hall (Shiba kaijo jō-tachiai), and served until the 8th month of the following saru/monkey year (1776).

- In the 9th month of the same year, due to construction work on the inner palace (oku-o-den go-fushin), he was appointed as a regular supervisor (jō-tachiai), and served until the 12th month of the same year.

- In the 11th month of the 7th year of the same era (inu/dog year, 1778), he was ordered to undertake a confidential duty (go-naimitsugoyō), and performed it accordingly.
