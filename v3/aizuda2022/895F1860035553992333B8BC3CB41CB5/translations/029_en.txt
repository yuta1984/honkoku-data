One: In the second month of the same thirteenth year (Year of the Boar), having already been assigned to serve as an inspector (tachiri-kin) for the upper construction department's (Kami-Ofushin-kata) allocation division (Warikata), he was additionally ordered to enter and perform duties relating to the receipt and payment of materials for the construction department.

One: In the fourth month of the same year, as the domain lord made his formal entry (nyūbu) into the domain, upon his own request, he was ordered to accompany the lord and return to the domain.

One: In the same month, as a foreign vessel had been sighted off Sōshū (Sagami Province, present-day Kanagawa Prefecture), he was informed that he might be dispatched to that location at short notice and was ordered to remain in a state of readiness. He accordingly reported that he had immediately prepared his equipment and provisions.

**[Left folio]**

One: In the first month of the same thirteenth year (Year of the Rat), he was ordered to travel to Kyoto as an official envoy to investigate and arrange lodgings (hatagoya) in advance of the lord's visit to the capital, with instructions to depart within two or three days. Furthermore, he was instructed that since the arrangement of lodgings is a matter of great importance bearing upon the domain's financial gain and loss, he should not rely on personal favors or hospitality, but should negotiate arrangements so that expenditures are kept to a minimum; at post stations such as ferry crossings, he was to make thorough inquiries at the preceding lodging as well. Additionally, since personnel for on-site negotiations at the lodgings would also be dispatched, all were to proceed together and consult one another so as to ensure there were no inconveniences, and to manage affairs appropriately.
