**[Right folio]**

　　His official stipend (o-yaku-ryō) of one koku and one person's ration (ichi-nin-buchi) was maintained at the same level as before.

- In the 3rd month of the 10th year of the same era (Tori, 1813), he was appointed as Minor Magistrate Attached to the Castle (o-shiro jōtsuki ko-bugyō).

- In the 5th month of the 11th year of the same era (Inu, 1814), as unrest had broken out in the vicinity of Nakajō in the Shirakawa Domain Custody Area (Shirakawa o-azukari-sho), a contingent of personnel was dispatched, and he was ordered to proceed to and guard the Akatani-guchi (Akatani Gate/Pass). He traveled there and remained stationed for several days, after which, as the situation had quieted down, he was ordered to withdraw and returned to his post.

- In the same month, the men who had been dispatched to the border (o-sakaime) had always been diligent in their duties, promptly mobilized, and worked hard throughout the summer heat. In recognition of this, they were presented with sake at the school.

**[Left folio]**

- In the 1st month of the 13th year of the same era (Ne, 1816), spanning three consecutive years—beginning with various repair works (o-shūfuku) within the castle, followed by the re-thatching of the shrine (o-miya), and extending through to the duties surrounding the Lord's entry into the domain (o-nyūbu)—he applied himself meticulously and with great care to reducing the expenditures (o-iri-kata) for these large-scale construction projects (o-fushinjō), and also reviewed and reformed the management procedures of the Construction Office (o-fushin-kata). In recognition of his diligent service, he was awarded a reward of five hundred hiki in gold (kin go-hyap-piki).

- In the 10th month of the 14th year of the same era (Ushi, 1817), in connection with the stone wall construction work (ishigaki no o-fushinjō) within the castle, including the rebuilding of the turrets (o-yagura) and tile walls (kawara-bei), he served with great diligence and was awarded a reward of three bu in gold (kin san-bu).

- In the 2nd month of the 5th year of Bunsei (Uma, 1822), in connection with the construction of a temporary palace (o-kariden) at the Third Citadel (San no O-maru), he remained on duty day and night without interruption, attending to various official duties (o-yōmuki).
