Item: In the sixth month of the same year, he was appointed to a confidential mission (mitsuji go-yō) and traveled to another domain to carry out his duties.

Item: In the third month of the fifth year (Year of the Monkey), he accompanied the lord on his sankin-kōtai journey to Edo as an escort on guard duty, returning in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: During his tour of duty, from the seventh to the tenth month, he was appointed as a regular inspector (sadame tachiai) for the construction works at Meiō-in in Ueno, traveling there daily to fulfill his duties, and was granted two bu of gold as a reward.

Item: In the seventh month of the first year of Kanpō (Year of the Rooster), he was appointed as a one-year inspector at the examination office (ginmi-sho), serving until the seventh month of the following year (Year of the Dog).

Item: In the ninth month of the second year (Year of the Dog), he was appointed as a six-month inspector at the carpenters' workshop (daiku-goya).

[Left folio]
Item: In the ninth month of the third year (Year of the Boar), he traveled to Niigata to serve as an inspector for the procurement of fish offerings (o-sakana chōgi), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, he was appointed by official designation (o-hitosashi) to manage the operations of the sewing/preparation room (o-shitateсho) at the Third Citadel (San no O-maru), serving until the third month of the following year (Year of the Rat).

Item: In the fourth month of the first year of Enkyō (Year of the Rat), he was appointed to escort a court lady (jochū) traveling to Edo, and was granted two bu of gold as a reward for his journey there and back.
