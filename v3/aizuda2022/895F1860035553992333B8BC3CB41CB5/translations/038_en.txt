...until the third month of the *申* (Saru/Monkey) year, he served in this role.
　　Note: In connection with this, he was granted one *ryō* of gold.

Item: In the third month of the same year,
　　When Lord Tsuchijo-sama (Lord Matsudaira Katanobu) traveled to Edo for the first time, the daughter of Ōkuma Mohei—an *o-kachi metsuke* (foot-soldier inspector) at the third enclosure of the castle—fell gravely ill, resulting in his being excused from serving in the escort. Consequently, on the day before the lord's departure, he was appointed acting *metsuke* (inspector) and traveled to Edo as part of the escort.
　　Note: Having been appointed acting *metsuke*, he promptly accepted and departed, and was commended for carrying out the journey without any hindrance. As a reward, he was granted two *bu* of gold. He was also assigned to inspect the Mito Highway and thereafter returned to the domain.

Item: In the tenth month of the same thirteenth year (*申*), he was appointed to assist with the rice revenue collection (*o-shūnō-kata tachiai*).

Item: In the third month of the fifteenth year (*亥*), he was appointed acting foot-soldier inspector (*o-kachi kari-metsuke*).

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, he was appointed to temporarily assist with construction matters (*o-fushin-kata tōza tachiai*).

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, while already serving as acting *metsuke*, he was additionally assigned to irregular duty (*fujiji kinban*). In the twelfth month of the same year, he traveled to Edo, and in the second month of the following *子* (Ne/Rat) year, he was appointed acting *metsuke*. In the fourth month of the same year, upon the lord's return to the domain, he was appointed permanent acting *metsuke* and returned as part of the escort.
