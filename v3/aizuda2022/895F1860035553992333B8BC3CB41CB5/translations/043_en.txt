[Left folio]
Item: In the tenth month of the third year of Hōreki [1753] (Year of the Rooster), he was granted twelve koku and two-person stipend (ninin-fuchi) from his late father's allotted stipend (go-gisaku), and was called into service as an O-kachi (foot soldier retainer).

Item: In the fourth month of the fourth year [of Hōreki, 1754] (Year of the Dog), he traveled to Edo for his rotation of guard duty (kinban), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: He was appointed to serve as a battle standard bearer/administrator (jinchū o-matoi saiban) during this period.

Item: In the second month of the fifth year [of Hōreki, 1755] (Year of the Boar), he was appointed as a temporary inspector at the Rice Transport Office (Mawarimai-kata tōza tachiai), serving until the third month of the same year.
　　Note: For this service, a compensation for his efforts (hone-ori-ryō) was granted in accordance with established precedent.

Item: In the third month of the seventh year [of Hōreki, 1757] (Year of the Ox), he was appointed as an inspector for the Community Granary food-relief rice loans (shaso fushoku kashimai tachiai), serving until the fifth month of the same year.
