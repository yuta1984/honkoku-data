Genzaburō Mitsunori — See separate lineage record
　Mother: Same as above (as previously recorded)
　　Note: In the genealogy of Higuchi Genta of the same surname, the name "Genzaburō Mitsuhira" appears; it is therefore possible that he was first known as Mitsunori and later changed his name to Mitsuhira.

- In the tenth year of Kanbun, the year of the Dog (1670), in the sixth month: [He] submitted a petition requesting that the stipend of 150 koku, which had been granted as a replacement stipend to his father Jūemon, be used to appoint his second son Genzaburō as a hereditary substitute (bandai) in his service position. The petition was granted, and Genzaburō was duly appointed as the hereditary substitute as requested.

Kosaburō — It is recorded that he died of illness in childhood. The date, posthumous Buddhist name, and place of burial are unknown.
