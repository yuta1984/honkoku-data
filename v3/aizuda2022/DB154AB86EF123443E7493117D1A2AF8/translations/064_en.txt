Item: In the 2nd month of the 5th year of An'ei, the year of the Monkey (1776), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (o-mie) with the lord.

Item: In the 12th month of the 8th year of Tenmei, the year of the Monkey (1788), he was appointed to serve as a Shimeri-kata (regulatory/supervisory officer) of the Shinten-ryū school of swordsmanship, and proceeded to attend at the Bugakuryō (Military Studies Institute).
