He received these gracious words with deep gratitude.

- In the same eighth year, the year of the Boar (1695), in the first month: In accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his official duties.

- In the seventh month of the same year: In accordance with his petition, he was granted permission to retire (inkyo).

- In the twelfth year, the year of the Rabbit (1699), in the twelfth month: Regarding the matter of comparing the administrative practices of the era of Lord Hiwada (Hoshina Masayuki, enshrined at Tōtsu-sama) with those of the current era, he received a private, handwritten inquiry from [the lord]. He was deeply moved and filled with gratitude upon receiving such a courteous and personal letter, and submitted his response of thirty-five articles under his seal.

- In the fourth year of Hōei, the year of the Boar (1707), in the ninth month: As his illness grew severe, [the lord] sent inquiries after his condition through the official secretary (goyōnin). On the fourth day of the same month, he died of illness. Posthumous Buddhist name: Chūshōin Gikō Anshin Koji. Place of burial: Shōunzan Zenryūji Temple.
