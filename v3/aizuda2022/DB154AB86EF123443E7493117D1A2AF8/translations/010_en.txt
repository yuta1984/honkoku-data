(Continued from previous page) ...for his diligent service in the construction works (普請) at Edo Castle, he was informed by the Senior Councillor (Rōjū), Lord Toda Yamashiro-no-Kami, that he was to receive twenty silver coins (hakugin), three seasonal garments (jifuku), and one formal haori jacket.

- In the same fourth year [of Genroku], the year of the Ram (1691), on the second day of the intercalary eighth month, [the lord] made his journey down [from Edo to the domain]. He was appointed as messenger to announce the lord's arrival at the domain, was summoned before the lord, granted leave, and received two seasonal garments. He immediately set out, arriving in Edo at the hour of the Fifth Watch in the morning (approximately 8 a.m.) on the seventh day of the same month. On that same day, he called upon Lord Abe Bungo-no-Kami to perform his messenger duties, and, following that lord's instructions, immediately proceeded to carry out his messenger duties to the Senior Councillors (Rōjū), including Lord Makino Bingo-no-Kami and Lord Yanagisawa Dewa-no-Kami, as well as to the Junior Councillors (Wakadoshiyori). Furthermore, he also carried out messenger duties to the Lord of Kōfu, the Three Gosanke Houses, Lady Chiyo-hime, Lady Tsuru-hime, and the lords' personal attendants (sobashū).

On the fourteenth day of the same month, he received instructions from Lord Bungo-no-Kami to present himself at the castle at the hour of the Fifth Watch (approximately 8 a.m.) on the following fifteenth day, to deliver formal expressions of gratitude for the lord's safe arrival at the domain. Accordingly, on the fifteenth day, at the appointed hour, he proceeded up to the castle bearing the tribute offerings (kenjōmono). At the White Audience Chamber (Shiro-shoin), he was granted an audience with Lord Jōken-in [the fifth Tokugawa Shogun, Tsunayoshi]. Through the formal announcement by Lord Kuze Izumo-no-Kami, and with Lord Bungo-no-Kami serving as intermediary, the tribute offerings were successfully presented.
