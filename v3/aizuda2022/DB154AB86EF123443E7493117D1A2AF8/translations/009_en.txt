- In the second year of the same era [Enpō], the year of the Tiger (1674), on the same grounds as before, he again traveled up to Edo and performed the same duties.

- In the twelfth month of the sixth year of the same era [Enpō], the year of the Horse (1678), it was heard that he had performed his official duties admirably, and he was granted an increase of fifty koku, bringing his stipend to three hundred koku.

- In the third month of the second year of Jōkyō, the year of the Ox (1685), he traveled up to Edo on business related to an official inquiry (穿鑿, sensaku). There, he was provisionally appointed as a御用人 (goyōnin, senior official attendant), and was instructed to don formal hakama attire (kamishimo) and present himself at the lower end of the lord's side chamber.

- In the ninth month of the same year, he was appointed to the post of Magistrate (bugyō), and was instructed to affix his seal of approval (kakuhan) to official documents. He was immediately assigned to duty rotation (kinban), and descended [from Edo] in the fourth month of the following year of the Tiger (1686).

- In the fourth year of the same era [Jōkyō], the year of the Rabbit (1687), on the occasion of the lord's journey to Kyoto, he was assigned to remain on duty in his absence (るsukinban), traveled up to Edo, and performed his duties there.

- In the fifth month of the first year of Genroku, the year of the Dragon (1688), in celebration of the lord's arrival at his domain (入部, nyūbu), he received as gifts one formal summer garment (kataginu) and one unlined robe (hitoe).

- In the third month of the second year of the same era [Genroku], the year of the Snake (1689), on account of construction works (普請, fushin) at the Third Enclosure (Sannomaru) of Edo Castle, he traveled up to Edo and was appointed as chief administrator (motojiме-yaku) overseeing the works.

- In the fifth month of the same year, he was granted an increase in stipend of one hundred koku.
