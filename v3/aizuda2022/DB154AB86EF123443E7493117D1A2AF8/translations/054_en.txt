Item: In the 9th month of the same year, Lord Dojō passed away, and as his honored remains were brought down (from Edo), he performed his duties accompanying the funeral procession, and returned in the 10th month of the same year.

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent and unhindered service in the field (jin-chū), he was granted two pieces of silver (gin-su) as a reward.

Item: In the 1st year of Hōreki, the year of the Sheep (1751), month unknown, upon the visit of the domain inspector (o-kuni-metsuke-shū), he was assigned to handle the related duties (nin-yaku), and served in this capacity, dealing with all matters (de-iri), for three years.

Item: In the 2nd month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent service during a period of heavy workload since the previous winter, he was granted two pieces of silver as a reward.

Item: In the 2nd year of the same era, the year of the Monkey (1752), month unknown, upon the arrival of Lord Dojō's sacred divine seal (go-shinji) from Kyoto at the Mineyama Shrine, he was assigned to handle the related duties.
