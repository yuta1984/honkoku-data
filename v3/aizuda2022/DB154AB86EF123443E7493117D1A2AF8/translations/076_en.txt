Item: In the second month of the fourth year of Bunsei [1821], the Year of the Snake, the hereditary stipend of 150 koku was granted without alteration; he was assigned to the Tozama-gumi [outside vassal group]; and since he was not assigned to regular duties in order to pursue training in the arts, he was instructed to contribute the ko-fusin-ryō [fee in lieu of active service].
