One item: In the eighth month of the third year of Bunsei (1820), year of the Dragon, he was granted without dispute the hereditary stipend (*atoshiki go-chigyō*) of one hundred and fifty *koku*, assigned to the Aizu Katte Tozama-gumi (the Aizu Domain's outer-retainer group), and ordered to present himself accordingly. As he was not yet assigned to regular duties during the period of training in the arts (*geijutsu*), he was instructed to pay the *kopushin-ryō* (a fee levied on those not yet in active service), and he was enrolled in the Obara Mino-gumi (unit under Obara Mino's command).

One item: In the ninth month of the fifth year of Bunsei (1822), year of the Horse, as he had attained a high level of proficiency in the martial arts, he was exempted from the *kopushin-ryō*, appointed to active duty, and took up service as a castle guard (*go-shiro go-ban*).

One item: In the eighth month of the sixth year of Bunsei (1823), year of the Sheep, he participated in combined-unit training (*gōtai keiko*).

One item: In the tenth month of the same year, regarding the matter of the combined-unit training — which had been suspended since the year of the Rabbit (Bunsei 2nd year, 1819) — a lighter form of drill (*sōren*) was ordered under the existing circumstances. It was announced that his conduct in the formation, including the coordination of marching steps and overall maneuvers, proceeded without any hindrance, and that it was the lord's view that he had long been applying himself with care and dedication to his training.
