Taichu Mosetoshi (initial name: Ne) — adopted son of Kagayama Unshō Moriaki
Mother: same as above

One item: In the second month of the ninth year of Kansei (1797), year of the Snake, as he had applied himself diligently to his studies and was reported to show a level of skill remarkable for his age, he was awarded a copy of the *Shunjū Sashiden* (*Zuo Zhuan*, the Commentary on the Spring and Autumn Annals) as a reward.

One item: In the eleventh month of the first year of Bunka (1804), year of the Rat, he was appointed to serve as a temporary assistant in the mathematics accounting office (sūgaku shimari-kata).

One item: In the fifth month of the eighth year of Bunka (1811), year of the Sheep, he was appointed to serve as an assistant mathematics instructor (sūgaku shinan tetsudai).

One item: In the eleventh month of the tenth year of Bunka (1813), year of the Rooster, as he had applied himself diligently in his assigned mathematics post and had handled his students well, he was awarded one piece of silver (ginsu ichimai) as a reward.

One item: In the eighth month of the fourteenth year of Bunka (1817), year of the Ox, in accordance with his parent's petition, he was appointed as son-in-law and adopted heir (muko yōshi) of Kagayama Unshō.
