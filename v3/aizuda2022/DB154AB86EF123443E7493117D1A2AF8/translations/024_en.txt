Item: In the sixth month of An'ei 8, the year of the Pig (1779), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (o-memie) [with the domain lord].

Item: In the eleventh month of Tenmei 8, the year of the Monkey (1788), he was appointed as a reading and recitation instructor (kudoku-shi) at the Western School (Nishi Gakkō).

Item: In the first month of Kansei 1, the year of the Rooster (1789), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was appointed as a guard substitute (o-ban-dai) and served as a castle guard in the unit of Kayano Gondayū.

Item: In the fourth month of the same year, he was appointed as an acting district magistrate (o-daikan kari-yaku).

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, he was appointed as district magistrate (o-daikan) and was assigned to the O-kinju Ichi-no-yoriai-seki [a rank/seat designation among attendants].

Item: In the seventh month of the same year, in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted permission to change his name to Todayū.

Item: In the third month of the second year [of Kansei], the year of the Dog (1790), having gone out on duty to the villages and townships, attending carefully and thoroughly to all matters and serving diligently, he was awarded three silver coins (ginsu san-mai) and two bundles of cotton (wata ni-wa) as a commendation.

Item: In the twelfth month of the third year [of Kansei], the year of the Pig (1791), having applied himself diligently to the dispatch of transported rice (mawari-mai) along the Asaka route, handling matters well and performing extraordinary service beyond his regular duties, he was awarded two silver coins (ginsu ni-mai) as a commendation.
