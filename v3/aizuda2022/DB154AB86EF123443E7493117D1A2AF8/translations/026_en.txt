Item: In the third month of the tenth year [of Kansei], the year of the Horse (1798), he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, although it was his first campaign (shojin), he applied himself diligently and carried out his duties without any delays or problems, as was officially noted and commended.

Item: In the same month, having also carried out the duties assigned by Lord Sadaaki (Teruaki-sama) in the field camp without any difficulty, he was granted one bundle of cotton (wata hitotaba) as a reward.

Item: In the first month of the twelfth year [of Kansei], the year of the Monkey (1800), he was appointed as Chief Officer of the Accounts Office (go-kanjōsho shuyaku), his seat rank was raised to the equivalent of District Magistrate (kōri-bugyō-kaku), and he was ordered to carry out duties related to routine affairs (go-jōyō) as well as investigative matters (ginmi-gakari). His stipend allowance (o-yaku-ryō) of thirty koku was continued as before, and he was ordered to continue his concurrent duties overseeing shipping affairs as previously.

Item: In the eighth month of the first year of Kyōwa, the year of the Rooster (1801), he was appointed as Acting Inspector (o-metsuke kari-yaku) and traveled up to Edo for duty rotation. In Edo, he was ordered to manage the allocation of various duties among different departments (shomuki no bunki) as well as the handling of unexpected expenditures (fuji-demawari uchidzume).

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation as an attendant (o-tomo), and returned in the fourth month of the following year of the Dog (1802).
