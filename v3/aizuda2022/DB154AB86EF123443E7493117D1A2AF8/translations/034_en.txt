One item: In the ninth month of the same year, as he had applied himself diligently while on campaign duty (jinchū), he was granted one tan of bleached cloth (sarashi) as a substitute for a formal hemp kamishimo (ceremonial divided skirt and surcoat), presented as a reward.

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, as he had applied himself diligently over many years and had rendered particularly dedicated service in his assigned duties, he was granted, as a reward, one hiki of silk as a substitute for a ko-sode (short-sleeved garment), along with three silver coins (gin-ko san-mai) and cotton as supplementary gifts.

One item: In the first month of the fourteenth year of the Ox (Ushi), Yajima Denzō was appointed to the position of assistant to the Permanent Duty Administrative Office Group Leader (jōzume go-yōsho kumigashira soe-yaku), and as the duty rotation of the Administrative Office Group Leader was to be discontinued henceforth whether His Lordship was in Edo or in the domain, he was instructed to return to the domain (Aizu) as soon as the said Denzō had moved and taken up his post.

One item: In the third month of the same year, as there were matters requiring investigation related to confidential affairs (mitsujи go-yō), he was instructed to remain on duty and maintain his current rotation as before until the circumstances of the investigation had become at least somewhat clear.
