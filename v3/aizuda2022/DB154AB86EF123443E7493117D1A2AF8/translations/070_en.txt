One. In the second month of the same third Year of the Tiger (Bunka 3, 1806), his request was granted; he was relieved of his current duties and appointed to the Tozama-gumi (outer/affiliated unit), and was placed into the unit of Takahashi Yogorō.
　　Note: As his ailment had not improved sufficiently, his request to submit his koshinryō (a reduced stipend paid to those on inactive duty) and convalesce at a leisurely pace was granted as requested.

One. In the ninth month of the same fifth Year of the Dragon (Bunka 5, 1808), as his condition had improved, his request to return to active duty was granted as requested, and he served as a castle guard (Oshiro Goban).

One. In the third month of the same seventh Year of the Horse (Bunka 7, 1810), he was appointed as Kuji Bugyō (Magistrate of Litigation/Legal Affairs).

One. In the third month of the same tenth Year of the Rooster (Bunka 10, 1813), he was appointed as Kōri Bugyō (District Magistrate).

One. On the twenty-sixth day of the first month of the same twelfth Year of the Boar (Bunka 12, 1815), he died of illness at the age of forty-four. His posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō) was Honkaku-in Jikiō Ryōshin Koji. His place of burial is the same as previously recorded.
