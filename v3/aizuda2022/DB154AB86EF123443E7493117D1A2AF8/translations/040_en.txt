Mantarō Mitsuyoshi (initial names: Mango, then Mitsumi)
Mother: daughter of the Yoshida clan
Wife: daughter of Nomura Ikinosuke Moriki

One item: In the first month of the twelfth year of Bunka (1815), year of the Boar, as he had applied himself diligently to his studies and was reported to show a level of skill remarkable for his age, he was awarded copies of the *Kōkyō Taigi* (Great Meaning of the Classic of Filial Piety), the *Shisho Shūchū* (Collected Commentaries on the Four Books), and the *Shōgaku* (Elementary Learning) as a reward.

One item: In the eighth month of the same year, he was appointed as a companion attendant (*o-aite*) to the lord, and was granted five ryō of gold per year as an expense supplement.

One item: In the first month of the fourteenth year of Bunka (1817), year of the Ox, as he had applied himself diligently to his studies and was reported to show a level of skill remarkable for his age, he was awarded a copy of the *Sanrei Kochū* (Ancient Commentaries on the Three Rites) as a reward.

One item: In the ninth month of the first year of Bunsei (1818), year of the Tiger, having previously been appointed as a companion attendant to the lord, he was relieved of this duty as the lord had come of age. Furthermore, in recognition of his diligent service as companion attendant, he was privately awarded three pieces of silver (ginsu sanmai) and two bundles of cotton (wata nihata) as a reward.
