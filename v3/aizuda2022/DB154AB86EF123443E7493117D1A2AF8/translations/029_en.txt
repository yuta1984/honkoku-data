One entry: In the eleventh month of the same year (Bunka 3, 1806), due to the abundance of his primary and concurrent duties, he was relieved of his concurrent post as Yama Bugyō (forestry magistrate). In recognition of his diligent service up to that point, he was awarded two bundles of cotton as a reward.

One entry: In the twelfth month of the same year, while serving in his temporary post as Kōri Bugyō (district magistrate), in connection with the dredging of the castle moat (o-hori-sarae), he went out to the construction site, directed the deployment of rural laborers (gō-ninzoku) and coordinated various arrangements. As the work was completed with exceptional success, he was granted a formal banquet meal (o-ryōri) as a reward.

One entry: In the seventh month of the fourth year of Bunka (1807, the Year of the Rabbit), as principal official (shuyaku) of the Go-kanjōsho (accounts office), he was ordered to oversee the affairs of the Shinryō Yaku-sho (shrine revenue office) in accordance with prior precedent.

One entry: In the same month, as funds had been arranged through the Naitō household in Sukagawa, he was ordered to travel there in person to offer greetings and handle matters appropriately. He departed on the ninth day of the eighth month, traveled to the site, and returned on the eighteenth day of the same month.
