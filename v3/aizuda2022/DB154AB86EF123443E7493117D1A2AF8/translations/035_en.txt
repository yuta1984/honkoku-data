One item: In the sixth month of the second year of Bunsei (1819), year of the Rabbit (Utō/Bōnen), he was instructed to concurrently assume the duties of the Kuji Oshiki-gakari (official responsible for ceremonial protocol and litigation affairs).

One item: In the eleventh month of the same year, as Yanase Sanzaemon had been instructed to proceed to Edo (shutsufӯ), he was instructed to make his way up to Edo (mairnoboru) without delay (fuji ni), and duly made his way up.

One item: In the same month, as the customary arrangements (o-furiaj) had been revised, he was relieved of his current duties (tōmu go-men), placed without post (muyaku) at his designated rank (mochi-seki), his stipend of ten koku of role-salary (oyaku-ryō) continued as before, and it was declared that he should serve as Group Leader of the Reserve Guard Commander's Unit (o-rusubigumi kumigashira), and furthermore that his diligent service up to that point was duly recognized.
　　Note: Although he was relieved of his current duties, it was declared that he should continue as before until his assigned affairs (kakari go-yō) were concluded.

One item: In the first month of the third year (Tatsudoshi/Dragon year) of the same era, he was appointed as a permanent attendant (jōzume) attached to Lady Juhōin (寿鳳院様), his rank being set as equivalent to that of Ō-metsuke (senior inspector/censor), with seniority determined by precedence among those of the same inserted rank, and it was declared that his role-salary of ten koku would continue as before.
