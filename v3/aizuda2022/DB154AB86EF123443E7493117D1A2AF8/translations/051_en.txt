Note: As the lord's journey to Kyoto (on-jōkyō) was postponed and came to take place at the end of the 8th month or in the 9th month, those who had been ordered to accompany him while on garrison duty (kinban) were excused from the accompanying duty (go-yōsha) and all returned together upon rotation.

Item: In the 4th month of the 1st year of Kanpō, the year of the Rooster (1741), in recognition of his diligent and commendable service during the campaign (jinchū), he was granted two pieces of silver (ginsu ni-mai) as a reward.

Item: In the 8th month of the 2nd year of Kanpō, the year of the Dog (1742), in recognition of his thorough, attentive, and unfailingly diligent performance of his official duties (go-yōsuji), he was granted an official stipend (yakuryō) of two koku and one person's rations (hitori-fuchi).

Item: In the 12th month of the 3rd year of Kanpō, the year of the Boar (1743), in recognition of his own diligent and attentive service, and of his father Kaemon's decades of faithful and upright service, their combined assessed productive yield (gi-saku) was consolidated and fixed at one hundred koku of enfeoffment (go-chigyō), which was granted to his father Kaemon, as announced by official decree.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 1st year of Enkyō, the year of the Rat (1744), he traveled up to Edo for garrison duty (kinban) as part of the accompanying retinue, performed one tour of duty, and returned.

Item: In the 4th month of the same year, at the time of the wedding ceremony (on-konrei) of Lady Baien'in, he was assigned to a specific role (yakudzuki) and was granted an allowance for a ceremonial formal outfit (noshime-kamishimo).
