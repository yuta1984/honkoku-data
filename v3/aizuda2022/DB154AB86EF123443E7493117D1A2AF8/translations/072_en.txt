One. In the eleventh month of Bunka 11, the Year of the Dog (1814), as it was reported that he had applied himself diligently to archery and showed a level of skill beyond his years, he was awarded two sets of target arrows (mato-ya futaté) as a commendation.

One. In the third month of Bunka 12, the Year of the Boar (1815), he was granted his hereditary stipend of 150 koku without change, was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit), placed under the command of Jinbō Kurannosuke's unit, and took up his duties as castle guard (o-shiro o-ban).

One. In the ninth month of the same year, he was appointed as o-koshō (page/personal attendant to the lord).

One. In the twelfth month of the same year, on the occasion of an unscheduled visit to Edo (fu-ji o-sanpu), he traveled to Edo as part of the lord's retinue.

One. In the fourth month of Bunka 13, the Year of the Rat (1816), he was appointed to accompany the lord on his visit to Kyoto (o-jōkyō).

One. In the twelfth month of the same year, in connection with the lord being of young age, all attendants coordinated and exerted themselves diligently, including standing sleepless night watches; in recognition of this service, he was privately awarded one piece of silver (gin-su ichimái) as a commendation.

One. In the third month of Bunka 14, the Year of the Ox (1817), on the occasion of the lord's recovery from smallpox (o-hōsō) and the celebratory sake-and-hot-water ritual (o-sake-yu), he was awarded two bundles of cotton (wata ni-wa) as a gift of congratulation.
