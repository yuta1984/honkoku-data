Item: On the 12th day of the 12th month of the same year, he died of illness at the age of sixty-eight.

　　Place of burial: Shōunzan Zenryūji Temple

Daughter
　Her mother was a daughter of the Sakai clan.

Kaemon Mitsunaga (first name: Jihachirō; born in the 3rd year of Tenna, the year of the Snake (1683))
　His mother was the same (a daughter of the Sakai clan).
　His wife was a daughter of Ōtani Sukeemon Kiyoaki.

Item: In the 2nd year of Hōei, the year of the Rooster (1705), in the 2nd month, two koku were deducted from the provisional stipend (gisaku) inherited from his late father, and he was granted thirteen koku and two ninbuchi (rations for two persons), and was summoned to serve as a Nakanokuchi-ban (gate guard).
　　Note: During this period of service, he also served concurrently in the temporary role of御書簡物書 (letter/document writing clerk).
