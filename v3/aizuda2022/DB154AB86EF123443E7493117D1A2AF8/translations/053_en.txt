Item: In the 12th month of the 4th year of the same era, the year of the Rabbit (1747), in preparation for next spring when Lord Kyōtei would make his first journey to Edo (on-nobori), he was assigned to coordinate and fulfill the necessary duties (nin-yaku). However, it was decreed that, as two foot soldiers (o-kachi) had been assigned to handle the clerical duties (fude-yaku), he was not to perform scribal work (kaki-yaku).

Item: In the 3rd month of the 5th year of the same era, the year of the Dragon (1748), the arrangements for accompanying Lord Kyōtei's entourage became urgent, and there were circumstances involving a reduction in the number of personnel. Nevertheless, he thoroughly discussed and negotiated across all relevant channels (suji-suji), diligently performed his duties with excellence, and in special recognition of this, was granted 500 hiki in gold as a reward. [sic]

Item: In the 5th month of the 2nd year of Kan'en, the year of the Serpent (1749), in recognition of his diligent and excellent service, it was decreed that he would be granted 5 ryō annually on an ongoing basis.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 3rd year of the same era, the year of the Horse (1750), he traveled to Edo (maka-ri-nobori) as part of the accompanying escort for a tour of duty (kinban), completed one tour of service (ichi-jin), and returned.

Item: In the 5th month of the same year, he was granted, without dispute, an hereditary stipend (atoshiki on-chigyō) of 100 koku.
　　Note: However, the monetary grants he had received up to that point were rescinded (meshi-age).
