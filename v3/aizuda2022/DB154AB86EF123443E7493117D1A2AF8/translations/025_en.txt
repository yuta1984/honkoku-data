Item: In the fourth month of the fifth year [of Kansei], the year of the Ox (1793), he was appointed as Mountain Magistrate (yama-bugyō).

Item: In the second month of the sixth year [of Kansei], the year of the Tiger (1794), due to the fire that spread to and burned the Shiba residence (go-yashiki), he was appointed to oversee the construction and repair works (go-fushin) [of the residence].

Item: In the eleventh month of the seventh year [of Kansei], the year of the Rabbit (1795), he was additionally appointed to oversee shipping affairs (o-fune-gakari) concurrently with his existing duties.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, having capably managed the duties of village inspections (mawari-mura-gata) related to the construction works at the Shiba residence, and having properly handled the disbursement of the supplementary levy funds (teisū-hosen), he was awarded one used garment (o-furu o-kosode) as a commendation.

Item: In the third month of the eighth year [of Kansei], the year of the Dragon (1796), having capably and thoroughly administered his area of jurisdiction, having applied himself with particular diligence to the collection of domain profit revenues (go-eki-kin), having properly managed the procurement of construction materials for the Writing Academy (shogaku-ryō), and having also carried out his duties overseeing shipping affairs — serving with great effort across all these responsibilities — he was granted a stipend allowance (o-yaku-ryō) of thirty koku.

Item: In the ninth month of the ninth year [of Kansei], the year of the Snake (1797), he was appointed as Inspector (o-metsuke) while retaining his current seat rank (mochi-seki), his stipend allowance (o-yaku-ryō) of thirty koku was continued as before, and he was ordered to continue his concurrent duties overseeing shipping affairs as previously.
