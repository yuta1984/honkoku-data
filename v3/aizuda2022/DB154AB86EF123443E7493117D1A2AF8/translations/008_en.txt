- In the tenth month of the second year of Keian, the year of the Ox (1649), he was granted an increase in stipend of ten koku.
　　Note: Regarding the above, where the family record contains an entry, a retained copy of an internal letter dated the eighth day of the first month of the fourth year of Shōhō, the year of the Pig (1647), reads: "Ten koku in rice stipend (kirime), [granted to] Higuchi Kanzaburō; the above-mentioned increase granted from the end of the year of the Dog — it appears that all of these are recorded in accordance with the Yamagata register."

- In the eleventh month of the third year of Jōō, the year of the Horse (1654), his rice-stipend certificate (okitte/okifuda) was converted to a domain stipend (gochigyo) of one hundred koku.

- In the fifth month of the first year of Kanbun, the year of the Ox (1661), as requested, he was relieved of his duties as official scribe (goYūhitsu) and was appointed as an attendant guard (otomobан, goともban).

- In the fifth month of the fifth year of the same era, the year of the Snake (1665), he was granted an increase in stipend of fifty koku, and was appointed as inspector (ometsuke) on permanent assignment in Edo.

- In the second month of the seventh year of the same era, the year of the Sheep (1667), with the lord's private approval, his stipend was converted to match that of his father's domain stipend of 250 koku.

- In the seventh month of the same year, he was appointed as the inspector/overseer (oyokome-yaku) for Lady Hōshōin.

- In the intercalary tenth month of the ninth year of the same era, the year of the Rooster (1669), he was appointed as Litigation Magistrate (Kuji-bugyō).

- In the first year of Enpō, the year of the Ox (1673), on business related to the investigation of Christian (Kirishitan) adherents, he traveled up to Edo and consulted with the relevant officials, carrying out the assigned duties.
