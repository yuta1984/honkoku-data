One. In the same month (the third month of Bunka 14, Year of the Ox, 1817), he was given items from the o-nando (lord's storage room) disposal: one set of hemp formal wear (asa no o-kamishimo), one o-kosode (formal short-sleeved kimono), and one o-katabira (summer kimono).

One. In the fourth month of the same year, as he had exerted himself diligently in service during the lord's bout with smallpox, he was awarded one piece of silver (gin-su ichimái) as a commendation.

One. In the fifth month of the same year, he traveled down (to Aizu) as part of the lord's retinue.

One. In the sixth month of the same year, it came to the lord's attention that the members of the Nakama (middle-ranking attendants' room), along with the subject, had in recent times been conducting themselves with notably proper decorum — not only within the Edo residence, but even when going outside the gate. The lord attributed this [written in red] to the fact that the members of the Nakama had made a mutual agreement among themselves, and issued instructions that they should continue to strive ever more diligently in upholding proper conduct hereafter.

One. In the tenth month of the same year, it was proclaimed that he had been diligently and commendably exerting himself in military drills (sōren).

One. In the first month of Bunsei 1, the Year of the Tiger (1818), he was appointed as o-zen-ban minarai (apprentice meal-service attendant).

One. In the eighth month of the same year, as the members of the Nakama had, together with the subject, cultivated exemplary personal conduct, sparing no effort and throwing themselves wholeheartedly into their duties, he was awarded one piece of silver as a commendation.

One. In the ninth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo as part of the lord's retinue.
