One item: In the second month of the tenth year (1798), year of the Horse, in accordance with his father's petition, he was relieved of his duties as assistant clerk (benju tetsudai) at the Calligraphy Academy.

One item: In the third month of the first year of Kyōwa (1801), year of the Rooster, he traveled to Nikkō and underwent training in ceremonial court music and dance (bugaku).

One item: In the eighth month of the same year, he was appointed to serve as a consultant on bugaku matters at the domain school (gakkō).

One item: In the eighth month of the second year of Kyōwa (1802), year of the Dog, he was appointed as an assistant instructor (shihan tetsudai) of bugaku.

One item: In the fourth month of the first year of Bunka (1804), year of the Rat, he was appointed to oversee the closing/administrative affairs of the Ichishi-ryū school of spearmanship (sōjutsu).

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he traveled to Nikkō at his own expense in order to research the costumes worn by court musicians (reijin shōzoku).

One item: In the tenth month of the second year of Bunka (1805), year of the Ox, when the bugaku costumes were completed, he contributed a personal monetary gift and assisted with the craftwork. As the result was excellent, he was awarded one piece of silver as a reward.

One item: In the sixth month of the third year of Bunka (1806), year of the Tiger, he was appointed as a trainee deputy magistrate (odaikan minarai), serving in a temporary capacity.

One item: In the first month of the fifth year of Bunka (1808), year of the Dragon, following Matsumoto Yodayū's departure for Ezo (Hokkaido), he was appointed to travel during the gaps in his duties as bugaku instructor and to fill in for him.
