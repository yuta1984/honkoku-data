(In the first month of the first year of Meireki, the year of the Sheep [1655], he traveled to Edo, and in the) fourth month of the same year, he accompanied Lady Seikōin on a visit to the residence of Lord Uesugi Harima-no-kami.

- In the seventh month of the first year of Manji, the year of the Dog (1658), Lady Seikōin passed away, and in the ninth month he was recalled to Aizu.

- In the third month of the second year of Manji, the year of the Boar (1659), he was appointed to the Yoriai-gumi (a retainer group).

- In the seventh month of the same year, on the occasion of the first death anniversary of Lady Seikōin, he traveled to Yonezawa as an envoy on behalf of Lady Seikōin (Shōkōin-sama) to offer incense in her stead.

- In the fifth month of the fourth year of Kanbun, the year of the Dragon (1664), following the death of Lord Uesugi Harima-no-kami, he traveled to Yonezawa and remained in attendance during the mourning period of Lord Danjō-daihi, performing the incense offering on behalf of Lady Shōkōin.

- In the seventh month of the same year, on the occasion of the seventh death anniversary of Lady Seikōin, he traveled to Yonezawa as an envoy on behalf of Lady Shōkōin to offer incense in her stead.
