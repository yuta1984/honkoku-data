One item: In the intercalary eleventh month of the same year, he was appointed to the temporary acting position of Group Leader (kumigashira) of the Administrative Office (go-yōsho).

One item: In the twelfth month of the same year, he was formally appointed as Group Leader of the Administrative Office.

One item: In the third month of the thirteenth year of the Rat (Nezumi), regulations for the Internal Affairs Office (go-naishō-kata) were established, and as he had applied himself diligently to the relevant investigations, he was granted three silver coins (gin-ko san-mai) as a reward.

One item: In the fourth month of the same year, he traveled to Edo to perform his duty rotation (kinban).

One item: In the fifth month of the same year, regarding His Lordship's journey to Kyoto, he was instructed as follows: concerning the expenses incurred up to that point, as well as the anticipated costs after His Lordship's return to Edo, and the procedures for procurement and funding arrangements, these matters were to be urgently investigated and communicated to the appropriate offices. Since he had already been assigned to the Internal Affairs Office, he was to coordinate his official duties as much as possible, attend the Accounts Office (go-kanjōsho), and not only address matters of economy and reduction of expenditures, but also thoroughly discuss and properly handle the aforementioned investigations. Furthermore, it was stipulated that on occasions when he was unable to attend due to his primary duties, the Accounts Group Leader (go-kanjō kumigashira) was also to come and consult.
