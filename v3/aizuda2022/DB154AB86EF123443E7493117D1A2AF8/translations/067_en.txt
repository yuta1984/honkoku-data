Item: In the third month of Kansei 6, the Year of the Tiger (1794), due to his excellence in scholarship, he was privately granted sake at the castle.

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, he was appointed to the provisional post of School Recitation Master (Jushi Kariyaku).

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, when the Shiba residence was destroyed in a fire spreading from neighboring buildings, he did not shrink from hardship and, as a token of goodwill, transported lumber [to aid in recovery]. This was recognized as a particularly praiseworthy act, and as a special mark of distinction, he was given a formal meal at the school.

Item: In the first month of Kansei 7, the Year of the Rabbit (1795), having diligently fulfilled his duties as provisional Recitation Master, and as the administration of the school was being reorganized, his provisional appointment as Recitation Master was discontinued, and he was appointed Assistant Instructor (Joshu Jokyō) of the school dormitory, and was assigned to reside in the dormitory. He was therefore expected to not only nurture and guide students, but also to devote himself to his own personal scholarship and discipline, and to maintain strict order.
