Mother: A daughter of the Kurasawa clan
　　Wife: A daughter of Higuchi Seizaemon Tametsugu

Item: In the seventh month of the eighth year of Genroku (1695), the year of the Boar, he was granted, without discrepancy, a hereditary stipend (katoku) of four hundred koku, and was appointed to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit).

Item: In the third month of the ninth year [of Genroku] (1696), the year of the Rat, he went up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned [to the domain] in the ninth month of the same year.

Item: In the fifth month of the tenth year [of Genroku] (1697), the year of the Ox, on the occasion of [the lord's] journey down [to the domain], Miyake Magobei was dispatched as a messenger to announce [the lord's] arrival; [the subject] was granted an audience (omemie) and received gifts (hairyōmono), and was then appointed as a messenger of thanks (orei no otsukai), went up [to Edo], and duly performed his duties.

Item: In the fourth month of the eleventh year [of Genroku] (1698), the year of the Tiger, he was appointed as御使番 (otsukaiban, a messenger/liaison official).
　　Note: It is believed that during this service there were trips back and forth to Edo, but the details are unclear.

Item: In the sixth month of the fourteenth year [of Genroku] (1701), the year of the Dragon, he was appointed as御刀番 (odachiban, sword-bearer attendant).
　　Note: Same as above [details unclear].

Item: In the tenth month of the fifth year of Shōtoku (1715), the year of the Sheep, he was appointed as御供番頭 (oともbangatō, head of the escort guard).
　　Note: Same as above [details unclear].
