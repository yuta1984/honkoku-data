Item: In the 10th month of the same year (Tenmei 7, 1787), in addition to his existing stipend supplement (yakuryō), he was granted a further stipend supplement of 20 koku.

Item: In the 1st month of Tenmei 8, the year of the Monkey (1788), he was appointed to serve as a field officer (shutsuyaku) at the rural district offices (gō-yakusho) across the four districts. Note: As a field officer's subsistence allowance for five persons, he was granted an expense supplementary payment (hiho-kin) of 3 ryō and 2 bu per year.

Item: In the 3rd month of Kansei 2, the year of the Dog (1790), since the reform of the rural administrative affairs (jige-torihakari), he had conscientiously dedicated himself to the work, and since the year before last had also diligently performed his duties as a field officer at the rural district offices. In recognition of his exceptionally meritorious service, an additional 50 koku was directly added to his existing stipend supplement (yakuryō) and granted to him.

Item: In the 5th month of Kansei 5, the year of the Ox (1793), having for four years conscientiously attended to various matters in the handling of rural administration (jige-toriatsukai), his guidance and instruction having spread widely, and having diligently performed his duties, he was awarded as a commendation 5 silver coins (ginsu) and 2 bundles of cotton (wata).

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, on the occasion of the Yukawa River (湯川) construction and repair works (gofushin), which differed from the usual annual works and constituted an extraordinary undertaking, he provided appropriate direction and supervision, for which he was granted sake.
