…(he was appointed to the Tozama-gumi and) was instructed to continue concurrently serving at the go-yōsho (official office) as before. Furthermore, it was proclaimed that he had diligently performed the duties related to the domain inspectors (o-kuni-metsuke-shū), and he was assigned to the unit of Hyūga Saburōemon.

Item: In the 3rd month of the same year (1753), he went up to Edo for guard duty (kinban), and returned in the 10th month of the same year.
　　Note: In recognition of his diligent and unhindered service in the field (jin-chū), he was granted three pieces of silver (gin-su) as a reward.

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, he was appointed to manage and carry out the duties related to the construction and repair (o-fushin go-yō) of Lord Dojō's tomb (go-fun).

Item: In the 8th month of the 5th year, the year of the Boar (1755), in recognition of his diligent service in the duties related to the construction and repair of the tomb at In'nai (院内), he was granted one piece of silver as a reward.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 6th year, the year of the Rat (1756), he went up to Edo for guard duty, and returned in the 10th month of the same year.
　　Note: In recognition of his diligent service in the field, he was granted three pieces of silver as a reward.
