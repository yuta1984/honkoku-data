(Continued from the previous page) As he was to be dispatched to the Kamigata region, he was ordered to travel there and conduct negotiations appropriately. He departed on the twenty-eighth day of the tenth month and returned in the first month of the following Year of the Snake (Bunka 6, 1809).

One entry: In the tenth month of the same year (Bunka 5, 1808), he was ordered to take on the additional concurrent post of Go-fushin Bugyō (construction magistrate).

One entry: In the third month of the sixth year, the Year of the Snake (Bunka 6, 1809), he was ordered to take on the additional concurrent post of Kaimai Uwayaku (supervisor of rice transport shipments), and was relieved of his concurrent duties as Go-fushin Bugyō.

One entry: In the eighth month of the same year, amid an abundance of official duties arising from the garrisoning and defense of Ezo (Hokkaido), he managed and directed affairs within his sphere of authority without delay, and diligently conducted negotiations concerning the procurement of funds. In recognition of this, he was awarded three silver coins (gin-su san-mai) as a reward.

One entry: In the eleventh month of the same year, having previously been ordered to enter and attend to the Shinryō Yakusho (the office administering shrine revenues/landholdings), on this occasion he was also assigned to an additional senior post. It was therefore declared that it was no longer necessary for him to continue his duties at the Shinryō Yakusho.

One entry: In the third month of the seventh year, the Year of the Horse (Bunka 7, 1810), while serving in his concurrent capacity as Go-fushin Bugyō, he applied himself diligently to the construction works at the two imperial/lord's mausolea (go-fun) within the Innai precinct. In recognition of this, he was awarded two bundles of cotton (wata ni-wa) as a reward.
