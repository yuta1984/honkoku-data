Item: In the 12th month of the 1st year of Kansei, the year of the Rooster (1789), in recognition of his service as Shimeri-kata (regulatory officer) at the Bugakuryō (Military Studies Institute), he was granted an annual stipend of two bu in gold as a subsidy for his expenses.

Item: In the 6th month of the 5th year of the same era, the year of the Ox (1793), he was assigned the task of copying the Reigi Ruiten (a compilation of rules of etiquette and ceremony).

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, in recognition of his continuous and diligent service as Shimeri-kata of the Shinten-ryū school of swordsmanship at the Bugakuryō from its very beginning, and furthermore for his careful and thorough maintenance and preparation of the record books used at the Bugakuryō, attending to various matters with great dedication — as a reward for all of the above, he was granted two silver coins (ginsu).
