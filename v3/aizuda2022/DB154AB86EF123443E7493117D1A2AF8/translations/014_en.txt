　　Note: There is no transmission of this from the main house; however, in the genealogy of Shiba Tetsujirō, it is recorded that "the wife of the first-generation Kanzaemon Moriyuki was a daughter of Higuchi Jūemon Mitsushige." The precise relationship is unclear. Furthermore, in the official records (kanki), it appears that on the fourteenth day of the twelfth month of the third year of Tenna (1683), a petition was filed to arrange a marriage between the adopted nephew (yōmei) of Higuchi Kanzaburō and the adopted son Hachiemon of Arakawa Buzaemon; however, it is unknown whose daughter this woman was.
