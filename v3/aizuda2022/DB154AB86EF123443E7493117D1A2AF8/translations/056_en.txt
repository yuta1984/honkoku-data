…in recognition of his diligent service in the duties following the lord's entry into the domain (go-jūbu no o-ato go-yō), he was granted a set of formal ceremonial hakama and vest (o-koma no kamishimo ichi-gu) as a reward.

Item: In the 11th month of the same year (1759), as the lord had suddenly undertaken a journey to Edo (go-sanpu), and there were many official duties to be attended to there, it was proclaimed that he was to be sent up to Edo for unscheduled guard duty (fuji kinban). He accordingly went up to Edo in attendance in the 12th month of the same year.

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, it was proclaimed that he was appointed to accompany the lord on his journey to Kyoto (go-jōkyō).

Item: In the 2nd month of the 10th year, the year of the Dragon (1760), it was proclaimed that during the lord's procession to Edo (go-sankin) in the previous winter, which had been an exceedingly urgent departure (go-hakka), he had diligently prepared the necessary arrangements and served without hindrance throughout the journey.

Item: In the 3rd month of the same year, the lord departed for Kyoto, and he traveled in attendance, returning in the 4th month also in attendance.

Item: In the 4th month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent and unhindered service in the field (jin-chū), he was granted three pieces of silver as a reward, and departed from Edo the following day to return home.
