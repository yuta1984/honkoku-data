Item: In the 4th month of the 2nd year of Genbun, the year of the Snake (1737), he was ordered to undertake the duty of reviewing and revising the old records (kyūki) concerning matters from the time of the previous lord's succession (go-katoku) through to his passing, as well as other matters that had come to follow established precedents.

Item: In the 1st month of the 3rd year of Genbun, the year of the Horse (1738), in recognition of his diligent and commendable service, he was granted an official stipend (yakuryō) of three koku and one person's rations (hitori-fuchi).

Item: In the 5th month of the same year, he was ordered to handle confidential affairs (mitsuji go-yō) at the Administrative Affairs Office (Goyōsho).

Item: In the 12th month of the 4th year of Genbun, the year of the Sheep (1739), in recognition of his sustained and diligent service throughout the examination of old records (kyūki-shirabe) since the year before last, he was granted one piece of silver (ginsu ichi-mai) as a reward.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 5th year of Genbun, the year of the Monkey (1740), in recognition of his diligent and commendable performance of his current duties (tōmu), his existing official stipend of three koku and one person's rations was increased (on-kazō).

Item: In the 10th month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for garrison duty (kinban), and returned in the 4th month of the following year of the Rooster (Kanpō 1st year, 1741).

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, following the announcement that the lord would travel to Kyoto (on-jōkyō) the coming spring, he was ordered to prepare for and carry out the related duties, which he duly performed.
