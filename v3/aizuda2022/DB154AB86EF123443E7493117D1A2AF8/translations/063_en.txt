Item: In the 11th month of the 2nd year of Kyōwa, the year of the Dog (1802), he was summoned and traveled up to Edo, returning home in the 12th month of the same year.
　　Note: During his stay in Edo, he was given one kosode (short-sleeved garment) as a year-end celebratory gift, and upon receiving his leave (o-itoma), he was also presented with one kosode bearing the lord's family crest.

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, he was additionally appointed to concurrently serve as Chief Officer of the District Administration (Kōrigata-shuyaku). As this role entailed personal expenses on his part, he was granted one gold coin (kin ichimai) per year.

Item: On the 13th day of the 1st month of the 3rd year of Kyōwa, the year of the Boar (1803), he died of illness at the age of sixty-five. His posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō) was Seikōin Junsei Jitsuon Koji. His place of burial is the same as previously noted.
