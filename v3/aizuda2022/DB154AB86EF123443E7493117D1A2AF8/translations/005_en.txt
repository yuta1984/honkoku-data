- In the fourth month of the third year of Shōho, the year of the Dog (1646), he traveled to Edo for guard duty (kinban) and served until the tenth month of the same year, then returned home.

- In the ninth month of the fourth year of Shōho, the year of the Boar (1647), he traveled to Edo for guard duty and served until the third month of the following year of the Rat (1648), then returned home.

- In the second month of the second year of Keian, the year of the Ox (1649), he traveled to Edo for guard duty and was ordered to accompany the lord on a pilgrimage to Nikkō in the fourth month of the same year; thereafter he remained in Edo and served until the eighth month, then returned home.

- In the second month of the third year of Keian, the year of the Tiger (1650), he traveled to Edo for guard duty and served until the eighth month of the same year, then returned home.

- In the fifth month of the first year of Jōō, the year of the Dragon (1652), he traveled to Edo for guard duty and served until the eleventh month of the same year, then returned home.

- In the second month of the second year of Jōō, the year of the Snake (1653), he traveled to the Nikkō area in connection with construction works there (Nikkō go-fushin), and returned home in late fourth month upon completion of his duties.

- In the ninth month of the same year, he was ordered to accompany the lord on a trip to Kyoto; in the tenth month he returned to Edo and immediately continued his guard duty, serving until the fifth month of the following year of the Horse (1654), then returned home.

- In the ninth month of the third year of Jōō, the year of the Horse (1654), he was granted an additional stipend of one hundred koku and was appointed to attend Lady Seikōin.
