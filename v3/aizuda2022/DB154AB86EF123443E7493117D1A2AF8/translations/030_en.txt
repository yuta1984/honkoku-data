One entry: In the first month of the fifth year, the Year of the Dragon (Bunka 5, 1808), as the post of Go-fushin Bugyō (construction magistrate) was at that time staffed by only one person, he was appointed to serve temporarily as acting Go-fushin Bugyō. In connection with this appointment, as his duties would become numerous, he was ordered to separate and carry out his responsibilities in the domain of the Ginmi-gakari (investigative/audit duties) independently.

One entry: In the intercalary sixth month of the same year, having applied himself diligently over many years, and as the management of the domain's internal finances (go-naishō) had become particularly strained, he applied himself with special industry. In connection with overseeing the initial arrangements for the distribution of provisions stipends (賄扶持, makanai-fuchi) to domain retainers, in addition to his existing stipend of office (o-yakuryō), he was granted an additional stipend of thirty koku.

One entry: In the eighth month of the same year, he was ordered on an official assignment to Sukagawa and was instructed to handle debt negotiations (go-shakkin-kata taidан) appropriately. He departed on the nineteenth day of the same month, traveled to the site, and returned on the twenty-eighth day of the same month.
