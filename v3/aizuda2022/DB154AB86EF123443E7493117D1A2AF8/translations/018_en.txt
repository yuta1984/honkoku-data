Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, as [he] had diligently performed his duties over the years, [he] was granted an increase of five koku to his stipend.

Item: In the same eighteenth year of the Ox (1733), [he] was granted a further increase of five koku to his stipend.

Item: In the ninth month of the eighteenth year of the Ox (1733), [he] traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban). During the campaign period, [he] was appointed as a companion/attendant in the lord's presence, and was personally bestowed by the lord's own hand a copy of the Rōeishū (anthology of poems for chanting), a fan, and brush and ink. [He] returned in the third month of the following year of the Tiger (1734).

Item: In the ninth month of the first year of Genbun, the year of the Dragon (1736), [he] traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the third month of the following year of the Snake (1737).
　　Note: During the campaign period, [he] was appointed as a companion/attendant [to the lord], and was bestowed a fan, large-character calligraphy (daimonji), brushes, and other items.

Item: In the ninth month of the third year [of Genbun], the year of the Horse (1738), [he] traveled up to Edo for duty rotation. In the twelfth month of the same year, as [he] had performed his current duties diligently and without negligence, [he] was granted an increase of five koku and one ration (ichi-nin-fuchi) to his stipend. In the fourth month of the following year of the Sheep (1739), [he] returned [to Aizu] as part of the lord's entourage upon the lord's entry into the domain (go-nyūbu).

Item: In the eighth month of the first year of Kanpō, the year of the Rooster (1741), [he] traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the third month of the following year of the Dog (1742).

Item: In the third month of the first year of Enkyō, the year of the Rat (1744), as [he] had diligently performed his duties, his previous assessed/nominal stipend (gigi-saku) was converted and formally established as a proper enfeoffed stipend (go-chigyō) of one hundred koku.
