Item: In the 5th month of the same year (the 14th year of Kyōhō, 1729), he was ordered to assist with the drafting of the compiled records of the Lord Tokuō's [official] biography (go-jijitsu).

Item: In the 12th month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent daily attendance and devoted service in the compilation of the official biography, he was granted one hundred hiki in gold as a fee for his labors (hone-ore-ryō).

Item: In the 5th month of the 16th year of Kyōhō, the year of the Boar (1731), he was summoned as an official of the Goyōsho (Administrative Affairs Office), and was granted ten koku and two persons' rations (futari-fuchi).

Item: In the 3rd month of the 17th year of Kyōhō, the year of the Rat (1732), since the previous autumn the workload had been particularly heavy and extremely demanding; in recognition of his diligent and commendable service throughout this period, he was granted one piece of silver (ginsu ichi-mai) as a reward.

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for garrison duty (kinban), and returned in the 3rd month of the following year of the Ox (1733).

Item: In the 3rd month of the 18th year of Kyōhō, the year of the Ox (1733), it was announced that, although this was his first active duty (hatsu-jin), he had served diligently and without any problems.

Item: In the 8th month of the 20th year of Kyōhō, the year of the Rabbit (1735), he traveled to Mineyama to carry out duties related to the grand shrine festival (go-sairei) there.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 1st year of Genbun, the year of the Dragon (1736), he was ordered to examine and compile documents related to legal/official matters (kuji) that were in the archive.
