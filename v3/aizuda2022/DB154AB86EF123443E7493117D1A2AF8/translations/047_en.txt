Item: In the 11th month of the 2nd year of Genbun, the year of the Snake (1737), as a result of his diligent service in his primary duties, he was granted a stipend supplement (oyakuryō) of three koku.

Item: In the 12th month of the 3rd year of Kanpō, the year of the Boar (1743), it was proclaimed that, as his son Kakuemon had also applied himself diligently and served with conscientiousness, and as Kaemon had served faithfully and honestly for several decades, the father and son's combined Gisaku (a form of supplementary stipend) would be consolidated and granted to Kaemon as a domain stipend (chigyo) of one hundred koku.

Item: In the 12th month of the 1st year of Kan'en, the year of the Dragon (1748), as requested, he was relieved of his official duties. Furthermore, it was proclaimed that, since the aforementioned domain stipend of one hundred koku in the form of the father and son's Gisaku had already been granted, and as his son Kakuemon was continuing in service, there would be no need for him to submit the Kobushin-ryō (a fee or contribution paid by those without active duty assignments).

Item: On the 8th day of the 3rd month of the 3rd year of the same era, the year of the Horse (1750), he passed away from illness at the age of 68.
