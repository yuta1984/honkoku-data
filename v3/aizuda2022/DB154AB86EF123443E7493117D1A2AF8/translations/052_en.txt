Item: In the 7th month of the same year, he was assigned to a role (nin-yaku) in preparation for the upcoming winter wedding ceremony (on-konrei) of Lord Dojō, and he remained on extended duty (tsume-koshi) to fulfill this assignment. Upon completion of the duties, he returned in the 12th month of the same year.
　　Note: He was granted an allowance for a ceremonial formal outfit (noshime-kamishimo).

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent service during the campaign (jinchū), he was granted two pieces of silver (gin-su ni-mai) as a reward.

Item: In the 2nd month of the 2nd year of the same era, the year of the Ox (1745), in recognition of his devoted efforts (hone-ori) in service of the wedding ceremony preparations, he was privately granted (go-nainai) a gift of 300 hiki in gold (equivalent to 3 ryō) by Lady Shōkaku'in, presented by means of a formal gift register (on-mokuroku).

Item: In the 3rd month of the same year, in recognition of his assigned service (nin-yaku) for the wedding ceremony duties, he was granted 500 hiki in gold (equivalent to 5 ryō) as a reward.

Item: In the 12th month of the 3rd year of the same era, the year of the Tiger (1746), in connection with the succession of domain leadership (on-daigawari), he was assigned to and performed duties related to dispatching the official domain documents bearing the lord's seal (on-hanmono) to Edo.
