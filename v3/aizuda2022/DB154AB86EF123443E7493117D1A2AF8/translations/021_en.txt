Item: Around the third month of Hōreki 1, the year of the Sheep (1751), in accordance with Numazawa Gonbei's petition, he was appointed as replacement on duty (go-bandai); the subsequent post assignment fell under Ueda Hachirobee's unit.

Item: In the first month of the same [era's] fourth year, the year of the Dog (1754), the inherited stipend (atoshiki go-chigyō) of one hundred koku was granted to him without any discrepancy.

Item: In the ninth month of the same [era's] fifth year, the year of the Boar (1755), his request to conduct a mutual exchange of residence (aitate-gae) with Node Seihachi's estate was granted in accordance with his petition.

Item: In the fifth month of the same [era's] tenth year, the year of the Dragon (1760), he was appointed to the role of escort guard (o-tomo-ban).

Item: In the sixth month of the same year, having been instructed regarding his wish to change his name to Kanzaburō, he continued performing castle guard duty.

Item: In the first month of An'ei 5, the year of the Monkey (1776), his request to take Kubota Yahei's third son, Koganji, as a son-in-law and adopted heir (muko-yōshi) was granted in accordance with his petition.

Item: In the first month of the same [era's] seventh year, the year of the Dog (1778), in accordance with the petition of his superior (kashira) Kayano Gonbee, he was granted permission to retire (inkyo); the subsequent post assignment fell under Harada Ihee's unit.

Item: In the seventh month of the same year, as his son Koganji had died of illness, his request to take Isoue Tōuemon's second son, Shōgorō, as a son-in-law and adopted heir (muko-yōshi) was granted in accordance with his petition.
