Item: In the 3rd month of the 4th year of Hōei, the year of the Boar (1707), he was appointed to the role of Okachi (foot soldier/infantry guard).
　　Note: During the four years of his service, he completed one rotation (return from duty) of the Kinban first division.

Item: In the 7th year of Hōei, the year of the Tiger (1710), he was appointed to the temporary role of Goyōsho Yakunin (official of the administrative office).

Item: In the 8th month of the same year, he was formally appointed as a Goyōsho Yaku (official of the administrative office).

Item: In the 4th month of the 2nd year of Shōtoku, the year of the Dragon (1712), he traveled to Edo as part of an official escort for Kinban (periodic guard duty in Edo), served in the first division, and returned home.
　　Note: In his primary duties, he served three divisions of Kinban.

Item: In the 10th month of the same year, it was proclaimed that, although it was his first campaign/tour of duty, he had applied himself diligently and served admirably.

Item: In the 10th month of the 9th year of Kyōhō, the year of the Dragon (1724), as a reward for his diligent service during Kinban duty, he was granted one piece of silver (ginsu ichimai) as a commendation.

Item: In the 9th month of the 14th year of Kyōhō, the year of the Rooster (1729), he was appointed as an official of the Otenshu Oheikirkata (castle keep armory/weapons division).
