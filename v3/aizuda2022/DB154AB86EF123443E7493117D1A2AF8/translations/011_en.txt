Immediately thereafter, he made rounds to offer formal thanks to the Senior Councillors (Rōjū), the Junior Councillors (Wakadoshiyori), and the Chamberlains (Sōjaban) who had made the formal announcements. On the eighteenth day of the same month, he again received word from Lord Bungo-no-Kami to present himself at the castle at the hour of the Fourth Watch (approximately 10 a.m.) on the following nineteenth day. Accordingly, on the nineteenth day, at the appointed hour, he proceeded to the castle, where, in the Hinoki Room (Hinoma), Lord Bungo-no-Kami personally handed him an official letter (hōsho) and conveyed a formal message (kōjō). He was furthermore informed that he was to receive two seasonal garments (jifuku) and a padded haori jacket as rewards. After descending from the castle, he immediately made rounds to the Senior Councillors and Junior Councillors, then departed Edo and returned to the domain, where he presented himself before the lord and reported on the various matters concerning Edo.

　　Note: On the occasion when Obara Kyūtarō called upon Lord Bungo-no-Kami to receive an official letter (hōsho) regarding the presentation of the first salmon (hatsuzake) as tribute, the lord's secretary (goyōnin), Ishiyama Kaemon, conveyed that Higuchi Jūuemon's audience had gone splendidly without any flaw, and that much praise had been given among the lord's companions (onakama). It was said that Lord Bungo-no-Kami had instructed that Gensuke and Jūuemon be informed of this matter.
