Died of illness. Posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō): Jisseiin Gizanryōtai Koji. Place of burial: Monryōin, within the precincts of Sengakuji (Bansōzan Sengakuji), Takanawa, Edo.

Daughters: [One daughter married to] adopted son Koganji Mitsumoto
　　　　　[One daughter married to] adopted son Jūdayū Mitsugō

【Jūtarō Mitsutomo】
　Mother: daughter of grandfather Kanzaburō Mitsunuki
　Wife: daughter of Yoshida Jirōzaemon Toshika

One item: In the third month of the sixth year of Kansei (1794), year of the Tiger, in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (o-mie) with the lord.

One item: In the twelfth month of the seventh year (1795), year of the Rabbit, having applied himself diligently to calligraphy studies and being reputed to show skill beyond his years, he was awarded five sticks of ink as a reward.

One item: In the first month of the eighth year (1796), year of the Dragon, he was appointed as an assistant clerk (benju tetsudai) at the Calligraphy Academy (Shogakuryō).
