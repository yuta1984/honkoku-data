Item: In the 6th year of Kansei, the year of the Tiger (1794), in connection with the construction and repair works (gofushin) at the Shiba residence (o-yashiki), he was jointly appointed with Kōbe Saemon to oversee the transportation and routing of various construction materials, serving as supervisor (saihan) of the route from Seizandō to the area before Shirakawa. He departed on the 27th day of the 4th month, traveled to that location, completed his duties, and returned on the 22nd day of the 5th month.

Item: In the 12th month of the 7th year of Kansei, the year of the Hare (1795), in connection with the transportation of timber and various materials for the construction works at the Shiba residence, as well as the dispatch and routing of construction materials, he carefully assessed the situation regarding gains and losses, managed the matter appropriately, and personally assisted with the transportation. In recognition of this, he was awarded 3 silver coins (ginsu) as a commendation.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 8th year of Kansei, the year of the Dragon (1796), having extended his management of rural administrative affairs (jige-toriatsukai) effectively, and having conscientiously and diligently devoted himself over the years to the matter of circulation funds (mawashi-kin) as a contingency measure for the domain's internal financial adjustments (go-naishō no go-kuriawase), and having performed his duties in an exemplary manner, he was, in recognition of his exceptional merit, promoted to the rank of jōge-kaku (常上下格; a rank permitting formal audience with superiors on regular occasions).

Item: In the 9th month of the same year, he was appointed as the Granary District Magistrate (o-kuraire-kōri-bugyō), and was granted a stipend supplement (yakuryō) of 100 koku.
