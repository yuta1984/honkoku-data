Item: In the 2nd month of An'ei 7, the year of the Dog (1778), upon the retirement of Kayano Gonbei, he was transferred to fill the resulting vacancy in the unit of Harada Ihei.

Item: In the 10th month of the same year, he was additionally appointed as land magistrate (daikan) for the Minami-yama御蔵入 (domain-administered) district.

Item: In the 4th month of Tenmei 1, the year of the Ox (1781), upon the death from illness of Harada Ihei, he was transferred to fill the resulting vacancy in the unit of Takahashi Koemon.

Item: In the 9th month of Tenmei 4, the year of the Dragon (1784), upon Takahashi Koemon's reassignment to a different post, he was transferred to fill the resulting vacancy in the unit of Kayano Gondayū.

Item: In the 7th month of Tenmei 5, the year of the Snake (1785), he was appointed to the post of Inspector (metsuke).

Item: In the 8th month of the same year, he was granted a stipend supplement (yakuryō) of 30 koku.

Item: In the 7th month of Tenmei 6, the year of the Horse (1786), he was appointed to the provisional post of District Magistrate (kōri bugyō).

Item: In the 10th month of the same year, the upper residence (kamiyashiki) of Satō Heizaemon was granted to him.
