One entry: In the ninth month of the same year (1804), he was appointed to the position of "ko-yaku" (minor official) with duties involving entering and working within the Hon-to (primary) Dual Treasury (Ryō Go-nando). He was instructed to model his conduct after that of Hosokawa Sadayoshi, and to carry out his duties with even greater diligence.

One entry: In the tenth month of the same year, as Kando Sanemon was departing for Edo, he was ordered to begin handling the duties of the Go-shakkin-kata (loan management office) in Sanemon's place. However, whenever Sanemon was absent or on official duty in another domain, he was ordered to serve as substitute on each such occasion.
　　Note: He was similarly ordered to take up the duties of entering the Shinryō (shrine revenue) accounts.

One entry: In the twelfth month of the same year, he received commendation for having appropriately arranged the procurement and transport of timber for the construction of the domain school.

One entry: In the eighth month of the second year of Bunka (1805, the Year of the Ox), he was appointed to the temporary post of Kōri Bugyō (district magistrate). During intervals when this duty permitted, he was ordered to continue both his primary and concurrent duties.

One entry: In the seventh month of the third year of Bunka (1806, the Year of the Tiger), as Kando Sanemon had been transferred to another post, he was appointed sole incumbent of the position of principal official (shuyaku) of the Go-kanjōsho (accounts office). Accordingly, he was ordered to also handle the Go-shakkin-gakari (loan management) duties. His concurrent roles as Yama Bugyō (forestry magistrate) and Kaimai Kamiyaku (rice transport supervisor) were to continue as before, while he was relieved of his temporary appointment as Kōri Bugyō and his concurrent post as Go-fune-gakari (shipping supervisor).
