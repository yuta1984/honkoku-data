Item: In the 11th month of Meiwa 3, the year of the Dog (1766), he was granted, without dispute, the hereditary stipend of 100 koku, was appointed to the Tozama-gumi (outer retainer unit), and served as castle guard under the command of Sugita Gorōbei's unit.

Item: In the 9th month of Meiwa 4, the year of the Boar (1767), in accordance with Sugita Gorōbei's request, he was relieved of his post, and was subsequently transferred to the unit of Kasuya Sakon to fill the resulting vacancy.

Item: In the 10th month of Meiwa 5, the year of the Rat (1768), it was officially proclaimed that he was regarded with favor for his diligent application to scholarship and his exemplary conduct.

Item: In the 11th month of Meiwa 6, the year of the Ox (1769), he was additionally appointed to the post of land magistrate (daikan).

Item: In the same month, he was ordered to transfer to the unit of Kayano Gonbei.

Item: In the 10th month of An'ei 3, the year of the Horse (1774), his wish to change his name to Kakuemon was granted as requested.

Item: In the 11th month of An'ei 5, the year of the Monkey (1776), due to difficulties in the domain's finances, he applied himself diligently to the temporary duties of fundraising and procurement under the accounts office (motojikata), and was therefore awarded two bundles of cotton (wata) as a reward.
