Item: In the eleventh month of the same [era's] ninth year, the year of the Rat (1780), as his superior (kashira) Harada Ihee had died of illness, the subsequent post assignment fell under Takahashi Kouemon's unit.

Item: In the eighth month of Tenmei 4, the year of the Dragon (1784), his superior Takahashi Kouemon was appointed to the position of karō (senior councillor); the subsequent post assignment fell under Kayano Gondayū's unit.

Item: In the second month of the same [era's] fifth year, the year of the Snake (1785), it was officially proclaimed that he had performed his castle guard duty without negligence or dereliction.

Item: In the first month of Kansei 1, the year of the Rooster (1789), in accordance with his petition, his son Shōgorō was appointed as his replacement on duty (go-bandai).

Item: On the twenty-first day of the first month of the same [era's] fourth year, the year of the Rat (1792), he died of illness at the age of fifty-eight.
