　　Note: He served in this role until the completion of the project in the tenth month of the seventh year of the same era, the year of the Sheep (1667).

- In the intercalary second month of the seventh year of Kanbun, the year of the Sheep (1667), with the lord's private approval, the stipend of 250 koku that had been granted to him until that time was transferred to his son Kanzaburō, and he himself was granted a stipend of 150 koku in its place.

- In the sixth month of the tenth year of Kanbun, the year of the Dog (1670), as requested, his second son Genzaburō was appointed as his duty substitute (gobandai).

- On the twenty-third day of the tenth month of the sixth year of Enpō, the year of the Horse (1678), he died of illness at the age of seventy-six. His posthumous Buddhist name was Gyokuzan Dōju Koji. His place of burial was Zenshōji Temple at Shōunzan.
