He was summoned as a permanent Edo attendant (jōzume) and registered in the Ohiroma records.

Item: In the 4th year of Enpō, the year of the Dragon (1676), month unknown, he was granted release from permanent Edo duty as he had requested.
　　Note: Although released from permanent Edo duty, he was not sent back to Aizu, and until the 8th year of Enpō (1680), he served in various additional and temporary posts. Furthermore, when Ishihiko-sama's (Lord Hoshina Masakane's) personal secretary, Ono Shichibe, became unable to perform his duties, Kakuemon stepped in as a temporary replacement. As a result, in addition to his regular stipend, he was repeatedly granted gifts of kosode (short-sleeved robes), gold, and silver.

Item: In the 8th year of the same era, the year of the Monkey (1680), in the 10th month, he was appointed as a clerk (monokakiyaku) at the Aizu Katte Kaisho (the domain's administrative office), and proceeded to Aizu.

Item: In the 3rd year of Genroku, the year of the Horse (1690), in the 10th month, he was appointed to the role of Bunko-yaku (archive keeper), as well as to duties overseeing the designs of curtains, pictures, and banners for both lecture halls (ryōkōsho).

Item: In the 11th year of the same era, the year of the Tiger (1698), month unknown, he petitioned for permanent retirement due to illness, whereupon he received instructions to rest and take care of his health.

Item: In the 1st year of Hōei, the year of the Monkey (1704), in the 1st month, he was granted an increase in stipend of three koku (record continues on next page).
