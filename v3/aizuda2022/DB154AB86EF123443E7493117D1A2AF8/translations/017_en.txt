Item: In the sixth month of the ninth year of Kyōhō (1724), the year of the Dragon, [he] was granted seven rations (ninin-fuchi) and summoned to serve in the kobан (small guard unit).

Item: In the fifth month of the twelfth year [of Kyōhō] (1727), the year of the Sheep, [he] was appointed as御右筆 (御右筆, official scribe/secretary) and was granted a stipend of fifteen koku in gigi-saku (assessed/nominal stipend) along with three rations (san-nin-fuchi).

Item: In the ninth month of the same year, [he] traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned in the third month of the following year of the Monkey (1728).

Item: In the ninth month of the fourteenth year [of Kyōhō] (1729), the year of the Rooster, [he] was appointed to御内用 (private/confidential duties for the lord).

Item: In the fourth month of the fifteenth year [of Kyōhō] (1730), the year of the Dog, [he] traveled up [to Edo] for attendant duty (kinban), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: During the campaign period in the fifth month, as he had performed his duties with great diligence, as a reward he was granted, in the lord's presence, one set of formal hemp ceremonial dress (asa-kamishimo). In the sixth month, he received as a gift from the御納戸 (lord's personal storehouse) a summer robe (katabira) bearing the lord's family crest. In the eighth month, he was appointed to handle the transmission of official correspondence (kakidashi-tsugi), and it was proclaimed that he had performed this duty admirably.
