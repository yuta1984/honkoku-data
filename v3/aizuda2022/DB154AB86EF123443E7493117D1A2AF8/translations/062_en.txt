Item: (Continued from the previous page, regarding the 10th year of Kansei, the year of the Horse, 1798) Having completed his official duties, he departed Edo on the 16th day of the 6th month and returned home.

Item: In the 4th month of the 11th year of Kansei, the year of the Sheep (1799), in connection with a boundary dispute (ryōzakai-deiri) between Yamagata Village in the Inawashiro River Eastern Group (Kawahigashi-gumi) and Hamaji Village in the Nihonmatsu domain, he was informed, given that this matter related to duties he had previously carried out, that he should consult thoroughly with Saitō Yoshichirō regarding this case and manage it appropriately.

Item: In the 5th month of the same year, regarding the conversion of the fixed tax rate (men-kirikae) for the granary lands (o-kuraire), he successfully obtained the necessary inquiry so that matters would proceed at the discretion of the local administration (jige), and furthermore, concerning the increased lands under the domain's custody, he diligently negotiated and coordinated the matter with the Edo resident staff (Edo-zume-ai). In recognition of his diligent service, he was awarded one used formal summer garment (o-furu o-katabira) as a commendation.

Item: In the 8th month of the same year, he traveled to the Echigo entrusted domain (o-azukari-sho), conducted a circuit inspection of the villages (kaison), then proceeded directly to the Niigata area, inspected the domain's granary (o-kurashō), and returned in the 9th month.

Item: In the 8th month of the 12th year of Kansei, the year of the Monkey (1800), he again traveled to the Echigo entrusted domain and returned in the 9th month.

Item: In the 5th month of the 1st year of Kyōwa, the year of the Rooster (1801), he was appointed as Senior Inspector (Ō-o-metsuke).
