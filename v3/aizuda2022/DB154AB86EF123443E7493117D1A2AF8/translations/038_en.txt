One item: In the tenth month of the same year (Bunka 5, 1808), he was additionally appointed to serve as an assistant in the horse inspection office (komakarame tetsudai) on a hired basis, in addition to his existing duties.

One item: In the fifth month of the sixth year of Bunka (1809), year of the Snake, as Matsumoto Yodayū was relieved of his position as instructor of ceremonial music and dance (bugaku) and his title was changed to Head (tōdori) of the Bugaku Division, the need for an assistant instructor was no longer necessary. He was thereafter appointed to attend on the days of the Court Music Assemblies (gagaku-kai) during gaps in his regular duties, as had been the case until then, and to assist Yodayū in looking after the training of students.

One item: In the fifth month of the seventh year of Bunka (1810), year of the Horse, as he had applied himself diligently to managing local affairs (jige toriatsukai) and had also performed additional temporary duties, he was awarded three bundles of cotton as a reward.

One item: In the sixth month of the ninth year of Bunka (1812), year of the Monkey, as he had made great efforts in managing the arrangements for the child-rearing fund (sanshi yōikukin), he was awarded two bundles of cotton as a reward.

One item: In the intercalary eleventh month of the tenth year of Bunka (1813), year of the Rooster, due to a reduction in personnel, he was relieved of his hired duties, and his responsibilities were transferred in full to his heir (chakushi). As he had applied himself diligently in his service until then, he was awarded one bundle of cotton as a reward.

One item: In the tenth month of the thirteenth year of Bunka (1816), year of the Rat, he was appointed as Head of Ceremonial Music (bugaku tōdori) on a hired basis, and was granted a stipend of three persons' worth of provisions (san-nin fuchi) from the school budget (gakkō-ryō)...
