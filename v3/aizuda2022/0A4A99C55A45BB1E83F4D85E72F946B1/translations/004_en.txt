One. In the third month of Enpō 5 (1677), the year of the Snake, he was granted a standard stipend of 8 ryō in gisaku-kin (simulated stipend gold) and provisions for four persons, and was summoned to serve as a page (okoshō).

One. In the fourth month of the following year, Enpō 6 (1678), the year of the Horse, at the time of the lord's departure for Edo (sankō), due to his young age, he was graciously excused from accompanying the lord to Edo, and it was declared that he would remain in the domain (Aizu).

One. In the fourth month of Enpō 8 (1680), the year of the Monkey, at the time of the lord's departure for Edo, he accompanied the lord and traveled up to Edo.

One. In the sixth month of the same year, as Lady Hōshōin had retired, he was immediately appointed as a page to the main household (omotesama okoshō).

One. From the seventh month of the same year, having fallen ill, he petitioned for leave to return to Aizu. His petition was granted, and he returned to Aizu in the tenth month.

One. In the sixth month of Tenna 2 (1682), the year of the Dog, as punishment (oshioki) was handed down to Hinata Hannojō and his son, [this person] was distanced from the lord's close attendants (gokinju) and placed in a position with no official duties (muyaku).

One. In the ninth month of Jōkyō 2 (1685), the year of the Ox, he was granted a stipend of 25 koku and provisions for four persons, and was appointed to serve as a travel escort (otomobан).
