Item: In the ninth month of the tenth year [of Bunka], the year of the Rooster (1813), he turned out for the *otoridori* (a hunting drive/military exercise in the form of a bird hunt).
　　Note: After this, he turned out on one further occasion and received game as a gift.

Item: In the intercalary eleventh month of the same year, during the winter hunting drive (*tō-okari*), it was proclaimed that the ranks, marching order, and coordination among the units were well organized, that the inter-unit operations proceeded without hindrance, and that he had long been diligent in joint-unit training drills (*gōtai-keiko*).
　　Note: After this, he received commendations (*go-shōbi*) on every subsequent occasion.

Item: In the tenth month of the fourteenth year [of Bunka], the year of the Ox (1817), as he had diligently applied himself to military exercises (*sōren*) and trained well, it was proclaimed that he would be granted one new spear (*atarashimi no yari ippō*).

Item: In the third month of the first year of Bunsei, the year of the Tiger (1818), he was granted his family headship (*katoku*) and a stipend (*go-chigyō*) of two hundred *koku*, without alteration.
