Daughter of Kurokouchi Oризaemon Tokioki [Born on the sixteenth day of the second month of the first year of An'ei, the year of the Dragon (1772).]

Item: In the intercalary seventh month of the ninth year of Hōreki, the year of the Serpent (1759), in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience [with the domain lord].

Item: In the second month of the fifth year of An'ei, the year of the Monkey (1776), he was granted without issue a hereditary stipend (*chigyō*) of two hundred *koku*, was assigned to the *tozama-gumi* [outer retainer unit], placed in the unit of Ueda Hachirozaemon, and undertook service as a castle guard.

Item: In the first month of the seventh year [of An'ei], the year of the Dog (1778), he was appointed to *otomo-ban* [escort guard duty].

Item: In the tenth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for rotational duty (*kinban*), and returned [to Aizu] in the fifth month of the following year of the Boar (1779), accompanying the lord's entourage.

Item: In the fifth month of the eighth year [of An'ei], the year of the Boar (1779), it was declared that he had applied himself diligently and performed his duties in camp without any lapse.

Item: In the ninth month of the ninth year [of An'ei], the year of the Rat (1780), he traveled up to Edo for an extraordinary rotational duty assignment, and returned in the fifth month of the following year of the Ox (1781), accompanying the lord's entourage.
　　Note: It was declared that he had applied himself diligently and performed his duties in camp without any lapse.

Item: In the fourth month of the third year of Tenmei, the year of the Hare (1783), he traveled up [to Edo] to return [with the lord] as part of the welcoming party.
