One. In the fourth month of the fourth year [Jōkyō 4, 1687], the year of the Rabbit, when the lord traveled up to Kyoto, he served as an escort for the entire journey (kami-shimo, both the upward and downward legs).
　　Note: While in Kyoto, when Lord Tsuchiya, Governor of Sagami (Sagami-no-kami), held a banquet in the lord's honor, he was additionally appointed to serve as a page (okoshō) and performed attendant duties at the banquet.

One. In the second month of Genroku 2 (1689), the year of the Snake, at the time of the lord's alternate attendance (sankō), he traveled up to Edo as an escort on duty-rotation, and returned in the ninth month of the same year.

One. In the ninth month of the third year of Genroku [Genroku 3, 1690], the year of the Horse, he traveled up to Edo as a duty-rotation guard, and returned in the third month of the following year of the Sheep [Genroku 4, 1691].

One. In the eighth month of Genroku 4 (1691), the year of the Sheep, when the lord made his return journey to the domain (gege), he traveled up [to Edo] to escort the lord back [to Aizu].

One. In the third month of Genroku 5 (1692), the year of the Monkey, he was granted leave and immediately withdrew from the domain capital (Aizu).

One. In the tenth month of Genroku 6 (1693), the year of the Rooster, he was graciously permitted to wander/reside in the domain capital (Aizu), and promptly returned there.
