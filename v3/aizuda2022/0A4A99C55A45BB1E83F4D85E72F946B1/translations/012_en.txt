One. In the twelfth month of the same year [Hōreki 3, 1753], it was declared that he had diligently performed his duties, including night watches without sleep, while Lord Kyōtei-sama was suffering from measles.

One. In the ninth month of the fifth year of Hōreki [1755], the year of the Boar, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the third month of the following year of the Rat [1756].
　　Note: As he had applied himself diligently during the posting, and had served attentively through three consecutive postings up to and including the current one, he was granted one kosode (short-sleeved kimono) as a reward.

One. In the seventh month of the eighth year of Hōreki [1758], the year of the Tiger, as he had diligently participated in and performed his duties during the investigation (sensakū) concerning the former shrine families (shake) of Takada and Isasumi Shrine, he was granted one tan of bleached cloth (sarashi).

One. In the ninth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the fourth month of the following year of the Rabbit [1759] as part of an official escort.
　　Note: As he had applied himself diligently during the posting, he was granted one set of formal ceremonial dress (kamishimo) made of old hempen cloth (furuma-asa) from the lord's wardrobe.

One. In the fourth month of the eleventh year of Hōreki [1761], the year of the Snake, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: It was declared that he had applied himself attentively and diligently during the posting.
