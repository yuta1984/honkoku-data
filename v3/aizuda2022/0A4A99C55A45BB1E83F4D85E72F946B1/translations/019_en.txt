Item: In the fourth month of the sixth year [of Tenmei], the year of the Horse (1786), he traveled up to Edo for rotational duty (*kinban*), and returned [to Aizu] in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: He was provisionally appointed to the role of *otodo-ban* [gate guard duty] in camp, and it was declared that he had performed his duties without any lapse.

Item: In the twelfth month of the seventh year [of Tenmei], the year of the Sheep (1787), an order was issued regarding food and drink in connection with the domain's austerity measures (*ken'yaku*). It was declared that [this retainer and] his fellow colleagues had all agreed amongst themselves to strictly observe [the restrictions] without violation, and that this was regarded as a commendable act.

Item: In the third month of the eighth year [of Tenmei], the year of the Monkey (1788), he was ordered to appear on the occasion of the *ohayashi* [musical performance/accompaniment], and thereafter attended every such *ohayashi* performance.

Item: In the same month, he traveled up [to Edo] to return [with the lord] as part of the accompanying escort.
　　Note: It was declared that he had performed his duties throughout the journey without any absences.

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, he was appointed to *otodo-ban* [gate guard duty].
