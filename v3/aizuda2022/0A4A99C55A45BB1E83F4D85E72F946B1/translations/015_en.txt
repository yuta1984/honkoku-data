One. In the intercalary ninth month of the same year [Meiwa 4, 1767], he was granted a stipend allowance (yakuryō) of fifty koku.

One. In the second month of the seventh year of Meiwa [1770], the year of the Tiger, he had devoted his full efforts to managing affairs within the domain (jige), handled matters well, and ensured general tranquility. Furthermore, the stockpiling of rice established by successive lords (honto o-kakoimono), which had fallen into abeyance, was gradually revived under his management, and in recent years he had applied himself diligently to ensure it was ready in time for urgent needs. For these services, he was commended and granted one set of formal ceremonial dress (kamishimo) made of old hempen cloth.

One. In the sixth month of the eighth year of Meiwa [1771], the year of the Rabbit, he was appointed to the provisional post of Town Magistrate (machi bugyō), serving until the seventh month of the same year.

One. In the eighth month of the first year of An'ei [1772], the year of the Dragon, timber dispatched from Yonezawa to Edo was transported through the domain's territory. As he had handled various matters pertaining to the overland and river transport (kawasage) of said timber at the magistrate's office, he was granted three bundles of cotton (men sanwa) as a gift of gratitude from Lord Uesugi Danjō Daihitsu.

One. In the twelfth month of the same year, due to financial difficulties in the domain's private finances (ōuchi-shō), he applied himself with diligence to the urgent procurement duties of the chief financial administrator's office (motojikata) on a temporary basis, and for this he was granted three bundles of cotton.
