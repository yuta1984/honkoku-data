Item: In the fifth month of the sixth year [of Bunka], the year of the Snake (1809), as there was an accumulation of previously worn lord's garments (*o-meshifuru*), by special favor (*oboshimeshi*) [one was] granted [to him].

Item: In the fifth month of the seventh year [of Bunka], the year of the Horse (1810), in accordance with his petition, he was relieved of his current duties (*tōmu*), and his son Kazen was appointed in his place (*o-bandai*).

Item: In the first month of the eighth year [of Bunka], the year of the Sheep (1811), as he had investigated the inventory ledger (*o-dōguchō*) of implements that had been sent down from Edo five years prior during his previous term of service, and had diligently applied himself to this special duty (*rinji no o-yō*), he was granted one piece of silver (*ginshi ichimai*) as a reward (*go-hōbi*).

Item: In the third month of the first year of Bunsei, the year of the Tiger (1818), in accordance with his petition, he was granted permission to retire (*inkyo*).
