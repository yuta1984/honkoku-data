In the third month of the eighth year [Genroku 8, 1695], the year of the Boar, he was due to travel up [to Edo] as a duty-rotation guard, but since his father Saburōemon had died of illness and he was in the period of mourning (imchū), he was temporarily excused from duty, and traveled up in the fourth month of the same year.

One. In the sixth month of the same year, he was granted one hundred koku as a divided portion (bunchi) of the stipend that had belonged to his late father Saburōemon.

One. In the same month, as the lord made his return journey to the domain (gege), he served as an escort and traveled down [to Aizu].

One. In the fourth month of the ninth year [Genroku 9, 1696], the year of the Rat, as the lord traveled up for alternate attendance (sankō), he returned [to Edo] and served as an escort.

One. In the fifth month of the eleventh year [Genroku 11, 1698], the year of the Tiger, he traveled up [to Edo] as an escort on duty-rotation, and returned in the ninth month of the same year.

One. In the third month of the thirteenth year [Genroku 13, 1700], the year of the Dragon, when he traveled up [to Edo] as an escort on duty-rotation, he was appointed to serve as a temporary gate guard (kari-otobari) during the lord's journey, performed this duty, traveled up [to Edo], and returned in the tenth month of the same year.

One. In the fifth month of the fifteenth year [Genroku 15, 1702], the year of the Horse, he traveled up [to Edo] as an escort for the lord's return journey to the domain.
