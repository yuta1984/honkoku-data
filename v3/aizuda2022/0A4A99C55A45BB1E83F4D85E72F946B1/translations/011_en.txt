One. (Continued from the previous page) Regarding the request to arrange a mutual exchange (aitate-gae) of the gate residence (mon-yashiki), the request was granted.

One. In the second month of the first year of Hōreki [1751], the year of the Sheep, he was appointed as an ometsuke (inspector/censor).

One. In the third month of the same year, he was granted fifty koku as an office stipend (yakuryō).

One. In the ninth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned in the third month of the following year of the Monkey [1752].
　　Note: Although it was his first posting (shojin), it was declared that he had performed his duties without any impediment or delay.

One. In the fifth month of the second year of Hōreki [1752], the year of the Monkey, he was granted the former residence plot of Hara Genhachi in Yonocho, Yonechiro district, as a reassigned property (ふりかえ).

One. In the tenth month of the same year, as the posthumous Buddhist title (goreigō) of Lord Tsuchitoko-sama was brought down from Kyoto, he went as far as Inawashiro, Yōjiمura (Yanagi Village), and performed duties until [the procession] was enshrined at the Mineyama Shrine (Minejinja).

One. In the ninth month of the third year of Hōreki [1753], the year of the Rooster, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the third month of the following year of the Dog [1754].
　　Note: As he had applied himself diligently to his duties during the posting, he was granted one tan of bleached cloth (sarashi) in lieu of a formal ceremonial dress (kamishimo).
