Item: In the ninth month of the third year [of Kyōwa], the year of the Boar (1803), as he had diligently attended to the management of the lord's garments (*o-meshimono*) for many years, and had furthermore served with diligence at the numerous and busy locations during the lord's return visit (*o-gekō*) to the domain, he was granted, by private/informal arrangement, one set of *o-meshifuru asa kamishimo* [a formal hemp garment previously worn by the lord].

Item: In the eleventh month of the second year of Bunka, the year of the Ox (1805), as mementos (*go-ibutsu*) from Lord Kyōtei [posthumous name], the following items were granted: *o-noshime* [a formal patterned silk garment], *o-kosode* [a small-sleeved robe], *shiro o-kosode* [white kosode], *shima o-awase* [striped lined garment], *moiwa o-kataginu* [a gauze-weave sleeveless garment], two *some o-kosode* [dyed kosode], one *o-ura* [lining garment], *some o-katabira* [dyed unlined summer garment], *asa o-kamishimo* [hemp formal wear], and additional items without the lord's family crest.

Item: In the eighth month of the third year [of Bunka], the year of the Tiger (1806), as mementos (*go-ibutsu*) from Lord Teishō [posthumous name], various crested (*go-mon-tsuki*) garments were granted.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, as he had diligently served as a companion (*o-aite*) to the current lord and had also applied himself assiduously to managing the lord's garments, he was granted, by private/informal arrangement, one bundle (*hitotaba*) of cotton (*wata*).

Item: In the sixth month of the fourth year [of Bunka], the year of the Rabbit (1807), as there was an accumulation of *o-hitare* [a formal court-style garment worn by the lord], [one was] granted [to him].
