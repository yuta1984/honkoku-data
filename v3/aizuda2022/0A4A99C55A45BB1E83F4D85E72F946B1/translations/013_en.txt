One. In the twelfth month of the same year [Hōreki 11, 1761], it was declared that, as he had applied himself diligently over a number of years, an additional fifty koku of stipend (oyakuryō) would be granted to him directly on top of his existing stipend.

One. In the same month, he was called out to participate in the inspection of the military weapons (heiki), and on this occasion, rather than conducting only a routine inspection, he was ordered to thoroughly examine each item in the weapons inventory. Accordingly, he inspected without exception the main keep (tenshū) as well as the various watchtowers (yagura) and storehouses (kura) throughout the castle.

One. In the first month of the thirteenth year of Hōreki [1763], the year of the Goat, he was granted his wish to change his name to Ichiemon.

One. In the sixth month of the first year of Meiwa [1764], the year of the Monkey, as he had applied himself with great diligence and hardship from early morning until very late in the evening during the handover inspection of the military weapons department, he was granted two silver coins (ginshi).

One. In the tenth month of the same year, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the fifth month of the following year of the Monkey [1765].
　　Note: As he had performed his duties without any impediment during the posting, he was granted one set of formal ceremonial dress (kamishimo) made of old hempen cloth (furuma-asa) from the lord's wardrobe.
