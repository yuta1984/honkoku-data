One. In the fourth month of the twentieth year [Kyōhō 20, 1735], the year of the Rabbit, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: When he traveled up to Edo for the duty rotation, he did so together with the page (okoshō) Inokuchi Hikonoshин.

One. In the seventh month of the first year of Kanpō [1741], the year of the Rooster, he was appointed as a daikan (local magistrate/land steward).

One. In the ninth month of the first year of Enkyō [1744], the year of the Rat, as he had applied himself diligently to the totategate duties (collection and assessment work), he was granted two bundles of cotton as a reward.

One. In the fourth month of the third year of the same era [Enkyō 3, 1746], the year of the Tiger, when the kaikoku-shi (circuit envoy) lodged at Takada Village, he went there and performed the required official duties.

One. In the second month of the third year of Kan'en [1750], the year of the Horse, as there was an investigation (osenkaku) to be conducted among the peasants of Inawashiro, Kawanishi, and Kawahigashi, he was ordered to apprehend them. Together with the unit commander (monoganashi) and the whole group, he went out, arrested a large number of people, returned, and handed them over to the bugyō (magistrate) for litigation.
　　Note: Regarding the aforementioned official duty, it was declared that he had applied himself diligently and performed his service with distinction.
