Hinata Ichiemon Tsugitake　(Marginal note: First called Kayano; first adult name [yomyō] Kyūma; also known as Tōma. Born in Hōei 3 [1706], the year of the Dog.)
　Mother: same as above.
　Wife: daughter of Ichiyanagi MataShichirō Naōtsune. (Marginal note: Born on the second day of the third month of Kyōhō 1 [1716], the year of the Monkey. Died of illness on the twenty-third day of the ninth month of Kōwa 3 [1803], the year of the Boar. Posthumous Buddhist name: Shōkan'in Myōjō Nikkō Daishi. Place of burial: Hōshōzan Jōkōji temple.)

One. In the eleventh month of Kyōhō 2 [1717], the year of the Rooster, he was granted his inherited stipend (atoshiki gochigyō) of one hundred koku without any discrepancy. He was appointed to the Tozama-gumi (outer-retainer unit). As he was still a minor, he was exempted from castle guard duty for the time being, and was ordered to submit the ko-bushin-ryō (a fee paid in lieu of active service).
　　Note: He was placed in Ikoma Gobei's unit.

One. In the twelfth month of the fourth year [Kyōhō 4, 1719], the year of the Boar, in accordance with his request, he was ordered to begin active service, and was relieved of the obligation to pay the ko-bushin-ryō.
