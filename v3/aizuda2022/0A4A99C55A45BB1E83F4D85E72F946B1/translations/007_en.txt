One. In the twelfth month of the second year [Hōei 2, 1705], the year of the Rooster, he was appointed as a gate guard (otobari).

One. In the fourth month of the third year [Hōei 3, 1706], the year of the Dog, when the lord traveled up for alternate attendance (sankō) via the southern route, he was appointed to handle the lodging arrangements (oyado-wari) during the lord's journey, traveled up [to Edo], and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: It was declared that he had performed the duty of arranging lodgings along the lord's route without any delay or difficulty.

One. In the third month of the fifth year [Hōei 5, 1708], the year of the Rat, he traveled up [to Edo] as an escort for the lord's return journey to the domain.

One. In the eleventh month of the sixth year [Hōei 6, 1709], the year of the Ox, as he had requested, he was relieved of his current post and appointed to the Tozama-gumi (outer-retainer unit), was placed in Sugita Gorōbei's unit, and served as a castle guard (oshiro o-ban).

One. In the third month of the eighth year [Hōei 8, 1711], the year of the Rabbit, following the death of unit head (kashira) Sugita Gorōbei, he was transferred to Ikoma Gobei's unit to succeed to that post.

One. On the eighth day of the ninth month of the second year of Kyōhō [Kyōhō 2, 1717], the year of the Rooster, he died of illness at the age of fifty-four. His posthumous Buddhist name (hōmyō) was Jushōin Jōsei Nisshin Koji. His place of burial was Hōshōzan Jōkōji temple.
