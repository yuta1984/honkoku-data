One. In the fourth month of the second year of Meiwa [1765], the year of the Rooster, as he had performed his duties without impediment during the posting, he was granted one set of formal ceremonial dress (kamishimo) made of old hempen cloth (furuma-asa) from the lord's wardrobe, presented before the lord himself.

One. In the fifth month of the same year, he was appointed to the provisional post of Construction Magistrate (ofushin bugyō).

One. In the ninth month of the same year, word arrived that Mori Ōnoshin had been detained in Mito awaiting arrest. He was therefore ordered by personal directive from above to travel to that location, take custody of Mori, and bring him back. He promptly traveled there, took custody of the individual, returned, and handed him over to the Litigation Magistrate (kōji bugyō).

One. In the fourth month of the third year of Meiwa [1766], the year of the Dog, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation, and returned in the tenth month of the same year.
　　Note: As he had applied himself diligently during the posting, he was granted one set of formal ceremonial dress (kamishimo) made of old hempen cloth.

One. In the fifth month of the fourth year of Meiwa [1767], the year of the Boar, he was ordered, together with Yokochi Chūemon, to divide into two groups and travel throughout the domain's districts in order to inspect the physical inventory (genbutsu) of the village granaries (gōzō) belonging to each unit (kumi).

One. In the eighth month of the same year, he was appointed to the provisional post of District Magistrate (kōri bugyō).

One. In the ninth month of the same year, he was appointed to the full post of District Magistrate (kōri bugyō).
