One. In that same month [the twelfth month of Kyōhō 4, 1719], his request was granted to change his family name to "Hinata" and to change his given name to "Tōma."

One. In the tenth month of the seventh year of the same era [Kyōhō 7, 1722], the year of the Tiger, he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned in the fourth month of the following year of the Rabbit [Kyōhō 8, 1723].

One. In the first month of the ninth year [Kyōhō 9, 1724], the year of the Dragon, he was appointed to the otomo-ban (escort guard duty).

One. In that same month, his request was granted to change his name to "Ichiemon."

One. In the second month of the tenth year [Kyōhō 10, 1725], the year of the Snake, in accordance with his request, he was relieved of his current post and appointed to the Tozama-gumi (outer-retainer unit). He was placed in Harada Gorōemon's unit and performed castle guard duty (oshiro goban).

One. In the second month of the thirteenth year [Kyōhō 13, 1728], the year of the Monkey, as he had been in financial distress for some time and found it difficult to fulfill his official duties, in accordance with his request, he was ordered to retire to his home province (zaikyō), and was granted an allowance of five-person stipend (gonin fuchi).

One. In the eighth month of the seventeenth year [Kyōhō 17, 1732], the year of the Rat, in accordance with his request, he was ordered to return to active service. He was placed in Yamazaki Sasuke's unit and performed castle guard duty.

One. In the third month of the eighteenth year [Kyōhō 18, 1733], the year of the Ox, he was granted the upper residence (uwayashiki) of Shiomi Heizaemon, located at Yoneshiro Shi-no-chō.
