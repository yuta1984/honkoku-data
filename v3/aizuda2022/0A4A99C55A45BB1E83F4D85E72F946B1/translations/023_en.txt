Tōma Tsugitake (first called Kayoshi [*sic*]; Born in the intercalary third month of the first year of An'ei, year of the Dragon [1772])
　　Mother: a daughter of the Tatsuno clan
　　Wife: a daughter of Ariga Gunbei Mitsubu — later divorced
　　　　　a daughter of Higuchi Kakuemon Yoshimitsu (Born on the eighth day of the eighth month of the sixth year of An'ei, year of the Rooster [1777])

Item: In the seventh month of the fifth year of Tenmei, the year of the Snake (1785), in accordance with his father's petition, he was granted his first formal audience (*o-mie*) [with the lord].

Item: In the tenth month of the third year of Kansei, the year of the Boar (1791), he was appointed to the post of *haizen shimar-kata* (supervisor of food service/provisioning).

Item: In the twelfth month of the ninth year [of Kansei], the year of the Snake (1797), as he had performed his duties as *haizen shimar-kata* satisfactorily, he was granted one piece of silver (*ginshi ichimai*) as a reward.

Item: In the fifth month of the seventh year of Bunka, the year of the Horse (1810), in accordance with his father's petition, he was appointed as a replacement (*o-bandai*) in the *tozama-gumi* (outer/affiliated corps), assigned to the unit of Ibuka Shizuma, and took up service as a castle guard (*o-shiro o-ban*).

Item: In the eleventh month of the eighth year [of Bunka], the year of the Sheep (1811), [he was assigned] to the third unit (*sanban-gumi*) under the chief retainer (*o-karō*)...
