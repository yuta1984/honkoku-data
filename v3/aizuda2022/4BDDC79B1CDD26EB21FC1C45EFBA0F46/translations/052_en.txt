**[Right folio]**

Note: After this, he was appointed to various posts on numerous occasions.

One item: In the third month of An'ei 1 (Year of the Dragon), the Kodowari-kata post was discontinued; however, it was decreed that he should remain in place as an acting junior magistrate (ko-bugyō kari-yaku) as before.

One item: In the eighth month of An'ei 4 (Year of the Sheep), he was appointed junior magistrate (ko-bugyō) and granted a role stipend (yakuryō) of four koku and five to.

One item: In the twelfth month of An'ei 7 (Year of the Dog), in recognition of his diligent service over three years up to the current year — during which he had personally conducted on-site inspections and managed various maintenance and distribution matters without needing to report upward for resolution — he was awarded a bonus of two bu of gold.

**[Left folio]**

One item: In the twelfth month of An'ei 8 (Year of the Boar), in recognition of his diligent service in the new construction works at the domain's guest residence (o-yado yashiki), he was awarded a bonus of one bu of gold.

One item: In the sixth month of An'ei 9 (Year of the Rat), he was ordered to proceed to the upper residence (kami o-yashiki) in connection with the new construction of the temple hall and related buildings there. He departed in the same month, traveled up to the capital, completed his assigned duties, and returned home in the ninth month of the same year.
　Note: He was also ordered to concurrently serve as a document writer (monokaki), and was granted two ryō and two bu of gold as a supplement for expenses—
