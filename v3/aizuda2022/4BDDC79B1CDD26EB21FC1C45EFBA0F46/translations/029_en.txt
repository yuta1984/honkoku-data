(Continued from the previous page) He was informed by official order that the aforementioned grant would be bestowed upon him.

One entry, the eighth month of the same year: As the workload of the permanent duty officers (常詰仲ヶ間) had recently increased considerably, and this had given rise to associated labor and expenses, it was officially decreed that the confidential duty officers (密事平) along with their colleagues would each receive an additional annual expense allowance (費金) of one ryō of gold per person.

One entry, the twelfth month of the same year: On account of labor and expenses incurred in relation to confidential duties (御内用), he was granted an expense subsidy (費補金) of three bu.

One entry, the tenth month of the second year (Year of the Rabbit): Should there be occasion for the commencement of Lady Teikain's new residence arrangements or a change of residence, he was ordered to consider himself appointed to handle such matters together with the construction and repair duties (御普請御用). Moreover, regarding the right—

One entry, the twelfth month of the same year: Following the re-establishment of the Magistrate's Office (奉行所御再興), he was ordered to additionally serve concurrently in the duties of the Administrative Office (御用部屋) at this location.
