**[Right folio]**

—[the *o-gisaku* stipend was] revoked. Having diligently served for several decades, and in recognition of the fact that [his] son Tanzō likewise had meritorious service, from within the father's *o-gisaku* grant, one *koku* and one person's stipend (*ichi-nin fuchi*) were reduced, and he was granted eleven *koku* and three persons' stipend (*san-nin fuchi*). He was summoned as a permanent-duty (*jō-zume*) *go-yōsho* official, and it was announced that he was to be placed in the *go-kinshū ni-no yoriai* (second-tier attendants' assembly) seat.

- In the same month, he was ordered to additionally serve in the *go-yōsho* temporary hire duties.
  - Note: He was instructed to regard himself as being assigned to the *go-naishō-kata keiko-gakari* (private/inner domain practice supervisor).

- In the ninth month of the same year, [his] father Kichirōji petitioned to be permitted to wear, as an act of thanksgiving (*myōga*), the formal hemp ceremonial *kamishimo* bearing the lord's crest (*go-mon*), which had previously been granted to him as a gift.

**[Left folio]**

- In the twelfth month of the same year, it was announced that during the preceding summer, he had diligently served despite the large volume of *o-mie* (audience/attendance) duties.

- In the sixth month of the eleventh year of the same era (Year of the Dog), it was announced that during the previous winter, at the time of the *go-ninkан* (official appointment ceremony), he had diligently served despite the numerous duties involved.

- In the eighth month of the same year, he was appointed to the temporary post (*tōza kari-yaku*) of *mitsugoto go-yō* (secret/confidential affairs duty).
