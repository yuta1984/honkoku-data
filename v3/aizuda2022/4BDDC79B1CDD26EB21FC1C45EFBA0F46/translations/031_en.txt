One entry, tenth month of the same year (Bunsei 4): Over the course of several years, [the retainer] had applied himself diligently to the duties of the *Onaisho-kata* (the department handling internal/financial affairs), including matters of financial coordination, serving with wholehearted dedication. Upon being appointed to oversee the duties related to the *go-nyūyo* (the bride's ceremonial procession/entry into the household), he distinguished himself with exceptional diligence amid a great multiplicity of duties. In recognition thereof, he was directly granted an increase of five *koku* in his stipend allowance (*oyakuryō*). Furthermore, since his supplementary service in the *Goyōbeya* (official business chamber) since the Year of the Rabbit (*usagi-doshi*, Bunsei 2, the year before last), he had applied himself industriously to ad hoc duties even while managing a heavy primary workload. As a reward for this, he was granted one bundle of cotton (*wata hitotaba*), as proclaimed by official order.

One entry, second month of the fifth year of the same era (Bunsei 5, Year of the Horse): A request was granted permitting [the retainer] to adopt *Shige*, the son of Suzuki Tamon, as his heir.
