**[Right folio]**

- In the ninth month of the same year (Meiwa 2), he was appointed to the provisional post of *Warijoba Monokaki* (document writer at the allocation office).

- In the first month of the third year (the Year of the Dog, Meiwa 3), having served diligently and well as *Ginmikata Sadatsuki* (assigned inspector), he was granted an increase of five *to* in stipend.

- In the same month, he was appointed *Warijoba Monokaki* (document writer at the allocation office) in the capacity of *Mochikaku* (a rank held without the full formal appointment).

- In the tenth month of the same year, having served well during the repair works on the damaged stone walls of the castle, he was awarded 700 *mon* in copper cash (*torime*) as a reward.

**[Left folio]**

- In the second month of the fifth year (the Year of the Rat, Meiwa 5), he was appointed *Demono Ukesōji no Mono* (inspector/receiver of outgoing goods) in the capacity of *Mochikaku*.

- In the twelfth month of the same year, it was recognized that during his previous duties, when the survey drawings (*oeszu*) for the damaged castle stone walls were completed, he had served with diligence and distinction, and he was accordingly commended.
*(Note: The original text contains a correction [*misekeshi*/strikethrough], changing "被仰出候事" to "御賞被成下候事")*

- In the twelfth month of the seventh year (the Year of the Tiger, Meiwa 7), he was appointed to the provisional post of *Kobugyo* (minor magistrate), and was further assigned to the role of *Warijoba Kodowari-kata* (small distribution officer at the allocation office).
