**[Right folio]**

...the expenditures were also reduced; therefore, as a reward, he was granted two *bu* of gold (*kin nifun*).

— In the third month of the same year, Kansei 8 (Year of the Dragon), as he had diligently attended to his duties on a daily basis, and in connection with the construction works (*ofushin*) at the Shogakuryo (calligraphy/writing academy), he personally contributed funds (*sunshi*) and worked with great diligence. As a result, he was granted a stipend of two-person rations (*futari fuchi*) and was ordered to carry out his duties in the same manner as a full-rank officer (*honraku*).

— In the fifth month of the same year, Kansei 11 (Year of the Sheep), as he had worked with great diligence and given careful attention to the domain's revenues (*o-irikata*), resulting in reductions in expenditure (*genkata*), he was granted three *bu* of gold as a reward.

**[Left folio]**

— In the twelfth month of the same year, Kansei 12 (Year of the Monkey): regarding the fire that broke out at the residence of Zentō Shirō the previous July, he was commended for having rushed promptly to the Furukawa Ochaya (domain tea house) and working with great diligence.

— In the fourth month of Kyōwa 2 (Year of the Dog), one *koku* was deducted from the *ogisaku* (hereditary stipend adjustment) that had been granted to his late father, leaving him with nine *koku* and two-person rations (*futari fuchi*); he was called up to serve as *kobugyo* (junior magistrate).
　　Note: The two-person rations that had previously been granted were rescinded.
