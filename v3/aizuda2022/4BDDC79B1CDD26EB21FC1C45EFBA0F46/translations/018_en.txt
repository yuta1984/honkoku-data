- In December of the eleventh year (Year of the Sheep), having applied himself diligently in both duties (*ryōmu*) and having been attentive and thorough in his daily service, it was proclaimed that, as a special recognition, his existing role stipend (*oyakuryō*) of two *koku* would be increased by one additional *koku*.

- In May of the first year of Kyōwa (Year of the Rooster, 1801), as Lady Jūhōin underwent the *obi-iwai* (ceremonial girding of the abdomen) and the *sodedome* (sleeve-fastening ritual), he was ordered and duly served in the duty of arranging the proceedings for said ceremony, including the ceremonial duties at childbirth (*osan no oyaku*), the seventh-night celebration (*o-shichiya*), the *omakura-naoshi* (ritual repositioning of the birth pillow), and other celebratory rites.

- In the same month, having served as the officer in charge (*goyōgakari*) during the celebratory observances of Lady Jūhōin's *obi-iwai*,

**[Left folio]**

he was granted three hundred *hiki* in gold.

- In October of the same year, having performed similar service during the seventh-night (*o-shichiya*) celebration, he was again granted three hundred *hiki* in gold.

- In June of the third year (Year of the Boar, 1803), during a period of abundant duties in both posts (*ryōmu*), he exerted himself to the fullest and rendered particularly distinguished service.
