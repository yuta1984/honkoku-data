- In December of the same year, he was appointed to the role of *Ginyaku* (inspector/examiner) at the *Omiya* (lord's private quarters) *Goyōninjo* (administrative office). His stipend of one *ryō* in gold was to remain as before, and his seat rank was placed next to that of the *Omotegoyōninjo Ginyaku* (inspector of the outer administrative office).

- In the same month, his role as *Omiya Omakanai-gashira* (head of the provisions/catering for the lord's private quarters) was discontinued; he was ordered to concurrently serve as *Omakanai-gashira* and also to take on the duties of *Omakanai-yakunin* (provisions officer).

- In May of the sixth year (Year of the Tiger), on the occasion of Lord Teruaki's *Omaekami wo toraseru* ceremony (the ceremony of cutting the forelock signifying coming of age), he served as the officer in charge of the relevant duties,

**[Left folio]**

and from the same lord, was granted three hundred *hiki* in gold.

- In December of the same year, he was appointed to the concurrent post of *Omiya Omakanai-gashira*, and despite the great busyness of the role, he applied himself diligently to managing various expenditures in meticulous detail, achieving a considerable reduction in outlays, establishing a precedent for future practice, and performing his duties with great dedication and thoroughness. In recognition of this, it was proclaimed that, as a special reward, an additional stipend of one *ninfuchi* (one person's ration allowance) would be added to his existing salary of one *ryō*.
