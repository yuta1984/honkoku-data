**[Right folio]**

- Lord Teisho (*Teishō-sama*) granted him one hundred *hiki* in gold.
  - Note: Thereafter, this was to be granted annually.

- In the second month of the ninth year of the same era (Year of the Snake), on the occasion of Lord Teishō's wedding ceremony, he was assigned to a specific role and was granted an allowance for formal clothing (*fuku-dai*).

- In the eighth month of the same year, having continuously and strenuously carried out his duties throughout Lord Teishō's illness, he was granted sake (*o-sake*) as a reward.

- In the seventh month of the tenth year of the same era (Year of the Horse), he was appointed to assist with the administration of kenjutsu (*ken-jutsu shime-ri-kata tetsudai*), and was ordered to serve as a witness/second (*taishō*) opposite Ōba Gendayū.

**[Left folio]**

- He was so ordered.

- In the ninth month of the same year, because his father Kichirōji had diligently served for several decades, and his son Tanzō had also diligently served as a hired scribe (*mono-kaki yatoi*), both were granted two portions of stipend (*futari-fuchi*), and it was announced that Tanzō was to be summoned as a resident scribe to the attendant office (*Teishō-sama jōzume go-yōnin mono-kaki*) of Lord Teishō.

- In the twelfth month of the same year, having diligently carried out his duties throughout the year despite a heavy volume of official business, he was granted two hundred *hiki* in gold by Lord Teishō.
  - Note: Thereafter, this was to be granted annually.
