- In the eleventh month of the same year (Meiwa 6, Year of the Ox), he was attached to Ichiemon, who served as the Motojimeriyū-gakari (finance section official), and traveled up to Edo, returning home in the first month of the following Year of the Tiger.

- In the third month of the seventh year (Year of the Tiger, Meiwa 7), as Yūgakari Ichiemon was ordered to travel to the Kamigata (Kyoto-Osaka) region on official business, he accompanied Ichiemon on this journey and returned in the sixth month of the same year.

- In the seventh month of the same year, while assisting with the accounting duties of the Gisō-kata (granary welfare office), he diligently managed the accounts from the very beginning and completed the financial records satisfactorily. As a reward for this, he was granted two kanmon in copper coin (toriame).

- In the eighth month of the same year, he was ordered to serve as an accounting assistant for the Gisō-kata at the town office (Machi-yakusho).

【Left Page】

　　He was appointed to this duty.

- In the eleventh month of the same year, a person fell ill among those serving at the Kaimai-yakusho (rice transport office), causing an impediment to operations. He was therefore returned to the town office, where he continued serving through the third month of the following year.

- In the first month of An'ei 1 (Year of the Dragon), he was ordered to assist with the accounting of the Otéate (stipend/allowance) funds at the town office. He completed the financial records by the third month of the same year, and was then continuously ordered to compile the accounts of the Gisō-kata.

- In the fourth month of the same year, having compiled the accounts of the Otéate funds satisfactorily, he was granted one bu of gold as a reward.
