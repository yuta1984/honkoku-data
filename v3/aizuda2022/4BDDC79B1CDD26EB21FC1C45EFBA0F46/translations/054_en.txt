Item: In the seventh month of the same fourth year (year of the Dragon), he was appointed as the castle-assigned junior magistrate (oshiro sadatsuki ko-bugyō).

Item: In the same month, having diligently and satisfactorily performed various assigned duties single-handedly for several months, he was privately granted one bu as a reward.

Item: In the sixth month of the same fifth year (year of the Snake), he was granted the residential plot (ato-yashiki) previously held by Nakajima Katsugoro.

Item: In the eighth month of the same year, it was proclaimed that he had diligently and satisfactorily performed his duties during the lord's residence in the domain.

[Left folio]
Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, having diligently performed his duties, he was granted the privilege of independent formal audience (dokurei), though this was considered a matter of considerable gravity/distinction.

Item: In the second month of the same sixth year (year of the Horse), his seat ranking was placed below Kobayashi Uhachirō and above the horse doctor (uma-i) Higuchi Ichirōbē.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same eighth year (year of the Monkey), having diligently and satisfactorily performed various duties related to personal/direct service (o-temoto go-yō), he was privately granted one bu as a reward.

Item: In the sixth month of Kansei 3 (year of the Boar), having diligently and satisfactorily performed duties during the lord's journey down to the domain (o-gekō), he was privately granted one hundred hiki as a reward.
