(Continued from the previous page) In connection with this, although he had already been serving as an official in charge of internal financial matters (御内証懸り), he was ordered to attend the Finance Office (御勘定所) as much as his duties permitted, and not only to conduct the aforementioned investigations, but also to discuss matters of retrenchment and economy (御省略筋) thoroughly, and to manage affairs in a manner beneficial to the domain.

One entry, the eighth month of the same year: While remaining in his current post, his title was changed to "御用所密事勤め" (Confidential Duties Officer of the Administrative Office). He was instructed to continue his duties as before, and that matters falling under the supervision of the Administrative Office Group Leader (御用所組頭) were to be submitted through said Group Leader.

One entry, the seventh month of the fourteenth year (Year of the Ox): In recognition of his diligent and thorough single-handed investigation of matters relating to the spring commendation review (御賞方吟味), he was rewarded as follows:

**[Left folio]**

He was granted two bundles of cotton (綿二把) as a reward.

One entry, the sixth month of the same year: He was appointed and ordered to handle the duties related to the wedding procession (御入輿) of Lady Teikain (貞鑑院様).

One entry, the eighth month of the same year: He was ordered to serve in the lord's general service appointment (殿様御用任).

One entry, the same month: He was ordered to serve in the appointment responsible for the allocation of row-house residences (御長屋割御用任).

One entry, the third month of the first year of Bunsei (Year of the Tiger): In recognition of his many years of diligent service and attentive management of assigned duties (懸り御用向き), he was granted an additional stipend allowance of five koku (御役料五石).
