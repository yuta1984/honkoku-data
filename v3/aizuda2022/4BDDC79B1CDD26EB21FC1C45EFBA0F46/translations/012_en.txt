- In the eleventh month of the sixth year (Year of the Rooster, An'ei 6), having completed the formal accounts of the Honto O-nando (main household treasury), and having done so diligently on his own due to his colleague's inability to perform their duties, he was granted one bu of gold.

- In the twelfth month of the seventh year (Year of the Dog, An'ei 7), having compiled the Honto O-nando transfer accounts using his own devised methods, completing them promptly, and having established a standard procedure for future reference, he was granted two bu of gold as a reward.

- In the ninth month of the eighth year (Year of the Boar, An'ei 8), he was appointed as a temporary acting official for the Kaimai Kura-kata (rice transport warehouse section), serving through the second month of the following Year of the Rat.

【Left Page】

　　　Note: During his service, as the Honto O-nando was also short-staffed and there were administrative difficulties there, he was ordered to serve in both offices to the extent possible, and he managed to fulfill duties in both posts concurrently.

- In the twelfth month of the first year of Tenmei (Year of the Ox, Tenmei 1 [1781]), having compiled the Honto O-nando transfer accounts satisfactorily, he was granted one bu of gold.

- In the tenth month of the sixth year (Year of the Ox, Tenmei 6 [1786]), he was appointed as the Jōdzume Goyōnin Monokaki (resident personal secretary/clerk) to Lord Sadaaki, and was granted one ryō of gold per year as an official stipend (yakunin-ryō).
