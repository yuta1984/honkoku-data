[Right folio]
Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, having diligently and satisfactorily performed duties related to personal/direct service (o-temoto go-yō), he was granted two bu.

Item: In the twelfth month of the same fourth year (year of the Rat), regarding the duties for Lord Sadaaki's upcoming visit to the domain in the coming spring, he was ordered to travel up to Edo around the second month of the following year.

Item: In the first month of the same fifth year (year of the Ox), he traveled up to Edo for the aforementioned duties, and returned in the fourth month of the same year.
　　Note: In connection with the above, he was granted three bu as a stipend to cover expenses, and additionally was privately granted one ryō.

[Left folio]
Item: In the fifth month of the same year, having carefully deliberated and managed various matters at the castle and at a number of other construction/repair sites (go-fushin-sho), yielding considerable benefit, and having diligently served over many years, he was directly granted an increase of three koku from within his official stipend (go-yaku-ryō).

Item: In the second month of the same sixth year (year of the Tiger), he was appointed as acting inspector of construction works (go-fushin-kata ginmi-yaku kari-yaku), and was ordered to concurrently manage the castle and all other sites previously assigned to his charge, which he continued to perform until the eighth month of the same year.
