**[Right folio]**

—[the seat/rank was] returned to the legitimate heir (*chakushi*). He was ordered to serve as an *o-heya go-yōnin-sho* temporary hire (*o-yatoi-kin*), was granted an increase of one person's stipend (*fuchi*), bringing his total to three persons' stipend (*san-nin fuchi*), and it was announced that the expense allowance (*hiryo-kin*) of two *ryō* per year would continue to be granted as before.

- In the eighth month of the second year of Bunka (Year of the Ox), his father Kichirōji was reassigned (*o-yaku-gae*) and traveled down to Aizu. However, as the father was suffering from frequent illness and had also advanced in years, [the subject, Tanzō,] wished to travel down with him to provide nursing care. He accordingly submitted a petition to be relieved of his duties as a temporary hire at the *go-yōnin-sho* (the officials' office). Although no official directive (*go-gechi*) was forthcoming, he traveled down as a road companion (*dōchū-tsuki*) upon submitting his petition.

**[Left folio]**

- In the third month of the sixth year of the same era (Year of the Snake), he was ordered to serve as a temporary hire (*tōza o-yatoi*) for document work (*shomono*) at the *go-yōsho* (administrative office).

- In the twelfth month of the seventh year of the same era (Year of the Horse), having diligently served as a temporary hire for reviewing historical records (*kyūki-shirabe*) at the *go-yōsho*, he was granted two *ryō* and one *bu* in gold as compensation for his labors (*honeori-ryō*).

- In the third month of the ninth year of the same era (Year of the Monkey), having diligently served as a temporary hire for document work at the *go-yōsho*, and additionally, in connection with the official duties for the Sagami Province defense preparations (*Sōshū o-sonae-kata go-yō*), there being various ad hoc writing tasks (*ninaimono*), he served with particular diligence, and was accordingly granted two *ryō* and one *bu* in gold.
