**[Right folio]**

— and had established a precedent for future practice and rendered meritorious service. In recognition of this, it was proclaimed that, as a special reward, his *okitte-kin* (stipend paid in gold certificate) of three *ryō* would be converted to six *koku* in rice certificates (*ishi-kitte*), with an additional two *koku* granted as a role stipend (*oyakuryō*).

- In February of the same year, he served as the officer in charge of Lord Teruaki's wedding ceremony, and was granted an allowance for formal clothing (*fukushiro*).

- In April of the same year, during the smallpox illness of Lady Jūfūin, he worked diligently in service, and as a result was granted two hundred *hiki* in gold by Lord Teruaki.

**[Left folio]**

- In the same month, having applied himself diligently to the duties related to Lord Teruaki's wedding ceremony, he was granted two hundred *hiki* in gold as a reward.

- In August of the same year, having worked diligently and continuously throughout Lord Teruaki's illness, he was granted two hundred *hiki* in gold from the same lord.

- In September of the tenth year (Year of the Horse), having applied himself diligently in service over several decades, and as his son Katsuzō had also been serving diligently as an *emonkaki oyatoi* (a hired copyist/clerk), they were granted a stipend of two *ninfuchi* (rations for two persons). It was further proclaimed that Katsuzō was to be summoned to serve as a copyist/clerk (*monokaki*) to the resident chamberlain (*jōdzume goyōnin*) of Lord Teruaki.
