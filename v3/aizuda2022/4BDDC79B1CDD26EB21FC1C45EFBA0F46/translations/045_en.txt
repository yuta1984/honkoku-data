**[Right folio]**

He was granted a stipend of two *nin-buchi* (rations for two persons) and was employed as a foot soldier (*ashigaru*) in the御手前組 (*o-temae-gumi*) unit.

- In the second year of Enpō (1674, Year of the Tiger), month unknown: He was granted permission to wear the formal *kamishimo* garment, and was appointed as the foreman (*tōryō*) of the stonework (*ishikata*) operations.

- In the second year of Tenna (1682, Year of the Rooster), month unknown: He received an additional stipend of two *koku* of rice and was appointed as foreman of the stonework.

- In the ninth year of Genroku (1696, Year of the Rat), ninth month: He received an additional stipend of five *to* of rice.

- In the sixth year of Hōei (1709, Year of the Ox), second month: In accordance with his petition, his *gisaku* (fictitious/temporary land allocation) was revoked.

**[Left folio]**

- In the third year of Shōtoku (1713, Year of the Snake), seventh month, third day: He died of illness at the age of seventy-five. His posthumous Buddhist name (*hōmyō*) was Tengai Shōshin (天外性身); he was interred at Chōeisan Rinshōji temple.
