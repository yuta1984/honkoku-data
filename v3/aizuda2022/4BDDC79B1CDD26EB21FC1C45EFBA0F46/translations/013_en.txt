　　(Continued from previous page) He was appointed as Goyōnin Monokaki (personal secretary/clerk) and placed in the position next to the Shokан Monokaki (correspondence clerk) within the secretarial office.

- In the eleventh month of the same year, as the household of his cousin Kaku Daihachi (角大八) was large and he was unable to support them all, permission was granted as requested for him to take in Daihachi's younger brother, Gen'nosuke (源之助), as a dependent (yakkai) in his own household.

- In the tenth month of the eighth year (Year of the Monkey, Tenmei 8 [1788]), having diligently and capably handled the arrangements for Lord Sadaaki's marriage proposal affairs, he was granted two hundred hiki of gold from Lord Sadaaki himself.

【Left Page】

　　He was appointed as the combined role of Omakanaiyaku (provisioning/household steward) for Lord Sadaaki. Prior to Lord Sadaaki's formal establishment of his own household (o-noridashi), he was ordered that, while important matters at the Goyōnin-sho (personal secretary's office) would be treated separately, all other duties should be shared roughly half-and-half with the provisioning duties.

- In the third month of the same year, having diligently served in the affairs related to Lord Sadaaki's formal establishment of his own residence (o-noridashi), he was granted one hundred hiki of gold.

- In the fourth month of the same year, as the investigation and handling of the aforementioned affairs was exceedingly numerous and busy, and having served diligently throughout, he was granted three hundred hiki of gold from Lord Sadaaki.
