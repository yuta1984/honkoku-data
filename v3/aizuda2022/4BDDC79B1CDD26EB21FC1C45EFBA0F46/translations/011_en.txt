【Right Page】

　　(Continued from previous page) He was appointed to this duty and served through the twelfth month of the same year, receiving one kanmon in copper coin (toriame) as compensation for his hard work (honeori-ryō).

- In the second month of the eighth year (Year of the Rabbit, An'ei 8), having completed two years' worth of accounting for the Gisō-kata (granary welfare office) and having compiled the records satisfactorily, he was granted two kanmon in copper coin.

- In the same month, he was ordered to continue compiling the remaining five years' worth of accounts for the Gisō-kata, and while engaged in this duty, in the fourth month of the same year, the Motojimermonokaki (head finance clerk) fell ill. He was therefore appointed as a temporary acting official for various administrative matters, serving in this capacity through the sixth month of the same year.

【Left Page】

- In the tenth month of the second year (Year of the Snake, An'ei 2), having completed five years' worth of accounting for the Gisō-kata, he was granted three bu of gold as a reward.

- In the second month of the fourth year (Year of the Sheep, An'ei 4), he was additionally assigned to serve at the Kaimai Kura-kata (rice transport warehouse section). Henceforth, he was relieved of his duties as Sairyō (cargo/transport supervisor) and ordered to serve in the same manner as those assigned to the warehouse section.

- In the eleventh month of the same year, he was appointed as a temporary acting official for the Honto O-nando Chōtsuki (head household treasury record-keeping) post.

- In the fourth month of the fifth year (Year of the Monkey, An'ei 5), he was formally appointed to the Honto O-nando Chōtsuki post.
