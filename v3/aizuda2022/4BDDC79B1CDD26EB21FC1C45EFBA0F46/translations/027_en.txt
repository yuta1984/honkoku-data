**[Right folio]**

One entry, the ninth month of the same year: Regarding the adjustment of financial regulations (矩調) for this domain's revenues (御入方), while serving in the temporary post for confidential matters (密事仮役), he was also directed to take charge of related duties, attend the Finance Office (御勘定所), and, beginning with the management of the circulation of gold, silver, and coin, if he had opinions on matters under review, to discuss them without hesitation with the Chief of Finance (御勘定頭). Furthermore, he was ordered to attend and conduct investigations without delay, as far as official duties permitted.

One entry, the second month of the twelfth year (Year of the Boar): He was appointed to confidential duties (密事御用).

One entry, the fourth month of the same year: He was ordered to serve in charge of practice duties related to the domain's financial administration (御勝手方稽古御用).

**[Left folio]**

One entry, the fifth month of the same year: Upon the occasion of the lord's entry into the domain (御入部), as His Lordship was to make a pilgrimage to Nikkōsan, he was ordered to return and serve in the escort party, departing from the domain and returning in the sixth month of the same year.

One entry, the sixth month of the same year: It was stated that he had diligently and without incident fulfilled his duties in escorting the lord during the domain entry (御入部) and along the road, and had taken particular pains in handling matters such as the allocation and distribution of personnel (人高割渡), managing affairs admirably.

One entry, the fifth month of the thirteenth year (Year of the Rat): Regarding this important financial matter (御大用), he was urgently ordered to investigate, along with all revenues up to the designated amount (号迄の御入方), the losses and shortfalls (御失墜) after the lord's return to Edo, as well as the progress of procurement and procurement methods.
