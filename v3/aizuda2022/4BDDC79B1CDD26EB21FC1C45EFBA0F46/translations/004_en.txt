Genealogy of Ōmori Katsuzō
Clan name: Unknown　Province of origin: Unknown
Family crest (jōmon): Kakukiri-kaku futokage tsuta (angular-cut, bold-shadow ivy)
Alternate crest (kawamon): Maru no uchi san-sasa (three bamboo leaves within a circle)

Ōmori Shirōemon Katsuyo (first name: Gonzaemon; born in the Year of the Dog, Genna 8 [1622])
　　Mother: Clan name unknown
　　Wife: Younger sister of Hasegawa Sawaemon (surname unknown)
　　　　(Died of illness on the 13th day of the 12th month of Jōkyō 4 [1687], Year of the Rabbit.
　　　　Buddhist posthumous name: Kikō Ryōetsu Zenjōni.
　　　　Officiant priest: Shōbōji Temple, Tennei Village.
　　　　Place of burial: Bodaiin-zan)

　　　The ancestors had served the Kanamori clan for generations;
　　　however, due to certain circumstances, Shirōzaemon and his two brothers,
　　　three siblings in total, became rōnin (masterless samurai).
