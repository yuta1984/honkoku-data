**[Right folio]**

- In the fourth month of the eleventh year of the same era (Year of the Sheep), on the occasion of Lord Teishō's visit (*go-gekō*) to Aizu, he was ordered to serve a six-month rotation (*kinban*) at the Aizu domain residence. He traveled down to accompany and manage the official duties of the said role, and returned to Edo in the ninth month of that year.

- In the fifth month of the same year, having diligently performed duties during the journey (*go-dōchū go-yō*), he was granted five *ryō* in silver as a reward.

- In the first month of the twelfth year of the same era (Year of the Monkey), having diligently carried out his duties, it was announced that he would be granted two *ryō* in expenses (*hikin*) each year on an ongoing basis.

- In the fifth month of the first year of Kyōwa (Year of the Rooster), on the occasion of the celebratory ceremony of Lord Teishō's consort, the Lady Juhōin (*Juhōin-sama*), donning the *obi* (the ritual girding of the sash marking pregnancy), he was assigned to manage the related official duties—

**[Left folio]**

—and for having carried out those duties, he was granted two hundred *hiki* in gold.

- In the tenth month of the same year, on the occasion of the seventh-night celebration (*o-shichiya*) ceremony, having served in the duties related thereto, he was granted two hundred *hiki* in gold.

- In the fourth month of the third year of the same era (Year of the Boar), as Lord Teishō was to make his visit (*go-gekō*) to Aizu, he was ordered to serve a six-month rotation (*kinban*) at the Aizu residence. He traveled down to accompany and manage the said official duties, and returned in the ninth month of that year.

- In the fifth month of the same year, having diligently performed duties during the journey, he was granted five *ryō* in silver as a reward.
