— In the ninth month of Bunka 6 (Year of the Snake), his request to change his name to Shichirōemon was granted.

— In the twelfth month of Bunka 7 (Year of the Horse), having worked with great diligence over many years, and regarding the construction works (*ofushin*) on the lord's residence (*tono-sama go-jūkyo*): although the works were urgent given the circumstances of the time, he did not simply leave matters to others but examined every detail closely, consulted appropriately with the craftsmen (*shokunin*), and made all the necessary arrangements, so that the work was completed with exceptional speed. — In the twelfth month of Bunka 9 (Year of the Monkey), regarding the construction works at the main shrine (*go-honsha*) at Mineyama, he consulted appropriately with all parties and worked with great diligence; as a result, he was granted a seating rank (*seki*) immediately following that of Ikari Gōsuke.
