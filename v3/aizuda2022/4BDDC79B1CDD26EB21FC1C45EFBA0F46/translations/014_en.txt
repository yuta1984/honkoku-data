【Right Page】

　　(Continued from previous page) He was formally appointed as Provisioning Official (Omakanaiyakunin) for Lord Sadaaki, and was ordered to serve concurrently as Goyōnin Monokaki (personal secretary/clerk). His seat ranking was set as the position next to the Oomote Omakanaiyakunin (Senior Provisioning Official), and his official stipend (oyakuryō) of one ryō of gold continued to be granted as before.

- In the sixth month of the same year, he was appointed as the official in charge (goyō-gakari) of the funeral arrangements for Lady Shinryōin (心了院様).

- In the twelfth month of the same year, he was appointed as Otoshiotoko (New Year's attendant) for Lord Sadaaki, and was granted one hundred hiki to cover the cost of formal hakama attire (kamishimo).

【Left Page】

- In the sixth month of the second year (Year of the Dog), having diligently served as the official in charge of Lord Sadaaki's investiture affairs the previous winter, he was granted one hundred hiki of gold from Lord Sadaaki as a reward.

- In the same month, while in the status of waiting appointment (machiseki), he was appointed as Goyōnin Monokaki (personal secretary/clerk) for Lord Sadaaki, and was ordered to serve concurrently as Ginmiyaku (inspector/investigator). Furthermore, he was ordered that when the Provisioning Office (Omakanaigata) became particularly busy, he should also attend and assist there.

- In the tenth month of the same year, regarding the previous Year of the Rooster (Tori-doshi) when the operating budget (gobunki) of the Provisioning Office (Omakanaishi) had been reduced and restructured, having served diligently and capably, the reduction in the operating budget was kept to a minimum...
