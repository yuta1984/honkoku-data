Item: In the same month, having diligently and satisfactorily served during the construction/repair works (go-fushin) of the Toyooka Shrine, he was granted two bu as a reward.

Item: In the third month of the same eighth year (year of the Dragon), having distinguished himself with exceptional diligence over many years and having rendered devoted service in connection with the construction/repair works of the Shogakuryō (calligraphy/writing academy), he was granted one ryō as a reward.

Item: In the sixth month of the same ninth year (year of the Snake), while serving in the role of o-temoto shōryaku nin-yaku (a post for the reduction of direct/personal service expenditures) among the pages (o-koshō),

— having obtained guidance and managed affairs to reduce expenditure (o-iri-kata), and as this was also a matter befitting the circumstances of the times, he was ordered to perform duties even in the capacity of hayaku (early/prompt service roles).

Item: In the fifth month of the same eleventh year (year of the Sheep), having diligently served for many years, and having paid close attention to reducing expenditures across the various construction/repair works including those related to personal/direct service (o-temoto go-yō), thereby rendering distinguished service, he was directly granted an increase of one koku and five to from his existing official stipend (go-yaku-ryō).
