- In June of the second year of Bunka (Year of the Ox, 1805), he was appointed to the post of *Aizu Katte Hontō Onandoyaku* (the regular treasury officer (*o-nandō*) responsible for the domain's finances in Aizu).

- In August of the same year, when he was about to return to Aizu, as he had been suffering from frequent illness of late and had also advanced in years, he petitioned that he be excused from his hired service (*o-yatoi-kin*) so that he might bring his son Tanzō along as a caretaker during the journey home. However, his request for leave was not granted; nevertheless, having formally petitioned, he departed for Aizu with his son Tanzō accompanying him as caretaker along the road.

- In September of the same year, in accordance with the petition of his son Tanzō, the latter was relieved of his hired service (*o-yatoi-kin*) at the chamberlain's office (*o-heya goyōnin-sho*), and the stipend (*o-fuchi-kata*) that had hitherto been granted to him

**[Left folio]**

was accordingly rescinded.

- In December of the same year, he was appointed to the role of *Edo-omote Goshōryaku Ninyaku* (a position established to reduce expenditures in the Edo establishment). His seat (*seki*) was placed above that of the *o-kanjō-gashira* (chief accountant). At this time he was ordered to travel up to Edo and remain stationed there until the spring of the coming Year of the Rabbit (*u no toshi*), to enter into dealings with various quarters, attend the offices of the various posts, and consult on measures to reduce expenditures (*o-irikata genshō*).

- In February of the third year (Year of the Tiger, 1806), he was ordered to carry out the *kami-o-yashiki hitowari goyō* (the duty of assigning personnel within the upper residence).

- In July of the same year, as Kawase Jūjirō was dispatched to Aizu on business relating to private financial affairs (*go-naishō-kata goyō*), [he was ordered to fill] the latter's post in the interim until Kawase's return to Edo.
