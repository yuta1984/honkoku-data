**[Right folio]**
　　He petitioned to resign from his post [due to hearing loss, continued from the previous page], but was told that his resignation could not be granted, and was instead ordered to take care of his health while continuing to serve in his duties.

Item: In the 3rd month of the 5th year [Enkyō 5, 1748], the Year of the Dragon, upon Lord Tōjō's [Doi Noto-no-kami Tsunehiro's] first official attendance on the Shogun (gosanpu), he traveled to Edo to serve as an escort on rotation duty, and returned home in the 8th month of the same year.

Item: In the 4th month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent service as escort to Lord Tōjō, he was granted a set of formal ceremonial dress (kamishimo) of old hemp cloth [御古麻御上下] in the presence of the lord.

**[Left folio]**

Item: In the 8th month of the same year, in recognition of his diligent service during the campaign [or military encampment], he was awarded, as a mark of commendation, a set of formal ceremonial dress (kamishimo) of old hemp cloth in the presence of the lord.

Item: In the 1st month of Kan'en 3 [1750], the Year of the Horse, his petition was granted and he was relieved of his official post, and was assigned to the Tozama-gumi (outer guard unit), entering the unit of Miyake Magobei, where he served as castle guard.

Item: In the 8th month of the same year, due to illness, upon petition, he returned his stipend for light guard duty (koban ukeryō).

Item: On the 19th day of the 5th month of Hōreki 5 [1755], the Year of the Boar, he died of illness at the age of 51. Posthumous Buddhist name: Kakushō-in Genyū. Place of burial: Same as above (as previously recorded).
