[Left folio]

One entry: In the tenth month of Kyōhō 1 (1716, year of the Monkey), upon the death by illness of his father Sasuke, being still young (a minor), he was granted a stipend of four persons' rations (yonin-fuchi) and was summoned to serve as an outside-the-unit retainer (kumigai no shi).

One entry: In the fourth month of the same ninth year (Kyōhō 9, 1724, year of the Dragon), he was appointed as a page (okoshō) to Lord Gyokurin'in, and was granted an annual expense allowance (hiryōkin) of three ryō per year.

One entry: In the second month of the same twelfth year (Kyōhō 12, 1727, year of the Goat), he was granted coming-of-age ceremony (genpuku) and was recalled to serve as an outside-the-unit retainer.

One entry: In the second month of the same thirteenth year (Kyōhō 13, 1728, year of the Monkey), he was appointed to the Aizu koban (lesser guard unit).
