[Right folio]
One entry: In the seventh year of the same era (Year of the Dog), in the eleventh month, his stipend of one hundred koku, which had until then been held as a provisional grant (gigi-saku), was converted into a formal domain stipend (go-chigyō) of one hundred koku.

One entry: In the ninth year of the same era (Year of the Rat), in the third month, he traveled to Edo for guard duty (kinban), completed one tour of service, and returned.

One entry: In the twelfth year of the same era (Year of the Rabbit), in the eighth month, he was assigned the duty of managing confidential documents (go-naiyō go-shomotsu).

One entry: In the thirteenth year of the same era (Year of the Dragon), in the tenth month, he traveled to Edo for guard duty, completed one tour of service, and returned.

One entry: In the fourteenth year of the same era (Year of the Snake), in the seventh month, he was granted a replacement residence (o-furikae) in the Yoneshiro Ni-no-chō district.

[Left folio]
One entry: In the first year of Hōei (Year of the Monkey), in the fourth month, he traveled to Edo for guard duty, completed one tour of service, and returned.

One entry: In the fourth year of the same era (Year of the Boar), in the sixth month, owing to his many years of distinguished service, he was awarded three pieces of silver (gin-su san-mai) as a reward.

One entry: In the fifth year of the same era (Year of the Rat), in the tenth month, he traveled to Edo for guard duty, completed one tour of service, and returned.

One entry: In the sixth year of the same era (Year of the Ox), in the second month, while in Edo, he was assigned the duty of drafting the lord's oath document (go-seishi), and having completed this task, he was granted a formal ceremonial garment bearing the lord's crest (go-mon no go-kataginu) in the lord's presence, and was additionally bestowed with gracious words of appreciation on various matters.
