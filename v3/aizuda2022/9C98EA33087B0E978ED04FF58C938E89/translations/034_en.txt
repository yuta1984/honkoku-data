【Right folio】
(Upon coming of age,) he was appointed to the role of Office Staff (御用所役人), retaining his previous stipend of fifteen koku in provisional allocation (御擬作) plus support for three persons.

Item: In the intercalary third month of Jōkyō 3 (Year of the Tiger), he was relieved of his duties together with Ōta Heikurō.

Item: In the seventh month of the same year, he was ordered into exile (御追放).

Item: In Genroku 6 (Year of the Rooster), in a month unspecified, through the intercession of the Head Abbot (大僧正) of Zōjō-ji Temple in Edo, he was permitted to return to service (帰参).

Item: In the eleventh month of the same year, he was granted a stipend of thirteen koku plus support for two persons, and was again appointed to the role of Office Staff.

【Left folio】
Item: In the eighth month of the sixteenth year of the same era (Year of the Sheep), the hereditary stipend (家督御知行) of one hundred koku was granted to him without discrepancy; the previously granted provisional allocation (御擬作) was rescinded, and he was ordered to continue serving as Office Staff as before.

Item: In the third month of Hōei 4 (Year of the Boar), due to arm pain that made writing impossible, he was, as requested, relieved of his duties and placed in a position without official role (無役).

Item: In the first month of Hōei 5 (Year of the Rat), he was appointed to the role of Weapons Officer (兵器役人).

Item: In the tenth month of Hōei 6 (Year of the Ox) — (entry incomplete)
