Bunuemon Naoomi (childhood name: Saburōtarō, also known as Izuemon; born on the 8th day of the 11th month of Hōei 3 [1706], the Year of the Dog)
　　Mother: Same as above (same mother as previously recorded)
　　Wife: Daughter of Nagaoka Katsubyōe Hisatada (died of illness on the 7th day of the 3rd month of Meiwa 2 [1765], the Year of the Rooster. Posthumous Buddhist name: Zuiō-in Myōjō Daishi. Officiating priest: Jōkōji Temple / Hōshōzan [written in red ink]. Place of burial: Bodai-in-zan)

Item: In the 6th month of Kyōhō 4 [1719], the Year of the Boar, at his father's petition, he was granted his first audience with the lord.

Item: In the 12th month of the 7th year [Kyōhō 7, 1722], the Year of the Tiger, at his father's petition, he was appointed to the Gobandai-tozama-gumi (outer guard unit), and served as castle guard under the command of Sugita Gorōbyōe's unit.

**[Left folio]**
　　He served on castle guard duty.

Item: In the 4th month of the 9th year [Kyōhō 9, 1724], the Year of the Dragon, he traveled to Edo for service rotation (kinban), and returned home in the 10th month of the same year.

Item: In the 5th month of the same year, while in Edo, he was granted his inherited stipend (atoshiki go-chigyō) of 150 koku without dispute.

Item: In the 9th month of Enkyō 1 [1744], the Year of the Rat, he was appointed as Inspector (Ometsuke).

Item: In the 9th month of the 2nd year [Enkyō 2, 1745], the Year of the Ox, he traveled to Edo for service rotation, and returned in the 3rd month of the following year [Enkyō 3, 1746], the Year of the Tiger.
