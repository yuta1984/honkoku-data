**[Left Folio]**

　　Wife: Daughter of Suzuki Gentsū Shigenori. (Predeceased him / separated by death.)

- Item: In the intercalary second month of Kansei 4 (1792, Year of the Rat), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted his first audience (o-mie) [with the lord].

- Item: In the twelfth month of the same year, in accordance with his parent's petition, he was appointed as a substitute retainer (bandai) attached to the tōzama-gumi, and served as castle guard within the Kayano Kurōdo unit.

- Item: On the third day of the fourth month of the seventh year [of Kansei] (1795, Year of the Rabbit), he died of illness at the age of twenty-four. Posthumous Buddhist name: Tokujōin Hōen Nissei. Place of burial: Hōshōzan.
