[Left folio]

One entry: In the sixth month of the same seventh year (Meiwa 7, 1770, year of the Tiger), he was granted one lined garment (oawase).

One entry: In the eleventh month of An'ei 1 (1772, year of the Dragon), he was granted permission to return to Aizu at his own convenience (Aizu katte), and departed [for Aizu] in the second month of the following year of the Snake (1773).

One entry: In the eleventh month of the same second year (An'ei 2, 1773, year of the Snake), his request was granted and he was relieved of his current duties (tōmu goimen), was appointed to the tozama-kumatsuke (outer-group attachment), entered the unit of Kayano Gonbei, and served in castle guard duty (oshiro goban).

One entry: In the twelfth month of the same year, although he had a son, as the child was young and frail, his request was granted to take Kingo, the second son of Ōkawara Kyōan, as his son-in-law and adopted heir (mukoyōshi).
