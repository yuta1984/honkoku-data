- Item: In the third month of Kansei 8 (1796, Year of the Dragon), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was granted his first audience [with the lord].

- Item: In the fourth month of the ninth year [of Kansei] (1797, Year of the Snake), in accordance with his parent's petition, he was appointed as a substitute retainer (bandai) attached to the tōzama-gumi, and [served as castle guard] within the Kayano Kurōdo unit—

**[Left Folio]**

—serving as castle guard.

- Item: In the eighth month of the same year, he turned out for the oidorīgari (a hunting exercise / military drill).

- Item: In the ninth month of the same year, during the oidorīgari, the order of formation (reigo), keeping pace, advance and retreat, and other such matters were well coordinated among all affiliated retainers (renzoku). This was recognized as the result of diligent preparation over time, and the entire unit received commendation from [the lord].
