One entry: In the eighth month of the same seventeenth year (Kyōhō 17, 1732, year of the Rat), upon Lady Baien'in and Lady Genshō'in ascending to Edo, he returned [to Aizu] to then accompany them and traveled up to Edo in their retinue.

One entry: In the first month of the same eighteenth year (Kyōhō 18, 1733, year of the Ox), he was raised to a stipend of ten persons' rations (jūnin-fuchi), and was appointed as a personal amulet keeper (okaimamore) to Lady Genshō'in.

One entry: In the ninth month of the same twentieth year (Kyōhō 20, 1735, year of the Rabbit), he traveled up to Edo for duty rotation (kinban), and returned home in the third month of the following year of the Dragon. 
　　However, regarding his diligent attendance throughout the entire journey, Lady Genshō'in issued a commendation, and thereafter on each subsequent occasion
