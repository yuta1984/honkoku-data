【Right folio】
He was appointed to the role of Official Attendant (御用役) to Lady Eiju-in, and his seat ranking was elevated to above that of the Catering Chief (御賄頭).

Item: In the intercalary fifth month of Shōtoku 3 (Year of the Snake), he was ordered that the title of his role be designated "Sannomaru Goyōyaku" (Third Enclosure Official Attendant).

Item: In the eleventh month of Shōtoku 4 (Year of the Horse), in recognition of his diligent and exemplary daily attendance without negligence, his seat ranking was raised to above that of the Kobantō (小番, junior guard duty).

Item: In the sixth month of Kyōhō 1 (Year of the Monkey), he was granted three pieces of silver (銀子三枚).

Item: In the twelfth month of Kyōhō 2 (Year of the Rooster), his request to take Bansuke, the second son of Yamamoto Jirōdayū, as his son-in-law and adopted heir (婿養子) was approved.

【Left folio】
Item: In the eleventh month of Kyōhō 4 (Year of the Boar), he was granted a stipend of two ryō as an official duty allowance (御役料).

Item: In the second month of Kyōhō 5 (Year of the Rat), following the death of Lady Eiju-in, he was relieved of his duties and placed in a position without official role (無役).

Item: In the fifth month of Kyōhō 6 (Year of the Ox), he was appointed as Deputy Administrator (御代官).

Item: In the ninth month of Kyōhō 8 (Year of the Rabbit), due to an incident in which his subordinate official (下代), Takita Jisuke, was found to have shown favoritism (引屓), he was ordered to leave the domain (御国退去).

Item: In Kyōhō 9 (Year of the Dragon), in a month unspecified, he was permitted to return to the domain (帰参).
