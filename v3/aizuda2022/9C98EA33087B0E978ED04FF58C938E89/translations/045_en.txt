**[Right Folio]**

- Item: In the tenth year of the same era (Year of the Horse, i.e., Tenmei 10 [1790]), in the tenth month, as the adopted son Chōhachi had been divorced/separated, a petition was submitted to return him to his original family, that of Isogami Tōdayū.

- Item: In the same month, since the adopted son Chōhachi had been separated, a petition was submitted requesting that [the subject] resume personal service once again.

- Item: In the twelfth year (Year of the Monkey), in the eighth month, a petition was submitted requesting permission to take Genzō, the younger brother of Sawai Shūzō, as a son-in-law and adopted son (muko-yōshi).

- Item: In the third month of Bunka 5 (Year of the Dragon, 1808), following the death by illness of the group leader (kashira) Obara Naiki, [the subject] was transferred to the group of Kayano Kurōdo as the successor group.

- Item: In the sixth month of the same year, in accordance with [his] petition, the son Genzō was appointed as a substitute retainer (bandai).

**[Left Folio]**

- Item: On the ninth day of the second month of Bunsei 6 (1823, Year of the Sheep), [the subject] died of illness at the age of seventy-five. Posthumous Buddhist name: Genshōin Keizan Nichiyō Koji. Officiating priest: from Hōshōzan Jōkōji temple. Place of burial: Bodaiin-zan.

Daughter: Born on the fifth day of the fifth month of Meiwa 4 (1767, Year of the Boar). Became the wife of adopted son Fujikichi Naonori.
　　Mother: daughter of the Kimoto clan.

Buemon Naoshige — first name: Eijirō — became the adopted son of his elder brother Fujikichi Naonori.
