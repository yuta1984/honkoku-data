## Catalog of Kanchūsen's Secret Mathematical Methods

### Myōken-jutsu (The Art of Wondrous Measurement)

This method deals entirely with matters beyond ordinary reason and logic, and therefore has neither conventional law nor conventional technique. When employing its methods, one uses *endan* (extended calculation steps) derived from the eight arithmetic operations (*hasan*) to obtain accurate numbers. Though ancient works contain methods dealing with topics such as "a person in a bucket," "an elephant," and "a ship," these are all matters of intuition (*kan*), and are therefore set aside here. In the *Sangaku Tanki* (Notes on Mathematical Learning), there are excellent problems: the first asks how to precisely measure the flow of a hot spring day and night; the second asks about the range illuminated by a candle before one's eyes; the third asks about the distance at which a drum can be heard — and similar problems. All of these belong to domains of mathematical inquiry that methods such as *endan* cannot reach. I have recorded the answers and techniques, and dedicated them before the great shrine of Atsuta Kōtaijingū in Ayuchi District, Owari Province.

Beyond these, the measurement of ships at sea, trees on distant mountains, the depth of the ocean floor, or obtaining the accurate time without a clock — all of these arise from this method. Perhaps it resembles what is said in the *Lièxiān Zhuàn* (Biographies of Immortals): "sometimes appearing, sometimes concealing." For this reason, it is called *Myōken-jutsu*.

### Ki-jutsu (The Art of the Compass)

This is a method of calculation using the *ki* (compass) applied to triangles and polygons of any number of sides. *A volume of secret transmission exists.*

### Ku-jutsu (The Art of the Square)

This is a method of using the *ku* (carpenter's square/right-angle ruler) to divide any indivisible or difficult quantity, and to resolve problems involving various angles. *Same as the above.*

The above two methods will be published in due course in the *Kiku Chōhōki* (Treasury of Compass and Square).

### Machimi-jutsu (The Art of Surveying)

*Genho-mi* (chord-based viewing), *Teki-mi* (target viewing), *Sora-mi* (aerial viewing) — what has not yet been released to the world is the *Machimi-jutsu* (Art of Land Surveying). The *Machimi-jutsu* of *Myōken* can reach distances of tens of thousands of *ri*.
