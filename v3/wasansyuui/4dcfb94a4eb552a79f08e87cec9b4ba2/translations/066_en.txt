Those who cannot exhaust the meaning and do not know the rules are practitioners of the "Way of Calculation" (sandō). Those who cannot exhaust the meaning but do know the rules are practitioners of "Calculation Beyond" (sangai). The Way of Calculation involves investigating the root to arrive at principle, while Calculation Beyond involves applying methods without investigating the source. Thus, these two approaches together are called the "Art of Subtle Measure" (myōken no jutsu). Students should make clear the correct numbers of these matters. Such a person is called a "divine sage and true person of the Way of Calculation."
