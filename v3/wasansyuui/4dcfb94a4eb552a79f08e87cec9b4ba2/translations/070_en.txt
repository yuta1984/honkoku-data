**Section Title: Gold and Silver Currency Exchange**
*(Note: In this volume, the koban is set at 60 monme, and all exchange items are recorded without exception.)*

○ Problem: If 1 ryō of gold is worth 4 kan 500 mon in copper cash, how much is 1 monme worth?

　**Answer: 72 mon.**

**Method:** Multiply 4 kan 500 mon by the standard factor of 16 to find the answer.

**Plain Explanation:**
There are two ways to find the value of 1 monme:
1. Multiply the market rate per ryō by 16.
2. Double the market rate per ryō and multiply by 8.

The origin of the factor 16 is as follows: dividing 96 mon (the value of 100 mon) by 60 monme yields 1.6, hence the factor of 16 is used. The reason for doubling and multiplying by 8 is that dividing 16 into two parts gives 8; therefore, the dividend (jitsu) is doubled while the divisor (hō) is halved.
