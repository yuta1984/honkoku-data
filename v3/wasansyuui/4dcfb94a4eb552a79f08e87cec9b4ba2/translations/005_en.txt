**Introduction to "Quick Transmission of Arithmetic for Beginners"**

The utility of arithmetic is indeed great. Its techniques are vast and its principles inexhaustible. Since the brilliant scholars of all ages have thoroughly discoursed upon the subject, why should one of little talent need to add further words? Now, the flourishing of mathematical learning in our country began when Yoshida Mitsuyoshi published the *Jinkōki* during the Kan'ei era. From that time onward, five or six publishers produced works on arithmetic, each presenting in their books some ten or twenty challenge problems, seeking to resolve the doubts and questions of beginning students.
