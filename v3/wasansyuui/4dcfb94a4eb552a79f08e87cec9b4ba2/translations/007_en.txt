Subsequent learners followed one after another, and works such as the *Ikkyoku Sanpō* and the *Sanpō Zusetsu* have already come to fill the world to overflowing — so much so that one might say the oxen sweat and the rafters are full (※1), or that they are piled up by the five cartload (※2). Yet despite this abundance, those of renown in the world remain preoccupied with the hidden problems (*fukudai*) (※4) of the endan method (※3) and fail to grasp the mysterious, transcendent techniques (*rigai no kūhō*) (※5). As a result, no one has produced a guidebook through which beginners (*shoshin*) can achieve a proper understanding. Therefore, I have here, at the Kanreiken (※6), taken the secret teachings — beginning from the multiplication tables (*kuku*) through the *hassen* and *ken'ichi* methods (※7) — rendered them into plain explanation (*wage*) and illustrated diagrams so that they may be readily learned. Furthermore, at the end of the volume, I have recorded mathematical texts that serve as a stepladder (*kaitei*) (※8) for young learners, clearly categorized and arranged. Moreover, I have set down the wondrous technique (*myōken no jutsu*) (※10) — which I myself have founded (*kaiki*) (※9) — that transcendent, lawless method which lies beyond ordinary principle, so as to guide the reader toward it. This work I have named the *Shoshin Sanpō Hayaden ju* ("Quick Transmission of Arithmetic for Beginners"). Truly, when one takes up this book, one ought to be able, without a teacher, to attain the benefit of immediately comprehending the arithmetic of all manner of commercial calculations (※11).
