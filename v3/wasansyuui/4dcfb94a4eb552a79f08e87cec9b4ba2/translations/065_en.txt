## Kōgō-jutsu (The Art of Scaling/Proportional Fitting)

This is a method for enlarging and reducing all manner of things — daytime [diagrams], figures, and written characters, and similar items of this kind.

## Measuring the Depth of the Ocean Floor (*Ryō Sōkai no Shintei*)

A method for measuring and knowing the depth of the great ocean from the surface of the waves. Even in depths that an anchor cannot reach, one can determine the depth. Furthermore, one can ascertain the presence or absence of rocks, mud and sand, and seaweed on the ocean floor.
