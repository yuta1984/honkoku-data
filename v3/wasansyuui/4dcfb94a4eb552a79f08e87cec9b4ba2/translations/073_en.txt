○ Problem: When one piece of chōgin silver bullion is valued at 2 bu in gold plus 14 monme 2 bu in silver, what is the exchange rate (in monme per ryō)?

　　　　　**Answer: 57 monme 6 bu.**

**Method:** Set the standard weight of one chōgin piece at 43 monme; subtract the fractional amount of 14 monme 2 bu, leaving 28 monme 8 bu; double this to obtain the exchange rate of 57 monme 6 bu.

○ Problem: If 10 barrels of oil exchange for 12 ryō, what is the price of 8 barrels?

　　　　　**Answer: 9 ryō, 2 bu, and 6 monme.**

**Method:** Multiply 12 ryō by 8 barrels to get 96. Subtract 95 (i.e., apply the conversion factor to extract whole ryō and bu) and carry upward as 9 ryō 2 bu; then multiply the remaining 1 by 60 monme.
