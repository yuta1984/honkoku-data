**Problem: When the money-changing rate is 6 kan 548 mon, what is the exchange value in mon for 1 momme of silver? (Given: the standard exchange silver is 60 momme.)**

**Answer: 108 mon, 8 bu**

Method: Take the exchange amount (6 kan 548 mon), divide by the fixed rate for amounts below 10 mon (9 bu 6 rin) to obtain 6 kan 550 mon. Multiply this by the fixed rate (1 rin 6 mō) to obtain the value in mon per 1 momme of silver.
[If the result exceeds 100 mon, add 4 mon commission fee (mesen) per 100 mon.]

· For the exchange value of 1 bu of gold: (divide by the standard rate of 9 bu 6 rin for amounts below 10 mon) and multiply by the fixed rate (6 rin 4 mō). [If the result exceeds 100 mon, add 4 mon per 100 mon.] This gives the value per 1 momme of silver.

· For the exchange value of 2 shu of gold: (divide by the standard rate of 9 bu 6 rin for amounts below 10 mon) and multiply by the fixed rate (6 rin 4 mō), then double the result. [If the result exceeds 100 mon, add 4 mon per 100 mon.] This gives the value per 1 momme of silver.
