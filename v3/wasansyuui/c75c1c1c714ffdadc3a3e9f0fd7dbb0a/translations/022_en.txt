As the method: to the perimeter of 7 shaku 6 sun 2 bu 5 rin, add the interior measurements — 8 sun 3 bu 3 rin 3 mō at two locations, and 8 sun 3 bu 3 rin 4 mō at one location, totaling three segments summing to 2 shaku 5 sun — this being understood as the "hand-tip" (projecting) dimension. Adding this to 7 shaku 6 sun 2 bu 5 rin gives 1 jō 1 sun 2 bu 5 rin. This is established as the total perimeter of all four sides. Subtracting 2 shaku 5 sun each for the upper and lower lateral portions — totaling 5 shaku — from the remainder of 5 shaku 1 sun 2 bu, dividing by two gives a length of 2 shaku 5 sun 6 bu, which is then multiplied by the width of 2 shaku 5 sun.

This yields an area of 640 tsubo. As this exceeds (the reference value of) 240 tsubo, the excess area (hiki-tsubo) must be found. The hand-tip measurement at the bottom is 2 shaku 5 sun, and at the top 8 sun 3 bu 3 rin 4 mō; together 3 shaku 3 sun 3 bu 3 rin 4 mō, divided by two gives 1 shaku 6 sun 6 bu 6 rin 7 mō. Dividing 240 tsubo by this yields the height of 1 shaku 4 sun 3 bu 9 rin (with remainder).
