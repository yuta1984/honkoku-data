**Topic: Diagram of Round Objects**

　Correction for Round Objects (circular timber)
　　　Thickness: 1 sun
　　　Volume: 399 tsubo, 9 gō, 3 shaku, 7 sai, remainder

Method: Divide the circumference of 7 shaku, 1 sun, 1 bu by 3.6 (an approximation of π), rounding down, to find the diameter (sashi-watashi): 2 shaku, 2 sun, 5 bu. Multiplying this (by itself) gives 5.0625.

Multiply this by 0.79 to find the volume.
