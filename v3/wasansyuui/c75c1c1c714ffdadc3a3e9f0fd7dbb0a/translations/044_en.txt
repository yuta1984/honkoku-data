## [Finding the Number of *Ken* from Corner to Corner of the *Tatenawa* (Vertical Rope / Baseline)]

The question asks: from the corner of this *tatenawa* to the diagonal corner, how many *ken* is it?
　　Answer: **67.5 *ken***.

**Method:** Multiply 30 *ken* by 50 *ken* to obtain 1,500 *tsubo*; multiply 20 *ken* by 60 *ken* to obtain 1,200 *tsubo*. Combining these gives 2,700 *tsubo*. Dividing by the width of 40 *ken* yields the *tatenawa* measurement of **67.5 *ken***.
