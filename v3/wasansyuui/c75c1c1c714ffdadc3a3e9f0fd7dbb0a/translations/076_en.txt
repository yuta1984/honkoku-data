## Hōen Hiken Shū, Volume Two (Lower)

▲ Suppose five people sell five kinds of grain — rice, soybeans, azuki beans, wheat, and buckwheat — and each of the five people receives the same amount of silver. The question asks: how many *koku* (石) did each person sell?

**Market Prices:**
- Rice: 45 *monme* (匁) of silver per *koku* (石)
- Soybeans: 30 *monme* of silver per *koku*
- Azuki beans: 27 *monme* of silver per *koku*
