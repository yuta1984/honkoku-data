When the four kinds of purchased goods are allocated in proportion to each merchant's contributed silver:

Silver: 64 kanme, 800 monme (the Kyoto merchant's share)
　　　　　　　　Ginseng:　　　　101 kin, 10 ryō
　　　　　　　　Aloe wood:　　　28 kin, 14 ryō
　　　　　　　　Silk thread:　　　3,402 kin
　　　　　　　　Scrolls:　　　　　113 rolls, 1 jō, 5 shaku, 2 sun

The allocation for the remaining two merchants need not be written out.
The method for the above is made clear in the Jinkōki.

Regarding the division of the four goods against 64 kanme, 800 monme of silver:

One then asks: what is the unit price (silver value) of each of the four items — ginseng, aloe wood, silk thread, and scrolls?

Ginseng: 101 kin, 10 ryō
　Silver value: 16 kanme, 200 monme
　That is: 160 monme of silver per kin

Aloe wood: 28 kin, 14 ryō
　Silver value: 16 kanme, 199 monme, 9 bu, 8 rin (and a remainder)
　Per kin: 571 monme, 4 bu, 2 rin, 8 mō, 4 shi
