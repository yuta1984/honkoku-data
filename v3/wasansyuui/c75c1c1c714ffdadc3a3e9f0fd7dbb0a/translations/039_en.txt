## [Tsutamaki-zan: Solution Method]

**Method:** Multiply the circumference of the log (3 *shaku* 5 *sun*) by the number of windings (8), which gives 2 *jō* 8 *shaku*. Multiplying these together yields 784 *tsubo* (square units). Then multiply by the length of the log (2 *jō*), which gives 400 *tsubo*. Adding the two quantities together gives 1,184 *tsubo*; taking the square root (*kaibyō*) of this yields 3 *jō* 4 *shaku* 4 *sun* [coefficient: 令六四]. This is the method for finding the length of the vine.
