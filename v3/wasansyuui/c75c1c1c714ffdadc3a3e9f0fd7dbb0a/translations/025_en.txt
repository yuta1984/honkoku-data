**[Problem Title: Square Tsubo — Triangular Area Calculation]**

Given 791 tsubo, 1 gō, 1 shaku as a equilateral triangle — what is the length of one side?

　　This area is 791 tsubo, 1 gō, 1 shaku.

**Method:**
Divide 791 tsubo, 1 gō, 1 shaku by the triangular coefficient (san-kaku no hō) .433, yielding decimal .82704 (令＝zero), with a remainder of .38 (decimal). Apply the square root method (kaihei-hō) to this, obtaining 42 ken .743, with remainder .7895 (decimal). Multiply this by 3 and carry into .744; multiply by 6 shaku 5 sun, and one obtains 42 ken, 4 shaku, 8 sun, 3 bu, 6 rin as the length of one side.

To verify by working back to the area: divide 4 shaku, 8 sun, 3 bu, 6 rin by 6 shaku 5 sun; multiply by 42 ken, 7 bu, 4 rin, 4 mō; then multiply by the triangular coefficient .433 — and one recovers 791 tsubo, 1 gō, 1 shaku (confirming the calculation).
