## [Finding Chord and Circumference of a Truncated Cone (continued), and Volume of Square Timber]

Multiplying the chord of [the circumference (*enman*) of 3 *shaku*] — namely 2 *shaku* 4 *sun* — by itself gives 576 *tsubo*. Subtracting this from 900 *tsubo* leaves 324 *tsubo*. Taking the square root (*kaihei*) gives 1 *shaku* 8 *sun*.

Multiplying the tip circumference (*enman*) of 2 *shaku* by itself gives 400 *tsubo*. Multiplying the chord of 1 *shaku* 6 *sun* gives 256 *tsubo*. Subtracting this from 400 *tsubo* leaves 144 *tsubo*. Taking the square root gives 1 *shaku* 2 *sun*.
