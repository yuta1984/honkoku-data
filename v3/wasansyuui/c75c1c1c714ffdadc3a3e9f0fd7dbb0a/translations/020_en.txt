**Section Title: Arrow-notch Long Object (Yahazu-naga-mono)**

　　　　Area: 400 tsubo

As the method: from the length of 4 shaku 6 sun, subtract half of the upper separate portion of 1 shaku 2 sun (i.e., 6 sun), leaving 4 shaku. Multiply this by the width of 1 shaku to find the area.
