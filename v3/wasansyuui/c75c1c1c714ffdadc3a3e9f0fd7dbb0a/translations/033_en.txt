## [Calculation of the Cubic Volume of Round Logs]

▲ Round log calculations — (There are four volumes on circular methods.)

　　　　Base chord: 2 *shaku* 4 *sun*　　Circumference (full circle): 3 *shaku*
　　　　　　　　Sagitta: 6 *sun*
　　　　Tip chord: 1 *shaku* 6 *sun*　　Circumference (full circle): 2 *shaku*
　　　　　　　　Sagitta: 4 *sun*

The question is: what is the tsubo volume?

　Answer: **3,272 tsubo, 2 *gō*, 2 *shaku***
