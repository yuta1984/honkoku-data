**Calculation for the Square Board**

By the method: multiplying 2 shaku by 2 shaku gives 400 tsubo (in sun-tsubo units), as is known.

To find the diagonal (corner to corner), one uses the kōko-gen (勾股弦) method — i.e., the Pythagorean theorem. Multiply one side of 2 shaku by itself to get 4 (i.e., 2² = 4), and likewise multiply the other side by itself to get 4 (i.e., 2² = 4). Sum both values to obtain 8. Applying the square root method (開平法) to this gives 2 shaku, 8 sun, 2 bu, 8 rin, 4 mō.
