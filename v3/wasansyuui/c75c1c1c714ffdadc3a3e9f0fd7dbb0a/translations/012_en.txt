**Topic: Triangular Objects**

　▲ Long triangular object (triangular prism-shaped timber)
　　　　　Thickness: 1 sun
　　　　　Volume: 400 tsubo

Method: Take 3 shaku, 2 sun; halve 2 shaku, 5 sun (dividing by two),

then multiply by 1 shaku, 2 sun, 5 bu to find the volume.

**Topic: Corrected Square Object (四角物直し)**

　　　　　Thickness: 5 sun
　　　　　Volume: 399 tsubo
　　　　　　　　　　　9 (bu) and 7 rin, remainder

Method: Multiplying by 8 sun, 9 bu, 4 rin, 4 mō gives 0.799995136… (approximately 0.8). Multiplying this by the thickness of 5 sun to find the volume.
