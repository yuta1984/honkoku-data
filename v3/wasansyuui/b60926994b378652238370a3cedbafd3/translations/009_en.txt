Method: Set down 384 mon. Subtract the "uchime-zeni" (interior deduction coins) of 3/4, which is 12 mon, leaving 372 mon. Divide by 95 mon to get 3 monme 9 bu. The fractional remainder is 1 mon 5 bu. ○ This demonstrates a case where there is a remainder in coins after calculating the silver amount.

## [Section 5] Calculation for Converting Coins (Zeni) to Gold

**Problem:** There are 25 kan 200 mon in coins. At an exchange rate of 6 kan 300 mon per ryō, what is the equivalent in gold? (Market rate: 60 monme)

**Answer: 4 ryō**

Method: Set down the coin amount and divide by 6 kan 300 mon.

**Problem:** There are 31 kan 234 mon in coins. At an exchange rate of 4 kan 876 mon per ryō, what is the equivalent in gold? (Gold market rate: 60 monme)

**Answer: 6 ryō 1 bu, with a remainder of 9 monme 1 bu ○ 8 mō in silver**

Method: Set down 31 kan 234 mon in coins. Multiply the portion of 100 mon and above by 0.96, yielding 29 kan 986 mon (700 mon). Use this as the dividend (jitsu). Separately, set down 4 kan 876 mon and likewise multiply the portion of 100 mon and above by 0.96, yielding 4 kan 684 mon. Use this as the divisor to divide the dividend. From the result, subtract 6.25 ryō (representing 6 ryō 1 bu), then multiply the remainder by 60 monme.
