**Method:** Set down 4 kan 870 mon. Take only the portion above 100 mon — that is, 4 kan 800 mon — and apply the 0.96 reduction, then add the remaining fraction of 62 mon, giving 4 kan 678 mon. Multiply this by 5 ryō to get 23 kan 390 mon *(interlinear note: this is the "chō-hyaku" / long hundred)*. Divide this by 96/16 (i.e., 0.96 ÷ 16) to obtain 96-hyaku (960), and the answer is thus known.

　**Method:** Set down 13 ryō 2 bu as 13.5 ryō, and multiply by 4 kan 900 mon to get 66 kan 150 mon.

## [Section 7] Calculations for Determining the Exchange Rate When Receiving Copper Coins

When 9 kan 824 mon in copper coins is received in exchange for silver worth 117 monme 9 fun, find the remaining exchange rate.

　　**Answer:** Exchange rate: 12 monme

　**Method:** Set down 9 kan 824 mon. To the fractional amount of just 24 mon, add 1 mon as the *mesen* (目銭, internal deduction fee) calculated at the 0.96 rate, making it 9 kan 825 mon. Use this as the divisor (法, *hō*), and divide into the silver amount of 117 monme 9 fun.
