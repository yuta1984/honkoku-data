Method: Set down 4 kan 870 mon. Apply the 0.96 multiplier only to the portion of 100 mon and above — that is, 4 kan 800 mon — then add the fractional remainder of 62 mon, yielding 4 kan 678 mon. **Problem:** There are 13 ryō 2 bu of gold. At an exchange rate of 4 kan 900 mon per ryō, what is the equivalent in coins?

**Answer: 66 kan 150 mon**

Method: Set down 13.5 ryō (13 ryō 2 bu = 13.5 ryō) and multiply by 4 kan 900 mon to obtain 66 kan 150 mon.

## [Section 7] Calculation for Determining the Coin Exchange Rate from a Coin Transaction

**Problem:** When 9 kan 824 mon in coins is received in exchange for 117 monme 9 bu of silver, what is the remaining exchange rate (i.e., how many monme of silver per kan of coins)?

**Answer: Exchange rate = 12 monme**

Method: Set down 9 kan 824 mon. Add the uchime-sen (handling deduction) of 1 mon — applied only to the fractional amount of 24 mon at the 0.96 rate — to obtain 9 kan 825 mon. Use this as the divisor to divide the silver amount of 117 monme 9 bu.
