## Sanpō Dōji Mondō, Volume One (First Volume)
### Section: Commerce (Shōko)

**[Problem 1] Quick Calculation Methods for Money Exchange (Zeni-wari Hayasan-yō)**
*(For exchange rates from 8 monme 4 bu to 12 monme 6 bu, calculated to the bu digit)*

**Problem:** When the exchange rate for copper coin (*zeni*) is 9 monme 4 bu 5 rin, how many *mon* of copper coin can one obtain per 1 monme of silver?

**Answer:** 105 *mon* 5 *bu*

**Method:** Set the base value (*jitsu*) at 20 monme, then subtract the current exchange rate of 9 monme 4 bu 5 rin from it, and the answer follows directly.

> The above method applies when the exchange rate is between 9 monme 4 bu 5 rin and 10 monme. When the rate exceeds 10 monme, multiply by the corresponding factor (*inhō*) for the given rate, then subtract that amount from the base value (*jitsu*) of the given rate, and the answer follows directly.
