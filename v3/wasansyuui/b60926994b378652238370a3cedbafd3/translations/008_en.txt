## [Section 2] Calculations for Exchanging Gold into Silver

There are 12 ryō of gold. At an exchange rate of 60 monme per ryō, how much silver is the equivalent?

　　**Answer:** 720 monme

　**Method:** Set down 12 ryō and multiply by 60 monme.

---

There are 15 ryō and 3 bu of gold. At an exchange rate of 58 monme per ryō, how much silver is the equivalent?

　　**Answer:** 913 monme 5 fun

　**Method:** Set down 15.75 ryō.
　*(Interlinear note: 1 bu = 0.25; 2 bu = 0.50; 3 bu = 0.75 — set these values accordingly.)*
　Then multiply by 58 monme.

## [Section 3] Calculations for Exchanging Silver into Gold

There are 1 kan 593 monme of silver. At a gold exchange rate of 59 monme per ryō, how much gold is the equivalent?

　　**Answer:** 27 ryō

　**Method:** Set down the silver amount of 1,593 monme and divide by 59 monme.

---

There are 1 kan 628 monme of silver. At an exchange rate of 60 monme per ryō, how much gold is the equivalent?

　　**Answer:** 27 ryō, 1 bu — with a remainder of 3 monme silver

　**Method:** Set down 1,628 monme and divide by 60 monme; this gives 27.3. From this, subtract 27.25
　*(Interlinear note: 0.25 corresponds to 1 bu of gold.)*
　Multiply the remaining 0.05 back by 60 monme to find the remainder of 3 monme in silver.

## [Section 4] Calculations for Exchanging Copper Coins into Silver

There are 384 mon in copper coins. At a rate of 95 mon per monme of silver, how much silver is the equivalent?

**Answer:** 3 monme 9 fun, with a remainder of 1.5 mon
