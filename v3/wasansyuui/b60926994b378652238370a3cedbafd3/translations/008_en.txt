## [Section 2] Calculation for Converting Gold to Silver

**Problem:** There are 12 ryō of gold. At a market rate of 60 monme per ryō, what is the equivalent in silver?

**Answer: 720 monme**

Method: Set down 12 ryō and multiply by 60 monme.

---

**Problem:** There are 15 ryō 3 bu of gold. At a market rate of 58 monme per ryō, what is the equivalent in silver?

**Answer: 913 monme 5 bu**

Method: Set down 15 ryō 3 bu as 15.75.
　　(1 bu of gold = 0.25; 2 bu = 0.50; 3 bu = 0.75)
　　Multiply by 58 monme.

---

## [Section 3] Calculation for Converting Silver to Gold

**Problem:** There are 1 kan 593 monme of silver. At a gold market rate of 59 monme per ryō, what is the equivalent in gold?

**Answer: 27 ryō**

Method: Set down 1 kan 593 monme of silver and divide by 59 monme.

---

**Problem:** There are 1 kan 628 monme of silver. At a market rate of 60 monme per ryō, what is the equivalent in gold?

**Answer: 27 ryō 1 bu, with a remainder of 3 monme silver**

Method: Set down 1 kan 628 monme of silver and divide by 60 monme, giving 27.3. From this, subtract 27.25 (since 0.25 represents 1 bu of gold); multiply the remaining 0.05 by 60 monme to convert back, and the remainder is found to be 3 monme of silver.

## [Section 4] Calculation for Converting Coins (Zeni) to Silver

**Problem:** There are 384 mon in coins. At an exchange rate of 95 mon per 1 monme of silver, what is the equivalent in silver?

**Answer: 3 monme 9 bu and 1 mon 5 bu (remainder)**
