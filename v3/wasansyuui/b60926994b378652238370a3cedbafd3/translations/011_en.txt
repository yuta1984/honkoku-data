## [Section 8] Calculations for Determining the Gold Exchange Rate When Receiving Silver

When 3 kan 363 monme of silver is received in exchange for 57 ryō of gold, find the gold exchange rate.

　　**Answer:** Gold exchange rate: 59 monme

　**Method:** Divide 3 kan 363 monme of silver by 57 ryō of gold.

## [Section 9] Calculations for Determining the Gold Exchange Rate When Receiving Gold

When 58 ryō 1 bu of gold plus a surplus of 5 monme of silver is received in exchange for 3 kan 500 monme of silver, find the gold exchange rate.

　　**Answer:** Gold exchange rate: 60 monme

　**Method:** Set down 3 kan 500 monme. From this, subtract the surplus silver of 5 monme, leaving 3 kan 495 monme. Divide this by 58.25 ryō (i.e., 58 ryō 1 bu expressed decimally).

　　**Answer:** Gold exchange rate: 59 monme 8 fun

　**Method:** Set down 1 kan 52 monme 5 fun of silver. From this, subtract 6 monme, leaving 1 kan 46 monme 5 fun. Divide this by 17.5 ryō (i.e., 17 ryō 2 bu expressed decimally).
