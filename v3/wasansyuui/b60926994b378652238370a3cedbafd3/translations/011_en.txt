## [Section 8] Calculation for Determining the Gold Exchange Rate from a Silver Transaction

**Problem:** When 3 kan 363 monme of silver is received in exchange for 57 ryō of gold, what is the exchange rate for gold?

**Answer: Gold exchange rate = 59 monme**

Method: Divide 3 kan 363 monme of silver by 57 ryō of gold.

---

## [Section 9] Calculation for Determining the Gold Exchange Rate from a Gold Transaction

**Problem:** When, in exchange for 3 kan 500 monme of silver, one receives 58 ryō 1 bu of gold plus a remainder of 5 monme of silver, what is the exchange rate for gold?

**Answer: Gold exchange rate = 60 monme**

Method: Set down 3 kan 500 monme. Subtract the remainder silver of 5 monme from this, leaving 3 kan 495 monme. Divide this by 58.25 ryō (i.e., 58 ryō 1 bu = 58.25 ryō).

**Problem:** When, in exchange for 1 kan 52 monme 5 bu of silver, one receives 17 ryō 2 bu of gold plus 6 monme of silver, what is the exchange rate for gold?

**Answer: Gold exchange rate = 59 monme 8 bu**

Method: Set down 1 kan 52 monme 5 bu of silver. Subtract 6 monme from this, leaving 1 kan 46 monme 5 bu. Divide this by 17.5 ryō (i.e., 17 ryō 2 bu = 17.5 ryō).
