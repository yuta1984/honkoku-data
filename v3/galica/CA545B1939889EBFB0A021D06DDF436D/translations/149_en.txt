**Ebisu**

He is the third child of Izanagi no Mikoto and the younger brother of Amaterasu Ōkami. Because he was cast adrift and washed ashore at Nishinomiya, he is enshrined and venerated as the Nishinomiya Daijingū.

---

**Hotei**

He is a wandering sage (sansei) of China and an incarnation of Maitreya (the Future Buddha). He himself was unaware of this; as he always carried a single cloth bag (futon), the people of his time called him "Hotei" (Cloth Bag). He has eyes in the back of his head.

**Funatama-gū (The Shrine of the Ship Spirit)**

She was the daughter of a fisherman who lived during the reign of Emperor Taizong of Song. In the fourth year of Yongxi (雍熙, 987 CE), on the ninth day of the ninth month, she ascended to heaven. A voice was heard from within the clouds, saying: "I am none other than an incarnation of Kannon (Avalokiteśvara). I now ascend in transformation. Henceforth, I shall broadly protect maritime navigation." It is for this reason that she came to be called Funatama-gū.
