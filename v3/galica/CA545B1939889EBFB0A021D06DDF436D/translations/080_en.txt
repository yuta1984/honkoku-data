【Upper Right】
【Upper Section】
■Semui (Fearlessness-Granting) Bodhisattva《22nd day》
【Lower Section】
The inner realization (naishō) is Kanzeon Bosatsu (Avalokiteśvara).

【Lower Right】
【Upper Section】
■Inari Daimyōjin (Great Illuminating Deity of Inari)《22nd day》
【Lower Section】
Enshrined in Kii District, Yamashiro Province. During the Daidō era, when Kōbō Daishi established Tō-ji Temple, the deity manifested before its gates.

【Upper Center】
【Upper Section】
■Daiseishi (Mahāsthāmaprāpta) Bodhisattva《23rd day》
【Lower Section】
The third prince of King Mujonenō. The "Vimalakīrti Sūtra" states: "Possessing the power of Daiseishi, flying to the ten directions, the lands where he resides tremble in six ways, and the evil realms come to rest."

【Lower Center】
【Upper Section】
■Sumiyoshi Daimyōjin (Great Illuminating Deity of Sumiyoshi)《23rd day》
【Lower Section】
Enshrined in Sumiyoshi District, Settsu Province. The honji is Shō Kannon (Avalokiteśvara in standard form).

【Upper Left】
【Upper Section】
■Jizō Bosatsu (Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva)《24th day》
【Lower Section】
The commentary on the "Jūrin-kyō" (Daśacakra Kṣitigarbha Sūtra) states: "'Ji' (Earth) means the beginning; 'zō' (Treasury) means the end." The "Jūō-kyō" (Ten Kings Sūtra) states that he is named Kakke Jōjizai Buddha (Puṣpakūṭa Samādhi Sovereign Buddha).

【Lower Left】
【Upper Section】
■Gion Daimyōjin (Great Illuminating Deity of Gion)《24th day》
【Lower Section】
Also called Gozu Tennō (Bull-Headed Heavenly King), this is Susanoo-no-Mikoto. Enshrined in Yasaka village, Yamashiro Province. One shrine is dedicated to Gozu Tennō, one shrine to Inadahime (also called Shōshōi), and one shrine to Hachiōji.
