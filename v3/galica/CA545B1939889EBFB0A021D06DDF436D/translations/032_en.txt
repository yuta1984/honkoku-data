【Upper Right】
■The Middle Grade, Middle Level of Birth (Chūbon Chūshō)

The middle grade, middle level of birth refers to one who upholds the various precepts. (At the moment of death,) a monk who is a manifestation of Amitābha appears before the practitioner bearing a lotus flower, and a voice from the sky declares: "Because you have followed the teachings of all Buddhas, I come to welcome you and lead you to be reborn in that land (the Pure Land)."

【Lower Right】
■The Middle Grade, Lowest Level of Birth (Chūbon Geshō)

The middle grade, lowest level of birth refers to one who, (at the moment of death's) end, encounters a good spiritual guide (kalyāṇamitra) who praises and expounds upon the joys of Amitābha Buddha's land, and who also explains the forty-eight vows of the monk Dharmākara (Hōzō Biku). Upon hearing this, in the time it takes to bend and extend one's arm, (the person) is immediately reborn in the world of Ultimate Bliss.

【Upper Left】
■The Lowest Grade, Highest Level of Birth (Gebon Jōshō)

The lowest grade, highest level of birth refers to one who has committed many evil deeds. When their life is about to end, they encounter a good spiritual guide who praises the wise teachers of the Mahāyāna and further instructs the practitioner to recite "Namu Amida Butsu" (Homage to Amitābha Buddha). The practitioner then sees a transformed Buddha and is reborn in the jeweled pond (of the Pure Land).

【Lower Left】
■The Lowest Grade, Middle Level of Birth (Gebon Chūshō)

The lowest grade, middle level of birth refers to one who has broken the precepts, shown contempt for the monastic community, and concealed their misdeeds when the moment of death arrives. (At that time,) they encounter a good spiritual guide, and upon reciting the nembutsu, the fires (of hell) transform into wind, which blows celestial flowers (toward them). Transformed Buddhas and bodhisattvas come to welcome this person, and they thus attain rebirth (in the Pure Land).
