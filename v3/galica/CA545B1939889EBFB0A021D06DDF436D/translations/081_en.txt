【Upper Section】
■Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva《25th day》
【Lower Section】
In the "Huayan Yinyi" (Glossary of the Avataṃsaka Sūtra), it is written as "Mañjuśrī," translated as "Exquisite Virtue" (Myōtoku), and also called "Exquisite Auspiciousness" (Myō Kissho).

【Lower Right】
【Upper Section】
■Sekizan Daimyōjin《25th day》
【Lower Section】
Sekizan is called Taizan Fukun in China. Currently located at the western foot of Mount Hiei. The honji (original Buddhist identity) is Kṣitigarbha (Jizō).

【Upper Section】
■Yakujō Bodhisattva《26th day》
【Lower Section】
The tenth volume of the "Monku" (Fahua Wenju) states: "This concerns the matter of King Myōshōgon."

【Lower Center】
【Upper Section】
■Takebe Daimyōjin《26th day》
【Lower Section】
Shiga District, Ōmi Province. The honji is Amitābha (Amida).

【Upper Left】
【Upper Section】
■Vairocana Buddha《27th day》
【Lower Section】
In this land, called "Pure Fulfillment" (Jōman): (Pure, because all evils are extinguished; Fulfillment, because all virtues are perfected.) This is the Enjoyment Body (Sambhogakāya). Also called "Light Illuminating All" (Kōmyō Henshō). (As stated in the "Daichido-ron.")

【Lower Left】
【Upper Section】
■Mikami Daimyōjin《27th day》
【Lower Section】
Ōmi Province. During the Yōrō era of Empress Genshō's reign, descended from Heaven to this place. Called the second sacred fire of Japan. The honji is Senju (Thousand-Armed Avalokiteśvara).
