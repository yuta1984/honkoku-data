## The Thirty-Three Forms of Kannon (continued)

---

**Byakue Kannon (White-Robed Avalokiteśvara)**

〔Figure〕

(Manifests in) the form of a *bhikṣu* (monk) and *bhikṣuṇī* (nun) (*biku-bikunishin*).

---

**Renga Kannon (Lotus-Reclining Avalokiteśvara)**

(Manifests in) the form of the True Response Body (*shōō-shin*).

〔Figure〕

---

**Takimi Kannon (Waterfall-Viewing Avalokiteśvara)**

〔Figure〕

The pit of fire (*kakō*) is transformed into a pond/lake.

---

**Seraku Kannon (Bliss-Bestowing Avalokiteśvara)**

〔Figure〕

(Kannon dwells in the void,) like the sun residing in empty space (*kokū*).
