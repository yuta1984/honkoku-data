The Twenty-Eight Attendants of Avalokiteśvara (continued)

**Nanda Dragon King**
The *Mingyi ji* (Dictionary of Names and Meanings) states that it is rendered as "joyful in goodness" (善歓喜). Because he is the most excellent among those who rejoice, he is called "good/virtuous" (善).

**Garuḍa King**
In the *Avataṃsaka Sūtra*, he is called the "Golden-Winged Bird" (金翅鳥). The Garuḍa bird has golden wings and fastens a wish-fulfilling jewel (cintāmaṇi) upon its head.

**Kiṃnara King**
Also called Candara (真陀羅). In Chinese (Qin), this is rendered as "human yet not human" (人非人). He is the deity of heavenly music and performance. He is the deity of strings and winds (music) for all the heavenly beings, and is a manifestation of Mañjuśrī.

**Asura King**
Kumārajīva states that in Chinese (Qin), this is rendered as "one who does not drink alcohol" (不飲酒). This is an evil realm of existence; males are ugly while females are beautiful. They constantly battle against the heavenly beings. According to the *Vimalakīrti Sūtra* and the *Avataṃsaka Sūtra*, the original body stands seven hundred yojanas tall.

**Gandharva King**
He is the music deity cherished (to the point of swooning) by Indra, the Lord of Heaven. He dwells in the Diamond Cave (金剛窟) to the south of Mount Sumeru. It is also said that he resides in the Ten-Treasure Mountain (十宝山).
