【Upper Right】
■Vajraratna (Kongō Hō, "Vajra Jewel")
*[Side note: The Four Honored Ones of the South]*

Homage to all the Tathāgatas' treasury of merit and virtue — Vajraratna and the [associated] Buddhas [we revere].

The *Shusshō-gi* ("Meaning of Emanation") says: "[We pay homage to] Vajraratna, who is born from the meaning of the great adornment (*mahāvibhūṣaṇa*) of all Tathāgatas."

---

【Lower Right】
■Vajrateja (Kongō Kō, "Vajra Radiance")

Homage to all the Tathāgatas' treasury of merit and virtue — Vajrateja and the [associated] Buddhas [we revere].

The *Shusshō-gi* says: "[We pay homage to] Vajrasūrya (Kongō Nichi, 'Vajra Sun'), who is born from the meaning of the great majestic brilliance (*mahātejas*) of all Tathāgatas."

---

【Upper Left】
■Vajraketu (Kongō Dō, "Vajra Banner")

Homage to all the Tathāgatas' treasury of merit and virtue — Vajraketu and the [associated] Buddhas [we revere].

The *Shusshō-gi* says: "[We pay homage to] Vajraketu, who is born from the meaning of the great fulfillment of wishes (*mahāpraṇidhāna*) of all Tathāgatas."

---

【Lower Left】
■Vajrahāsa (Kongō Shō, "Vajra Laughter/Smile")

Homage to all the Tathāgatas' treasury of merit and virtue — Vajrahāsa and the [associated] Buddhas [we revere].

The *Shusshō-gi* says: "[We pay homage to] Vajrahāsa, who is born from the meaning of the great joy and delight (*mahāprīti*) of all Tathāgatas."
