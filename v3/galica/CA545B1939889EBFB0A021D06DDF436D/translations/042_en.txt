Four Honored Ones of the South

【Upper Right】
■Vajraratna (Diamond Jewel)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' accumulation of merit Vajraratna and other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "Diamond Jewel that arises by virtue of the great adornment of all Tathāgatas"

【Lower Right】
■Vajrateja (Diamond Radiance)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' accumulation of merit Vajrateja and other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "Diamond Sun that arises by virtue of the great majestic brilliance of all Tathāgatas"

【Upper Left】
■Vajraketu (Diamond Banner)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' accumulation of merit Vajraketu and other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "Diamond Banner that arises by virtue of the great wish-fulfillment of all Tathāgatas"

【Lower Left】
■Vajrahāsa (Diamond Laughter)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' accumulation of merit Vajrahāsa and other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "Diamond Laughter that arises by virtue of the great joy of all Tathāgatas"
