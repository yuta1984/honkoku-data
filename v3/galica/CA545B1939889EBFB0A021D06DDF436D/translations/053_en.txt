## The Seven Kannon (Continued)

---

### **Nyoirin Kannon (Cintāmaṇicakra Avalokiteśvara)**

The text of the *Nyoi Honsei* ("Fundamental Vow of Wish-Fulfillment") states: "I vow that within the midst of great compassion, not a single person shall fail to be fulfilled in the vow of two lifetimes. Even those mired in falsehood and transgression shall not fail to be returned to Original Enlightenment (*hongaku*). I shall not abandon great compassion."

Nyoirin Kannon is the transformative lord of the heavenly realm. Thus, the wish-fulfilling jewel (*nyoi-ju / cintāmaṇi*) is the supreme treasure of the heavens.
*Also known as: Daibon Jin'on Kanzeon (Avalokiteśvara of Great Brahma's Profound Depth)*

---

### **Juntei Kannon (Cundī Avalokiteśvara)**

The *Shikan* (*Mohe Zhiguan* / "Great Calming and Contemplation") states: Kannon enters into the samādhi of *Nyoyō Zanmai* ("Samādhi Like a Tender Child") and other such samādhis, thereby causing all beings in the human realm to break through the Three Obstacles (*san-shō*), subdue arrogance and pride (*kyōman*), and thereby be shown the Buddha-nature.
*Also known as: Tennin Jōbu Kannon (Avalokiteśvara, the Excellent One of Heavenly Beings)*

*(Illustration)*

---

### **Fukū Kenjaku (Amoghapāśa Avalokiteśvara)**

The *Senju-kyō* (*Thousand-Hands Sūtra*) records the Buddha's words: "This bodhisattva is named Kanzeon Jizai (Avalokiteśvara-Sovereign), and is also named Kenjaku (Lasso/Noose)." ○ [This Kannon] scatters the bait of the Wondrous Dharma Lotus upon the great ocean of birth-and-death, and with the mind-contemplation of the unfailing lasso (*fukū no saku*), fishes up the fish of sentient beings and delivers them to the farther shore of bodhi (enlightenment).

*(Illustration)*

---

### **Yōryū Kannon (Willow-Branch Avalokiteśvara)**

Kannon, with a responsive and compassionate heart, accords with the desires and inclinations (*gyōyoku*) of all beings — however they may be — just as the willow branch bends and sways in the spring breeze. For this reason, this Kannon pervades all Six Kannon in their totality.

### **List of the Seven Kannon**
- Nyoirin Kannon 如意輪観音 (*Nyoirinku wan'on*)
- Juntei Kannon 準胝観音 (*Juntek uan'on*)
- Fukū Kenjaku 不空羂索 (*Fukūkensaku*)
- Yōryū Kannon 楊柳観音 (*Yōryū kuan'on*)
