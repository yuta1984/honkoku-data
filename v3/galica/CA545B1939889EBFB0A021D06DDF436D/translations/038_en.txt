【Upper Right】
■Ekākṣara-uṣṇīṣacakra (One-Syllable Golden Wheel)
This is used in the practice of esoteric rites (goshuhō).
[Note beside figure, upper left] Chiken-in (Wisdom Bond Mudrā)

【Lower Right】
■Buddhalocanā (Buddha-Eye Buddha-Mother)
The Bodhicitta-artha states, and the Gike (義决) clarifies: "(Buddhalocanā) is Vairocana. Among the five translations [of her name], she is also called Buddha-Mother."
Thus it is known that Prajñā-Buddha-Mother is likewise an alternate name for Vairocana.

【Upper Left】
■Prajñā Bodhisattva (Hannya Bosatsu)
She is the presiding deity of the separate assembly of the Mahāprajñāpāramitā (Sūtra).
Prajñā means the free pervasion of great wisdom.

【Lower Left】
■Acalāpada (Inexhaustible/Unceasing) Bodhisattva (Fukyūsoku Bosatsu)
The Fanyi mingyi ji states: "Gandhahati — in this land [China/Japan] this is rendered as 'Unceasing (fukyūsoku).' Moment by moment [this bodhisattva] flows into the sea of all suffering (sarva-duḥkha-sāgara), and from the very beginning there is no rest."
[Note above figure] Keman-in (Floral Garland Mudrā)
