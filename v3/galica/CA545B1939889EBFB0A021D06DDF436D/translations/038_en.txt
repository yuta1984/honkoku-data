【Lower Right】
■Buddhalocanā Buddha-Mother (Buddha-Eye Buddha-Mother)
The Bodaishin-gi states: "According to the Gikketsu, she is Vairocana. Among the five translations of her name, she is also called Buddha-Mother. Therefore, it is known that Prajñā Buddha-Mother is likewise an alternate name for Vairocana."

【Upper Left】
■Prajñā Bodhisattva
She is the principal deity of the separate assembly of the Mahāprajñāpāramitā.
Prajñā refers to the great wisdom that pervades and circulates through all things.
[Drawing included]

【Lower Left】
■Avikopya Bodhisattva (Unceasing Bodhisattva)
The Myōgishū states: "The Sanskrit term gandha-hati is rendered in Chinese as 'unceasing' (fukyūsoku). Moment by moment, [this bodhisattva] flows into the ocean of all suffering (sarva), and from the very beginning there is no rest."
[Drawing of Keman-in (Floral Garland Mudra) included]
