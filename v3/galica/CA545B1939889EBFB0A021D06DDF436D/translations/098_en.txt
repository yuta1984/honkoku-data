## The Twenty-Eight Lunar Mansions

**Horn Mansion (Jiao/Kaku), No. 1**

According to the *Shujing Tongkao* (Comprehensive Study of the Book of Documents), the two stars of the Horn Mansion form the Celestial Gate. The space between them is the Heavenly Gate, and the interior is called the Heavenly Court. The ecliptic passes through its center. It is therefore said to preside over military affairs.
[Star diagram: Jiao/Kaku, Píngdào]

**Neck Mansion (Kang/Kō), No. 2**

According to the *Tongkao*, the four stars of the Neck Mansion constitute the inner court of the Son of Heaven.
[Star diagram: Kang/Kō]

**Root Mansion (Di/Tei), No. 3**

According to the *Tongkao*, the four stars of the Root Mansion are the official lodgings of the Son of Heaven.
[Star diagram: Di/Tei]

**Room Mansion (Fang/Bō), No. 4**

According to the *Tongkao*, the four stars of the Room Mansion form the Míngтáng (Bright Hall), the palace from which the Son of Heaven issues his governance. (The remainder is abbreviated.)
[Star diagram: Fang/Bō, Gōuqián]
