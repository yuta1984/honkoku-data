## The Seven Kannon (Shichi Kannon)

### **Senju Kannon (Thousand-Armed Avalokiteśvara)**
In reality, the "thousand hands" consist of forty arms. Since each of the Twenty-Five Realms of Existence (*nijūgo-u*) has forty arms associated with it, the total comes to one thousand hands.
*(Illustration)*
Mudras: Root Seal (*konpon-in*) and Nine-Headed Dragon Seal (*kudaryū-in*).
*Also known as: Daihi Kanzeon (Avalokiteśvara of Great Compassion)*

---

### **Batō Kannon (Horse-Headed Avalokiteśvara)**
The profundity of this venerable one's responsive benefits is like that of a horse, which thinks only of water and grass and knows nothing beyond that — conveying the meaning that his compassionate activity is entirely single-minded and instinctive.
*(Illustration)*

---

### **Jūichimen Kannon (Eleven-Faced Avalokiteśvara)**
The three faces at the front display an expression of compassion; the three faces on the left display an expression of wrathful fury; the three faces on the right have their fangs pointing upward. These faces express joy upon seeing good, and mockery and laughter upon seeing evil. The main central face of the fundamental body neither laughs nor grows angry, representing the non-duality of good and evil. Furthermore, the Buddha at the crown of the head is the *Shōhōmyō Nyorai* (Tathāgata of Correct Dharma Radiance) from a past age, and this is referred to as *taika gyōin* ("manifesting the cause in the form of the fruit").
*(Illustration)*
*Also known as: Daikō Fushō Kanzeon (Avalokiteśvara of Great Universal Radiance)*

---

### **Shō Kanzeon (Sacred/Holy Avalokiteśvara)**
In the left hand, he/she holds an *mifure renge* — a lotus blossom not yet opened. This form expresses the intent to cause the wondrous Dharma-lotus of all sentient beings, not yet bloomed, to open and bloom.
*(Illustration)*
The right hand forms the *daihi semui-in* (seal of bestowing fearlessness through great compassion).
*Also known as: Daiji Kanzeon (Avalokiteśvara of Great Loving-Kindness)*
