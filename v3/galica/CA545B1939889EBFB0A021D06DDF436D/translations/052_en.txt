## The Seven Avalokiteśvaras (Shichi Kannon)

### **Senju Kannon (Thousand-Armed Avalokiteśvara)**
The thousand hands are, in reality, forty arms. Since each of the Twenty-Five Existences (nijūgo-u) has forty arms associated with it, when totaled together, they amount to one thousand hands.

*[Figure]*
Root Seal (konpon-in); Nine-Headed Dragon Seal (kuzuryū-in). *Also known as: Daihi Kanzeon (Avalokiteśvara of Great Compassion)*

---

### **Batō Kannon (Horse-Headed Avalokiteśvara)**
The profundity of this venerable one's responsive benefits and merits is like that of a horse which thinks of nothing but water and grass, with no awareness of anything else beyond that — such is the intent of the expression.

### **Jūichimen Kannon (Eleven-Faced Avalokiteśvara)**
The three faces at the front bear expressions of compassion; the three faces on the left bear expressions of wrathful fury; the three faces on the right have their fangs pointing upward. These faces rejoice upon seeing good, and mock and laugh upon seeing evil. The main, central face of the principal form is neither laughing nor angry, expressing the non-duality of good and evil. Furthermore, the buddha at the crown of the head is Shōhōmyō Nyorai (the Thus-Come One of Correct Dharma Brightness) of the past age. This is referred to as *taika keiin* ("manifesting the cause in the form of the fruit").

*[Below figure]*
*Also known as: Daikō Fushō Kanzeon (Avalokiteśvara of the Great Light that Illuminates Universally)*

---

### **Shō Kanzeon (Holy Avalokiteśvara)**
In the left hand, this bodhisattva holds an unopened lotus blossom (*mifurenге*). This form represents the bodhisattva's intent to cause the wondrous lotus of the Dharma within all sentient beings — not yet fully opened — to bloom and open fully.

*[Figure]*
The right hand forms the seal of Great Compassion Bestowing Fearlessness (*daihi semui-in*). *Also known as: Daiji Kanzeon (Avalokiteśvara of Great Loving-Kindness)*
