**Upper Right**
■ **5. Hōkai Raion Nyorai**
(Tathāgata of the Dharma-Ocean Thunder-Sound)

"To the east, beyond eight Ganges-sands' worth of Buddha-lands, there is a world called 'Hōdō-koku' (Land of the Dharma Banner). [The Buddha of that world] is called Hōkai Raion Nyorai (Tathāgata of the Thunder-Sound of the Dharma Ocean). He made four great vows."

---

**Lower Right**
■ **6. Hōkai Shōe Yuge Jintsū Nyorai**
(Tathāgata of Supreme Wisdom of the Dharma Ocean, Playfully Manifest through Supernatural Powers)

"To the east, beyond nine Ganges-sands' worth of Buddha-lands, there is a world called 'Zenjū Hōkai-koku' (Land of the Dharma Ocean of Righteous Abiding). The Buddha [of that world] is named Hōkai Shōe Yuge Jintsū Nyorai (Tathāgata of the Dharma Ocean's Supreme Wisdom, Freely Manifesting through Supernatural Powers). He made four great vows."

---

**Upper Left**
■ **7. Yakushi Rurikō Nyorai**
(Medicine Buddha, Tathāgata of Lapis Lazuli Light)

"To the east, beyond ten Ganges-sands' worth of Buddha-lands from here, there is a world called 'Jōruri-koku' (Pure Lapis Lazuli Land). The Buddha [of that world] is named Yakushi Rurikō Nyorai (Medicine Buddha, Tathāgata of Lapis Lazuli Light). He made twelve great vows."
*(The above is according to the Sutra of the Merit of the Seven Buddhas.)*

---

**Lower Left**
■ **Hōshō Nyorai**
(Ratnasambhava; Jewel-Born Tathāgata)

The *Ryōbu Mandara Shiki* (Private Record of the Two-Part Mandala) states: "Hōshō [the Jewel-Born] is the Honored One from whom all treasures are generated; he is the Buddha adorned with merit and virtue."
