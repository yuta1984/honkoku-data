The Ten Rakshasas (continued)

Fifth, Kokushi (Black Teeth)
In the secret teachings, this corresponds to Fudō (Acalanātha) and also Dainichi (Mahāvairocana). In the main sūtra, this is named "Giving Darkness" (施黒).

Sixth, Tahatsu (Many Hairs)
In the secret teachings, this corresponds to Fugen (Samantabhadra). In the main sūtra, this is named "Disheveled Hair" (被髪).

Seventh, Muensoku (Never Satisfied)
In the secret teachings, this corresponds to Munōshō (Aparājitā). In the main sūtra, this is named "Non-attachment" (無着).

Eighth, Jiyōraku (Wearing Jeweled Necklaces)
In the secret teachings, this corresponds to Kannon (Avalokiteśvara). In the main sūtra, this is named "Holding Flowers" (持華).

Ninth, Sōtai (Gautami)
In the secret teachings, this corresponds to Monju (Mañjuśrī). In the main sūtra, this is named "Where To?" (何所).

Tenth, Datsu-issai-shujō-seiki (She Who Steals the Life Force of All Sentient Beings)
In the secret teachings, this corresponds to Datsu-issai Dākinī. In the Kokaу (去華), she is identified as the deity Uga (Ugajin). She is also called the Great Sovereign Bodhisattva (Daijizai Bosatsu / Maheśvara).
In the main sūtra, this is named "Taking All Essence" (取一切精).
