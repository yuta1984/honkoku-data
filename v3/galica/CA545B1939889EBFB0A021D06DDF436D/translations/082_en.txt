【Upper Section】
■Mahāvairocana (Dainichi) Nyorai《28th day》
【Lower Section】
He is the teaching master of the Jakkōdo (Land of Tranquil Light), and in relation to this, there are three types of Dainichi: the Dainichi of the Diamond Realm (Kongōkai), the Womb Realm (Taizōkai), and the Soshitsuji (Susiddhikara). (And so on.)

【Lower Right】
【Upper Section】
■Hyōzu Daimyōjin《28th day》
【Lower Section】
Located in Yasu District, Ōmi Province, in the village of Hyōzu. The honji is Fudō (Acalanātha), and also Dainichi (Mahāvairocana).

【Upper Center】
【Upper Section】
■Yakuō (Medicine King) Bodhisattva《29th day》
【Lower Section】
In the commentary of the Sannōin, it states: "Yakuō is none other than Fudō Myōō, and this is a manifestation of Daitsūchishō (Mahābhijñājñānābhibhū) Buddha." Fudō's Fire-Light Samādhi is precisely the burning of the body.

【Lower Center】
【Upper Section】
■Naoka Daimyōjin《29th day》
【Lower Section】
Ōmi Province. The honji is Amida (Amitābha).

【Upper Left】
【Upper Section】
■Śākyamuni (Shaka) Nyorai《30th day》
【Lower Section】
The commentary on the "Ninnōkyō" (Sutra of Benevolent Kings) states: Śākyamuni is translated as "Nōnin" (Capable of Benevolence). "Nōnin" signifies compassion (jihi). Since he bestows compassion upon others, he is called "Nin" (benevolent).

【Lower Left】
【Upper Section】
■Kibi Daimyōjin《30th day》
【Lower Section】
Kaya District, Bitchū Province. He is Kibitsu Hiko no Mikoto, son of Emperor Kōrei. The honji is Kokūzō (Ākāśagarbha).
