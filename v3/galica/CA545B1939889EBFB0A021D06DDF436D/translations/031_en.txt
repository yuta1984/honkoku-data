【Title above frame】
The Nine Grades of Amida Buddha (Kubon no Mida)

---

【Upper Right: Highest Grade, Highest Level (Jōbon Jōshō)】

**Highest Grade, Highest Level of Rebirth**

Those who are born in the Highest Grade, Highest Level are: first, those who are compassionate and do not kill, and who fully observe all the precepts; second, those who read and recite the Mahāyāna Vaipulya sūtras; third, those who practice the six mindfulnesses (roku-nen), dedicate the merit, make a vow, and aspire to be born in that land (the Pure Land).

---

【Lower Right: Highest Grade, Middle Level (Jōbon Chūshō)】

**Highest Grade, Middle Level of Rebirth**

Those who are born in the Highest Grade, Middle Level are those who do not necessarily receive, uphold, read, and recite the Vaipulya sūtras, but who thoroughly understand their meaning and import.

Amida Buddha, surrounded by a great assembly, carries the purple-golden lotus dais and comes before the practitioner.

---

【Upper Left: Highest Grade, Lowest Level (Jōbon Geshō)】

**Highest Grade, Lowest Level of Rebirth**

Those who are born in the Highest Grade, Lowest Level are those who also believe in causality (karma), and who simply arouse the mind of the Way (dōshin) and aspire to be born in the land of Ultimate Bliss (Gokuraku).

At the end, Amida Buddha, Kannon (Avalokiteśvara), Seishi (Mahāsthāmaprāpta), and five hundred transformation Buddhas (ke-butsu) offer praise, saying: "Child of the Dharma (hōshi), you have now aroused the aspiration for rebirth."

---

【Lower Left: Middle Grade, Highest Level (Chūbon Jōshō)】

**Middle Grade, Highest Level of Rebirth**

Those who are born in the Middle Grade, Highest Level are those who receive and uphold the five precepts, the eight precepts, and all the other precepts, do not commit the five grave offenses, and aspire to be born in the Western Pure Land.

Amida Buddha appears together with many monks (bhikṣus); one makes a gesture of reverence toward the Buddha, and immediately one is born in the world of Ultimate Bliss.
