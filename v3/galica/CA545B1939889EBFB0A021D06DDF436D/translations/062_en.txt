【Header (horizontal, above frame)】
The Six Jizō (Roku Jizō)

【Upper Right】
Yotenga Jizō (地蔵菩薩 of Heavenly Rejoicing)
The left hand holds a wish-fulfilling jewel (nyoi-shu); the right hand forms the mudra of teaching the Dharma (seppō-in).
Saves the beings of the heavenly realms (deva/ten-nin).

【Lower Right】
Hōkōō Jizō (地蔵菩薩 of Radiating Light, the King)
The left hand holds a khakkhara (staff with rings); the right hand forms the wish-granting mudra (yoganshi-in).
Causes rain to fall and brings the five grains to a bountiful harvest.

【Upper Center】
Kongōgan Jizō (Vajra-Vow Jizō)
The left hand holds the Yama Banner (Emma-dō); the right hand forms the mudra of accomplishment and discernment (jōben-in).
Saves beings from the suffering of falling into hell.

【Lower Center】
Kongōhō Jizō (Vajra-Jewel Jizō)
The left hand holds a precious jewel (hōju); the right hand forms the nectar mudra (kanro-in).
Saves beings of the hungry ghost realm (gaki-dō).

【Upper Left】
Kongōtō Jizō (Vajra-Banner Jizō)
The left hand holds a vajra-banner (kongō-dō); the right hand forms the mudra of fearlessness (semui-in).
Saves beings of the Asura realm (shura-dō).

【Lower Left】
Kongōhi Jizō (Vajra-Compassion Jizō)
The left hand holds a khakkhara (staff with rings); the right hand forms the mudra of guiding and receiving [souls] (insetsuno-in).
Saves beings of the animal realm (chikushō-dō).
