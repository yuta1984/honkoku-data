【The Six Jizō (六地蔵 / Ṣaḍ-Kṣitigarbha)】

**Yotenga Jizō (預天賀地蔵)**
In the left hand, holds a wish-fulfilling jewel (nyoi-ju); the right hand forms the mudra of expounding the Dharma (seppō-in).
Saves and liberates the beings of the Heavenly Realm (devas).

**Hōkōō Jizō (放光王地蔵)**
In the left hand, holds a khakkhara (monk's staff); the right hand forms the wish-granting mudra (yogan-in).
Causes rain to fall and brings about the fulfillment of the five grains (i.e., a bountiful harvest).

**Kongōgan Jizō (金剛願地蔵)**
In the left hand, holds the Banner of Yama (Enma-dō); the right hand forms the mudra of accomplishment (jōben-in).
Saves beings from the suffering of falling into Hell.

**Kongōhō Jizō (金剛宝地蔵)**
In the left hand, holds a sacred jewel (hōju); the right hand forms the amrita mudra (kanro-in).
Saves beings of the Hungry Ghost Realm (gaki-dō).

**Kongōtō Jizō (金剛幢地蔵)**
In the left hand, holds the Vajra Banner (kongō-dō); the right hand forms the mudra of bestowing fearlessness (semu-in).
Saves beings of the Asura Realm (shura-dō).

**Kongōhi Jizō (金剛悲地蔵)**
In the left hand, holds a khakkhara (monk's staff); the right hand forms the mudra of welcoming and guiding souls (insho-in).
Saves beings of the Animal Realm (chikushō-dō).
