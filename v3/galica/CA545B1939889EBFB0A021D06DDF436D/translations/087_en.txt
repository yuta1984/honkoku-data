【Upper Right】
【Upper Section】
■Konda Hachiman
【Lower Section】
This is Emperor Ōjin, the son of Emperor Chūai. His mother was Empress Jingū. At the time of his birth, four white banners and four red banners descended from heaven, and thus he was given the name "Hachiman" (Eight Banners). His honji (original Buddhist form) is Amida Nyorai (Amitābha).

【Lower Right】
【Upper Section】
■Zenzai Dōji (Sudhana)
【Lower Section】
According to the *Avatamsaka Sūtra* (*Kegon-kyō*), he is the son of the elder of Fukujō (Dhanaśreṣṭhi). At the time of his first conception in the womb, a seven-treasure tower appeared within the household, and a buried treasure (*fūzō*) was found underground. Therefore, he was called Zenzai Dōji. He is the foremost among Mañjuśrī's five hundred boy attendants.

【Upper Center】
【Upper Section】
■Uhō Dōji (Rain-Treasure Boy)
【Lower Section】
The *Jindai no Maki* (chapter on the Age of the Gods) states: Izanagi-no-Mikoto and Izanami-no-Mikoto deliberated together, saying, "We have given birth to the great land of Oyashima. How could we not give birth to a lord of all under heaven?" And so they gave birth to the deity of the sun. This is Amaterasu Ōmikami.

【Lower Center】
【Upper Section】
■Kongō Dōji (Diamond Boy / Vajra Boy)
【Lower Section】
The Sanskrit term "vajra" (跋闍羅) is rendered in this land (Japan) as "kongō" (diamond/adamantine).

【Upper Left】
【Upper Section】
■Aka Dōji (Red Boy)
【Lower Section】
Ame-no-Oshihomimi-no-Mikoto is the ancestral deity of the Ōnakatomi clan, and is the divine imperial grandson, Kasuga Daimyōjin. When he descended upon the peak of Takachiho in Hyūga Province, he was a retainer who provided support and protection (to Amaterasu Ōmikami).

【Lower Left】
【Upper Section】
■Zennishi Dōji
【Lower Section】
This is the side attendant (*wakidachi*) of Bishamonten (Vaiśravaṇa).
