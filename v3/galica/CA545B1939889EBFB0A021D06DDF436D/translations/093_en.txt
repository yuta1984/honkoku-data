【Lower Right】
【Upper Section】
■Kishimojin (Hārītī, Mother of Demon Children)
【Lower Section】
The *Kensei-ron* states: she is the perfectly endowed Yakṣa Demon Mother Deity. As for the name "Demon Child Mother," the *Myōgi-shū* states, and the *Kōmyō* states: [she is called] Hāritī. She holds an auspicious fruit. According to one interpretation, the name of her infant child is called Higan-maru (Ball of Compassionate Vow).
　　【Figure】
