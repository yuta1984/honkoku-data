**Nanda** (Elder Brother)
In Chinese translation, he is called "Kangiryu-ō" (the Dragon King of Joy). The name derives from his role in bringing rain at the appropriate time, causing the five grains to flourish and thereby bringing joy to the people.

**Shōaku Dōji (Evil-Virtue Youth)**
(Illustration only; no explanatory text)

**Upananda** (Younger Brother)
In Chinese translation, he is called "Zen-Kangiryu-ō" (the Dragon King of Good Joy). These two brothers protect the Buddhist Law (Dharma). At the time of Śākyamuni's birth, the two dragons Nanda and Upananda, dwelling in the sky, blew forth warm water and drew the birth-bathing water (ubuyu) to bathe the newborn.
