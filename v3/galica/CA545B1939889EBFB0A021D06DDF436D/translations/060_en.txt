【Upper Right】
Six-Times Avalokiteśvara (六時観音)
Manifests in the form of a Lay Buddhist (居士身)

【Lower Right】
Universal Compassion Avalokiteśvara (普悲観音)
Manifests in the form of Maheśvara (the Great Sovereign Heavenly Deity)

【Upper Left】
Horse-Herder's Bride Avalokiteśvara (馬郎婦観音)
Appeared during the Yuanhe era of Emperor Xianzong of Tang (recorded in the *Guanyin Ganying zhuan*)
Manifests in the form of a Woman (婦女身)

【Lower Left】
Joined-Palms Avalokiteśvara (合掌観音)
Manifests in the form of a Brahmin (婆羅門身)
