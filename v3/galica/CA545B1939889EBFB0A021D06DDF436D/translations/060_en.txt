Thirty-Three Forms of Kannon (continued)

**Rokuji Kannon (Six-Times Avalokiteśvara)**
Manifests in the form of a Layman (Koji)

**Fuhi Kannon (Universal Compassion Avalokiteśvara)**
Manifests in the form of Daijizaiten (Maheśvara/Śiva)

**Barōfu Kannon (Horse-Keeper's Wife Avalokiteśvara)**
Appeared during the Yuanhe era of Emperor Xianzong of Tang [as recorded] in the *Kannon Kannō Den* (*Guanyin Ganying zhuan*)
Manifests in the form of a Woman

**Gasshō Kannon (Hands-in-Prayer Avalokiteśvara)**
Manifests in the form of a Brahmin (Baramon)
