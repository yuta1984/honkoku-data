【Header (horizontal text)】
The Seven Shrine Manifestations of Sannō (Sannō Shichisha Gongen)

【Upper Right】
【Upper Section】
■Second Shrine: Kohie
【Lower Section】
Five-colored flowers bloomed on a great cedar tree, and the deity descended from heaven. This deity is called "Kadai Bosatsu" (Flower-Dais Bodhisattva). The honji (original Buddhist form) is Yakushi (Bhaiṣajyaguru).

【Lower Right】
【Upper Section】
■Third Shrine
【Lower Section】
During the Enryaku era of Emperor Kanmu's reign, the deity descended in the form of a noble lady, riding a purple cloud and holding the Lotus Sutra, alighting near Mt. Ōtake. The honji is Fugen (Samantabhadra).

【Upper Center】
【Upper Section】
■Ōmiya Gongen (Great Shrine Manifestation)
【Lower Section】
In the first year of Emperor Kinmei's reign (kanoe-saru year), the deity of Miwa in Yamato descended from heaven, and in the first year of Emperor Tenji's reign appeared on Mt. Ōhie. This is Ōnamuchi (Ōkuninushi). The honji is Shakyamuni Buddha.

【Lower Center】
【Upper Section】
■Hachionji (Eight Princes)
【Lower Section】
This is Kunisatsuchi-no-Mikoto. In the first year of the reign of the tenth Emperor Sujin, the deity descended near Mt. Ōtake in Shiga District. This is the Misaki Myōjin (Ryōzan Myōjin). The honji is Senju (Thousand-Armed Kannon, Sahasrabhuja-Avalokiteśvara).

【Upper Left】
【Upper Section】
■Shōshinshi (Jōshinshi)
【Lower Section】
In the first year of Emperor Monmu's reign, the deity appeared in Shiga District, Ōmi Province. This is Hachiman Daibosatsu (Great Bodhisattva Hachiman). The honji is Amida (Amitābha).

【Lower Left】
【Upper Section】
■Kyakujin Gongen (Guest Deity Manifestation)
【Lower Section】
In the first year of the Enryaku era of Emperor Kanmu's reign, the deity appeared at the foot of Mt. Hachionji. High Priest Keimei constructed and dedicated a shrine. This is the Hakusan Myōri Gongen (Manifestation of Wondrous Principle of Mt. Hakusan). The honji is Jūichimen (Eleven-Faced Kannon, Ekādaśamukha-Avalokiteśvara).
