Thirty-Three Forms of Kannon (continued)

**Hae Kannon (Leaf-Robe Avalokiteśvara)**
Manifests in the form of Indra (Taishaku)

**Ruri Kannon (Lapis Lazuli Avalokiteśvara)**
[Alternative name:] Kōō Kannon (Fragrant King Avalokiteśvara)
Manifests in the form of Jizaiten (Īśvara/Maheśvara)
Appeared during the Tianping era of Eastern Wei dynasty [as recorded] in the *Mingxiang ji* (*Myōshōki*)

**Tara Son Kannon (Tārā Venerable Avalokiteśvara)**
Protection from being surrounded by hostile bandits and enemies

**Kōri Kannon (Clam Avalokiteśvara)**
Manifests in the form of a Bodhisattva
Appeared in the fifth year of Dahe (Taihō) during the reign of Emperor Wenzong of Tang
