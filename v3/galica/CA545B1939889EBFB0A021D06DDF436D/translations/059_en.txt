**Hagolomo Kannon (Leaf-Robe Avalokiteśvara)**
Manifests in the form of Indra (Taishaku-shin)

**Ruri Kannon (Lapis Lazuli Avalokiteśvara)**
Also known as: Kōō Kannon (Fragrant King Avalokiteśvara)
Manifests in the form of Jizaiten (Īśvara/Maheśvara)
Appeared during the Tianping era of Eastern Wei
(Interlinear note: *As recorded in the Míngxiáng jì* [冥祥記])

**Tara-son Kannon (Tārā Avalokiteśvara)**
When one encounters and is surrounded by vengeful bandits [this Kannon protects]

**Kōri Kannon (Clam Avalokiteśvara)**
Manifests in the form of a Bodhisattva
Appeared in the fifth year of the Dahe era [831 CE], during the reign of Emperor Wenzong of Tang
