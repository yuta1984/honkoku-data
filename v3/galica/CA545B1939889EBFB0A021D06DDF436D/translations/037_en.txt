【Upper Right】
■Candrasūryapradīpa Buddha (日月燈明仏, "Sun-Moon-Lamp-Light Buddha")
The first fascicle of the *Lotus Sūtra* states: "Just as in the immeasurable, boundless, inconceivable asamkhyeya kalpas of the past, at that time there was a Buddha called Candrasūryapradīpa Buddha... and so forth... the World-Honored One."

【Lower Right】
■The Twenty-Thousand Lamp-Light Buddhas (二万燈明仏)
The *Hokke Teichin* states: "What the twenty-thousand represents is the ten-thousand practices (万行) and the ten-thousand virtues (万善). Furthermore, if the 'Thousand Suchnesses' (千如) are applied to the Ten Good Precepts (十善), they become ten-thousand. Considered in terms of the provisional and the true (権実), this makes twenty-thousand."

【Upper Left】
■The Thirty-Thousand Lamp-Light Buddhas (三万燈明仏)
(The thirty-thousand) represents the Three Truths (三諦) of Emptiness (空), Provisionality (仮), and the Middle Way (中). In the *Lotus Sūtra*, they are referred to as the "Twenty-Thousand Lamp-Light Buddhas."

【Lower Left】
■Amoghasiddhi Buddha (不空成就仏, "Buddha of Unfailing Accomplishment")
The *Bodhicittaśāstra* (菩提心論) states: "The northern Buddha, Amoghasiddhi, is so named because he accomplishes the Wisdom of Accomplished Action (所作智), which is called the Karma Wisdom (羯磨智)."
