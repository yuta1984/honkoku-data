【Upper Right】
■Candrasūryapradīpa-buddha (Sun-Moon Lamp Light Buddha)
The first chapter of the Lotus Sutra states: "In the incalculable, boundless, inconceivable asamkhya kalpas of the past, there was a Buddha called Sun-Moon Lamp Light Buddha... (and so forth) ... called World-Honored One."

【Lower Right】
■Twenty-thousand Lamp Light Buddhas
The Hokke Jōchin states: "What the twenty thousand represents is the ten-thousand practices and the ten-thousand virtues. Furthermore, if the thousand suchnesses are applied to the ten good precepts, they become ten thousand. This, considered in terms of the provisional and the true, becomes twenty thousand."

【Upper Left】
■Thirty-thousand Lamp Light Buddhas
"These represent the three truths of emptiness, provisional existence, and the middle way. In the Lotus Sutra, they appear as the Twenty-thousand Lamp Light Buddhas."
(Left of figure) Lamp Light Mudra

【Lower Left】
■Amoghasiddhi-tathāgata (Unfailing Accomplishment Buddha)
The Treatise on Awakening Bodhicitta states: "The Buddha of Unfailing Accomplishment of the northern direction is so named because of accomplishing the wisdom of action (kriyā-jñāna); he is called Karma Wisdom (羯磨智, karmajñāna)."
