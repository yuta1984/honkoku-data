【Upper Right】
■Seven-Syllable Mañjuśrī (Shichiji Monju)
The *Yuanjue jing shu* (Commentary on the Sūtra of Perfect Enlightenment), fascicle 1, states: "Here [in China] he is called 'Wondrous Head' (Miàoshǒu / Myōshu) and also 'Wondrous Auspiciousness' (Miàojíxiáng / Myōkichijō)."
[Drawing included]
The *Zhu Weimo* (Commentary on the Vimalakīrti Sūtra) states that in Qin [i.e., China], he is called 'Wondrous Virtue' (Miàodé / Myōtoku).
The sūtra states: "He has already formerly attained buddhahood, and his name is called 'Dragon-Lineage Honored One' (Lóngzhǒng Zūn / Ryūshū Son)."

【Lower Right】
■Akṣayamati Bodhisattva (Mujinni Bosatsu)
The *Mingyi ji* (Mahāvyutpatti / Compendium of Names and Meanings) states: "The Sanskrit *Akṣayamati* is rendered here [in Chinese/Japanese] as 'Inexhaustible Intention' (Wújìnyì / Mujin'i)."
[Drawing included]
Tiantai [Zhiyi] states: "Because one knows that the dharma-nature of all things is inexhaustible, the mind of awakening (bodhicitta) that the bodhisattva arouses is likewise inexhaustible."
[Inscription above drawing: Hōman-in (Seal of Dharma-Fulfillment)]

【Upper Left】
■Ākāśagarbha Bodhisattva (Kokūzō Bosatsu)
The sūtra states: "The one who is able to fulfill all vows through great compassion is called Ākāśagarbha ('Treasury of Vast Space')."
[Drawing included]
"In the right hand, [he] holds a sword; in the left hand, [he] holds a wish-fulfilling jewel (cintāmaṇi)."

【Lower Left】
■Fukuichi-man Ākāśagarbha (Fukuichi Man Kokūzō)
"Vast Space" (kokū / ākāśa) symbolizes the inexhaustibility of great compassion in benefiting others.
[Drawing included]
The *Nirvāṇa Sūtra* (*Nehan-gyō*) states: "Vast Space has three meanings: first, it undergoes no change (muhen'i); second, it has no boundary or limit (muhensei); third, it has no obstruction (mukei-ge / anāvaraṇa)."
