【Upper Right】
■Seven-Character Mañjuśrī
The first volume of the Commentary on the Perfect Enlightenment Sutra says: "Here [in China] it is called 'Marvelous Head' (Miaoshou), and also 'Marvelous Auspiciousness' (Miaojixiang). In the Annotated Vimalakīrti Sūtra, it is said that in the Qin [Chinese] tongue it is called 'Marvelous Virtue' (Miaode). The sutra says: 'Having already become a Buddha in the past, [he] was named Nāgavaṃśāgra, the Honored One of the Dragon Lineage.'"

【Lower Right】
■Akṣayamati Bodhisattva
The Collection of Names and Meanings says: "Akṣayamati [in Sanskrit]; here [in Chinese/Japanese] it is called 'Inexhaustible Intent' (Mujin'i). Tiantai [Zhiyi] says: Because one knows that the dharma-nature of all things is inexhaustible, the bodhisattva's aspiration for awakening is likewise inexhaustible."
【Above figure】
Hōman-in (Dharma-Fulfilling Seal)

【Upper Left】
■Ākāśagarbha Bodhisattva
The sutra says: "Able to fulfill all wishes, [he] is named the Great Compassionate Ākāśagarbha. In the right hand [he] holds a sword; in the left hand [he] holds a wish-fulfilling jewel."

【Lower Left】
■Fukuichi-man Ākāśagarbha
"Ākāśa" (Void/Space) represents the inexhaustible quality of great compassion and benefiting others. The Nirvana Sutra says: "Void/Space has three meanings: first, no change or alteration; second, no boundary or limit; third, no obstruction or hindrance (mukei-ge)."
