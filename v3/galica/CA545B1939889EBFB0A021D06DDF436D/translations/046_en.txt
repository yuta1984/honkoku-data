【Upper Right】
■Samantabhadra Bodhisattva (Universal Worthy)
In Sanskrit "Viśvabhadra" or alternatively "Samantabhadra," translated as "Universal Worthy" (Fugen). In the Karuṇāpuṇḍarīka Sūtra it is written: "My practice shall surely surpass that of all bodhisattvas."

The Buddha says: "By this cause and condition, I now change your name and call you Samantabhadra."

【Lower Right】
■Dhāraṇī Bodhisattva
In Sanskrit "Dhāraṇī," translated in this land as "Sōji" (Total Retention). In the Mingyi ji (Mahāvyutpatti): "Total Retention means to retain the good so that it is not lost, and to retain the evil so that it does not arise."

【Upper Left】
■Dharmapratīśvararāja Bodhisattva (Sovereign King of Dharma Freedom)
The so-called "Dharma" refers to the Buddha-Dharma, and "Sovereign King of Freedom" together signifies the meaning of unobstructed freedom. This is an alternate name for Mañjuśrī. In Sanskrit "Mañjuśrī," translated as "Wondrous Virtue" (Myōtoku). In the Karuṇāpuṇḍarīka Sūtra, referred to as "Buddha Ānandagarbhamaniratnaśrī." In the Śūraṅgama Sūtra, called "Dragon-Lineage Supreme Honored King Buddha."

【Lower Left】
■Śvetaśvararāja Bodhisattva (White Elephant King)
The white elephant symbolizes and represents principle (truth/li). "King" signifies the meaning of unobstructed freedom. That is to say, in accordance with the inherent nature (理性) of the bodhisattva-being (sattva), fully endowed with all ten thousand dharmas, and for the sake of liberating sentient beings, the name is given in accordance with the majestic appearance of freely wielding wondrous function.
