**Mahoraga**
The *Mingyi ji* (Dictionary of Names and Meanings) states: "This is an earth-dragon that moves by crawling on its belly." Sengzhao (Zhao) states: "It is the great python deity that moves on its belly."

**Sañjñeya (Sañjaya) Great General**
He is the son of Hāritī (Kishimojin, the Mother of Demon Children) and the elder brother of Śrī-devī (Kudokutennyo, the Goddess of Merit and Virtue).
