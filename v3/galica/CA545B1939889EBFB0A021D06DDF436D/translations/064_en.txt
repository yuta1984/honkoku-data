【Upper Right】
■Metsuzakushu (Destroyer of Evil Realms)

The *Jūō-kyō* (Ten Kings Sutra) states: it is also called "Destroyer of Evil Realms" (破悪趣), and further "Remover of All Evil Realms" (除一切悪趣). Abiding in the samādhi of great compassion,
　　【Illustration】
this is the venerable one who destroys the three evil realms, including hell.

【Lower Right】
■Jōgyō (Superior Practice) and Muhengyō (Boundless Practice)

The *Hoshōki* states: "Jōgyō" is so named because one practices the supreme great Dharma.
　　【Illustration】
"Muhengyō" is so named because merit and virtue are boundless.

【Center Upper】
■Dhāraṇī Bodhisattva

The *Yūyo Mandara-shō* states: the inner realization (naisho) is Avalokiteśvara (Kannon). Furthermore, this
　　【Illustration】
bodhisattva particularly takes pity on sentient beings in hell and leads the karmically connected sentient beings (unto salvation).

【Center Lower】
■Jōgyō (Pure Practice) and Anryūgyō (Firmly Established Practice)

"Jōgyō (Pure Practice)" is so named because the Dharma-nature (tathatā) is pure and undefiled.
　　【Illustration】
"Anryūgyō" is so named because the three-thousand (fold world) is perfectly manifest as it is. According to the *Hokke-shō*, the four bodhisattvas of the Original Enlightenment teaching (honke) are precisely these.
