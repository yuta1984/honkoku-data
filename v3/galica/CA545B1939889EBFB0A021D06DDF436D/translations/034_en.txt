【Upper Right】
■The Mountain-Crossing Tathāgata (Yamagoshi no Nyorai)

As has been transmitted: When the venerable form of Amida (Amitābha) appeared atop the ridge of Yokawa on Mount Hiei, the Sōzu Genshin (Eshin Sōzu) reverently gazed upon it and immediately drew the figure upon the sleeve of his plain white silk robe, preserving it for posterity. For further details, the painted image he copied, together with its associated legend (engi), is preserved at the Eshin'in of Yokawa.

【Lower Right】
■Prabhūtaratna Tathāgata (Tahō Nyorai)

The *Lotus Sutra* states: "In the distant past, in the world to the east, across immeasurable, thousands of millions, billions of asamkhya world-realms, there was a land called Hōjō (Treasure-Pure). Within it there was a Buddha whose name was Tahō (Prabhūtaratna, Many-Treasures)."

【Upper Left】
■Mahāvairocana of the Vajradhātu (Diamond Realm)

The Vajradhātu arises from the wisdom of the Buddha-fruit of Vairocana. "Vajra" (Diamond/Thunderbolt) is the very nature of the Bodhi-realm (realm of awakening).

According to the *Dainichikyō Kahōshō* (Commentary on the Jewel of the Fruit in the Mahāvairocana Sūtra): "Vairocana Buddha is another name for the sun — it signifies the removal of darkness and the universal illumination of all things."

【Lower Left】
■Mahāvairocana of the Garbhadhātu (Womb Realm)

In Sanskrit this is called *Vairocana*; in Chinese/Japanese it is translated as "Saikō Kenkō Genzō Nyorai" (the Tathāgata of the Supremely Exalted, Clearly Manifest, Vast Eye-Treasury).

The Garbhadhātu (Womb Realm) arises from the place of the afflictions (kleśa) and desires of sentient beings, and is expressed through the wisdom-seal (mudrā) of the Tathāgata.
