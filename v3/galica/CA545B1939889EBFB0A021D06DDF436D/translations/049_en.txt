The Twenty-five Bodhisattvas (continued)

**Nissho-ō Bosatsu (日照王菩薩 / King of Solar Illumination Bodhisattva)**
Using the sunlight of wisdom, this bodhisattva illuminates and dispels the darkness of delusion in which sentient beings are lost. Thus, the name is derived from this virtue of wisdom.
The *Avatamsaka Sūtra* (Huayan Jing) states: "The sun of wisdom illuminates the world, dispelling the clouds of birth and death."

**Shūhō-ō Bosatsu (衆宝王菩薩 / King of Manifold Treasures Bodhisattva)**
The character 衆 (*shū*) is read as "moromoro" (meaning "manifold" or "all manner of"). The character 宝 (*hō*) is glossed as meaning "that which is rare and precious," and read as "takara" (treasure). The character 王 (*ō*) carries the meaning of "sovereign" or "unobstructed freedom." Hence the name means "the sovereign of all treasures." The treasures referred to include the seven precious gems and the seven treasures of a wheel-turning king, but the name encompasses all treasures without limitation.

**Gakkō-ō Bosatsu (月光王菩薩 / King of Moonlight Bodhisattva)**
The virtuous qualities — such as the three virtues and others — that surpass those of the moon disc of this world are fully embodied by this bodhisattva. Therefore, these qualities manifest directly in the bodhisattva's honorific name, which is thus adopted as the name.

**Zanmai Bosatsu (三昧菩薩 / Samādhi Bodhisattva)**
This bodhisattva has attained perfect freedom within all states of meditative concentration (*samādhi*), and thus takes this as the name. The term "zanmai" (三昧) is more precisely rendered as "sammaji" (三摩地, *samādhi* in Sanskrit). In Chinese translation, this is rendered as 正定 (*shōjō*, "right concentration") and interpreted as 正心行処 (*shōshin gyōsho*, "the place of correct mental practice and conduct").
