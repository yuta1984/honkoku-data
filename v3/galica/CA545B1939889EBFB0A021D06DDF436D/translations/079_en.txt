【Upper Right】
【Upper Section】
■Nikkō Bodhisattva (Sūryaprabha)《19th day》
【Lower Section】
This is the great bodhisattva who serves as the left attendant of Yakushi Buddha (Bhaiṣajyaguru). When in heaven, he is called Nitten-shi (Sūrya, the Sun Deity).

【Lower Right】
【Upper Section】
■Shōjinshi Gongen《19th day》
【Lower Section】
This deity manifests in Shiga District, Ōmi Province. Also identified in some accounts with Hachiman Daibosatsu. The honji (original ground) is Amida Nyorai (Amitābha).

【Upper Center】
【Upper Section】
■Gakkō Bodhisattva (Candraprabha)《20th day》
【Lower Section】
Likewise, this is the great bodhisattva who serves as the right attendant of Yakushi Buddha. Also called Gattō Daishi (Great Master Moon-Lamp). This deity is also Gatten-shi (Candra, the Moon Deity).

【Lower Center】
【Upper Section】
■Kyakujin Gongen《20th day》
【Lower Section】
Enshrined in Shiga District, Ōmi Province. It is Izanami-no-mikoto. The honji is Jūichimen (Kannon, the Eleven-Faced Avalokiteśvara).

【Upper Left】
【Upper Section】
■Mujini Bodhisattva (Akṣayamati)《21st day》
【Lower Section】
The "Daihon Hannya-kyō" (Mahāprajñāpāramitā Sūtra) teaches that "emptiness is indeed inexhaustible." The "Daijiku-kyō" (Mahāsaṃnipāta Sūtra) sets forth eighty gates of inexhaustibility.

【Lower Left】
【Upper Section】
■Hachiōji Gongen《21st day》
【Lower Section】
Enshrined within the seven Sannō shrines of Ōmi Province. The honji is Senju Kannon (Thousand-Armed Avalokiteśvara).
