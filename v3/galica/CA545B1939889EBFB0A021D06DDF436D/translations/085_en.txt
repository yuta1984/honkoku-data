■Izuna Gongen

The honji (original Buddhist form) is Fudō Myōō (Acalanātha).

【Upper Section】
Suzuka Gongen
【Lower Section】
One deity enshrined in Suzuka District, Ise Province.

【Upper Center】
【Upper Section】
■Atago Gongen
【Lower Section】
This deity enshrines Kagutsuchi-no-kami. This god perished, burned by fire, and therefore took a vow to save people from fire. The rear general of the inner sanctuary, Tarōbō, enshrines Jizō (Kṣitigarbha) as the main deity.

【Lower Center】
【Upper Section】
Izu Hakone Gongen
【Lower Section】
The main shrine is dedicated to Hikohohodemi-no-mikoto. There are also Komagan Gongen, Shirowaryūō (White Harmony Dragon King), Right Magpie, Left Magpie King, and the Marebito-no-miya shrine.

【Upper Left】
【Upper Section】
■Yoshino Zaō Gongen
【Lower Section】
Manifesting a great wrathful form, this deity appeared on Mount Kinpusen. In the right hand, a three-pronged vajra (sankō) is grasped, the arm is raised, and the left hand presses against the abdomen, with both eyes clearly glaring in fury. This is the aspect of subduing demonic obstacles.

【Lower Left】
【Upper Section】
Dewa Yudono Gongen
【Lower Section】
This deity manifested as Yudono Gongen through the "trace-revealing" principle (wakō) at the headwaters of the Obōjigawa River in Dewa Province. The honji is the Dharmakāya Dainichi (Mahāvairocana).
