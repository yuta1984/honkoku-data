【Upper Right】
【Upper Section】
Nachi Hiryū Gongen
【Lower Section】
As stated in the *Ainōshō*: This is a retainer assisting Amaterasu Ōkami. This is the place where [the deity] descended at the beginning of the kalpa (the origin of the world). It is a manifestation (ōke) of Nanda Ryūō (Nanda Dragon King).

【Lower Right】
【Upper Section】
Tōtō Tenjin (Tenjin Who Crossed to Tang/Song China)
【Lower Section】
This is a depiction of the spirit of Sugawara no Michizane traveling to Jingshan Temple (Jingshan-si) in Song China, practicing Zen under the monk Wuzhun Shifan (Mujun Wajō), and receiving the dharma robe from him.

【Top Frame, Horizontal】
The Four Shrine Deities of Mount Kōya

【Upper Center】
【Upper Section】
Niū Daimyōjin
【Lower Section】
She is the younger sister of Amaterasu Ōkami. The reason she manifests in a female form is that, as the mother of all things under heaven, she bestows the blessings of growth and nurturing abundantly.

【Lower Center】
【Upper Section】
Kehi Myōjin
【Lower Section】
She is the daughter of Niū Gongen and the younger sister of Kōya Myōjin. She is the revered deity known as Kehi Taruhime. She is also called "the Shrine of the Box of Rice" (Ke-no-ii-no-miya). She is also named "Isasakawake-no-kami" (去来沙別の神).

【Upper Left】
【Upper Section】
Kōya Daimyōjin
【Lower Section】
He is the first son (eldest child) of Niū Gongen. His honji (Buddhist original ground) is Mahāvairocana of the Vajradhātu (Diamond Realm). He is the teaching master who eternally expounds the Dharma in the Mani Hall.

【Lower Left】
【Upper Section】
Itsukushima
【Lower Section】
The Shinomiya Gongen is a child of Niū Gongen. The honji is none other than Mahā-Sarasvatī (Great Benzaiten).
