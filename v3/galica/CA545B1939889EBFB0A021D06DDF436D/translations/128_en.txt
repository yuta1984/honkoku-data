Birurokusha Zenjin (毘廬勒叉善神 / Virūḍhaka Guardian Deity)
【Lower section】
This is rendered as "Zōchō" (増長 / Growth and Prosperity). He is the one who causes the virtue and dignity of self and others to grow and flourish. His father's name is Washukitsu (和修吉 / Vāsuki), and his mother's name is Ouchōtai (慪惙帝). This is found in the Dhāraṇī Collection (陀羅尼集 / Tuoluoni ji).

【Upper center】
【Upper section】
■ Bazō Sennin (婆藪仙人 / The Sage Vāsava)
【Lower section】
The Fangdeng Dhāraṇī Sūtra (方等陀羅経) states: "At that time, Vāsava emerged from hell, leading ninety-two billion sinners, and came to present himself in the Sahā world. The same occurred throughout the ten directions."

【Upper left】
【Upper section】
■ Daiben Kudoku Ten (大弁功徳天 / Mahāsarasvatī, Deity of Great Eloquence and Virtue)
【Lower section】
This refers to the Goddess Kissho Ten (吉祥天女 / Śrī Lakṣmī). She presides over the world among the heavenly deities and governs the arising of wisdom.

【Lower left】
【Upper section】
■ Missha Kongō (密遮金剛 / Guhyapāda Vajra)
【Lower section】
The Shukke-ron (出家論 / Treatise on Leaving Household Life) states: "The Misshaku (密迹 / Guhyaka), serving as the left-hand guardian, takes the severing of evil as his merit; without moving, he transmits [his protection] to all the heavenly deities and guards the teachings of the thousand Buddhas."
