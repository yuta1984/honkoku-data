**Iwato Kannon (Cave-Dwelling Kannon)**
[Saves beings from] great serpents (蚢蛇) and vipers and scorpions (蝮蝎)

**Nōjō Kannon (Kannon of Ability to Calm)**
[Saves those whose] ship's moorings are blown by the wind, drifting [at sea]
and falling into the land of Rākṣasa demons

**Anoku Kannon (Kannon of Anuttara / No Dirt)**
[Saves beings from] the perils of dragons, fish, and all manner of demons

**Amata Kannon (Amatei Kannon)**
Manifests in the form of Vaiśravaṇa (Bishamonten)
