**Iwato Kannon (Cave-Door Avalokiteśvara)**
Vipers, serpents, and scorpions

**Nōjō Kannon (Able-to-Calm Avalokiteśvara)**
Blown off course, the ship drifts
and falls into the land of rākṣasa demons

**Anoku Kannon (Anavatapta Avalokiteśvara)**
Perils of dragons, fish, and various demons

**Amata Kannon (Amadeva Avalokiteśvara)**
Vaiśravaṇa beholds [this Kannon]
