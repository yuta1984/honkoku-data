Thirty-Three Forms of Kannon (continued)

**Iwato Kannon (Rock-Cave Avalokiteśvara)**
Venomous serpents (*kōja*) and vipers and scorpions (*fukukatsu*) [i.e., protection from venomous creatures]

**Nōjō Kannon (Calming Avalokiteśvara)**
[When winds] blow and set adrift one's moored vessel,
causing one to fall into the land of Rākṣasa demons

**Anoku Kannon (Anavatapta Avalokiteśvara)**
The perils of dragons, great fish, and all manner of demons

**Amata Kannon (Amadei Avalokiteśvara)**
Appearing before Vaiśravaṇa (Bishamonten)
