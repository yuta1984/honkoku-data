**The Sixteen Arhats (Jūroku Rakan)**

---

**First: Bhadra-the-Venerable (跋羅駄闍尊者, Hatsu-ra-ta-sha Sonja)**

(Illustration)
Resides in the western continent of Godānīya (西瞿耶尼州, Sai Kuyani-shū).

---

**Second: Kanakavatsa-the-Venerable (迦諾迦伐蹉尊者, Kanoka-hasha Sonja)**

Resides in the northern land of Kaśmīra (北方迦湿彌羅國, Hokuhō Kashimira-koku).
(Illustration)

---

**Third: Nakula-the-Venerable (諾迦跋釐駄尊者, Daka-hari-ta Sonja)**

Resides in the eastern continent of Pūrvavideha (東勝身洲, Tō-shōshin-shū).
(Illustration)

---

**Fourth: Suvinda-the-Venerable (蘇頻陀尊者, Sohinda Sonja)**

(Illustration)
Resides in the northern continent of Uttarakuru (北倶盧洲, Kita-Kuru-shū).
