【Upper section】
■ Daibon Tennō (大梵天王 / Mahābrahmā, the Great Brahma Heavenly King)
【Lower section】
He is the lord of the First Dhyāna Heaven (初禅天) of the Form Realm (色界), and also the lord of the Great Trichiliocosm (大千世界). Because he possesses *vitarka* and *vicāra* (覚観 / initial and sustained thought), he stands above all and presides over all below. Therefore, he is given the title "Lord of Command" (令主). The Vibhāṣā Śāstra (婆沙論) states: "The expanse of the First Dhyāna Heaven is like that of the Four Continents."

【Lower right】
【Upper section】
■ Makei Shura Ō (摩醯首羅王 / Maheśvara, the Great Lord)
【Lower section】
The Śāstra on the Great Prajñāpāramitā (釈論 / Mahāprajñāpāramitā-upadeśa) states: "Maheśvara is called 'Daijizai' (大自在 / The Great Self-Existent One)." He is the lord of the Heaven at the Summit of the Form Realm (色頂天) and also the lord of the Three Realms (三界). Therefore, he is referred to as "Songoku" (尊極 / the Most Exalted).

【Upper center】
【Upper section】
■ Taishaku Tennō (帝釈天王 / Śakra Devānām Indra, the Heavenly King Indra)
【Lower section】
The Liaoyi Deng (了義燈 / Lamp of Definitive Meaning) states: "It is like how Indra's name is rendered as 'Sengen' (千眼 / Thousand-Eyed). This does not mean he literally has a thousand eyes; rather, it is because he is capable of perceiving a thousand dharmas simultaneously."

【Lower center】
【Upper section】
■ Tōhō Ten (東方天 / Deity of the East)
【Lower section】
This refers to Jikoku Ten (持国天 / Dhṛtarāṣṭra, Guardian of the East). He commands the Gandharvas (乾闥婆 / Kentatsuva) and the Piśācas (毗舎闍 / Bishaja), and protects the people of the Eastern Continent (東洲 / Pūrvavideha).

■ Birurokusha (毘楼勒叉 / Virūḍhaka, Guardian of the South)
【Lower section】
