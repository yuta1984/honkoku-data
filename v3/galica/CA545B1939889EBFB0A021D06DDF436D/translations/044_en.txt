Four Honored Ones of the North

【Upper Right】
■Vajrakarma (Diamond Action)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' great-diligence-gate Vajrakarma and all other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "Vajrakarma that arises from the gate of skillful craftsmanship and artistry of all Tathāgatas"

【Lower Right】
■Vajrarakṣa (Diamond Protection)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' great-diligence-gate Vajrarakṣa and all other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "Vajrarakṣa that arises from the gate of the great compassionate armor of all Tathāgatas"

【Upper Left】
■Vajrayakṣa (Diamond Fang/Tusk)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' great-diligence-gate Vajrayakṣa and all other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "Vajrayakṣa that arises from the gate of fearless subjugation of all Tathāgatas"

【Lower Left】
■Vajramuṣṭi (Diamond Fist)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' great-diligence-gate Vajramuṣṭi and all other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "Vajramuṣṭi that arises from the gate of sustaining and accomplishing of all Tathāgatas"
