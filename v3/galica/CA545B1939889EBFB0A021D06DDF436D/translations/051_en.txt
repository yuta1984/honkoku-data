The Twenty-Five Bodhisattvas (continued)

**Muhenshin Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of Boundless Body)**
"Boundless Body" is an alternate name for Kṣitigarbha (Jizō) Bodhisattva. The *Sutra of the Original Vows* (Hongan-gyō) states: "Within a dream, one fully beholds Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva manifesting his boundless body, and at the place where that person dwells, he bestows the water of consecration (kanjo)," and so forth. Truly, because this bodhisattva is a being of profoundly deep great compassion, he manifests in various emanation bodies and transforms and guides beings throughout the six realms.

**Mujini Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of Inexhaustible Intent)**
The voice of this venerable one's gāthā (sacred verse) awakens ordinary beings from the slumber of delusion. The *Mahāprajñāpāramitā Sūtra* (Daihon Hannya-kyō) states: "Emptiness is, in other words, the infinite," and so forth.

**Myōon Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of Wondrous Sound)**
Jōchin stated: "Spreading wondrous sound throughout the realm of the ten directions, he widely proclaims the wondrous Dharma. For this reason, he is named and called 'Wondrous Sound.'"

**Mangetsu Bodhisattva (Full Moon Bodhisattva)**
This bodhisattva holds a flower garland (keman).
