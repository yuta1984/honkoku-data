Twenty-Five Bodhisattvas (continued)

【Upper Right】
■Twenty-fifth: Muhenshin Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of the Boundless Body)
"Boundless Body" (無辺身, muhenshin) is another name for Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva (Jizō Bosatsu). The Hongan-kyō (Sūtra of Fundamental Vows) states: "In the midst of sleep and dreams, one fully sees Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva manifesting a boundless body, and at the place of that person, the water of consecration (kanjo) is bestowed," and so forth.
Truly, because this is a bodhisattva of profoundly deep great compassion, he manifests in various divided forms and guides and transforms beings in the Six Realms.

【Lower Right】
■Mujini Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of Inexhaustible Intent)
The sound of this revered one's gāthā (verse) awakens ordinary beings from the slumber of their deluded thoughts.
The Daihon (Mahāprajñāpāramitā Sūtra) states: "Emptiness is, in itself, without measure (boundless)," and so forth.

【Upper Left】
■Myōon Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of Wondrous Sound)
Jōchin stated: "Because [this bodhisattva] pervades the ten-directional realm with wondrous sound and broadly proclaims the wondrous Dharma, he is named and titled 'Wondrous Sound.'"

【Lower Left】
■Mangetsu Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of the Full Moon)
[This bodhisattva] holds a flower garland (keman).
