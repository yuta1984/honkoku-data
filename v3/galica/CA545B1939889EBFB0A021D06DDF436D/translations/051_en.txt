The Twenty-Five Bodhisattvas (continued)

**No. 25 · Muhensin Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of Boundless Body)**

"Boundless Body" (無辺身) is an alternate name for Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva (Jizō Bosatsu). The *Hongan-gyō* (Sutra of Original Vows) states: "Within a dream, one fully beholds Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva manifesting a boundless body. To the place where this person dwells, he bestows the water of abhiṣeka (consecration)," and so forth. Truly, because this bodhisattva is a being (sattva) of profoundly deep great compassion, he manifests in various emanation bodies and transforms the beings of the six realms.

**Mujini Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of Inexhaustible Intent)**

The sound of this honored one's gāthā (verse) awakens ordinary people from the slumber of delusive thoughts. The *Daihon* (*Mahāprajñāpāramitā Sūtra*) states: "Emptiness is none other than the immeasurable," and so forth.

**Myōon Bodhisattva (Bodhisattva of Wondrous Sound)**

Teichin stated: "By means of wondrous sound, pervading the realms of the ten directions, he widely proclaims the wondrous Dharma. Therefore, he is named and designated 'Wondrous Sound.'"

**Mangetsu Bodhisattva (Full Moon Bodhisattva)**

He holds a flower garland (keman).
