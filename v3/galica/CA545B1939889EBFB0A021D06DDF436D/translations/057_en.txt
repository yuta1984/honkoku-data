**Seitō Kannon (Blue-Headed Kannon)**
Manifests in the form of a Buddha

**Itoku Kannon (Kannon of Majestic Virtue)**
Manifests in the form of the Heavenly Great General (Ten Daishōgun)

**Enmei Kannon (Life-Prolonging Kannon)**
[Saves beings from] curses and all manner of poisonous substances
※ As recorded in the *Xuanyan ji* (*Sengenki*)

**Shūhō Kannon (Kannon of Many Treasures)**
Manifests in the form of an Elder (wealthy householder)
