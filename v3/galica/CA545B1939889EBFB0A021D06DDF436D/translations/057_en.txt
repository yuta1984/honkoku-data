**Seizu Kannon (Blue-Headed Avalokiteśvara)**

[Appears in the form of] a Buddha immediately manifest (Sokugen-busshin)

**Itoku Kannon (Majestic-Virtue Avalokiteśvara)**

[Appears in the form of] a Heavenly Great General (Tendaishōgun-shin)

**Enmei Kannon (Life-Prolonging Avalokiteśvara)**

[Saves those harmed by] curses and all manner of poisonous substances (Juso shō dokuyaku)
　※ [This form] is found in the *Xuanyan ji* (宣験記)

**Shūhō Kannon (Many-Treasures Avalokiteśvara)**

[Appears in the form of] a wealthy elder (Chōja-shin)
