【Upper Right】
■Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva
He is the great guide from the remote past, and the mother of wisdom through which all Buddhas of the three ages (past, present, and future) appeared in the world.
[Drawing included]
He is also the bodhisattva (sattva) from whom all the tathāgatas of the ten directions aroused the aspiration for awakening (bodhicitta).

【Lower Right】
■Child Mañjuśrī
The Nirvāṇa Sūtra states: "In the worlds of the ten directions, whether there are Buddhas or not, wherever Mahāyāna [teachings]
[Drawing included]
flow and are expounded—all of this is by the power of Mañjuśrī."

【Upper Left】
■Samantabhadra Bodhisattva
The benefiting of beings pervading everywhere is called "Universal" (fu/普); benevolence, compassion, and enlightened understanding are called "Worthy" (gen/賢).
The Vows of Samantabhadra chapter of the Avataṃsaka Sūtra states: "I vow that when I face the moment of death, I shall completely remove all obstructions and hindrances, and beholding the Buddha Amitābha face to face,
[Drawing included]
I shall thereupon attain rebirth in the Land of Peace and Bliss (Sukhāvatī)."

【Lower Left】
■Child Samantabhadra
The Commentary on the Sarvatathāgatatattvasaṃgraha (Rishu-kyō-shō) states:
"Following conditions and reaching everywhere universally is called 'Universal' (fu/普);
[Drawing included]
Leading and drawing [beings] into the Buddha's path is called 'Worthy' (gen/賢)."
