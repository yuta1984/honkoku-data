【Upper Right】
■Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva (Monjushiri Bosatsu)

He is the Great Teacher (*daidōshi*) of the remote past, the mother of wisdom for all buddhas of the three ages (*sanze*) when they appear in the world.
(Figure)
He is the *sattva* (being) from whom the tathāgatas of the ten directions first aroused the aspiration for awakening (*bodhicitta*).

【Lower Right】
■Chigo Monju (Mañjuśrī as a Child Acolyte)

The *Nirvāṇa Sūtra* (*Nehangyō*) states: "In the worlds of the ten directions, whether in lands that have a buddha or lands that have no buddha, all places where the Mahāyāna flows and is propagated — this is entirely due to the power of Mañjuśrī."
(Figure)

【Upper Left】
■Samantabhadra Bodhisattva (Fugen Bosatsu)

That whose meritorious benefits (*toku no ri*) pervade all things universally is called "*fu*" (universal/pervading); that which bestows benevolent compassion and the awakening of wisdom is called "*gen*" (worthy/sagely).

The "Chapter on the Vows and Practices of Samantabhadra" (*Fugen Gyōgan-bon*) in the *Avataṃsaka Sūtra* (*Kegonkyō*) states: "I vow that when I approach the moment of death, I shall completely remove all obstacles and hindrances, and beholding the Buddha Amitābha face to face, I shall straightway attain birth in the Land of Peace and Bliss (*Anrakukoku*, i.e., the Pure Land)."
(Figure)

【Lower Left】
■Chigo Fugen (Samantabhadra as a Child Acolyte)

The *Rishukyō-shō* (*Commentary on the Prajñāpāramitā-naya Sūtra*) states: "Following conditions and reaching everywhere universally is called '*fu*' (universal); leading and guiding [beings] into the Buddha's path is called '*gen*' (worthy/sagely)."
(Figure)
