Taizurata Zenjin (提頭賴吒善神 / Dhṛtarāṣṭra Guardian Deity)
【Lower section】
He is called here "Holder of the Nation" (持国 / Jikoku). That is, he is the one who protects and upholds the land. The Dhāraṇī Collection (陀羅尼集 / Tuoluoni ji) states: his father's name is Kennanda (𦬖難陀), and his mother's name is Missha (密闍).
