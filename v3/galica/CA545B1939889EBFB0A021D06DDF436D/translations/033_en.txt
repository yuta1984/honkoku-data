【Upper Right】
■Lower Grade, Inferior Birth
Those of the lower grade, inferior birth are those who have committed the five grave offenses. At the time of death, a good spiritual friend encourages them to recite the nembutsu, and with each recitation their sins of birth and death are extinguished. Immediately, a golden lotus flower appears before them like a solar disc, and they are straightaway able to attain birth in the Land of Ultimate Bliss.

【Lower Right】
■Amida of Wondrous Contemplative Wisdom
According to the Shokyo Yōshū (Essentials Compiled from Various Sutras): Amida's father was the Wheel-Turning Sacred King Gatsujō (Skt. Candrottara-cakravartin), and his mother was Shushō Myōgan (also known as Kōhariki-shoku, "Crimson Crystal Color"). Amida's personal name was Kyōshika (Skt. Kauśika). He renounced his royal position and became a monk, taking the name Dharmākara Bodhisattva (Hōzō Biku).

【Upper Left】
■Amida of the Mandala
The mudrā of the three bodies: the right palm is displayed openly, and the left palm is concealed. On the chest is the manji (swastika) symbol. The thumb is joined with the ring finger and middle finger. This is the mudrā of the Dharma Body (Hosshin) and Reward Body (Hōshin). On the right hand, the thumb is joined with the index finger. This is the mudrā of the Manifestation Body (Ōjin).

【Lower Left】
■Amida of Five Kalpas of Contemplation
The three characters "Muryōju" (Immeasurable Life/Amitāyus) represent the Three Truths of Emptiness, Provisionality, and the Middle Way of all buddhas of the three ages. The name and title contemplated over five kalpas is the root and origin of the eighty-thousand dharma treasuries.
