Twenty-Five Bodhisattvas (continued)

【Upper Right】
■Ākāśagarbha Bodhisattva (Space Treasury)
Being the dharma-body of the Five Dharmas and Five Buddhas, there is nothing within the dharma-realm of empty space in which awakening cannot be attained. Just as empty space itself is called a "treasury," that realm of emptiness has neither limits nor particular locations. Likewise, this honored one's compassionate wisdom for benefiting sentient beings is inexhaustible, and the name arises from comparing this limitlessness to the treasury (storehouse) of empty space.

【Lower Right】
■Dharmakara Bodhisattva (Virtue Treasury)
This bodhisattva is a great being (mahāsattva) endowed with vast and profound compassionate merit. In accordance with the countless capacities of all living beings, he opens the millions of storehouses of merit and great compassion, guiding all sentient beings. Thus he is named Virtue Treasury Bodhisattva.

【Upper Left】
■Ratnagarbha Bodhisattva (Jewel Treasury)
"Jewel" refers to various precious treasures; "treasury" means a storehouse. Just as one stores the world's blessings and precious treasures in a storehouse to manage all affairs, this great being opens the great treasury of merit and compassion in accordance with the wishes of all sentient beings, and thus delivers them.

【Lower Left】
■Kanazō Bodhisattva (Gold Treasury)
Gold is an indestructible substance — it does not drown when placed in water, and it does not change color when placed in fire. This bodhisattva, in the stage of practice (in causal position), responds to the merit of invoking the sacred name (nembutsu), becomes one of the holy assembly of the Western Pure Land, and attains the virtue of golden indestructibility. Thus he is called Gold Treasury.
