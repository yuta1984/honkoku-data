Four Honored Ones of the West

【Upper Right】
■Vajradharma (Diamond Dharma)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' wisdom gate Vajradharma and all other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "The Diamond Dharma that arises through the wisdom of unobstructed, unstained freedom of all Tathāgatas"

【Lower Right】
■Vajratīkṣṇa (Diamond Sharpness)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' wisdom gate Vajratīkṣṇa and all other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "The Diamond Cause (Vajrahetu) that arises through the wisdom of all Tathāgatas that permanently severs residual karmic tendencies"

【Upper Left】
■Vajrahetu (Diamond Cause)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' wisdom gate Vajrahetu and all other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "The Diamond Cause that arises through the wisdom of turning the great Dharma Wheel of all Tathāgatas"

【Lower Left】
■Vajrabhāṣa (Diamond Speech)
"Taking refuge in all Tathāgatas' wisdom gate Vajrabhāṣa and all other buddhas"

The Meaning of Generation says: "The Diamond Secret Speech that arises through the wisdom of all Tathāgatas that transcends verbal expression and conceptual elaboration"
