The Sixteen Great Bodhisattvas (continued from previous page)

【Upper Right】
■ (The Four Honored Ones of the West) Vajradharma

I humbly take refuge in the wisdom gate (jñāna-dvāra) of all Tathāgatas. [I pay homage to] Vajradharma and all the Buddhas of that [company].
The Shusshō-gi (Meaning of Arising) says: "Through the wisdom of unobstructed, unstained freedom (svātantrya-anāsrava-jñāna) of all Tathāgatas, Vajradharma is born and manifested."

【Lower Right】
■ Vajratīkṣṇa

I humbly take refuge in the wisdom gate of all Tathāgatas. [I pay homage to] Vajratīkṣṇa and all the Buddhas of that [company].
The Shusshō-gi says: "Through the wisdom of all Tathāgatas that permanently severs residual habitual tendencies (vāsanā), Vajrahetu is born and manifested."

【Upper Left】
■ Vajrahetu

I humbly take refuge in the wisdom gate of all Tathāgatas. [I pay homage to] Vajrahetu and all the Buddhas of that [company].
The Shusshō-gi says: "Through the wisdom of all Tathāgatas that turns the great Wheel of the Dharma (mahā-dharmacakra-pravartana), Vajrahetu is born and manifested."

【Lower Left】
■ Vajrabhāṣa

I humbly take refuge in the wisdom gate of all Tathāgatas. [I pay homage to] Vajrabhāṣa and all the Buddhas of that [company].
The Shusshō-gi says: "Through the wisdom of all Tathāgatas that transcends verbal expression and conceptual proliferation (prapañca), Vajraguhyabhāṣa (the Diamond Secret Speech) is born and manifested."

【List of the Four Honored Ones of the West】
