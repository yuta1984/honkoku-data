■Kōshin Shōmen Kongō (Blue-Faced Vajra of the Kōshin)
【Lower Section】
According to the Laozi's Scripture of the Three Corpses (Sanshi-kyō), from the time of a person's birth, entities called the "Three Corpses" reside within the body, never leaving, and seek to harm one's life. On the night of Kōshin, they report one's sins to heaven. If one stays awake and calls the names of the Three Corpses, misfortune will be removed and good fortune will come. After midnight, facing south, bow twice and recite: The Upper Corpse, also called Hōzoku, is blue; the Middle Corpse is white; the Lower Corpse, also called Hōkyō, is red. One should chant "Hōkōshi, Hōjōshi, Meijishi" three times.
