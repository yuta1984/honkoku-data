【Upper section】
Biruhakusha Zenjin (毘留博叉善神 / Virūpākṣa Guardian Deity)
【Lower section】
He is called here the "Miscellaneous-Speech Heavenly King" (雑語天王), because he is able to produce various kinds of speech. The Dhāraṇī Collection (陀羅尼集 / Tuoluoni ji) gives his name thus. The Dhāraṇī Collection states: his father's name is Bāgara (婆伽羅), and his mother's name is Jūshūhi (渋秀卑).
