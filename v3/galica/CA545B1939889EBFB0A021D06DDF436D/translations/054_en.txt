## The Thirty-Three Forms of Kannon

---

**Ryūzu Kannon (Dragon-Head Avalokiteśvara)**

〔Figure〕

(Manifests in the form of) Heavenly Dragons (*tenryū*) and Yakṣas (*yasha*).

---

**Jikyō Kannon (Sūtra-Holding Avalokiteśvara)**

(Manifests in) the form of a Śrāvaka (*shōmon-shin*; a hearer/disciple of the Buddha).

〔Figure〕

---

**Enkō Kannon (Radiant-Halo Avalokiteśvara)**

When correct contemplation (*shōkan*) arises, all attachments and involvements naturally cease; responding to (the spiritual) resonance (of sentient beings),

〔Figure〕

(Kannon) manifests within the light (of the halo).

---

**Yūge Kannon (Playful/Freely-Moving Avalokiteśvara)**

(Saves those who) fall from Mt. Kongō (*Kongōzan*).

〔Figure〕
