## The Thirty-Three Forms of Kannon

---

### **Ryūzu Kannon (Dragon-Head Avalokiteśvara)**

[Manifests in the form of] heavenly dragons (*tennryū*) and yakṣas (*yasha*) [to save sentient beings].

*(Illustration)*

---

### **Jikyō Kannon (Sūtra-Holding Avalokiteśvara)**

[Manifests in the form of] a *śrāvaka* (voice-hearer/disciple) [to save sentient beings].

*(Illustration)*

---

### **Enkō Kannon (Halo-Light Avalokiteśvara)**

When correct contemplation (*shōkan*) arises, a natural resonance (*kan*) is felt; and for this reason, [Kannon] manifests within the light [of the halo].

*(Illustration)*

---

### **Yūke Kannon (Playful/Wandering Avalokiteśvara)**

[Kannon] descends (*datsurak*u) from Mount Kongō [to come among sentient beings].

*(Illustration)*
