## The Twenty-Five Bodhisattvas

### 【Upper Right】
**Kanzeon Bosatsu (Avalokiteśvara)**

In Sanskrit, this bodhisattva is called "Avalokiteśvara" (阿縛盧枳多伊濕伐羅), which is translated into Chinese as "Guanzizai" (観自在, "Sovereign Observer"). Another reading is "Varokitesei" (婆婁吉低稅), as referenced in the *Ōjō Yōshū* in connection with Amida Buddha.

After the Dharma has perished [in this world], Kanzeon will attain perfect enlightenment and will be called "Fukkō Kudoku San'nō Nyorai" (普光功徳山王如来, Tathāgata King of Mountains of Universal Radiance and Merit).

---

### 【Lower Right】
**Yakuō Bosatsu (Bhaiṣajyarāja)**

In Sanskrit, this bodhisattva is called "Akaun" (阿迦雲). This bodhisattva, wielding the arts of great compassion, freely relieves all sentient beings from the suffering of illness caused by karmic actions, and bestows upon them happiness. It is for this reason that he bears the name "King of Medicine" (Yakuō).

He also burned his own body as an offering to the Buddha. The flames burned for 1,200 years without being extinguished. (As recorded in the 16th volume of the *Daichidoron* [Mahāprajñāpāramitopadeśa])

---

### 【Upper Left】
**Daiseishi Bosatsu (Mahāsthāmaprāpta)**

In Sanskrit, this bodhisattva is called "Makanahatsu" (摩訶那鉢, Mahāsthāmaprāpta), translated as "Daiseishi" (Great Arrival of Power). The *Śūraṃgama Sūtra* states: "In my original causal ground, I entered the acceptance of non-arising through the mind of buddha-recollection. Now, in this world, I gather those who recite the Buddha's name and guide them back to the Pure Land."

The *Vimalakīrti Sūtra* refers to "[the bodhisattva who] attained great power" (得大勢至).

---

### 【Lower Left】
**Yakujō Bosatsu (Bhaiṣajyasamudgata)**

In the time of the past Buddha Rurikōshō (Vaiḍūryaprakāśa), there was a monk called Nichizō (Sun Treasury). He listened to the teachings of an elder named Seishukukō (Star-Constellation Light). As a result, he offered up various medicinal herbs including訶黎勒 (harītakī), and thus became known as "Yakuō" (Medicine King). He had a younger brother,

called Denkōmyō (Lightning Brilliance), who offered the supreme medicine of ghee (醍醐). This [younger brother] is therefore called "Yakujō" (Medicine Superior).
