【Top Frame, Horizontal Text】
The Seven Medicine Buddhas

【Upper Right】
■(1) Supriya-nāma-śrī-rāja Tathāgata (善名称吉祥王如来, Tathāgata of Auspicious and Glorious Name)
The Sūtra of the Meritorious Deeds of the Seven Buddhas states: "In the eastern direction, beyond four Ganges' sands' worth of Buddha-lands from here, there exists a world called 'Undefeatable' (無能勝). The Buddha there is called Supriya-nāma-śrī-rāja Tathāgata, and he made eight great vows."

【Lower Right】
■(2) Ratna-candra-vimala-ghoṣa-svara-rāja Tathāgata (宝月智厳音自在王如来, Tathāgata of Jewel-Moon Wisdom, Glorious Sound, and Sovereign)
In the eastern direction, beyond five Ganges' sands' worth of Buddha-lands, there is a world called "Wondrous Jewel Land" (妙宝国). The Buddha there is called Ratna-candra-vimala-ghoṣa-svara-rāja Tathāgata, and he made eight great vows.

【Upper Left】
■(3) Suvarṇa-ratna-prabhāsa-śrī-vikrīḍita-abhijña Tathāgata (金色宝光妙行成就王如来, Tathāgata of Golden Radiant Jewel Light and Perfected Wondrous Practice)
In the eastern direction, beyond six Ganges' sands' worth of Buddha-lands, there is a world called "Perfectly Complete Fragrant Accumulation Land" (円満香積国). The Buddha there is called Golden-Radiant Jewel Light Perfected Wondrous Practice King Tathāgata, and he made four great vows.

【Lower Left】
■(4) Aśoka-śrī-rāja Tathāgata (無憂最勝吉祥王如来, Tathāgata of Sorrowless Supreme Auspiciousness)
In the eastern direction, beyond seven Ganges' sands' worth of Buddha-lands, there is a world called "Sorrowless Land" (無憂国). The Buddha there is called Sorrowless Supreme Auspicious King Tathāgata, and he made four great vows.
