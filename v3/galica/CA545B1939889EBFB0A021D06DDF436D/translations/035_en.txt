【Header (written horizontally above the frame)】
The Seven Healing Buddhas (Shichi Butsu Yakushi)

---

【Upper Right】
■ The First: Tathāgata of Auspicious and Virtuous Renown (Zenmiōshō Kichijō-ō Nyorai / *Supratiṣṭhitaśrī*)

The *Sūtra of the Meritorious Deeds of the Seven Buddhas* (Shichi Butsu Kudoku-kyō) states: "To the east, beyond four times the number of buddha-lands equal to the sands of the Ganges from this world (Sahā), there exists a world called Unvanquishable (Munō-shō / *Anuttara*)."
That Buddha is titled: Tathāgata of Auspicious and Virtuous Renown.
He made eight great vows.

---

【Lower Right】
■ The Second: King of Sovereign Sound, Jewel-Moon Wisdom and Majesty (Hōgetsu Chigon Onjirai-ō / *Candraprabha Vaiḍūryaprabhāsa*)

To the east, beyond five times the Ganges-sands of buddha-lands, there is a world called the Wondrous Jewel Land (Myōhō-koku).
That Buddha is titled: Tathāgata, King of Sovereign Sound, Jewel-Moon Wisdom and Majesty.
He made eight great vows.

---

【Upper Left】
■ The Third: King of Perfect Accomplishment Through Golden-Hued Jewel-Radiance and Wondrous Practice (Konjiki Hōkō Myōgyō Jōju-ō)

To the east, beyond six times the Ganges-sands of buddha-lands, there is a world called the Land of Complete Fragrant Accumulation (Enman Kōshaku-koku).
That Buddha is titled: Tathāgata, King of Perfect Accomplishment Through Golden-Hued Jewel-Radiance and Wondrous Practice.
He made four great vows.

---

【Lower Left】
■ The Fourth: King of Supreme Sorrow-Free Auspiciousness (Muu Saishō Kichijō-ō)

To the east, beyond seven times the Ganges-sands of buddha-lands, there is a world called the Sorrow-Free Land (Muu-koku).
That Buddha is named: Tathāgata, King of Supreme Sorrow-Free Auspiciousness.
He made four great vows.
