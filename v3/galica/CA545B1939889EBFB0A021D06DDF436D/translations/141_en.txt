## The Zenkōji Nyorai (Amida Triad of Zenkōji)

The Ikkō Sanzon Nyorai (One-Halo Trinity) of Zenkōji was cast and fashioned using 3,700 ryō of Jambūnada gold by the elder Gatsukai (Moon World) of the kingdom of Bizhari in Central India (Madhya Pradesh region).

[illustrated]

In Japan, this image was brought to our land from King Seimei of Baekje in the thirteenth year of Emperor Kinmei's reign (the year of Mizunoe-Saru), making it an image transmitted through three countries.

## The Inaba Yakushi (Medicine Buddha of Inaba)

This is known as Inabadō (Byōdōji Temple). The Yakushi Nyorai (Bhaiṣajyaguru) was the principal image of the infirmary within the Jetavana Monastery in Central India. During the reign of Emperor Seiwa, the image appeared in a fishing net at Suruga-ura in Inaba Province.

[illustrated]

In the fifth year of Chōwa (the year of Hinoto-Tatsu), Middle Counselor Yukihira received the image through a sacred vow, enshrined it upon a go board, and built a hall for it — thus the place came to be known as Inabadō.

## The Saga Shaka Nyorai (Śākyamuni of Saga)

The monk Chōnen resided at Tōdaiji and studied the Sanron (Three Treatises) school. In the autumn of the first year of Eikan, he traveled to Song China, made pilgrimages to sacred sites, visited the Seizen'in, and made a replica of a five-shaku-tall image of Śākyamuni carved in red sandalwood, said to have been made by King Udayana. Upon his return journey, he received a divine message in a dream,

[illustrated]

and brought back the original Buddha image in secret, with no one aware of it. In the third year of Yōngxi (Yōki), he boarded the ship of Tei Jintoku of Taizhou, returned to Japan in the fifth year of Chōwa, and passed away at the age of sixty-seven.

## The Kōshin Image of Tennōji (Tōji Kōshin-zō)

On the seventh day of the first month of the Taihō era (the year of Kōshin), during the hour of Kōshin, a heavenly child appeared before the monk Minbu-sōzu of Shitennōji Temple in Settsu Province, carrying an image of Kōshin. The child declared: "I am a messenger of Taishakuten (Śakra/Indra). Every year, on each of the six

[illustrated]

Kōshin days, purify your body and mind, face south, and worship during the hour of Kōshin. You shall obtain the virtues of the three ages, be freed from all calamities, and receive good fortune." With that, the child ascended to heaven.
