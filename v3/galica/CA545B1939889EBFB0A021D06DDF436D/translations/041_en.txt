【Header (horizontal writing above frame)】
The Sixteen Great Bodhisattvas

【Upper Right】
■Vajrasattva

I take refuge (namu) in the bodhi-mind (mind of awakening) of all Tathāgatas. Vajrasattva and the others, pervading all of empty space and throughout the dharma-realm, in their single unified essential nature, born from the Vajradhātu—[I pay homage to] all Buddhas thus arisen.
【Lower left of figure】
The Four Honored Ones of the East

【Lower Right】
■Vajrarāja

I take refuge in the bodhi-mind of all Tathāgatas. Vajrarāja and the others, pervading all of empty space and throughout the dharma-realm, in their single unified essential nature, born from the Vajradhātu—[I pay homage to] all Buddhas thus arisen.

【Upper Left】
■Vajrarāga

I humbly take refuge in the bodhi-mind of all Tathāgatas. [I pay homage to] Vajrarāga and all the Buddhas of that [company].
The Shusshō-gi (Meaning of Arising) says: "From the pure and undefiled body of the bodhi[-mind] of all Tathāgatas, Vajrarāga is born and manifested."

【Lower Left】
■Vajrasādhu

I humbly take refuge in the bodhi-mind of all Tathāgatas. [I pay homage to] Vajrasādhu and all the Buddhas of that [company].
The Shusshō-gi says: "From the body of the praise [offered] in response to all Tathāgatas, Vajrasādhu ('Diamond Excellent!' / Diamond of Rejoicing) is born and manifested."
