【Header above frame (horizontal writing)】
The Sixteen Great Bodhisattvas

---

【Upper Right】
■Vajrasattva (Kongō Satta)

Homage (namu) to all the bodhicitta of the Tathāgatas — Vajrasattva and all such [bodhisattvas], who pervade all of empty space and the entire dharmadhātu, who are of one and the same essential nature, and who are born from the Vajradhātu [Diamond Realm], [and to] all the buddhas of [that] body.
[Drawing included]
[Note in lower left of drawing: The Four Honored Ones of the East]

---

【Lower Right】
■Vajrarāja (Kongō-ō)

Homage (namu) to all the bodhicitta of the Tathāgatas — Vajrarāja and all such [bodhisattvas], who pervade all of empty space and the entire dharmadhātu, who are of one and the same essential nature, [who are] born from the Vajradhātu, [and to] all the buddhas.
[Drawing included]

---

【Upper Left】
■Vajrarāga (Kongō Ai)

Homage (namu) to the bodhicitta of all the Tathāgatas — Vajrarāga and all such buddhas.
[Drawing included]
The *Shussho-gi* (*Chūshutsu shūgi* / Treatise on the Meaning of Arising) states: "From the unstained pure body (*amalavijñāna*) of the bodhicitta of all the Tathāgatas, Vajrarāga is brought forth."

---

【Lower Left】
■Vajrasādhu (Kongō Ki)

Homage (namu) to the bodhicitta of all the Tathāgatas — Vajrasādhu and all such buddhas.
[Drawing included]
The *Shussho-gi* states: "From the body of the universal praise (*zuishō-shō*) of all the Tathāgatas, Vajrasādhu ('Diamond Well-Done!') is brought forth."
