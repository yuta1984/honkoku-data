Preface to the Second Volume of Tokyo Commerce and Industry Exhibition Illustrations

The prosperity of the Eastern Capital (Tokyo) is unparalleled within the country. Jimon Seiken captured it in prose, and Hasegawa Settan depicted it in illustrations, leaving no detail unrecorded. However, as times have changed, the character of the city differs between past and present. The waters of the Sumida River flow on unchanged, yet this is no longer the Edo of old. Great establishments have perished without a trace, while smaller ones have risen to prominence, and those that existed in former days have become rare indeed.
