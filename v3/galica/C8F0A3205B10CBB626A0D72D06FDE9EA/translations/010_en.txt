...even those who erect grand buildings and towers to conduct their business cannot fulfill their ambitions when their social connections are narrow and their credit is thin. Mr. Fukamachi, moved by this realization, published the first volume of the Tokyo Commerce and Industry Exhibition Illustrations some time ago, and it met with considerable acclaim from the business community. Now, upon publishing this second volume and making it available to the public, he has asked me to write a preface. As I have been engaged for several years in the editing of the Yomiuri Shimbun, and since newspapers too belong to the realm of commerce, I found myself unable to decline.
