In the second month of the eleventh year (of Eiroku, 1568), Samanosuke Minamoto no Yoshihide was appointed Sei'i Taishōgun (Shogun). In the fifth month, Uesugi Kenshin made peace with Hōjō Ujiyasu, and took Ujiyasu's youngest son, Saburō, as his adopted heir. In the seventh month, Yoshiaki, from Echizen, dispatched Hosokawa Hyōbu no Taifu Fujitaka and Ueno Nakatsukasa no Taifu Kiyonobu as envoys to Gifu in Mino province, requesting that Oda Nobunaga lead the campaign to destroy the Miyoshi. Nobunaga consented, and, accompanying Yoshiaki with Fuwa Kawachi no Kami and Azai Bizen no Kami Nagamasa as escort, went forth to receive him. Yoshiaki thereupon proceeded to Gifu. In the eighth month, Nobunaga advanced to Ōmi province (Gōshū) and, through envoys, discussed the matter of pursuing and destroying the Miyoshi with Sasaki Jōtei. Jōtei did not consent. In the ninth month, Nobunaga led his troops and attacked and captured Mitsukuri Castle, Wada Castle, and other fortresses within Jōtei's domain. Jōtei and his son Yoshimasu abandoned Kannonji Castle and fled into obscurity. All the castles throughout Ōmi province fell. Yoshiaki traveled from Gifu to Moriyama in Ōmi province.
