(Yoshinori commanded Norizane to attack Yūki.) Norizane declined. Kiyokata, acting as Yamauchi's representative (nadai), set out from Kamakura together with Ōgigayatsu-Uesugi Shuri-no-tayū Mochitomo and advanced toward Yūki. From Kyoto, Uesugi Nakatsukasa-no-shōyū Mochifusa descended to the east, having been bestowed with the (shogunal) battle standard. Norizane, also taking the commands of Yoshinori seriously, advanced from Izu as far as Oyama in Shimotsuke Province. Kiyokata and Mochitomo led the Kantō forces, while Mochifusa led the Kyoto forces. From the seventh month onward, they besieged the castle of Yūki and fought. Popularly, this is known as the "Battle of Yūki" (Yūki no Senjō).

In the tenth month,教基 (Noriki), son of Konoe Left Minister (Sadaijin) Fusatsugu, came of age (genpuku). Yoshinori performed the cap-crowning ceremony (kakan) and bestowed upon him a character from his own personal name.

In the second month of the first year of Kakitsu (1441), Hatakeyama Owari-no-kami Mochikuni was appointed to Junior Third Rank (Jushii). It is customary for all three (Kanrei) families to hold Fourth Rank as their established precedent, but Mochikuni alone was promoted to Third Rank. It is said that the appointment of Shiba Yoshishige to Junior Third Rank appears in genealogical records, but it does not appear in the Kugyō Bunin.
