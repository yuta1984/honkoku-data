In the seventh month of Enbun 1 (1356), Koga no Dainagon Minamoto no Michisuke was appointed Inner Minister (Naidaijin).
In the eighth month, Yoshiakira was conferred Junior Third Rank (Jushii).

In the second month of Enbun 2 (1357), Retired Emperor Kōgon, the Kōmyō-in, and the Sūkō-in were pardoned and returned to the capital from the remote mountains of Yoshino.

In the second month of Enbun 3 (1358), the Junior Second Rank (Jūnii) was posthumously conferred upon Tadayoshi. On the twenty-ninth day of the fourth month, Minamoto no Takauji, Shogun (Sei-i Taishōgun), Senior Second Rank (Shōnii), and Dainagon, passed away at the age of fifty-four. He was posthumously awarded the rank of Senior First Rank (Jūichii) and the position of Minister of the Left (Sadaijin). He was given the Buddhist posthumous title Tōji-in-dono Jinzan. In Kamakura, he was referred to as Chōjuji-dono. From Kenmu 3 (1336) to Enbun 3 (1358), his reign lasted twenty-three years. Counselor and Middle Captain (Saisho Chūjō) Yoshiakira succeeded him as military commander (Shogun).
