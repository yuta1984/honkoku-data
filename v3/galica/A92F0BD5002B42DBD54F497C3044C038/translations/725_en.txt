In the twelfth year [of Eishō, 1515], fourth month, Kanesuke was transferred to the position of Safu [Minister of the Left]. Sanjō Naifu [Minister of the Center] Sanehika was appointed Ufu [Minister of the Right]. The Dainagon [Major Counselor] of the Ōgimachi-Sanjō family, Sanemochi, was appointed Naifu [Minister of the Center]. This person was also a collateral branch of the Sanjō family. In the twelfth month, Sanemochi resigned from the position of Naifu. Nijō Right General Tadafusa was appointed Naifu.

In the thirteenth year [1516], fourth month, the former Kanpaku of the Kujō family, Masamoto, passed away. He was seventy-two years of age. In the same month, the former Naifu of the Nishisanjō family, Sanetaka, shaved his head [took Buddhist vows] and took the religious name Gyōkū. He became known by the sobriquet Shōyōin. He was sixty-three years of age at the time. In the seventh month, Hōjō Sōun attacked and captured the castle of the Miura clan. Miura Dōsun was slain in battle. Sōun's power resounded throughout the Kantō region, and the Uesugi clan gradually fell into decline. In the twelfth month, Konoe Hisatō [Hisamichi] resigned from the position of Daijō-daijin.

In the fourteenth year [1517], on New Year's Day, the Kochōhai [New Year's Court Ceremony] was performed as customary. The Kanpaku Takatsukasa Kanesuke was unable to attend due to an indisposition, and Sanjō Ufu [Minister of the Right] Sanehika presided as the senior official and conducted the proceedings. In the tenth month, the former Kanpaku and Shōkoku [Chancellor] of the Takatsukasa family, Masahira, passed away. He was seventy-three years of age. He was the father of the current Kanpaku, Kanesuke.
