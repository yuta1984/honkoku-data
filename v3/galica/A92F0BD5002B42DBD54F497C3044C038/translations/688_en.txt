In the eighth month, Tōin Udaijin (Minister of the Right) Sanehiro was appointed to the position of Sadaijin (Minister of the Left). Ichijō Naidaijin (Inner Minister) Norifusa was appointed Udaijin (Minister of the Right). Konoe Dainagon (Major Counselor) Motomoto was appointed Naidaijin (Inner Minister). Yoshimasa concurrently assumed the post of Udaishō (Commander of the Right Gate Guards).

In the first month of Kōshō 2 (1456), Yoshimasa became the superintendent (Gokan) of the Bureau of Horses (Maryō). In the third month, Yoshimasa made a pilgrimage to Iwashimizu (Hachimangū Shrine). Court nobles (kugyō) and senior courtiers (tenjōbito) performed their attendant duties. In the eighth month, the Retired Emperor (Dōkin-in) passed away at the age of eighty-five. He was posthumously titled Gosūkō-in. In the same year, Yoshimasa sent a letter to the King of Joseon (Korea).

In the second month of Chōroku 1 (1457), the posthumous titles of Sadaijin (Minister of the Left) and Juichii (Junior First Rank) were conferred upon Chōtoku-in Yoshitoyo. In the fourth month, Tōin Sanehiro resigned from his position as Minister of the Left and took the tonsure. He took the name "Higashiyama no Safu" (Minister of the Left of Higashiyama). In the sixth month, Ichijō Udaijin Norifusa was transferred to the position of Sadaijin. Former Naidaijin Sanjō Kazushige (Sanekazu) was appointed Udaijin. In the eighth month, Saionji Kinna resigned from the position of Shōkoku (Daijō-daijin, Grand Minister of State). In the ninth month, Sanekazu resigned from the position of Udaijin. Konoe Naidaijin Motomoto was appointed Udaijin. Ōgimachisanjō Sanemasa was appointed Naidaijin.
