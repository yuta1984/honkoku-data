In the fourth month, Harumoto led the forces of Shikoku, encamped at Higashiyama, set fire to the area around Kitashirakawa, and withdrew to Settsu Province. In the seventh month, Harumoto entered the capital and set up camp at Shōkokuji temple. Sasaki Danjō Sadayori, being Harumoto's father-in-law, mustered the soldiers of Ōmi Province and surrounded the castle at Kitashirakawa. Yoshiharu and Yoshiteru set fire to the castle and made their way to Sakamoto. Harumoto and Sadayori were both pardoned for their offenses and went to Sakamoto to offer their apologies.

In the sixth month of Tenbun 17 [1548], Yoshiharu and Yoshiteru returned to the capital. Harumoto remained as Kanrei [Deputy Shogun]. In the twelfth month, Ichijō Fusамichi resigned as Kampaku [Imperial Regent]. Nijō Sadaijin [Minister of the Left] Haruyoshi became Kampaku. In this same year, Ōuchi Yoshitaka was granted the court rank of Junior Second Rank (Jūnii).

In the third month of Tenbun 18 [1549], a dispute arose in Settsu Province between Miyoshi Chikuzen-no-Kami Nagayoshi [Chōkei] and Miyoshi Sōzan. As Hosokawa Harumoto favored Sōzan exclusively, Nagayoshi grew furious and attacked and destroyed the castle at Nakajima where Sōzan resided. Sōzan retreated into Enami Castle.
