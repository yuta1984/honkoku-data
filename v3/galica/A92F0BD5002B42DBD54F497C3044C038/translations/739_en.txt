Perceiving that the chief retainer Suō Harukata harbored treasonous intentions and that a rupture with Yoshitaka was likely, [Motonari] returned to Yoshida Castle in Aki Province and waited, watching for the change [that was to come].

In the eighth month [of Tenbun 11, 1542], Imagawa Yoshimoto, lord of Suruga Province, having subjugated Tōtōmi Province, advanced into Mikawa, and there he battled Oda Danshō-no-jō Nobuhide of Owari Province at Azukizaka, where Yoshimoto was defeated.

On the twenty-sixth day of the twelfth month [of Tenbun 11, 1542], Lord Minamoto Ieyasu was born in Okazaki in Mikawa Province. He was the twenty-sixth-generation descendant of Emperor Seiwa, the legitimate grandson of Hachimantarō Yoshiie, and the son of Chinjufu Shōgun [posthumously granted] Nitta Ōi-no-suke Yoshishige. He was the sixteenth generation from Tokugawa Yoshisue, and the son of the posthumously granted Dainagon, Lord Hirotada. His mother was known as Dentsu'in-dono, the daughter of Mizuno Uemon-no-Tayū Tadamasa.

In the seventh month of the twelfth year [of Tenbun, 1543], Nishisanjō Kinjō resigned as Minister of the Right [Udaijin]. Sanjō Naidaijin Kinyori was transferred to Minister of the Right, and Imadegawa Dainagon Kimihiko was appointed Naidaijin [Minister of the Interior].
