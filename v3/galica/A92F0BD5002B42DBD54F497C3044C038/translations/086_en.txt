...(The Emperor was greatly shocked) and issued a decree to Prince Naka no Ōe, saying, "What is the meaning of this?" Naka no Ōe prostrated himself and reported, "Iruka intends to destroy the various imperial princes and overthrow the imperial throne. How could the heavenly throne be exchanged for [the rule of] Iruka?" The Emperor thereupon rose and withdrew into the inner chambers. Komaro and Tsugita finally struck down and killed Iruka. That day, rain fell and puddles of water filled the courtyard. Iruka's corpse was covered with mats and screens. From that point, Naka no Ōe established a fortification at Hōkō-ji Temple. All the imperial princes followed [him]. There were also those who went over to Emishi's side. Naka no Ōe had someone deliver Iruka's corpse to Emishi. He also appointed Kose no Toku as commander to attack Emishi, and [Kose] announced to Emishi's followers: "Throughout all ages, who would not know the proper way between lord and vassal? Why do you follow a treacherous vassal?" At this, all of Emishi's followers fled and dispersed.
