In the eleventh month of the third year of Meiō [1494], Yoshimichi was elevated to Senior Fifth Rank Lower Grade [Shōgo-i-ge] and appointed Head of the Left Stables [Sama no Kami], and changed his name to Yoshitaka. In the twelfth month, Yoshitaka was appointed Sei'i Taishōgun [Shogun]. He was sixteen years of age at the time.

In the same month, Ise no Shinkyūrō borrowed troops from the Imagawa of Suruga Province, crossed from Izu into Sagami Province, captured Odawara Castle, and took up residence there. Shinkyūrō shaved his head and styled himself Hōjō Sōun. From this point, his power gradually grew. His son was called Ujitsuna. Father and son both possessed military valor, and eventually went to battle against the Uesugi, contending for dominance over the Kantō region.

In the second month of the fifth year of Meiō [1496], Akamatsu Masanori was elevated to Junior Third Rank [Jushō-i], and on the twenty-fifth day of the fourth month he passed away. He was forty-two years of age. It is said that he was elevated to the Third Rank [Sanbon] for the achievement of slaying the Southern Emperor and seizing the Sacred Regalia [Shinji]. In the eleventh month, Kazan-in Masanaga resigned as Minister of the Left [Sadaijin]. In the twelfth month, Regent [Kanpaku] Konoe Hisamichi transferred to the position of Minister of the Left, and former Minister of the Center [Naidaijin] Imadegawa Kinoki was appointed Minister of the Right [Udaijin].
