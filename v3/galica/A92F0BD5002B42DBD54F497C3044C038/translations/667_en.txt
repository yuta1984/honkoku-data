From this point on, discord arose between the lord (Mochiuji) and his subordinates (particularly Norizane), and a reconciliation could not be reached. In the eighth month, Mochiuji assembled troops and attempted to attack Yamauchi (the base of the Uesugi clan). Norizane was about to commit suicide, but was restrained by his retainers; he departed from Yamauchi, made his way to Kōzuke Province, and reported the situation to Kyoto. Mochiuji immediately dispatched Isshiki Naokanе and Isshiki Tokiie to attack Norizane, and Mochiuji himself departed Kamakura and encamped at Kōanji in Musashi Province. Miura-no-suke Tokitaka was appointed to guard Kamakura in Mochiuji's absence.

In the tenth month, a directive (migyōsho) from Yoshinori was sent from Kyoto along with an imperial edict (rinjи), ordering Norizane to carry out the subjugation of Mochiuji. The relevant instructions were also circulated to Miura-no-suke and the warriors of the Kantō region. Furthermore, a battle standard was granted to Uesugi Nakatsukasa-no-shōyū Mochifusa, and he was instructed to mobilize forces along the Kaidō (Tōkaidō) and attack Kamakura. Imagawa Kazusa-no-suke Noritada, along with Ogasawara Masakane, Takeda Nobushige, and others, advanced on the campaign. Uesugi Jibu-no-tayū Noritomo advanced from the Hokuriku Road.
