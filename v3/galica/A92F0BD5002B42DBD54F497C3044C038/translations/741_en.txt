[Continued from previous page] There were many who depended on the hospitality of warriors. Even those who happened to remain in the capital were so lacking in their daily provisions that some were said to be begging for food at people's homes.

In the first month of Tenbun 15 [1546], Ichijō Kampaku Fusatō resigned as Minister of the Left (Sadaijin). Sanjō Kinyori was appointed Minister of the Left. In the third month, Kinyori resigned, and Imadegawa Kimihiko was appointed Minister of the Left. Nijō Haruyoshi was transferred to the position of Minister of the Right (Udaijin). Madenokōji Dainagon Fujiwara no Hidefusa was appointed Naidaijin [Minister of the Interior].

In the seventh month, Hidefusa resigned, and Ichijō Dainagon Kanetsuyu was appointed Naidaijin.

In the same month, Yoshifuyu [Yoshiteru], son of Shōgun Yoshiharu, aged eleven, was granted the court rank of Junior Fifth Rank, Lower Grade (Jūgoi-no-ge).

On the eighteenth day of the twelfth month, Yoshiharu took Yoshifuyu with him to Sakamoto. On the nineteenth day, the coming-of-age ceremony (genpuku) of Yoshifuyu was held at the residence of Kinoshita, the chief priest (kannushi) of the Hiyoshi Shrine. In accordance with the custom of the military government's Kanrei [Deputy Shōgun], Sasaki Rokkaku Danjō-no-Shōhitsu Sadayori was granted the Fourth Rank and, serving as acting Kanrei (Kanrei-dai), performed the role of placing the cap (kakan) upon the initiate. The role of hairdressing (rihatsuyaku) was performed by Hosokawa Nakatsukasa-no-Taifu Harutsune.
