Era names: Kōtoku 3rd year, Kōshō 2nd year, Chōroku 3rd year, Kanshō 5th year
Total: thirty-six years

The 104th Sovereign
Go-Tsuchimikado-in: Personal name (imina), Narinobu (Fushimi-no-miya). Son of Go-Hanazono-in. His mother was Karaku-mon'in, daughter of Ōinomikado no Naidaijin (Minister of the Center), Fujiwara no Nobumune. In the seventh month of Kanshō 5th year, he received the abdicated throne. He was twenty-three years of age at the time. The Kampaku (Imperial Regent) was Nijō no Daijō-daijin (Grand Minister of State), Mochimichi. Go-Hanazono [In] was designated the In no Gosho (Retired Emperor's Palace). The Shōgun, Minamoto no Yoshimasa, became the In no Shitsuji (Administrative Officer of the Retired Emperor's household). In the eighth month, Hatakeyama Masanaga was appointed Kanrei (Deputy Shōgun). In the ninth month, Tokudaiji Kinyū resigned from the office of Ufū (Minister of the Right). In the eleventh month, the former Naidaijin, Minamoto no Michihisa, was appointed Ufū. In the same month, Yoshimasa was granted the rank of Jun-Sangū (Honorary rank equivalent to that of the Three Empresses). Yoshimasa had already governed for many years; now thirty years old, he still had no male heir. Therefore, he had his younger brother, Jōdoji-no-monzeki Yoshihiro (Gijin), return to secular life, changed his name to Yoshimi, appointed him to the rank of Jugoi-no-ge (Junior Fifth Rank, Lower Grade) and the post of Samanokamī (Head of the Left Stables), gave him the title of Imadegawa-dono, and intended to eventually cede rule of the realm to him.
