Akaeni falsely agreed, and then immediately attacked the Prince's residence, captured him, and sent him to Kishū (Kii Province). The Crown Prince directly interrogated him, and as the treasonous plot became clear, Prince Arima was strangled to death at Fujishiro Slope. He was nineteen years of age at the time. His associates were either executed or sentenced to exile. It is also said that Prince Arima tied together the branches of a pine tree at Iwashiro in Kishū, composed a poem, and died by hanging himself. [Note: based on an alternate manuscript] In the same year, Abe no Hirafu was appointed commander and attacked the land of the Mishihase (Sushen), obtaining two live bears and seventy bear hides. The land of the Mishihase is a country to the north and lies within the territory of the Tartars (Dattan). Hirafu also led a naval force, pacified the Emishi, and returned. Since the eastern expedition of Yamato Takeru no Mikoto, the Emishi had sometimes submitted to imperial rule and sometimes rebelled. This time, Hirafu achieved a great victory, established a administrative office (mandokoro), and returned.
