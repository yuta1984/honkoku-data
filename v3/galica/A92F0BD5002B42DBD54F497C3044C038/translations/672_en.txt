On the sixteenth day of the fourth month, Uesugi Kiyokata and his forces advanced their armies, attacked the castle of Yūki, set it ablaze, and the castle was finally taken. The castle of Koga also fell. Yūki Ujitomo and his son died in battle, and those who were killed numbered as many as ten thousand. Haruo and Yasuo, disguised as women and placed in a palanquin, attempted to flee the castle, but Nagao Inaba-no-kami captured them alive. Inaba-no-kami was immediately assigned as their escort and made to bring them up to the capital. Twenty-nine heads, including that of Ujitomo and the other ringleaders, were sent to Kyoto.

In the fifth month, when Haruo and Yasuo arrived at Tarui in Mino Province, a certain Sasaki was dispatched from Kyoto as an inspector (kenshi) and ordered Inaba-no-kami to kill both of them. Haruo was at that time thirteen years old, and Yasuo was eleven years old. Yasuo's younger brother, Eijuō, secretly fled to Shinano Province and hid himself under the protection of a man called Ōi Mochimitsu. Yūki Ujitomo's youngest son, Naritomo, also fled to Hitachi Province. Norizane once again left Kamakura...
