In the first year of Kōji (1555), Mōri Motonari of Aki Province and Sue Zenkyo (Harukata) of Suō Province engaged in battle year after year. In the eleventh month of this year, Motonari suddenly launched an attack on Zenkyo and emerged victorious. Zenkyo committed suicide. Ōtomo Yoshinaga also committed suicide in Nagato Province. Motonari thus succeeded in pacifying Nagato and Suō. His eldest son Takamoto, his second son Kikkawa Motoharu, his third son Hoda Motokiyo, and his fourth son Kobayakawa Takakage were all accomplished in military affairs. From there, Motonari advanced into Bitchū Province. As Ukita Naoie of Bizen Province sought peace, Motonari withdrew his forces, leaving Takamoto in Suō to hold back the Ōtomo of Bungo, while Motonari, along with Motoharu and Takakage, advanced into Izumo, attacked Toda Castle, engaged in battle with Amago Haruhisa, and the months and years passed.
