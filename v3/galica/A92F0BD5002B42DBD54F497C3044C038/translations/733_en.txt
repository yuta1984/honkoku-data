In the second year [of Kyōroku, 1529], Yoshiharu continued to stay in Kutsuki.

In the first month of the third year [of Kyōroku, 1530], an imperial envoy, the Daigeki [Senior Secretary of the Grand Council] Kiyohara no Yoshio, proceeded to Kutsuki. Yoshiharu was appointed Major Counselor [Dainagon] and was granted the rank of Junior Third Rank [Jū Sanmi]. He was twenty years of age at this time. In the seventh month, the former kampaku of the Kujō clan, Hisatsune, passed away. He was sixty-three years of age.

In the sixth month of the fourth year [of Kyōroku, 1531], Miyoshi Kaiun and his allies installed Harumoто, the thirteen-year-old son of the late Hosokawa Sumimoto, as their commander, and engaged Hosokawa Takakuni in battle in the area of Amagasaki and Tennōji. Takakuni suffered a great defeat and fled. The Miyoshi forces pursued him, discovered him hiding inside a large jar in a commoner's house, and killed him. Among Takakuni's partisans was a man called Shimamura [something], a warrior of great valor.
