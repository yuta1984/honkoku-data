[Continued from Entoku 2] From the third year of Kakitsu [1443] until this year, his reign lasted forty-nine years. In the third month, Kazan-in Masanaga resigned as Minister of the Right [Udaijin]. Konoe Major Counselor [Dainagon] Hisamichi was appointed Minister of the Right. In the fourth month, Konoe Masaie resigned as Grand Minister of State [Daijō-daijin]. In the seventh month, Yoshimura was elevated to Junior Fourth Rank Lower Grade [Jushii-ge], appointed as Consultant Middle Captain [Saisho Chūjō], and assigned as Seii Taishōgun. He was twenty-five years old.

On the seventh day of the first month of the third year of Entoku [1491], the lay monk Major Counselor [Nyūdō Dainagon] Minamoto no Yoshimi passed away. He was fifty-three years old. He was posthumously titled Daichi-in. He was posthumously awarded the rank of Grand Minister of State [Daijō-daijin] and Junior First Rank. In the third month, Kimimoki resigned as Minister of the Center [Naidaijin]. Nijō Major Counselor [Dainagon] Naomoto was appointed Minister of the Center. In the fourth month, Jishō-in's younger brother, Junior Third Rank, Captain of the Left Gate Guards [Sahyōe no Kami], Minamoto no Masatomo, passed away in Izu Province. He was fifty-seven years old. He was posthumously titled Shōdō-in. It is said that Masatomo's son Yoshimichi had an agreement to be adopted by his uncle Jishō-in.

In the eighth month of the first year of Meiō [1492], Yoshimura led his forces to attack Rokkaku Takayori of Ōmi Province, encamping at Miidera. Takayori hid in the mountains of Kōka. Yoshimura returned to the capital.
