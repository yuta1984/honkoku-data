In the eighth month, the sacred imperial seal (shinshi) was returned from the Southern region to the imperial palace. This came about because a retainer of the Akamatsu clan, a man called Iwami Tarōzaemon, had become a rōnin (masterless samurai) and entered the service of Sanjō Udaijin (Minister of the Right) Sanekazu, all while lamenting the extinction of the Akamatsu clan. Sanekazu said: "Could there not be some service rendered that might atone for the great treachery of Kakitsu?" Iwami replied: "If I were to go south, assassinate their king, and recover and return the sacred seal—what would come of that?" Sanekazu reported this to the Emperor and also informed the military government (bakufu), and the plan was approved. Rejoicing, Iwami sent Mashima, a member of the Akamatsu clan, and Nakamura, a retainer, to serve at the Southern Court. Growing close to those there and waiting for an opportune moment, they assassinated the Southern Emperor, seized the sacred seal, and returned. The villagers of Yoshino gave chase, and Nakamura was killed in the fighting. Mashima carried the sacred seal back to the capital and presented it to the imperial palace. The Southern Court was completely destroyed at this time.
