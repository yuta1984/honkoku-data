In the eighth month of the second year of Chōkyō [1488], Ichijō Minister of the Center Fuyuyoshi became Regent [Kanpaku]. In the ninth month, former Regent Kujō Masatada passed away. He was fifty years old. In the same month, former Regent Konoe Masaie was appointed Grand Minister of State [Daijō-daijin]. In the same month, Yoshihisa was appointed Minister of the Center [Naidaijin]. Yoshihisa also changed his name, taking the name Yoshihiro [義凞].

In the first year of Entoku [1489], on the twenty-sixth day of the third month, Seii Taishōgun, Junior First Rank, Minister of the Center, Minamoto no Yoshihiro passed away in camp at the village of Magari in Ōmi Province. He was twenty-five years old. He was posthumously titled Jōtoku-in. He was posthumously awarded the rank of Grand Minister of State [Daijō-daijin]. From Bunmei 5 [1473] until this year, he had served in office for seventeen years. His father Yoshimasa grieved deeply and, further lamenting the absence of an heir, made peace with Yoshimi.

In the fourth month, Yoshimi came up to the capital from Mino Province and took holy orders [shukke]. Yoshimi's son Yoshimura was adopted by Yoshimasa as his son. Prior to this, Yoshimura had already held the rank of Junior Fifth Rank Lower Grade and the office of Head of the Left Stables [Samaranokami].

In the seventh month, Imadegawa Major Counselor [Dainagon] Fujiwara no Kimimoki was appointed Minister of the Center [Naidaijin].
