In the seventeenth year [of Bunmei, 1485], in the third month, Regent [Kanpaku] Takatsukasa Masahira was appointed Grand Minister of State [Daijō-daijin]. Tokudaiji Saneatsu resigned as Minister of the Center [Naidaijin]. Nakanoin Dainagon [Great Counselor] Minamoto no Michisue was appointed Minister of the Center, but resigned after three days. Kazanin Dainagon Masanaga was appointed Minister of the Center. In the fourth month, Masahira resigned as Grand Minister of State. In the sixth month, Yoshimasa shaved his head and entered Buddhist orders, taking the name Kizen [Kiyama]. His Buddhist name was Dōtei, which he later changed to Dōkei. In the eighth month, Yoshihisa concurrently assumed the post of Commander of the Right [U-daishō]. In the same year, Furukawa's Shigeuji requested a peace settlement, and Yoshimasa and Yoshihisa granted their approval.

In the eighteenth year [of Bunmei, 1486], in the first month, Yoshihisa became Director [Goken] of the Bureau of Horses [Maryō]. In the seventh month, Hosokawa Ukyō-no-daibu Masamoto was appointed Deputy [Kanrei]. He was the son of Katsumoto. In the same month, Yoshihisa presented himself at court [sankei] for the celebration of his appointment as Commander [hanga]. The ceremony was conducted with great solemnity, based on careful consideration of precedents. Higashiyama-dono Yoshimasa set up a viewing stand [sajiki] to observe the proceedings. In the twelfth month, Kazanin Masanaga resigned as Minister of the Center. Ichijō Dainagon Fuyuyoshi was appointed Minister of the Center. Fuyuyoshi was the second son of Kaneyoshi.
