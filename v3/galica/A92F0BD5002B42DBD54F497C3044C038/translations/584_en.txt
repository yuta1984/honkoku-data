There was a battle at Musashino. During this battle, the lay monk Ishidō secretly communicated with the Nitta forces and plotted to strike down Takauji. His son, the Umanosuké, did not agree with this plan. Therefore, Ishidō, fearing the consequences, joined the Nitta side. Yoshioki and Yoshiharu attacked and entered Kamakura, pressing their assault against Motouji, who then fled the city. After this, Takauji and Yoshimune fought a battle at Usui Pass (Fuefuki Tōge), where Yoshimune was defeated and withdrew to Echigo Province. When Takauji then attacked Kamakura again, Yoshioki and Yoshiharu retreated and took refuge in the castle of Kawamura. In the third month, Yoshiakira mustered his forces and marched up to the capital, attacking Yawata in repeated battles. In the fifth month, the Southern Court's imperial forces were defeated, and the Southern Emperor fled Yawata and returned to Yoshino.
