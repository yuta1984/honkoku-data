[Nijō Masa]tsugu resigned from the post of Minister of the Left [Sadaijin]. In the third month, Kujō Motonori was transferred to the post of Minister of the Left, and Takatsukasa Masahira was appointed Minister of the Right [Udaijin]. The Major Counsellor [Dainagon] Konoe Masaie was appointed Minister of the Center [Naidaijin].

In the fourth month, Yoshihisa was appointed to the Senior Fourth Rank, Lower Grade. In the eighth month, Yoshimasa dispatched an envoy ship to Great Ming. Around this time, all envoys sent to Great Ming were Zen monks. In the ninth month, Yoshihisa was appointed to the Council of State [Sangi], while retaining his post as Middle Captain [Chūjō] as before. In the tenth month, Yoshihisa performed the ceremonial first reading [yomihajime]. This person was fond of learning, and also devoted himself to the composition of waka poetry and the martial arts of archery and horsemanship.

In the eighth year [of Bunmei, 1476], in the first month, Yoshihisa was appointed to the Junior Third Rank. In the fifth month, Nijō Masatsugu resigned from the post of Regent [Kanpaku]. Kujō Motonori was appointed Regent, whereupon he resigned from the post of Minister of the Left. Hino, the former Minister of the Center, Katsumitsu, was appointed Minister of the Left. Katsumitsu was the elder brother of Tomiko, the mother of Yoshihisa. It was for this reason that he rose to prominence despite not being of a Seiga house. In the sixth month, Katsumitsu resigned from the post of Minister of the Left. In the eighth month, Takatsukasa Masahira was appointed Minister of the Left, Konoe Masaie was transferred to the post of Minister of the Right, and the Major Counsellor of Sanjō, Fujiwara no Kimiatsushi, was appointed Minister of the Center.
