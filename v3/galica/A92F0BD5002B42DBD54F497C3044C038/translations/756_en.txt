Since Motonari had by now grown old, he had Kikkawa Motoharu and Kobayakawa Takakage lead the forces into battle in his stead. Naoie was originally a retainer of the Akamatsu clan. A certain retainer of the Akamatsu named Uranami (Uragami) expelled the Akamatsu and seized Harima, Mimasaka, and Bizen. Naoie then in turn overthrew the Uragami and is said to have taken possession of those territories.

In the seventh year (of Eiroku, 1564), Oda Nobunaga attacked and seized Mino province, subdued the kinsmen of Saitō Tatsuoki, and relocated from Kiyosu in Owari province to Gifu Castle in Mino province.

In the fifth month of the eighth year (of Eiroku, 1565), it was rumored that the shogun, Minamoto no Yoshiteru, angered by the growing insolence and lawless power of the Miyoshi and Matsunaga clans as his own military authority declined, had been secretly deliberating plans to have them punished. On the nineteenth day of that same month, Miyoshi Sakyō-no-daibu Yoshitsugu, together with Matsunaga Danjō's son, Uemon-no-suke Hisamichi, and others, led their troops to surround the shogunal palace. Those guarding the palace fought and died. Yoshiteru himself came out to resist and fight, but as his strength was exhausted, he set fire to the palace, burning the compound, and perished.
