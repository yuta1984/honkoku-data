(The Miyoshi finally seized control of) the capital, the Kinai, and the Nankai regions, and took up residence in Settsu. Matsunaga Danjō Hisahide, a retainer of the Miyoshi, resided in the capital, and his power and influence rivaled that of the Miyoshi. In the twelfth month, Nijō Kanpaku Haruyoshi resigned from the position of Sadaijin (Minister of the Left).

In the first month of the twenty-second year (of Tenbun, 1553), Nijō Haruyoshi resigned from the position of Kanpaku (Imperial Regent). Ichijō Udaijin (Minister of the Right) Kanetomo became Kanpaku and was appointed Sadaijin. Konoe Naidaijin (Minister of the Center) Harutsugu was appointed Udaijin, and Saionji Dainagon (Major Counselor) Kimitomo was appointed Naidaijin. In the seventh month, the Shogun Minamoto no Yoshifuji pardoned Hosokawa Harumoto and allowed him to return to the capital. In the eighth month, Miyoshi Chikuzen no Kami Nagayoshi led twenty thousand men into the capital. Yoshifuji and Harumoto fled to Tamba, but returned to the capital after more than ten days.

In the second month of the twenty-third year (of Tenbun, 1554), Ichijō Kanpaku Kanetomo passed away. He was twenty-six years of age. In the same month, Yoshifuji changed his name to Yoshiteru.
