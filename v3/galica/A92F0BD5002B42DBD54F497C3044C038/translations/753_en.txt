At this time, Lord Ieyasu entered the castle of Okazaki in Mikawa. In the ninth month, the former Regent (Kanpaku) Konoe Sakihisa traveled down to Echigo. This was the first instance of a serving chief minister residing in a province outside the capital.

In the tenth month, Nagao Kagetora changed his name to Uesugi Terutora, departed from Echigo, advanced into the Kantō region, and called himself Kanrei (Deputy). He attacked and captured various castles in Kōzuke, including Numata, Umayabashi, Nawa, and others.

In the first month of the fourth year (of Eiroku, 1561), Miyoshi Shūri no Daibu Nagayoshi's son, Yoshinaga, Governor of Chikuzen, came up to the capital. He had an audience with Yoshiteru, built a new residence, and hoped for Yoshiteru to pay him a visit. Yoshiteru consented. In the third month, Nagayoshi entered the capital from Iimori Castle in Kawachi. On the last day of the same month, Yoshiteru, having arranged his ceremonial appearance, came to Yoshinaga's residence. There were banquets and Sarugaku performances.
