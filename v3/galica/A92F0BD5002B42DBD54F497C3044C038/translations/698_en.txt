[Katsumoto,] whose power had waned and who had been keeping to himself, felt deep resentment at having been unable to save Masanaga, and harboring hatred for the growing dominance of Sōzen and Yoshihiro, finally made preparations for battle. [His forces comprised:] The Hosokawa clan and forces from Awaji, Sanuki, Awa, Tosa, Izumi, Settsu, Tamba, Mikawa, and Bitchū. Hatakeyama Masanaga, and forces from Kii, Kawachi, and Etchū. Shiba Yoshitoshi with five hundred troops. Kyōgoku Mochikiyo, and forces from Ōmi, Hida, Izumo, and Oki. Akamatsu Masanori with five hundred troops. Takeda Kunishige, and forces from Aki and Wakasa. The Togashi Deputy [Governor] with five hundred troops. Among others, great and lesser lords such as Kira Yoshizane and Niki Shigenaga — those who followed Katsumoto were said to number one hundred and sixty thousand men.

[On the other side,] the Yamana clan and forces from Tajima, Harima, Bingo, Inaba, Hōki, Mimasaka, and Iwami. Shiba Yoshikado and forces from Tōtōmi, Owari, and Echizen. Hatakeyama Yoshihiro and forces from Yamato, Kawachi, and Kumano. The same [clan's] Yoshitsuna and forces from Noto. Isshiki Yoshinao and forces from Tamba, Ise, and other provinces. Toki Shigenari and forces from Mino. Rokkaku Takayori and forces from within Ōmi. Ōuchi Masahiro and forces from Suō, Nagato, Buzen, Chikuzen, and parts of Aki and Iwami.
