From the first year of Shōchō to the present year, his reign had lasted fourteen years. The Kanrei (Deputy Shogun) Hosokawa Ukyō-no-daibu Mochiyuki, Hatakeyama Saemon-no-kami Mochikuni, Ōuchi-no-suke Mochisuke, and others convened and agreed to support Yoshinori's son, Yoshikatsu, as their new lord. He was at this time merely eight years of age. Hosokawa Sanuki-no-kami Mochitsune, Akamatsu Izumi-no-kami Sadamura, and Takeda Daizenno-daibu Nobukata were appointed as commanders of the main force (ōte), while Yamana Uemon-no-kami Mochitōyo, the same clan's Shuri-no-daibu Norikiyo, and the same clan's Sagami-no-kami Noriyuki were appointed as commanders of the flanking force (karamete). They advanced toward Harima Province to suppress and punish Mitsusuke and his son. However, Mochitsune, being on friendly terms with Mitsusuke, did not press the attack.

In the eighth month, an imperial edict (rinji) was bestowed upon the besieging forces in Harima Province, ordering the punishment of Mitsusuke. In the same month, Yoshikatsu was conferred the rank of Junior Fifth Rank, Lower Grade (Jū-go-i-ge).
