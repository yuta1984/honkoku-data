His Buddhist sobriquet (dōgō) was Tenzan, and his Buddhist name (hōmyō) was Dōgi. In the seventh month, Former Naidaijin Minamoto no Yoshinari was appointed Minister of the Left. Kinsada resigned as Naidaijin. In the eighth month, Yoshinari resigned from his office and took the tonsure. In the ninth month, Kinsada was appointed Minister of the Right. Sanjō Dainagon Fujiwara no Kintomi was appointed Naidaijin. In the twelfth month, Sanjō Dainagon Sanefuyu was appointed Naidaijin. In this same year, Yamana Mitsuyuki was executed.

In the second month of the third year of Ōei (1396), Minamoto no Tomomiichi resigned as Grand Minister of State. In the fourth month, Minamoto no Yoshimochi was conferred the rank of Shōshiinoge (Junior Fourth Rank, Lower Grade). In the seventh month, Tōin Minister of the Right Kinsada was appointed Minister of the Left. Sanjō Sanefuyu resigned as Naidaijin. Dainagon Fujiwara no Tsugifusa was appointed Naidaijin. In the ninth month, Yoshimochi was appointed Sangi (Councillor). In the same month, Minamoto no Dōgi (Yoshimitsu) ascended Mount Hiei (Eizan). The ceremonial protocol for this was placed on a level equal to that of an imperial progress (miyuki). The practice of referring to the shogunate as "Kubō" (the Public Authority) is said to have originated around this time.
