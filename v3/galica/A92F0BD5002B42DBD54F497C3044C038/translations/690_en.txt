In the twelfth month, Nijō Mochimichi resigned from the position of Kampaku (Imperial Regent). Ichijō Norifusa was appointed Kampaku. Norifusa was the son of Kaneyoshi.

In the third year, second month, Yoshimasa constructed a new residence, which he named the Katei (Flower Pavilion). In the twelfth month, Kampaku Norifusa resigned from the position of Safu (Minister of the Left). The former Udaijin (Minister of the Right) of the Sanjō clan, Sanekazu, was appointed Safu.

In the first year of Kanshō, sixth month, the former Kampaku Mochimichi resigned from the position of Shōkoku (Daijō-daijin, Grand Minister of State). In the seventh month, Sanekazu resigned from the position of Safu. In the eighth month, Yoshimasa was appointed Sadaijin (Minister of the Left) from the position of Naidaijin (Inner Minister). His position as Udaishō (Major Captain of the Right) remained unchanged. Tokudaiji Dainagon (Grand Counselor) Kimitomo was appointed Naidaijin. In the ninth month, Hatakeyama Uemon-no-suke Yoshimasa again defied the orders of Yoshimasa (the shogun) and went to Kawachi, where he barricaded himself in Wakae Castle. (The shogun) ordered Hatakeyama Owari-no-kami Masanaga to attack Yoshimasa, and Wakae Castle was destroyed. Yoshimasa then fortified himself at Kongaiji Temple on Mount Dake, and the fighting between him and Masanaga did not cease.

In the same year, people from Chōsen (Korea) came to pay a visit to the court.
