In the seventh month of the third year [of Hōtoku, 1451], people from Ryūkyū arrived. In the eighth month, Yoshinari sent a letter to Great Ming (China). In the ninth month, a retainer of Kanrei Hatakeyama Tokuhon killed a follower of Kyōgoku Mochikiyo, the head of the Samurai-dokoro (Board of Retainers). Mochikiyo, enraged, sought to fight against Hatakeyama. Hosokawa Umanokami (Head of the Right Stables) Narikata intervened to restrain him, and caused Hatakeyama to hand over the perpetrator from his subordinates, allowing Mochikiyo to execute him.

In the tenth month of the first year of Kōtoku [1452], the former Naifu (Minister of the Center) Koga Minamoto no Kiyomichi was appointed Daijō-daijin (Chancellor). Sanjō Sanenaga resigned as Naifu. The Major Counselor of Ichijō, Norihusa, was appointed Naifu. Hosokawa Ukyō-no-daibu (Senior Assistant Minister of the Right Capital) Katsumoto was reappointed as Kanrei (Deputy Shogun).

In the third month of the second year [of Kōtoku, 1453], Yoshinari was granted the senior first rank (jūichii). In the fourth month, Ichijō Kaneyoshi resigned as Kampaku (Imperial Regent). Nijō Udaijin (Minister of the Right) Mochimichi became Kampaku. In the sixth month, Yoshinari changed his name to Yoshimasa. In the same month, Kaneyoshi was granted the status of Jun-sangu (Honorary status equivalent to the three empresses), food fief (shokuyu), personal bodyguards (zuijin), and armed escorts (hyōjō), in accordance with the precedent of Chūjin-kō (Fujiwara no Yoshifusa). In the ninth month, the Chancellor Koga Minamoto no Kiyomichi passed away, at the age of sixty-one. In the twelfth month, Shōgun Yoshimasa became the head of the Minamoto clan (Genji no Chōja) and (was appointed) the superintendent (bettō) of the Shōgaku-in and Junna-in academies.
