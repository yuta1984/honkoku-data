In the ninth month of the third year of Kōji (1557), Regent Konoe Sakihide resigned from the position of Sadaijin. Saionji Kintomo was appointed Sadaijin. Kazanin Iesuke, who held the position of Naidaijin, was appointed Udaijin. Hirohashi Dainagon Kanehide was appointed Naidaijin, but immediately took Buddhist vows. On the fifth day of the same month, the Emperor passed away at the age of sixty-two. The era name used from the year following his accession was Daiei 7. Following that came Kyōroku 4, Tenbun 23, and Kōji 3. His total reign spanned thirty-one years.

The 107th Sovereign
Emperor Ōgimachi: his personal name was Michihito. He was the son of Emperor Gonara. His mother was the posthumously titled Empress Yoshiko, daughter of Counselor Fujiwara no Katafusa. On the twenty-seventh day of the eleventh month of Kōji 3 (1557), he acceded to the throne. He was forty-two years of age at the time.
