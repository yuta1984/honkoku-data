As the enemy pressed in pursuit, along the way Dōyo's son Hidetsuna was killed in battle. It is said that the Emperor was carried upon the back of Hosokawa Kiyouji. A temporary imperial residence was established at Tarui in Mino Province, where Yoshiakira stood guard over the Emperor. Gathering forces from the various provinces, they prepared to strike down the Yamana. The Yamana father and son, their forces exhausted, retreated to Hōki. Yoshiakira then escorted the Emperor back to the capital in a return imperial progress.

In the spring of the third year (of Bunna, 1354), Nitta Yoshioki and Wakiya Yoshiharu fled from Kawamura Castle, and as the eastern provinces grew calm, Takauji appointed Hatakeyama Kunikiyo as senior retainer (karō) to Motouji and set him to guard the Kantō, while Takauji himself returned to the capital (Kyoto). Nikki Sakyō-no-taifu Yoriyuki was appointed to the post of Administrative Head (shitsuji-shoku) of the warrior government. Yoshiakira was dispatched to Harima Province to muster troops and strike down the Yamana. Upon hearing this, the Yamana welcomed Tadafuyu and made him their commander-in-chief. As a result, Tadafuyu joined the Southern Court's side and came into conflict with Takauji (his father).
