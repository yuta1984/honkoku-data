In the first month of the sixth year (of Eiroku, 1563), Satomi of Awa, together with Ōta Sanraku of Iwatsuki in Musashi province, fought a battle against Hōjō Ujiyasu and his son Ujimasa at Konodai in Musashi province. Satomi and his allies were defeated. In the twelfth month, the retired monk of Shōmyō-in of Sanjō, Jōkaku, passed away at the age of seventy-seven.

That same year, Mōri Motonari attacked and captured Toda Castle in Izumo province. Amago Haruhisa surrendered. Motoharu (Kikkawa) and Takakage (Kobayakawa) advocated for killing him, but Motonari restrained them from bringing about his death, and instead sent Haruhisa to Aki province, where he was kept as a prisoner. From the second year of Kōji until this year — a period of approximately seven years of prolonged confrontation — the Amago clan finally fell. The territories under Motonari's rule were: Aki, Suō, Nagato, Bitchū, Bingo, Inaba, Hōki, Izumo, Oki, and Iwami — ten provinces in total. He came to be called the lord of ten provinces (Jisshū no Taishu). From this point onward, he went to war against the Ōtomo of Bungo, dispatched forces to Shikoku as well, and also fought year after year against Ukita Naoie of Bizen.
