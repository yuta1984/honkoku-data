Emperor Kōtoku was the younger brother of [Empress] Kōgyoku. After Iruka was executed, he ascended to the throne upon receiving the abdication of his elder sister Kōgyoku. At this time, Ōtomo no Nagatoko and Inukami no Takebe stood to the left and right before the imperial presence, wearing golden quivers. The hundred officials performed the ceremonial bow in rows. The honorary title of Kōsobo no Mikoto ("Venerable Imperial Grandmother") was conferred upon [the retired Empress] Kōgyoku. Prince Naka no Ōe was made Crown Prince. Abe no Kurahashi-maro was appointed Minister of the Left, and Soga no Kurayamada (referred to as Maro in the text, but as Ishikawa in the Nihon Shoki) was appointed Minister of the Right. This was the beginning of the offices of Left and Right Ministers. Kamatari was bestowed the Kinkan (golden court cap), granted the office of Naidaijin (Minister of the Interior), had his stipend increased, was placed above the hundred officials, and was entrusted with the governance of the realm. That the nation remained at peace after Iruka's downfall was owing to this man's achievement. After that, he was further granted the Shikan (purple court cap) and his stipend was increased further. Takamukou no Genri and the monk Min were appointed as Hakase (learned scholars). Both had traveled to Tang China to pursue their studies. For the first time, an era name was established, called the first year of Taika. The names of the Eight Ministries and the hundred officials were all established at this time.
