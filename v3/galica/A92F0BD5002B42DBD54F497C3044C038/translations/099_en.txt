An Overview of Japan's Royal Reigns, Volume 2

　　　The Thirty-Ninth Reign

Emperor Tenji　　He was the son of Emperor Jomei. His mother was Empress Saimei.
　He was first known as Prince Naka no Ōe. He was also called Prince Katsuragi and Prince Hirakiwake. He conspired together with Nakatomi no Kamatari. The details of this are described in the section on Empress Kōgyoku. He served as Crown Prince during the two reigns of Emperor Kōtoku and Empress Saimei. In the seventh year of Empress Saimei's reign, as the Tang Dynasty and Silla had destroyed Baekje, at the request of Fukushin, a retainer of Baekje, he assembled troops. Empress Saimei made an imperial visit to Asakura in Tosa Province. As Tenji was Crown Prince at this time, he administered the affairs of government within the military camp. He built a palace of unstripped timber in the mountains, practiced frugality, and did not burden the people with hardship. This is what is known as the "Palace of Round Logs at Asakura." He also established a checkpoint at a place called Karukaya, took precautions against emergencies, examined those passing in and out, and inquired into their names, [checking the movements of travelers...]
