There was a banquet of (poetry and music). On the twenty-sixth day, the imperial return (kankō) took place.

In the eighth month of the tenth year of Eikyō [1438], the Middle Counselor (Chūnagon) of the Asukai clan, Fujiwara no Masayo, presented the *Shinzoku Kokin Wakashū* [New Collection of Poems Ancient and Modern, Continued] for imperial inspection. The *Kokin*, *Gosen*, *Shūi*, *Goshūi*, *Kin'yō*, *Shika*, *Senzai*, and *Shin Kokin* are collectively called the "Eight Anthologies" (Hachidaishū). This matter has already been noted above.
