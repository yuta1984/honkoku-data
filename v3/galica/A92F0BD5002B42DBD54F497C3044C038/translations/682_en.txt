(Continued from the previous page) Prince Sadatsune inherited Fushimi Palace, and down to the present day, successive generations have received the imperial declaration of princely rank (senka).

In the fifth year [of Bun'an, 1448], in the second month, an imperial visit (gyōkō) was made to the Retired Emperor (Dōkin). Retainers of Hosokawa Katsumoto stood guard at the crossroads (tsujigатame). In the eighth month, Akamatsu Samanosuke returned from Chosŏn (Korea), plotting to revive his clan. However, the plot was discovered and he was executed; his head was brought to the capital. In the twelfth month, Yoshinari was appointed Chief of the Left Stables (Samakami no Kami).

On the sixteenth day of the fourth month of the first year of Hōtoku [1449], Yoshinari came of age in the genpuku ceremony. [Marginal note: Aged fifteen.] The kanrei (Deputy Shogun) Hosokawa Musashi-no-kami Katsumoto performed the capping ceremony (kakan), and Hosokawa Minbu-no-shōyū Noriharu conducted the haircutting ritual (rihатsu). All others who performed roles in the ceremony were members of the Hosokawa clan. The imperial envoy and court intermediary (tensō), Nakayama Counselor Chikamichi, presented a ceremonial sword. From the warrior government, gold dust, a sword, and a horse were offered. Chikamichi was also presented with a sword and a horse. The banquet (ōban) that day was arranged by Katsumoto, and the banquet the following day was arranged by Hatakeyama Yoshihiro. [Marginal note: Yoshihiro is the son of Tokuhon.] On the nineteenth day, court nobles and various daimyo came to offer their congratulations.
