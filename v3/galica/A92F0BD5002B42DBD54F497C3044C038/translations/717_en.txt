In the second month of Bunki 1 [1501], Kujō Hisatsune, the Naifu [Minister of the Center], was appointed to the post of Ufu [Minister of the Right]. Saionji Dainagon Kinfuji was appointed to the post of Naifu [Minister of the Center]. In the sixth month, Ichijō Fuyuyoshi resigned as Regent [Kanpaku]. Kujō Hisatsune became the new Regent. In this same year, the former Shogun Yoshimura had his official posts revoked. Yoshimura took refuge with the Ōuchi no Suke of Suō Province, changed his name to Yoshitada, and sought to win over the warriors of the western provinces. Many followed him. In the Home Provinces [Kinai] and their vicinity, Hosokawa Masamoto had imposed his control, and for a time there was peace.

In the seventh month of the second year [Bunki 2, 1502], Shogun Minamoto no Yoshitaka was promoted to Junior Fourth Rank, Lower Grade [Jushii no ge], and appointed to the Council of State [Sangi]. He concurrently held the post of Middle Captain of the Left [Sachūjō] and entered the palace for an audience. In the same month, Yoshitaka changed his name to Yoshizumi.

In the first month of the third year [Bunki 3, 1503], Yoshizumi was promoted to Junior Third Rank [Jushami]. In the summer, there was a great drought.

In Eishō 1 [1504], there was a famine. In the tenth month of the same year, Yamanouchi Uesugi Akisada and Ōgigayatsu Uesugi Tomoyoshi clashed in battle at Kawagoe in Musashi Province [Bushū].
