In the eleventh year [of Bunmei, 1479], in the first month, Yoshihisa was appointed to the Junior Second Rank. In the second month, Kujō Masamoto resigned as Regent [Kanpaku]. Konoe Udaijin [Right Minister] Masaie became Regent. In the third month, Takatsukasa Masahira resigned as Minister of the Left [Sadaijin]. Masaie became Minister of the Left. Sanjō Naidaijin [Minister of the Center] Kimitaka became Minister of the Right [Udaijin]. Ōinokami Dainagon [Great Counselor] Nobukazu became Minister of the Center [Naidaijin]. In the eleventh month, Yoshihisa, now fifteen years of age, performed the Hajime no Han [inaugural document-signing ceremony] and the Hajime no Hyōjō [inaugural deliberative assembly]. From this time, he conducted governmental affairs in place of Yoshimasa. Yoshimasa retired to the Tōgūdō on the Eastern Hills [Higashiyama], where he amused himself with antique vessels and old paintings, collected tea utensils, and passed his days in leisure. He took the title "Higashiyama-dono" [Lord of the Eastern Hills]. He constructed the Silver Pavilion [Ginkaku], modeled after the Golden Pavilion [Kinkaku] of Kitayama.

In the twelfth year [of Bunmei, 1480], in the third month, Sanjō Kimitaka resigned as Minister of the Right. Imadegawa former Naidaijin Norisue was appointed Minister of the Right. Yoshihisa was appointed Great Counselor [Dainagon]. In the seventh month, at Yoshihisa's request, the Grand Chancellor [Daikō] Ichijō Kaneyoshi compiled the *Shōdan Chiyō* [Conversations on the Essentials of Government].
