The Lesser Deputy (*Shōni*) of the Dazaifu, Fuyusuke, harbored treacherous intentions and was killed by the *Tandai* (regional commissioner) Imagawa Ryōshun.

In the tenth month, the Imperial purification ceremony (*misogi/harai*) was performed.

In the eleventh month, since the *Daijōe* ceremony had long been postponed, the military government (*buke*) submitted a formal recommendation regarding the matter. In the same month, Yoshimitsu was appointed to the Junior Third Rank (*Jushii*).

In the twelfth month, Nijō Moronaga resigned from his posts as Regent (*Kanpaku*) and Minister of the Left (*Sadaijin*). The Minister of the Right of the Kujō clan, Tadamoto, was transferred to Minister of the Left and became Regent. Moronaga's younger brother, the Major Counselor (*Dainagon*) Moretsugu, became Minister of the Right, and Konoe Dainagon Kanetsugu became Minister of the Center (*Naidaijin*). At this time, five individuals — Nijō Yoshimoto, Kujō Norinori, Konoe Michitsugu, Takatsukasa Fuyu-michi, and Nijō Moronaga — were all serving simultaneously as former Regents (*saki no Kanpaku*). This year, the Southern Court changed its era name to Tenju.

In the first month of the second year [of Eiwa, 1376], the Zen monks Zekkai and Joryō returned from the Great Ming (China). When this monk had an audience with the Taizu Emperor of the Great Ming, the Emperor inquired about the matter of Xu Fu (*Jofuku*), whereupon Zekkai presented a poem. He was in return granted a poem composed in harmonizing rhyme (*wain*) by the Emperor himself.
