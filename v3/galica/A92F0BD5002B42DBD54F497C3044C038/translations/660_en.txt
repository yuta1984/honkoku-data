On the twenty-third day of the same month, Yoshinori entered the imperial palace and visited the Retired Emperor's residence. On the twenty-ninth day, he was appointed Provisional Major Counselor (Gondainagon) and received the rank of Junior Third Rank (Ju Sanmi). In the fourth month, Yoshinori performed the ceremony of the first official seal (hanshihajiime) in the court noble style. In the eighth month, Yoshinori made a pilgrimage to Iwashimizu Hachimangū Shrine. In the same month, Yoshinori established regulations for the bugyōnin (administrative officers), and rulings were issued regarding confiscated properties (kessho) in the various provinces. Shiba Uhyōe no Kami Yoshiatsushi was reappointed as Kanryō (Deputy Shogun). In the same month, Regent Mochimoto resigned as Left Minister (Safu). Ichijō Udaijin Kaneyoshi was appointed Left Minister. Konoe Naifu Fusatsugu was appointed Right Minister. Koga Dainagon Minamoto no Kiyomichi was appointed Inner Minister (Naifu). Yoshinori was appointed Right Great General (Udaishō). In the ninth month, Yoshinori made pilgrimages to Hiyoshi and Kasuga Shrines. In the twelfth month, Yoshinori was elevated to Junior Second Rank (Ju Nii). In the same month, the imperial enthronement ceremony was held.

Second year of Eikyō [1430]. In the first month, Yoshinori became the Director of the Bureau of Horses (Maryō no Mikan). In the tenth month, Yoshinori was elevated to Senior First Rank (Ju Ichii). In the same month, there was an imperial procession for the purification ceremony (misogi gyōkō), and Right Great General Yoshinori attended in escort.
