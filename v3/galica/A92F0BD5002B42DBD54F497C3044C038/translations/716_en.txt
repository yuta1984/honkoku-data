The reign era and the year of enthronement begins with Kōshō 6. Subsequently: Bunshō 1, Ōnin 2, Bunmei 18, Chōkyō 2, Entoku 3, and Meiō 9 — totaling thirty-six years on the throne.

　　The One Hundred and Fifth Sovereign
Emperor Go-Kashiwabara-in: His personal name [imina] was Katsuhito. He was the Crown Prince of Emperor Go-Tsuchimikado-in. His mother was the Jugo [honorary title equivalent to an Empress Dowager], Minamoto no Asako, daughter of the Provisional Major Counselor [Gon-Dainagon] Nagakata. In the twelfth year of Bunmei [1480], he received the declaration of Imperial Princeship [Shinnō senge], and performed his coming-of-age ceremony [genpuku] at the residence of Shogun Minamoto no Yoshimasa, at the age of seventeen. Yoshimasa served as the one who placed the ceremonial cap [kakan]. He ascended to the throne [senzo] in the ninth year of Meiō [1500], at the age of thirty-seven. The Regent [Kanpaku] was the former Grand Minister of State [Saki no Shōkoku] of the Ichijō clan, Fuyuyoshi. The Shogun was Minamoto no Yoshitaka. The Kanrei [Deputy Shogun] was Hosokawa Ukyō no Daibu Masamoto.
