The Retired Emperor Hanazono himself compiled the *Fūga Wakashū* [Collection of Elegance]. By the imperial edict of Emperor Go-Kōgon, Fujiwara no Tamesada compiled the *Shin Senzai (Wakashū)*; at the same time, Fujiwara no Tameaki compiled the *Shin Shūi (Wakashū)*; and by the imperial edict of Emperor Go-En'yū, Fujiwara no Tametō and Fujiwara no Tameshige compiled the *Shin Goshūi (Wakashū)*.

Now, with the addition of the *Shinzoku Kokin (Wakashū)*, these are collectively designated the "Twenty-One Anthologies" (Nijūichidaishū).

In the sixth month of this year [1438], there was a matter concerning the coming-of-age ceremony (genpuku) of Kenwōmaru, the eldest son of Kamakura (kubō) Mochiuji. Uesugi Awaji-no-kami Norizane submitted that, in accordance with precedent, (Kenwōmaru) ought to receive one character from the personal name (imina) of the Kyoto shogun. However, (Mochiuji) refused to accept this, and declaring instead that the distant precedent of (Minamoto no) Yoshiie should be followed, he had (Kenwōmaru) undergo the coming-of-age ceremony at Tsurugaoka Hachiman (Shrine), giving him the name Yoshihisa. (Mochiuji) deliberated that if Norizane came to offer congratulations, he should be immediately executed.
