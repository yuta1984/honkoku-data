[Continued from previous page: Takatsukasa Kanesuke was appointed to the post of Ufu.] Sanjō Dainagon Sanekа was appointed to the post of Naifu [Minister of the Center]. He is the legitimate heir of the main Sanjō line and bears the title "Tenpōrin" [転法輪].

In the sixth month, Kakō Matasaburō [Kōsai Matarokū], a retainer of Hosokawa Ukyō no Daibu Masamoto, plotted rebellion and bribed Togura [某, a certain person], a minor retainer of Masamoto. On the night of the twenty-third of that month, Masamoto had entered his bathing chamber [yudono] for the purpose of ritual Atago abstinence [purification]. Togura followed him inside and secretly killed Masamoto. Masamoto's close attendant Habakabe hurried to the scene, whereupon Togura stabbed him once with his sword and fled. Habakabe also died [from his wounds].

From the time of [Ashikaga] Takauji onwards, the Hosokawa clan had held dominion over the four provinces of Shikoku. Since [Hosokawa] Yoriyuki, the main line [chakuryū] had served as Kanrei [Deputy Shogun] and therefore resided in the capital, being called the "Upper Mansion" [Kami no Yakata]. Yoriyuki's younger brothers Norishige [Noriharu], Mitsuyuki, and others remained in their home provinces of Awa and Sanuki. These were called the "Lower Mansion" [Shimo no Yakata]. Masamoto habitually claimed to practice esoteric arts [mahō] and on this pretext observed ritual purification. As a result, he had no children. He adopted as his son Rokurō Sumimoto, the child of Motokатsu [Motokatsu], the Governor of Sanuki, who belonged to the Lower Mansion. However, before Sumimoto had yet made his way up to the capital, Masamoto was suddenly struck down.
