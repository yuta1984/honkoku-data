　　August 7th, 1870, at 10 o'clock in the evening, at the city of Paris.
　　　　　　Written in obedience to the command of the Empress Regent.

Upon the issuance of the above order, Napoleon III and his government decreed the reassignment of officials. ○ Marshal Le Bœuf was relieved of his position as Chief of Staff, and Marshal Bazaine was appointed in his place. Furthermore, General Trochu was appointed Major-General in military affairs, and concurrently [was assigned responsibility for Paris——]
