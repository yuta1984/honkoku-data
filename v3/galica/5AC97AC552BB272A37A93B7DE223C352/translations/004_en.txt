On the 2nd of August, he arrived at the temporary encampment at Mainz, and the following day, the following brief proclamation was circulated throughout the army. The text reads:

"The states of Germany have stood united against the neighboring country [France] that has started an unjust and unprovoked war. Now, as our nation faces intimidation, the safety of the people and the honor of the state are at stake. The time has come to decide the outcome decisively. Therefore, today I take command of the entire army, and by fighting to defeat the enemy, we shall not tarnish the honor displayed by our ancestors of old, but shall together forge glorious military achievements. The entire nation believes in your success. I, too, do not doubt it. Be steadfast — God [be with us]—"
