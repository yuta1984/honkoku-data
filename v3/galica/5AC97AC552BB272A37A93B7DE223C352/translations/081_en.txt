On the evening of August 7th, alarming news spread among the people of Paris. ○ A false report had circulated claiming that the French national forces had achieved a victory at Wissembourg, but the people were skeptical. The Empress, wishing to know clearly the true state of the army's misfortunes, issued the following proclamation—though it was dated as morning, it was in fact dispatched that evening:

"At the outset of hostilities, our troops found no advantage and suffered greatly. ○ Now, turning this misfortune around, let the army quickly —— [continued on next page]"
