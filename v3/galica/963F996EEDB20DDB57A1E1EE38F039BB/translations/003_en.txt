In the year 1813 of the Western calendar, the eleventh year of Bunka in our country, the year of Kinoe-Inu (甲戌), on the first day of the twelfth month, the nations of the coalition held a council and swore to punish Napoleon. Thereupon, the allied nations sent a letter to Napoleon from Frankfurt (place name). Napoleon read the letter but showed no inclination to change his ways. Nevertheless, the armies of the allied nations had already crossed the Rhine River and were pressing forward, and the British military commander Wellington (personal name) crossed the Pyrenees Mountains and encamped in the fields of the Garonne (place name).
