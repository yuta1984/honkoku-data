(The clan of Pindaros) was spared (from slaughter) by special grace. Thus all of Greece came under the subjugation of Emperor Alexander. At this time, Persia raised a great army and attacked Greece. The people of Greece all petitioned to receive the Emperor's protection and be spared from the calamities of war. Among them, only one person — "Diogenes" of the place called "Sinope" — made no petition whatsoever. Diogenes was a great scholar of that land, who made his home outside the city gates, with a mind that was free and expansive. The Emperor himself personally went to visit Diogenes, and upon beholding his appearance, found him impoverished and shabby, his clothing dirty and unwashed. Diogenes was basking in the sun, exposing his back to its rays. The Emperor stepped forward and asked, "Is there anything that you lack?" Diogenes replied: "Young master, you are standing before me and blocking the sun from shining upon me — that is the only thing I lack."
