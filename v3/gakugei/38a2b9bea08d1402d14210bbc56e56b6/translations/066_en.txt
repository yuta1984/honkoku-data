**Secret Tradition for Bone-Setting (接骨, *Hone-tsugi*)**

Take a living crucian carp (*funa*) and fillet it into three pieces while still alive. Do not discard the entrails outside—cast them into the sea or a river. Next, take rice plants from a green, growing paddy field, and dry them beforehand in the shade. Even during the day, keep them in a shaded spot away from direct sunlight...

**Method for Subduing a Dog That Is Barking and About to Bite**

With the left hand, form the sign for "Tora" (Tiger), eliminate "Tei" (the cyclical sign), and grasp "Inu" (Dog) (i.e., form specific hand gestures corresponding to these signs). Recite: "Cloud-dragon (*Unryō*), Wind-tiger (*Fūko*)—subdue this fierce beast!" If one does so, the dog will stop barking and flee.

**A Wondrous Incantation for Ringworm (癬, *Tamushi*)**

【Left folio, lower section】

Grind good-quality ink to a thick consistency, trace a round border around the ringworm, and write the character 南 (*minami*, "south") three times within the circle. The cure is remarkably effective. If only half the characters are written, only half the ringworm will heal. Should anyone claim the cure was not due to this incantation, write the character 北 (*kita*, "north") three times, and the ringworm will flare up again.
