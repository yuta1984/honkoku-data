　**A Method for Storing Dried Persimmons (*Tsurushigaki*) for a Long Time**

- If one wishes to store Mino dried persimmons (*tsurushigaki*) for a long time, one should arrange the persimmons on the bottom of a leaf-tea jar (*hacha tsubo*) and place loose-leaf tea (*hacha*) on top of them. The dried persimmons will retain their flavor for a long time without changing. Furthermore, the tea itself will become better in flavor.

　**A Method for Copying Small Characters as Large Characters**

- There are methods such as cutting out characters and transferring them using fire, but these are difficult to execute freely with good results. Simply by using a grid division like that of a *goban* (Go board) to copy a character, not the slightest deviation will occur. For example, trace the outline of one small character, draw horizontal and vertical grid lines through it, and measure the width of each cell. On a separate sheet of paper, construct a grid with the same number of cells as the first, but with each cell made larger—

—and, looking at where each stroke and dot falls within which numbered cell of the small-character grid, recreate the character within the corresponding cell of the larger grid. The force of the brushwork and the overall shape will not deviate in the slightest. By repeatedly constructing such grids in this manner and copying, one can produce characters of any size whatsoever. To reduce large characters to small ones, follow the same method as above, gradually making the grid cells smaller. The same principle applies equally to pictures.
