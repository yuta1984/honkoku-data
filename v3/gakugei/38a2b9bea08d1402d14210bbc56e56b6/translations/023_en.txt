　**Instructions on the Method of Writing on the Fore-Edge of Books**

- When writing on the fore-edge (*koguchi*) of a book, if you simply write as you would normally, the ink will run and not adhere well. With old books in particular, the ink will adhere even less readily. Whether the fore-edge is new or old, dampen a hand towel with water, wring it out well, and wipe the area of the fore-edge where you intend to write, just once. While this slight dampness

**[Left Page — Lower Section]**

remains, proceed to write. However, if the edge is made too wet, the ink will bleed, so only a slight dampening is needed. The brush will move smoothly, the ink will adhere with a deep, rich black, and you will be able to write just as you wish.

　**A Method for Creating Mottled Patterns on Bamboo**

- Finely grind the following four ingredients: *gosa* (礜砂, orpiment-type mineral — grind fifty *me* until fine); *rokuha* (碌礜, a mineral compound — grind thirty *me* until fine); *danha* (胆礜, a mineral substance — grind twenty *me*); and lime (*ishibai* — five *fun*). Mix these into concentrated lye (*aku*) and knead them together well. Using this preparation, apply any pattern or design (*mon*) you wish to the bamboo, and when it has dried, wash it off — the mottled pattern will appear as though it had occurred naturally.
