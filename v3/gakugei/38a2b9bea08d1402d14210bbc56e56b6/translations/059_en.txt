　　A Method for Removing Tobacco Resin (*Yani*) from Clothing

- When tobacco resin has adhered to clothing, apply the juice (lye) from tobacco ash — that is, from the spent remains of a smoked pipe — and wash the garment. This will remove the stain.

　　A Method for Keeping Caterpillars Away from Trees

- The method for preventing caterpillars from infesting trees is to take the thin, fluttering frilled membrane found at the head of a piece of dried squid (*surume*)

　　Treatment When a Sharp Stake Has Been Driven into a Horse's Hoof

- When a sharp stake has been driven into a horse's hoof, pull out and discard the stake, then shave off some dried bonito (*katsuobushi*) and pack it into the remaining wound. The healing effect is remarkable.

　　Transmitted Method for Getting a Horse That Refuses to Board a Ship to Board

- When a horse refuses to board a ship, write the character *fu* (賦) on the horse's forehead, then transfer the dot-stroke belonging to the character *bu* (武) — which forms the right-hand component (*tsukuri*) of the character *fu* — by writing it inside the ship. The horse will board with astonishing ease.
