**A Method for Removing Ink Spilled on a Tatami Mat**

- When ink has been spilled on a tatami mat, pouring water on it and wiping it will cause the ink to seep into the weave of the tatami, which is undesirable. Leave it as it is, allow the ink to dry thoroughly, and then rub it with a new pair of straw sandals (*zōri*). This will remove it well.

**A Method for Removing Oil and Ink Stains from Clothing**

- *Hange* (半夏; pinellia rhizome), *ika no kō* (烏魚骨; cuttlefish bone), *kasseki* (滑石; talc/soapstone), *myōban* (白礬; alum) [re-calcined]—

---

**[Left Page — Lower Section]**

Grind equal parts of each into powder. For oil stains, moisten the area by applying a little oil and leave it overnight. For ink stains, moisten the area with diluted ink (*usuzumi*), then apply this powder, wash it, and rub over it with a wick (*tōshin*). Alternatively, for ink stains, chew raw apricot kernel (*kyōnin*) and spit it out onto the stain, then wash it out. Rubbing on cooked millet (*awa no meshi*) and washing it off is also effective.
