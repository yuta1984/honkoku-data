　　Method for a Long-Burning Lamp (*Kantō*)

- Frankincense (*nyūkō*) 〈half a *ryō*〉 · Sulphur · Pine resin (*matsuyani*) · Dried lacquer (*kanshitsu*) 〈one *ryō* each〉 · Black soybean powder 〈four *ryō*〉 · Saltpetre (*enshō*) 〈half a *ryō*〉

Grind all six of the above ingredients into a powder, knead and harden them with raw lacquer (*ki-urushi*), and fashion the mixture into a long cylinder like a candle.

　　Method for a One-*Sun* Candle

- Chinese wax (*tōrō*) · Pine resin · Pagoda tree flowers (*enjū no hana*) 〈one *kin* each〉 · Pumice (*karuishi*) 〈forty *me*〉

Warm and melt all of the above together in one vessel. Take one bundle of wick, wrap it in cloth, and soak it thoroughly in the above wax compound until it is saturated all the way through, then set it aside coated. When lit, it will burn only about one *sun* over the course of an entire day and night.
