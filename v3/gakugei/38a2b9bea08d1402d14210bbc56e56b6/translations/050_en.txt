　　Method for Firing Coloured Paintings onto Tea Bowls

- Grind glass (hiidoro) into a fine powder, mix it with any type of paint/pigment, and paint a design onto a tea bowl. Place the tea bowl into a ceramic vessel such as a fire-box (hi-ire), cover the top with a roof tile, seal the opening tightly, and then

　　Method for Quickly Pickling Kazunoko (Herring Roe)

- When one wishes to pickle kazunoko quickly, add a small amount of sufficiently aged wall clay (plaster) to the brine and pickle [the roe] in it.

　　Method for Storing Katsuobushi (Dried Bonito)

- To store katsuobushi, apply sake to it and set it aside. Even on hot summer days, insects will not get into it.

　　Method for Keeping Green Yuzu with Leaves Attached for a Long Time

- If you wish to keep green yuzu (citrus) with its leaves attached for a long time, split a length of fresh bamboo lengthwise, place the yuzu inside, and then fit the split ends of the bamboo back together so that they align perfectly without any gap. 　　Method for Correcting Miso That Has Spoiled and Become Sour [or Mouldy]

- Wash three stalks of seri (Japanese parsley / water parsley) — a mixture of female seri (mezeri) and male seri (ozeri) — thoroughly, shake off the moisture well, and plant them with their roots still attached in the north-western (inu-i) corner of the miso [container]. When the seri has turned red, remove it. The miso will then regain a good flavour.

　　How to Keep Soba Noodles from Spoiling Even After Being Left for a Day

- When kneading soba, add a small amount of go-jiru (the liquid drained from ground soybeans) and knead it in. Even if left for a day after making, the soba will not spoil.
