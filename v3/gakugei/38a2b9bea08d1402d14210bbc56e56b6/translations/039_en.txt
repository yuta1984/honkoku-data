**Honchō Geibun Sansai Zue (Illustrated Encyclopedia of Japanese Arts and Letters), 5 volumes complete**
This work contains color illustrations of the Three Great Port Cities, together with illustrations of famous places throughout the country, and collects matters that shall serve as entertainment, comfort, and instruction for children in the various arts. It includes numerous tales of virtuous and wise women, poems that move the gods of heaven and earth, and accounts of various remarkable deeds of virtue, organized by category. It covers the etiquette and manners of the Ogasawara school, guidance on conduct, and the proper ceremonies from a child's birth through shrine visits, the hakama-wearing ceremony, the first-wearing ceremony, and wedding rites. It also includes illustrated Chinese wedding ceremonies, the moral teachings of Kyoto's Teshima family arranged in one hundred waka poems with explanatory notes, and collects numerous matters that should serve as instruction for women and children, with illustrations throughout. Furthermore, as it contains material on etiquette, waka poetry, and learning, even adults will find benefit in reading it and have their hearts comforted.

**Renga Shiyō Shō (Essentials of Linked Verse), 1 volume complete**
Beginning with the origins of linked verse, this work records all 214 items of secret transmission. It is a book that beginners should possess.

**Horikawa Ensho-awase (Horikawa Romantic Letters Matching), by Kumagai Sanjin, 3 volumes complete**
Records the refined style of letter-writing practiced by court nobles, as well as methods of composing waka poems and the proper forms of waka composition.

**Tsurezuregusa Kashiragaki (Annotated Essays in Idleness), 5 volumes complete**
While there are many annotated editions, this is a work that explains the true principles clearly and systematically, dividing matters with exceptional thoroughness.

**Karin Kotoha — Ubugi (Silken Swaddling), 3 volumes complete**
Citing ancient poems to annotate the vocabulary of waka poetry, this work serves as a guide to composing waka and includes forms of renga composition rules.

**Moshiobukuro (Salt-Weed Bag), 5 volumes complete** — by Kikuoka Senryō
(This work presents one hundred poems on seasonal subjects across the four seasons, recording famous poems, Chinese and Japanese literary writings, historical anecdotes, tales, and the hokku of master poets, organized by topic. Famous places are accompanied by illustrations, making it a greatly useful reference for broad learning.)
