- No matter how prone to dripping a candle may be, simply rub the oil from one's nose all around [the base of the wick] before lighting it. It will not drip in the slightest.

　　A Charm for Stopping a Dripping Candle

- Face the dripping edge of the candle and write the character 舩 (*fune*, "boat/ship") three times with a small knife; it will immediately stop, which is a wondrous thing.

　　A Rain-Proof Torch — It Will Not Go Out Even in Wind or Rain

- Cut a section of bamboo measuring six or seven *sun* in circumference and about two *shaku* in length. Leave one node at the base, and if there are any other nodes inside, remove them thoroughly. Fill this bamboo tube with camphor (*shōnō*) as tightly as possible,

【Left Page, Lower Section】
filling it all the way to the mouth, then seal the mouth with paper and keep it prepared at all times. If the paper seal is glued on with paste, the camphor will diminish; it is better to wet the paper with water and press it on. Once lit, this torch will not be extinguished by even the heaviest rain. Even when passing through water, if held upside down (inverted), the flame will not go out. However, if it is struck forcefully by a strong current flowing against it, it will be extinguished. If you wish to put this torch out, cut off the burning end and discard it. It can also be extinguished by rubbing it against sand.
