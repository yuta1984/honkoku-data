**A Method for Displaying Narcissus Flowers When They Are Out of Season**

During the peak blooming season of narcissus, place fresh narcissus flowers beneath a tatami mat, and they will keep well for a long time. When narcissus are out of season, take them out from under the tatami mat; upon giving them water for a flower arrangement, the flowers will revive and come back to life — providing a delightful surprise for guests.

**A Method for Preserving Chrysanthemums Until Spring**

Do not use the main large blooms of the chrysanthemum. Take branch flowers instead, fill a tube to the brim with water warmed to body temperature, and submerge the chrysanthemums up to the flower heads. Place this in a cupboard and close the door tightly. After the warm water cools, discard it and replace it with fresh water. Rot tends to enter from the base of the stem, so take the flowers out, inspect them carefully, and cut away and discard any rotted portions.

**A Method for Preventing Camellia Flowers from Falling**

Place a small amount of salt into the center of the stamens of camellia flowers. Whether the flowers are still on the tree or are cut flowers, the flower heads will not fall off.
