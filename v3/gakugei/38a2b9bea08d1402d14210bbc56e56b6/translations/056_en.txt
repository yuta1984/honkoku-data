　　Method for Keeping Fire Alight for a Long Time

- Place one walnut on a fire; when it is half-burnt and has become an ember, bury it in hot ash. It will remain alight without going out for four or five days.

　　Method for Treating a Cat's Illness

- If a cat is ill, grind *uyaku* (lindera root) alone into a powder and administer it in water. This is extremely effective.

　　Method for Treating Various Illnesses in Horses

- Chop together the roots and leaves of white *hōsenka* (garden balsam / *Impatiens balsamina*), boil them in a pot, pound the mixture, and prepare it like a poultice. If a horse is ill, apply it above all four corners of the eyes. The horse will immediately break into a sweat and recover.
