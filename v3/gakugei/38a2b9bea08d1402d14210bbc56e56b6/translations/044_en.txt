　Method for drinking sake without becoming drunk:
- Soak millet (kōryan/sorghum) and bakumondō (Ophiopogon japonicus root, a Chinese herbal medicine) in fox blood, dry them in the shade, and form them into pill-like balls. Each time you drink sake, hold one pill under your tongue, and it will prevent intoxication. It is also effective at dispelling the toxins of sake (i.e., curing hangovers).

　　Section on Cooking (料理門)

　　　Secret Technique: How to Shave Dried Bonito (Hanagatsuo)
- Take one block of good dried bonito (katsuo) and scrape off and discard the hard outer skin. Then, using the sharp, jagged edge of a broken teacup, draw the bonito inward (toward you) in a scraping motion. The result is astonishingly fine, thin shavings — a truly wondrous technique that seems beyond human craft.
