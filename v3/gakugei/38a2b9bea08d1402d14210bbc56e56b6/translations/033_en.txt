**A Method for Knowing Whether the Weather Will Be Good or Bad**

- When the sun rises, if the clouds move facing toward the sun and travel eastward, the weather will be fine.

- If a rainbow appears at sunrise, the weather will be fine.

- If a rainbow appears in the eastern sky, the weather will also be fine.

- If the first day of the month (*tsuitachi*) is clear and the weather is fine, it will remain fine for up to three days. If it stays fine for three days, it will remain fine for up to twelve or thirteen days.

- If the full moon day (the fifteenth) is clear and the weather is good, fine weather will continue for about twelve or thirteen days.

- If the tip of the crescent moon (three-day moon) is sharp and pointed, the weather will be fine.

- If the tip of the fourth-day moon is sharp and the moonlight is strong, the weather will be fine throughout that month.

- If the moonlight appears white, the weather will be fine.

- If the western sky is red at sunset, the weather will be fine.

- If black clouds appear in the east at sunrise, or if there are red clouds, the weather will be bad for about four or five days consecutively.
