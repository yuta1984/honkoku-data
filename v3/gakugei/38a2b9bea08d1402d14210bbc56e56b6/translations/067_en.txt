**An Incantation to Quickly Stop Bleeding**

When a person has received a wound and is bleeding, write the three characters "我大宝" (*Ga Daihō*) on that person's forehead. The blood will stop swiftly—a truly wondrous effect.

**Method for Blocking Rat Holes**

On the first *Tatsu* (Dragon) day of the first month of the year, as well as on each month's *Kanoe-Tora* (Metal-Tiger) day, *Mizunoe-Tatsu* (Water-Dragon) day, and the *Mitsu* (Full) day of the upper register of the almanac, seal up rat holes. Furthermore, catch a rat on the *Kanoe-Uma* (Metal-Horse) day of the third month, cut off its tail, and smear its blood on the roof beams of the house—rats will cease to appear.

**Method to Prevent Weasels from Taking Fish from a Garden Pond**

【Left folio, lower section】

Wrap black pepper in paper and stand the packets at the four corners of the pond. There is also a separate talisman (*fuda*) with an incantation. This method is particularly effective.

The incantation (*ju*) reads:

*"Itachi no majinai wa takanno no nejikiri nari*
*Kore zo Godai Tennō no iware sōran"*

("The charm against weasels is the 'twisted screw of the hawk's name'—
This is said to derive from the Five Great Heavenly Kings.")

Write this verse in kana (*hiragana*) on a small talisman (*fuda*) as shown, and stand it at the edge of the pond. Weasels will not come near.
