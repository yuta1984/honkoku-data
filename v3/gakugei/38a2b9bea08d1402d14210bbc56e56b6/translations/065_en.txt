**Method for Making Fireworks Incense (Hanabi Senkō)**

Ingredients:
- Saltpeter (焔硝, *enshō*)
- Realgar (雄黄, *yūō*)
- Camphor (樟脳, *shōnō*)
- Iron powder
- Hemp ash

Adjust the proportions so that the realgar is in a suitable amount.

Grind the above ingredients with a *yagen* (mortar-like grinding tool), knead with a thin paste like rice cake glue, attach to a spiked grass stalk (*igara*), and let dry.

**Treatment for a Centipede Sting**

Using your finger, write the character 王 (*ō*, "king") on a dry patch of ground. Take the soil from the center of that character and apply it to the sting. It will heal immediately.

---

**Treatment for a Bee Sting**

On the ground, use a bamboo stick to write the three characters 丙丁火 (*Hinoe, Hinoto, Fire*). Recite these seven times silently in your mouth. Take the soil from where the characters were written and rub it onto the sting.

**Incantation to Repel Mosquitoes**

*Tenchi* (Heaven and Earth), *Taisei* (Great Clarity), *Jitsugetsu* (Sun and Moon), *Taimei* (Great Brightness), *In'yō* (Yin and Yang), *Taikwa* (Great Harmony), *Kyūkyū* (Swiftly, swiftly)—

—as the edict of the law commands! Face a dark place and recite this text seven times. Blow upon the tip of a lamp wick, then light a fire using that same wick. All mosquitoes will be driven away.

**Incantation to Drive Away Fleas and Lice**

Write the seven characters 欠我青州木瓜銭 on paper and affix it under the floorboards. They will vanish immediately.

---

**Method to Drive Away Flies**

Store water collected from a Shinto shrine (*miya-mizu*) in the twelfth month. In summer, sprinkle it on sweets, food, and drink, and flies will naturally go away.

Another method: At the Hour of the Horse on the fifth day of the fifth month, write the character 白 (*shiro*, "white") on paper and affix it upside-down on the four sides of pillars.
