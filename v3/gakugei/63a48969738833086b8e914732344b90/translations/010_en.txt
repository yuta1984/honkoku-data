**[Right folio]**

The "wave stroke" (*hasei*) is like a wave rolling in and then retreating; the brush technique involves entering from right to left, pulling down slightly, and then sweeping horizontally outward.

**The "Facing-Glance Method" (*Koben-hō*)**: 丷 ⺌ 灬

**[Left folio]**

Radicals such as 氵 (three-dot water) and 心 (heart) — when brought together, they face each other like "併目" (*heimoku*, eyes turned toward one another); when apart, they turn away from each other like "抵背" (*teihaii*, backs turned against one another). Each individual dot and stroke must not deviate from proper principle and feeling (*jōri*).

**The "Facing-and-Turning-Away Method" (*Kyōhai-hō*)**: 川　口 (meaning *kyō*, "facing toward")
