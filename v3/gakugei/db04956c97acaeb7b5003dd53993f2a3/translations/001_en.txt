## GOAL: Kyoto (Miyako)

*Setting out with high hopes, traversing all fifty-three stations,*
*not a cloud in the sky — today we arrive at the capital!*

**Arriving from the East (Nobori)**

[Text within the illustration:]
Attendants, Retainers, Celestial beings (*tennin*), Court nobles (*kuge*), Two persons

---

## Overshoot Rules (when dice exceed the goal)

- Overshoot by 1 ―― Return to Ōtsu
- Overshoot by 2 ―― Return to Kusatsu
- Overshoot by 3 ―― Return to Ishibe
- Overshoot by 4 ―― Return to Minakuchi
- Overshoot by 5 ―― Return to Miya
