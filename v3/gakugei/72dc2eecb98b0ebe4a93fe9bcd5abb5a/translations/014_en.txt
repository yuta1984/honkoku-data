To speak of the state of this fleeting world: the area around Yagurakashita in Fukagawa Nakachō had been reduced to little more than an open field by the earthquake and fires of the previous year, yet now rows of temporary dwellings stand in splendid array, and the coming and going of pleasure-seekers and cultured visitors never ceases. Furthermore, owing to the public display (*kaichō*) of the Fudō-son (statue of Acalanātha),

**[Left Page]**

the crowds swelled even further, becoming so packed that there was not even room to plant a needle (i.e., not an inch of space to spare). Truly, the prosperity of the great city of Edo is herein recorded for the benefit of future generations.
