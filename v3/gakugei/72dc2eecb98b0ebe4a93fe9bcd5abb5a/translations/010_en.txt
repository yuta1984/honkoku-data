**[Fire Damage Areas]**
To the south: Kaji-chō, Kanō Yashiki Gorōbei-chō; to the north: Konya-chō, Tatami-chō, Daikon Kashi, Tokiwa-chō, Ōta Yashiki, Gusoku-chō, Sumi-chō, Inaba-chō, Yanagi-chō, Zaimoku-chō 8-chōme, as far as the riverside — all burned. In these neighborhoods, not a single earthen warehouse (kura) survived.

**[Earthquake Damage Areas]**
△ Kaizone Bridge / Sakamoto-chō: The northeastern section of Lord Makino's upper residence was damaged. Lord Kuki's upper residence suffered major damage on the front-facing side.
Lord Hosokawa's middle residence was damaged. In the Kanda replacement land, collapsed houses were found. Lord Etchū's upper residence suffered major damage. Five riverside earthen warehouses collapsed.
Matsuya-chō and Takanawa new replacement land suffered damage but no collapsed houses. Damage extended along Hon-Hatchōbori street up to the 5th block. Storehouses, stone walls, etc. along the riverside road were damaged.
Between Kinroku-chō and Hibiya-chō, major damage with collapsed and destroyed houses. Kamejima-chō and the Hatchōbori official residences suffered damage and collapses.

On the night of the 8th day of the 12th month of the same year (Ansei 2, 1855), fire broke out in Mizutani-chō. On the south side of the river: Mizutani-chō 4-chōme, Jōchi-chō, Takeshima-chō (2 blocks), Yosaku Yashiki, and as far as Jizōbashi street — all burned. This area was left looking like an open field.

△ North side of the same (river): Kayaba-chō, the Yakushidō (medicine hall) was damaged. At the Sannō Shrine, stone lanterns, side stones, and torii gate were broken, with other major damage. In the same area, Ura-Kayaba-chō suffered major damage, with collapsed houses for over half a block.

**[Left Page]**

In the same area, the sake warehouses along the riverside road suffered major damage.

- Item: It should be noted that Edo's Ryōgoku Bridge was first constructed in the 3rd year of Manji (1660) as a crossing on the boundary between Musashi and Shimōsa provinces. It spans 96 ken (approximately 175 meters), and the traffic of people across it never ceases. The neighborhoods at its eastern and western approaches are bustling and dazzling to behold, with entertainments for all four seasons never coming to an end.
On the 23rd day of the 11th month of Ansei 2 (1855, the Year of the Hare), the reconstruction work was at last completed. A person of great longevity was selected and appointed to the honor of performing the inaugural crossing (*watari-zome*). Truly, this must be called an auspicious occasion.

> *Ara-tama no / haru wa chitose no / tsuru yori mo / kasumi no hashi no / watari-zome shite*
> ("The bridge veiled in spring haze — its inaugural crossing surpasses even the thousand-year crane of the new year")
