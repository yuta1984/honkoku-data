Section Two

(The spirit of recovery) grows ever more vigorous and spirited. Since this arises entirely from the benevolent compassion of the great lord (the Shogun), it is recorded here simply so that the debt of gratitude owed to the nation shall not be forgotten.

**[Left Page]**
△ In the same area to the south: the middle residence (*nakayashiki*) of the Lord of Awa, the middle residence of Lord Toda Uneme-no-shō, and the lower residence (*shimoyashiki*) of Lord Matsudaira Shimōsa-no-kami suffered major damage, but no fires occurred. △ The Sanjūsangendō in the same area split in the middle of its ridge, the eaves collapsed, approximately seventy percent of the roof tiles fell fore and aft, with other extensive damage besides. △ From Kiba in the same area throughout the surrounding quarters, there were many collapsed and destroyed structures among both samurai residences and townspeople's houses — too numerous to record in full.

㊃ In the same area, Wakura, Saga-chō substitute land (*daichi*), Honjo Ishihara substitute land block, and the annex of Hirano-chō all burned. To the northwest, Imagawa-chō, Nagahori-chō, Mannen-chō, and the Shōgakuji Bridge thoroughfare suffered collapses and destructions. To the north of Tomioka Bridge, Yōgakuin, Hōzenji, Shingyōin, Kaifukuji, Zōrinji, Eineniji, Shōgakuji and other temples suffered major damage. In all the surrounding quarters, the number of collapsed and destroyed residences — both samurai and townspeople — was exceedingly great.

△ An emergency relief shelter (*osukuigoya*) was erected at Kaibe Daiku Shinjō.
- Item: 135 *kanmon* in cash — donated to this shelter — Kiba: Yorozuya Kazusuke
- Item: 800 *mon* per person, plus 20 bales of taro/sweet potatoes — Donor unknown, identity withheld
- Item: 2 barrels of pickled radish (*takuan*), 1 barrel of ginger, 1 barrel of *rakkyo* (pickled shallots), 3 barrels of pickled plum (*umeboshi*) — Shimōsa Province, Sōma District, Fusa Village: farmer Shichirōbei
- Item: 5 barrels of miso — Hongō Haruki-chō: Ōmitsu, Shinzaburō
　　 The donations from the five other locations besides the above are recorded under the entries for the respective temporary-residence districts of those individuals.
