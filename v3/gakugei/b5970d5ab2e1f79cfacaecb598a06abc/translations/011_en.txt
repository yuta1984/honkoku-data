The stone lanterns (within Hirakawa Tenjin Shrine's precincts) swayed and spun in place, as though they had been deliberately repositioned. This must truly be called a marvel. The surrounding areas saw major damage and numerous collapses in both samurai residences and townspeople's homes. △ In Kōji-machi, the main street side had some damage, but collapses were few. The back streets to the north and south of the same area are omitted. △ In Banchō, with its many streets running both vertically and horizontally, all had damage, collapses, and fallen buildings, too numerous to fully record. Around Kudan-zaka slope, Iidamachi, and Manaita-bashi bridge, there was especially severe damage and numerous collapses.

【Entry No. 42】　Within the Ogawa-chō area, at the corner of Ichibanhara, the residences of Lord Honda Bungo, Lord Kōke Toda Takejirō, and Lord Naitō Suruga burned. The front gate remained standing. The residence of Lord Sakakibara Shikibu was damaged. At Sanbanhara, the residences of Lord Matsudaira Buzen and Lord Hongō Tango burned. To the north of the same area, at Sarugaku-chō, the residence of Lord Hotta Bitchū burned. △ Among the hatamoto (direct retainers of the shogunate), the northernmost residence of Lord Washinosu Awaji (burned), with the fire stopping at the residences of Hasegawa and Arai. The residences of Lord Naitō in the same area and Lord Tanaka at the facing corner were spared. Across from the residence of Lord Matsudaira Bungo, the residences of Hanawa Sōetsu, Watanabe Sannnosuke, Isshiki Tangonokami, and Isshiki Kuninosuke and others burned. Furthermore, in front of the Hotta residence, the residences burned from that of the physician Nakarai Izumo, through Mizoguchi Hachigoro, Satō Kinenojō, Fuseya, Ōkubo, and as far as Tsuge. On the opposite side of the same area, residences from the Yoda household onward were spared, while those of Kan'orbe, Arakawa, Soga, Kondō, Aoki, Honda, Shinmi, Matsudaira, Kawauchi, Kobayashi, and Satō all burned. △ In the Ura-Jinbō-kōji alley behind the same area, residences from Mashimo, Nagasaka, Terashima, and as far as Arai Jinnonjō burned.

【Entry No. 43】　Within the Koishikawa gate, the residence of Lord Matsudaira Suruga burned. The group residences (kumiyashiki) in the same area, as well as those of Katō, Ōhara, Watanabe, and as far as Fuyuki, also burned. In the same area, the residences of Honma Heibei, Ōmori Yūzaburō, Honme, the kōke official Nakajō Nakatsukasa, Yamamoto, and Torii burned, while Kurokawa was half-burned and the fire stopped there. △ In Surugadai, there were damages in various places, but collapses and fallen buildings were relatively few. △ Within the Sujikaibashi gate, in the areas of Muramatsu-chō, Iwai-chō, Kuken-chō, Toshima-chō, the Yanagiwara district magistrate's residence, Hashimoto-chō, Benkei-bashi, and Konya-chō, there were numerous major damages and collapses.
