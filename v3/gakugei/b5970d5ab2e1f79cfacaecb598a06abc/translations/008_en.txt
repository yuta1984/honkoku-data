△ The damage to the upper, middle, and lower residences of the various lords in the Atagoshita and Yabukōji areas is too extensive to record in full.

△ Tentokuji Temple, including all its sub-temples, suffered damage. △ Mannenzan Seishōji Temple likewise. △ Atago Shrine sustained severe damage. △ San'enzan Zōjōji Temple, along with all its subsidiary halls, sustained severe damage, with numerous sections collapsed. △ The large and small samurai residences along Kiritōshi suffered major damage (details omitted). △ Kōnchin Temple in the same area also sustained severe damage.

【Entry No. 37】　The residences of the various lords to the south of Shinbashi suffered major damage (details omitted). △ Hongō Daichi-machi sustained severe damage, with collapsed structures. In Kenbō-machi to the south, there were so many collapsed houses as to give the impression of roughly one chō having been burned. From the residence of Matsudaira Hyōbu to the south there were rows of buildings; Fushimi-chō, Kaji-chō, Kubo-chō, Izumi-chō, Zen'emon-chō, and surrounding areas all sustained severe damage, with many collapsed houses.
