## [Left Folio]

△ The towns in front of Daionji Temple to the west of the same area also suffered tremendous collapse and fire damage, as described above. In the Tanaka and Shin-Torikoe areas, an extremely large number of houses were destroyed.

△ At Chōkokuji Temple in Chizuka-gō, the main shrine of the Washi Daimyōjin was damaged, and the monks' quarters and stone monuments suffered severe damage. The small estates and townhouses throughout this area also suffered great collapse.

△ In Ryūsenji-chō and the surrounding four directions, many houses collapsed.

**[Entry 21]** In the Yoshiwara, Nihon Tsutsumi, Tamachi, both sides of Amigasa Chaya Street for two blocks, Sodesuri Inari, the same Icchōme, and extending to the mouth of Miyabori — all burned. The townhouses remaining to the west of this area suffered major damage, equivalent in effect to being burned down.

**[Entry 22]** To the east of the same area, the main shrine of Mazaki Inari on the Sumida River sustained no damage; however, its torii gate and stone lanterns were shattered. In the Asajigahara area nearby, many houses in all directions collapsed. From the Hashiba ferry landing southward to the Zeniza (copper mint), the riverbank area along the Ōkawa (Sumida River) burned. On the western side, Fukujuin and Hōgenji Temple suffered severe damage — the monks' quarters collapsed and the fire spread as far as the temple gate district. In Imado-chō, the main halls of Rensōji, Myōkōji, and Chōshōji Temples were damaged, their monks' quarters severely so, with numerous other collapses throughout. Anshōji and Shōfukuji Temples sustained damage in many places.

**[Entry 23]** To the north of Imado Bridge in the same area, one block on the eastern side of Imado-chō burned. On the western side, Shōrinji, Hon'iji, and Keiyōji Temples suffered severe damage with many collapses. At all of the aforementioned temples, stone monuments and stone lanterns fell without exception, leaving scenes of such scattered devastation as to defy description. △ To the south: Sanyabashi, Jarib, Shin-Torikoe Icchōme, Kawarachō below it, and the village areas to the east of Matsuchiyama — all were completely destroyed.
