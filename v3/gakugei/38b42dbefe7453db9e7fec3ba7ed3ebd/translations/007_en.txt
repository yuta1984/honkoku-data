**APPENDIX**

Here again, while the city had not yet settled down after the earthquake, various single-sheet nishiki-e prints and small pamphlets began circulating from all directions, amounting to over three hundred and twenty different kinds in total. They were sold at illustrated-book shops and by peddlers at crossroads throughout the city. Everyone eagerly competed to purchase them. Before long, the authorities banned them as prohibited items, and all were ordered out of print. Nevertheless, because great Edo is so vast and prosperous, even after the ban, many copies of various kinds remained. I record one or two of them here at the end, as mementos, to be passed down to later generations as a comfort on rainy days.

"Miraculous Yuri-dashi" (The Marvelous Shake-Out Remedy)

I humbly offer this advertisement with nothing but spirit and vigor:

Now then, regarding this "Marvelous Yuri-dashi Kuzure" — some years ago, when we tested its shaking in Shinshū province, the tremors were so great and caused such tremendous hardship that we firmly decided never to push-shake or squeeze-shake it into other provinces again. However, in recent years, various confusingly similar counterfeit "kuzure" (collapses) have been appearing throughout the country. Particularly along the routes of Kyoto, Osaka, and the Tōkaidō highway, push-shaking has been carried out, and again, counterfeit kuzure has been pushed all the way to Edo — and on top of that, fire-prayers have been performed, and the conduct has been outrageously rough. We have therefore urgently issued orders to stop the shaking, and on our end have conducted a one-sided shaking. We have not, however, shaken too much; as of this October, we have given notice to the "shaker-child" (earthquake agent), so there is no need for the alarm of fleeing or sleeping outdoors. Please rest assured, relax, and with your spouse in good harmony — if you use the night-time shaking yourself, two or three doses each night, your descendants shall prosper, and you shall have no idle, good-for-nothing children. Please hold on tight, declare "Ah, what a wonderful world this has become!", and we humbly ask for your patronage.
