## New Edition: Shiritori Sugoroku (Word-Chain Game Board)
**Illustrated by Yoshifuji**

Submitted for approval: Meiji 20 (1887), Month, Day
42 Komagata-chō, Asakusa Ward
Artist and Publisher: Kodama Matashichi

---

### How to Play
(Start from the "furihajime" square at the lower right and proceed in a spiral pattern)

**START**

Peonies with Chinese lions, bamboo with tigers /
Treading on a tiger — Watōnai /
The Naitō family crest is the hanging wisteria /
Saigyō viewing wisteria, facing away /
Shelled clams, fool's pillar (barnacle) /
Pillars are found on the second floor and under the veranda /
Shitaya, Ueno, and mountain kudzu /
Kuzunoha Bunji is a rakugo storyteller /
The dendenden drum and the shō flute /
Emma (the King of Hell) goes "bon!" — it's the New Year! /

Lord Katsuyori bears the Takeda diamond crest /
The diamond-shaped mochi of March, the Doll Festival /
Festival with ten-thousand lanterns, festival floats /
Sea bream, bonito, octopus, and tuna /
London — a great foreign harbor /
What you climb is Mount Fuji /

Turn around three times and take a smoke /
Formal ceremony — Shōtayū, things of Ise /
Koto shop, shamisen, flute, and drum /
The Taikō (Toyotomi Hideyoshi) was the Regent (Kampaku) /
The shining white snake is at Yanagishima /
In the island purse, fifty ryō /
Gorō and Jūrō — the Soga Brothers /
The brothers' lacquer box and tobacco tray /
A bon season baby is a good child — go to sleep /

The courtesans of Shinagawa charge ten monme /
The iron-rod ball weighing ten monme /
Tamaya's fireworks are the original and greatest /
The one who lives under that name is Bashō-an /

Bean curd with thick sauce, and night-hawk soba noodles /
Market-price money goes roaring — "Mama, give me four mon!" /
If the delay passes, it's New Year's! (STAY) /
The treasure ship of New Year's /
On the treasure ship ride the Seven Gods of Fortune /
Empress Jingū, Takenouchi (no Sukune) /
The "Utsu" is Kenbishi, Nanatsu-Ume /

Plum, pine, and cherry belong to Sugawara /
Bundled in straw — the thrown shimada hairstyle /
Shimada, Kanaya — the great Ōi River /

It is because I love you that I come all the way from Kanda /
Coming to Fukakusa — the devotion of a hundred nights (STAY) /
Sake and snacks for six hundred — spend as you please /
"Oh well!" — wearing the three-ridge hat tilted to the side /
Wearing it upright and shaking — the woman of Sagami /
A widowed woman blooms like a flower /

Why tie your horse to the blossoming cherry tree? /
Tie the hairpiece — and the great elephant stops here /

**GOAL (Agari)**

---

### Rules for Overshooting the Goal

- Overshoot by 1: Return to "the great elephant"
- Overshoot by 2: Return to "the blossoming cherry"
- Overshoot by 3: Return to "widowed woman"
- Overshoot by 4: Return to "wearing it upright and shaking"
- Overshoot by 5: Return to "Oh well, the three-ridge hat"
