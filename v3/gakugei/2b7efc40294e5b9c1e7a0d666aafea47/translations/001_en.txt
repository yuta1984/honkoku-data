### How to Play
[Read from the "Start" square in the lower right, proceeding around the board. Read the column titles together with the text in each square in sequence.]

---

**START**

Peonies with Chinese lions; bamboo with tigers /
Treading on a tiger: Watōnai [the hero] /
Lord Naitō's family crest is the drooping wisteria /
Gazing at wisteria: the monk Saigyō, turning away /
"Mukōmi" [facing away] is a clam; "baka-bashira" [fool's post] /
A post [hashira] is in the second floor and under the veranda /
Shitaya and Ueno's mountain kudzu [yamakatsura] /
Katsura Bunji is a rakugo storyteller [hanashika] /
The "denden" drum and the shō flute /
Enma [King of Hell] comes at Obon and New Year's

Lord Katsuyori's emblem is the Takeda diamond crest /
The diamond-shaped rice cake of the third month: the Doll Festival /
The Doll Festival's lanterns and festival floats /
Sea bream, bonito, octopus, and tuna /
London — a great harbor of a foreign land /
The one people climb [tozan] is Mount Fuji

"Three times around, then have a smoke" /
Properly so, Shōtayu [a performer's name], things of Ise /
Koto, shamisen, flute, and drums /
The Taikō [Toyotomi Hideyoshi] was the Imperial Regent [Kampaku] /
The white snake's glow is at Yanagishima /
In the island wallet: fifty ryō

Gorō and Jūrō — the Soga Brothers /
[The brothers'] sewing box, tobacco tray /
"A sleepy child is a good child — go to sleep now"

To the Shinagawa courtesans: ten monme /
Ten monme musket ball /
Tamaya's fireworks are the great originator /
The haiku master's dwelling is Bashō-an [Bashō's Hut]

Bean curd in thick sauce, and nighthawk soba noodles /
"The money from speculation is all spent — hey, Ma, give me four mon!" /
If [the money] runs out, wait for New Year's (Stay/Forfeit a turn)

The New Year's treasure ship /
On the treasure ship: the Seven Lucky Gods /
Empress Jingū and Takenouchi [no Sukune] /
The drawn crest is the Kenbishi; the Seven Plums [Nanatsu Ume]

Plum, pine, and cherry blossom — [all associated with] Sugawara [no Michizane] /
Bound in straw: the "nage-shimada" [a hairstyle] /
Shimada, Kanaya — the Ōi River

"It's only because she's dear to me that I travel all the way from Kanda" /
Commuting through Fukakusa — a hundred nights of devotion (Stay/Forfeit a turn)

"With sake and snacks, spend six hundred freely, as you please" /
"Well, never mind" — wearing the three-tiered sedge hat, cocked to the side /
Wearing it tilted forward — the woman of Sagami /
A woman widowed [will eventually see better days]: flowers bloom for the widow

On the blooming cherry tree — why do you tether your horse? /
To the tethering-post of false hair [katsura/wig], the great elephant stops

**GOAL**

---

**Return Rules (when dice overshoot the goal):**

Roll 1 over → Return to "Great Elephant"
Roll 2 over → Return to "Blooming Cherry Blossoms"
Roll 3 over → Return to "Woman Widowed"
Roll 4 over → Return to "Wearing it tilted forward"
Roll 5 over → Return to "Well, never mind — three-tiered hat"
