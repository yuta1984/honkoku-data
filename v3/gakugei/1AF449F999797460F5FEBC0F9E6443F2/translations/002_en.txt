【Preface to the Kokon Sanpōki】

Numbers begin at one and end at nine. One is the progenitor of nine, and nine is the foundation of eighty-one. In ancient times, Fuxi, who succeeded to Heaven and became king, received the River Diagram (Hetu) and drew the Eight Trigrams.
