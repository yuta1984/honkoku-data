**Right folio:**
(Attributed to: Izumi Shikibu)
(Heading: "Though steeped in darkness, it is not blackened")

Both the blossoms grieve me,
and the storm is cruel to bear—
yet together they conspire:
because they scatter, they beckon one another;
because they beckon, they scatter.

**Left folio:**
(Attributed to: Nichiren Shōnin)
(Heading: "Compassionate love")

The father strikes;
the mother, wincing in pain,
flees away—
which of the two
will people see
as the one who has acted cruelly?
