【Right Page】
《Annotation: The noble person seeks the cause within themselves》

　　Even if you feel wronged,
　　　if you yourself
　　do not forget the other person,
　　　then surely—
　　can the bond between you
　　　ever truly
　　　　be broken?
　　　（No, it cannot.）


【Left Page】
《Annotation: The benevolent person has no enemies》

　　Show compassion—
　　　for compassion
　　is not only
　　　for the sake of others.
　　To think kindly of others
　　　is to think kindly
　　　　of yourself.
