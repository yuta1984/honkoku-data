【Right Page】
《Hōjō Yasutoki》　"Do not befriend those who are not your equal (in virtue)" (Analects of Confucius)

　People, simply put,
　　　ought to choose their friends wisely—
　　　　　just look at the mugwort
　　　　　　　growing amid the hemp of morning.

(A person's character is shaped entirely by the company they keep. Consider the mugwort growing among the hemp plants in the morning field — supported by the upright hemp around it, even the wayward mugwort grows straight and tall.)

【Left Page】
　"When the father is strict, the child will be filial."

　From the time of tender youth,
　　　parents begin to sow (virtue's seeds)—
　a heart that wanders at its will
　　　is but a patchwork bridge.

(From earliest childhood, parents begin to instill virtue — but a heart left to its own wayward devices remains as unreliable as a bridge of makeshift planks. Only when a father is firm and strict in guidance will his child grow to be truly filial.)
