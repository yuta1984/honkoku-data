【Right Page】
《Hōjō Yasutoki》　"Do not make friends with those who are not your equal (not as good as yourself)" (Analects of Confucius)

【Upper verse】
　People, truly,
　　　are shaped by nothing
　　　　so much as
　　　　　the friends
　　　　　　they keep—
　　　this is how it is.

(What a person becomes — for good or ill — depends above all on the company they keep and the friends they choose.)

【Lower verse】
　Even looking
　　　at the mugwort
　　　　growing up
　　　　　amid the hemp—

(One can see the truth of this just by observing mugwort that grows among stalks of hemp — the naturally crooked mugwort, when it grows surrounded by straight hemp, is itself drawn upward and straight. So too does one's environment and company determine one's character.)

【Left Page】
　　　　"When the father is strict, the child is filial."

　From the time
　　　　of tender youth,
　　　the influence a parent
　　　　　begins to lay down—
　left to a wandering heart,
　　　　it is but a patchwork bridge.

(From earliest childhood, the guidance a parent begins to give their child — if left to a distracted and undisciplined heart, the child's moral character becomes like a bridge cobbled together from mismatched planks: precarious and unreliable. When a father is firm and strict in his upbringing, the child in turn grows up to be filial and virtuous.)
