【Right Page】
《Prince Wakana (Jakusei Shinnō)》　Illustrious Virtue (明徳 meitoku)

　Do not think
　　　　　that the light
　　　　　　　came only after the clouds cleared—
　from the very beginning,
　　　　the moon of the lingering dawn
　　　was already there in the sky.

(Do not mistake the clearing of clouds for the source of the moonlight. The moon of the dawning sky was there all along. — So too is virtue: it is not something bestowed from without, but something originally inherent within the human heart.)

【Left Page】
《Former Archbishop Jien》　"Without instruction, one is close to the birds and beasts."

　How joyful it is
　　　　to have been born
　into this world as a human being—
　yet what a wretched heart is mine,
　　　　to let that joy
　go utterly to waste.

(What a sorrowful thing it is that my own heart squanders the precious gift and the joy of having been born into this world as a human being, through idleness and worthless conduct.)
