【Right Page】
　　《Annotation: Misfortune and fortune have no fixed gate》

　　Good and ill—
　　　the gate through which they come and go
　　　　　is not fixed;
　　they follow
　　　wherever one's own self
　　　　beckons them

【Left Page】
《Saigyō Hōshi》　　　Be gentle in blaming others

　　O my heart,
　　　that thinks only with resentment
　　　　of others as cruel—
　　know that it is
　　　I myself
　　　　who am the wretched one
