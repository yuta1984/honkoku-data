【Right Page】
《Annotation: It is not that one is unable; it is simply that one does not do it》

　　There is truly nothing
　　　in this world
　　that one "cannot" do——
　　　to say "I cannot"
　　is, in the end,
　　　simply to say
　　"I will not"

【Left Page】
《Annotation: He did not abandon his one joy》

　　When I look upward,
　　　I see only desires
　　yet unfulfilled——

　　there are those in this world
　　　who do not have
