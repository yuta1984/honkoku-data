【Right Page】
《Heading: Minamoto no Sanmi Yorimasa》　　　　《Annotation: A name is the guest/expression of reality》

　　Among the trees
　　　of the deep mountains,
　　　　it was not apparent—
　　yet the cherry tree
　　　revealed itself
　　　　in its blossoms


【Left Page】
《Annotation: To guard softness is called strength》

　　Do not boast before the world
　　　and wound others—
　　　　the heart that endures
　　and bears hardship
　　　is the truly strong one,
　　　　worthy of admiration
