【Right Page】
《Lord Ietaka》　"Not to study is the child's fault"

　It is through the lazy thought
　　　　of "I'll do it tomorrow"
　　　　　　that we fall into negligence—
　and so we pass today
　　in vain and idle hours,
　　　　　　how fleeting and wasteful.

(Because of the complacent thought "I can always do it tomorrow," one falls into idleness, and the precious day of today slips away empty and unproductive. How transient and regrettable that is.)

【Left Page】
《The Venerable Genshin》　"To dwell among the benevolent is beautiful" (The Analects)

　Making my dwelling
　　　　at the foot of a tree,
　　　　　　it came about naturally—
　I became one
　　who gazes upon
　　　　　　the blossoms.

(By simply making one's home at the base of a tree, one naturally becomes a person who appreciates and admires the blossoms. — This reflects the Confucian teaching that living in a virtuous environment, among good people and good influences, naturally nurtures one into a person of virtue and refined sensibility.)
