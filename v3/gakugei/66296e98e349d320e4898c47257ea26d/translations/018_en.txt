【Right Page】
　　《Annotation: Those who favor victory will inevitably meet defeat》

　　Just as the heavy dew
　　　　upon the grass blades
　　falls in its own due measure—
　　　so too does the dew that settles
　　　　　　　　finds its weight
　　　　in this world of ours

【Left Page】
《Musō Kokushi》　　　Do not attack the faults of others

　　My own heart
　　　is something
　　　　that cannot be
　　　　　　tamed to my will—
　　if another's turning away
　　　　　is to be called a fault,
　　　　　　　　then so be it
