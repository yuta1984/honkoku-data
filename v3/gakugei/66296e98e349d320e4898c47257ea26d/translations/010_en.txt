【Right Page】
《[Attribution illegible]》　"Do not make the heart one's master / Do not leave the heart unguided by a teacher"

　If you are led astray
　　　　by your own heart,
　　　　　　you may well fall
　　　　　　　　into a wicked path—
　do not slacken the reins
　　　　on the steed of your heart.

(If one simply follows wherever one's own heart leads, one may easily stray into the path of wickedness. Do not loosen the reins on the horse that is your heart — keep it firmly under discipline and guidance.)

【Left Page】
《The Venerable Myōe (Kōben Shōnin)》　"Human nature is good" (Mencius)

　Long ago, someone
　　　　who knew the hearts of people—
　　　　　　"If dyed, it takes the color"—
　made it so.

(Long ago, there was one who truly understood the human heart — knowing that, just as white cloth takes on whatever color it is dyed with, the human heart too can be dyed in the color of goodness. Human nature is fundamentally good, and with proper teaching and a virtuous environment, it will be dyed in virtue's hue.)
