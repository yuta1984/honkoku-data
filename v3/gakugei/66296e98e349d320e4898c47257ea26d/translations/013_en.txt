【Right Page】
《Lord Minamoto no Yorinobu》　"Rashness and haste bring no achievement."

　Had I not rushed,
　　　I need not have been drenched—
　　　　　for behind the traveler,
　the passing shower
　　　over the field-path
　　　　　has already cleared.

(Had one not hurried out in haste, one would never have been caught in the rain — for the shower that swept the field-path has already passed and cleared behind the very traveler who rushed through it. So too with rashness in all things: impatience leads one into hardship that patience would have spared.)

【Left Page】
《Lord Kōjun》　"Where others succeed in one attempt, I shall make it one hundred."

　If one walks on
　　　without faltering,
　the far end
　　　of a thousand leagues
　　　　shall come into sight—
　　　　　　even at
　　　　　　　the slow pace
　　　　　　　　of an ox.

(If one continues walking without slackening, even the end of a thousand leagues shall eventually come into view — even if one's pace is no faster than that of an ox. Where others may master something in one attempt, one who persists a hundredfold will, however slowly, surely reach even the most distant goal.)
