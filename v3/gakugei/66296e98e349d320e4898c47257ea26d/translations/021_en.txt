【Right Page】
《□□□□》　　　　《Annotation: In times of fortune and times of hardship, the true nature of friendship is revealed》

　　How moving—
　　　indeed,
　　　　would that I had
　　　　　friends who cling to me
　　　　　　in times of sorrow;
　　a person's warmth and feeling
　　　endures only
　　　　so long as one
　　　　　is of the world

【Left Page】
《□□□□》　　　　《Annotation: It is difficult to be poor and yet without resentment》

　　Do not resent
　　　a world in which
　　　　things fall short—
　　even the duck,
　　　with its short legs,
　　　has shallows enough
　　　　in which to float
