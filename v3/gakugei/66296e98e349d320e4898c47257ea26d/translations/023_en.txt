【Right Page】
《Annotation: Do not forget the grace/kindness you have received》

　　Whether painful events
　　　or joyful ones—
　　once they have passed,
　　　one no longer
　　remembers how it felt
　　　at the time
　　（And so, one must never forget the kindness one has been shown）


【Left Page】
《Annotation: Ten thousand virtues are not equal to a single act of forbearance》

　　Press a blade
　　　against
　　your heaving,
　　　seething breast—
　　and hold back
　　　what you wish to say,
　　　　for just a moment
