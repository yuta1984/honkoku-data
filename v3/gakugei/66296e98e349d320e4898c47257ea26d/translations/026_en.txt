【Right Page】
《Annotation: One who knows sufficiency is rich》

　　When things are enough,
　　　if you surrender to that sense of sufficiency,
　　you will never truly be satisfied—
　　but the one who finds sufficiency
　　　even in want
　　lives in true peace


【Left Page】
《Annotation: Learning from below, one attains what is above》

　　Though the arms grow heavy
　　　and weary from drawing,
　　draw on, draw on—
　　　for in the end
　　the water of the mountain well
　　　will run clear
