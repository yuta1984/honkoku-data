**Right folio:**
(Heading: "Do not deceive yourself")

One might say to others,
"Such a reputation is groundless,
a name I do not deserve"—
but if one were to ask
one's own heart,
what answer could one give?

**Left folio:**
(Heading: "Return it whole, as it was received")

Even to my own eyes,
how dear it is to me—
this keepsake left behind
by one now gone,
cherished above all others
as something they especially
set aside for me.
