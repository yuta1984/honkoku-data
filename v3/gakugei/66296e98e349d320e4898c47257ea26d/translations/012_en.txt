【Right Page】
　　　　"What baseness is there in this?" (Analects of Confucius)

　Plant and see—
　　　there is no village
　　　　where flowers will not grow.
　　　It is from within the heart
　　　　that lowliness comes.

(Plant, and you shall see — there is no village where flowers cannot grow. With the right spirit and intention, flowers bloom anywhere. If one is base or lowly, it is not because of one's birth or circumstances, but because of the disposition of one's own heart.)

【Left Page】
《Imperial Composition of Emperor Go-Mizunoo》　"Only by raising a child does one truly come to know the grace of one's own parents."

　When the child crawls, we long
　　　for it to stand;
　when it stands, we pray
　　　for it to walk—
　all the while forgetting
　　　the age that gathers
　　　　upon our own bodies.

(When a child begins to crawl, we yearn for it to stand; when it stands, we yearn for it to walk — consumed entirely by hopes for our child, we forget altogether the years that are accumulating upon ourselves. Only through raising a child does one come to understand, at last, the profound depth of the love and grace one's own parents gave.)
