(Inspired by the words of Zengzi from the Analects of Confucius — daily self-reflection on one's conduct:)

Those who are not faithful,
　who possess no sincerity —
　　　such people merely
remain
　　in the fault
　　　of not having been taught and not having learned.

(Those who lack integrity and sincerity are simply guilty of never having properly received and internalized righteous teaching.)
