**Muneoka Village (宗岡里) — Uchikawa River (内川)**

The "Iroha Conduit" (*Iroha-toi*): In former times, the lord of this district had a wooden conduit constructed from Hikimata across the river to the land of Muneoka, in order to assist agricultural irrigation. Because the conduit was hung in forty-eight sections, it came to bear this name, it is said.

*(Marginal note)* The *Kaikoku Zakki* (Miscellaneous Notes on Traveling the Provinces) records: "As I was passing through the place called Muneoka, I saw the evening
