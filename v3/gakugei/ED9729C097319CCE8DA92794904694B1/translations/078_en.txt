Tsuki Shrine (Tsuki no Miya)

This is a shrine listed in the *Engishiki* (the official register of shrines under the延喜式). In the present day, it is mistakenly referred to as "Tsukiyomi (Moon-Reading)" and associated with the "Twenty-Third Night" observance.
