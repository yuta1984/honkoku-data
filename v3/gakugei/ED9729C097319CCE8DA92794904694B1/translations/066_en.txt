**[Right Folio — Classical Poem Inscribed on the Illustration]**

From the *Senzaishū* (Collection of a Thousand Years):

*Musashino no / Horikane no i mo / aru mono wo / ureshiku mizu no / chikaduki ni keri*

(Even the well of Horikane on Musashino Plain does exist after all — how joyful it is that water has now drawn near!)

　　　　　　　　— Shunzei

　　　　　　　Horikane Village
　　　　　　　　　　(Haiden / Oratory)
