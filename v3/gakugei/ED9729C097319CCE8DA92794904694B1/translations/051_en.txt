### [Right Page]
Kume River (*Kumekawa*)

### [Left Page]

**From *Kaikoku Zakki* (Miscellaneous Notes on Travels through the Provinces):**

"There is a place called Kumekume River. The households in the village have no wells or the like; they draw only from this river for their daily needs morning and evening, I was told — and so:

> The villagers call it the Kumekume River — as evening falls, the water turns to pressing ice

— Dōkō Jugo

[Variant reading: *kōri mo zo suru* — "the water even freezes"]
