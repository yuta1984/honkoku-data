**[Double-Page Spread: Illustrated Map of the Temple Grounds]**

**[Right folio — names of structures and features within the precinct]**

- Shinkōji (真光寺) — the temple proper
- Yōgō Kajisui (影向加持水) — "Sacred Manifestation Healing Water" (a sacred spring or well)
- Hondō (本堂) — Main Hall
- Tsuyandō (通夜堂) — All-Night Vigil Hall
- Jizō (地蔵) — Jizō Bodhisattva (statue or small shrine)
- Chikai-zakura (誓桜) — "Vow Cherry Tree" (a sacred cherry tree)
- Inari (いなり／稲荷) — Inari Shrine

**[Left folio — names of features along the approach and outer precincts]**

- Yamaguchi Kannon (山口観音) — title label for the overall site
- Kumano (くまの／熊野) — Kumano Shrine
- Niōmon (二王門) — Two Kings Gate (temple gate guarded by Niō, the two guardian kings)
- Chaya (茶屋) — Teahouse / Refreshment stall
- Emma (えんま／閻魔) — Enma (Yama, King of Hell; statue or shrine)
- Benten (弁天) — Benzaiten (goddess of music, water, and fortune; shrine)
