(Continued from the previous page) Reflecting deeply upon this matter: even the rare treasure of the Linked-City jade (*Renjo no chinpo*) and the night-luminous *bi*-disk, if stored away uselessly in a box, will shed no light upon the world. For this reason, I have annotated the meaning of the words, rendered them in illustrations, recorded the genealogy of Shrine Master Moritake and included his portrait, and had the whole carved onto woodblocks so that it might be widely available as an aid to the learning of young children. Surely this will serve as a guidepost along the way of loyalty and filial piety — with this conviction, the proofreading was completed at Kōkodō.
