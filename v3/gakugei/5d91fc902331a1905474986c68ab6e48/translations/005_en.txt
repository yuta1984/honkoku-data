[Upper section]

The way of filial piety is exceedingly vast, encompassing a myriad of ever-changing circumstances, making it difficult to record each instance individually. It is described in detail in the *Classic of Filial Piety* (*Kōkyō*), the *Analects* (*Rongo*), and the *Book of Rites* (*Raiki*), among others. Here (we note in particular) that within the filial devotion a parent shows toward a child — in the love and care a parent bestows — the way of cherishing one's children is also contained. Furthermore, not only in the human world, but also the tiger and wolf that run a thousand leagues, and even the pheasant crossing the burned plain — all are willing to give their lives for the sake of their young. This must be the compassion inherent in all living things. If, moreover, one were to serve one's sovereign with the same obedient heart with which one practices filial piety toward one's parents and fraternal devotion toward one's elder brother, and were to govern the people with the same heart of care with which one nurtures one's younger siblings and cherishes one's children —
