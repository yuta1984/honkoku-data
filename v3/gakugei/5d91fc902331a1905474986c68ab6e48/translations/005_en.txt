[Left folio]

[Upper section]

The way of filial piety is exceedingly broad. There are a thousand changes and ten thousand transformations within it, and it is difficult to record each instance one by one. It has been written about in detail in such works as the *Classic of Filial Piety* (*Xiaojing*), the *Analects* (*Lunyu*), and the *Book of Rites* (*Liji*). Here, within the piety that parents show — that is, within the love parents bear — is also contained the way of cherishing one's children. Moreover, to say nothing of human beings: even the tiger and wolf that race a thousand leagues, and even the pheasant that forages across the burned fields, will lay down their lives for their young — is this not the compassion of all living things? Furthermore, if one were to serve one's lord with the very heart of "honoring one's parents, fulfilling the way of the younger sibling toward the elder, and following those above," and were to govern the people with the heart that protects one's younger siblings and cherishes one's children —
