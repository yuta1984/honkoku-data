Preface to the *Yononaka Hyakushu Eshō*

These one hundred verses were composed by Moritake, the Shinto priest (kannushi) of the Arakida clan, who served as the *ichino negi* (First Priest) of the Grand Shrine of Ise — holding the office of Sonoda Chief (長官). Devoting himself to the service of sacred rites and offering prayers on behalf of the Imperial Court, he used his moments of leisure to sit at a bright window before a clean writing desk, where he studied Chinese classics and cultivated a love of the way of Japanese poetry (*shikishima no michi*), composing and chanting verses without cease. He also mastered the inner mysteries of *renga* (linked verse), and his work was selected for inclusion in the *Shinsen Tsukubashū*.
