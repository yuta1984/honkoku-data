【Right Folio】

**Return poem (Episode 121, reply verse)**

　*Uguisu no / hana wo nu fu chō / kasa wa ina* / *omoi wo tsuke yo / hoshite kaesan*

(A hat woven of the blossoms the warbler is said to stitch — no, I have no need of that. I shall dry (my soaked robe) and send it back to you, with all my feelings attached to it.)

---

**Episode 122**

Long ago, a man (sent a poem) to a person who had broken a promise:

　*Yamashiro no / Ide no tamamizu / te ni musubi* / *tanomishi kai mo / naki yo nari keri*

(Like the jewelled waters of Ide in Yamashiro, which I cupped in my hands and trusted — I put my faith in you, yet this world has proved to be one in which such trust goes unrewarded.)

He sent this, yet received no reply.

---

**Episode 123**

Long ago, there was a man. He had begun to grow weary of a woman with whom he had lived in Fukakusa — or perhaps he had set his heart upon someone else — and he composed this poem:

　*Toshi wo hete / sumikoshi sato wo / idete inaba* / *itodo Fukakusa / no to ya nari nan*

(If I were to leave and go away from this village where I have lived these many years, would not Fukakusa become, more than ever, nothing but a desolate moor?)

The woman replied:

　*No to naraba / uzura to nari te / naki oran* / *kari ni dani ya wa / kimi wa kozaran*

(If it is to become a moor, then I shall become a quail and cry out within it — would you not come, if only to hunt?)

Moved by this poem, his resolve to leave dissolved entirely.

---

**Episode 124**

Long ago, a man composed the following — it is unclear what circumstances prompted it:

　*Omou koto / iwade zo tada ni / yami nu beki* / *ware to hitoshiki / hito shi nakereba*

(All that I think and feel must simply pass away, left unspoken — for there is no one in this world who is the equal of myself [who could truly understand me].)

---

**Episode 125**

Long ago, a man fell gravely ill and felt that he was likely to die, and so he composed:

　*Tsui ni yuku / michi to wa kanete / kikishi kado* / *kinō kyō to wa / omowazari keri*

(That there is in the end a road we all must travel — this I had long heard tell. Yet I had not thought that road would come for me as soon as yesterday or today.)

**Colophon / Postscript**

In recent times it has come about that this work is taken to be the origin of the tradition of the *kari no tsukai* (hunting envoy); however, this is merely a notion held by people of later generations and should by no means be adopted. As for this tale, the explanations of scholars of old differ from one another: some say it was written in the hand of the Zaichūjō (Ariwara no Narihira himself), while others claim it was composed by the brush of Ise (Lady Ise). Given that there are things which have been written down and then lost, one should not rashly seek to determine its author — for such a person belongs to high antiquity. One need only take pleasure in the beauty of its *shikka* (flowers of language) and its words.

**[Middle section: waka poems]**

*Ōshikōchi no Mitsune*

　*Toshi goto ni / au to wa suredo / Tanabata no* / *nuru yo no kazu zo / sukunakari keru*

(Though every year they say the two stars meet at Tanabata — how very few are the nights they actually sleep together.)

*Nōin Hōshi* (The Priest Nōin)

　*Aki no yo wo / ari nagara / nagaki mono to wa* / *kage hoshi-ai no / minu hito no iu ni zo*

(People say the autumn night is long — but that is what those who have never seen the meeting of the shadow-stars say. [For those who wait, the night passes all too swiftly.])

**[Lower section: waka poems]**

*Minbu-kyō Tamefuji* (Minister of Popular Affairs, Tamefuji)

　*Ama no kawa / sono minagami wa / au seba / hate mo / omou to zo arashi*

(Even if the headwaters of the Milky Way were to run dry — the longing to meet, the desire, could never be exhausted.)

*Nakatsukasa-kyō Shinnō* (Prince of the Ministry of Central Affairs)

　*Tanabata no / koi ya tsumorite / Ama no Kawa* / *mare naru naka no / fuchi to nari ran*

(Has the love of Tanabata, piling up and accumulating, become a deep pool — rare and profound — in the midst of the Milky Way?)
