**Episode 114**

Long ago, when the Emperor of Ninna (Emperor Kōkō) made a royal progress to the Seri River, an elderly courtier attended him. Though such active service was no longer fitting for a man of his age, since he had served the court continuously from the very beginning, he served as a falconer for the great hawk. He wrote the following on the sleeve of his suriginu hunting robe:

　*Oi nasabi / hito togame so / karigoromo* / *kyō bakari to zo / tazu mo naki naru*

(Do not reproach me for this, people — though I am old and worn, I wear my hunting robe. Even the cranes cry out as if to say: "today alone, today alone.")

The Emperor's expression turned troubled. The old man had composed this poem thinking of his own advanced age — but those who had not understood its meaning had apparently let it pass by unheeded.

**Episode 115**

Long ago, a man and a woman were living together in the province of Michinoku (the far north). When the man said he intended to return to the capital, the woman was deeply saddened, and wishing at least to give him a farewell drink (a send-off for his horse), she sat herself down and, at a place called Miyakoshima, gave him wine to drink and composed:

　*Oki ni ite / mi wo yaku yori mo / kanashiki wa* / *Miyakoshima be no / wakare nari keri*

(More agonizing even than burning one's own body out on the open sea — such is this parting at the shores of Miyakoshima.)

**Episode 116**

Long ago, a man wandered aimlessly all the way to the province of Michinoku. He sent word to the one he loved in the capital:

　*Namima yori / miyuru kojima no / hama hisashi* / *hisashiku nari nu / kimi ni ahi mite*

(Like the shore of the little island glimpsed between the waves — so distant and so far — it has been a long, long time since I have seen your face.)

He sent word that "all things have now settled and become well."

**Episode 117**

Long ago, an Emperor made a royal progress to Sumiyoshi. (He composed:)

　*Ware mite mo / hisashiku nari nu / Sumiyoshi no* / *kishi no himematsu / ikuyo he nu ran*

(Even since I myself last saw it, long years have passed — the little pine on the shore at Sumiyoshi: how many generations might it have lived through?)

Thereupon, a divine oracle descended:

　*Mutsumashi to / kimi wa shirana mi / mizugaki no* / *hisashiki yo yori / iwai some te ki*

(You did not know that I have held it dear — yet from ages long past, since the sacred fence was first raised, I have been venerating it still.)

**Episode 118**

Long ago, a man, who had long sent no word at all, now wrote saying: "My heart has not forgotten you. I intend to come." In response (the woman composed):

　*Tamakatsura / hau ki amata ni / nari nu reba* / *taenu kokoro no / ureshige mo nashi*

(The jewel-vine now has so very many trees to climb upon — and so, your heart which "can bear it no longer" brings me no joy whatsoever.)

---

**Episode 119**

Long ago, a woman, looking upon the keepsakes left behind by a faithless man, composed:

　*Katami koso / ima wa ada nare / kore nakuba* / *wasururu toki mo / arashi mono wo*

(These keepsakes are now themselves the fickle, tormenting things. Were it not for them, there might at least come a time when I could forget you.)

---

**Episode 120**

Long ago, a man, believing that a certain woman had not yet become known to the world (i.e., was unmarried or unattached), had secretly spoken words of love to her at someone's residence. After some time had passed, he composed:

　*Ōmi naru / Tsukuma no matsuri / toku se nan* / *tsurenaki hito no / nabe no kazu min*

(Let the Tsukuma Festival of Ōmi come quickly — I want to count the pots of that cold-hearted woman and see how many men she has known.)

---

**Episode 121**

Long ago, a man, seeing someone emerge from the Umetsubo (Plum-jar Pavilion) soaked through by the rain, composed:

　*Uguisu no / hana wo nu fu chō / kasa mo ga na* / *nureru hito ni / kisete kahesan*

(If only I had a hat woven of the blossoms that the warbler is said to stitch together — I would put it on that rain-drenched person and send them safely home.)
