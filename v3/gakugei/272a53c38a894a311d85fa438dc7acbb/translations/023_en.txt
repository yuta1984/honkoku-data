**Notes on Reading the Ise Monogatari (Yomikuse)**

▲Section 49: "ito okashige" should be read with voiced consonants. The character "ka" in "urawakemi" should be read with an unvoiced consonant.

▲Section 50: "adakurabe" should be read with voiced consonants. "Katami" should be read [with "ka" and "ta" lengthened, and "mi" voiced]—this means "mutually/reciprocally." Reading "katami" with an unvoiced "mi" refers to "keepsake/memento."

▲Section 55: "Eumajiu"—this means that something ultimately will not come into one's possession. Reading it as "yō" is incorrect.

▲Section 58: "Miyahara" should be read with voiced consonants. Read as "sudaku." "Ochiho tazura" should be read voiced. "Yukamashi" should be read with unvoiced consonants.

▲Section 60: "Shisō no kannin" should be read with unvoiced consonants. Read "kannin" as a complete unit, then continue with "sate me nite." The word written is "妻" (wife/spouse). "Sarazuba" means "sa arazuba" (if it were not so).

▲Section 63: Should be read as "saburau." "御男" should be read as "on'wonoko."

▲Section 65: "Makate" should be read as "makante."

▲Section 69: "Sokonigosasakeri" should be read with voiced consonants.

▲Section 77: "Mehatakai nagara" should be read with voiced consonants.

▲Section 78: Should be read as "Yamashina no Senshi no Mikoto."

▲Section 81: "Taijiki" should be read with voiced consonants.

▲Section 82: "Yamato uda" should be read voiced—it refers to Yamato Uda (a place name). "Sono ki no moto" should be read as "kono moto." "Notamaukereba" should be read voiced. "Kahekahe zuji tamau"—"kahesukahesu" should be read unvoiced. "Mine mo tahira" should be read as "taira."

▲Section 85: "Kotodatsu" should be read voiced. "Kohosu ga goto furite" should be read voiced.

▲Section 87: Should be read as "yomikeru sono koto." "Iza kono yama" should be read voiced. "I naka hito wa" should be read as "inakabito."

▲Section 93: "Auna auna" should be read as "auna auna."

▲Section 95: "Nani shite" should be read as "nan to shite."

▲Section 97: "Ōi mōchigimi" should be read voiced.

▲Section 101: "Marauto sane" should be read voiced.

▲Section 108: "Kaze fukeba towa" should be read as "ni."

▲Section 109: "Kohin to ka mishi" should be read with unvoiced consonants.

▲Section 111: "Torafu yō ni te" should be read as "tomurau." "Saru to nigenaku" should be read voiced.

**"Section 49"**

Long ago, a man, gazing upon his sister who was remarkably charming in appearance, composed:

*Young grass that appears to be growing up beautifully and well—I cannot help but think of someone plucking and binding it for themselves.*

He presented this poem to her. In reply:

*Like new young shoots of grass, what a surprising verse this is—you have been thinking such innocent, guileless thoughts.*

**"Section 50"**

Long ago, there was a man. Resenting someone who resented him:

*Even if you were to stack bird's eggs ten by ten in ten layers (treating me so harshly), would I ever yearn for someone who does not care for me? Never.*

When he said this, (she replied):

*Morning dew may linger and remain awhile, but who in this world can truly place their hope in another?*

Again the man:

*Even if last year's cherry blossoms did not scatter in the blowing wind—ah, yours alone is a heart so difficult to rely upon.*

Again the woman's reply:

*More fleeting than writing numbers on flowing water is the act of longing for one who does not long for you.*

Again the man (continues)
