[Upper Section: Reading Notes on the Ise Monogatari]

Reading Conventions for the Ise Monogatari
(Notes on correct pronunciation and interpretation)

▲ Section 49: "Ito okashige" should be read with voiced consonants (nigori). The character "ka" in "urawakame" should be read with unvoiced consonants (sumu).

▲ Section 50: "Adakurabe" should be voiced. It should be read as "katato-to," meaning "the mutual feeling/heart." Reading it as "katami" is an error (it is a matter of new interpretation).

▲ Section 55: "Eumajiu" — this ultimately means "something that shall not come into my hands." Reading it as "yō" is incorrect.

▲ Section 58: "Miyahara" should be voiced. Read as "sudaku." "Ochibotazura" should be voiced. "Yukamashi" should be read unvoiced.

▲ Section 60: "Shisō no tomo (official?)" should be read unvoiced. Read and pause at "friend/companion," then continue with "sate me ni te." (It is written as "wife/spouse.") "Sarasuba" means "sa arasuba" (otherwise/if that were not so).

▲ Section 63: Should be read as "saburō." "Onwonoko" (御男) should be read as "on-wo-no-ko."

▲ Section 65: "Makate" should be read as "makante."

▲ Section 69: "Soko ni gosasekeri" should be voiced.

▲ Section 77: "Meha tagahinagara" should be voiced.

▲ Section 78: "Yamashina no senshi" should be read as "no mikoto."

▲ Section 81: "Taijiki" should be voiced.

▲ Section 82: "Yamato Uda" should be voiced (referring to Uda in Yamato Province). "Sono ki no moto" should be read as "kono moto." "Notamaukereba" should be voiced. "Kaegaezu" is read with "ujitamō," and "kaesugaesu" should be unvoiced. "Mine mo tahira" should be read as "taira."

▲ Section 85: "Kotodatsu" should be voiced. "Kohosuga goto furite" should be voiced.

▲ Section 87: Read as "yomikeru zo kono to." "Iza kono yama" should be voiced. "Inakahito wa" should be read as "inaka-udo."

▲ Section 93: "Afuna-funa" should be read as "auna-auna."

▲ Section 95: "Nado shite" should be read as "nanto shite."

▲ Section 97: "Ōimōchikimi" should be voiced.

▲ Section 101: "Marōdo-sane" should be voiced.

▲ Section 108: "Kaze fukeba" should be read as "towa ni."

▲ Section 109: "Kohin to ka mishi" should be read unvoiced.

▲ Section 111: "Tōrau yō ni te" should be read as "tomurau." "Saru to nigenaku" should be voiced.

**Section 49**
Long ago, a man looked upon his (step-)sister, who was exceedingly charming in appearance, and sent (this poem):

*Urawakame, neyoge ni miyuru wakakusa wo / hito no musuban koto wo shi zo omou*
(Young and fresh, this tender grass that looks as though it sleeps well — I am troubled at the thought of another tying it to himself.)

She replied:

*Hatsukusa no nado mezurashiki koto no ha zo / uranaku mono wo omoikerukana*
(Like new spring shoots, what a remarkable word you have sent — you who have thought of me so guilelessy and innocently.)

**Section 50**
Long ago, there was a man. He resented the one who resented him (and composed):

*Tori no ko wo tō-zutsu tō wa kasanedo mo / omowan hito wo omou mono ka wa*
(Though one cannot stack bird's eggs ten by ten to a hundred, would I really think of one who does not think of me? — Yet I do.)

She replied:

*Asatsuyu wa kie noko rite mo arinubeshi / tare ka kono yo wo tanomi hatsubeki*
(The morning dew may linger and remain a while, yet who in this world can rely on anything to the very end?)

The man again:

*Fuku kaze ni kozo no sakura wa chirazu tomo / ana tami gata hito no kokoro wa*
(Even if last year's cherry blossoms had not scattered in the blowing wind, how impossible to grasp is the heart of a person like you.)

The woman replied again:

*Yuku mizu ni kazu kaku yori mo hakanaki wa / omowan hito wo omou narikeri*
(More fleeting than writing numbers in flowing water — that is the act of loving one who does not love you.)

And the man again (continues on next page)...
