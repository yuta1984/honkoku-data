[Right folio]

*(Poem)*
If only people
　would hold
　　in their hearts
　　　a spirit
　of honesty —
　　surely the grace
　　　of gods
　　and buddhas
　　　　would be theirs.

*(Commentary)*
It is said that "the gods dwell upon the heads of the honest," and the Buddha has laid down precepts against false speech. There is a poem that goes: "If only the heart walks the true path, the gods will protect you even without prayer." That an honest heart accords with the divine will of the gods and buddhas admits of no doubt.

[Left folio]

*(Poem)*
Even if you make
　pilgrimages
　　and offerings,
　if your heart
　　is crooked,
　you shall receive
　　not blessing
　　　but divine punishment.

*(Commentary)*
This poem presents the reverse of the preceding one. Since it is the natural principle that an honest heart receives the blessings of the gods, it follows without doubt that one whose heart is dishonest, crooked, and wicked will incur divine punishment. The people of the world, without understanding this principle, believe that simply making pilgrimages and performing acts of worship is sufficient to obtain blessings. This is truly foolish. The popular saying goes, "Punishment comes from the god one is close to" — for the gods do not accept what is contrary to propriety and rite.
