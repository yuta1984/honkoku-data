**Right Page**

**Verse**
One who constantly wears
a flattering, fawning face
before your very eyes —
such a person
will without fail
be dark-hearted behind your back.

**Prose Commentary**
"Tsuishō" (追従) means flattery and sycophancy. Those who flatter others have no sincerity and make deception their principal occupation. Without any distinction between good and evil or benefit and harm, they behave solely in ways calculated to please others in the moment. While such behavior may make a gathering seem pleasant and harmonious, their words and actions are ultimately inconsistent, and they will without fail be dark-hearted behind one's back. A loyal and sincere person will openly argue about right and wrong, benefit and harm, without distorting their words. While this may seem to go against others' wishes in the moment, it will without fail lead to good outcomes in the future. One should understand and remember this well.

**Left Page**

**Verse**
One who speaks ill
of others to your face —
surely they must be speaking
of you in just the same way
to others behind your back.

**Prose Commentary**
One who gossips about and slanders others in your presence either speaks ill of people without any restraint or reservation, or is likely a flatterer and slanderer by nature. If that is so, there is no doubt that they will also speak ill of you to others elsewhere, embellishing and fabricating things as they go. One should be thoroughly wary of and cautious toward such people.
