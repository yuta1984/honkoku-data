**Ken (Mizubuki; Gordon Euryale / Prickly Water Lily, *Euryale ferox*)** [from the *Bencao Gangmu*]　　　　Addendum: **Suiren (Hitsujigusa; Water Lily, *Nymphaea tetragona*)** [from the *Hokuroroku* (*Beihu Lu*)]

It grows in ponds and marshes. The leaves that first emerge have lobes (forked indentations) and are smooth and glossy, with no wrinkles. As they grow into large leaves, the lobes disappear. The upper surface of the leaves is green, lustrous, wrinkled, and bears sharp spines. The leaves float on the water's surface according to the depth of the water, resembling the large leaves of the lotus (*Nelumbo nucifera*). The undersides of the leaves are purple, and the leaf veins form a net-like pattern. The leaf stalks are covered with bristly spines (*bōshi*), and the inside of the stem closely resembles the hollow channels of a lotus root. Below each stem, a root is produced from each individual stem, and these interlock with one another. In summer, flower stems emerge from between the leaf stalks, bearing bristly spines. Buds form at the tips of the stems; they resemble chestnut burrs (*kuri no iga*) and are covered with many spines. When the flower has not yet opened, its shape is entirely like the beak of a bird. Around noon (*goji*), it opens in full bloom, turning to follow the sun. The flowers are deep purple, with yellow stamens at the center, and have four petals arranged in four layers, totaling sixteen petals.
