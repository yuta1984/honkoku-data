【Right folio】

From above the roots, fine rootlets emerge, enter the ground, and form pods containing seeds within. This is a plant that forms seeds in two different locations.

　　　　[The *Zhifu Quanshu* (致富全書 / *Complete Book of Enrichment*) states:] The peanut plant (落花生) has vines, stems, and leaves resembling those of the hyacinth bean (扁豆). It opens flowers which fall to the ground; each flower, upon touching the ground, forms one fruit. The shape of the fruit is similar to that of the yam (香蕷). Likewise, seeds are sown in the second month; the plant favors loose, soft soil. It should be hilled up with aged compost-ash (fertilizing ash from the previous year) and is best planted in a shaded location. At the end of autumn and the beginning of winter it is harvested; when boiled and eaten, the flavor is exceedingly sweet and delicious, and it is highly prized and appreciated by people. If it has not yet been touched by frost, the taste is bitter and difficult to eat. Together with the yam (香蕷・山薬), those that come from the coastal lands of Jiading (嘉定) are considered the finest.
