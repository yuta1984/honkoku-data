　　　　Lotus (*Nelumbo nucifera*)

The lotus grows in ponds and marshes. Its rhizome (the root, known as *renkon*) spreads horizontally through the mud. Its nature is such that, broadly speaking, in a leap year it produces thirteen nodes, while ordinarily it produces twelve nodes. This corresponds to the number of months in a year. Initially, each node of the old rhizome produces two buds. One of these puts forth *zeniba* (small coin-shaped leaves) in late spring, while the other produces a *gūga* (a young rhizome shoot).
