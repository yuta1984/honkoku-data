Preface

Among plants and trees, there are those that serve as medicines and those that are poisonous; there are those that are well known by name and those whose identity is well established; there are those that share the same name despite being different things, and those that are the same thing despite having different names; and there are those that merely resemble the genuine article without being so. To distinguish all of these with clarity is truly a difficult task. Nevertheless, this is something that physicians, by the nature of their profession, are duty-bound to endeavor upon; for those outside the medical profession, it may be something they can set aside.
