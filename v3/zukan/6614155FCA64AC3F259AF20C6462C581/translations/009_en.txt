One: In cultivating all manner of plants and trees, there are appropriate lands and appropriate seasons; one must allow each plant to follow what is suited to its nature with regard to moisture and dryness, cold and heat, and the character of the soil. For example, there are plants that grow in the north and plants that grow in the south. In the north, water (*sui* / 水) is the prevailing element, and its nature is cold; therefore, such plants can endure cold. In the south, fire (*ka* / 火) is the prevailing element, and its nature is warm; therefore, such plants do not fear heat. Even if one aids their cultivation through human effort, without the workings of *造化* (the creative power of nature) it is impossible to bring their natures to full fruition.
