One cannot help but admire them. And so I have made plants and trees my friends, cultivating them in the garden, and made them my teachers, keeping them in vases and jars. Though I take my ease and diversion among them as my guides and companions, it is still not enough to fully give expression to the depth of feeling I hold within. Thus I have traveled through mountains, forests, thickets, and marshes to pursue these pleasures to their fullest extent. Therefore, of the plants and trees I have examined and observed over many years, I have made sketches of those with remarkable and unusual natures,

[Left Page]
and titled the work *Seifu* ("Nature Record"). Together with this, I have compiled illustrated explanations of poisonous plants and trees, appending them at the end, so as to instruct the young beginners among my companions. However, as I, untalented as I am, have been engaged in official duties since my early years and have had no leisure to accumulate the painstaking labors of diligent study, the crudeness and carelessness of my writing cannot be excused. Should a gentleman of later times supplement and correct the omissions and errors herein, there would be no greater fortune than this.
