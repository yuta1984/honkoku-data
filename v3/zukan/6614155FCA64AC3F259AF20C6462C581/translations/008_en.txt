　Editorial Notes

One: This book is devoted exclusively to plants and trees of remarkable and unusual natures, examining and correcting errors in their names. The book titles noted beneath the Chinese names indicate the sources from which those names are drawn. For those whose Chinese names have not yet been clearly established, they are listed by their Japanese readings, with their Japanese names (*wamyō*) recorded alongside the Chinese names. That said, as regional variants are exceedingly numerous, only those names familiar to the ear are recorded here. A modest personal view is offered to highlight cases where the natures of plants are found to be contradictory or inconsistent with one another. However, as the subject matter is vast and there is insufficient time to enumerate everything individually, what is presented here through the narrow lens of my own observation amounts to no more than one-tenth of a thousand or a hundred cases.
