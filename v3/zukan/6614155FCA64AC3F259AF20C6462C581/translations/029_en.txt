[Classical Chinese citation: *Bencao Gangmu* (Honzō Kōmoku), "Collected Explanations" section]

> Li Shizhen states: The plant grows one or two *cun* tall, spreading over the ground in dense clumps, and multiplies extremely easily. Each branch bears three leaves, and each leaf consists of two leaflets; toward evening they fold together of their own accord, closing neatly as if they were one. In the fourth month, small yellow flowers open and small pods form, one or two *fen* in length, containing fine seeds within. Even in winter the plant does not wither.
