Preface to the *Sōmoku Seifu* (Author's Own Preface)

Zheng Zichan said: "The differences in people's hearts are like the differences in their faces." And so it is that what people are drawn to in their daily lives is also not the same for each person. The zither, poetry, wine, the tea ceremony, the game of Go — each according to what one's own nature inclines toward, these things bring delight to the human heart. What my own nature has always been inclined toward is, of course, plants and trees. Although plants and trees are without sentiment, they flourish and decline in turn, endlessly, through all four seasons.
