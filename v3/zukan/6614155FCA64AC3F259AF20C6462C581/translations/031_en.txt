**Senninshō / Saboten** (仙人掌, cactus) [*Bichuan Huajing* / *Secret Transmission: Mirror of Flowers*]　　　[Appended entry:] **Ryōkotsuboku / Kirinkaku** (龍骨木, *Euphorbia* sp., "dragon-bone tree") [*Guihai Yuheng Zhi*]

This plant originates in southern lands and is exceedingly sensitive to cold. In winter it must be stored in a heated greenhouse (*muro*), and water must not be poured upon it. If water is applied during the cold season, the plant will rot and decay and cannot survive. It puts forth branches but has no leaves; it is deep green in color, has spines (*hari*), and is simply flat. Large specimens can reach eight or nine *shaku* in height. In midsummer it produces new shoots at the tips. These grow alternately flat in opposing directions. Plants growing in warm regions will produce flowers, which appear suddenly at the tips of the flattened pads — the point of emergence being irregular. The flowers are double-petaled (*yaezaki*), brownish in color, and have fine vertical striations. Yellow stamens grow thickly, with white pistils at the center. The flowers are about the size of a *sazanka* (camellia) blossom. After flowering, a five-ridged fruit develops. If a single pad is broken off and dried in the sun for a short time before being planted as a cutting (*sashime*), it will take root immediately and produce young shoots. It bears spines. According to one account, placing this plant in the garden is said to ward off fire. [*Huajing*]
