I (the author) note:
In the same book's section on wetland plants,
Hakukkusai (Bai Qu Cai, Greater Celandine) is also listed.

The Nōsei Zensho (Agricultural Administration Compendium) states: "The stem and leaves are green with white coloration; the stems have hairy thorns, and branches divide at the top of the shoots, opening flowers." These are the four-petaled yellow flowers — this is the same plant as Kusano-o. Could it be that one plant has been listed as two separate species? In Yamato Honzō, it is listed under the name Hakukkusai (Kusanō).
