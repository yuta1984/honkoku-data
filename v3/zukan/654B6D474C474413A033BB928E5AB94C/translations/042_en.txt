　　　　　　　　　　　　　Year of Mizunoto-Hitsuji, 6th month, 15th day (full moon) copied


　　　　　　　　　　The Wakan Sansai Zue, in the section on fragrant grasses, states:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Turmeric (Kyōō)　Shù　Hōtei-kō (Precious Cauldron Fragrance)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　《Tang reading: Kyan  Haan》


　　　　　　I [Baien] state:
　　　　　　　　In the Bencao Gangmu, what is called "Maran" is listed separately; this refers to purple chrysanthemum. Upon examination of that explanation,
　　　　　　　　it is recorded as being Haran (Aspidistra). They are one thing yet treated as separate. Although two illustrations are provided to explain,
　　　　　　　　it is not entirely clear which is correct.
