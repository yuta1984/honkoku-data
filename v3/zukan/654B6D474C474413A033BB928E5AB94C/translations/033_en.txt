> *Awa-mori-shōma* (淡盛升麻) — this is also called "Japanese *shōma*" (*wa no shōma*, 和の升麻), or alternatively "*Yaku-sotoma*" (藥外麻).

> "*Yaku-sotoma*" refers specifically to those specimens bearing pale purple (*awaki murasaki*) flowers.
