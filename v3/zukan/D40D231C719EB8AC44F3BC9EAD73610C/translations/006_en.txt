**Miyako-dori (Eurasian Oystercatcher)**

In the province of Shima (Shishū), it is also called "Nikkobe," "Uba-chidori," and "Uba-shiki."

In form, it resembles a snipe (*shigi*) but is larger. The eye has a yellow iris with a red ring around it, and a white outer border. The beak is long and large, reddish-brown at the base and yellow at the tip. The legs are tall (long), pale pinkish-red, with three toes. From the crown of the head, running over the chest and the upper back feathers, through to the tip of the tail, the plumage is black. From below the chest, wrapping around to the base of the wings, the wing tips and the base of the tail are pure white.

In *The Tales of Ise* (*Ise Monogatari*), a bird is described as "a large snipe-like bird with a red beak and red feet, white in color," which does not entirely match this description. However, since this bird has abundant white feathers, it may be that the author simply described it as "white," and it is difficult to say with certainty whether or not they refer to the same bird.
