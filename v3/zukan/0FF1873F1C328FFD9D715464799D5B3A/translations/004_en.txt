**As stated in the "Zōho Tashiki-hen" (Expanded Edition of the Encyclopedia of Knowledge), in the section on hemp, wheat, and rice:**

**Small Wheat / Wheat (Haku)**
- Japanese name: *komugi* (古牟岐); also called *mamugi* (未牟岐)
- Alternative name (from expanded edition): *Rai* (来)
- Sanskrit name: *Kashisaku* (迦師錯)

---

As recorded in the *Nihon Kōki* (Later Chronicles of Japan):
When Empress Shōtoku reigned, the Minister Kibi no Makibi, receiving an imperial decree, proclaimed:
"Wheat is the finest of all grains for rescuing people from want when food supplies have been exhausted."
He then ordered all the common people throughout the land to plant both barley and wheat.

---

On the twentieth day of Zhonglü (the fourth lunar month) in the year of Itsuyū (乙酉),
[the author] visited fields and gardens, cut a stem, and drew it from life.
