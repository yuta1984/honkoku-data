The "Tashikihen," in the section on vine vegetables (rakusairui), states:
　　　Sika (Silk gourd / Loofah; 絲瓜)　Japanese name: as commonly called today,
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Hechima

　　　　　　Alternative names:　Tensika (original character form)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Tenra (from "Gōheki") 　Fuka (same source)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Banka (original character form)　Gyoji
　　　　　　〇Tenraroku (from "Shūi")

"Bencao Gangmu" (Honzō Kōmoku): It first came from the southern regions, and therefore was named Banka (蠻瓜, "Barbarian Gourd"). Before the Tang and Song dynasties, it was unheard of. Today it has become a common vegetable.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kinoto-Tori year, 6th month (Rinshō), 21st day
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Drawn from a cut vine　[Vegetable plants]
