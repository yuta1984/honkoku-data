In the "Wakan Sansai Zue" (Japanese-Chinese Illustrated Encyclopedia of the Three Powers), in the section on trees, it says:
　　Biyo-yanagi (美容柳)　The correct character form is unknown.

In the "Yamato Honzō" (Great Japanese Herbal) it says:
　　Kinshibai (金糸梅)　Common name: Kinshibai
　　《small text: Biyo-yanagi》

　　　As illustrated in the Sansai Zue: it resembles a willow, and at its center
　　　has yellow stamens that spread and scatter outside the flower,
　　　like golden threads.

【Flowering Trees】

Kinoto-Tori year, 6th month, 10th day
Drawn with a cool breeze through half-open window

Biyō-yanagi　(from "Gunsho")　Its tree has leaves resembling willow; during the early summer rains it
Biyō (美陽)　(from "Chikinsho")　blooms with yellow flowers, said to continue through the sixth month.
　　　　　　　　　　　　This is indeed the true Biyo-yanagi.
