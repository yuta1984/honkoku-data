　　　　Kenran (おらん)　《Reference: Bencao Gangmu, "Orchid Corrections"》　《Supplement:》 Shōran (めらん)　《Reference: Shōshū Fushi》
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Tōran (はくらん)　《Reference: Tsūga 《and: Author's own consideration》》

"Yūran" (幽蘭, secluded orchid) is the collective name for all these plants. They grow in the mountains of warm regions. By their nature, they love wind yet fear cold; they delight in shaded places yet dislike damp ground. In cultivating them, the proper measure of sandy/gravelly soil is essential.
