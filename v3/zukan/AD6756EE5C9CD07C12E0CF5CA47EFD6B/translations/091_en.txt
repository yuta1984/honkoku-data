*Kakura* — Found in the seas of Ezo (Hokkaido). This is a term from the dialect of Matsumae. It is likely a variety of sea urchin (*sunomi*). In the dialect of Shiranutka (Shiraぬka), it is called *Tatsuko*.

**[Left page]**
Regarding this book: in the days when I used to visit the Senkōshitsu (the study of Kushimoto Tsuneyoshi), I wrote the title label "Senchūfu" (A Register of a Thousand Insects) in three characters at the request of the master. Now that I hear it has passed into the possession of Kinka-sensei, I suddenly recall those former times, which feel distant and dream-like. "Senkōshitsu" refers to Kushimoto Tsuneyoshi, whose common name was Sakyō and whose literary sobriquet was Ryokui; he held a highly stipended position among the physicians serving the Shogunate.
— Written on the 16th day of the 11th month, in the year Mizunoto-Tori (癸酉), by Mori Tateyuki.

This *Senchūfu* is a copy I made in former years, and I now record this note for the Kinka/Itō elder.

Meiji 5, year of Mizunoe-Saru (壬申), mid-spring (the 2nd month), the 19th day. Age: 66.
