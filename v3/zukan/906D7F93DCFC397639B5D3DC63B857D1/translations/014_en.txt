**[Right folio]**

**One variety: Ekakigaki / Mokudegaki / Mojigaki**
(絵描き柿・木で柿・文字柿 — "picture-drawn persimmon," "wood-marked persimmon," "letter persimmon")

Its shape resembles that of the Kōshū-maru (Kōshū round persimmon), and on its skin it produces black mottled markings resembling writing (characters) or drawings (paintings). The flavor is sweet, and it is a superior variety.

---

**[Left folio]**

**One variety: Utsumaru**

Its shape resembles that of the *Zenji* (Zenjimaru persimmon) and is elongated, with a flat top. On its upper portion there are black mottled markings resembling a spiral (*utsumaki*), from which it takes its name. Its flavor is so sweet and fine that it surpasses all other persimmon varieties.
