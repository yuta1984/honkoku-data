*One variety*

**Yamazakura (Mountain Cherry)**

This is a famous product of Yoshino in Yamato Province (present-day Nara Prefecture). It grows abundantly in deep mountain forests. The leaves resemble those of the plum (*ri*/Prunus salicina) and are broad; the flowers bloom in the third month (approximately April by the modern calendar). The blossoms are single-petaled, of a pale pinkish-red (suikō) color, and approximately the size of plum blossoms. The fruit resembles a cherry (*ōtō*/cherry) and is round, with a long stalk. When pickled in salt and eaten, it has the effect of sobering one up from the intoxication of alcohol.
