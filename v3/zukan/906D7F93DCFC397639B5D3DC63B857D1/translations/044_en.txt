**Right folio:**
One variety —
　　*Yukō* (柚香, "Fragrant Yuzu")

　　The shape resembles that of the yuzu, with little acidity and a sweet flavor — suitable to be eaten as a fruit.

**Left folio:**
*Shuran* (朱欒) [from the *Shakumyō* / *Shiming*] — also known as: *Zabon*; *Sanbo* [Chikuzen]; *Shabo* [Tosa]; *Shakatara mikan* [Kyoto]

　This plant differs from the yuzu. The leaves are seven to eight *sun* (approximately 21–24 cm) in length; the flowers have five petals and are white, resembling those of the yuzu. The fruit is extremely large, resembling a gourd (*hyōtan*). The outer rind is also yellow like that of the yuzu, with a thickness of approximately one *sun* (about 3 cm). The flesh is pale yellow and bitter, and cannot be eaten raw. It is consumed mixed with sugar.
