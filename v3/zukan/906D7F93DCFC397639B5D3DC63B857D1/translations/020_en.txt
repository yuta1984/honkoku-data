**[Right folio]**
A variety:
　　*Mamegaki* (known in Edo and Sendai)
　　*Kurokaki* (known in Tosa Province)

　　　The leaves resemble those of the persimmon but are narrower and longer. The fruit is about the size of a fingertip, perfectly round, and grows in clusters on the branches, resembling grapes. When ripe, it turns yellow. This corresponds to the *Dīngxiāng shì* (丁香柿, Clove Persimmon) described in the *Jijie* (集解) section of the *Bencao Gangmu*.
