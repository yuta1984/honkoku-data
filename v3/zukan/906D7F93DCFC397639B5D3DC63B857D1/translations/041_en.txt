One variety:　　*Taitai* — *Kafusu* [Chōshū]
　　　　*Kaiseito* (回青橙, "Green-reverting Orange") [from the *Bāmǐn Tōngzhì* / *Hachimin Tsūshi*]

　The tree and leaves closely resemble those of the *kunenbo* and are difficult to distinguish from it. However, the fruit is green, and after being exposed to frost in winter, it gradually turns reddish-yellow. The flavor is sour and bitter. The peel has recently come to be used in treating *senshaku* (a condition involving abdominal pain and accumulation). The fruit, which turns red in winter, changes back to green in spring, then returns to red again the following winter. Because it thus persists and ages through the years, it is called *taitai* ("year after year").
