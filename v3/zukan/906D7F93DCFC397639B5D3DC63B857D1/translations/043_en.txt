**Right folio:**
One variety — *Tokoyu*
　　Also known as: *Shōyu* ／ *Hanayu*
　　*Raiyu* (邏柚) [from the *Guangdong Xinyu*]

　　The fruit is small in shape and hard. Even when spring arrives, the fruit remains on the tree, and its flavor is inferior (compared to the common *yuzu*).

**Left folio:**
One variety — *Kin'yu*

　　The shape of the fruit closely resembles (that of the *yuzu*), but the skin is thin and smooth, like that of a *kan* (mandarin orange). The color is pure yellow, and it has little acidity.
