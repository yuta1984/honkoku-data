*Hō-kitsu* (包橘, "Wrapped Orange") [ref: *Jí-jiě* / Collected Explanations]
　　　*Kōji* [a type of small citrus]

　　This is the common, ordinary *kōji*.
　　Smaller than the *kan* (mandarin orange), its peel is
　　thin, its surface/texture is smooth,
　　it is yellow with a certain luster, but
　　it is of inferior quality. The taste
　　is bitter.

[Left folio]

One variety:
　　Fúzhōu *tachibana* ["Tachibana orange from Fuzhou"]

　　　　The fruit resembles the *kōji*,
　　　　with a thin peel, fine-grained
　　　　texture, and a sour taste.
