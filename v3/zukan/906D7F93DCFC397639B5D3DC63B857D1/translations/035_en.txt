**Left folio:**
*Kan* (Mandarin) — *mikan* — *Kanshi* [from the *Yūyō Zatso/Youyang Zazu*] — *Jōkō* [from the *Jimono Imei/Shiwu Yiming*]
— *Suikinnu* [from the *Gunpōfu/Qunfangpu*]

*Kan* (mandarin) and *kitsu* (tangerine/橘) are both products of warm regions, and they are difficult to cultivate in cold regions. Both their flowers and leaves resemble those of *kitsu* (kōshi/tangerine). The skin of the fruit is thick, the pores (surface pits) are coarse, and the flesh is sweet in taste. There are many varieties. What is described above is the general name (*sōmei*) for *kan* as a whole.
