*Sōō-kitsu* (早黄橘, "Early Yellow Orange") [ref: *Jí-jiě* / Collected Explanations]

　　*Wase-kōji* (early-ripening *kōji*)　　*Sōkō-kitsu* (早紅橘, "Early Red Orange") [ref: *Rǔnán Pǔshǐ*]

　　In recent years, in the area of Tōgane in Shimōsa Province,
　　many varieties of *tachibana* (橘) and *kan* (柑)
　　are being cultivated. The early-ripening
　　*kōji* that appears in markets before autumn
　　is precisely this fruit. It resembles the common
　　*kōji*, is green in color,
　　and is slightly larger. The taste is bitter and
　　sour.

[Left folio]

*Senshin-kitsu* (穿心橘, "Hollow-centered Orange") [ref: *Jí-jiě* / Collected Explanations]

　　When the peel is removed, at its center
　　there is a hollow cavity large enough
　　to insert one finger. This feature is also
　　found in *kan* (mandarin oranges).
