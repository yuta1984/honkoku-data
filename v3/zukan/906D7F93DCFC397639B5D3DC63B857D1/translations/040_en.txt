**Right folio:**
*Tō* (橙) — *Kunenbo*
　　　　　　　　*Tō* (棖) [from the *Gunpōfu* / *Qunfangpu*]
　　　　　　*Mitsutō* (蜜橙, "Honey Orange") [from the *Hiden Kakyō* / *Mìchuán Huājìng*]

　The tree grows tall and large. The leaves resemble those of the *yuzu* and are large, with small leaflets at the base of the leaf stalk. There are thorns between the branches. The flowers also resemble those of the *yuzu* and are large. The fruit is similar to the *tachibana* of Unshū (Izumo Province), with a smooth and thick skin. The flesh contains little juice, but the flavor is sweet and fragrant.

**Left folio:**
One variety:　　*Hon-taitai* (ほんたいたい)

　　　　　Found in Chōshū (present-day Yamaguchi Prefecture). The shape is like that of the ordinary *taitai*, but the stem end is doubled (has a double calyx). Like the *manjūkan*, it has little bitterness and is good to eat. Also like the *taitai*, it turns yellow in winter and changes back to green in spring, and similarly persists on the tree from one year to the next.
