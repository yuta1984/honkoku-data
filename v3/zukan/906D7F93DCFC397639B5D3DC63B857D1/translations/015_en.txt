**[Right folio]**

**One variety: Kōshū-maru / Shinken-maru**
(甲州まる・しんけんまる — "Kōshū round persimmon" / "Shinken round persimmon")

Its shape resembles that of the *Zenji* (Zenjimaru persimmon), and its lower portion is broad. When it ripens, it takes on a yellow color. The flavor is slightly astringent at first, becoming sweet afterward.

---

**[Left folio]**

**One variety: Higaki**
(ひがき — "frost persimmon")

Its shape resembles that of the *maru* (round persimmon), with the top portion slightly pointed. It has no astringency from the very beginning, and even as winter comes, it does not fall from the tree but remains attached. After being subjected to frost and fully ripening, its skin turns black, the flavor becomes extremely sweet, and it is very juicy.

---

**One variety: Kurogaki**
(くろがき — "black persimmon")

Its shape is perfectly round, resembling the *higaki* but somewhat smaller. After autumn has passed, it remains on the tree and ripens, at which point the entire fruit turns black, like lacquer. The flavor is sweet.
