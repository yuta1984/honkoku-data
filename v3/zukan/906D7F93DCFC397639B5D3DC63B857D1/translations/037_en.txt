**Right folio:**
*Tōteikan* (Dongting Mandarin — 洞庭柑) [from the *Honzō Shūkai* / *Bencao Jijie*]
　　*Rifujin*
　　　*Tachibana*

In former years, the lord of Yanagawa Domain in Chikugo Province brought this variety back from Korea and cultivated it, and from that time it has become widespread in the present age. The shape of the fruit resembles that of the *Jagatara mikan* (Javanese/Batavian mandarin), and it is somewhat flattened. The surface is fine-grained, and the skin is thin. The flesh is sweet in taste, and the seeds are few.

**Left folio:**
One variety — *Kinokuni mikan* (Mandarin of Kii Province)

　　The fruit is large in shape, the skin is thick, the surface is coarse and soft, and the flesh is sweet and delicious.
