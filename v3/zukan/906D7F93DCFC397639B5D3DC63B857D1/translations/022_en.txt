**[Right folio]**
One variety:
　*Kawairo-zakuro* (leather-colored pomegranate)
　　*Menō-ryū* 瑪瑙榴 (Agate Pomegranate) [from *Runan Pushi* 『汝南圃史』]

　　*Senyō ginkō sekiryū* 千葉銀紅石榴 (Thousand-petaled Silver-red Pomegranate)
　　　　　　　　　　[from *Kanen Sōmoku Shiki* 『灌園草木識』]

　　It has double flowers; the base of the petals is red, while the tips are yellowish-white.
　　It does not bear fruit. The entry in the *Jijie* section [of the *Bencao Gangmu*] stating "there are two colors, yellow and red"
　　refers to this variety.

**[Left folio]**
*Ka-sekiryū* 火石榴 (Fire Pomegranate)
　　　　　　　　[from the *Jijie* section of the *Bencao Gangmu*]

　　*Hana-zakuro* (Flowering Pomegranate)
　　　　*Hyakuyō* 百葉 (Hundred-petaled) [from *Tenka Fushi* 『典花府志』]

　　　*Senyō kō-ryū* 千葉紅榴 (Thousand-petaled Red Pomegranate) [from *Runan Fushi* 『汝南府志』]
　　　　Double-flowered and red in color.
　　　　It does not bear fruit.
