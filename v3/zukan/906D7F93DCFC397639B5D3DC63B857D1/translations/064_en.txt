**Ginkgo (銀杏, ginkyō)**
　*Ichō* (Japanese common name)
　　　Name of the tree

**List of Alternative Names:**

〔Names for the fruit〕
- **Ginnan** – the name for the fruit; that is, the Tō-on (Tang Chinese reading) of the characters 銀杏.
- **Chichi-no-ki** (lit. "father tree" or "breast tree")
- **Ninkyō (仁杏)** – according to the *Runan Poshi* (汝南圃史)
- **Hakugan (白眼)** and **Reigan (霊眼)** – both also from the same source
- **Hakukyō (白杏)** – according to the *Jiwu Yiming* (事物異名). The above are all names for the fruit.

〔Names for the tree〕
- **Kōsonju (公孫樹)** – according to the *Runan Poshi* (汝南圃史)
- **Heichūboku (平仲木)** – according to the *Zhengzi Tong* (正字通)
- **Katakuboku (火槖木)** – according to the *Tong Ya* (通雅). The above are all names for the tree.
