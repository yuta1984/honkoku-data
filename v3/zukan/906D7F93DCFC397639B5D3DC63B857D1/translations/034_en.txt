**Right folio:**
*Tōkitsu* (Frost-surviving Tangerine) [from the *Jijie/Shūkai*]

"Okute no kōshi" (Late-ripening tangerine)

This is a variety whose fruit remains attached to the tree even into winter, without falling.

**Left folio:**
*Reishikitsu* (Lychee Tangerine) [from the *Jijie/Shūkai*]

"Ukasa mikan" [possibly "kasa mikan"]

This fruit is similar in size to a *kōshi* (tangerine), round in shape, with a thick and hard skin bearing yellowish-white wart-like protuberances, resembling the skin of a lychee (*Litchi chinensis*).
