**[Right folio]**
One variety:
　　*Suishō-zakuro* (Crystal Pomegranate)

　　The flowers are double (multi-petaled) and white in color,
　　their form resembling that of the *Hanazakuro* (Flowering Pomegranate).
　　It does not bear fruit.

**[Left folio]**
*Sea Pomegranate* 海石榴 (*Kai-sekiryū*) [from the *Jijie* section of the *Bencao Gangmu*]
　　*Nankin-zakuro* (Nanjing Pomegranate)

　　　　The tree is small, reaching a height of two to three *shaku*.
　　　　The leaves are also small, and the flowers are likewise
　　　　small. Single-petaled and red in color,
　　　　it does bear fruit, but the fruit is small, and its flavor
　　　　is exceedingly sour.
