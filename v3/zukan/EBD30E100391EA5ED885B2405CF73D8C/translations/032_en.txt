**[Right folio]**

*One variety:*
Its size is similar to that of the *hon-yamato*, and the entire surface is white with fine longitudinal striations (ribs/grooves).

*Another variety:*
Its shape is like that of the *hon-yamato*; the skin is white with yellow—

**[Left folio]**

—vertical stripes (longitudinal ribs), and the colour of the flesh is the same as that of the *hon-yamato*.

*Another variety:*
Its shape is like that of the *hon-yamato*, and the skin is yellow; it is plump and finely-textured, and the flesh is a blue-green to yellow colour (*seikō-shoku*).
