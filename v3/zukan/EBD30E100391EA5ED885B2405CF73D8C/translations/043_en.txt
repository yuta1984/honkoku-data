【Right Page】

　　　　A separate variety
　　　　　*Nobudo* (wild grape / *Ampelopsis glandulosa*)
　　　　　　　*Jyabudō* (snake grape) 《Annotation: As recorded in *Jiuhuang Bencao*》

　　　　　　　　The shape of the leaves is the same as described above,
　　　　　　　　but they are thin and without hairs. The fruit is likewise
　　　　　　　　similar, but displays a mixture of lapis-blue, purple,
　　　　　　　　red, and white colors. When they fall (wither),
　　　　　　　　they all turn black.
