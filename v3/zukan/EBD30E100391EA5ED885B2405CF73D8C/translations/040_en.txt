【Right Page】

Crystal Grape (*suishō budō*) 《Annotation: Common name in Japanese and Chinese sources》

　　　The leaves resemble those of the Purple Grape (*murasaki budō*),
　　　with deeply incised lobes. When the fruit ripens,
　　　it becomes pale green
　　　and translucent.

【Left Page】

*Sasa* Grape (*sasa budō*) 《Annotation: From the *Jijie* (集解) section》

　　　Recently, in the garden of Lord Shirakawa,
　　　there is a variety whose fruit is approximately
　　　the size of a soybean, pale purple in color,
　　　seedless, and sweet in taste.
　　　This is a remarkable and rare specimen.
　　　This plant corresponds to what Li Shizhen describes as
　　　"resembling *gomishi* (Schisandra) in size,
　　　but without seeds."
