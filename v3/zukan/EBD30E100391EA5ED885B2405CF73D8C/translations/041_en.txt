【Right Page】

One variety: *Vitis canadensis trilifolia* 《Annotation: Dutch》

【Left Page】

　　In the Dutch book (Inman), there is described a variety
　　with deeply incised leaves, whose fruit turns yellow when ripe.
　　This corresponds to what Kou Zongshi (*Sōseki*) cites from Duan Chengshi,
　　who states that "grapes come in three varieties: yellow, white, and purple" —
　　this being the yellow variety in question.
