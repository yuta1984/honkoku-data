[On Hanakammi / Fragrant-Fruited Plum]

　　　This variety has lustrous and beautiful flowers,
　　　and its fragrance is also intensely sweet. Furthermore, its fruit
　　　surpasses even the Bungo plum. It is named
　　　Hanakammi (花香実, "flower-fragrance-fruit") for these reasons.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Painted from life from a branch cutting
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　requested from the garden of a Mr. Shiibashi,
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　in the 2nd month of the Year of the Sheep,
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Tenpō 6 (1835), on the ■17th day
