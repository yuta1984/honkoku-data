【Dangiku (Caryopteris incana / Bluebeard)】

Rangiku — commonly called "Dangiku" (段菊, "tiered chrysanthemum") or also "Dangiku" (檀菊)
　　Dutch/Western name: Nepeta japonica
　　Brought again by ship from Ryūkyū in the Bunsei Kanoto-Mi year (1821)

　　　　Yama-kajū (Mountain Elsholtzia) — from the Western Lands (China)

　　　　　　　　　　　　2nd day of the 9th month, Mizunoto-Mi year [1833],
　　　　　　　　　　　　drawn true-to-life from a single potted plant beneath a desk
