【Mizuoobako – Water Plantain relative (*Ottelia alismoides*)】

3rd day of Nanryo (7th month), Mizunoto-Mi year [1833]
Drawn true-to-life from a water basin placed beneath a desk in my own garden

From the *Tashikiben* [Encyclopedia of Japanese Names for Chinese Natural History Terms]:
　Ryūzetsusō (Dragon's Tongue Grass / *Ottelia alismoides*)
　　Also known as: Mizu-hokori (a Western Chinese name)
　　　　　　　　　Mizu-oobako [Water Plantain]
　　　　　　　　　Tōkamori
　　　　　　　　　(Round-leaved variety, one kind)

　The flowers float and bloom upon the surface of the water, while the leaves
　do not float but sink beneath the surface and grow luxuriantly there.

From *Yamato Honzō* [Japanese Materia Medica]:
　Mizuaoi [Water Hollyhock], also called Ryūzetsusō

As recorded in *Honzō* [Bencao / Materia Medica]:
　Shinran [True Lan] (one variety)
　　Also known as: Sarudahiko-sō
　　　　　　　　　Isobeso

2nd day of the 9th month of the same year [1833]
Drawn true-to-life from a single potted plant placed beneath a desk in the garden
