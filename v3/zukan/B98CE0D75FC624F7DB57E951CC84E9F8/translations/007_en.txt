In the *Wakan Sansai Zue* (和漢三才図会), it is noted that there is a white-flowered variety of *shiyōka* (hydrangea). Its stem and leaves are the same as the common type, and it blooms in the 5th month. Its form is quite different: small five-petaled flowers of pure white gather together in clusters. It is popularly called "the true *shiyōka*." [Note: *Miken* — the author has not personally observed this variety.]

When purple-flowered hydrangeas are regarded as the authentic variety, the white-flowered one is treated as a separate species. This view does not seem correct [*fukōse*, i.e., does not accord with reason] to the author. Nevertheless, common usage refers to (this white-flowered variety) by the popular name *Tō-ajisai*, causing it to be conflated with the purple variety.
