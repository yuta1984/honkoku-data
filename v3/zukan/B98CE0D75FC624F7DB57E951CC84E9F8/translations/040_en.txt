【Likely a relative of hops or Humulus (Japanese hop), with a round-leafed form】

15th day of the 7th month, Mizunoto-Mi year [1833]
Cut a vine from my own garden
Drawn true-to-life

As recorded in the *Jiuhuang Bencao* [Materia Medica for Famine Relief]:
　Gēlèzǐ yāng [Kudzu-like climbing plant] (one variety)
　　Also known as: Oranda-kanamugura [Dutch Kanamugura]
　　　　　　　　　Marubanokogiri-kusa [Round-leaved Saw-grass]
　　　　　　　　　◦Hop Kruid [Dutch name] (Kōmō [Dutch/Western])
