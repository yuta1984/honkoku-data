Listed in the "Bencao Gangmu" (Compendium of Materia Medica) and in the poisonous plants section of the "Wakan Sansai Zue" (Illustrated Sino-Japanese Encyclopedia of the Three Powers)

　　　Suigyosō (酔魚草)　Also known as: Nàoyúhuā, Lòumù, Yúwěicǎo
　　　[Butterfly bush / Buddleja lindleyana]

　　　　　Fujiutsuki [Fuji-utsuki] (per the "Chikinsho")
　　　　　Kakufuchi
　　　　　Nikkō-utsuki

　　　　　　　When fishermen gather the flowers and leaves and use them to poison fish,
　　　　　　　the fish promptly die. Suigyosō blooms most commonly in the summer months;
　　　　　　　those that bloom in autumn are late-blooming specimens
　　　　　　　from new shoots.

　　　　　　　　　　　　18th day of Nanryo (8th lunar month), year of Kinoto-Ushi [1865]
　　　　　　　　　　　　Mr. Yaku sent one potted plant to my desk, and I sketched it from life.
