---

**Based on *Honzō Wage* and various other herbals**

**Yokuі (薏苡)** — Job's tears (*Coix lacryma-jobi*)
Alternate names: Gairei (解蠡), Kijitsu (芑実)
　　　　　　　　Kanmai (𧆐米), Kanshu (簳珠), Huihui-mai (回々米, "Muslim rice")
　　　　　　　　Yokushuushi (薏珠子)

Japanese name: Juzudama (数珠陀磨, "rosary beads")

〇 Tōmugi (唐麦, "Chinese wheat")
　 Juzugodama (数珠子玉)
　　　　In the customs of this country, these seeds are used as rosary beads (juzu), and hence the plant received this name.

Tsushitama (芋珠) — *as recorded in the Wamyōshō*

---

There are two varieties of Yokuі. One variety has large, round seeds with a thick husk. This is called Bodaishi (菩提子, "Bodhi seeds").
