**[Hamamatsuna / Beach Matsuna]**

**[From the *Kyūkō Honzō* (Famine Relief Materia Medica)]**

*Kenpō* (鹻蓬): Also known as *Enpō* (塩蓬 / Salt Bush)
　　○ *Matsuna*　○ *Hamamatsuna*
　　　　In Japan, it is commonly called "*Matsuna*" (Pine Herb/Greens)

　　　　　　This is the Japanese variety of *Matsuna* (松菘). *Kenpō* is the
　　　　　　original species of *Hamamatsuna* (beach Matsuna), and there exists
　　　　　　a method for extracting salt from its leaves. It is for this reason
　　　　　　that it is called *Kenpō* (Alkali/Salt Bush). Among Japanese-native
　　　　　　specimens of this plant, there is also a separate, true distinct species.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　18th day after Bon, in autumn of the year *Ushi* [丑, 1829]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Drawn from a stem cut in my own vegetable garden
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　*Teru* (照 — drawn from life / faithful depiction)
