　　Pine (shō / matsu)　[Source: Bencao Gangmu]　(Supplementary entry:) Mistletoe growing on Pine (shōjō kisei / matsu no yadorigi)　[Source: same text, "Shōra," in the section "Shakumei"]

The pine is the chief of all trees and is a yang tree. Its nature is gentle yet intense; it possesses dignity without being fierce. When viewed from afar, it stands tall and majestic, as if possessing the broad and generous virtue of a gentleman (junzi). Those that have lived for a hundred years extend their branches at oblique angles; those reaching a thousand years are remarkably long-lived.
