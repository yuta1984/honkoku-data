**Pine** (shō / matsu)　〔Source: Honzō Kōmoku〕　　(Supplementary: Mistletoe on Pine / Shōjō Kisei)　〔Same work, section: Shōra Shakumyō〕

The pine is the chief among the hundred trees and is a tree of yang (positive energy). Its nature is gentle yet vigorous; it possesses an air of dignity without being fierce. When one gazes upon it, it stands tall and majestic, as if embodying the virtue of a noble gentleman — broad-minded and magnanimous. Those trees that have lived a hundred years have branches that spread out horizontally and obliquely; reaching a thousand years of age, they are truly long-lived beyond measure.
