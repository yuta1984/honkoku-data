【Right folio】
A variety:
　Ikada-nanten (Raft Nandina)

【Left folio】
　　The shape of the leaves resembles that of beni-nanten (red Nandina), being slender with pointed tips. The stem differs from that of the common variety in that it does not branch, and its form resembles a raft. The flowers are the same as those of the common variety.
