【Right folio】
A variety: Maruba-no-nishikigi (Round-leaved Spindle Tree)

【Left folio】
　The leaves are rounded in shape, resembling those of the camellia, but thinner and smaller. The flowers are the same as those of the previous entry (Yamanishikigi), and the fruits also resemble those of the previous entry. Neither the branches nor the trunk bear any wing-like corky ridges. This plant corresponds to the one described by Su Song (蘇頌) as having "leaves resembling those of wild tea (野茶)."
