【Right folio】

**Kūkyoku** 枸棘 (*Oni-kuko*; thorny wolfberry / *Lycium chinense* var. with thorns; cited in *Shūkai* [Collected Commentaries])

Planted around human dwellings and used as hedges. The form of the tree resembles that of the preceding entry (*kuko* / goji), and it can grow to a height of approximately two *jō* (about six meters).

【Left folio】

The shape of the leaves also resembles that of the preceding entry, though slightly smaller. Both old and new stems bear thorns. The flowers and fruit are likewise similar to those of the preceding entry, though the fruit is somewhat more elongated. This corresponds to what the Song-dynasty scholar Su Song described when he said: "Those that bear thorns are *kūkyoku* (枸棘)."

---

**Chikotsuhi** 地骨皮 (Root bark of the wolfberry / *Lycii Cortex Radicis*)

This is the bark of the root of *kuko* (wolfberry; *Lycium chinense*).
Its surface resembles the skin of rotting wood: dark brownish-black in color and soft in texture.
