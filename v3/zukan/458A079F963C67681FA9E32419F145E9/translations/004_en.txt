　　Shrubs (灌木類)

**Ikuri** (郁李) — *Niwaume*; also *Kōume* (in Banshū province); also known as *Tōshi* (棠李) in *Jiwu Yiming* [Dictionary of Synonyms for Things]

This is a small tree commonly planted in gardens, not exceeding three or four *shaku* in height, growing densely in clusters from the root. In spring, new leaves emerge; they resemble plum (*sumomo*) leaves in shape but are smaller, and grow alternately along the stem. At the same time that the leaves have not yet fully extended, buds emerge from the base of the leaves. When they open, the flowers have five petals, are white tinged with pink, and measure approximately four or five *bu* across — a beautiful sight. Thereafter, fruits are produced. The fruits are round, about four or five *bu* in size; they are green at first, and by summer ripen to red. The flavor is both sour and sweet. Within the fruit is a pit (stone), and the white kernel (*hakujin*) inside this pit is extracted for medicinal use.
