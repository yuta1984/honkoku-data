**A related variety**

*Kuroumemodoki* (Kyoto)

The leaves of this variety are considerably smaller than those of the preceding entry. In spring, small pale yellow flowers grow in clusters at the base of the new leaves. The plant subsequently bears fruit. The berries are smaller in form than those of the preceding entry, and upon ripening turn an indigo-black color.

**[Left folio]**

**Jotei (女貞)** — *Ligustrum lucidum* (glossy privet)

Regional and common names: *nezumimochi* [from *Wamyōshō*]; *miyatsukogi* [from *Honzō Wamyō*]; *tatsunoki* [ibid.]; *yabutsubaki*; *teratsubaki* (Banshū / Harima Province); *fuyunari*; *itashi* (Matsumae)

Chinese/literary names: *Suitōsei* (水冬青) [from *Butsuri Shōshiki* / *Wuli Xiaoshi*]; *Tōsei* (冬生) [cited in *Honzō Wamyō*, quoting *Shakuyakusei* / *Shiyaoxing*]; *Tōseiju* (凍青樹) [from *Kyūkō Honzō* / *Jiuhuang Bencao*]

The tree reaches a height of over one *jō* (approximately 3 meters). Its branches and stems grow in dense profusion, and its leaves do not wither in winter. The shape of the leaves resembles those of *dōsei* (冬青 / *Ilex*), with both ends tapering to a point; the branches and leaves grow in opposite pairs. In summer, the tips of the branches form spikes, upon which small white flowers bloom in dense clusters. The plant subsequently bears fruit. The berries are rounded with a slight elongation, resembling in shape the droppings of a mouse. Upon ripening, they turn a deep purple-black color.
