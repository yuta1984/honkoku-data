**Left folio:**
　*Hamabō*　*Hōnoki* (Izu dialect name)
　　*Ōkin* [cited in *Riyō Bunshū*]　*Kinmokuran* [cited in *Fukushū Fushi* and *Chūzan Shitsumon*]

　It grows wild along the coastline of Izu Province. The tree resembles *mukuge* (rose of Sharon, *Hibiscus syriacus*) and reaches a height of about three to four *shaku*. The leaves are large and thick, similar to those of *hamahai* (*Vitex rotundifolia*, the beach vitex), yellowish-green in color, and arranged alternately. In summer, flowers open from between the leaves. The form resembles that of *mukuge* but is smaller; the petals are thick and suffused with deep crimson at their base.
