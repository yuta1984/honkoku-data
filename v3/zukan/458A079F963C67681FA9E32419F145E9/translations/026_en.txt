【Right folio】
A variety: *Ito-nanten* (Thread-leaved Nandina)
　　The leaves are extremely narrow in shape; newly sprouted leaves resemble threads. As they grow, they reach about one *bu* (approximately 3 mm) in width. The flowers and berries are the same as those of the common variety.

【Left folio】
A variety: *Shiromi-nanten* (White-berried Nandina)
　　Both the flowers and leaves resemble those of the common variety, but they lack red coloration, and when the berries ripen, they turn white.
