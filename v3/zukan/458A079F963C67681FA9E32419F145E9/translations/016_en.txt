【Right folio】
A variety: Yamanishikigi
　Also known as: Masaki (name used in Edo)
　　　　　　　　Otokonishikigi (name used in Etchū province)

【Left folio】
　The leaves are large and resemble those of a peach tree, deep green in color, with fine serrations (teeth) around their edges. In spring, long stems grow from among the leaves and bear flowers. The shape of the flowers resembles that of the previous entry (Euonymus alatus). The fruits are round, approximately three to four bu in size, and when they ripen, they split open to reveal the seeds inside. This form is also the same as the previous entry. Although the branches do have wing-like corky ridges, they are fewer in number and smaller in size. This plant corresponds to the one described in the writings of Wu Jin (呉晋) as having "leaves like those of the peach tree."
