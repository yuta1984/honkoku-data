【Right folio】

*A variant:* This variety is widely cultivated at private residences and used as hedging. The tree reaches a height of eight or nine *shaku* (approximately 240–270 cm). The bark is the same as in the preceding entry. Both old and new stems bear numerous thorns, which make harvesting the leaves quite troublesome. Leaves emerge in spring. Their form resembles that of the preceding entry, but they are somewhat smaller. The flowers and fruits are likewise the same as in the preceding entry.
