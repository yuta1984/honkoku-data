【Right folio】

*A variant:* This variety is widely cultivated in people's gardens and used as a hedge. The tree reaches a height of eight or nine *shaku* (approximately 240–270 cm). The bark is the same as in the preceding entry. Both old and new stems bear many thorns, making the harvesting of leaves quite troublesome (and injurious to the hands). In spring, leaves emerge. Their form is similar to that of the preceding entry but somewhat smaller. The flowers and fruits are likewise the same as those of the preceding entry.
