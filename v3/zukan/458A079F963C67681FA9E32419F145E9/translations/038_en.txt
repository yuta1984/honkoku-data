【Right folio】

*One variety*　　***Mitsuba-utsugi***
　　　　　　　　***Kurohaze***
　　　　　　　　***Komegome*** *(interlinear note: Nikkō)*

　　It does not grow into a large tree, but produces many branches. The leaves grow in threes on a single stem, resembling those of *rengyō* (forsythia). In April, it forms spikes at the tips of its branches and opens its flowers. The shape is similar to the preceding entry. The fruit resembles the shape of a *gunbai* (a fan-shaped military commander's baton).

【Left folio】

*One variety*　　***Fusuma-utsugi***

　It grows wild in the mountains of Nikkō. The leaves resemble those of *utsugi* but are broader, growing in opposite pairs, with five longitudinal veins. It forms spikes at the branch tips and opens white, four-petaled flowers in the summer months. The flowers measure over six *bu* in size (approximately eighteen millimeters), making them larger than those of ordinary *utsugi*.
