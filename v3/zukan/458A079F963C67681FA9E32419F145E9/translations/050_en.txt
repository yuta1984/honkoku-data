Shishu (紫珠, *Callicarpa*) 《Annotation: from Jijie (集解, "Collected Explanations")》 — *Komurasaki*
　*Mimurasaki* 《Annotation: name used in Banshū (Harima Province)》
　　*Komegomenoки* 《Annotation: a name also found in Bishū (Owari Province)》

It grows in the mountains. The branches and leaves are arranged in opposite pairs. The shape of the leaves resembles that of *Nishikigi* (桃葉衛矛, *Euonymus alatus*), being pale green with fine serrations along the edges. Between the leaves, small five-petaled purple flowers bloom in dense clusters. Afterward, round berries are produced. Initially green, they ripen to a purple color after autumn. Their shape resembles the berries of *Natsuhaze* (南燭, *Vaccinium oldhami* / Japanese blueberry). When the leaves have fallen and withered, the berries become all the more beautiful.

A related variety:
　*Murasaki-shikibu* (*Callicarpa japonica*)
　*Murasaki-shikimi*
　*Tama-murasaki* / *Tome-utsugi*

The shape of the flowers and berries is the same as that of *Komurasaki*. The shape of the leaves is also the same, but larger.
