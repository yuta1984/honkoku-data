【Right folio】
A variety:
　Tsuru-nanten (Climbing Nandina)

【Left folio】
　　The shape of the leaves resembles that of nanten (Nandina domestica), with the leaf veins contracted and gathered, forming wrinkles. The stem twists and bends, resembling a vine (tendril). The flowers are the same as those of the common variety, and the berries are likewise red.
