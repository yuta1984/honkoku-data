**Right folio:**
A variety

　The tree and leaves are the same as described in the preceding entry (i.e., *Fusō*). The form of the flower is likewise as described in the preceding entry, but the flower is yellowish-brown (*kawashoku*) in color, tinged with red at the base.

**Left folio:**
A variety

　The tree and leaves are the same as described in the preceding entry. The flower is also as in the preceding entry, but it is white in color, tinged with red at the base.
