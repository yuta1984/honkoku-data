[Right folio]
A variety

**Nananoki** (as recorded in *Yamato Honzō*) / also called **Nanamimouchi**

This plant is commonly cultivated in gardens. Its leaves resemble those of the Ternstroemia (*tsukoku* / モッコク, *Ternstroemia gymnanthera*) in shape, and are thick; it does not shed its leaves in winter. The fruits grow in clusters, producing six to seven berries per bunch.

[Left folio]
A variety

**Fukuramouchi**

The leaves are rounder and larger than those of the preceding entry (Nanamioki), with a pointed tip. The flowers bloom among the leaves, and their form is also the same as the preceding entry.
