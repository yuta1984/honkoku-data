A variety

**Mokukeishi** (common name used by florists/gardeners) / **Hamamouchi** (Satsuma Province) / **Mochinoki** (a shared name; also a dialect term from Atami in Izu Province) / **Hegobana**

This tree is said to grow abundantly in the mountains of Shikoku. Its form resembles that of the *Tōsei* (Chinese holly / *Ilex chinensis*), and its leaves likewise resemble those of the *Tōsei*, being broad and pointed at both ends. Small white flowers with five petals bloom among the leaves at the tips of the branches. Their form, too, is similar to the flowers of the *Tōsei*.
