A variety

**Mokukeishi** (木茘枝, a name used in popular parlance among gardeners / *kako*) / **Hamamouchi** (a name used in Satsuma Province) / **Mochinoki** (a name also shared with another plant; a dialect term from the Atami area of Izu Province) / **Hegobana**

This tree is said to be found abundantly in the mountains of Shikoku. The tree resembles *Tōsei* (冬青, *Ilex* sp. / Japanese Holly), and its leaves likewise resemble those of *Tōsei*, being broad with both ends tapered to a point. Between the leaves at the tips of the branches, it opens small white flowers with five petals, whose form also resembles the flowers of *Tōsei*.
