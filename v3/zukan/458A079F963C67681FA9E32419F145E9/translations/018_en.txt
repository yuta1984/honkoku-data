**Sanban (山礬)**

[Regional names]: Someshiba; Tochishiba (Chikuzen province); Okoshikomeshiba (same region); Shimakuroki (Hyūga province); Kurohai; Akushiba; Hanamochi (Jō province/Yamashiro)

[Literary alternative names]: Haitei 梅弟 [Meibutsu Hōgen]; Yūkaku 幽客 [Tentseki Benran]; Kyūkokukō 九黒香 [Butsuri Shōshiki]

This plant grows in mountain forests. Its trees reach over one *jō* (approximately 3 meters) in height. The leaves do not fall even through winter; their shape resembles those of the gardenia (*kuchinashi*), being deep green in color and lustrous. Both branches and leaves grow in alternate arrangement. In spring, several stems of flower spikes, one to two *sun* (approximately 3–6 cm) long, emerge from among the leaves, opening five-petaled white flowers with yellow stamens. The fragrance is exceptionally pleasant.
