【Left folio】
Shikei (*Cercis chinensis*, Chinese Redbud)
　　Japanese names: *hana-suō*, *suō-hana*, *suō-no-ki*, *hana-murasaki*
　　Alternative names: Manzōkō [from *Qunfang Pu*]; Kaki [from *Zhengzitong*]; Waika [from *Gujin Yitong*]

　This tree is commonly cultivated in residential gardens. It grows to a height of approximately one *jō* (about 3 meters). In spring, it blooms before the leaves emerge. The flowers are purple-red in color and resemble those of *hagi* (Japanese bush clover, *Lespedeza*), but with thicker petals. Unlike other trees, the flowers bloom without a fixed location — they may appear on the trunk, on branches, or in the forks between branches — producing clusters of several flowers per spray. After flowering, the tree bears fruit resembling bean pods, containing flat, bean-like seeds inside. Cuttings planted in the ground will sprout readily. The leaves emerge after the flowers; they resemble simple bean leaves in shape, are thick, and reach three to four *sun* (approximately 9–12 cm) in size. The bark of this tree is harvested for medicinal use.
