A related variety:
　*Yabushiro*
　　*Yamajiro*

It grows in the mountains of Mount Tsukuba in Jōshū (Hitachi Province, present-day Ibaraki Prefecture). The tree reaches a height of about one *jō* (approximately 3 meters). The shape of the leaves resembles that of *Komurasaki*. In the summer months, small five-petaled white flowers bloom in dense clusters between the leaves. Afterward, small berries are produced. Initially green, they turn white upon ripening. The overall form is the same as that of *Komurasaki*.
