**Right folio:**
The tree and its leaves both resemble those of the preceding entry. The flowers bloom in clusters of seven from a single calyx. Their form is like that of a variety of plum (*ume*) known as "*Hanasaron*," and they are pale pink in color, resembling peonies (*botan*).
