【Right folio】
A variety:
　Kō-nanten (Red Nandina)

　　The shape of the leaves resembles that of nanten (Nandina domestica), being narrow and elongated.
　　From the time the new leaves emerge, they bear a red tinge and are beautiful. The shape of the flowers
　　is also the same as nanten; the buds are red,
　　and when they open, they become a pale red (light pink).
　　The berries, too, are likewise red.
