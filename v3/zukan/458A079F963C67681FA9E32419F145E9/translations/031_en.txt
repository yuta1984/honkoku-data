【Left folio】

The tree reaches a height of eight to nine shaku (approximately 240–270 cm). The bark has a whitish hue. In spring, young shoots emerge from the old stems, each stem bearing five leaves. The leaves resemble those of the *ninjin-boku* (牡荊, *Vitex negundo*), but the tips are not pointed, and the margins bear shallow serrations. Each leaf is approximately two sun (roughly 6 cm) in length. While still tender, the leaves are harvested and used as food. In summer, small flowers open in globose clusters among the leaves, and fruits subsequently form. The fruits are round, resembling those of ginseng (*Panax ginseng*), and turn black by autumn. The leaves also wither and fall in autumn. The *Yamato Honzō* states that the leaves are good to eat, either fresh or dried, and that they may also be used to make tea.
