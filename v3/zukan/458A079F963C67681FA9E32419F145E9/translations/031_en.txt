【Left folio】

The tree reaches a height of eight or nine *shaku* (approximately 240–270 cm). The bark has a whitish hue. In spring, young shoots emerge from old stems. Each stem bears five leaves, similar in form to those of *Ninjin-boku* (牡荊, *Vitex negundo*), but the leaf tips are not pointed, and there are shallow serrations (teeth) around the margins. Each leaf reaches about two *sun* (approximately 6 cm) in length. These leaves are harvested while still tender and used as food. In summer, small flowers open in spherical clusters (*mari*) among the leaves, and fruits subsequently form. The fruits are round, resembling those of ginseng (*Panax ginseng*), and turn black by autumn. The leaves also wither and fall in autumn. The *Yamato Honzō* states: "The leaves may be eaten fresh, or dried for consumption; they may also be made into tea."
