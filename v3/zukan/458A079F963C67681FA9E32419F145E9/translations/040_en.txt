【Left folio】

*Another variety*　　***Hakone-utsugi***　***Jūshimai*** *(interlinear note: Sansai Zue)*　***Kindaika*** *(interlinear note: same source)*
　　　　***Hinkyō*** *(interlinear note: cited in Tankō Sōroku from Seito Kokon-ki)*　***Kaisen-ka*** *(interlinear note: Minsui Endan-roku)*　***Nichigetsu-ka*** *(interlinear note: Chūzan Shitsumon)*

　The tree reaches a height of six to seven *shaku* (approximately 180–210 cm). In spring, young shoots emerge from the old stems. The shape of the leaves resembles that of *tani-utsugi* but is larger and thicker, with serrated edges around the margins. From early summer, flowers appear among the leaves — notably, six or seven flowers grow on one side; combining both sides, the number reaches fourteen or fifteen flowers. The shape of each individual flower resembles that of *tani-utsugi* but has a shorter tube and is larger overall. When the flowers first open, they are white; the following day, they become pale pinkish-red. As the days pass, they deepen to red. When a single tree is in full bloom, the several hundred red and white flowers are intermingled together, and the sight is exceedingly beautiful.
