**[Right folio]**
**Variety: (name not recorded)**

Both the tree and its leaves resemble those of the preceding entry (*niwazakura*). In spring, buds emerge from the base of the leaves. When the flowers open, their form is likewise the same as that of the preceding entry, but the color is white. Although the plant does occasionally bear fruit afterward, this is rare. When fruit is produced, its form is the same as that of *niwaume* (庭梅, garden plum).
