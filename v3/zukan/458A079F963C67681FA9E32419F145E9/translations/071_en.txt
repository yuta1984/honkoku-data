**Right folio:**
One variety: *Sui-fuyō* (醉芙蓉, "Drunken Hibiscus") — cited from *Hiden Kakyō* (秘伝花鏡)
　　　　　　*Shu-fuyō* (酒芙蓉, "Wine Hibiscus") — cited from *Kōtō Shingo* (広東新語)

**Left folio:**
　　Both the tree and leaves are the same as in the preceding entry. The flower's form is likewise double-petaled (multipetaled) as in the preceding entry; when it first opens it is white, then gradually takes on a rosy hue, and finally becomes deep crimson. It is for this reason that it is called *Sui-fuyō*, meaning "drunken hibiscus."
