　　Shinchou (New Butterfly)

　　Haruchou (Spring Butterfly)

　　　This ground caterpillar (jisan) overwinters underground, enduring the cold, and when spring arrives and the weather warms, it undergoes metamorphosis and becomes a butterfly—

【Left Page】

　　Nabanachou (Rape Blossom Butterfly) 〈Colloquial term〉

　　　As stated in the *Wakan Sansai Zue*: "Upon examination, butterflies come in several varieties. Those measuring approximately one sun (about 3 cm) in size and white in color are the most common, and they frolic among the flowers of green vegetables. There are also yellow butterflies, as well as those of a yellowish-brown color."
