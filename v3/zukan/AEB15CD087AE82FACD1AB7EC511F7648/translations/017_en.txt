【Right Page】
　　The *Kōmoku Shōsetsu* states: There are also those with various patterns on their wings, and such butterflies are exceedingly large. In Japanese custom, they are called "Ageha-no-chō" (Swallowtail Butterfly).
　Oni-chō (Demon Butterfly)　Kuro-ageha (Black Swallowtail)

　　Commentary: Li Shizhen states that the *Gujin Zhu* says that the larva (橘蠧, a wood-boring pest of citrus trees) transforms into a butterfly.

【Left Page】
　Oni-kagachō (Demon Brush-footed Butterfly)　Kuro-ageha (Black Swallowtail)

　　This too is a variety of the Genbu-semi (Dark Cicada), with body, legs, and antennae all black, and all four wings bearing numerous green-colored spots. This is the creature that transforms from the caterpillar (蠋) that feeds on white dittany (*Dictamnus albus*), and it lays its eggs on the undersides of leaves.
