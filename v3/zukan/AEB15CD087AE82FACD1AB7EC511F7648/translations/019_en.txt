　　Kuro-fuchō (Black Butterfly)

【Left Page】
　　In the illustrations of the *Honzō Kōmoku Keimō*, this is named "Kuro-fuchō" (Black Butterfly) — it belongs to the category of Genbu-semi, and is a particularly large specimen, entirely black in color. ○ Furthermore, the *Kōmoku Yakusetsu* states: the very largest of these measure approximately one shaku (about 30 cm). In recent years, there was an occasion when Japanese butterfly specimens were being collected at Kamigamo in Yamashiro Province, and at that time a very large butterfly was found.
