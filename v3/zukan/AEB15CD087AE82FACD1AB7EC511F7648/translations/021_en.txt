　　The "Moon-and-Sun Butterfly" (Tsukihi-chō) is also a type of Ryōzanpaku butterfly, characterized by yellow markings resembling the sun and moon.

　　One species: Bekko-chō (Tortoiseshell Butterfly)

　　The "Revised Gazetteer of Zhenjiang Prefecture" (Chōshū Chinkō Fushi) states: it is as large as a bat; some are black, and some have bluish mottled markings resembling tortoiseshell (hawksbill turtle shell), and are called "Fengzi" (鳳子, Phoenix Offspring). The common name is also Ryōzanpaku.
