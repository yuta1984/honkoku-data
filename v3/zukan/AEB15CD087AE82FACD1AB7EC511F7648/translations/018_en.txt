　　Alternative names: Kisha (鬼車)・Oni-kagachō (鬼蛺蝶)・Oni-chō (鬼蝶)《Interlinear note: from *Jiwu* and *Konju*》

　　The *Guangdong Xinyu* states: It is as large as a fan, has four wings, and the one that likes to fly above lychee trees is called "Kisha" (Demon Carriage), and is also called "Oni-kagachō" (Demon Brush-footed Butterfly).
　　The *Honzō Kōmoku Shōsetsu* states: There are also those with various patterns on their wings, and such butterflies are exceedingly large. In Japanese custom, they are called "Ageha-no-chō."
