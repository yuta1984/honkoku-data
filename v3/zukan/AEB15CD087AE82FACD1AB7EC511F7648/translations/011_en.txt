**Hosshindō** (菩薩蝶 / White Cabbage Butterfly)

This butterfly can be found throughout all four seasons. In spring, it emerges and sips the nectar from the stamens of turnip (*Brassica rapa*) flowers. There is also a somewhat smaller variety bearing black spots.

**[Left folio]**

During the rainy season (tsuyu), these butterflies fly in pairs over apricot and plum trees. All of them are transformed from inchworms (loopers/geometrid caterpillars). The caterpillars exist in several varieties, and accordingly the shape and markings of the resulting butterflies also differ from one another.
