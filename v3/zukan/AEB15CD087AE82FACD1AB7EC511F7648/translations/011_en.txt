【Right folio】

　　The Hosshintchō (Hosshin Butterfly)

　　This butterfly can be found throughout all four seasons. In spring, it emerges and sips nectar from the flowers of turnip/greens (菘). There are also individuals that are somewhat smaller and bear black markings.

【Left folio】

　　During the rainy season (梅雨), this butterfly flies in pairs above apricot and plum trees. All of these butterflies are the transformed forms of inchworms (geometrid moth larvae). The larvae come in various types, and accordingly, the shape and markings of the butterflies are not uniform.
