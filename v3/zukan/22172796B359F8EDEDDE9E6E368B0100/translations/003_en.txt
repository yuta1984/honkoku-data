The first fascicle of the Spring volume, which I had previously compiled together, represents my repeated efforts at sketching from life beginning in the early years of the Bunsei era. However, although the brushwork was accomplished, the true spirit and shading (atmosphere) of the plants were not fully captured. Now, while wishing to correct these errors, I have chosen not to discard the old volume, and instead will re-render the flowers and petals anew in this one. Furthermore, I ask that any deficiencies in the nomenclature and meanings of plant names be supplemented, and humbly present this work close to the knees of learned gentlemen, requesting their guidance.

　　Written at Kakaisho, on a day in the year Kōshin (1844), the fifteenth year of the Tenpō era.
