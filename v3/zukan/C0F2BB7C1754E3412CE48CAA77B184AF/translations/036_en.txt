**From "Jibutsu Genshi" (Origins of Things):**
"The 𩸎 fish (eso/lizardfish) is white as silver, with a round body, a wide (■) mouth, fresh-tasting flesh, measuring over one shaku (approx. 30 cm) in length. Both its bones and flesh are soft. If left without being salted for a long time, it turns entirely to water, leaving only the bones behind."

**From "Nansan Shi" (Records of Southern Products):**
"The 𩸎 fish is called 'Mian Yu' (cotton fish) by people in Guangdong, and 'Xinling' by people in Funing. Large specimens exceed one shaku in length, as thick as a gray pipe, and are also called 'Huoguan Ling.' In eastern Zhejiang, it is wind-dried and called 'Feng-■.'"

**[Upper section] From "Izu Kaijima Fudoki" (Topography of the Izu Island Chain):**

Shake (salmon) is caught offshore from late spring through autumn. Its nature is fierce, and it commonly bites through fishing lines. It measures 5–6 shaku (approx. 150–180 cm) in size, and is named as it is because its form resembles the sake (salmon) found in various provinces. It is an oily fish. It is sliced thin, dried, and stored.
