【Middle section】
Anako (Conger Eel): A large fish. Caught using rope boats (longline fishing vessels). Its color is black.
Also known as: Meshiro Unagi ("white-eyed eel") — has white eyes
Also: Meshiro Hamo ("white-eyed pike conger")

What Edo fish merchants call "Kawa Anako" (River Conger): its skin is black, and some individuals are tinged slightly red.
The largest specimens can reach six to seven shaku (approximately 180–210 cm).
They sometimes come to market mixed together with common anago.

【Lower section】
【Sticky note (fusen)】Also known as: Hafu
The name "Dainan" (Great Southern/Large) is given because these fish come from the open sea off Mito, Chōshi, or the Miura Peninsula. Large ones reach four to five shaku (approximately 120–150 cm).
Compared to ma-anago (common conger), the flavor is not notably different. The snout is slender, the flesh is plump, and the cross-section is nearly perfectly round.

Marine product of Sōshū (Sagami Province). Lacks pectoral fins. Black coloration with white star-like spots; filleted and eaten.
Kuro Anako (Black Conger): The flesh is soft but lacks firmness, and there are extremely many small bones. The price is also low.
