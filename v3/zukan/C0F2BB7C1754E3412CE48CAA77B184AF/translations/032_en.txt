From the "Wakan Sansai Zue," entry for "䲍 (okoze)":
The Japanese name is "Okoshi" (乎古之), commonly called "Okoze" (乎古世).
Upon investigation, both "䲍 (okoze)" and "鱖 (ketsu)" are said to inhabit lake waters; however, both species as seen in fish markets are in fact products of rivers and the sea.
The form of the okoze is extremely ugly, and it is thus compared to an ugly woman (shikome); its spines sting people.
