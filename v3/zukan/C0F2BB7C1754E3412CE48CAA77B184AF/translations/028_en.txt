**Shirahake**

This is a local dialect name from the coastal waters of Kii Province. In Edo (present-day Tokyo), it is classified as a type of *Kawahagi* (filefish, *Stephanolepis cirrhifer* or related species). In Sado Island, there is a variety of the same shape but larger and yellowish-brown in color; its skin is stripped off and used in place of sharkskin as a grater for grinding food. It is also called "*Okikōguri*."
