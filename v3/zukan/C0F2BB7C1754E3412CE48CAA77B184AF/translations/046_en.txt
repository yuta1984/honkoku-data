*Katanaki* — from Etchū Province (present-day Toyama)

(Marginal note: *Datsu* [needlefish/garfish])
The body width is about one sun five bu (approx. 4.5 cm),
the length is about three shaku five sun (approx. 106 cm), white in color,
and has a lustrous sheen.

**Right folio, middle section:**
*Aka-datsu* — a variety of *Shiira* (dolphinfish/mahi-mahi)
Has scales. Has yellow star-shaped spots, with fins
above and below. The head is not particularly large.
(Note citing *Keimon* [an encyclopedic reference work]: *Kome-utsubo*. The entire body is crimson, with deep scarlet markings.
Its appearance while swimming in the sea is exceedingly beautiful.)

**Left folio, middle section:**
*Rinzu-uo* (Satin-ribbon Fish) — from Echigo Province (present-day Niigata)

It rarely gets caught on hooks set for mackerel,
or is occasionally taken in nets. It also sometimes
washes ashore with the waves.
Its shape resembles an obi sash, with a width of three or four
sun (approx. 9–12 cm), flattened in form, and a length of six or seven shaku
(approx. 180–210 cm),
or sometimes over one jō (over 300 cm). The mouth is pointed,
there is a fin along the back, and the tail is forked like that of a mackerel.
No scales. The color is white and lustrous, as if mica had been
