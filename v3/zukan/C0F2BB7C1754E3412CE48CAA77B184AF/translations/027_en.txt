## Right Page (with illustration)

### Upper Section

**Fish from Tsushima (Ēbari)**

This fish resembles the roppau (i.e., nizadai / surgeonfish). At the beginning of the dorsal fin near the head, there are two spines, which cause considerable pain if one is pricked by them. It is caught in spring and autumn. Length: approximately five or six sun. Scales are fine; color is mouse-gray (grayish). The mouth is small; the tail is not forked. It is used to make kamaboko (fish-paste cake).

**Manbōdai (Sunfish / Mola mola)** — This was brought to Nagasaki by Chinese traders. In former times it was called "Tenuo" (天魚, "heavenly fish"), and that fish which was brought over is said to be broadly similar to this one.

### Lower Section

**Okiainame** (from visual reference / specimen)

**Dashi** — A species of ayuname (greenling). The back is pale purple; the lower portions are pale blue and moss-green, with white spots. The jaw is slightly short; the tail is not forked. The second dorsal fin and the anal fin (the fin below the belly) are long, reaching to the abdomen. The fin below the jaw is large, and from below the belly there protrudes a penis-like appendage (a genital organ). It is therefore called "Dashi" (meaning "that which protrudes / is put out"). The tip of the tail is red. The mouth is close in shape to that of a shark (fuka / same).
