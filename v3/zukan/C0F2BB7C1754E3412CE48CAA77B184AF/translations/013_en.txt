**Left Folio (with illustration)**

*Yamato Honzō* ("Japanese Materia Medica" by Kaibara Ekiken) states: "The *Sufuta-ei* resembles the *kaime* (a type of ray). It is broader and larger than the *kaime*. The eyes, fins, and tail are the same as those of the *kaime*."

Item description: *Urashi* — the mouth is reddish-black tinged with yellow.

　In Geshū (Aki Province), *Touran* is abundant; it is dried and eaten.

　**Nukumochi** — its color is lighter than the *Aka-ei* (red ray / common stingray), and its shape is round. The tail is somewhat flattened. It is very slimy and not good to eat.

　**Jiurotakuta (?)** — also called *Nukumochi* (or *Kumochi*?) (#2). It is very slimy but is delicious when boiled. It is not suited for eating as-is; it is best prepared as *nuta* (vegetables or fish dressed with vinegar-miso) (#1).

　**Touran** — its color is white; it resembles the *Mae-ei* (frontal ray) but has thin flesh.

　**Shirata** — large specimens are called *Douran*. The fins of the *Shiro-ei* (white ray) are of the same shape as those of the *Aka-ei*, but lighter in color, with a flattened tail.
