**Left Folio (with illustration)**

According to *Yamato Honzō* ("Japanese Materia Medica"): The *Sufuta-ei* resembles the *Kaime* [shell-eye ray]. It is flatter and larger than the *Kaime*. Its eyes, fins (*kohire*), and tail are the same as those of the *Kaime*.

**Species listings** [#3]:

- **Urashiro** — Reddish-black in color, tinged with yellow.
- **Tōran** of Geishū [Hiroshima region] — Most commonly dried and eaten.
- **Nukumochi** — Paler in color than *Aka-ei* (Red Stingray), round in shape. The tail is somewhat flattened, and it is very slimy; the taste is not good.
- **Jiurotak(u)ta** [?] — Also called *Nukumochi* [or *Kumochi*?] [#2]. Very slimy; delicious when boiled. Not suitable for *nuta* [#1].
- **Tōran** — White in color, resembles *Mae-ei*, but with thin flesh.
- **Shirata** — The large variety is called **Dōran**. *Shiro-ei* (White Ray) has the same shape as *Aka-ei* (Red Stingray), but is paler in color, with a flattened tail.
