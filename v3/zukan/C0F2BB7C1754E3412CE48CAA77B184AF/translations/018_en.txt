**Middle Section**
Inhabits the sea of Iki Island at a depth of eighty *hiro* (approx. 145 meters).
*Yahagi* (resembles *kawamuki* but is black in color)

**Lower Section**
Resembling the *Umazura-kawahagi* (elongate filefish), with a long body and a shape similar to that of *ishimochi* (croaker/drum). Its fine scales are raised like bristles, forming a spine-like texture. The eyes are large, and the mouth resembles that of *kawahagi* (filefish) or *fuefuki* (emperor fish/spangled emperor). There is a spine behind the head, and another spine behind that, pointing backward. There are also spines on the chest facing each other. The dorsal fin is short. There are two small fins on the jaw. The tail is forked (*bifurcated*); the color is purplish-blue, and the belly is white. Fishermen regard this as a variety of *kawahagi*.

Size: one *shaku*; smaller specimens about five to six *sun*. This is a variety of □□□ (three characters obscured by illustration). The skin is rough like shagreen (shark skin), and the skin is peeled before use. The flesh has a wood-grain-like pattern. It is commonly regarded as a poisonous fish.

　*Chikararippu* — seaweed/tidal grass (a type of marine algae)

***Tsunohagi***
There are many of this type. Its skin is rough like shagreen, its belly has a luster, and it resembles the fish called "*hara-gichi*." It was found among *tazukuri* (dried small fish, typically anchovies or sardines used as processed food). There are two horn-like spines beneath the belly.
Given this, it belongs to the same category as the *umuu-wo* (likely *umazura-kawahagi*) described in the preceding entry.

*Wakan Sansai Zue* states: "*Niragi* resembles *ubose* in shape but is smaller, with a bluish-white color. It has one spine at the throat. It appears in the eighth and ninth months [of the lunar calendar]. It is found abundantly in Settsu and Izumi provinces. The body is about one to two *sun* in size. Whether made into *shiokara* (salted fermented paste) or *kasuzuke* (sake-lees pickling), both preparations yield a fragrant and delicious flavor."
