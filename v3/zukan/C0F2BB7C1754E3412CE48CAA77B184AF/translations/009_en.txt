**Right Folio (with illustration)**

The Tonbi-ei (Kite Ray) has a distinctively shaped head, resembling a ladle (*shakushi*), and is thick. Its mouth is located on the underside. Its color is *tobi-iro* (kite-bird brown, a reddish-brown).
(Classical Chinese annotation): "Its true appearance is rounded; the head overall resembles that of a toad (*hikigaeru*). The back is bluish-black, the belly is white, the fins run along continuously to the tail, the flavor is of low quality, and its price is cheap."

**Left Folio (with illustration)**

- In Kujūkuri (Chiba Prefecture), it is called *Tonbi-ei*, with the alternative name *Takamarū*.
- *Hato-ebi* is another name for *Tobi-ebi*.
- The *Tobi-ei* from Geishū (Aki Province, modern Hiroshima) grows to about five shaku (approx. 150 cm) and can be caught by fishing.
- Note from *Buppin* (物品): *Hatsu-ei*, *Tonbi-ei*, *Tobi-ei*, and *Tori-ei* are all names for the same creature.
- According to *Ise no Tadashi*: "The flesh is thick and somewhat firmer than that of the *Mae-ebi*."
- According to a fish merchant: "The Tonbi does not exceed about three shaku (approx. 90 cm) in size."
- According to *Yamato Honzō* (大和本草): "The *Tobi-ei* is mouse-gray (*nezumi-iro*) in color, and its head resembles that of a turtle."
