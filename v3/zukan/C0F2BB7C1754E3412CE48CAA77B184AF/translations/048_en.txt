In Tsuruga, Echizen Province (present-day Fukui), there is a fish called *Umi-gigi* (sea gigi catfish), also referred to as *Shade*. Its color is pale yellow with black spots. The body length is one to two shaku (≈30–60 cm), or about three shaku (≈90 cm). It is the same fish as the one described in Ranzan's account, though it is a different fish (from the river variety).

**Upper section:**
*Kin-fugu* (Golden Pufferfish): The back is pale black, and its form is like the *ma-fugu* (true pufferfish). It has an angular body shape. The belly resembles *shiosai* (a type of pufferfish), and it has a few spines.

**Second row:**
The *Taiwan Fushi* (Gazetteer of Taiwan Prefecture) states: *Toshitsu*.
The head is flat and the body slightly round, black in color.
Length is five to six sun (≈15–18 cm). Found in irrigation ponds,
marshes, and burrows.

**Right margin:**
*Konzu*: Found in the sea around (Anegasaki?) in Kazusa Province (present-day Chiba). It resembles the *gigi* catfish, about five to six sun (≈15–18 cm) long. It is the color of sooty bamboo (*susutake*) with vertical stripes, and it stings people.

The *Kaisa-so* (Comprehensive Sea Creatures Account) states: *Toshitsu* lives in the mud. It is named after the louse (*shirami*) which it resembles. Also called *Toshitsu*. It has spines that pierce people (like plucking a lute). Another name is *Danshitsu* ("lute-plucker"). Found frequently at the bottom of paddy field banks and ponds. Another name: *Denshitsu*.

　　*Gonzu*: Lives at the boundary between fresh and salt water. Has white stripes. Being stung by it is extremely painful.
　　*Gonzō*: Yellow body with black markings. About six sun (≈18 cm) long.
　　One variety produced in the sea is called *Umi-gigi*. In Settsu Province it is also called *Shade*; in Echizen Province, *Kugu*; in Tosa Province (another name). The form is no different from the river variety. However, it has firm black-and-white vertical stripes. About three sun (≈9 cm) in length. The *Yamato Honzō* (Japanese Materia Medica) states it does not exceed five sun (≈15 cm).

　　*Gon'i*: Lives in the inner bay of Nago, Bōshū Province (present-day Chiba). It resembles the *kibachi* (yellow hornet/a fish species). It has two barbels. It stings (people's hands?) with its fins. This occurs in the second or third month (of the lunar calendar). It burrows into sea mud.

**Lower section:**
Lives in shallow sea around Iki Island (present-day Nagasaki). Resembles a goldfish or catfish and is reddish. Being stung by it is extremely toxic/venomous.
