【KOBUSHI / MOKUREN (Japanese Magnolia / Kobus Magnolia and related species)】

**Shin'i (辛夷, Magnolia kobus)**

According to the *Honzō Wage* (Japanese Interpretation of the Materia Medica):
　　Alternate names: Shinkei (辛雞), Kōtō (侯桃), Bōboku (房木), Mokuhitsu (木筆, "wooden brush"),
　　　　　　　　　Geishun (迎春, "welcoming spring")
　　Japanese name: Kobushi

[Seal] Medicinal Tree

Drawn directly from life on the twenty-first day of Kisaragi (second month), Mizunoe-Uma year.

　　Listed in the Flower and Tree section (Kaboku-bu) of the *Yamato Honzō*.

The author (Ju/Kotobuki) states:
　Shin'i has several varieties. Gyokuran-ka (玉蘭花 / Ōyama-renge, *Magnolia sieboldii*),
　Hakumokuren (白木蘭, white magnolia), Mokuren (紫木蓮, purple magnolia / *Magnolia liliiflora*),
　Shite-kobushi (石喬木), and Hō-no-ki (朴の木, *Magnolia obovata*) are all
　members of the Shin'i family.
