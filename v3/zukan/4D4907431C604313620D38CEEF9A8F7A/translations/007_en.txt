## [Broad Bean - *Vicia faba*]

Broad bean (Soramame)

　　The *Shokkyō* states:
　　　This can be used as food.

　　The *Yamato Honzō* states:
　　　Its pods point upward toward the sky, hence the name.

　　Soramame (sky bean)　　In the western provinces, it is called "Tō-mame" (Tang/Chinese bean)
　　　Copied in Kōshin spring (1820)
　　　　　　[Seal] Grain category
　　　　　　[Seal] Medicinal herb

The *Zōho Tashiki* (Expanded Polyglot Dictionary), under the legume section, states:
　　Broad bean (San-mame)　Japanese name: Soramame　Alternative name: Kōzu (Hu bean / Chinese bean)
　　　　　　　◦ Oyataoshi-mame [possibly meaning "parent-toppling bean," i.e., a bean so productive it outdoes its origin]
　　　　　　　　Yukiwari-mame (Snow-splitting bean)
　　　　　　　　　　　(These are) names used by farming households

　　　　　In Yamato province, it is frequently cultivated and thus also called "Yamato-mame"
　　　　　In the western provinces, it is called "Tō-mame"
