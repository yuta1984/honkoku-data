## 【Asebi (馬酔木; Japanese Andromeda, *Pieris japonica*)】

**As recorded in the *Wakan Sansai Zue* (和漢三才図会; *Illustrated Sino-Japanese Encyclopaedia of the Three Realms*), in the section on Shrubs (*Kanboku-rui*):**

　　*Ma-suimoku* (馬酔木; "horse-drunken tree") — [Tang Chinese pronunciation: *Asemi*; Japanese reading: *Asehonoki*] — Also called *Asemi* in popular usage, and colloquially "Aseho" (*Asebo*).

---

**According to the *Bencao Gangmu* (本草綱目; *Compendium of Materia Medica*):**

　　*Kinboku* (梫木; *Qinmu*) — [Japanese name: *Ashimi*; *Asebo*] — [Japanese name per the *Wamyō-shō* (和名抄): *Ma-suimoku* (馬酔木)]

---

　　　The *Yamato Honzō* (大和本草; *Japanese Materia Medica*) states: "If a horse eats these leaves, it will die."
　　　The *Chikinsho* (地錦抄; a horticultural encyclopaedia) states: "If a horse eats *Ma-suimoku*, it will become intoxicated."
