【Shidareyanagy (枝垂れ柳; Weeping Willow, *Salix babylonica*)】

**From the *Jikinshō* (地錦抄; "Embroidered Ground Digest"):** Dokuyō-ryū (独榣柳; Shidariyanagy)

[Seal: Garden Trees (園木)]

---

**From the *Yamato Honzō* (大和本草; "Japanese Herbals"):**

　Willow Flower (柳花) / Yōryū (楊柳)　"Yōryū" is the general name, referring specifically to the Shidariyanagy (weeping willow).

　Li Shizhen [*Bencao Gangmu*] states: "The yōryū has supple, drooping branches that trail like flowing water, and it is therefore called '*ryū*' (柳; willow)."

　This is precisely the Yōryū (Weeping Willow; Shidareyanagy).

---

Sketched from life at the Rain-Leisure Pavilion (雨閑亭; *Ukantei*) on the twenty-ninth day [#1] of the final period of *Kyōshō* (夾鐘; the second lunar month), year of Kinoe-Saru (甲申; 1824).

---

**From the *Wamyō Ruijushō* (和名類聚抄; "Japanese Names Classified and Collected"):**

　Willow (柳; Shitariyanagy)　The *Kenmeien* (兼名苑; "Garden of Combined Names") states: the willow's alternative name is *Shōyō* (小楊; "small poplar/willow").

　　The pronunciation of 柳 (*ryū*) is rendered by the *fanqie* spelling: 加久の反 (*kaku no han*).

　　Japanese name: Shitariyananraki (之太里夜奈良木)

---

**From the *Zōho Tashikiben* (増補多識編; "Expanded Compendium of Many Identifications"), section on Tall Trees (喬木類):**

　Willow (*Ryū*, 柳)　[Japanese name:] Shidari-yanagi (志多利也那岐)　[Alternative names:] Shōyō (小楊; "small yang/poplar") [cited from the *Shuowen* (説文)]; Yōryū (楊柳); Nikuridamoku (尼倶律陀木; *Nyagrodha*-wood) [cited from Buddhist scriptures]

---

This species (Weeping Willow) has an especially large number of related varieties. The author, Ju [Baiyen], notes:

　○ Willow (Shitariyanagy)　Yōryū (楊柳) refers to the *yō* (楊; round-leaved willow, *Salix* spp., whose branches do not droop). Water-willow (水楊) = Kawayanagy (river willow) or Inokoyanagy. White poplar (白楊) = Hakoyanagy (箱柳; *Populus* spp.). *Ihashiba* [#2] (栘枎) = Shideyanagy. Tamarisk-willow (檉柳) is the *Gyoryū* (御柳; *Tamarix* spp.). *Kobu-yanagy* (杞柳; *Salix integra*) etc.
