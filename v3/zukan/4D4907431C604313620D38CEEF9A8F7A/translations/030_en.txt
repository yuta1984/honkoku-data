【A Species of Wild Raspberry/Bramble】
Flower of Kenkōshi (懸鈎子, wild raspberry/bramble)
　　Ōmomiji-ichigo (Large-leafed Raspberry)
　　Tō-ichigo (Chinese Raspberry)

　　　　　　According to Shokkyō:
　　　　　　　　In Japanese custom, there is one called "Kara-ichigo (韓伊知己, Korean raspberry)."
　　　　　　　　There is also one called "Tō-ichigo (唐伊知己, Chinese raspberry)," which is the same species.
　　　　　　　　What the Jikinsho calls "Ki-ichigo (黄苺, yellow raspberry)" is this very plant.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Faithfully copied on the 10th day of the first third of Koshō month, Mizunoto-Hitsuji year
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　【Category: Fruit-bearing Vines】

According to Zōho Tashikihen, Climbing Herb Category:
　　Kenkōshi (懸鈎子)　Japanese name: Upon investigation, "Ki-ichigo (幾伊知古)"
　　　　　　　　　　　Alternative name: Sanbo (山苺, mountain berry) [from Erya]
　　　　　　　　　　　Additional alternative names: Enkōshi (沿鈎子) [from Nichiyō],
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sen (葥) [from Erya],
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Moku-bō (木苺) [from Guo Pu],
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ju-bō (樹苺) [from Nichika]

According to the investigative commentary in Wakan Sansai Zue:
　　Moku-bō (木苺, wood raspberry) has leaves similar to those of bōboku (蒲蔔), which are hard and deep green in color, and its stems have no thorns.
　　What Li Shizhen describes corresponds to the stem and leaves of hebi-ichigo (蛇苺, snake strawberry / Duchesnea indica).
　　This plant (木苺) corresponds precisely to [Ki-ichigo].
　　The Yamato Honzō identifies it as Tō-ichigo (唐苺, Chinese raspberry).
