## Hōran (茅蘭) [Chinese name]

　[Also known as:] Chigo-ran / Jiran (児蘭, "child orchid")
　[Also known as:] Ōran / Kōran (黄蘭, "yellow orchid")
　Japanese name: Suzuran (鈴蘭, "lily of the valley"), also called Yamaran (山蘭, "mountain orchid")

---

According to Yamato Honzō (大和本草):
　　Kōzan-ran (黄山蘭, "yellow mountain orchid")

【Seal】 Hōsō (芳艸, "Fragrant Grasses/Plants")

---

According to Sansai Zue (三才図会):
　　Arima-sō (有馬草) / Kinran (キンラン)
　　　[This plant is abundant in Arima in Settsu Province, hence it is used as the regional common name.]

---

Around the middle-to-end of the third spring month of Bunsei 6 (1823), on or around the望日 (full moon / 15th day)
Sketched directly from the living specimen at Shūkarōkaku (集花楼閣)
