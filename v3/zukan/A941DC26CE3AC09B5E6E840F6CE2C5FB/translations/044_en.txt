## Record of Herb Category

Source: Various Materia Medica (*Honzō*) texts

---

**Joon (女菀 / *Aster fastigiatus* or related species)**
Alternative names: *Hakuen* (白菀), *Shokujooen* (織女菀), *Jomō* (女茆)
《marginal note: Pronunciation reading — "*Ryū*" (桺)》

　　　*Tōjion* (唐女菀 / Chinese Aster)
　　　*Himejion* (姫女菀 / Small Fleabane)
　　　*Kojion* (小女菀 / Little Aster)

---

　　　　　　　　　　This is the true *Joon* (女菀). It bears white flowers and resembles
　　　　　　　　　　*Shion* (紫菀 / *Aster tataricus*); this is what is called *Joon*.
　　　　　　　　　　However, the Japanese and Chinese [usages/specimens] differ greatly from one another.

---

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　27th day of Late Autumn (*Kashū*), Year of Boshi —
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　a stem was sent by Mr. Yaku,
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　drawn from the specimen.
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　【Herb Category】
