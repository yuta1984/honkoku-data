"Tashiki-hen" (Diverse Knowledge Compilation), Mountain Grasses section, states:
Kidokuyu　Japanese name: Otowo　《Alternative name》 Dokuyō (独揺) grass
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Toshi

○Hakuma　Kasana

Hinoe-Inu year, Autumn, Nanryo (8th month), middle period, 10th day, copied

My opinion:
"Honzō Kōmoku" (Compendium of Materia Medica) states that Kidokuyu is a different species from the plant described here. It has a single stem, sparse leaves, nothing at its tip, and moves of its own accord in the wind. Therefore it is named "Dokuyō" (independently swaying). Dokukatsu (独活, Aralia cordata) shares the same name yet is a different plant of the same class. Its form and shape belong to the same category as Umano-mitsuba. This plant is called "Ebi-te no Kidokuyu" and is a separate variety. There are several varieties of Kidokuyu, and the number of types is exceedingly large.
