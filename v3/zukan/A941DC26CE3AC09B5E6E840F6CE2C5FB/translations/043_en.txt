## Record of Herb Category

　　　　　　　　　　　　22nd day of Nanryō (南呂, the 8th lunar month), Year of Boshi
　　　　　　　　　　　　Drawn from life in the garden
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　【Herb Category】

---

**Jakōsō (麝香草 / Musk Herb)**

The *Wakan Sansai Zue* and *Chikinshō* state:
　　　*Jakōsō* (麝香草, Musk Herb) — a popular name is known, but the true botanical name remains uncertain.

---

The *Iien Shōchū* states:
　　　*Jakōsō* (麝草) — one variety
　　　《marginal note: Jakōsō》

---

　　　　　　　　　　The *Chikinshō* and *Wakan Sansai Zue* state:
　　　　　　　　　　The Musk Herb carries a faint (*chito*) fragrance of musk,
　　　　　　　　　　though its scent is more strongly reminiscent of fennel (*uikyō*).
　　　　　　　　　　It blooms in the third month, with single petals of pale crimson — and so forth.
　　　　　　　　　　[However,] the claim that it blooms in spring is quite at odds [with what I have observed].
　　　　　　　　　　When the seedlings sprout in spring, there are no flowers; and whether it blooms separately
　　　　　　　　　　as a spring-flowering plant is unclear.
　　　　　　　　　　I find this doubtful.
