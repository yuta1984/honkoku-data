## Record of Herb Category

　　　　　　　　　　　　【Herb Category】
　　　　　　　　　　　　23rd day of the 7th month of autumn, Year of Bosshi — Drawn from life at the medicinal herb garden

---

**Gyokkyūka (Daikonsō / 玉毬花)**

*As appearing in the Getsurei Kōgi*

Japanese names:
- Itanokiku (板野菊)
- Matsumushisō (松虫草)
- Rinhōkiku (輪鳳菊)
- Nukenmokkou (ヌケンモツコウ)

---

The *Chikinshō* states:
　　　This is a variety of *Rōga* (狼牙 / "Wolf's Fang"). Upon investigation, what is called Daikon [here] — its leaves are referred to as *Kenmokkou*.

---

The *Kyūkō Honzō* states: *Ryūyōsō* (柳葉草 / "Willow-leaf Herb") — one of two varieties:
　　　Willow-leaf Herb (柳葉草)

A nurseryman (plant shop keeper) says:
　　　*Suiyōsō* (水楊草 / "Water Willow Herb")

　　　　　　　I (Baien) state: *Suiyō* (水楊) is commonly called *Kawayagi* (川柳 / "River Willow").
　　　　　　　This herb's leaves resemble those of the willow, and so it is called *Suiyōsō* ("Water Willow Herb").
　　　　　　　This is the same plant as *Ryūyōsō* ("Willow-leaf Herb").
