**Chinese name: Jiangnan Flower (江南花)**

**Morning Glory (Asagao, 朝顔)** — One of the Seven Autumn Flowers

*Shun* (蕣, Asagao): The common name. In current popular usage, the character 蕣 is mistakenly applied to the name of *mukuge* (Rose of Sharon, 木槿), and this error has persisted for a long time. In ancient times, *mukuge* was also read as "asaka," making the distinction difficult to establish through textual research. Today, *mukuge* is called "hachisu."

【Medicinal Herb】

---

**From the *Honzō Wage* (本草和解):**

**Morning Glory (Kengokhwa, 牽牛花)**

Alternative names: Koku-chū (黒丑), Grass Golden Bell (草金鈴), Pencao Zeng (盆草甑), Dog-ear Grass (狗耳草, Kunigusa)

Japanese name: Asakaho (鴉左伽保)

---

**From the *Kyūkō Yafu* (救荒野譜):**

Black Morning Glory (黒牽牛, Asagao)

In Prince Zhou Xian Wang's *Jiuhuang Bencao* (救荒本草), this plant is referred to as "Dog-ear Grass" (狗耳草).

---

**From the *Wamyō Ruijushō* (和名類聚抄):** Under the entry for *Morning Glory seeds* (牽牛子, Asagao): The annotation in Tao Hongjing's (陶隱居) *Bencao* states: "The Japanese name is *Asakaho* (阿左加保). This plant spread from rural areas, where people would lead oxen to trade for the medicine; hence it received this name."
