The "Soumoku Ikushuu" (Cultivation of Plants and Trees), section on Beautiful Flowers, states:
　　Indigo chrysanthemum (Ezo-giku / Ainu chrysanthemum)
　　　　Mannen-giku (everlasting chrysanthemum) — from Suruga Province

[Baien's personal commentary:]
　The indigo chrysanthemum (藍菊) came to us in recent times from the island of Ezo (Hokkaido), and it is presumably named for that reason. If so, it should more properly be called "Ezo chrysanthemum" (蝦夷菊). There is also an Ezo violet (蝦夷菫), which is similar to it in this regard. The origin of the name "indigo chrysanthemum" (藍菊) is unclear and warrants further investigation. It does not appear in any other herbals or materia medica texts.
