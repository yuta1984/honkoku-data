### *Shitsutake* (濕菌) *(also called Kitsu-kō-take; juvenile form noted)*
**Toxic — not edible**

　　　　　　　　　Collected fresh in the mountains of Hakusan on the 17th day of the 3rd month of the *Tori* year of spring.
　　　　　　　　　Drawn directly from life.

---

### *Hanacistanche* / *Hi-kage-take* / *Kiseru-take* (花蓯蓉)

　　　　　　　　　Two varieties. Produced in the mountains of Nemura. Gathered by exploring the mountains on the 13th day of the 8th month. Drawn from life on the 14th.

---

### *Tengu-take* (天狗蕈 / Fly Agaric)

---

Its form resembles *shimeji* (占地), as shown in the illustration. It frequently grows intermingled among *shimeji* mushrooms. One must not mistakenly eat it. It carries a great poison and will kill a person.
