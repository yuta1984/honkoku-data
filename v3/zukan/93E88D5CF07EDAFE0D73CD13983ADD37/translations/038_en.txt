## *Kaki-shimeji* (柿占地; "Persimmon Shimeji")

Edible. The flavor is good, but is greatly inferior to *Daikoku-shimeji*.

---

## *Daikoku-shimeji* (大黒占地; "Daikoku Shimeji")

This is the finest variety among the *shimeji* mushrooms. It resembles *Ko-matsu-take* and grows in dense clusters. It is sweet, fragrant, and delicious. Its form resembles a wooden carved figure of Daikoku (the deity Daikokuten), and it is named accordingly.

## *Kawara-take* (土器蕈; "Tile/River-bank Mushroom")

*From the* Bencao Gangmu *(本草綱目):*
- 雚蘆 (*Yoshi-take* / Reed Mushroom)

*From the* Matsuoka Kinpu *(松岡菌譜):*
- 雚菌 (*Yoshi-take* / *Yoshi-ko* / "Shaka's Head" 〔a colloquial expression from the Saga district of Yamashiro Province〕 / *Ogi-take*)
  Prefers to grow in dense clusters among reeds (*yoshi*) and miscanthus reeds (*ogi*). Edible. Flavor is excellent.

Edible. The flavor resembles that of *shimeji*. No fragrance.
