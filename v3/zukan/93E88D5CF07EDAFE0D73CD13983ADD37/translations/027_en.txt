## The Above Seven Varieties

　*Kinoto-Hitsuji* year, *Mizunoe* 7th month, 16th day — obtained from within the mountains of Hakusan, and drawn from life.

　Each of the seven varieties grows from rotted cut grass or from layers of decomposed fallen leaves. The names of each are unknown.
