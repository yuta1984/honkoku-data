## *Tsuchi-kaburi* (土ムクリ)
*(Regional names: "Tsuchi-kafui" in Jōshū [Hitachi Province]; "Tsuchi-kafuri" in Aizu)*

---

All varieties are edible, but their flavor is not particularly fine. The cap (*jin*) is thick and tough. That said, they contain a slight toxicity and are mildly bitter.

---

**Four varieties — Collected in the mountains of Nemura on the 13th day of the 8th month; drawn from life on the 14th day**

---

***Tsuchi-kafuri*** *(one variety: also called **Nejitake**)*
