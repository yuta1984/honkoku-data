【Left Folio, Upper Section】

　　　*Yakuen* 　《Produced in Ezo 〔Hokkaido〕》
　　　　　　*Iburiko*
　　　　　　*Eburiko*

　　　*Agarikosu* (Agaricus)
　　　　　　i.e.,
　　　　　　*Anburiko* (*Eburiko*)

〔From the *Rokubutsu Shinshi* 〕

In the land of Ezo, this tree-ear fungus grows. There are two kinds of trees on which it grows. One is found at the foot of the mountains of Ishikari; the indigenous people (*Ainu*) call this tree "*Jungu*." Its trunk resembles that of the Jezo spruce (*Tōhi*) but is shorter. They call the mushroom growing on it "*Taboshi*." This is what is known as the "*Tōhi*" (Jezo spruce), also called the "Ezo cypress."

The other variety is found in the eastern reaches of *Etorofu* (Iturup Island)—《one of the so-called Thirty-Six Islands, with a circumference of some three hundred *ri* or more, it is said》—which is an extremely remote and isolated place. The tree greatly resembles the five-needled pine (*goyō-no-matsu*), and in the Ainu language it is called "*Kui*." The mushroom that grows on this tree is called "*Kui-karushi*," and is also referred to as "*Kusuri-karushi*." "*Karushi*" means "tree-ear mushroom" (*kinoko*) in the Ainu language.

【Lower Left of Left Folio】

A variety of *Sarunokoshikake* (hard bracket fungus). White in color and light in weight; its flesh, when rubbed between the fingers, becomes like cotton. Its flavor is bitter and acrid. It is said to be highly effective in treating abdominal pain and *shakki* (accumulated qi / digestive blockages). It is said to grow mostly on the branches of larch (*karamatsu*) trees.
