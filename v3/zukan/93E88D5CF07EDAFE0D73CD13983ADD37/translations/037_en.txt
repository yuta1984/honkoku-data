## *Shiba-shimeji* (芝蕈; "Lawn Shimeji")
Edible. The flavor resembles that of *Matsu-shimeji*, and is quite good. Gathered in large quantities for food.

---

### *Tsutsuji-take* (躑躅茸; "Azalea Mushroom")

Prefers to grow at the base of azalea (*Tsutsuji*) roots.
Edible. Non-toxic.

- *Hime-take* ("Princess Mushroom")
- *Hime-shimeji* ("Princess Shimeji")

---

## *Abura-shimeji* (脂湿地茸; "Oily Shimeji") — From the *Wakan Sansai Zue* (和漢三才図会)

Its form resembles that of *Ibo-shimeji*, and it is smooth and poisonous.
Both the cap and the stem appear translucent, as if oiled.
For this reason, eating too much of it causes abdominal pain.

---

### *Ai-take* (藍蕈; "Indigo Mushroom")

Its form resembles either *Hatsu-take* or *Shimeji*.
Highly poisonous — must not be eaten.

---

## *Ibo-murasaki* (紫面蕈; "Warty Purple Mushroom")

Grows in mountain forests and open fields. When many grow together in one place, they resemble the blossoms of *Fuji* (wisteria).
Edible. The flavor is excellent.
