**Sugi-take (杉菌, Cedar mushroom) — Another Variety**

*(Illustration)*

In the *Mizunoe* (壬) seventh month of the *Kinoto-Hitsuji* year (乙未, 1835), I obtained a specimen that had grown on rotting cedar (*sugi*) wood in my back garden and drew it from life. This was fifteen days after it was obtained.

---

**Kurinoiga-take (粟菌, Chestnut-bur mushroom)**

This mushroom grows on the decayed spiny burrs (*iga*) of chestnuts. Many specimens emerge clustered together from a single burr.

---
