## Four Varieties — Produced in the Mountains of Nemura
*Drawn from life on the 8th day of the 8th month of the Hinoe-Saru year* (1836)

---

### *Onagi-take* / *Unaki-take* (Demon Mushroom; 鬼蕈)
**Highly poisonous — must not be eaten.**

---

### *Usu-beni-take* (Pale Crimson Mushroom; 紅項蕈)
**Highly poisonous — must not be eaten.**

---

### *Ki-ō-jin* (Yellow King Mushroom; 黄王蕈) — one variety — *Kishimeji* / *Sugi-shimeji*

---

### *Nunohiki-take* (Cloth-pulling Mushroom; 布引蕈)

The manner in which it grows is continuous and unbroken, resembling white glossed silk (*neri-kinu*) stretched out at length. It grows in great numbers in a straight line — hence it is called *Nunohiki* ("cloth-pulling").

---

**Edible, but the flavor is poor. It resembles *Sugi-take*.**
