## *Shimeji* Group (占地属; *Shimeji* · *Matsu-shimeji*)

This *Shimeji* group has an exceptionally fine flavor.

---

### *Kō-tō-kin* (黄糖蕈; *Ki-hatsu-dake* / "Yellow *Hatsu-take*")

Its flavor is inferior to that of the ordinary *Hatsu-take*.

---

### *Shi-tō-kin* (紫糖蕈; *Murasaki-hatsu-dake* / "Purple *Hatsu-take*")

Their flavors differ from one another, but all are edible and non-toxic.

---

### *Hatsu-take Modoki* (初タケモドキ / "*Hatsu-take* Lookalike")

This fungus is sold in the city mixed together with *Hatsu-take*. It closely resembles *Hatsu-take*. Edible. The flavor is the same as *Hatsu-take*.
