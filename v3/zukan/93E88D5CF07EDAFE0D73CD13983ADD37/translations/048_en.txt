### *Kiri-shimeji* (Paulownia Shimeji)

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Drawn from life from a cluster growing in a warm spot in the rear garden on the 5th day of the 4th month of the *Hinoto-tori* year.

---

### *Mafun-kin* / *Makuri-take* (馬糞菌; Dung Mushroom)

They grow abundantly from dried horse dung. For this reason, they also grow in great numbers among eggplant seedlings. Later, they turn black and the cap falls away.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Collected and drawn from life on the 5th day of the 5th month of the *Hinoto-tori* year.

---

### *Meiju-shimeji* (茗樹しめじ; Tea Tree Shimeji)

They grow abundantly in summer beneath the tea trees in my own garden. However, they are not of the *shimeji* type. It is decided that they shall not be eaten.
