## *Tama-shimeji* (玉蕈; One variety — *Shiro-shimeji* / "White Shimeji")

Edible. The flavor is not particularly good. The flesh is firm.

---

**Poisonous — Must not be eaten.**

---

## *Tama-shimeji* (玉蕈; One variety — *Aka-shimeji* / "Red Shimeji" · *Ō-shimeji* / "Large Shimeji")

Edible, though the flavor is not particularly good. The taste is somewhat similar to *Hatsu-take*.

---

**Three varieties — Produced in the mountains of Nemura**
8th day of the 8th month of the Hinoe-Saru year (Tenpō 7, 1836) — *Drawn from life*
