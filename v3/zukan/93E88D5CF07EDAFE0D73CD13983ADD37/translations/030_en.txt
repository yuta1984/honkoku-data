## *Komо-take* (also: *Wara-take*)

Grows on rotted *komo* (rush/sedge matting). Its flesh is soft in texture and difficult to pick up by hand. It is not suitable for eating.

---

## *Tsukiyo-take* ("Moonlight Mushroom" / *Getsuya-shin*)

A certain person has said:
"This mushroom has a glossy, smooth surface, and glows by itself on moonlit nights. That is why it is so named."

Upon my own reflection, I believe there exists another separate variety of *Tsukiyo-take*, one that differs greatly in form and appearance. I shall record it at a later point. Could it be that there are two distinct varieties of *Tsukiyo-take*?
