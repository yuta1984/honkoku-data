### *Kiri-motase* (桐モタセ; Paulownia Cluster Mushroom) · *Nabe-kaburi* (鍋カブリ; "Pot-Cap" Mushroom)

*Beni-ikuchi*, *Nabe-kaburi*, *Aburabōtsu*, and *Tsuraharashi* are all of the same species, and the underside of the cap has no gills cut away, resembling scattered millet grains. The *Sakamoto Kinpu* states that *Nabe-kaburi* is edible, but this has not yet been confirmed by the author. Obtained in the mountains of Ōji. Drawn from life on the 14th day of the 7th month of the *Hinoto-tori* year.

　　　　　　　　　　　　They do not grow from rotting paulownia wood, but rather grow abundantly in the damp ground of paulownia groves. They are ground mushrooms (*jijin*).
