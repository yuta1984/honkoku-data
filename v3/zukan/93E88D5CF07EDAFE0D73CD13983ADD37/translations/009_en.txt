**[Right folio]**

**Keyaki-take** (欅蕈) *[colloquial names: Keyaki-take / Kataha]*

*(Side notation:)*
The term "Kataha" refers specifically to shiitake mushrooms that have been dried and stored. Fungi of the local/earthen variety (*doki*) are not called "Kataha."

---

**[Left folio]**

**Genshi** (玄芝 — Black/Dark *Shii*) — *Kuro-mannen-take* (Black Eternal Mushroom) — *Note: One account states this was collected growing on a dead pine tree.*

*(Side notation:)*
This fungus grows on rotting *keyaki* (zelkova) logs, sprouting in dense, overlapping clusters. The stalk cannot be seen from the surface of the host material. It is dried in a manner similar to shiitake, and local people (*domin*) are said to eat it.

*(Lower notation:)*
All three specimens are products of the mountains of Nemura.
Drawn from life on the 8th day of the 8th month of the Hinoto-Saru year (1836).
