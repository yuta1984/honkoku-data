**[Right folio — Heading and annotation]**
*Keyaki-take* (欅蕈) *(local readings: Keyakitake / Kataha)*

※ Note: The term "*Kataha*" refers to mushrooms of the shiitake type that have been dried and stored. The "*Dokin*" (土菌, earth/soil fungus) is not called "*Kataha*."

---

**[Left folio, upper right — Heading and collection record]**
*Genshi* (玄芝, "Black Reishi") — *Kuro Mannen-take* (クロマンネンタケ, Black Bracket Fungus) — One variety. Collected from specimens growing on dead/fallen pine wood.

**[Left folio, lower right — Descriptive annotation]**
This fungus (*Keyaki-take*) grows on rotting zelkova (*keyaki*) wood. It grows in dense, overlapping clusters (*jūjō sōsei*). The stalk is not visible from the surface. Like shiitake mushrooms, it is dried, and it is said that the local people eat it.

**[Left folio, lower left — Collection record]**
Produced in the mountains of Mitane village.
Drawn from life on the eighth day of the eighth month, year of Hinoe-Saru (丙申, 1836).
