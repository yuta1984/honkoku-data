### From *Matsuoka Kinpin* (松岡菌品):

***Imo-take*** (芋タケ, "Potato/Tuber Mushroom")

---

***Tsuba-take*** (鍔菰, "Collar Mushroom")

　This mushroom grows in the bamboo grove of my own garden. The collar (*tsuba*) is not present on every specimen — out of ten, only three or four have one. The form resembles the tube through which a spear-shaft passes, and it remains fixed in one place, neither moving up nor down. 〔Classified among poisonous mushrooms〕

---

　　　　7th month (*Mizunoe*), 11th day of the *Kinoto-Hitsuji* year [1835] — drawn from life.

---

　This grows at the base of tea trees in my own garden. It conceals itself underground and does not appear above the soil, so people are unaware of its presence. I happened to dig beneath a tree and discovered it. Its globular body is neither fully hard nor fully soft — it resembles a konjac (*konnyaku*) cake. In Master Matsuoka's *Kinpin*, it is recorded as edible; however, it appears to me to be of a poisonous variety.

　　Same month, 12th day — drawn from life.

---

### *Semi-take* (蝉花, "Cicada Flower / Cicada Fungus")

　A detailed explanation of the *semi-take* is given in the main volume of my own work, *Suiriku Chūzu* (水陸虫図, Illustrated Compendium of Land and Aquatic Insects/Creatures).

---

　　8th month, 22nd day of the *Hitsuji* year — collected from the mountain forest of Mr. Doi of Sōkyū, and drawn from life.

### From *Igan-sai Myōhin* (怡顔斎苗品):

***Kumo-take* / *Mō-take*** (雲蕈, "Cloud Mushroom")　〔From Echigo Province〕

　This mushroom grows beneath trees in damp ground. They grow gathered together in dense, overlapping clusters at a single spot, like clouds billowing up into the sky.

---

　　*Kinoto-Hitsuji* year, *Mizunoe* 7th month, 15th day — obtained and drawn from life in the mountain forest.

　　*Kinoto-Hitsuji* year, *Mizunoe* 7th month, 12th day — obtained and drawn from life.
