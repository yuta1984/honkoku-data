## Bamboo Mushroom (*Chikutake* / *Chikushin*) and *Chikujo*

[Source: *Dōsei Hassen* (導生八牋)]

**Sasayu** — Sasayu is of the finest flavor. It appears before *shimeji* (early in the season). It is good to eat in large quantities.

---

**Naratake** (*Nara-shimeji*)

　　5th day of the 8th month of the *Kinoto-Hitsuji* year — drawn from life.

　　Grows in the forests of Nara. Edible. It differs from the variety that grows on rotting wood in Nara.

---

**Kitsune-no-sakazuki** ("Fox's Cup") [Japanese common name / *Kokuzoku*]

**Tanukigusa** (one variety) — also called: *Tanuki-take*, *Tanuki-motase*

---

This fungus has a cap measuring approximately three to four *sun* [roughly 9–12 cm] in diameter. The cap is concave and curves upward at the edges, so that after rain, water collects in the hollow — resembling a cup (*sakazuki*). It belongs to the category of *Tengu-take* and *Shiba-take*, and its nature is similar to *shimeji* (玉蕈). Although some say it is edible, it should not be eaten.

---

　　*Kinoto-Hitsuji* year, *Mizunoe* 7th month, 16th day — obtained from within the mountains of Hakusan and drawn from life.
