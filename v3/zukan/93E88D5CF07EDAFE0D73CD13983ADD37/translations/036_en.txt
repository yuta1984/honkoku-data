## Listed under the Entry for *Kan-kin* (雚菌) in the *Honzō* [*Honzō Kōmoku*]

### *Ino-hana-dake* (蜀格)
One variety — *Edible, but the flavor somewhat resembles take yet is not particularly good.*

---

### *Sawa-hatsu-take* / *Shiba-hatsu-take* (糖蕈; "Meadow Russula")

Its form resembles the *Hatsu-take* (初菌) but is thinner and smaller.
The fragrance and flavor are poor — markedly inferior to *Hatsu-take*.

---

*(Annotation within illustration)*
**Yellow variety (*Ki-tachi*) — Edible, but inferior to ordinary *Shimeji*.**
