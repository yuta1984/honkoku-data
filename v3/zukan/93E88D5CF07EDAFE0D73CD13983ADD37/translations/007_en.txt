【Right Folio】

From the *Tashiki-hen* (Diverse Knowledge Compilation):
　　Black Zhi (*Kurotachi* / *Kuroshijiku-take*)

From the *Honzō Kōmoku* (Bencao Gangmu):
　　Dark Zhi (*Genshi*)
　　　　　　　　　　This variety is found in shaded, damp locations, growing on rotting logs.
　　　　　　　　　　[Another specimen] grew on a plank through which water flows at my own home —
　　　　　　　　　　not on rotting wood, but sprouting through a gap in the plank.
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Both specimens obtained on the fifth day of the seventh month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　of Kinoto-Hitsuji [1835], drawn from life in the back garden.

*Goson-gan* (Japanese name: *Saru-no-koshikake* / Monkey's Seat)　— a specimen growing in two layers

【Left Folio】

　　The *Youyang Zazu* (Miscellaneous Morsels from Youyang) states: In the region of Daibei, there exists something called *juki* (Tree Chicken), shaped like an upturned and curled cup, which the common people call *goson-gan* (Monkey's Eye). And so forth.

　　*[Author's note:]* The term *juki* (Tree Chicken) is a general name for fungi (*take*) that grow on wood; it is not limited to *Saru-no-koshikake* (bracket fungi) alone, but is used as an umbrella term.
