## *Kishimeji* (黄玉蕈 / Yellow Jewel Mushroom)
*(Flavor is refined and excellent; edible. Non-toxic.)*
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　*Shimeji* group: *Shimeji* / *Ko-shimeji*

---

All varieties within the *shimeji* (占地) group are edible, and while their flavors are broadly similar, they are considered to be of inferior grade.

---

### *Kuro-shimeji* (黒蕈 / Black Shimeji)

### *Murasaki-shimeji* (紫蕈) *(one variety)* — the form with a shorter stipe

### *Tanuki-take* (狸タケ) *(one variety — the white-colored form; also called **Tanuki-no-wani-take**)*

---

These mushrooms grow abundantly after rain in the 8th month. The cap opens and faces upward, taking on a shape like the character 凹 (*ō*, meaning concave). After rainfall, water collects on the cap, much like a teacup. Although they are edible, they carry a slight toxicity.
