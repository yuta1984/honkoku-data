## *Gakō-shin* (Bamboo Pith Fungus / *Kinugasa-take*)
[Source: *Tashiki*]

It is draped in a thin veil-like covering (*kinu*) on both the upper and lower surfaces of the cap. It is so named because it appears as though the skin has been peeled away [or: as though it is wearing a gauze robe].

## [From Chen Renyu's *Kinpu* (Mushroom Compendium):]

**Jade Mushroom (*Gyokujin* / *Shimeji*)**
[Annotation: *Shimeji*, *Nezumi-shimeji*. Also *Senbonshimeji*. There is also a separately named "thousand-stem *Shimejii* (*Senbon Shimoji*)" which differs from this one.]

The same text states:

**Cold Rush Mushroom (*Kanhojin*)**

This mushroom has its underside without any gaps, as if paper had been pasted over it. It differs from other mushrooms. If that paper-like membrane is removed, the underside below shows normal mushroom gills. It is a poisonous mushroom and must not be eaten.

In the section on "earth mushrooms" (*do-kin*) in the *Bencao Gangmu* (Chinese Materia Medica), it states: "Eating earth mushrooms kills people." It is said that this mushroom is the one that gets confused with the mountain *Kinugasa-take*; this is precisely that mushroom.

## *Seitō-kin* (Green-Headed Mushroom) / *Hatsu-take*
[Source: *Yunnan Tongzhi*] Alternative name: *Tōjin* (Sugar Mushroom)

**Hatsu-take** (*Lactarius* sp., "First Mushroom")

The variety produced in Owari Province is deeply blue in color, and so the local people call it "*Ao-ha*" (Blue Eight).

- *Aizuri* ("Indigo-rubbed") — Nanto (Nara)
- *Matsu-mimi* ("Pine Ear") — Western provinces
- *Ai-take* ("Indigo Mushroom") — Bitchū Province

★ (Author's note) *Hatsu-take* grows mostly on pine-forested plains. Among all mushrooms, its flavor is supreme.
In the mountains around West Lake (*Seiko*) in China, there is a mushroom called *Shōka-kin* ("Pine Flower Mushroom") which is extremely delicious. Its color is a beautiful red, it is clean in appearance, and its flavor is most agreeable — fresh and exquisite in taste. It grows during the 9th month. Commonly called "*Enshi-jin*?" (Cochineal/Rouge Mushroom).
[Author's annotation: "I believe this is *Heni-take* (Red Mushroom)."]
