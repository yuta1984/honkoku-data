**[Right Folio]**

**Hōki-motase (箒モタセ, Broom-carrier mushroom)**

Collected from atop rotting wood deep in the mountains of Hakusan, and drawn from life on the 9th day of *Nanryo* (the 8th month).

*(Illustration)*

---

**Ume-take (梅蕈, Plum mushroom)**

Drawn from life on the 24th day of the 8th month of the *Kinoto-Hitsuji* year (乙未, 1835), from a specimen that grew on rotting plum (*ume*) wood in the night garden.

*(Illustration)*

---

**[Left Folio]**

This mushroom grows close to the ground and greatly resembles a flower in bloom. Its texture is extremely hard. It belongs to the same category as *kikurage* (wood ear fungus).

---

**Bosatsu-take (牡冊タケ, Bodhisattva mushroom)**

*(Illustration)*

Collected personally from within the mountains of Hakusan on the 10th day of the 8th month of the *Hinoto-Saru* year (丙申, 1836), and drawn from life.

---

**Kaji-take (楮蕈, Paper mulberry mushroom)**

Obtained on the 13th day of the 10th month of the *Kinoto-Hitsuji* year (乙未, 1835), having grown on rotting wood of *i* (猪, a plant or tree) in the grove of the back garden, and drawn from life.
