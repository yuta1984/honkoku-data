## *Keiran-take* / *Tamago-take* (Egg Mushroom)

　　　Its early-stage sprout (young fruiting body / egg stage)

[Collection Record]
6th day of the 8th month of the *Hinoe-Saru* year — collected from beneath dense trees and drawn from life.

---

　　　　　　　　　Collected from the mountain interior of Nemura.
　　　　　　　　　14th day of the 8th month — drawn from life.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　*Samatsu-modoki* (False Pine Mushroom)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　*Fukuro-take* (Sack Mushroom)

---

In its early stage of growth, this mushroom has a shape resembling an *inrō* (medicine case) — that is, an egg shape. Each egg splits into three parts and the mushroom emerges. The cap does not spread wide; the shape resembles that of *Samatsu* (matsutake / pine mushroom). It is said that *Samatsu-modoki* and *Nezumi-take* are edible; however, mushrooms of this type (the *Fukuro-take* group) must not be eaten.

## *Keitei*-type (?) — one variety: *Nezumi-take* (Mouse Mushroom), the yellow-colored kind

[Collection Record]
6th day of the 8th month of the *Hinoe-Saru* year — collected from within a bamboo grove and drawn from life.

---

This mushroom grows in bamboo groves. Its flavor is excellent and it is fit to eat. As there are specimens that bear several stalks from a single base, it is called "*Senbon-sasako*" (Thousand-stemmed Sasako).
