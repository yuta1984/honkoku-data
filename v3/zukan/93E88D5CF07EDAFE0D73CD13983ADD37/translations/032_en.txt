## *Nigiri-take* (Grasped Mushroom) — a variety of *Tengu-no-kasa* (Goblin's Umbrella); highly toxic.

In common regional parlance, it is called "*Kiri-take*." It is also called "*Nikiri-take*."
There is separately another mushroom called *Nikiri-take*, which differs from this one.

---

　　　　　　　　　　*Kanoko-take* (Fawn-spotted Mushroom) — a variety that grows in the ground.
　　　　　　　　　　Belongs to the category of poisonous mushrooms.

The form of this mushroom resembles the seed of a lotus; its interior is hollow and it is light in weight. The surface of the mushroom stands up in a manner resembling fine hairs or scales. It is a poisonous mushroom and must not be eaten.
