**Hard Wood Ear (硬木耳 / *Sarunokoshire*, Bracket Fungus)** — Three varieties growing on plum trees

**[Right folio, lower section]**

*Kosongan* (胡孫眼, bracket fungus / *Sarunokoshire*) grows on all manner of trees. Large specimens may reach three to four *shaku* [approximately 90–120 cm] in size, while small ones are no larger than a fist. All are shaped like *reishi* (霊芝) but without a stalk, growing directly attached to the wood. Those growing on various trees are collectively called "*Sarunokoshire*" ("Monkey's Seat"). However, their form and color differ according to the tree, and it is said that when each is decocted and taken medicinally, the efficacious properties differ accordingly. Those growing on red plum (*kōbai*) are said to have especially numerous beneficial properties, being effective against *mashitsu* (麻疾, leprosy), *chishitsu* (痴疾, mental illness / dementia), and *kakke* (脚気, beriberi). Those growing on red plum are brownish-red in color, while those growing on white plum (*shiro-ume*) are white with a yellowish tinge. Both are said to have medicinal efficacy.
