**Hard wood-ear** (*Saruno-koshikake* / 硬木耳) — Three varieties growing on plum trees

**[Right folio, lower section]**

*Kosongan* (胡孫眼) grows on all manner of trees. Large specimens reach three to four *shaku* in size; small ones are no bigger than a fist. All are shaped like *reishi* (霊芝), lacking a stipe, and grow attached directly to the wood. Those growing on various trees are collectively called *Saruno-koshikake* ("monkey's seat"); however, their form and color differ from one another, and it is said that when those growing on each respective tree are decocted and consumed, their medicinal properties differ accordingly. Those growing on red plum (*kōbai*) are said to have particularly numerous benefits, including efficacy against skin ailments (*mashitsu*), mental disorders (*chishitsu*), and beriberi (*kakke*). Those growing on red plum are brownish-red in color, while those growing on white plum (*shiro-ume*) are white with a yellowish tinge. It is said that both have beneficial properties.
