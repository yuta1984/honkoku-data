**[Right folio, upper right — Heading]**
*Mannen-take* (万年蕈) *(local readings: Mannengoke, Iwaitake / Shiragoke)*

**[Right folio, upper left — Descriptive annotation]**
Grows on the rotting wood of paper mulberry (*kōzo*). It resembles *Reishi* (霊芝) but has no stalk; it resembles wood-ear fungus (*Saru no Koshikake*, shelf fungus) but is not hard. It is thin and soft, forming several hundred layers. There are black-coloured and red-coloured varieties.

**[Right folio, lower right — Collection record]**
Grown on a rotting branch from Inoki in the [Higo?] province. Collected on the eighteenth day of the [third-ninth? / Sanku?] yang [month], in summer. Drawn from life.

**[Left folio, upper — Heading and main text]**
*Kōshi* (黄芝, "Yellow Reishi")

This is the young, early-stage form of *Reishi* (霊芝). When seedlings/sprouts are numerous, [the fungus] is called "*Kōshi*" (Yellow Reishi). From the very early stages of *Reishi* growth, there are black and red varieties. *Shishi* (紫芝, Purple Reishi) is precisely one of these. In terms of colour, there are five varieties: yellow, red/crimson, purple, green, and white.

**[Left folio, lower left — Inscription]**
Drawn from life on the eighteenth day of the ninth month, year of Hinoe-Saru (丙申, 1836).
