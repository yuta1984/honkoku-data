【Right Folio】

*Mannen-goke* (Ten-Thousand-Year Moss / *Iwai-take* / *Shira-goke*)

Grows on rotting logs of paper mulberry (*kōzo*). Similar to *reishi* (Ganoderma) but without a stipe; similar to wood-ear fungus (*Saru-no-koshikake* / bracket fungus) but not stiff — thin and pliable. Forms several hundred layers. There are both black and red varieties.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Collected from a rotting branch of a paper mulberry tree
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　in the back garden; drawn from life on the eighteenth day
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　of the Double Yang [festival month].

【Left Folio】

Yellow Zhi (*Kōshi*)

This is the early stage (*shotō*) of *reishi* (Ganoderma / Spirit Fungus). Specimens bearing many shoots are classified as "Yellow Zhi." From the earliest stage of *reishi*, there are also dark-red specimens; Purple Zhi (*Shishi*) is of this kind. The colors include five varieties: yellow, red, purple, green, and white.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Hinoe-Saru [1836], ninth month, eighteenth day.
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Drawn from life.
