**[Right folio]**

**Mannin-take** (万年蕈) *[colloquial names: Manchin-goke / Iwai-take / Shira-goke]*

This fungus grows on rotting logs of *kōzo* (paper mulberry). It resembles *reishi* (霊芝, *Ganoderma*) but has no stalk; it resembles wood-ear fungus (*saruno koshikake*) but is not tough — it is thin and pliable. It forms hundreds of layers. There are black-colored specimens and red-colored specimens.

*(Side notation:)*
Collected from a rotting branch of a *inoki* tree in the Hishiro province [possibly Sagami Province]. Drawn during summer, on the ninth [month], eighteenth day of yang.

**[Left folio]**

**Kōshi** (黄芝 — Yellow *Shii*)

This is the early-stage form (*hatsuatama*/initial growth stage) of *reishi* (霊芝). When there are many sprouts/seedlings, they are designated as "Kōshi" (Yellow *Shii*). From the initial stage of *reishi*, there exist black and red specimens. The *Shishi* (紫芝, Purple *Shii*) belongs to this category. In total, there are five color varieties: yellow, red, purple, green, and white.

*(Side notation:)*
Drawn from life on the 18th day of the 9th month of the Hinoto-Saru year (1836).
