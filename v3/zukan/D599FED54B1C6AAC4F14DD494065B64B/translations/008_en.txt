...resembling the shape of the letter "S." Both the upper and lower lips each have inner and outer lips, and on the slightly upper portion of the inner upper lip, there are black or dark gray stripes that spread following the shape of the lip. These lips are smooth, entirely black in color, and their curvature is like a circle divided into four equal parts (i.e., a quarter-circle arc).

Furthermore, according to Hübner, the thickness of the lip, including blubber and flesh together, amounts to one *ell* [equivalent to approximately 2 *shaku* 2 *sun* 8 *bu* in Japanese *kanejaku* measurement].

**Mouth** (*kuchi*)

- *Tana no uchi* ("inside the shelf") [Since the lips are commonly called *tana* ("shelf"), this term refers to the interior of the mouth behind the lips.]

The structure of the mouth differs greatly from that of other fish.

　　[Author's note:] The diet of whales is not yet fully understood. What I heard when I was in Hirado was that, despite being such an enormous creature, nothing was ever found inside the whale's stomach. [The *Geiki* ("Record of Whales") also states that food has never been found in the belly, and remarks that it is a mysterious creature, as one wonders what it eats to grow so fat.] Among whales, the *Zatō* (humpback whale) has occasionally been found to have eaten something called *naebiei* (*ami*), a small shrimp-like creature, but nothing else has ever been found in the stomachs. In the eastern seas of Ezochi (Hokkaido), it is said that humpback whales frequently approach the coastal areas to feed on a creature called *Soe*.
