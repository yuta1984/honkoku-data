The lower jaw (*shitaago*) has two bones, which originate from the left and right sides of the *tei* bone (a central jaw bone) and curve forward. Further details will be given under the entry for "Bones" later in the text.

The flesh at the joint between the upper jaw and the lower jaw is called *Dentsuru*.

**Lips** (*kuchibiru*)

- *Tana* ("shelf/ledge")
　　Appendix: *Lippen* [Dutch]
- *Tana no kawa* ("shelf skin") [The skin of the cheek is called *tana-gawa*; according to the *Geiki* ("Record of Whales")]

In general, the whale's lower lip curves upward, covering the upper. In the case of the *Semi* (right whale), the front of the lip is low,

**[Left Page]**

while the left and right sides rise somewhat, forming a half-moon shape. Toward the corners of the mouth on both sides, the front lip and the sides become level in height. The calf of the right whale resembles the adult in this respect, but differs in that the lips on the left and right form a triangular shape. The *Zatō* (humpback whale) and the *Nagasu* (fin whale / rorqual) have lips that are uniformly level across the front and sides, gradually rising higher than the front as one moves toward the corners of the mouth. The upper jaw consists only of baleen, and the area where the lower lip meets it forms a faint shadow outlining the lower lip.

　　Appendix: According to Dutch accounts, whales of the ice seas (Arctic Ocean) have short hairs on both the upper and lower edges of their lips. The shape of these lips is peculiar, resembling the letter "S"...
