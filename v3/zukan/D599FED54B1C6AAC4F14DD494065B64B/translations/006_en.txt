**Nose**

- *Shiofuki* ("spout" / "blowhole")
　　Appendix: *Blaassgat* [Dutch]

This is the breathing hole — that is, the nasal passage. **[Figure]** As shown in the illustration, the opening is located at the top of the head, piercing through the cranial bone (*nōkotsu*) and connecting to the area near the base of the tongue. The whale normally blows water (spout) through this hole. When struck by several dozen harpoons, after it has expelled all its spout, it begins to blow blood, eventually leading to death.

---

**[Left Page]**

When [healthy], it blows only air. Ordinary fish take in water through the mouth and release it through the gills (*eke*); however, whales differ from other fish in that they inhale through the mouth and exhale through the nose.
