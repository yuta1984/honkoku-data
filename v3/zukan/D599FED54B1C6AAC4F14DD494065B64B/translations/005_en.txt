　　　The following entries present an overview of the major terms.

**Head**

- *Kabachi* — the common name for the whale's head
- *Yama* ("mountain") — the common term for the entire upper surface of the *kabachi*
- *Chausuyama* ("tea-mortar mountain") — the common term for the raised, protruding portion of flesh within the *yama*
　　Appendix: *Hokkoru* — meaning "protuberance" or "hump" [Dutch]

Regarding the shape of the head: the *semikujira* (right whale) and the calf (*kogujira*) have raised, humped heads, while the *zatōkujira* (humpback whale) and the *nagasukujira* (fin whale) have flat heads. On the *chausuyama* of the right whale, clusters of oysters, barnacles, and lice-like creatures adhere.

　　　Appendix: The whales caught by the Dutch in the Arctic/polar seas have heads that account for one-third of the total body size. Some are even larger than this; some of the largest measure fifty *hiro* [fathoms], and there are also those measuring twenty *hiro*.

**Brain**

- *Nōmiso* (brain)
　　Appendix [Dutch terms]: *Herssem*, *Walvishbrein*, *Walviskot*, *Walskothi*

The brain of the *makkōkujira* (sperm whale) is used to produce *ryūzenkō* (ambergris). [Already discussed in detail in Volume 2.]

Appendix: *Witte Fonhel* [translated here as "white dragon saliva" / white ambergris] — said to come from the brain of the whale.
