○The Tsukihi-gai (月日介, "Moon-and-Sun Shell") is what is known as a "black star joint" (黒星合, kurohoshi-gō). It refers to those shells that join together with only the black star markings, without hinge teeth (牙歯). Care must be taken not to confuse these with shells such as the Mirukui, Omote-gai, and Umigiku (sea chrysanthemum shell), which have both hinge teeth and black star markings.
