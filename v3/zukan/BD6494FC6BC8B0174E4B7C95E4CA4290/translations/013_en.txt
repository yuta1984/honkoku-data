**As stated in the Wamyō Ruijushō (和名類聚抄):**
Zhimu (知母, *Anemarrhena asphodeloides*; Japanese reading: Yamashi)　The Bencao states that Zhimu is also known as Erkao (兒草), and its Japanese name is
　　　　　　　　　　　　Yamanoshi (夜萬之)

**The Flower of Zhimu (知母)**　As stated in the Wamyō Ruijushō:
(Hanasuge)　　　　　　　Yamashi
　　　In the Shinsen Jikyō (新撰字鏡):
　　　　　　Yamatokoro

The root of Zhimu resembles the shape of a gadfly (蚳蝱, Abu), which is why it was named Chibo (蚳母), which then became corrupted and pronounced as Chimo (知母).

Zhimu (知母)　Japanese name: Yamashi (也未志)
