【Danchōboku (段長木) — A variety of Hakuchōka (白丁花)】

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Two varieties; sketched from life on the first day of the fifth month, in the year Kōshin.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　[Category: Trees / 木類]

---

According to the "Chaseki Sōkashū" (茶席挿花集, "Collection of Flower Arrangements for Tea Gatherings"):
　　Tanshunga (探春花) — pronounced Mokkōka
　　Mokkōka (木香花)


　　　　　My own note:
　　　　　　What is called "Mokkō" (木香) is in fact an herb (草). Its leaves are wrinkled,
　　　　　　resembling those of Tsurushirami (鶴虱, Torilis japonica) and
　　　　　　burdock (gobo), being narrow and long. The shape of the leaf
　　　　　　is similar to that of Shion (紫苑, Aster tataricus). It produces yellow flowers,
　　　　　　which resemble chrysanthemums. It is also called Aomokkō (青木香)
　　　　　　or Minamimokko (南木香). The plant Madourei (馬兜鈴, Aristolochia)
　　　　　　is also referred to as Aomokko (青木香). These are all medicinal plants.
　　　　　　That this particular tree (Mokkōka) bears the name "Mokkō"
　　　　　　　　　is not recorded in the various books (群書), yet everyone calls it Mokkō.
