【Habotan (Ornamental Cabbage / Brassica oleracea)】

In flower shops, it is called:
　　Habotan (葉牡丹, "Leaf Peony")


　　　　　　　　　　　According to herbalists (Honzō scholars):
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kanran (甘藍, read: Habotan)


　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sketched from life on the first day of the fifth month, in the year Kōshin.


---

According to the "Chikinsho" (地錦抄):
　　Shokatsuna (諸葛菜, "Zhuge Liang's Vegetable")


　　　　　　It is said that Zhuge Kongming (Zhuge Liang)
　　　　　　had soldiers carry this plant to military encampments,
　　　　　　sowing it there to serve as provisions (food supply).
　　　　　　The turnip (kabu / Brassica rapa) is also
　　　　　　referred to as Shokatsuna.
