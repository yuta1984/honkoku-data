1. Ginkgo Shell (銀杏介)　　[Reference: Gunbun Hinkai]

　○ The *Gunbun Hinkai* states: This belongs to the clam category. Its shape resembles the Chiyo-no-hana Shell (千代ノ花介), with a white surface and horizontal striations. The shell is thin and has a lustrous sheen.

　○ Sekiju states: The shape resembles the Iris Shell (菖蒲介). The color is water-white or snow-white, with fine, dense horizontal banding. The surface is smooth and glossy. The inner surface (where the animal lives) lacks luster, while the remainder is lustrous and white. The size does not exceed seven or eight *bu* (approx. 2.1–2.4 cm). The shell has interlocking teeth (牙合).

※ The ○ marks and sequential numbers are written in red (vermilion), with the numbers enclosed in circles.
