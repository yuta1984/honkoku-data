**The *Nōzen* (農全) states:**

　*Shi'un-sai* (紫雲菜, "Purple Cloud Vegetable")
　　(*Ruri-chōja-sō*, "Lapis Lazuli Clove Herb")

　　　　Upon reviewing the assessments of flower merchants and the general opinions of various people,
　　　　the matter is not clear. Upon my own careful consideration (*jukō*), I conclude that *Shi'un-sai* is
　　　　to be identified with *Tachinami-sō* (立浪草, *Scutellaria* sp.).
