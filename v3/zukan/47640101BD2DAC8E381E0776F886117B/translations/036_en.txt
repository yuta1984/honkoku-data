【Tōkishi (冬葵子, Winter Mallow / Seeds of Winter Mallow)】

Intercalary Rinshō (6th month), 1st cycle,
12th day, Year of Teigai (1827) — sketched from life (真写)
in the garden of the Sōsha (草舎, "Grass Cottage / Study").

---

〔Author's note:〕
　According to common knowledge (国俗), there are two varieties of *tōkishi* (冬葵子, Winter Mallow).
　One variety has crinkled/puckered leaves (縮面, *chichimi*) — the leaf surface is not flat.
　This is called *Okanori* (岡海苔, "Land Nori"), and is also referred to as *tōkishi*.
　The true variety has flat leaves with no crinkle, possesses a glossy sheen, and its seeds are used medicinally.
　There is yet another variety called *Kan-aoi* (冬葵, literally "Cold Mallow"),
　which belongs to the group of *Bokkō* (牡蘅) and *Futaba-aoi* (二葉葵, *Asarum caulescens*).
　The true *tōkishi* grows abundantly at various places along the Kanagawa Kaidō road
　in Bushū (武州, Musashi Province). The *Okanori* plant is not found there.
