Yamato Honzō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Collected on the twenty-fourth day of Rinsho (sixth month), year of Hinoe-inu.　Listed under [Medicinal Plants].
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Drew from life at the waterside of Komagata-dō.

---

The Tashiki-hen, section on fragrant grasses, states:

　　Jīng (Kei) Sānléng (Sanryō)　Japanese name: Suke
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Mirusu
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　As it shares the same alternate name as Grass Sānléng (Kusa-sanryō),
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　it was recorded in a previous volume and is omitted here.

　　　　　There are four varieties of Sānléng: Jīng-sānléng (Kei-sanryō), Cǎo-sānléng (Kusa-sanryō),
　　　　　Hēi-sānléng (Koku-sanryō), and Shí-sānléng (Seki-sanryō).
　　　　　Their forms are similar to one another but not identical, and their roots also differ.
　　　　　All are used medicinally.
