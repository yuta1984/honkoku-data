　　　　　　　　　　　　The twenty-fourth day of Rinsho (sixth month), Year of Hinoe-inu.
　　　　　　　　　　　　Drew from life at the waterside near Komagata-dō.


The Tashiki-hen, section on fragrant grasses, states:
　　Jīng (Kei) Sānléng (Sanryō)　Japanese name: Suke
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Mirusu
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Other name: Shares the same name as Grass Sanryō (Kusa-sanryō),
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　and is therefore noted briefly in the previous volume.


　　There are four varieties of Sanryō: Jīng-Sānléng (Kei-sanryō), Cǎo-Sānléng (Kusa-sanryō),
　　Hēi-Sānléng (Koku-sanryō), and Shí-Sānléng (Ishi-sanryō).
　　Though they resemble one another in form, they are not identical,
　　and their roots also differ. All are used in medicine.
