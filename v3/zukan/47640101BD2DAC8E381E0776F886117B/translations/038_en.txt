**From the *Tashikiden* (多識編), section on Hemp, Wheat, and Rice Plants (麻麦稲類):**

　**Tōzu (刀豆, Sword Bean)**
　　　Japanese name: *Natamame* (那多末米)
　　　Alternate name: *Tōgendzu* (挟剣豆, "Bean that Clasps a Sword")

　〔Explanatory note:〕
　　"A *katana* (刀, blade) used for cutting firewood is called a *nata* (奈多, billhook/hatchet).
　　Because the shape of this bean resembles it, the plant was given this name."
