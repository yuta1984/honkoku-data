---

　　　　　　　　　According to *Honzō Kōmoku* (本草綱目): There are two varieties of Bakan (馬莧).
　　　　　　　　　The large-leafed variety is not suitable for medicinal use. The small-leafed variety has
　　　　　　　　　mercury said to be present between the nodes and
　　　　　　　　　leaves. This plant has green leaves,
　　　　　　　　　red stems, yellow flowers, white roots, and black seeds. For this reason it is named
　　　　　　　　　Gogyōsō (Five-Phase Herb).
　　　　　　　　　Bakan is named so because its leaves resemble horse's teeth (馬の歯). And because it is
　　　　　　　　　soft and smooth (柔滑), it is called Suberihiyu
　　　　　　　　　in Japanese. "Suberi" is a Japanese word meaning "smooth" (滑らか).
