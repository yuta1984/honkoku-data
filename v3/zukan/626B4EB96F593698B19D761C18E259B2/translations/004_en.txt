**[Yomo no Akara]**
Even surpassing the snow on Mount Kōroho—
I scoop up this white sand
and gaze: a jeweled-blind shell (玉すだれ貝)

**[Karakoromo Kittō]**
Into the sea,
lure the warbler, you flock of sparrows—
let us add its song and see:
the plum-blossom shell (梅の花貝)

**[Yomibito Hakuju]**
Though I steal it,
a cherry blossom shell
from the flowering tidal flat—
let my name be known, like the white waves
breaking on the rocky shore

**[Tsururi Hikaru]**
Search and you shall find—
right here, plain as day, on the tidal flat:
a purple shell,
pinched between the fingers like a salt-crystal

**[Yadoya no Meshimori]**
Picking up two together:
a pink (nadeshiko) shell—
seen in the tidal current,
in the waves,
a shell that colors and blurs the view

**[Katsube Magao]**
Like a Chinese robe, in spring
at the tidal flat, resting a moment—
even the waves refrain from beating:
a fulling-block shell (砧貝), how still
