On the ninth day of the sixth month, as directed by Lord Gotō Nakauemon and Lord Suzuki Matakiyozaemon, three lumbermen (soma) were instructed to receive the survey map drawn up for the inspection of the disputed mountain. Iwajūrō of Nishimachi Village traveled to the site on the tenth and eleventh days [of the sixth month] to conduct the inspection. An inspection was also conducted on the sixteenth day (exact date uncertain), and then on the nineteenth and twentieth days a joint inspection was carried out with both parties present. Regarding the reference maps (karishazu), the parties were instructed to compile their records after confirming all matters on both sides. The villages of Akita agreed to this, but Majima and Koide Village did not. After further discussion with both parties, the survey map was duly completed.

On the twenty-second day of [the fourth month?], [the official] returned, and on the twenty-third day, went out again and reported the details [to the authorities].
