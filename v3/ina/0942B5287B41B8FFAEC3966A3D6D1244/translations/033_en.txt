[Continuing from the previous page — regarding the boundary matter:] "What sort of place do you take this to be?" [they asked]. [We replied that,] as has been stated year after year, the boundary for entry has from before been limited to Noda ■ and the aforementioned Hosoya (Hosotani). Moreover, even within the three locations, horses have been brought through from time immemorial — they have been brought through generally as far as Hiratsu-no-kami-sama [a local deity or guardian], but horses have never at all passed [beyond that point]. When we gave this reply, [the Minami-Shinden men] requested that from now on they be permitted to pass with horses as well. However, we stated that such a thing could not be permitted.

[Left page]

Item: As evidence of their having had access [to the area], [Minami-Shinden Village] put forward their own claims, stating that they should be permitted entry, and that [they had been coming to] bring down [goods/timber] — forty [people?] bringing things down — sufficient for daily use, and so forth. However, not only on this mountain but [also at] the locations of Chōsan and Kanezaemon, [others] have been bringing down wood and grass, and [the people of Minami-Shinden] stated [they found it] enviable [that they should be permitted to do the same].

Item: Minami-Shinden Village stated that there is standing timber and forest in the mountain land belonging to the original village (moto-mura), but [we affirm that] there is absolutely no standing timber forest [there].

In accordance with the above, we have prepared and submitted this written reply.
