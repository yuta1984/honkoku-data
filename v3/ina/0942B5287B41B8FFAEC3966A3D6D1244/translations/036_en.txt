A Formal Pledge Hereby Submitted

Item: Regarding the dispute that has arisen this time over a single mountain (hitomoto-yama) pertaining to the shared use (iriai) between the two home villages of Koide Village and Tojima Village on one side, and Minami-Shinden Village on the other, and in connection with the official inspection (kenbu) of the disputed site to which you have graciously come, we humbly accept the order for the village officials to serve as guides. Concerning any inquiries made during the guided inspection, we shall report nothing falsely and shall state matters exactly as they are.
　We shall in no way engage in quarrelsome behavior during the guided inspection. Should any inquiries be directed to us, we shall respond truthfully and as matters stand.

Item: Regarding the route of the guided inspection, we shall follow your instructions accordingly.

Item: Regarding provisions (hospitality/lodging arrangements), we shall act in accordance with established regulations and shall commit no improprieties whatsoever.
