The bearings from the above survey by Mr. Kawachi Nesaku and Iwasaki Hakushū are in agreement. The method of measuring bearings using the unit of 12 jō is not found in current surveying practice, and therefore government officials revised the notation to the style as written on the benchmark — namely "degrees east of south" and "degrees east of north." Regarding the elevation angles as well, there was a notice sent to Ueda indicating that in Hakushū's surveying method, 1 degree of elevation (ten) is equivalent to 3 degrees in the standard method; accordingly, Kawachi corrected all of these in accordance with the benchmark records.
　　Line-of-sight (estimated) distances: Shirakuzure — just under 5 ri; Tateshina — just over 11 ri; ■ga-sawa Ridge — just under 1 ri.
The above line-of-sight distances were estimated by Hakushū and have not been formally submitted as an official report.
