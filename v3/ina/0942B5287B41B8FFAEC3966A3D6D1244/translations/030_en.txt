Without sufficient farmland, cultivation is impossible. Furthermore, the villages in this area have no private-use forests (tate-hayashi) whatsoever, and we have sustained our livelihoods solely through the grasses and timber of the open mountains and fields. Should we be denied access to the communal mountain, not only will it become impossible for both villages to sustain themselves going forward, but we will also be unable to fulfill our annual tax obligations (nengu) to the authorities.

We therefore humbly request that Koide Village be summoned and examined, and that, following the precedent of previous years, an order be issued permitting us unobstructed access [to the mountain] as before. We most earnestly beseech you that, in your benevolence, you grant our petition as requested, and if it should please you to do so, all the peasants of our villages will be deeply and gratefully relieved. Respectfully submitted.
