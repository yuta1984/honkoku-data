【Right Folio】

On the 26th day, after consulting with Rokuzaemon-dono verbally, it was communicated that both individuals are to be sent back until further instructions [go-sata] are received for the time being. The above is noted.

One item: On the 21st day, a circular notice [kaijō] was received from the Pages' Affairs Office [o-koshō-yōshū]:
　Regarding the repair works [go-fushin] on Futao Bridge, a message was sent requesting that this notice be circulated, informing that [people] should use the temporary bridge for passage.

One item: Official documents submitted on the 29th day, as follows:

　　　Memorandum

　Cash: 84 mon
　　[Split seal]

The above is submitted as a request to receive and distribute payment for 14 tan of official rope [o-tsuna], covering the cost of dye-marking [kioi-shirushi-dai]. The above is noted.
　26th Day of the 5th Month — Kyūemon

　　　Memorandum

　Rice: for 28 persons
　　[Split seal]

The above is submitted as a request to receive and distribute rice rations — at a rate of 3 shaku of rice per meal — for the rope-braiders [o-ami-uchi], two persons each, who reported for duty both day and night, covering their midday and night meals.
　29th Day of the 5th Month — Kyūemon

　　　Memorandum

　Rice: for 19 persons　(of which: 8 persons — lower-ranked officials [shita-yaku]; 11 persons — manservants [o-chūgen])

The above is submitted as a request to receive and distribute rice rations, at 3 shaku per person [yori-ai], for those who performed the official duty [o-yō] of clearing and mowing the fields at Urahata [浦畑].
　29th Day of the 5th Month — Kyūemon
