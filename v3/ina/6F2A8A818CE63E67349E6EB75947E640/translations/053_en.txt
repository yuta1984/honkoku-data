【Left Folio】

3rd Day

One item: From past the Hour of the Rooster [approximately 2–3 p.m.], Kyūbei, a subordinate official [shita-yaku], set out accompanied by one manservant [o-chūgen] to conduct an inspection of the fish weir [yana] on the Yamamuro River [Yamamuro-gawa]. This matter was duly reported to Lord Jūzō, and upon return the details were recorded below.

4th Day

One item: Set out to [inspect] the fish weir post [yana-yaku] at the Benzaiten riverbed [Benzaiten kawara]. [The party consisted of] Kyūbei and one manservant, as noted below. [They] have now returned, and [the matter] has been reported to Lord Jūzō.

　　　Memorandum [Oboë]

Two manservants [o-chūgen]:

　　　Memorandum [Oboë]

On the coming 5th day, [we] are scheduled to set out to inspect the small fish weirs [ko-yana] along the lower reaches of the river. In connection with this, it is requested that an order from the village/district [gō-gun] authority be issued to provide a midday meal at Kaminshinden [Kami-shinden] for the party of three, [namely those of] upper and lower ranks. Respectfully submitted.
