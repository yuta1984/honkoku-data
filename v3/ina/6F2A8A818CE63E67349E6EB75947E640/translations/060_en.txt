　　Boar Year, 7th Month, 21st Day　　Kyūemon

One item: At the lower Nochimochi [Village], Shimo[-kawahara], [the official] departed for the river works [inspection/construction] after the Ninth Hour [approximately noon], and from there was ordered to attend to the river [further duties]. [We] were apprehensive upon receiving the order. [We] took note of a few minor matters. Regarding the documents, [the official] has returned. [The loaned personnel] are to follow and return accordingly. [The official] appears to be in good health and spirits.

28th Day:
One item: A letter from Lord Jūzō, as follows.

　On the 25th, weather permitting, please proceed to the location of the previous days and attend to the river works [inspection/construction]. Regarding all matters, please prepare and submit the same documents, notations, etc. as on the previous occasion. ■■ The above is all.
　　Intercalary 7th Month, 24th Day　　Jō Heizō
