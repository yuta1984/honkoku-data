【Right Folio】

5th Day:
One item: For the purpose of inspecting [kenchi] firewood [maki] at Katsuma Riverbed and Benzaiten Riverbed, [I] set out accompanied by Kyūbee and one *o-chūgen* (attendant/servant).

　　Firewood — 164 *ken* [units of measure] — Katsuma Riverbed
　　Same — 246 *ken* — Benzaiten Riverbed

The above figures were inspected and confirmed as recorded.

One item: From the 5th day onward, [I] have been going out daily for duties related to the fish weir [o-yana].

16th Day:
One item:
　A letter was received from Lord Jōzaemon, as follows:

　"On the 15th, the day after tomorrow, there are [official] matters at Ashizawa and Shimokawadaira. Please take note in accordance with past precedent [sen-kaku], and proceed yourself to the location ahead of time to attend to the coordination of all parties [shote]. This follows the precedent established when Tamura Rokuemon performed this duty in previous years, so please make inquiries [regarding that practice]. Respectfully submitted.

　　16th Day of the 7th Month
　[There is] the matter of going out for ■ inspection/survey.
　　　To: Satō Zensuke　　Kamiya Jōzaemon"

Having received the above letter and its instructions, [I] immediately proceeded [to the location] and consulted Lord Jōzaemon regarding all matters in accordance with past precedent. — 18th Day
