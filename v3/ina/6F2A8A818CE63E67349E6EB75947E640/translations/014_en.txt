【Right folio】

Item: The following documents were submitted on official business on the 25th day:

　　　Memorandum

Regarding those engaged in the dyeing trade (kōya) within the domain — in connection with the domain's period of suspended operations (mōshiyasumi) — I humbly request that an order be issued instructing [such persons] to submit their notification documents by the end of next month, as has been customary in previous years. Respectfully submitted.

　　25th day, 2nd month　　Kyūemon

　　　Memorandum

　　Upper ■ Village — one quire (jō) of paper

I wish to receive the above. Respectfully submitted.

　　25th day, 2nd month　　Kyūemon

Item: I appeared on the 25th as directed for official business. There being no discrepancies in the account ledger (kanjōchō) for the official loan funds (okashitsuke-kin), Jūzō-dono affixed the combined/overlapping seal (oshiai goingyo), and I received two volumes of the division ledger (warichōmen), returned [to the office], and placed them in the official cabinet (otansu).

Item: The following document was submitted on official business on the 29th day:

　　　Memorandum

　　Hemp fiber (asaoa-asa) — 250 monme

The above is to be purchased upon official order for use in Kyoto, [being required] for the inspection cloth samples (otankire), Tsuzukihara [cloth], and additionally for binding rope (mawarinawa). Respectfully submitted.

　　29th day, 2nd month　　Kyūemon
