【Left Folio】

The following is a letter [stating that] on the 12th — tomorrow — [the writer] will travel to conduct an inspection of the small fish weirs [koyana]. [The writer] requests that arrangements be graciously made regarding the midday meal for the three persons — upper and lower ranking — from Sakurai Village. Furthermore, depending on how the journey proceeds, there may also be occasion to stop at a village and lodge there for one night. Additionally, [the writer] intends to take the midday meal at Umeii Village on the 13th. Respectfully submitted.

　　　11th Month, 11th Day, Year of the Boar　　Kyūemon

　　　　Memorandum [Oboë]

One manservant [chūgen].
Regarding the above: as [I] will be traveling out tomorrow, the 12th, for the inspection of the small fish weirs [koyana], [I] would like to receive [the manservant] from the 6th hour [approx. noon] onward.

　　　7th Month, 11th Day, Year of the Boar

One item: The number of weirs [yana] visited for the official construction/embankment inspection [o-tsuki aratame]:
　　　　1 location
　　　　　　　Hara Village
　　　　[Reported] to the authorities [o-kami]
　　　　　　　Shimoshinden
　　　　2 locations
　　　　　　　Kamishinden

The above [inspection] was completed as recorded, and [the official] returned.

14th Day.
One item: [I] proceeded to the castle and reported for the official review. Lord Jūzō's instructions [were as follows]: Regarding the subordinate official [shitayaku] Kyūjō — concerning the river bed [kawaboshi] and the condition of the river that he had assessed previously — there are no changes to report. [He] was instructed to proceed once again on the 16th for another inspection [kenbu], and accordingly [the order was communicated] to Kyūbei.
