【Reports to Edo and Miscellaneous Records】

(Continued from previous page) Regarding the matters that were reported during the visit to Edo, it was stated that the御用人 (official attendants/administrators) had also conducted an inspection related to said matter.

16th Day
One item: A circular notice arrived reporting that Kyūbei's aunt, who had been residing in Tagiri, died of illness on the 14th of last month. In response to this, a notification document for Jūzō (殿) was to be passed to the Matsudaya establishment, which happened to be engaged in rural provisioning (gōkata ojikomi). Regarding the matter of "kōgen-gin" (residual silver/back payment) at Katsuma Kawahara, a report had come from Kyūzaemon's household, and the matter was reported to Jūhei-dono as a pressing case requiring instruction. As this was entirely a matter for lower-ranking officials (shimoeki), and since a prior petition had already been submitted promptly, it was directed that a formal pardon/exemption (go-men) be officially conveyed to (a certain) Hyōe without delay. Regarding this matter, it was directed that he proceed to pay his respects to the Monthly Duty Officer (tsukiban).

One item: On the 16th, a delivery notice sent from Tanizaemon's household (佐太):
　Fifty-four pieces of kajiki timber, plus one piece of substitute timber (go-daigi).
The above were floated downriver from Mikose Village by Asuhei to the riverside landing at Iijima for official use. An inspection request is filed.

22nd Day
One item: The head official, Heibei, received and took custody of the ledger of the indigo-vat registration (aigame-chōmoto) for the dye houses (kōya) throughout the domain.

25th Day
One item: Fujizaemon of Sugishima Village received one unit of furisatsu (a type of trade license/floating bill) and one sake license (chijimi-sakafuda) in the second month of this year; however, from the 4th day of the third month, he fell into a state of mental derangement (ranshin), rendering him unable to conduct business.
