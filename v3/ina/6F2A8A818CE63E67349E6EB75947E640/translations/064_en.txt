【Right Folio】

5th Day

1. Regarding the order to carry out the small river-draining duty (kawaboshi) on the Tenryū River in front of Nakaharashima Village: from the Hour of the Dog (around 9 o'clock) on the 5th day, [Kyūbei] brought along Kyūbei [and] one attendant (chūgen) and proceeded [to the site], [conducting] all matters in accordance with established precedent.

6th Day

1. Sweetfish (ayu): 15 [fish]
1. Miscellaneous small fish (sako): approximately 1 string
1. Eel (unagi)

The above catch was abundant. [The lord's] attendants were [in good spirits]; [His Lordship] returned at noon in good humor. On the 6th, [we] returned from serving in the escort party, and immediately distributed 80 [monme] among the Senior Elders (karō) and the Officials (yōnin).
Regarding items to be instructed [to distribute]: the matter was raised by the mat-carrying attendant (mushiro-mochi no o-chūgen) in person, and was reported after the [lord's] departure on the 6th.
