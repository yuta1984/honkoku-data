【5th Day】

1. Regarding the order to conduct the riverbed-clearing work (kawahoshi) on the Tenryū River in front of Nakaharajima Village, [Kyūbei] departed from the 9th hour (around noon) on the 5th day, bringing along Kyūbei and one manservant (chūgen), and handled all matters in accordance with precedent.

【6th Day】

1. Sweetfish (ayu): 15 [fish]
1. Miscellaneous small fish (sako/zako): approximately 1 bundle
1. Eel (unagi)

The above [catch] was plentiful; the assistant (sukebito) was in good spirits and returned home at noon. On the 6th day, [he] returned from accompanying [the official], and immediately distributed 80 [mon?] to the senior councillors (karō) and the administrators (yōnin). As for the items to be arranged, the manservant carrying the straw mat (mushiro) lodged the claim, which was reported after the 6th day.
