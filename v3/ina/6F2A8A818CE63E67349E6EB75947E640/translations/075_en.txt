【Left folio】

14th day of the 9th month　Kyūuemon
　　　Memorandum
　　One o-chūgen (manservant/middle-rank attendant)

The above is to be dispatched carrying two loads of goods to Iijima and Kaigun. We wish to receive [the said attendant] by the coming 19th day. Respectfully submitted.

　14th day of the 9th month　Kyūuemon

　　Memorandum
Item: Five hand-barrels (te-taru), each containing five shō of sake — for five locations
Item: Salted foodstuffs (shio-zakana) — same as above
　　　Note: Dispatch labels (sashi-fuda) are recorded to the left.

The above is to be forwarded to Iijima and Kaitō as gifts (o-chakumono). We wish to receive [the said items] by the coming 18th day. Respectfully submitted.

　14th day of the 9th month　Kyūuemon

　　Memorandum
Item: Two plain-wood document boxes (shiraki jōbako)
　　　Note: Including oil paper (aburagami), blue rope (ao-nawa), and dispatch labels (sashi-fuda)

The above is to be forwarded via courier (o-hikyaku) to Iijima and Kaitō. We wish to receive [the said items]. Respectfully submitted.
