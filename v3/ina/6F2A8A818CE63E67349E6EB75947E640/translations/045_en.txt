【Left Folio】

　　Sixth Month, Year of the Rabbit [1759]　　Ikegami Kyūbee (seal)

　　To: Tamura Yozaemon
　　To: Ikegami Mataemon [Matazaemon]

The foregoing is without error or discrepancy, as written above.
　　　　　　　　Respectfully submitted.
　　　　　　Satō Nin'uemon (seal)

20th and 21st Days:

One item: Firewood [maki] — 490 *ken* [units of measure]
　　Of which:　194 *ken* — Katsuma Riverbank [Katsuma Kawara]
　　　　　　　296 *ken* — Benzaiten Riverbank [Benzaiten Kawara]

The above quantities were confirmed: the subordinate official [shitayaku] Kyūbee, accompanied by one attendant [o-chūgen], went out to the site and conducted an inspection and survey [kenchi].

20th Day:

One item: The official business [goyō] was put aside / postponed due to the day's flow of affairs [nichryū — i.e., it was a day off / a day when business did not proceed].

25th Day:

One item: Goyō Kinzaemon-sama and Gonzaemon-dono have returned from Edo [Edo-omote], and accordingly ■ [the matter / a meeting] has taken place.

Same Day:

One item:　　　Memorandum [Oboegaki]

　　Old Chinese/Imported Bronze [furu tōdō] — 2 tan [bolts/pieces]

The above items are requested to be received for official use. Respectfully submitted.
　　25th Day of the 6th Month　　Itō Genzō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Haru■ Shichizō

The above were submitted and handed over by Jūzō-dono. They have accordingly been delivered [received and transferred].

26th Day:

One item: Denshirō of Himochi Village [Himochi-mura] came for the official inspection [kenchi] of pine timber [matsuki] and ■ timber. The subordinate official Kyūbee, accompanied by one attendant [o-chūgen], reported the matter to Jūzō-dono.
