29th Day

One item: Kyūbei has returned from his inspection of the riverbed [kawara kenbu]. In the lower section of the current cultivated area [tōsaku-shimo], extending from below Mizoguchi to the confluence of two streams [Futase], there is a small channel [ko-yaku] branching off toward the Katsuma area, measuring over three chō [approximately 330 meters] in total length — this is all in one stretch. Apart from this, there appears to be no other waterway suitable for use as a river-diversion channel [o-hi-kawa]. The above findings are to be reported to Lord Jōzaemon. The route [for the inspection party] is via Hiorihira, ascending ■ [a road], and proceeding to Shikawara; it is expected that [the party] will proceed accordingly. Today, the above matters are to be formally reported [to the appropriate authority].
