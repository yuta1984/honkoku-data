【Right folio】

29th day

Item: An official seal document (goyō-in) was submitted, as follows.

　　　Memorandum (Oboegaki)

　　　30 monme [valued at] 60 mon
　　　70 monme [valued at] 154 mon
　　Cash: 218 hiki [in total]

· For 90 monme of net material (o-ami-hara), [representing] the price from the sea (i.e., the wholesale price from the coast), I wish to receive and hand over [said payment]. Respectfully submitted.

　　3rd month, 29th day　　Kyūemon

　　　Memorandum (Oboegaki)

Item: Two matched surcoats (buai-haori)
Item: Two pairs of gaiters (kyahan)
Item: Two signboards (kanban)

The above items have been inspected, and I wish to receive and hand them over from both persons [concerned]. Respectfully submitted.

　　3rd month, 29th day　　Kyūemon

Same [date]

Item: Regarding the sake-label permit (age-sake-fuda) for Ueno village — as [the village] wishes to make [this] a village-level petition, an official petition (negaisho) was submitted, and accordingly the petition has been forwarded.

29th day

Item: Tanaka Saemon returned yesterday from Nakazawa, having brought back a reply letter (hensho) from Iijima. He reported that [the matter] had been settled without delay or difficulty.

Item: A letter from Jōzaemon-sama, [the contents of] which are as follows:

　　Regarding the three village petitions (mura-kata negaisho) that have been communicated to [you] on various occasions recently — [I] trust that [you] will be pleased to grant approval in accordance with the petition concerning long cloth (naga-nuno). Respectfully submitted.

　　3rd month, 29th day　　Kamiya Jōzaemon
