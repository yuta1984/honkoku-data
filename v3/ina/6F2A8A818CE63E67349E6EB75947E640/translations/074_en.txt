[The] 10th day

Item: Regarding official business, the following was reported to Iijima Kaitō.
　As in previous years, a courier (hikyaku) is to be dispatched to Iijima and Kaitō. All matters are to be handled as per prior precedent (sen-kaku). It has been so ordered by Jūzō-dono. Furthermore, from this year onward, instructions have been given to negotiate arrangements regarding the levy on high-value goods and sake (o-takamono-shu), and all cuts/assessments (shokiri). It was also ordered that the investigation officers (ginmi-kata) be informed of the above matters. Accordingly, the relevant matters are noted here. It is expected that [the courier] will depart around the 18th or 19th of the coming month. This is hereby reported.

The 14th day

Item: Today, the following was submitted for official use:
　　　Memorandum
　　One ashigaru (foot soldier/attendant)

The above is to be dispatched as a courier (o-hikyaku) to Iijima and Kaitō on the coming 19th day. Accordingly, we wish to receive [the said ashigaru] by the coming 18th day. Respectfully submitted.
