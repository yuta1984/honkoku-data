【Right folio】

As stated above, [I] prepared the accompanying letter and handed over to Tanaka Jōzaemon six copies of the said documents (tegata), together with one copy of the timber transport contract (for downstream river transport) from the previous Year of the Tiger (Tora-doshi), pertaining to long-term use (kyūyō).

Item: A letter received from Jōzaemon-dono, as follows.

　I write to you by letter. Regarding the rope-making (tsuna-uchi) matter of which you informed me the other day, [the matter] has now come to a conclusion, and it appears that a formal report should be submitted perhaps as early as tomorrow. As for the endorsement (okugaki), [I] was instructed [as] you had informed [me]. I write to ask whether [it] will be possible for [you] to issue whatever instructions may be required regarding handling and any such measures.
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Respectfully,
　　3rd month, 22nd day — Kamiya Bungo-zaemon
　　To: Satō Kyūzaemon-dono

In connection with the above, [I] reported on the 23rd day, received the two rope-makers (tsuna-uchi), and from the 24th day onward ordered them to continue cutting two lengths (tan) of rope from the barrel-straining [process]. Upon completion, [I] intend to submit the rope certificate (tsuna-fuda).

Item: Regarding the planting of lacquer seedlings (urushi-nae) — as there were no attendants (chūgen) available at this time, [I] reported to Jōzaemon-dono, received one hired day-laborer and one attendant (chūgen) assigned [to assist], and Kyūbei came forward to carry out the planting on the 24th. [He] planted [the seedlings] from the Obara area down toward the riding grounds (baba). Upon completion of one tan of the inspected [area], the assignment with Jōzaemon-dono was concluded. The corresponding payment in cash was duly submitted.
