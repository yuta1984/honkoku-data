Item: The accompanying letter (soejō) to Kaijima [office/post], as follows.

I humbly write to you. Now that the weather has grown warmer, I trust each of you gentlemen is ■■ [in good health] and ■ [carrying out your duties well]. In connection with the matter at hand: Matsudaya Rokurōzaemon has submitted timber contract documents for the downstream river transport to Enshū (Tōtōmi Province), and [I] have affixed the endorsement (okugaki) and duly handed [the documents] over to the said person. The seal register (han-kagami) exchange will be arranged upon [receipt]. I write so that you may be informed of the above; I am in awe and respectfully submit this. Respectfully concluded.
