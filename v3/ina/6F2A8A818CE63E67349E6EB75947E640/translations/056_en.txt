14th Day.

One item: Regarding official duties [go-yō]. Concerning this year's matsutake mushroom official duties [matsutake go-yō]: it is hereby reported that [the official] has been serving in a single-person capacity [ichinin-yaku]; although [he] has been serving in rotation [nenban] with the same office [dōyaku — names illegible], [the situation] is such that [it remains] a single-person assignment. Respectfully submitted.

One item: Letters regarding matsutake mushroom official duties [matsutake go-yō] were dispatched to Mizoguchi Village and Itayama Village.

[Content of the letter:]
This letter is to inform you of the following. As the season for matsutake mushrooms in your village is gradually drawing near, the village guards [ban'nin] assigned to the matsutake mountain — in accordance with the customary arrangements of previous years — are to carry out their inspections diligently and without lapse; and upon spotting even a single mushroom, they are to promptly dispatch a written report [kami-chūshin]. Please make the necessary arrangements accordingly. ■■.

　　　7th Month, 16th Day, Year of the Ox　　Kyūemon
　　　　　Mizoguchi Village
　　　　　　　Village Headman [Nanushi]

11th Day.
