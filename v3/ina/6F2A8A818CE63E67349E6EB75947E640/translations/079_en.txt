**[Letter]**

I humbly write to you. Although the season has turned cold, I trust that all of you continue to perform your official duties in good health, which I hold in the highest regard.

Now, although I am not fully acquainted with the matter, I am coming forward to present a report concerning one unit (*hitokabu*) of salt from this location, specifically regarding the apportioned one-tenth share (*wari-ichi-ori*). As the officials here have requested that I convey this matter to the party responsible for delivery and obtain your esteemed guidance, I write accordingly.

Respectfully submitted in haste.

　　19th Day of the 9th Month　　Satō Kyūzaemon *(seal)*

To: Iijima Office

　　Lord Sakata Tomozaemon

　　Lord Kaneko Shirōhachi
