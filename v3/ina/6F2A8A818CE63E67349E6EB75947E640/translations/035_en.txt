(Continued from previous page, regarding Fujizaemon of Sugishima Village) — Concerning the above matter, a village official (mura yakunin) personally brought and submitted a petition requesting to pay the prescribed levy (oyaku) on pine timber. Since the customary practice (osahō) was to receive and review the transport levy (unjō) through the third month, the half-period levy (han'yaku unjō) from [the third month] through the seventh month is to be returned (refunded). The above is so stated.

Regarding the above matter, having reported it accordingly, the instruction was received that the matter be acknowledged and acted upon as per current precedent. The order was conveyed by Sajūrō-dono. The village official was accordingly summoned and informed of the directive.

25th Day

For pressing official matters (sashi-kakari no goyōsuji) that arise, he will come out and fulfill his duties. This matter was reported to Jūzō-dono, who acknowledged it and took note.
