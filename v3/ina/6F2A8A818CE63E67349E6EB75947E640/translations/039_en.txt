25th Day
One item: A letter from Jōzaemon-dono, as follows:

　I write to inform you by letter: the upper rope-braiding [uwatsuna-uchi] work is not to be carried out for the time being. You are to have the worker Rokūemon return immediately to his post at the gate [kadokata]. The above is noted.
　　25th Day of the 5th Month — Kamiya Sōzaemon
　　To: Satō Kyūemon-dono

Regarding the above, both rope-braiders [o-tsuna-uchi] were promptly summoned, and the aforementioned instructions were conveyed to them.
