We received a barrel of sake as a courtesy, and served it to those who frequent the office. However, it was noted by several officials that the quality of the sake this year was poor.

Regarding the overall circumstances of Gontarō, there appears to be some matter of concern, but it seems to differ from what was written on the cover label—so reported Rokuemon in his account. As this could affect our public reputation, we would like to arrange for all items to be sent henceforth in four-to (四斗) casks. We summoned Iseya and made inquiries, and were told that, as has been the practice going back some time, the exchange gold (kōkin) for the Yoda and Katada (Shinano-no-Kami) portions had been arranged and settled. We also confirmed this with Shichirōemon of the purchasing section (okaigata), and received the response as noted to the left.

Therefore, even if there might be a slight price advantage otherwise, we would like going forward for all goods to be dispatched in four-to casks, as we have proposed.
