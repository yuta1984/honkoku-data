【Left Folio】

A man named Rihachi from Higuchi Village in Kami-Ina, whose contract [furi, i.e., subcontracted operations] was being managed by Seki Matsuemon, died of illness on the 2nd day of last month. In consequence, a petition was submitted requesting exemption from the Sugibashi transport levy [un'jō]. As stipulated by the regulations, the levy is waived entirely through the 3rd month, and half-duty [han-yaku] is waived from the 3rd month through the 7th month; accordingly, this ruling was communicated [to the relevant parties]. The above has been duly reported.

One item: In connection with the official repair works [goyō uke-tsukuroi], the midday-meal vouchers [tegata] for 3 manservants [o-chūgen] were countersigned [o-urahan] and completed, received, and handed over to Riki Kyūbei.

One item: [Partially illegible] — at the Oyana River [o-yana-gawa], conditions in the various villages in this vicinity have become favorable. In due course, an official inspection [o-kenbun] is requested. The above is noted.

14th Day:

One item: Official duties were completed, and no report was made to [the official overseeing] the Otsukikawa [築川]. Regarding the recent flooding [de-mizu] and the fish weir [o-yana] below the teahouse [o-chaya], it would be appropriate to issue instructions accordingly. As reported, [due to circumstances related to ■■■], the situation has become such that the water intake point [mizu-iriguchi] is being managed. Nevertheless, since the weir [o-yana] is being managed and maintained under the current official jurisdiction [o-yaku-suji], there will likely be considerable expenses [o-mono-iri]. If, as in previous years, a petition is directed to the village communities [gō-kata], it can be handled promptly. The above has been duly reported.
