　　Intercalary 7th Month, 4th Day　　Kyūemon

26th Day:
One item: This morning, [I] went to Lord Jūzō's place [and discussed the matter of] the matsutake mushroom mountain watchmen. As the 19th will be the Equinox, [I] conveyed that [the watchmen] should be dispatched on the 19th. [We] shall see to it accordingly. Upon looking into the matter, reports [came in] from various mountains that [only] one mushroom had grown, but [we] were instructed to submit a formal report regardless. [We] have arranged that if even one mushroom is sighted and reported, the watchmen should be sent to file the report.

One item: On the 29th, [the official] went out accompanied [by attendants] for the Ashizawa-Shimoi River [inspection], departing at the Ninth-and-a-Half Hour [approximately 1 p.m.]. I, along with a representative of the Hisa household, went over [there, staying] nine days at two parts [expenses], completed all matters according to prior precedent, and returned directly upon [official] convenience.
