17th day

1. Before the fifth hour (approximately 8:00 a.m.), [we] proceeded [to Takami Village] for the timber inspection (kenchi) duties at Takami Village. [We were accompanied by] one attendant (chūgen) assigned to Kyūbei[-hei]. Nakamura Shinroku [had come] from Ōkubo Village and was already in attendance at the lodging in Takami Village. On the morning of the 18th, [we] proceeded to the ferry crossing, inspected and tallied the number of timber [pieces], and upon completing [the inspection], returned [home] past the eighth hour (approximately 2:00 p.m.) on the same 18th day. Thereafter, [we] made the rounds to the Senior Councillors (karō-chū), the Elders (otoshiyori-chū), and the Officials (oyōnin-chū) [submitting reports and borrowing ■], and reported that the duties of the Nakazawa timber inspection had been completed.
