　　20th Day of the 7th Month　　Kyūemon

One item: Regarding the person [tōnin] responsible for the matsutake mushroom mountain [matsutake-yama] in Nakasokura Village, Lord Jōzaemon has conveyed the instruction that the petition [submitted] is to be accepted as presented. Accordingly, this matter is to be submitted so that the rural affairs office [gōkata] may issue the appropriate directive [o-sata]. ■

　　　Memorandum [Oboë]

Concerning the guardian/warden [bannin] of the matsutake mushroom mountain that has been assigned to Nakasokura Village: as the village community [murakata] has submitted a petition regarding this matter, [we] hereby report that from this year [the warden] shall be assigned instead to Takami Village. We humbly request that the rural affairs office [gōkata] issue a formal directive [o-sata] regarding this matter. Respectfully submitted.

　　24th Day of the 7th Month　　Kyūemon
