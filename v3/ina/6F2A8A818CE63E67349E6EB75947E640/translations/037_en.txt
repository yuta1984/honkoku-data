【Left Folio】

5th Month — Kyūzaemon

1st Day
One item: Five ayu (sweetfish) of the first catch [hatsugyo] presented by the cormorant fisherman of Tahara Village.

4th Day
One item: Eleven ayu of the first catch from the cormorant fisherman of Taka Village. (Jinsuke / Funauе)

5th Day
One item: Twelve or more ofuda [protective talismans] for the Boys' Festival [Tango no Sekku] — reported as above.

7th Day
One item: In Edo, the Young Lord [Wakadono-sama] presented his first formal greetings [o-rei], and accordingly, dressed in hemp ceremonial garb [asa-kamishimo], we made the customary rounds to express congratulations. On the same day, at the residence of Mokusuke-dono, it was conveyed that, in addition to the Young Lord's message, as a gesture of celebration, soup [o-suimono] and sake were to be bestowed. Regarding the date [for further formalities], it was said that instructions would follow. As for today, it was declared that it is not necessary to make the rounds of congratulations today; rather, when one goes to receive [the gift], one should make the rounds at that time — this was today's directive.

8th Day
One item: As Kyūbei has somewhat recovered, he attended [returned to] the office today.

11th Day
One item: Today, in accordance with the announcement, we received soup [o-suimono] and sake in the study/reception hall [shoin]. It was announced that the stipend recipients, retainers, junior attendants, and escort guards would receive [these gifts] on the 12th day and the 5th day [of the following month]. To offer thanks, rounds were made to the religious affairs office [go-shūmon], the elders [o-toshiyori-chū], and the administrators [go-yōnin-chū].

18th Day
One item: Today the following official matter was submitted:
　The subordinate officer Kyūbei has recovered from his side ailment [wakiyamai], and accordingly, the payment [harai] for firewood from the lacquer fields [urushi-batake] at various locations has been submitted.

　　　Memorandum

　　Two manservants [o-chūgen]
The above concerns the interest payment [kōri-harai] for the lacquer [transactions]; we wish to submit a request to receive [payment] by the 15th of next month. The above is so submitted.
