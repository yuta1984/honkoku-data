Concerning the matter of Matsukiya Anzaemon's request to increase his indigo vats by one: all petitions are to be granted as requested. Respectfully submitted.

　　14th day of the 2nd month

Furthermore, both of the aforementioned documents (shōmon) will be returned after being noted with the monthly duty officer (tsuki-ban). 

　　To: Satō Kyūemon
　　　　　　　　　　Zō (Jūzō)

**15th Day**

Item: A circular (mawashijō) arrived from the rural page inspector (gōchū-koshō metsuke) stating: His Lordship had his first audience with Lord Yai in Edo. Accordingly, all are to make their rounds today — in cotton dress and hemp formal wear (asa-kamishimo) to offer congratulations (kyōetsu) — to the senior councillors (karō), elders (toshiyori), and chamberlains (yōnin) ■ (one character illegible).

**17th Day**

Item: Today, the levy (un'jō) on hemp taro and hemp cloth for this town was paid. Kyūbei appeared and one attendant (ochūgen) received it.

Item: The levy on unfinished timber (shiraki un'jō) was settled today.

　　　Memorandum

　　One attendant (ochūgen)

The above is in connection with the payment of the aforementioned tax money (un'jō-sen); receipt and distribution will take place from the 5th hour (approximately 8 a.m.). Respectfully submitted.

　　17th day of the 2nd month　　Kyūemon
