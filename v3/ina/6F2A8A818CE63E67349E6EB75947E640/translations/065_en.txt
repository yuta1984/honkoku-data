　8th month, Year of the Rabbit (Hare)
　　To: Itō ■uemon-sama

The foregoing official matter has been carried out without discrepancy. Respectfully submitted.

1. Today, [I] went to the castle and presented a report (chūshinsho) on matsutake mushrooms from the various mountain areas. Having submitted it, [I was to] proceed to Nakazawa on the 8th of tomorrow. Upon inquiring [about this], [I was] ordered by Gonzaemon-dono to go to Ho (保) instead. Now that the submission has been made, there is also the matter of a single-person duty assignment. The designated person (kiribito) is, for all official matters of a public-facing nature, to consult with the lower officials (toishitayaku), the middle pages (ochūkoshō), and the inspectors (metsuke), and to receive their instructions accordingly. Furthermore, in the event of flooding at the fish weir (yana), [he] is to promptly proceed to the castle town and manage all matters as they arise. Additionally, during my absence, [he] is ordered to attend at the designated residence (Aishō-taku) from the 4th hour (around 10 a.m.) to the 8th hour (around 2 p.m.) for matters related to the weapons office (bugukata). Respectfully submitted.
