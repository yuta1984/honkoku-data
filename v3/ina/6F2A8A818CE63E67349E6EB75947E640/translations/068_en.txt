【Right folio】

　　　　　　Kyūemon　[seal]

The 24th Day

1. It was reported by Matsushiya Rokuzaemon that Endō Sukejirō, younger brother of the official at Asajima, Tōyama Gorōbee, had been appointed to a temporary post (kariyaku). This information was originally conveyed from Ikadarinori (raft-man) Yazaemon.

Same day

1. A written note was brought to Lord Jō Zaemon regarding the presentation (献上) of matsutake mushrooms. When the presentation was made at his residence, [it was noted that] since the mushrooms were now fully in season, the [mountain workers / log-haulers] should be withdrawn [from the mountain]. The records (hikae) from last autumn were reviewed, and on the 25th the matter was to be reported officially. After receiving the ceremonial sake, and upon reviewing the autumn records, it was found that the date for the mountain [work] had been set on the 23rd of last month, and on the 24th the [workers] were immediately withdrawn. Accordingly, the details of this matter were reported on the 25th as an official duty, as noted below.

The 25th Day

1. Due to a departure from the village (shukkyō), [the official] did not go out to the middle attendants (o-nakakoshō) or the duty village personnel on the official duty day. In the morning, [he] went out to Itsukamachi for construction/repair work, clearing away obstacles at various locations. The [lord's] procession also passed through, clearing the way. Two manservants (chūgen) and two official-duty men were brought along.
From around the Hour of the Rooster [approx. 2:00 p.m.], the Lord entered ■-machi [a specific town name], and then departed.
