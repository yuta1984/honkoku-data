The 24th day

Item: Today, around the hour of the seventh-and-a-half (approximately 5 p.m.), the messenger (hikyaku) returned from Iijima and Kaijima. All official items and documents were brought back without delay; the return letters from both places were received. Upon his return, I went directly to Jūzō-dono to report and promptly presented the return letters. One foot-soldier (okachi), the group leader (yotō), the village headmen (shōya), and the attendants (chūgen) — all were consulted as per the previous precedent, and additional arrangements were made. I instructed that the official accounts be settled and handed over accordingly.

Same day

Item: Two and a half special-authorization tags (betsu-fuda) for the new forest lacquer grove (Shinrin Urushihata) were received from the Construction Office (Ofushin-kata). In accordance with instructions previously given to Kyūbei, I directed that the said special tags be returned to the Construction Office.
