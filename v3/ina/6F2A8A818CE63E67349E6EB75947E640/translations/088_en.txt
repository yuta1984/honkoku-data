1st day of the 10th month of the year of the Hare — [traveling] from Kurokouchi, five days' journey
　　　Official Buichi-kata Inspector (go-shita-yaku)
　　　　　To: Ikegami Kyūbei-dono

Same, 2nd day

Item　　Memorandum

　　　One ashigaru (foot soldier / lower retainer)

The above [person] was sent out to inspect and confirm the arrival of the purchased firewood. As the [originally assigned] lower official (shita-yaku) is ill, we wish to have [the goods] received together with [a replacement] lower official from the Hour of the Cock (approximately 4 p.m. onward). Respectfully submitted.
　　　10th month, 2nd day　　Kyūemon
