Item: Rice, 3 shō 6 gō — at a rate of 4 gō per person
　　Total persons: 9
　Breakdown:
　　1 shō 2 gō — Ashizawa Village　　Nanushi (headman): Manbē　／　Kumigashira (sub-headman): Sadashichi
　　1 shō 2 gō — Shimō-ōshima Village　Nanushi: Hachizaemon　／　Kumigashira: Okushichirō
　　1 shō 2 gō — Sakai Village　　　　Nanushi: Rihachi　／　Kumigashira: Zenshichi

The above rice was distributed as provisions (fuchi-mai) on the occasion of the visit by Satō Kyūbē and Akabane Kōsaku, in connection with the official duties of the autumn small weir (ko-yanagi/ko-yana) service. It was disbursed from within the cotton-rice (wata-mai) to be paid as tax, and has been duly and certainly received. As stated above.

　Hōreki 8th year, Year of the Dog (Inudoshi) / Tiger [month], 12th month
　　[Addressed to:] Nabeshima Zenzaemon-sama

The foregoing is correct and without discrepancy. Respectfully submitted.
