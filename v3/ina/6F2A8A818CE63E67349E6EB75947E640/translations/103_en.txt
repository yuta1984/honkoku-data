8th Day

Item: The collection of miscellaneous levies (un'jō) on gama (cattail/rushes) for the same post up through the 8th day has been completed.

---

　　　Memorandum

　　Gold: 5 ryō
　　Cash: 180 kan-mon

The above are the gold and cash for various miscellaneous levies (shoji un'jō) that have been placed at our disposal. We wish to remit payment on the 9th of the following day at the hour of the Fours (approximately 10 a.m.). Respectfully submitted.

　　12th Month, 8th Day　　　Kuemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Shichirōemon

---

　　　Memorandum

　　Pack horse (o-konida): 1 horse

Regarding the above, as we are remitting the prescribed river levy (o-ari un'jō-sen), we humbly request that permission be granted for river access to and from the main temple lodging (hon-bō). Respectfully, we await your instructions.

　　12th Month, 8th Day　　　The above two persons
