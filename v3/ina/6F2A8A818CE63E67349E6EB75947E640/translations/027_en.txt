【Left folio】
　　Satō Kyūemon

4th month, 2nd day

Item: A reply letter from Endō Gorōbei's office, the contents of which are as follows:

　　I am writing to inform you by separate paper. Our colleague Jibei is currently on guard duty (o-ban) with one prisoner (torimono) in his custody at present. I am writing to notify you of this matter. Respectfully submitted.

　　3rd month, 27th day　　Endō Gohei
　　To: Satō Kyūzaemon-sama

The above matter has been reported and deliberated with Jūzō-dono, as it is a matter for the 5th-day official duties.

2nd day

Item: Today, four small certificates (ko-tegata) for [the transport of] rafts down to Tōtōmi Province (Enshū-kudashi ikada) were brought [here], and [the matter of] the profit seal/stamp (ri-ingyō) [is as follows — text damaged/illegible].

Item: Regarding the sake-label permit (age-sake-fuda) for Kamishima village — as the petition (three-by-three [i.e., multiple] requests) has been resolved, [I] have sent a letter via Kyūbei below, entrusting Teiroku-dono [to see] that the permit label is duly brought [to the village].

4th day
