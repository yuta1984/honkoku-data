【Left folio】

25th day

Item: [The following] official request (goyō) was submitted, as follows.

　　　Memorandum (Oboegaki)

　　Four hundred and fifty mon in cash

The above is the payment for one tan of rope (o-tsuna). I wish to receive and hand over [said payment]. Respectfully submitted.

　　3rd month, 25th day　　Kyūemon

　[In response,] a large receipt (ō-tegata) was issued.

　　　Memorandum (Oboegaki)

　　Four hundred and fifty mon in cash

The above represents payment for one tan of rope (o-tsuna), which I wish to receive and settle. Respectfully submitted.

　　3rd month, 25th day　　Ikegami Kyūbei　[seal]

　　To: Satō Rizaemon-dono

[I confirm that] the two documents above are consistent with one another, with no discrepancy. Respectfully submitted.

□　　　　　　　　Kūzaemon　[seal]

---

Item: From Zenzaemon and Ōhankichi of Kurosawa village, [a petition was received] requesting [permission] to commence [operating] a dye-house (kōya). As [they stated] that their circumstances this spring were unfavorable (fukatte), they wished to submit a village map (shōzu). Upon their bringing the petition through Jinpei [as an intermediary], [I] received it and submitted it today. Together with this, a petition from Ōshima village concerning a new dye-house (shin-kōya) and the number of indigo-processing goods (ai-ma shinakazu) was also submitted.

25th day

Item: Kyūbei, the head of the dye-house guild (kōya-gashira), [reported] that the count of indigo vats (ai-game) throughout the domain had been assembled. Bringing along both the register (chōmen) and the notification documents (todokesho), [he] came in person, and [these were] submitted for official use (goyō) on the 29th day.
