5th day

Item: The following is reported regarding official business matters.

　　　　　Tomiya Shihei
　　　　　Ōmiya Yaemon
　　　　　Tomiya Shinnosuke

The above three individuals have each year submitted bids to undertake the tax levy (運上) on hemp, taro, and hemp cloth. They have submitted a petition this year. In recent years, in the southern districts, it has previously been the case that undertaking such contracts has not been profitable (lit. "has not been worthwhile"). They petitioned that the levy payments be settled through a special arrangement (思召), but this has not yet been resolved. They have petitioned again, and although it appears to have been around the 10th month [of the relevant year], the matter was treated as an official duty (御役義), and the levy was submitted. Therefore, they are once again reporting their concerns. They state that they will find it difficult to continue undertaking the contract in the coming year. They submit their opinion as a further petition. This matter has been reported to the officials (御用人). We request that this be duly noted. The response was: "I too shall consider the matter and address it accordingly." [Signed by]御中 (a senior official).

---

Item: Matsudaya Rokuzaemon has submitted documents (証文) concerning the timber and lumber (才木) from Nakazawa Mountain (中沢山), including measurements of lengths and angles (歩・角廻). Accordingly, Kyūemon alone is to provide the countersignature (奥書). Since no colleague (同役) was available at that time, it was reported that the matter would be communicated by letter. The matter was then duly resolved [through whatever arrangement was made].

Item: After the 20th, Matsudaya is to submit the large timber permit (大手形) for the lumber. This too is to be handled by Kyūzaemon alone [i.e., Kuzaemon acting as sole signatory].
