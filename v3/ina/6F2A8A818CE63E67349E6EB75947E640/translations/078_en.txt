**Memorandum (Oboegaki)**

Item: One shō and two gō of rice, allocated per person

Number of persons: Three
Breakdown:

- Eight gō: Yamadera Village — Village Headman (Nanushi): Kōshirō / Group Leader (Kumigashira): Uzaemon
- Four gō: Hikage Village — Village Headman (Nanushi): Kyūzaemon / Group Leader (Kumigashira): Genzō

The above records that during this winter, on the occasion of the official visit by Lord Ikegami Kyūbei for the inspection of indigo vats (ai-game aratame), rice was distributed as relief provisions. This rice was delivered out of the rice collected as payment for fish (o-sakana no nōmai). We confirm that we have duly received the same, as stated above.

Hōreki 8, Year of the Dog-Tiger (Inui-tora / Tsuchinoe-tora, 1758)
To: Lord Tomishima Kichizaemon

The above is correct and accurate, with no discrepancies. Respectfully submitted.
