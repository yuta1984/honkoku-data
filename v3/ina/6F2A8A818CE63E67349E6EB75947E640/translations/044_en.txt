【Right Folio】

(Continuing from previous page) Things appear to be proceeding well [in any case]. However, as in the year before last, the matter of requesting assistance from the village communities [gō-kata] has made no progress whatsoever. As it pertains to our official duties [o-yaku-suji], we are expected to handle the river dredging [kawa-zarai] and related works ourselves, and have been so informed this year. Regarding how many laborers [ninsoku] from Ōtsuki can be mustered for this purpose, we have been asked about this now. In the year before last, at the time the embankment works [o-tsuki] were completed, the village communities alone — with Misawa also participating — managed to complete all the river dredging and related works. We humbly request that [the order] be issued before the tenth-day petition [tōka-negai], as has been done previously. Next, we reported that the Tonosaka teahouse [o-chaya] should be inspected. As has been reported previously, due to changes in the current [water flow direction], the channel course [se-suji] has shifted, and there is no longer a midday [waterway / shallows]. We reported these matters. In any case, it was announced that [the order / instruction] would be issued in the 11th month.

17th Day:

One item: Regarding Udō Sakuhei of Yamajiri Village [Yamdera Village], it is considered difficult for him to continue serving as village elder [toshiyori]. Therefore, we humbly petition that the Udō [position/role] be reconsidered [reformed]. The above petition was brought by the neighborhood group leader [kumigashira], received, and duly reported. It was submitted for official use [goyō] ■.
