【Right Folio】

14th Day of the 5th Month — Kyūemon

Same (20th Day)
One item: Today the following was submitted as official business:

　　　Memorandum

One item: Superior hemp fiber [jō-asao] — 40 momme
One item: Rope transfer [tsuna-utsushi] — 100 momme

As described above, we wish to receive payment for the various uses of the inspection ropes [megurinawo] and related fittings, along with seven [units of] "iuke," and for all associated expenses. The above is submitted.
　　20th Day of the 5th Month — Kyūemon

Same (Day)
One item: The petition from Sugishima Village has been resolved, and it has been returned to Jōzaemon-dono, who had lodged it on behalf of the villagers.

Same (Day)
One item: Regarding the area below the teahouse on the slope by the weir [o-yana] river — it is in order, as per the previous inspection. The above is noted.
　As for the firewood allocated for the proxy [nadai], the side facing the river has been split [or: sand has encroached from the river side]; although the recent high waters have pushed sand up [onto the area], there will likely be a need for further splitting [of firewood / clearance work], and this is hereby reported.

Same (Day)
One item: A circular notice [kaijō] arrived from the junior attendant inspectors [o-ko-shō metsuke-shū]. Regarding the Young Lord's petition in Edo, it was announced on the aforementioned date [that the matter had been granted] as requested. [He has] been performing the customary seasonal greetings [o-setsu-muki o-rei] in due succession — this is reported.
