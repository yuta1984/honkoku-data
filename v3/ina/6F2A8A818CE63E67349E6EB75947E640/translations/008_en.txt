【Right folio】

We had inquired about affixing the endorsement (okugaki) and official seals, and were re-instructed by a colleague of the same rank. Additionally, Lord Jūzō issued the following instructions:

**5th Day**

Item: When we went out to inspect (kenchi) the aforementioned lumber, we reported the details of the inspection that Kōsaku had carried out during his visit last year. Further, various reports were made concerning the method of inspection, which is the same as for the current domain purchases. Although the matter of Matsudaya is also the same as for domain purchases, regarding the inspection of purchases from Benzaiten-kawara, we are to record and report only the current number of pieces — how many sun-kaku (square-cun) through what size, what types, and how many pieces. This matter has been noted for inquiry, and further instructions will follow.

**7th Day**

Item: There were remaining miscellaneous certificates (shofuda), and the surplus was delivered to Shinsuke, the head carpenter (daiku-gashira), who accepted them. An attendant (ochūgen) received them on his behalf. The received items were handed over.

Item: From the 8th day onward, the exchange of miscellaneous certificates proceeded as usual. Kyūbei, a carpenter, and one attendant were in charge, completing the work by the 8th hour (approximately 2 p.m.). The lower-ranked attendants were provided with a midday meal upon receipt, and the carpenters were given a midday meal upon completion at the 8th hour ■ (one character illegible).

**10th Day**

Item: For official business, after the 5th hour in the morning (approximately 9 a.m.), Lord Jūzō indicated that approximately 5 kan of the total levy (un'jō) on hemp fiber cloth (asa-o-nuno) from this period might be remitted (goyōsha). Further instructions will follow.

**12th Day**

Item: The exchange of miscellaneous certificates was completed by today. The carpenters rested on the 13th and were returned to the construction office (go-fushin-kata) from the 14th.

Item: The document to be submitted on the 14th official business day is as follows:
　　　Memorandum
　　　Levy (un'jō) on hemp taro and hemp cloth
