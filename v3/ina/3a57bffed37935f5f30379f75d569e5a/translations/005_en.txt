During last year, [Nisaburō] petitioned his relatives and the head store's [manager] Matasaemon, and sold off what household goods he had little by little, directing the proceeds toward repaying debts, and using whatever remained to provide for his master Chūbei's medical care, food, and clothing. Nevertheless, as these resources were meager, you [Nisaburō] exerted yourself in various ways: on days when Chūbei felt well enough, [Nisaburō] would go out to the surrounding area to buy and sell eggs and the like, and during the intervals [when at home], he provided attentive nursing care...
