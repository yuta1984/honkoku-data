10th month — Kōsuke

Memorandum

One ashigaru (foot soldier)

The above: as the honorable taru-ki (barrel timber/lumber) has arrived at the crossing point, [I] shall conduct an inspection (machi) at the Hour of the Cock (approx. 2 p.m.) on the 2nd day of the morrow. Accordingly, I humbly request that a mess attendant (meshita-yaku) be assigned [to receive him]. Respectfully submitted.
　　1st day of the 10th month

Memorandum

One chūgen (manservant/attendant)

The above: as [this person] has been sent over for the purpose of conducting an inspection, I humbly request that [I] be permitted to receive the loaned person (kashinin) at the Hour of the Cock on the 2nd day of the morrow. Respectfully submitted.
　　1st day of the 10th month
