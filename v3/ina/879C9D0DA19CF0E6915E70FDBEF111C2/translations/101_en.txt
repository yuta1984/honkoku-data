Item: The petition (negaisho) of Matashirō, dye-house worker of Akaha village, has been returned via Kumezaemon. In a previous year, when Kamisaemon was serving in the Bunichi-kata [the official revenue office], at the time when the established procedures (sadamehō) for various matters were being enquired into, it was found that only the affairs of the dye-house were in question, and so an inquiry was made to the ■ office. [The rule states that] from the time of death by illness in the 3rd month through the 7th month, an exemption of eight-tenths (hachi-bu no menjo) is granted, and from the 8th month onward, the collection of operating levies (un'jō) is the established rule. Accordingly, the expression of thanks (rei) has not yet been settled. [It has been directed] that, even within our own official duties, keeping this matter in mind, henceforth [all such things] are to be handled according to proper procedure (jippō). The petition in question has accordingly been returned, as Kyūbei submitted it through the east [office/route] ■, and instructions were sent to convey the above-mentioned points.
