(Continuing from previous text) From now on, during the rotation of monthly duty (tsukiban), one is expected to remain at one's lodgings (in residence). If there is an unavoidable reason to go out, one must notify the lodging in advance before proceeding to one's destination, so that official orders and instructions from the duty officer (go-yōban) may be properly conveyed.

【A circular ink mark appears above the entry for the 12th month】

29th Day of the 12th Month

Having been summoned by Lord Jūzō, the following was duly announced:

　　　　　　Staff of the Bun'ichi-kata (Lower Officials)

Item: Two hundred mon in coin (toriме) — to Jiue Kyūbei
The said individual has consistently and reliably fulfilled his duties; having continued his service since the 4th month of this year, this sum is hereby granted.

　　　　　　Great Hall (Ōbeya)
Item: Five hundred mon each — to two chūgen (manservants)
The said individuals have diligently served day and night during the large net fishing operations (ōami-uchi), and this sum is hereby granted.

The above amounts are to be conveyed as irregular employment wages (fují yatoichin). That is all.
　　29th Day of the 12th Month, Year of the Tiger (Tora)
　　Bun'ichi-kata

---

Same Day

Item: The aforementioned written order was received from Lord Jūzō; the recipients were duly summoned, informed of the matter, and the coin (torime) was handed over. Kyūemon reported that notices of gratitude were circulated to the Chief Elders (go-karō-chū), the Senior Councilors (go-toshiyori-chū), and the Officials (go-yōnin-chū). Kyūbei likewise made his rounds to offer his thanks.
