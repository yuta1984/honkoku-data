In accordance with the above petition, an authenticated and sealed document (okugaki inshō) has been prepared and sent to Tōhachi-dono.

20th day
Item: Today, regarding official business, a courier (hikyaku) was dispatched to Iijima Kaiden, and the letter sent out is as noted to the left.

20th day
Item: Having been summoned to Jōzaemon-dono past the Hour of the Rooster [around 2–3 p.m.], I duly attended. Regarding the matter ordered on the 17th — namely, the courier documents to be sent to Iijima and Kaishima at three locations — after consulting with the Deputy Intendant's office (gundai-shū), it was ordered that even for Iijima, the three deputy officials (tedai) should understand the arrangement regarding the bestowal of clothing (okimono). As for the hand-barrel (tezaru) [of sake], it was communicated that delivery should be postponed by two or three days once it is ready. Regarding today's matter of the honorable high tax collection (o-takō nō), I immediately called upon Mōuemon-dono and discussed the details at length. It was discussed that the matter be finalized by the 24th, or at the latest by the 25th.

Item: Henceforth, it was ordered by Jōzaemon-dono that the three deputy officials (tedai) of Iijima shall be dispatched to the three tax collection offices (go-san-nō). Accordingly, a memorandum for the formal duty day (jōjitsu) of the 23rd was drawn up and returned.

Item: Village elder (shōya) Jinpei's illness has not improved. In connection with the inspection of indigo vats (aigame aratame) throughout the domain, which is to take place shortly this year, a request has been submitted once again that his son (segare) Shige — who also wished to be sent out last year — be dispatched in his place.
