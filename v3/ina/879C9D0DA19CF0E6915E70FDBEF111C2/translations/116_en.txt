As per the above dates, merchants and those who have petitioned for [the use of] the river (kawa-negai hōshi) are requested to bring their various tax payments (shō-unjō kin-sen) together with their seals (in-gyō), and to present themselves at Kōsaku's residence between the Hour of the Ox [approximately 10:00 a.m.] and the Hour of the Rooster [approximately 2:00 p.m.] (■■■). We humbly request that [you] be pleased to issue the appropriate orders. That is all.
