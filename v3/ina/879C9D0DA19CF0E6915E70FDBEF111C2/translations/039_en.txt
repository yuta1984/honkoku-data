　　Transport tax per load (da): 450 mon
One item: Fine Yukishiro cloth [reading uncertain]
　　Same as above: 350 mon

　　4th month, 2nd day　　Kōsaku

Same month, 3rd day
One item: As there was official business, I went to the anteroom (o-tsugi), whereupon a verbal message arrived from Heijūemon, and I immediately went out. The matter communicated to Jōzaemon was as follows: Regarding the seven oak trees (kashi) located in the lacquer grove at Sainokami-saka, which our colleague had previously reported upon — as four of those trees had been identified as poor-quality timber [and a proposal had been made] to dispose of them — he was now asked to confirm whether the number of trees to be disposed of was indeed four. Upon consulting with our colleague on the various particulars of the matter, [we confirmed that] the number was indeed four trees.
