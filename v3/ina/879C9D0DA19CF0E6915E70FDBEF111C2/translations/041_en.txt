10th Day
One item: Today being an official business day, I went to the castle to report on matters.
　Jōzaemon-dono informed me that: Regarding the petition submitted last month by the three Ōmiya [merchants] for a contract license (請負運上) on hemp fiber and hemp cloth,
　the petition has been returned (i.e., not accepted at this time). This year, they should hold off, and either submit a new petition at the end of this year,
　or submit it again at a later date, as instructed. The instruction was that they should submit their petition in accordance with the progress of this year's hemp crop.
　Accordingly, Ōmiya was summoned and notified of this decision.

Same day
One item: Regarding the accounts for the official loan money (御貸付金) from last year, there was a slight discrepancy,
　so the ledger was returned to Jōzaemon-sama.
　There are attachment slips (付紙) in the ledger. I reported that I would consult with my colleague of the same role, correct the accounts, and submit it again in due course.
