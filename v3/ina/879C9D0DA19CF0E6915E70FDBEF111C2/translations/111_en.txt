　　　Eleventh Month, 10th Day — Kyūemon

10th Day
Item: Concerning the rest days when the honorable net-drivers (o-amiuchi) had finished their work — Lords Rokuemon and Jōzaemon stated that, regarding the large communal room [workers] (ōbeya), if three days of rest were granted, [the workers] should take back [their] morning and evening provisions [from storage]. Accordingly, as had been the practice previously, [they] wished to arrange that rest days be given in the manner customary for the less-well-off (hin-kata) [workers]; this was duly reported. [However, it was noted that] if [workers] were to take back three days' worth of provisions and supplies, this would cause hardship. [Therefore,] in accordance with the prior precedent noted below, it was settled that rest days be arranged within the jurisdiction of the Bun'ichi-kata [office], as requested through Kyūzaemon, and the matter was duly resolved in accordance with the said prior precedent.
