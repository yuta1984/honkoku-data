**[Right Folio]**

1. A letter from Jōzaemon-dono, as follows:

"At this time, [we] have heard that fish [runs/season] are favorable. Accordingly, [we] have received the net-casters (tsunauchi) and wish to promptly dispatch one [of them]. As Kuzaemon is away on a distant errand today, [I] submit this communication. The above is submitted.
　　9th Month, 11th Day　　Kamiya Jōzaemon
　　　Akaba Heisaku-dono"

The above has been communicated and sent to colleagues of the same post. Accordingly, both net-casters (otsunauchi) have been received. [We] inquired as to whether Jōzaemon-dono might come over, and reported that two persons each should be dispatched to the third [watch/station]. [The instructions] were conveyed in the manner described below, and dispatch has begun from today.

---

　　　**Memorandum (Oboegaki)**

　Red rain-capes (aka-gappa)　— two items

The above [two items] have been drawn out from the two net-casters.
Drawn out from both persons.
[These] should be issued upon instruction from the senior authority (ōmoto).　The above is submitted.
　　9th Month, 12th Day　　　Kyūemon

**14th Day**

1. On an official duty day, [the following was] reported to Jōzaemon-dono, as follows:

1. Regarding dispatching the official messenger (ohikyaku) for Iijima and Kaishima as per custom in previous years — [we] inquired about dispatching [the messenger] on the 23rd of next month. Regarding official goods, purchases, firewood, the official attendant ■, [we] presented [these matters] for [Jōzaemon-dono's] inspection; moreover, regarding the arrangement of sake [offering/serving] ■, there was no approval or [formal] permission granted. In that case, [the response] was that all matters should be ready for dispatch around the 20th of next month. [It was conveyed that] in recent years, Nakajitsu Oshida Mohei, and in place of the Motojime-tedai (chief clerk) Kojima Jitsudayū, Sakata Tomozaemon-dono — if that is the case, [the matter] was to be entrusted to Matsudaya Rokuzaemon, and [we] have received and noted this [instruction].
