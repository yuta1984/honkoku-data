[Petition]

I humbly beg your indulgence, and though I am deeply ashamed to impose upon you with yet another repeated petition, I respectfully submit the following regarding my land (jisho) in Ōmachi:

On the 12th day of the last month of the previous year, I received a directive stating that a notification would come from the land of Maebayashi Hanji in Edo. Wishing to travel there in the spring to negotiate directly with the said Hanji, I humbly requested a letter of introduction (soejō) from your office. On the 12th day of the 5th month, I received word that orders had been given for Katoji to be reasoned with and persuaded, for which I was most grateful. However, as the year has now nearly come to its end, I humbly and repeatedly implore you to once again counsel and persuade Katoji to promptly return the land to me.

Furthermore, should there be a representative (dainin) of the aforementioned Hanji present in this area who makes various claims, I wish to confront that person directly, and humbly request that this matter be heard before the magistrate's court (oshirasu).

I most earnestly and sincerely beseech you to arrange for the return of the said land.
