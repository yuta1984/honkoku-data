**Memorandum of Oral Statement (Kōjō no Oboegaki)**

On this occasion, as Miyashita Heibei has submitted a petition regarding my household, I hereby humbly set forth my own understanding of the matter as follows.

Item: Regarding my household — during the Genroku era (1688–1704), an official inspection was conducted, at which time [our house was registered under] Fujinoya Kyūemon; during the Shōtoku era (1711–1716), another official inspection was conducted, similarly, and we have continued in unbroken succession ever since. It should be noted that there are also ancestral Buddhist items (*butsu*) predating the Genroku era; however, it is my understanding that under the previous lord's administration, a ruling was issued that such items would not be taken into consideration.
