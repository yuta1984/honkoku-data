Item: In the 10th year of An'ei (1781), a dispute arose between Heibei's household and my own, which proceeded to litigation. At that time, a detailed written judgment (*saikyosho*) was issued to both parties, which I gratefully received and continue to hold in my possession. Furthermore, in the town official's duty ledger (*chōdai yakuyō hikaechō*) from the An'ei era (1772–1781), the names Itō Soemon and Endō Kyūemon are recorded. In continued succession under the Endō name, my branch household (*bekke*) — Fujinoya Shōbei of Sericho — has been using the Endō name. Therefore, while Minatoya Heibei's household may have an old kinship connection [with the main Endō line], there is no such relationship with [my] branch household.

Item: I requested that Mankōji temple provide mediation (*gorikai*), and while they did engage in discussion, there was no formal mediation as such.

Item: Regarding the matter that Heibei has petitioned about — when members of my household have passed away, [Heibei] had been entrusted with [the grave site] on the condition that [my family's remains] would not be interred in the grave site that I have long maintained and that they would not be allowed to use it. Accordingly, I am submitting a separate document describing the particulars of the grave site.

The foregoing is hereby respectfully submitted as my oral statement in written form.

　　9th month, Year of the Rabbit　　　　　　　Fujinoya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kyūemon

The above was submitted in the form of a horizontal ledger (*yokochōmen*).
