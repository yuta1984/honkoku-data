　Entry No. 7　　　　　　　　　Sanage Daimyōjin
　Item: Sake brewing quota — 50 koku　　　Sacred sake levy
　　　(Including base rice, supplementary rice, and malt)　　Shimizu Tōdayū
　Entry No. 8
　Item: Same as above　　　　　　　　　　Same as above

The above two brewing licenses have been transferred to us on this occasion from Shimizu Tōdayū, a retainer of Lord Miyake Higo. Accordingly, we wish to conduct sake brewing and commerce at our own residence in Takasago-machi, and therefore respectfully submit this petition.

We humbly pray that, in your benevolence, you will grant our petition as requested, for which we would be most grateful. Respectfully submitted. Takasago-machi.

　Keio 4, Year of Tsuchinoe-Tatsu (1868), Fifth Month　　　Petitioner: Keijirō

　　To the Magistrate's Office

[Memorandum:] As noted above, the petition was submitted to Mr. Yazawa at the castle gate on the 3rd day of the month. It was returned by the Magistrate and brought back. Furthermore, on the 6th day, it was submitted again through Mr. Mitsuhashi at the residence of Lord Kawaike, whereupon an order was issued to rewrite and resubmit the petition. The town representative was summoned and informed of this instruction.
