[A Petition Humbly Submitted in Written Form]

On the tenth day of last October, my father Monjirō, who had been practicing the trade of blacksmithing, passed away. Therefore, we wish to return his official trade license (鑑札) to the authorities. We hereby respectfully submit this petition.

Regarding the above matter, should you mercifully grant our request as stated, we would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　　　　　　　Umemachi, Komatsуya
