**[First Petition]**

Humbly submitted:

Seibei, a member of our merchant cooperative, has been engaged in the sale of *yōshu* (willow wine / a type of liquor). However, having received the honorable order to vacate the town district, we humbly submit this request. Accordingly, we respectfully petition to return the merchant license (御鑑札) to the authorities.

We humbly present the above.　　　　　　　　　Yokochō
　　8th Month, Keio 3, Year of the Rabbit　　　Petitioner: Yoshiya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Group Head (kumigashira): Den'emon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Merchant License Inspector (shōsatsu metsuke):
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Jinemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ikegami Kan'emon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative (machidai): Heijūrō
To: The Honorable Magistrate's Office
