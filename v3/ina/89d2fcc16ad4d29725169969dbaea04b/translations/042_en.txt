Petitioned to Shimo-Ōshima Village

Item: 1 koku, 7 to, 8 shō of rice

　　　　　　　　　　　From the 1st day of the 8th month
　　　　　　　　　　　to the 29th day of the 1st month of the Year of the Dragon
　　　　　　　　　　　combining both long and short months:
　　　　　　　　　　　a total of 178 days

The above represents the rice stipend (fuchīmai) allocated for the horse-handler (umazashi) of this town. It has been duly delivered from the Kawashimo-gō district using the rice tax collected in this Year of the Rabbit, and we hereby confirm receipt. In witness whereof, this document is submitted accordingly.
