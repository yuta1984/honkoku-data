　　Eighth month, Year of the Rabbit [1867]　　Town
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sake brewers, collectively

On the 20th day of the eighth month, this was submitted through the intermediary of Mr. Takahashi at the magistrate's office.
Upon inquiring of the sake brewers, they informed us that the sake currently being brewed is produced at a cost based on the exchange rate of one tō and three shō [of rice per ryō], and therefore they wished to sell it at the above price. We hereby report this matter. The current market rate [for rice] ranges from one tō eight shō to even two tō per ryō, and we report this point as well.

On the 20th day of the eighth month, an official directive was issued that new sake should be sold from the 2nd day of the ninth month at a price of 906 mon per shō. Accordingly, on the 1st day of the ninth month, the sake brewers' representative (gyōji) was summoned to the wholesaler's office and informed of this decision.
