"Since this money is irregular/illicit funds, there are no remaining amounts owed on our side," they argued back and forth. Regarding Tanizaemon of Misono Village, he stated he had absolutely no recollection of borrowing from Mago-jūrō, saying: "It is possible that Kanbei of our group borrowed it, and I do recall having sent payments such as kajishi (land rent) up until now. I have no recollection of receiving any financial assistance (naisuke-kin) from the other party, and it is for such reasons as these," as he argued his case. Regarding Matsuzō, the village officials stated that he had run away from home and has had no involvement in this matter since then. Regarding Hanzaemon, he had died by drowning, his household was now headed by a widow, and there was no means for repayment; it was said that this situation had been reported to Mago-jūrō as well. The two men, Genzaemon and Yōzō, stated: "While there is no doubt about the said borrowing, since this money was irregular/illicit funds, we received punishment from the authorities and were imprisoned. Moreover, [they argued] that Mago-jūrō was also an associate at the time." After various arguments back and forth, the two persons from Mikoshiba Village said: "Even if we continue to argue amongst ourselves, nothing will be resolved. The matter shall be settled at the御白洲 (public tribunal)," and with that, the meeting at the town assembly hall broke up for the night. A rough summary of the parties involved was put into writing, and the following day, the 12th, at the government office, the matter was reported to Miwake Gonzaburōhyō(e), and it was further petitioned, in accordance with Mikoshiba Village's request, that "since the dispute remains unresolved, we request that an official inquiry be conducted." In response, [the official replied regarding] "the negotiations that took place last night..."
