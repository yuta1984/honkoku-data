As there was a notification that both parties had reached a private settlement as described above, this matter was duly reported to Lord Akabane Jūrō.

One item: As the two persons from Nekoshiba Village had come forward to request a private settlement, and as the magistrate was also to be absent, they submitted a petition requesting that they be allowed to return to their lodging quarters in the meantime. Accordingly, the written pledge below was obtained from them, and they were quietly instructed to return to their village.

---

　　　Written Pledge

Regarding the matter of the loan made by Magojiūrō of our village, we came before this honorable magistrate's office to present our petition and caused considerable inconvenience. However, as an urgent private matter was communicated to us from our lodging quarters, we humbly requested permission for a private settlement. We are most grateful that we have been permitted to return on the basis of such a settlement. With that said, we pledge without fail to appear before this office within the two days of the 21st and 22nd of the coming month. This written pledge is hereby submitted for the record, and is accordingly executed.

　　　　　　　　　　Nekoshiba Village
　　　　　　　　　　　Hachirozaemon
