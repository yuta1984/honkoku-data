Regarding the above: As Toranosuke of Odō-Sogai Village, within the domain of Lord Naitō Yamato-no-Kami, in Fujisawa-gō, had failed to repay a loan, we this time obtained a letter of endorsement (添翰) from the magistrate's office under the jurisdiction of Lord Matsudaira, and submitted a formal petition to the Takatō magistrate's office. By the gracious benevolence of the authorities, the matter was referred to the town officials (町請役人), and upon negotiation, through your kind mediation and guidance to both parties, a private settlement was agreed upon.
