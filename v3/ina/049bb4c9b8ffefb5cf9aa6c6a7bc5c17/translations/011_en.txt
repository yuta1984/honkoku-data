Regarding the matter of the negotiations [of the previous night] and the renewed petition, we were instructed to ensure that both would be forwarded as petitions accompanied by a letter of introduction (soekkan). Accordingly, on the following day, the 13th, we accompanied the inn/post-station representative, summoned the two persons from Mikoshiba Village, and conveyed these instructions to them.

Same day, the 13th

Item: Regarding the matter of Toranosuke of Odō-Sogai Village, upon thorough investigation, it was found that Mago-jūrō held received payments and other sundry goods related to commerce from Toranosuke's side, and as a result, the parties reached an amicable private settlement. Notification was received from both parties. The following is the written agreement they exchanged. The private settlement (naizai) has been concluded.

　　Written Agreement for Private Settlement (Naizai Shōmon)

Item: Ten ryō and two bu

The above sum represents a loan made by Mago-jūrō of Mikoshiba Village, within the territory administered by Lord Matsudaira Tanba-no-kami, to Toranosuke of Odō-Sogai Village in Fujisawa-gō, within the domain of Lord Naitō Yamato-no-kami, for which repayment had not been made. A petition for litigation was submitted to the Takatō Government Office, accompanied by a letter of introduction obtained from the Dōen (Salt) Office. By the grace [of the authorities], the matter was referred to the town officials, and after negotiations, with the mediation of representatives from both inns/post-stations, it was investigated and found that received payments and sundry commercial goods could be applied toward the settlement of the said account. Both parties received guidance to this effect, and upon mutual understanding and agreement, a private settlement and amicable resolution were reached. Therefore, henceforth, there shall be absolutely no further objections or claims regarding this matter. Given this resolution, no litigious actions shall be taken in the future regarding this matter.
