[BEGINNING OF DOCUMENT MISSING]

It became dark while I was at Tono-Sogai, so I borrowed a lantern from a sake shop and arrived safely at Echigoya in Omidō-Sogai. Please be at ease.

Item: On the occasion of Daisan's departure, please send two sheets of nakaoré (folded paper) as farewell gifts.

Item: When you visit Mr. Takizawa, please inquire about the Imamura courier route and the Nakayama courier route, and make arrangements accordingly.

Item: The other day, one packhorse load arrived from Sakashita, which contained seven or eight bottles. With that, one batch of brewing — and for the remaining amount, it would be difficult to get fifteen or sixteen, let alone twenty bottles from Tsuraya. Although they say one batch yields three koku, please tell them that such an amount is quite impossible. Consider how to decline Sakashita, and for now, it would be best to proceed as with the previous shipment.

Item: Regarding the document/notice around the date marked with 〇 [unclear passage] — there should also be a gag order (kuchi-dome). Please consider the other matters as well.

Item: Above all, please be mindful of fire safety.

Item: My stomach is in good condition, I have no diarrhea, and I am feeling well, so please have no worries on that account either.

Item: Please repeatedly convey to my mother that she need not worry.

Item: I have been walking in wooden clogs (geta) since yesterday morning, and came all the way here in geta. Last night I was utterly exhausted. I am grateful for today's weather, which allowed me to cross the mountain pass. I will write about other matters in a future letter. In haste, that is all.


　　　March 19th
　　　　　　　　Omidō-Sogai
　　　　　　　　　Echigoya
　　　　　　　　　　　Honuemon

　　　To: Kihei
