[Continued from previous page] ...to Shōuemon. Last year, in the sixth month of the year of the Snake, Kyūzō and Shōuemon came to negotiate, but due to continuing economic hardship with no profit from sake brewing, they requested an extension of the repayment period. However, no agreement was reached, and though negotiations continued repeatedly into the current year, no settlement was achieved. The matter was brought to a formal lawsuit, which we find deeply regrettable — so they stated.

Having heard the above complaint and response, there were discrepancies between the statements of both parties. Rather than pursuing each and every point through argument and dispute, the mediators took the matter into their hands, and the terms of repayment are recorded as follows:
