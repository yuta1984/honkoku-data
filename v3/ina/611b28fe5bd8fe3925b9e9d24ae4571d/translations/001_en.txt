**Letter of Settlement (Saiguchi Issatsu)**

Item: I (Tomiya Jirozaemon) have been continuing the household succession (atoshiki) of Takichi up to the present time. However, due to the foster mother's poor physical and mental condition, I submitted a petition to the official authorities requesting a separation (rien). After the circumstances were investigated in detail, the officials, considering it pitiable that the future continuation of Takichi's household succession would be uncertain should a separation occur, explained the practical consequences to all parties and admonished us to reach a mutual understanding. They also arranged for the foster mother to remain in the household as before, with all necessities provided without inconvenience. We humbly and gratefully accept these arrangements. In accordance with the above, and following the foster mother's consent, all matters have been handed over. Henceforth, I intend to care for her with great filial devotion, hold dear the continuation of the household, and shall maintain a harmonious relationship with all parties. Therefore, this letter of settlement is hereby submitted as stated above.

　　　　　　　　　　Tomiya
