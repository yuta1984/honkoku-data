A Humble Petition Submitted by Way of Written Statement

Since the death of my husband Hansuke on the fourteenth day of the twelfth month of the past year of I (亥), the matter of designating an heir has yet to be settled to this day. The circumstances are as follows: as Hansuke's younger brother Magonzō had expressed certain views while Hansuke was still alive, it has been my intention to have my nephew Wakichi succeed to the household. I myself had indeed heard of the views that were expressed during Hansuke's lifetime. In that year of I, Magonzō told Hansuke that "it would be proper to take in an adopted son, and the nephew Wakichi would seem suitable." To this, Hansuke replied that "being still inexperienced, it is by no means certain he could manage the succession; nevertheless, let us first observe how he fares in the trade and provisioning of the craft, and then decide whether to formally adopt him," and so Wakichi was taken in on that basis. However, thereafter Hansuke repeatedly expressed the view that "it is by no means certain that Wakichi could manage the succession." Given such circumstances, and as this does not accord with my own intentions either, I have absolutely no intention whatsoever of making said Wakichi my adopted son going forward. On this occasion, Shōgorō has come here from the household of [my] younger brother [Magonzō], and I have thoroughly explained to him the purport of my intentions; furthermore, the relatives have gathered together and discussed the matter on several occasions, yet despite all deliberation, no conclusion has been reached.
