　　　Statement of Acknowledgment

Item: Four ryō in gold

The above sum is hereby acknowledged as a gracious loan. Previously, the field at Nishikakigai, which had been deposited as collateral for a former gracious loan (valued at twenty-five ryō, and redeemed accordingly), was humbly requested back. From the proceeds thereof, the aforementioned sum of money has been graciously borrowed by me as working capital for my trade, and this is without any doubt. Therefore, from this day forward, I shall devote myself diligently to continuing my business, and upon doing so, I shall repay the said sum of money. As this loan has been extended to me through your kind arrangement for the purpose of sustaining my livelihood in the gold trade, I shall not forget this matter for a single moment, and shall devote myself day and night to making repayment. This borrowing certificate is hereby issued as proof for future reference, and is as stated above.
