## Deed of Property Transfer

I hereby transfer the residence in my possession, located on the western side of Hokoji-machi, with a frontage of two and a half ken and a depth of twenty-one ken, to your party. This is an absolute certainty. I confirm that I have duly received thirty ryō as the consideration (礼金) for this transfer.

From this point forward, you shall fulfill all the town's obligatory duties and services. Regarding this property, neither relatives nor any other parties will raise any objections whatsoever. Should anyone bring a complaint, the guarantor will appear wherever necessary to resolve the matter promptly, and we assure you that no inconvenience will be caused to your group. This transfer deed is hereby written as proof for future reference. As stated above.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　Hokoji-machi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kanaya
Tenpō 13th year, Mizunoe-Tora　　Transferor: Kichibei
　　　　　　2nd Month　　　　　　Fujisawaya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Guarantor: Chōbei

　　To the Mutual Financing Association (無尽) of Kiinokuya Kanbei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Fellow Members

The foregoing is correct without any discrepancy.　　　Town Representative (町代): Sōhachi

Please entrust this property to us from the spring of the coming Year of the Rabbit. The annual rent shall be three ryō, three bu, two shu in gold, and one momme five fun in silver. We will continue to pay this faithfully until the mutual financing association (無尽) concludes. Should the principal party fall into arrears, the guarantor will take responsibility and continue payments. Written as proof for future reference.
