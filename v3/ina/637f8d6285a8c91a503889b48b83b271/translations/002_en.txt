## Memorandum (29th Day of the 2nd Month)

Following discussions held in the 2nd month of the Year of the Tiger, regarding the admission to the "Sangen-kai" (a three-*ken* mutual financing association, 無尽) in the coming Year of the Rabbit: the remaining sum of fifteen *ryō* was received this spring. Concerning this matter, a written acknowledgment (一札) confirming receipt of the fifteen *ryō* was received from the intermediary (*sewanin*) Kisoया Tokubei.
