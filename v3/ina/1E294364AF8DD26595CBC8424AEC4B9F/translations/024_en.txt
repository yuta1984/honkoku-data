12th Day

Item: Regarding the river transport duties of Nakayama Danroku, an official went out on duty to receive the materials necessary for the construction of the meeting hall (kaisho) at Jingūgawa in Nakazawa-gō.

Item: A person named Gentarō from Tojima Village submitted a request to temporarily reside with Gotō Seichi of the gō-gumi (village group) in order to receive instruction in bowl lacquerware work. The matter was duly recorded and noted.

12th Day

Item: A request was submitted asking that Kyūbei, a woodturner (kijishi) residing in Takami Village, be officially authorized to sell woodturned goods. He was called in and notified by the subordinate officials.

Regarding Okano Jōuemon: As he is of advanced age and has also become hard of hearing, he submitted a petition requesting to be relieved of both his official duties. However, as there is currently a shortage of personnel, an order was issued to retain him in his post. The monthly duty official conveyed that this matter should be communicated to the supervising authority. Please extend this notification to your fellow officials and colleagues. That is all.
