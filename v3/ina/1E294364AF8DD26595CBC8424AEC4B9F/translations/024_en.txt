**12th Day**

One entry: Nakayama Danroku went out on official duty regarding the transportation boat service (tsūsen-kata), specifically to oversee the receipt of materials needed for construction/repair works at the meeting hall (kaisho) at Jingūgawa in Nakazawa-gō.

One entry: Gentarō of Toshimamura requested to temporarily reside with Gotō Seichi of the gō-gumi (village/district group) in order to receive instruction in lacquerware bowl finishing (wan-nurimono). The matter was duly recorded and reported.

**12th Day** (continued)

One entry: A wood-turning craftsman (kijishi) named Kyūbei, residing in Takami village, submitted a petition requesting that he be permitted to purchase raw turned-wood stock (kiji). He was summoned and the matter was communicated to him through the subordinate officials.

Okano Jōuemon, being of advanced age and also hard of hearing, submitted a petition requesting to be relieved of his two official posts. However, as there is currently a shortage of personnel, an order was issued to retain him in his positions. The monthly duty officer (tsukiban) instructed that this matter be communicated to the supervising authorities (go-shihai-kata). Please also convey this notice to your fellow officials and colleagues. This notice is hereby conveyed. Respectfully.
