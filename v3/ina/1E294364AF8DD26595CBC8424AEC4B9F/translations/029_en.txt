**1st Day (Tsuitachi)**

One entry: Today, Denzō of Ōkubo Village was summoned, and upon affixing his seal to a transit pass (tōritekata) at the Mishima Guard Station, a letter addressed in the name of Sukehachi, bearing the name of the addressee, was sent along to Chōji and Sanzaburō of Matsushima Village.

One entry: Regarding the area in Rokudōhara where paulownia (kiri) trees have been planted — it had been previously approved to erect notice boards (kōsatsu) at approximately five locations. Seven such boards were received from the Construction Office (go-fushinkata), and they were inscribed and erected as follows:

　　　Anyone found breaking off or taking branches from these paulownia trees
　　　shall be subject to severe punishment.
　　　　　　Fourth Month, Year of the Sheep [1859]

---

**6th Day**

One entry: Concerning the soil found on the land bordering the plot held by Naozaemon of Minuma Village — this time, Gentarō of Shimotonosejima Village submitted a petition requesting that this soil be reserved exclusively for his use as a base powder (ji-no-ko) in lacquerware production. A petition was filed [by the relevant parties], and after the matter was duly reported and approved, an official directive was issued to the rural administration (gōkata). Specifically, the directive was sent from the administrative office to Katsuzō of Imamura, who in turn conveyed it to the said village. As it was desired to erect a notice board in connection with this matter, the relevant supervisory officers (o-torishimari-chū) were duly notified, and the board was erected as follows:

　　　No person shall carelessly dig up and take away
　　　the soil of this place.
