**Fourth Month, Year of the Sheep [Ansei 6, 1859]**

One entry: The roof of the Kamaya (cauldron house/brewing building) had evidently been deteriorating over the years, and it was reported by Kitahara Tōshichi, the sake brewery attendant (sakaya-zume), that it had collapsed around the Hour of the Rooster [approximately 2:00 p.m.] on the fifth day of this month. He also reported that although he ought to have informed us yesterday, the matter was delayed due to some confusion in the hearing process. The supervisory officers (o-torishimari-chū) also came down and together inspected the damaged areas. As repairs and construction work are to commence immediately, an order was issued to survey and compile a list of the necessary timber and materials.

**8th Day**

One entry: As the lord is in a period of mourning (kichū), an official directive (ofuregaki) was issued stating that the fire watch duty at the Momijiyama Mausoleum Shrine (go-tamaya) is hereby suspended. A copy of this directive has been recorded in the register of official notices, but is omitted here for brevity.

One entry: The subordinate official (shitayaku) Itō Sukehachi submitted notification of a change of his personal seal (ingyō-gae). The matter was duly reported, as follows:

　　　　　　　　　Subordinate Official
　　[Seal]　　　　Itō Sukehachi

The above-mentioned change of seal has been duly reported and notified.
Respectfully submitted.
