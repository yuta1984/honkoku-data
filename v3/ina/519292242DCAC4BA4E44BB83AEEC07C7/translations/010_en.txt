Item: One hoe (kuwa)
Item: One sickle (kama)
Item: One storage bell (monooki-gane)
Item: One rain cape (kappa)
Item: One firefighting/duty haori jacket (hikaji-haori)
Item: One almanac/calendar record (saireki-guchi), one volume

The above items were duly received; [regarding] the hoe, rusted sword, and the stored [item ■], a receipt (ukesho) was arranged through Shichirōemon's quarters, and [the items] were handed over to Toshihiko.
