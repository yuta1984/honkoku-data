(Continued from previous page) Accompanying one another, Kondō Rihei, Kyūemon, Jihei, and Hineno Bunzō stated: "Today, Rihei has been appointed to the position of lower official (shita-yaku). On account of this, though we are sending him along, he is an inexperienced/unskilled person [remainder damaged], and we humbly ask for your indulgence." This message was received.
Thereupon, both Kyūemon and Shichibei replied formally (kōtō), and the matter was conveyed back to Heisaku.

Item: From Lord Kawashiro Kahei, the squad leader (kogashira) Ikegami Jihei came forth and stated: "Regarding Daijō Kyūbei, the handover has been duly carried out, and (he) has been received with certainty [remainder damaged]; the matter of taking full charge (sōtori) is hereby conveyed."

Item: In connection with the above, both persons made the rounds to the senior councilors (o-karō-chū), the elders (o-toshiyori-chū), and the officials (o-yōnin-chū), and reported that the lower official appointment had been promptly issued, and that they were grateful to render their service (hōeki).
