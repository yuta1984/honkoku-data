28th Day
One item: Today, as Shichirōemon-dono informed us that there was official business and requested our attendance, we duly presented ourselves, whereupon the following was conveyed to us in writing:

　　　　　　　　Large Room (Ōbeya)
One item: 500 mon in coin each — two intermediate servants (chūgen)
　The above is granted in recognition of their dedicated and diligent service
　in net-casting duties.
　The above constitutes an extraordinary reward
　which is to be bestowed; please make the announcement accordingly. Respectfully submitted.
　　12th Month, 28th Day

　　To those of the Buichi-kata

4th Day
One item: Tsunahei and Kōrōemon were both summoned,
　the contents of the written notice were conveyed to them, and 500 mon
　in coin each was distributed.
Following this, both men made their rounds to offer their thanks.
