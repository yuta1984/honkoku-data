　2nd month, 6th day

7th day

Item: At around half past the sixth hour (approximately 7:00 a.m.), I proceeded to Shichirōemon's quarters in my capacity as the monthly duty officer (tsukiban) from the 7th day onward, and received the formal written petition/inquiry document (origaki-ukagai).

Item: On the same day, at the fifth hour (approximately 8:00 a.m.), I instructed Toshihiko regarding the formal written petition, and with three co-signatories (sangō): the Junior Page Inspector (o-chū-koshō metsuke), Sazawa Kazuemon, the Senior Steward Rotation Duty Officer (o-yōnin-chū-iribanyaku), Sazawa Tōuemon, along with their colleagues, gathered at my private residence, and the formal written petition was duly appended/submitted.

Item: Immediately thereafter, I proceeded to Gyōda Shichirōemon's quarters, presented the formal role petition (o-yaku origaki-ukagai) on behalf of Kondō Toshihiko, and duly submitted the notification. After this, when I brought the aforesaid formal petition to present it, I received a gracious reply indicating that it should be submitted [as is], and returned home.

Item: Regarding the completion of Toshihiko's formal written petition duties, I coordinated with Kazuemon regarding the notification slips (o-fuda), and instructed Rihei to make the rounds — ensuring that [the notifications] were circulated without exception to all, including the Senior Councillors (o-karō-jū), the general retainers (およそな衆), the Elder Officials (o-toshiyori-jū) likewise, and all of the Senior Stewards (o-yōnin-jū) — in response to [their] written correspondence requests.
