　　To: Nezu Ryūzaemon-dono

The above as written is confirmed without discrepancy. Respectfully submitted.

Regarding the above guarantor (ukenin): as Kyūbei was relieved of his duties (oyaku-gomen) on the 5th day of the 2nd month, from the 2nd month onward, ■■moto, Suekichi took over the writing and recording in his place, and the matter proceeded directly (jikkō).

　　　Memorandum

　　One o-chūgen (attendant/manservant)

The above is requested for official duties attending to various sick persons (byōnin); it is hoped that this can be arranged around the hour of Five (approximately 8 o'clock in the morning) on the 7th day of next month.

　5th day of the 2nd month　　Shichizaemon

---

　　　Memorandum

　　Charcoal — 3 small straw bales (kotatami)

The above is to be received for official use in the branding (yakiin) of various tax/transport certificates (unjōfuda). Respectfully submitted.

On behalf of the same village

　5th day of the 2nd month　　Shichizaemon

　5th day of the 2nd month

Item: Today, having gone out at the hour of Four (approximately 10 o'clock in the morning) for official business, there was a pressing matter that arose, and I returned home.

Item: On the same day, around midday, a messenger arrived from Shichirōzaemon-dono on behalf of the office (onshoku), with word that "I am presently out." I set out accordingly, and what follows is as recorded below.
