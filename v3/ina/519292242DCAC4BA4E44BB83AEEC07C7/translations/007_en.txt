【Left folio】

The transport fee (un) of 4 monme having been accepted, the written notice regarding the dismissal of Ikegami Kyūbei from his post as a lower official (shita-yaku) of the bunichi-kata (分一方) office is as follows.

---

　　　Bunichi-kata Office
　　　　　Ikegami Kyūbei

Regarding the above: as this official post is being reassigned, Kawachi Kahei, who holds a stipend of 5 koku (classified as "no-land stipend," mudaka), is hereby appointed in this capacity.
The vacancy is to be filled and the appointment made to the second position accordingly.
In accordance with the above, please make the arrangements and transfer (the duties and responsibilities) to Kahei. Respectfully submitted.

　5th day of the 2nd month
　Bunichi-kata (Office seal/stamp)
　　　From the kumametsuke (inspector) of Okudaira Seiemon's unit:
　　　　　　　　　　Chikagō Toshihiko

---

Lower official of the Bunichi-kata:
Stipend: 5 koku 6 to
　　　Two-person ration (futari-buchi)
　　　Of which, 3 to is designated as "御名米" (on-nammai, i.e., an honorary/named rice allotment) from this time forward.

The above: from this time forward, (this person) is appointed as a lower official (shita-yaku) of the danna-kata (旦方) office.
As the transfer will be made from the unit commanders (monogashira-jū), please receive (the person) accordingly. Respectfully submitted.

　　5th day of the 2nd month
　　Bunichi-kata (Office)
