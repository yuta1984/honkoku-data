　　　Statement of Pledge Hereby Submitted

Item: Within the domain's reserved mountain (on-tateyama) of Kurokochi in Irinoya-gō, [the area] from Gakigakuni to Taki-no-Izumi, along the Monodaira road/passage.

Regarding the said mountain, we are most gratefully informed that our petition to conduct board-sawing (hikiita) has been granted. We will not engage in any unauthorized activities beyond the said board-sawing. In accordance with the regulations (o-jōmoku) that have been issued to us on this occasion, we humbly accept and affix our seals. Henceforth, we shall strictly observe all matters [set forth herein]. With respect to woodcutters (soma), sawyers (kokibi), and day laborers (hiyatoi), we will carefully screen reliable individuals before permitting them to enter the mountain.

Item: We will be especially careful not only within the domain but also at the boundaries with other domains' mountains, and will absolutely not enter beyond the boundaries of this domain. We humbly accept this condition. We will strictly convey this instruction to all woodcutters, sawyers, and day laborers, and ensure it is observed. Should any misconduct occur and come to light through others, and upon investigation any punishment whatsoever be imposed, we will have no objection.

Item: Regarding the annual tribute (un'jōkin), we will duly pay two ryō and two shu of gold per craftsman per year.

As stated above, we undertake the contract for the said mountain for a period of three years, from the current Year of the Rabbit (U) through the coming Year of the Snake (Mi). During the Year of the Snake, we will undergo an inspection and make the required payments. Should we wish to continue thereafter, we will submit a petition with the designated place names (meisho-tsuke) of the mountain by the end of the Year of the Snake. This pledge document is submitted accordingly.


　　　　　　　　　　　Miroku Village
　　　Tenpō 2, Year of Kanoto-U (1831)　　Petitioner: Jūrobei　[Seal]
