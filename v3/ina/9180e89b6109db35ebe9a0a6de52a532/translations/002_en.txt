**License for Festival Sumo: Four-Pillar Ring Ceremony and Drum Tower**

In the town of Takato, Ina District, Shinano Province, sumo tournaments have been held on various occasions through the efforts of all parties concerned. As there was a sincere wish to make an offering to the local guardian shrine, the Hokoji Sanza Daigongen Shrine, the four-pillar ring ceremony equipment and drum tower have been dedicated thereto. We request that the persons listed hereunder maintain and preserve these in perpetuity and continue to hold sumo tournaments for generations to come. Accordingly, even should practitioners of the same profession visit in the future, they shall in no way interfere with or lay claim to the said four-pillar ring or drum tower.

This license is hereby granted, as stated above.
