　Okeguchi Tarōshichi
　Kuroda Matsujirō
　Itō Kazutsugu
　Ushiyama Nihēji
　Shimura Jingōrō

Total: Fifty persons

Additionally, Ikegami Kanzō (transferred/representing from Naka-machi)

The aforementioned persons have been informed of the order that they are to have dealings with the honorable lord, and in accordance with this directive, they have conferred among themselves. This document records the details of the occasion when a modest gift of fish-money (sakanaryo) was presented as a token of respect and goodwill toward the lord.
