Bunkyū 3, year of the Boar (1863)
Register of Collected Craftsmen's Levies (*un'jō*)
12th Month

The foregoing [records that] on the 4th day of the 12th month, [the monthly duty officer] proceeded to the official residence of Lord Kamei Gorōbei to collect and remit the levies, and received a receipt in return.
Prior to remittance, [the accounts] were presented for inspection to the Monthly Duty Magistrate (*go-bugyō go-tsukiban*), after which [the officer] proceeded to Lord Kamei's residence.
Furthermore, the said receipt was also presented to the Magistrate for his inspection.
