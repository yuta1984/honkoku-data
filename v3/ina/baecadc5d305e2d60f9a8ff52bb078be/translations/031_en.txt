Bunkyū 3, Year of the Boar (*Mizunoto-i*, 1863)
Register of Collected Craftsmen's Levies (*un'jō*)
12th Month

The foregoing [levy collection] was carried out on the 4th day of the 12th month: [the monthly duty officer] proceeded to the official residence of Lord Kamei Gorōbei to submit the levy payment, and received a receipt in return.
Moreover, before making the payment, [the accounts] were presented for inspection to the Town Magistrate (*go-bugyō*) and the monthly duty officer, and only thereafter did [the officer] proceed to Lord Kamei's residence.
Furthermore, the aforementioned receipt was also presented for inspection to the Town Magistrate.
