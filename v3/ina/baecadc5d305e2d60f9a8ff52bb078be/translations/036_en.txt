The above ledger of accounts and the monies were reviewed via the intermediary Yamashita Shinji, and thereafter, as the responsible official Fujita Zenemon was occupied with other matters, the documents were submitted to Itō Shigouemon. Although the scheduled date was the 4th, on the 3rd day, the ledger was reviewed at the castle, the receipts were duly settled, though the receipt document was not presented to the Bugyō (magistrate) for inspection. In the event of difficulty for the month-duty officer, Ikegami of the Eastern [branch] was requested to make the payment on behalf.
