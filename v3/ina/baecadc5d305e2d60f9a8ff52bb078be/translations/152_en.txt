On the 27th day of the 8th month, the following was communicated by Messrs. Ikeda and Sakai:

Regarding the expression of respectful congratulations upon the lord's journey to Kyoto, it was announced that the town elders (machidoshiyori), the district representatives (chōdai), the sword-bearing official fund holders (taitō goyōkin), and the dye-shop guild heads (kōnya gashira) are to present themselves on the 2nd day of the following month. This matter was duly conveyed to them.
