One item:　Cooper (barrel-maker)　　　Kudari-machi　　Takeshichi

One item:　In previous years, name registers were recorded in a horizontal ledger (yoko-chō); however, since the recent issuance of a notice (fure-gaki), it has been conveyed that [records] should be prepared in a horizontal ledger with a cover inscription (uwagaki-tsuki) before being submitted.
