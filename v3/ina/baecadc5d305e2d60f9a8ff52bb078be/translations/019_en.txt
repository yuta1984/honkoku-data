The above was personally delivered by Rizaemon to Sakai Shōzaemon on the 13th day of the 11th month.

On the 15th of the same month, Shimizu Tanzaemon conveyed the following instructions:
Regarding Takeshichi of Kudari-machi — as he holds the position of inspector (metsuke-yaku),
he is exempt from the occupational levy (yakukin); however, from this point forward,
his name is to be recorded and submitted as shown below.

One item:　Cooper　　Kudari-machi　　Takeshichi
The above is exempt from the water duty (mizu-yaku) due to his service as inspector (metsuke-yaku).
It was instructed that at the time of submitting the name register,
Takeshichi's name should also be added and included.

One item:　It was further instructed that whereas in former years the name register
　　was submitted in a horizontal-format book (yoko-chō), recently it had come
　　to be submitted on a separate slip (hanahaki/tanagaki). Henceforth, it is to be
　　returned to the horizontal-format book, with a cover label attached, and submitted accordingly.
