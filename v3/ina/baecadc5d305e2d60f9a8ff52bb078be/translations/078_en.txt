　Regarding the coming New Year's greetings, the [duty of] chōdai go-yōi [preparing the town representatives' duties] — as it has long been the custom that the matter of o-tsukimi [monthly observances] be handled by a general representative (sōdai) — please compile the said names [of those representatives] as well, and submit them to the Office by the Hour of the Ox and a half [around 5:30 a.m.] on the eighteenth, tomorrow.
