One item: One bu (金壱分)　　Seri-machi　　Stonemason　　Ichiemon
　　　On the twenty-third day of the first month of the Year of the Rooster, a petition was submitted and an inquiry was received from the district office (go-gun).

Total: Two ryō, three bu, two shu (金弐両三分弐朱)

The above represents the accounting of the levy (un'jō) collected from the various craftsmen of this district; it stands as recorded above. Respectfully submitted.

　　　Twelfth month of the Year of the Monkey　　Month-duty officer (月番)
　　　　　　　　　　Miyazawa Rokurō

　　The title inscription reads as follows:

　　From the Year of the Dragon to the Year of the Monkey
Register of Levy Collection from Various Craftsmen (諸職人御運上取立書上帳)
　　Twelfth month

The foregoing has been recorded in a横帳 (horizontal-format ledger) as described, and there are those who have submitted [such records] to the District Magistrate (go-gundai) and his officials each year as customary.
