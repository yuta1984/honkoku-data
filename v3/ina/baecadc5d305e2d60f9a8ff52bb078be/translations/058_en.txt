(Regarding the customary annual receipt,) since the matter of presenting it to the magistrate (bugyō) for review had already been reported to the Civil Administration Office (Minsei-kyoku), this arrangement had been conveyed as stated above, with Mr. Kondō serving as intermediary, and the aforementioned instructions were thus issued.
