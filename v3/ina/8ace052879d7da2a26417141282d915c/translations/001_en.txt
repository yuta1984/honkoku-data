## A Settlement Document Herewith Submitted

Regarding the matter petitioned by Sakai Kichibei of Suganuma Village: In former years, during the administration of Lord Ishikawa Jūzaemon, he was registered as cultivator (*nawauké*) of paddy fields at穴山耕地前河原 (Anayama Kōchi Maekawara). However, all of these fields were washed away by flooding. Subsequently, in the 4th year of Bunsei (1821), the area that had become a riverbed, along with one plot of grassland (*shibayama*), were offered as collateral for a loan of 25 ryō of gold borrowed from Chōgorō and Ichibei of Ina Village, and Kanzaemon of the home village (居村). All of the said land was handed over as security. Additionally, at the same time, one plot of persimmon grove and wooded land was sold for 4 ryō. Furthermore, it was agreed that upon redemption, the amount would be calculated at a rate of 1 *to* 2 *shō* of rice per ryō as interest (*ko*).

The aforementioned three persons had developed and cultivated the land, but it was subsequently washed away again. Therefore, Chōgorō and Ichibei of Ina Village transferred the land to Hyōemon and Umanojō of the home village, who took it over and cultivated it. In these circumstances, Sakai Kichibei fell into increasing poverty and found it difficult to sustain his livelihood.
