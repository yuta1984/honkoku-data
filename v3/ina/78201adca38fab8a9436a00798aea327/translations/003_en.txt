The documentation procedures were promptly completed, and around the fourth hour of the night (approximately 10:00 p.m.), they proceeded to the residence of ■emon, the headman of Harakatsuma Village, where they lodged for the night.

12th Day

Negotiations were conducted in Harakatsuma Village, and at the fourth hour (approximately 10:00 p.m.) they proceeded to the residence of Kyūgorō, the headman of Himochi Village, where they began consultations. As the village comprised some one hundred households, they continued negotiations through the night without sleeping in order to conclude the discussions before morning.

13th Day

They proceeded to the residence of Matasaemon, the headman of Himochiyama Village, and conducted repeated rounds of negotiations. After dusk, they lodged at the residence of Genzaemon, the headman of Yamamuro Village.

14th Day

As the officials of Yamamuro Village gathered from early morning, the intentions (of the domain) were formally conveyed to them.
