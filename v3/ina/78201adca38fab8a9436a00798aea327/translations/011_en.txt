(The three children) were separated and raised (by different parties), but at present the family line has been severed entirely, and the village officials and relatives alike are united in their grief and lamentation. On the occasion of this one-hundred-and-fiftieth anniversary celebration, we humbly petition that, through special benevolence and compassion, he be pardoned and permitted to return to the village. This request has been brought forward by the village officials and relatives in unison.
