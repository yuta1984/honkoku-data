Item: Six bales of rice　　（Himochi-yama Village / Hanjirō）
The above individual had received rice as relief assistance during a poor harvest, and despite the continued hardship of the bad harvest season, managed to sustain his household. He has therefore come forward expressing his wish to offer this as a celebratory gift.

Item: Five ryō in gold　　（Yamamuro Village / Genzaemon）
The above individual has served repeatedly in the role of village headman (nanushi) and has received great benevolence. He hereby presents this offering in gratitude.
We humbly request that both of the above individuals be commended.

Item: One ryō in gold　　（Himochi Village / Matasaburō / widow）

【Left folio】
The above woman's late husband, Matasaburō, became subject to official sanction in the seventh year of Bunsei (1824), the Year of the Monkey. It is thought that, had he still been alive at this time, he would have certainly offered a votive contribution (myōgakin) and petitioned for official recognition. However, as she is a woman (a widow), she is unable to undertake such matters fully on his behalf, and she has come forward wishing to make a modest contribution. As the amount is small, it may not warrant formal commendation, but as it represents her sincere devotion, we respectfully bring it to your attention.
