9th Day

Kanemon, Hombemon, Zenbemon, Denbei, and Sobei set out early in the morning to Kamiyamada Village, where they entered into discussions with the village headman, Osome Emon, and other officials. As this was the opening of negotiations, the talks proved exceedingly difficult, and it was not until past the ninth hour of the night (around midnight) that matters were finally settled. After that, around the eighth hour (around 2 a.m.), they proceeded to the residence of Gōemon, the headman of Shimoyamada Village. Note that Denbei and Sobei had gone ahead to Obara Village.

10th Day

Negotiations in Shimoyamada Village commenced from early morning, but the discussions with just one headman dragged on until past the eighth hour of the afternoon (around 2 p.m.). Finally, at the fourth hour of the night (around 10 p.m.), matters were resolved, and they proceeded to Obara Village, where they immediately gathered the officials and conducted their official business through until dawn. (Current official: Saibei)
