## Memorandum of Oral Statement (Translation, continued)

*(Continued from previous page)* ...things have become unmanageable, and beyond our own power to remedy, resulting in a state of affairs utterly different from what this town was in former times — a situation that we all find deeply lamentable.

Item: Regarding the sake brewers — in former years, there were as many as fourteen or fifteen establishments in the town, holding sake brewing licenses (*sakakabu*) totaling over eight hundred *koku*, and they actively brewed sake. Because they shipped it primarily to Kōshū (Kai Province), Bushū (Musashi Province), and other neighboring provinces, the revenue from these sales flowed back into the town, which helped maintain the financial circulation among all residents. However, as the town has fallen into decline in recent years, most of these brewing licenses have been transferred to people in the surrounding rural villages (*zaichū*), and at present there are no more than two establishments that are doing any substantial brewing to speak of — a situation we find deeply lamentable.

Item: In former years, there were wealthy and prosperous merchants in the town, and so from the Three Metropolises (Edo, Kyoto, and Osaka)
