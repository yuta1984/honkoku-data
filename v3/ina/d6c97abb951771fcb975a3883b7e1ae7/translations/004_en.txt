## Memorandum of Oral Statement (Translation, continued)

*(Continued from previous page)* ...we find [the situation] deeply troubling. In recent years, those in the surrounding rural villages (*zaihō*) have come to carry on various livelihoods with vigor, while the townspeople have grown progressively weaker.

*(Interlinear note)* ◯ Of late, Yorozuya Shōzō's establishment has also fallen into difficulties with its finances, and even when goods are procured, the quantities are drastically reduced compared to former years. Furthermore, during last year, Fujisawaya Ban'emon passed away...△

Even when [buyers] travel to the neighboring domains to purchase vegetable oil (*mizu-abura*), the town merchants cannot meet the demand, and [those buyers] end up going to oil dealers in the rural villages to do their business. While it may be said that this is entirely due to the poor financial management of the townspeople, we nonetheless find this state of affairs deeply lamentable.

Item: Regarding candles (*rōsoku*) and *bintsuke* hair oil and similar goods — in former years, there were those in the town who made their principal livelihood from this trade and wholesaled to the surrounding rural villages, which was convenient and beneficial for all. However, in recent years there is no one in the castle town carrying on such commerce. As goods are now procured only in small quantities from Nagoya, Iida, and Matsumoto, this has become a most inconvenient and unfavorable situation. Should there be anyone wishing to engage in this trade in the town,

*(Left folio)*

even if there are those who are so minded and determined to carry on such commerce in the town, they are invariably hampered by a lack of startup capital and are unable to act as they wish — a situation we find deeply lamentable.
