…the cost of packhorse transport was until quite recently approximately 1,500 mon per three horse-loads, but at present it has risen to around 20 monme per three horse-loads, and at times even as high as approximately 30 monme, so that the prices of all manner of goods have risen accordingly,

[Left folio]

and although this is an unavoidable circumstance brought about by poor weather conditions, trade has naturally diminished as a consequence, which we find deeply lamentable.

　〇 Regarding the purchase of salt by the townspeople — the procurement of salt sent from other places has been prohibited by the nakama packhorse operators (of Fujisawa-gō), and as a result the procurement of supplies has become difficult, naturally driving prices up and causing us considerable hardship.
　　[Interlinear note] We have recently heard that in the Suwa area, competition has arisen with salt being sent from Suruga Province, as a result of which prices there have reportedly fallen exceptionally low.

Item: Regarding the population of the town — in former years, as stated in the foregoing, there were several sake breweries, and a great many people engaged in large-scale commerce lived here. Accordingly, the town was home to a large population, and the temple parish registers (shūmon-ninbetsu) recorded approximately 2,200 to 2,300 persons of both sexes.
