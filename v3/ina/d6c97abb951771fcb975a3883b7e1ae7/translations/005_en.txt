though there are those who are inclined to do so, they are invariably hindered by a lack of startup capital, and thus cannot act as they wish, which we find deeply lamentable.

Item: Regarding the dyeing trade (kōya, indigo-dyeing establishments) and similar crafts — in former years there were more than ten such establishments in the town, which made their living exclusively from this trade, and such businesses were rare in the surrounding villages. However, at present there are said to be some sixty establishments throughout the domain, and while some among them are prospering, the town's dyers have been gradually weakening in their means, which we find deeply lamentable.
