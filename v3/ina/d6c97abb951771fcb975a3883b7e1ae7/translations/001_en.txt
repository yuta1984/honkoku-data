## Memorandum of Oral Statement (Translation)

Item: Regarding our castle town,
since travelers passing through are rare,
we have been making our living solely
by serving the needs within the domain.
However, the town has been in decline for some years,
and particularly, during the recent Year of the Horse,
the entire town suffered a devastating fire.
At present, along the main road, we have managed
to rebuild some houses,
but with continued poor harvests on top of this,
our finances are in dire straits.
Our stock of goods has become thin,
and as a result, our annual profits have
been decreasing year by year, leaving us
increasingly unable to make ends meet.
Therefore, we have all conferred together and
devised various measures hoping somehow to bring
prosperity back to our castle town, but originally and fundamentally...
