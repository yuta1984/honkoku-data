**Regulations**

One item: 250 mon — cost of the wooden bucket
(From the spring of the Year of the Tiger [壬寅, 1782], this was revised to 300 mon.)

One item: Funeral candles — 12 sticks

One item: Same [candles] — one stick of 20 monme weight

One item: Hemp cloth (苧麻布/今田) — one tan

One item: Early-harvested cotton cloth — one tan
(If provided in lieu of payment, the equivalent value is 300 mon.)

One item: Sake — two shō

One item: Cauldron fee — 500 mon

The above provisions apply to the member himself/herself, their spouse, and the male member's parents (four persons in total).
In the case of cremation, [costs/provisions] shall be divided [according to separate rules].
