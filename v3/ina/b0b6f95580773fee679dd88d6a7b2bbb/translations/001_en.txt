**Certificate of Agreement (Ikisatsu no Koto)**

Item: Regarding the rental property held in connection with official duties for the purpose of funding repairs to the Gongen shrine of our village — after due consultation, the Miyasaka Group (Miyasaka Kumiai) of [Hokojimura] has agreed to lease said property from the second month of the current year (Bunka 9) through the second month of the coming Year of the Ox (Bunka 12), a term of five years. This is without question correct.

Although the rent hitherto has been two bu of gold per year, during the term of this lease, regardless of whether the rental property is occupied or vacant, we shall pay one bu of gold per year, due without fail by the twenty-eighth day of the twelfth month each year, to be delivered to the monthly duty officer (tsukiban).

Furthermore, we shall undertake all repairs at the expense of our group, including — needless to say — re-thatching of the roof, ensuring that the property does not fall into disrepair. Moreover, since the property is to be particularly well repaired this spring, it has been agreed that rent for this first year shall be waived.

Should any manner of difficulty arise in connection with the occupants of said rental property, our village group shall take full responsibility and promptly resolve the matter, causing no trouble whatsoever to any of you. This certificate is issued as proof for the future, as stated above.
