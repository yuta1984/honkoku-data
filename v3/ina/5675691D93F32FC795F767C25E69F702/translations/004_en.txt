Kaei 4th year (Kanoto-i/Xinhai year [1851])

Record of receiving the ceremonial seasonal robes (jifuku) in the O-katte (the Shogun's private quarters), on account of an ailment (painful condition), in lieu of the formal New Year's attendance at Edo Castle.

1st day of the 1st month — Naitō Suruga-no-kami
