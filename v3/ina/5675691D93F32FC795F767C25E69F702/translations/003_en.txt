【Main Letter (Standard Version)】

Although the weather has improved, there is still a lingering winter chill. I am most gratefully pleased to hear that you continue to enjoy good health and peace. Now, regarding myself: as I was about to make my formal introductory visit (o-mie), I had requested from the very beginning that you guide and introduce me [to others], and you have kindly extended your support and instructions to me. I am deeply and overwhelmingly grateful for this great favor. I humbly request that you continue to kindly instruct and guide me in all matters going forward. On this occasion, though it is but a humble gift, I enclose one basket containing two sets of formal hakama cloth (kamishimo-ji), presented with the wish that the colors may appear fresh and pleasing to you. It would be my greatest joy should you be pleased to accept this with good humor. I look forward to conveying the rest in person. Respectfully submitted.

25th day of the 12th month

　　To: Lord Shuri-tayū　　Lord Doi Noto-no-kami
　　Lord Tsuchiya Noto-no-kami　　Lord Iyo-no-kami

【Note for Lord Nagai (Variant Version)】

For Lord Nagai's letter, add at the opening: "How are you faring with your cold? I am most eager to inquire in detail after your condition."
From "First, regarding myself——" onward, the text is the same as the copy above.
The passage "...your kind support and instructions——" is likewise the same.
However, the phrase "o-hikmawashi" (guidance/introductions) is to be omitted.
A humble gift of two rolls [of cloth] is to be noted as being presented with freshness and care.
