Although the weather has improved, there is still lingering cold. I humbly offer my respectful congratulations on your continued good health and well-being. First and foremost, I wish to say:

Regarding myself, as I had the honor of presenting myself before you, and having requested your guidance and support from the very beginning, you were so kind as to extend your consideration and direction to me. I am deeply and exceedingly grateful for this most generous favor. I humbly ask that you continue to instruct and guide me henceforth. In connection with this, although it is a most humble and inadequate offering, I am enclosing and presenting one basket containing two sets of formal hakama fabric (*kamishimo-ji*), as a small token of my sincerest gratitude. I would consider it my greatest honor should you be pleased to accept this with a smile. I look forward to speaking with you in person on many further matters. Respectfully.

The Twenty-Fifth Day of the Twelfth Month

To: Lord Shuri-tayū　　　　　Lord Doi Noto-no-kami
To: Lord Tsuchiya Noto-no-kami　　To: Lord Iyo-no-kami

For Lord Nagai (a variation of the letter):

How have you been faring with your cold? I very much wish to inquire in detail about your condition. As for myself——(same as above)
Your kind consideration and direction——(same as above)
　The phrase "御引廻し" (guidance and introductions) is to be omitted,
and "two humble scrolls" replaces the original wording, described as "a small and trifling token."
