With great deference, I humbly submit this petition in writing:

As I have no male heir, I have privately discussed the matter of adopting as my son a man called Kanjū, the younger brother of Sugiemon of Kohara Village in Iritani-gō, who is of good and suitable lineage. I hereby humbly request permission to take him in as my adopted son.

I most humbly beg for your benevolent consideration of the above matter, and should this petition be graciously granted, I would consider it a most fortunate blessing. Respectfully submitted.
