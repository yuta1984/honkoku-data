…Having reported the matter as described, the town representative (丁代) was summoned and given instructions.

---

【First Petition】

Respectfully, I humbly submit this written petition:

I humbly wish to establish myself in the trade of dyeing (紺屋) beginning this year, using twenty-two vats of indigo (藍瓶). I therefore respectfully make this petition.
If, in your benevolence, you would be so gracious as to grant this request as petitioned, I would consider it a most grateful and fortunate outcome. Respectfully submitted.

Tenpō 2, Year of the Xinmao [1831]　　　　Honmachi, Shirokiya
　　　Third Month　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Rokubei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Chūzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same role: Shōzō

　　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

---

【Second Petition】

Respectfully, I humbly submit this oral petition in writing:

Some years ago I was engaged in the oil trade, which I suspended for a period of time. However, I now wish once again to engage in the pressing of raw oil (油元絞). I therefore respectfully make this petition.
If, in your benevolence, you would be so gracious as to grant this request as petitioned, I would consider it a most grateful and fortunate outcome. Respectfully submitted.
