A humble petition respectfully submitted as a verbal statement in writing

Item: I humbly wish to travel to Edo, to Nihonbashi-dōri Ni-chōme, to serve as a sales apprentice (uriko hōkō) at the establishment of Manya Chūzō, for a term of ten years, for the purpose of learning the trade. Accordingly, I humbly petition that, during the term of service, my religious registry seal (shūmon ingyō) be issued without an official stamp (muhan).

Regarding the above matter, if it should please you in your benevolence to grant our petition as requested, we would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　　Tenpō 2nd Year, Kanoto-u (1831)　　　Hokomochi-chō
　　　　　　Third Month　　　　　　　　　　Fuji-ya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Enkichi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town representative (Chōdai): Toemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same (as above): Sōhachi

　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above three petitions — from Seishichi of Naka-ya, Honjō; Buemon of Hokomochi-chō; and Enkichi — were submitted at the castle on the eighth day, through the intermediary of Mr. Inokuchi. They were granted approval immediately on the same day, by written notice.
