Humbly Submitted Petition

Regarding Kinzaemon, a carpenter from Okoshiba Village, within the territory overseen by Lord Matsudaira Tangono-kami:
I hereby vouch for him and respectfully request that he be permitted to serve in the suiyaku (water-related duties) and perform koshizaiku (traveling/itinerant carpentry work) [in this area].
It is for this reason that we humbly submit this petition.

Should you, in your great benevolence, graciously grant this request as stated above,
we would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　　Bunsei 13th year, Kanoto-Tora (1830)
　　　　　　11th month　　　　　　　Hokocho District
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ichoiya [Inn/Shop]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Sōhachi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative (Chōdai): Yaohyōemon

　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office
