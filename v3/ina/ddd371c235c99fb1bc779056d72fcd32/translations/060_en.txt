　　11th month, Year of the Tiger　　　　　　　　　　　Sake merchants

Regarding the above petition that was submitted: on the 5th day of the 12th month, at the magistrate's office, through the intermediary of Mr. Izawa, the petition was returned [to us] on the grounds that the sake prices were too high. We therefore reformulated and resubmitted [our petition] honestly and without inflated prices, whereupon, in accordance with official instructions, Iseya Heihachi was summoned and notified. On the following 6th day, he was again instructed to reformulate and resubmit [the pricing proposal], and he resubmitted it with a 2-mon reduction. On the 7th of the same month, the document was submitted at the office through the intermediary of Mr. Gotō.
