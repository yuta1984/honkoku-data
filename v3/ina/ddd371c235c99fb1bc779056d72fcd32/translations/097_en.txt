A Petition Humbly Submitted by Word of Mouth

Regarding this matter: Ine, who is under the care of Suzuki Hikobei, the supervisor of the construction office (go-fushin-kata), is not in a common-law relationship with him. Therefore, I have had private discussions about taking her as my wife. Furthermore, as she has one mother, I also wish to take the mother in as well. I therefore humbly submit this petition.

Should you, in your great benevolence, graciously grant our petition as requested above, we would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.
