Humbly, I submit this written petition:

Item: Although I am an unskilled and incompetent person, I was graciously appointed to the position of chōdai (town representative), for which I have been deeply grateful and have diligently served in this role. However, as I have fallen into poor health, I find myself unable to continue fulfilling these duties. I therefore humbly petition to be relieved of my responsibilities as chōdai.

I most humbly request that, in your great benevolence, you grant this petition as submitted above. I would be most grateful if this should be approved. Respectfully submitted.

　　Tenpō 2, year of Kanoto (1831)　　　　　　Shimizu-chō (district)
　　　　　　First Month　　　　　　　　　　　　Chōdai: Gihei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kumigashira: Rihēji
