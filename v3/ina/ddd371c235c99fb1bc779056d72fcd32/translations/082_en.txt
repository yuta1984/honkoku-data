Memorandum

One item: Rice, 3 koku 7 to 6 shō　　　From the 1st day of the 2nd month of U to the 29th day of the intercalary 7th month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Total of 176 days (accounting for both long and short months)

The above represents the stipend rice (fuchi-mai) allocated for the horse-handler (umashi) of this town. We hereby confirm receipt of the rice paid as tribute (ōnōmai) from Kawashimo-gō for the preceding year of Tora (Tiger). This memorandum is issued accordingly.

Tenpō 2, year of Kanoto-u (1831), Second Month　　　Toiya (wholesale agent/official)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Riemon

Ishikawa [domain]
　To: Lord Takeiri Jūzaemon

The above two documents were submitted on the 5th day through the intermediary of Mr. Iguchi. We have also requested that they be forwarded to Lord Ōiwa.
