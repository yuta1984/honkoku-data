**[Fifth Petition]**

Humbly submitted in writing as a verbal petition:

As I wish to engage in the trade of tofu beginning this year, and as Kohei of Tomiya in the same town has decided to suspend his tofu business, I humbly petition to receive a transfer of his tofu mold box (katawako) from said person, and to use it to make my living. I therefore humbly submit this petition for your consideration.

Should the above request be graciously granted in accordance with this humble petition, I would consider it a most fortunate blessing. Respectfully submitted.
