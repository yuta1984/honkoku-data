## Memorandum

Item: Nami-zake (ordinary sake), one shō
　　Note: Purchase rice calculated at 1 koku 1 shō per ryō
　　Price: 110 mon
　　Note: At the time of currency exchange, calculated at 16 kan 600 mon, which is 200 mon cheaper
　　→ Officially set at 104 mon per shō

Item: Katashiro (semi-refined sake), one shō
　　Price: 212 mon

Item: Morohaku (fully refined sake), one shō
　　Price: 140 mon

Item: Gozen-zake (premium sake for honored guests), one shō
　　Price: 172 mon

We humbly wish to sell our sake at the prices stated above. Respectfully submitted.

　　12th month, Year of the Ox　　　　　　Town sake merchants, collectively

The above petition was submitted to the magistrate's office on the 17th, handled by Mr. Izawa.
On the 22nd, a letter was received from Mr. Izawa instructing that sales should commence from the following day, the 23rd, at the price of 104 mon. Accordingly, this was conveyed to Iseya Heihachirō.
