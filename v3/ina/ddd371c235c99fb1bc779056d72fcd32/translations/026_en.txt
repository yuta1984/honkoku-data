**[Petition Submitted by Written Statement]**

With utmost deference, we hereby humbly submit this petition by written statement.

A man by the name of Genzō, who is registered in the parish records (*shūmon*) of Seiryūji Temple, along with his wife and children, has a personal connection (*yūsho*) to me (Fujirōemon). With this in mind, we have come to an agreement, and I hereby respectfully petition that Genzō and his family be transferred from their current temple parish registration and added to the household register (*ninbetsu*) of Hokomochi-machi, beginning with this year's Tora year parish inspection.

Should your honorable compassion grant this petition as humbly requested above, I would consider it a most grateful fortune. Respectfully submitted.

　Bunsei 13th year, Kanoto-Tora (1830), 2nd month　　　Hokomochi-machi, Fuji■
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Fujirōemon
