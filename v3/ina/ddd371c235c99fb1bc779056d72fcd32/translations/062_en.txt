A Petition Humbly Submitted by Word of Mouth in Writing

I am a person of limited ability and competence; nevertheless, I was graciously entrusted with the duties of town representative (chōdai), for which I have been most grateful, and have continued to serve in that role for a long time. However, I have now fallen gravely ill and find myself unable to carry out the duties of the office. I therefore humbly petition that I be relieved of my responsibilities.

Should you, in your great benevolence, be pleased to grant this request as petitioned, I would consider it a most grateful and fortunate outcome. Respectfully submitted.

　　Bunsei 13th year, Kanoe-Tora (1830)　　　Kudari-machi
　　　　　　11th month　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Yagorōemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Yaemon

　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office
