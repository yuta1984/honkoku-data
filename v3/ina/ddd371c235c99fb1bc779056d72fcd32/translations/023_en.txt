**[Sixth Petition]**

Humbly submitted in writing as a verbal petition:

As I have been engaged in the tofu trade until now, but have come to find it financially difficult to continue, I humbly petition for permission to suspend said business. Furthermore, I wish to transfer the tofu mold box (katawako) that I have hitherto possessed to Goshichi of the same town. I therefore humbly submit this petition for your consideration.

Should the above request be graciously granted in accordance with this humble petition, I would consider it a most fortunate blessing. Respectfully submitted.

2nd Month, Bunsei 13 (1830), Year of the Boar　　　　　　　　Kudari-machi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Son of Tomiya Chūbei
