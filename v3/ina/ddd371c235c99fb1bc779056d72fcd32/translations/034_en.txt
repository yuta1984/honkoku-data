A matter humbly petitioned by means of this written statement:

Concerning Tetsu, the younger sister of Eikichi of Matsushima Village, which falls under the domain administration of Lord Hakura Geki — as there is also a connection between us — I wish to take her as my wife. For this reason, I humbly submit this petition.

If, through your benevolence, you should be pleased to issue an order in accordance with this humble petition as stated above, I would consider it a most grateful blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13, Kanoto-Tora year, 3rd month　　　Shinmachi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Nagaseya [shop/house name]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner:　Haruji
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ward representative (chōdai):　Chūzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same:　Denzaemon

　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above two petitions from Shinmachi were submitted on the 7th day at the magistrate's office through the intermediary of Mr. Gotō, and were immediately approved and granted. Ward representative Denzaemon was summoned and informed of the decision.
