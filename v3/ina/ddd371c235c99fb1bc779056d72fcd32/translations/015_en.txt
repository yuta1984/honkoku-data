**Memorandum**

Item: One foot soldier dispatched by Lord Suwa Iseno-kami; arrived at the hour of Kokonotsu (noon) on the 28th day of the 7th month; meals provided through the morning of the 29th — 3 times.

Item: One courier foot soldier from Kiso Atsukawa post station; arrived in the evening of the 7th day of the 9th month; meals provided through the morning of the 8th — 2 times.

Total meals provided: 5 times
　　Of which: Evening and morning meals — 4 times　(64 mon, 2 bu per meal)
　　　　　　　　　Midday meal — 1 time　(42 mon)

　　Grand Total: 310 mon

The above payment in cash has been duly received as indicated. Respectfully submitted.
