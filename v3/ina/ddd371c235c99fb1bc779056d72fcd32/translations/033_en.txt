A matter humbly petitioned by means of this written statement:

Concerning a dependent (*yakkai*) of Lord Fukuzawa Ryūsaku, who belongs to the unit under Lord Fukuhara Jinbei — as there is a connection between us — I have privately discussed the matter of taking her as my wife. For this reason, I humbly submit this petition.

If, through your benevolence, you should be pleased to issue an order in accordance with this humble petition as stated above, I would consider it a most grateful blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13, Kanoto-Tora year, 3rd month　　　Shinmachi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sekiya [shop/house name]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner:　Kin'ya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ward representative (chōdai):　Chūzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same:　Denzaemon

　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was immediately approved and granted on the 7th day.
