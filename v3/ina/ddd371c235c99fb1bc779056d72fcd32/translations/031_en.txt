[The headman] Ichibei and Taya affixed their joint seals to a supplementary promissory note which was sent and kept on file. However, they delayed once again, and when I pressed them for payment, they asked for yet another extension of two months, or sometimes three months at a time. Even when those deadlines arrived, the same situation repeated itself, and the delays have continued without end to this day. I am suffering extreme hardship on account of this matter. I humbly request that you give this matter your serious consideration, and that you issue an order compelling them to repay the principal and interest on the said sum of money.

If, through your benevolence, you would be so gracious as to issue the order as petitioned above, I would be most grateful and consider it a great blessing. Respectfully submitted.
