A humble petition respectfully submitted in the form of a verbal statement in writing

My daughter Mitsu has a familial connection (by marriage) with Hachizaemon of Nakatsubo-mura, Tera-gō, within the territory administered by Lord Chimura Heiemon, and I have discussed privately the matter of sending her there as a dependent. Accordingly, I humbly submit this petition.
