A humble petition respectfully submitted in writing

Item: I, the undersigned, hereby wish to commence the business of selling kōji (malted rice) on this occasion. For this reason, I humbly submit this petition.

Regarding the above matter, I humbly request that, through your benevolent mercy, you grant permission as I have petitioned. Should this be granted, I will be most grateful and consider it a great blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Tenpō 2nd year, Year of the Rabbit (Kanoto-u), Second month　　　Yoko-machi (Side Street district)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Awaya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Yokurō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative (Chōdai): Heijūrō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same as above: Katsuemon

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

Regarding the above petition: it was processed on the 5th day of the third month at the Honorable Office, through the mediation of Mr. Gotō. Accordingly, the Yoko-machi town representative was summoned and the decision was conveyed to him.
