Bunsei 12, Year of the Ox (1829), 11th Month

　　　　　　　　　　　　Same: Shirobei
　　　　　　　　　　　　Toiya (merchant agent): Riemon

To: Yoshida Tazaemon

**Memorandum**

Item: Oil, 3 shō 6 gō 5 shaku　　　　　　(58 mon per 1 gō of oil)
　　Cost: 2 kan 205 mon
　　From the 20th day of the 8th month to the 3rd day of the 11th month of the Year of the Ox, totaling 73 days
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　At a rate of 5 shaku per day and night

**[Left folio]**

Item: Oil, 1 shō 3 gō　　　　　　　　　(38 mon per 1 gō of oil)
　　Cost: 514 mon
　　From the 4th day of the 11th month to the last day of the same month of the Year of the Ox, totaling 26 days

Total: 2 kan 719 mon

The above [expenses were incurred] because there are prisoners in the jail, and eta (outcaste guards) were stationed for night watch with a lantern. We respectfully request that the above oil expenses be reimbursed. We have duly received the funds and delivered them to the eta. Recorded as above.
