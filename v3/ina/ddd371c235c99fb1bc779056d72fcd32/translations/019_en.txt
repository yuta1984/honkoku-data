Bunsei 13th year, Kanoto-Tora (1830), 1st Month　Shimo-machi, Izumiya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Izaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Chōdai: Tōji
To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

---

We humbly submit this petition by written statement.

I was appointed to the role of chōdai, for which I was most grateful, and have been diligently serving in this capacity. However, as I have become ill, I find it difficult to continue fulfilling the duties of this role. Therefore, I respectfully request that I be relieved of my duties.

Regarding the above matter, if it please your honorable compassion to grant our petition as stated, we would be most grateful. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13th year, Kanoto-Tora (1830), 1st Month　Naka-machi, Kii-ya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Tōzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Chōdai: Kanzō
To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

---

Regarding the four petition letters above, I endorsed them in the customary manner and, taking advantage of a convenient opportunity at Mr. Ikegami's office, presented them with a request for his favorable consideration. They were approved immediately. All four individuals were then accompanied to make their rounds of grateful greetings without delay.
