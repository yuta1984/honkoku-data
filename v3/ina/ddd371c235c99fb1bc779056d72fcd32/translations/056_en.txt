Humbly submitted by means of this written petition

Regarding the matter of my son Sōbei, I had filed a lawsuit against Momenyā Sōbei, Momenyā Kanzaburō, Yoshidaya Kanzō, and the latter's son Kanemon. As the proceedings advanced, we were repeatedly summoned and subjected to official inquiry, and it now appears that censure may be forthcoming. Should yet stricter reprimand be handed down, we would find ourselves in a most distressing position. At this juncture, however, Kitahara Kunitarō and Kitazawa Katsubei have graciously intervened of their own accord, and have diligently mediated between all parties, offering thorough counsel to each side. We have been informed that they have agreed to accept the terms put forward by both parties, and we humbly submit to this resolution. In light of the foregoing, we shall henceforth raise not the slightest complaint nor make any further troublesome petition regarding this matter whatsoever.

Accordingly, we humbly beseech your compassionate consideration. Should it please you to return all documents pertaining to both the plaintiff and defendant, we would regard this as an act of the deepest gratitude and good fortune.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Nakamachi
　Bunsei 13th year, Kanoe-Tora (1830)　Onoya
　　　　　　　　11th month　　Petitioner: Jiemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　 Sōbei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Chōdai:　Tōzaemon
