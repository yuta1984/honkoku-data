To: Iizawa Iwaemon
　　　　To: Inokuchi Okuroku
　　　　To: Gotō Shinuemon

The aforementioned document (tegata) was submitted on the thirteenth day at the official office (o-yakusho), through the intermediary of Mr. Inokuchi. The one hundred mon was received on the spot, and [the bearer] returned home.

[Note: Text is recorded on the left folio, but the entire entry is marked with an × (cancellation mark).]
