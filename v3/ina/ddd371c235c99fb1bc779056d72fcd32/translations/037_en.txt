A matter humbly petitioned by means of this written statement:

As my adopted son Shōbei and I were incompatible (did not get along well), I dissolved the adoption and had previously petitioned to return him to Shōzaemon of Nakатsubo Village in Tera-gō, under the jurisdiction of Lord Chimura Heizaemon. However, on this occasion, I wish to reinstate the adoption (restore the relationship). Therefore, I humbly petition to take him back into my household.

If, through your benevolence, you should be pleased to issue an order in accordance with this humble petition as stated above, I would consider it a most grateful blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13, Kanoto-Tora year, 3rd month　　　Fukuro-machi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Takedaya Sōkichi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Widow (goke)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Group head (kumigashira): Rikichi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ward representative (chōdai): Uzaemon

The above petition, with endorsement and seal affixed, was submitted on the 7th day at the magistrate's office through the intermediary of Mr. Gotō. It was immediately approved and granted. The ward representative Uzaemon was summoned and the decision was conveyed to him.
