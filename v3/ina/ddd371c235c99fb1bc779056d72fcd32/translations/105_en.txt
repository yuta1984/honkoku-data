Tenpō 2nd year, Kanoto-u (1831), Third Month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kudari-machi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Myōgaya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Hyōzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative (Chōdai): Heibei

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

---

**Petition submitted respectfully by oral statement in written form**

Up until now, I have been operating an out-service dyeing workshop (de-zaiku) in Honmachi using twenty-four indigo vats. However, as I have been left without workers, I wish to discontinue the branch operation. I therefore respectfully submit this petition.

Should you, in your benevolence, be pleased to grant this request as petitioned, I would consider it a most grateful blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Tenpō 2nd year, Kanoto-u (1831), Third Month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Naka-machi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kinokuni-ya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Chūzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative (Chōdai): Tōzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same role: Kanzō

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

---

The above three documents were endorsed (okugaki) in the customary manner and submitted on the 25th day at the magistrate's office through the intermediary of a Mr. Gotō. On the 29th of the same month, notification of approval arrived by letter, and the town representatives were summoned and duly informed.
