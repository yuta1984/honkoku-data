Second Year of Tenpō, Year of the Rabbit (1831)
Auspicious Day of the First Month

　　　　　　Memorandum

Item: One hundred mon in coin

The above sum of one hundred mon was duly received, as customary in previous years, as a celebratory offering (goshugi) for the New Year's first market festival (hatsuichi sairei) held on the fourteenth day of the first month of this Year of the Rabbit. The necessary goods were procured and promptly distributed to the shrine ritualists (negi) and those associated with the market (ichi). This is to confirm the above. Respectfully submitted.
