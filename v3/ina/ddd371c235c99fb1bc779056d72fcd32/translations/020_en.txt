**[First Petition]**

We humbly submit this petition by way of oral statement:

I respectfully wish to engage in the trade of unfinished timber (shiraki) from this year onward. I therefore humbly submit this petition.

If, in your great benevolence, you would be pleased to grant our request as stated above, we would consider it a most grateful blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13th year, Year of the Canh Dần (1830), First Month — Kudari-machi, Izawaya
To: The Honorable Magistrate's Office　　Petitioner: Yoneshichi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Block Representative: Kasazaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same as above: Jingorōzaemon

**[Second Petition]**

We humbly submit this petition by way of oral statement:

Item: 115 logs of chestnut timber, measuring from 6 shaku to 3 ken in length.

The above timber is to be purchased from the forest owned by Sakubei of Kurogouchi Village, and we wish to float it downriver to Benzaiten. We therefore humbly submit this petition. We most earnestly request that, in your great benevolence, you would be pleased to grant our request as stated above, and we would consider it a most grateful blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13th year, Year of the Canh Dần (1830), First Month — Kudari-machi, Izawaya
