To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

Humbly, I submit this written petition:

Although I am an unskilled and incompetent person, I was graciously appointed to the position of chōdai (town representative), for which I have been deeply grateful and have diligently served in this role. However, in recent years I have fallen into poor health and find myself unable to continue fulfilling the duties of chōdai. I therefore humbly petition to be relieved of my responsibilities.

I most humbly request that, in your great benevolence, you grant this petition as submitted above.

Tenpō 2, year of Kanoto-u (1831)　　　　　New Town (Shin-machi)
　　　　　First Month　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Denpei (Den'emon)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Chōdai: Chūzaemon

　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

Regarding the five petitions submitted above, with the required closing statements and seals affixed and duly presented: the chōdai of three districts — Hokocho-machi, Shin-machi, and Shimizu-chō — have been granted relief from their duties. As for the two remaining districts of Yokochō and Hon-machi, their petitions were stayed (withheld) at the hands of the clerical officials (shoyaku-shū). This outcome was duly communicated to those two districts. The three approved districts completed their resignation proceedings (hikiyaku) and made their formal rounds of thanks to both magistrates.
