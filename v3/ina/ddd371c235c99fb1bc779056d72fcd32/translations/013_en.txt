【A Respectful Petition Submitted by Verbal Statement】

Regarding the daughter of Kasuga Rizaemon, a member of the unit under the supervision of Fukaya Chūzaemon, as there is (an established connection/background, as well as) a prior informal relationship, I humbly request permission to receive her as my wife. (This petition is submitted as a formal request on her behalf.)

With regard to the above matter, should you, in your great benevolence, graciously grant our request as petitioned, we would consider it a most grateful blessing. Respectfully submitted.

12th month, Bunsei 12, Year of the己丑 (1829)

　　　　　　　Honmachi
　　　　　　　　Mogamiya
　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Iemon
　　　　　　　　Town representative (chōdai): Chūzaemon
　　　　　　　　Same: Sōzō

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above endorsement and seal were submitted through the intermediary of Gotō Shinzaemon at the magistrate's office, and approval was granted immediately. This has accordingly been communicated to Sōzō, the town representative (chōdai). [Written on the] 25th day.
