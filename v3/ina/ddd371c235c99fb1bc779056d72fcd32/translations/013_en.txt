**Respectful Petition Submitted by Oral Statement**

Inasmuch as there is already an informal relationship (inner connection) with the daughter of Kasuga Rizaemon, who belongs to the group under the supervision of Fukaya Chūzaemon, I humbly request permission to take her as my wife.

I most humbly petition that, through your benevolent consideration, the above request be granted as stated. Should this be permitted, I shall consider it a most fortunate and grateful outcome. Respectfully submitted.

12th month, Bunsei 12 (1829), Year of the己丑

　　　　　　　Honmachi
　　　　　　　　Mogamiya
　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Iemon
　　　　　　　　Town Representative (Chōdai): Chūzaemon
　　　　　　　　Same: Shōzō

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition, complete with endorsement (okugaki) and official seals, was submitted through the intermediary of Gotō Shinemon at the official office, whereupon it was immediately approved. This decision was communicated to town representative Shōzō on the 25th day.
