Humbly, I submit this written petition:

Although I am an unskilled and incompetent person, I was graciously appointed to the position of chōdai (town representative), for which I have been deeply grateful and have diligently served in this role. However, in recent years, my circumstances have become increasingly difficult, and I find myself unable to continue fulfilling these duties. I therefore humbly petition to be relieved of my responsibilities as chōdai.

I most humbly request that, in your great benevolence, you grant this petition as submitted above. I would be most grateful if this should be approved. Respectfully submitted.

　Tenpō, year of Kanoto-U (1831)　　　　　　Hokomochi-chō (district)
　　　　　First Month　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Yaōemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Chōdai: Sōhachi

　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office
