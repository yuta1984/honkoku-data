To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above three petition documents, with seals affixed at the end, were submitted at the official office through the intermediary of Mr. Izawa. On the 15th of the same month, the petitions were promptly approved, and the town representative (chōdai) Uzaemon was summoned and notified of the decision.

---

Respectfully, a petition submitted in the form of a verbal statement

One: I wish to send my younger brother, Gunsaku, to Kenfukuji Temple to become a disciple and enter the Buddhist priesthood. I hereby respectfully submit this petition.

If, in your great benevolence, you would kindly grant the above request as petitioned, we would consider it a great honor and blessing.

Bunsei 13, Kanoto-Tora year (1830), 3rd month　　Fukuro-chō, Naka-ya

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same (chōdai): Tōji

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was prepared in the customary manner (fair copy, with seals), and on the 1st day of the month, submitted at the residence of Mr. Izawa.
