[A Petition Respectfully Submitted in the Form of a Written Statement]

I have been appointed as a purveyor of confectionery to the domain, and have served in this capacity with great gratitude and honour. However, as I have now been relieved of this appointment, I humbly wish to change my trade. I therefore respectfully submit this petition.

Should it please Your Honour, in your benevolence, to grant the request as stated above, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　Tenpō 2nd year, Kanoto-U (1831), 2nd month　　　Kudari-machi [district]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Nakaya [shop name]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: [An?]bei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Mokuemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same [Town Representative]: Tōji

To the Honourable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was submitted on the 15th day. On the 17th day, a letter was received from Mr. Gotō informing [us] that the matter had been approved and settled. This was conveyed to the town representative Tōji.
On the 19th day, the petitioner was accompanied [to the office] and thanks were expressed to both Honourable Magistrates.
