**[Record of Disposition of Previous Petitions]**

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office:

The aforementioned petitions were endorsed in the customary manner and submitted to Mr. Gotō at the magistrate's office on the 23rd. On the 25th of the same month, approval was graciously granted; with regard to the petition for floating timber downstream, the official countersignature was affixed and the documents were returned to us. The town representative (chōdai) Kasazaemon was immediately summoned and duly informed of this.

**[Third Petition]**

Humbly submitted in writing as a verbal petition:

As I wish to engage in the trade of tofu, I have entered into private discussions regarding the acquisition of a tofu mold box (katawako) by transfer from Katsuzō of Hokomochi Village. I therefore humbly submit this petition for your consideration.

Should the above request be graciously granted in accordance with this humble petition, I would consider it a most fortunate blessing. Respectfully submitted.

1st Month, the Year Bunsei 13 (1830), Kanoto-i [Year of the Boar]
Serimachi
Masudaya
　Petitioner: Mohei
　Group Head (kumigashira): Morisaemon
　Town Representative (chōdai): Jisaemon

**[Fourth Petition (opening section)]**

Humbly submitted in writing as a verbal petition:

Item: As my adopted son Shōhachi has proven incompatible [with our household], we have dissolved the adoption, and he has been returned to the home of Gihēji in Shimoteryō Village, Teryō Township, within the domain of Lord Hakura Geki. I therefore humbly submit this petition for your consideration.
