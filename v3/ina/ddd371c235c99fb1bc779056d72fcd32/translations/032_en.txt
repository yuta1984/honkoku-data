One item: In this matter, Nagaosuke's paternal aunt, a resident of Nakatsubo Village in Tera-go district — which falls under the jurisdiction of Lord Chimura Heizaemon — is related to me by a bond of close kinship. As the said Nagaosuke has passed away, I have no choice but to wish to take her in as a dependent in my household. I therefore humbly submit this petition.

If, through your benevolence, you would be so gracious as to issue the order as petitioned above, I would be most grateful and consider it a great blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13, Kanoto-Tora year, 3rd month　　　　　Yokomachi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kazuya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner:　Chūbei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ward representative (chōdai):　Katsuzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same role:　Heijūrō

　　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was endorsed and sealed at the magistrate's office, and submitted through the intermediary of Lord Gotō Shinzaemon, whereupon it was immediately approved. The ward representative Heijūrō was summoned and the decision was conveyed to him.
