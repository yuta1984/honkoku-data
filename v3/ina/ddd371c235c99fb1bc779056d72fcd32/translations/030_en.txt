Respectfully, a petition submitted in the form of a verbal statement

During the 10th month of Bunka 14, the Year of the Ox [1817], the village headmen (nanushi) of Miyada Kitagawari village — Soemon, Gen'emon, and Yojirōemon — came to visit, stating that they had a matter to bring before the authorities (kōso) and requesting that I provide them lodging during the period of official inquiry (otadashi). As I had never previously provided lodging for such purposes, I declined; however, as they earnestly pressed me, I had no choice but to comply. From the 10th month through the 28th day of the 12th month, I provided lodging and meals, totaling 120 meals (including overnight stays and lunches) at my establishment. I also advanced payment for various other necessary goods on their behalf, and the combined total of lodging fees and the cost of miscellaneous goods came to 5 ryō in gold, which became a loan. As the year-end was approaching, they informed me that they would settle the accounts by gathering the funds in due course. Even when the following Year of the Tiger arrived, no settlement was made, and despite repeated demands for repayment, as winter came on, they brought a promissory note (shōmon) bearing the joint seals of all three men, stating that they would add interest at the rate of 12.5% and would definitely repay the amount by the 12th month of the coming Year of the Rabbit, earnestly pressing me to accept these terms. Having no choice, I acquiesced to their proposal. However, at the close of the Year of the Rabbit, they brought only 2 bu 2 shu as interest payment, and informed me they wished to extend the deadline until the following spring. I repeatedly pressed for repayment thereafter, but they made no payment whatsoever and continued to delay, until in the intercalary 8th month of the Year of the Monkey, they stated that they would repay by the end of the current year...
