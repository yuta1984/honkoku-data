A matter humbly petitioned by means of this written statement:

As I have no male heir, there is a man called Minzō who currently resides as a dependent (yakkai) of Chūkichi in Kamiyokoda-machi, Matsumoto, within the domain of Lord Matsudaira Tango-no-kami. As there is also a familial connection with this person, I have privately discussed the matter of taking him in — together with his father, mother, and one daughter — and having him succeed to my household. For this reason, I submit this humble petition.

If, through your benevolence, you should be pleased to issue an order in accordance with this humble petition as stated above, I would consider it a most grateful blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13, Kanoto-Tora year, 3rd month　　　Serimachi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner:　Mon'emon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Group head (kumigashira):　Uhei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ward representative (chōdai):　Jizaemon

　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above two petitions, with endorsements and seals affixed, were submitted on the 7th day at the magistrate's office through the intermediary of Mr. Gotō. They were immediately approved and granted. The ward representative Jizaemon was summoned and the decision was conveyed to him.
