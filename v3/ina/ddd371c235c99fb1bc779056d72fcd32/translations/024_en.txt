**Petition Submitted via Written Statement**

I have been engaged in the retail sale of white wood (hakugi/shiraki), but I humbly wish to temporarily suspend this business for a period of time. I therefore respectfully submit this petition.
Should you, in your great benevolence, graciously grant this request as petitioned, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　　Bunsei 13, Year of Kanoe-Tora (1830), Second Month　　Hon-machi, Masuya
　　　　　　　　Petitioner:　　　Nakauemon
　　　　　　　　Town Representative (chōdai):　　　Chūzaemon
　　　　　　　　Same:　　　　Shōzō

　　　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above three petitions, bearing signatures and seals at their conclusions, were submitted to the magistrate's office on the seventh day of the same month, whereupon they were immediately approved. Notification of the decision was conveyed through the intermediary of Mr. Iguchi, and accordingly the town representatives of both districts were summoned and informed of the ruling.
