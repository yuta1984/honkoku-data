**Petition Submitted via Oral Statement**

With great reverence, I hereby humbly submit this petition in the form of a written statement.
I have been engaged in the retail sale of white wood (shiraki), but I wish to suspend this business for a period of time. I therefore humbly submit this petition.
Should you, in your great benevolence, be pleased to grant this request as petitioned, I would be most grateful. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13th year (1830), Year of the Kōin (庚寅), Second Month　　Honmachi, Maruya
　　　　　　　Petitioner:　　Nakauemon
　　　　　　　Town Representative (Chōdai):　　Chūzaemon
　　　　　　　Same:　　Shōzō

　　To: The Honorable Magistrate's Office

---

**Administrative Note Regarding Processing**

The above three petitions were marked with red ink notations and seals affixed, and were submitted to the magistrate's office on the 7th of the same month. We subsequently received word through the intermediary Mr. Iguchi that the petitions were immediately approved. Accordingly, the town representatives (chōdai) of both towns were summoned and duly informed of the decision.
