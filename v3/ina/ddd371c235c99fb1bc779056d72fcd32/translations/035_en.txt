A matter humbly petitioned by means of this written statement:

Regarding my relative Shirataya Rihei — as his household has been temporarily dissolved and left vacant — I humbly wish to have his son Torajirō reestablish the family line. As the boy is still of young age, I intend to have him reside with me, and once he has grown older, to have him set up as a separate household. For this reason, I submit this humble petition.

If, through your benevolence, you should be pleased to issue an order in accordance with this humble petition as stated above, I would consider it a most grateful blessing. Respectfully submitted.

Bunsei 13, Kanoto-Tora year, 3rd month　　　Serimachi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Fujinoya [shop/house name]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner:　Shōbei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Group head (kumigashira):　Chūkichi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ward representative (chōdai):　Jizaemon

　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was immediately approved and granted on the 7th day.
