**[New Petition Submitted by Written Statement]**

With utmost deference, we hereby humbly submit this petition by written statement.

My adopted son Yaichi has proven incompatible (with our household), and I have dissolved the adoption. Having petitioned Lord Matsudaira Tamba-no-Kami in this matter, he has been returned to the home of Yaemon of Naganoka Village in Kami-Ina district. It is on account of this that I humbly submit this petition.

Should your honorable compassion grant this petition as humbly requested above, I would consider it a most grateful fortune. Respectfully submitted.

　　Bunsei 13th year, Kanoto-Tora (1830), 2nd month　　Fukuro-machi
