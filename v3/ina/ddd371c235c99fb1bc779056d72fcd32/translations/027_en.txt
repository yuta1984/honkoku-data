**[New Petition Submitted by Written Statement]**

With utmost deference, we hereby humbly submit this petition by written statement.

My adopted son, Yaichi, having proven to be an unsuitable match, I have dissolved the adoption. Having petitioned Lord Matsudaira Tanbanokami in this regard, Yaichi has been returned to the household of Yaemon in Nagaoka Village, Kamiina district. On account of this, I respectfully submit this petition.

Should your honorable compassion grant this petition as humbly requested above, I would consider it a most grateful fortune. Respectfully submitted.
