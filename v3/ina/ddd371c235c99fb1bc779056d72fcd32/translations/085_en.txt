[A Petition Respectfully Submitted in the Form of a Written Statement]

The family line of Fujiya Iwazaemon has been in abeyance for some time. On this occasion, Tsunekichi, who resides in my household as a dependant, has a familial connection [to the Fujiya line]. We have therefore discussed the matter privately, and I wish to have him succeed to the family line and be added to the religious census register of Naka-machi. I therefore respectfully submit this petition.

Should it please Your Honour, in your benevolence, to grant the request as stated above, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　Tenpō 2nd year, Kanoto-U (1831), 2nd month　　　Shimo-machi [district]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sakanaya [shop name]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Relative / Petitioner: Kichiemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Naka-machi Town Representative:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Tōzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same [Town Representative]:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kanzō

To the Honourable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was submitted on the 15th day. On the 17th day, a letter was received from Mr. Gotō informing [us] that the matter had been approved and settled. The town representative Tōzaemon was summoned and informed accordingly.
