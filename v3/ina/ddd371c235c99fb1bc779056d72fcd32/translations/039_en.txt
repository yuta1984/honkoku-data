The above petition was duly endorsed (okugaki) as customary and submitted through the intermediary of Lord Izawa at the official office on the 5th day. On the 15th of the same month, approval was granted and the document was returned bearing the official reverse seal (urajirushi). Accordingly, the block representative Jingorōzaemon was summoned and notified.
