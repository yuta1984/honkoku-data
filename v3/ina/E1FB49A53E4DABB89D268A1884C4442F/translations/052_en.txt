【Right folio】
　　Humbly submitted by written petition

Regarding the two mountains known as "Hokubō" and "Nantō" within the Ōmagari Valley at the entrance of the Mibu River: we had previously petitioned to conduct trade in small white timber (kozashiki) during the current year of Mi (Tenpō 4, 1833). However, due to difficulties arising in the logging operations, we were unable to produce the required number of pack-horse loads. We therefore humbly request permission to operate in the next designated mountain during the coming year of Uma (Tenpō 5, 1834). We most respectfully ask that, in your great benevolence, our petition be granted, for which we would be most deeply grateful.

【Left folio】
　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kyūgorō (seal)

We confirm that the above petition submitted by Kyūgorō is accurate and without any discrepancy. Should your honorable mercy see fit to grant this petition as requested, we would all be deeply and humbly grateful, as we have always been. Respectfully submitted.
