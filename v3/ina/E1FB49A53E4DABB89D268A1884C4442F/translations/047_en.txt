**Letter of Acceptance and Undertaking**

Regarding this matter:

We hereby submit this letter of acceptance concerning the unwanted/undesirable trees (akugi) within the domain forest of the Higashiwari area of Higuchimura, for which we have submitted our petition. Now that we have received the disposal rights to the previously felled timber from said forest, we pledge that we will cause no inconvenience or trouble whatsoever with respect to the said forest. Furthermore, we pledge that we will under no circumstances use an axe or engage in any cutting of the remaining trees. We hereby submit this written pledge of acceptance as confirmation thereof.

Thus it is as stated above.

　　　　　　　　　　　　Matsudaira Tanbanokami's Entrusted Territory
9th month, Tenpō 4, the year Mizunoto-Mi (1833)
