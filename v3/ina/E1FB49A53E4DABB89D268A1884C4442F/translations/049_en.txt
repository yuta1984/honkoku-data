【Right Page】
　　Humbly submitted petition in written form

Regarding Taihei, a woodturner (kijishi) residing in the Ogurokawa御立山 (domain forest), as the conditions of that mountain have changed and he finds it difficult to continue his craft there, he has expressed his wish to relocate to Ōtawara. We most humbly beseech your compassionate consideration and respectfully request that permission be granted as petitioned. We would be deeply grateful for such a benevolent decision. Respectfully submitted.
