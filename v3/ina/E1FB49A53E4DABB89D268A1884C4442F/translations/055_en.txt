[Left folio]

We humbly submit this written petition:

Regarding the area known as Konaginata-guchi-dōri Iriya, within the lord's reserved mountain (o-tateyama), we respectfully request permission to harvest undergrowth (shitagusa) as has been customary in previous years. Should you graciously grant our request out of your benevolence, we would consider it an immense honor and blessing. Respectfully submitted.
