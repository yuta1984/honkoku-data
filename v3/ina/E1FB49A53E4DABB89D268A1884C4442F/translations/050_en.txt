To: Itō Genta
To: Takada Yoshiharu (Zenjī)
To: Misawa Fukaemon

The above petition was submitted to the Monthly Duty Officer (Getsuhan) on the 27th day of the 9th month, and on the same day approval was granted and notification was made to the village.

　　Humbly submitted petition in written form

Item: Regarding the domain forest (御林) of Kojinyama, which was granted/transferred to the village, a mountain-hand rice levy (yamate-mai) of eight shō per tan has been imposed, and we have been making the payments accordingly. Of the said forest's total area of 3 chō, 3 tan, 3 se, and 10 bu, we humbly request that the newly cultivated field area of 1 chō, 7 tan, 7 se, and 9 bu be exempted from the levy, and that the mountain-hand rice levy be applied only to the remaining 1 chō, 5 tan, 6 se, and 1 bu. We most humbly beseech your compassionate consideration and request that this be permitted. We would be deeply grateful for such a benevolent decision.
