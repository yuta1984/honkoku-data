　　　　A humble petition respectfully submitted in writing

Item: Until now, I have been under the care and employ of Yūemon, a lumber craftsman [mokusenshī] of the Funagata domain forest, working as a lumber craftsman myself. However, due to poor health, I find it extremely difficult to continue making a living. Accordingly, I have separately entrusted my wife, children, and relatives [to others' care], and humbly petition to dissolve my household [retire from the occupation] in the spring. I most earnestly request that through your benevolence, my petition be granted as stated, [and should it be so...]
