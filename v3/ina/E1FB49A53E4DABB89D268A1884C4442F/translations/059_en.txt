**[Right leaf]**

We humbly submit the following petition in writing.

Regarding the bamboo grove belonging to this village: during the year of Bunsei Inoi (1827), when, in accordance with your intentions, the grove was put up for sale, we were graciously permitted to receive it upon payment of a requested sum of one ryō and two bu. This grove, which had been maintained and protected under domain management, has for many years allowed the villagers to carry out their farming duties in peace and security under your benevolent governance. We are deeply grateful for this great act of compassion.

**[Left leaf]**

In light of the above, we humbly wish to dedicate the said bamboo grove to the domain as "village mountain" (mura-yama) land, offering replacement land from within our privately-held woodland in exchange. We most humbly request that you look upon this petition with compassion and grant our wish.

　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Toshisaburō

The above petition submitted by Toshisaburō is accurate in every respect, without the slightest discrepancy. We humbly request that this petition be granted with your great compassion. Respectfully submitted.
