We humbly submit this written petition.

We respectfully request permission to purchase the portion designated as "inferior timber" (akugi) within the eastern section (higashiwari) of the domain's forest in Higuchimura, for the sum of twenty-six ryō. Of this amount, nine ryō has already been remitted during the past winter. The remaining balance shall be paid upon your honorable instruction. We most humbly implore that, in your gracious benevolence, you grant our petition as stated above. We would consider it a most grateful fortune. Respectfully submitted.
