**Respectful Petition Submitted in Writing**

We humbly petition regarding the following matter:

We wish to purchase the unwanted/undesirable trees (akugi) within the domain forest located in the Higashiwari area of Higuchimura, within your domain's territory, for the sum of twenty-six ryō. Of this amount, nine ryō was already submitted as payment during last winter. The remaining balance will be paid promptly upon your instruction. We most humbly and respectfully request that, in your benevolence, our petition be granted as described above. Should this be approved, we would be most gratefully obliged.

Respectfully submitted.
