　　Humbly submitted by written petition

Regarding the forests of Irino-ya-gō and Kogure River, which I have been managing up until now under contract: as the term of said contract expires this year, I humbly petition for permission to operate in the next designated mountain over a period of three years, from the coming year of Uma (Tenpō 5, 1834) through the year of Saru (Tenpō 7, 1836).
