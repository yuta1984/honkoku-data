**[Left leaf]**

We humbly submit the following petition in writing.

Regarding the protected forest (*tatebayashi*) belonging to this village: during the year of Bunsei Inoi (1827), when, in accordance with your intentions, the forest was put up for sale, the village officials submitted a petition and were graciously permitted to receive it on behalf of the village upon payment of thirteen ryō. This forest, which had been maintained and protected under domain management, has for many years allowed the villagers to carry out their farming duties in peace and security under your benevolent governance. We are deeply grateful for this great act of compassion.
