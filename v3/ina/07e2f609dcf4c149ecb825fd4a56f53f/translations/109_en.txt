**[Right folio]**

*We humbly submit this petition in the form of a verbal statement:*

As I find myself unable to make a proper living, I wish to travel to the establishment of Ōmiya Shōbei, located at Shibaguchi 3-chome in Edo, to serve as a traveling salesperson (uriko) for a period of five years beginning in the second month of this Year of the Boar (Kaei 4). Accordingly, I humbly request that I be granted an exemption from the religious census seal (*shūmon muhan*) for the duration of said term.

With the above in mind, I humbly beseech your benevolent consideration. Should our request be granted as stated, we would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Hokomochicho [district]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Tamaya [shop name]
Kaei 4, Year of Kanoto-I (1851), 2nd month　　Petitioner: Naoshichi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Jūsaburō

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was submitted through the intermediary of Mr. Nakayama on the 20th, and was approved on that same day. Notification was received from Mr. Izawa and duly conveyed to the town representative.

**[Left folio]**

*We humbly submit this petition in the form of a verbal statement:*

As my dependent (*yakkai*) Shichijūrō has a familial connection (*naien*) to Shōuemon of Noguchi Village in Tera-go, which falls under the domain of Lord Chimura Heizaemon, I wish to send him to reside there. I therefore humbly submit this petition.

With the above in mind, I humbly beseech your benevolent consideration. Should our request be granted as stated, we would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Yokocho [district]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Hotei-ya [shop name]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Shōzō
Kaei 4, Year of Kanoto-I (1851), 2nd month　　Town Representative: Heijūrō

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

This petition was approved on the 14th of the 3rd month, and the town representative was duly notified.
