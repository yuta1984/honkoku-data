**[Right folio]**

A Petition Humbly Submitted in Writing

From the Year of the Boar to the Year of the Snake — Second son
　　Seven-year apprenticeship　　　　Kichiya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Age: 16 years old

I humbly petition that my second son, Kichiya, although not yet formally taken on as a live-in apprentice (*uchi-deshi*), be allowed to learn the trade of a shingle roofer (*kaki-yane-shi*). Although my son has not yet entered an apprenticeship period, I request that, as a second apprentice, he be permitted to pay the *omizuyaku* (water tax/guild fee) and be allowed to practice the trade independently.
I humbly ask that, in your great benevolence, this petition be granted as requested, for which I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　Kaei 4, Year of the Boar (1851), 3rd Month

**[Left folio]**

　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office　　　　Shinmachi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Kanya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Sōshichi

A Petition Humbly Submitted in Writing

I have heretofore resided in Shinmachi, fulfilled my *omizuyaku* obligations, and worked as a carpenter. On this occasion, as I am moving to Shimizumachi to continue my trade, I humbly request that my official license (*kansatsu*) be reissued in accordance with my change of address.
I humbly ask that, in your great benevolence, this petition be granted as requested, for which I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　Kaei 4, Year of the Boar (1851)　　　　　　Shinmachi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Mokuzaemon
　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office　　Town Representative: Sōshichi
