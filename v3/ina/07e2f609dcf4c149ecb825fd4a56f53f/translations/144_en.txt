　　　　A humble petition respectfully submitted in writing

　　I humbly wish to commence trading in used goods (secondhand merchandise) from this year onward. I therefore respectfully submit this petition.
　　Should it please you, in your benevolent compassion, to grant our petition as stated above, we would consider it a most gracious and fortunate favor. Respectfully.

　　　　To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was submitted through the intermediary of Mr. Izawa at the Office on the 16th day. On the 27th, word came from Mr. Suzuki that we were to bring our personal seal and present ourselves at the residence of Lord Yasuda Sauemon on the last day of the month. Accordingly, this was communicated to the Honmachi town representative.
