[Left folio]
　　A humble petition respectfully submitted in writing

Regarding Kumatarō, the younger brother of Iseya Shinuemon of Nakamachi, who is a relative of ours: in Tenpō 3rd year of the Dragon (1832), we submitted a petition and sent him to stay with a relative, Taeuemon, in Mishima-juku in Izu Province. Subsequently, in Tenpō 11th year of the Rat (1840), he returned home having separated from the household in Mishima-juku. However, before we were able to submit a petition for his re-registration in the temple registry, he committed an offense against the laws during the 6th month of the following 12th year of the Ox (1841) and was ordered into exile. Since then, he has gradually reformed himself and deeply regretted his past transgressions. During this time, in the 4th month of this year, his elder brother Shinuemon died of illness, leaving only one female sibling, and the family line now faces extinction. We therefore humbly beseech your compassionate mercy to grant a pardon allowing Kumatarō to return to the family.

We most humbly pray that, through your benevolent compassion, our petition as stated above may be granted,
