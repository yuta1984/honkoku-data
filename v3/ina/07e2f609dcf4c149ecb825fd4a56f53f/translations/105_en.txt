　　Humbly submitted by way of oral petition

My elder brother Hanbei was, as previously petitioned in the past Year of the Dragon, sent to serve as a shop apprentice (uriko hōkō) at Sakaiya Jihei's establishment in Edo Tadokoro-chō.

【Left folio】

However, said Jihei passed away in the 5th month of the past Year of the Dog, and there was no one to succeed to his estate. As we are also related by family ties, I have privately arranged to send my elder brother Hanbei as an adopted son, so that he may succeed to Jihei's estate. I therefore humbly submit this petition.

In light of the above, [I humbly beseech your] benevolence... (abbreviated)
