【Left folio】

20th day of the 8th month:
Item: Kinzaemon and Mataemon appeared at the magistrate's office upon official summons, whereupon they received instructions from (the party in) Niekawa.
〃
Item: Regarding Denji, he returned to his village to examine the documents, and appeared on the 22nd for cross-referencing and verification.

24th day:
Item: Denji's son Kikuhachi appeared and announced that he would serve as his father's proxy representative. It is said that notification of this was submitted to the administrative office on his behalf.

Same day:
Item: A meeting was held at Matsudaya, and both parties questioned Kikuhachi regarding his understanding of the matter.
Item: Both parties convened together and made a representation to Yamaya Ichirōbei regarding the suit and response.

25th day:
Item: A meeting was held at Yamaya, and an explanation/settlement proposal was given to Kikuhachi.

26th day:
Item: A meeting was held at the same location; an explanation was given to Kikuhachi, and the two parties from Kisō were summoned for discussion.

27th day:
Item: Both parties traveled to Arakawa post-station and Iwasaki post-station, where they spoke with Kikuhachi regarding his understanding of the matter. They were instructed to proceed to the magistrate's office.
