21st Day
One item: A message arrived stating that someone other than Kiso Niikawa's Seiemon would come to take charge of the mediation (waridashi), and requesting that arrangements be made so that the matter could be concluded and a reply letter issued by the following day. We indicated that we intended to attend the meeting and discuss matters, but conveyed that in any case it would be impossible for Kikuhachi to arrange the funds (saikaku) by the next day, and asked that this be taken into consideration.

〃
One item: At the meeting, it was agreed that the reply letter would be issued on the 25th, and arrangements were made regarding the transfer of funds. Kikuhachi returned to his village in order to gather the funds.

22nd Day
One item: Kinzaemon and Magohachi brought a draft of the provisional agreement and settlement document to the magistrate's office and presented it for review, whereupon they received instructions to formally write up the settlement document based on this draft text.

23rd Day
One item: The settlement document was drafted at Tomishima's place.

24th Day
One item: The remaining drafting was completed, after which Kikuhachi arrived, the transfer of funds was carried out, and the signing and sealing (chōin) was completed.

25th Day
One item: All three parties were accompanied to the magistrate's office together, and the matter was brought to a conclusion.

One item: The details of the resolution of this case, along with a written pledge (issatsu) from Kikuhachi and a written pledge from Kuzō, were exchanged between the parties, and all documents are [kept] at Tomishima's place.

【Written vertically at the bottom of the page】
26th Day: Settlement of accounts (kantō) completed at Tomishima's place.
