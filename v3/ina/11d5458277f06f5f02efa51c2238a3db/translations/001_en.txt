On the night of the 27th of this month, at the upper quarter of the Hour of the Tiger (approximately 3:00 AM), an official notice (furegaki) issued from Unuma-juku in Mino Province arrived at Inabe Village in the Kawashimo district and reached Takato. At the middle quarter of the same hour (approximately 4:00 AM), it was forwarded to Kurita Village in Fujisawa district. We hereby respectfully report to you regarding this matter.

Item: The said official notice stipulated that it should be transmitted via the Iida road and the Ina Kaidō as far as Kōshū-Fuchū. However, there is a road running from Inabe Village through Tonomura and Matsushima to Shimosawa. [Correction in original: "from the same place" → "however, from Inabe Village"] The notice came through to Takato Town and continues via the Fujisawa route...
