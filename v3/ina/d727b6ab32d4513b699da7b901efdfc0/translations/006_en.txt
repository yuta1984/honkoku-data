The above written pledge (*ukesho*) and the itemized petition (*kajōgaki*), two documents in total, were submitted on the fourth day of the third month of the Year of the Mi (Kyōhō 10, 1725), in the presence of Takeda Shichirōemon, to Yamagata Gozaemon. Consequently, on the thirteenth day of the same month, the ten persons were summoned to the castle, where the Senior Councillor (*karō*) Hoshino Nuido, Naitō Kurando, the Administrative Officials (*yōnin*) Kanbe Gorōemon, Yokota Jinuemon, and Watanabe Gonnosuke, and the Magistrates (*bugyō*) Takeda Shichirōemon and Yamagata Gozaemon were all in attendance. The Senior Councillor conveyed that, as we had undertaken this undertaking, His Lordship above was also pleased with the matter, and that they themselves regarded it with great joy. Furthermore, Magistrate Takeda Shichirōemon expressed his wishes that, as we had accepted this most important official duty, he would convey to the Senior Councillor that we should not be abandoned in future. Accordingly, one certificate (*shōmon*) issued for this purpose, along with a written note (*kakitsuke*) detailing the monthly financial requirements, were received by us. Respectfully submitted.

1. Regarding the remittance of the household finances (*okatte-muki*) as ordered on this occasion: we have undertaken the contract for the sum of eight thousand ryō in gold, and we hereby confirm our intent to remit the said gold in successive installments.
