The seals (*ingyō*) of Lord Hoshino Nuido, Lord Naitō Kurando, Lord Takeda Shichirōemon, and Lord Yamagata Gozaemon were affixed, and accordingly, on the thirteenth day of the third month, a formal discussion (*danji*) was held within the castle, on behalf of the ten persons named.
