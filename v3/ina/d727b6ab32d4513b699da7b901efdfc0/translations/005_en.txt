1. Should there be any further matters we wish to request beyond the above, we will report them to you at a later time. We humbly ask that they be duly heard and granted.

1. Regarding the Kiso rice payments (*harai-mai*), we humbly request that henceforth, for Kami-Ina alone, these be conducted at the officially set price (*tate-naoshi-dan*) as much as possible. However, should delays arise on our part in making the rice payments, we may need to request — depending on the circumstances — that the rice from the inner villages (*naikō-mai*) also be paid out to outside parties. We ask that such a request be granted when the occasion arises.

1. As we have undertaken the management of this remittance (*okuri*) on this occasion, we are unfamiliar with the details of the remittance operations. In particular, regarding the securing of funds and various other matters, we expect that issues will arise which we will wish to consult and discuss. Accordingly, we privately approached Chaya Heishirō to ask for his assistance, but he did not consent. We therefore humbly petition that he be ordered to give his consent, as we believe that with his cooperation, the continuation of the remittance will be assured.

We would be most grateful if the above requests were granted as stated. Respectfully submitted.

　Kyōhō 10, Year of the Kinoto-Mi (1725), Third Month — Signed jointly by ten persons
