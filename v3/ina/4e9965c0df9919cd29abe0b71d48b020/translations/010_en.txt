One: Along the route of passage, latrines and other unsightly
　areas have all been enclosed with bamboo screens, and in some places
　they have been enclosed with wooden boards.
One: Gaps between rows of continuous houses where one could see through
　have been enclosed with bamboo fencing and such,
　and overall, considerable efforts have been made to make everything presentable.
One: There are distance markers (chō-kui) along the route of passage. These were not
　erected by official order, but we were told that they were put up
　by the villagers themselves as a matter of local custom.
One: We were told that the officials of Lord Ajima
　proceeded to the Namiawa checkpoint.
One: The officials from Iijima came out to meet us,
　and we encountered them along the way.
　　Notably, there are administrative offices (shihai-sho)
　　of the domain at various points along the route.
One: At places like Asuke, it is not that the shops
　are completely closed, but it appears that
　goods are by no means placed on the display platforms
　that extend outward toward the street.
