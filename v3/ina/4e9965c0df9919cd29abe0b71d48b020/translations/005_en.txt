"You have come a long way. How far is the road
you have traveled?"
　In answer: "It is thirty-three ri."
"That is indeed a great distance; it must have been
particularly arduous." At this point, Kankeyū-sama
was seated in the upper room of the ten-mat
reception chamber and said:
"I have just now heard that you have come from Takatō—
thirty-three ri, you say. You have come well from such
a great distance; it must have been particularly
arduous. First, let it be said that the officials
may be at ease.
At this season, there should be plenty of udo and bracken fern.
Tofu should also be made well here,
so that will suffice. There is absolutely no need
to present any fish dishes. If you feel you absolutely
cannot refrain from offering something, eggs would be good.
Are there river fish? If there are, they may be acceptable.
（strikethrough: Other than that, there is absolutely no need to worry about anything further.）
（strikethrough: Are there river fish?）
■ There are fish.
（strikethrough: Regarding [the area] beyond Takatō, I am quite unfamiliar with it.）
If there are good items available, and if they are miso-pickled, that would be even better.
As for procuring fish from faraway places, you should absolutely not do so."
