The above persons proceeded to the large tea room (ō-chanoma) and the lower room (shimo-no-ma) of the same place (Mankōji temple). The magistrate (o-bugyō-sama) took his seat in the upper room (kami-no-ma), and the town officials attended in the same room, where the magistrate graciously addressed them.

Upon the magistrate's departure, the town officials saw him off: they prostrated themselves to the left-front of Hosoda Kyōjaku. The ten district representatives prostrated themselves on the west side, outside the two upright posts (nihon-date).
