On the fifth day of the fourth month, the following notice was issued:

Regarding the pilgrimage to Nikkō this month, the following rules shall be observed from the night of the twelfth day through the evening of the twenty-first day:

Item: As we will patrol the town quarters once each, day and night, the town representatives (chō-dairi) of each district must be present. Furthermore, during patrols, stops shall be made at the offices of the three town officials (san-yakunin).

Item: Members of the patrol groups shall conduct frequent rounds through the town quarters, both day and night, without restriction.

Item: The self-governing guard posts (jishinban) and night watch (yoban) of the town quarters shall be duly ordered to carry out their duties.

Item: The three town officials, the御仕送り (o-shisōkuri, supply/escort officials), and the 大町任 (ō-machi-nin, senior town administrators) shall themselves patrol frequently, day and night without fail, and shall issue instructions regarding fire prevention.
　　Note: A junior inspector (shita-metsuke) will make rounds to verify compliance, so take note accordingly.

Item: Records shall be kept of persons passing through the town quarters. Even in the case of male or female pilgrims (junrei) lodging elsewhere, the household at which they are staying, their home province, and their identity must be reported immediately to the magistrate's office in writing.
