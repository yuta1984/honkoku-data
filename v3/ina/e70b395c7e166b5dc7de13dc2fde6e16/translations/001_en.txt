## Certificate of Mutual Exchange (Agreement Document)

During the fire that destroyed part of the town in the fourth month of Tenpō 5 (1834), the Year of the Horse, an order was issued to widen the streets and improve sightlines as a precaution against the spread of fire. Accordingly, all townspeople widened the street frontages and rebuilt their homes. However, regarding the two storehouses (dozō) of Ariga Jirōemon, which should have been cut back to align with the new street frontage, construction was repeatedly delayed due to the presence of adjacent reserve land (hikae-yashiki).

Then, in the fourth month of this year (Kaei 4, 1851), a directive was issued stating that storehouses which had not burned in the previous fire and which had already been protruding into the street could be rebuilt as-is without any problem. All parties humbly accepted this directive.

However, with regard to the section of Ariga Jirōemon's storehouse in Shinmachi that protrudes into the street alignment by a span of 7 ken and 2 sun, leaving it as it is would result in poor sightlines along the row of buildings, and could well become a cause of decline for the neighborhood in the future. The town officials (machiyakunin) submitted a petition and engaged in repeated discussions on the matter.
