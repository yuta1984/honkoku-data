　4th month, 6th day of the Year of the Boar
　　　　　　　　　Densaku came and [regarding] principal and interest
　　55 ryo in gold　　Repayment; as [Denji] was away, handed to Kyuhachi
　　3 ryo in gold　　　Amount to be handed to the mistress of the house
　　　　　　　　　　 as repayment
　The above receipt is correct without error.

4th month, 18th day
One item: 20 ryo in gold　　　　　[To] husband Masuzo
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　 [and] son Kyuji
The above: went to Kyuzo's temporary residence in Naiekawa, where Kyuzo had returned from Nakazawa on the 16th, and the money was certainly handed over to him in person.
At that time, Sho-Daimon-dono had also returned to Kyuzo's residence.

Kaei 5th year, Year of the Rat (1852)
　　4th month, 8th day: Kyuzo-dono came and [they] examined the sake brewing output and various accounts, divided 20 ryo each, and on the 11th
　　returned via Oguchi road; the two of them together, with Oguchi district's deputy official Bungo
　　coming, [they] went out to attend to approximately 100 koku of stored rice available in Goshu (Omi Province),
　　and after relaying the matter regarding Goshu, [they] returned to Naiekawa from there.

Same year, 10th month, 14th day: Kyuzo-dono came and said [they] should discuss the matter of Goshu; in the meantime the deputy arrived, and the matter of the 100 koku was settled.
　　　　　　　　　Allocation received from Lord Kobayashi
　　100 koku of rice
　　36 koku of rice　　[Denji's] own rice

At this occasion, Den declined [direct involvement], and Naiekawa post-station's Wataya Kyuzo made a direct request, which was passed on as a continuation.
The above rice arrived at Wataya by the 8th day of the 12th month, and on the night of the 9th day of the 12th month
it was destroyed by fire; [they] went to offer condolences on the 12th day.
