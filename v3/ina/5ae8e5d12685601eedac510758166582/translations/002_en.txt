## License for Ceremonial Sumo: The Four-Post Ring Ceremony and Watchtower Drum

Whereas sumo wrestling tournaments have been periodically held in Takatō-machi, Ina District, Shinano Province, through the kind assistance of the parties named herein, and whereas there is a matter of sincere prayer and devotion to the Hokoji Sanza Daigongen Shrine, the local guardian deity of that place, we hereby certify that the Four-Post Ring Ceremony and the Watchtower Drum have been formally dedicated to said shrine. We respectfully request that those named jointly herein shall maintain these rites and continue to hold sumo tournaments in perpetuity. Accordingly, we hereby declare that any persons of the same profession who may visit in the future shall raise no objection whatsoever regarding the aforementioned Four-Post Ring Ceremony and Watchtower Drum. This license is issued to that effect, as stated herein.
