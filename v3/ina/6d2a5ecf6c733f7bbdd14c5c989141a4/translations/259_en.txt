…persuading the gates of the various lords (*daimyō*), appearing and disappearing while making their presence known, shrinking from no hardship, and staking their very lives — all of this arose from the deepest sincerity of purpose: to make clear the righteous cause and to restore the fortunes of the Imperial House (*kōun*). Their resolve is truly worthy of the highest praise. How much more so for those who have rendered great meritorious service to the nation — could it be suffered that their deeds should fade and be lost to time? It is with deep sorrow and compassion that His Majesty reflects upon this. Therefore, in order to display their devotion and steadfastness to all under heaven and to console their loyal souls, it has been decreed that a shrine (*sha'u*) shall be erected in an auspicious location in the Higashiyama district, where the spirits of these persons shall be enshrined together in perpetuity. Furthermore, it is hereby ordained that the good people of the realm shall broadly honor loyalty and righteousness, and shall strive and press forward with renewed vigor.

　　Fifth Month

Since the Battle of Fushimi this past spring, in the subsequent punitive campaigns of the Eastern Expedition (*Tōsei*) in various locations, those who died in battle in loyal service — who, day and night, traversed mountains and rivers, were exposed to wind and rain, and endured countless hardships, ultimately giving their lives for the sake of [the nation and] their families — are regarded with especially deep compassion and sympathy by His Majesty. Their loyal devotion and righteous valor are truly the standard and model of the way of the warrior (*shidō*). Moved by profound Imperial admiration (*eikan*), it has been decreed that a new shrine shall be newly established in the Higashiyama area, where these spirits shall be enshrined and worshipped in perpetuity.
