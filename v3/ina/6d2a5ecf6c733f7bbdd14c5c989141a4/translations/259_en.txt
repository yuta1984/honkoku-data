…going from gate to gate among the feudal lords to persuade them, appearing and disappearing, leaving traces of their presence, shrinking from no hardship, and risking their very lives — all of this was the product of their earnest devotion and strenuous effort to make righteousness manifest and to restore and recover Imperial fortunes. Their determination is truly worthy of the highest praise. How much more so could one endure seeing the great meritorious deeds rendered to the nation by such persons gradually fade into obscurity and oblivion with the passing of years? His Majesty has pondered this with the deepest sorrow and concern. Therefore, in order to make their steadfast resolve known throughout the realm and to comfort their loyal souls, it has been decreed that a shrine (*shau*) shall be established in a fine and auspicious location on the Eastern Hills (*Higashiyama*), where the spirits of these individuals shall be permanently enshrined together. Furthermore, it is hereby ordered that the good people of the realm shall broadly esteem loyalty and righteousness, and shall exert themselves with renewed vigor and dedication.

　　Fifth Month

Since the Battle of Fushimi this past spring, in the subsequent punitive campaigns of the Eastern Expedition in various provinces, those who died in battle through loyal devotion — having traversed mountains and rivers day and night, exposed to wind and rain, enduring a thousand hardships and ten thousand toils, ultimately giving their lives solely for the sake of [the Imperial] household — are regarded with especially deep compassion and sorrow by His Majesty. Given that their loyalty and righteous valor truly stands as a model and standard for the Way of the Warrior (*Shidō*), in an outpouring of Imperial admiration and gratitude, it has been decreed that a new shrine shall be newly established at Higashiyama, where they shall be enshrined and venerated in perpetuity.
