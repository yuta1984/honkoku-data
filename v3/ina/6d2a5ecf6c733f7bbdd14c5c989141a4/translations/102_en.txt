　　　　　Takasago-chō [district]
　　　　　Izawaya [merchant house]
　　　　　　Sōemon

Regarding the above-named individual: in response to last year's poor harvest, [he] made a formal petition, contributing from his own means for the purpose of disaster relief. In recognition of this exceptional service, he has been granted, as a special dispensation, the permanent privilege of wearing [the garments appropriate to] the chōdai rank, and the right to an audience (o-memie). Furthermore, from the earliest opportunity, he has been permitted to wear a single sword (one blade) in lieu of the chōdai [insignia]. He is further encouraged to continue [his diligent service]. That is all.

　　10th day of the 5th month
