Item: Regarding the location of the port opening on the western coast — it has been determined that Niigata shall serve as the designated port, where foreign nationals shall reside for the purposes of trade. In all matters, following the precedent established at Kanagawa, Nagasaki, and Hakodate, goods and products from various provinces may be transported broadly and sold freely. However, as there are certain circumstances requiring consideration, and following consultations with the envoys (ministers) of the respective foreign nations, the opening of the port, which was scheduled for the 7th day of the coming 12th month, has been postponed until the 9th day of the 3rd month of the coming Year of the Dragon (Tatsu). This matter should be duly noted and understood.
