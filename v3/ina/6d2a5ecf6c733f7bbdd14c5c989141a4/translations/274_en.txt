【Right folio】

1. When a general on campaign (征討の将軍) traveling through Kyoto does so during a period of heightened security, he too shall obtain the official seal of this office. When retainers (幕下の士) stationed in other provinces travel in various directions, they shall press the seal of their affiliated military headquarters (軍府) into the horse-and-porter registry (人馬帳), carry it with them, and proceed accordingly.

1. When officials of the various bureaus (諸局官吏) and merchant purveyors (用達町人) travel to Kyoto on official business, they shall present the horse-and-porter registry to this office along with a covering letter (添翰) from the Grand Council of State (太政官) and the relevant bureau, describing the nature and circumstances of their official duty, obtain the official seal of this office, and proceed with their own advance notification (先触れ).

1. In cases where the various provincial fu and ken (府・県), feudal lords (諸侯), and other large-precinct shrines and temples use their own individual seals, they shall register those seals with this office and also submit them to each post station in advance, so that when their retainers travel, the said seal shall be pressed into the horse-and-porter registry and carried with them for passage.

1. Until official restrictions on the allotted number of horses and porters for feudal lords (諸侯御定人馬遣高) are formally established, travel along the various highways shall for the time being follow the customary practices hitherto observed.
