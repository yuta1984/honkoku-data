First, for morning and evening meals, beginning with porridge made from coarse grains, all food should be simple and plain, and the practice of storing miscellaneous foodstuffs should be considered of the utmost importance.

First, kudzu roots, bracken roots, mountain burdock, mugwort leaves, daikon radish, turnip greens, taro, eggplant, cowpeas, taro stems and leaves, carrots, burdock leaves, and butterbur seeds — all of these should be collected and stored. Those currently in season and suitable should be used as supplementary foods. Even plants such as red herbs and various greens should be gathered during slack periods in agricultural work, and even children should be encouraged to collect and store these items.
