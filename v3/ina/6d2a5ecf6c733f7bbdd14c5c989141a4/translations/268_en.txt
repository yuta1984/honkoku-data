　　　　　　　　　　　　　Brand mark (焼印)
Reverse side:　Kyoto　　　商法（Commercial Law）　【"商法" and "司印" are each enclosed in separate rectangles】
　　　　　　　商法会所　　司印（Official Seal）
　　　　　　　【The above is enclosed in a rectangle, excluding "裏" (reverse)】

Dimensions: Length (vertical) 5 sun by standard carpenter's square, width 3 sun 5 bu, finished thickness 5 bu.

In accordance with the above template, at each administrative jurisdiction (支配所), both the split seal (割印) and brand mark (焼印) shall be decided upon, licenses (鑑札/鑑折) distributed, and a levy of 20 ryō of gold per 100 koku shall be collected and remitted to the authorities.

　However, within each administrative jurisdiction, the amount of rice used for sake brewing and the names of brewers shall be recorded in full detail in a ledger, and the license fee (鑑折料) shall be enclosed when remitting payment.

As the above has been decreed, it shall be strictly observed without any misunderstanding.

As the above has been decreed by the Imperial Court, it shall be communicated to all sake brewers. Hereunto.

　　　　　　　　Details regarding various monetary matters
　25th day of the 6th month:　are recorded in the expense ledger (控帳).

The above was circulated to towns where various sake brewers under the same officials are located, and [they were instructed] to convey [the orders] with great strictness upon receipt of the copies.
