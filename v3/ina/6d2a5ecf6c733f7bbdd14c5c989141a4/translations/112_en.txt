Regarding the aforementioned persons: they were ordered to serve a penalty (kaka-kin) for the improper and forbidden act of gambling (bakuchi); however, as they are unable to fulfill said service obligation due to illness, by special clemency, a monetary fine (karyō-sen) has instead been imposed upon them. Said fine must be paid within three days.
