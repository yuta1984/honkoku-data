In accordance with the above, as decreed by the Imperial Court, this shall be circulated throughout the town for a period of five days. That is all.
　(Signed:) Intercalary Fourth Month

Since it has been decreed that one copper coin (dōsen) shall circulate at the value of six *nin-zeni* (recognized coin), this was circulated during the Third Month. However, it appears that there remain those who are in error regarding Bunkyu coins (Bunkyu-sen), and it has come to our attention that there are already inconveniences arising from such misunderstanding. Regarding Bunkyu coins, they shall continue to be used as before—one-mon coins and eight-mon coins remaining in circulation as usual. Henceforth, any misunderstanding (shall not be permitted)...
