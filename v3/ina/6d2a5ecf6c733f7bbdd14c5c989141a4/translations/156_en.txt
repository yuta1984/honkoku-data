Item: Regarding persons traveling on official business (goyō) — the post-horses and porters (jinba) allocated to such travelers, as well as goods carried by those persons and official goods (goyō-mono) dispatched from various offices — all such are hereby exempted from fares (muchinsen-tate go-men). [However,] travelers shall pay the officially fixed fares respectively for both overland transport (jinba) and river-crossings (kawaba). As for official goods dispatched without an accompanying person, the fares shall be calculated by weight (kanme-kake) and the appropriate fare shall be attached when dispatching such goods. Please be duly informed of the foregoing.
