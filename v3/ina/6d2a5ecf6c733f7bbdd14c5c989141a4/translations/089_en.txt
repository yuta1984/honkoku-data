　　　　　Honmachi (district)
　　　　　Yamakin
　　　　　　Hanshi

The above-named person is also hereby appointed to the role of purveyor of goods for official use (o-kaimono go-yōtatsu). He shall attend diligently to his duties under the direction of the chief administrator (motojime-kata), applying himself with thoroughness and care.

　　　　The last day of the 2nd month

On the 10th day of the 3rd month, three persons appeared at the magistrate's office on official business, whereupon [the following was declared]:

The title that has hitherto been used for the town headman (machi-nanushi) shall henceforth be changed to "town elder" (machi-toshiyori).

　　　　The 10th day of the 3rd month

The above having been officially declared by written notice, this matter is hereby communicated throughout the town. There are various further official matters to attend to.
