[This notice is to be] shown to fellow officials and forwarded through the proper channels (koguchi-suji) to the branch offices (edaba) of Takasago-chō.

Regarding the relief shelter (o-sukui-goya) at Tonosakawithin the castle town: notice is hereby given. Since the beginning of this spring, there have been persons in extremely dire circumstances throughout the domain; and although relief assistance has been provided, and as previously communicated, the season for the barley harvest has now arrived. Therefore, by the first day of next month (朔日, the 1st), the shelter is to be dismantled and closed, and those receiving relief are to be sent back to their home villages.
