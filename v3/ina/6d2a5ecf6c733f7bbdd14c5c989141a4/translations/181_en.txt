Furthermore, with regard to the branch highways (waki ōkan), effective from the First Month of the coming year of Tatsu (1867), the same increase of sixty-five percent above the base fee shall likewise be collected; as this has been officially notified by the Magistrate's Office (Gobugyo-sho), the matter should be thoroughly understood. In addition, as the notice boards (kōsatsu) will be rewritten and distributed at a later date, this written notice is hereby issued to the transport agents (toiya) for their awareness. Accordingly, there must be no misunderstanding in this matter.
