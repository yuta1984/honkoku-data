　Notification of the Twenty-ninth Day of the Fifth Month
　　　Regulations

Item: Regarding sake brewing — while established rules (*teihō*) have existed since ancient times, on the occasion of the present reformation (*go-isshin*), it has been ordered that brewing licenses (*kansatsu*) be reviewed and renewed. All parties are to submit [their licenses] promptly.

Item: Increasing production beyond the prescribed limits is strictly prohibited. Any relevant parties are to be duly investigated and held accountable.
