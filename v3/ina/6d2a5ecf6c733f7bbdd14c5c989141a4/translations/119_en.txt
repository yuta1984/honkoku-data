In recent years, the transport of regional products throughout the country has been stagnating, causing prices to rise ever further and making domestic distribution increasingly inconvenient. To address this situation, both shippers and the designated Regional Products Office (Gokokusan Yakusho) in Edo and Osaka are to carry out the prescribed procedures. At these two locations, shipments sent from shippers to various wholesalers (toiya) shall be verified against their accompanying shipping documents (okurijō), stamped with a certification seal (oiin), and then forwarded; the method of sale shall be handled as before. Regarding ports and harbors: in shogunate-controlled territories (goryō), the [Regional Products] Office shall apply the official seal; in privately-held domains (shiryō), the domain office shall do the same. Similarly, at other locations and their respective administrative offices and domain offices, ships under sail are required to report to the Regional Products Inspection Office (Gokokusan Aratamesho), declaring the quantities of goods both incoming and outgoing.
