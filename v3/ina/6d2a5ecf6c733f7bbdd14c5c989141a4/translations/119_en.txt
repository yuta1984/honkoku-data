In recent years, the transportation of local products throughout the various provinces has been delayed, causing prices to rise further and creating increasing inconvenience across the country. To address this situation and implement proper controls, joint offices of the "Gokokusan Yakusho" (Office for Local Products) are to be established at both Edo and Osaka. At these two locations, all goods sent by shippers to various wholesale dealers (toiya) must be accompanied by a shipping invoice (okurijō), which will be verified and cross-referenced, after which a receipt stamp (oiin) will be affixed. The handling of sales shall continue as before. Regarding other harbors and ports: for territories under direct shogunal control, the administrative offices (shihai yakusho) shall affix the official seal (okuin), and for private domains (private lords' territories), the domain offices (yakuba) shall do so. For ships arriving at ports other than the two designated locations (Edo and Osaka), both incoming and outgoing quantities of cargo must be reported to the Gokokusan Aratamesho (Office for Inspecting Local Products).
