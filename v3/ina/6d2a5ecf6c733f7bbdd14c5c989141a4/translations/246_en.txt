【Right Folio】
　Fourth Month of the Year of the Dragon 　　Grand Council of State (*Dajō-kan*)

Article 1: Upon the manufacture of paper currency (*kinsatsu*), loans shall be extended to the various domains (*reppan*) in proportion to their assessed rice yield (*kokudaka*), at the rate of ten thousand *ryō* per ten thousand *koku*. [Domains wishing to avail themselves of this] shall submit applications through the appropriate channels.

Article 2: Regarding the method of repayment, [domains] must repay using only paper currency (*kinsatsu*), submitting one-tenth (*ichi-wari*) of the borrowed amount at the end of each year, beginning from the current Year of the Dragon, completing full repayment over thirteen years through the next Year of the Dragon.

Article 3: The paper currency loaned to the various domains shall be received in accordance with the Imperial intent to lay the foundations for a wealthy nation (*fukoku*), and [the domains] shall diligently use it to promote and develop local products and industries, thereby raising the national benefit (*kokueki*) of their respective domains. However, it is strictly prohibited to make unauthorized or indiscriminate use of [the currency] within the domain's administrative offices.
