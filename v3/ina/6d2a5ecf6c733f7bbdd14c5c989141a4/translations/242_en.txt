Following that, with the dispatching of troops to various locations, unforeseen expenditures have further accumulated. Now that matters have reached this point, there is no longer any means of sustaining the domain's finances; and as yet there is no indication of when the personnel (troops and staff) might be withdrawn, nor any clarity as to what course of events will unfold. Truly, we have arrived at an unprecedented state of affairs, and the times have become such that not even a moment's peace of mind can be enjoyed.

The flooding that has recently occurred has inflicted acute distress upon the villages and hamlets throughout the domain, and the situation is one in which relief aid would ordinarily be most urgently desired. The fact that the order has nonetheless been issued [i.e., that financial levies must continue] is a matter of deep regret and consternation; yet all avenues for borrowing from outside sources have been exhausted, and given that even in the service of the Imperial cause (sonnō / kin-nō), the domain has been reduced to such a state of exhaustion that it cannot sustain itself, this must be understood as a truly unavoidable circumstance...
