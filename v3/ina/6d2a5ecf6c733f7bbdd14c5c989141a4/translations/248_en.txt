【Right Folio】

The foregoing is issued with the intent to alleviate the present state of financial stagnation (*fu-yūzū*), in accordance with [the authorities'] benevolent and compassionate (*jinjutsu*) intentions. There must be no misunderstanding in this regard. Furthermore, since the prescribed method entails lending by means of paper currency (*kinsatsu*) and receiving repayment in specie (*kinryō*), there shall be absolutely no exchange [of paper currency for specie].

　*Mizunoe* [cyclical year], Fourth Month.

The foregoing has been proclaimed by the Imperial Court (*Chōtei*); accordingly, it shall be duly communicated to villages, towns, temples, and shrines alike. Thus.

　Sixth Month.
