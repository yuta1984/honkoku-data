【Left folio】

The entire domain is hereby ordered to submit *goyōkin* (emergency monetary levies). It is true that an extension of the repayment period has been issued for payments owed to the domain retainers (*gokachu*), but this alone will by no means be sufficient to sustain the domain's finances. With all of this duly and carefully understood, [the village and town officials] are to explain and instruct all persons, down to the lowest commoners, and are to ensure that the collection and submission of the levied funds (*goyōben*) is duly carried out. Further details shall be communicated by the district magistrates (*daikan*).

　　Sixth month [of Keiō 2, i.e., 1866]

The above [directive] was issued on the ninth day, upon the summoning of the town elders (*machi-doshiyori*) and the representatives of the twelve town districts (*chō-dairi*). At the *o-shirasu* (the official public court), with the attendance of the District Superintendent (*gundai*), the Chief Administrator (*omotoime*), the District Magistrate (*daikan*), the *oshiokuri* (liaison/dispatch official), and the Head Clerk (*monokaki-kogashira*), the directive was formally pronounced by the duty officer (*tsukiban*), Lord Sakamoto.

All matters pertaining to the amounts and submission of the levy are recorded in the *goyō-dome* (official administrative register), and on the morning of the tenth day, [the relevant records] were retrieved and handed over to the representative of Ume-machi district (*umemachi chōdai*).
