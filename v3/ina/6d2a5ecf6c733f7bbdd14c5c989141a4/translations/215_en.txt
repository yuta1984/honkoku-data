【Ordinance】

The Christian heterodox religion (Kirishitan jashumon) is strictly prohibited. Should any suspicious persons be found, they must be reported to the appropriate authorities. A reward shall be granted.

Keio 4th year (1868)
　　March
　　　　　Daijō-kan (Grand Council of State)
