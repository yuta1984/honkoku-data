One: Regarding the passage of persons, special inspections shall be conducted; in general, persons shall pass in the same manner as males. Young women (ko-onna) may also pass freely regardless of whether they wear furisode (long-sleeved kimono) or tomesode (short-sleeved kimono).
[However, lingering in certain areas is restricted.]

One: Regarding hair styles such as teihatsu (shaved head), sōgami (loose/unbound hair), or head coverings (kaburimono), no special inspection shall be conducted in general.

One: Regarding severed heads, corpses, persons who have lost their senses (ranshin-mono), wounded persons, prisoners, and others — those accompanying any such persons who do not possess a passage permit (tegata) must submit a written statement before being permitted to pass.
