The foregoing content had been inquired into in Kyoto on the 6th of the month, and accordingly, instructions have been issued that the matter of the [port] opening is also to be publicly announced in this locality.

As the above has been issued as an official notice (o-kakitsuke) from the shogunal authorities (kōgi), it is to be duly circulated throughout the town. Thus stated.

　　　　Twelfth Month

Four copies of the official notices were delivered on the night of the 28th, in four separate rounds, accompanied by the gyōji (administrative officer/messenger).

Those who had been collecting [service] fees were each called forth and given instructions. However, given the troubled and difficult circumstances of the times, the matter was entrusted to Mr. Tanaka, and the notices were delivered to all the chōdai (town representatives) by a single month-duty officer (tsukiban).

Separately, an inquiry has been made regarding the notice that was issued upon the crossing into [another] domain or district on a prior date.
