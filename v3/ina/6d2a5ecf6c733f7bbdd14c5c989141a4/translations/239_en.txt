【Left folio】

　　　　　Tachō (district)
　　　　　Nakaya (shop/house name)
Two bales of honorable rice　　Kichizaemon

The above [recipient], having suffered great hardship as his residence was half swept away in an exceptionally rare flood that occurred recently, is hereby granted rice as disaster relief. [He is] to be well aware of this benevolent favor, and is to continue to strive together diligently in his trade and livelihood under the [authorities'] guidance.

　2nd day of the 6th month
The above was officially proclaimed at the inner adjudication hall (oku-go-shirasu) by the monthly duty official (o-tsuki-ban), Lord Sakamoto.
The written notice was passed along, as communicated by fellow guild members, to Koshichōdai Chūzaemon.
