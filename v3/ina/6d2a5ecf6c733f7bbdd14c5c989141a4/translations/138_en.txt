【Right folio】

Regarding the Goryōsho (Shogunal lands under the administration of a daikan/magistrate) and private domains (shiryō): as for the method of collection and payment by the domain lords, they should consult with Ōkubo Shōzenno-shō and Hoshino Bungo-no-kami, the officials in charge of Imperial Palace affairs, and make payment within the current year of U (卯, the Year of the Rabbit).

　However, regarding temple and shrine lands: it is also acceptable to make payment to the nearest daikan (magistrate).

The foregoing should be communicated, without omission, to all Goryōsho, private domains, and temple and shrine lands alike.
The above is to be duly circulated and communicated.
　　9th month
