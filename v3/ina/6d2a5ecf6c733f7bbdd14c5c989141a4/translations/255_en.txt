【Left Folio】

For the sake of the Imperial Nation, should the meritorious work of pacification not be swiftly accomplished at this very time, the countless multitudes of the people will continue to suffer prolonged misery and hardship. It is with reverence that we note that His Majesty is consumed with Imperial anxieties (*shin'yū*) over this matter day and night.

Therefore, not only those engaged in all manner of affairs both within and without [the Court], but all subjects throughout the realm (*futensotsudo*) shall receive and uphold the Sacred Will (*seishi*), feel deep gratitude for the grace of the Court (*chō-on*), and resolve that their lifelong duty of loyal service (*hōkō*) lies in this very moment. Those who possess military strength shall exert that strength to the utmost; those who possess wealth shall contribute that wealth. With all people, high and low alike, joining forces, and in order to render assistance to the achievement of pacifying the realm (*shikai hōkan no kō*), each and every person must cast aside all private self-interest, reflect upon the great affairs of the realm, and, grounded in the natural principle (*tenri*) of those with surplus compensating for those in want, contribute gold and grain (*kin-koku*) according to their respective means and fulfill their duty of loyal service to the Court. Only in doing so — giving one's life as a warrior who has stepped forward — does one fulfill the fixed duty (*jōbun*) of those who, together with the Court and the realm, serve as its subjects. Let this intent be thoroughly understood and taken to heart.

　However, regarding the matter of repayment (*go-henben*), this shall be discussed and arranged through the appropriate channels.
