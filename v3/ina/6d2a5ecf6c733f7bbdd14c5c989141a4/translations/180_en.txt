The aforementioned three official notices were sent by Takahashi Ōemon on the eighth day of the First Month; they were reviewed together with fellow officials at the town office, and on the morning of the ninth day, were delivered to the residents of Kawamachi and Takasagochō by means of Kishichi (a messenger).

---

As the post towns (shukuba) along the Five Highways have successively fallen into hardship, successive increases to the transport fees for men and horses have been ordered over time; however, as it has been conveyed that continued operation under the existing arrangements is no longer sustainable, the relevant regulations (goshihō) have now been revised accordingly, and effective from the eleventh month of the current year of U (1866), an increase of sixty-five percent (six parts and five tenths) above the base transport fee has been ordered——
