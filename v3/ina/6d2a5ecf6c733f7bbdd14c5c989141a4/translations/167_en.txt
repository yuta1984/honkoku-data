Regarding the opening of Edo to trade (Edo kaishi) from the 7th day of the coming 12th month — although notice had already been given previously to that effect — as there are certain circumstances requiring consideration, and following deliberation and endorsement (dokuhan) with the envoys (ministers) of the respective foreign nations, the opening has been postponed until the 9th day of the 3rd month of the coming Year of the Dragon (Tatsu). This matter shall therefore be duly communicated and announced.

The above notice is to be conveyed, without exception, to all lands under direct shogunal domain, private domain, and shrine holdings alike.
