【Right folio】

　　　　　Shimura Buhei

The aforementioned person: A memorandum recording that during the previous year, the Year of the Boar, [he] was appointed as head of the dyers' guild (*konya-gashira*), and a certificate of authorization was issued to him.

Item: Regarding the inspection and oversight of fellow guild members (*dōshoku*) throughout the domain, and furthermore, at ceremonial and festive occasions (*rei* and *zōmai*), he was seen wearing a sword — yet on this occasion, he overstepped his station and made impertinent and presumptuous declarations. Therefore, [appropriate] disciplinary measures shall be imposed upon him through extraordinary proceedings.

Item: [He] is hereby appointed — in the capacity equivalent to town elder (*machidoshiyori-nami*) — as head of the dyers' guild (*konya-gashira*) of the ships [or riverside district]. Furthermore, [the matter] shall be subject to further deliberation and accounting.
