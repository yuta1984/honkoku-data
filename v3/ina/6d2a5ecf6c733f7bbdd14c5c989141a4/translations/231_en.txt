【Left folio】

At the inspection office (aratame-sho) located within the Gofukubashi Gate, at the residence of Makino Suruga-no-Kami, arrangements have been made to collect the inspection stamp tax. Accordingly, the said goods shall first be forwarded to Edo, where they will be inspected and receive the official stamp, after which they may be sold to foreigners in Yokohama.

However, regarding any goods that do not bear the registered stamp seal of the Accounting Office (Kaikei-sho), it has been agreed that no transactions whatsoever shall be conducted at the said location. Taking note of this matter, the relevant authorities shall issue notifications to all appropriate offices so that [compliance] shall begin from the 1st day of the 5th month.
