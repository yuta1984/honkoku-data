[Right folio]
Received at the official office on the 15th day of the 10th month.

Regarding the passing (薨去) of the Shogun (Taiju), the grief felt by all, high and low, is well understood. Accordingly, an official notice has been issued that: lawsuits and disputes (公事) concerning new fields (新田) are to be suspended; and that in the territories bordering Nagato and Suō provinces (Chōshū domain), all parties are to withdraw and remain calm and settled.

As stated above, since this official notice (御書付) has been issued by the bakufu (公儀), please ensure it is duly circulated.

　　15th day of the 10th month — passed from the [official messenger?] to the main town (本町).
