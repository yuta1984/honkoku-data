【Left folio】

　　　　Shimomachi (Lower District)
　　　　　Ōtaya (shop name)
　　　　　　Jinemon

Regarding the above-named individual: upon investigation into suspicious circumstances concerning him, he claimed that he had accepted the goods as collateral (*shichimono*) without knowing them to be stolen, and had advanced money in exchange for them. However, as he presented no witnesses, and the provenance of the goods was not sufficiently established, his handling of the matter was utterly careless and constitutes an act of misconduct (*furachi*). Accordingly, the goods in his possession are hereby confiscated, and he is sentenced to house confinement (*oshikome*). He shall conduct himself with strict propriety henceforth.
