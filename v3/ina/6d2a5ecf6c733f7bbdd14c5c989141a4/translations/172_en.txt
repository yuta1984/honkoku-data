(Continuing from the previous page) …Such conduct that shows no deference whatsoever to one's superiors is an utterly outrageous impropriety. This has, of course, long been a matter of common knowledge; however, should there henceforth be any person who submits an etsuso (unauthorized appeal), they shall be handed over to the relevant authority regardless of the merits of their grievance, and after a standard inquiry, shall without fail be subjected to strict punishment. Should there nonetheless remain a matter they still wish to petition, they must do so through proper and legitimate channels, bearing the co-signed seal of the town officials (machiyakunin). If they proceed in this manner, a judgment (saikyo) will be handed down following due deliberation (ginmi). It should be noted, however, that matters which fall outside the established regulations — [illegible characters: cases difficult to take up] — shall be returned after an explanation is given. All parties are therefore to be duly informed of and comply with the foregoing.
