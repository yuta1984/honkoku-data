The thirteenth memorial service (thirteenth-year death anniversary) for Lord Itoku-in will be held at ■■-ji Temple from the evening of the tenth day of this month through the twelfth day. In connection with this, outside of one's regular trade and livelihood, killing of living creatures (sesshō) is naturally prohibited; furthermore, the playing of musical instruments and raising of voices shall be refrained from, and extra care shall be taken regarding fire prevention.
　　Note: Construction and repair work (fushin) need not be suspended out of deference.

In accordance with the above, the self-guarding watch (jishinban) throughout the town shall perform their duties from yesterday, the tenth, until the memorial service concludes on the twelfth. In particular, those on night-watch duty shall carry out their service with especial diligence.

The road from Takasago-machi Bridge to the front gate of ■■-ji Temple is to be ordered swept and cleaned.

The above is hereby proclaimed throughout the town.

　　Ninth Day of the Second Month
