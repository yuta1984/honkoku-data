(Regarding the stolen goods,) there is no objection [to the handling of the matter]. On this basis, whether or not the items were stolen is to be conveyed and determined by the person in charge.

　　　　Tenth month, twenty-fifth day
Likewise, regarding the matter within the district (gun-chū), [this notice] is handed over to Shimizu-machi.

---

As the season [of increased fire risk] is now upon us, particular attention is to be paid to fire prevention; on the night of the eleventh of each month, fire sources are to be carefully inspected, and [residents] are to be instructed to remain especially mindful of ash containers (haidai) and similar items.
The foregoing is to be circulated throughout the town. The above is hereby ordered.

　　　　Eleventh month
