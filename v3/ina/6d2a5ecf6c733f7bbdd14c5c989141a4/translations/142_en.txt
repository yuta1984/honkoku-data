Regarding the foregoing matters, this notice is to be disseminated without exception across all domains, whether under direct shogunal control, private lordship, or temple and shrine jurisdiction.
　　Ninth month [September]

The above is to be circulated accordingly.

On this occasion, the shipping agents (*kaisō goyōtashi*) have undertaken to establish a steam courier vessel (*jōki hikyakusen*), which will commence service to Osaka from this month onward. Not only those traveling on official business, but also all retainers, peasants, townspeople, and women who wish to travel by this courier vessel may do so at their own discretion, and should apply to the Shipping Office (*Kaisō Kaisho*).
