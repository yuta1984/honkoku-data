　　Ninth Month

The above is to be duly circulated and announced.

This time, the authorized shipping agents (kaiso goyōtashi) have undertaken to establish a steam dispatch vessel (jōki hikyakusen), which shall commence service to Osaka from within this month. Therefore, anyone wishing to travel by said steam vessel — whether those traveling on official business, retainers of various houses, farmers, townspeople, or women and children — may do so at their own discretion by submitting an application to the Shipping Office (Kaiso Kaisho).
