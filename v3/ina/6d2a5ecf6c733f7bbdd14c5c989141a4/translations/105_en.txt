【Left folio】

Takasago-machi
　Tsubo-ya (shop name)
　　Jinzō

Regarding the above-named individual, in recognition of his conduct and contributions during the previous year — specifically, his efforts in providing relief by offering his financial resources — he is hereby appointed to the role of *chōdai* (town representative) in accordance with established precedent. He is granted permanent permission to use a family surname (*myōji*) and to wear a sword (*tōsashi*), and is granted the privilege of an audience (*omemie*) with the lord. In his daily duties, he shall be accorded the same rank as a *chōdai* and shall be provided with formal ceremonial dress (*kamishimo*). He shall continue to diligently fulfill these duties.

　　Sixth month, seventh day
