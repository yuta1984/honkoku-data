【Right folio】

Regarding the aforementioned persons: on the ninth of last month, at the residence of Matasaburō of Shimomachi, who had hired them, they were found to have violated the prohibition [against gambling], and were therefore arrested and ordered to be imprisoned. Upon investigation and interrogation, they admitted to having engaged in gambling, expressing remorse; however, as their conduct was improper, the following penalties are hereby pronounced:

One: Regarding Matasaburō — as he has repeatedly engaged in gambling on previous occasions, and has once again participated in gambling, and furthermore provided his residence as a venue [for gambling], his conduct is extremely improper; in addition to the reprimand [already given], a monetary fine is hereby imposed upon him.

One: Regarding Jingoemon — in a previous year, there was also an improper matter [on his part]…
