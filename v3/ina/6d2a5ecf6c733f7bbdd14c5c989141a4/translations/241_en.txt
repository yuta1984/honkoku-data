The aforementioned official notice was issued after the Hour of the Eighth (approximately 2:00 p.m.) on the 5th day; accordingly, it is being circulated among fellow officials.
　Coordinator (sewayaku)　Umemachi　Minato (signatory)

Since the Year of the Ox [Keiō 1, 1865], due to an extended period of residence in Osaka (zaihan), extraordinary miscellaneous expenses have mounted unavoidably, and it has been ordered that all persons within the domain contribute as a matter of service (hōkō). With regard to the domain retainers (kachū), on top of the existing deductions from stipends (hikkata), further forced borrowings from stipends (kariage) have been imposed, and while both high and low alike have been enduring hardships,

Since the renewal of the imperial capital (teito go-isshin — i.e., the Meiji Restoration/Imperial Restoration), additional extraordinary expenditures have further accumulated: the journey of Lord Takamatsu to the east, followed by the passage of the pacification envoys (chinbu-shi) bearing the imperial seal along the Nakasendō and Kōshū Kaidō, and the provisioning of food and horses for the troops passing along those routes...
