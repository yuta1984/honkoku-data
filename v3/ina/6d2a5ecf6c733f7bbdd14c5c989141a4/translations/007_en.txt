On the 20th of last month, as His Lordship the Shōgun passed away, all construction work and music (the playing of instruments and other noise-making) are hereby suspended. You are strictly enjoined to conduct yourselves with restraint, and to take particular care with regard to fire.

Please ensure that the above instructions are communicated throughout the town.
The above is hereby ordered.
　28th day of the 8th month

　　　Memorandum

Item: The firing of guns within the town and its surroundings is to be suspended.

Item: Residents of the town and surrounding areas are to refrain from traveling to other places. Those with unavoidable circumstances may be permitted to travel only after their reasons have been heard and accepted by the authorities.
