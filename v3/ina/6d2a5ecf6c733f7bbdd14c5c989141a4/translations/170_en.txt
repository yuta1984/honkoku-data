【Right folio】

Regarding the opening of Hyogo port on this occasion, a commercial guild (shōsha) has been established with the aim of making trade increasingly prosperous. However, it appears that some people have come to believe that direct trade transactions cannot be conducted outside of this commercial guild. This is entirely a misunderstanding. Those who wish to engage in commerce may freely conduct trade transactions at their own discretion, just as is the case in Kanagawa, Nagasaki, and Hakodate.

Regarding the above: for territories under direct shogunal administration (goryō), the matter is to be duly announced without omission by the daikan (revenue/administrative magistrates) and administrators of entrusted lands (azukari-sho); for private domains (shiryō), the same is to be done by the domain lords (ryōshu) and their local administrators (jitō).

The foregoing is to be duly circulated and announced accordingly.
