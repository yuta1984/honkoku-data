【Right folio】

Regarding the opening of Hyogo port, a trading company (shōsha) has been established with the aim of expanding the scale of commerce and trade. However, it appears that some people have come to mistakenly believe that direct transactions outside of this trading company are not permitted. While such a misunderstanding is understandable, it is nonetheless an error: anyone who wishes to engage in trade is free to conduct transactions at their own discretion, just as is the case in Kanagawa, Nagasaki, and Hakodate.

Concerning the foregoing: in the case of lands under direct shogunal administration (goryō), the above is to be duly communicated without exception by the daikan (local magistrates) and the azukarisho (administrative deputies); in the case of private domains (shiryō), it is to be communicated by the domain lords and their local administrators (jitō).

The above is to be duly circulated and announced accordingly.
