…having been found in serious breach of conduct, is hereby ordered to serve a penalty of disciplinary confinement (kaitakin).

Item: Regarding Shinzō, having participated as a member of the gambling party and engaged in the gambling himself, this being a serious breach of conduct, he is hereby ordered to pay a fine (karyōsen) [amount illegible] and to serve a penalty of disciplinary confinement.

Item: Regarding Daiginyū Kichizaemon, having previously been reprimanded for gambling on prior occasions and ordered to conduct himself with due restraint thereafter, yet having again participated in gambling, this being a serious breach of conduct, he is hereby ordered to pay a fine and to serve a penalty of disciplinary confinement.

Item: Regarding Ushitarō and Tōshichi, having previously been appointed to the post of infantry foot soldiers (Hohei), yet having participated in gambling, which constitutes a serious breach of conduct warranting strict punishment, they are, by way of exceptional leniency, hereby relieved of their infantry posts and ordered to serve a penalty of disciplinary confinement.

Item: Regarding the relatives and neighborhood association members (kumiai) of Denshichi and the other eight persons involved — as it cannot be deemed that they were unaware that these individuals were engaging in gambling, which is strictly prohibited by official law, yet they negligently turned a blind eye — this being a serious breach of conduct, they are hereby ordered to pay a fine (karyōsen).
