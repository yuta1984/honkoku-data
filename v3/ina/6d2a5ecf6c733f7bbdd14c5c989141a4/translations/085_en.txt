...and is hereby ordered to perform penitential service (kaitō-kin) as punishment for said misconduct.

Item: Regarding Shinzō, having been present at the gambling venue and having participated therein, and such participation being a serious breach of conduct, he is hereby ordered to pay a fine (karyo-sen) [amount illegible], and to perform penitential service.

Item: Regarding Daiginkichizaemon, having been previously punished on prior occasions for participation in gambling, and having been ordered at that time to strictly conduct himself with due restraint befitting his station, yet having again participated in gambling—this being a serious breach of conduct—he is hereby ordered to pay a fine and to perform penitential service.

Item: Regarding Ushitarō and Tōshichi, having previously been assigned to the role of foot soldier (Kabuki-ashigaru), and having participated in gambling in serious breach of conduct—though strict punishment would ordinarily be imposed—as a special act of leniency, they are hereby relieved of their foot soldier duties and ordered to perform penitential service.

Item: Regarding the relatives and neighborhood association members of Denshichi and the other eight persons: as they could not have been unaware of the prohibited gambling taking place, yet treated the matter with negligence, they are hereby ordered to pay a fine for said misconduct.
