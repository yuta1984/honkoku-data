【Right folio】

One: Regarding Jingobyōe — as in a previous year there was an instance of improper conduct for which confinement within the village had been ordered, [and he has now sneaked out into the town district — this conduct being improper, disciplinary confinement is imposed upon him, together with a monetary fine for misconduct.]

One: Regarding the members of the relatives' and neighborhood-group associations of Matasaburō and three others — it is inconceivable that they were unaware of the gambling that was taking place; yet they had hitherto carelessly associated with [the offenders]; as this conduct was thoughtless and negligent, a monetary fine is hereby imposed upon them.

One: [With regard to the document bearing the joint signatures of] the nanushi (village headman), the machidai (town representative), and the kumigashira (group leader) — should there be any persons who violate the provisions of the prohibitions [set forth therein], [you are reminded that] the manner of handling [such cases] must be carried out with strict [diligence], and this duty must be attended to with sincerity and must not be neglected.
