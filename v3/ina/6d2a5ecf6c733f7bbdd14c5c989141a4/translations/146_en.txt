【Right folio】

…shall henceforth be referred to with reverence as Midai-sama (御台様).

The foregoing was proclaimed in Kyoto on the twenty-first of last month. This matter is to be circulated to all relevant parties.
　　Ninth month

As the above has been issued by the shogunate (kōgi) in the form of an official written notice (o-kakitsuke), it is to be circulated throughout the town. The above is hereby stated.
　　Tenth month

As this was issued on the tenth day, it is addressed to colleagues of the same office.
Delivered to Kōyō and Shimomachi.
