【Right folio】

Previously, it had been ordered and communicated that Lord Iwakura was to be placed under house arrest (gonkinshin) and prohibited from entering the capital (Kyoto), as directed by the Main Headquarters (Gohonei). However, on this occasion, in recognition of his meritorious conduct in managing provisions (ryōshoku), it has been declared that his house arrest is hereby lifted. Accordingly, all celebratory music and other activities are to be conducted as in normal times.

The contents of this notice are to be circulated throughout the town. That is all.

　　21st day of the 3rd month

As this was sent from Hida-shimo, it was shown to colleagues of the same office, and immediately handed over to the town representative (chōdai) of Kōyōshimochō.
