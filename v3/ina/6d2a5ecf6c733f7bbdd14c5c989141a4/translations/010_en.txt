Item: Carpenters, blacksmiths, and all manner of artisans and craftsmen may resume their respective trades, limited to small-scale work appropriate to their craft, quietly and as their regular occupation, from the fourth day of the month.

Item: Regarding the opening of shop front shutters (shimoshitomi) in the town, and the setting up of display stalls, these are permitted to resume as normal from the fourth day of the month. However, given the present circumstances, all matters are to be conducted with restraint and without raising one's voice.
　　Exception: Regarding fish and fowl, these shall be traded discreetly and inconspicuously for the time being.

The above is to be conveyed as a notice throughout the town. That is all.
　　September 3rd

Due to the passing of the Former Right Minister of Hanazono-in, there shall be a three-day suspension of musical instruments and festive sounds (narimono teishi) beginning today. For those in distant areas, the provisions stated in this document shall take effect from the day this notice is received.
　　Note: Although this suspension of musical instruments is in effect, the above provisions regarding commerce and trade are to be understood and observed as stated.
