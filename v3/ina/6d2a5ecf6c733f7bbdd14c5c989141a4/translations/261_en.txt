　　　　　Fifth Month

[Regarding official seals, it is hereby decreed as follows:]
The seal of the Grand Council of State (*Dajōkan*) shall measure two *sun* and five *bu* [approximately 7.6 cm] on the carpenter's square scale;
The seals of the various government offices, domains (*han*), and prefectures (*ken*) shall measure two *sun* and two *bu* [approximately 6.7 cm];
The seals of the various bureaus (*shoshi*) shall measure two *sun* [approximately 6.1 cm].
The foregoing having been officially decided, this is hereby decreed.

　　Fifth Month

17th Day of the Fifth Month — Official directive (*o-tasso*) [received]:
　　Written notices (*o-kakitsuke*), copies — four items
　　Oral directive (*o-kuchitasso*), copy — one item

Throughout the highways (*kaidō*) of the various provinces, the unauthorized private construction and installation of black gates (*kurokamon*) or guard posts (*bansho*), and the like, is hereby prohibited.

　　Fifth Month

In these times of national exigency, given the immense expenditures incurred — beginning with military costs and extending to all manner of expenses — it is hereby decreed that economies and reductions shall be made in all matters, including not only construction and public works, but also preparations and arrangements for the Imperial Court.

　Proviso:
　With respect to the *Ōmiya Gosho* (the Imperial Palace of the Grand Empress), [an exception is granted,] and it is hereby swiftly ordered that [the necessary arrangements shall proceed as] directed.
