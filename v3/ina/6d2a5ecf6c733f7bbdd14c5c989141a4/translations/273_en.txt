(Continued from the right folio) …Should any unlawful conduct occur, in the case of territories under direct shogunal administration (御料), the matter shall be reported to the fu-chiji (府知事) or ken-chiji (県知事); in the case of private domains (私領), it shall be reported to the domain lord (領主). Should further objection arise, the offending party shall be detained on the spot, and the matter shall be reported to this office without delay.

As the foregoing has been duly circulated to each of the post stations, domain lords as well as all those passing through are likewise expected to take note of and abide by its meaning.

　　Fifth month, Year of the Dragon　　Ekitei-shi (Office of Postal and Transport Administration)

1. Since the Fushimi post station (伏見宿) has until now had no auxiliary transport villages (助郷), it has reportedly been collecting substantial fees under the name of "relay horse-and-porter dispatch charges" (継立人馬附出し賃銭). Now that auxiliary transport villages are to be established, relay services shall henceforth be conducted at the officially set fees (定賃銭).

1. The Moriguchi post station (守口宿) has until now been handling relay transport using porters only; henceforth, with auxiliary transport villages now established, both horses and porters shall be used for relay transport, as at other post stations.

As the foregoing has been duly conveyed to both post stations, all those passing through are likewise expected to take note of this.

　　Fifth month, Year of the Dragon　　Ekitei-shi

1. The court noble households (堂上家) and lower court officials (地下官人) at Miya shall henceforth cease using their own personal seals, and when departing on official business, shall report each instance to this office, obtain the official seal of this office in the horse-and-porter registry (人馬帳), and then proceed with their own advance notification (先触れ).
