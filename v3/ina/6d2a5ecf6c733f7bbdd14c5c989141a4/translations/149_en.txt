【Left folio】
　Sakubei, and likewise Minamiwari Zenbei,
　Shimomachi Matasaburō, and four others —
　at the time of the arrest for gambling,
　they fled the scene and only returned later. Furthermore, in previous years as well,
　they had on numerous occasions hosted gambling sessions [at their residences].
　As this conduct is utterly outrageous, they ought to be punished most strictly;
　however, as a special act of leniency,
　they are hereby sentenced to pay a fine (karyō-sen).

Item: As for the relatives, neighborhood group members, and neighboring households of the aforementioned persons — there should have been none among them who were unaware of the gambling that was taking place; yet they negligently allowed it to pass without reporting it. As this is an outrageous matter, they are hereby sentenced to pay a fine.
