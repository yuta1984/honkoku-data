**[Preliminary note regarding Kichishichi's matter]**

It was requested that the documents be sent after making copies. Subsequently, on the 2nd day of the 4th month, Kichishichi attempted to obtain the documents from the village headman (nanushi) and district head (guntō), but as copies had still not been made and the documents had not yet been forwarded to this village, the items kept on hand as copies have not been formally received. Both parties concerned have been notified of this refusal. Fellow officials have also been informed of these various matters in writing.

**[Official Notice]**

Item: Regarding the use of the designation "Kinri go-yō" (Imperial Court Official Use)

It has been observed on various occasions that phrases such as "Kinri go-ryō" (Imperial Court Domain) or "Kinri go-nai" (Imperial Court Interior) are being written alongside transit passes (kafu) or inscribed on stakes and signboards, which is entirely improper. Henceforth, this practice is to be strictly corrected, and it is hereby commanded that only the terms:

"Go-yō" (Official Use)
"Go-ryō" (Domain)

are to be written and used.
