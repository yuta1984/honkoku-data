　　15th day of the 5th month

On the 15th day, the town-side bridge-toll/post [officials] were summoned, and it was announced that [this matter] had become an established domain precedent (okuni-rei). [The notice] was duly handed over to both Ikegami Kanemon and Jiemon. Those who have hitherto received street-vending permits (furisatsu) are to take note [of these regulations]; [the permits] are to be retrieved from the pack-horse keepers (daikai) [by the relevant parties]. Regarding Kumatarō, a ruling was issued today, and [he] was given a combined/unified directive. The above was communicated [to the parties]. 

27th day: The written directive was passed down and handed over to Jiemon's residence.
