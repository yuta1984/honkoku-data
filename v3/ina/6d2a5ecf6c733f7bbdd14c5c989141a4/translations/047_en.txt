(Regarding meal provisions) Although four gō per payment is the established rule (sadamehō), taking into account the hardships of those at the lower levels, it has been ordered that those serving as kannyu-gakari (officials in charge of guidance and persuasion) alone shall receive eight gō. Understanding this intention fully, in accordance with all established rules, nothing beyond one soup and one side dish shall be served. However, there is no objection to serving plain, simple food. In light of this year's poor harvest, frugality and economy in all matters shall be the foremost priority.

The above shall be communicated as a notice throughout the town.
