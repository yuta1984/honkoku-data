【Left folio】

In recent years, it has come to our attention that in some areas, houses have become so densely packed that constructing new buildings has become difficult. In order to provide greater convenience, henceforth the construction of three-story buildings shall be permitted in all areas. However, for the purposes of firefighting and fire prevention, a penthouse roof (lower eave / hisashi) shall be installed at each floor level. Furthermore, in cases where locations that were previously private become visible from such buildings, screens or concealing structures shall be installed so that such areas cannot be seen. Care shall be taken to ensure that nothing unseemly or improper results from this.

As the above has been issued by the shogunate (kōgi) in the form of an official written notice (o-kakitsuke), it is to be circulated throughout the town. The above is hereby stated.
　　Tenth month

As this was issued on the seventeenth day, it was delivered to Shimokawa for the inspection of colleagues of the same office.
