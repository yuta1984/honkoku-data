【Right folio】
Eta (outcast)
　Kanetarō

The aforementioned individual, although acting upon the request of "Kane" from Hokomochi (village), failed to exercise proper judgment and unlawfully detained Iemon of Itatown Village—a law-abiding farmer (御百姓)—as if he were a vagrant (無宿者). On account of this improper conduct, he is hereby sentenced to house confinement (押込め). He is strictly ordered to reflect upon his actions and remain in confinement.

The above directive is hereby officially communicated. That is all.
　10th day of the 10th month

At the magistrate's office, Mr. Kondō and the two of us jointly delivered the written testimony (口書), as well as the official written decree. Regarding the sentence of house confinement, it is hereby communicated that he is to continue in a state of strict self-restraint (■ illegible character).
　Delivered on the 11th day by Asa(—)　　Attending witness:
　　　　　　　　　　　　Ichirōemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　Kanetarō
