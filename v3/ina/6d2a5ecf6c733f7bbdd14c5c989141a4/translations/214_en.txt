【Ordinance】

Regardless of the reason, it is strictly prohibited to: conspire in large groups over improper matters, form factions (tōtō), commit acts of coercive petition (gōso), or to collectively abandon one's town or village of residence, which is called "chōsan" (desertion/flight). Should any such matters arise, they must be reported immediately to the appropriate authorities. A reward shall be granted.

Keio 4th year (1868)
　　March　　Daijō-kan (Grand Council of State)
