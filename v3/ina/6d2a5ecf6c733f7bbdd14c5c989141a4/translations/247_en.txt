(*Continued from previous page*) Those wishing to apply [for loans related to commercial activities in Kyōbashi and Ōmi] shall submit their applications to the Paper Currency Office (*kinsatsu-yakusho*). The amount to be lent shall be determined in proportion to the value of the goods (*sanbutsu-daka*) handled [by the applicant].

Article 5: When farmers and merchants within the domains of various provinces, including those under the jurisdiction of the various regional magistracies (*saiban-sho*), apply for loans, [officials] shall assess the applicant's financial standing and creditworthiness (*moto-kata no kōhaku*), lend an appropriate amount accordingly, and ensure that [the funds] are used to establish and develop industries. Regarding repayment, [borrowers] shall be required to submit appropriate principal and interest payments on an annual basis.

　　*Proviso*: Even in remote villages and out-of-the-way localities, the handling of paper currency (*kinsatsu*) shall be managed in accordance with the customary practices of wholesale dealers (*kusa-toiya*) and traders.

Article 6: When making annual installment repayments (*toshi-wari jōnō*) on borrowed amounts, fractional amounts shall be rounded down (*kirisute*) at the Accounts Bureau (*Genkei-kyoku*).

　　*Proviso*: For amounts borrowed between the First and Seventh Months, one-tenth (*ichi-wari*) of the principal shall be repaid within that same year. For amounts borrowed between the Seventh and Twelfth Months, one-half of one-tenth (*go-bu-wari*, i.e., five percent) shall be repaid.
