(*Continuing from the previous article*) Those who wish to submit a petition shall do so at the ■■ paper currency office (*kinsatsu-yakusho*). The amount of currency to be lent out shall be determined in proportion to the value and volume of local products handled.

Article [cont.]: In cases where farmers and merchants within the territories of the various provinces — including those under the jurisdiction of regional magistracies (*saibansho*) and the domains of various holdings (*shosan ryōchi*) — submit applications for loans, [the relevant office] shall assess the applicant's financial standing and creditworthiness (*mimoto*), lend an appropriate amount accordingly, and ensure that such loans serve to establish and sustain productive industries. As regards repayment, [borrowers] shall be required to submit appropriate principal and interest payments on a yearly basis.

　　However, even in remote or rural villages ■■, matters relating to the handling of paper currency (*kinsatsu*) shall be managed in accordance with the customary practices of itinerant or local trade (*kusakuzuri shōbai*).

Article: Regarding the schedule of annual installment repayments (*toshi-wari jōnō*) on the borrowed sum, fractions shall be rounded down (*sute*) as recorded at the ■■ *Genkei-kyoku* (Accounts Bureau).

　　However, for amounts borrowed between the first month and the seventh month, one-tenth (*ichi-wari*) of the principal shall be repaid within that same year; for amounts borrowed between the seventh month and the twelfth month, one-half of the one-tenth installment (*go-bu-wari*, i.e., five percent) shall be submitted as repayment.
