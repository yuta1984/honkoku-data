【Left Folio】

Following the previous decree, upon further deliberation in recent days, it has been decided that for domains assessed at over ten thousand *koku* with any remaining fractional yield (*hashidaka*), the amount up to whatever thousands, hundreds, or tens of *koku* shall be paid in cash (*kinsen*). Further details of this shall be proclaimed.

　　Seventeenth Day of the Fifth Month
Military Defense Bureau (*Gunbō-kyoku*) — Official Seal

Notice of proclamation: From the tenth day of the Fifth Month, the [Office] has been renamed the Office of Military Affairs (*Gunmu-kan*).
　Seventeenth Day of the First Month — Office of Military Affairs

Directive of the twenty-first of the same month:

　　　　To Tokugawa Kamenosuke:

In recent days, those among the lower ranks [of the Tokugawa household] have acted contrary to proper understanding. Despite the Court's gracious and benevolent intent — having graciously accepted their lord's expression of submission and compliance — they have acted against the spirit of that submission. While their lord himself remains in a state of penitent confinement (*kinshin*), these retainers proceeded to hold celebratory gatherings (*shugen*), assembled at Ueno (*Ueno Sannai*) and other locations, [illegible] killed Imperial troops, plundered the property of commoners, and committed acts of violence in broad daylight, thereby…
