【Right folio】

In connection with the Great Reform of Imperial Government (the Meiji Restoration), and following careful deliberation on the official exchange rates for currencies throughout the realm, the following regulations concerning gold, silver, and copper coinage in general circulation — both past and present — are hereby proclaimed as set forth in the attached document. This notice is to be disseminated without exception to all, from the highest to the lowest.

　　Issued by the Grand Council of State (Dajō-kan), Third Month of the Year Tatsu (Dragon; Keiō 4 / Meiji 1, 1868)
　　　Fourth Month of the Year Ushi (Ox) [date of local circulation]

Attached Document:

　　Exchange value at this rate: 905 ryō, 3 bu, 2 [rin], converted to rice
