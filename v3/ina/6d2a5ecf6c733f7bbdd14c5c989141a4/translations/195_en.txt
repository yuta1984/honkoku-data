Regarding the matter of repairs to the Nakasendō highway, notice having been received from Haideni-iwashi [the issuing authority], and given that His Lordship is currently in a state of confinement [御謹慎], although this had been communicated previously, it is hereby formally re-announced that the matter is to revert to the previous arrangement, effective immediately.

Regarding the above, these matters are to be circulated throughout the town. That is all.
