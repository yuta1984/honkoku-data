Furthermore, regarding passage along various roads and highways: it goes without saying that misconduct of the sort previously perpetrated by bakufu officials and their like must on no account occur. Should those of reckless or negligent intent — down to the very lowest servants accompanying the forthcoming processions of Imperial Court nobles, daimyō lords, minor officials, and retainers — commit any act unbefitting their official authority, engage in extortion or intimidation toward persons along the way, or in general conduct themselves unlawfully, such matters are to be reported immediately to the local tribunal (saibansho) or the relevant government office (yakusho) of the appropriate jurisdiction.
