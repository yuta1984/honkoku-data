(Continued from previous page) …the buying, selling, or consumption [of opium tobacco] is strictly forbidden. Should anyone violate this prohibition and be found out by others, they shall be subject to severe punishment. Therefore, let all persons, down to the lowest ranks, be without any misunderstanding and abide by these regulations.

　　Year of the Dragon (Tatsu)
　　　　Intercalary 4th month (Urū-shigatsu)

As the above has been proclaimed by the Imperial Court, this notice is to be circulated throughout the town. The above is hereby communicated.

　　5th month

As [the notice] was handed down on the 25th day of the 5th month, it was passed along to fellow guild members, the shop supervisory official (mise-gyōji), and Honmachi.
