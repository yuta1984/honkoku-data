【Right folio】

…and on account of the foregoing, suspicion deepened further; however, the manner of interrogation had been considerably lacking in thoroughness, and the matter had been reported as a complaint without any supporting evidence. Accordingly, Torasaburō and the other parties involved were each subjected to formal inquiry (ginmi). As a result of being questioned strictly, it appears that Torasaburō may have made various false statements, and there were numerous suspicious points. Upon further inquiry into the overall circumstances of the matter, he expressed deep remorse and contrition. Moreover, given that Torasaburō is still young in age, he earnestly petitioned, stating that he sincerely repented his previous wrongdoing and wished to withdraw his complaint. In response to this petition and request for withdrawal, while his intent was duly noted and accepted, the matter was from the outset without evidence and had been entirely reckless and improper. Therefore, the punishment of house confinement (oshikome) is hereby imposed. He is strictly ordered to conduct himself with due care and circumspection henceforth.
