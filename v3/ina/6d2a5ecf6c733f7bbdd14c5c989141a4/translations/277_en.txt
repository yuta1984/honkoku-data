【Left folio】

　　　　　　　　[Eta community]
　　　　　　　　　Acting on behalf of Yaichi:
　　　　　　　　　　Rihachi

Yaichi, on account of illness, is hereby duly relieved of his position as *omoteyaku* (表役), as requested.

The foregoing was verbally communicated by the month-duty officer (月番). The *kogashira* (小頭, sub-headmen) were present as witnesses.

---

　　　　　　　　[Eta community]
Appointed to the post of *omoteyaku*:　　　　Kantarō

Appointed to the post of *shoyaku* trainee (書役見習):　　Magonaemon

The above matters shall be duly communicated. Herewith.

　　Twenty-seventh day of the sixth month　　Town elders (町年寄共)

The aforementioned two individuals were summoned to the magistrate's office, accompanied by the *omoteyaku* Ichirōemon. At the Goyōkikibashi bridge, with two *kogashira* present as witnesses, the month-duty officer formally conveyed the instructions.
