　　To: Inoue Unzaburō
　　From: The Secretariat (Monokaki-chū)

On this occasion, in connection with the opening of the port of Hyōgo and the establishment of commercial associations (shōsha), [the following is to be observed]: funds are to be pooled as working capital for engaging in foreign trade, or alternatively, goods may be contributed in kind to participate in trade. Summer goods are to be reported to the Commercial Association Office (Shōsha Kaisho) at Nakanoshima in Osaka. Regarding the commercial procedures on the other hand, gold and silver [currency] shall be distributed to each person in proportion to their assessed productivity (gōdaka), [and] even should [a general] assembly be convened, as regards the trade [— the remainder is illegible].
