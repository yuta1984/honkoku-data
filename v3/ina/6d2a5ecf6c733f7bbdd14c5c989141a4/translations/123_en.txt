Yokochō [Side Street Ward]
　　　　　　Jizaemon

Regarding the above-named individual: there were suspicious circumstances, and various instructions had been issued through the machidai (town representative). Although he had fled, he returned of his own accord after some time due to a misunderstanding of proper conduct. Upon being placed under restraint and subjected to inquiry (ginmi), he made a statement claiming he had committed only a minor offense (kosara). This was deemed improper and outrageous (furachi), and while a strict punishment was to be imposed, given that he had already served time in the training/preliminary detention (keiko rōsha), as a special act of leniency, he is hereby instead sentenced to hand-shackles (tekusari). He is firmly ordered to conduct himself with utmost restraint.

　　30th Day of the Eighth Month [August]
