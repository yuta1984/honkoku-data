【Right folio】

Now that Hyogo (Kobe) is to be opened [to foreign trade and residence], foreign nationals shall be permitted to travel on foot within an area of ten ri (approx. 40 km) from Hyogo, excluding the region within ten ri of Kyoto. Should there be any persons who become involved in disputes or matters of contention with foreign nationals, they shall be summoned before the Hyogo Magistrate (Hyogo Bugyō) and questioned accordingly. It is therefore to be understood in advance that all parties must conduct themselves without impropriety or disorder.

The above notice is to be conveyed, without exception, to all lands under direct shogunal domain, private domain, and shrine and temple holdings in the vicinity of Hyogo.
