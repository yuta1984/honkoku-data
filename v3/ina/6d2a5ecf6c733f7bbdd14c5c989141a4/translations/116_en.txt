　　　　　Honmachi (Main Town)

One monme [of silver/fee]: Kitahara Rizaemon

Regarding the above: Some time ago, concerning the matter of a farmer named Jirōzaemon from Nakatsubo Village in Tera-gō, and in relation to a dispute over front paddy fields (maedachi) between the said party and two individuals — Masaemon and Kinzaemon of ■■ Village — a formal petition/settlement (negai-sadame) was reached, with offerings (soe-zakana) brought in accompaniment. Accordingly, mediation was ordered, both parties' claims were heard and investigated, and the matter was resolved through internal settlement (naisai) handled by the relevant official, reaching a satisfactory conclusion for all concerned. As this matter involved another domain (taryō), and public reputation (osotogiki) being also favorable, the affair having caused considerable trouble over a number of days, a token of gratitude — one [unit] of taiyu-tori (a type of bird, likely a type of fowl presented as a gift) — is hereby offered in humble appreciation.
