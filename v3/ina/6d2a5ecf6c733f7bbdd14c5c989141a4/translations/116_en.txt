　　　　　Honmachi (Main Town)

One kanmon in zeni (copper coins) — [awarded to] Kitahara Rizaemon

The above is in recognition of the following: Some days ago, a farmer named Jirōzaemon from Nakatsubo Village in Tera-gō came forward bearing a letter of introduction (soekан) and filed a petition against two individuals, Masaemon and Kinzaemon of Kasahara Village, over a matter concerning farmland. [Rizaemon] was therefore instructed to intervene as a mediator. After duly hearing the statements of both the plaintiff and the defendant and thoroughly investigating the circumstances, the matter was handled and settled through a private, amicable agreement (naisai). Both parties were brought to satisfactory resolution; and given that the matter also involved another domain's territory, the outcome reflected well in terms of public reputation (osobogiki). Throughout all of this, [Rizaemon] labored for many days over various difficulties, which was a considerable burden. Therefore, as a modest token of reward (gobōbi), one kanmon in zeni

[Left folio]
is hereby granted.
