One: When officials are on urgent business, they may pass through at any hour of the night, regardless of whether they are wearing formal attire (kamishimo) or not.

One: Regarding firearms (teppō), weapons, and the like — those carrying such items who possess the relevant authorizing documents (sashigami) shall submit said documents and may then proceed.

One: Regarding the practice of verifying passage by matching seals and names (inshō hikiai), which has been followed up to this point — this procedure shall continue to be observed henceforth as before.

The foregoing shall be understood and adhered to accordingly.

As the above has been officially proclaimed by the authorities (kōgi), it shall be disseminated throughout the town. The above is hereby conveyed.

　　　　8th Month, 5th Day

The foregoing was shown to fellow officials (dōyaku-chū), and then handed over to Shin'yō Hongō.
