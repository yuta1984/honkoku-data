【Right folio】

(Continued from the previous folio) — this is hereby officially decreed.

1. Regarding goods produced and stored by the various domains (国産蔵物) and other cargo used by townspeople: after filing notification with this office, such goods shall be forwarded by relay transport (継ぎ立て) at a rate of 85% above the base fare (i.e., a 50% surcharge above the base rate).

　　Proviso:
　　Among the aforementioned goods, there are those who disguise their cargo as goods for commercial use by townspeople, or who conceal and reclassify their loads [using concealment methods — one character illegible], and travel under false pretenses. Therefore, when suspicious-looking cargo is observed, it shall be unpacked and inspected (吟味) at the relevant post station.

The foregoing has been officially decreed as stated above.
　　Fifth month of the Year of the Dragon　　　Ekitei-shi (Office of Postal and Transport Affairs)

As the foregoing has been decreed by the Imperial Court, it shall be duly communicated to the townspeople of this district. Herewith.
　　Sixth month

As the above official notice (御触書) was issued on the twenty-fifth day of the sixth month, it was shown to fellow officers (同役中), and copies were distributed to the guild administrators (行司中) and the town district representatives (町丁代).
