【Translation】

【Right folio】

Regarding copper coins (dōsen): after due consideration of current market exchange rates across the various provinces, it is hereby officially decreed that henceforth one mon (文) shall circulate as equivalent to six mon in kama-sen (鎌銭, a type of currency denomination).

　The reason for the above measure is as follows: because up until now copper coins have not been handled at their proper value, unscrupulous merchants (kanshō) have consequently been exporting them to foreign lands. Therefore, it is hereby ordered that this proclamation be promptly disseminated throughout the entire realm.

　　Third Month (March)　　Dajō-kan (Council of State / Grand Council of State)

As the foregoing has been decreed by the Imperial Court, this notice shall be circulated throughout the town districts accordingly. The above is hereby communicated.
