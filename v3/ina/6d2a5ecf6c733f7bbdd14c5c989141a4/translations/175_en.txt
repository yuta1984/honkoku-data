(Continued from the previous page) …the forces of Sakai Saemon-no-jō, who were stationed there, broke in and commenced firing. As this could not be overlooked, a demand was made to the said person to apprehend and hand over the suspects; however, without reason or justification, they opened fire, and as a result, armed conflict became unavoidable. In light of this, it is expected that there are still a number of fugitives who have fled the scene. Should any such individuals be sighted or heard of, they are to be apprehended immediately; and even if they naturally escape capture, they must not be left as they are — the matter is to be reported without delay. Should anyone who has knowledge of such individuals leave them undisturbed, they shall be subject to punishment as an accessory.

The foregoing is to be communicated without exception to all domains — whether shogunal lands (goryō), private domains (shiryō), or temple and shrine holdings (jisha-ryō) — leaving none uninformed.
