(Continued from previous page) …the forces of Sakai Saemon-no-jō burst into that place and opened fire. As this lawless conduct could not be overlooked, negotiations were made demanding that the said person apprehend and hand over the fugitives; however, without any reasonable cause, they again opened fire. Consequently, armed conflict became unavoidable. In connection with this, since the number of those who have since fled and absconded cannot be estimated, whenever such persons are seen or heard of, they are to be swiftly apprehended; and should it naturally prove impossible to restrain them by hand, they are to be cut down on the spot, after which the matter must be reported immediately. Should anyone happen to see or hear of such persons and yet leave them in place without acting, that person shall be subject to punitive measures (tatsuryō).
The foregoing is to be communicated without exception to all territories — whether shogunal domain, private domain, or temple and shrine lands.
