*(Continuing from previous page)* …shall remain seated and waiting at that location. Furthermore,

Item: As regards horse rain-cover palanquins (*uma-kappa-kago*) and other articles belonging to lower-ranking attendants (*shimo-tomo*), once the waiting benches (*koshikake*) outside the Maru-mon (Round Gate) are completed in the near future, [said items and persons] shall be kept waiting at that location.

Item: As has already been proclaimed by official notice, the use of tobacco by escort samurai within the Kara-mon is strictly prohibited. Although all matters are to be conducted with the utmost strictness, it has recently come to our attention that [the regulations] have naturally grown lax and that disorderly conduct has been observed. Therefore, henceforth, all parties are to conduct themselves so that no such conduct occurs.

Item: On this occasion, inspectors (*kansatsu no mono*) shall conduct periodic patrols for the purpose of supervising and regulating the conduct of escort samurai. All parties are to bear this in mind.

Item: Attendants (*jūsha*) shall not consume packed lunches (*bentō*) within the interior of the Kara-mon.
