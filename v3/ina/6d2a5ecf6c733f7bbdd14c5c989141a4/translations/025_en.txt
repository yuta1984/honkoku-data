As fifty days have now passed since the beginning of the mourning period (go-chūin), the nightly watch and patrols throughout the town are hereby discontinued, and normal daily activities are to resume.

Item: Regarding the playing of musical instruments, musical performances, and the killing of living creatures (sesshō) for purposes other than one's trade and livelihood — these matters are to be refrained from until further official notice is given.

　　Tenth day of the tenth month

This notice was received from Lord Sakai Masaemon at the official magistrate's office, whereupon it was promptly carried (on the twenty-seventh) to be shown to fellow officials, and thereafter handed over to Sōzō, the town representative (chōdai) of Honmachi.
