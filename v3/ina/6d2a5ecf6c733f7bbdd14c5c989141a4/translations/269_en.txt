【Left folio】

Imperial Decree of the 24th day of the 5th month:

Now that Yoshinobu has submitted in contrition for his offenses, regarding the continuation of the Tokugawa family name, His Majesty, in consideration of the family's long-standing meritorious service, has by special imperial will decreed that [the headship of the family] be entrusted to Tayasu Kamenosuke.

　However, regarding matters such as castle lands and stipend amounts, these shall be decreed at a later date.

Following the above, on the 29th day of the 4th month, Tayasu Kamenosuke was summoned and the matter was decreed in the presence of the Grand Commander-in-Chief (大総督).

　　　5th month:

　　　　　　　　Hitotsubashi Dainagon:
It has been decreed that [he] shall henceforth be added to the ranks of the feudal lords (藩屏).

　　　　　　　　Tayasu Chūnagon:
Same as above.

　　　　　　　　To the Kōke [families]:
It has been decreed that members of the Kōke shall henceforth be recognized as courtiers (朝臣) of the Imperial Court.

　　　　　　　　Tokugawa Kamenosuke:
In light of the recent decree regarding the succession of the family name, [he] shall proceed to the capital (Kyoto) to offer thanks [to the Emperor].

　　　　　　　　Hitotsubashi Dainagon:
In light of the recent decree regarding [his] inclusion among the ranks of the feudal lords, [he] shall proceed to the capital (Kyoto) to offer thanks [to the Emperor].
