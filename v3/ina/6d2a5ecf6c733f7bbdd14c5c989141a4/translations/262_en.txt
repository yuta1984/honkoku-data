【Right Folio】

It has come to our attention that in recent times, there have been various parties conducting transactions under the name of members of the Imperial family (*miyake*) and court nobles (*tōjōkata*), calling such dealings "official loans" (*o-kashitsuke-kin*). This is a most outrageous matter. At this time of the great renewal (*go-ishin*), such things ought not to exist at all. Should there be any person who handles affairs even superficially resembling such practices — whether through copied documents or otherwise — they shall be subject to investigation and severe punishment. Accordingly, it is hereby decreed that there shall be no misunderstanding on this point.

　　Third Month

The aforementioned revocation order (*kyakurei*) was to have been issued earlier, but as the official directive was delayed, it is hereby re-communicated.

Although the recent official proclamation (*furei*) prohibited the practice of handling loans conducted under the names of Imperial family members and court nobles, it has come to our attention that some parties are using said revocation order as a pretext to avoid repaying funds they have already borrowed. This is most outrageous. It is hereby further decreed that henceforth there shall be no misunderstanding on this matter.

　Proviso: With regard to transactions that have been conducted up until now, even though they may be considered loans made under false pretenses (*meimoku-kin*), repayment shall be made promptly.
