Regarding the above-named individual: during the third month of this year, he had his chōshi [sake vessels] stolen by a vagrant (mushuku) named Masutarō, yet he did not report [the theft] to the authorities as an appeal for assistance (josso), which constitutes a failure of [proper] conduct; accordingly, he is to be strictly reprimanded (kitto shikari).
