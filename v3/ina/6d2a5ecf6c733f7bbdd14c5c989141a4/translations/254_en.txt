**Notice of the Eighth Day of the Fifth Month**

The fortunes of the Imperial House have been newly restored, and the national policy (*kokuzei*) is gradually being established. The opportunity has arisen from the new Imperial rule (*go-shinsai*), and all matters are on the verge of being fully prepared for the next hundred years. At this juncture, the one thing that remains lacking is gold and grain (*kin-koku*, i.e., financial and material resources). This is entirely because at the time when Tokugawa Yoshinobu (*Tokugawa Keiki*) returned political authority (*taisei hōkan*), it was, of course, expected that he would simultaneously return [to the Imperial Court] all resources for national expenditure. However, as that matter had not yet been arranged, and as events from spring onwards have unfolded as they have, the Imperial Court has found itself with no revenues coming in, while the expenditures it must meet have been extraordinary and manifold. Furthermore, in these very days, soldiers engaged in punitive campaigns have abandoned their homes and offered their lives in single-minded devotion to serving their nation. Should military funds fail to be provided and provisions for the troops fall short, there is a risk that the spirit of those who press forward with dedication and diligence in their service will be undermined…
