The following is hereby officially pronounced:

Regarding Kihei: Due to the lack of rice distribution, he conspired with prominent villagers Kanetaro and Hisahachi and others, and a large group of people went to make a petition to Kochi village group-leader (kumigashira) Seishichi. Seishichi counseled and admonished them and had them return home temporarily. Nevertheless, Kihei again circulated a written notice calling for another gathering, rang the great temple bell at the Gongen shrine grounds to signal the assembly, and furthermore called out to people in a loud voice, with the intent of proceeding to the grain merchant to make demands — thus escalating the situation into riotous mob behavior. This being an extreme act of misconduct, he is hereby firmly sentenced to prolonged confinement (heisha), and furthermore [the sentence continues on the next section]...
