　　Fifth Month

　In this time of the Great Renewal of Imperial Governance (*Taisei go-isshin*), it is desired that rewards and punishments be properly administered, that loyalty and righteousness be made manifest, and that the hearts of all people under heaven be inspired and uplifted. Already, posthumous commendations have been decreed for the steadfast loyalty, noble benevolence, and outstanding excellence of *Toyotomi Taikō* (Toyotomi Hideyoshi) and *Kusunoki Chūjō* (Kusunoki Masashige). Accordingly, since approximately the Year of the Ox (*Ushi*, c. 1865), those knights and men of high purpose among the common people (*sōmō no yūshi*) who proclaimed their loyalty, who, foremost in the realm, dedicated themselves to national affairs and suffered hardship and calamity — these persons, in the course of their actions, abandoned the bonds of love between parent and child, left behind their hereditary stipends, departed from the lands of their ancestors' graves, and, braving the wind and rain, moved covertly in all directions.
