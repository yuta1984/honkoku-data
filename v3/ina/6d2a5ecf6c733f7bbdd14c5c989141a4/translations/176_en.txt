As the foregoing notice has been issued by the shogunal authorities (kōgi), it is to be communicated throughout the town. That is all.
　　First Month (January)

In order to maintain control over the town and surrounding areas, checkpoints (kanmon) are to be established for the time being at all locations leading out of the shogunal capital (gobunai). In the case of samurai retainers (shoshi), a written declaration (dangosho) issued by their master or a senior official, stating to where and how many retainers are being dispatched, must be presented. Farmers and townspeople must carry a letter of endorsement (soesho) issued by their local official. Those who arrive without such documents shall be prohibited from passing through in either direction — entering or exiting — without exception.
