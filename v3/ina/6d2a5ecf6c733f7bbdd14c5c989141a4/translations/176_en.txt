　　First Month (January)

In order to maintain order in the town and surrounding areas (ichi-zai), checkpoints (kanmon) are to be established for the time being at locations leading into the city (go-fu-nai). For those of samurai status (shoshi), a written declaration (kotowari-sho) from their master or senior officer stating to which destination and how many retainers are being dispatched must be presented; for farmers and townspeople (hyakushō chōnin), a letter of endorsement (soesho) from the officials of their place of residence must be carried. Anyone without such documents shall be barred from passage entirely, whether entering or exiting.
