We had been deeply troubled upon hearing that the rice had not headed, and then on the 6th of this month, heavy rain continued to fall, and on the 8th, an unusually severe storm struck, causing considerable damage by flattening both rice paddies and dry fields, leaving numerous areas in a state of total devastation. How the situation within the domain will continue until cultivation can resume is a matter of great concern, and depending on the condition of each village, severe hardship appears inevitable, causing us profound anxiety and distress.

In light of this, the most important matter for each village is to make provisions for food supplies (fushoku). Already last year there was flood damage to the crops, and in recent years the prices of rice and all manner of goods have risen extraordinarily, creating a period of considerable hardship.
