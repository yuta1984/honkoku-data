【Left Folio】

As the following has been proclaimed at Kōfu, it shall be disseminated throughout the towns. The above is hereby communicated.
　　Sixth Month

At this juncture of the renewal of imperial governance (*kōsei kōshi*), with the desire to lay the foundations for a wealthy nation (*fukoku*) and exhausting every means available, it has been proclaimed that paper currency (*kinsat*, gold notes) shall be manufactured through temporary emergency measures (*gonpō*). In accordance with the Imperial will to relieve the hardship and distress of all people throughout the realm, for a period of thirteen years, from the current Year of the Dragon to the next Year of the Dragon, [these notes] shall be in circulation throughout the entire imperial realm (*kōkoku*). The regulations (*o-shihō*) governing this are as set forth below.

　However, regarding the specific deadline for the period of circulation, this shall be proclaimed at a later date.

As the above has been proclaimed, [this notice] shall be disseminated by each relevant office to all levels [of society] without omission.
