Regarding the present renewal of imperial governance [朝政御一新], the primary duty is the benevolent care and nurturing of all the people. However, taking advantage of the times when, amid the many affairs within the domain, the path of ensuring the people's peace and security naturally tends to be neglected, there are those who gradually spread across the land, exploiting the situation freely, falsely invoking the name of loyalty to the Emperor [勤王] to ensnare good and honest people, greedily hoarding grain, and covertly exhausting and ruining the people's strength and resources. Such acts are exceedingly contrary to the intent and spirit of benevolent governance. Should there be any grievances [regarding such matters], as these all touch upon imperial governance, they are to be reported without the slightest hesitation. Furthermore, even in cases where [complaints] are brought from within [the jurisdiction of] domain lords and local magistrates, with due regard to the spirit of [this decree], when such reports are submitted, they shall promptly be forwarded to the Grand Council of State [太政官].
