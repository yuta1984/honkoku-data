【Right folio】

During the Tenpō era, licenses (株, kabu) for the trial brewing of premium sake (jōshu) in the Kantō Eight Provinces (Kan-hasshū) were granted on loan to brewers within the Kantō region (kannai) as well as to those in distant provinces. However, in recent years such trial brewing has continued, and since locations within the Kantō region include places in close proximity to the shogunal capital (gofu-nai, i.e., Edo), a special order to reduce production has been issued. Nevertheless, there are no small number of individuals who have been conducting clandestine brewing or brewing in excess of permitted quantities. It is understood that violations have been especially prevalent because, despite the allocated brewing volume being small (measured in koku), the number of workers engaged in the trade is large.

Therefore, regarding the loaned licenses (kashikabu) within the Kantō region, as there will likely be further directives issued in due course, brewing operations are to be suspended (kyūkabu) in the meantime.
