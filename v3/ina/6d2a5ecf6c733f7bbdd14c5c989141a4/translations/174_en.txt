Keiō 4, Year of the Dog (Boshin), 1868
From the first month onward
　　　　Monthly duty officer:
　　　　　Ikegami Motoemon

At this time, a band of villains has been committing acts of violence within the city, and furthermore, in Yashū (Shimotsuke Province) and other areas, they have been forming factions and instigating matters of a most serious nature. Consequently, a number of these individuals have recently been apprehended.
