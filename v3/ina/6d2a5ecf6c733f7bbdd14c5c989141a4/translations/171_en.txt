　　　Eleventh Month

Regarding the matter of etsuso (unauthorized appeals bypassing proper official channels): although the shogunate has issued strict prohibitions on this matter, in recent years the moral order of society has been swept along by prevailing currents and grown increasingly lax, with people conducting themselves in a self-indulgent and disorderly manner in all things, and the proper understanding of conduct among the populace has become thoroughly negligent. Of late, there have been an exceptionally large number of cases of etsuso and petition appeals (nagewan), and among them there are even those who have submitted direct appeals (jikiso) to high-ranking officials.
