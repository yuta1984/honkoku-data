Shinmachi [New Town Ward]
　　　　　　Bitaku (residence)
Fine: 2 kanmon　　Nijirō

Regarding the above: the said Nijirō purchased goods from Genjizaemon of Hokomochi Village — goods that had been brought to the official office by Jizaemon of Yokochō. Although he claims he was unaware of the full details (isai), his handling of illicit goods is deemed improper and outrageous (furachi). Accordingly, the goods are to be returned to their original owner (motogata), and a monetary fine (karyōsen) is hereby imposed. The fine is to be paid within three days.

　　30th Day of the Eighth Month [August]

As for the said Nijirō, he has fulfilled his duties up to this day.
Four copies of this official notice (okakitsuke) have been summoned to the colleagues in the same office (dōyaku), and each copy has been duly distributed. Herewith concluded.
