[Regarding guides who come forth when official travelers are passing through:] Officials need not come out in large numbers — only one person shall come forth. Likewise, with regard to the matter of gratuities (tsukai-barai): except in special cases such as the passage of items bearing the vermilion seal (go-shuin) or other matters of particular gravity, or when various officials are traveling, there is no need to present [a gratuity] on such occasions, and this shall cause no difficulty.

As stated above, the villages along the branch highways (waki-ōkan) throughout the provinces where post-stations (tsugiba) are located — whether under domain lands (go-ryō), private lands (shi-ryō), or temple and shrine lands (jisha-ryō) — shall all be duly notified without exception.

　　Eleventh Month

The foregoing shall be duly circulated and notified. The above.
