As for the aforementioned young nobles [kōdachi], measures are to be taken to ensure that no harm whatsoever comes to them suddenly; however, with regard to the men accompanying them, each and every one is to be apprehended and detained, and upon the Sōtoku's arrival [or: toward the end of the month], instructions regarding their disposition are to be sought. Such is the directive that has been issued.

　Addendum: Regarding the men who were formerly under the command of Lord Ayakōji — after Lord Ayakōji returned to Kyoto, it has come to be heard that said individuals conspired with lawless elements, falsely assumed the name of the Imperial Army [Kangun], and, invoking an old provision [or: presenting old documents] relating to the clearing of barriers/checkpoints [sekisho-ake] to officials [village elders/year magistrates] in various localities, have been suppressing and driving out farmers and merchants, and have been making their way eastward one after another. Since such persons are to be treated in the same manner as the men of Lord Takamatsu, it has been ordered that each and every one of them is likewise to be apprehended and detained.
