　　Son of Ikegami Hon'emon
　　　　　　　　Kichibei □

Regarding the aforementioned person: his family has served in official duties since his great-grandfather's time, and his father, Hon'emon, has likewise served with sincerity and diligence. In view of all this, and for future service to the domain, he is hereby appointed as Apprentice Town Elder [町年寄見習]. Accordingly, he is granted five bales of rice [役米五俵] as a stipend for this role.

　　Fifth day of the third month

Naitō Yobe'e and Kawachi Kanbe'e were in attendance, and on this occasion, the Monthly Duty Officer [御月番], Lord Kawachi, conveyed the order. The Magistrate [御代官], Lord Kakigi, the Official Scribe [御物書], Inoueda, and others attended as customary.
