　　However, those wishing to increase production beyond the prescribed limits must submit a petition through the appropriate channel; upon inquiry, a ruling will be issued according to the circumstances of each case.

Item: In years of poor harvest (*kyōnen*), brewing is to be carried out in installments (i.e., divided and reduced quantities).

Item: It is hereby ordered that a sum of twenty *ryō* in gold per one hundred *koku* of brewed sake be paid as a levy (*ōnō*).

Item: Those who, in the previous year, through misunderstanding of the rules, increased production beyond the prescribed limits and had their brewing licenses (*kansatsu*) revoked — should they repent and submit a petition, they shall be pardoned upon payment of fifty *ryō* in gold per one hundred *koku*.
