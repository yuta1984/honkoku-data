　　Provided, however, that those who apply through the appropriate channels regarding increased production shall be investigated, and a ruling shall be issued according to the particulars of each case.

Item: In years of poor harvest (*kyōnen*), brewing shall be conducted in installments [i.e., production shall be divided and reduced accordingly].

Item: It is hereby ordered that twenty *ryō* in gold shall be paid per one hundred *koku* of sake produced.

Item: Those who, in the previous year, through a misunderstanding of the rules, produced sake beyond the prescribed limits and consequently had their brewing licenses (*kansatsu*) revoked — should any such persons repent and submit a petition, they shall be pardoned upon payment of fifty *ryō* in gold per one hundred *koku*.
