Things to be kept in mind:

First, not only those who own rice paddies and fields, but even those with only a small vacant plot of land, should make every effort to sow and cultivate whatever can serve as supplementary food.

First, it is heard that miscellaneous expenses at auspicious and inauspicious occasions (ceremonies and rituals) are not inconsiderable. From now on, one should be mindful to reduce such expenses according to one's means, and make simplicity and frugality the foremost priority in all things.

The above matters are to be communicated without exception to everyone, down to tenants and back-alley residents. Each town and each household should unite in practicing frugality. Town officials are to make frequent rounds, and those who show a lack of understanding should be thoroughly instructed. If proper understanding is not achieved from this point forward, each individual will inevitably fall into poverty and extreme hardship. This notice is therefore issued in advance.
