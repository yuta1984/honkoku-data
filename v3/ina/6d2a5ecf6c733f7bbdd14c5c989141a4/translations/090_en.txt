【Right folio】

The wife (okukata-sama) of the Lord of Shimōsa-no-kami, the ■ grandfather of Toda Ginzō-sama, the official inspector of fire brigades (hikeshi o-aratame), who had been ill, passed away in the early morning at the middle of the Hour of the Ox on the 25th day of the 1st month, having been unable to recover despite medical care.

As the deceased was related to [Toda Ginzō-sama] through [his mistress's] paternal great-aunt's line, mourning attire (go-chakufuku) prescribed by official custom is to be worn ■■.

Accordingly, until the 15th of the coming month, the sounding of musical instruments and entertainment music shall be refrained from, and particular care shall be taken regarding fire hazards and the like.

The above is hereby communicated throughout the town. That is all.
