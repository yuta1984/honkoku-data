regulations have long been in place stipulating that
[sales] must be handled in small measures (komashu) of one shō or less.
However, in recent times this has become disorderly, and among [the merchants],
there have been reports of practices by sake-brewing households
that are irregular and confusing, amounting to a complete disregard of
the established regulations — a most improper state of affairs.
