During last year's crop failure (island-wide poor harvest), the manner in which [the aforementioned persons] went about distributing [goods] to the townspeople in hardship was truly admirable. While it would be proper to formally commend them at the office, given the nature of the times, they shall be spared the formality of being summoned; instead, the matter shall be communicated to all without exception at the town assembly hall (machi-kaisho).

　25th day of the 12th month

At the inner御五側 (Go-gawa-gawa), it was conveyed by the Honorable Magistrate's office that Matsui Shūen and others shall accompany [the officials]. Likewise, the same instructions are to be conveyed in the rural areas (zaichū) at the local administrative office (yakuba).

Upon returning, [the instructions] were copied and left for fellow officials, and word was sent to Kishichi. A separate letter was also attached and sent.

Given the nature of this time, regarding whether it would be appropriate to call a gathering of the respective [parties] on the evening of the 28th of tomorrow and convey the instructions [in person], a circular inquiry (kaigō) has been made as to how this should be handled.
