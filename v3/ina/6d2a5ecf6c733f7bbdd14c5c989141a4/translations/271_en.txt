…should there be any instances of overlooking or improperly handling [fees at post-station branch points], [offenders] shall be strictly called to account and punished.

　　Proviso:
　　It has come to our attention that, in the past, when the term "negotiated rate" (相対, aitai) was used, it was commonly understood to mean that additional bale-surcharges (俵増し) could be added on top of the fixed standard fees. This is a misunderstanding. Henceforth, excessive charges shall not be levied.

1. Regarding the practices of "distant-view clearing fees" (遠見材払) and elevated [platform?] fees (高■), these shall no longer be required of the Imperial envoy (勅使) or any other parties who have hitherto been subject to such levies.

1. When official government travelers (御用通行) of various households stop to rest and take refreshment, two or three knowledgeable post-station officials shall station themselves at the entrance of the lodging, where a posted notice bearing the name of the lodging establishment shall be displayed, and they shall direct [travelers] accordingly.

　　Proviso:
　　It shall not be necessary to accompany and guide [travelers] all the way to the lodging.

1. When official government travelers record entries in the human-and-horse register (人馬帳) at their lodgings, if post-station officials were to go one by one to the lodging to attend to this, the post-horse station (伝馬所) would be left short-handed and unable to carry out official duties. Therefore, henceforth, the proprietor of the lodging shall bring the register to the post-horse station for the entries to be recorded there.
