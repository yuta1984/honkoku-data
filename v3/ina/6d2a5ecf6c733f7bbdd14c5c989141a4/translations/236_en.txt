【Right folio】

〇　　　　　To those engaged in the retail sale of yanagiZake (low-grade/small-quantity sake)

Regarding the business of selling yanagizake: regulations have long been in place stipulating that [such merchants] shall sell [sake] in quantities of one shō (升) or less. However, in recent times [the practice] has become disorderly, and it has come to our attention that among [these merchants] there are those who have been conducting barrel sales (taru-uri) resembling the operations of sake brewers. This constitutes a complete disregard of the established regulations and represents a serious misunderstanding of [proper conduct].

Therefore, on this occasion, we hereby formally re-issue this directive. [All parties] are to take note equally, and henceforth [merchants] shall handle [sake] using the small measure (komasu) as per the previous established rules. Barrel sales by pack-horse measure (dabyō) are, needless to say, strictly prohibited, as is handling [sake] in one-shō measures — none of this shall be permitted whatsoever.
