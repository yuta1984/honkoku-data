【Left Page】

The ringleader(s) among the treacherous retainers [of the Mito domain] had previously fled from their home domain (kunimoto), and their whereabouts had been unknown. However, it has recently come to light—through a report filed by the resident deputy (rusuii) of the same colleague—that there are rumors of their having slipped into this area (Kyoto).

Therefore, not only regarding the aforementioned old metal goods, but also [it must be investigated whether] their associates (tōrui) have been secretly moving [persons or goods] through temples, various storehouses, residences, or from the home domain and surrounding areas; or whether among the retainers of various domains, any persons with old acquaintances or connections have been providing shelter and assistance—by way of guidance—to [these fugitives] during the period of their retreat within and around the capital (Kyoto) and the surrounding countryside.

A thorough investigation (ginmi) shall be carried out, and if anything becomes clear in this regard, it shall immediately be reported to the Daijō-kan (Grand Council of State) Office of Penal Law Affairs (Keihō Jimu). Should [the matter] come to light from outside [your jurisdiction] due to a failure to conduct [the investigation] diligently, a severe reprimand shall be imposed.
