Item: Shikaku, having committed gambling in previous years, and having again organized [others] with the intention of engaging in gambling matches on this occasion, is hereby ordered to pay a fine (karyō-sen) on account of this outrageous conduct.

Item: Regarding Jihei — six years prior, gambling was [conducted / discovered] at his temporary residence. His relatives, neighborhood association, town representative (chōdai), neighborhood head (kumigashira), and neighboring households, [knowing that] such improper conduct ought not to exist, and [knowing that] they were obligated to firmly remonstrate (iken) with anyone who violated the prohibitions (go-hatto), instead passed the matter over negligently. This conduct is outrageous and they would ordinarily be subject to a fine; however, since Jihei himself has already been sentenced to banishment (tsuihō), there is no need to pursue the matter further against them [on that count], and they are hereby strictly reprimanded (shikari).

In accordance with the above adjudication (saikyō), the parties are to take note of this ruling, and henceforth must strictly observe the laws and regulations (go-hatto/suji), and must reliably keep their subordinates and dependents in order.
