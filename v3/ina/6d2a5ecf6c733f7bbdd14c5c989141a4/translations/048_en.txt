For villages where there are people suffering from hunger and extreme poverty, the number of households and persons shall be investigated at each location, and reports shall be submitted promptly by the rural district office (gōkata yakusho), the kannken-gata (frugality enforcement officials), village officials (mura yakunin), and the fushoku-tsumeyaku (food provision duty officers).

Item: Beyond the above, regarding all matters pertaining to food provisions (fushoku), even if there are villages where it is difficult to manage adequately, the person in charge, as well as the kannyu-gata (guidance and persuasion officials), shall be notified accordingly.

Item: The sewayaku (welfare/assistance officers) shall appear at the office as circumstances allow, and shall acknowledge their duties with respect to the work of the kannyu-gata.

Item: The aforementioned kannyu-gata are assigned on days of the 2nd, 7th, 5th, and 10th of each month, and as they are currently stationed at the office on the 2nd, matters should be reported on the designated days as much as possible.
