Both samurai and commoners are strictly prohibited from deserting their home domain. In the first instance, should a deserter commit any improper act, the responsibility shall fall upon their supervising authority. However, in this present time, regardless of social rank, for those who genuinely intend to submit a petition (kengi) for the sake of the Imperial nation or their own lord's household, a proper avenue shall be opened; with a sincere and impartial heart, they may present their views and, if they so wish, submit their case to the Daijō-kan Dai (Deputy Grand Council of State).

Addendum: Henceforth, when taking into service any person — not only those of samurai rank, but even those of agricultural or merchant class — their background and origins shall be thoroughly investigated beforehand. Should it come to pass that a deserter is taken into service, an improper incident occurs, and harm results, the responsibility shall lie with the employer (master of the household).

　　March　　　Daijō-kan (Grand Council of State)
