【Right folio】

On the fifth day of the eighth month [August 5], the following was proclaimed at the magistrate's office:
Regarding the manner of passage through checkpoints (sekisho): whereas regulations have existed from previous times, new orders have now been issued by [official proclamation]. From the first day of the coming eighth month [August 1], [all parties] are to conduct themselves in accordance with the attached separate document. Matters that have heretofore been handled by the留守居 (rusuigumi / resident officials acting in their lord's absence) shall henceforth all be handled by the checkpoint inspector (sekisho-gakari metsuke).

The above is to be communicated without exception to all persons, whether their domain yields ten thousand koku or more, or less than ten thousand koku.
