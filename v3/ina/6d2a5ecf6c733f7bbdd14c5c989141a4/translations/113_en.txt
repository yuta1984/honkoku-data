【Right folio】

On the fifth day of the eighth month [August 5], the following was proclaimed at the magistrate's office:

Regarding the method of passage through the checkpoint (sekisho): whereas regulations have previously been established concerning this matter, it is hereby proclaimed that a revision shall now be implemented. Accordingly, from the first day of the coming eighth month [August 1], [all parties] are to conduct themselves in accordance with the attached separate document. Furthermore, matters that had until now been handled by the resident official (rusui) shall henceforth be handled entirely by the checkpoint inspector (sekisho-gakari metsuke).

The above is to be communicated, without exception, to all persons of ten thousand koku or above and below [i.e., to lords and retainers of all ranks].
