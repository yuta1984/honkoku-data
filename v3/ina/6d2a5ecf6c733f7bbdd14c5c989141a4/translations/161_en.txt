Regarding the above-named individuals: since the year before last, they were ordered to conduct inspections of those dispersed or relocated to other areas (tashossan aratame), and they carried out this duty with great diligence, which is most commendable. Although it would be appropriate to formally convey commendations (shōbi) at the office, given the circumstances of the current times, as a special act of consideration (kakubetsu no yōsha), they need not be summoned; instead, notice is to be given without exception at the town's meeting hall (kaishō).

　　Twenty-fifth Day of the Twelfth Month

In accordance with the customary formal written notice (okushiro), both Honorable Magistrates (go-bugyō) made an official visit.
The above was conveyed by the honorable Matsui Shūzō.
Those who received the ceremonial cup (o-sakazuki) while in the countryside are to receive their respective notifications at each location. For those who received formal acknowledgment (o-todoke), the notification was conveyed by the Honorable Monthly Duty Officer (tsukiban) Kawachi Kahei.
