　　Ninth month

Regarding the matter of those practicing onmyōdō: it has been communicated that officials of the Tsuchimikado family will be making rounds for the purpose of inspection. Should said officials visit, [you] are to report their names and other particulars [to the authorities]. Furthermore, [you] should exercise due care in handling this matter. Additionally, those who have hitherto engaged in said practice are to have their names investigated and submitted [to the authorities].
