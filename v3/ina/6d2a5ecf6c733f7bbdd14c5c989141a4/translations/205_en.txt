However, regarding signboards (hyōfuda): there is no objection to inscribing one's surname, or alternatively, one's official title or role name.

Item: It is considered highly improper that the Imperial Crest (go-mon) is being painted or drawn on lanterns, ceramics, and other items for sale. Henceforth, the private affixing of the Imperial Crest to such articles is strictly prohibited, as has been commanded.

However, even those who have previously been granted permission to use the Imperial Crest for official purposes (go-yō) must, without exception, submit a fresh inquiry to obtain renewed authorization.

The above having been commanded, this notice is to be conveyed without omission to all, down to the very last person.
