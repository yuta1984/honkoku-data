Regarding the appointment of Kitahara Risaemon as Town Elder [町年寄] on this occasion, his stipend [御宛行] shall be granted in accordance with the standard allotment.

Item: As Ikegami Kichibei has been appointed as Apprentice Town Elder [町年寄見習], he is hereby granted five bales of rice [役米五俵] as his official stipend.

Item: On the seventh day, two days hence, he is to appear at the office dressed in formal hemp ceremonial attire [麻上下]. Following established precedent, we shall meet with him here, after which the oath of office [誓伺] regarding his official duties shall be administered.
　　Note: As for Kitahara Risaemon, since his oath of office was already completed on a prior occasion, no further action is required in his case.

All parties are to take heed of the above and conduct themselves accordingly.

　　Fifth day of the third month
