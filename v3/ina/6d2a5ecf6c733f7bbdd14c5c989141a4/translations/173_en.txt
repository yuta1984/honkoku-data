(Continuing from the previous page) …To instill such ideas into persons of insufficient judgment and cause them to submit petitions is something that must never occur; indeed, should this happen, the heads of the household and leading members of the community shall bear an even heavier culpability than the petitioners themselves. As the foregoing is hereby once again formally proclaimed, the town officials (machiyakunin) are to ensure that it is thoroughly and without omission conveyed to all persons of the lower orders (shōzen-suematsu). Henceforth, all are to conduct themselves without any misunderstanding of these regulations.

　Eleventh month

The foregoing is to be duly proclaimed throughout the entire town.
That is all.
