…[porters] have been made to carry loads up to seven kan per person, while for the households [of official travelers], the allowance was limited to five kan — this has been an utterly unreasonable arrangement. Therefore, henceforth, whether for official (御用) or private (私用) purposes, the load per porter shall be set at seven kan.

　　Proviso:
　　For bulky or high-piled cargo (高荷), the amount that can be carried shall remain as it has been up to now.

1. Although a weighing inspection station (貫目改所) has been separately established, in order to further investigate irregularities, official permission has now been granted for the use of a steelyard balance (千木秤) at each post station. Therefore, any load that appears questionable shall have its weight inspected, and for any amount exceeding the prescribed weight limit, charges shall be collected in accordance with the proportional rate of the officially set fees.

1. Regarding official communications (御用状) dispatched from the various offices in Kyoto as well as from the prefectural governments (府県) of the various provinces, henceforth fees shall be duly paid for each and every one. Should there be any case where no fee is tendered, relay forwarding shall not be carried out.

1. For official goods (御用物) dispatched without a superintendent (宰領) in attendance, an approximate estimate of the transport fees from the point of dispatch to the destination shall be attached and sent along with the goods. At each post station, the receiving party shall be recorded, and the final lodging station (留まり宿) shall subsequently report any surplus or shortfall in fees.
