【Right folio】
The following charges and punishments are hereby declared:

One: Regarding (correction: Ikitarō→) Kanjirō — in connection with the investigation into lost property at the residence of Genbei in Yamamuro village, he acted improperly by following the instructions of Makiya Mahachi without proper verification and conducting an unjust investigation of Tsuahibei of the same village. He is hereby sentenced to confinement (oshikomi). He is strictly admonished to reform his conduct.
　　Fifteenth day of the eleventh month

　　　　　　　番非人 (Ban-hinin)
　　　　　　　　■shichi

The above individual, in connection with the matter of lost property at the residence of Genbei in Yamamuro village, followed the instructions of Makiya Mahachi and Mahachi's associate Kanjirō, and conducted an unduly harsh and forceful investigation of Tsuahibei of the same village. This conduct being improper, he is hereby sentenced to confinement (oshikomi). He is strictly admonished to remain reformed in his conduct.
　　Eleventh day

The above is hereby officially declared.
