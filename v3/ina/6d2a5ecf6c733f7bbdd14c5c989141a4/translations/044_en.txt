The following charges are hereby declared:

One: Regarding (crossed out: Ikutarō →) Kanjirō — In the matter of the investigation of a missing item at the residence of Genbei of Yamamuro Village, [Kanjirō] acted upon the instructions of Makiya Magohachi without exercising any restraint or judgment of his own, and conducted an unduly harsh interrogation of Tsutabei of the same village. This conduct being improper and unruly, the punishment of confinement (oshikome) is hereby ordered. He is strictly admonished to conduct himself with due restraint henceforth.

　　Eleventh month, fifteenth day

　　　　　　　番非人 (Ban-hinin)
　　　　　　　　Teishichi

Regarding the above-named individual — In the matter of the missing item at the residence of Genbei of Yamamuro Village, [Teishichi] followed the instructions of Makiya Magohachi and his associate Kanjirō, and subjected Tsutabei of the same village to a severe and coercive interrogation. This conduct being improper and unruly, the punishment of confinement is hereby ordered. He is strictly admonished to conduct himself with due restraint henceforth.

　　Eleventh day

The foregoing is hereby officially declared and communicated.
