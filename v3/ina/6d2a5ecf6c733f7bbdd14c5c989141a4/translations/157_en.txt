However, matters pertaining to the Kyoto office (Kyō-ya) and those for whom post-horses and porters have previously been provided as a customary arrangement, as well as document boxes (jōbako) dispatched with accompanying written certificates, official goods (goyō-mono), and official correspondence (goyō-jō) dispatched from various offices — all such shall continue to be relayed (tsugitate) free of charge, as has been the practice heretofore.

Item: The practice of charging lodging fees (mokosen), meal costs (kome-dai), or reduced inn rates (yasu-hatagopai) for persons traveling on official business shall henceforth be abolished. For the time being, regardless of rank (upper or lower), a standard rate of 700 mon per person per night and 300 mon per daytime stay shall be paid. Furthermore, accommodations shall be arranged in a simple and unpretentious manner.
