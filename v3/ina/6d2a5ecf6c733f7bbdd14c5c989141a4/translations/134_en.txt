[Right folio]

　　　　　　Yokochō (横町, Side Street)
　　　　　　　Shirokiya (白木屋)
　　　　　　　　　Chūji

Regarding the said individual: some time ago, in connection with a boundary dispute (sakaichi-ron) brought by Bungo Tōhei, [Chūji] had his wife file a complaint by circumventing the proper channels of appeal (osso), and although [he] made improper claims, upon investigation, his stated assertions were found to be without reasonable grounds. Accordingly, a mediator (rige-nin) was appointed and conciliation proceedings were initiated, but [Chūji] refused to accept a settlement. Therefore, [he] was ordered to submit household registers, room measurements, and land [records], but [he] continued to raise difficulties regarding [his] claims. Consequently, a judgment (saikyo) was handed down ordering that the disputed land be seized and transferred to Tōhei. However, [Chūji] stubbornly refused to submit and accept [the ruling], and was therefore placed under hand-restraints (te-kusari). Subsequently, when members of the village/neighborhood group (gō-gumi) were sent to check on [him], he was found to have removed his hand-restraints, and given this fearless and brazen conduct showing no deference to the authorities, [he] was arrested and ordered to be imprisoned.
