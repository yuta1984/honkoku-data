　　　　　　Yoko[ume]-machi
　　　　　　　Shirakinoya
　　　　　　　　　Chūji

Regarding the said person: some time ago, in connection with a boundary dispute (sakaichi-ron) with Bungo-ya Heiroku, [Chūji] caused his wife to file an unauthorized direct appeal (etsuso) to higher authorities, which was an improper act. However, upon investigation, his claims could not be found to be clearly established as legitimate. Accordingly, a mediator (rige-nin) was appointed and an attempt was made to reach a settlement, but [Chūji] refused to submit. Therefore, an inspection of the household register and lot measurements (ie-chō kensu ji-oshi) was ordered and carried out; but since [the results] were found to be at odds with his stated claims, a verdict (saikyo) was handed down ordering that the disputed plot of land be seized and transferred to Heiroku. Despite this, [Chūji] stubbornly refused to accept the ruling and submit, and was sentenced to hand-restraints (te-kusari). Subsequently, when members of the village group (gō-gumi) were dispatched to check on him, he had removed his hand-restraints and was going about freely. This being an act of brazen disregard for authority, he was arrested and ordered to be imprisoned.
