　　　　　　Umemachi [district]
　　　　　　　Shirokiya [shop name]
　　　　　　　　　Chūji

Regarding the aforementioned individual: some time ago, in connection with a boundary dispute with Bungo-ya Heiroku, [Chūji] caused his wife to file an appeal bypassing proper channels (o訴/koso), which was a most improper act. However, upon conducting an inquiry, his stated claims were found to lack any clear and legitimate basis. Accordingly, a mediator (rikaidō-nin) was appointed to bring about a settlement through negotiation, but [Chūji] refused to submit [to the arrangement]. Thereupon, an inspection of the household registry [recording] the number of rooms and a land survey (ji-oshi) were ordered and carried out, [the results of which] were found to be inconsistent with [Chūji's] stated claims. A judgment (saikyо̄) was therefore handed down ordering that the disputed land be seized and transferred to Heiroku. Nevertheless, [Chūji] stubbornly continued his objections and refused to comply, and was consequently ordered to be placed in hand-shackles (te-kusari). Subsequently, when members of the local village group (gō-gumi) were dispatched to conduct a check-up visit, [Chūji] was found to have removed his hand-shackles — a bold and insolent act showing no fear of authority — and he was therefore arrested and ordered imprisoned.
