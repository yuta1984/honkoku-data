【Left Folio】

As His Majesty the Emperor (*Shujō*) is of tender years, he has heretofore resided in the inner chambers (*shōmon* / inner sanctum). However, in light of the intent of the recent Imperial Oath (*go-seiyaku*), and in accordance with His Majesty's various wishes and intentions, henceforth:

[His Majesty] shall reside in the outer palace precincts (*omote*), proceed each day to the Imperial Study Hall (*go-gakumon-jo*), and hear the ten-thousand affairs of state (*banki no seimu*). Accordingly, [His Majesty] has commanded that [matters] be duly reported to him through the *Hoshō* (Chief Imperial Adviser / Regent-equivalent). Furthermore, His Majesty shall from time to time proceed in person (*rinkyo*) to [the chamber of] the eight-day interval ※[one character illegible], and during his leisure from affairs of state, shall devote himself to the study of both civil and military arts (*bunbu*). This has been officially proclaimed.

　　Fifth Month.

**Iwashimizu Hachiman Daijin (Iwashimizu Hachiman Great Deity)**

With regard to the above [shrine]: as the title of *Daibosatsu* (Great Bodhisattva) [previously conferred upon the deity] has been rescinded/returned, it has been proclaimed that [the shrine] shall henceforth offer fish (*gyomi*) as ritual food offerings (*hōgu*).

　　Fifth Month.
