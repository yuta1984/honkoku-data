【Right Folio】

[These retainers] have resisted and clashed with the Imperial forces — they are truly innumerable traitors to the nation (*kokuzoku*). Therefore, it being unavoidable, it has been proclaimed that they shall be subjugated and punished (*chūbatsu*) on the fifteenth day of this month [i.e., tomorrow]. This matter is hereby communicated as a confidential directive (*mitsu go-sata*) from the Commander-General (*Daisōtoku*), for the purpose of being duly noted.

　Fifth Month

[Regarding the] attached document: on the twentieth, a memorial (*sōmon*) was submitted to the Commander-General Prince (*Daisōtoku-no-miya*), [stating that] the situation is truly grave and difficult to manage, causing great distress and anxiety. In response, it has been proclaimed that [all concerned parties] should redouble their efforts and cooperate, proclaim the Imperial authority far and wide, and exert themselves to achieve the meritorious service of pacifying the realm.

　Fifth Month

In accordance with the above, as this has been proclaimed by the Imperial Court, [this notice] is to be circulated throughout the town. That is all.

　Sixth Month

The above two official proclamations (*ofuregaki*), together with the accumulated register of official proclamations, were issued on the evening of the twentieth.
