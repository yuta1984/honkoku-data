【Left folio】

One item: Regarding Torasaburō. On the night of the twenty-sixth day of last month, there were items reported missing at the residence of his employer, Momeniya Shōji. When questioned about this matter, rather than giving an honest account, he fabricated various stories each time he was asked, regardless of how many times he was questioned. As a result, he came under increasing suspicion, and caused great hardship to many people who had no connection whatsoever to the matter — including persons entirely uninvolved even at the present time — which is an exceedingly improper and reprehensible act. However, as Shōji gradually came to understand the full particulars of the inquiry and submitted a petition for withdrawal of the complaint, stating that his suspicions had been cleared, and in accordance with this petition, the punishment of confinement in the stone cell (ishiiri-rō) is hereby remitted. He is nevertheless strictly reprimanded. Henceforth, he is to conduct himself with due restraint and apply himself diligently to his trade and livelihood.
