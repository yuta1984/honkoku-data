【Right folio】

(The town representatives and neighborhood group heads) ought to have offered admonishment, and if it went unheeded, they should have escalated the matter [to the authorities]; yet they negligently allowed it to pass without doing so. As this is an outrageous matter, they are hereby sentenced to pay a fine.

As judgment has been rendered as stated above, [all parties] shall comply accordingly, henceforth exercise due restraint, and diligently devote themselves to their respective occupations. Furthermore, the fines (karyō-sen) are to be paid within three days.

　　　　　　Tenth month, seventeenth day

This official notice is to be forwarded along to the fellow officers (dōyaku-chū) and the spear-bearers (yarigashira).


　　　　　　　In place of Shimizu,
　　　　　　　　Toemon
