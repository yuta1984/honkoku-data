【Right folio】

...the aforementioned [ferry crossings and river crossings along the Five Highways] shall all receive a 30% increase, which is hereby designated as the official fixed fare (sadame-chinson) for the time being. New official notice boards (kōsatsu) will be issued [to the stations], and the stations are instructed to receive them accordingly. This matter is hereby communicated for your acknowledgment.

The above contents are to be duly circulated and made known at each relevant location.
　　9th month

As the above has been issued as an official notice (kakitsuke) from the shogunate (kōgi), it is to be circulated throughout the town. That is all.
　　10th month
