【Left folio】

Shrine official (社家) of Ochiai-juku:
　　Mizuno Tanba

Regarding the above-named individual, as there is an inquiry from the Sōtokufu (General Headquarters), we have received word from retainers of Sanada Shinano-no-Kami that it has been ordered, via the main honjin of Aiwake-juku, that (this person) should be investigated and handed over, and that this notice should be communicated not only throughout our domain's territory but also to the Ojūjō-sama (the Tokugawa domain or relevant authority).

Should the above-named individual be found anywhere, he is to be detained on the spot and reported to the appropriate authorities. Likewise, should anyone come to hear of or witness anything relating to this matter, that too should be reported.

The contents of this notice are to be circulated throughout the town. That is all.

　　3rd month

Note:
This was delivered at the magistrate's office on the 20th day. Shown to colleagues of the same office, then handed over to Kōyōshimochō.
