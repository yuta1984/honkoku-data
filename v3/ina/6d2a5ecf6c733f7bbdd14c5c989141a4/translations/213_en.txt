　　　Fourth Month (April)

The aforementioned official notice was handed down on the twentieth day (of the fourth month), and after being shown to fellow colleagues, it was forwarded to Shinmachi (the new town district).

　The five official notice boards (kōsatsu) ordered to be replaced on the twentieth day of the fourth month read as follows:

　　　Regulations (Sadame)

　Article 1. Every person shall conduct themselves uprightly in accordance with the Five Human Relationships (gorin).

　Article 1. One shall show compassion and care for widowers, widows, orphans, and those with disabilities.

　Article 1. One shall not commit wicked acts such as killing people, burning down homes, or stealing property.

　Keiō 4th Year (1868)
　　　　Third Month (March)
