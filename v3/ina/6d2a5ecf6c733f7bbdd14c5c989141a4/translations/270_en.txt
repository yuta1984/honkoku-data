Same as above.　　Tayasu Chūnagon

In light of the recent decree that members of the Kōke families shall be recognized as Imperial courtiers (朝臣), [they] shall proceed to the capital (Kyoto) to offer thanks [to the Emperor].

Regarding the official court ranks held by retainers of the Tokugawa family, these shall henceforth be abolished.

　　　5th month

The foregoing was decreed on the 24th of last month; this notice is issued for your information and guidance.

Regarding those among the former hatamoto (直参旗下) who have gone up to the capital and submitted to the new government: now that the disposition of the Tokugawa [family] has been decreed, in accordance with a particularly gracious Imperial will, all former hatamoto are hereby granted security of tenure over their home domains (本領安堵). Accordingly, the old designations of rank from the Kōke families downward shall be abolished, and [they] shall henceforth be assigned to one of three grades: ■Chūtayū (中太夫), Shimotayū (下太夫), or Jōshi (上士). This notice is issued for your information and guidance.

　　　5th month

Imperial Decree of the 3rd day of the 6th month:

1. In accordance with the particular intent [of the new government] on this occasion, the practice of demanding free-of-charge human porters, horse transport, firewood fees, rice provisions, lodging, and other such services shall be entirely prohibited. In keeping with this intent, post stations (宿駅) shall set their hire fees, lodging charges, and other rates in accordance with current market prices, reducing them as much as possible, and shall ensure uniformity by posting written notices on each lodging establishment at each station without exception.
