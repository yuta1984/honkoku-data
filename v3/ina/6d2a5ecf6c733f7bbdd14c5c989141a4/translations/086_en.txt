【Right folio】

Item: Regarding the neighbor of Denshichi—there can be no claim of ignorance that gambling was being hosted on the premises; yet having treated the matter with negligence, this being a serious breach of conduct, said person is hereby ordered to pay a fine.

Item: Regarding the nanushi (village headman), machidai (town representative), and kumigashira (group leaders): when persons in violation of prohibited conduct are found to be present, such persons ought to be reported to the designated supervisory officials (kohito/shuchaku) and dealt with accordingly; yet having left the matter unattended, this being a serious breach of conduct, they are hereby ordered to pay a fine.

In accordance with the above judgments hereby rendered, all officials concerned shall henceforth conduct themselves with proper restraint befitting their station. Those ordered to perform penitential service (kaitō-kin) shall carry out their duties correctly as directed by the appropriate authorities. The fines (kaitō-ryō-sen) shall be paid within three days.

　　　　　　27th day of the 2nd month
