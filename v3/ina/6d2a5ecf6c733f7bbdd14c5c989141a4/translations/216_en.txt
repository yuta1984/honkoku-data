It has been commanded that all shall receive and uphold the Imperial will (eishi) without any misunderstanding. Henceforth, those who wantonly kill foreigners or commit improper acts shall not only obstruct the Imperial commands and bring about national calamity, but shall also fail to uphold the dignity and prestige of the Imperial nation before the countries with which diplomatic relations have been established. As such conduct is regarded as utterly inexcusable, commensurate with the gravity of their offenses, even those of samurai rank shall have their samurai status (shiseki) revoked and be subjected to appropriate legal punishment. It has therefore been proclaimed that each and every person shall abide by the Imperial commands and refrain from any acts of violence.
