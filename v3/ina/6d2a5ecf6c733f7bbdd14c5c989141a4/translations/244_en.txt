【Right folio】

Recently, when the *Yūgekitai* (raiding corps composed of Tokugawa deserters/defectors) launched an attack, [the Takatō domain troops] urgently dispatched to this office [the *Chinbufu*] and thereafter maintained their garrison duties with diligence and full dedication. In the meantime, the rebel forces in the vicinity have become scattered and dispersed, and moreover, troops from various domains have been successively deployed. Given these circumstances, it has become a hardship for a small domain such as yours to continue maintaining a prolonged encampment. Accordingly, you are hereby ordered to withdraw your troops and return home for the present. Upon your return to your domain, you are to rest and restore your forces. However, as it cannot be said that the realm has been fully pacified, you may well be ordered to dispatch troops again depending on the situation; therefore, you are to maintain readiness and preparation for this eventuality.

　　　　　　*Chinbufu* (Pacification Government Office)
　Sixth month, fifth day　　*Tanoshi-bō* [official seal]

　　　　　　To the Takatō Domain (*Takatō-han*)
