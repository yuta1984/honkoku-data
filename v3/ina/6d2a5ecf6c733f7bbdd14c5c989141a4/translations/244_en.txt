【Right and Left Folio】

Recently, when the *Yūgekitai* (Rapid Strike Corps), composed of deserters from the Tokugawa [shogunate], made an incursion, [the domain's troops], in urgent response, were dispatched to this headquarters [of the Pacification Government (*Chinbu-fu*)], and have since remained encamped while diligently attending to their guard duties. As the rebel forces in the vicinity have now scattered and dispersed, and as troop contingents from various domains are being successively dispatched [to take over], it has been determined that for a small domain (*shōhan*) to continue maintaining a prolonged encampment would constitute a considerable hardship. Accordingly, an order has been issued for the troops to return home for the time being. Upon returning to your domain, [the troops] should be afforded rest and recuperation. However, as the situation within the realm (*kainai*) cannot yet be said to be fully settled, there is a possibility that, depending on how circumstances develop, another order for deployment may be issued. [The domain] should therefore remain in a state of preparedness.

　　　　　Pacification Government (*Chinbu-fu*)
　Sixth month, fifth day　　*Tanoshi-mō* [seal]

　　　　　　To Takatō Domain
