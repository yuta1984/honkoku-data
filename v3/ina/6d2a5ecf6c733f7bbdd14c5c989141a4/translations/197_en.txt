Town Supply/Liaison Officer [町仕送]

　　Kitagen — Rizaemon

Regarding the aforementioned person: he has served with sincere dedication, and furthermore has previously served as an assistant town elder [先年寄], having acquired thorough knowledge and understanding of the duties. In view of all this, he is hereby appointed as Town Elder [町年寄]. He is to carry out the duties of Town Supply/Liaison Officer [町仕送] as before, consult with his colleagues without reservation or concealment, and attend diligently to all matters.

　　Fifth day of the third month

Naitō Yobe'e and Kawahika Kanbe'e were in attendance, and on this occasion, the Monthly Duty Officer [御月番], Lord Kawachi, conveyed the order. The Magistrate's official [御代官], the Hakigoshi [掃木腰] storage officer, Inoue, and others attended as customary.
