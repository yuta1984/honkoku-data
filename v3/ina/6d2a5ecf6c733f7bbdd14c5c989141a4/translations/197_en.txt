　　　　Town Shiokuri [Town Supply Officer]
　　　　　Kitahara Rizaemon

Regarding the above-named individual: he has served with sincerity and diligence, and moreover, having previously held the same post, he is thoroughly familiar with its duties. For these reasons, he is hereby appointed Town Elder [町年寄]. He is to carry out the role of Town Shiokuri as before, to consult openly and without reservation with his colleagues in the same office, and to attend to all matters with due care and diligence.

　　　　3rd Month, 5th Day

Naitō Yohei and Kawachi Kahei were in attendance. The monthly duty officer [御月番], Kawachi, conveyed the orders. The Deputy Magistrate [御代官] Haki [Sweeping-Tree?], the Clerical Officer [御物書] Inoue, and others were present as customary.
