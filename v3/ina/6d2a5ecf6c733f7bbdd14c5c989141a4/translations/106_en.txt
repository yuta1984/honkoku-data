【Right folio】

Nishimura
　Rokuta
　　Ryūzō
　Acting representative (*myōdai*)
　　Magonaemon

Regarding the above-named individual: in recognition of his dedicated service in official craft work (*go-yō saiku*), he is hereby granted, as a special distinction, permission to wear a *haori* (formal short coat). He shall furthermore continue to apply himself diligently to his duties.

　Seventh month, second day

The foregoing shall be duly conveyed. That is all.

　　　　　The town elders (*chō-toshiyori*)

　　　　[Note: In attendance]

The two *kogashira* (sub-officials) received the notification from the monthly duty officer (*tsukiban*), Hon'emon.
They appeared together with the surface officer (*omote-yaku*) Soichi.
In order to show the matter to all colleagues, on the following third day, Soichi was summoned to the town office (*machi-yakusho*) and the written order was handed over to him.
