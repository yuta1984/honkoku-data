【Translation】

(Continued from previous page) ...matters should be conducted simply and with minimal ceremony, and all parties should take care to ensure that those traveling along the roads are not caused undue inconvenience or hardship.

　　For those of middle rank (chūyō) and above:
　　　　Personal attendants (kinshū): up to 10 persons; hand-service staff (temawari):
　　　　up to 15 persons in total

　　For those of lesser rank (shōyō):
　　　　Personal attendants (kinshū): up to 6 persons; hand-service staff (temawari):
　　　　up to 10 persons in total

The foregoing has been issued as an official directive (as ordered from above).

　　Fourth Month (April)

The above-mentioned written directive was issued on the seventeenth day of the fourth month, and on the morning of the eighteenth day, it was passed along by fellow officials to Fukuromura (袋村), the original administrative office, and was then transmitted as notification to the representatives of the twelve town districts (jūni-chō).
