Matters are to be handled simply and with minimal ceremony, and those traveling along the roads are to take care that no inconvenience is caused to others.

　　For those of middle rank (chūyō) and above:
　　　　Up to ten personal attendants (kinjū) and fifteen general attendants (temawarі) in total.

　　For those of lesser rank (shōyō):
　　　　Up to six personal attendants (kinjū) and ten general attendants (temawari) in total.

The foregoing has been issued as an official directive.

　　Fourth Month (April)

The above official notice (o-kakitsuke) was issued on the seventeenth day of the fourth month, and on the morning of the eighteenth day, it was passed on to Gyōji Fukuromura by the colleagues of the same office, and further circulated to the representatives of the twelve chō districts.
