〇　Regarding Opium Tobacco (Ahentabako)

Regarding opium tobacco: as it is a substance that damages people's health and poses a danger to human life, [this matter] is addressed in the official ordinance. The bringing in of [such substances] by foreigners has already been strictly prohibited; however, it has recently come to our attention that among foreigners there are those who appear to be secretly importing opium tobacco. As the said tobacco constitutes, as stated above, a great harm to the populace (seimin)…
