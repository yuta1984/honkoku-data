10th month

As the post stations (shukueki) along the Five Highways (Gokaidō) have been falling into prolonged financial distress, and in recent years prices have risen considerably, making it difficult to sustain operations in the current manner — given these circumstances, various administrative reforms (shihō kaishō) have now been implemented. Although successive supplementary increases to the horse-and-porter fees (jinba chinsen) at these post stations had already been ordered on a step-by-step basis, in addition to those increases, from the 11th month of the current Year of the Rabbit (卯年, 1867), the base fees shall be further increased by 65% (six-fold and five-tenths increase); and furthermore, the ferry fares at Imagiri and Atsuta along the Tōkaidō shall be increased by 35% (three-fold and five-tenths increase); and for the remaining ferry crossings and river stations (kawaba) along the Five Highways...
