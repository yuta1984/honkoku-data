**[Right folio]**

Item: Since the medieval period, there has been no small number of shrines that use the style of "Gongen," the Gozu Tennō, and similar forms, or that employ Buddhist terminology to designate their divine titles (shingō). Each and every one of these shrines is to submit a detailed written account of its history and origins as soon as possible.

However, regarding shrines that receive Imperial ritual (chokusai), or those in possession of Imperial autograph documents (goshinkan) or imperially inscribed plaques (chokugaku), these too are to submit an inquiry \[to the central authorities\], after which an official ruling will be issued. For all remaining shrines, the matter is to be reported to the judicial authority (saiban), garrison commander (chindai), domain lord (ryōshu), or administrative overseer (shihaigashira), as appropriate.

Item: Shrines that have been using a Buddhist image (butsuzō) as their object of divine worship (shintai) are henceforth to rectify this practice.
