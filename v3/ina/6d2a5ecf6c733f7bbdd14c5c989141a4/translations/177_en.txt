…should there be no suspicious circumstances, a travel pass (kitte) will be issued on the spot at the checkpoint. Any traveler found beyond the checkpoint without such a pass shall not be permitted to lodge at any inn, not only within the city (go-fu-nai) but also along the highways and in all rural villages (zaizai). Furthermore, anyone who attempts to force their way through without submitting to the inspection, or any traveler found without a valid travel pass, shall be arrested without mercy; and should they resist, they are to be cut down on the spot. However, those who are already en route to the city (shutsufū) at this time and are unfamiliar with the checkpoint procedures shall be thoroughly questioned at the checkpoint, and if no particular grounds for suspicion are found, they shall be permitted to pass.

The foregoing provisions apply equally to shogunal lands (go-ryō), private domains (shiryō), and temple and shrine lands (jisha-ryō)…
