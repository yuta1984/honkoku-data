At the time of the New Year's greetings, [the person in question] shall appear following the Town Elder (machidoshiyori), in the same manner as Miyashita Heibei, and offer formal greetings.

Item: When a memorial Buddhist service is held at Mankōji Temple, the same procedure as described above shall be followed at the time of offering obeisance.

Item: Regarding various petitions and requests, the previous practice of handling these verbally through town representatives (machidai) shall be discontinued; henceforth, the Town Elder shall handle such matters.

Item: On occasions such as the Five Seasonal Festivals, when appearing before superiors (upper authorities) to offer formal greetings, [the person] shall serve as a single representative, in the same manner as the Town Elder.
