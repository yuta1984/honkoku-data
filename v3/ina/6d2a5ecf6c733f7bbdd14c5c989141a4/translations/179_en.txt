(At the checkpoint,) an official seal shall be affixed to the travel permit (tegata/kitte) issued there; those who do not carry either said permit or a written declaration from their master or a senior official shall not be permitted to pass. However, those who are currently in the midst of travel and have not yet been informed of the intent of this newly circulated notice shall be thoroughly questioned, and if no suspicious circumstances are found, they shall be permitted to pass even without carrying said permit.

The foregoing provisions shall be communicated without omission, regardless of whether the territory in question falls under direct shogunal domain, private domain, or temple and shrine lands.

As the foregoing has been issued by the bakufu (public authority) in the form of an official notice, it shall be duly circulated throughout the town. The above.
