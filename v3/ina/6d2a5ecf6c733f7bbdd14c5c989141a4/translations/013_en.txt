Even those who are able to manage their affairs should be as frugal as possible with miscellaneous expenses, make thrift their guiding principle among one another, and set aside whatever goods may serve as food provisions. It is common knowledge among all that even those in higher positions have been financially strained from the outset; and while the dispatch of officials (御出張) will inevitably incur further enormous expenses, it is expected that relief measures will be provided so that the domain's people do not suffer from hunger and thirst. Nevertheless, you are earnestly urged to unite as one and to store up food provisions in order to sustain yourselves.

The principal points of austerity are as follows:

Item: On occasions such as festivals, inviting relatives and close acquaintances in the various towns and districts shall be strictly refrained from by all parties. Matters should be handled simply, limited to offering a modest amount of sacred sake (o-miki) before the shrine.
