【Right folio】

Regarding the death of the former Minister of the Right of the Kazanin family,
the prohibition of music and entertainment has been decided as described in the attached document.
Please ensure that this is understood and conveyed to those in each town
who were previously notified.
　Eighth month

Regarding the death of the former Minister of the Right of the Kazanin family,
construction work and music are to be suspended for seven days.
For those in distant areas, the suspension shall be counted
from the day they receive notice of this document,
as stated in the written notice.
Please circulate the above accordingly.
　Twenty-seventh day of the Eighth month

As a written order (ofuregaki) has been issued by the shogunate (kōgi)
regarding the above matter,
please circulate it throughout the town. That is all.
　Twelfth day of the Ninth month

The above two documents were shown to fellow officials and
handed over to the Gyōji-machi (administrative town representatives).
