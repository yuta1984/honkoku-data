　　Eighth month, tenth day [of Keiō 2 (1866)]

The above matter was concluded for the month, with Kitahara Rizaemon in attendance, at the residence of the Ohiro Shōya (village headman).

Item: Regarding the official notice (ofuregaki) and the arrow tax (yayaku) as instructed by Lord Matsui Shūzō — this was conveyed and announced at a general meeting attended by Kujiman, Matsuzawa Ritōta, and Taira [or Hei].
