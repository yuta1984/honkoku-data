Regarding the circumstances of [the person who] humbly went [to the said place]: as this conduct was improper and unruly, [he] is to be removed from his post, and the duties of bridge [inspector] and inspector's assistant are hereby notified by the following notice, and he is additionally ordered to serve [in a reduced capacity].

Item: Regarding Kirobei — [he engaged in] gambling, which is forbidden by law, as well as repeated fighting; moreover, having conspired with Yonezaburō, he accompanied a large number of people and went to [the residence of] Heihachi [■■] of Shimo-Ōshima village, bringing money [as a bribe or for gambling purposes], and [committed further] ■ misconduct. As this conduct was wholly improper and unruly, he is hereby ordered to pay a penalty fine (過怠動).

Item: Regarding Shichikajū — [he acted as] an intermediary for illicit rice dealings on behalf of Kirobei, who was imprisoned [in the jail], and as this conduct was improper and unruly, he is hereby ordered to be placed under house confinement (押込め).

Item: Regarding Yūjirō — he provided lodging for gambling, which is forbidden by law, and as this conduct was wholly improper and unruly, he is hereby ordered to pay a penalty fine (過怠動). The above.
