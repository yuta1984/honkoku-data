**Articles for Deliberative Inquiry (*Giji On-mon*)**

1. Military preparedness (*gunbi*) is the means by which the peace and safety of the people (*minan*) is upheld. [The question is:] how shall we establish a military system (*heisei*) and develop both naval and land forces?

2. Gold and grain (*kin-koku*) are the foremost necessities of expenditure, and all governance and administration (*dosei*) depends upon them. By what means shall the proper course of accounting and finance (*kaikei*) be established in the present day?

3. The Eastern Army (*Tōgun*) has yet to accomplish its objectives, and the hearts of the people still harbor fear and trepidation (*kiku*). It is unclear by what means one shall discharge one's duties and achieve the proper settlement and pacification (*chintei*).

---

Regarding newly written works (*shinchou*) as well as reprinted publications (*honkoku*), it was intended that all such works be published only under official authorization (*kankyō*) from above. However, in recent times, various works have been published indiscriminately, which is an unacceptable matter. Henceforth, the buying and selling of all items that lightly disregard official authorization shall be entirely suspended.

　　Intercalary Fourth Month.

---

On this occasion, the standard prices (*teika*) for currency (*kahei*) throughout the realm (*udai*) have been deliberated upon and proclaimed for circulation (*tsūyō*). However, it appears that [some] have come to understand that [this currency] cannot be applied toward various tax payments and public levies (*shō-nōnyū*), and furthermore, there are reports that [some] are pursuing private profit by setting various handling fees (*uchi-chin*) at a considerable level…
