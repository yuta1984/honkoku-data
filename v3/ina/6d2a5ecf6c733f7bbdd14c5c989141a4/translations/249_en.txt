…the foregoing having been proclaimed, each [party] shall set down their considered opinions and present themselves at the Provisional Assembly Hall (*Kinchū Kari-tateshо*) within the Imperial Palace precincts on the second day of next month.

　Intercalary Fourth Month.

---

**Deliberative Inquiry (*Giji Shimon*)**

1. Military preparedness (*gunbi*) is the means by which the peace and security of the people are maintained. [The question is:] how shall the military system (*heisei*) be established and both the naval and land armies (*kairi-gun*) be developed? What is the appropriate method for achieving this?

2. Gold and grain (*kinkok*) are the foremost necessities of expenditure, and all administration of governance (*taisei*) depends upon them. By what means shall the way of fiscal administration (*kaikei*) today find its proper footing?

3. The Eastern Army (*Tō-gun*) has not yet achieved success, and the hearts of the people still harbor anxiety and fear. It is unclear by what means [one] shall faithfully discharge one's duties and bring about pacification in a fitting manner.

---

Regarding newly written works and reprinted publications (*honkoku*): although such materials ought to be published only with official government authorization (*kankyо*), in recent times various publications have been issued indiscriminately, which is an unacceptable state of affairs. Henceforth, the buying and selling of all items produced without due official authorization shall entirely be suspended.

　Intercalary Fourth Month.

---

On this occasion, the fixed prices (*teika*) of currencies throughout the realm (*udai*) have been deliberated upon and their circulation (*tsūyō*) has been proclaimed. However, [it has come to our attention that] some parties appear to have understood this as inapplicable to various tribute payments (*shо-jōnō*), and furthermore, [there are those who] seek private profit by levying excessive handling fees (*uchichin*) and the like…
