　　　Nakamachi (Middle Town district)
　　　　Kiinokuya
　　　　　　　Higashi-jū (East Ten [shop identifier])

Regarding the aforementioned person: although he purchased old metal goods (furugane-rui) from Fujiyoshira of Aramachi village, he failed to recognize that said goods were of improper origin, and moreover, without verifying the background and provenance of the same person, he proceeded with the purchase. This conduct is deemed utterly improper (furatsu no kyoku). While a strict formal punishment (kuchisūji) ought to be imposed, as a special act of leniency, the money [paid for the goods] is to be confiscated and a fine (karyō) imposed instead. This fine shall be paid within three days.
