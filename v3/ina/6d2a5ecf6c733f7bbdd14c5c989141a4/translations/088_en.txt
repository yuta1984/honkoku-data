(Continued from previous page) [The accused] were called upon by a person named Ka— of Hokoochi Village and went there, having contrived to arrange matters in a manner resembling the soliciting of prostitutes. Having been found to have violated the official prohibitions, this being a serious breach of conduct, a strict punishment would ordinarily be in order; however, by special act of leniency,

They are hereby ordered to be banned from residing within the castle town and its surrounding areas. They shall vacate their dwellings in the town immediately. Henceforth they shall conduct themselves with proper restraint and shall abide by the official prohibitions.
