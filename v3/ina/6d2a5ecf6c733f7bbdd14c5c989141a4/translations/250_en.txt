Now that the aforementioned standard prices (*teika*) have been proclaimed, [all parties] shall, without hesitation or doubt, exchange and circulate not only gold, silver, and copper coinage (*kin-gin-sen*) but also foreign silver (*yōgin*) in accordance with the established regulations. Furthermore, since various levies (*un-jō*) and disbursements (*o-harai*) by the Grand Council of State (*Daijō-kan*) shall also be handled entirely in accordance with the established regulations, [all parties] are to take due note of this. Should there be any person who raises an objection (*igi*), the appropriate official measures (*go-sata*) shall be taken against them.

　　Intercalary Fourth Month.

---

1. Petitions (*negai*), inquiries (*ukagai*), and notifications (*todoke*), except on days of the first and sixth [of each ten-day period], shall be received every day at the relevant offices (*seki*) within the Imperial Palace precincts (*kinchū*) and at samurai residences (*buke*), from the Hour of the Dragon (*tatsukoku*, approx. 7–9 a.m.) until the Hour of the Snake (*mikoku*, approx. 9–11 a.m.).

　　*Proviso:* Matters requiring direct, urgent presentation are not subject to this restriction.

**To the Various Domains (*Shohан*)**

On this occasion, with respect to the troop levy (*chōhei*): domains that are directed to furnish forty-eight men or more in proportion to their assessed rice yield (*kokudaka*) shall attach a commanding officer (*shikikan*) [to the contingent and]:

1. Submit [the troops accordingly]. Note, however, that [the commanding officer] shall be counted within the aforementioned quota of personnel.
