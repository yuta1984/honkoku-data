Regarding the above: in connection with the matter of Chūji's boundary dispute with his neighbor Toyowataya and the twenty-six [Buddhist] temples, the said man's wife filed an appeal by circumventing proper channels (osso), and upon examination, [the claims] were found to be without proper legal grounds. A mediator (rige-nin) was appointed, and [the parties] are to comply with and accept the terms of a settlement (jidan).
