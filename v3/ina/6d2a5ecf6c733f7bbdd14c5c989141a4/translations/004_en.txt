(Continued from the previous page) As the Shogun's fatigue has been increasingly worsening, should the worst come to pass and he fall critically ill, the matter of succession is to be entrusted to the Lord Hitotsubashi Chunagon (Middle Counselor). Furthermore, as the subjugation of Chōshū and Nagato is a matter of utmost urgency, it has also been decreed that he (Lord Hitotsubashi) shall lead the campaign in the Shogun's stead.

As the above has been issued in writing by the Bakufu (Shogunate), it should be duly communicated throughout the town. The above is hereby noted.
