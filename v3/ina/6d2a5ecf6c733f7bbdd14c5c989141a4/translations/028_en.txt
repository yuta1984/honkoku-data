In the city of Edo and at the post stations along the Five Highways, the use of horse-drawn carts (馬車) for the transport of goods has been officially permitted. Accordingly, travel routes are to be established in conformity with the existing arrangements for the main roads (赤通路), and matters are to be managed so that no inconveniences arise.

As the above has been sanctioned (by the Bakufu), this has been communicated to the Road Magistrate (道中奉行) and the Town Magistrate (町奉行), and all relevant parties are to be duly notified.

　10th month

In accordance with the above, as the official notice (御書付) from the Bakufu has been issued, it is to be circulated throughout the town. That is all.
