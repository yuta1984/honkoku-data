【Left folio】

[Continuing the case of Jingoemon:] Although he had been ordered to undergo confinement within the village, he repeatedly sneaked out into the town district; furthermore, as he had previously engaged in gambling and has now done so again, his conduct is improper; therefore, in addition to the disciplinary confinement already imposed, a monetary fine for misconduct is hereby levied upon him.

One: Regarding Kisaburō [lit. "Kisroku Zendai"] — as his participation in gambling was improper, disciplinary confinement is hereby imposed upon him.

One: Regarding the neighbors of Matasaburō — on the grounds that it is inconceivable that they were unaware that his residence was being used as a venue for gambling, [the penalty] has been raised beyond the standard [level]; as this [conduct] was thoughtless and negligent, a monetary fine is hereby imposed upon them.
