【Right folio】

Ōtaya
　Matsujirō

The above-named individual is hereby appointed as official purveyor of fish (御肴御用達) to the domain. He shall diligently carry out his duties in accordance with the relevant guidelines and future instructions.

17th day of the 3rd month

Mukadaya
　Jūbee

The above-named individual is hereby appointed as official purveyor of confections (御菓子御用達) to the domain. He shall diligently carry out his duties in accordance with the relevant guidelines and future instructions.

17th day of the 3rd month

---

This written order, as issued, shall be shown to colleagues of the same office and then handed over to their representative.
