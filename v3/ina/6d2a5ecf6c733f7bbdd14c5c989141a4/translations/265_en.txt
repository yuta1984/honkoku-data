【Left Folio】

On this occasion, in accordance with the stated intentions [of the authorities], the Office for the Development of Industries (*Kaisan Yakusho*) has been established. Aoyama Sanzō and Komatsu Tanaka have been appointed as *Kaiza Bugyō* (Overseers of the Development Office). Similarly, Takizawa Masashirō has been appointed as *Kaisan-gata* (Officer for Industrial Development). Furthermore, Ikegami Sanemon, Kanashima Yagotsune, Okamura Yoji, and Kondō Hikozō have been appointed as *Kaisan-gata Soeyaku* (Assistant Officers for Industrial Development). The previous designations of *Yamakawa* (Mountain Affairs Officer), *Hayashi-gata* (Forestry Officer), *Takigi-gata* (Firewood Officer), and *Takeyabu Gimmi-yaku* (Bamboo Grove Inspector) are hereby abolished. All parties are hereby ordered to take note of this matter.
