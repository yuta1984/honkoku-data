Regarding the above-named individuals: there were suspicious circumstances concerning Jizaemon within the town, and the machidai (town representative) was summoned by bell (suzuri). It was reported that the party had been serving as a house guard (takunban), but then committed the improper act of fleeing. However, since they have now been placed under restraint, as a special act of leniency, they are hereby firmly subject to this ruling.

　30th Day of the Eighth Month [August]

In accordance with this firm ruling regarding both of the aforementioned individuals, notification has been conveyed to kumigashira (group leader) Denzaemon that they are to resume regular duties from the first day of the coming month. Furthermore, it has also been communicated to both officials of the kujikata (judicial/administrative office) that extended duties are to be carried out accordingly.
