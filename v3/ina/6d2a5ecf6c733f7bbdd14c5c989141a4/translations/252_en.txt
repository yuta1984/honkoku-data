【Right Folio】

It has been proclaimed that, from the coming 7th day, the *Gunmu Kaikan* (Military Affairs Transfer Officers) shall be relocated to the Army Bureau (*Rikugun-kyoku*).

　　Fifth Month.

---

The other day, regulations concerning escort retinues (*tomo-zure*) were issued; however, as the interior of the *Kara-mon* (Chinese-style Gate) is narrow and confined, should large numbers of persons enter therein, disorderly and unregulated conduct may naturally arise. Therefore, henceforth, [all parties] are to conduct themselves in accordance with the following instructions, as has been officially decreed.

**Escort retinues permitted within the Kara-mon — as follows:**

　**Imperial Court Nobles and Feudal Lords** (*Miya Dōjō Shokō*):
　　Attendant samurai: 2 persons
　　Servants (*shimobe*): 2 persons
　　On rainy days, umbrella-bearer: 1 person

　**Within the Heisa-mon (平左門)**:
　　Attendant samurai: 2 persons
　　Sandal-bearer (*zōri-tori*): 1 person
　　※ Note: Of the escort samurai, the limit is 2 samurai only.

Item: Escort samurai shall not rest/loiter in the covered corridors (*kairō*).

Item: Up to the exterior of the Kara-mon, as per the recent proclamation, [retinues may comprise] 6 samurai-class persons and 3 servants, assembled and waiting at [that location]...
