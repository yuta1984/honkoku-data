[Right folio]

It goes without saying that those who practice onmyōdō (the Way of Yin and Yang) should be under the jurisdiction of the Tsuchimikado family. However, in recent years the situation has become extremely disorderly, and it has come to our attention that there are those who conduct [these] practices improperly and without authorization. Henceforth, [practitioners] must obtain a license from the Tsuchimikado family and comply fully with their directives and supervision.

The above was already circulated in the third year of Kansei [1791], the year of the Boar; however, it has come to our attention that in recent times there are again those who conduct [these] practices improperly and without authorization, which constitutes a misunderstanding of proper conduct. The contents of said notice are to be firmly understood and adhered to, without omission or neglect.

The above is to be circulated without exception.
　　Second month
