　　　　　Hokocho (Hokoji-machi)
　　　　　　Bunshi (Bun'shirō), represented by Saburō
　　　　　　Sōzaemon

The individuals named above, on the 19th day of the 8th month, upon seeing smoke rising in ■■-machi, promptly rushed to the scene and rendered distinguished service in firefighting efforts. In recognition of this praiseworthy conduct, they are each to be awarded one hundred mon (鳥目百文) as a gratuity (sake money).

　　　　　27th day of the 10th month

The two persons in question did not appear in person. As there appears to have been some confusion/mix-up, they could not be summoned directly; therefore, representatives (格代) were appointed to receive on their behalf. For ■Shichi, the representative was Bunshi's portion (代); for Inoshichi, Sōzaemon concluded the matter. Having withdrawn with due respect (拝罷), Jūdai Jūsaburō of the town was summoned, and the written notice (御書き下げ) along with one hundred mon each in coins was duly handed over.
