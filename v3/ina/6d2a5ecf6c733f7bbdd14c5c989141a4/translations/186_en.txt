　　13th day of the Second month

---

Regarding Lord Takamatsu: it is reported that he has fled Kyoto, leading a body of men and proceeding eastward toward the eastern provinces. This matter is by no means to be understood as a loyalist deployment made with proper dedication and purpose. It is surmised, rather, that a band of reckless and innumerable ruffians have deceived innocent young nobles [kōdachi] and drawn out a great number [of followers] under false pretenses. These lawless individuals are impersonating the vanguard of His Excellency the Sōtoku [the imperial/shogunal military commander-general], and it is feared that outrages and disturbances may occur in various places, the extent of which is difficult to predict. Therefore, all domains are to thoroughly understand the import of this notice and take care not to be deceived by such elements. Furthermore…
