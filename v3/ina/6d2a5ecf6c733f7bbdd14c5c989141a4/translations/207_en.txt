**[Left folio]**

Addendum:
Buddhist images venerated as *honji* (the "original ground" manifestations) that have been hung before shrine sanctuaries, as well as items such as *waniguchi* (metal ring gongs), *bonshō* (temple bells), Buddhist altar implements, and similar objects that have been placed \[at shrines\] are to be removed without delay.

The foregoing has been proclaimed by official order.
　　　Third month \[of Keiō 2, 1866\]

As the foregoing has been proclaimed by the Imperial Court, this is to be circulated throughout the town for the people's information and guidance. That is all.
　　　Fourth month \[of Keiō 2, 1866\]

As the above official notice was issued on the seventh day of the fourth month, it has been handed over to the *yagjōji* (coordinator) among the fellow officers, \[for delivery\] to Fukuro-machi.
