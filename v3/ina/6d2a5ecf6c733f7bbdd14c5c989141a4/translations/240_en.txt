【Right folio】

Lord Toda Tosa-no-Kami, now retired, formerly known as Echizen-no-Kami, having fallen ill and been unable to recover despite efforts toward recuperation, passed away on the twenty-eighth day of last month, at the middle of the Hour of the Dragon (approximately 8:00 a.m.), at Utsunomiya. The aforementioned [Echizen-no-Kami] was the younger brother of the [Lord's] wife (Oku-sama); however, as he had been adopted as the heir of the late Lord Inaba-no-Kami, the familial relationship became that of nephew [to the current lord]. Nevertheless, as he was in fact a blood younger brother, [the lord] shall observe a half-reduced period of mourning (hangen no kibuku). Accordingly, until the 7th day of this month, the playing of musical instruments and music shall be refrained from, and particular care shall be taken regarding fire hazards, and [those concerned] are to be duly instructed to this effect. Note, however, that construction work (fushin) shall not be subject to any restriction.

The above matter is to be duly circulated throughout the town.
That is all.

　　5th day of the 6th month
