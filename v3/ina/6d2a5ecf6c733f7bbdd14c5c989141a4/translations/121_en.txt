Regarding the above: there were suspicious circumstances concerning Jizaemon within the town, and he had been placed under custody. 　30th Day of the Eighth Month [August]

Regarding the matter of the above-mentioned Heijūrō's role as gōyado (lodging manager for rural visitors), Hachiemon and other gō officials came on official business, but as to where they might seek lodging and whether such arrangements should be suspended during the period of confinement — regarding Hachiemon's lodging, we inquired with the Omononkaki-sama (the official scribe/clerk) as to whether an exception might be granted. We were informed that Hachiemon's continued lodging was acceptable, and that matters concerning other rural districts should be left to their own discretion. This has accordingly been communicated to the kumigashira (group leader).
