　　　　　　Yokochō [side street district]
　　　　　　　Machidai [town representative]
　　　　　　　　Heijūrō

The above-named Heijūrō had been entrusted with keeping watch over Jiizaemon of the town, who was under suspicion. However, Heijūrō improperly allowed Jiizaemon to return home, and Jiizaemon subsequently fled, which was a most reprehensible matter. Nevertheless, as Jiizaemon was brought back and placed under restraint, and this was duly reported, as a special consideration [Heijūrō] was reprimanded and placed under house confinement (oshikome).

　Last day of the Eighth Month [August 30th]

Regarding the above-mentioned Heijūrō serving as gōshuku (rural lodging keeper for visiting rural officials): Hachiemon and other rural officials (gōyakunin) have come on official business, and [the question arose as to] whether someone should be asked to handle [visitors] on his behalf while he is under confinement. As for Hachiemon's matter of lodging, an inquiry was made to the Mono-kaki-sama (official scribe/clerk) as to whether an exception might be granted; the response was that Hachiemon may lodge [there] as usual, and that for visitors from other rural districts, they should be left to follow the instructions from above. Accordingly, this matter was communicated to the Kumigashira (neighborhood group head).
