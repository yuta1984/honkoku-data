【Right Folio】

　　Fifth Month　　　　　Grand Council of State (*Daijō-kan*)

Three copies of official directives (*o-tatsu o-kakitsuke*) dated the ninth day of the fifth month.

Item: Seals bearing the names of prefectures (*fu*), domains (*han*), and counties (*ken*) shall be established.
　　Provided that they shall be engraved as: "Seal of [Such-and-such] Prefecture," "Seal of [Such-and-such] Domain," or "Seal of [Such-and-such] County."

Item: Social families (*shake*) and temples (*jiin*) and the like belonging to the jurisdictions of their respective prefectures, domains, and counties shall henceforth be placed under the direction and administration of those same prefectures, domains, and counties.
　　Provided that, for matters that cannot be resolved at the prefecture, domain, or county level, the relevant administrative office shall dispatch its seal and submit the matter to the *Benji Dentatsu-sho* (Bureau of Communication and Transmission).
　　With the exception of the Two Grand Shrines of Ise, the great shrines (*taisha*), and the imperially-designated festival shrines (*chokusai jinja*), the *Jingikan* (Department of Divinities) shall henceforth no longer exercise administrative control over shrine families (*shake*).

　The foregoing has been officially promulgated.
