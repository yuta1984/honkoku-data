Item: Regarding the neighbors of Yūjirō — it cannot be said that they were unaware that [Yūjirō's residence] was being used as a gambling house; yet they were negligent in their duty of oversight and allowed the situation to pass without action. As this conduct was improper and unruly, they are hereby ordered to pay a penalty fine (過料銭).

Item: Regarding Yonezaburō and two other persons, together with their relatives and neighborhood association members — it cannot be said that they were unaware that gambling was taking place; yet they neglected their duty of oversight and allowed the situation to pass without action. As this conduct was improper and unruly, they are hereby ordered to pay a penalty fine (過料銭).

Item: Regarding the village headman (名主), deputy seal-holder (代判), town deputy (町代), and group leader (組頭) — when gambling was taking place, they should have referred the occupants of the relevant premises to investigation (吟味), and if [the offenders] had not complied, appropriate measures of handling should have been taken; yet [these officials] were negligent and allowed the situation to pass without action. As this conduct was improper and unruly, they are hereby ordered to pay a penalty fine (過料銭).
