Item: Regarding the neighbors of Yūjirō — it is inconceivable that they were unaware that [his house] was being used as a gambling lodging; yet they negligently overlooked [the obligation to] maintain proper vigilance, and as this conduct was improper and unruly, they are hereby ordered to pay a penalty fine (過料銭).

Item: Regarding Yonezaburō and two other persons, together with their relatives and associates (親類組合) — it is inconceivable that they were unaware that gambling and fighting were taking place; yet they negligently overlooked [their duty to] maintain proper vigilance, and as this conduct was improper and unruly, they are hereby ordered to pay a penalty fine (過料銭).

Item: Regarding the village headman (名主), deputy seal-holder (代判), town representative (町代), and group leader (組頭) — if gambling and fighting were occurring, [they should have conducted] an inspection of the premises (小屋敷検見) and, if [the offenders] failed to comply, there were measures available to them for handling the matter; yet they negligently overlooked [their responsibilities], and as this conduct was improper and unruly, they are hereby ordered to pay a penalty fine (過料銭).
