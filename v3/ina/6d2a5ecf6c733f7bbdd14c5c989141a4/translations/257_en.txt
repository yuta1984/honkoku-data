Item: Those in possession of old-style *chōgin* (bar silver) and *mameitagin* (bean-shaped silver) shall have these purchased by the government using the newly reformed gold currency, which will be implemented shortly. Accordingly, the relevant parties shall report the quantities in their possession to the *Kaikei-kan* (Accounting Office) in due course.

　　Fifth Month, Year of the Dragon (*Tatsu*, i.e., Meiji 3, 1868)

　The paper currency (*kinsat*su) that was previously announced shall be issued beginning on the fifteenth day of this month. It shall be exchanged and circulated without delay. Furthermore, as it has been ordered that specimen notes be displayed at the five designated currency exchange offices (*gohon ryōgae*), this matter shall be duly communicated to all relevant parties without omission.

　　Fifth Month

　Five copies of official directives (*o-watashi o-kakitsuke*) dated the tenth day of the fifth month.

　On the occasion of the recent triumphant return of the Imperial Procession (*taiga*) from Naniwa (Osaka), it has been decreed that a shrine (*yashiro*) to *Toyotaikō* (Toyotomi Hideyoshi) shall be constructed. The *Taikō*, from of old, restored order from great turmoil, rendered loyal support and judicious service, and his meritorious deeds span the ages both past and present.
