　　　　　　　　　　　　Eta
　300 mon in copper coins　　　　　　Kanetaro
Regarding this person: the other day at the Benzaiten ferry crossing, when a boat broke loose, he quickly rushed to the scene and worked [to help]. Therefore, copper coins are given as drinking money.

The above matters shall be announced. That is all.
　August 22nd　　Town Officials

The above official document was shown to fellow officials and distributed to each of them.
