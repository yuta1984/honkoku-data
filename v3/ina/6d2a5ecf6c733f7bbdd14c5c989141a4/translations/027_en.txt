Received from the previous day; from the 7th day of the 9th month,
the ban on music and entertainment (御鳴物) is hereby lifted. Musical performances have been permitted since yesterday.
This is to be noted for your reference.
In accordance with prior precedent (先格), and given that
the prices of all goods are currently high,
this would cause hardship for the common people (下々). Therefore, from the 7th day of the 9th month,
the ban is to be lifted.

The above matter was proclaimed in Osaka on the 4th day (of the month),
and accordingly, at this location as well,
the lifting of the ban is to be understood and communicated
to all relevant parties.

　9th month

In accordance with the above, as the official notice (御書付) from the Bakufu has been issued, it is to be circulated throughout the town. That is all.
