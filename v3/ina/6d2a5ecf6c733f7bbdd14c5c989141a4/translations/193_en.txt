[His Lordship is to proceed] directly to the Nakasendō, having made the necessary inquiries and arranged a palanquin [for official travel], stopping over in Nagoya, and from there proceeding with the intention of going to the capital [Kyoto/staying in the three central cities].

Regarding the above, these matters are to be circulated throughout the towns. That is all.

　　26th day of the 2nd month

―

Regarding the notification to the domain retainers [御家中], the following is communicated in confidence. His Lordship was summoned by Lord Iwakura, and was directed to take the Nakasendō, and from there had the intention of proceeding to the capital [or: staying in the three central domains]. However, there had previously been matters of suspicion [concerning certain individuals], and upon His Lordship's return to the domain, those who had been involved in [those suspicious matters] submitted petitions [for clemency], which had resulted in punishments being handed down.

[However,] it is hereby proclaimed that, going forward, [those individuals] may have their crimes pardoned [宥免] in accordance with their devoted service and performance in battle as soldiers.
