[His Lordship is to proceed] directly along the Nakasendō [Central Mountain Road], and after making inquiries regarding the use of palanquins [駕籠] for official duties [御家役], he shall stop at Nagoya, and from there it is intended that he shall make his way up to the capital [Kyoto].

The above matters are to be circulated throughout the towns and villages. That is all.

　　26th day of the 2nd month

―

As this matter has been communicated to the domain retainers [御家中], the following is relayed in confidence [内々]:

Regarding His Lordship, he has been summoned by Lord Iwakura and directed toward the Nakasendō, with the intention of proceeding thereafter to the capital [Kyoto]. There have, up until now, been certain matters regarded with suspicion [御不審の筋]. Upon His Lordship's return to the domain, those in service who submitted petitions [願い申し立て] regarding these matters have now come to be acknowledged.

It is hereby proclaimed: henceforth, depending on each soldier's performance in battle [兵士の戦の切り], their [previously held] offenses shall be pardoned [御宿免].
