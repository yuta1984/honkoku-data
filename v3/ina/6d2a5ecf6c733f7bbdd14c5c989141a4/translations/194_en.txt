[His Lordship] is to remain in a state of confinement [御謹慎] at his residence for the time being, and notification has been issued to the effect that the days of refraining from entering the capital [Kyoto] will continue. In due course, once this matter settles, it is expected that [His Lordship] will be able to return to the domain.

With that understanding, the domain retainers [御家中] are to gather in small groups and conduct themselves quietly and with restraint.

Regarding the above, these matters are to be circulated among the domain retainers. That is all.
