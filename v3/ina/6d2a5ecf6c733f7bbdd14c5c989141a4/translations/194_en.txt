[His Lordship has been instructed] to remain at his residence and conduct himself with due restraint until the matter is resolved, and that he should refrain from proceeding to the capital [Kyoto]. In accordance with this, it has been arranged that he will return here shortly.

Understanding the matter as such, all retainers of the domain [御家中], even while in the capital, are to conduct themselves quietly and with appropriate restraint.

The above is to be circulated to all domain retainers [御家中]. That is all.

　　Last day of the 2nd month [Midsommar]
　　　From the Senior Inspector [Ōmetsuke]

It is hereby made known that Naitō Yohei has been appointed this time to the post of District Magistrate [Gundai]. Understanding this matter accordingly, [this notice] is to be circulated throughout the towns.
