Until [further notice], it is hereby directed that [His Lordship] is to exercise restraint while at his residence [御在所], and to refrain from entering the capital [Kyoto]. In light of the above, His Lordship will shortly be returning here [to the domain]. Bearing this in mind, all members of the domain retinue [御家中] are to conduct themselves quietly and with due circumspection in all matters.

Regarding the above, these matters are to be circulated among the domain retainers [御家中]. That is all.

　　Last day of the 2nd month [晦日]
　　　[Signed:] The Senior Inspectors [大目付中]
