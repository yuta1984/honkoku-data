【Left folio】

25th day of the 9th month

Regarding the aforementioned confiscated land, it was verbally communicated that it should be entrusted to the town representative (machidai).

Regarding the matter of the Jugo (Honorary title for the Emperor's consort):

It has been decreed that (she) be elevated to the rank of Empress (Kōgō).

As the Ōmiya御所 (the Grand Imperial Palace) is to be newly constructed, regarding the expenses for said construction works: while the precedent had been to order assistance from those holding domains of 10,000 koku or more, on this occasion the assessment (kokueki-wari) has been ordered upon the Goryōsho (directly administered Shogunal lands), as well as upon the territories of various daimyo, and upon temple and shrine lands.
