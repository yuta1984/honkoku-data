As this has been proclaimed [by imperial authority], domain lords and local magistrates are to thoroughly instruct and admonish [the people] accordingly.

　However, since it is difficult to predict the extent to which longstanding bad customs and habits will gradually be dispelled and free channels of communication opened, when commoners and ordinary people wish to submit complaints directly to the Grand Council of State [太政官], such matters shall be left to the discretion of that office.

Item: Regarding the post stations along the Gokishichidō [the Five Home Provinces and Seven Circuits], it has always been the rule that those without a proper seal certificate [印鑑] should not be provided with post-horse relay services. However, in recent times, there have been those who, claiming to be retainers of imperial or noble households, yet without any verifiable seal certificate, have gone so far as to coercively demand the relay of people and horses without payment. This is an utterly outrageous matter. Henceforth, for those without verifiable seal certificates, and for those requesting services without payment or with deferred payment, relay services shall absolutely not be provided.
