Fine (karyo): 1 kanmon in coin

Regarding the above-named individual: during the third month of this year, without making the required report to the authorities, he purchased chōshi [sake flasks/vessels] from Masu-bushi and sent them on to Matsumoto. As the said chōshi were articles of irregular/illicit origin, [he was at fault in that] he purchased them without investigating their provenance, which constitutes a dereliction [of duty/proper conduct].
