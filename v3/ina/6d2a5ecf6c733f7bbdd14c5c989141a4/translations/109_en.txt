(Continued from previous page...) As a stipend (*o-teate*) for the exchange, twenty ryō per one hundred ryō shall be granted. Regarding the two-shu gold coins (*nishu-kin*) minted during the Tenpō era, it goes without saying that their circulation shall be suspended as previously notified. As for the exchange stipend, whereas it had previously been sixty ryō per one hundred ryō, it shall now be ninety ryō per one hundred ryō. Accordingly, those in possession of either the two-bu coins (*nibu-ban*) or the two-shu gold coins (*nishu-kin*) shall exchange them without delay. Given that such an exceptionally generous increase in the stipend has been granted, the exchange must be carried out promptly. Should there be anyone found to have hoarded or transferred twenty [ryō] or more, or engaged in fraudulent transactions, they shall be investigated, their holdings confiscated, and they shall be severely punished.
