Item: Regarding [Zō]zō — although it was acknowledged that his household was in financial hardship (nanjū), and relief rice (yasugome) had been granted to him, he sold it elsewhere. Regarding Yojirō, he lent his relief rice tickets (yasugome-fuda) to others. As this conduct is outrageous and improper (furachi), a monetary fine (karyō-sen) is hereby imposed.

Item: Regarding Yojirō's mother Noyo and Kisaburō — their conduct is likewise highly improper (jō/furachi). They engaged in buying and selling [relief rice] obtained from those who had received it as relief, and furthermore facilitated the lending of tickets (fuda-kashi).

Item: Regarding the manner in which the petition register (kin-gan-chō) was handled — as this is deemed improper (furachi), a strict reprimand (kitto shikari) is issued.

Item: Regarding Kitarō's mother Fusa and Yoshikichi — their statements and the particulars thereof [correction mark: lower/insert] have been heard... [illegible].

As adjudicated above, henceforth all parties are to exercise proper conduct without any misunderstanding of their obligations, to conduct themselves with diligence, and to pursue their respective livelihoods (nariwai) in an upright manner.
Note: The fine (karyō-sen) must be paid within three days.
