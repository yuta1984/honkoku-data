【Right Folio】

After the gathering had concluded, gifts were presented in a separate reception room. The hosts in attendance were Lord Aoyama Shichizō and Lord Asai Yasushichirō, who joined the guests for the light tea (thin tea) service, after which they proceeded to the banquet seat (sake banquet), where various pleasantries and greetings were exchanged. The guests were generously entertained, and it was not until deep into the night that they returned home — this was reported to the author by the two individuals in attendance.

　Fifth Day: The following persons appeared at the residence of Lord Asai Yasushichirō at the same hour:

　　　　　　Araki Tazō (honorific)
　　　　　　Kitazawa Kunitarō
　　　　　　Kitazawa Katsuhyōe
　　　　　　Sakai Seibē
　　　　　　Ikegami Motoemon

The above-named individuals attended, and after the gathering concluded, as at Lord Okamura's residence, gifts were presented in a separate reception room. Lord Okamura attended as host from the time of the thin tea service onward. For the sake banquet, Lord Asai Seizaemon and Lord Okamura attended and provided hospitality.

Item: All three individuals were ordered to depart on the ninth day to conduct a tour of the villages, in connection with their duties for the 150th anniversary celebrations. Regarding affairs during their absence, it was reported that all official matters would be duly communicated to the clerical staff (shoeki-kata) through the town deputy (chōdai) [name illegible]. This notification was conveyed through Lord Izawa's intermediary, and was duly acknowledged. Furthermore, at the time of Lord Asai's kaiseki meal, the two individuals — Seibē and Motoemon — privately informed Lord Yasushichirō of the matter, and it was indicated in reply that while the matter was entirely reasonable, since it concerned the three individuals among themselves, it should be handled accordingly — this was conveyed as an informal instruction.
