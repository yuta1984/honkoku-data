This individual is the daughter of Tanaka Zenshirō of Kirigōchū-machi. Her family of origin likewise served as a town official (machi-yakunin) performing the "okurimono" duties, and was deeply indebted to the domain's benevolence. At present, her husband's household trades under the name Ōmiya-ya Heiumon and finds itself in straitened circumstances. She married into the family of her father-in-law Rizaemon, who had also served for several generations in the okurimono duties, and to this day Rizaemon continues to receive the domain's gracious favor and to fulfill his official duties. Feeling the utmost gratitude for this divine fortune (myōga), he has come forward expressing his wish to present as an offering the modest sum of money he has set aside in reserve.
