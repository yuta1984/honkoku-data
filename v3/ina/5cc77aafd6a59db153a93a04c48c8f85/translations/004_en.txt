Furthermore, in the current eleventh month (Shimotsuki), the lower residence (shimo-yashiki) also suffered damage from a spreading fire. Given that the domain lord was thus burdened with considerable expenses, it was decided that it would be most appropriate—and conducive to a celebratory atmosphere—for both the rural villages and the townspeople to jointly present their offerings with a single combined gift register (gomokuroku).

Accordingly, on the 17th day of the 12th month, the following individuals gathered at the town meeting hall (machikaisho): Kitahara Kunijirō, (Hara) Kumasaburō, Kitazawa Katsube, and Kobayashi Tōzaemon, along with Motoemon representing this household. The village caretakers (sewayaku) were also summoned, and after discussions, it was firmly resolved to proceed along the aforementioned lines.

It was further decided that the townspeople should take the lead in initiating the process. On the 22nd day of the 12th month, the said four individuals from the rural side and three from our side gathered together and settled upon the items and amounts for the gift register, as listed to the left below.
