Notes kept since the winter of the Year of the Boar (i) regarding the celebratory events, as follows:

Item: In the coming Year of the Rat (ne), as the granted lands (gohaichi) will be marking their 150th anniversary, it has been determined that celebrations will be held during the first month of the new year. Accordingly, notice was given during the winter. At the time of the 100th anniversary celebration in former years, townspeople including town officials (machiyakunin), district representatives (chōdai), and suppliers (goyōtashi) made offerings and presented celebratory gifts of sake and food on tiered stands (shimadai) and the like. However, on this occasion, the domain lord's upper residence (kami-yashiki) in Edo suffered a fire that spread from neighboring properties (ruishō) the year before last...
