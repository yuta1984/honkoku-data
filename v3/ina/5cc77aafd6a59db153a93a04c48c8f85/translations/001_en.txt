Tenpō 11, Year of the Kanoe-Ne (1840)
First Month (January)
Fourth Day

Item: At the castle, through the intermediary of Lord Gotō Shinuemon, the following official instructions were delivered:

　　　　　　　　　Ikegami Riuemon
　　　　　　　　　Sakai Seibē
　　　　　　　　　Kitahara Rizaemon

The above-named individuals were ordered to conduct a tour of the villages in connection with the 150th anniversary celebrations. Regarding the details, they were instructed to coordinate and carry out their duties in consultation with the following five persons: Kitahara Kunitarō, Hara Kumasaburō, Kitazawa Katsuhyōe, Kobayashi Tōzaemon, and Ikegami Motoemon. Accordingly, the three individuals were promptly notified of this.

Item: Last winter, on the occasion of the celebrations, when sake was served to the five persons who had assisted with the arrangements, the following instructions were received: on the fourth and fifth days of the coming first month, both the household of Okamura Jūrōbyōe and the household of Asai Matashichirō would be offering tea, and all were to prepare themselves in advance to attend, as directed by Lord Okamura. Furthermore, it was understood that the domain lord himself had issued instructions to Lord Okamura to this effect.

　Fourth Day: The following persons appeared at the residence of Okamura Jūrōbyōe at the seventh hour (approximately 4:00 p.m.):

　　　　　　　　　Urushiyama Zenzō (honorific)
　　　　　　　　　Komatsu Gunzō (honorific)
　　　　　　　　　Hara Kumasaburō
　　　　　　　　　Kobayashi Tōzaemon
　　　　　　　　　Ikegami Riuemon
