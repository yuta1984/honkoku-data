(Fair copy — formally submitted version)
Respectfully submitting this oral petition

Since my adopted son Mozaemon and I have not been getting along, I wish to dissolve the adoption at this time and return him to his elder brother Sen'ya in Shimoshinden village, and I hereby submit this petition.

If you would mercifully grant this request as I have petitioned, it would be a most grateful blessing. Above stated.

Tenpō 8, Year of the Metal Rooster, 3rd month　　　Shimo-machi, Blacksmith
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Heizō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Sōzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same: Ichizaemon
To the Magistrate's Office

The above petition was approved on the 1st day of the 4th month, and the town representatives were so notified.

Respectfully submitting this oral petition

My husband passed away in the 5th month of the past Year of the Monkey. As our son Hamakichi is still young, I wish to appoint a dependent relative, Mosaku, as the head of our household starting this year, until Hamakichi comes of age, and I hereby submit this petition.

If you would mercifully grant this request as I have petitioned, it would be a most grateful blessing. Above stated.

Tenpō 8, Year of the Metal Rooster, 3rd month　　Shimo-machi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Izumiya Rikichi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Widow
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Sōzaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same: Ichizaemon
