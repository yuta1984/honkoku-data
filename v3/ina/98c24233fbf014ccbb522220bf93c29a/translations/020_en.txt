During outbreaks of epidemic disease (流行り病), use the following remedies to avoid its suffering.

Item: For epidemic disease, take one gō of large-grained black soybeans, roast them well, add one monme of licorice root (kanzō), decoct in water, and drink from time to time. This is found in the "Igai."

Item: For epidemic disease, pound the roots and leaves of myōga ginger, extract the juice, strain it, and drink. This is found in the "Zhòu Hòu Bèi Jí Fāng" (Handbook of Prescriptions for Emergencies).

Item: For epidemic disease, pound burdock root, squeeze out the juice, and drink half a teacup worth twice. Furthermore, take about a handful of mulberry leaves, roast them well over fire until they turn yellow, then add four cups of water to the teacup, decoct in two stages, and drink at once, inducing a sweat. If mulberry leaves are not available, the branches may be used. This is found in "Sunzhen Shiji" (Sun Simiao's Dietary Prohibitions).

Item: For epidemic disease where the fever is exceptionally severe and the patient raves as though delirious,

　　For all cases of food poisoning, as well as illness caused by eating various plants, trees, mushrooms, fish, birds, or beasts, use the following to avoid harm.

Item: For all cases of food poisoning accompanied by suffering, lick roasted salt, or dissolve it in lukewarm water and drink.
　　Note: This is particularly effective when poisoned by eating the leaves of plants or trees. This is found in the "Nóng Zhèng Quán Shū" (Complete Treatise on Agricultural Administration).

Item: For all cases of food poisoning accompanied by suffering, roast barley flour until fragrant, and take it repeatedly in hot water. This is found in the "Běncǎo Gāngmù" (Compendium of Materia Medica).
