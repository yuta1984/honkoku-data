Item: For all cases of food poisoning and accompanying illness, decoct large-grained black soybeans in water and take repeatedly. This is particularly effective for fish poisoning.

Item: For all cases of food poisoning and accompanying illness, char adzuki beans into a black powder, and take approximately one clamshell's worth at a time with water. This is particularly effective for poisoning caused by consuming beasts. This is found in the "Qiānjīn Fāng" (Prescriptions Worth a Thousand Gold).

Item: For illness caused by eating mushrooms, chew the stems and leaves of Japanese honeysuckle (suikazura) raw together, and swallow the juice.

The above [remedies were circulated] because during the Monkey Year (申年), there was a widespread crop failure across the land, and many people were eating not only roots and leaves of trees but also foods they would not ordinarily consume. Accordingly, from Lord Matsudaira Noto-no-kami of Iwamura in Mino Province,

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　15th day of the 2nd month, Year of the Rooster

---

Humbly, I respectfully submit this petition by word of mouth in writing.

The sake brewing quota (sakkabu) of 45 koku that I possess — one license — was transferred to Warito Tōzaemon of Miyata-machi in the previous Year of the Horse. I now humbly petition, as he has privately consulted with me regarding his wish to transfer it back to me.

If, by your benevolent grace, you would be pleased to grant my petition as stated above, I would consider it an honor for which I am most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

Tenpō 8, Year of the Hinoto-Tori (1837), 3rd month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Honmachi, Iseya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Rokurōbei
