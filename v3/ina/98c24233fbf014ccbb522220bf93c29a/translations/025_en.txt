Humbly, I respectfully submit this petition in writing (verbal petition form).

Regarding myself: for the rice fields I own in Sakamachi village, the tax payment (nengu) for last year, the Year of the Monkey, was delayed. As a result, the said village filed a formal complaint [with the authorities], and I am deeply and profoundly mortified to have caused trouble for the honorable authorities. Accordingly, I was ordered to submit a written response, and while the matter was under investigation, I am most gratefully humbled to have been granted, through extraordinary compassionate consideration, a settlement and mediation (naizumi-jidan). In the meantime, the village officials and the said village reached a resolution through negotiation, arriving at an amicable settlement. I therefore humbly petition that, by your gracious benevolence, the aforementioned written response [previously demanded of me] be returned to me.

Should this petition be graciously granted by your benevolent mercy as stated above, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.
