Humbly, I respectfully submit this petition in writing (verbal petition form).

I had previously transferred one sake brewing license (sake-kabu) of 180 koku capacity that I owned to Nakamura Bunzō, and received in return one of his sake brewing licenses of 45 koku capacity. On this occasion, as both parties have come to an agreement through discussion, we wish to exchange [these licenses] back [to their original holders]. I hereby humbly petition [for permission to do so].

Should this petition be graciously granted by your benevolent mercy as stated above, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　Tenpō 8, Year of the Hinoto-Tori (1837), 3rd month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Honmachi, Iseya
　　　　To: The Honorable Magistrate's Office　　Petitioner: Rokubei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Hikobei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same [role]: Chōbei

Regarding the above petition, on the 22nd day of the 4th month, it was reported via letter from Kitano Yosobei that [the petition] had been approved. This was communicated to the other town officials, and town representative Hikobei was summoned and informed of the decision.

Humbly, I respectfully submit this petition in writing (verbal petition form).

　　　Under the jurisdiction of Lord Chigusa Heizaemon
　　　　　　　　　Kashioshio Village, Carpenter:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Fujitarō

The above-named individual has been working as a traveling craftsman (koshi-zaiku) under my guarantee; however, as he returned to his home village in the 12th month of last year, I hereby wish to return (surrender) the water-role license tag (mizu-yaku-fuda) [that had been issued to him]. I humbly petition [for permission to do so].

Should this petition be graciously granted by your benevolent mercy as stated above, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　Tenpō 8, Year of the Hinoto-Tori (1837), 3rd month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Shitamachi, Kisoya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Tōji
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Jōemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same [role]: Ichibei
　To: The Honorable Magistrate's Office

　　　On the 25th day of the 3rd month, [the petition] was approved, and town representative Jōemon was informed of the decision.
