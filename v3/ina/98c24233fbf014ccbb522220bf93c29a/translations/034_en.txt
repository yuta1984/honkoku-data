The above has been granted to us from the prison stipend rice allocation, and we hereby confirm that we have duly received it. Respectfully submitted.

　　　11th month　　　　　　　　　　Ryūzō

　　　Memorandum / Petition

One item: Sesame oil (aburae)　　5 to 3 shō per ryō
One item: Price of 1 shō of oil　　900 mon
　　　　Regarding the above petition, it has been decreed at 878 [mon].

[Left Page]

One item: Price of 1 gō of oil　　90 mon
　　　　　100 mon cheaper at the current exchange rate — 6 kan 700 mon

We humbly petition to be permitted to sell oil at the above-stated prices. Respectfully submitted.

　　　11th month, Year of the Tori　　　　　Oil merchants (Aburaya-domo)

The above written petition was submitted on the 25th day at the government office (goyakusho), through the mediation of Mr. Izawa.
On the 3rd day of the 3rd month, upon instruction from Lord Inokuchi Teizaburō, it was decreed at 878 [mon].
Regarding the above matter, Sakaiya Chōbei was summoned and duly notified.
