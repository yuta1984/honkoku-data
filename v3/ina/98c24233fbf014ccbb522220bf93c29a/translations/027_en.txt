Humbly, I respectfully submit this petition in writing.

Item: My son Kasaburō, aged seventeen this year of the Tori, absconded on the 29th day of the 4th month. I have searched for him in various places, but his whereabouts remain unknown. As he has repeatedly engaged in improper conduct up until now, it is difficult to predict what manner of trouble he may cause wherever he may have gone. I therefore humbly petition that he be removed from the religious registry (shūmon-chō).

Should this petition be graciously granted by your benevolent mercy as stated above, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

6th month, Tenpō 8, Year of the Tori (1837)　　　Hokomochi-machi district
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kana-ya [shop/house]
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Jinzaemon

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The contents of the above petition are without error. I respectfully request that it be granted as stated. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　[Signed by] three persons

Regarding the above petition: on the 15th [day of the month], Mr. Higashi Ikegami submitted it on [the petitioners'] behalf at the magistrate's office. Through the intermediary of Lord Kitano Yosōbē, it was immediately approved, and Town Representative Heiemon was summoned and duly notified.
