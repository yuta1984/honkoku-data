Humbly, I respectfully submit this petition in writing.

Hikouemon, a member of my household, previously petitioned and established a separate household; however, in the matter of temple registration (shūmon), he has remained under my household's registry. I hereby humbly petition that, from this year forward, he along with his wife and children be registered under a separate household's temple record (shūmon).

I most humbly beg that, by your benevolent grace, this petition be granted as stated above, for which I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.　　　　Eta [social status designation]

Tenpō 8, Year of the Hinoto-Tori (1837), 3rd month
