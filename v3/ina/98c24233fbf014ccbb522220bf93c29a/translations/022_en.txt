Humbly, I respectfully submit this petition in writing.

Hikouemon, a member of my household, previously submitted a petition and was permitted to establish a separate household (bekke); however, in matters of religious registration (shūmon), he has remained registered under our household. I hereby humbly petition that, from this year onward, he together with his wife and children be registered as a separate household in the religious register.

I most humbly beg that, by your benevolent grace, this petition be granted as requested above, for which I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.　　　　[Eta]

　Tenpō 8, Year of the Hinoto-Tori (1837), 3rd month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Yajiuemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Intermediary: Yūzō

　　　To: Ikegami Riuemon
　　　　　Sakai Seibei
　　　　　Ikegami Motoemon

The above petition was presented for review to Mr. Izawa Iwauemon at the official office, and [the officials] conveyed that if he found it acceptable they would be willing to approve it. Upon discussion, it was decided that a formal written petition was unnecessary; the matter was settled orally, and it was instructed that [the household separation] be submitted as a notification entry in the religious register (shūmonchō).
