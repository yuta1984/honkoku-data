　　　A Humbly Submitted Written Petition

I hereby respectfully petition regarding my wish to transfer my sake brewing quota rights, with an original assessed value of sixty koku, to Zensaburō of Kurita Village, Fujisawa-gō. I humbly submit this request accordingly.

Should this matter be graciously granted in accordance with this petition, through your benevolent consideration, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　10th month, Tenpō 8, Year of the Tori (1837)　　　Honmachi, Nakaya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Seihachi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Chōbei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same: Hikobei

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was endorsed in the customary manner and submitted. On the 17th day, it was duly approved, and the town representative was notified accordingly.

　　　A Humbly Submitted Written Petition

One lot of:　Pine planks (横板): 500 sheets
　　　　　　Small split timber (小割物): 700 pieces
　　　　　　Roofing boards (屋根板): 300 bundles

The above timber, purchased from sawyers and roofers, we wish to sell and distribute to the domain and neighboring domains from this 10th month through the coming 12th month. We therefore humbly request that passage be granted without delay at the Hokomochi-guchi checkpoint inspection station.

Should this matter be graciously granted in accordance with this petition, through your benevolent consideration, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Nakamachi, Matsuoya
　10th month, Tenpō 8, Year of the Tori (1837)　　Petitioner: Shin'emon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Ichirōemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same: Manbei

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office
