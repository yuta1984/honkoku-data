Humbly, I respectfully submit this petition in writing (verbal petition form).

My elder sister, Rie, has a prior personal connection (informal marital tie) with Nakamura Gorohachi, who is under the command of Lord Koyama Gudayū. As she is to be received by that household as an adopted daughter, I wish to send her there [with your permission]. I hereby humbly petition [for permission to do so].

Should this petition be graciously granted by your benevolent mercy as stated above, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

　Tenpō 8, Year of the Hinoto-Tori (1837), 3rd month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Shinmachi, Yamaguchia
　　　To: The Honorable Magistrate's Office　　Petitioner: Matsutarō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Tazaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same [role]: Chūzaemon

　　Regarding the above petition, it was approved, and the town representatives were duly informed.
