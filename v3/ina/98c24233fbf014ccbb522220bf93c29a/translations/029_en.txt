Humbly, I respectfully submit this petition in writing.

On the 29th day of the 7th month, a male infant was abandoned at the front of the inn of Junji within our town district. As it proved difficult for that household to raise the child, we were ordered that the entire town district should collectively provide for his upbringing. We humbly accepted that order and have been raising the child up until now. However, on this occasion, through the good offices of Yamagataya Kannosuke within our town district, we have privately arranged for a man named Fujinosuke of Kamifuku-machi, under the jurisdiction of Lord Kondō Oribu, who is a person of reliable character, to take the child and raise him. We therefore respectfully petition that permission be granted to send the child to him.

Should this petition be graciously granted by your benevolent mercy as stated above, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

8th month, Tenpō 8, Year of the Tori (1837)　　　Shinmachi Town Representative
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Tazaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same: Chūzaemon

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above was endorsed and submitted through the intermediary of Mr. Kitano at the office on the 15th, and was immediately approved.
