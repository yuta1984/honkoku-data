　　　A Humbly Submitted Written Petition

One lot of:　Pine planks (横板): 500 sheets
　　　　　　Small split timber (小割物): 700 pieces
　　　　　　Roofing boards (屋根板): 300 bundles

The above timber, purchased from sawyers and roofers, we wish to sell and distribute to the domain and neighboring domains from this 10th month through the coming 12th month. We therefore humbly request that passage be granted without delay at the Shimahata checkpoint inspection station.

Should this matter be graciously granted in accordance with this petition, through your benevolent consideration, we would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

10th month, Tenpō 8, Year of the Tori (1837)　　　Nakamachi, Matsuoya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Shin'emon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Ichirōemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same: Manbei

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was submitted to the magistrate's office, and both copies having been duly approved, the town representative was notified accordingly.

　　　A Humbly Submitted Written Petition

My elder sister, Moto, was previously married — with approval duly petitioned for — to Hatake Gōta, the son of Hatake Yahachi of the Ojiro-gumi. However, as the marriage has proved incompatible, a divorce has been carried out, and I hereby humbly petition for permission to take her back into my household.

Should this matter be graciously granted in accordance with this petition, through your benevolent consideration, I would be most deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

10th month, Tenpō 8, Year of the Tori (1837)　　　Kudari-machi
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Komeya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Rihei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Town Representative: Jinpei
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Same: Heibei

To the Honorable Magistrate's Office

The above petition was endorsed in the customary manner and submitted. It having been duly approved, the town representative was notified accordingly.
