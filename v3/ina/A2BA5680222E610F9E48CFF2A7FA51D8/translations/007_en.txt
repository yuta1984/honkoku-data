...make use of, and there must be absolutely no extravagance or indulgence whatsoever. As the officials serve as a model and example of proper conduct for society at large, you must exercise even greater care and restraint, and in your principal duties concerning matters beneficial to your lord, you should apply yourselves to the very fullest extent of your abilities. Now then, even if there have been some shortcomings or misguided conduct up to this point, it is hereby decreed that all such past matters shall be entirely set aside and forgiven. From this day forward, following the issuance of the above-stated shogunal directive, rewards and reprimands shall be firmly meted out in accordance with whether one's conduct and dedication are good or poor — you are to bear this in mind from this time hence. It goes without saying that there must be no instance of calculating only for short-term gain while failing to consider what is truly beneficial for the domain in perpetuity. To strive so that his mind may be set at ease as swiftly as possible is the highest form of loyal and faithful service. Through the merits of such loyal service, one may rise in position...
