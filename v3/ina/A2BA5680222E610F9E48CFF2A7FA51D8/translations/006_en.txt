**[Modern English Translation]**

...that this is how he wishes things to be is truly awe-inspiring, and we receive it with both reverence and joy. Henceforth, the officials should unite in harmony even more than before, set aside all personal matters, and without leaving affairs to be managed solely by those below, act with utmost determination so that the intent of the above-stated shogunal directive may be carried out as swiftly as possible, and so that his mind may be set at ease through faithful and diligent service. To preserve oneself and maintain one's household intact is itself for the sake of fulfilling one's duty of service, especially at a time such as this. As all those of the same rank have discussed and agreed together with the utmost earnestness, from this point forward, with singular devotion, not only in matters of official service, but also in the management of household affairs, as well as in governing the retainers and domain territory, one must apply oneself with deep care and attention...
