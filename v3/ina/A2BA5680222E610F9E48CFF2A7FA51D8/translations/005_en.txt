**Memorandum of Understanding**

We hereby convey the matters that should be understood and kept in mind.

The recent pronouncements from above (the shogun's will) are most gracious and appreciated. Time and again, orders have been issued to thoroughly investigate the regulations and protocols established during the Kyōhō era, and furthermore, detailed instructions have been given concerning everything from the conduct of officials down to the hardships of those of lower station. And with the intention of carrying forward the wishes of the previous era, so that the intentions of the Lord of Kōmei-in may be fulfilled...
