**Year of the Tiger (Meiji 3 / 1870), First Month (January)**

Income: 16 ryō 1 bu
　　　→ From the shipping bureau (tsūsen-kata), Iizuka Tōshichisuke, pertaining to the Year of the Monkey (Meiji 1 / 1868)

Expenditure: 1 ryō 1 bu 3 shu + 3 kan 371 mon (in copper cash)
　　　→ Various expenses for paulownia, ginseng, and other goods during the Year of the Ox (Meiji 2 / 1869)

---

**Second Month (February)**

Expenditure: 10 kan mon (porterage fee)
　　　→ Cost of collecting various tree seedlings (porterage rate: 4 mon per load)
　　　　Payment made to Suehiro Village

Expenditure: 19 kan 800 mon
　　　→ Cost of tending paulownia and ginseng at Rokudōhara

Expenditure: 14 kan 848 mon
　　　→ Weeding/cutting labor fee for 675 tsubo of mulberry fields at Rokudōhara
　　　　Payment in cash to Suehiro Village

Expenditure: 66 kan mon
　　　→ Same work for 5,620 tsubo of paulownia fields at the same location
　　　　Payment to the same person

---

**Third Month (March)**

Expenditure: 80 kan mon (in silver)
　　　→ Purchase of 100 mulberry seedlings
　　　　Payment to Kyūbei of Nishi-Arima Village (西洗馬村)

Expenditure: 70 kan mon (in cash)
　　　→ Expenses for planting paulownia and mulberry in Suehiro Village

Expenditure: 3 kan mon (in silver)
　　　→ Receiving/tending paulownia seedlings
　　　　Payment to Yasukichi of Suehiro Village
