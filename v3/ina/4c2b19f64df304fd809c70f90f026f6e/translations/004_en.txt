Item: On the 28th day of the 11th month, we received a summons, and as we had been lodging at Kisoya in good faith regarding the matter of the money from Niekawa-juku—in which sake brewing equipment and shares had been listed as collateral in the petition—we were ordered to seek a settlement. Through Matsudaya Rokurōemon, we went on the morning of the 29th to present the circumstances fairly and request a settlement. Although efforts toward a settlement had been made on the 25th, they stated that unless the settlement returned to the basis of the principal plus interest totaling 260 ryō, they could not agree to any settlement that deducted 55 ryō from the principal. We received this reply from Rokurōemon and have duly taken note of it.

Item: On the 1st day of the 12th month, having considered the matter at length, we concluded that regarding the 55 ryō, since this was causing trouble for the authorities, and since without the money being substantiated by proof it could not stand, we would respectfully file a petition supported by documentary evidence of the money with the appropriate parties. This intention was communicated as a formal notice to the御調度様 (Go-chōdo-sama) through Fujisawa Rokurōemon.
