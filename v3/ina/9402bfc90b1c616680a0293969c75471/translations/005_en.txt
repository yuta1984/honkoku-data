　　　Memorandum

　The sum of thirty ryō in gold

The above amount has been received and held as an emergency loan (saikaku-kin), and this is a true and accurate record thereof. With interest at the rate of 12.5%, the principal and interest shall be repaid in full during the fourth month of the coming Year of the Horse. This document is hereby issued as proof for future reference.

　Bunsei 4, Year of the Kanoto-Mi (1821), 12th month
