Memorandum

Ten ryō of gold

The above sum has been received and held as a saikaku-kin (emergency loan / arranged funds), and this is duly confirmed. With interest at the rate of 12.5% (one wari, two bu, five rin), both principal and interest shall be repaid by the third month of the coming Year of the Horse. This document is issued as proof thereof.
