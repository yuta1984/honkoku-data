【Front side of single sheet document】

　　　Memorandum

Item: Seven ryō in gold　　Received on the 5th day of the 7th month
Item: Eight ryō in gold　　Received on the 5th day of the 8th month

The above amounts have been received as emergency loan funds (saikakukin), and this is confirmed to be true and correct. [We] shall repay both principal and interest, with interest added at the rate of one-tenth (10%). This document is written as proof for the future, and thus stands as stated.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　【Paper join seal　㊞】

　Kansei 5th year, Mizunoto-Ushi (1793), 8th month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Satō Chūgorōemon　　【seal】
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Maeyama Hirōemon　　【seal】

　　　　　　　　　To: Yoshikō-ō [i.e., Ikegami Kinzaemon or associate]
