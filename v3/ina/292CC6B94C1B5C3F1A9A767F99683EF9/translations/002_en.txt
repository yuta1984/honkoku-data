Regulations

Article 1: Now that permission has been granted for woodcutters to enter the mountain as requested, all due care must be taken, and it is strictly prohibited to allow persons of unknown origin or those of bad character and misconduct to remain (on the mountain).

Article 2: It is strictly forbidden to enter any part of the mountain outside of the contracted and designated areas that have not been included in the contract.
