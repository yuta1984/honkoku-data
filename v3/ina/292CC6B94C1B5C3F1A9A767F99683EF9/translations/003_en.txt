(Supplementary Provision) Regarding the contracted mountain areas, officials may be dispatched at any time for unannounced inspections.

Article 3: It is forbidden to cut down hinoki cypress, chinquapin (shii), or kurobé (trees of these designated species).

Article 4: Regarding all loans and borrowings, transactions must be conducted using written instruments (tegata), and accounts must be clearly settled at the time of final reckoning. If matters are handled carelessly and payments fall into arrears at a later date, any such disputes without supporting documentation shall be resolved between the parties themselves, and no petition shall be brought before the magistrate's office.

Article 5: Regarding the mountain usage fees, each woodcutter shall pay the fixed amount of one bu and two shu in gold without fail and without delay, as per the established regulations.

[Left folio]:

The woodcutters (mokushi) shall strictly abide by the above articles. Should anyone violate them, they shall be subject to punishment.
