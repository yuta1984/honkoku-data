One: Regarding those who do not comply with the regulations, the guard inspectors (bante metsuke) shall carefully observe them, and upon returning, shall strictly report such conduct.

　　【Instructions for those dispatched as arresting officers】

One: Regarding the advance guard unit (o-sakite-gumi), select approximately two of their sub-leaders (kogashira), instruct them thoroughly, and beginning two or three days beforehand, even during nighttime hours, visit each person's home individually, call out the person alone, and communicate the scheduled date and time of departure. Furthermore, instruct them that the operation is to be conducted in strict secrecy, and that even family members must not be told anything before departure. It should be made clear that if the information were to leak, the authorities' intentions would be undermined, and should the village be thrown into further turmoil, it would become a matter of grave concern for the higher authorities. They should also be thoroughly instructed on conduct at the destination. Small slips of paper bearing the name of the guard leader and the names of the three members of their unit shall be prepared and distributed to each individual.

One: Although it was decided to construct a jail to house the large number of prisoners, when asked whether the size should be twenty tsubo or fifteen tsubo, it was repeatedly stated that, calculating six persons per tsubo, ten tsubo would be sufficient. The size was thus set at ten tsubo, and the jail was secretly constructed during the night at the residence of Itō Chōhaku in Wakamiya.

One: On the twenty-eighth day of the tenth month, a consultation was held at the Motojikata Office regarding payment of the accompanying-rice provisions (bandeme-mai), and dated invoices were issued to Miyaki-juku, Hiraide-juku, and other post stations. As a result, arrangements were made for parties to assemble at Miyaki Village on the sixth day of the tenth month.
