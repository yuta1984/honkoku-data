During the eleventh month of the past Year of the Boar (1791), when we presented five ryō of funds procured through your assistance, we have lost the receipt that was issued to us by the Monthly Duty Officer, and despite urgent searching, we have been unable to locate it, causing us considerable difficulty. However, we are deeply grateful that the matter has been graciously resolved through your private consideration and leniency. Should the receipt be found hereafter, we pledge to present it to you without delay. We hereby submit this written pledge as a record of the above.
