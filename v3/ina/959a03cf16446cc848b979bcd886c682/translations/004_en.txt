(Continued from previous page) In cases where someone is unable to attend the castle (tōjō) due to illness or other reasons, the matter is to be reported in the same manner to the monthly duty official (tsukiban) and clerical staff (kakiyaku) of the upper town officials (kamimachi-yaku).

On the 4th day, the town officials (machiyakunin), district representatives (chōdai), and supply agents (goyōtashi) are to assemble and proceed to the castle together at around midday (nakakoku, approximately noon).
　As a guide, one person from the rural district (gōkata) is to come from the town guardhouse (machi-bansho).
　However, while the rural area's (zakata) formal audience (omemie) is still in progress,

　the town officials are to wait at the entrance step (hachinodai/shikidai) of the main gate (go-genkan), while the district representatives and supply agents wait in a new (■) building situated in front of the main gate, each in their designated places.

One item: During the period of the formal audience documents (kakiwata), the castle kitchen (mikuri) is closed, and therefore one shō of sake is to be sent via a voucher (kitte/hikkaeken). Note that sake is to be served directly to the town officials.
