On the 17th day, provisions for the survey party were received from Yokkaichi-ba village. During a brief rest, travel expenses and light meals (ko-tsuke-meshi) were provided, and the party arrived at Midō-gaikaku. Kankeyū-sama and a total of five persons (including attendants) stayed at Magoshichi's establishment, while Shimokobe-sama, Aoki-sama, and Nagai-sama, along with their attendants, totaling six persons, also lodged there.

On the 17th day, due to rainy weather, surveying could not be conducted.
On the 18th day, due to rainy weather, the same (surveying could not be conducted).
On the 19th day, the party departed before dawn and conducted surveys from the front of the honjin to the border below Kanazawa Pass, completing the passage without any hindrance.

As for the party that proceeded via the Matsushima route: they surveyed from the bottom of the slope, lodged at Matsushima on the 15th day, remained there on the 16th due to rain, departed on the 17th, conducted surveys as far as Hiraide, and lodged there.

On the 18th day, they remained due to rainy weather. On the 19th day, they departed, conducted surveys from in front of their lodgings to the border, and completed the passage without any hindrance.
