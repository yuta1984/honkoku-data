Meiji 3, Year of Kanoe-Uma (1870), 25th Day of the 2nd Month — Miyasho Village
Petitioner: Nakauemon
Group Head (Kumigashira): Zensaemon
Same: Rokouemon
Proxy Seal: Zenjū
Village Headman (Nanushi): Kyūuemon

To the Officials of the Kaisan-kata (Office for the Promotion of Local Products)

We humbly submit this written petition.

Item: My father, Kichie, was appointed to serve as the supervisor of lacquer tree cultivation and had been fulfilling his duties. However, as he has now passed away due to illness, we respectfully request that he be relieved of said official duty. We would be most grateful if, in your benevolence, you would be pleased to grant this request.

Petitioner's son,
Kichibe

The above petition submitted on behalf of Kichie is accurate in every respect, with no discrepancies whatsoever. We most humbly request that, in your benevolence, the petition be granted as stated. Respectfully submitted.

Meiji 3, Year of Kanoe-Uma (1870), 2nd Month — Kamitakami Village
Group Head (Kumigashira): Jinpei
Same: Ujū
Same: Tamejirō
Proxy Seal: Mohei
Village Headman (Nanushi): Rihei

To: Ikegami Kiyoo

We humbly submit this written petition.

Item: I have hitherto been appointed to serve as the supervisor of river transport (tsūsen-kata), but due to a prolonged illness I am in considerable hardship. I therefore humbly request to be relieved of the role of supervisor.
