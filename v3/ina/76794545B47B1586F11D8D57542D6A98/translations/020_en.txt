[New Petition]

　　　Respectfully submitted by written document

Item: It is a cause of great distress that in the fifteen villages of Nakazawa-sotogō, as well as in the inner villages (uchigō), sake is purchased from several establishments in other domains, resulting in large sums of money being sent out to other areas during the season of payment.
　Therefore, we humbly request permission to hold the sake brewing license (sakkabu) and the associated land for a period of ten years. In connection with this, we intend to pay one hundred ryō in myōgakin (license fee) annually. We most earnestly and humbly request that, through your special compassion, our petition be granted as stated above. Most grateful.

　　Meiji 2nd Year,己巳, 3rd Month　　　Kitahara Seizō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kinoshita Magoaburō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kitazawa Nakaemon
