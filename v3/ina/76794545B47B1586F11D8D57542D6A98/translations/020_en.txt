**Petition to Mr. Iwasaki Kakuzaemon (Petition ①)**

　　　　　　　　　　　　Village Head (Nanushi): Tarōemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　Inspector (Metsuke): Yagosaburō
　　To: Mr. Iwasaki Kakuzaemon

　　　Respectfully submitted by written petition:

Item: Not only the fifteen villages of Nakasawa Sotogō, but also the inner villages (Uchigō) are purchasing sake from several breweries in other domains, and during times of high prices, large sums of money are being sent out to other areas, which we find deeply lamentable.
　Therefore, we humbly petition to be permitted to hold sake brewing licenses (sake kabu) and the associated land for a period of ten years. In connection with this, we intend to pay a myōgakin (tribute fee) of one hundred ryō in gold per year. We most earnestly beseech your exceptional compassion and mercy in granting our petition as stated above. We would be most deeply grateful for your benevolence. Respectfully submitted.

　　Meiji 2, Tsuchinoto-Mi year (1869), 3rd month　　Kitahara Seizō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kinoshita Magosaburō
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Kitazawa Nakauemon
　　[Office of] Kaisan (Industrial Development)
　　　To: The Office
