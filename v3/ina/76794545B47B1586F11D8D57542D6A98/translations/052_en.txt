[To the] Kaisan [Development Office]

We humbly submit this written petition.

One: Regarding Kami-Ina district, during this past summer, a levy of two ryō per lime kiln was paid, with permission to fire the kilns from Hachijūhachi-ya [the 88th day from the beginning of spring] until the end of Doyō [midsummer period]. However, I wish to pay three ryō and operate the kilns during the slack season within the year [i.e., outside the restricted period]. I humbly and earnestly beseech that this matter be graciously approved.

　　　　　　　　Petitioner:
　　　　　　　　　Nakaemon [seal]

If Nakaemon's petition above is granted as requested, we would be deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.
