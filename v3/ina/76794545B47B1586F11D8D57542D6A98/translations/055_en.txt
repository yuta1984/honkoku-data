　　　　　Iwasaki Minato, Esquire

We humbly submit this written petition:

Item: Two lime kilns　　　　　(Note: these are "irkata" kilns)

Regarding the above, since the year of the Rabbit [1867], the two of us have taken charge of these kilns and, thanks to the generous benevolence of the authorities, have been able to operate them without any difficulty, for which we are deeply grateful. However, as the current year is coming to its end, and after due consultation among the parties concerned, we humbly request permission to transfer the kilns to Yasuemon and Kazusuke. We most respectfully ask that, in your great mercy, our petition be granted, which would be a matter of the deepest gratitude.

　　　　　　　　　　　Petitioners:
　Intercalary 10th month, 20th day　　Genshirō
　Noted and acknowledged　　　　　Same as above
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Manbei

We confirm that the above petition is accurate in every respect. We humbly ask that, in your great mercy, the petition be heard and granted, which would be a matter of great gratitude for all concerned. Respectfully submitted.
