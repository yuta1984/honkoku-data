With the utmost humility, I hereby submit this written petition.

Item: I have until now been engaged in the trade of cotton-beating during the intervals between agricultural work. However, as I have become ill, I wish to cease said trade, and I respectfully submit this petition. Should you be so merciful as to grant this request, I would consider it a most fortunate and grateful outcome.

　　　　　　　Akahane Village
　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Chiyozō

The above petition submitted by Chiyozō contains no discrepancies whatsoever. We humbly request that you issue an order in accordance with the petition as stated. Respectfully submitted.
