**First Petition**

To: Kitahara Masutaro

With great deference, we humbly submit this written petition.

I humbly wish to engage in cotton fluffing/batting (wata-uchi) as a side occupation during agricultural off-seasons. We most earnestly request that, in your benevolence, you grant this petition as submitted. If so, we would be deeply and humbly grateful.

　　　　　　　　　Omaise Village
　　　　　　　　　Petitioner: Kaemon

The petition submitted by Kaemon (※1) above is accurate without any discrepancy. We humbly request that it be granted as submitted, which would be a matter of profound gratitude for all. Respectfully submitted.

　　Third month of the Year of the Dragon (Keio 4, 1868)
　　　　　　　　　Group leader (kumigashira): Naemon
　　　　　　　　　Same: Kuemon
　　　　　　　　　Acting signatory: Kichiemon
　　　　　　　　　Village headman (nanushi): Kiemon
　　　Addressed to the governing office

Note: This petition was approved on the first day of the fourth month.

**Second Petition**

With great deference, we humbly submit this written petition.

By order of the lord/governing authority, construction of a storehouse (dozō) and administrative office (kaisho) has been undertaken at Shingū Kawato in our village. In connection with this construction, the agricultural land that had been held for many years by two men from our village — Giemon and Jirōemon — was appropriated as land for official use (yōchi), upon which the administrative office and storehouse were then built. The remaining portion of said officially appropriated land continues to be land that these two men have held from prior years. Therefore, since we intend to pay the annual land tax (nengu) on it in accordance with the tenancy farming (shita-zukuri) arrangement from this year onward, we humbly wish to borrow the use of said land. We most earnestly request, in your benevolence, that said land be entrusted to these two men as petitioned. If granted, this would be a matter of profound and humble gratitude. Respectfully submitted.

　　Second month of the Year of the Dragon (Keio 4, 1868)
