Respectfully submitted by written petition:

Ten ryō (金十両)

Regarding the above: As the new riverside storehouse (新河岸御蔵) is to be constructed on this occasion, we humbly wish to offer a monetary contribution, modest as it may be, as assistance toward this effort. In connection with this, we humbly request the loan of a plot of land measuring two ken by three-and-a-half ken on the south side of the administrative office (御会所), so that we may construct a small shed there. Respectfully submitted.

Meiji 3, Year of Kanoe-Uma (1870), 4th month　Shimotakami Village
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ichiemon

The foregoing is accurate and correct. Respectfully submitted.

　7th day of the 5th month　　Kinoshita Magosaburō
