To the Kaisan [Development] Office,
　To the Honorable Officials therein:

We humbly submit this written petition.

One: I have been engaged in lime kiln firing as a means of supplementing my livelihood alongside my farming work for some years now. I have duly paid the water levy (mizuyaku-kin) of two ryō per year, and I wish to carry on this trade from early in the second month of spring through to the beginning of the Doyō period in the sixth month, in order to earn a living. I humbly and earnestly beseech that this petition be graciously approved.

　　　　　　　　Petitioner:
　　　　　　　　　Giemon [seal]

The petition of Giemon above is accurate without the slightest discrepancy. We humbly beseech that, in your benevolence, the petition be granted as requested. If so, all of us would be deeply grateful. Respectfully submitted.

Meiji 3rd year, Kanoe-Uma [1870]　　　Miyasho Village
　　Intercalary 10th month　　　　　　Group Head (Kumigashira)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Zensaemon [seal]
