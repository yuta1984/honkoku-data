Item: Even if a request is made to substitute for the iron pot (釜) contribution, it shall under no circumstances be provided.

Item: At the time of the death of one's father-in-law or mother-in-law (舅・姑):
　　　Iron pot fee: 50 hiki
　　　Sake: 2 shō
　　　(However,) this is settled at a monetary equivalent of 200 mon.
　　　The above shall be dispatched accordingly, and one shall attend (the funeral service) taking into account the convenience of the chief mourner (施主).

Item: Regarding one's wife — in the event of the death of one's first (legal) wife, the stipulated monetary contribution shall be sent. No obligation shall be borne with respect to a second wife. However, should the chief mourner make a request, assistance with labor and support (足手間・合力) shall be provided. Two shō of condolence sake shall be sent.
　　　Monetary equivalent: 200 mon

Item: Regarding the father-in-law and mother-in-law — obligations shall apply only to the parents of one's first (legal) wife; no obligation whatsoever shall be borne toward the parents of a second wife. However, in the event that the wife dies or the marriage is dissolved by divorce while the parents of the first wife are still living…
