【Left page】

　　　Regulations

Item: 300 mon — cost of the wooden bucket (桶代)
Item: Roadside [ritual?] — 12 chō
Item: 20 monme of candles — 1 chō (per stop/station)
Item: Imada paper — 1 jō (one folded booklet)
Item: Haya-momen (plain cotton cloth) — 1 tan (one bolt)
　　　These items to be provided in lieu of payment: 300 mon
Item: Sake — 2 shō
　　　However, as prices are exceptionally high at this time,
　　　the monetary equivalent of 200 mon shall be accepted instead.

Item: Iron pot (釜) — 500 mon
Item: Shichiji-gami (seven-character paper) — 50 sheets
　　　Monetary equivalent: 32 mon

The above provisions are fixed for the four persons consisting of the member couple and the husband's parents. At the time of cremation, one bundle of straw (わら一束) shall be provided.
Item: Regarding the condolence sake (見舞い酒), it shall not be provided for very young children (小児根才); however, when a young child [up to a certain age — text unclear] dies within the household, it shall be provided.
