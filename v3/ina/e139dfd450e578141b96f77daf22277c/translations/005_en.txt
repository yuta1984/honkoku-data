(Continuing from previous page) …(in the event that the first wife has died or the marriage has been dissolved by divorce,) the iron pot fee and sake shall not be sent (to the first wife's parents); instead, the stipulated monetary contribution shall be sent upon the death of the second wife's parents.

Item: For a person who has come (into a household) as an adopted son (養子), no obligation shall be borne by the household regarding the death of his (birth) parents.

Item: Should there be a person who wishes to newly join the association (仲間), after deliberation among the existing members and upon review and confirmation of the applicant's character and standing, he shall be admitted. On that occasion, the applicant shall pay an entry fee (敷銭) of 200 mon, and shall furthermore fulfill his duty at the first rotation (初当).■ (illegible character)

Item: Should a member within the association wish to withdraw for unavoidable reasons, he shall pay 400 mon to the association. Naturally, in the event that someone declares his intention to withdraw improperly — before having even received the various benefits such as the funerary offerings (着菓等) for his parents, father-in-law, or mother-in-law — he shall first return the monetary contributions that had been sent to him, and only then may he withdraw.

Item: Given that this association (溝/講) shall be continued for one hundred years ■ (illegible), members shall treat one another with exceptional goodwill and intimacy…
