On the 16th day of the 1st month of Tenpō 8 (the Year of the Rooster), at the residence of Barrel-maker Banzaemon, the members gathered to discuss the monthly rotation of duties (tsukigyō) from this year onward. It was decided that the said duties would be held in half-year rotations.
All members have acknowledged and agreed to this arrangement. Hereby stated.

　Due to crop failure this year, prices for all goods are high; however,
　as the longstanding and dependable members of the association,
　as well as those who stop by along the way (at the gathering), are present as usual,
　one shō of sake and two blocks of tōfu have been prepared as per custom,
　and are to be delivered to the duty holder (current host).
　Though it may be unnecessary to note, the market prices are recorded here for reference.

Item: One shō (approx. 1.8 liters) of sake — standard price
　　　Cost: 332 mon

Item: Two blocks of tōfu — same as above
　　　Cost: 88 mon
　　　Subtotal: 424 mon
