Item: All timber at both ferry/transfer points (渡場) is to be received and managed with due care and attention.

Item: When conducting land surveys (検地) at the timber-harvesting mountain (取山) and during expeditions to Shimokawa (下川), an accompanying official (添役) from the firewood management office (薪方) is to be assigned and dispatched.

Item: When distributing various timbers, official stamps (極印) are to be affixed and handed over; these are to be recorded and then passed on to the respective parties. Any timber bearing no such official stamp is to be inspected and examined wherever it is found.

Item: At both transfer points, firewood and transported timber (越木) are to be re-stacked and handed over, after which the head official (頭) is to receive and add them to the stockpile.

　Matters beyond the above shall be communicated in due course.

　　Year of the Rat (子)
　　　Eighth Month


Seventh day of the Intercalary Eighth Month (閏八月七日)

Item: It was decreed that those who salvage driftwood (流木) shall receive half the payment of the mountain purchase price (山買代).

Item: It was decreed that regarding locations where driftwood has floated downstream and been retrieved in the village/district (郷), those who wish to report to the head official (頭) must not do so directly on their own initiative.
