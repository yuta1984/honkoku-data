27th day of the 11th month:
One entry: Taniyaemon of Kurogouchi submitted a written notice of absence (on behalf of a proxy), along with all relevant documents, to the Go-gyō-sama (the magistrate's office). The petition was granted as requested, and this outcome was communicated to Zenemon.

11th month:
One entry: Regarding Ogachiya Shinshichi of Etsugo Danemon's unit in Kurogouchi Village, who had suffered the loss of seventy ken of land due to flooding, an order had been issued to collect the payment. However, as he submitted a petition claiming financial hardship, the matter was discussed and brought to the attention of the Go-bugyō-sama (the magistrate). An instruction was subsequently issued to allow payment by installments. It was therefore arranged, after consultation, to divide the payments into three volumes/periods (the original text shows a correction from "three seasons" to "three books/installments").

25th day of the 12th month:
One entry: A notice was sent out that, since there is official business on the 27th, the following persons are summoned:
　　　　　From Kurogouchi: Zenemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　Dainosuke
