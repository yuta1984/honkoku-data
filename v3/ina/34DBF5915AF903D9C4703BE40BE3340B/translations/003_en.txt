Eighth day of the eighth month

Item: Regarding the settlement (勘定) for the Year of the Rooster (酉年) and the exchange of documents, inquiries were made at the residences of Denzaburō (伝五左衛門) and Hikone, among others. It was decreed that Kyūbei need not attend to this matter immediately. On the same evening, it was similarly decreed that the other person in question need not attend to the matter, and the settlement documents for the Year of the Rooster were duly delivered.

Item: On the fifteenth of the same month, Mizooki Zaemon (三溝置左衛門) was appointed to oversee the firewood management (薪方) and was to administer all relevant account books and documents. As fellow officials, Matsu Kinbei and Kyūbei were instructed to take primary charge of the areas including Yamanobo (山登り) and Shimokawa (下川), and others.

Item: During the same period, while the head of day laborers (日雇頭) and Ruidaisuke were engaged in firewood management duties, it was decreed that the day laborers and their associates should continue as before — wearing their sashes (急帯) — and work under the supervision of Kaneko Chūta. Matters relating to various temples were also to be directed through the firewood management office.

Item: The same person was to remain until officially handed over at the government office (役所) by Lord Chūta.

Item: A copy of the written instructions regarding the same person's duties was handed to the magistrate (代官).

　Item: On this occasion, it was decreed that the intermediate workers (中間) engaged in firewood duties be assigned lodging (枕入). The head of day laborers is to continue wearing their sash (着帯) as before. However, as they are under the supervision of Kaneko Chūta, any personal petitions or requests are to be submitted in writing to Chūta's office.
