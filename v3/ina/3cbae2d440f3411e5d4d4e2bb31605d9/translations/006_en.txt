　　　　Letter of Guarantee, Hereby Submitted

Item: Jinuemon, son of Gen'hachi of this village, is moving to Shimizumachi in Takatoō and establishing his residence there. A request has been made regarding his religious affiliation (sectarian registration), and he has been duly enrolled in the sectarian register of Shimizumachi. [One character illegible]

　　Regarding this person, should any matter whatsoever arise, we hereby pledge to appear promptly, clarify the circumstances without delay, and ensure that you are not caused any trouble whatsoever. We send this along together with the temple certification document (terauke shōmon). In witness whereof, this document is submitted as stated above.

　　　　Hōreki 4th Year, Kinoe-Inu (1754), 2nd Month　　　Ibaraguchi Village
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Group Head (Kumigashira): Zenbei　　[Seal]
