Prepare one porter and six horses. For the purpose of conducting a surveying mission traveling from Edo along the Nakasendo and Kiso Highway, then from Yodo in Yamashiro Province along the Saigoku Highway, through Akamagaseki, the coastal areas of Buzen, Bungo, Hyuga, Osumi, Satsuma, and Higo provinces, and the islands in those areas, as well as Iwami, Owari, Mikawa, and the Koshu Highway (both outward and return journeys), the following personnel will be passing through: Ino Kankeyū, assistant (tezuke) to Takahashi Sakuzaemon of the Astronomical Bureau (Tenmonkata); Sakabe Teihei, serving as assistant helper (tetsudai kinkata); and subordinate officials (shitayaku) Shimokōbe Masagorō, Aoki Katsujirō, and Nagai Yōsuke. Accordingly, one porter and two horses are to be provided to Kankeyū, and one horse each to Teihei, Masagorō, Katsujirō, and Yōsuke.
