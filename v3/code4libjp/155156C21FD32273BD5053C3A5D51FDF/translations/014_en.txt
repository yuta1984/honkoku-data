Regulations for the Urban Planning Law Preparatory Matters Committee

Article 1: In order to prepare for the implementation of the Urban Planning Law, the City Hall shall establish an Urban Planning Preparatory Matters Committee.

Article 2: The number of Urban Planning Preparatory Matters Committee members shall be ten, comprising the deputy mayor, revenue officer, section chiefs, and city auditor.

Article 3: (The mayor) shall serve as the chairperson of the Urban Planning Preparatory Matters Committee and shall oversee all matters.

Article 4: The committee members shall investigate the following matters:
　　1. Matters relating to the designation of urban planning areas
　　2. Matters relating to the development of transportation systems
　　3. Matters relating to sanitation facilities
　　4. Matters relating to building restrictions and other safety facilities
　　5. Matters relating to parks, public halls, and other social and public facilities
　　6. Matters relating to financial resources
　　7. Any other matters necessary for urban planning beyond the above

Article 5: The materials referenced in the preceding article shall be investigated by each relevant administrative division in accordance with the distribution of duties prescribed in the administrative procedures regulations. The details shall be determined by the committee.

Article 6: The chairperson shall convene committee meetings on a regular or ad hoc basis to conduct research and investigations, and shall report the results to the mayor.

Article 7: These regulations shall come into effect on the day of their promulgation (June 4, Taisho 10 [1921]).

2. Petition for Application of the Urban Planning Law

After spending one and a half years since the establishment of the Urban Planning Preparatory Matters Committee, and having completed and organized all necessary investigation items, a resolution to petition for the application of the Urban Planning Law was adopted at the committee meeting on February 22, Taisho 12 (1923), and was subsequently submitted to the city (council).
