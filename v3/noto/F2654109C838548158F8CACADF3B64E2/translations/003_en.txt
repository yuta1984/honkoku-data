**[Main Text]**

A map of the grave site of Lord Hasebe Nobitsura.

The location of the grave site lies within the domain of Yamagishi Village, at a place called "Hōzen" (宝善), also known as "Wachō." It is situated approximately eight chō (八丁, roughly 870 meters) into the mountains to the left when departing from Wajima Kawaimachi.

---

The *Azuma Kagami* (吾妻鏡), Volume 23, states: "On the 27th day of the 10th month of Kenpō 6 (1218), the year of Tsuchinoe-Tora, Sabyōe no Jō Hasebe no Ren Hōshi passed away at Kawarada in Ōya Estate, Noto Province."

Upon reflection: the twenty-four villages in the present area of Wajima are called "Ōya Estate" (大屋庄), while eighteen villages including Yamagishi, Takuda, Sugihira, and Yokochi are collectively known as "Kawarada-gō" (川原田郷). From Kenpō 6 to the present year of Kaei 7, approximately 637 years have passed.

---

The above map was drawn before the grave on the 26th day of the 4th month of Kaei 7 (1854). — Morita Yoshimi
