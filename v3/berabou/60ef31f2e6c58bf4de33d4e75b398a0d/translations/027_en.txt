*(Hanuta tries to look out at the front, but the komosō [mendicant monk] has already passed by, so she takes a nearby standing mirror and moves it toward the front bamboo blind to try to catch a reflection of him. Just then, from downstairs —)*

**[Chūrō / 中郎]** *(arriving)* "I've been asked to bring this — would you be so kind as to add a little to this account ledger?"

*(He shows her the account book [kanke-chō].)*

**[Hanuta / 花哥]** "How much are we talking about?"

**[Chūrō / 中郎]** "Just one line, if you would."

**[Itagoto / 琴]** "Mm — I'll add to it now."

*(Just as she says this, someone arrives.)*

**[Nakai / 中居 (serving woman)]** "Gebi-kawa-san, there seems to be a dish missing in this room over here — would you mind taking a look?"

**[禿 (gebi)]** "Don't know anything about it."

**[Nakai / 中居]** "You lot are always like this, saying such things. Just the other day there was a flat lid here too. Since we're the ones responsible for keeping track of things, if they go missing it's a real headache for us. How aggravating!"

*(She leaves, grumbling.)*

**[禿 (gebi)]** "Knew it? Didn't know it?" *(brushing the matter aside.)*

*Just then, in the corridor, a full-room courtesan [zashikimochi] appears —*

**[Uwakigi / 浮気木]** *(She appears slightly unwell; she wears a white undyed headband, and has removed her ohaguro [black tooth dye], presenting a startling appearance with her natural white teeth.)*

*[Her attendant 禿 is whispering into her ear.]*

**[Uwakigi / 浮気木]** "Now listen — go to that merchant's shop and get a tin incense box, and — go down to the herbal medicine shop and buy some styptic [blood-stopping medicine] and silver foil. And on your way back, get a little cotton from the sewing room —"
