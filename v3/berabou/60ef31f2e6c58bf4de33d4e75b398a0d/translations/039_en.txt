Unexpectedly, from within the long chest (*nagamochi*) in the adjoining room:

"Hey, hey! Now I can see plainly what lies in your heart for Yūgiri!"

— crying out in the manner of Ōboshi in the tenth act of *Kanadehon Chūshingura*, the man flung open the lid and emerged with a start.

The young male workers, the moment they heard him, seized the man and twisted him up; another, giving free rein to his strength, shoved and shoved — kicking here, kicking there —
