"That's a bad omen [for tonight's business]."

*(At this, [Kawatake] lights a pipe and passes it to [O-Kichi]. Because her hands are oily, she holds it with a piece of paper as she smokes.)*

**[Kawatake / 川竹]** "Yukino, take this and empty it out, will you."

*(She thrusts out a *hanzō* [a small spittoon/container]. "Inside the hanzō there is ohaguro-spittle [blackened tooth-dye spit] filled to about eight-tenths full, with two little spots of rouge-tinged spittle in the middle, and five or six scraps of used tissue paper that appear to be inferior-grade paper." On the latticed window-shelf [renji], a small-sleeved robe is hung out to air; here and there, bleached hand-towels stained with rouge, washed and still rank-smelling, are also hung to dry.)*

*Before long, the hair-dressing is finished. [O-Kichi] goes outside; [Kawatake] goes to wash her hands; and [Yūgiri] is left alone, huddled despondently before the brazier, her face half-buried in her collar, lost in thought. Even a chopstick of cedar wood, pressed into service as a pair of fire-tongs, snaps and smolders.*

*At this moment, from the middle room [nakano-ma] —*

**[Illustration]** *The Hour of Nine [九つ時 / kokonotsu-doki, approximately midday]*

*(Just as [Yūgiri] notices her surroundings and is about to compose herself —)*

**[Teahouse manservant / 茶屋の男]** *(arriving)* "Excuse me, Oiran — Chūko-san has arrived."

**[Yūgiri / 夕霧]** "Oh, really? I'll be right along — but please take Yukino ahead with you first."

**[Teahouse manservant / 茶屋の男]** "Very well. Please come at your leisure."

*(He says this and leaves.)*

**[Kawatake / 川竹]** *(returning)* "I say —"

**[Kawatake / 川竹]** "On top of everything else, how ridiculous."

**[Yūgiri / 夕霧]** "What are we to do..."

**[Kawatake / 川竹]** "I'll take it to heart on your behalf, so don't you worry — just go on. Yofune-san, help the oiran into her outer robe."
