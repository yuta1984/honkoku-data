**Afterword (continued)**

If one is lost [in illusion], the eyes conjure forth a Xi Shi [a vision of supreme beauty]; if one is enlightened, the nose detects only the stench [of decay]. Whether lost in illusion or awakened to truth, [it is] the paper lantern of the teahouse that escorts [souls] from the path of defilement (*urodji*) to the path of purity (*murodji*). A human life is but a brief — merely fifty *ken* [approximately 90 metres] of road. Let all living beings take care of themselves. And furthermore, the passage through this mortal world is but a single, fleeting lodging of the spirit.
