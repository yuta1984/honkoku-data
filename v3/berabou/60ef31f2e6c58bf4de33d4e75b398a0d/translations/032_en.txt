**[Kizaemon / 喜左衛門]** "Well, that's a worrying business. I do hope she gets better soon."

**[Mother / おや]** "Yes, indeed."

**[Kizaemon / 喜左衛門]** "Well, at least stay and have something to eat."

*(Just then, all manner of mendicants come in through the shop curtain [noren].)*

**[Tendai mendicant monk / 天台道心]** *"Namu sahato togiya, tobarokitei on sanmora sanmora un..."* *(Reciting a Buddhist mantra, Note 1)*

**[Kizaemon / 喜左衛門]** "Here, girl — give him something."

*(With that, he tosses out a one-mon coin. And after that —)*

**[Kotsujiki / 骨皮 — a skeletal beggar]** *(clicking four-piece bamboo clappers [yotsudake])* "Sumo-o, tori-i-ni-i-te-i, shira-fuji-i, gen-da-a, choko-cho..."

**[Old man / 老人]** *(chanting the Eguchi kuse passage from Nō)*
*"The waterbird, in noble purity — those who shared their pillow, lovers once so close — how swiftly, unawares, they drift apart..."*

**[Kizaemon / 喜左衛門]** "Ugh, what a racket."

*(And just as he is about to give something again —)*

**[Yarite / 遣手 — the brothel manageress]** "We're short of a kaburo [attendant girl] for Miss Tsukihana — could you please lend us one?"

**[Proprietress / 女房]** "What happened to the girl we borrowed from next door recently?"

**[Yarite / 遣手]** "We sent her back."

**[Proprietress / 女房]** "Is there anywhere you know of where we might hire a kaburo?"

**[Yarite / 遣手]** "I can't think of anyone at short notice."

**[Kizaemon / 喜左衛門]** "There's no way we'll find someone by today."

*(And with that — at the latticed front of the shop —)*

**[Kaburo / かぶろ]** *(calling out)* "Rental space available— come, come, in five-coloured threads and writing paper..."
