"Drag that wretch out of here!"

Hearing this cry, the young male workers of the brothel came rushing in one after another, and a hail of fists rained down—

How pitiful — blow after blow fell upon Izaemon's head.

Yūgiri, weeping, threw herself over him across the space between them, shielding Izaemon's body with her own:

"This is dreadful! Please, I beg you — everyone, stop! Stop, I say! Izaemon-san has done nothing wrong. If you are going to strike this man, then kill me first before you lay another hand on him."

And if they would not listen — she reached for the razor-box on the nearby dressing stand.

The yarite [brothel matron] panicked and rushed to restrain her:

"Now listen here, Kiri! You think I'd ever let you damage such a precious commodity?!"

— and wrenched the box away from her.

In that moment, the crowd of young men grabbed Izaemon by the leg and hauled him out.
