*(——The needlework finishing is exquisitely executed: the decorative accent pieces [akemon-dokoro] feature a wisteria-roundel [fuji-no-maru] motif, sewn in twisted purple thread; the body [dō] of the unlined [muku] robe is of glossy satin [hai-donsu], with borders [heri] of solid-color Hachijō silk in kite-brown [tobi-iro], the same pattern worked in colored thread using suga-nui [a type of embroidery stitch]; lined with scarlet crêpe [hi-chirimen] on a small-brown [kobicha] satin ground, and finished with a brocade sash [shishi-obi]. Standing before the full-length mirror [sugatami] to adjust the drape of her collar [emon], she presents as an oiran whom even the most discerning appraiser [han'nin] would value at seventy-five monme.)*

**[Yūgiri / 夕霧]** "Yukino, there's a guest staying over next door at Ukisato-san's place, isn't there?"

**[Yukino / 雪野]** "Yes, there is."

**[Yūgiri / 夕霧]** "Tell them I sent you — borrow one of their attendants and have them go buy a bottle of sake [chōshi] for me, will you. Please, I'm asking you."

**[Kawatake / 川竹]** "Don't worry about it."

**[Yūgiri / 夕霧]** "Without fail, mind you."

*(Just as she says this —)*

*(Someone brings a sake bottle. [Yūgiri] drains a teacupful in one gulp, and with that, sets out. In the middle room [nakano-ma], the proprietor [Tei-shu], Kizaemon, is seated beside the sunken hearth [irori], copying figures from the tally board [kanban-ita] into the master ledger [hon-chō]. ○ The "tally board" [kanban-ita] refers to lacquered tallying tags [nuri-fuda] on which the number of clients served by each courtesan is provisionally recorded.)*

*[The mark for three bu 「三」; one bu two shu 「一△」— thus they appear.]*

*(Seeing [Yūgiri]—)*

**[Kizaemon / 喜左衛門]** "Oiran, you're early today."

*(He calls out to her—)*

**[Yūgiri / 夕霧]** *(approaching him)* "Master, thank you so much for last night."

**[Kizaemon / 喜左衛門]** "I heard you liked it, so I sent some over. Is today a client from the shop staff [tana-no-shū], then?"

**[Yūgiri / 夕霧]** "No, it's a guest from Ukikusaya."

**[Kizaemon / 喜左衛門]** "Oh? Turn around and let me have a look at you. My, my, the hair is quite —"
