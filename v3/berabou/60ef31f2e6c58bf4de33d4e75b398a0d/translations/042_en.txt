**(Main text — closing passage)**

The two [Izaemon and Yūgiri] press their hands together and bow in reverence.
《Interlinear note: Just at that moment, in the middle chamber —》 【Illustration】

The Hour of the Seventh Bell [approximately 4 o'clock in the afternoon]: the sun on the road ahead is now nearly at its slant [descending toward the west].

From here, the story transforms into the "Face of Brocade" (*Nishiki no Omote*). As for the splendour of the night-time scenes, please refer to the many earlier small volumes that have come before.
