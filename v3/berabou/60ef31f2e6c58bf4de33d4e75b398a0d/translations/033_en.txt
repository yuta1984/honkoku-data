**(Main text, continued)**

*(That is to say, this [kaburo calling out] is likely a courtesan's errand-girl, going to make a vow at the Kōka Shrine [a local shrine associated with good fortune]. And meanwhile, upstairs, in the back of the room —)*

**[Courtesan / 女郎]** "Sawabe! Sawabe!"

**[Kaburo / かぶろ]** "Coming, coming!"

**[Courtesan / 女郎]** "Listen — go to the Kaedeya [Maple House] and have that two-shu coin and the tobacco box wrapped up in paper and tied with a mizuhiki cord, and bring it back. And don't you go dawdling to watch the Daikagura performers on the way."

*(This is evidently a return gift [hana-gaeshi] of around three ryō in value.)*

*(While the courtesan is blowing out [cleaning] the inside of her [smoking pipe], in comes —)*

**[Furishin / 振袖 — a junior courtesan]** "Who was on shamisen duty last night? The bachi [plectrum] is nowhere to be found, and the motoshū [senior staff] are grumbling about it."

*(And just as she says this —)*

**[Yū / 夕]** *(rushing in breathlessly; Kawa puts her mouth to Yū's ear)*
"Oh my — today the situation at home changed, and I was able to slip away and come straight back. Oh, what a relief — it was painful."

**[Kawa / 川]** *(who had been writing in the guest ledger)*
"Oh, that's good to hear."

**[Yū / 夕]** "Until just now I'd been hiding in the fish tank [ikeshu] at Mito-jiri."

**[Kawa / 川]** *(lowering her voice)*
"Listen — when evening comes, there'll be more people about, so you should go into the main room wearing a face like you've suddenly been struck by a stomach cramp [shaku], and go to bed."

**[Yū / 夕]** "Do you think that would work all right?"
