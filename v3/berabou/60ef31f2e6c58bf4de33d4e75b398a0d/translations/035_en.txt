**(Main text, continued)**

**[I / 伊 — Izaemon]** "Don't say such foolish things. In this world at large — when it comes to buying a courtesan's favors, her true feelings are never revealed while a man has plenty of money. The fact that you stand by me now that I've come to this sorry state — *that* is what shows your true heart."

*(And with that — at this moment, in the middle room: [Illustration: depicting the Hour of the Eighth Bell, around 2 o'clock in the afternoon] — the afternoon shift at the brothel has already begun, and the plucking of a shamisen in the hon-chōshi tuning of the Naka-Ōmi style can be heard. The upper floor gradually grows lively. ○ Just at this moment, in the next room over — the room of Ukazato [a neighboring courtesan] — the shinzō [apprentice courtesans] Edomachi, Naniwa, and Miyako are playing uta-garuta [poem card game].)*

**[Yū / 夕]** "That feeling of yours reaches me — and yet it does not truly reach me. My wish is— Because there are eyes watching, I keep a smile on my face just like everyone else — but whenever I think of you, I find myself wanting to die."

〽 *[From the neighboring room:]*

**[Edo / 江戸]** "Sanjō-in — *'Kokoro ni mo / aradeukiyo ni / nagaraeba / koishikarubeki / yowa no tsuki kana'* [Though I linger unwillingly in this floating world, how I shall miss the moon at midnight!]"

**[Miyako / 都]** "*Koishikarubeki yowa no tsuki kana.*" [claiming the card]

**[Naniwa / 難波]** "Oh! There it is!"

**[Miyako / 都]** "Be careful not to scatter [the cards]!"

**[Edo / 江戸]** "Izumi Shikibu — *'Arazaran / kono yo no hoka no / omoide ni—'* [As I shall soon be no more, let this be a remembrance beyond this world—]"
