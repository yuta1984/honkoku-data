"With such a beautiful face — bringing me to ruin for no good reason at all, making me lose my senses like this." When she playfully pretended to strike [him] with her pipe, it was embarrassing, yet in that moment he had felt truly overjoyed. And that, it seems, was the ill-fated bond between them — it is the way of courtesans to feel gloomy even when speaking of the man they love; and so Umekawa, caught up in this exchange with Umeharu and carried along by the flow of the story, found herself recalling the days gone by. When she made to emerge from the bedding that lay upon her, Umeharu attentively pulled it back over her from beside her. Before long, the bell struck the fourth hour [around 10 p.m.], and weary as she was, Umekawa drifted off right there into a soft, peaceful sleep. Umeharu tidied up the bedding, drew the folding screen closer, and opened the copy of the jōruri libretto she had set aside earlier.
