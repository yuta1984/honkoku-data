### Chapter One
**In the two paths of duty and compassion, there is no room even for an intermediary piece — Umekawa's rook-move check**

The apprentice at the sugar shop dislikes sweets; the cat at the eel shop takes no interest in the fragrant smell of things it should desire. It is said that Li Bai could compose a hundred poems after drinking just one *to* of wine — even such a seemingly bottomless drinker, in a hot tub with Kenbishī sake...
