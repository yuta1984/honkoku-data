Self-Preface to "Tedate Tsumemono Shōgi Kinuburai"

Setting the pleasure quarters (色里) upon the board of shōgi (Japanese chess), and observing the comings and goings of the courtesans in their wooden clogs, one finds: at the teahouse, there are pieces waiting for a client (waiting pieces); behind the lattice fence, there are "between pieces" waiting for a secret lover (mabu). The sophisticated man of the world (daisū) moves straight ahead, like the lance piece in shōgi, while the boorish man moves crookedly...
