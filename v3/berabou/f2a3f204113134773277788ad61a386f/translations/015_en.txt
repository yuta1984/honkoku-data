"...it all felt somehow presumptuous of me, and I was growing impatient — but to meet you like this tonight, I feel as though I must be dreaming—" and with that, he made his move most skillfully. "My, Chūbei truly has a way about him — you really are so clever, aren't you." Then I said this: "Even for a fleeting, lowly woman of pleasure like myself — why, even if it's just flattery, how happy it makes me to hear you say such things."

That is what one says, of course — but as for me, well, I was not especially moved. And yet, from the feeling of not being entirely indifferent, I found myself taking his words to heart, wondering what it might mean — but then, if after all this he were not to come again, wouldn't that just be a great loss and sorrow? Better, I thought, if he were simply honest: "I have another regular lover, but tonight, since she is still young and inexperienced, there is nothing to be done, and so I spend the night..."
