The customer climbs two rungs up the ladder.
The courtesan, with a face as if washing up (indifferent, going through the motions), moves sideways. He plays a geisha-piece (one card/piece). The courtesan retreats two rooms away.
He opens up and delivers a check (ōte).
She retreats again into another room. He drops a silver piece from above. The courtesan, dissatisfied, does not take it, and moves to the side. He strikes again, drawing closer. The courtesan is checkmated there (dies).

　The above: Sō Keise (All Courtesans)
