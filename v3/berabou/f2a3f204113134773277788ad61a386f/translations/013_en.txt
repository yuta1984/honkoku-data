"...don't say such foolish things. Just as the saying goes when getting moxa treatment — one calls those who care for you 'elder sister,' and those with whom you share your sorrows 'younger sister.' For those of us adrift in this floating world, we take turns caring for one another; it's something we share between us. Please don't trouble yourself over such things. Now then, please do take your medicine."

And as she held out the medicine bowl, Umekawa took it in her hands and said:

"And yet, what does weigh on my heart is the matter of Chūbei."

"Oh, is that troubling you as well? If only your illness gets better, I will manage things somehow — settle the outstanding debts, smooth things over on the discreet side of the house, and make sure a room upstairs is freed up for him. Fate is a curious thing, isn't it — the first time Chūbei came here was in March of the year before last, on the very first day of cherry blossoms..."
