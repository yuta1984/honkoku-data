(If one were to set up a hot bath with Kenbishī sake and) left it steeping for about three days, it's obvious that even the most devoted drinker would say "Please, no more, I've had quite enough." Similarly, Saigyō, who once declared he wished to die beneath the cherry blossoms, would surely let out a yawn if made to sit continuously at Asukayama. Likewise, if Kume the Immortal were made a guard at the women's bathhouse, he would surely not lose his refined sensibility (*tsū*) at the sight of white shins; and even Yuge So-and-so, if exiled to the Island of Women, would promptly say "I beg to take my leave." No matter how beautiful a woman may be, if she is constantly loitering before one's eyes morning and evening, she becomes undesirable. The pleasure of visiting courtesans lies precisely in the fact that one must spend money — the most precious thing next to life itself — and yet, despite that, must scheme and contrive to arrange the visit, seek a favorable outcome, and slip away unseen past the four watchful eyes of master and parents, stealing past all prying gazes. If one, like someone who buys sake by the shō without ever knowing when enough is enough, were to indulge freely in the pleasures of the barrel...
