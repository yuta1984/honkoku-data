《On this side: a group of idle onlookers》《Licensed quarter street》《Peering in through the lattice like so》 "Look here — that courtesan leaning on her elbow, as though she were the Nyoirin Kannon herself appearing in a gilded hall — she must be a 'Senpū,' a woman who's been spurned at least once before." 《Hana-akebono》 "Hmph — what a brazen-looking woman she is. She's wearing a flimsy comb the likes of a pickled vegetable from a tea-shop, with a hairpin that looks as though it was glued on with water glue. What a head — you daren't even step near it. — Say, it would have been better to leave your short sword at Akebono along with the rest of it."
