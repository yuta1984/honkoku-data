Matters of dispute concerning the management (shomu) of Tomiyas-no-shō in Kii Province, between the estate manager (zassho) Tsunekiyo and the deputy land steward (jitō-dai) Yoriyuki

Regarding the above: According to the mutual agreement (wayo-jō) jointly signed by both parties on the 25th day of the intercalary 10th month of Shōwa 5 (1316), submitted by Rokuhara:

Article 1: Concerning arrears of annual tribute (nengu)
The estate manager (Tsunekiyo) filed a complaint stating that the outstanding annual tribute amounted to over 600 koku of rice and over 600 kan of cash. In response, the land steward (Yoriyuki) stated that while annual inspections (naiken) were conducted in the estate each year, payments from the land steward's named holdings (jitō-myō) could not be made at the proper time, and repayments were made only from what had already been obtained. He further stated that the accumulated arrears from the year of the shogunal order (gechijō) of Kōgen 1 (1256) up to the present year amounted to over 3,000 koku of rice and over 2,000 kan of cash.

(In adjudicating these disputes,) since there are complications concerning both estate management and annual tribute, henceforth the estate shall permanently be converted to a jitō-ukesho (a system in which the land steward assumes management responsibilities in exchange for a fixed payment), and regardless of whether there is profit or loss, the land steward shall remit 50 kan per year to Kyoto (the estate proprietor's side). Beyond this, all estate management (shōmu) shall be handled exclusively by the land steward, and the estate superintendent (azukaridokoro) shall not interfere in estate affairs.

Article 2: Concerning land surveys (kenchu) to be conducted once per shogunal reign
Although the estate manager (Tsunekiyo) submitted detailed arguments, since there is no precedent for such surveys, this lawsuit is hereby dismissed.
