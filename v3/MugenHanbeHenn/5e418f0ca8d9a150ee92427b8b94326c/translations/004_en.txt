Secret Transmission of Military Law — Volume One

　The Scroll of Great Peace (Taihei no Maki)

　One: When a ruler is wise and virtuous, there is nothing that will prevent the realm from being governed in peace. Therefore, one who possesses benevolence has no enemies. A gentleman of virtue does not wage war in the first place. If it should come to battle, he will without fail be victorious. He does not strike the enemy out of personal interest or selfish desire, but rather strikes down those who defy the principles of Heaven. He punishes the solitary tyrant — Jie and Zhou — who have been abandoned by both Heaven and man.
