【Right Page】

That one cannot achieve victory in such circumstances is because even the Advantage of Terrain cannot surpass the Harmony of the People (hito no wa). Now, what is meant by the Harmony of the People is this: to conduct oneself according to the proper way of things; to pardon those who are innocent from death; to resolve people's hardships; to rescue people from their sorrows and urgent crises; to share in the pleasures of the common people; and to inspire in them such devotion that they offer their very lives to their lord without begrudging even a drop of dew. One who loves others deeply, holds profound compassion and mercy, and is expansive in rendering aid will attain the Principle of Heaven (tenri). If one loses the Way, and offers little aid, even one's relatives, kin, and those most intimately close will turn against them. When the reach of one's aid extends to all without exception, there is none among the ten thousand people who will not…

【Left Page】

…yield and follow. When one uses the allegiance of all under heaven to call to account those who, despite their former close ties, have turned against you, victory is assured. When all under heaven yields and follows, there is no need to call anyone to account at all. This is why the true gentleman-ruler (kunshi) does not wage war.
