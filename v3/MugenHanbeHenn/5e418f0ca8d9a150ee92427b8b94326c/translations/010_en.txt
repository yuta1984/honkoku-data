【Right Page】

…Even if a person happens to be such a favorite or intimate of the commander, those who bend the proper way of things, disregard the hardship of the people, and pursue the path of self-interest and greed for their own personal gain—those who cause the people to resent and distance themselves from the commander—shall be made to commit seppuku. Make the people's lives prosperous and comfortable; extend compassionate care even to widows and widowers, orphans, and the elderly with no family; ensure there are no destitute outcasts suffering from hunger and cold; and in all things, make the people feel that there is no lord but this one. Arrange matters so that even from within enemy states, men will come over in allegiance. If one then goes to war thereafter, there shall be no defeat.

《Section Seven》 There are those who wage war by relying on the Timeliness of Heaven (ten no toki). The Timeliness of Heaven refers to the selection of auspicious days and hours, the choosing of favorable directions, and making the yin and yang of the four seasons one's primary guiding principle…

【Left Page】

…surrounding an enemy castle and attacking it, yet being unable to take it, passing the days in vain, and finding oneself in a prolonged stalemate. However, even the Timeliness of Heaven cannot surpass the Advantage of Terrain (chi no ri). The various conditions that constitute the Advantage of Terrain are as follows: the topography is favorable; the castle is steep and difficult to approach; the stone walls are high; the ramparts and watchtowers are solidly constructed; the moats are deep and layered in multiple rings; bows, firearms, and all other weapons and horse equipment are plentiful; provisions are abundantly stocked; and even the water supply is inexhaustible. Yet even fighting with such Advantage of Terrain, if the hearts of all the soldiers are fragmented and at odds, in the end there will be those who abandon the castle, flee, and surrender.
