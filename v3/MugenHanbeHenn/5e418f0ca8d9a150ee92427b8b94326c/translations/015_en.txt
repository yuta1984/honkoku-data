《Section Seven》 On Signal Flags

▲ Five flags for the vanguard, five for the central formation (nakasonae), and five for the bannermen (hatamoto). Furthermore: a black flag for moats and trenches (horikiri); a blue flag for thickets; a yellow flag for marshes; a white flag for rivers; and a red flag for when the enemy appears. Signal codes are thus established using five colors. The vanguard raises flags of the appropriate color to inform the central formation; the central formation in turn raises its signals to inform the bannermen. The bannermen receive the signals, and in this manner each responds in succession down the line.

【Left Page】

《Section Eight》 On Lighting Signal Fires (Noroshi)

▲ Signal fires are at all times a primary means of communication. During the day they are seen by their smoke; at night, by their flames. Wolf dung and moxa (mogusa) are combined together. With this mixture, smoke rises high and straight immediately. On a mountain ridge or elevated ground with a clear line of sight to the bannermen's position, straw, refuse, and firewood are piled up, the aforementioned mixture is placed within, and fire is applied.

《Section Nine》 On Thin Ropes (Hosobiki)

▲ Every soldier in the entire army is to have one length of blue thin rope (ao-hosobiki) fastened at the waist. It is to be used for constructing temporary shelters, crossing rivers, scaling castle walls, and all manner of purposes.
