《Section Ten》 On Black-Lacquered Hoes (Kurokuma)

▲ A commander should prepare hoes lacquered in black, keeping five hundred or even a thousand of them in readiness. They are used to build roads ahead of the advancing force, as well as for constructing encampments, building siege approaches (shiyori), and all manner of tasks. It is said that on the march, one black-hoe laborer should be assigned to every three foot soldiers (ashigaru).

《Section Eleven》 On Five-Man Groups of Archers and Gunners

▲ One supervisor (bugyō) is to be assigned to every five archers or gunners. On the march,

this supervisor shall instruct them in proper conduct and discipline. Furthermore, should any one of the five need to step aside for personal business, the supervisor holds that person's bow or firearm. When the person returns, it is handed back. When the time comes to fire the guns, this supervisor assists with the powder-loading (tamegome). This arrangement is beneficial in all respects.

《Section Twelve》 On One-Night Encampments

▲ Temporary shelters should be constructed as lightly and simply as possible. It is best to arrange them so that they may be cleared away easily the following day. In places where one is to remain for two or three days, it is essential to take care and construct the encampment solidly.
