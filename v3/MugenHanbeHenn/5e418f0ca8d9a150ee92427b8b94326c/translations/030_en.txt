Chapter on Encampment (Jindori no Maki)

　【Article 1】Concerning large armies, small armies, and terrain

▲If a large army takes up position in a narrow terrain, the deployment and movement of troops will be hindered. If a small army occupies a wide terrain, its forces will be spread thin, becoming fatigued from repeatedly closing and opening ranks. Furthermore, the size of one's forces will be easy for the enemy to discern, which is highly disadvantageous. In any case, it is best to carefully survey and assess the terrain of the location.
