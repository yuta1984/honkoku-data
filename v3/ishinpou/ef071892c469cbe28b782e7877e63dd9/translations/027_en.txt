## [Section Four: Things Prohibited When Taking Medicine, continued]

The *Honzō-kyō* (本草経, *Classic of Materia Medica* [*Shennong Bencao Jing*]) states: When taking medicine, one must not eat large quantities of raw *ōhiru* (葫, large garlic, *Allium sativum*), *kohiru* (蒜, small garlic / wild garlic, *Allium bakeri* or similar), or various kinds of raw vegetables.

It also states: When taking medicine, one must not eat any slippery or slimy foods (*nameritaru mono*), fruits, or vegetables.

It also states: When taking medicine, one must not eat large quantities of the fatty flesh of pigs or dogs, fatty thick soups (*atsumono*), or fish prepared as *namasu* (膾, raw fish dressed with vinegar). [Note: The character 膾 is read *sō*, or alternatively read by the *fanqie* spelling *so-rō-han*; the Japanese reading is *tsukusaki*.]

It also states: When taking medicine, one should in all cases avoid exposure to corpses, women in childbirth, and all manner of defiling and unclean (*en-wai*, 淹穢) things and events.

It also states: If a medicine contains *jutsu* (术, *Atractylodes* rhizome), do not eat peaches, plums, sparrow meat, large garlic (*ōhiru*), small garlic (*kohiru*), or pickled fish (*sushi*) made from young green-tinged fish. If it contains *Croton* seeds (*hazo*, 巴豆), do not eat soup made from reed shoots (*taka-muna*, young *Phragmites* sprouts) or pork.
[Note: Consulting the *Han Wang Fang* (范汪方, *Formulary of Fan Wang*), it states: "Eating these will cause a person to suffer *shuku-san* (縮産, contraction/difficult delivery), and the medicine will not go down [be absorbed], so the illness will not be cured." The same source also states: "Essence (*sei*, 精) will leak out and [the person] will have few children."]

If a medicine contains *ōren* (黄連, *Coptis* rhizome; Japanese reading: *kakumakusa* or *amana*) or *kikkyō* (桔梗, *Platycodon* root; Japanese reading: *arino-hifuki*), do not eat pork.
[Note: Consulting the *Han Wang Fang*, it states: "Eating this will cause a person's essence to become contracted and to leak, will cause them to have few children, the medicine will not be absorbed even if swallowed, and the illness will not be cured."]

If a medicine contains *hange* (半夏, *Pinellia* rhizome; Japanese reading: *hosokusa*) or *shōbu* (昌蒲, *Acorus calamus* / sweet flag; Japanese reading: *ayame-kusa*), do not eat malt sugar (*ame*, 飴糖) or mutton.
[Note: Consulting the *Han Wang Fang*, it states: "The person's illness will not be cured." The *Zempukei* (膳夫経, *Classic of the Chef*) states: "For three days, do not eat glutinous rice (*mochi-gome*, 糯)."]

If a medicine contains *saishin* (細辛, *Asarum* root; Japanese reading: *ara no ne-kusa*), do not eat raw vegetables.
[Note: Consulting the *Han Wang Fang*, it states: "Eating this will increase the illness."]

If a medicine contains *kanzō* (甘草, liquorice root; Japanese reading: *amaki-kusa*), do not eat *takanana* / *karashi-na* (菘, a type of mustard green / *Brassica*).
[Note: Consulting the *Han Wang Fang*, it states: "If the medicine contains *kanzō*, do not eat *takana* for three days; the illness will not be cured." The *Zempukei* states: "Do not eat *bui* (蕪荑, elm-seed paste) or *tade* (蓼, smartweed/knotweed). Violating this will cause the person to become thin and will damage their *yōdō* (陽道, male reproductive function/vitality)."]

If a medicine contains *reiro* (䔧蘆; Japanese reading: *yama-ubara*, a type of veratrum or similar plant), do not eat raccoon dog (*tanuki*) meat.
[Note: Consulting the *Han Wang Fang*, it states: "Eating this will cause the person's *suidō* (水道, urinary tract/waterways of the body) to reverse upward, resulting in abdominal bloating (*fuku-chō*, 腹脹)."]

If a medicine contains *botan* (牡丹, tree peony root bark), do not eat raw large garlic (*ōhiru*) or *namomi* (葈, *Xanthium* / cocklebur).
[Note: Consulting the *Han Wang Fang*, it states: "Do not eat garlic (*hiru*) for one day; the illness will increase." The *Zempukei* states: "Do not eat raw small garlic (*kohiru*) for two days; the illness will increase."]

If a medicine contains *kōzan* (恒山, *Dichroa febrifuga*; Japanese reading: *kusaki*), do not eat raw scallions (*nagi*, 葱) or vegetables.
[Note: Consulting the *Han Wang Fang*, it states: "Eating these will increase the illness."]

If a medicine contains *kūsei* (空青, azurite) or *shusha* (朱沙, cinnabar), do not eat things with raw blood.
[Note: Consulting the *Yōjō-yōshū* (養生要集, *Essential Compendium of Health Cultivation*), it states: "The illness will not be cured."]

If a medicine contains *bukuryō* (伏苓, *Poria cocos* / tuckahoe fungus; Japanese reading: *matsuhodo*), do not eat any sour (*sukki*) things.
[Note: Consulting the *Zempukei*, it states: "Do not eat anything sour or hot." The *Gyokuhako-hō* (玉箱方, *Formulary of the Jade Box*) states: "*Bukuryō* is incompatible with (*imashimu*, 忌む) carp (*koi*)."]

The *Yōjō-yōshū* (養生要集) states: When taking medicine, one must not eat any slippery or slimy foods, fruits, vegetables, oil, flour-based foods (*mukiko*, 麺), raw and cold things, or sour things.

It also states: When taking medicine, one must not eat large quantities of raw large garlic (*ōhiru*), small garlic (*kohiru*), various raw vegetables, or pork… [Continues on the next page.]
