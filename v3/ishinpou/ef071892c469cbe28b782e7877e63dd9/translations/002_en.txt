**Chapter 1: General Principles of Treating Disease**

The *Qianjin Fang* [Prescriptions Worth a Thousand Gold Pieces] states: Zhang Zhan said: "The reason that mastery of classical medical prescriptions (*jingfang*) is so difficult is that the tradition stretches back through deep antiquity. There are cases where the internal condition is the same yet the external symptoms differ, and likewise cases where the internal condition differs yet the external symptoms appear the same. Therefore, the fullness and depletion of the five viscera (*wuzang*), and the free flow or obstruction of the blood vessels and the *ying-wei* (nutritive and defensive qi), cannot truly be discerned through the ears and eyes alone. One must examine the pulse in order to make them clear. Within the blood vessels there are the disorders of floating (*fu*), sinking (*chen*), wiry (*xian*), and tight (*jin*) [pulse qualities], and at the *shu* acupoints, there is the flow and concentration of qi..."
