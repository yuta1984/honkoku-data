## Chapter 1: The Fundamental Principles of Treating Disease

The *Qianjin Fang* (Prescriptions Worth a Thousand Gold) states: Zhang Zhan said, "The difficulty in achieving thoroughness in the study of classical prescriptions has long been such that [even the wisest have struggled]. There are conditions that are internally the same yet externally different, and there are also conditions that are internally different yet externally the same. Therefore, the fullness and emptiness of the five viscera, and the free flow or obstruction of the blood vessels and the nutritive and defensive qi, truly cannot be discerned by ear and eye alone. One must examine the pulse in order to understand them clearly. In the blood vessels there are disorders such as floating, sinking, wiry, and tight [pulses]; and the flow passes through the acupoints (small openings)…"
