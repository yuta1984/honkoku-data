## [Section Five: Prescriptions for Being Poisoned by Medicine, continued]

(Continuing from the previous page)

**(Prescriptions from the *Kasshi-hō*):**

Scrape a small amount of earth from the eastern wall, and dissolve it in three *shō* (升) of water and drink it.

Another formula: Pound indigo plant (*aigu sa*, 藍青, *Indigofera* sp.) and wring out the juice, then take several *shō*. If there is no indigo (*ai*), immediately wash a new green cloth, or cloth of *kon* (紺, dark blue) or *hanada* (縹, light blue) colour in water, take the juice (the colour-steeped water) that comes out, and drink it.

Another formula: Burn rhinoceros horn and grind it into powder, then take one *hōsumpi* (方寸匕, a standard measure of powdered medicine — approximately one square *sun*, or about 2 ml). Another formula: Pound *myōga* (蘘荷, *Zingiber mioga*, Japanese ginger) root and extract the juice, then drink one or two *shō* of it. In summer, use the leaves of *namomi* (葈耳, *Xanthium strumarium*, cocklebur).

---

It also states: A formula for a person who has taken medicine beyond the proper measure and suffers severe anguish (*hata*, 苦煩) in the abdomen.

It is best to drink the juice of fresh (*nama*) kudzu (*Pueraria montana* var. *lobata*, 葛) root. If fresh root is not available, pound dried root and take five *gō* (合) with water. It is also permissible to boil and decoct it. Another formula: Grind the yellow earth from beneath the cooking stove (*kamado*, 竈) into powder, and take one *hōsumpi*.

---

It also states: A formula for a person who has taken medicine and cannot stop vomiting.

Take pig (*inoshishi*/pork, 猪) fat (*abura*, 膏) of a piece about the size of a finger's length of three *sun* (寸), boil it until hot, and swallow it all. Another formula: Drink one *shō* of freshly drawn cold water, and (the vomiting) will immediately stop.

---

It also states: A formula for the case where the medicine contains *hazo* (巴豆, *Croton tiglium*, croton seeds) and diarrhoea (*geri*, 下利) does not stop.

Grind *kankyo* (干薑, dried ginger, *Zingiber officinale*) and *ōren* (黄連, *Coptis japonica*, Japanese goldthread) into powder, and take one *hōsumpi*. Another formula: Boil the broth of *kuki* (豉, fermented black soybeans, *douchi*) and take one *shō*.

---

It also states: Each of the various medicines has its own (specific) antidote (*gedoku*, 解毒), but it is difficult to always have these on hand. Here, only one substance is selected which can simultaneously counteract (*kate*, 兼ねて) all manner of poisons (*shūdoku*, 衆毒) and is easy to obtain. Take *kanzō* (甘草, liquorice root, *Glycyrrhiza uralensis*), chew it coarsely (or pound it roughly), boil it to a strong concentration, and drink a great deal of the broth. There is nothing it does not govern (i.e., it is effective against everything), as it is said. For poison from food, a small amount of *mitsu* (蜜, honey) [is good]…
