#### The Ten Branches of the Yogācāra (瑜伽十支論)

The *Yideng* (義燈 / *Vijñaptimātratāsiddhi-śāstra-vyākhyā*) states: "The intent of the treatises composed by the various Bodhisattvas was not merely to propagate the Buddha's sūtras, but also to broadly illuminate the doctrines within the *Yogācārabhūmi*. Although the ways of elucidating the teachings differ, there are roughly ten branches (*zhī* 支). 'Branch' means a division or subdivision. All of these are branches (*zhīfēn* 支分) belonging to the *Yogācārabhūmi*."

1. The branch treatise that briefly presents names and numbers: ***Mahāyānaśatadharma-prakāśamukha* (百法明門論)**
2. The branch treatise that roughly explains substance and meaning *(also called the* Yīmíng shìyì lùn *[依名釈義論])*: ***Pañcaskandhaka* (五蘊論)**
3. The branch treatise that comprehensively encompasses various discussions: ***Prakaraṇāryavācā* (顯揚論)**
4. The branch treatise that broadly encompasses great meanings *(also called "the branch treatise that comprehensively gathers the meanings of Mahāyāna")*: ***Mahāyānasaṃgraha* (攝大乘論)**
5. The branch treatise that distinguishes names and numbers *(also called "the branch treatise that broadly presents great meanings")*: ***Abhidharmasamuccaya-vyākhyā* (雜集論)**
6. The branch treatise that departs from bias and abides in the middle: ***Madhyāntavibhāga* (辨中邊論)**
7. The branch treatise that demolishes the mountain of heterodoxy: ***Viṃśikā* (二十唯識論)**
8. The branch treatise that raises high the banner of the Dharma: ***Triṃśikā* (三十唯識論)**
9. The branch treatise that adorns substance and meaning: ***Mahāyānasūtrālaṃkāra* (大乘莊嚴論)**
10. The branch treatise that gathers the scattered and returns to contemplation: ***Vibhāgayoga* (分別瑜伽論)**

**Lower Section:**

Alternatively, among the ten branches, some would remove the *Mahāyānasaṃgraha* (攝大乘論), since that treatise separately explicates the *Mahāyānābhidharma-sūtra* and does not constitute a branch of the *Yogācārabhūmi*. In that case, the *Nyāyamukha* (正理門論) should be added in its place.

*(End of the Ten Branch Treatises)*
