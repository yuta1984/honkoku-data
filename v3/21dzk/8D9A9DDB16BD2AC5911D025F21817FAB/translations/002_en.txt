**[Upper Section]**

Five-Buddha Crown · Maṇḍala Vase Form

**Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva** — Kiśōkongō (Śrī Vajra) — ■■■■■■■■■
Seed syllable: ■ — Samaya form: Blue lotus
Golden color. The left hand holds a blue lotus upon which a five-pronged vajra is placed; the right hand holds a Brahmin ■ (brush/scroll). On the head is a single powerful topknot, behind which the original topknot is shaved, and a transformation-buddha (nirmāṇakāya) is placed thereon. The right hand forms a fist placed near the waist, holding a sword.
*Commentary says*: "The color of turmeric (or a three-pronged vajra atop the flower). The maṇḍala resembles smoke and a rainbow. Vajra banner."

**Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva** — Shōhōkongō (Right Dharma Vajra) — ■■■■■■■■■■■■
Seed syllable: ■ — The Dharma dwelling constitutes the samaya form.
Flesh color. The left hand is open with the palm held near the waist; the right hand holds a lotus. Atop the crown, Amitāyus Buddha is placed.
*Commentary says*: "Crystal (sphaṭika) color."

Maṇḍala: Void square form

**Maitreya Bodhisattva** — Jinsoku Kongō (Swift Vajra) — ■■■■■■■■■
Seed syllable: ■ — Samaya form: Swiftly upon a lotus
Flesh color. The left palm faces outward near the waist; the right hand holds a lotus upon which a kuṇḍikā (water vase) is placed. Within the crown is a stupa.
*Commentary says*: "Golden color."

Maṇḍala: Void square form · Blue color.

Note: The four bodhisattvas differ in color between the commentary and the old text. East: (Samantabhadra); West: (Mañjuśrī); Northeast: Avalokiteśvara; Northwest: Maitreya.
Furthermore, the four-row bodhisattvas in the earlier text all sit on blue lotuses; in the later text, each sits on a red lotus.

*Commentary says*: "The innermost heart of the central platform's eight petals is a wondrous white lotus. This is the secret symbol of the wondrous Dharma lotus flower — the Pundarīka flower — of sentient beings' original mind. The eight petals of the flower platform are uniform and level, like the form of a fully blooming flower. This lotus-flower treasury is the very body of the Great Compassion Womb Realm Maṇḍala. The remaining three enclosures are the pathways through which the merit of this direct realization flows forth, enabling all spiritual friends (kalyāṇamitra) to enter the gateway of the Dharma realm. The four cardinal directions, four divisions, and outer boundaries..."

**[Lower Section]**

"These are the four wisdoms of the Tathāgata, the four corners, and the four petals (of the four-row layout)."

*Commentary says*: "In the central womb treasury, five vases should be placed. The central vase of Mahāvairocana is placed on the flower platform; the remaining ones are placed among the petals. When constructing the central womb treasury, the four Buddhas and four bodhisattvas follow their respective seats within the flower platform. Those who serve Vairocana place (their vases) stacked before the flower platform. If one has sufficient means, a single vase is stacked before each individual deity. If one is unable to do so, place them before the principal deities of the inner and outer directions. In each of the four directional gate chambers, place two vases; in each of the four corners, place one vase. Outside the gate passage, there is an additional separate vase."

*Also says*: "The radiance from the navel of Mahāvairocana downward corresponds to the third level of position. The radiance generated from the eyes upward to the forehead is the second level of position. The radiance from the eyes upward to the crown protuberance (uṣṇīṣa) is the first level of position. The central womb treasury is precisely Vairocana's own-mind eight-petal lotus."

*Also says*: "Assuming the central womb treasury is eight shaku in both length and breadth, the first enclosure should be four shaku wide, the second enclosure two shaku wide, and the third enclosure one shaku wide. The ācārya states: The original method is thus. If there is a discrepancy in size and the proportions do not correspond, adjust them by one's own judgment and there will be no loss in principle. This concludes the record."

*Also says*: "The broad meaning of the 'Great Compassion Womb Realm Arising Maṇḍala' is as follows: When a practitioner directly perceives the central womb treasury, they immediately know that all sentient beings universally possess the capacity for Buddhahood, and they release that awareness to pervade the entirety of the Great Compassion Maṇḍala's Dharma realm. Moreover, vajradhara (Vajrapāṇi) assemblies numerous as dust-motes across ten ages of worlds, if one gate is made the maṇḍala master and the rest become retinue, then one type of maṇḍala is formed."
