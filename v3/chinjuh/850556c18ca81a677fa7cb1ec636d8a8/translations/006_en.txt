〽 (The woman clings to Jūrō) "How cruel — how heartless! If you were to part from me tonight and live on in safety, at least I might have the hope of meeting you again someday, and find some comfort in that thought..."

〽 "But the vows we have exchanged, the pledges and written oaths between us — they have all come to nothing, all turned to dust."

〽 "Then do you mean to die? If you are resolved, then the River of Three Crossings awaits us — like this, hand in hand, together, the two of us..."

〽 "Why will you not say so? Kill me before you go — please, take me with you!" she cried, clinging to the man's knees, her body trembling as she wept and collapsed before him.

At that moment, the voice of Kaya, the brothel's manageress, called out from outside: "Children! Midori! Is anyone there—"
