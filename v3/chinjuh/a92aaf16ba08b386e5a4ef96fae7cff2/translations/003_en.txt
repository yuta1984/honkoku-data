## The Catfish Great Peace Chronicle: A Tale of Chaos

**A Playful Work by Ichū-Inshi (Hermit of the City) and Dōdō-Sanjin (Wandering Man of the Great Road)**

As the saying goes, "Even in peacetime, do not forget the possibility of upheaval." Here there was a creature known as Namazu Nura-Kurō, Lord of the Eight Depths of Hell, also called Yuritaka ("High Shaker") of the Deep Waters. Proud of his unrivaled strength, he repeatedly threatened houses, storehouses, halls and shrines, took human lives, and defied the Way of Heaven.

Long ago, the Great God of Kashima and the deity Futsunushi no Mikoto, bearing the imperial decree of the Goddess Amaterasu, had marched forth, captured him alive, buried him in an earthen prison, and pressed down upon him the vital cornerstone known as the Kaname-ishi (the Keystone), thus subduing him. And so the realm had flourished in an unshakeable age of peace.

Yet—was it the turning of time? Was it the will of Heaven?—in the early winter of the second year of Ansei (1855), the eight million gods had all rushed ahead of one another to depart for the province of Izumo, leaving the land unguarded. Seizing upon this as his golden opportunity, Yuritaka instantly stirred up his rebellious spirit, revealed his treacherous intentions, and sent out circular letters to summon his allies. The members of the conspiracy, who had long awaited this moment, came rushing in one after another:

**First to arrive: Tobihino Hayata Iyaki ("Swiftly Spreading Wildfire, House-Burner")**. Charging forth with the blaze at its peak — wearing armor adorned with fire-threatening designs, a helmet fitted with a close-pressed crest, bearing a fire-pillar standard, and mounted on a "Hizakurige"-style horse with flaming crimson thick tassels — he joined the fray with great ferocity.

**Second: Kaminari Gorō Otodaka ("Rumbling Thunder, High Sound")**. Wearing a helmet with two-horned crest, armor decorated with evening-shower designs, a sword shaped like a lightning bolt with a tiger-skin scabbard, he straddled a dark-cloud steed and came thundering in, striking a single mighty blow as he joined the assault.

**Third: The resident of Izu Province, Tsunami no Kanja ("The Wave Rider")**. Together with Demizu no Tarō ("Flooding Waters") and a host of monsters and demons of all manner of strange forms and appearances, each determined not to be outdone, they all swarmed in together.
