《Caption for illustration: "Scene of Travelers Crossing over an Avalanche (Nadare) at the Mikuni Pass"》

Like jade vines coiled and gathered — the peaks of Mikuni;
Cold light pierces to the bone, yet resolve cannot be shaken.
Who is it that so dearly loves the snow? — amid twin flowers and the moon,
Along the plank-road path, soaring past the clouds, treading upon a white dragon.
　　　　　　　　　　　Composed by Kyōzan-jin

● Traveling back and forth over the nadare (avalanche mound)

《Illustration caption: Painted by Kyōsui》
