《Label: A Scene of Snow Accumulated in a Post-Town (Ekichū Yuki no Tsumorita ru Zu)》

How many years have houses been buried under fathoms of snow?
Year after year, one grows accustomed to this cold, radiant landscape—
Yet holds no resentment toward the heavens. Plum blossoms and willows await the coming spring,
And even when the third month draws to a close, the six-petaled flowers (snowflakes) still remain,
Taking the place of blossoms, displaying their pure and serene beauty.
　Inscribed by Suzuki Bokushi

《Label: Brushwork by Kyōsui (Santō Kyōsui)》

1. The practice of digging through snow around houses is as described in the main text. 2. Snow is hollowed out to create a cave-like space, and shelves and stands are all fashioned from snow to sell goods. This is called "sat-□□□." 3. The mountain-like forms visible in the illustration are all made of snow.
