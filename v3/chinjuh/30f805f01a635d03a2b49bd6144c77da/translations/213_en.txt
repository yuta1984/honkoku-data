### ○ A Boat Found Underground

About one *ri* (approximately 4 km) from Gosen in Kambara District, there is a village called Shimoshinden. One year, the villagers, while digging along the bank of the Agano River for some purpose, unearthed a boat approximately three *ken* (about 5.4 meters) long from within the earth.

The entire vessel showed not the slightest decay, and not only did its form differ from boats of the present day, but every place where metal fittings would normally be used was instead fastened with whale baleen, with not a single piece of iron employed anywhere. No one could identify what kind of wood it was made from, and it was said to likely be a foreign ship. When I visited lower Echigo, I saw at the home of Ono Sagoemon in Sugita Village a writing-box (*suzuribako*) made from the wood of that boat; the quality of the wood seemed as if it might be of Chinese origin. Could it perhaps have been a barbarian vessel (*isen*) that drifted ashore in ancient times?

### ○ A White Crow

As I have said before, writing about things other than snow in a work titled "Snow Chronicles" is like composing a poem that strays from its assigned topic (*rakudai*); but I shall write about snow again later, and for now simply record whatever comes to mind.

In the fourth month of the Year of the Dragon, Tenpō 3 (1832), in the Nakamachi district of Shiozawa where I live, near the house of a man called Kagiya, there stood a tall tree. A crow had built its nest in this tree, and just when the chicks were beginning to poke their heads out, a bird with a white head was spotted in the nest. The master of the house found this strange and sent someone to catch it. Upon capture, the bird proved to be a crow chick whose entire body was white, but with a red beak, red eyes, and red feet — an albino crow chick. People gathered as a curiosity to view it. The master quickly had a cage made and devoted himself to raising it...
