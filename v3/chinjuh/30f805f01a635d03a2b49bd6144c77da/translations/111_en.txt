### ○ Castle Towns of Echigo

According to historical records, in ancient times the province of Echigo extended into Dewa and Etchū. Today it is comprised of seven districts (gun). To the east lies Iwafune District (written in ancient times with the character for "stone" [石]; located near the sea), Kanbara District, and the port of Niigata; to the west lies Uonuma District (far from the sea), which belongs to this group of districts. To the north are Mishima District (near the sea) and Kariwa District (near the sea); to the south are Kubiki District (with parts near the sea) and Koshi District (far from the sea)—these constitute the seven districts in total.

As for castle towns: in Iwafune District, Murakami (under Lord Naitō, 59,000 koku); in Kanbara District, Shibata (under Lord Mizoguchi, 50,000 koku) and Kurokawa (under Lord Yanagisawa, approximately 10,000 koku); in Mishima District, Yoita (under Lord Ii, 20,000 koku); in Kariwa District, Shiiya (under Lord Hori, 10,000 koku, jinya/sub-castle); in Koshi District, Nagaoka (approximately 74,000 koku); in Kubiki District, Takada (under Lord Sakakibara, 150,000 koku) and Itoigawa (under Lord Matsudaira Hyūga, 10,000 koku, jinya). Beyond these castle towns, particularly prosperous places include: Ojiya in Uonuma District, Sanjō in Koshi District, Teradomari and Izumozaki in Mishima District, Kashiwazaki in Kariwa District, and Imamachi in Kubiki District. The port of Niigata in Kanbara District is the foremost harbor on the north sea, and is thus a place of great wealth and prosperity.
