**Jatōseki (Snake Stone / Dipsas Stone)**

The *jatōseki* (snake stone) is an item brought over by the Kōmō-jin (the Dutch, "Red-Haired People"), called *Slangensteen* (スランカステン) in their tongue. When this stone is applied to a wound inflicted by any beast or insect, it adheres to the poisoned area by suction and will not come off. One waits for it to fall away naturally, then immerses it in milk (乳汁), whereupon after a short while the poison passes out into the milk. It is then washed thoroughly in clean water, dried in the sun, and applied again (this process is repeated). There are no few who counterfeit this item. The genuine article is that which is kept as a treasured possession in the household of one Yūrin-something (猶林某) in Nagasaki. It is white in color with black markings. **Zūtohōtodorottsupu (Dutch Drops / Liquorice Drops)**

*Zūtohōtodorottsupu* is a phlegm-cutting remedy (expectorant) brought over by the Southern Barbarians (Nanban people). It is lacquer-colored and sweet in taste. It is apparently made by boiling liquorice root (*kanzō*) as the principal ingredient into a thick paste or lozenge. It is effective at dissolving phlegm and moistening the voice.

**Kōhii (Coffee)**

*Kōhii* (coffee) is a bean that the Southern Barbarians (Nanban people) brew and drink. Its shape resembles that of the white hyacinth bean (*hakuhendō*), and it has a somewhat moist quality; it splits apart from the center into two halves. The beans are roasted until they become jet black, and they are taken regularly, just as the Japanese drink tea. Its medicinal effects are as follows: it aids the functioning of the spleen (*hi*) and promotes *unka* (the digestion and absorption of food); it dissolves *ryūin* (retained fluids or stagnant drink in the body); and it brings down the *ki* (qi, vital energy — i.e., settles reversed qi). It promotes urination well and relieves stuffiness and oppression in the chest. For these reasons, adding it to formulas such as *Heii-san* (平胃散, Calm the Stomach Powder) or *Bukushin-in* (茯参飲) produces remarkably good results.
