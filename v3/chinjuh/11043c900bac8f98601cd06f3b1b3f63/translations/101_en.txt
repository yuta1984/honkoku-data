## Dutch Bottle (*Oranda Bin*)

Dutch bottles come in varieties such as "*Zoppirumāto*," "*Here*," and "*Hishitā*." They are used to hermetically store all manner of powdered medicines and the like, so that the medicinal vapors do not escape. Unlike flasks (*furasuko*), they are compact enough to be stored in boxes and are convenient to handle. Those brought from the Netherlands are made of considerably thick glass, and their shapes vary freely in size, with no fixed standard. The price is approximately five *monme* per piece. They are also manufactured in Nagasaki, where they are made with thinner glass, priced from two *monme* five *fun* per piece, with various grades available.
