**Tokuhire**

The *tokuhire* is a fish caught in the seas around Sado Island, and as it is considered an auspicious fish, it is used in celebratory occasions. Even in Sado, however, it is rarely obtained. It is only brought out on a stand (*dai*) when an honored guest visits a noble household, and each person receives it reverently as part of a celebration. This illustration was made from a specimen sent as a gift from Sado to the residence of a certain Mr. Murayama of Nagasaki. **Tobacco-Cutter Knife Fish (Tabakokiri-hōchō)**

The name *tabakokiri-hōchō* (tobacco-cutting knife) is a local expression (*tokoro kotoba*) used in Nagasaki, and its proper name is not known. Its size and thickness are truly like those of a tobacco-cutting knife; the back is blue-colored, while the belly gradually becomes white — it is a beautiful fish. Its shape is as illustrated to the left.

This is the same fish that is called *Kagami-uo* (mirror fish) in Amagasaki in Settsu Province. [Illustration included.]
