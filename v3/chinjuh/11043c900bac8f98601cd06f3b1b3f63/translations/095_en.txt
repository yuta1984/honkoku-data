**Kōhii-kan (Coffee Pot / Coffee Brewer)**

The *kōhii-kan* (coffee vessel) is a utensil for steeping coffee. It is made of brass. First, boiling water is poured into it, and the coffee beans are steeped within. At the bottom of this vessel there is a spout, and just above the spout there is a turning handle (a tap or stopcock). By grasping this turning handle and rotating it in the forward direction (clockwise), the coffee liquid flows out. It is received in a teacup, and when a suitable amount has been poured, turning the handle back in the reverse direction (counterclockwise) closes the spout. This is as illustrated in the figure.

**Oranda Kashi (Dutch Confectionery)**

This is a confection that the Dutch ("Red-Haired People") make for their own amusement and enjoyment. It is made from wheat flour. Inside, the filling consists of plum flesh (*bainiku*) mixed with sugar, and the outside is deep-fried in lard (pig's fat). It has an unpleasant odor and is something that the people of this region (Nagasaki) find difficult to enjoy. The shapes of one or two varieties are illustrated here.
