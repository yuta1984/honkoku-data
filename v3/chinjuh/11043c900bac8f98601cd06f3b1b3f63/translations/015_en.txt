**Silkie Chicken (Ukokkei)**

In Nagasaki, there are many people who are fond of keeping Silkie chickens. Their feathers are entirely white, and some have black speckles. Their crests and feet are all tinged with a dark, blackish color. The eggs of these chickens are an excellent food for restoring vitality to the weak, and their price is double that of ordinary eggs.

---

**Hirukui (a type of clam)**

The shell of this shellfish is a pale blackish color, and from its side protrudes a long piece of flesh. This flesh, which extends and contracts, is its mouth. It is a rare shellfish used when entertaining distinguished guests. Its size is roughly equal to the palm of one's hand.

---

**Lionhead Goldfish (Shishigashira Kingyo)**

This goldfish is named for its head, which resembles that of a lion. It is an exceptionally rare and prized fish, and its price is a hundredfold that of other fish. In Nagasaki, there are quite a few people who are fond of keeping them. It is a most fascinating fish.
