**Canarium (Kанран / Chinese Olive)**

The canarium tree can be found at Sōfuku-ji, the Chinese temple in Nagasaki. Many people try to grow it from its fruit, but the plant invariably dies once it reaches about one shaku (approximately 30 cm) in height. It cannot withstand frost and snow.

**Tsushima Wind Orchid (Tsushima Fūran)**

This is a large-leafed variety of wind orchid (*fūran*), and it is exceedingly difficult to cultivate. A plant bearing six or seven leaves is considered to have been successfully grown. If care is inadequate, the leaves will gradually rot and fall off one by one. It is best planted on a rotting tree. Alternatively, it can be wrapped in palm fiber and grown as a hanging plant. It should be hung in a shaded spot and watered occasionally. It blooms in the third month (spring). The flowers are white, and their fragrance strikes the nose powerfully. It is truly a plant to be cherished and admired.
