**The Narcissus of Foreign Lands (Bankoku no Suisen)**

The Dutch narcissus is a narcissus brought over by the Dutch. Its bulb is flat and resembles the *gindai* (silver stand, an alternative name for the narcissus). When one places water in a pot, submerges the bulb, and tends to it in this manner, it will produce flowers. It is truly a plant worthy of the name "suisen" (water-immortal/narcissus, literally "water hermit").

**Marvels of the Great Ocean (Taikai Kibutsu)**

**The Benevolent Fish (*Jingyo*)**

The *jingyo* is a great fish said to measure some twenty-four or twenty-five *jō* in length. When a vicious fish (*akugyo*) attempts to harm a ship, the *jingyo* comes and protects the vessel, keeping the vicious fish at bay. It also protects smaller, weaker fish (such as sardines) when a vicious fish attempts to devour them, preventing them from being eaten. For this reason, it is said that fishermen (*umi-bito*, "people of the sea") forbid the catching of this fish.
