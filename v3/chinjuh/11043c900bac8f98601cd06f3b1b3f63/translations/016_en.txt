**Pinecone Fish (Matsukasa-uo)**

The pinecone fish is found in Nagasaki. It is an exceptionally beautiful fish, with a hexagonal, extremely hard outer skin. It is boiled and then the skin is peeled before eating. Its flavor can be said to be delicious. It is about four to five *sun* in size.

---

**Kōmuki**

The people of Nagasaki call this fish *kōmuki*. It has a protruding piece of flesh on its head. It is an unusual-looking fish, measuring from one foot five or six *sun* to approximately two feet in length.

---

**Kaime**

The people of Nagasaki call this fish *kaime*. It is likely a type of shark (*fuka*). Its skin resembles that of a flounder (*karei*), and its body is of a peculiar form. Its Chinese name is 犁頭鯋 (*lítóu shā*). [Illustration included, indicating the mouth.]

**Hammerhead Shark (Shūmoku-buka)**

The people of Nagasaki also call this fish *kasenosō*. Its Chinese name is 双髻鯋 (*shuāngjì shā*). [Illustration included, indicating the belly and back.]

**Kidako**

The people of Nagasaki call this fish *kidako*. Its shape resembles that of a snake, and it is yellow in color. Its flavor is said to be exceedingly delicious. [Illustration included.]
