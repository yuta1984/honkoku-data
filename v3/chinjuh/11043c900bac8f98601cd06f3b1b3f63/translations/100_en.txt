## Kaijo (海女) — The Mermaid (Ningyo)

The upper half of the body resembles that of a woman, while the lower half belongs to the class of fish. The bones of the mermaid are a marvelous medicine for stopping hemorrhages (rectal or uterine bleeding). In the barbarian tongue, it is called "*Peishi Mutoru*." Dutch traders occasionally bring them over.

---

## Kaijin (海人) — The Sea-Person

The entire body is covered in a skin of flesh that hangs downward, resembling the appearance of wearing *hakama* (pleated trousers). In all other respects, it does not differ from a human body. Both hands and feet have webbing between the fingers and toes. It is said that even when this creature comes ashore and is left for several days, it does not die.
