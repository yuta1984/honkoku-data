**Gotan Nanshō (Ox Bile with Arisaema / Bile-Processed Southern Star)**

Gotan Nanshō is a substance brought over by foreign traders (Nanban people). Its color is slightly dark (blackish). Its shape varies — sometimes resembling a fist in size, sometimes round like a ball — and there is no fixed form. Its preparation consists essentially of kneading ox bile (牛膽, gyūtan) together with Arisaema (天南星, tennanshō, a medicinal plant) and forming it into a solid mass. The older the specimen, the higher its quality is considered to be. As for its medicinal efficacy: it is taken dissolved in hot water (plain boiled water) for all manner of acute conditions, phlegm, asthma, and respiratory distress, and its effect is said to be rapid.

**Shokurato (Chocolate)**

Shokurato is a kidney tonic (jinyaku) brought over by the Kōmō-jin (the "Red-Haired People," i.e., the Dutch). Its form resembles an animal's horn, and its color is similar to that of Asen (阿仙, catechu — a dark brown astringent extract). Its flavor is mild and understated. The method of its production is not entirely clear. To take it medicinally: first, prepare hot water; then shave approximately three *bu* (a small measure) of the shokurato into it; next, add one egg (鶏子) and a small amount of sugar. Using a tea whisk (chasen), mix these three ingredients together thoroughly, as one would whisk tea, until fine bubbles (蟹眼, "crab's eyes") appear. This preparation should then be consumed.
