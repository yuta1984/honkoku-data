## Flying Fish (Tobiuo)

The flying fish is about one shaku (approximately 30 cm) in length. It flies over the surface of the water like a bird. When it encounters a large fish lying in wait, watching for its shadow and opening its mouth to devour it, the flying fish becomes frightened and repeatedly leaps high into the air. It is said that in former times, one flew into a Dutch ship and was brought back as a specimen.

## Horiha (薄里波)

The Horiha is about one jō (approximately 3 meters) in length, and its entire body is transparent like crystal. Its body color changes to reflect whatever surrounds it — for example, if near a rock, it takes on the color of the rock; if near seaweed, it takes on the color of the seaweed. It is that sort of creature.

## Ragaru

The Ragaru lives in the seas of the southern lands. Its body is about two jō (approximately 6 meters) long, with a long tail, and its green scales (armor) are so hard that they surpass even metal and stone. Spear blades cannot pierce them. Its teeth are like a saw, and its feet have sharp claws. Its nature is exceedingly ferocious: it catches and devours all manner of fish. At times it comes ashore and even chases after beasts. It also eats humans. However, because it moves slowly on land, beasts are fortunate enough to be able to escape. In the sea, it swims with great speed. Nevertheless, it does not eat small fish. Therefore, hundreds of species of small fish constantly follow the Ragaru to avoid the danger of being swallowed by other fish. When it gives birth, the young are said to be as large as a chicken's egg. When it gives birth on land, it discharges saliva and foam. This substance carries a powerful poison, and if a person or livestock mistakenly steps on it, they immediately collapse and die. This Ragaru has a loud voice, and upon seeing a person, it becomes enraged and cries out. However, it has a soft patch about two shaku (approximately 60 cm) wide on its belly. There is a fish, about three shaku (approximately 90 cm) in length whose name I have forgotten, that kills the Ragaru by stabbing it in this soft underside. There is also a strange creature on land.
