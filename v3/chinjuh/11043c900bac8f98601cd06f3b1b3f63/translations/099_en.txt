## Ragaru (continued)

…watching for the moment when [the large beast] opens its mouth, [the mouse-like creature] instantly leaps inside and devours the entrails. When large creatures are bitten by this thing, they suffer terribly and all die. There is also a plant called *zatsufukuran* (雑腹蘭). When people of the southern barbarian lands plant this grass around their homes, the Ragaru is frightened and will not approach.

## Rashimo (落斯馬)

It is about four jō (approximately 12 meters) in length, with short legs at the front. It is said to rarely surface and appear above the water. Its scales and armor are truly hard, and neither swords nor blades can pierce them. It has two horns on its forehead, curved like hooks. It is said to hang these horns on rocks along the shore when it sleeps.

---

## Kō-fish (航魚)

This creature belongs to the class of shellfish and crustaceans. It is barely one shaku (approximately 30 cm) in size. Its shell resembles that of a clam (hamaguri), and it has six legs. When it wishes to move, it uses its shell as a boat and its legs as sails, drifting along with the current.
