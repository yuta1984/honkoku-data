## Musk Shrew (Jakō Nezumi)

The musk shrew originally came from China and is found in great numbers in Nagasaki. It is a creature not seen in other provinces. This animal has a long snout and emits a fragrant odor. It has poor eyesight during the day and cries at night; its voice resembles that of a weasel. The people of Nagasaki, upon hearing the cry of this shrew, consider it a good omen and rejoice.

## Wild Cattle (Yakyū)

Wild cattle serve as food for the Chinese and Southern Barbarians. They are raised in the area around Inasa-Tateyama and sold to the Chinese and Southern Barbarians. In size, they are about three times larger than a dog. Compared to pigs, however, they are considerably smaller, and their flavor does not match that of pork either. Nevertheless, as they are considered extremely warming to the body, they are in great demand and therefore fetch a higher price than pigs. Their coats are uniformly white. They become very attached to people, making it hard to bear the thought of eating them.

## Domestic Pig (Kacho / Buta)

Domestic pigs are a staple food for the Chinese and the Dutch. Many are kept and traded at Tateyama and Inasa in Nagasaki. They are also found in the Chinese Residence (Tōjin Yashiki) and the Dutch Trading Post (Oranda Yashiki). They resemble wild boars and are well-fattened creatures.
