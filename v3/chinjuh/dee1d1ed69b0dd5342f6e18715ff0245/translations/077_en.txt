**Chapter Six: A Hunter Catches Something of Unknown Name**

A hunter from Hidaka District in Kii Province went alone into the mountains and was waiting while blowing a deer call. The pampas grass field across from him rustled with a sound, the grass parting in two as something moved through it. Thinking it might be a deer, he blew the deer call from time to time, and the creature came following the sound of the call. He parted the kaya grass, thrust out his musket, and waited. When the distance had closed to about seven or eight ken, he at last peered forward to look — and the width of its face appeared to be about three shaku, and when it opened its mouth, that too was about three shaku across. Its crimson tongue spread wide and long, yet its height was a mere one shaku and four or five sun. Thinking it was advancing upon him like a great serpent, and that if he were too slow he would be swallowed by the thing, he thrust out his musket and fired without hesitation. It struck without fail. The creature soon tumbled into the valley, but its actual size was surprisingly small. So it was not a serpent after all.
