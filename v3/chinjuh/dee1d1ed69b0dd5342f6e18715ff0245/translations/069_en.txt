　　Chapter One: The Story of a Stupa Giving Birth to a Child

In times past, there was a man called Hazama, a renowned master of the waki role in Noh theater, known throughout the land. It is said that he was the child born of a stupa.
His father was the village headman of a village called Hazama, situated to the west of Tonda in the province of Settsu. Though he tilled the small paddies and worked the fields, his livelihood was meager, and he appeared utterly worn and bedraggled, like a bird that had lost all its feathers.
Unable to endure such circumstances any longer, he called together one or two of his servants and his wife, saying: "Take good care of the house; tend the fields and paddies without neglect. I have something in mind and wish to try my hand at trade." With that, he set off toward Kyushu. Upon arriving, he conducted himself outwardly as a merchant, but nothing came of it in any meaningful way.
