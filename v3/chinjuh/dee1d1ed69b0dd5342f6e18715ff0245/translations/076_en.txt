**[Continuation of the story of the tanuki's belly-drumming]**

How strange, I thought, and while keeping a close watch and listening, the sound grew ever nearer, and there at the foot of the hill where I had been sleeping, the drumming was just like the skillful strokes of a master drummer — like the finale beats, five or so combined in what seemed like a melodic percussion rhythm. It was so delightful, words cannot do it justice. I listened on and on, and after about an hour, it went off in the direction it had come from. What a thing to have to part with. It is said in this world that "tanuki drum their bellies," and this was surely one such occasion.

**Chapter Five: The Matter of the Mountain Princess (Yama-hime)**

A certain rōnin (masterless samurai) related the following: When he was in Okayama in Bizen Province, he went to a mountain village for leisure. A person there told him this story: Once, while venturing deep into the mountains for hunting, he encountered a woman who appeared to be about twenty years of age. Her face and eyes were strikingly beautiful, without equal in the world. She wore a small-sleeved robe of rare, unusual color, and her black hair had a luster far beyond the ordinary. She did not look at all like someone who ought to be in such a place. Finding it deeply suspicious that she should be in a mountain wilderness with no roads, he took up his musket and fired directly at her. She caught the shot with her right hand, and there she stood smiling gently, her lips parted in a grin amidst the deep grass — an unsettling sight indeed. He then loaded two balls with powder and fired quickly, but she caught these too with her left hand and laughed as if nothing had happened. By this point, he had exhausted all his ammunition. Terrified at what might happen, he hurried away, but she did not give chase and he managed to return safely. Afterward, he told the story to an elderly person, who said: "That must be what is called a Yama-hime (Mountain Princess). It is said that if she takes a liking to you, she will give you treasures." Well, perhaps it was just as well that he received no treasure.
