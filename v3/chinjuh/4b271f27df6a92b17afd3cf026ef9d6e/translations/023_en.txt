Item: On the model forms (hinagata)
One sheet of thick paper is cut diagonally, folded as shown in the diagram, and inserted between bamboo. This is used in the ground-purification ceremony (jisai) performed when constructing an azuchi (archery target mound). This is what is referred to as the "thirty-six hinagata." Among these, five use paper of the five colors, curved like the butterfly motif on a chōshi (ritual sake vessel), and are not threaded onto the skewer — these number thirty-one in form; the "kizakata" (notched forms) number five.

[In red ink:] The method of attaching these folds is transmitted orally.

Kushisaburō, square, length one shaku two sun [approx. 36 cm]
Made of bamboo.
