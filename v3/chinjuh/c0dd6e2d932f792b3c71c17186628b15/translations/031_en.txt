This [the *hakarai*] is surely of the same kind as the *kagemu jina* (Shadow Badger), and might not the name *hakarai* (Grave Washer) simply be a corruption of *hakarashi* (Grave Ravager)?

**Kuda**

Found in Asahina Mountain, Yokouchi Village, Shida District. The villagers say: In the area around Asahina Mountain in this village, there lives a creature called *kuda*. Its shape and size resemble those of a weasel. When a person captures it alive, tames it, and puts it to use, gold, silver, clothing, and food become available at will, and nothing is ever lacking. This creature takes miso as its regular food, and all food must first be set aside and offered to the *kuda* before the people eat — just as one would offer food to a deity. If by mistake its care and feeding become neglectful, it will immediately attach itself to another person, causing them to speak ill of its keeper, cursing and shaming them for the weakness of their devotion. It truly resembles a persistent and troublesome spirit. The practice of using a *kuda* is a great prohibition throughout the land, and while people know they cannot escape punishment for it, local custom has it that some are still tempted to use one in hopes of gaining wealth. This must arise from an indulgence in greed, so the account goes. This creature may perhaps be of the same kind as the *osaki-gitsune* (tail-split fox).

**Raijū (Thunder Beast)**

According to accounts, in Hanazawa Village, Mashizu District, on Mount Takakusa, there lives a creature called the *raijū* (Thunder Beast). By nature it is gentle and mild, and often sleeps during the day; even when it wakes, it appears as though it cannot see. On days of thunder and heavy rain, it rides upon clouds and flies through the sky, and when it falls by mistake, it splits trees apart and injures people. Its fierce power is impossible to withstand. Its shape is like that of a cat, resembling a weasel. The fur across its entire body grows in a disheveled manner, is pale reddish with a tinge of black, and from the belly to the region of the thighs it is pale yellow.
