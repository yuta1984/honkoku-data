**Kagemu jina (Shadow Badger)**

This creature appeared in Yamahara Village, Ihara District. According to accounts, during the Kyōwa era (1801–1804), smallpox spread through the area and many young children died. At that time, a strange beast appeared and came every night to dig up freshly made graves and devour the corpses. The monks and laypeople alike grieved and lamented over this, and when they erected a strict enclosure around the area, the creature began digging from a distance away, dragging out the corpses and devouring them. This continued for several months without stopping, but eventually the creature was shot down using a firearm. Its appearance was like that of a tanuki (raccoon dog). An elder of the village identified this strange beast, saying it was a creature known as the *kagemu jina* (Shadow Badger).

Upon reflection, in an old illustrated scroll of a hundred monsters, there is a depiction of a beast called *hakarai* (Grave Washer). Its shape is like that of a tanuki, with a short tail, three claws on each of its four feet, red claws, a wide mouth, narrow eyes set toward the back of its head, and fur patterned with sesame-seed-like spots across its entire body.
