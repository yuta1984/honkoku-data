**Kirigirusu (Cricket/Katydid)** [*Sagyoku*]
The "Memorandum of the Castle Guards of Suruga Castle" states: "Among the creatures found within the castle grounds: *kirigirusu*." Currently, the *kirigirusu* of Fuchū do not cry in a manner resembling the sound of a loom, but simply call "kiri-kiri." Only in the vicinity of Mukaishikiji Village in Abe District do they call "gihitō." This insect begins to appear around the sixth month. According to a resident of Kamiiide Village in Fuji District: "The *sagyoku* cries as if saying '*tsunzurusashi kozashi mochi wo tsuite kurenjyo*' (a phrase mimicking the insect's call)." Perhaps "*tsunzurusashi*" refers to the rubbing of wings together (*tsubasa wo suraseru*). The remainder has not yet been examined.

**Firefly** [*Hotaru*]
The "Supplementary Records of Suruga Province" (*Suruga Kokushi Hoi*) states: "Fireflies are found in Kamitori Village, Watari District." Currently, they are found in particularly great numbers around the moat of Fuchū Castle in Abe District and along the banks of the Abe River. They begin to appear from the end of the third month and are exceptionally large. After dusk on summer evenings, they gather in swarms like a ball and appear to engage in battle, eventually falling into the water and scattering. At that time, about half of them die. This phenomenon is called "Hotaru Kassen" (Firefly Battle) and is considered a most delightful spectacle.

**Spider** [*Kumo*]
The "Memorandum of the Castle Guards of Suruga Castle" states: "Among the creatures found within the castle grounds: a large spider." Spiders of four to five *sun* in length, or even those exceeding eight *sun* (possibly a transcription error for "eight *sun*" rather than "eight *shaku*"), are commonly found in great numbers.

**Centipede** [*Mukade*]
The "Memorandum of the Castle Guards of Suruga Castle" states: "Among the creatures found within the castle grounds: centipede." The same is noted for the guard post (*kaban-goya*).
