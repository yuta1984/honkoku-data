**Mirukui (海蜌 / Geoduck Clam)**
According to the *Junsonki* (Village Survey Record): Found in the inner sea of Miho Village, Arito District. It is quite rare. The local people call it "mirukuhi." And so on.

**Tanishi (田蠃 / Freshwater Snail)**
It is said: Tanishi (freshwater snails) are found in the paddies and small ditches of various villages. In the Fuji and Sunto Districts, they are called "tsubo" (壺) in the local dialect. This refers to the same creature.

**Suppon (鼈 / Soft-shelled Turtle)**
According to the *Suruga Kokushi Hoi* (Supplementary Records of the Suruga Provincial Gazetteer): Soft-shelled turtles are caught from Asahata Pond in Abe District, sold in Yokota-machi, and served to travelers. And so on.

**Yadokari (寄居 / Hermit Crab)**
It is said: The yadokari (locally called "gauna" or "gaunahi") is found at the foot of Mt. Fuji and in small salt-water channels in various places. The local people call it "gaunahi," which is a dialectal corruption of the proper name.

**Tsukihigai (月日貝 / Moon-and-Sun Scallop)**
According to the *Suruga Kokushi Hoi*: The tsukihigai is considered a famous product of Shimizu Port in Arito District. And so on. Its shape resembles that of a hotategai (scallop), being round, thin, and ridged. Those that are white in color are called "Moon" (月), and those that are red are called "Sun" (日).
