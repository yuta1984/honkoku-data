**Okinoishi (沖の石, "Offshore Stone Bird")**

According to tradition, this bird is found in Miho-ura and Maehama in Udo District, Tagonoura in Ihara District, and Shironokoshi Beach in Masutō District. The local people call it "Okinoishi," and it is also known as "Shimatori" (Island Bird). It is about the size of a wild goose, with broadly spread wings, and its color is black, or mottled, or gray. Its cry resembles the bellowing of a cow, and it also resembles a domestic duck. It lives permanently on the waves and is not skilled at flying. It waits for small fish to surface and leap of their own accord, then catches and eats them. When this bird happens to come offshore, if one frequently throws stones at it, it responds to the sound and approaches the shore, eventually coming up onto land, where it can be quickly caught. It has a very strong fishy smell. Awamoto, the Hōin of Zuisen-in, states: "This is the *kaiga* (海鵞, sea goose) recorded in the *Taiwan Fuzhi* (Taiwan Prefecture Gazetteer). Its common name is *Nanfeng Dòng* (南風戇)." It is also called *Hotei-ga* (布袋鵞, "Hotei Goose") or *Kotsuhancho* (乞飯鳥, "Beggar-Rice Bird"); all of these names refer to the same bird.
