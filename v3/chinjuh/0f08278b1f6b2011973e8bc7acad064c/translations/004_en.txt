The Morning Sun General, Kiso Yoshinaka, summoned Datenosuke, Noinodaiyata, and Higuchinojiro and commanded them: "Interpret the omen of my dream — whether it be auspicious or inauspicious."

"The character for 'sheep' (羊), when written with eight strokes topped by the character for 'king' (王), leaves extra strokes. Those extra strokes — the hanging marks — represent the tail. If you remove the two tail strokes, what remains is the character 王, meaning 'king.' There is no doubt whatsoever that you shall rightly ascend to the supreme throne of the Ten Virtues, lord over ten thousand chariots." With such flattering words and crooked eloquence, the treacherous sycophants fawned upon him, and Yoshinaka rejoiced greatly — and from this moment, his wicked ambitions grew ever stronger.
