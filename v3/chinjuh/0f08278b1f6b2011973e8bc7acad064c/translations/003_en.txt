[Handwritten note]
The twelfth year of Hōreki, the year of Mizunoe-Uma (1762)
　　　　　　Illustrated by Fusanobu

[Main text]
It was the time of the Juei era. Having driven the Heike clan to the western seas, Yoshinaka grew ever more arrogant, indulging in drink day and night, dismissing his loyal retainers, and drawing treacherous sycophants close to him — how lamentable this was.

One night, Yoshinaka dreamed that he became a sheep, borrowed its horns, broke off its tail, and ascended to the heavens.
