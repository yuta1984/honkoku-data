## Chushingura: Instructions for Folding Paper Dolls
### (A Guide to Folding Origami Figures)

> ※ Using the paper dolls made from these folding diagrams, one can recreate famous scenes from all eleven acts of the Chushingura.

---

**[Basic Form, Steps One through Seven: These steps are common to all figures]**

**Step One**
Fold a square piece of paper in half, then fold it again into thirds. [Diagram shown.]

> ※ "Cut" → Cut along this vertical crease line.

**Step Two**
This diagram shows the reverse side of the figure in Step One.
Fold the sections marked with ○ upward as shown in the diagram; cut away only the portions marked ○ along the dotted crease lines; color as appropriate, then fold as shown in Step Three.

**Step Three**
Fold all six sections inward.

> ※ Refer to and compare with the diagram marked "I" (イ).

**Step Four**
Tuck the fold inward and flatten as shown in the diagram.

**Step Five**
Open and fold.

> ※ "Cut" → Insert the head piece from this point (slot indicated).

**Step Seven**
Reverse-side diagram:
Push the head out into a rounded shape from the back using a toothpick.
