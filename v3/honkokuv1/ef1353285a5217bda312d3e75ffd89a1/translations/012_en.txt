Between this Shimo-Yokota and Tanbajima Village there is a distance of approximately two *ri* (roughly 8 km), and within this stretch, in the river itself, the flooding and loss of homes to the floodwaters were tremendous. That the waters swept all the way out to the great sea of Echigo in this manner was a calamity of a kind never before seen in recorded history.
