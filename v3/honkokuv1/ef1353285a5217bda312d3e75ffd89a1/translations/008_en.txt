&emsp;Assistant (Tedai): Lord Yoneyama Tominojō

As relief (measures), unhulled rice (momi) of seven bales, or six, four, or two bales (was distributed according to the degree of damage). This was an immense act of benevolence, most gratefully received.

In this way, the Matsumoto domain also conducted charitable distributions (segyō) at Yasuhara-guchi for those who had suffered acute hardship in various places around the Zenkō-ji temple's special opening ceremony (o-kaichō) and the Inariyama area, as they were making their way home. For those suffering extreme hardship—

**Left folio:**

—according to the degree (of their hardship), (donations were made) down to clothing, sashes, food, money, sedge hats, and straw sandals, given not only to those within the domain's territory and entrusted territories, but also to people from various provinces and islands. It is said that news of this act resounded far and wide.

Furthermore, during the season of flooding, an official notice was issued to the domain's territory (to the following effect): "Should persons from flood-affected areas of the Matsushiro domain come to this domain's territory and be found to be in extreme distress, their village names and personal names shall be verified, food shall be provided so they do not go hungry, and their lives shall be preserved. The expenses incurred shall be recorded in a ledger and submitted." We are filled with awe and admiration at the benevolent intentions (behind this notice). Those who heard of it shed tears.
