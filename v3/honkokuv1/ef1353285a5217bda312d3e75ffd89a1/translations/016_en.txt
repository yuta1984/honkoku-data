(The mountains) sweeping round and round, blocking (the river) in two layers.

Concerning the mountains to the south along the Iwakura mountain range — Utami Village and Sakurai Village: In Utami Village, there was only (collapsed) broken earth, and a small portion of the ground gave way. In Sakurai Village, the collapse reached as far as the village itself, and a considerable portion of the ground broke away, coming to rest at the middle slope of the mountain. There was no direct impact on the Sai River.

　　Sakurai breaks away — hear the sounds of singing (utabi)!
　　　　"Not into the river!" — it stops mid-air (in the middle slope).

Furthermore, downstream of the landslide fill, a mountain collapsed in Koichi Village, and across the Sai River, Komatsu-

　　Kamiichi — and now Koichi's mountain has collapsed;

The waters of the Sai River had been dammed for twenty days,

　　then drawn away in an hour and a half — like a great sea receding.

As an offering of memorial prayers for the great many people who departed this world,

　　I bind these words together and offer them as flowers to the dead.
