However, the main hall (Hondō), the main gate (Sanmon), and the Daikanjin (the head administrative temple) were unharmed. Beyond these, the Niōmon (temple gate), Hongan-ji, twelve temples of the Chūshū sect, and many other temples suffered damage. In Daimon-chō, Ushiro-chō, Gondō-chō, and other areas throughout the town, buildings collapsed from the earthquake and were subsequently destroyed by fire. Furthermore, in the town of Inariyama, every single house collapsed and was then consumed by fire. In Hachiman, a small number of townhouses collapsed but the rest were spared, and there was no fire; the Hachiman Shrine also escaped harm. Iiyama Castle, along with its castle town, was severely shaken, but there was no fire. Shinmachi suffered total collapse, and on top of that, fire was followed by flooding — a truly dire emergency. In Matsushiro, there was some structural collapse, but the damage was reportedly light. Areas that similarly suffered only minor damage include: Ueda, Matsumoto, Aida-gumi, Omi-gumi, Kawate-gumi (of the same region), Ueno-gumi, Narial-gumi, Hotaka-gumi, and the Southern Districts (Minami-gumi),

【Left folio】
Suwa, Saku, Ina, the area from Zenkō-ji toward Echigo, and furthermore Matsukawa-gumi, Ōmachi-gumi — also northward from Shikajō, to Itoigawa on the border with Echigo, and Togakushi-yama, Nakanshinmachi, and the deeper mountain regions — all suffered only light damage. It is also said that the Kantō region experienced only a minor tremor. Furthermore, among the nearby areas that suffered heavy destruction from the great earthquake, the most severely affected were Kawanakajima, and in the mountain areas, Ōoka, Nakamaki, and Minami-maki. Additionally, in Kamijō, the Zen temple Shinshin-ji had its main hall completely destroyed and also suffered flooding. The Zen temple Anyō-ji had its main hall completely destroyed but was not flooded. The Zen temple Unsō-ji suffered ground collapse, was struck by fire, and was subsequently flooded. Furthermore, the village of Shimohito suffered a large-scale landslide and flooding. In Tanaka, the Zen temple Kōzen-ji was half destroyed. In Miyahira-gumi, Imaizumi Village, Tenshū-ji (of the same sect) had its main hall completely destroyed. Furthermore, in Makinoshima, a large number of houses collapsed.
