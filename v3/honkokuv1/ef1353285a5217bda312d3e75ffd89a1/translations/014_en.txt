18th, the night of the 20th of the 1st month, and the night of the 26th of the 1st month — the earthquakes recorded up to this point did not shake continuously, occurring occasionally and sporadically until early in the 5th month. After that, while there was an earthquake at the seventh hour [around midnight] on the night of the 12th of the 6th month, it was of the sort that occasionally occurs in ordinary times.

One should feel gratitude for having survived such a great earthquake with one's life, and devote oneself to religious faith. Furthermore, although I wished, in my spare time beyond the duties of loyalty and filial piety, to visit the famous scenic spots and historical sites in the vicinity, due to exhaustion from the earthquake and falling behind in agricultural work, we barely managed to complete payment of this year's tribute [nengu] and saw the year out.
