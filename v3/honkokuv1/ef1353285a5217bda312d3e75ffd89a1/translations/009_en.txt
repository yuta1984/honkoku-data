**Right folio:**

Those who had been driven to extreme distress by the floodwaters came (to the domain), saying they wished to work as day laborers and the like. If they were persons of reliable background, (the notice stated that) it was permissible to employ and keep them. Words alone cannot do justice to (the lord's) deeply compassionate intentions.

Furthermore, the official notice stated that, regardless of whether one was from within the domain's territory or from another domain, a *segaki* (ceremony of offerings to hungry spirits) service would be held on the sixth day of the fifth month at Danseiji temple in Ōmachi, for those who had perished in the earthquake, floods, and disasters. All were urged to come and pay their respects at the temple, and everyone wept, wringing their sleeves (with grief and gratitude).

After the waters receded, on the twenty-sixth day of the fifth month, a party departed from Matsumoto, led by:
