Itahana Post Station
Nakasendo, Usui District, Kōzuke Province

According to a complaint filed by the above post station: From the 28th day of the 6th month through the 5th day of this month, Mount Asama had been erupting greatly and ash had been falling, to a depth roughly equivalent to frost. Then, from around the sixth hour of the evening on the 6th of this month until the hour of the Sheep (approximately 2 PM) on the 8th, earthquakes continued day and night without cease, and stones and sand fell without interruption. For approximately two and a half hours between the hour of the Horse (noon) and past the hour of the Monkey (approximately 4 PM), it became as dark as night, and people could barely manage their affairs using paper lanterns and lamps. During this time, the accumulated depth of falling stones and sand reached approximately one shaku and one sun (roughly 33 cm), and in places where it gathered, it reached one shaku five or six sun (approximately 45–48 cm). Of the damaged houses, two-tenths of those belonging to persons serving the post-horse relay duties collapsed, while many other back-houses and small buildings were crushed, as stated in the complaint.

Not only were crops devastated, but even grass had been completely lost, causing great difficulty as there was no fodder for horses at this time. This was the substance of the complaint. End of report.

The above concerns the four post stations along the Nakasendo in Shinano and Kōzuke Provinces under my jurisdiction as magistrate. Due to the falling stones and sand from this eruption of Mount Asama, in particular the three post stations in Shinano Province appear to have been rendered virtually uninhabitable (equivalent to complete abandonment). However, as the eruption has not yet subsided, our agents remaining on-site have been reporting one after another that they are unable to go and conduct inspections. Although the details shall be reported at a later time, we hereby submit this preliminary report for now. End of report.
