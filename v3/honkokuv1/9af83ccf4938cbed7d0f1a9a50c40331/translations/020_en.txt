Sand falls—even the gods know not why—
　　　　　　(Rice market prices)
With nothing left to eat,
　　　　　　Now the water runs dry

[Note: At this time in Edo, the water supply pipes broke and water ran out. Water cost about 132 mon per load [hika], and rice was only about eight gō for 100 mon. The tenement dwellers could not possibly sustain themselves in such conditions.]

　　　　Composed in the style of Noh chant [Utai]

"Takasago-ya"—in this back-alley tenement, opening the door,
Going out to buy polished rice,
The tears of that old woman—how pitiful.
With only one striped padded kimono to her name,
Having pawned away all she had,
She has already died—passed away, passed away.

The above describes the situation in Edo, where rice prices have risen extraordinarily high. Since the eruption of Mt. Asama, prices have not fallen at all, and life has become utterly unsustainable. The tenement dwellers can no longer make a living. It is for this reason that the above has been composed.
