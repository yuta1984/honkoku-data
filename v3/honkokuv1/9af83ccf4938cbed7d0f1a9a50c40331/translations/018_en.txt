Nakai Village
　　　　　　　　　　Village Headman: Asauemon

Item: In Ōmae Village, all houses were swept away. Of the approximately 430 persons residing there, about 400 managed to flee and survived. The remaining approximately 30 persons were either swept away or buried, and their bodies have not been found at all.

Item: In Kamanohara Village, all houses were swept away. Of the approximately 600 persons residing there, about 90 men and women managed to flee and survived. The whereabouts of approximately 500 persons are unknown. Since all village officials perished, this petition is submitted without an official seal. In the mud, five or six horses appear to be buried, perhaps unable to be swept away after dying.

Item: In Nakai Village, more than seven houses remain, while all others were swept away. Of the approximately 260 persons residing there, about 230 managed to flee and survived. The remaining approximately 27 or 28 persons were either swept away or remain unaccounted for.

Item: In Haneo Village, of the approximately 260 persons residing there, about 230 managed to flee and survived. The remaining approximately 28 persons were either swept away or remain unaccounted for.

The above contents are as stated in this document, without error. We hereby submit this petition.
