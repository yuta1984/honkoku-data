The reports (o-todoke-sho) submitted by neighboring provinces and the various petition documents (sho-so-sho) concerning the eruption of Mt. Asama in Shinshū and the mudflow disaster in Kōzuke during Tenmei 3rd year [1783] were borrowed from the collection of Mr. Kobori Toyokichi of Akudo Shinden, Furukawa-machi, Sashima-gun, Ibaraki Prefecture. These documents were then copied out by the assistant secretary (roku-ji-ho) so that they might be preserved permanently and serve as a reference for future generations.

January 17th, Meiji 33rd year [1900]
　　　　　　　At the Tone River Flood Control Association
　　　　　　　　　Secretary　　Negishi Yūkichi (seal)
