　　July 8th

1. Copy of successive reports submitted by Lord Itakura Ise-no-kami to Lord Tanuma Tonomo-no-kami, who is on duty, as follows:

Concerning the eruption of Mount Asama in Shinano Province, which has been ongoing since late last month: from around midday on the 1st of this month, the eruption intensified markedly, and occasional small amounts of volcanic ash fell upon Annaka in Kozuke Province, which is where my domain is located. On the 5th of the same month, the rumbling grew extremely strong, and volcanic ash mixed with volcanic rocks fell and accumulated to a depth of six to seven sun in some places. From around midday on the 7th, conditions became as dark as night, to the extent that walking became difficult. As our retainers at the domain have reported to us that the volcanic ash and rumbling have still not ceased, we hereby submit this report at this stage. End.
