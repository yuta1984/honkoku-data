From the 29th day of the 6th month, Mount Asama in Shinano Province began to erupt violently, with enormous clouds of smoke rising, and large stones, small stones, volcanic sand, and volcanic rocks falling, destroying all crops within approximately one ri of nearby villages. Within the lord's domain, spanning one and a half ri from Imanuma toward Oiwake, and over twenty-eight chō in width, at the fifth hour of the night on the 4th day of the 7th month, a sound like thunder was heard and [volcanic material] fell. Following this, smoke rose up, and in alarm, all twenty-three nearby villages evacuated. Not only crops, but bamboo and trees were entirely killed and buried.

One item: A copy of the report submitted by Lord Matsudaira Yamato-no-kami, as follows.

In my domain of Maebashi in Kōzuke Province, this month, in the direction of Mount Asama in Shinano Province, there were occasional tremors, and small amounts of glittering sand fell; the following day was the same. After that, small volcanic rocks began to fall continuously, followed by rain falling like mud, whereupon the Tone River and Hirose River suddenly flooded, and enormous quantities of mud-water together with volcanic rocks flowed down, the entire surface covered in rising smoke as they flowed. As a result, private residences were swept away and people and horses were injured; however, the mud pushed up from the rivers has accumulated so deeply that investigation at this time is difficult. This was reported to us by the retainers posted at Maebashi. We hereby submit this preliminary report. Further details shall be reported at a later date. End of report.
