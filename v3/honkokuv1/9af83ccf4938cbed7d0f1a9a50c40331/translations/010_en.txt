Although the water gradually receded, in low-lying areas the water could not drain away and pooled for several days, causing the rice and grass to rot, while the dry fields suffered from withering. Furthermore, from around the sixth hour of dawn on the 6th of this month until the evening of the 7th, volcanic ash fell intermittently upon the fields. From the night of the 8th, the ashfall intensified markedly, and by the 9th, ash had accumulated to approximately three to four sun in some places, causing further severe damage to the rice crops that had just begun to recover. Our retainers stationed in that area have reported this to us. Regarding the total amount of crop damage, we will report once the harvest has been assessed, but we hereby submit this preliminary report. End.
