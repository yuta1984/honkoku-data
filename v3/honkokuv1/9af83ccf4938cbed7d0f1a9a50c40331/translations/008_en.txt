Furthermore, the rumbling sounded like an earthquake, and by the 7th, daybreak came without those present even realizing it, as volcanic stones and ash fell in increasing quantities, accumulating in some places to over one shaku (approximately 30 cm) in depth. Throughout the day, rumbling and thunder and lightning continued without cease, and as night fell the tremors intensified further, continuing until around midday on the 8th, with conditions as dark as night. From the Hour of the Sheep (around 2 PM) on that same day, it became possible to see one's surroundings again, and the ash, thunder, and tremors all ceased. However, on that same day, muddy floodwaters surged into the area called Shibamachi, carrying with them large boulders, miscellaneous goods, and boats, burying Shibamachi entirely under mud and rocks, with large boulders forced even into the interiors of homes. Nonetheless, the Tone River appeared to be at its normal water level. The fields and paddies of Amidaiji Village, adjacent to the same area, were also buried under small stones. As for the domain office, there was no particular damage, and injuries to people and horses have not yet been ascertained. This report has been relayed to us by our retainers at the domain, and we therefore report it hastily at this stage. Further details will be reported in due course. End.
