11th day of the 7th month

Item: A separate official report from the same lord, as follows:
On the 8th day of this month, at the Hour of the Goat, a sudden great flood rushed down the Tone River (Mokugawa) in Kozuke Province, sweeping away the drawbridge and the checkpoint. Accordingly, the guards have withdrawn to Kanai Village nearby, as reported to us by our retainers stationed there. We hereby submit this preliminary report. Respectfully submitted.
