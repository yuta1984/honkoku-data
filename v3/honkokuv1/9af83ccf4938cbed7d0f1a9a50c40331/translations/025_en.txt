The foregoing content was inspired by hearing, this very year, vendors selling chili peppers while singing songs such as "tonton tōgarashi, hirihiri karai, kasaya no ko" ("tonton hot pepper, burning spicy, the umbrella-maker's child"), and so forth. I have set it down here in this manner. There were many other interesting anecdotes and items of note beyond what is recorded here, but for the most part they have not been included in this volume. Only those things deemed, upon careful consideration, to be worthy material for storytelling have been selected and excerpted, compiled into this single volume.

　　Tenmei 3rd year [1783]
　　　　　8th month, 9th day of the Year of the Rabbit — copied herein
