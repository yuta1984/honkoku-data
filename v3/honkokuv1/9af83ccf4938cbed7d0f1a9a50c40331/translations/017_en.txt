Reports of sand fall were received on two occasions, and upon reviewing them, since all reports were of the same nature, there is no need to record them here; as there were no other major incidents, they are omitted.

One item: A copy of a petition submitted to the magistrate's office of Lord Harada Seiuemon, as follows.

　　　　　　　Agatsuma District, Kōzuke Province
　　　　　　　　　　Ōmae Village
　　　　　　　　　　Kamanohara Village
　　　　　　　　　　Nakai Village
　　　　　　　　　　Haneo Village

From the eighth hour [approximately 2:00 PM] of the 6th day of the 7th month of this year, Mount Asama in Shinano Province began to erupt, and from around midday on the 8th, the rumbling became extremely severe, reducing both men and women to a state resembling unconsciousness. At that point, from the direction of Mount Asama, a black cloud-like mass, approximately two to three jō in height, consisting of volcanic sand, muddy water, rocks, and fire all mixed together, came rushing down all at once, instantly sweeping away houses, bamboo groves, and trees together. In Kamanohara Village, there was a temple called Jōrinji, a sub-temple of Tōeizan, and it appears that not only the main hall but also the head priest and attendants were swept away without exception, though this could not be confirmed. Therefore, the two of us hereby submit this petition to the honorable Ueno mountain [i.e., Kan'eiji]. End of report.

Arriving in Edo on the 18th day of the 7th month, Tenmei 3, year of the Rabbit.

　　　　　Ōmae Village
　　　　　　　Village Headman: Gorōshichi
