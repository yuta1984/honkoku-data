On the night of the 2nd day of the 10th month of Ansei 2nd year (November 11, 1855), past the Hour of the Four (around 10 PM), a great earthquake suddenly began to shake the land. Within a four-ri radius of Edo — to say nothing of the city itself — villages and settlements throughout the surrounding areas suffered widespread destruction. Afterward, fires broke out in various locations, causing severe damage to samurai residences, temples, townspeople's houses, storehouses, and other structures. The number of dead and injured was beyond counting. Though it was a natural disaster, people were overcome with feelings of sudden and desperate crisis; all took to sleeping outdoors, waiting for the night to give way to dawn. The following morning, at the Hour of the Five (around 8 AM), the fires throughout the various districts finally began to die down, and only then were people able to find some peace of mind.
