[mu] Within Yukibashi Gate, the residences of Lord Matsudaira, Governor of Kai, and Lord Ito, Junior Superintendent (Shuri-no-taifu), burned. The residence of Lord Nanbu, Governor of Mino, burned. The Satsuma domain residence burned. [Additional unspecified residences] suffered minor fire damage. Outside Yamashita Gate and Hibiya Gate, there was minor damage.

[u] From the neighborhood watch station (jishinban) in Sakurada Kenbō-machi, fire burned as far as the front row house of Lord Matsudaira, Governor of Hyōbu, in the same area. To the west, in Fushimi-machi, Kaji-machi, Kubo-machi, Tazaemon-machi, Bizen-machi, Inaba-machi, Izumi-machi, and Sazaemon-machi, there were many collapsed buildings. From Atagoashita through Nishinokubo, Iikura, Azabu, Ichibei-machi, and as far as Roppongi, there was no major damage. Outside Hanzō Gate, the various daimyo residences and townspeople's houses both suffered minor damage. Soto-Sakurada, the area within Akasaka Gate, Sanbe-zaka, and Sanno Shrine were undamaged. Kasumigaseki, Toranomon, and Shinshi Bridge suffered minor damage. However, this is merely a general account of conditions throughout Edo — the full details defy description by the brush.

On account of this great earthquake, collapsed buildings were extremely numerous throughout all of Edo; accordingly, the benevolent lord generously distributed relief rice to each [affected person]. Furthermore, for those who had lost their homes and storehouses and were in difficulty finding lodging, relief shelters were erected. Truly, this profound benevolence is a matter for the deepest gratitude.
