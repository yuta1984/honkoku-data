## Road Conditions Along the Highways and in the Surrounding Districts

As far as [our investigators] traveled: Kanagawa-juku suffered collapses in many places. Damage extended as far as Honmoku, Kanazawa, Kamakura, Enoshima, and Uraga.

▲ On the Nakasendō, the damage extends as far as Takasaki in Kōzuke Province. Along this road, sand erupted from the ground and clear water gushed up in many places. Between Warabi-juku and Ōmiya, no problems were observed.

▲ On the Kōshū Kaidō, the damage extends as far as Hachiōji; nothing particularly notable occurred along the road.

▲ On the Nikkō Kaidō, the damage extends as far as Utsunomiya; road collapses occurred as far back as the Sōka area.

▲ On the Mito Kaidō, the damage extends as far as Tsuchiura; road collapses occurred in many places. In Shimousa, shaking was particularly severe near Sakasai; the same was true around Gyōtoku and Funabashi. In the Kasai domain and the Nigōhan-ryō territory, collapses occurred in many places. Matsudo and Ichikawa, and the surrounding areas along that road, also suffered many collapses. In general, throughout both the countryside and town areas, high-ground locations suffered no particular damage. Low-lying areas, however, appear in most cases to have suffered more severe damage.
