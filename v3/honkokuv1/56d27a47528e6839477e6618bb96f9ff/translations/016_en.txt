## Extract from a Letter Received from Osaka

On the night of the 28th day of the 9th month, an earthquake struck, and the areas around Hiranobashi, Hōkimachi, and Sakaibashi were heavily damaged by collapses. Many people in this vicinity suffered losses. Furthermore, it is reported that the castle towns of Nagoya in Owari Province and Hamamatsu in Tōtōmi Province also suffered some fires. It is conveyed that these [earthquakes] occurred simultaneously [with the Edo earthquake].

---

## On the Edo Theatre District

Among the actors' residences that survived the fires in Edo's theatre district (Shibai-machi): the house of Bandō Shiuka's son Kichiya, Nakamura Fukusuke, Ichimura Uzaemon, Morita Kanya, Onoe Kikujirō, and two or three teahouse establishments.

---

## Damage Statistics

- Earthen storehouses (dozō) severely collapsed: more than 65,700; those slightly damaged: more than 87,200; those destroyed by fire: number unknown.

- Deaths: more than 132,400 persons; injured persons: more than 101,000; those attending surgical treatment: more than 31,200 persons.

- Deaths in Shin-Yoshiwara: more than 1,430 persons; injured: more than 2,300 persons.

---

## Memorial Services and Segaki Rites

- At Asakusa Kanzeon (Sensō-ji), a grand segaki rite was held from the 1st to the 4th day of the 11th month.
- Grand segaki rite at Zōjō-ji; memorial services (ekō) held at Honjo Ekō-in.
- Grand segaki rite held at Nishi Hongan-ji.
- Grand segaki rite held at Higashi Hongan-ji.
- Kiya Kyūbei [sponsored a] segaki rite at Ekō-in from the 19th day of the 10th month to the 21st day of the 11th month.
- Grand segaki rites were also held at various other religious sects and temples.
