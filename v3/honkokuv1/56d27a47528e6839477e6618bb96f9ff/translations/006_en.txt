From past the Hour of the Snake (around 10 AM) on November 10th, Ansei 1st year (1854), a great earthquake began to shake the land. Odawara post station in Sagami Province was largely destroyed. From there, a great tsunami struck Shimoda in Izu Province, causing many deaths. Along the Tokaido highway: Mishima post station was largely destroyed and half-burned; Numazu post station was largely destroyed; Hara post station suffered minor damage; Yoshiwara post station was largely destroyed and partially burned; Iwabuchi post station was largely destroyed and half-burned; Yui post station and Okitsu post station suffered minor damage; Ejiri post station burned east of the bridge; and Fuchu post station burned east of the public notice board (kosatsu). Hakone Mountain suffered minor collapses, but the fact that Hakone Gongen shrine remained unharmed was due to the divine virtue of the gods.
