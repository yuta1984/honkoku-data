[Right page (Poems)]

Guided by the teaching of the Water God, my life is saved —
To be counted among the six-tenths spared,
How joyous!

That we are protected
Even from heavenly disaster —
How grateful we are;
This is the blessing
Of our divine land.

Whoever carries these two poems
upon their person
shall come to no harm.
