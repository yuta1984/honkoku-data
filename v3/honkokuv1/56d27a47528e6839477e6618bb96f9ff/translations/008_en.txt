**[Zone Ni-i]** From South Tenmacho 2-chome (Nakabashi area) and South Kajimachi, through Kanō Shindō, Gorōbei-chō, Tatami-chō, Kita Kon'ya-chō, Shirauo Yashiki, the eastern part thereof, Suzuki-chō, Inaba-chō, Tokiwa-chō, Ōta Yashiki, Yanagi-chō, Gusoku-chō, Sumi-chō, Hon Zaimoku-chō, and onward to the Hatchō-me riverside — all burned. A few storehouses (dozō) survived in this stretch. △ From Kyōbashi to Shinbashi, both samurai residences and townspeople's houses suffered major damage. △ Tsukiji, Odawara-chō, Minami Iida-chō, and Moto-Yanagihara-chō — severe damage throughout, with many collapsed houses.△ The main hall of Nishi Hongan-ji was unharmed. Several buildings within the temple compound suffered major damage. Samurai residences in this vicinity were severely damaged, with many collapsed storehouses.

**[Zone Ni-ro]** In Shiba, Shibai-chō burned on both sides of the street. In Udagawa-chō, Mishima-chō, and Shinmei-chō, many houses collapsed and no storehouses survived. The Shinmei Shrine itself was undamaged. The northern section of the Zōjō-ji temple grounds suffered minor damage; there was also some slight damage within the temple itself. △ From the southern part of that area through Kanasugi to the Fuda no Tsuji intersection — no particularly severe damage.

**[Zone Ni-ha]** Teppōzu, Jukken-chō, and the residence of Lord Matsudaira Awaji-no-Kami were burned. Akashi-chō suffered an extremely large number of collapsed houses. Funamatsu-chō and the residence of Lord Hosokawa Noto-no-Kami were unharmed. Most other structures suffered major damage. △ Tsukuda-jima suffered major damage. △ From Hon-Hatchōbori northward: the main road, the Hatchōbori compound residences, Kayaba-chō, and the Yakushi-dō (medicine hall) were unharmed. Townspeople's houses in all directions suffered damage in various places, with many collapsed structures.

**[Zone Ni-ni]** Reigan-jima, Hama-chō, Yokkaichi-chō, Shirogane-chō, Ōkawabata-chō — everything up to the south side of the Shinbori riverbank burned. The secondary residence (naka-yashiki) of Lord Matsudaira Echizen-no-Kami survived. The remaining areas — Minato-chō and Nagasaki-chō — suffered major damage. △ To the north of that area: Kita-Shinbori, the residences of Lords Kuze, Izu, and Doi suffered slight damage; the residence of Lord Tayasu was undamaged; all others suffered major damage. △ Gyōtoku-gashi, Koami-chō, Tōkanbori, and the residences of Lords Sakai, Honda, Hayashi, Kishū (Wakayama domain), Mikawa, Inoue Kawachi-no-Kami, and Hori Izumo-no-Kami — the residences in this vicinity, along with the area from Ginza and Jinzaemon-chō through Ningyō-chō-dōri to Hatago-chō — all suffered major damage. One house burned in the middle of Hori-dome-chō. Across from it, Kobune-chō, Horie-chō, Hon-fune-chō, Setomono-chō, and Ise-chō — all completely and severely damaged. Further to the east along the Ōkawabata: the residences of Lords Andō, Shinjo, and Suganuma suffered major damage. On the back streets: the residences of Lords Akimoto, Makino Tōtōmi-no-Kami, and Nagai Hizen-no-Kami — the entire area in all directions, from Ōkawabata to Yokoyama-chō, suffered major damage. △ North of Nihonbashi: from Hon-chō to Imagawa-bashi, major damage. East of Jukken-dana: through Koku-chō-dōri, Ō-Tenma-chō, Ko-Tenma-chō, Hatago-chō, Abura-chō, Bakurō-chō, Yokoyama-chō, Ryōgoku Hiroko-ji, and onward to the embankment of Yanagihara — major damage throughout.
