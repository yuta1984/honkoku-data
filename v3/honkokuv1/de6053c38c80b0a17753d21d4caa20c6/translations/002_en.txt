**The Great Earthquake of the 14th Night of the 6th Month of Kaei 7th Year (1854), Year of the Tiger**
*Rumors from all directions*
(Additionally: fires broke out from the night of the 14th and continued until the evening of the 16th, with a total of 73 tremors recorded)

○ **Nanto (Nara), Yamato Province** — From around the Hour of the Ox, Eighth Watch (approx. 2:00 AM) on the morning of the 15th, earthquakes struck; a great tremor hit at the Fifth Hour (approx. 8:00 AM). Seven to eight parts in ten of all houses collapsed. Immediate deaths exceeded 300 people; the number of injured was beyond counting.

○ **Furuichi and Kizu areas (same province)** — At the same time, the shaking was particularly severe. The ground split and cracked, and muddy water gushed forth. Numerous deaths and injuries resulted.

　**Koriyama (same province)** — Shook at the same time, with a great tremor at the Eighth Watch of the Hour of the Ox (approx. 2:00 AM). Three parts in ten of houses in the town collapsed. Deaths numbered approximately 120–130; injuries were numerous. All nearby villages suffered the same devastation.
