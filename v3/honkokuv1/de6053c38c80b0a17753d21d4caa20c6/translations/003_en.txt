[The fire spread] as far as the Kuromon (Black Gate) area. Furthermore, from Shimanoharā to Ōkawara, the area became like a sea of mud due to the *hora* (the tsunami), and the resulting chaos and devastation defies description with brush and paper.

○ **Yokkaichi, Sei Province (Ise Province)** — Shaking began at the Fourth Hour (approx. 10:00 PM) on the night of the 14th, and a great earthquake struck at the Sixth Hour (approx. 6:00 AM) the following morning. Approximately 300 houses were shaken and collapsed. Fires broke out from the Fifth Hour (approx. 8:00 AM), burning approximately 400 houses. Deaths numbered approximately 140–150 people; persons unaccounted for exceeded 200. The entire surrounding area was in great turmoil — one can well imagine the extent of the damage.

○ **Ōshū (Ōmi Province)** — At the same time, at the Seventh Hour (approx. 4:00 AM), a great earthquake struck. From below Miidera temple in Ōtsu to the Obana River area, approximately 100 houses collapsed. Zeze Castle was damaged, and the stone lanterns at Ishiba fell into the lake (Lake Biwa). Shigaraki suffered extensive damage, with deaths and injuries reported. The areas of Kusatsu, Ishibe, Minakuchi, and Tsuchiyama were not especially severe compared to others, yet the damage was still worse than in Kyoto and Osaka.

**The Great Earthquake — Rumors from All Directions**
*(Inset note: Kyoto, Osaka, Sakai, Kawachi, Kii, Harima, Tamba, Tango — though with minor differences, all occurred simultaneously and in similar fashion)*

○ **Fukui, Echizen Province** — On the morning of the 13th day of the 6th month, at the Fifth Hour (approx. 8:00 AM), a fire broke out from Kajiya-machi (Blacksmiths' Quarter) and burned down the entire castle town without exception. Driven by strong winds that morning, the fire spread for approximately 200 *chō* (roughly 22 km) from Tsukumo Bridge, burning approximately 100 temples, including both Honganji branch temples, as well as approximately 10 villages in the surrounding area. The fire was finally extinguished at the Fourth Hour (approx. 10:00 PM) that night.

　Furthermore, from the Eighth Watch of the Hour of the Ox (approx. 2:00 AM) on the night of the 14th, a great earthquake struck, turning the farmlands into a sea of mud.
