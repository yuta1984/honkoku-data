As all this unfolded, smoke began to rise from houses in every direction, and flames burst forth, until in an instant the entire area became a sea of fire. The screams of those being burned alive were such that one could only think this must be what the hells of Avīci and the Scorching Flames truly looked like. It was pitiful and yet terrifying in equal measure, and the sight of it filled the onlooker with ever-deepening dread. There were a rare few who managed to break through the rooftops and escape,

yet others lay with half their bodies crushed beneath the rubble, vomiting blood and writhing in agony. Most pitiable of all was the sight of a child with one foot trapped and pinned, unable to pull free. Though the parents, frantic with a desperation bordering on madness, struggled to free the child, they were overwhelmed by the billowing smoke and the encroaching flames, and some were said to have been left with no choice but to abandon their child and flee. Others perished together with their loved ones. Then there was a very young woman who had become wedged between two pillars, with only her head protruding to the outside, calling out her husband's name and screaming for help.
