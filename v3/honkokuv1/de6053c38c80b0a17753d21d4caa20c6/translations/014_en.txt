Realizing this was to be their final parting in this world, he urged his wife to resign herself to her fate. Taking his travel sword, he recited the nenbutsu, and with it severed her black hair, weeping tears of blood, keeping it as a memento to bring back to their home province. Then there were those who, though not crushed by falling debris, found themselves trapped in the gaps between wreckage — like mice shut inside a box — with no way to escape, and many such people likely perished in the flames where they were. Their suffering must have surpassed even that of those crushed beneath the rubble. A family of three, barely managing— having rescued the mother, whose foot had been wrenched and injured, they then rushed back to save the father as well. Returning to where he had been, they found that his arm was pinned fast, and no matter what they tried, it could not be pulled free.
