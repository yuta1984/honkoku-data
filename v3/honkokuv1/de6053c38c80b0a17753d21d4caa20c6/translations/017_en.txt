There was no one to help them, and as the waters steadily rose, many were swallowed beneath the surface. Beyond this, the countless pitiful sights and the cries of anguish echoing in all directions were such that the heart seemed to dissolve at the mere witnessing of them — beyond the power of words to describe, beyond the reach of the brush to convey. Yet even amid such calamity, those who had taken refuge inside the temple hall suffered not so much as a scratch, and were spared from fire as well — this, surely, was entirely through the protection of the Buddha. "Oh, how grateful — Namu Amida Butsu! Oh, how sacred — Amida Butsu!" — and as these voices of prayer rose up, the dream suddenly broke. It was the night of the twenty-fourth day of the third month, and already the dawn of the twenty-fifth had arrived.
