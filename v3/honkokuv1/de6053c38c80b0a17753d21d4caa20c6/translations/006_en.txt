**The Great Earthquake Chaos** *(Styled after "Taikōki," Act Ten)*　Composed by Iryōsaku

**[Resigned Deliberation — "Throwing One's Head Back in Thought"]*

There is nothing left to regret.
A person who prepared ahead and took up living on a boat —
Sea and mountains are both equally beyond one's power to change.
Whether the mountain collapses or the tsunami comes,
what a frantic rush it all is!
"My goodness — right in the middle of giving birth, and they tell you to hurry up and come out!"
A mother, her tears falling like rain,
seeing that her child has been injured —
she rushes to his side, bowing her head low.
A person who has injured the bones of their neck.
