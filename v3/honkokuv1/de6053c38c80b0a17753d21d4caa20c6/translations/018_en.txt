There was no one to help them, and as the water gradually rose, many were swallowed up into the depths below.
Beyond this, the various pitiful scenes and the cries echoing in all directions were such that, upon witnessing them, one's heart seemed to vanish entirely — it is impossible to put into words, and the brush falls short of conveying it all. Yet even amid such devastation, those who had taken refuge in the temple hall suffered not even the slightest injury, and there had been no threat of fire — this was surely entirely due to the salvation of the Buddha. With voices crying out in gratitude, "How blessed! Namu Amida Butsu! How sacred! Amida Butsu!" — the dream suddenly broke. This occurred on the night of the twenty-fourth day of the third month, and it was already dawn of the twenty-fifth day.
