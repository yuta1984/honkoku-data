**Chronicle of Great Earthquakes in Our Land (Continued)**

　Meiō 3rd year, Year of the Tiger (*Kinoe-Tora*), 5th month, 7th day; same reign, 4th year, Year of the Rabbit (*Kinoto-U*), 8th month.
○ Reign of Emperor Go-Kashiwabara, the hundred-and-fifth sovereign, Eishō 7th year, Year of the Horse (*Kanoe-Uma*), 8th month, 7th day. Tremors continued without ceasing for seventy-five days thereafter. The stone torii of Tennō-ji in Settsu Province collapsed. On the 27th day of the same month, a tsunami struck Tōtōmi Province, forming the Imagire inlet.
○ Reign of Emperor Go-Nara, the hundred-and-sixth sovereign, Tenbun 2nd year, Year of the Dragon (*Hinoe-Tatsu*), 2nd month, 13th day — a star fell into the sea. Same reign, 13th year, an earthquake occurred.
○ Reign of Emperor Ōgimachi, the hundred-and-seventh sovereign, Tenshō 13th year, Year of the Rooster (*Kinoto-Tori*), 11th month, 29th day.
○ Reign of Emperor Go-Yōzei, the hundred-and-eighth sovereign, Bunroku 4th year, Year of the Sheep (*Kinoto-Hitsuji*), 7th month, 3rd day — a rain of hair fell. On the night of the 13th day of the same month, a great earthquake struck the Go-Kinai region, Ōmi, and Tanba together. At this time, the Great Buddha Hall in Kyoto collapsed.
○ Reign of Emperor Go-Mizunoo, the hundred-and-tenth sovereign, Kan'ei [*#1*] 7th year, Year of the Horse (*Kanoe-Uma*), 1st month, 7th day — a great earthquake struck Odawara in Sagami Province.

○ Reign of Emperor Go-Kōmyō, the hundred-and-eleventh sovereign, Keian 1st year, Year of the Rat (*Tsuchinoe-Ne*), 4th month, 22nd day. Same reign, 2nd year, a great earthquake struck Edo.
○ Reign of Emperor Go-Sai, the hundred-and-twelfth sovereign, Kanbun 2nd year, Year of the Tiger (*Mizunoe-Tora*), 5th month, 1st day.
○ Reign of Emperor Reigen, the hundred-and-thirteenth sovereign, Tenna 3rd year, Year of the Boar (*Mizunoto-I*), 4th month, 5th day — great earthquakes struck both Nikkō and Edo. In the 10th month of the same year, the province of Ōsumi became sea and land [*i.e., submerged*].
○ Reign of Emperor Higashiyama, the hundred-and-fourteenth sovereign, Genroku 16th year, Year of the Sheep (*Mizunoto-Hitsuji*), 11th month, 23rd day — a great earthquake in the Kantō region, tremors continuing for more than two hundred days. On the 4th day of the 10th month of Hōei 4th year, a great earthquake struck Osaka; it is said that the dead crushed beneath the rubble formed mountains. At this time, along the coastal inlets of Kii, Ise, Mikawa, and Tōtōmi, blue-green mud welled up, tsunamis arose, and the dead numbered in the tens of thousands. Mt. Fuji shook violently. Ash fell upon the surrounding provinces. At this time, Mt. Hōei was formed.
○ Reign of Emperor Nakamikado, the hundred-and-fifteenth sovereign, Kyōhō 17th year, 9th month, 26th day — Hizen, Nagasaki. Same reign, 11th year, Year of the Horse (*Hinoe-Uma*), 3rd month, 19th day — an earthquake struck Katsuyama in Echizen Province.
