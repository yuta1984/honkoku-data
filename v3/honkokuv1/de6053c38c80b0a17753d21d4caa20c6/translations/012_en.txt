Those living in this defiled world who can escape this calamity are few indeed. Even those who possess some degree of faith cannot easily escape the karmic bonds of their previous lives; not a few of those who have journeyed from afar to this place shall meet their end here. Nevertheless, the Original Vow of Amida Buddha extends universally to all, and every departed soul shall attain liberation and achieve Buddhahood without fail. You must harbour no doubt in your heart. Look — look — it is happening now—"

No sooner had the monk spoken these words than the heavens and earth shook violently, and a tremendous, thunderous roar resounded all around. It seemed as though even this very hall would be shaken apart and crumble. The narrator clung to the sleeve of the venerable monk's robe

and made their way out to the front of the hall. Peering through the gaps in the folds of the monk's three-piece vestments, the narrator looked out at the scene in all directions. When another great tremor struck, before their very eyes, the temple sub-buildings, private homes, and travellers' inns toppled one after another like a row of falling *shōgi* pieces, crumbling utterly — and as a result, people were crushed beneath the ruins—
