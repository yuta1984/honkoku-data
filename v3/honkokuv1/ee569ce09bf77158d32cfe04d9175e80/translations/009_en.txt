It goes without saying that one should pray to the gods of fire prevention, but since even a nearby fire can be escaped if the direction of the wind shifts, one ought always to pray to the God of Wind (*Fūjin-son*). It is also said that the deity Hitomaro offers protection because the name "Kakinomoto" (*kaki* = persimmon/fence, *moto* = base/origin) is a wordplay on "fire stopping" (*hi ga tomaru*). Furthermore, if signs of fire disaster appear in divination (*eki*) or physiognomy (*ninsō*), one should quickly perform prayers and rituals and burn some object among one's possessions as a charm.

1. Among the Twenty-Eight Lunar Mansions, there is a day called *Kō-shuku* (the Neck Mansion). It is said that if one begins construction on this day, some misfortune will occur within ten days. It is for this reason that fires frequently break out from construction site huts. There are those who observe the auspiciousness or inauspiciousness of the Eight Mansions (*hachi-shuku*) on a daily basis. The matter of the Eight Mansions shall be set forth in due course.

1. Fires spreading from neighboring properties (*ruishō*) cannot be helped, but one ought to be especially careful about fires originating from one's own home (*jika*). This is the single most important matter of daily mindfulness. First and foremost, one must not stamp out tobacco embers on the road; they should be extinguished using a stone or a roof tile.

1. Every year, on the twenty-fourth day of the first month and the twelfth month, one should strictly abstain from alcohol. From the first quarter of the Hour of the Rabbit (around 6 a.m.) to the first quarter of the Hour of the Rooster (around 6 p.m.), no alcohol should be used—not even as a cooking ingredient (*sakajio*)—and even if guests arrive, one should firmly decline to serve it. This is a charm to prevent fires from breaking out in one's own home. Though this is something the people of the world already know, it is recorded here nonetheless.
