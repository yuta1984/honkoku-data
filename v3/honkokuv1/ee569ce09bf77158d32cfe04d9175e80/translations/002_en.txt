As the saying goes, "The plan for a year lies in spring; the plan for a day lies in the morning." Thus, at the beginning of spring one should divine the fortunes of the coming year, and should also seek consultation from a reputable physiognomist. Since the seven misfortunes are said to manifest in one's facial complexion and features, there is nothing better than having one's face read from time to time. When I was in the prime of my life, from the end of the Kansei era through the spring of the third year of Bunka (the Year of the Tiger), I had not caused a single accidental fire and was living in a state of peaceful ease. During this time I had my face read by a physiognomist, who said: "Before long you shall undertake a journey. Signs of fire disaster are also visible, but you shall escape it." I paid little heed to these words.
