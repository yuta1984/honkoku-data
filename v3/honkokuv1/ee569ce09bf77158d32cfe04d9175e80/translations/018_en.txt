**[Diagram with explanatory text]**

The years of *tekisetsu* (的殺, "personal deadly direction") are as shown in the figures. Of the four [critical years], one is the person's own birth year; opposite it is the seventh [year], to the left is the fourth [year], and to the right is the tenth [year]. These four years are greatly inauspicious in all matters. It is a serious error to call the seventh year auspicious for married couples.

**Record of Major Fires in Edo, with the Twenty-Eight Lunar Mansions and the Five Phases**

1. **The 3rd year of Meireki, the year Hinoto-Tori (丁酉, 1657)**
[Lunar mansion: *Shi* (觜宿, "Turtle Beak"); Day: Fire (*kayō*); Nature of fire: *Okuyama no hi* ("fire of the deep mountain")]

From the Hour of the Dragon (*tatsu no koku*) on the 18th day of the 1st month to the 20th day:

The fire originated at Hongō Maruyama and spread as far as the Fukagawa seaside and the vicinity of Ushijima. On the 19th day at the Hour of the Snake (*mi no koku*), another fire broke out at Koishikawa Shin-Takajō-machi. Furthermore, at the Hour of the Rooster (*tori no koku*) on the same day, a third fire broke out at Kōjimachi — from these two simultaneous outbreaks, the areas around Sannō, both inside and outside the *o-kuruwa* (the castle's outer enclosure), the residences of great and lesser lords (*daimyō* and *shōmyō*), and all the townspeople's districts of Edo without exception, as far as the Shiba seaside, were consumed. Approximately 9,000 or more storehouses (*kura*) were burned and destroyed. The fire was extinguished after three days and three nights. Full details are recorded in *Musashi Abumi* (武蔵鐙).

Furthermore: from the 11th month of the preceding year of the Monkey (*申*) to the 1st month of the year of the Rooster (*酉*), there had been a drought (*kanbatsu*) lasting more than eighty days, and on the 21st day of that same month a heavy snowfall occurred.
