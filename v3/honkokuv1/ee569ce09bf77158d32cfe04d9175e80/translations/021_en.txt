**Record of Major Fires in Edo, with the Twenty-Eight Lunar Mansions and the Five Phases (continued)**

[Continuing from the previous entry: Bunka 3, the year Hinoe-Tora (丙寅, 1806)]
　　Fire spread from Takanawa Ushimachi as far as Asakusa; emergency shelters (*osukui-goya*) were erected at various locations. The fire also reached Shiba Shinmonzen Daichi (a substitute residential area) and Shimbori-bata.

1. **The 8th year of Bunka (same era), the year Kanoto-Hitsuji (辛未, 1811)** — 11th day of the 2nd month, lower portion of the Hour of the Sheep (c. 2–3 p.m.)
[Lunar mansion: *Bi* (尾宿, "Tail"); Phase: Fire (*hi*)] — *Michi no Tsuchi* ("Earth of the Road")

Fire spread from Yotsuya Tanima-chō as far as Shiba Akabane-bashi; the fire also reached Nagaimachi Daichi (a substitute residential area) and Akasaka Kiribatake.

2. **The 1st year of Bunsei, the year Tsuchinoe-Tora (戊寅, 1818)** — 17th day of the 10th month
[Lunar mansion: *Shitsu* (室宿, "Encampment"); Phase: Wood (*ki*)] — *Miyama no Tsuchi* ("Earth of the Deep Mountains")

Fire spread from Asakusa Yamanoshuku as far as Honjo Ōgibashi.

3. **The 2nd year of Bunsei (same era), the year Tsuchinoto-U (己卯, 1819)** — 9th day of the 2nd month
[Lunar mansion: *Heki* (壁宿, "Wall"); Phase: Earth (*tsuchi*)] — same earth type (*Miyama no Tsuchi*, "Earth of the Deep Mountains")

Fire spread from Kyōbashi Shin-Sakanamachi through the Owari-chō area as far as Kobikichō; on the last day of the same month, from Ryōgaechō as far as Muromachi and Ishichō. On the 25th day of the 12th month, from the Satake domain lord's residence in Shitaya as far as Okura-mae (the rice warehouses).

4. **The 3rd year of Bunsei (same era), the year Kanoe-Tatsu (庚辰, 1820)** — 29th day of the 12th month
[Lunar mansion: *Kei* (奎宿, "Legs"); Phase: Wood (*ki*)] — *Shiroganē no Kin* ("Metal of Silver")

Fire spread from Shiroganechō as far as the Honchō area.

5. **The 6th year of Bunsei (same era), the year Mizunoto-Hitsuji (癸未, 1823)** — 12th day of the 1st month
[Lunar mansion: *Bō* (昴宿, "Hairy Head/Pleiades"); Phase: Earth (*tsuchi*)] — *Yanagi no Ki* ("Wood of the Willow")

Fire spread from Azabu Furukawa as far as Yatsuyama; flying embers caused the fire to spread further from Shinagawa Honjuku as far as Samejima (Samejū).
　　— Also: on the 25th day of the 12th month of the same year, fire spread from Kōjimachi as far as the Sannō Shrine area, Akasaka, and Aoyama.
