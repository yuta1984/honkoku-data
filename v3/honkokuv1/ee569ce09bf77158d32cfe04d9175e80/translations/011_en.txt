In the 1st, 4th, 7th, and 10th months: the day of the Boar (*i no hi*); in the 2nd, 5th, 8th, and 11th months: the day of the Tiger (*tora no hi*); in the 3rd, 6th, 9th, and 12th months: the day of the Horse (*uma no hi*). (These are days to be avoided at the start of house construction.)

On the above days, if one ties "wart knots" (*ibo-yubi*) in rope, it is said that one "knots fire into" the building—that is, invites fire upon it. The burning of even a fine storehouse (*dozō*) may be attributed to such examples.

　　　　Days inauspicious for ridgepole-raising (*munage*)

1st and 2nd months: day of the Rabbit; 3rd and 4th months: day of the Snake; 5th and 6th months: day of the Goat; 7th and 8th months: day of the Rooster; 9th and 10th months: day of the Boar; 11th and 12th months: day of the Ox.

On days marked "*Tenka*" (Heavenly Fire) in the almanac, construction work at heights should be avoided.

　　　　Two great inauspicious days each month — said to derive from a teaching of Mañjuśrī Bodhisattva (*Monju Bosatsu*).

1st month: 7th, 20th　　2nd month: 2nd, 9th　　3rd month: 21st, 23rd　　4th month: 18th, 25th　　5th month: 6th, 23rd　　6th month: 17th, 23rd　　7th month: 8th, 23rd　　8th month: 18th, 25th　　9th month: 3rd, 22nd　　10th month: 1st, 3rd　　11th month: 2nd, 20th　　12th month: 9th, 23rd

On these days, all affairs are greatly inauspicious, and these are such evil days that even offering prayers will bring about calamity rather than relief.

　　　　Daily *Jippō-fusagari* (Blockage of the Ten Directions)

Direction of the Rabbit—east (day 1); Between Dragon and Snake—ESE (day 2); Direction of the Horse—south (day 3); Between Goat and Monkey—SSW (day 4); Direction of the Rooster—west (day 5); Between Dog and Boar—WNW (day 6);
Direction of the Rat—north (day 7); Between Ox and Tiger—NNE (day 8); Heaven (day 9)—[do not climb to high places; rooftop construction should be avoided]; Earth (day 10)—[do not enter into the ground; do not disturb the earth].

For example, if on the first day of the month one wishes to travel east, but that direction is inauspicious, one should either take a detour, or alternatively walk about one hundred paces to the west, then turn back and proceed in the direction of the Rabbit (east)—and this will suffice.
