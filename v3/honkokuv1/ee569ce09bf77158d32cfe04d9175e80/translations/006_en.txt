【Left folio】
　There was an elder known as Old Man Giyu, who was exceedingly well-versed in astronomy. Wishing to learn and memorize the configurations of the Twenty-Eight Lunar Mansions and the Seven Luminaries, I followed this old man and went out into the garden every night to receive his instruction—yet I could not easily attain mastery of the subject. Even so, I came to understand the cardinal directions of the world, to be able to find my bearings even when traveling to distant provinces, and to discern the time of night. The elder would often say: "Born into this age of great peace, there are many among the people who sleep soundly, wear fine clothing, eat delicious food, and pursue pleasure to its fullest extent. In particular, in recent times, the construction of residences has become extraordinarily splendid and ornate..."
