**Record of Major Fires in Edo, with the Twenty-Eight Lunar Mansions and the Five Phases (continued)**

1. **The 8th year of Kanbun, the year Tsuchinoe-Saru (戊申, 1668)**
Great Fire of Edo. [Lunar mansion: *Tei* (氐宿, "Root"); Phase: Fire (*hi*)] — *Ōsawa no Tsuchi* ("Earth of the Great Marsh")

2. **The 2nd year of Tenna, the year Mizunoe-Inu (壬戌, 1682)**
Great Fire of Edo. [Lunar mansion: *I* (胃宿, "Stomach"); Phase: Water (*mizu*)] — *Taikai no Mizu* ("Water of the Great Ocean")

3. **The 11th year of Genroku, the year Tsuchinoe-Tora (戊寅, 1698)**
Great Fire of Edo. [Lunar mansion: *Shin* (心宿, "Heart"); Phase: Fire (*hi*)] — *Miyama no Tsuchi* ("Earth of the Deep Mountain")

4. **The 16th year of Genroku, the year Mizunoto-Hitsuji (癸未, 1703)** — 29th day of the 11th month
[Lunar mansion: *Jo* (女宿, "Girl"); Phase: Wood (*ki*)] — *Yanagi no Ki* ("Wood of the Willow")

Fire spread from Ushigome to Toranomon Gate, which was destroyed.

5. **The 2nd year of Kyōhō, the year Hinoto-Tori (丁酉, 1717)** — 21st day of the 1st month
[Lunar mansion: *Ryū* (柳宿, "Willow"); Phase: Wood (*ki*)] — *Okuyama no Hi* ("Fire of the Deep Mountain")

Fire spread from Hongō to the Tsukiji seaside.

6. **The 9th year of Kyōhō, the year Kinoe-Tatsu (甲辰, 1724)** — 3rd month
[Lunar mansion: *Tei* (氐宿, "Root"); Phase: Fire (*hi*)] — *Tōmyō no Hi* ("Fire of the Votive Lamp")

Shibaguchi Gate was destroyed by fire.

7. **The 10th year of Kyōhō, the year Kinoto-Mi (乙巳, 1725)** — 2nd month
[Lunar mansion: *Bō* (房宿, "Room"); Phase: Water (*mizu*)] — same fire type (*Tōmyō no Hi*, "Fire of the Votive Lamp")

Fire spread from Azabu Toriizaka to Shinagawa.

8. **The 3rd year of Enkyō, the year Hinoe-Tora (丙寅, 1746)** — [from the last day of the 2nd month to the 1st day of the 3rd month]
[Lunar mansion: *Sei* (星宿, "Star"); Phase: Fire (*hi*)] — *Irori no Hi* ("Fire of the Hearth")

Fire spread from Honjo and also from Tsukiji as far as Senju.
