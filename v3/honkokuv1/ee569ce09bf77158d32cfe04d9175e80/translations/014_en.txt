Comic Verse (*Kyōka*) on Fire Safety　　　　*Ko* [Old]　Tatekawa Enba

Even the "Chinese folk" of the kitchen — morning and evening, do not be negligent with your fire here in the Land of the Rising Sun (*Hinomoto*).

1. On knowing the scale of a fire disaster. This is a method that people generally know well, but I record it here for reference.

　Left hand fingers:　Thumb = Fire　　Index finger = Wind　　Middle finger = Water
　　　　　　　　　　[1st, 4th, 7th, 10th months　　[2nd, 5th, 8th, 11th months　　[3rd, 6th, 9th, 12th months
　　　　　　　　　　 Tiger, Snake, Monkey, Boar]　　Rabbit, Horse, Rooster, Rat]　　Dragon, Sheep, Dog, Ox]

The method of counting is as follows: designate the thumb as the 1st month, and count through the three fingers up to the 12th month in order. If within the 1st month, begin counting from the index finger starting from the 1st day, the 2nd day, and so on; wherever the count lands — if it falls on "Fire," it will be a great fire; if on "Wind," a moderate fire; if on "Water," a small fire. Although the middle finger corresponds to Water, when the count of three falls upon it, it becomes the number of Fire. Hence fires of the magnitude of the Meireki 3rd year, Enkyō 3rd year, and Bunka 3rd year occurred. Furthermore, the reason there are great fires in the 3rd month is that the 1st month being the month of the Tiger (Wood) brings much wind, and the 3rd month, being the month of the Dragon (Earth), is favorable to fire. On the 21st day of the 3rd month of the current Bunsei 12th year, the Ox year, although the count fell on Water and yet a great fire occurred, it was a Fire year, and moreover the fire broke out during the middle of the Snake hour (around 10 a.m.), when fire is at its most powerful. Two is the number of Metal, and Fire overcomes Metal. The 2nd month is the month of the Rabbit (Wood), and Wood generates Fire — hence there may be many instances of accidental fire.
