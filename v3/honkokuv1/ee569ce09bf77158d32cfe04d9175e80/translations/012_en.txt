[Diagram of Kōjin Directional Positions]　　The *Kōjin* listed in the almanac is called the "*Ni-shi-roku Kōjin*" (Kōjin of 2, 4, and 6), whose curse is relatively light. Within this category there are the *Ten-Kōjin* (Heavenly Kōjin) and *Chi-Kōjin* (Earthly Kōjin), but above all, the curses of the *Daikōjin* (Great Kōjin) and *Hime-Kōjin* (Princess Kōjin) are such that they can cost one's life and bring ruin upon one's household — and thus are to be greatly, greatly feared.

Beyond these, there are also the greatly inauspicious directions of the *Honmei-tekisetsu* (one's personal destructive direction) and the *Kōjin-fusagari* (Kōjin Blockage) found in the almanac, as well as the greatly inauspicious directions associated with the wandering deity's schedule of five days at each position. Methods for *Kata-tagae* (direction-changing avoidance) will be described in due course.
