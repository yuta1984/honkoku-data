*(Marginal note: Established in the 8th month of the 3rd year of Meireki [1657])*

**Record of Dates When the Yoshiwara Burned**

1. The 4th year of Enpō, the year Hinoe-Tatsu (丙辰, 1676) — 7th day of the 12th month
2. The 5th year of Meiwa, the year Tsuchinoe-Ne (戊子, 1768) — 6th day of the 4th month
3. The 8th year of Meiwa, the year Kanoto-U (辛卯, 1771) — 23rd day of the 4th month, during the Hour of the Tiger (c. 3–5 a.m.)
4. The 9th year of Meiwa (same era), the year Mizunoe-Tatsu (壬辰, 1772) — 29th day of the 2nd month
    *(Marginal note: Fire originated at Gyōninzaka, Meguro)*
5. The 1st year of Tenmei, the year Kanoto-Ushi (辛丑, 1781) — last day of the 8th month, during the Hour of the Ox (c. 1–3 a.m.)
    *(Marginal note: Fire originated at Edo-chō Nichōme, at the establishment called "Kataya." It was said to be the vengeful spirit of Sumaginu.)*
6. The 4th year of Tenmei (same era), the year Kinoe-Tatsu (甲辰, 1784) — 26th day of the 4th month, during the Hour of the Ox (c. 1–3 a.m.)
7. The 7th year of Tenmei (same era), the year Hinoto-Hitsuji (丁未, 1787) — 9th day of the 11th month
8. The 6th year of Kansei, the year Kinoe-Tora (甲寅, 1794) — 2nd day of the 4th month, during the Hour of the Boar (c. 9–11 p.m.)
9. The 11th year of Kansei (same era), the year Tsuchinoto-Hitsuji (己未, 1799) — 7th day of the 3rd month
    *(Marginal note: Fire leapt from Ryūsenji-machi to Kyōmachi)*
10. The 9th year of Bunka, the year Mizunoe-Saru (壬申, 1812) — 21st day of the 11th month
    *(Marginal note: Fire leapt from the eta/hinin (outcast) huts to Shinmachi)*
11. The 13th year of Bunka, the year Hinoe-Ne (丙子, 1816) — 3rd day of the 5th month, during the Hour of the Monkey (c. 3–5 p.m.)
