This solid matter was taken, alcohol was poured over it, and after filtration, the residue was dried to a solid state and weighed, yielding seventy-two sen and seven rin. Therefore, the amount dissolved in the alcohol was twenty-one sen, nine fun, and three rin; this is designated as fraction ㋑.

Next, distilled water was taken and poured over the portion that had not dissolved in the alcohol. This was shaken repeatedly and then filtered, and the residue remaining on the filter paper was dried to a solid state. Its weight was five sen, two fun, and six rin. Therefore, the amount dissolved in the distilled water was sixty-six sen, eight fun, and one rin; this is designated as fraction ㋺.

Furthermore, a large additional amount of distilled water was poured over this residue, which was then boiled over a flame for approximately half an hour. After that, it was filtered and the residue was collected; its weight was one sen, five fun, and six rin. This is designated as fraction ㋩. Therefore, the amount dissolved in this distilled water was three sen and seven fun; this is designated as fraction ㊁.

The four fractions ㋑, ㋺, ㋩, and ㊁ were tested as follows.

Fraction ㋑ was a colorless liquid, from which it was determined that no iron content was present. Next, in order to test the acidity of the salts contained in this liquid, it was evaporated to dryness, sulfuric acid was added, and it was then gently heated over a low flame, upon which white fumes were produced. From this, it was determined to be a hydrochloride salt. Furthermore, by applying stronger heat, the free sulfuric acid within was driven off, and the residue was converted into a sulfate salt. When water was then poured over it, an immediate precipitate formed.
