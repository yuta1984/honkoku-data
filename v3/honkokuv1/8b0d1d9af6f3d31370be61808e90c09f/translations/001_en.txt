【Upper Prose Text】

The great whale moistens seven villages around it,
while the catfish shakes the ground for four leagues in every direction, setting the hands of workers of all trades in motion, letting them earn to their hearts' content.
Furthermore, the wheel of money turns well,
scattering the blossoms of the "wealth plant," causing the shoots of fresh grass to flourish,
mixing together the poor and the rich, and delivering righteous judgment — or so they say.

〽 "Give us one good shake!"

〽 "Come quickly, come quickly! I'm planning to put it on display as a show. Moor it here, moor it here — come on!"

〽 "To show it to people, the bigger the better — but the one during the last earthquake was too big."
