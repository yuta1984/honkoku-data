Not fleeing when one should is lack of wisdom — regard those without wisdom as already among the dead.

The pledged goods inside a storehouse may burn — inside an underground storehouse [*anagura*], nothing burns.

Even if you brace a thousand logs [to prop up a structure] — it will not serve you for a hundred days [i.e., it is no long-term solution].

There are no mirror-stand shelves [they have been destroyed] — use a comb box as your mirror stand.

Most merchandise will be lost/damaged — treat only undamaged goods as merchandise.

Earthquakes startle people day after day — the devout cannot sleep at night [out of anxiety and prayer].

---

If you do not flee when things begin to move — even if you cry and scream after being buried under rubble,

there is still no benefit in sake — therefore, do not drink sake to the point of intoxication.

Do not carry angular [sharp-cornered] objects on your back — spotting a gap and fleeing through it is the wiser course.

Though the embankment of Hatchō [Yoshiwara's *Nakanochō*] is wide — those who do not purchase the services of the women's quarter do not go there.

Though the pleasure quarter of idleness is enjoyable — those without a disposition for dissipation do not play there.

Respect the landlord as one would a father or mother — love the tenant as one would a younger sibling or child.
