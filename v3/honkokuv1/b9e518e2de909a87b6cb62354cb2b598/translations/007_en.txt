If one does not associate with companions whose homes have been damaged — who then will extinguish the fires raging in all directions?

Do not forget the hardship of sleeping outdoors — never abandon frugality and thrift.

Therefore, those who would be virtuous for all generations to come — must first reflect deeply upon this earthquake.

This is the beginning of (building) a good name and reputation — do not let (your home or property) be lost to fire until the very end of your life.

---

"Jishin-gokyo" (Post-Earthquake Teachings) — *End*

Written on the 2nd day of the 10th month of the 2nd year of Ansei (1855), the Year of the Wood Rabbit, by Daishindō.
