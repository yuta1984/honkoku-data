## Post-Earthquake Teachings

*Post-Earthquake Teachings*, authored by the Old Man of Kyūōsai, from the Southern Provinces

A tall house is not noble by virtue of its height — regard the single-story house as noble.

Heavy-bottomed [those slow to flee] are hard to save — regard making one's escape as being saved.

Fire is a disaster for one's entire life — if you do not extinguish it, you will burn together with it.

Earthquakes are a "manzairaku" [an eternal, ever-present thing] — they will accompany you with their shaking until the end of your life.

Without searching for a shop stall, you will certainly find none — those without a shop should set up an open-air stall in the fields.
