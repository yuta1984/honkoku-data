Upon hearing these details, Lord Genkan fell into lovesick torment over a lady he had never yet seen. Unable to bear his suffering any longer, he prayed to the heavens: "Please grant me this wish — or else take my life." So deeply did he make this vow. Mercifully, a sign of divine blessing (rishō) was granted: Lord Genkan was given the province of Shimotsuke, and he promptly became the Governor of Shimotsuke. Word came that he had taken up his post in the province.

Now, setting that aside — the Goman Chōja of Shimotsuke Province had one daughter. One evening, as she was calmly reciting sutras and clearing her mind in meditation, something wondrous occurred: a single童子 (divine boy) came gliding in and spoke thus: "Listen, princess — I am the Man of the Moon Laurel (Katsura-otoko). Having certain reasons and intentions, I wish to borrow your womb so that I may be born into the human world and save all sentient beings lost in the cycle of delusion. Therefore, I ask that you lend me your womb."

The princess heard this and replied: "What a strange thing to say. Nevertheless, I have not yet taken a husband. If I were to become with child, word would reach my father and mother, and I know not what wretched fate I might suffer. I cannot grant this wish." Upon hearing her words, the童子 replied: "What you say is perfectly understandable, but if my wish is granted and it becomes the cause for the salvation of all sentient beings (shujō saido), no ill fortune shall befall you in the future —"
