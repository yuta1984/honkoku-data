The Lady of the North together with others, as a mark of mourning for the magistrate's spirit, had ninety-nine golden Buddha statues made. Then, proceeding to Kiyomigaseki, they brought an end to the Shaken elder's line, performed a memorial service in the Chinese style for the magistrate, erected eighty-eight thousand sotoba (memorial grave markers), and offered sixty thousand, three hundred and eighty-four golden images in memorial service — such acts of piety were beyond all description. That they achieved Buddhahood through the original vow of Amida Buddha was truly a thing of profound gratitude. With the vow that all members of the family and their dependents would become deities and protect all living beings, the festival held on the third day of the third month for Lord Tayu is called the "Sangatsu-e" (Third Month Assembly). The provincial lord manifested as the Sōsha Daimyōjin, and the Lady of the North appeared as the Asama Daibosatsu (Great Bodhisattva of Asama). This, it is said, is the founding legend of Konohanasakuya-hime. The princess became the deity of Yamomiya. Each year, on the first Day of the Monkey in the fourth month, the fifteenth day of the ninth month, and the first Day of the Monkey in the eleventh month — forty-five festivals in all throughout the year. Furthermore, the princess was raised until the age of thirteen, and the couple of the Yoman-chōja (forty-thousand elder) also ascended, planted a thousand-year-old pine at Miho no Ura, and became the Goshasha Daimyōjin (Five-Shrine Great Illuminating Deities).
