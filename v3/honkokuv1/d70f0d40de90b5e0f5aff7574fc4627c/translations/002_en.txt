Now, inquiring into the true nature (honji) of the Great Bodhisattva Asama, who is enshrined at Shizugatake in Suruga Province: in what age might this have taken place? In those days, in the flowering capital (Kyoto), there resided a gentleman of noble lineage known as Lord Genkan of the Minamoto clan. He was twenty-three years of age, and his appearance and bearing were so superior to all others that there was no one, whether of high or low station, who did not adore him. However, he had not yet taken a wife (a "Northern Lady"). It was at just such a time that a person who had traveled up to the capital from Shimotsuke Province came and said: "In our province there is a man known as the Goman Chōja (the Millionaire of Five Myriads), a most wealthy and distinguished person, who has one daughter in his care. This princess surpasses all in the world in her beauty and grace — she plays among the moonlight, delights in flowers, and harmonizes her koto with the wind blowing through the sky..."
