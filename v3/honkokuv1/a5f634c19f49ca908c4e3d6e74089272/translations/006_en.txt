(Verse 19)
The people in desperate struggle and hardship
Come now in countless numbers—
To hear of it is pitiful, how wretched they are.

(Verse 20)
The relief funds suddenly granted (to them)—
Looking up to the heavens, thinking of their own lives,
Sorrow and joy came upon them all at once.

Meiji 29 [1896], Saitama Prefecture, Minami-Saitama District, Wado Village, No. 15
Printed and published on July 9th and July 12th by commoner Kawashima Okujirō
