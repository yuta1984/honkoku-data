(Verse 13)
Oh, how pitiful is this great tsunami—
From the Iwate Prefectural Office, a telegraph message
Was sent to notify the officials in Tokyo.

(Verse 14)
The officials and ministers were all astonished,
And immediately orders for an inspection were issued.
An overview investigation was conducted—how sorrowful it was.

(Verse 15)
The government, recognizing the great importance of the matter,
Had officials gather the bodies of the tens of thousands of dead,
And piled them into burial mounds to hold memorial services.

(Verse 16)
Through the unblemished imperial grace of the officials,
From both His Majesty the Emperor and Her Majesty the Empress,
Ten thousand yen in relief funds was granted.

(Verse 17)
Minister Inoue contributed one hundred and fifty yen,
Many other ministers contributed one hundred yen each,
Such were the charitable donations from the various officials.

(Verse 18)
Quickly, for the people suffering hardship,
Relief shelters were erected,
And doctors diligently provided medicine and medical care.
