[Verses describing the Great Tsunami — likely in a popular ballad style]

Verse 1:
Our nation, so civilized and prosperous —
how pitiful, this tsunami that has struck us now,
Alas, the fifteenth day of June.

Verse 2:
Strangely, out at sea, something terrifying —
waves seven *jō* high come surging in:
Is it a great whale? No — a great tsunami!

Verse 3:
In the moment of seeing, hearing, speaking —
parents and children, brothers and sisters, all separated beyond recognition.
How dreadful, how fearful — the great tsunami!

Verse 4:
The surging tsunami [swept through] Morioka-shi [or: the Morioka record / various listed places] —
Kozake, Shiku-ura, Maineba-ji [place names along the coast] —
crushed beneath the tsunami, left without form.

Verse 5:
Ishihama, Sakihama — [the waves reached] even to the waist;
Nakai, Ōtako — great chaos and panic;
Hiraiso, Nagaiso, Akedō village.

Verse 6:
Beyond them: Sugishita, Koizumi,
Kesaiso, Ōshima, Tsuru-no-ura —
twenty-five hamlets and villages, large and small.
