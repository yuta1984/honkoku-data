Fire is a disaster of the gravest weight among all calamities, and therefore one must at all times, day and night, take every possible care and precaution. It is a matter that disturbs the realm and the nation at large, and yet it is also something that concerns both others and oneself personally. Therefore, this single volume should be read aloud to all members of the household two or three times each month, so that they may fully understand it; it should also be taught to others; and one must never be negligent in ordinary times. When a fire breaks out, one must with particular sincerity and speed work to suppress it, making no distinction whatsoever between those close to oneself and those more distant.

The merit of such good deeds shall be without limit. Should a great fire occur, how many tens of thousands of people would grieve, and how immeasurable would be the loss of treasures and property. Therefore, when the word "fire" is spoken, one must fight to prevent it with genuine resolve — as though extinguishing a flame that has caught upon one's own hair. If one does so, it is beyond all doubt that this will reach the gods (*shinmei*) and that one will receive the aid of Heaven (*tendō*).
