　　　　　A Collection of Fire Prevention Tips (Kairoku Yōjin-shū)

Article 1: Water is the means by which fire is suppressed. Therefore, in the event of a fire, if there is a path near a water source, one should break down fences and walls if necessary, quickly draw water, and douse the flames to extinguish them. Always be mindful to maintain a constant supply of water, and make it a habit — day and night — to remind others to be vigilant about fire prevention. This is of the utmost importance.
