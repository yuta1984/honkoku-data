**Things to Be Mindful of in Ordinary Times**

1. A tinderbox (*hiuchibako*) should be kept together with *tsukegi* (fire-starting kindling sticks) and candles, and placed in every room where people sleep.

2. Firewood and any other materials that are liable to catch fire must not be stored anywhere around the perimeter of a storehouse (*kura*).

3. The interior of the storehouse should be inspected frequently and kept tidy; the *nerituchi* (kneaded clay plastering) around the door fittings [*note: there is worm damage at this point in the original*] should also be checked from time to time and maintained in good condition.

4. Water for general use and rainwater (*tensui*) should be stored separately. In particular, during spring, water should be drawn and kept inside the storehouse on both the upper and lower floors. Care should be taken to ensure that water for daily use and water in the hand-washing buckets (*chōzu-oke*) never runs dry, day or night.

5. For the elderly, wives, children, and others who should evacuate early, a change of clothing appropriate to each person's station and physique, and not unseemly in appearance, should be prepared and kept stacked and ready.
