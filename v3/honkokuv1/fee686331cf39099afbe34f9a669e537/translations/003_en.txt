To extinguish flying embers as well, if you throw water with a rice bowl, the water will reach exactly where you intend, and the fire will go out. First and foremost, one must focus on suppressing and extinguishing the fire at its source. One must never delay firefighting efforts by being preoccupied with household belongings. If the source of the fire is extinguished, household goods will naturally be preserved. When one thoroughly understands this principle and takes careful, skillful measures to prevent the fire's spread, others will be moved to help, and above all, the gods will surely lend their assistance.

Article 3: When a fire breaks out, it is difficult to predict from which direction (east or west) the wind will blow. Wind changes direction as it blows. Furthermore, a fire can break out at any time. Therefore, when the wind is strong, without any negligence, fill empty buckets, pots, cauldrons, and standing baths with water in advance, and in addition, be mindful of gathering and securing your household belongings.
