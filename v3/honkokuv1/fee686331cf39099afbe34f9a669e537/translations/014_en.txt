1. For those who have no place to dig a hole in the ground and no storehouse to rely upon: change into the best clothes available to you, carry money appropriate to your station and means, and evacuate carrying the chests and leather-covered wicker trunks that have been compactly packed as described in the preceding section. If one's appearance is slovenly and unkempt, one will be unable to conduct necessary business at one's destination. Furthermore, having gold coins but no small copper cash will cause hardship when it comes to paying for meals and other minor everyday needs. Since nothing is preserved as reliably as what one carries on one's own person, those of modest means should always bear this in mind.

1. For those who intend to carry out household goods using a wheeled long chest (*kuruma nagamochi*): pack important items compactly into it in advance, and in the event of a sudden fire, pull it out quickly and with ease. Do not give in to greed by piling many more items on top of the chest...
