In general, applying water to a burn wound causes considerably greater pain. For the pain of a burn wound, holding that spot near a flame again will stop the pain. Such knowledge should be learned in ordinary times and also taught to others.

One item: For those who come to assist with firefighting but are strangers whose faces are unknown, put those people to work on top of the roof or in distributing water.

One item: Even in the event of a fire breaking out deep in the night, or any other urgent matter, one must not carelessly open the door simply because someone is knocking on it. This is because it is well known that opening the door hastily can lead to break-ins and violence. Listen carefully to the circumstances, light a lamp, and only then open the door. In the event of a fire or other urgent situation, light candles at a place decided upon in advance and proceed to work from there.
