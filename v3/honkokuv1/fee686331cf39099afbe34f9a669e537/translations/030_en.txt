One must be vigilant about fire at all times, day and night. When there is pressing business that requires one to go out on a day when the wind is blowing unfavorably, one should turn back at the gate and instruct the household two or three times to take good care with fire before departing.

It has been said by elders of old that those who are devoted in their hearts to the gods and buddhas, or who make offerings to wandering monks and ascetics (*kangin* and *shugyōja*), will never be the cause of a fire.

　Kyōwa 3rd year　　　　Honshakuchō 4-chōme, Ōmochō
