(Continued from previous page) ...if one piles items up and proceeds, one will either get caught in a scramble with others on the road, or when fire suddenly bears down, one's strength will give out; and since it is not worth exchanging one's life for such things, one will have no choice but to abandon everything and flee. This has been witnessed and heard of on numerous occasions. Particularly when women and children are made to pull the chest, it is all the more important to bear this in mind: pack it lightly, and above all, move it away quickly before getting caught in the crush. This is a matter of the utmost importance.

1. For those who do not own a wheeled long chest and are extremely poor: always keep a paper mosquito net (*shichō*) nearby, and in the event of a sudden fire, place items into it. Those who are not strong enough can place it on a straw mat (*mushiro*) and drag it out. Alternatively, one can cut open the edge of a thin rush mat (*usuberi*) to make a sack, put things into it, and carry that away as well. Items that are difficult to carry can be placed on a straw mat and dragged out. Either method is quick and easy to manage. These are things one should know in advance.
