1. During celebrations, construction work, funerals, or any occasion when members of the household are exhausted, the master and servants must take special care throughout the night, remaining vigilant in all directions, and must not be negligent in guarding against fire and burglars.

1. If water is thrown onto a fire caused by a lightning strike, the fire will on the contrary burn more vigorously. If one quickly adds further fire from one's own side to the burning fire, it will immediately be suppressed. One should bear this in mind in advance.
