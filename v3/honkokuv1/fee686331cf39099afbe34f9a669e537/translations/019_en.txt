(Continued from previous page) — one should never strike a fire and then leave the fire-striking box (*hiuchi-bako*) sitting on a shelf as it is. When the tinder (*hokuchi*) inside naturally catches and the fire spreads from the shelf to the wall, the distinction between one's own property and that of one's neighbor is lost, and disputes over the cause arise before anyone realizes what has happened. Therefore, fire-striking boxes should be replaced with new ones when they become old. The boxes should be made of paulownia (*kiri*) or chestnut (*kuri*) board. Furthermore, if the interior of the tinder compartment is lined with sheet metal (*itakane*), fires originating from it will be extremely unlikely ever after. Reflecting carefully on this matter, it should be treated as more important than anything else.

1. Regardless of whether one lives in the countryside or in town, cases of fire breaking out when poor persons or those living alone kindle pine needles, fallen leaves, wood shavings, or the like, and then suddenly step outside leaving the fire unattended — such incidents have also occurred numerous times in the past. One should be mindful of this in advance, and should kindle fires at a remove from any such combustible materials.
