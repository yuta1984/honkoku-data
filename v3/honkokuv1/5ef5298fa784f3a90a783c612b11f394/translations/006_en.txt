The above post station is located at the foot of Mount Asama. From the 29th day of last month, Mount Asama erupted violently, with earthquakes and thunder being fierce and houses shaking loudly, causing the farmers to evacuate one after another. Then, around the hour of the Ox on the night of the 7th day of this month, large rocks began to fall in great numbers. A large flaming rock fell onto the roof of the house belonging to an elder named Matahachi, which instantly caught fire. From there, four or five locations became engulfed in flames all at once, and the entire post station burned down without exception.

The village head, a man called Rokuemon, together with his son, worked desperately to retrieve the water register (mizucho) and other official documents. While doing so, large rocks fell twice onto the bamboo hat he was wearing, knocking him to the ground. He barely managed to get up and flee. As for Rokuemon's daughter, grandchild, and two or three female servants — since it was the middle of the night, it was unknown where they had gone, and Rokuemon reported that they had most likely been struck by rocks and perished. It was also reported that the extent of casualties and deaths beyond this was impossible to determine.
