Upon examination, of these six people, four were women and two were men. Upon questioning them, we learned that some were from a place called Uba-mura, approximately ten ri upstream from Maebashi, and one was said to be the wife of the headman (nanushi) of Shirai-machi. Having confirmed their identities, we arranged to have records made and to have them sent home to their respective places. That same night, around the Hour of the Rat (midnight), something resembling a shark, measuring approximately twenty ken (roughly 36 meters) in length, passed through the Tone River — the sound it made was so terrifying that there are no words to describe it. Again, around the fifth hour (approximately 8 PM) of that same night, a similar object came with a powerful roar from upstream and eventually sank to the bottom of the river.

Item: From the morning of the 10th, approximately two hundred people from Maebashi and Isesaki set out for flood-control work, but not a single one of them has returned. Even now, people continue to go out to the riverside both day and night, where smoke rises from within the water, great boulders glow and burn like fire, and at times the rumbling is tremendous — when the rocks and sand blaze up, it appears as though a great fire is burning in Edo. Because of this, it has become impossible to sleep or rest at any hour, causing us great hardship.
