Humbly submitted via post-town relay (shuku-okuri)

Item: We, the toiya (transport agent) and toshiyori (town elders) of Satte post town on the Nikkō Highway, respectfully report the following.

In the Gongen-dō River, located approximately eight chō to the east of this post town (which is a branch of the Tone River), from last night through noon today, the 9th day, there has been an enormous volume of debris flowing downstream, sufficient to damage warehouses and buildings. Among the items observed: vertical posts of five or six sun square, beams of four or five shaku in circumference, thresholds, lintels, door panels, penetrating tie beams (nuki), ridge beams (keta), bamboo, miscanthus thatch (kaya), wheat straw of recent harvest, and household goods of all kinds, all broken and shattered into fine pieces. Furthermore, living trees of pine and cedar, logs of four or five shaku in diameter, were snapped and crushed at heights of five or six shaku to about one jō from the ground, their bark stripped away, their tops and roots alike reduced to the condition of a tattered fringe (sasara). All of this flowed in such extraordinary quantities as to completely fill the river, which is sixty or seventy ken in width, making navigation by boat impossible.

For more than ten days prior to this, there had been no rain whatsoever, leaving the river nearly dry; yet suddenly, the water rose four or five shaku with pitch-black muddy water. As to what and how much was flowing through the mid-river, we could not determine at all. Accordingly, we boarded a boat to inspect, and found a broken saddle upon which was written "Kawashima Village, Gunma District, Kōzuke Province (Jōshū)" [with one character unclear]. Having retrieved this from the bank of the Gongen-dō River and made inquiries, it was [said to have come] from Fujiki-ho...
