7th Month

Regarding the volcanic mudflow (yamatsunami) that struck the villages in Agatsuma District and Gunma District of Kōzuke Province under my jurisdiction, I previously submitted a report on this matter. However, additional reports have continued to come in. According to these reports, on the 8th day of this month, at around the Hour of the Rabbit (approximately 10 a.m.), Mount Asama in Shinano Province erupted, and a volcanic mudflow sent mud, large rocks, and burning stones surging to a height of some five to six jō, rising like a black cloud. Within this mass, fire and smoke rose up, and a torrent resembling boiling water poured forth in tremendous quantities. In the vicinity of Mount Asama and throughout the villages along the Agatsuma River, the number of people who drowned and perished is unknown, and there are areas where rice fields, farmland, and dwellings have been entirely swept away — so the surrounding villages have reported. It is unclear whether any who may have been out working in the fields or performing other tasks might have survived. Even if any survivors remain, it is reported that there is nothing in store for them but starvation.

Due to the dire nature of the situation, roads and bridges have been destroyed, making passage impossible, and as such, the full details cannot yet be ascertained, as reported. Accordingly, I have immediately dispatched my deputies (tedai) to conduct an on-site inspection. A detailed report will be submitted in due course; however, I hereby submit the above as an initial report. In addition, the above matters have also been reported separately to the Office of Tax Collection (o-torikago-kata) by means of a separate document.

　　　　　　　　　Magistrate (Go-daikan)
7th Month　　　　Harada Seiemon
