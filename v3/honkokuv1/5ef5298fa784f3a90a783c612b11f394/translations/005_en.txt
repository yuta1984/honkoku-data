　　Itahana-juku post town, Jōshū [Kōzuke Province], Nakasendō

[Left folio]
　The said post town has come forward with the following complaint: On the 28th day of the 5th month, the 28th day of the 6th month, and the 5th day of the current month, Mount Asama erupted, depositing ash to a depth of approximately five bu. Then, from the evening hour of Roku-tsu (around 6 p.m.) on the 6th day of the current month until the Hour of the Ram (around 2 p.m.) on the 8th, there were continuous tremors, thunder, and lightning both day and night, with an unceasing fall of stones and sand. From the Hour of the Horse (around noon) to the Hour of the Monkey (around 4 p.m.) on the 7th—a period of about two and a half hours—it became dark as night, and people could only just manage their affairs by the light of paper lanterns and hand lanterns. In total, stones and sand accumulated to a depth of approximately one shaku and one sun, with drifts reaching as much as one shaku four to five sun in places. As for buildings, two bays of stabling belonging to those who perform the post-horse service were destroyed, as were numerous back-houses and outbuildings. Needless to say, fields, paddies, and crops were devastated; not even green grasses remained. The post town has lodged this complaint, stating that they are in immediate difficulty regarding fodder for their horses and are suffering great hardship.
