Domain of Hisamatsu, Lord of Chikuzen

Item: Our villages are located fourteen ri from Mount Asama, which stands on the border between Shinshū (Shinano Province) and Jōshū (Kōzuke Province). Since this spring, the mountain has erupted repeatedly, and while ash and sand have fallen upon us until now, there have been no particular difficulties. However, from the night of the 5th day of the 7th month of this year, the sounds of eruption became tremendous. Although there was no wind or rain whatsoever, violent tremors occurred, the sky grew so dark it seemed as though even great mountains might crumble, and the heavens roared and rumbled. Even as dawn broke on the 8th day, ash and sand continued to fall without distinction between day and night, through to the morning of the 8th. Around the Hour of the Serpent (approximately 10 a.m.), burning rocks rained down like a shower of stones, and the tremors did not cease. Past the Hour of the Rooster (approximately 2 p.m.), the rain turned to mud, and its smell was so extraordinarily foul that we were unable to even take our meals. The mud and gravel accumulated to approximately one shaku in depth...
