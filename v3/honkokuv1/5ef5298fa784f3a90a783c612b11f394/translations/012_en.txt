Around the Hour of the Monkey (approximately 2 p.m.) on the 8th day, the Tone River was filled with rocks and muddy water; large boulders flowed downstream while on fire, smoke rose across the entire surface of the river, and [the flow] pushed up onto the land.

As a result, the road passage at Yotsukawa between this post town [Tamamura-juku] and Goryō-juku was blocked, the Nikkō Highway traffic was suspended, and the Mikuni Highway was likewise closed. Therefore, we humbly submit this report through the post town headman (shuku-gashira).
