Item: From around the end of last month, Mt. Asama in Shinano Province has been shaking and volcanic ash and sand have been falling, occurring on numerous occasions. On the night of the 5th day, approximately five sun in depth fell. In particular, from the Hour of the Rooster (around 6 p.m.) on the 6th, it began to fall with great intensity; thunder and lightning roared throughout the night, and the ashfall continued until the Hour of the Snake (around 10 a.m.) on the following 7th day. The depth of the ash accumulation reached over two sun and seven bu; when measured over one tsubo of ground, the quantity came to over one koku, five to, and three shō.
