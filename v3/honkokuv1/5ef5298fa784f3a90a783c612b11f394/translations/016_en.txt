(Continued from the previous page) ...and there are also said to be large ones of note. When we picked up the burning stones that had fallen and examined them, they were extremely light, and upon striking anything they would crumble to pieces. The ash and sand that have fallen in various locations are said to be the result of these burned rocks breaking apart and being scattered by the wind.

Item: That Mount Asama has erupted is without doubt a certainty; however, some have said that the hot springs of Kusatsu have burst forth, or that the banks of the Yukaho hot springs have collapsed — but these are reported to be mere rumors without foundation. Regarding the situation beyond this, we are exceedingly uncertain, and as we find ourselves in particularly great turmoil, we submit herewith a general account in writing. For further details, we shall report [at a later time]. The above is our report.


　　　On the night of the 6th day of the 7th month of Tenmei 3, the Year of the Rabbit,
　　　tremors reached as far as Edo at around the half-past sixth hour (approximately 7 PM),
　　　and on the 7th and 8th, ash fell and the tremors grew
　　　increasingly stronger, subsiding from the night of the 9th onward.
