The aforementioned two post stations are located at the foot of Mount Asama and, as stated above, are reported to be experiencing a catastrophe similar to that of Karuizawa Post Station. However, since the entire population of both stations appears to have fled somewhere, there has been no word whatsoever regarding their safety at the local administrative office (jinya) in that area. Furthermore, though deputies (tedai) were dispatched to conduct an inspection, they reported that they were unable to carry one out.

The above concerns four locations along the Nakasendo Highway in Shinano Province, all under my jurisdiction as magistrate (daikan). In this incident, burning rocks and ash from Mount Asama have fallen upon them, and in particular, the three post stations in Shinano Province appear to have been rendered effectively abandoned. As the eruption has yet to subside and a thorough investigation cannot yet be submitted, I hereby report the above matters for the time being. Respectfully submitted.

7th Month　　Endō Hyōemon

Then, from around the 29th day of the 6th month of this year, a major eruption occurred, with smoke billowing up into the sky across the entire area.
