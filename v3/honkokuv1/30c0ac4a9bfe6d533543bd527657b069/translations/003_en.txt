[Continuing from the previous page:] Heaven has a body, but it does not form that body by itself — there is that which causes it to have a body. Earth has a form, but it does not form that form by itself — there is that which causes it to have a form. Both Heaven and Earth have an origin. The Book of Odes says: "King Wen, with careful and reverent heart, served the Lord on High in brightness." Guanyinzi said: "Heaven is not heaven by itself — there is that which makes it heaven. Earth is not earth by itself — there is that which makes it earth."

[Interlinear note from Xinyu (New Discourse): "Within the Great Void, the primordial qi swirled and stirred, steaming into dense white mist. Yin grew lush and Yang grew strong; breathing in and out without cease, it suddenly became hollow within, resembling floating foam. The sun, moon, and stars were arrayed from this point forth. Thus the origin of Heaven's creation arose from subtle vapour. Within this hollow foam, the Yang qi burst forth, drying into 'wild horses' [heat haze] and dust, flying daily and gathering as dew, revolving and condensing at the centre, until it became solidly substantial within, like a cannonball. The great earth, its mountains and rivers, thus had their beginning. Therefore the Earth originally arose from minute dust."]

The Gezhi Cao (草稿 on the Investigation of Things) says: The ten thousand things, in their teeming multitude, fall into disorder without a master. The role of the emperor with respect to the people is both manifest and great. Does this not all speak of Heaven having a supreme ruler? Indeed, among birds there is the phoenix as their king, among beasts the qilin, among bees the queen, among ants the queen — all have their rulers and chiefs. How much more so must this be the case for something as vast as Heaven and Earth, through which time moves and things are born, reaching to the utmost above and coiling to the depths below? The round vault of the Nine Heavens — who measures and orders it? Its revolutions, its resting — who presides over and directs them? Furthermore, consider the river barges, ocean vessels, boats of Yue, and craft of Shu — riding the wind and drifting through the vast skies, crossing shores and cleaving through waves: is this something the boats do by themselves? There is a helmsman who steers them, as if a spirit were present within. The Supreme Ruler of Heaven and Earth has no beginning before Heaven, and no end after Heaven. The full power of its pivot and axis operates through that which revolves in serene and ceaseless motion — and there is in this something that goes beyond what human thought and deliberation can reach. Therefore, we simply record this and attribute it to Heaven, and leave it at that.

　　On the Structure of Heaven (Tentai)

　Question: Heaven stretches vast and blue, high and distant without limit. Its stars and constellations are interwoven in complexity, and their forms and images revolve in motion. May I hear an explanation of this?

Answer: The body of Heaven is like blue crystal [lapis lazuli glass], transparent and perfectly round, with the Seven Luminaries and the ranked constellations revolving in layer upon layer, enveloping the Earth. [Interlinear note: The Seven Governors revolve at different heights, hence the term "layer upon layer."] The Earth is like a cannonball, sitting at the very centre of Heaven, permanently still and unmoving, with people dwelling on all four sides.
