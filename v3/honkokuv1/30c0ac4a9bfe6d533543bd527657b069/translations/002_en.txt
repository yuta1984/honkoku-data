　　On the Origins of Heaven and Earth

　Question: The origin of all things derives from Heaven and Earth. Yet regarding the origin of Heaven and Earth itself, no one throughout the ages has spoken of it. Zhuangzi said: "What lies within the Six Directions [the universe] is discussed but not deliberated upon; what lies beyond the Six Directions is acknowledged but not discussed." Does this mean that [the sages] knew but chose not to speak? Or is it that there are things not yet known?

Answer: From ancient times to the present, it has been said that the beginning of Heaven and Earth is so vast and obscure that one ought not to speak of it speculatively; rather than groping at it and opening up doubts, it is better to hold one's tongue and preserve one's faith.
