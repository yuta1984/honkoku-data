　　On the Origins of Heaven and Earth

Question: The origins of all things emerge from Heaven and Earth. But what are the origins of Heaven and Earth themselves? From ancient times to the present, there has been no one who could fully explain this. Zhuangzi (Mengzhuang) said: "What lies within the Six Directions [i.e., the physical universe] may be discussed but not debated in depth. What lies beyond the Six Directions may simply be acknowledged to exist, but not even discussed." Does this mean that [the sages] knew but did not speak? Or is it that, in truth, it is something that has yet to be known?

Answer: From ancient times to the present, when people speak of the beginning of Heaven and Earth, it is said to be so vast, deep, and obscure that one cannot yet venture to speculate about it. Rather than groping at it with conjecture and thereby arousing doubt, it would be better to seal one's lips and preserve it as a matter of faith.
