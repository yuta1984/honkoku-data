...The fifth is called "Overseer of Fate" (*Sī Mìng*), and the sixth is called "Overseer of Criminal Justice" (*Sī Kòu*). The six stars to the north are called the "Inner Staircase" (*Nèi Jiē*). The single star of the Chancellor (*Xiāng*) is located to the south of the Northern Dipper, overseeing and directing the hundred offices, and thereby gathering all affairs. The Grand Yang Star (*Tài Yáng Xīng*) is located to the west of the Chancellor. The four stars to the northwest are called "Vigor" (*Shì*). The six stars of the Celestial Prison (*Tiān Láo*) are located below the Dipper bowl of the Northern Dipper. This is the sequence of the Literary Glory Palace (*Wén Chāng Gōng*).

The Supreme Tenuity (*Tài Wēi*) is the court of the Son of Heaven, the seat of the Five Emperors, and the bureau of the Twelve Feudal Lords. Its outer fence (*wài fān*) corresponds to the Nine Ministers. One account says: "The Supreme Tenuity serves as a balance scale (*héng*), and the balance scale governs impartiality." It is also regarded as the Celestial Court (*Tiān Tíng*), which governs law, renders judgments fairly, supervises and confers advancement upon the virtuous, bestows credentials (*fú*) upon the arrayed lunar lodges, examines the propriety (*jié*) of the various spirits, expresses sentiment, and adjudicates doubts. The two stars in the center of the southern fence (*nán fān*) are called the "Upright Gate" (*Duān Mén*). The eastern star is called the "Left Enforcer of Law" (*Zuǒ Zhí Fǎ*), the image of the Commandant of Justice (*Tíng Wèi*). The western star is called the "Right Enforcer of Law" (*Yòu Zhí Fǎ*), the image of the Imperial Censor-in-Chief (*Yù Shǐ Dà Fū*). To the east of the Left Enforcer of Law is the Left Flanking Gate (*Zuǒ Yè Mén*), and to the west of the Right Enforcer of Law is the Right Flanking Gate (*Yòu Yè Mén*). Among the four stars of the eastern fence (*dōng fān*): the first is the "Supreme General" (*Shàng Jiāng*), the second is the "Second General" (*Cì Jiāng*), the third is the "Second Chancellor" (*Cì Xiāng*), and the fourth is the "Supreme Chancellor" (*Shàng Xiāng*); all together they are called the "Four Assistants" (*Sì Fǔ*). The three stars outside the corner to the southwest are called the "Hall of Brilliance" (*Míng Táng*), the palace in which the Son of Heaven proclaims governance. The three stars to the west are called the "Numinous Terrace" (*Líng Tái*), and they oversee the observation of cloud phenomena and celestial occurrences. The one star to the northeast of the Left Enforcer of Law is called the "Court Usher" (*Yè Zhě*), which oversees the reception and honoring of guests. The three stars to the northeast of the Court Usher are called the "Inner Seats of the Three Ducal Ministers" (*Sān Gōng Nèi Zuò*), and serve as the location for court assemblies. The three stars to the north are called the "Inner Seats of the Nine Ministers" (*Jiǔ Qīng Nèi Zuò*). The five stars to the west of the Nine Ministers are called the "Inner Five Feudal Lords" (*Nèi Wǔ Zhū Hóu*). This is the sequence of the Supreme Tenuity Palace (*Tài Wēi Gōng*).

The Seat of the Yellow Emperor (*Huáng Dì Zuò*) is situated within the Supreme Tenuity. It is the divine spirit (*shén*) that contains the pivotal axis (*shū niǔ*). Four stars flank the Seat of the Yellow Emperor: the east is the "Azure Emperor" (*Cāng Dì*), the south is the "Red Emperor" (*Chì Dì*), the west is the "White Emperor" (*Bái Dì*), and the north is the "Black Emperor" (*Hēi Dì*). The one star to the north is called the "Crown Prince" (*Tài Zǐ*). The one star to the north of the Crown Prince is called the "Attending Official" (*Cóng Guān*). The one star to the northeast of the Emperor's Seat is called the "Favored Courtier" (*Xìng Chén*). The four stars of the Screen (*Píng*) are situated within the Upright Gate, serving to shelter and protect the Emperor's Seat. The fifteen stars of the Guard Positions (*Láng Wèi*) are situated to the north of the Emperor's Seat. The Guard General (*Láng Jiāng*) is situated to the north of the Guard Positions. The one star of the Martial Escort (*Wǔ Bēn*) is situated to the north of the western fence of the Supreme Tenuity. The seven stars of the Everlasting Array (*Cháng Chén*) are situated to the north of the Emperor's Seat, serving as the night guard of the Son of Heaven, and thereby set up as a defense against powerful enemies. This is the sequence of the Five Emperors' Seats (*Wǔ Dì Zuò*).

The six stars of the Three Terraces (*Sān Tái*) are arranged in pairs of two, beginning at the Literary Glory (*Wén Chāng*) and reaching to the Supreme Tenuity (*Tài Wēi*).
