...in less than thirty thousand years, [the Sun] returns to the degree of its original lunar mansion. The principle underlying this discrepancy is such that one cannot hold to a single method to exhaust it completely.

Shizhai Huang Gong [Huang Daozhou, styled Shizhai] used the *Yi* [*Book of Changes*] to establish the calendar, and applied Shoujing's [Guo Shoujing's] ten-thousand methods to accumulate calculations, yet in the end he could not avoid the fault of certain schools absorbing vacuous numbers into their systems. The *Yi* takes numbers as its incipient signs (*duanji*), and fixing them upon the cyclically revolving, static heaven — it is likewise so. Arranging and fixing the limits equally, distinguishing the seasonal nodes (*jieqi*), and recording at which fen and seconds of which node the Sun's path arrives — one records daily where the five planets arrive and likewise records the movements of the fixed stars. Then the reason for the discrepancy and the concordance of the accumulated precession (*zhui cha*) will become self-evidently clear.

Someone says: "Heaven cannot be seen; one can only use the positions of the fixed stars as a basis for investigation. Where does one find the basis for the static heaven?" Qianfu Fang Gong [Fang Yizhi, styled Qianfu] says: "When arithmeticians encounter a number that cannot be directly ascertained, they establish a method of division [*shang fa*, i.e., an interpolation method]. By measuring from both sides, one arrives at a middle degree and settles upon that." Now, using the fixed and definite fen and seconds of the ring of the static heaven, and allowing the Sun, Moon, and five planets, together with the fixed stars, each to proceed according to their own slow and fast motions — how could they escape [detection]?

Just as those who penetrate to the subtle principles (*ji*) of the *Yi* [find that] there is nowhere that is not [the pattern of] the River Diagram and the Luo Writing (*He Luo*), so that before all things had yet come into existence as things, all was already the manifest [pattern] of the River Diagram and Luo Writing — for this reason I establish this idea and await a person of divine intelligence to [develop it further].

Mizhi Fang Gong [Fang Yizhi] says: "This establishes a constant law, and the surplus and deficit (*yingxu*) observed through measurements taken at successive times are all contained within it. The operations of heaven involve surplus and deficit: within one year there is surplus and deficit; within one day there is surplus and deficit; and moreover, over many years there is surplus, and over many years there is deficit."

Taixi Mu Gong [the Western scholar, i.e., Johann Adam Schall von Bell] says: "The earth too has motion (*you*). Can one truly rely on the measurements of a single year to determine it? If one wishes to understand the principle, the principle is as follows: heretofore, people only used the heaven of the fixed stars as the [reference for] the Sun's daily circuit, not knowing that the fixed stars themselves move across the static heaven, and that the static heaven cannot be seen. Hence there was no one who perceived this."

Jie Zixuan [Jie Xuan, styled Zixuan] says: "The year is of itself a year; precession is of itself precession. The year originally has no precession. Heaven has the static and the moving. The static heaven has degrees that are fixed and constant; the moving heaven has arrivals (*zhi*, i.e., solstice points) that are fixed and constant. [Interlinear annotation: This is speaking of the moving and the static as united into one.] By completing a circuit of the degrees of the static heaven, and coinciding with the arrivals of the moving heaven, one year is formed.
