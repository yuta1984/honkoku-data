**Copy of the Re-Issued Notice**

Since the great earthquake of last month, flooding and other extraordinary disasters have brought widespread hardship to the people. As there is now a great deal of work involved in erecting temporary shelters and carrying out other construction, carpenters and other craftsmen are to exert themselves with compassion and diligence. Should any among them, through misunderstanding of proper conduct, receive wages or day-labor payments in excess of the established rates, this would be inexcusable. Village officials in villages where various craftsmen reside are hereby to communicate that each craftsman is to observe this instruction strictly, harbor no misunderstanding, and labor with sincerity and honesty. Each person is to take heed accordingly.

　　　　　　Twentieth Day of the Fourth Month
