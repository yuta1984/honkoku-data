○ In Koami-chō, Tōkan-bori, and around the residence of Lord Sakai Uta-no-kami, there were many collapses. From Eikyu Bridge along the Ōkawa (Sumida River) riverside and Manabe-gashi avenue, damage was severe.

○ In Hama-chō, the residence of Lord Mizuno Dewa-no-kami burned. Residences in this area suffered many collapses, including the middle residence of Lord Akimoto Tajima-no-kami, and the residences of Lord Andō Nagato-no-kami, Shinjō Kamejirō, and Hishinuma Oribe-no-shō, among others—residences great and small in this area all sustained damage.

○ Likewise, from Kaigarachō through Ōsakamachi, Yoshichō, Ningyōchō avenue, and the Tomizawa-chō area, damage was severe. From Yanokura through to the Ryōgoku area, including Yokoyama-chō and Bakurōchō, there was no particular damage, while the Ōdenma-chō area sustained severe damage.

○ Koku-chō and Moto-Ginchō suffered minor damage. Honchō and Muromachi also sustained similar minor damage. The fish market (Uogashi) and Kugitana also suffered little damage, though all earthen storehouses (dozō) were heavily damaged.

○ South of Nihonbashi, the main avenues suffered few collapses. However, from the second block of Minami-Denma-chō, through Minami-Kajichō, Kanō-Shinmichi, Gorobei-chō, Tatami-chō, Kita-Konya-chō, Shirauo-yashiki, the eastern section of the same, Suzuki-chō, Inaba-chō, Tokiwa-chō, Ōta-yashiki, Yanagi-chō, Gusoku-chō, Sumi-chō, Moto-Zaimoku-chō, all the way to Hatchōme-bashi, the area burned. In this zone, only the occasional earthen storehouse survived.

○ North of Imaigawa Bridge, in Kanda, collapses were numerous both to the east and west, with the eastern side suffering particularly widespread destruction.

[Left folio, upper section]

Along Sujikai-gomon avenue, because buildings had been newly constructed after last year's fire, house damage was limited.

○ To the east of there, the areas around Otamagaike, Egawa-chō, Toshima-chō, and Yanagiwara avenue suffered collapses in various places.

○ Outside Kanda-Shin-Ishibashi, Kyūemon-chō and Sakuma-chō suffered few collapses. In the Nanakuari area, the residences of lords suffered many collapses. Around Shamisenbori, everything collapsed, though for the most part the damage did not reach catastrophic levels.

○ From Izumibashi to Yamashita, the Okachi-machi avenue suffered many collapses. Along the Onari-dō road, the area around Yamamoto-chō's substitute land, as well as Kanazawa-chō and Hatagochō, had a particularly high number of completely destroyed houses. Below Kanda Myōjin shrine, collapses occurred in various places, but the shrine precincts themselves were unharmed. Yushima-yokochō and the first block of the same suffered minor damage. Outside Shōheibashi, the Seidō (Confucian academy) was undamaged, but the facing tenement row (nagaya) was heavily damaged. Below Tsumakoi-zaka hill, the residences of Lord Tatebe Takumi-no-kami, Lord Naitō Bungo-no-kami, and Lord Ōzeki Shinano-no-kami, along with several dozen bannermen (hatamoto) residences, collapsed. Tsumakoi Inari Shrine and the townspeople's houses alike suffered no particular damage.

○ In Yushima Sangumi-chō and the precinct entrance of Tenjin shrine, collapses occurred in various places, but the shrine's torii gates were all undamaged. Many residences below Tenjin hill collapsed, and the front section of Lord Itakura Settsu-no-kami's residence collapsed. Above the Kiridōshi pass the damage was light, but at the bottom of the slope everything was destroyed.

○ The main shrine of Nezu Gongen was undamaged, but the area in front of the gate was completely destroyed without exception. Ikenohata Shichiken-chō was destroyed. In Kayachō second block, the side facing Sakai Inari Shrine was destroyed, and the middle section—
