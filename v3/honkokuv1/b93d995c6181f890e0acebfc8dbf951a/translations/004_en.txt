(The area) burned on top of the great collapse. From there, along the avenue in front of Kōtokuji temple and the back-street temples, damage was extensive. The areas of Yamazaki-chō, Okitte-chō, and Iriya were largely destroyed in various places.

○ Sakamoto 2-chōme and 3-chōme burned, with fire spreading into both Takoya Yokochō and Otansu-chō. Kanasugi, Daionji-mae, Minowa-chō, and Shin-chō all suffered many collapses. Nezu suffered some shaking, though there were collapsed houses.

○ The Tōeizan (Kan'eiji) complex on Ueno hill suffered collapses in various places but was otherwise unharmed. The nine-ringed finial (kurin) of the five-story pagoda at Yanaka Tennōji fell and was buried in the ground. The temples in this area suffered minor damage. The Nezu Shrine was unharmed, though everything in front of the gate collapsed. The Misaki and Sendagi areas suffered few collapses. Komagome suffered collapses in various places, while Hongō plateau suffered relatively few collapses. In many locations, the roads subsided near the kōji-muro (fermentation cellars), and logs were laid across to allow passage. From Maruyama-dachō through Kawakatsumae, Tozaki-chō, Yanagi-chō, and Shimo-Tomizaka, all low-lying areas had many collapsed houses. Hakusan and Sugamo suffered few collapses, while Koishikawa, the road in front of Dentsu'in temple, Otansu-chō, Ōtsuka, and Otowa areas were approximately half destroyed. From Suwa-chō in Koishikawa through the area below the Mito domain's hundred-unit tenement row, there were collapses, and the opposite side suffered scattered fires. The banks of the Edogawa river suffered many collapses on both sides.

○ The Ushigome Riding Grounds suffered few collapses, while the areas from below Akagi Shrine, Kaidai-chō, Kohikata, and Suidō-chō through Sekiguchi suffered many collapses.

[Left folio, upper section]
The Ichigaya area suffered major destruction in many places, though the Hachiman Shrine there was unharmed.

○ The areas of Iidamachi, Manaita Bridge, and toward Ogawa-chō suffered extensive damage. Both inside and outside the Koishikawa Gate suffered considerable damage. The residence of Lord Matsudaira Suruga-no-kami within the gate area burned, and the smaller residences in this area burned completely, with the remaining collapses being immense.

○ In the Ogawa-chō area, the residences of Lord Inaba Nagato-no-kami, Lord Tsuchiya Uneme-no-shō, Lord Matsudaira Saemon-no-jō, Lord Doi Noto-no-kami, and other neighboring residences suffered extensive damage. Lord Toda Ōi-no-kami's residence burned, though the front tenement row survived. Lord Naitō Suruga-no-kami's residence was similarly situated, with the front gate tenement row surviving. Lord Hotta Bitchū-no-kami's residence and others in the vicinity were completely burned. Lord Nakarai Izumo-no-kami's residence survived. Most of the smaller residences in this area burned. From there, the residences along Omote Jinbō-koji, the residences of the standing fire brigade (jōbikeshi) officials, and Ura Jinbō-koji all burned completely.

○ Along Hitotsubashi Gate Road, the bannermen's (hatamoto) residences survived, while Lord Sakakibara Shikibu-no-tayū's residence on the Hara side was half burned and completely collapsed. Lord Honda Bungo-no-kami's residence was also half burned. Lord Toda Kaga-no-kami's residence burned.

○ Along Kijibashi Gate Road, starting with Lord Hongō Tango-no-kami's residence, all residences in that area burned completely, with the fire stopping at Sanbanhara.
