When the harmony of heaven and earth is disturbed, yin energy stagnates within the ground and erupts at a given time. This is what we call an earthquake. When it occurs in the mountains, it is called a "hora" (cave-in); when it erupts in the sea, it is called a "tsunami." Thunder and lightning are said to be of the same nature. To guard against such natural disasters is beyond the reach of human wisdom, and without the blessings that come from accumulated good deeds, it will be difficult to escape them. People, accumulate good deeds in great measure, and use them as a shield of precaution. The reason for this is as follows: the earthen storehouses (dozō) built to survive fires collapse in earthquakes, and houses constructed as earthquake refuges are burned down in fires, rendering them useless as shelters. While this may be attributed to one's fortune or misfortune, in the end it comes down to the difference between virtuous and non-virtuous conduct. If one is always careful in one's behavior, neither one's person nor one's home should come to harm. One should not merely be afraid when the earth shakes, but should be mentally prepared at all times. Furthermore, after a great earthquake, small tremors continue day and night without ceasing — much like the lingering resonance of a bell — though they are never as strong as the initial shock. It has been said since ancient times that all earthquakes behave in this manner. People of the world, you must not let this frighten you into sleeping outdoors for a prolonged period. Doing so will bring about illness and harm. ○ The locations of earthquake damage and fires are recorded here in detail, noting only the facts, large and small.
