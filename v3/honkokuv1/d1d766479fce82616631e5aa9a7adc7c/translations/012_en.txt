Item: In Inariyama in the Ueda domain《interlinear note: this is a post station on the highway from the Kamigata region to Zenkoji Temple》, five hundred elderly male and female pilgrims on their way to Zenkoji were lodging in this village and were all crushed to death at once; moreover, fires broke out and it burned. Some three hundred local residents also perished, it is said. What is called a "kara-jishin" [a "bare" earthquake, i.e., one that strikes without any foreshock warning] collapses houses instantly, leaving no time to flee; some managed to break through walls or crawl out through the roof, but most lodgers had been put up in two-story buildings — kept there for the convenience of lodging travelers — and as the upper floors collapsed, a great many deaths resulted.

Item: In Hachiman Village and Shinoi Village in the Matsushiro domain as well, great numbers of travelers perished. Furthermore, Zenkoji was in truly catastrophic condition: some three thousand or more travelers had been lodging there, and the townhouses fell one after another like dominoes, resulting in injuries and deaths; on top of this, a great fire broke out, leaving only the main hall and the mountain gate standing, with everything else burned to the ground. Among those from our district office who had gone on pilgrimage and happened to be lodging there — the moment the earthquake struck, one person immediately rose and tried to walk away on two feet, but before he could do so, the second floor collapsed; he managed to squeeze through a gap just barely wide enough for one person and came back naked. Meeting others who had had similar experiences and hearing their accounts: three hundred or even five hundred people had been crammed into a single inn,

Item: The moment the earthquake struck, fires broke out in so many locations that they cannot be counted. To the north, the sky appeared red. I believe Echigo must have experienced the same earthquake. ◯ When fires break out amid an earthquake, there is no way whatsoever to extinguish them; one can only see to one's own safety, and the fires burned unchecked, leaving entire villages in the same state as if they had been utterly destroyed by the quake itself. ◯ Within my own area of administration, reports have arrived from three villages — each and every village is reported to be completely destroyed, with not a single house standing; there are thought to be upwards of a hundred injured, though the full picture has not yet been ascertained, as reported to me. These are the villages beyond Zenkoji. In addition, similar reports arrive day after day — it is terrifying, and it truly feels as if one is witnessing hell right before one's eyes.
