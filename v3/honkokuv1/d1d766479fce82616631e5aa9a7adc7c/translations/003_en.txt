**[Right folio]**

Furthermore, there are villages where fires broke out and everything burned to the ground without exception. In a single village, there have been anywhere from thirty or forty to two or three hundred people killed outright or injured. In the town of Zenkōji, virtually all the houses along the streets were toppled by the quake and subsequently burned, resulting in an enormous number of instant deaths and injuries. Along the main highway, a great multitude of travelers had been lodging in connection with the memorial services (kuyō) at Zenkōji at this time of year, and as a result, there are also many deaths among them. The situation in the Yamanaka area is difficult to ascertain as it lies in a remote and out-of-the-way location. However, there has been a landslide in the upper reaches of the Sai River, which has dammed the river so that no water flows through it at all; at Tanbajima, the ferry crossing has dried up, and people are said to be wading across on foot. As for the situation in Echigo Province, we have been unable to determine what has occurred there. Since all of the above is based on rumor and cannot yet be confirmed with certainty, I will immediately dispatch an agent (tedai) to conduct inspections and investigations of the collapsed houses in the territory under our administration, and will also verify the rumors regarding damage in nearby villages, reporting the full details in due course. As for the villages of Hikage jin'ya and Saku District in the same province, which are under our charge, a major earthquake struck at the same time as described above; however, whether due to the considerable distance separating them from the Zenkōji area, the jin'ya and the territory under administration, along with neighboring villages within my private domain, suffered only minor injuries and—

**[Left folio]**

—some damage to structures, but there was nothing of serious consequence, and there were no deaths among the injured. For the time being, I submit this report in haste. That is all.
