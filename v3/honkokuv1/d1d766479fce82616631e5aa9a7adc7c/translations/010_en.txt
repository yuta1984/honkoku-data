We hereby submit this preliminary notice. Respectfully submitted.

　Note: This great earthquake appears to have affected the entirety of Shinano Province. While no reports of fires had yet been received from within our administered territory at the time, numerous fires were visibly breaking out in various locations during the earthquake itself. The castle town of Iiyama, the seat of Honda Bungo-no-kami's castle, lies close to our administered territory, and we have learned that the entire town was leveled by the quake, after which fires broke out in multiple locations, resulting in the complete destruction of both the castle town and the surrounding town by fire. Respectfully submitted.

**[Left folio]**

A copy of the notice dispatched to the duty senior councilor, Lord Toda Yamashiro-no-kami, before his attendance at the castle.

At my residence in Matsushiro, Shinshū, a great earthquake struck from around the Hour of the Boar (approximately 10 p.m.) on the 24th, the day before yesterday. The residential buildings, turrets, and surrounding walls within the castle suffered extensive damage, and a great number of houses belonging to domain retainers' residences, the castle town, villages throughout the domain, and other administered territories were destroyed, with an enormous number of deaths and missing persons. Particularly in the rural villages, fires also broke out. Furthermore, in the mountain districts, landslides occurred and debris was pushed into the Sai River, causing water to back up and gradually fill to capacity — with the natural flow of the river completely stopped. The ferry crossing at Tabajima post station on the Hokkoku Highway has dried up entirely. Should the backed-up floodwaters break through in any direction hereafter, it is difficult to predict what manner of geological calamity may result. Moreover, tremors are still continuing intermittently even now. We will report the full details in due course, but for the present we hereby submit this preliminary notice. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　26th day of the 3rd month　　　　　　Sanada Shinano-no-kami

Dispatched to the duty senior councilor, Lord Toda Yamashiro-no-kami, on the 3rd day of the 4th month, Year of the Ram.
