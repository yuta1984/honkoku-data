**Copy of the Report Submitted by Sanada Shinano-no-Kami**

Item: As previously reported from my domain seat of Matsushiro in Shinano Province, owing to the great earthquake, Iwakurasan — within the villages of Yama-Hirabayashi in Sarashina District — gave way and collapsed, damming the Sai River at two locations. Water had been gradually accumulating to a depth of several tens of jō. From one or two days prior, water had been held behind the dam, but when it crested the lower of the two blockage points — at which time over two jō of water still remained — it appears to have suddenly broken through. Yesterday, the 13th, shortly after the seventh hour of the evening (approximately 4 p.m.), a great rumbling arose from the direction of those mountains, followed by the sound of rushing torrents becoming fully audible, whereupon a furious flood (激水, *gekisui*) burst forth all at once into the river channel, instantly breaking through the great embankments on both sides, or in some places cresting over them, and the river officials (kawa-yakunin) sent successive dispatches reporting that there was no way to hold it back. Before long, several villages throughout Kawanakajima were inundated, the floodwaters surged into the Chikuma River causing it to flow in reverse, and the water rose so high as to press up to the very edge of the castle.
