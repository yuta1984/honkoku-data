**Kōka 4th Year (Year of the Sheep) — Official Report on the Great Earthquake in Shinano Province**

On the 24th of this month, throughout the day and night the weather was beautifully clear, perfectly calm, and exceptionally warm. However, at the fourth hour of that same night (approximately 10 o'clock in the evening), a great earthquake struck. The compound walls (neribei) of my administrative outpost (jin'ya) in Nakanojō, Shinshū, were toppled in several places. Furthermore, farmhouses in the villages surrounding the outpost headquarters that could no longer be managed had their outbuildings and surrounding structures shaken to the ground, and the tremors were extremely severe. After some time, the shaking gradually subsided, but thereafter continued without cease, with small tremors at intervals. A sound resembling thunder rumbled from the direction to the north of the outpost. Until dawn, there were approximately eighty or more tremors. By the following morning, things became slightly quieter, but the trembling has not yet ceased even now. Within the territory under our administration, in the villages of Mimi-gun (Mizuchi District), there are reports of collapsed houses, injured persons, and deaths, though no formal petitions have been submitted as yet. Information continues to arrive through rumor, indicating that the area from Kawanakajima in the same province to Zenkōji temple, and further south to the region known as Yamanaka, appears to have suffered the most severe earthquake damage. In the area around Kawanakajima, not a single farmhouse in entire villages, or more than half of them, was left standing without being toppled…
