In my domain of Takai District, Shinano Province, a violent earthquake struck from around the Hour of the Boar (approximately 10 p.m.) on the 24th, the day before yesterday. The district administrative office (jinya) and the residences and tenements of my retainers were damaged in multiple places, farmhouses in the villages were destroyed, and from several hundred cracks in the fields and paddies, sand and mud erupted and spread into the cultivated land without exception. Tremors are still continuing intermittently even now. Within my domain, there are no casualties or injuries to persons or horses. However, I have heard that among those who had traveled to worship at Zenkoji Temple or gone out for seasonal labor, some fatalities may have occurred; as the investigation has not yet been completed, full details will be reported in due course. For the present, I hereby submit this preliminary notice. Respectfully submitted.

　　　26th day of the 3rd month　　　　　　Hori Nagato-no-kami

An extract copied from a letter sent by Kawakami Kinnojō to his son Kawakami Itarō:

Item: In Nakanojō, the eaves of the earthen storehouses and the walls of the sake breweries have largely collapsed. The plastered earthen walls surrounding the district administrative office have mostly crumbled. There are no injured persons. I have been told that some farmers have gone two nights and two days without eating up until today — this is because they are reluctant to light fires inside their houses, and so are enduring the hardship. As for myself, I am eating meals, but for two out of three meals I find I cannot eat with any real appetite [literally: cannot eat sweetly/well].
