**[Right folio]**

Please recommend hanging lamps to your uncle as well. One never knows when an earthquake may strike, and when a major earthquake hits, there is nothing to do but get outside quickly — however, since reports say the ground is splitting apart two to three feet at a time (《interlinear note: In this disaster too, there were several places at Zenkoji where the ground cracked open about three feet, and in other places the ground rose a level on one side and dropped a level on the other》), it is best to throw the door open and get out without delay. This is entirely unlike a fire — there is no way to guard against it, and because the light goes out and everything becomes dark, many people end up getting injured.

Item: From Nakanojō to Niigata in Echigo Province is some sixty ri, and since it lies to the north, I wonder how things are there. The ground has been shaking frequently in a northward direction, with the tremors reverberating through the mountains and shaking things violently and directly.

**[Left folio]**

This is vastly different from earthquakes in Edo. I had long heard that a mild winter the previous year is said in Echigo to be a sign of an earthquake to come.

Item: The great river called the Sai River is at Tabajima, but the flow of that river has stopped. A landslide upstream has blocked and swept across the river. By now, they say the area upstream has become like a lake. Fearing that the villages downstream would at any moment be washed away by floodwaters, people have taken their food and gone up into the mountains. My own administrative jurisdiction also lies downstream of that river course, and I am deeply worried.
