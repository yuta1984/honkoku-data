**[Right folio]**

…within [Mizuchi] District, the riverside villages were submerged as though the very course of the river ran through them. Damage was found at several locations [*misekezchi* (struck-through): villages], and not only were crops covered in mud, but there were also a great many places where the ground had been gouged and scooped out. However, as it was nighttime, it was difficult to ascertain the full extent of the damage. After midnight, the water level appeared to gradually stabilize, and by dawn the water had begun to recede. Although I had previously ordered the villagers to make preparations for flood prevention, this was an unprecedented and extraordinarily swift inundation — entirely beyond expectation — and I am greatly concerned that there may be not only houses swept away, but also deaths by drowning. Furthermore, it is feared that extensive damage to agricultural land will also result, and I am deeply distressed. While I shall report the full details in due course, I submit this report for now. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　Fourteenth day of the Fourth Month　　　　　Sanada Shinano-no-Kami

**[Left folio]**

In the villages of Kubiki District, Echigo Province — which fall under my magistracy (*daikanjo*) and are currently under my provisional jurisdiction (*tōbun o-azukari-sho*) — from around the fourth hour of the night (approximately 10 p.m.) on the 24th of last month (the Third Month), there were severe earthquakes with repeated aftershocks. At the *jinya* (magistrate's office), the *honjin* (principal lodging), and the row-house (*nagaya*) of Kawaura Village, the corner posts (*mukaikibashira*) buckled and the walls suffered extensive damage. In the same village and neighboring villages, a great many houses collapsed, and there were reports of immediate deaths and injuries. Although reports have not yet arrived from more distant villages, similar conditions are understood to prevail there as well. While I shall report further details in due course, I submit this report for the time being. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　Hitsuji year, First day of the Fourth Month　　　　　Ogasawara Nobusuke
