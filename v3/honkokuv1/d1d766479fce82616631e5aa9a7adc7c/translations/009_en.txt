Regarding the great earthquake that struck the villages of Kubiki District in Echigo Province, which falls under the domain of Sakakibara Shikibu-no-tayū, on the night of the 24th of last month, causing collapsed houses and deaths — we had already submitted a preliminary report on this matter. Since then, minor tremors have continued on a daily basis without ceasing, and all staff have been conducting official business in improvised shelters made of reed screens outside the government office. However, yet again on the afternoon of the 29th of last month, a great earthquake struck, and reports came in of collapsed houses and deaths in various villages, as well as seedling beds and rice paddies being buried under soil and debris. There were also reports that the tribute rice (gorōmai) that had been transported downriver and stored in the Machimina-tomachi warehouses was completely destroyed and the bales of rice left in disarray. We immediately dispatched officials to the scene. From the 24th through the 29th, not a single drop of rain had fallen, but from the early morning of the 30th (the last day of the month), heavy rain began to fall, and thereafter strong winds arose intermittently, with reports of sleet and hail falling in the villages of Kamigō. We will report the full details in due course, but for now submit this preliminary report. Respectfully submitted.

Official Notification Regarding the Great Earthquake

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Takagi Seizaemon
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　(Administered territory)

In the villages of Minochi District, Shinano Province, which fall under my daikan office and are currently held as entrusted territory, a great earthquake occurred on the night of the 24th of this month, from around the middle of the Hour of the Dog (approximately 8 p.m.) to the early part of the Hour of the Boar (approximately 9 p.m.). From that point on, tremors continued without interruption, with earthquakes breaking out at intervals, until they finally subsided around the middle of the Hour of the Hare (approximately 6 a.m.) on the following day, the 25th. However, the ground split open and muddy water gushed forth; there were numerous instances of collapsed houses and deaths among people, oxen, and horses; there were even cases where every single member of a household had perished; the number of injured persons was enormous; and there were villages where every house in the entire village was destroyed. As reports of this unprecedented catastrophe came in one after another, we dispatched our agents (tedai) without delay. Furthermore, at Nakano Village, the location of the domain office (jin'ya), there were some damaged houses, but the office itself suffered no particular harm.
