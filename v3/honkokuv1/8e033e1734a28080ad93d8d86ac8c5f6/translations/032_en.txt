**Uonuma District — (Local) Ōyu**　Located in Ōyu Village. Spring quality contains sulfurous vapor. Extremely hot. Recommended for stroke, hernia/colic, beriberi, syphilis, minor sores, and sprains/contusions.

**Same (Local) Tochiomata**　Located in Shimooridachi Village, eight *chō* from Ōyu. Spring quality is clear and mild, with a slightly acidic taste. Very cold in autumn and winter. Recommended for digestive blockage/stagnation, headaches, and sprains/contusions.

**Same (Local/Ra) Yunosawa**　Located in Yunosawa Village. Temperature: 107.5 degrees. Spring quality contains both sulfurous and saline elements. Recommended for syphilis and other ailments. The spring water flows from ancient volcanic rock; its discharge rate is approximately 3 *to* 7 *shō* per minute. The other three locations are all the same as above.

**Same (Local) Kanekake**　Located in Mitsumata Village. Spring quality is sweet, acidic, and salty, changing three times during the day. Recommended for eye diseases.

**Same (Local) Sakamaki**　Located in Kettō Village. Spring quality contains both sulfurous and aluminous elements. Recommended for flushing/congestion of blood to the head, eye diseases, swellings, and bruises/contusions.

**Same (Local) Koide**　Located at Yunokuradaira in Shimofunato Village. Spring quality carries aluminous properties. Therapeutic effects same as above.

**Kubiki District — (Local) Sekiyama**　Located three *ri* to the west of Sekiyama Village. Divided into four sections. ○ Sōyu (*General Bath*): spring quality contains both sulfurous and aluminous elements; taste is bitter and salty. Recommended for spleen/stomach deficiency and accumulation/colic. ○ Wakiyu (*Boiling Bath*): spring quality and therapeutic effects same as above. ○ Kawarayu (*Riverbed Bath*): spring quality same as above. Recommended for sword cuts, burns, various sores, and all types of hemorrhoids. ○ Wata-no-Yu (*Cotton Bath*): spring quality same as above. Recommended for stroke and circulatory stagnation.

**Same (Local) Matsunoyama**　Located in Yumoto Village. Spring quality contains sulfur and saline elements; saline properties are particularly abundant. Recommended for hemorrhoids, accumulation/colic, and scabies. ○ (Ra, Type 1) Temperature: 163 degrees. Spring quality contains sulfur and iron; taste is acidic and astringent. It likely also contains green vitriol (*ferrous sulfate*). Furthermore, there is an intense odor of hydrogen sulfide and white precipitate. The spring water flows from alluvial soil (*alluvium*), and its output is approximately 2 *to* 5 *shō* per minute. (Type 2) Temperature: 156 degrees. There is a red precipitate. However, since the spring water turns silver black, it must contain sulfurous vapor. All other characteristics are the same as Type 1.

**Same (Local) Akakura**　Located in Hitomoki Shinden. Spring quality contains both sulfurous and aluminous elements. Recommended for retained fluids/indigestion, spleen/stomach deficiency, and chest pain. ○ Moto-yu (*Original Bath*): spring quality same as above. Recommended for lower back pain, all types of hemorrhoids, accumulation/colic, and stroke.

**Same (Local) Sekikawa**　One and a half *ri* from Sekikawa Village. Discovered in Meiji 2 [1869]. Spring quality and therapeutic effects unknown.

**Same (Local) Renge**　Five *ri* from Ōdokoro Village at the foot of Renge Mountain. Located at six spots. ○ So-yu (*Plain Bath*): spring quality contains both sulfurous and aluminous elements. Recommended for scabies and bruises. ○ Kuro-yu (*Black Bath*): same as above. ○ Yakushi-yu (*Physician Bath*): spring quality same as above. Recommended for digestive blockage/stagnation and retained fluids. ○ Sangokuichi-yu (*Best-in-Three-Provinces Bath*): spring quality contains sulfur, alum, and magnesium sulfate. Recommended for syphilis, hemorrhoids, and other ailments. ○ Kogane-yu (*Golden Bath*): spring quality contains sulfurous, aluminous, and metallic (gold/iron) elements. Recommended for eye diseases and flushing/congestion of blood to the head. ○ Shin-Kogane-yu (*New Golden Bath*): same as above.

**Same (Local) Hiraiwa**　Located in the same village. Spring quality contains sulfurous vapor. Recommended for scabies and edema/dropsy.
