**Shinobu District — (Local) (Official) Iizaka Village**　Approximately 3.5 *ri* from Fukushima. Located in Iizaka and Yuno Village, on both banks of the Surikamigawa River.

○ Sabako-yu: Temperature at the source is 111 degrees; at the bathing area, 106 degrees. Spring quality and therapeutic effects are the same as Yumoto in Iwase District (see leaf 41). One bathing establishment; more than 10,000 bathers per year.

○ Tōtatsu-yu: Temperature and therapeutic effects the same as above. One bathing establishment; approximately 4,700 bathers per year.

○ Kanataki-yu: Temperature, etc., the same as above; approximately 1,200 bathers per year.

○ Tennōji-yu: Temperature, spring quality, and therapeutic effects the same as above.

○ Akagawa-no-yu: Temperature: 120 degrees. Contains saline elements. Recommended for scabies and similar conditions. One bathing establishment; approximately 1,400 bathers per year.

○ Akagawabata-no-yu: Temperature, spring quality, and therapeutic effects the same as above; 1,900 bathers per year.

○ Taki-no-yu: Temperature and therapeutic effects, etc., the same as above. Contains hydrogen sulfide gas; 1,400 bathers per year.

○ Hako-yu: All the same as above; more than 1,200 bathers per year.

**Same (Local) (Official) Niwasaka Village**　Five hot springs. More than 5,700 bathers per year.

○ Uchi-yu: Temperature: 106 degrees. Spring quality contains hydrogen sulfide. Recommended for poor blood circulation.

○ Taki-no-yu: Temperature: 111 degrees. Spring quality the same as above. Recommended for scabies, colic, and burns.

○ Tamago-yu: All the same as above.

○ Takayu: Temperature: 112 degrees. Spring quality contains sulfur and carbonate salts. Recommended for scabies, tinea cruris (*inkintamushi*), hemorrhoidal conditions, leprosy, goose-pox (*ganso*), rheumatism, syphilis, and similar conditions.

○ Senkiyu: Temperature and therapeutic effects the same as above. Contains sulfur, etc.

○ Shimo-no-yu　○ Suginoashita-yu　○ Kawakami-no-yu: Temperature, spring quality, and therapeutic effects all the same as above.

**Same (Local) (Official) Nuruyu**　Located in Sakuramoto Village. Spring quality contains alum (*banki*). Taste is sweet and acidic. Temperature: 96 degrees. Effective for eye diseases, blood loss, anal fistula (*jirō*), sword cuts, and similar conditions. More than 500 bathers per year.

**Same (Official) Nurutaki-yu**　Temperature: 104 degrees. Recommended for eye diseases and stomach pain. Approximately 1,200 bathers per year.

○ Kiri-no-yu　○ Kitsune-no-yu: Spring quality, therapeutic effects, and temperature all the same as above.

**Rikuzen Province — Shibata District — (Local) Aone**　Located in Maekawa Village. Recommended for women's blood disorders.

○ Myōgō-yu: Spring quality contains sulfurous vapor. Recommended for eye diseases.

○ Shin-yu: Spring quality the same as above. Recommended for colic, sword cuts, and similar conditions.

**Natori District — (Local) Akiho, also known as Natori-no-Oyu**　Two locations in Yumoto Village. Each spring quality contains both saline and sulfurous elements. Effective for scabies, sword cuts, *senkichu* (hernia/colic), and similar conditions.
