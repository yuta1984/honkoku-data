**Yama District — (Official) (Lyman) Atsushio**　Located in Atsushio Village, more than 7 *ri* northwest of Wakamatsu, approximately 4.5 *ri* southwest of Ōshio Village, and 2 *ri* from Kitakata Village. There are three springs. The spring water has a briny taste, contains 7 *monme* of common salt per *shō* of water, and takes on a reddish color due to iron rust. Recommended for apoplexy, beriberi, headaches, and similar conditions. Temperature: 200 degrees. There are two bathing establishments, with approximately 6,000 bathers per year.

**Same (Official) Ōhata-yu**　Located in the same village. Temperature: 85 degrees. Approximately 2,000 bathers per year.

**Same (Official) Numajiri**　Located on Mt. Azuma in Shirakishiro Village. Contains saline vapor. Recommended for colic, retained fluids (*ryūin*), phlegm and dyspnea (*tanshō*), accumulation (*shaku*), eye diseases, and similar conditions. Temperature: 212 degrees. One bathing establishment, with approximately 3,000 bathers per year.

**Same (Official) Yokomuki**　Located in the depths of Takamoriko-ya in Sanukawano Village, or alternatively said to be in Wakamiya Village. The spring water is clear and sparkling. Recommended for apoplexy, beriberi, sword cuts, bruises, eye diseases, and similar conditions. ○ Kami-no-yu (Upper Bath): temperature 60 degrees; spring quality contains sulfurous vapor. ○ Shimo-no-yu (Lower Bath): 80 degrees; spring quality the same.

**Same (Official) Kawakami-yu**　Located on the western bank of the Hinohara River, north of the Yunokami district of Shibuya Village, or alternatively Iwase Village. In autumn and winter it becomes cold and difficult to bathe in. Recommended for scabies. Tasteless. Temperature: 55 degrees.

**Same (Official) Bandai-shimo-no-yu**　Temperature: 90 degrees. Located approximately 2 *ri* from Ōtera Village, on the mid-slope of Mt. Bandai. Spring taste is sweet and acidic. Recommended for headaches, accumulation (*shaku*), eye diseases, and similar conditions. ○ Shin-yu: temperature 100 degrees; spring quality and therapeutic effects the same as above.

**Same (Local) Nichinaka**　Located in the Ōhata area of Nichinaka Village. A cold spring; recommended for sword cuts.

**Same (Miscellaneous) Ōshio**　Located on the right bank of the river in the center of Ōshio Village. Approximately 1,400 feet above sea level; approximately 5.5 *ri* northeast of Wakamatsu. Extremely briny taste. Recommended for scabies. There are two locations: Spring A at a temperature of 100 degrees, Spring B at 102 degrees. The water is turbid and brownish with a reddish tinge. The daily discharge volume is more than 10 *koku*. Until around Meiji 8 (1875), common salt was produced using this spring water.

**Same (Official) Oshitate-yu**　Temperature: 45 degrees. Taste is sweet and acidic.

**Same (□) Nishikubo-yu**　Temperature: 35 degrees. Taste is sweet and acidic.

**Iwase District — (Local) (Official) Yumoto**　Located in Yumoto Village. The spring quality is clear, without any odor, contains a saline element, and has a faint astringent taste. Temperature: 120 degrees. Effective for blood stagnation (*chi no kori*), poor digestion (*konare ashiki mono*), chlorosis (*ioō-byō*), exposure to cold (*bōkan*), and various diseases of the genital region. ○ Futamata-no and Tarāo Village's Nakagawa-yu are almost the same.

---

**Asaka District — (Local) (Official) Yasumiishi**　Located in Tadano Village, also said to be in Yamanobe Village. Temperature: 80 degrees. Spring quality contains sulfurous and saline vapors; recommended for scabies, scalp sores, sword cuts, burns, hemorrhoidal conditions, and similar ailments. One bathing establishment, with more than 2,000 bathers per year.

---

**Adachi District — (Local) (Official) Atami**　Located in Takadama Village. Spring quality and temperature: same as above. Particularly known for remarkable efficacy against eye diseases, syphilis, scabies, hemorrhoidal conditions, dog bites (*inu kamare*), insect and snake bites (*mushi hebi ni sasaretaru mono*), bamboo and wood injuries, and similar conditions. One bathing establishment, with more than 7,400 bathers per year.

**Same (Local) (Official) Takeno-yu, also known as Fukabori-yu**　Located in Nagata Village. Temperature: 110 degrees, or alternatively 101 degrees. Contains sulfurous and aluminous vapors. Recommended for rheumatism, hemorrhoidal conditions, scabies, scalp sores, goose-pox (*ganso*), burns, sword cuts, and similar conditions. Two bathing establishments, with more than 12,000 bathers per year.
