**Suō Province — Yoshiki District — (Ra) Yuda**　Located in the western corner of Yamaguchi town. Within a span of approximately twenty *ken*, there are seven springs. The spring water issues from alluvial soil (*alluvium*).

(1) Temperature: 107.6°F　(2) Same: 109.4°F　(3) Same as above　(4) Same: 111.2°F　(5) Same: 113.0°F　(6) Same: 114.8°F　(7) Same: 123.8°F

The spring quality in all cases contains a considerable quantity of hydrogen sulfide, and is recommended for scabies and skin diseases. ○ Each bathing tub (*yuburne*) has a capacity of approximately 45 cubic *shaku*, and the water is apparently changed three times per day at intervals of roughly five to six hours. Judging from this, the volume of the spring output must be extremely small.

**Nankaidō (Nankai Road / Region)**

**Kii Province — Hidaka District — (Reference work "Ge") Ryūjin**　Located in Ryūjin Village. Temperature: 119.8°F. The spring quality contains salt and is of an alkaline nature; it is remarkably clear, colorless, and odorless. It is similar to Atami in Izu Province and Komono in Ise Province, though somewhat stronger than both. Refer to pages 7 and 5. Its therapeutic effects are similar to those of Komono in Ise.

**Same (Local) Kanba**　Located in Kaise Village. Spring quality contains sulfurous vapor; it is a cold spring and is heated before use. Recommended for minor sores and hemorrhoidal disease.

**Muro District — (Reference work "Ge") Kanayama**　Also known as Yuzaki. Located in Kanayama Village. Spring quality and therapeutic effects are equal to those of Ryūjin-yu. The following seven springs are collectively referred to as the "Seven Springs of Kanayama" (*Kanayama Shichi-yu*).

(1) *Hamayu* (Shore/Beach Spring)　Temperature: 106°F. Has a lye-like taste (*aku*; alkaline), carries carbonic vapor, and exhibits an alkaline reaction that further intensifies upon boiling. No precipitate forms.

(2) *Awayu* (Foam/Millet Spring)　Same: 93°F. Clear, colorless, and odorless. When agitated/shaken, it produces bubbles.

(3) *Moto-no-yu* (Original/Main Spring)　Same: 83°F. Spring quality same as above.

(4) *Saki-no-yu* (Cape/Point Spring)　Temperature unclear. Spring quality is similar to the following ones.

(5) *Mabu-no-yu* (Mine Spring)　Same: 110°F.

(6) *Senkiyu* (Hernia/Colic Spring)　Same: 122°F.

(7) *Yakata-yu* (Pavilion Spring)　Temperature same as above [122°F].

**Same (Local) Hamayu**　Located in Yukawa Village. Spring quality contains sulfurous vapor. Recommended for beriberi, apoplexy/stroke, and related conditions.
