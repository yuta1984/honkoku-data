**Hyūga Province — Morokata District — (Local) Takaharu**　Located in Fumoto Village. The spring quality contains two components, alum and saline vapors, forming a tepid spring (*bionsen*). Recommended for abdominal accumulation (*senshaku*), sword wounds, and soft chancre (*gekan*), among other conditions.

**Same (Local) Shiratori**　Located in Suenaga Village. The spring quality contains two components, alum and saline vapors, and is recommended for abdominal accumulation.

**Same (Local) Yoshida**　Located in Shōmyōji Village. Contains two components, alum and saline vapors; mild in nature, and recommended for hernial/abdominal pain (*senki*) and sword wounds.

**Same (Local) Kakutō**　Located at Higashi-Nagae-ura Village and Ebi-no. Recommended for abdominal accumulation and scabies.

**Ōsumi Province — Kuwahara District — (Local) Kinofusa**　Located approximately ten *ken* (roughly 18 meters) to the west of the Ōtsugawa River in Uchi Village. Contains two components, sulfurous and alum vapors, and is recommended for abdominal accumulation and minor sores.

**Same (Reference work "Ge") Iōdani (Sulfur Valley)**　Temperature: 212°F. Contains sulfuric acid and a large quantity of iron. Spring quality and therapeutic effects are similar to those of Tarutama in Higo Province. Refer to page 70. ○ (Local) Einoo — located at the western foot of Karakuni-dake in Nakatsu-gawa Village, approximately five *chō* (roughly 545 meters) from Iōdani. The spring quality contains sulfurous vapor and is recommended for abdominal accumulation and scabies. ○ Myōban-yu — located three *chō* (roughly 327 meters) into the interior from Iōdani; contains alum vapor and is recommended for eye ailments. ○ Tono-no-yu — located at the same site; spring quality is mild and recommended for constitutionally weak individuals. ○ Nakakawauchi, ○ Tara, ○ Hokonagi, ○ Tearai — spring quality and therapeutic effects all same as above.

**Same (Local) Shiobitashi**　Located in Odori-gō, Sukubo-ta Village. The spring quality contains alum vapor; mild in nature, and recommended for gonorrheal conditions (*rinshitsu*), rheumatic ailments, sword wounds, syphilis (*baidoku*), and malignant sores, among other conditions. Three bathing pools have been established. ○ Hiraochi — same village; spring quality and therapeutic effects same as Iōdani.

**Same (Local) Anraku**　Located on the western bank of the Anraku River in the same village. Contains sulfurous vapor and is recommended for abdominal accumulation. Particularly effective for softening muscles and bones.

**Same (Local) Kurinodake**　Belonging to Koba Village, located on the hillside of the mountain. Contains two components, sulfurous and alum vapors; therapeutic effects same as Iōdani.
