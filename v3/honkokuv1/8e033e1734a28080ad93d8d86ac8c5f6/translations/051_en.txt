(Continued from previous page) Although bathing methods must inevitably differ according to the patient's condition, swimming-style bathing is generally considered the best approach. This is because swimming naturally promotes physical movement in the patient. The bath water should circulate continuously so that fresh water is constantly supplied and the entire volume of water can be sufficiently replaced. Furthermore, the air inside the bathing room must be adequately ventilated. Under such conditions, there need be absolutely no fear of contracting infectious diseases through the bath water.
