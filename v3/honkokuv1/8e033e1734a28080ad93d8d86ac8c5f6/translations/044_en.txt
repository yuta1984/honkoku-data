**Same (Reference work "Ra") Ōfune**　Four locations; one *ri* inland from Kumatomari and "Usuzubi." ○ (1) Temperature: 173°F. Spring quality contains hydrogen sulfide. The spring water is small in volume and wells up from an "alluvial gravel" layer. ○ (2) Temperature: 165°F. ○ (3) Same, 163°F. ○ (4) Same, 154°F. The spring quality at all three locations is the same as (1).

**Nishi District — (Reference work "Ra") Yumoto**　Located one and a half *ri* from the western coast, upstream along the "Kennichi" River. Temperature: 109°F. Spring quality contains iron. Wells up from granite (*mikageishi*) or syenite.

---

**Shiribeshi — Kudo District — (Reference work "Ra") Yunosawa**　Located in Mombetsu, near Kudo; one *ri* from the sea, close to the Yunosawa copper mine. Two locations. One has a temperature of approximately 109°F; the other approximately 90°F. The spring water contains iron and wells up from pale green, altered argillite.

**Furuu District — (Reference work "Ra") Yudomari**　Four locations in the sea beside the Yudomari copper mine. The spring water wells up from "volcanic porphyry" (a type of volcanic rock) of a gray color tinged with pale green. ○ (1) Temperature: 91.5°F. Spring quality contains sulfur, green vitriol (ferrous sulfate), and gas. The local inhabitants use it as an eye-wash medicine. ○ (2) Same, 91°F. ○ (3) Same, 84°F. ○ (4) Same, 82°F. The spring qualities and therapeutic effects of all the above are the same as (1).

**Iwanai District — (Reference work "Ra") Yūnai**　Three *ri* to the southwest of Iwanai Village. Located on Mount Raidono; three locations within fifteen *ken* of one another. Three *chō* further away there is the coldest of the springs. ○ (1) Temperature: 121°F. Spring quality contains hydrogen sulfide; spring water wells up from pale blue-gray volcanic rock, with gas bubbles rising. ○ (2) Same, 117°F. Spring quality and other characteristics same as above. ○ (3) Same, 115°F. Spring quality and other characteristics same as above.

---

**Ishikari — Sapporo District — (Reference work "Ra") Jōzan**　More than six and a half *ri* from Sapporo, upstream along the Toyohira River; six locations within fifty *ken* of one another. ○ (1) Temperature: 192°F. Spring quality contains a small quantity of hydrogen sulfide; spring water wells up from granite-like volcanic rock. ○ (2) Temperature and spring quality same as above. ○ (3) Same, 189°F. Spring quality same as above. ○ (4) Same, 185°F. Spring quality same as above. ○ (5) Same, 181°F. Spring quality same as above. ○ (6) Same, 177°F. Spring quality same as above.

---

**Iburi — Horobetsu District — (Reference work "Ra") Noboribetsu**　Ten locations at and around the Noboribetsu sulfur grounds. Elevation approximately 900 *shaku* (roughly 273 m) above sea level. ○ (1) through (6): Temperature: 210°F; contains sulfur and gas; spring water wells up from alluvial soil (*alluvial fills*). ○ (7) Temperature: 181°F. ○ (8) Same, 178°F. ○ (9) Same, 173°F. ○ (10) Same, 118°F. The spring qualities of all the above are the same as (1).

**Yuko (Hot Lake)**　Temperature: 210°F. Spring quality contains sulfur and gas. Located approximately four *chō* to the northwest of Noboribetsu Hot Spring; approximately four *chō* in breadth; wells up from black mud. Situated at an elevation of 900 *shaku* above sea level.
