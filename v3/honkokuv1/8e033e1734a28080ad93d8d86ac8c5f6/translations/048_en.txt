Consequently, this naturally aids one's health and leads to the cultivation of the wholesome habit of retiring to bed early at night. Although mineral water is most efficacious, one must not mistakenly assume that it can digest excessive eating and drinking, dispel the ill effects of distilled spirits and intoxication, or compensate for the fatigue of dancing through the night.

The appropriate dosage for drinking mineral water is not the same across the various types of mineral springs, and therefore it is not possible to present a single fixed standard. Generally speaking, however, it may be said that one can take two to three cupfuls in the early morning each day and an additional two to three cupfuls during the course of the day. The cup in question is defined as one of ordinary, moderate capacity. The recommended method of drinking is to allow an interval of fifteen to twenty minutes between each cup, during which time it is advisable to stroll through a nearby forest. If the weather should prove to be less than fine, one should take a walk under the eaves of buildings in the vicinity of the mineral spring.

After drinking mineral water, one must without fail wait at least half an hour to one hour before taking a meal. Drinking water to the point that it overflows and floods the stomach is among the most strictly contraindicated things, and it can on occasion give rise to severe symptoms. There have been cases in which persons with chronic illness, through this very error, suffered an acute inflammation of the kidneys...
