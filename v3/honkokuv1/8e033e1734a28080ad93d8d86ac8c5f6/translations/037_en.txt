**Same (Reference work "Ge") Katsuura**　Located in Katsuura Village. An alkaline hot spring containing both sulfurous and saline vapors. Temperature: 97.5°F. The spring water is somewhat clear, but when exposed to open air for a long period, it becomes nearly milky in color. The odor of hydrogen sulfide is very strong; the taste is lye-like (*aku*) and unpleasant due to the presence of hydrogen sulfide. The spring quality and therapeutic effects are similar to those of Ōyu (*Izunoōyu*) in Izu Province. Refer to page 9.

**Same (Reference work "Ge") Hongū**　Located in front of Tōkōji Temple in Yunomine Village. Temperature: 191°F [?] ※1. The spring quality is a salt-containing alkaline spring with a small amount of sulfurous vapor. The water is somewhat clear with an unpleasant odor of hydrogen sulfide. Therapeutic effects are similar to those of Ryūjin-yu. Refer to page 63. ○ Adjacent to this, there is a spring called *Tsubakiyu* (Camellia Spring). Temperature: 82°F. The water is clear with a strong odor of hydrogen sulfide. ○ (Local) The bathing facilities are divided into six sections. The spring quality has a strong sulfurous content and is recommended for pain in the shoulders, spine, lower back, and legs; muscular cramps/joint spasms; beriberi; toxic wind ailments; various malignant sores; and related conditions.

**Same (Local) Kawayu**　Located in the river water of Uke River (*Ukegawa*), to the northwest of Kaisegawa Village; approximately one *ri* south of Yunomine Village. The spring quality contains sulfurous vapor and is recommended for numbness and pain in the four limbs and joints, toxic wind ailments, and moist/weeping sores.

**Same (Local) Maeyama**　Located in Maeyama Village. The spring quality contains sulfurous vapor and is recommended for minor sores and various swellings/abscesses.

**Iyo Province — Onsen District — (Reference work "Ra") Dōgo**　Located in Dōgo Village, slightly more than half a *ri* east of Matsuyama. This hot spring is extremely famous. Temperature: 104°F, which is comfortable for the human body. The spring quality has the smell and taste of sulfur, and the fact that it causes silver to turn black confirms the presence of a small amount of sulfurous vapor. The spring water issues from a stone cylinder of approximately four *shaku* in diameter, covered with a lid. The volume is sufficient to fill eight or more large bathing tubs. ○ (Local) It is known in antiquity as *Nigitatsunō Iwayu* ("Stone Hot Spring of Nigitatsu"). Recommended for cold-injury poisoning and minor sores.

**Niigori/Nii District — (Local) Yunotani**　Located at Yamazaki in Sunouchi Village. A cold spring containing sulfurous vapor. Recommended for hernia/colic, lower back pain, and minor sores.

**Ochi District — (Local) Kusukubo**　A cold spring. Located in Nibukawa Village. Spring quality contains sulfurous vapor. Effective for uterine disease in women.
