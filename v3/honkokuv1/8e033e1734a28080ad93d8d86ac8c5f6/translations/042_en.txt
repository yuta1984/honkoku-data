**Taki District — (Reference work "Ra" / Local) Yuda**　Located in Yuda Village. Within a single bathhouse, there are three springs within approximately fifteen *shaku* (roughly 4.5 meters) of one another. Additionally, approximately fifty *ken* (roughly 91 meters) away, there is one spring inside the bathhouse of Yamada Shōdayū. Its temperature is 109.4°F. The spring quality has the smell of sulfur and turns silver black. The temperature and volume of discharge of the three springs are as follows. ○ Gozu-yu — temperature same as above; discharge volume approximately 7 *to* 5 *shō* (roughly 135 liters) per minute. ○ Omae-yu — same, 105.8°F; discharge at minimum quantity. ○ Jitō-yu — same, 104°F; discharge approximately 1 *to* 2 *shō* (roughly 22 liters). All three of these springs have the smell of sulfur and leave behind white sulfurous precipitate. In their vicinity, soft, brownish-tinged *tuff* ("Chufa") is exposed at the surface. However, the spring water appears to well up directly from alluvial soil. ○ (Local) The spring quality contains two components, sulfurous and alum vapors, and is effective for abdominal accumulation and muscular/skeletal cramping (*sujibone no tsuru*), among other conditions.
