**Izumo Province — Ou District — (Ra) Tamatsukuri**

Located in Tamatsukuri Village. Within a span of approximately twenty *ken*, there are three springs. The spring water issues from slightly hard sandy rock of a greenish-gray or pale brownish color.

(1) Spring A is approximately five *ken* from the riverbank. Its output is approximately 1 *koku* 2 *to* 5 *shō* per minute, with a temperature of 147.2 degrees [Fahrenheit].

(2) Spring B is situated inside the guesthouse (Nishimura). Its output is the same as above; temperature is 144.5 degrees [Fahrenheit].

(3) Spring C is located on the riverbank. Its output is 3 *koku* 1 *to* 2 *shō*; temperature is 148.2 degrees [Fahrenheit].

○ The spring water appears to contain only a very small amount of mineral matter. It has no odor or taste, and likewise leaves no precipitate—though it does leave a trace of a white substance, which is likely common salt (*sodium chloride*). ○ In this village and in Yumachi (the spa town), craftsmen carve white agate, red agate, agate, "blue jasper," and black crystal (*morion*) into beautiful small ornamental articles.

**Ohara District — (Reference work "Ge") Ushio**　Located in Yumura.

**Nita District — (Local source) Misawa**　Located in Yumura. The above three locations all have spring water containing sulfurous vapor. Recommended for syphilis, various sores/ulcers, apoplexy/stroke, hernia-type colic, and beriberi.

**Iwami Province — Anno District — (Local) Koyahara**　Spring quality contains sulfurous vapor. Recommended for various minor sores.

**Same (Local) Shikaku**　Spring quality and therapeutic effects same as above.

**Nima District — (Local) (Ra) Yunotsu**　More than one and a half *ri* from Nishida, two springs are located on the coast. Temperature is approximately 109 degrees [Fahrenheit]. One spring contains alum, sulfur, and saline elements; recommended for head congestion/hot flushes, eye ailments, hernia-type colic, hemorrhoidal disease, and scabies. ○ The other spring contains four elements—sulfur, salt, iron, and niter—and is recommended for hernia-type colic, beriberi, and numbness in the hands and feet.

**Same (Local) Fukumitsu-Shimomura**　Spring quality contains two elements—sulfur and salt—and is lukewarm in temperature. Recommended for scabies and related ailments.

**Same (Local) Amakōchi**　Spring quality is clean and possesses a slight odor. Recommended for syphilitic sores, genital ringworm/eczema, fluid retention, and muscular/skeletal cramps and spasms.

**Naka District — (Local) Arifuku**　Spring quality contains sulfurous vapor. Recommended for head congestion/hot flushes and headaches. The water is clear and is also used for making tea and other beverages.

**Mimasaka Province — Katsunan District — (Reference work "Ge") Yunogō**　Located in Yunogō Village. Spring quality and therapeutic effects are similar to those of Atami in Izu Province. Refer to page 7.

**Nishinishijō District — (Local) Okutsu**　Located in Okutsu Village. Spring quality contains sulfurous vapor. Recommended for sword cuts/wounds, scabies, leprosy-type sores, and suppurating/ulcerating sores.

**Ōba District — (Local) Yubara**　Located in Yumoto Village. Spring quality same as above. Recommended for various festering sores and toxic ulcers.

**Majima District — (Local) Maka**　Located in Nakama Village. Two *ri* from Yubara hot spring. Spring quality same as above. Recommended for various malignant sores and weeping/ulcerating toxic sores.
