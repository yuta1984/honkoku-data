The residence of Kii-no-kami burned. In this vicinity, the interiors of Sakurada Gate and Babsaki Gate were largely collapsed. Outside Babasaki Gate, the residences of Endo Tajima-no-kami (12,000 koku) and Matsudaira Sagami-no-kami (325,000 koku) were half-burned. The fire-brigade residence also burned. Inside Sukiyabashi Gate, there were many collapses, and additionally the residences that were destroyed by spreading fire included those of Nagai Totomi-no-kami (36,000 koku) and Honda Nakatsukasa-no-taifu (50,000 koku). The guard station at Hibiya Gate burned. The residences of Matsudaira Tosa-no-kami and Tsuchiya Oigashira-no-kami suffered great damage. Outside the same gate, the residence of Matsudaira Hizen-no-kami (over 357,000 koku) burned. Inside the rear gate of the Choshu domain (over 369,000 koku), there was minor burning. The residences of Arima Bingo-no-kami (10,000 koku) and Nanbu Mino-no-kami (200,000 koku) burned. On the opposite side, the front tenement row of the Satsuma domain's (770,800 koku) costume/ceremonial residence (shozoku yashiki) was slightly burned. Inside Saiwai-bashi Gate, one middle tenement building each belonging to Yanagisawa Tokinosuke (151,280 koku), Ito Shuri-no-taifu (51,080-plus koku), and Kamei Oki-no-kami (43,000 koku) burned. The fire stopped there. At the corner of Tatsunokuchi, the residence of Morikawa Dewa-no-kami (10,000 koku) burned. In front of Ote (the main castle gate), the residence of Sakai Uta-no-kami (150,000 koku) burned, though the main gate survived. The facing residence also burned. In this area, the residences of the Echizen domain, the Hitotsubashi domain, and the Ogasawara domain suffered many collapses. From inside Gofuku-bashi through the interior of Sukiya-bashi, collapses were found in various places throughout.

《Section 19》 In the outer Sakurada area, since there are exclusively large daimyo residences with no small estates, the majority of the plastered-wall tenement rows (nurikome nagaya) collapsed. In Kasumigaseki, the Geishu (Hiroshima) domain residence suffered no particular damage, while at the Kuroda domain residence, tenement rows collapsed from the corner watchtower to the main gate. Many other domain residences also suffered considerable collapses. Around the top of Edomi-zaka and Nagata-cho, minor collapses were visible here and there. The Sanno Shrine was undamaged, and within Akasaka Gate, collapses were found in various places throughout the daimyo residences.
