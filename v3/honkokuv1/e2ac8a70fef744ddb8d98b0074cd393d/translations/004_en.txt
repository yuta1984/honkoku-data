[Section 5] Sakamoto 2-chome and 3-chome burned down. Otansu-machi was partially burned. However, from 1-chome toward Kanasugi, Miwa, and Daionji-mae areas, there was extensive collapse. Negishi suffered no particular damage. Various places in Yanaka were damaged; the five-storied pagoda of Tennoji Temple lost its metal spire rings (kurin). The Misaki and Dango-zaka areas suffered minor collapse.

[Section 6] Toeizan (Kan'ei-ji Temple) suffered no particular damage; only the lodging quarters (shukubo) sustained minor damage. In Ikenohata Kayamachi, both 2-chome and 1-chome were completely burned down after collapsing. The hillside areas remained, though many collapses occurred. Below Kiridoshi there was extensive collapse; Nakamachi had few buildings remaining on either side. Single-frontage streets suffered much collapse. Osukiya-machi was entirely collapsed. The estates along the road below Tenjin suffered major collapse.

[Section 7] Along the front and back of Hiradeji-mae Street in Shitaya, the group residences (kumiyashiki) suffered no particular collapse. A fire broke out from the middle of the eastern side of Ueno Hirokoji, burning Kita-Daimon-machi, Moto-Kuromon-machi, Ueno-machi 1-chome and 2-chome, Dobo-machi, Shin-Kuromon-machi, the retainer residences of Ueno, and along Onari-michi; the northeastern corner of the residence of Lord Inoue Chikugo-no-kami (10,000 koku) [family crest shown] was slightly burned. Daimon-machi, Kurumaza ka-machi, Choja-machi 1-chome and 2-chome, Shitaya-machi 2-chome, and the replacement lots (daichi) burned, stopping at one side of Naka-okachi-machi. In Sanmai-bashi Yokocho, the residences of Suzuki, Ishikawa, Sakamaki, Fukui, and Yamazaki burned. From the corner of Sanmai-bashi, the Nakane residence was half-burned. The residences of Onuma, Otsuka, Yamaki, Ota, and Otsuka burned. The residences of Minakawa and Matsui were spared. The residences of Narita, Horie, Tamura, Iihara, Tonegawa, Sugiyama, Mashimo, Tsuda, Hirano, and Oiwa, as well as Sugie and Yamamoto, burned. The residences of Akai, Yamada, Toya, Fujikawa, and Okamura burned. The Ito residence was half-saved while surrounded by fire. The residences of Ota, Sugita, Maeda, Shimizu, and Fujiyasu burned. The residences of Mitani, Hasegawa, and Nakanishi were spared. The residences of Funabashi, Tanimura, Watanabe, Hayami, Kawashima, Deguchi, and Murata burned. The Kano residence was half-burned.
