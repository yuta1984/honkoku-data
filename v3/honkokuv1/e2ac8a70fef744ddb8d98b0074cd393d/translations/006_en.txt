[Section 9] Inside Asakusa-mon, along Bakuro-machi-dori, around Ryogoku, and along Yanagihara-dori, there was little collapse. Around Hashi-machi, Yagenbo ri, Muramatsu-machi, and Hama-machi, collapse was minimal. Tomizawa-machi suffered severe collapse. Along Ningyocho-dori, both east and west, collapses were extremely numerous throughout. In the areas of Oyaji-bashi, Aramebashi, Kobuna-machi, Horie-machi, Horidome, Ise-machi, Setomono-machi, Uogashi (the fish market), Takara-machi, Ryogae-machi, Kugidana, Honcho, Odenma-machi, Koku-machi, Shirogane-machi, Abura-machi, and Shio-machi — storehouses (dozo) throughout were heavily damaged and collapsed, and as a result many people were injured when their homes collapsed.

[Section 10] In Kanda, Hashimoto-machi and Toyoshima-machi suffered slight damage; Koyanagi-machi and Hiranaga-machi suffered great collapse. From Sujikaigomon to Imagawa-bashi-dori, collapses were numerous throughout. Western Kanda (Nishi-Kanda) suffered minor damage throughout. Kamakura-gawara-dori suffered little collapse.
