《Section 22》 The northern and southern parts of Hatchobori suffered minor collapses. Teppōzu suffered many collapses, particularly heavily around Funamatsu-chō, and the residence of [family crest] Lord Matsudaira Awaji-no-Kami (15,000 koku) burned down. The daimyo residences throughout this area were almost entirely damaged, while the Inari Shrine suffered no particular harm. In Reiganjima, the districts of Minato-chō, Nagasaki-chō, Tomishima-chō, Kawaguchi-chō, the Minato-bashi thoroughfare, Kita-Shimbori, Minami-Shimbori, and Hakozaki-chō all suffered many collapses. Half of Minami-Shimbori, one side of Shio-chō, and the area up to Ōkawabata-chō burned. The residence of Lord Matsudaira Echizen-no-Kami survived. From there, the areas along the Koami-chō thoroughfare, Inaribori, around the residence of Lord Sakai Uta, from Jinzaemon-chō through the Ginza residences, the Kakigarachō area, and the Matsushima-chō area suffered many collapses. Hama-chō, the Manbe riverside thoroughfare, and the area up to the Great Bridge (Ōhashi) were mostly damaged. The residence of Lord Mizuno Dewa-no-Kami burned down.

《Section 23》 In Honjo, the temples were almost entirely destroyed, and the residences of daimyo lords and hatamoto (bannermen) were mostly collapsed, with those that escaped unscathed being rare. Among these, the lower residence (shimo-yashiki) of Lord Matsudaira Suō-no-Kami, across from the East Bridge (Higashi-bashi), burned down. Beyond that, many buildings collapsed in the Matsushima-chō area, and Koume and Kawara-chō suffered minor fires. In the Nakanogō area, there was extensive damage to temples. Arai-chō was entirely demolished. Around Ishihara, the temporary shelter of Ushinomizen (ox shrine deity), and the area around Benten-kōji, small residences burned. In addition, at Hitotsu-kuchi, fires broke out in two locations: in front of the Tenman-gū Shrine and in Kameido-chō. Around Yanagishima and Oshiage, there were many collapses in various places. A small number of townhouses near Hōonji-bashi also caught fire. Yoshida-chō, Yoshioka-chō, and the small residences around the Minami-warishita-sui (southern drainage canal) suffered great damage. Collapses were numerous as far as the vicinity of the Tsugaru domain's residence. The small storehouses and sheds along the O-takegura-mae thoroughfare (the shogunate's bamboo storehouse) were greatly collapsed.
