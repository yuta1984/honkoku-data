Collapsed houses were visible in various locations, but there was no major damage as far as Naito-Shinjuku and Naruko-cho areas. However, the stone wall of the Mannen-hi (a permanent sluice) of the Tamagawa Aqueduct was damaged and water overflowed. There was considerable damage around Tenno-yokocho and Samegahashi. The street in front of Aoyama Zenkoji Temple suffered great damage. There was minor damage extending through Dogenzaka-cho, Shibuya, Kitazawa, Kirigaya, and Shimo-Meguro. In addition, slight collapses were seen in various spots around Chojagamaru, Kogai-bashi, Higakubo, and Ryudo. Azabu overall had relatively little collapse, and there were only minor collapses scattered around Hiroo, Shirokanedai, and Meguro.

《Section 15》 Around Hongo and Yushima, minor collapses were visible in various places, but nothing particularly serious. However, the koji storehouse (mold-cultivation room) collapsed and suffered severe damage. The areas of Koishikawa-sakai, Kikuzaka, and Shimoda-cho suffered many collapses.

《Section 16》 Around Komagome and Sendagi, there were minor collapses in various places. From below Hakusan through Sashigaya-cho and Shimo-Tomizaka, collapses were numerous, while Sugamo and Somei had relatively few collapses. Minor collapses were found all the way to Oji and Itabashi.

《Section 17》 Outside Koishikawa Gate, the Suifu (Mito domain) residence's row tenement houses of one hundred units, and all the residences on the opposite side, collapsed completely, with five or six buildings burned. From below Ushitenjin Shrine through Suwa-cho to Ando-zaka, collapses were widespread. At Ryukan-bashi, the residence of Matsudaira Sanuki-no-kami burned. From there, Dentsu-in-dori had few collapses, while the back gate street had many collapses. Collapses were seen in various spots around Kohinata, Suido-cho, Sekiguchi, and Otowa. Mejiro and Shiina-cho were unaffected. Various collapses occurred in the Ushigome-tera-cho area, and Kaishinro-cho suffered major collapse. Furthermore, in the Ichigaya and Takada areas, collapses were numerous only in low-lying areas.
