《Section 20》 Outside Saiwai-bashi (Kōbashi), along the Atago-shita thoroughfare, through Kubo-chō, outside Shimbashi, along the Kiridōshi pass, and around the Zōjō-ji Temple's御成門 (O-nari-mon gate), collapses were observed in various places. From Shibaguchi to around Rōgetsu-chō and as far as the Shiodome area and the vicinity of the Sendai domain's upper residence, the shaking was relatively mild, and Shibai-chō (柴井町) first block burned. From there northward toward the Ōmon (Great Gate) area, the earthquake was severe, and in Mishima-chō, Myōjin-chō (Shinmei-chō), and the seaside daimyo residences, a great many buildings were entirely collapsed. From Hamamatsu-chō northward through Nakamon-mae, Katamon-mae, Kanasugu, Motoshiba, Tamachi, Mita, Hijiri-zaka, and Koyama, minor damage was observed here and there, but nothing of particular severity. Around Akabane, the residence of Lord Arima suffered severe collapse. The Suitengū Shrine was undamaged. From Hijiri-zaka to Isarago-dai, collapses were numerous. Takanawa Dai-machi suffered considerable damage. Shirogane, Nihon-enoki, and Goten-yama suffered no particular damage, and damage along the Takanawa thoroughfare was minimal. One watchtower (monomi) of the Satsuma domain lord's residence collapsed. Shinagawa post-town (shuku) suffered minor damage. One inn called "Ichiya" was completely destroyed, and beyond the bridge, there were many collapses elsewhere.

《Section 21》 South of Nihonbashi, along the east-west central thoroughfare, the Gofuku-bashi riverside road, and the Shinba riverside road, the area around Naka-bashi suffered no particular damage. From Minami-Tenma-chō, collapses became numerous, and from its second block through to the edge of Kyōbashi, fires burned. To the west, Minami-Kaji-chō, Minami-Daiku-chō, and Gorōbei-chō burned. Tatami-chō had a small portion that survived amid the fires, and the burning extended as far as Daikon-Kashi (Daikon Riverside). To the east, one side of the streets of Gusoku-chō, Yanagi-chō, Inaba-chō, Suzuki-chō, Tokiwa-chō, and Matsukawa-chō burned, and the fire stopped at Sumi-chō and Take-gashi (Bamboo Riverside). Across Kyōbashi toward Yumi-chō, damage was considerable in various places. Ginza and Sanjūkkenbori suffered few collapses. Kohiki-chō and Tsukiji also sustained little damage.
