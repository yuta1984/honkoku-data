The small estates in this vicinity were largely destroyed by collapse, and the Iga residence burned. Along Myojin-shita-dori, the front row tenements (omote-nagaya) of the residences of Lord Tatebe Takumi-no-kami and Lord Naito Bungo-no-kami collapsed. The areas of Myojin-shita, Odaidokoro-machi, Dobo-machi, Kanazawa-machi, and Hatagomachi were largely damaged. Shohei-bashi and surrounding areas suffered minor collapse. Yushima Yokocho and 1-chome suffered little collapse. Kanda Myojin Shrine suffered no particular damage. Outside Sujikaigomon, Hanabusa-machi, Nakamachi 1-chome, and 2-chome — along Onari-michi and in the townspeople's quarter (machiya), both east and west sides extending to the rear streets — were largely collapsed. Matsunaga-machi and Aio-machi were similarly affected.
