**Classical Chinese passage (kanbun):**
Though one may scheme and plot for immediate profit and gain, one shall inevitably incur the punishment of the gods and divine spirits.
Though honesty may not always appear to bring immediate favor, in the end it shall receive the compassion of the sun and moon (i.e., the benevolence of Heaven).

**Japanese verse:**
Gods and Buddhas alike,
I am told,
despise it —

Falsehood and deceit
are a person's greatest
flaw and blemish.
