　A Poem on the Earthquake and Tsunami
The earthquake and the raging waves (tsunami) — such devastation has not been seen since the Hōei [disaster].
Standing on the brink of peril, one may luckily escape that very peril,
yet in fleeing calamity, one may turn and find oneself caught in calamity again.
Houses collapse and bury the roads; bridges fall and ships' masts tower above the waters.
If one wishes to understand and record the events of this recent disaster,
one must surely seek out this very publication.
　　　　　　　　Sankō Rōjin, composed in jest
