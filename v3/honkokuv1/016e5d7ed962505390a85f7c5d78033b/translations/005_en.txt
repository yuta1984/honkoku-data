**Dialogue section:**

Master (Danna):
"Last year, no sooner had I bought the rice cakes than I gobbled up thirteen of them — but every now and then there were pebbles and grit mixed in, and they were altogether dreadful. This year's rice cakes, though, are excellently made — delicious, delicious, delicious!"

Maidservant (Gejo):
"And well they should be, sir! This year's rice cakes are, first of all, made from fine-quality rice, and on top of that, Usa-san pounded them so carefully and thoroughly with the pestle. The reason there happen to be no grit or pebbles mixed in is that, rather than leaving it to a hired rice-cake pounder, he shook and pounded them himself — just like an earthquake!"

**Explanatory text:**

*Yonaori Zōshi* (A Booklet on the Restoration/Reform of the World)
　○ An Explanation of Earthquakes and Great Waves (Tsunamis)

Earthquakes are caused by the two forces of Yin and Yang. In their most violent form, they topple mountains, fill in the sea, and crush human dwellings. That is to say: when Yang energy, suppressed beneath the earth, cannot emerge, and Yin energy, pressing down upon it, cannot ascend — then, at last, with no other recourse, it erupts and causes trembling and shaking. In spring and summer, Yang energy rises out from beneath the earth while Yin energy sinks into it; in autumn and winter, Yin energy floats upward from beneath the earth while Yang energy descends and submerges within it — such is the regular principle of Yin and Yang.
