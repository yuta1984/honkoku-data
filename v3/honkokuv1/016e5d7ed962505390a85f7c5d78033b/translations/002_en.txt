How auspicious! The realm united as one, peacefully
governed — a time when all things are set right anew.

　The above: A celebratory verse on the occasion of the earthquake

　　Author's Preface

In general, thunder and earthquakes arise from the disorder of the seasons and climate,
and represent the furious energy (*toki*) of Yin and Yang. The reason they occur is precisely because people's
conduct is not virtuous, evil influences abound, and people are cold-hearted and
fail to live in harmony with one another. Because of this, Yin and Yang also fail to harmonize,
falling into disorder and bringing down calamity. This is Heaven's [warning]...
