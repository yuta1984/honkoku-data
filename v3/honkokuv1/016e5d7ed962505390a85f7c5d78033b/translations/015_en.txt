Seven Calamities Instantly Extinguished,
Seven Blessings Instantly Born.

One who upholds the Way
of benevolence, righteousness, and the Five Constants,
and who practices hidden virtue,
shall see their household grow wealthy and flourish,
their descendants multiply and prosper,
enduring long — how auspicious!
　　　　　　How auspicious indeed!

　　○ The Method for Escaping Heavenly Disaster and Attaining Happiness

Now, although it may be said that encountering Heaven's calamities is fate — Heaven's decree — from which there is no escape, Heaven's decree likewise originates from within oneself and is never fixed or immutable.

Yuan Liaofan of the Ming dynasty heard a prophecy from a remarkable stranger that his fate was to remain poor and childless and to die at the age of fifty-three. Afterward, he visited the Chan master Yungu at Qixia Temple and inquired of him about the teachings of the Dharma. The master expounded the learning of "establishing one's own destiny," explaining that all things in the ten thousand phenomena are furnished within oneself, and that Heaven's decree, too, originates from within oneself and is never fixed.
