The carpenters, for their part, turn out slapdash work with no care for the future, so that damage comes very quickly: buildings lean in the wind and collapse in earthquakes, causing great hardship to many.

Furthermore, those who have risen in station by scraping together a small amount of money become arrogantly puffed up, looking down on others and knowing nothing of proper conduct. Buddhist clergy use the Buddha as a signboard to collect money for their own luxury and comfort, passing through the world in violation of both monastic precepts and the law. Farmers, for their part, always complain and grumble with the calculated intent of making it appear that they have less to contribute in taxes and levies than they truly do.

Wherever one looks, there is nothing good to be seen — only self-interest, favoritism, and selfish convenience. Those of high standing oppress those of low standing and possess not a shred of compassion; consequently, those of low standing do not respect those above them, and feelings of resentment arise again and again.

Thus the yin and yang of the people do not harmonize; this malign energy naturally reaches up to Heaven, the warmth and cold of the four seasons fall into disorder, and disaster has arrived at this point. Alas — if we do not reform our hearts at this very moment, what further calamities from Heaven may yet await us?

Above all, if the spirit of the people is reformed here and now, and if inhumanity and injustice are held in check, then — as rain falls and firms the ground — disorder becomes the very foundation of order. Naturally, Heaven and Earth, yin and yang, will harmonize; the climate of the four seasons will right itself; the waves and winds of the four seas will grow calm; and under a great peace throughout the realm, with the five grains brought to fruition, joy shall be added upon joy.

　　If only one's heart accords with the true Way,
　　　　the gods will protect you — even without prayer.
