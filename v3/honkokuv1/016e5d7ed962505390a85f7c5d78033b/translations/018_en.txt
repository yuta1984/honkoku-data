**[Copy of the Official Notice Board]**

**Ordinance**

Article 1: Be close with parents, children, siblings, spouses, and all other relatives, and show compassion to servants and those of low station. Those who serve a master shall each devote themselves diligently to their duties.

Article 2: Devote yourself to your household occupation without neglect, and in all matters, do not exceed the bounds appropriate to your station in life.

Article 3: Do not engage in deception, do not make unreasonable demands, and do not do anything that would generally cause harm to others.

Article 4: Gambling of all kinds is strictly prohibited.
