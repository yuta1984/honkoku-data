(The earth too) revolves and is said to move gradually. However, the nature of the earth is such that north is yang and south is yin. Most mountain ranges are in the north. The nature of heaven is such that south is yang and north is yin. Therefore, the sun travels through the south. This is the form of heaven and earth being round and complete, linked together as one. Thus, even in ancient sayings: "If the earth decreases by one foot, one foot of heaven is born. In its original nature, without distinction, where could south or north be?" Furthermore, just as the yolk of an egg is round and complete in form, this is why it is called the "earth-sphere" (*chikyū*). Its circumference is said to be roughly ninety thousand *ri*. There is also the saying, "Six seas, three mountains, one flat land," meaning the seas comprise six-tenths, the mountains three-tenths, and the (flat) land one-tenth. It is for this reason that the character "埊" was used to write "地" (earth) — this is its compound ideographic meaning.

Now, the reason earthquakes occur is that yang energy (*yōki*) is submerged beneath the yin and, pressed upon by yin energy (*inki*), is unable to rise upward. At this point, the earth cracks, moves, and comes to shake. This is because the yang energy loses its proper place and is blocked by yin energy. Moreover, within the earth there are cavities (*ana*) like a beehive, through which water seeps and yang energy constantly flows in and out. It is normal for yin and yang to harmonize through this process and achieve proper balance.
