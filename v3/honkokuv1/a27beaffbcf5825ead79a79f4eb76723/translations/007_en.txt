## A View of the Imagire of Enshū (*Enshū Imagire no Kei*)

**Haiku inscribed in the illustration:**

*Imagire ya / Fuji yori tsuki no / issenri*
"At Imagire — the moon, a thousand leagues away from Fuji."

## Historical Notes:

In the reign of the 105th human emperor, Emperor Gototsumikado, on the sixth day of the eighth month of the seventh year of Meiō (1498), a great earthquake struck, and the lake (Lake Hamana) and the sea merged into one, becoming an inlet (*iriumi*). This is called the Imagire ("the newly opened cut").

In the reign of Emperor Gokashiwabara, on the twenty-seventh day of the eighth month of the seventh year of Eishō (1510), there was a sound like a conch shell (*hora*), followed by a landslide, it is said. [This is recorded in the *Ōnin Chronicle* (*Ōnin-ki*).]
