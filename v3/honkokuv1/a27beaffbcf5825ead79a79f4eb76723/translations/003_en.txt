Record of Earthquakes in Our Country (*Honchō Jishinki*)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Compiled by Toyo Tokishige of Heianjo [Kyoto]

Now, the character "地" (earth/ground) was in ancient times written as "埊." This is a compound ideograph (*kaiji*). In the *Shiji* (Records of the Grand Historian) and the *Hanshu* (Book of Han), it is written as "墜." The character "震" means movement, and it is also said to mean rage or anger. Heaven moves and creates the four seasons; the earth remains still and nurtures all things. And yet, heaven revolves to the left, and the earth also revolves to the right without ceasing. It is like a person sitting in a boat with the windows closed — they do not realize that the boat is naturally moving forward. For this reason, heaven moves, and the earth too revolves (*meguri*)...
