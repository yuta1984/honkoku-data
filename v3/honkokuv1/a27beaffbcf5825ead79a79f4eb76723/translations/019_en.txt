This being, as it has always been, an age of great peace, the authorities especially issued commands for prayers to be offered at the various shrines and temples throughout the land. As a result, by around the end of the seventh month, the frequency of the tremors had at last begun to diminish, and now, in early the eighth month, they have dwindled to a mere five or six times per day and night. This is truly a blessed and enlightened reign, and all people rejoice together. With deepest reverence.

　This single volume was written not in the least as a diversion or entertainment for the world. It is because in distant provinces and remote regions, all manner of rumors have been circulating, and I have heard that those who have relations or acquaintances in Kyoto have been unable to find peace of mind, day or night. It is for this reason that I have set down the general outline of events, with the sole wish that those in far-off places may be put at ease.
