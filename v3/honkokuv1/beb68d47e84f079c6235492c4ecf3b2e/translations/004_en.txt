[Sensō-ji Temple Precinct and Surrounding Areas – Continued]

Various shrines within the precinct of (Sensō-ji Temple) suffered damage. Within the same temple grounds, the following sub-temples sustained major damage: Shōchi-in, Chōju-in, Shōzō-in, Jumyō-in, Shōfuku-in, Chikō-in, Baimei-in, Jissō-in, Shōju-in, Kinzō-in, Kanchi-in, and Nichion-in. Iroha tenement houses located behind these sub-temples also suffered major damage. The main Kannon Hall, the main gate (Sanmon), the bell tower (Shōrō), the Zuishin gate, the Raijin gate (Thunder Gate), and the Sanza Nembutsu-dō—all of these survived unscathed. The five-storied pagoda's nine-ringed finial (kurin) was slightly tilted. The area from Higashi-bashi (East Bridge) along the river survived as far as Sanya-bori canal, though many houses there had collapsed. Among these, Matsuchi-yama was unharmed. The area along Sanya-bori burned for approximately half a chō (roughly 55 meters) as far as Imado-bashi bridge. The areas in front of and behind the Zeni-za (coin mint) suffered severe fire damage and had an exceptionally large number of collapsed houses. The eta-machi in the same area survived the fire. From Henshō-ji Temple and Sōsen-ji Temple through to Masaki Inari Shrine, many houses were severely damaged. Two restaurants called Kawaguchi and Yanagiya were destroyed by fire. Continuing through Sanya-machi, Asakusa-machi, Nakamura-machi, Kozukappara-machi, and Senju-shuku, there was extensive structural damage as far as Ōhashi (the Great Bridge). There were also many buildings lost to fire in Senju. Details on this area shall be issued as an addendum.

[Annotation ㊁ – Around Asakusa Hongan-ji Temple]

The main hall of Asakusa Hongan-ji Temple survived intact. Within the temple complex, Kyōkaku-ji, Senshō-ji, Gyokusen-ji, Shōgan-ji, Enshō-ji, Hōyū-ji, Ungyō-ji, Raiō-ji, and Kōen-ji, along with other sub-temples, suffered major damage. The Monzen-machi (gate town) of Seigan-ji in the same area had many collapsed houses. The area around the Monzen-machi of Nichirin-ji and its surroundings was so thoroughly destroyed that it was difficult to distinguish the damage by individual town.
