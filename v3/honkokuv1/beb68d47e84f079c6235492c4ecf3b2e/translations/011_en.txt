⑯In the same area (Fukagawa), from the replacement-land district (daichi) of Honjo Ishiwara-machi to the addition-land (soichi) of Hirano-machi, approximately one chō burned. Many houses collapsed in this vicinity. To the east, from Rokumantsubo through Mōri Shinden and Suna-mura, and further east still, damage was widespread in all directions, with an uncountable number of collapsed houses. Along the temple-town street (tera-machi-dōri) in this area, temples of various sects suffered major damage. At Jōshinji, the main hall was undamaged, but three sub-temples within the precincts, the inner gate, front gate, and the water ablution pavilion (temizusha) all collapsed, and the odaimoku stone monument at the gate fell over. At Reiganjji, the front gate collapsed, and the main hall and the temple precinct suffered major damage. Honseiji suffered major damage. From there, along Shōgakuji-bashi-dōri, Mannen-machi, Isshiki-machi, and Sankaku — the storehouses, godowns, townspeople's dwellings, and various temples in this area largely collapsed. To the left of Eitaibashi: Saka-machi, Mannenbashi, Reiunin, Kaibe Daiku-machi, Isesaki-machi, and Kiba — collapsed houses were numerous in these areas. In Hirano-machi, east and west, Lord Tachibana's residence suffered major damage. To the west, Okugawa-machi, Ensokuji, and Mantokuin all suffered major damage. To the south along the Onagigawa waterway, Saruen and its surroundings suffered damage so extensive in all directions that words alone cannot describe it.

⑰To the right of Ōhashi (the Great Bridge), from Fukagawa Motomachi and Lord Matsudaira Tōtōmi's lower residence (shimo-yashiki) all the way to Saruko-bashi, everything collapsed entirely. To the left, the residence of Kinoshita Zusho collapsed. The Atake government boat storage (Go-funagura) was unharmed. The Honjo Hitotsume Benten shrine suffered major damage. The Hachiman temporary shrine (tabidokoro), Saikōji, and Hatsune Inari — many structures in this area collapsed. A fire broke out from the street in front of the Go-funagura, and the small residences to the south of the Daibutsuden (Great Buddha Hall), the government office, the Meijin-sama shrine, and Akiba-yado-dera were damaged and subsequently burned. To the east of this area, a fire broke out half a chō north of Rokken-bori and Abegawa, and sparks flew south to Morishita-machi. There are areas of collapse and burned ground in this vicinity. The rice granary (momigura) along Rokken-bori-dōri to the north of Yanagawa-machi burned slightly. In this area, at the Shinmei monzen-machi (the gate-front town of the Shinmei shrine), the main shrine survived, but the entire surrounding area burned. Lord Inoue Kawachi's residence burned. Fires broke out from Tokiwa-machi, and Lord Ogasawara's residence burned slightly. The long tenement building (omote-nagaya) at Lord Ōta's residence burned slightly. The fire stopped at Takabashi.
