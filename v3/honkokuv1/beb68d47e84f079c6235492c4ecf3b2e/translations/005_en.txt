[Annotation ㊁ – Around Asakusa Hongan-ji Temple – Continued]

(The half-chō in front of Gyōan-ji Temple across Chaya-bashi bridge) burned. The area opposite to the west of the same location burned for over one chō (approximately 110 meters) as far as Shinbori-bata. The vicinity of Abekawa-machi had many collapsed houses. On the left side of Komagata-machi, everything on the south side from the corner just before a restaurant called Hatsufuji burned completely. In this area, not a single earthen warehouse (dozō) survived. Both sides of Suga-machi burned. Kurofune-machi burned. However, the plasterer's shop and earthen warehouse in the same area survived. The rice wholesaler on the right corner was half preserved. In Miyoshi-machi, the riverside section survived. To the south, the fire was stopped at Omaya-kashi (the horse stable riverside district). The Kuramae thoroughfare suffered no damage.
