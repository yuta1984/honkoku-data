(Continued from previous page) Among the residences and neighborhoods that escaped fire, collapsed houses were exceedingly numerous. The front gate of Mirokuji collapsed, and Yōtsuji and Keichōji suffered major damage. To the east along Iyobashi-dōri,

⑱ fires burned through Tokuuremon-machi as far as the second and third blocks. All areas suffered heavy collapse and damage.

⑲ To the right (west) of Ryōgokubashi, the stone embankment of Ichinohashi collapsed. At Ekōin in Motomachi, the bell tower collapsed. Lord Honda Kuranoskē's residence suffered major damage. From the first through the fifth blocks of Aioi-machi, all suffered major collapse. The first and second blocks of Midori-machi burned; the fire stopped at the corner of the third block. The fourth and fifth blocks of the same area, and Hana-machi, burned as far as the vicinity of Shukibashi. Lord Tsugaru's residence suffered major damage. Yanagiwara 5-chōme, Kayaba-machi 4-chōme, Kameido Tenjin, the travelers' lodgings, Honjo Kawara-machi, Matsushiro-machi, 4-chōme Nakanogō, and on to Itsutsubashi-machi — all suffered complete and major collapse, with not a single storehouse (dozō) left standing.

⑳ Near the ferry crossing at Honjo Itsutsu-me, approximately ten houses burned and the fire extinguished itself. Kameido Tenjin, Fumon'in, Kōmyōji, Hagiji (Ryūgenji), Kōzōji, Chōjuji, Kawabe Chōtokuji, Kameido-machi, Yanagishima Hōshōji, Myōken-gū, Jōshōji, Oshiage-mura, Saikyōji, Daiunji, Tokushōji, Eisenji, Zenshōji, Shunkeiji, Hōninji-bashi-dōri, Reizanji, Honpōji, Daihōji, Shinseiji, Minami-Honjo Idemura-machi, and Kita-Warishimizu — in this area, many small residences collapsed and several storehouses crumbled. On the Ōkawa (Sumida River) side, the middle residence (naka-yashiki) of Lord Tōdō Izumi and the warehouse residence (kura-yashiki) of Lord Tsugaru suffered major damage. Lord Matsumae Izunokami's bamboo storehouse (o-takegura) was unharmed. Along Komadome-ishi-dōri, Lord Sugiura Ikino-kami's residence, the lower residences (shimo-yashiki) of various daimyō in the same area, Lord Mogami's residence, Sekiunji in Ishiwara-machi, the government ship inspection office (o-fune-aratame-yakusho), the government stables (o-umaya), and on through to the riverside street — all suffered major damage, with not a single storehouse left standing.

㉑ In Honjo Ishiwara-machi, a stretch of half a chō burned. Lord Mukai Shōgen's residence, Lord Tokuyama's residence, the Ushijinja temporary shrine (tabidokoro), Ishiwara-machi, the residential area of the o-gumi (guard units) Arai-machi, and Matsukura-machi — all of these suffered complete collapse.

㉒ 【Circled】 In the same area, half a chō of Nakanogō Omotemachi burned.
