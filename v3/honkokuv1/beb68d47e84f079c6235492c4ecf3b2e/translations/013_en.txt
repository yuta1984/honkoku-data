Lord Tsugaru's warehouse residence (kura-yashiki) suffered major damage. Lord Matsumae Izunokami's bamboo storehouse (o-takegura) was unharmed. Along Komadome-ishi-dōri, Lord Sugiura Ikino-kami's residence, the lower residences (shimo-yashiki) of various daimyō in the same area, Lord Mogami's residence, Sekiunji in Ishiwara-machi, the government ship inspection office, the government stables, and on through to the riverside street — all suffered major damage, with not a single storehouse left standing.

㉑ In Honjo Ishiwara-machi, a stretch of half a chō burned. Lord Mukai Shōgen's residence, Lord Tokuyama's residence, the Ushijinja temporary shrine (tabidokoro), Ishiwara-machi, the residential area of the o-gumi (guard units) Arai-machi, and Matsukura-machi — all of these suffered complete collapse.

…bridge, Nanzōin, Enmeiji, and Lord Hōjō Shinzō's residence — in this area, few of the group residences (kumi-yashiki) survived. Lord Hosokawa Noto's, Lord Matsudaira Echizen's, and Lord Takenokohi Hyōbu's residences, as well as the tile-firing kilns of Shimo-Kawara-machi, all collapsed. In addition, a great many storehouses crumbled — too numerous to count.

㉓ 【Circled】 A restaurant called Ogura-taka in Koume-mura and Kawara-machi burned. The surrounding area also caught fire. Lord Mito's lower residence (shimo-yashiki), the precincts before Jōsenji gate, Azuma-mori, the Sanjūri (Three-Circuit) Inari shrine, Ushijinja, Chōmeiji, and the embankment of the Sumida River all suffered damage — the riverbank cracked open and mud was expelled from the ground in great quantities. The sakura-mochi (cherry blossom rice cake) shop and many other buildings also collapsed. In the same area, Kōfukuji of the Ōbaku sect (Gozuzan Kōfukuji) completely collapsed. Suwa Myōjin, Shirahige Myōjin, Umeyashiki, Hōsenji, Kiboji, and Umewaka-zuka and other sites suffered major damage. Many teahouses within the precinct grounds (in'nai) also collapsed. Kamogafuchi and the Suijin-no-mori (Water God's Grove) suffered severe damage. Beyond that, Suda-mura, Terashima-mura, Wakamiya-mura, Hikifune-dōri, Maroudo Daimyōjin (Marebito Daimyōjin), Kinokawa, Jōkōji, Yakushidō, Minami-Funabori, Kasai, Shibamata, Kamewari (Kameari), Nigōhan-ryō, and other surrounding villages all suffered major damage. The area in front of the Nakagawa government checkpoint (goban-sho) also collapsed entirely.

㉔ 【Circled】 In Ogawa-machi, the residences of Lord Hotta, Lord Toda, and the kōke (ceremonial official house) of Lord Toda, along with a small number of smaller residences, suffered major damage throughout the surrounding area. A small area also burned.
