To the north of Nihonbashi, from Honchō as far as Imagawa-bashi, there was major damage. East of Jikken-dana, the areas of Honjoku-machi-dōri, Ōdenma-machi, Kodenma-machi, Hatagomachi, Abura-machi, through to Yokoyama-machi, Ryōgoku Hirokōji, Bakurō-machi, and as far as Yanagiwara-dote all suffered major damage. Likewise, the Gundai-yashiki (the magistrate's estate), Toshima-machi, Lord Hosokawa Genba's residence, Benkeibashi, Otamagaike, Lord Ichihashi Shimōsa's residence, Koyanagi-machi, the rice-storage granary, and Suta-machi all suffered major damage, with many storehouses collapsing. Within the Sujikaigomon gate, the residences of Lord Aoyama, Lord Sakai, Lord Doi Noto, Lord Matsudaira Saemon, Lord Inaba Tango-no-kami, Lord Tsuchiya Uneme, Lord Toda Takejirō, Lord Naitō Suruga, Lord Hotta Bitchū-no-kami, Lord Itō Wakasa, Lord Sakakibara Shikibu, Lord Honda Iyo-no-kami, Lord Honda Buzen-no-kami, the Office of the Finance Magistrate (Go-kanjō-bugyōsho), and Mikawa-machi — all residences and townspeople's houses in this entire area suffered major damage.

⑭Eitaibashi, Aikawa-machi, the government boat storage (Go-funagura), and the naval guard unit (Go-funade-gumi) all suffered damage. Only about three buildings near the gate survived; Seigenji temple in Kumai-machi burned. Tomiyoshi-machi, Nakajima-machi, Kitagawa-machi — the right side of Kuroe-machi had about half a block remaining. To the east of this area, Hamaguri-machi, Ōjima-machi, and Sainenji temple burned. At the first torii gate, in front of Eitaiji temple, Yamamoto-machi, and Nakamachi, the fire on the right side stopped at the corner of Eitaiji's main gate. On the left side, it stopped half a block short. The Hachiman Shrine was unharmed, but two stone torii gates suffered major damage, and many of the subsidiary shrines within the precincts were damaged. Many houses in the temple's monzen-machi (gate-front town) collapsed. Sanjūsangendō and the timber district (Kiba) suffered major damage. To the east of there, Irifune-machi collapsed. The Suzaki Benten shrine, teahouses, and other structures were damaged. The lumber storage yards were damaged. The residences of Lord Ashū, Lord Etchū, and Lord Sakakibara, as well as Zuiunji temple, suffered slight damage. There were no fires in this area.
