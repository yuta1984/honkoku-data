〽 The upheavals of heaven and earth are beyond knowing even now, in this floating world of uncertainty. This year, on the fifteenth day of the sixth month, around the sixth hour of the afternoon [approximately 7:30 p.m.], along the Ōshū coastline and extending as far as the distant northern land of Ezo — Hokkaidō — a great tsunami raged over a stretch of more than one hundred ri. Let me recount the events in some detail.

It being the fifth day of the fifth month by the old calendar, from the fishing towns to the villages far and near, people were resting for the seasonal festival. But from around midday, black clouds began to rise, and the constant roaring of the sea and trembling of the earth filled the people with a sense of dread. Then came a thunderous rumbling shock, as though a great cannon had been fired all at once —

before one could even cry out, how terrifying! Waves surging to more than ten jō in height crashed down all at once, sweeping over forty-four or five ri of coastline. Mountains crumbled, and whole households were swallowed by the sea. Such was its fury: along the coastal districts of Iwate Prefecture, across seven towns and twenty-nine villages, more than six thousand homes were swept away, and over thirty-seven thousand people lost their lives. In Kesen District, across fifteen villages, some seventeen hundred homes were washed away, and more than six thousand three hundred people perished.
