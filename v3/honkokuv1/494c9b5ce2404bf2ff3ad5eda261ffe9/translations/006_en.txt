Throughout the country, schoolchildren, sumo wrestlers, kabuki actors, courtesans, women of the pleasure quarters, geisha, and all manner of people with warm and generous hearts vied with one another to offer relief. Truly, how heartening such spirit is! And all of this, it is said, is none other than the manifestation of the benevolent virtue and august grace of the Imperial Court — a matter most reverend and humbling to hear. Yare!
