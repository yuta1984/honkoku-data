Below (Akagi Shrine): In this vicinity, town houses (machiya), small residences (koyashiki), and group residences (kumiyashiki) alike show many collapsed buildings throughout. Enoki-chō suffered considerable damage, and small residences and group residences along Otansu-machi street also have collapsed buildings here and there. Yanagi-chō and the Takada area suffered damage across the entire district; the group residences along Wakamatsu-chō street and Fukuro-chō were completely devastated. In Ōkubo and Nuke-Benten, many town houses and small residences collapsed, and the gate tenement of the Nagasawa estate crumbled. Brothels in Shinjuku were damaged in various places, and the group residences around Tani-machi and Kappa-zaka were largely damaged. The lateral residences along Banchō street suffered damage in places, and the area outside Ichigaya Gate was slightly damaged. The mansion of the Lord of Owari suffered damage in places; group residences along the Yotsuya street and around Iga-chō were severely damaged in various locations. Within the precincts of Akasaka Hikawa Shrine, an extraordinary number of large trees were snapped. The tenement of Lord Sanada's residence collapsed, as did the tenement of Lord Sōma's residence. Samegabashi, Gon□-hara, and the Aoyama thoroughfare were all devastated, with innumerable large trees toppled. Between Hyakunin-chō and the Shibuya–Dōgenzaka area, collapsed buildings were visible in various places.
