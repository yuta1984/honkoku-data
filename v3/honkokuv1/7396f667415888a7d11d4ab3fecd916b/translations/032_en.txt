Following the flood, houses floated and drifted upon the water's surface, and the sense of helplessness grew ever greater—it seemed as though this could not be happening in this world. Yet having steeled themselves for the worst, one family's drifting house struck a peach tree in the neighboring garden, and fortunately did not float away to any great distance. Having escaped unharmed, a husband and wife clasped hands and rejoiced together—and rightly so. Furthermore, in Ōtsuka Village, within Sunamura, a sunken peasant's house was carried approximately three *ken* (about 5.4 meters) away, eventually coming to rest in the front garden of Shōchiin Temple on Ōshima, belonging to Rakanjji. Thirteen people were riding atop the roof of this drifting house, but one child among them, terrified by the ferocity of the water and apparently overcome with dizziness, fell into the water and perished.

◯ In the same area—along Kamezawa-chō Avenue, Midori-chō Avenue, and Tatekawa Avenue—banners (*nobori*) had been erected for the Tenmangū festival, but they were all either snapped by the wind or toppled from their bases, crushing nearby houses. At the bank of Hitottsume Bridge, many small boats were damaged, and three large vessels were driven ashore at the stone-landing (*ishiageba*). The licensed quarter's temporary residences (*karitaku*) in Matsui-chō were damaged or tilted in various places. Although the townhouses along Hayashi-chō Avenue were flooded only up to floor level, on the side of the estate grounds, the water rose

**[Left page]**

more than three *shaku* (approximately 90 cm) higher. The tenement houses (*nagaya*) of Lord Nagai collapsed, and those of the Lord of Tsushima collapsed and crumbled from the front gate onward.

◯ At a certain estate near Honjo Warigesui, the storm surge rose two *shaku* five or six *sun* (approximately 75–78 cm) above the floorboards, causing a chest of drawers to float out and tip into the water, whereupon the drawers fell out. In a panic, the residents retrieved them and replaced them without concern for the water inside. After the water receded and they later opened one of the drawers, they found a frog that had been scooped inside, still hopping about. At another estate in the same vicinity, a flounder was spotted swimming about inside the house; even the following day, after the water had gradually receded, it could still be seen in the garden. Furthermore, at Shiba Shinsenza, after the floodwaters receded and the wreckage of a collapsed house was cleared away, three squid, one *kochi* (flathead fish), and two sea bream were found. Since the sea bream were still flopping about, they were taken and made into sashimi—or so a certain person told.

◯ In Fukagawa, around Rokken-bori, there was damage in various places, and approximately five buildings of the Atake御船蔵 (official shogunate boat storehouse) were severely damaged. The entire area was flooded. Around Tokiwa-chō there was damage in various places as well, the Takabashi Bridge collapsed and traffic came to a halt, and the streets of the area resembled the open sea.
