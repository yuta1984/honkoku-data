From there, along Kaya-chō Street, most of the houses that had survived the earthquake were leaning, the Bishamondon hall was largely destroyed, and more than half of the small houses along the path by the rear pond were damaged, with some having completely collapsed.

◯ Along Hongō Street, the damage to the front-facing side was relatively minor; however, the small estates and row-houses (*kumiyashiki*) to the east and west along the back streets suffered severe damage, and many were completely destroyed. West of the 4th block (*yonchōme*) of the same street, at the base of Honmyō-ji temple, a ginkgo tree of more than twenty *ken* in height and approximately five arm-spans in girth broke off from its roots, reportedly killing two or three people. Houses over a span of four or five *ken* were crushed [by the falling tree]. The tinder-shop district (*tsukegidana*) and row-houses (*kumiyashiki*) had only recently been rebuilt with temporary structures (*kari-fushin*) following a fire, and those temporary buildings suffered severe damage. As for the estate of the Lord of Kaga (*Kashū-kō*), it is very extensive and entry through the gate is strictly controlled; therefore it was not possible to gather information firsthand, and it is accordingly omitted here.

◯ In Oyumi-chō, the front tenement (*omote-nagaya*) of Lord Honda collapsed, and one tenement building of Kainoshō also collapsed. The main gate of Lord Ogasawara's secondary residence (*naka-yashiki*) collapsed, and the front enclosures of Lord Toda's and Lord Sakai's estates, as well as other small estates, were all damaged. A fir tree at Lord Yonekizu's estate fell and blocked the thoroughfare. In the areas of Maruyama-dai-machi, Kiku-zaka, and Nashi-zaka, the damage to townspeople's houses was countless. The houses of Tamamachi were slightly damaged. The military estates, large and small, along the road in front of Kawakatsumae and around Maruta-bashi, were all severely damaged. In Kojūnin-machi—

◯ In Koishikawa, around the rear gate of Dentsu-in temple and Yanagi-chō, both townspeople's houses and row-houses had many collapsed buildings. The temples within the Dentsu-in complex were damaged, and a great many large trees within the precinct fell, causing damage in various places. The small estates along Sanhyaku-zaka and Gokurakuzu were all severely damaged. The row-houses around Ōtsuka had many collapsed buildings, and several large trees at the estate of Lord Matsudaira, the Director of the University (*Daigakunosuke*), were broken and felled. In the areas of the Medicinal Garden (*Oyakuen*), Kirishitan-zaka, and the Hakusan Palace, a small number of houses were destroyed.

◯ From Komagome Oiwake along the road toward Ōji, the areas of Jūjō and Inage suffered damage throughout. Furthermore, from Keiseiga-kubo along Sugamo Street to the area around Kōshin-zuka, the number of collapsed or leaning houses, row-houses, and small estates in the side streets and back alleys was immense, and within the precincts of temples and the grounds of estates, several hundred large trees were broken and felled. The brothels (*yūjoya*) of Itabashi post-town were damaged and leaning, and the townspeople's houses collapsed and were severely damaged.

◯ The shrine of Ōji Gongen and the Inari shrine suffered little damage, and the damage to the townspeople's houses was also minor.
