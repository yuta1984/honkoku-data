**[Upper Section]**

○ The residence of Sakai Utanokami outside the Ōtemon Gate was burned down. The main gate and the watchtower survived. The residence of Morikawa Dewanokami was burned. The Tensō residence was undamaged. The damage among the various daimyo houses along Daimyō-kōji was severe: the Honda, Doi, Inshū (Tottori domain), and Hosokawa residences each suffered partial burning. The Ogasawara, Sakai, Etchū, Ōta, Manabe, and Natsume residences, and indeed the entire surrounding area, were all heavily damaged.

○ At Gojiin-ga-hara outside the Hitotsubashi Gate, the residences of Matsudaira Buzen-no-kami and Hongō Tango-no-kami were both burned down. Additionally, five or six smaller residences in the area also burned.

○ The stone walls of Dōsanbashi Bridge collapsed.

○ The fire-watch tower at the fire brigade headquarters on Yayosu-gashi snapped and fell, but the people atop it were unharmed.

○ The residences of the Nabeshima and Chōshū (Hagi domain) lords were burned down.

○ The front row tenement (omote-nagaya) of the Kuroda residence collapsed.

**[Lower Section]**

○ The residences of the Geishū (Hiroshima domain) and Ii lords suffered minor damage.

○ The residence of Matsudaira Kai-no-kami was burned down, though the main gate survived.

○ The residences of Itō Shuri-no-tayū and Nanbu Mino-no-kami were burned down. The tenements of the Kamei and Satsuma (Satsuma domain) shōzoku residences suffered minor burning.

○ The daimyo residences and the areas around the tenements inside the Toranomon Gate were all heavily damaged.

○ Many residences along Atago-shita-dōri, outside the Atarashibashi Gate, were damaged. The stone steps of Mt. Atago were undamaged.
