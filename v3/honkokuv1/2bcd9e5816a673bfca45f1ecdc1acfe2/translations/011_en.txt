○ In Shin-Yoshiwara and along Nihon-zutsumi (the embankment), the shaking was particularly violent. The ground suddenly split and cracked open, and a white vapor (or mist) rose up across the entire area. This vapor shot off at an angle and struck the nine-ringed finial (kurin) atop the five-story pagoda of Kinryū-zan Sensō-ji temple in Asakusa, bending and breaking it, scattering it in all eight directions. The light it emitted was so intense, it was said to pierce the eyes of all who witnessed it.
