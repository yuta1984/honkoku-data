For the destitute people who have been forced to sleep outdoors due to the great earthquake and the fires that spread from it, the honorable authorities, in their compassion, have on this occasion established:

　　　Outside Saiwai-bashi Gate　　　　　　And additionally:
　　　Asakusa Hirokōji　　　　　　　　　　Within the precincts of Fukagawa Hachiman Shrine
　　　Fukagawa Kaibe Daiku-chō　　　　　The fire-prevention grounds below Mt. Tōei (Kan'ei-ji)

Relief shelters (o-sukui-goya) have been erected at these five locations. As a result, even the poorest residents of the city have been freed from the hardships of exposure to rain and dew, and from the suffering of hunger and thirst, and have been given a means of sustaining their livelihoods.

The depth of Our Lord's compassion and benevolence is so great that even the most reverent admiration falls short of expressing it — truly, these are matters for which we are profoundly grateful.

[By Official Order]

Looking up in reverence to Our Lord's benevolence, the property-owning townspeople of the city have also, each to the extent of their means, sent provisions as charitable offerings to those in the relief shelters. The goods pile up like mountains, and each day they grow more abundant. Truly, this is an example of those below following the example set by those above — a most admirable and grateful hallmark of this blessed reign.
