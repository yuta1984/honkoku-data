**[Upper Section]**

○ The residences of the Sendai, Aizu, and Wakizaka lords were heavily damaged. The Shinbashi area sustained nothing out of the ordinary.

○ The Kyōbashi and Owari-chō areas experienced nothing particularly severe, yet the earthen storehouses (dozō) shook loose their soil, and the roof tiles fell off entirely.

○ The main hall of Nishi Hongan-ji temple in Tsukiji was undamaged. All fifty-seven sub-temples within the precinct were completely destroyed.

○ The residences of the Okudaira, Kii, Owari, Aki, Hitotsubashi, and Suō lords sustained minor damage.

○ The residence of the Awaji-no-kami lord in Teppōzu burned down. In the same Jikken-chō area, approximately three houses per block burned.

**[Lower Section]**

○ In the same Teppōzu area, Akashi-chō suffered great damage, with houses collapsing entirely and many people injured.

○ In the same location, the residences of the Nakagawa, Tōtōmi, I-i (Ii), and Awa lords were heavily damaged.

○ Tsukuda-jima (Tsukuda Island) was heavily damaged, with many collapsed buildings.

○ The Inari shrine at Inaribashi was undamaged, but the tiled walls and other structures suffered major damage. The artificial Mt. Fuji (Fujizuka) in the same location collapsed.

○ Hatchōbori suffered major damage in various places. Isobe Daijingū shrine was undamaged, but the surrounding area was heavily damaged.

○ In Reigan-jima, Ōkawabata-chō and Ginmachi burned for approximately two chō (city blocks) in all directions.

○ In both Kita-Shinbori and Minami-Shinbori, the earthen storehouses shook entirely, with all walls crumbling away.
