**[Upper Section]**

○ In the area around Inari-bori (Tōka-bori) in front of the Great Bridge (Ōhashi), the residences of the Tayasu, Kuze, Sakai, Hayashi, Andō, Kii-no-kami, and Owari-no-kami lords (middle-rank residences, nakayashiki) were all heavily damaged.

○ Ginza suffered major damage. The riverside storehouses (kashi-gura) in Koami-chō were entirely and greatly damaged.

○ The Yakushi-dō (Yakushi Medicine Buddha Hall) in Kayaba-chō was undamaged.

○ The residence of the Makino lord was heavily damaged.

○ The stone walls of Isseki-bashi bridge collapsed, completely blocking passage at that location.

**[Lower Section]**

○ From the northern end (kita-zume) of Kyōbashi bridge, the following areas burned: Sumi-chō, Yanagi-chō, Gusoku-chō, Inaba-chō, Suzuki-chō, Hon-Zaimoku-chō 8-chōme, Gorōbei-chō, Tatami-chō (text damaged by worm holes), Daikon-gashi, Take-chō, Kita-Kon'ya-chō, and as far as Bikuni-bashi. On the southern side, Kaji-chō 1-chōme, 2-chōme, and Kanō-jindō (Kanō Shintō-chō) saw four or five earthen storehouses survive. From Minami-Tenma-chō 3-chōme and 2-chōme southward to Minakuchi, everything burned without exception. By the hour of the seventh watch (approximately 4:00 a.m.) of the following morning (the second day), the fire finally subsided. Casualties and injuries were especially numerous.
