○ Near Tennō Bridge on Onkuramae Street, there was a water teahouse (mizuchaya) known by the name of a certain proprietor. One day, some palanquin bearers entered the shop intending to rest while still carrying their passenger. As one of them stepped over the threshold at the entrance, he broke through the earthen floor of the shoe-removal area (kutsunugi no doma). To their surprise, when they looked at what had happened, pure, clear water was welling up in a steady flow. The people marveled at this and spread word, saying, "Surely this must be the sacred water of Kōbō Daishi!" However, four or five days later, an earthquake struck that very location. When a great earthquake is about to occur, well water sometimes dries up or becomes turbid — this is believed to be due to disturbances in the underground water veins. Perhaps this water, too, had its underground veins disturbed and, having lost its normal course, came bubbling up in a place where it had no reason to appear.

【Lower section】
○ In the vicinity of Ushigome, there was a person who was possessed by a fox spirit. On the first day of the tenth month of that year, this person suddenly blurted out: "Because something terrible is going to happen, I will not return here until the eighteenth day." With those words, the person shook off those who tried to restrain them and dashed away, and — no one knew where they went — they did not return. The following day, the second, the great earthquake struck. The person's own residence, along with a great many other houses that were shaken to the ground, crushed many people beneath them, resulting in numerous deaths. And yet, as that person still has not returned, there are apparently people in the area who continue to sleep outdoors to this day, not ceasing even now.
