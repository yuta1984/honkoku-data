**[Upper Section]**

○ Sakurada Kubomachi and Hongō Daichi, outside the Saiwai-bashi Gate, were heavily damaged throughout the area.

○ In the same area, the front tenement (omote-nagaya) of Matsudaira Hyōbu's residence in Kenbō-chō suffered minor burning.

○ The main gate of the Mōri Sanuki-no-kami residence collapsed.

○ Shibai-chō burned down entirely. Udagawa-chō, Shinmei-chō, and Mishima-chō largely collapsed, with many injured.

○ The main shrine of Shiba Shinmei (Shiba Daijingū) was undamaged. The temple gate town (monzen-chō) suffered minor damage. The area directly in front of Shinmei was particularly severely damaged, with collapsed buildings blocking the road.

○ The precinct (san'nai) of Zōjō-ji temple suffered minor damage.

**[Lower Section]**

○ Nakamon-mae-chō and the Kanasugibashi-dōri area sustained nothing out of the ordinary — only minor damage.

○ The tenement quarters of the Satsuma, Naitō, and Oda lords, among others, suffered considerable damage.

○ The Arima lord's tenements at Akabane collapsed in several places.

○ In the area of Isarago Daimachi, the kōji (mold-fermentation) rooms collapsed, causing many injuries.

○ All of the temples in Takanawa suffered major damage.

○ Shinagawa post town sustained only minor damage, yet there were many killed outright and many injured.

○ The No. 2 Odaiba (fort) was damaged.

○ The area around Kanagawa-dai experienced only a light earthquake (with little damage).
