○ The Washi Daimyōjin Shrine collapsed; Ryūsenji Temple, Daionji Temple, and Chōkokuji Temple suffered major damage.

○ In the same area, Saitokuji Temple — a temple of the Jōdo Shinshū (Ikkōshū) sect affiliated with Bukōji — had its main hall (hondō), kitchen quarters (kuri), and front gate (omotemon) destroyed, though the Taishi-dō (Prince Shōtoku Hall) was left unharmed.

○ Nakamura in Senju Kozukappara-chō was burned down.

○ In Hashiba: Sōsenji Temple, Tamahime Inari Shrine, Masaki Inari Shrine, and the Shinmei Shrine all suffered major damage. In the same area, the lower residence (shimo-yashiki) of Lord Sakai Utanokami, Asajigahara, Chōshōji Temple, Hōgenji Temple, Honshōji Temple, and Myōonji Temple all sustained major damage.

【Lower section】
○ A fire broke out in Imado-chō, and the coin mint (zeniza) was partially burned. The restaurant and teahouse "Kawaguchi" in the same area was completely destroyed. The Hachiman Shrine in the same area, along with other temples, also suffered major damage. Approximately half a chō of Imado-chō burned.

○ The main hall of the Shōten (Kangiten) deity at Matsuchiyama was unharmed, but the temple precincts suffered major damage. Saihōji Temple and the Batō Kannon (Horse-headed Kannon) along the embankment sustained major damage. In Shōten-chō, Nii-Torigoe, and Sanya-chō, both temples and townspeople's houses collapsed entirely.

○ Hanakawado suffered major damage; Azuma Bridge was unharmed. Zaimoku-chō, Namiki-chō, Chaya-chō, and Komagata-dō all suffered major damage.

○ Rokugō-sama suffered major damage, with the front gate collapsing.
