◆ During the reign of Emperor Gokōmyō, on the 13th day of the 5th month of Shōhō 4 (1647): Great Earthquake in Edo. The residences of daimyō were damaged. (According to *Aruki*)
◆ Keian 1 (1648), 22nd day of the 4th month: Great earthquake. The slopes of Mt. Hakone in Sagami Province collapsed. (According to *Gōunzu*)
○ The 2nd year of the same era (1649), 5th day of the 2nd month: Great earthquake around Matsuyama and Uwajima in Iyo Province. Stone walls within the castles collapsed, and many ordinary residences were damaged. On the 20th day of the 6th month of the same year, Edo suffered similarly—daimyō residences and ordinary homes were broken and collapsed, and many people perished.
○ The 3rd year (1650), 23rd day of the 3rd month, at night: Great earthquake in the Kantō region. The above is according to *Aruki*.
◆ During the reign of Emperor Gosai, Kanbun 2 (1662), 1st day of the 5th month: Great earthquake. The stone bridge at Gojō in Kyoto collapsed; a mountain in Kutsuki Valley in Ōmi Province collapsed, burying all the dwellings there. A great many people of all social ranks perished. In the 11th month of the same year, a mountain in Ōsumi Province collapsed, turning part of the sea into land. Aftershocks occurred repeatedly, continuing until the year's end. (According to *Honchō Nendaiki* and *Jishin-kō*)
◆ The 3rd year of the same era (1663), 25th day of the 7th month: Great earthquake in Matsumae, Ezo (Hokkaido). On the 6th day of the 12th month of the same year, Kyoto likewise suffered—from the hour of the Dog (around 8 p.m.) until the hour of the Ox (around 2 a.m.), Nijō Castle and many other places throughout the city were damaged. On the 27th day of the 12th month of the 4th year (1664):

**[Left folio]**

Great earthquake in the Takada domain of Echigo Province. Many dwellings collapsed and there were numerous deaths. (*Honchō Nendaiki* records this as the 5th year.) (According to *Aruki*)
◆ During the reign of Emperor Reigen, Tenna 3 (1683), 22nd day of the 5th month: Great earthquake. The sacred shrine of Futarasan (Nikkō) was damaged. (According to *Honchō Nendaiki*)
◆ During the reign of Emperor Higashiyama, Genroku 16 (1703), 22nd day of the 11th month, at the hour of the Ox (around 2 a.m.): Great earthquake in Edo. Both samurai residences and townspeople's homes were damaged. Odawara in Sagami Province suffered more severely than other regions—dwellings collapsed, Mt. Hakone crumbled and blocked roads, and fires broke out in many places simultaneously. Many people perished. Those who fled to the seashore were killed by the tsunami.
◆ Hōei 3 (1706), 15th day of the 9th month: Great earthquake. The above is according to *Aruki*.
○ The 4th year of the same era (1707), 4th day of the 10th month, at the hour of the Sheep (around 2 p.m.): Great earthquake along the Tōkaidō. The ground split open, great waves (tsunami) struck the coast, and many people perished. In the 11th month of the same year, Mt. Fuji erupted and the Hōei crater appeared. (According to *Aruki* and *Wakan Sansai Zue*)
◆ During the reign of Emperor Nakamikado, from the 20th day of the 11th month of Kyōhō 8 (1723) through the 12th month: Great earthquake in Kyushu.
○ The 10th year of the same era (1725), 25th day of the 9th month: Great earthquake in Nagasaki. More than eighty aftershocks occurred, day and night. (According to *Aruki*)
