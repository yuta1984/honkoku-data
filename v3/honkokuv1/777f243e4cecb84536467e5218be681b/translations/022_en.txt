【Right folio】

The mansions (o-yashiki) suffered severe damage. In this area, more than 300 houses and 92 structures collapsed, 97 storehouses (dozō) collapsed, and the number of deaths was enormous.

③ Outside Asakusa Gate, the districts of Kayachō, Kawarachō, Moritamachi, and Okuramaekatamaachi suffered no particular damage. Likewise, the shrine of Moto-Toriyoe Myōjin sustained no damage. However, along the Nanakumagarai area, the residences of Lord Ikeda, Lord Kose, Lord Koide, and Lord Abe suffered severe damage. The residences of Lord Matsubara and Lord Satake Iki-no-kami shook greatly. Both sides of Shin-horibata were damaged. The main hall of Okuramaehachiman Shrine sustained no damage, but the shrine grounds suffered major destruction. A fire broke out in Komagatamachi in the same area, and Aramachi and Kurofunemachi were burned down. The government rice storehouse (o-kura) in the same area sustained no damage.

The districts of Namikimachi and Tawaramachi were damaged. The main hall of Higashi Hongan-ji Temple was unharmed, but many structures within the temple grounds collapsed. The areas in front of Seigan-ji Temple and Nichirin-ji Temple were completely devastated. Lord Nakada Ho Rokugō's residence was damaged.

Shin-Yoshiwara was entirely collapsed and then burned down, with only the headman's (nanushi) house in Edochō Icchōme surviving. The official notice board (kōsatsu) on Gojikken-dō road sustained no damage, but the ground of Nihon Tsutsumi embankment cracked and split open. From there, a fire broke out in Tamachi, burning down Icchōme, Nichōme, and Yamakawachō. A few teahouses at the base of Ōte survived. A fire also broke out in Kita-Shinmachi. The three theaters in Saruwakachō Sanchōme, Nichōme, and Icchōme burned down. The east side of the actors' alley (yakusha shindō) in the same area survived. Both sides of Kita-Umamichi (the road along the north horse path) within the grounds of Sensō-ji Temple burned down, while the southern horse path fire stopped at the north side of the Tozawa tenement houses. Sensō-ji Temple's various gates and main hall sustained no damage, but the halls of Okuyama were damaged. Hanakawadochō was half burned. Yamanoyadomachi and Shōtenmachi suffered major damage. The areas of Sanyababa-mukō, Shin-Toriyoechō, Motoyoshichō, and Asakusamachi were completely devastated. The Tamahime Shrine and Sōsen-ji Temple suffered major damage. About half a chō (approximately 55 meters) from the corner opposite Imadodobashi was burned. Hashibacho was completely devastated, and Lord Miyake's residence suffered major damage. Mazaki Inari Shrine and Shinmei-gū Shrine sustained no damage. In total, 1,447 houses collapsed, 71 storehouses collapsed, and the number of burned-down storehouses beyond that was incalculable. The number of unnatural deaths was enormous.
