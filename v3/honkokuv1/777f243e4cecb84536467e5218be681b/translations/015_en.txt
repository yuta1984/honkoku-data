(Continued from previous page: Great earthquake on the 3rd day of the 7th month of Genkō 1st year [1331].) The distant tidal flats of Chisato-no-hama on the coast of Kii Province suddenly became dry land for a distance of more than twenty chō. On the 7th of the same month, at the Hour of the Rooster (around 6:00 PM), an earthquake caused the summit of Mount Fuji to collapse several hundred jō. **[Taiheiki]**

**[Kenmu]** 1st year (1334), 27th day of the 8th month: great earthquake at dawn. 13th day of the 12th month of the same year: likewise.

**[Emperor Kōmyō / Ryakuō]** 1st year (1338), 19th day of the 7th month: likewise. 22nd of the same month: earthquake.

**[Kannō]** 1st year (1350), 2nd day of the 7th month: earthquakes at the Hour of the Monkey and the Hour of the Boar (around 4:00 PM and 10:00 PM). The Shōgunzuka (Shogun's Mound) rumbled with extraordinary intensity. ○ 2nd year (1351), 19th day of the 2nd month: great earthquake. The Shōgunzuka rumbled again. 17th day of the 3rd month: celestial anomaly; another earthquake that night. 11th day of the 4th month, Hour of the Dragon (around 8:00 AM): likewise.

**[Emperor Gokōgon / Kōan]** 1st year (1361), 20th day of the 6th month: great earthquake. Thereafter, great earthquakes continued without cease. Halls and buildings in Kyoto and the Southern Capital (Nara) collapsed. The above is from **[Kōnen Daiki Ryakki, Headnote]**.

**[Enbun]** 5th year (1360), 6th month: great earthquake. Fires broke out repeatedly as well. Beginning from the 18th, tremors continued without cease into the 7th month. In Awaji Island, a tsunami killed people and horses without exception. On the 22nd day of the 7th month, snow fell; on the 24th, snow fell in mountain-like quantities and the earthquakes did not cease.
