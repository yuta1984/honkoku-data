## Jishin Nendaiki (Chronicle of Earthquakes)

### Right Page

In the ancient age of the gods, when Hayasusanoo no Mikoto was about to depart for the Land of Roots (Ne no Kuni), he ascended to Takamagahara to bid farewell to Amaterasu Ōmikami. At that time, every mountain and river stirred, and the entire land trembled.
[From the Kojiki, Volume One] This may be called the very beginning of earthquakes.

**Reign of Emperor Ingyō:** 5th year, 7th month, 14th day — an earthquake occurred.

**Reign of Empress Suiko:** 7th year, 4th month, 27th day — the earth moved and all buildings were destroyed. Accordingly, orders were issued throughout the land to hold rites honoring the god of earthquakes.

**Reign of Empress Kōgyoku:** 1st year, 10th month, 8th day — an earthquake occurred and rain fell. The next day there was again an earthquake, and that night another earthquake struck, accompanied by wind. On the 24th day, in the middle of the night, there was an earthquake.

**Reign of Emperor Tenmu:** 4th year, 11th month — the earth shook greatly. 6th year, 6th month, 14th day — a great earthquake. 7th year, 12th month — a great earthquake struck Tsukushi Province (Kyushu), and the ground split open. The fissure was two jō wide and more than three thousand jō long. Many farmhouses in the villages collapsed and crumbled. At this time, one peasant's house stood on a hilltop; it was shaken loose and moved to another location entirely. Yet the house remained completely undamaged,

### Left Page

and the people inside did not realize that the location of their house had changed — only the following morning did they discover this, to their great astonishment.
[All from the Nihon Shoki]

11th year, 8th month, 12th day — the earth shook greatly. 17th day — another earthquake. [Ruijū Kokushi]

13th year, 10th month, 14th day, Hour of the Boar (approximately 10 p.m.) — a great earthquake. Mountains collapsed, river waters gushed forth, and the destruction of government offices, private homes, and temples and shrines across the provinces was beyond counting. Many people and livestock were killed or injured. The hot springs of Iyo Province were buried, and more than 500,000 *kyō* of farmland in Tosa Province [one *kyō* equals one hundred *se*, equivalent to over 500,000 *chō*] became ocean.

14th year, 12th month, 10th day — an earthquake originated from the west.
[All from the Nihon Shoki]

**Reign of Emperor Monmu:** **Taihō** era, 1st year, 3rd month, 24th day — earthquakes continued for three days in Tanba Province.

**Reign of Empress Genmei:** **Reiki** era, 1st year, 5th month, 25th day — an earthquake struck Tōtōmi Province; mountains collapsed, the Aratama River became blocked, and after several tens of days the waters overflowed, inundating more than 170 villages across the three districts of Fuchi, Chōge, and Iwata, damaging young rice seedlings. The following day, the 26th, an earthquake struck Mikawa Province, causing 47 government storehouses and many private homes to collapse and fall in.

**Reign of Emperor Shōmu:** **Tenpyō** era, 6th year, 4th month, 7th day — a great earthquake. Homes across the provinces collapsed; many were crushed to death; mountains crumbled, rivers were blocked, and the earth split open in ways too numerous to recount. Same year, 9th month, 24th day — the earth shook greatly. 16th year, 5th month — (continues)
