An earthquake occurred at the Hour of the Sheep (around 2:00 PM), when people had gathered in the upper western samurai hall of the Kamakura Palace. On the 22nd, after the military lord had divination performed and was informed that this portended a grave event, he retired to the residence of the Governor of Sagami. On the 6th day of the 9th month, a great earthquake at the Hour of the Ox (around 2:00 AM). On the 8th, another great tremor at the Hour of the Tiger (around 4:00 AM). On the 11th, at the Hour of the Tiger, another small tremor likewise. Tremors also on the 13th, 14th, and 16th. On the 17th, tremors three times. On the 21st, prayers were offered: Chikamoto officiated the festival to thirty-six thousand deities, and Nobugata served as magistrate for the earthquake ritual.

**[Shijo Emperor / Bunryaku]** 2nd year (1235), 13th day of the 4th month: earthquake at the Hour of the Horse (around noon). 28th of the same month: earthquake at the Hour of the Sheep (around 2:00 PM). 29th: earthquake at the same hour. On the 8th, prayers were offered and deliberations on benevolent governance were held.

**[Ninji]** 2nd year (1241), 7th day of the 2nd month: great earthquake at the Hour of the Snake (around 10:00 AM). According to the elders, there was a great tremor like this during the Kenryaku era (1211–1213).

(Interlinear note: Referring specifically to the 1st year of Kenpō [1213]) — This, they say, was an omen of the rebellion of Wada Saemon-no-jō Yoshimori. (And so forth.) On the same day, two small tremors occurred at the Hour of the Horse and the Hour of the Rat. On the 8th, another tremor at the Hour of the Snake. The total number of tremors in recent days was five or more. **[Azuma Kagami]**

**[Emperor Gofukakusa / Kenchō]** 6th year (1254), intercalary 5th month, 11th and 19th days: great earthquake. **[Kōnen Daiki Ryakki]**

**[Shōka]** 1st year (1257), 23rd day of the 8th month: great earthquake in Kamakura. There was a rumbling sound. Not a single shrine or temple building was left intact; mountains collapsed,

houses fell, earthen walls were entirely destroyed, the ground split open and water gushed forth. Among these events, a blue flame was said to have burst from the cracks in the ground near Gebabashi (the "Dismounting Bridge"). On the 4th day of the 9th month, light rain fell, and an earthquake occurred at the Hour of the Monkey (around 4:00 PM). Since the great tremor of the previous month, tremors have continued without cease to this day.

**[Emperor Fushimi / Einin]** 1st year (1293), 13th day of the 4th month, Hour of the Tiger (around 4:00 AM): great earthquake. Mountains collapsed, houses were destroyed, and the number of deaths was beyond counting. Starting with the jōroku hall of Daihi-ji temple in Kamakura, buildings were crushed and buried; Jufuku-ji and Engaku-ji collapsed and burned. The destruction across various locations was too numerous to count. Deaths: 21,024 people. **[Hōjō-ki]**

**[Emperor Gofushimi / Shōan]** 1st year (1299), 25th day of the 4th month: great earthquake. The golden hall (kondō) of Shitennō-ji temple in Settsu Province collapsed, and the hall of Nanzen-ji temple in eastern Kyoto fell. Deaths in the Kinai region exceeded 10,000. **[Honchō Nendaiki]**

**[Emperor Hanazono / Bunpō]** 1st year (1317), from the 1st month onward: successive great earthquakes in Kyoto. The pagoda of Tō-ji temple was brought crashing down; Kiyomizudera temple was consumed by fire, and even the portrait of General Tamura (Sakanoue no Tamuramaro) was burned and lost. **[Hōjō-ki]**

**[Emperor Godaigo / Shōchū]** 1st year (1324), 11th month: great earthquake. Chikubushima Island in Ōmi Province partially collapsed and sank into the lake. **[Honchō Nendaiki]**

Genkō 1st year (1331), 3rd day of the 7th month: great earthquake.
