**[Date unclear]** Rain fell and an earthquake struck Higo Province (present-day Kumamoto Prefecture). In the three districts of Yatsushiro, Amakusa, and Ashikita, government offices, more than 290 *chō* of farmland, more than 470 villages of private homes, and more than 1,500 people were swept away and submerged by water. More than 280 locations suffered landslides, causing more than 40 deaths by crushing. Relief was provided by the imperial court.

Same era, 17th year, 4th month, 27th day — earthquakes continued through the night, for two days and three nights. Watchtowers, government storehouses, temples, and private homes in Mino Province collapsed. Continuing thereafter, earthquakes struck on the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th days of the 5th month; on the 5th day they did not cease day or night; further earthquakes occurred on the 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 16th, and 18th days, with the ground breaking apart and cracking and water gushing up. From the 2nd day, for the span of one week (seven days), the *Saishōōkyō* sūtra was ceremonially read in rotation at the temples of the capital; from the 8th day, for the span of three weeks (twenty-one days), the *Daishūkyō* sūtra was read at Daian-ji, Yakushi-ji, Gangō-ji, and Kōfuku-ji.

**Reign of Emperor Ōi (Junnin):** **Tenpyō Hōji** era, 6th year, 5th month, 9th day — earthquakes struck Mino, Hida, and Shinano Provinces. Those whose homes were damaged were each granted two *koku* of rice.
[All from the Shoku Nihongi]

**Reign of Emperor Heizei:** **Daidō** era, 2nd year, 5th month — the earth shook greatly.

**Reign of Emperor Saga:** **Kōnin** era, 9th year, 7th month — earthquakes struck the provinces of Sagami, Musashi, Shimōsa, Hitachi, Kōzuke, and Shimotsuke, among others. Mountains collapsed and valleys were buried for several *ri* around. The number of farmers crushed to death was beyond counting. In the 8th month, relief was granted, this year's taxes and tributes (*sochō*) were exempted, homes were repaired, and those who had been crushed or buried were given proper burial. In the 9th month, prayers were offered.

**Reign of Emperor Junna:** **Tenchō** era, 4th year, 7th month, 12th day — an earthquake struck and many buildings collapsed. Within a single day there was one large tremor and seven or eight smaller ones. On the 14th of the same month, the earth continued to shake without pause; at the Hour of the Boar (approximately 10 p.m.) it shook greatly, and each tremor was accompanied by a rumbling sound. The 15th also saw tremors. On the 16th, twice. On the 19th, two major tremors. The 21st and 22nd also saw tremors. On the 24th, three times throughout the day and night.
