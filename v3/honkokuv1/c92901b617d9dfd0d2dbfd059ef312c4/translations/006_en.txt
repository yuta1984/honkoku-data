Viewed from around Mochizuki post town, the color of the smoke was white as snow, rising and spreading across the sky like white clouds, with lightning-like flashes seen frequently throughout. On the same day it calmed around the Hour of the Sheep (approximately 2 PM), but from the Hour of the Sheep on the first day of the month it erupted again, with the rumbling growing even stronger. Weak shacks and small buildings around Oiwake post town were shaken and collapsed, and people feared even large houses might fall, causing panic and weeping among the population. While this continued, around the Hour of the Rooster (approximately 6 PM) it calmed again. According to someone who had stayed overnight at Oiwake post town on the first day of the month, after nightfall and continuing each morning, the eruption went on daily.

At a place called Ozasa, stones the size of temari balls rained down, and around Odai post town and Ozasa, lions and wolves gathered in packs and attacked people and horses. Though hunters shot at them with firearms, their numbers were so great that they could not be driven away. Furthermore, upon inquiring about the surrounding regions within fifteen to sixteen ri, or even twenty ri, we learned that ash fell to depths of five to six sun, and in some areas as much as eight to nine sun.
