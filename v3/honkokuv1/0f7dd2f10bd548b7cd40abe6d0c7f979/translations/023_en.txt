In the thirteenth year of the same reign [Emperor Tenmu, 684 CE], during winter, mountains collapsed and rivers surged; dwellings and temple pagodas in various provinces were destroyed, and many people and horses perished.

The sixteenth year of Tenpyō [744 CE], fifth month: a great earthquake in Higo Province.

The sixth year of Tenpyō-Hōji [762 CE]: an earthquake in the regions of Hida, Shinano, and Mino.

The ninth year of Kōnin [818 CE], seventh, eighth, and ninth months: a great earthquake in the Kantō region.

The seventh year of Tenchō [830 CE], first month, and again in the fourth and fifth months: a great earthquake in Dewa Province.

The eighth year of Jōwa [841 CE], second month: an earthquake in Shinano and Izu.

The third year of Kajō [850 CE], tenth month: an earthquake in Dewa Province.

The fifth year of Jōgan [863 CE], sixth month: a great earthquake.

The eleventh year of the same era [869 CE], fifth, seventh, ninth, tenth, and twelfth months: great earthquakes in the provinces of Mutsu and Higo.

The first year of Tengyō [938 CE], fourth month: a great earthquake.
