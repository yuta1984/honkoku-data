The Eleventh Month of Genroku 16, Mizunoto-Hitsuji Year (1703)
　A Map of the Great Earthquake《Marginal note: Genroku printed map》

On the twenty-second day of the eleventh month, lightning flashed intensely from early evening.
At the hour of the eighth bell in the night (approximately 2 a.m.), the earth rumbled like thunder,
and a great earthquake struck. Doors and sliding screens fell over, and houses
swayed as if they were small boats tossed upon waves.

The ground cracked open from two or three sun to, depending on the location,
as much as five or six shaku, churning up sand
and in some places
even spurting out water.
Stone walls crumbled, houses and storehouses collapsed,
and underground cellars were heaved upward.
