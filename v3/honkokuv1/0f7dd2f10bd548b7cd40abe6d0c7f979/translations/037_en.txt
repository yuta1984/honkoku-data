It is precisely in such moments as these that the spirit of loyalty and filial piety reveals itself. One must not be startled when faced with an emergency. While it is true that encountering a crisis and dealing with it swiftly is the mark of a person of great capacity, this can only be achieved through composure of heart and mind, and through habits of preparedness and forethought cultivated in daily life. And so on.
