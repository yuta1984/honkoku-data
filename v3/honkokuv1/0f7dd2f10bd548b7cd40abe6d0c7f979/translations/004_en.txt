When the qi (vital energy) of Heaven and Earth loses its proper order, and the yang energy hidden within the earth cannot emerge and instead builds up as vapor, an earthquake occurs. This is said to be a fearful phenomenon, caused by the yang energy losing its proper place and being blocked by yin energy.
It is said that one must always change one's conduct in response to thunder and violent winds — this reflects how the sages revered and held in awe the anger of Heaven. The same spirit of mindfulness should apply to earthquakes as well. The earthquake of this year is truly unprecedented (the text continues on the next page)…
