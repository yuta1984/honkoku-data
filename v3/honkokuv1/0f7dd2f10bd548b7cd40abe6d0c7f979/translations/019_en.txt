[Continuation of the Earthquake Precursor Detection Method Using a Magnet]

...One should place beneath it an object that will resound when struck. When there is no earthquake, the nails remain attached [to the magnet] and do not fall. But if an earthquake is about to occur, the magnet loses its power of attraction to iron, causing the iron nails to immediately fall into the receptacle below, thus alerting people through sound of the impending signs. This is considered one excellent method of foreknowing earthquakes. Please examine the figure and understand it well. —Thus it is written.

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Written by Takatsuki Itsumín

[A Brief Account of Earthquakes]

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Compiled by Ryōshū Sanjin

According to Western natural philosophy (*kyūri*), the vibration of the earth originates from fire pits (*kakō*) located underground. These fire pits exist in great numbers throughout the entire globe, and within our country [Japan] there are two such sources: one in the Central Provinces (Suruga, Tōtōmi, Kai, Shinano, Izu, and Sagami), and one in the land of Ezo [Hokkaido], with their fire veins extending far into foreign lands...
