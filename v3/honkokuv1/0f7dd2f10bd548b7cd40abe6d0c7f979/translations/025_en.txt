The twentieth day of the sixth month of the same year [the second year of Keian, 1649 CE]: a great earthquake in Edo. The residences of daimyo and commoners' houses were damaged and destroyed, and there were many fatalities.

The twenty-third day of the third month of the third year of the same era [the third year of Keian, 1650 CE], at night: a great earthquake in the Kantō region.

The twenty-fifth day of the seventh month of the third year of Kanbun [1663 CE]: a great earthquake in Matsumae, Ezo (present-day Hokkaido).

The sixth day of the twelfth month of the same year: a great earthquake in Kyoto. The shaking continued from the hour of the Dog (around 8 p.m.) until the hour of the Ox (around 2 a.m.). Nijō Castle and various places throughout the capital sustained damage.

The twenty-seventh day of the twelfth month of the fifth year of the same era [1665 CE]: a great earthquake in the Takada domain of Echigo Province.

The fifth day of the fourth month of the third year of Tenna [1683 CE]: a great earthquake at Nikkō-san.

The twenty-seventh day of the fifth month of the seventh year of Genroku [1694 CE]: a great earthquake in Akita, Dewa Province. Commoners' houses collapsed, and many people and horses perished.
