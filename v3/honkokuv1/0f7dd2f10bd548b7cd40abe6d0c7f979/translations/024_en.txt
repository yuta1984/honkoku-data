The ninth year of Ōei [1402 CE], winter: an earthquake.

The sixth year of Meiō [1497 CE], eleventh month: a great earthquake across various provinces.

The twenty-ninth day of the tenth month of the thirteenth year of Tenshō [1585 CE]: a great earthquake. It did not cease even after the year turned.

The seventeenth year of the same era [1589 CE], second month: a great earthquake in the two provinces of Suruga and Tōtōmi.

The first year of Keichō [1596 CE], intercalary seventh month, twelfth day: a great earthquake. Many people in the Gokinai region perished; particularly in the area of Kyoto and Fushimi, great mansions and commoners' houses alike collapsed, and many were crushed to death beneath the rubble.

The nineteenth year of the same era [1614 CE], twenty-fifth day of the tenth month: a great earthquake.

The fourth year of Kan'ei [1627 CE], first month: a great earthquake across various provinces.

The fourth year of Shōhō [1647 CE], thirteenth day of the fifth month: a great earthquake in Edo. The castle and the residences of samurai and townspeople throughout the city sustained damage.
