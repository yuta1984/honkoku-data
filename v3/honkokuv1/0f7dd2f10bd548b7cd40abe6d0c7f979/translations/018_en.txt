[On the Theory and Practical Instructions for Detecting Earthquake Precursors Using Magnets]

...One should not think (that this discovery belongs solely to the school of Paris). Since the principles of "Electromagnetism" (*Erekki-teiri-teki-tomangu-nechi-sumisu* [transliteration of a Western scientific term for electromagnetism]) and of attraction have already been clarified, even in the realm of academic inquiry, this theory has gradually come to have some presence. Moreover, it is already common knowledge among the people of the world that the power of electricity (*erekki*) is disrupted by earthquakes.

In order to explain the method of detecting earthquake precursors, and for the benefit of beginners (*dōmō*, lit. "young and unlearned"), we present the method in illustrated form and translate its instructions as follows.

[Figure — an illustration of the apparatus using a magnet and iron nail]

As shown in the figure, tie a magnet with a cord and suspend it from the ceiling or from a door lintel (*kamoi*), then attach iron nails or similar objects to the magnet by magnetic attraction. Directly beneath it, place a copper basin or any similar vessel to catch the falling nails...
