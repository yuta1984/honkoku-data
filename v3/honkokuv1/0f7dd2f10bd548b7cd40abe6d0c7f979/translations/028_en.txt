From the twentieth day of the eleventh month of the eighth year of Kyōhō [1723 CE] through the twelfth month, a great earthquake struck Kyushu.

On the twenty-fifth day of the ninth month of the tenth year of Kyōhō [1725 CE], a great earthquake struck Nagasaki, with more than eighty tremors occurring both day and night.

On the twenty-ninth day of the second month of the eleventh year of Kyōhō [1726 CE], in the Katsuyama domain of Echizen Province, there occurred a tsunami, violent winds and rain, and earthquakes. People's homes and farmlands were inundated, muddy water welled up from the ground, and the flatlands became like a great river. Many people, cattle, and horses drowned.

On the twenty-ninth day of the second month of the first year of Hōreki [1751 CE] [the era name was changed in the eleventh month], a great earthquake struck Kyoto. Around this time, red snow fell in the northern provinces.

On the twenty-fifth day of the fourth month of the same year, a great earthquake struck Takada in Echigo Province. From the hour of the Rooster to the hour of the Ox, there were more than thirty tremors; mountains collapsed, people's houses fell, and those who perished numbered approximately sixteen thousand...
