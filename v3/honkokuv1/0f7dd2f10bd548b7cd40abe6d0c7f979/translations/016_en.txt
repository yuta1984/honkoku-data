[On Aftershocks and Volcanic Activity]

The reason the earth continues to tremble little by little is that the subterranean fire (*fukukwa* — the yang energy still lying dormant) has not yet been fully expelled. In the most extreme cases, a mountain may burst into flame — that is, a volcanic eruption may occur. In ancient times, in the fifth year of Jōgan, there was a great earthquake, and the following year Mount Fuji erupted. More recently, the same occurred in the fourth year of Hōei.
