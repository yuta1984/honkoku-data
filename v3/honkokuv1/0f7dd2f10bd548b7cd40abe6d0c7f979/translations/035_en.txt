In the 9th month of Kaei 1 (era name changed in the 3rd month), in Echizen Province, continuous rain and wind struck, and brine welled up from the mountains; earthquakes occurred, causing damage to private homes and farmland, with many deaths.

On the 4th day of the 11th month of Ansei 1 (era name changed in the 11th month), a great earthquake struck Edo. At this time, Sunshū [Suruga Province] and Tōshū [Tōtōmi Province] were particularly severely affected; Hakone Pass collapsed, blocking passage for eight or nine days. Also, on the same day, an earthquake struck Shikoku, and Tōshū [Tosa Province] in particular experienced strong tremors from around the Hour of the Dragon [approximately 8 a.m.]; that night, things calmed somewhat.

On the following day, the 5th, at the Hour of the Monkey [approximately 4 p.m.], the tremors once again became violent. Fires broke out in various private residences throughout the area, and the ocean tides rose and fell without cease.

On the night of the 2nd day of the 10th month of the same era's 2nd year [Ansei 2], at the Hour of the Boar [approximately 10 p.m.], a great earthquake struck Edo. It is said that since the founding of Edo, there had never been an earthquake of such magnitude.
