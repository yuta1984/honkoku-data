In general, those who dwell along the coast should always respect their elders, listen to what they have to say, and pass down and preserve the oral accounts of dangerous events that have occurred since ancient times. Moreover, in their daily lives, they should remain vigilant, and should there be any unusual signs in the appearance of the clouds or in the rising and falling of the tides, they should immediately inform one another and constantly keep watch on how the situation develops. If one lives in such a manner, a natural readiness of mind will gradually develop, so that when a crisis does strike, one will not panic, and one will not come to lose one's life. At all times, one should simply keep a watchful eye on the winds, clouds, and the movement of the sea tides.
