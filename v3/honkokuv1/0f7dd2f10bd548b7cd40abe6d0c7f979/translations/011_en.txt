【The Perilous Situation of Tsunami Waves Crashing onto Shorelines and Pulling Houses into the Sea】

Those who reside along the coast should always respect and seek wisdom from their elders, and pass down knowledge of the dangers that have occurred since ancient times. In ordinary times, they should remain vigilant, and if there are any unusual changes in cloud formations or the rise and fall of the tides, they should inform one another and constantly observe such developments. By doing so, one will naturally come to develop a state of mental preparedness, and when a crisis does arise, one will not be thrown into panic and will not lose one's life. At all times, one must simply keep a watchful eye on the winds, clouds, and ocean tides.
