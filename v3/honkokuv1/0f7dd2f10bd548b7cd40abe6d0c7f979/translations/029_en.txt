On the last day of the seventh month of the sixth year of Hōreki [1756 CE], a great earthquake struck Ōmi Province.

On the second day of the fifth month and the second day of the sixth month of the eighth year of Meiwa [1771 CE], great earthquakes struck Edo on both occasions.

On the fourteenth day of the seventh month of the second year of Tenmei [1782 CE], at the hour of the Ox, a great earthquake struck Edo. On the fifteenth day of the same month, another great earthquake struck, causing people's homes and other structures to be damaged and toppled. Through the following morning, there were fifteen or sixteen further tremors. At this time, Odawara in Sagami Province was particularly severely affected: the stone walls of Mount Hakone and within Odawara Castle collapsed, and many houses in the castle town were damaged. During this month, snow fell in Shimōsa Province. On the fourth day of the eighth month, a tsunami struck the Edo coastline, destroying the worship hall and subsidiary shrines of the Benzaiten shrine at Fukagawa Susaki. Only the main sanctuary remained. The teahouses in front of the shrine gate were destroyed and toppled, and were utterly swallowed into the sea by the receding waves, with many people drowning.
