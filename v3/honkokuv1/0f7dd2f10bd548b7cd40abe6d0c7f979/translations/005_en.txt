This is a catastrophe of unprecedented scale, and it is a time when all people must conduct themselves with utmost care and prudence. Therefore, it would be fitting for artisans and merchants alike to devote themselves to frugality and simplicity, and to be circumspect in both word and deed.
There are many conflicting theories circulating in society. For this reason, I have here compiled a detailed explanation of earthquakes, and have also researched and recorded the dates found in historical documents since ancient times, so as to serve as a guide and reference for future generations.
Written on a day in the tenth month of the second year of Ansei [1855], by Hokuho the Recluse.

From ancient times, the trembling of the earth has been
a work of Heaven and Earth, and from those primordial ages up to the present day,
it has occurred a countless number of times — so many thousands of times that the number cannot be known. Among these,
the events that shook the earth with particular violence and brought suffering to the people,
as well as an examination of the manner of these tremors, I shall set down in writing, engrave upon woodblocks,
and offer as a guide for generations to come.
