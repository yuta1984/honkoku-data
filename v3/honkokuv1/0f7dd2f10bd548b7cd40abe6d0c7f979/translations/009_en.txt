Seven thousand and some more persons (dead in Edo).
　　　Of these, at the time of the fire on the 29th day,
　　　739 persons perished
　　　on Ryōgoku Bridge.

At this time, the Sanjūsangen-dō
(Hall of Thirty-Three Bays) collapsed. From the night
of the 24th day, rain fell, and
by dawn the trembling ceased.

(Waka poem)
O earthly deity (Kunitsukami),
　Even the rocks of a thousand ages
　　you have shaken and set firm—
　　　Let this be
　　　an example of a reign
　　　that shall never waver.
　　　　　Lord Nakanoin Michimochi
