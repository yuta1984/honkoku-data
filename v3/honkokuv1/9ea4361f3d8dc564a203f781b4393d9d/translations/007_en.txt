◯An Account of a Man Who Lost the Flesh of One Leg in the Earthquake

The ancients said: A general commanding three armies must not so much as blink even if Mount Tai collapses before him, and must show no sign of fear even if great deer charge toward him, commanding his troops with composure. This is said for no other reason than to warn that if one's heart is disturbed, the mind becomes darkened and one's strategies will go astray. Even if ordinary people cannot achieve such a standard, one who makes a habit of keeping this in mind, remaining unswayed by circumstances, and exercising sound judgment in moments of crisis may rightly be called a person of true character. Without such mental discipline, one's soul departs from the body, causing one to lose all sense of judgment, and regret is seldom small. For example, in the event of a sudden fire, people may abandon their most precious belongings and instead carry off worthless things like old sandals or brooms as they flee the flames. This happens because the mind becomes so disordered in the chaos that one loses all sense of priority.

No matter how poor a person may be, surely they possess household goods more valuable than a worn-out pair of sandals. The reason one forgets this and reaches first for whatever trivial object catches the eye is that the soul is not truly present—one has lost one's senses. Thus, the "Great Learning" (Daigaku) also teaches: "If the mind is not present, one looks but does not see, listens but does not hear, eats but does not taste the flavor." It is also observed there that "where there is fear and alarm, one cannot attain correctness." Here is the story of a man who lived near Ōgibashi in Fukagawa, eking out a modest livelihood in a very humble rented house. When this recent earthquake struck, he was startled and attempted to rush outside, but at that moment, a beam above the threshold fell and trapped him around the thighs, so that he could not get out and was suffering greatly. Just then, the neighbors and people from all around had rushed outside and were noisily calling out to one another inquiring about safety, when this man raised his voice and cried out for help. They all came running and looked upon the scene: from the waist up he was outside the house, but one leg alone remained...
