○ Foundation stones stacked three or five tiers high were observed to have completely collapsed in the great earthquake of the recent Year of the Rabbit (U). Furthermore, considering that stone Jizō statues, stone lanterns, stone walls, and the like all likewise crumbled and toppled, it is clear that foundation stones piled too high are not suitable for withstanding earthquakes. If one wishes to stack them three tiers or more, all stones must be fastened together with clamps (kasugai). The method for doing so should be understood by referring to the figure on the left.
