【Right Page】
The foremost priority is to construct the roof as extraordinarily sturdy as possible. For this reason, ridge beams (munageta), joined rafters (gasshōgi), purlins (moya), common rafters (taruki), and similar elements are dispensed with entirely. Instead, thick back-cut boards (seita) of pine, cypress, or similar timber are nailed directly to the ground ridge beam (jimune) and the perimeter beams (jimawari no keta) using large ship nails (funakugi), as shown in the figure to the left.
