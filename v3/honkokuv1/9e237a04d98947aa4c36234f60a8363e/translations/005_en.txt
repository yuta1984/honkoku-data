Workers focus solely on completing jobs quickly, inserting thin tenons into large holes, and similarly using the cheapest and most inferior lumber throughout, devoting themselves entirely to making dishonest profits. As a result, during the great storm that struck on the twenty-fifth day of the eighth month of this year, even newly built houses were blown down in great numbers, causing many deaths, injuries, and casualties. Since contract construction became the established bad custom of the age, building craftsmen have grown unaccustomed to constructing solidly, and some have no understanding of proper techniques whatsoever. Nevertheless, people leave everything to these craftsmen and prioritize only getting the lowest price, and for this reason they cannot escape such calamities as earthquakes and great storms. Among them,

there are those who wish to build solidly, yet fearing the high cost of labor and materials, they have no choice but to entrust the work to the same slovenly construction methods as before. Meanwhile, the transformations of the energies of heaven and earth, having accumulated over many years, naturally cause coal veins and fire veins to gradually spread and penetrate the earth's interior, and one cannot know when or what kind of violent calamity may erupt. Particularly in recent years, great earthquakes have struck various provinces repeatedly, and it is difficult to say with certainty that another will not occur within the next year or two, without even waiting five or seven years. Yet to sit at ease and dwell in a poorly-built house—the danger of this is truly like standing before the mouth of a great cannon...
