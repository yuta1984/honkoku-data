An Illustrated Guide to Disaster Prevention Methods for Earthquakes and Violent Storms

Among all things in the universe, disasters never cease, and earthquakes are among them. While minor earthquakes are not particularly cause for alarm, when they become violent, they destroy houses, harm human lives, and are inevitably followed by fire. Truly, earthquakes are one of the greatest disasters between heaven and earth. Nevertheless, there are few people who know in advance the signs indicating whether a coming earthquake will be weak or strong.
