【Left Page】
The figure on the right depicts a two-story building with a frontage of two and a half ken, in which three front pillars are erected, a shutter box (tobukuro) of three shaku is attached, the span of two ken is left without a pillar, and on the second floor, shutter boxes are attached on both the left and right sides, making the window opening (mado) nine shaku wide — this is a figure showing diagonal braces (sujikai) cut and fastened into such a structure. Even if eaves (hisashi) are to be added, the diagonal braces should first be fastened as shown in the figure, and only thereafter should the eaves be attached from below the second-floor partition doors. That said, in places where it is difficult to drive the diagonal braces from the exterior, they should be driven from the interior. Furthermore, for the back entrance and side entrances as well, wherever there are small partition doors at entryways, one should consult this figure, carefully consider the arrangement, and then drive the braces accordingly.
