〇 In general, between the underside of the ridge beam (munagi) and the top of the main beam (hari), short upright posts (tsuka-bashira) are erected with horizontal penetrating tie beams (yoko-nuki) passed through them — this construction method is the same in all directions. However, in cases such as tile-roofed buildings, the load is particularly heavy, and therefore such structures are extremely dangerous during a major earthquake. For this reason, as shown in the figure to the left, diagonal braces (sujikai) should be cut and securely fastened into the short upright posts (tsuka-bashira).
