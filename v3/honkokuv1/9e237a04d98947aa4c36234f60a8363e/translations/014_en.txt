The notching on the exterior small-front (komae) surfaces should also be kept shallow, and only the pillars at the four corners need be of eight-sun square timber; however, these too should be finished only with adze-work (chōna-uchi) without planing, and merely the interior corners should be cut away and removed to a depth of about two sun. Furthermore, if one attempts to construct the joinery (sashiguchi) with great rigidity and strength, the cost becomes unbearable; therefore, it is better to proceed with competitive-bid construction (nyūsatsu-fushin), and, as shown in the figure on the left, diagonal braces (sujiちがい) should be installed in a cross pattern on the interior surfaces. These diagonal braces should be made by sawing knotty, poor-quality cypress (hinoki) timber of one-shaku square cross-section into boards one sun thick, cutting them to appropriate lengths, notching each pillar to a depth of one sun, driving the braces in firmly, and then driving three or more large nails of over three-hundred-me in weight into each pillar.
