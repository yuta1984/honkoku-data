A Shortcut to Diagnosing Illness (Satsubyō Shōkei)　　　Three volumes
　When one examines the treatment methods of those who call themselves eye doctors in remote mountain communities, the preparations, pills, and powders they claim as their household secrets amount to no more than one or two methods, and regardless of whether the symptoms differ, they attempt to treat all conditions with the same medicine. Consequently, in cases of serious illness, there are those who ultimately become incurable invalids. This is truly a matter for the deepest lament. In general, many of the various eye conditions have their origins in various internal and external diseases of the body. Unless one attacks those underlying conditions, no effect whatsoever may be obtained. Yet when one seeks to master all branches of internal and external medicine in their entirety, the expense is not inconsiderable, and one may fear that one's capabilities may fall short. This book presents the secret orally-transmitted art that the venerable author received from his teacher, and it is a remarkable work by which anyone — even a layperson — can swiftly examine and freely prescribe an appropriate remedy for not only eye diseases, but all conditions of internal medicine, surgery, women's medicine, and pediatrics, as well as conditions that are difficult to identify such as similar diseases, related conditions, and concurrent illnesses. It is by no means intended for the perusal of erudite expert physicians,

A Quick Guide to Auspicious and Inauspicious Directions (Compiled Through Many Years of Testing)　　Complete
　It is said that when one transgresses the great inauspicious directions such as one's birth-star direction (honmei), the directly lethal direction (tekisetsu), the Dotensha direction, the Five Yellow direction (gokō), the Golden Deity direction (konjin), the Three Calamities direction (sansetsu), the Great General direction (daishōgun), and the Demon Gate (kimon), difficult illness, death, and disaster will arrive together. These words are not false. Therefore, when one tries to select and use an auspicious direction, inauspicious forces may continue to afflict a single direction for three or five years, making it impossible to obtain the desired direction. This book records what the venerable author discovered through many years of testing and distinguishing whether disasters actually occur: namely, that there exists a technique by which even an inauspicious direction may be entered without suffering any curse from it whatsoever, causing no hindrance year after year and month after month. It omits what is useless, records what is practical, explains in detail the auspiciousness and inauspiciousness of directions, and aims to bring good fortune to all people. It is truly a remarkable book that allows one to quickly determine what is auspicious and what is inauspicious.

A True Record of Blessings and Goodness (Fukuzen Jitsuroku)　　　　Complete
　This book records actual events of recent times, collecting accounts of people rescued from mortal peril, near-death experiences, and grave illness, and the visible rewards (yōhō) that clearly came to those who helped them, as well as accounts of hidden evil deeds and how swiftly their curses manifested. Written entirely in hiragana, it is a book that is easy and entertaining to read even for women and children.
