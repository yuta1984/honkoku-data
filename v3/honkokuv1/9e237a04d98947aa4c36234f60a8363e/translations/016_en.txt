The reason for cutting away approximately two sun from one corner of the interior face of each of the four corner pillars, as shown in the figure on the right, is to make it convenient to fit the interior left and right split boards (itawari) into the notched area and nail them in place with large nails. Note that when five-sun square timber is used for the pillars on all four sides, seven-sun square timber will suffice for the four corner pillars. When six-sun square timber is used for the four-sided pillars, the four corner pillars should be eight-sun square. Furthermore, when rough timber of four-by-six or five-by-six dimensions is used for the four-sided pillars, and eight-sun square timber is used for the four corners, the result will be both inexpensive and sturdy.
