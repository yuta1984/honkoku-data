Furthermore, given the irregular small earthquakes that occur day after day, month after month, since one cannot know how severe they may be, even the slightest tremor causes alarm and fear, making it impossible to feel at ease in ordinary daily life. It is here that I have devised measures to prevent the great disasters of earthquakes and violent storms, wishing to share this good fortune with all people alike. However, since methods involving complex and costly construction may be beyond the means of some, I have devised an approach that even someone as extremely poor as myself can readily put into practice—one that I have tested through many years of personal experience—requiring only the lightest of expenses, yet rendering structures as solid as bedrock.
