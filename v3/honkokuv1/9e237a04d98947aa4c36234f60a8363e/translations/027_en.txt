【Right Page】
　This book selects and compiles the discussions of various military strategists of the present day, as well as the outstanding theories never before put forward by earlier authorities, covering everything from methods of coastal defense, the merits and demerits of naval and land tactics, to the gains and losses of military training. It makes clear how, by fully understanding the strengths and weaknesses and the true and false states of both sides, and by wielding the martial might of our divine nation against the great enemies of the entire globe, victory is assured. It is a book designed to draw out the innate subtle brilliance of all people, and to cause ingenious strategies and wondrous plans for winning every battle to spring forth from within each person's own heart.

Essentials of Naval Warfare (Suisen Yōryaku)　　Complete
　This book records methods of defending the coasts of the Imperial nation, with particular attention to Osaka — which is close to the Imperial capital — and Uraga — which is close to Edo — both of which are positions of critical importance. It describes the military techniques for establishing preparations that guarantee victory, and for annihilating in an instant enemy barbarian fleets of thousands of warships, whether they press upon Osaka harbor or push into the waters along the shores of Bōsō and Sagami. It also records the essential secrets of naval warfare for defeating a large enemy with a small force and winning every battle, even should the barbarians of every nation on the entire globe press in simultaneously and surround and attack the Imperial nation.

Essentials of Land Warfare (Rikusen Yōryaku)　　Complete
　This book records the techniques for defensive combat against the furiously charging barbarian troops from overseas who, despising us, force their way ashore and attempt to overwhelm us with weapons such as large and small cannons, bombs (bonpen), poison smoke, grenades, and grapeshot. It does not adhere rigidly to the doctrines of ancient military law, nor does it follow the Western-style methods currently in vogue, but instead preserves the strength developed through the Imperial nation's training in archery, horsemanship, swordsmanship, and spearmanship, applies them with flexible ingenuity, and records the techniques for defeating a large force with a small one and winning every battle. The venerable author declares these three books above to be three secret works of a quality never before seen in all of history.
