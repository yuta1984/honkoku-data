This is a document, accompanied by a map, compiled by Kawakami Kingorō, the magistrate (daikan) of Nakanojō in Shinano Province, inquiring into and recording rumors and reports. Around the Hour of the Boar on the night of the 24th day of the 3rd month [approximately 10 p.m.], a sudden great earthquake struck. The majority of the earthen walls and defensive perimeter walls of the Nakanojō jinya (magistrate's office compound) collapsed, though the main buildings sustained no particular damage. The villages in the vicinity of the jinya similarly had their eaves shaken loose, but there were no completely collapsed houses or injured persons. Even within the jinya compound itself, people set up makeshift outdoor encampments in the gardens, and no one remained living inside the main building. From that night until dawn, there were eighty to ninety tremors of various sizes. From the following 25th day through to the last day of the month, tremors continued at a rate of approximately one hundred fifty or one hundred per day, with five or six major shocks among them. From the 1st through the 5th of the current month, tremors numbered thirty to forty per day, and while the intervals between them have grown longer, there have still been two or three major shocks among them, and even now the tremors have not ceased. In the villages, many people are sleeping outdoors.

Item: Within Mizuuchi District under this magistrate's jurisdiction, there are six villages where private homes have been completely destroyed and many deaths have occurred, while the remaining villages have sustained various degrees of damage. Inspection officials (tedai) have been dispatched, but the full investigation has not yet been completed.

Item: Within Mizuuchi District of the Matsushiro domain, there is a township known as "Yamanaka," a place assessed at thirty thousand koku. The villages therein were struck by an especially powerful earthquake; landslides occurred in every village, all residences were destroyed, and deaths were numerous.
