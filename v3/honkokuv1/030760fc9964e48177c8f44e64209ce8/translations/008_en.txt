The waters burst forth so suddenly that this was an unprecedented, rapidly rising great flood — entirely beyond expectation. I am deeply distressed to think that, in addition to the loss of homes swept away by the current, there must be a great many who have drowned, and furthermore, that extensive damage to fields and land will surely have resulted. Although I shall report the full details once a proper investigation has been conducted, I hereby submit this preliminary notification for the time being.

　　14th day of the 4th month (*2)
　　　　　　　　　　　Sanada Shinano-no-kami (*1)
