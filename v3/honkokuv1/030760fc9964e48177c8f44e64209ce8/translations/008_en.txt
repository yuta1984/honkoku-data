The floodwaters burst forth so suddenly and with unprecedented speed, catching us entirely off guard, that we fear not only many houses have been swept away, but also that a considerable number of people have drowned. Furthermore, we anticipate that extensive damage to agricultural land will have occurred, and this causes us deep distress. We will investigate the full details and report them to you in due course, but for now we humbly submit this preliminary notification. Respectfully.

　　14th day of the 4th month

　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sanada Shinano-no-kami
