As the floodwaters burst forth so suddenly and with unprecedented speed, and the disaster being entirely unforeseen, there are no doubt numerous houses swept away and many drowned. Furthermore, I am greatly troubled that there will likely be extensive damage to farmland as well. The full details will be investigated and reported in due course, but for now I humbly submit this notification. Respectfully submitted.

　　Fourteenth day of the fourth month

　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sanada, Governor of Shinano
