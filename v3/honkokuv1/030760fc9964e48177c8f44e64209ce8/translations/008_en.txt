As this was a sudden and unprecedented flood surge beyond all expectation, it is feared that not only have many houses been swept away, but that there are also numerous victims of drowning. Furthermore, it is anticipated that a great deal of agricultural land will have been damaged (*1). I am deeply distressed by this situation. While the full details will be investigated and reported in due course, I humbly submit this initial notification for the time being. Respectfully submitted.

　　14th day of the 4th month
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Sanada Shinano-no-Kami (*2)
