Regarding my domain of Matsushiro in Shinano Province, as previously reported to you, within Yamahirabayashi Village in Sarashina District, Iwakura Mountain collapsed and fell into the Sai River, blocking it at two locations. The water gradually accumulated to a depth of several tens of jō. Although water had been pooling for a day or two, it rose up over the lower of the two blocked sections, reaching a height of over two jō at the far end, and then suddenly appeared to burst through the barrier. On the evening of the 13th, just past the Hour of the Rooster (shortly after 4:00 p.m.), a great rumbling arose from the direction of the mountain, followed by a sound like the roaring of rapids, whereupon a violent torrent suddenly rushed into the river channel, instantly breaching the old embankments on both sides, or overtopping them, making it impossible to mount any defense — so the river officials reported to us in rapid succession. Before long, several villages on Kawanakajima were entirely inundated, the waters poured into the Chikuma River and flowed back upstream, and had already pushed up as far as the edge of the castle. From around dusk until about the Hour of the Rat (around midnight), the Chikuma River rose approximately two jō above its normal level. Not only Kawanakajima, but also the riverside villages in Takai District and Minnai District were submerged, with several locations where the current's path could be discerned. It goes without saying that crops were covered in mud, and there must have been extensive erosion and scouring, though the full extent was difficult to assess in the darkness.
