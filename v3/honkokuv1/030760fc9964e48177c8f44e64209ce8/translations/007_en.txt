As previously reported to the authorities regarding my home domain of Matsushiro in Shinano Province: within Yamanaka Hirabayashi Village in Sarashina District, Iwakura Mountain collapsed and was pushed into the Sai River, blocking it at two locations, where water gradually accumulated to a depth of several tens of jō (*1). Although water had been pooling for a day or two beforehand, it rose up over the lower of the two blocked locations, reaching a depth of over two jō at its furthest point, whereupon it suddenly appeared to have burst through. On the evening of the 13th of the same month, just past the Hour of the Rooster (*2), a great rumbling arose from the direction of the mountain, followed immediately by a sound like the roaring of rapids. All at once, a violent torrent of water surged into the course of the Sai River, instantly breaking through or overtopping the old embankments on both banks, and the river officials (*3) sent successive urgent reports that there was no way to hold back the flood. Before long, several villages throughout the Kawanakajima area (*4) were entirely inundated; the water flowed into the Chikuma River, causing it to flow in reverse, already pushing a great volume of water as far as the foot of the castle (Matsushiro Castle). From around dusk until around the Hour of the Rat (*5), the Chikuma River rose approximately two jō above its normal water level. Not only Kawanakajima, but all riverside villages in Takai District and Minnai District were submerged. Although in several places the course of the current appeared to be discernible, the crops were certainly coated in mud, and the damage from scouring and erosion must have been immense — though it was impossible to assess clearly in the darkness. After midnight, the water level appeared to stabilize, and by dawn, the waters had gradually begun to recede. I had previously ordered the villagers to prepare flood-control measures, however...
