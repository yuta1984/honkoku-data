Along the Hokurikudō highway, there were likewise destroyed houses and deaths, and there are places where cleanup has not yet been possible. From Tabajima to the vicinity of Zenkōji, the stench remains extremely strong even now, and travelers passing through are said to be covering their noses.

Item: The nature of this earthquake appears to have its origin (epicenter/primary zone of damage) in Mizuchi District of Shinano Province, where landslides and ground ruptures were especially numerous, and there are places where entire villages were completely destroyed. Takai, Sarashina, Hanishina, Chikuma, and Azumi — these five districts — likewise have many locations with collapses and destroyed houses, but these are, comparatively speaking, secondary and peripheral in nature; even among them, the severity varies depending on local terrain. In the two districts of Chiisagata and Saku, the damage was not as extreme, and there were no cases of houses being crushed by the shaking. Even so, compared to ordinary earthquakes, it would still be described as a great earthquake.

Item: Echigo Province is also said to have experienced a similarly great earthquake, but the rumors are varied and inconsistent, and the actual situation remains entirely unclear.
