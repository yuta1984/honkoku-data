(Continued from previous page) Along the Hokurikudō post road likewise, there are destroyed houses and deaths, and in some locations the clearing of debris has not yet been possible. From Tabashima to the vicinity of Zenkōji, the stench is still so severe that travelers cover their noses, it is reported.

Item: The nature of this earthquake appears to have its origin (epicenter and center of destruction) in Mizuchi District of Shinano Province, where landslides and ground fissures are numerous, and there are locations where entire villages have been completely destroyed. The five districts of Takai, Sarashina, Hanishina, Chikuma, and Azumi similarly have many places with collapses and destroyed houses, but these present more the appearance of peripheral (secondary) damage; even within these areas, the severity varies depending on local ground conditions. In Chiisagata and Saku districts, the force was weaker and there were no houses destroyed by collapse. Even so, compared with ordinary earthquakes, this too might well be called a great earthquake.

Item: It is said that Echigo Province likewise experienced a great earthquake, but reports vary and the facts remain entirely unclear.
