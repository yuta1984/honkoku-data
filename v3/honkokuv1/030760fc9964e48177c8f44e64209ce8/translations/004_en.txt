Along the main highway (Hokuriku Road, continued from the previous page), there are similarly destroyed buildings and dead bodies, and in some places the clearing away of debris has yet to be accomplished. From Tabashima through to the vicinity of Zenkōji, the stench remains so severe even now that travelers passing through must cover their noses.

Item: The pattern of this earthquake appears to have its origin (epicenter) in Mizuuchi District of Shinano Province, where landslides and ground ruptures are numerous, and in some places entire villages have been completely destroyed. In the five districts of Takai, Sarashina, Hanishina, Chikuma, and Azumi, there are likewise many locations with collapses and destroyed buildings; however, these represent a comparatively secondary degree of damage. Even among these, there appear to be variations in severity depending on the nature of the local terrain. In the two districts of Chiisagata and Saku, there are no buildings that were destroyed by such force, yet even so, compared with ordinary earthquakes, it would still be fair to call this a great earthquake.

Item: Echigo Province is said to have experienced a similarly great earthquake, but reports are varied and inconsistent, and the true facts remain entirely unclear.
