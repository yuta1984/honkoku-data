[The following is transcribed from pasted notes only]

[Pasted note to the left of "Matsushiro"]
The river has run dry, leaving only a riverbed. The villages downstream lived in terror of being inundated by the backed-up waters, and the inhabitants fled up into the mountains or escaped to other places.

Hirabayashi

In the area of the two villages of Nagai and Hirabayashi, the mountainside split and collapsed into the Sai River, carrying with it houses and trees exactly as they stood. The river upstream has become a lake.

[Pasted note in the river channel, center-left]
The width [of the landslide dam] is said to be approximately eight chō.
The height is approximately eighty to ninety ken.
At present, the water level stands approximately thirty ken above [the surface of the collapsed material].

The width [of the lake] is five to six chō, and in some places as much as fourteen or fifteen chō.

The villages in this vicinity — some fifteen villages in all — have been submerged, and their houses have been swept away.
The length [of the lake] is said to be six to seven ri.
