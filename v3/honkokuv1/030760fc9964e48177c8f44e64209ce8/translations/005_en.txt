[The following are transcriptions of sticky notes affixed to the document]

[Note affixed to the left of "Matsushiro"]
The riverbed had dried up (due to the dam),
but villages downstream feared flooding (from a potential dam breach),
and fled to the mountains or escaped to other places.

Nagahira (area)

In both Nagai and Hirabayashi villages, the villages
split apart from mid-slope of the mountain
and collapsed into the Sai River; houses and
trees fell in just as they stood.
Upstream, (the river) became a lake.

[Note written in the river area, center-left]
The width (of the landslide dam) is said to be about 8 chō.
The height is about 80–90 ken.
Currently, the water surface (above the blockage) is about 30 ken.

The width (of the lake) is 5–6 chō,
or in some places
14–15 chō.

The villages in this vicinity — 15 villages in total — have become submerged,
and their houses have been washed away.
The length (of the lake) is said to be 6–7 ri.
