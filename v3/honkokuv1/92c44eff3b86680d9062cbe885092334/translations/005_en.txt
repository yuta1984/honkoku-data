Preface to Brief Words on Hot Springs

Hot springs, lying within the earth, rise upward through fiery, steaming force, gushing forth in a continuous, vigorous flow. Some are warm, some are hot, and others are half cold and half warm. Within the space of just a few steps (several *bu*), water and fire coexist together as if in a cauldron—yet one does not know what it is that makes them so. Those who offer explanations say that sulfur (*石硫黄*) forms the root cause thereof. Others say that wherever hot springs are found, white alum (*白礬*), cinnabar (*丹砂*), and sulfur necessarily form their root, and it is these that, through steaming, cause the waters to flow forth.
