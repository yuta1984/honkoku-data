However, upon careful reflection, this is not the case. The theory that hot springs well up from substances such as arsenolite (yoseki), alum (banseki), realgar (ōō), and arsenic (hiseki) is likewise fundamentally mistaken. As for the claim that hot springs well up through the vital energy (ōki) of dragons, this is the most egregious of nonsense and not even worthy of refutation. Tang Zixi's statement — "They simply exist between heaven and earth, and constitute a category unto themselves; their nature is inherently thus from the beginning, and it is not necessarily the case that they must await some condition before becoming warm" — is a theory that cannot be improved upon, and yet even this ultimately amounts to not knowing the reason why hot springs are hot springs, and thus cannot be called satisfactory.
