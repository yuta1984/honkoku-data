The slight flaw (微瑕) in a perfect white jade (白璧) does not rise to the level of damaging the jade's essential beauty, yet in the end it cannot be said to leave the reader entirely without a sense of dissatisfaction. For this reason, I will now faithfully set forth (表章) this theory in full, and then, taking from it what is valuable and setting aside what is not, finding a middle path (取捨折中), I shall present my humble views as follows.

Item One: The Supreme Ultimate (太極) moves and generates yang; it becomes still and generates yin. Yin qi produces cold, and cold qi generates water. Yang qi produces heat, and heat qi generates fire. Now, fire in its original nature has no fixed form or substance: when it disperses, it is simply the qi of yang; when it gathers, it takes on the substance of fire. The ancients said: "Fire is one of the Five Phases; it has qi but no substance. Its qi circulates in Heaven, lies hidden in Earth, and is put to use by humanity."
