**Postscript to** ***Onsen Shōgen*** **(Brief Remarks on Hot Springs)**

In the summer of the *Kansei Kichū* year [1793], I aired out the surviving manuscripts of my family founder, the late Master Sōkei. From among them I took up the *Onsen Shōgen* ("Brief Remarks on Hot Springs") and spoke to my elder [or: the late author's spirit / a senior family member], saying: "In general, those in this world who bathe in hot springs all fail to do so in the proper manner (*hō*). Therefore, not only are they unable to cure their ailments, but on the contrary some return home having added a new affliction, and then lay the blame upon the hot spring itself. Is this not also a grave error? By now engraving this book and transmitting it to the world, the benefit it shall render is by no means small." The elder [my senior] sighed quietly and with feeling.
