...and this is by no means some idle, unfounded tale. In ancient times, before the heavenly grandchild had yet descended to earth, the deity Ōanamuchi no Mikoto, together with Sukunahikona no Mikoto, ruled over this Land of Abundant Reed Plains (Toyoashihara no Nakatsukuni), and taking pity upon the people of this land who were dying young, the two deities established the methods of medicine, ritual incantation, and hot spring therapy, and thereby saved the people from their suffering and affliction. At one point during this time, Ōanamuchi no Mikoto fell into a condition of ill health, whereupon Sukunahikona no Mikoto promptly had him bathe in a hot spring, and the deity's illness was at once cured. From this point forward, the two deities traveled throughout the land, surveying suitable locations, and established hot springs in various places—a fact that is clearly attested in the ancient histories. Following this, it is recorded in old chronicles that the two emperors Jomei and Kōtoku also bathed in hot springs to treat their ailments...
