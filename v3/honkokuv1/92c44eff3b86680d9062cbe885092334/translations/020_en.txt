If one does nothing but plunge recklessly into the waters, neglecting proper diet and care of one's body in all other respects, one will instead bring great harm upon oneself. In the work known as the *Arima no Ki* (Record of Arima) by a man called Kawai Akitaka of Bizen Province, it is written: "The reason those who undergo spa treatment fail to maintain the proper degree of health cultivation is that they take the hot springs for granted. Not only do people of all four estates (samurai, farmers, artisans, and merchants) waste days that ought to be treasured, but those who serve in official positions—having absented themselves from their busy duties at their lord's side to come to this place—to then neglect their health cultivation is a deplorable thing indeed. One should think of the hot spring as one's lord, as one's deity, and devote one's mind entirely to finding the means by which to cure one's illness." What is written here is indeed quite right.
