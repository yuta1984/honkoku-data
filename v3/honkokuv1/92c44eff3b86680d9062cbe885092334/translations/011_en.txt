*Onsen Shōgen* (Brief Remarks on Hot Springs)

Article 1. The efficacies of hot springs in general are: to spread and circulate the yang qi; to transform and guide out stagnant blood (留瘀); to warm the skin and body; to bring comfort and ease to the joints; to open and unblock the channels and collaterals (経絡); to circulate the qi and blood; to break up all stagnant blood (瘀血); to expel and thrust out foul stagnation (穢瘀) and pathogenic poisons (邪毒); to circulate and dissolve obstructions and stagnation (癕滞); to dispel cold (寒); to drive away dampness (湿); and (to treat) accumulations and gatherings (積聚), mounting and concretion ailments (疝癥), and conditions of the chest, abdomen, flanks, and so forth...
