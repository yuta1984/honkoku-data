However, since hot springs also exist in cold countries, this theory too is fundamentally difficult to believe, as he states. For this reason, Tang Zixi's own conclusion was: "Hot springs are simply one distinct category of thing that exists between heaven and earth, and the reason why hot springs well up is something that is exceedingly difficult to determine. Between heaven and earth, water is water, tides are tides, and hot springs are hot springs — it is not necessarily the case that they become warm only after waiting for some specific condition to be met." Such, then, are the various theories that have been advanced throughout the ages to explain the principles of hot springs, and these represent the full extent of those explanations. Now, if I were to render my own humble judgment on the matter, all of the above theories are mistaken and unworthy of consideration.
