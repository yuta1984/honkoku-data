What principle could there be that would hold that within the great earth, yang qi does not gather and become fire? People of the world simply do not know of its existence merely because it is not visible to the eye. What manifests in Heaven is the image of Li (離, the trigram of fire); what lies latent within the Earth is the image of Kan (坎, the trigram of water). Indeed, that which lurks, lies hidden, accumulates, gathers, and condenses as yang qi within the earth — all of this is called "fire within the earth" (地中の火). That which, depending on the location, erupts and issues forth from this subterranean fire is called a "fire well" (火井). In Zhang Hua's Bowuzhi (博物志, "Records of Investigation of Things") from China, it is recorded that fire wells exist in the world, and in other texts as well there are numerous passages stating that fire issues forth naturally and constantly from within fire wells. Indeed, (in Japan too,) there are the fire and smoke of Mount Fuji and Mount Asama, the fire burning forth from the ground in Myōhōji Village in Echigo Province, the fire and smoke in the valley of Mount Aso in Higo Province, the fire of the Sulfur Mountain (硫黄山) in Bungo Province, and beyond these, in the mountains, valleys, and fields of various provinces, smoldering up from within the earth...
