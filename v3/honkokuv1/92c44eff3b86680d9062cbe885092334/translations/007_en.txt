This year, in the seventh month, his grandson Hakkei wished to have the work cut into woodblocks and transmitted to the world. He therefore came to me to request a preface, and I accordingly composed these humble words.

Third day of the beginning of autumn, Kansei *kinoto-tora* (1794) — Court Physician Sugimoto Ryōshiki

*Onsen Shōgen* — Preface
　　　　　Composed by Yamamoto Kiroku Nobuyū of Kitayama

One day, Hara Keichū came, bringing his son Hakkei, and entrusted him to me to receive instruction. He wished for Hakkei to carry on the family craft and follow in the footsteps of his grandfather. Now, Hakkei's grandfather, the late Master Sōkei, was a private scholar (*shoshi*) of Heian (Kyoto). He had once studied at the school of Master Tōgai. Among all the students at the academy at that time, there was none who surpassed him. Tōgai frequently praised him among those of the younger generation...
