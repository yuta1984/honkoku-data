(Continued from previous page) ...that which roams and moves through the space between Heaven and Earth: in Heaven, it is the yang qi of blazing flames (炎々), glowing redness (赭々), gentle warmth (煦々), and living vitality (生々); in Earth, it is the yang qi of pervasive warmth (温々), rising steam (蒸々), transformation (化々), and nurturing growth (育々). Without this qi, Heaven cannot generate things, and without it, Earth cannot nurture things. Now, when this yang qi gathers and condenses into form: in Heaven, this manifests as the true fire of the Sun (太陽真火) and as the shooting fires of stellar essences (星精飛火). Among those found on the surface of the earth are the fires produced by drilling wood (鑽木), scraping metal (戞金), and striking stone (撃石). The burning mirror (陽燧), when it faces the sun, ignites and produces fire; when metal and stone strike against each other, sparks are forcefully produced. All of these are instances of yang qi condensing and gathering. Therefore, although when it gathers and condenses it may take on form by attaching itself to physical things, its fundamental nature is simply qi, and it does not truly possess fixed substance or form.
