However, water that is exceptionally strong in saltiness, resembling the taste of *nigari* (bittern, the liquid remaining after salt is extracted from seawater), is again a case of excess. As for the color of the water: water that is black, red, blue, or otherwise strangely colored is not good. A color that is simply clear and white, like pure water, is to be considered desirable. Water that bears a slight yellowish tint is also acceptable. Even so, water that is so excessively yellow as to appear muddy and turbid is likewise a case where the properties are too overpowering.

Item: In various provinces, there are places where several separate hot spring pools (*yutsubo*) exist in one location. One must not assume that, simply because the land is all one and the same place, there is no difference between the springs. Even where one pool is separated from another by a mere two or three *ken* (approximately 3.6 to 5.5 meters), the properties of the water can differ greatly.
