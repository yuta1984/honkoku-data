When you feel that warmth has penetrated thoroughly throughout your body, you should get out of the bath immediately. Soaking for a long time at once will cause your vital energy (genki) to leak away, your bodily fluids (shin'eki) to dry up and become parched, which will be greatly harmful to the body.

Now, after getting out of the bath, you must not expose yourself to the wind. After bathing, the heat of the water penetrates deeply into the body, making one unaware of the cold, and it is common to sit for a long time wearing only a yukata (light cotton robe) and be attacked by wind and cold. Furthermore, since daily bathing opens up the surface energy (hyōki) of the body, one is prone to being affected by wind and cold even apart from immediately after bathing. Therefore, one must be very careful about this. One must absolutely not take daytime naps or doze off. If one absolutely must doze off, one should thoroughly dress oneself in bedding, draw screens around oneself, and then lie down. During a course of spa treatment (tōji), alcohol must be forbidden. Sexual activity goes without saying. Even after the course of treatment is completed, one must for a time refrain from these (alcohol and sexual activity)...
