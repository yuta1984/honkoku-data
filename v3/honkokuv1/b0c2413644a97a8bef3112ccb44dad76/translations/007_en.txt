This is what is meant by the saying: "The father created it, and the son has transmitted it." One day, the Physician-in-Attendance of the rank of *Hōgen*, Nagao Buntetsu, came carrying in his sleeve both the *Tenkei Wakumon* (Queries on the Celestial Classics) and the *Tengaku Meimoku-shō* (A Digest of Astronomical Terminology), and informed me, saying: "These two books have already been presented to the authorities (*kōchō*), and I wish to combine them and have them engraved on woodblocks, so that they may be treasured together with all under Heaven. I humbly request that you write a preface for them." I am a man of little talent — how could I possibly be worthy of such a request? Nevertheless, if those who read this book come to know that the Way of Heaven and one's own nature both issue from the same single source, and if this causes their hearts of reverence for Heaven and respect for their own nature never to slacken in the slightest, then this is indeed a book of great benefit to the moral instruction of the world. Cherishing the accumulated excellence of the Nishikawa family across the generations, I set down these words and present them as a gift.

At the time of:
The fifteenth year of Kyōhō, the year *Kanoe-inu* (1730), Winter Solstice.
