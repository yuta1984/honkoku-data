**Editorial Guidelines (Hanrei) for the Newly Printed Edition of the *Tenkei Wakumon***

Item One: The *Tenkei Wakumon* is, in essence, the model work of celestial learning (*tengaku*). Therefore, at the request of the Physician-in-Attendance of the rank of *Hōgen*, Master Nagao Buntetsu, we have now collated and revised the original text, added punctuation marks, and proceeded to publish it. Now, in celestial learning there are four paths: principle (*ri*), number (*sū*), instrument (*ki*), and phenomenon (*shō*). Therefore, even the most seasoned scholars cannot attain true mastery of this field without teachers and companions in study. For this reason, we have composed one additional volume and named it *Tengaku Meimoku* (Astronomical Terminology), intending to enable students to know the broad path forward without weeping at the crossroads, and to make understanding ever easier. This has accordingly been appended to the end of the work, and it too serves as a ladder and a ford leading into celestial learning. We ask that gentlemen of learning not ridicule it as mere vernacular talk or trivial accounts, and scatter and trample it underfoot.
