**Preface to the Combined Edition of "Tenkei Wakumon" and "Tengaku Maimoku Shō"**

Heaven consists of nothing more than Principle (ri) and Vital Force (ki). When referred to as that which governs and presides, it is called "Principle"; when referred to in terms of form and appearance, it is called "Vital Force." Therefore, among those who have discussed Heaven throughout the ages, some have spoken of it in terms of Principle, while others have spoken of it in terms of Vital Force, and their explanations are not uniform. Nevertheless, since the Two Forms (Heaven and Earth) first separated and came into being, whenever there is Heaven, there must necessarily be Earth to pair with it. Thus, Heaven is positioned above, where the sun, moon, stars, and constellations hang, and where cold, heat, wind, and rain move.
