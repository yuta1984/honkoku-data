〽 How rare and extraordinary an event this is — surpassing anything seen in the Chūgoku region, the northern provinces, or the western provinces, unprecedented across all lands! Across twelve provinces, the places ravaged by this great earthquake — let us recount the full account of events. It was the tenth month of this very year; the twenty-eighth day, at the break of dawn. In the province of Owari, in the district of Ōno, the highest peak is Hakusan. From the evening of the twenty-fifth day, the mountain began to rumble and tremble, and day and night the shaking grew ever more violent. The villages at the foot of the mountains had no moment of ease, and then, from the dawn of the twenty-eighth day, it was as if heaven and earth shattered all at once — a great burst of fiery light blazed forth — there was no time even to cry "Look out!" How terrifying! The villages of Higashi-Ōtani, Nishi-Neya, Hosoyo (Neodani)—

Nagashima — the villages and hamlets across a circle of three ri in every direction became a flat expanse of lake water, five to ten jō deep, and houses, people, and horses all plunged to the bottom, losing their lives without exception. How pitiful, how tragic!

And so — **[Mino · Owari]** What an accursed day this is! The destruction extends even to **[Etchū · Echizen]**, and **[Hida]** too crumbled away, bringing hardship to tens of thousands of people. In **[Shinano]**, wretched suffering — **[Mikawa]**, all people [cry to] Heaven's way; in the province of **[Suruga]**, one corner of the great peak of Mount Fuji crumbled, spewing sand, raining down scorched rocks, and it seemed as though heaven and earth themselves were breaking apart.
