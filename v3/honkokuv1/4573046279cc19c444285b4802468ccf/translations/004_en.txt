Suffering such bitter hardships, oh province of Ōmi! In Iga and Ise, the prayers are desperate. The injured and dead, beyond all help, fall and pile upon one another. In Yamashiro, the hills too, and Kawachi, the very ground shakes. Spanning Izumi, Settsu, Wakasa, and Tamba - across twenty-two provinces, within a brief span of time, the dead and injured are beyond counting. In the city of Nagoya, 2,585 houses collapsed; every brick building crumbled and was reduced to dust. Yet amidst all this, the great turret of Owari's Nagoya Castle did not lose a single roof tile - a truly extraordinary wonder in this calamity. Oh my, oh my!

《Song of the Great Earthquake - Part II (Lower Volume)》

And so! The great devastation of the earthquake: in Mino, in Gifu Prefecture, around Ōgaki, over six thousand houses were destroyed. Furthermore, the confirmed death toll reached approximately 974 people, and beyond that, the number of injured is beyond counting. Most pitiful of all was a textile company - its building stood three chō by thirty ken on each side, a three-story brick construction - with 74 staff officials and over 500 female factory workers, who, caught without warning by the unexpected earthquake, had no time even to cry out.
