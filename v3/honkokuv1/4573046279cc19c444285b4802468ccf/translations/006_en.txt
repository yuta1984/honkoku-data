At last the earthquake subsided, and the people felt relief for the first time—yet having lost their homes, they wandered destitute, parents and children, brothers and sisters, husbands and wives all separated, parted by death. In the midst of such hardship, the former lord of Owari bestowed upon the peasants of his old domain a relief sum of fifty thousand yen. Furthermore, the government convened a session and granted a relief allowance of three hundred and fifty-five thousand yen to the two prefectures of Aichi and Gifu. For the landslides, the repair of roads, and countless other arrangements—the boundless benevolence of the authorities left nothing wanting. What a grateful and noble tale of comfort this is to hear. *(Yan-re!)*
