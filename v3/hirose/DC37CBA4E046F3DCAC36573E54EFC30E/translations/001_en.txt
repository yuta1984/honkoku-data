**Right Margin**
On the 4th day of the 11th month of Kaei 7 (1854), the Year of the Tiger, an earthquake lasting approximately half an hour began at around 9:00 a.m. (hontsuirabe/primary survey). *Note: The great shaking at around 6:00 a.m. on the 5th is recorded later.*

**"Hontsuirabe" (Primary Survey): Account of the Great Osaka Earthquake — First Volume**

---

**[Upper Section]**

**Semba District**

The torii gate, stone lanterns, and gate of Zama Shrine collapsed.

Two or three houses north of a tofu shop on Kita-Kutarou-machi collapsed.

The tall wall at the corner of Shio-machi and Sanoya Bridge collapsed westward, resulting in three dead and injured persons.

The northwestern side of the main hall of Minami Mido (South Honganji) suffered minor damage.

The tall wall of a temple on the east side of Kitsune Koji (Fox Lane), Honmachi, collapsed.

The well-house (idobata) within the precincts of Goryō Shrine collapsed extensively.

The house at the corner of Junkei-machi and Doi-ike suffered great damage.

Seven or eight tenement houses behind a bamboo shop at the north end of the Sanoya Bridge on Nagahori suffered great collapse.

A fire caused by the earthquake (accidental fire) broke out in the vicinity of Honmachi and Doi-ike.

The corner at the north end of the Itaya Bridge, Nagahori (entering east) suffered great damage.

**West of the River (Awaza / Tōyamachi area)**

The southwestern corner of a sundries shelf at Awaza-Toyamachi suffered great damage (severe tilting).

Seven or eight houses a little to the northwest from there collapsed greatly.

Both sides of the street, twenty-five houses at a corner estate half a block west from there, collapsed greatly.

Approximately ten houses at the northeast corner a little to the west collapsed greatly.

The reception hall (taimenjo) of Gankyōji Temple collapsed greatly.

The mud-plastered wall (neribei) extending north along the same street became greatly warped.

Five or six houses half a block east-southeast from the south end of Okazaki Bridge on Awaza-bori collapsed.

One warehouse half a block east of Eidai-hama (Eidai Beach) collapsed.

Seven or eight corner estate houses at the north end of Ryōkoku Bridge, one block north (northwest and south corners), collapsed greatly.

The corner at the north end of Jōan Bridge became greatly warped.

At the corner of the north end of Hagoita Bridge on Kyōmachi-bori, approximately three ken (about 5.4 meters) collapsed, causing injuries.

Approximately 90% of the northeast corner at the north end of Shinnaka Bridge suffered great damage. East of the north end of Inusai Bridge, the walls of a warehouse-style house fell; also, a tall wall east of the crossroads collapsed. The walls of the side street (yokomachi) also suffered great damage.

---

**[Middle Section]**

**Dōjima / Fukushima**

The east side of the Five-Hundred Rakan Hall (Gohyaku Rakan-dō) collapsed entirely; the main hall suffered great damage.

The wing walls (sode-kabe) of its gate collapsed entirely.

The entrance (genkan) of Kōchi-in collapsed greatly.

The number of shrines and temples damaged in this vicinity is beyond counting.

The bathhouse at the north end (east side) of Shio Bridge collapsed greatly.

At the south end of the same bridge, one warehouse approximately 15 ken (about 27 meters) long lost all its plaster walls; only the pillars remained standing.

The gate of Kami Tenjin (Upper Tenjin Shrine) collapsed; the torii of the rear gate became greatly warped.

The worship hall (heiden) of Naka Tenjin (Middle Tenjin Shrine) collapsed greatly.

The votive picture hall (emadō) of Shimo Tenjin (Lower Tenjin Shrine) collapsed greatly.

The main hall of Kitano Fudō-ji Temple collapsed into a diamond (rhombus) shape.

The votive picture hall of Hachiman Shrine at the Nobeo-oka estate, entered from the west at the south end of Umeda Bridge, suffered great damage.

Two houses in the back street entered from the west at the north end of Umeda Bridge collapsed. The tall wall entered from the east at the same location was shattered. Four or five houses in this vicinity suffered great damage.

Several establishments from the corner, approximately four or five houses to the west, at the northwest corner of the north end of Midori Bridge, Kita Shinchi, suffered great damage.

Two or three houses on the beach entered from the west at the south end of Sakura Bridge collapsed. Minor damage was scattered throughout the vicinity. A board concealing the rafter ends (odare) fell from a house west of the crossroads, entering from the west.

**Tenma**

The well-house within the precincts of Tenman Shrine collapsed; small houses near the rear gate were damaged.

Minor damage occurred in the vicinity of Ikeda-machi.

Minor, scattered damage extended east through various locations in Tenma.

▲ Approximately thirteen wax-storage sheds (rō-naya) in the upland fields (畑地/bakuchi) of Kami-machi collapsed; wax and plaster mixed together in great confusion.

Minor damage was scattered at various locations near Honmachi's O-harai Suji.

▲ Mud-plastered walls within the precincts of Tennōji Temple collapsed at various locations, causing damage, but no other major incidents occurred.

The platform (butai) of Kiyomizu (Temple) collapsed greatly and sustained damage. It was rumored that the front wall of Dairen-ji Temple on Shimo-tera-machi became warped and damaged.

Minor, scattered damage was found at temples throughout the vicinity.

The great torii of Bishamonten Shrine on Naga-machi collapsed. Behind Naga-machi in this vicinity, great damage was found in many places.

---

**[Lower Section]**

Approximately ten new houses at the Niken-jaya (Two-teahouse) area of Tamatsukuri suffered great damage. Great damage occurred near Ebisu on Tenma-bori-kawa. A house a little south on the west横堀 (Nishi-yokobori) of Kaifune-machi became greatly warped. ▲ The tatami-floored room supported by four pillars in the Ogiya (fan shop) east of Shinmachi collapsed greatly. Other minor damage was too numerous to count throughout the town; a small number of injuries were sustained throughout, but no deaths.

---

**[Lower Upper Section — Southern Districts]**

**Southern Area**

Four or five houses south of the pipe/gutter on the east back side of Saiwai-machi were damaged. Three houses at the corner one block west of the west end of Kōei Bridge collapsed. The smithy and forge (kamaya tataraba) in Saiwai-machi collapsed greatly. There was great damage in this area.

Damage was scattered here and there from the vicinity of Shinkawa to near Tsuchi Bridge. Four or five rear houses on the west side of Mizo-no-kawa in Namba Shinchi suffered great damage. Damage was scattered throughout Kizu market district. The bell tower of An'yōji Temple collapsed. Great damage was found at various locations in Namba village.

Shinka (new houses area): same as above. Approximately 80% of the stone lanterns, etc., within the precincts of Sumiyoshi Shrine were damaged. Minor damage occurred to subsidiary shrines. Imamiya: same as above.

---

**[Lower Section — Horie Area]**

**Horie**

Great damage extended approximately half a block in the 3rd block of Tachibana-dōri. Newly built warehouses along the beach street in this vicinity sustained minor damage.

The kagura hall and sumo arena within the O-tabi (portable shrine resting place) of Horie Inari Shrine suffered great damage.

Warehouses at the west and east corners one block west of the south end of Yotsu Bridge sustained minor damage.

About four houses at the southeast corner of the 5th block of Oike-dōri collapsed.

The wall of the Tosa estate was damaged for approximately three ken. Additionally, approximately three houses at the east corner of the 6th block along Amida-ike Yokomon-suji collapsed and sustained damage.

○ The back side of Terashima sustained minor damage. Minor, scattered damage was found at various locations near Minami Ajikawa and Kujō. It is difficult to describe everything in detail.

---
