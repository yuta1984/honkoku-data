【Right Page】

Having lodged in a valley said to be the place where the Ogura no Miya had once resided, on a dawn when the lingering moon was beautiful — intending to gather various flowers to offer to the Buddha — he recalled how, on a morning when the moon still remained and dew gathered, someone had spoken of plucking flowers from the hedge to offer them to the Buddha-realm. Remembering this now afresh:

　　*Mukashi omou / magaki no hana wo / tsuyu nagara / taoriteimamo / tamuke tsuru kana*

(Thinking of the past, I pluck the flowers of the hedge — dew and all — and offer them up, just as was done then.)

【Left Page】

Word came that Ichijō of the Enchōmon'in, too, had fallen on hard times and had come to dwell in some wretched place. In reply to this message:

　　*Omoiyare / kakaru fuseya ni / sumai shite / mukashi wo shinobu / sode no namida wo*

(Imagine it — living in such a humble hut, with sleeves wet with tears longing for the past.)

Response:

　　*Shinoburam / mukashi ni kawaru / yo no naka wa / narenu fuseya no / sumai nomi ka wa*

(In a world so changed from the past one longs for, is it only the unaccustomed dwelling of a humble hut that has altered?)
