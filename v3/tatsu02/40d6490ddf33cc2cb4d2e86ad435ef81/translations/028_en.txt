【Left Page】

Having composed this poem, he departed from that place as well. Feeling now that there was nowhere left in the world to which he could truly attach his heart, he resolved himself fully to wandering, and composed:

　　*Somuku mi wa / sasuga ni yasuki / aramashi ni / nao yamafukaki / yado mo isogazu*

(Though mine is a body that has turned its back upon the world, I cannot deny that I still harbor a longing for ease and comfort — and yet, even so, I do not hurry to seek out a lodge deep in the mountains.)

He walked on and on, until at last he arrived at Kanazawa in Musashi Province. He found the house in which he had once lived to be greatly fallen into ruin and decay, and there he lodged for the night. On a moonlit evening —
