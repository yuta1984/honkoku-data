...was there, beating its wings violently, bristling its beak, and swooping toward them. Everyone was seized with fear and fled in confusion. Just as the bird was about to burst into the palace hall, Kenkō took a bowstring of *Murashigetō* and fitted an arrow to it, and shot — striking true without fail. The second bird, startled by the sound of the bowstring, made to turn and flee, but this one too he brought down with a shot. All the people came forward and gathered around to look at the birds.

【Left Page】

One resembled a duck in form, but its feet were covered with black fur. The other was shaped like a wild goose, yet its color was an extreme, vivid red. Presently, the Doctor of Letters (*Monjō Hakase*) and the Doctor of Medicine (*Kusuri no Hakase*) were summoned, and they were asked to identify the names of these birds. Yet not a single one among them dared to come forward and offer an answer to the imperial inquiry; they simply stood in brooding silence, thinking only that the creatures were deeply strange, and watched over them. After a short while, both birds transformed into foxes and vanished.
