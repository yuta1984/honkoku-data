...even if he wished to confide these feelings, to whom on earth could he entrust such a message? Unless it were the pillow — that pillow of glossy black night, of white-spread bedding — there was nothing else that could know the depths of his longing; and so he sighed and sank deeper into his grief. Then someone came and asked, "Are you lost in thought over something?" Perhaps his secret love had grown too great to be contained within his heart alone, for it seemed already on the verge of spilling out. And so, with a heart in turmoil that he felt he could no longer conceal, he let slip the name — the one whose image haunted him. At this, the other person seemed to recognize who was meant, and said: "Ah — that would be the one from Iga..."
