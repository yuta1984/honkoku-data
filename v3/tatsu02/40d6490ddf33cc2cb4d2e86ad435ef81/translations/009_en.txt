...As for someone to whom he might direct his yearning heart — whom on earth could he rely upon to convey his feelings? He sank ever deeper, and thought to himself: save for his pillow in the darkness of the night — white as unbleached cloth — there was no one who could know the depth of his longing. So he continued to lament. Then, after some time, a person came and asked, "Are you brooding over something?" Perhaps his secret love had grown too great to be contained within one heart alone — for it seemed to have already shown itself outwardly. Thinking it was already laid bare regardless, he unburdened himself entirely and spoke his feelings aloud. When he mentioned the name of the one whose image haunted him, the person seemed to recognize her at once: "That must surely be the one from Iga—"
