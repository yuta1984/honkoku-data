【Right Page】

The Retired Emperor was deeply moved and impressed by this, and bestowed a reward (*roku*) upon Kaneyoshi. At that time, both in the capital and in the provinces, this was spoken of as a truly remarkable occurrence. Now, from the direction of that person who had made a certain promise, day after day piled upon day as he waited, wondering "will she come?", until so much time had passed that even the thought of that person had become a source of longing and nostalgia, and so he lamented:

　　*Kaku shitsutsu / itsu wo ka kagiri to / shira ma yumi / okifushi sugosu / tsukihi naru ramu*

（Living like this, ever waiting — when shall I know the end? Like a taut bow, the days and months pass, as I rise and lie down in yearning.）

【Left Page】

He voiced his complaints and grievances, yet the other was clever and coolly indifferent, and so even the one he had relied upon was beyond his help. It may have been on a summer evening that:

　　*Tobu hotaru / mata tsukekosanu / kumoi yori / yukikau aki to / kaze ya fukuramu*

（O flying firefly — from those high cloudy heavens where my message has yet to reach, does the wind blow, journeying back and forth, heralding the coming autumn?）
