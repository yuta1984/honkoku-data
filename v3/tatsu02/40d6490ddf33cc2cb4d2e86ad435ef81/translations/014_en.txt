【Right Page】

　　*Koyoi dani / uchi-harawade / samushiro ni / tsumoreru chiri wo / hito ni mise mashi*

（If only, even this very night, I were to leave unswept the dust that has gathered on my thin rush mat — I would show it to you just as it is.）

With these words she sent word to him, and at last they met. That night they spoke together with tender intimacy, and the man — Kenkō — composed the following poem at dawn:

　　*Kinuginu no / nagori bakari ni / shiorame ya / kaeru tamoto no / kusunoki shitamichi*

（Could I bear to walk away wilting, carrying only the lingering memory of our robes laid together? — as I take the path home beneath the camphor trees.）
