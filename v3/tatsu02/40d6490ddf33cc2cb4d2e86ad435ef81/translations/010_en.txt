"...the daughter of the Governor of Iga" (the daughter of Tachibana no Naritada, Governor of Iga; known at court as Chūgū no Shōben). "If that is indeed who she is, then her appearance is fine enough, but it is her heart that surpasses all others." Hearing this, his longing drew all the closer within him, and wishing at least for the bond of hearing her voice, he composed the following poem:

　　*Kiite koso odorokarenuru yuki no uchi mo*
　　*Mateba karikeru uguisu no koe*

("Even amid the snow, once heard, how it startles the heart — like the voice of the warbler, for which one has so long been waiting.")

"Will you not let her hear of this, and take pity?" he asked. At this,

the companion apparently found it impossible to simply let the matter pass. "Very well, let me at least convey a word on your behalf," he said. So overjoyed was he that no words came to express what he wished to say. He composed:

　　*Shirasebaya konohagakure no umoremizu*
　　*Shita ni nagarete taenu kokoro wo*

("I wish she could know — like water buried beneath fallen leaves, flowing unseen beneath the surface, this heart of mine that flows on, never ceasing.")
