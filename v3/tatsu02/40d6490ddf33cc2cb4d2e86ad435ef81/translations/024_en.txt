【Right Page】

Messengers came from the Kantō and took away Middle Counselor Suketomo and the Right Minor Controller of the Left Toshimoto. From that time on, the capital was no longer as it had been — everything was shifting and changing, and those familiar faces one had known were disappearing one by one. On this:

　　*Katarubeki / tomo sae mare ni / naru mama ni / itodo mukashi no / shinobaruru kana*

(As even the friends with whom I might speak grow few, how much more deeply the past is longed for.)

Once, to the priest Ton'a, placing the words "yonetamahe se ni mo hoshi" (I would like some rice; I also want spending money) as the first and last syllables of a poem, he sent this verse:

【Left Page】

　　*Yo mo suzushi / nezame no kakiho / tamamakura mo / masode mo aki ni / hedatenaki kaze*

(The night too is cool; the hedge at the hour of waking, the jeweled pillow, even these long sleeves — the wind of autumn makes no distinction among them.)

Ton'a, composing his reply, placed the words "yonetanashi se ni sukoshi" (I have no rice; I have a little spending money) likewise, and replied:

　　*Yoru mo ushi / netaku wagaseko / hate wa kozu / naozari ni dani / shibashi toi mase*

(The night too is dreary; my beloved, longing for sleep, comes not at all — visit me, if only briefly, even in passing.)
