【Right Page】

...even at the Retired Emperor's palace, women of a flirtatious nature would make suggestive hints and spread rumors about him. Yet Kenkō was not in the least drawn to such sweet words. Day and night, he maintained a quiet, composed demeanor, and conducted himself with the utmost propriety and reserve.

At the beginning of the reign of Emperor Go-Daigo, when the Retired Emperor [Go-Uda] was overseeing the affairs of government, Kenkō served at court, standing night watch as a Takiguchi guard.

【Left Page】

On one occasion, from the direction of the connecting corridor (watadono), there appeared an exceptionally elegant lady-in-waiting, dressed in robes of a particularly lustrous beauty. Her eyes and features were lovely, her hair flowed long and gently swaying — her entire bearing was so remarkably refined and graceful that his gaze was involuntarily drawn to her and he watched until she passed from sight. He asked someone nearby who she was, and the reply was simply: "She is a person who has come as a messenger from the Consort's household." He felt it would be too forward to inquire further in detail, and so the matter rested there...
