【Right Page】

　　*Yo mo sugara / ame mo namida mo / furu mono wo / nado ka kaeri ko nu / wakare naru ramu*

(All through the night, rain falls — as my tears fall. Why, then, is this a parting from which you cannot return?)

He presented himself at the Retired Emperor's palace, and was received with remarkable grace and affection, and was kept in service there. At this time, His Majesty had entrusted the governance of the realm to the Emperor in the inner palace, and had himself withdrawn to the Daikaku-ji Palace in Saga; morning and evening, he devoted himself wholly to his devotional practices (*gyōhō*), entering into them with deep spiritual earnestness,

【Left Page】

and dwelt there in quiet seclusion. To alleviate the tedium of his retirement, His Majesty called for the poems that Kaneyoshi had composed and wished to peruse them. Upon receiving this summons and presenting his verses, Kaneyoshi sent word of his joy at this honor to the Sōjō (Dōga) of Seikan-ji——

　　*Hito shirezu / kuchi hate nu beki / koto no ha wo / amatsusora made / kaze ni chiru ramu*

(These leaves of words, which were destined to rot away unknown to any soul — how is it that the wind has carried them scattering all the way to the heavens above?)
