...Kaneyasu, Senior Long-service Officer, Junior Fourth Rank Upper Grade. Kanesada, Senior Deputy Head of the Jingikan, Senior Long-service Officer, Chamberlain. Kanena, Junior Fourth Rank Lower Grade, Master of the Right Capital. His son Kaneaki is said to be a descendant of the forty-eighth generation from the Great Imperial Ancestor, Lord Amenominakanushi no Mikoto. Kaneaki had three sons: the first was the Grand Bishop Jihen, who was a monk to whom the Southern Court paid reverence. The second was Kaneo, Junior Fifth Rank Upper Grade, Vice Minister of Popular Affairs. The third son was called Kaneyoshi, Lieutenant of the Left Gate Guards — this being Kenkō. It is said that this Kaneyoshi was born around the Kōan era [fifth year, the year of Mizunoe-Saru, i.e., 1282]. From his earliest youth he was gifted with remarkable intelligence,

【Left Page】

...and his devotion to serving his parents was deep, as was his compassion and love for others. In the literary arts, he followed in the footsteps of the renowned Xiangshan (Bai Juyi), turned his heart toward the rolling waves of waka poetry, fixed his eyes upon the snow in the garden, and studied by the light of fireflies through the window. In the martial arts of archery and horsemanship, it was only natural given the family into which he was born, and yet he excelled beyond all others in the world.
