【Right Page】

...and so the matter ended there without further inquiry. After he withdrew and returned to his quarters, only that lady's image lingered in his mind, and he could not forget her. Feeling forlorn, he shut himself away and fell into melancholy reverie — and even the snow as it continued to fall seemed to him something he might liken to his own accumulated longing:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*In the falling, piling snow, there is no path —*
*on Mount Yoshino,*
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;*who was it that pressed through the drifts,*
*lost in deep thought?*

How fickle my own heart is, he thought to himself — and yet all this from having glimpsed her for but a moment...
