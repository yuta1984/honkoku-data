【Right Page】

...and so the matter ended there without his learning anything more. He withdrew and returned home, yet only her image lingered in his mind, with no way to forget her. Remaining confined to his quarters in melancholy, lost in brooding reverie, the snow that fell and accumulated outside seemed to him comparable to his own piling thoughts:

　　*Furu yuki ni　michi koso nakere　Yoshino-yama*
　　*Tare fumi-wakete　omoi-iri-ken*

(Rough translation: "With snow falling deep, there is no path into Mount Yoshino — yet who was it that trod through and let their thoughts sink so far within?")

Even to himself, he thought: what a fickle, wayward heart I have — and all from having caught but a single fleeting glimpse of her...
