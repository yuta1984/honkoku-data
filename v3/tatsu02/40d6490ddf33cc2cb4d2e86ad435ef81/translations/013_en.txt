【Right Page】

　　*Tachinaru ru / kami no mimuro no / sakakiba no / kawaranu iro wa / tsurasa narikeri*

（Like the sacred sakaki leaves that stand ever familiar before the divine shrine, unchanging in their evergreen hue — it is your unchanging coldness that is so cruel to me.）

Feeling wretched that he was perhaps despised for being of no account in the world, within his jealous and resentful heart:

　　*Hakanashi ya / itowaru ru mi no / inochi mote / au ni kae n to / tanomu bakari wa*

（How fleeting and forlorn — that my only wish now is to offer up even this despised life of mine in exchange for just one meeting with you.）

Finding it also painful that she did not inquire after the darkness in his heart:

　　*Mono omou / waga na ya yoso ni / tachi nu ran*

【Left Page】

　　*Ima wa namida wo / tou hito mo nashi*

（Has the rumour of my grief-stricken brooding already spread abroad to distant ears? — There is no one now who comes to ask after my tears.）

As he continued to express himself in this way, the woman, being no block of wood or stone, said that she would meet him — yet it seemed there was no way or means by which they could actually do so. And so the months passed without it coming to pass, and, worn out with longing and prayer, he sent word:

　　*Tanome oku / kotonoha nakuba / awanu ma ni / kawaru kokoro wo / nagekasarамashi*

（Had you not left me with those words of promise — "I shall surely meet you" — I would not have had to grieve so over the heart that changes during these long months apart from you.）
