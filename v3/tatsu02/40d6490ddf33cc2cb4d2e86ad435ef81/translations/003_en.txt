【Tanesho-den (Biography of Origins)】

Upon tracing the ancient lineage of Kenkō Hōshi,
In the beginning of heaven and earth, there existed a deity known as Amenominakanushi no Mikoto, said to be the younger brother of Kuninotokotachi no Mikoto. This deity was also referred to by the name Amenoyakutari no Mikoto.
