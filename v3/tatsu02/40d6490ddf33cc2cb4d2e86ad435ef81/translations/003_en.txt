Taneshō-den (Biography of the Lineage)

Upon investigating the origins of Monk Kenkō,
In the beginning of heaven and earth, there was a deity known as Amenominakanushi-no-Mikoto, said to be the younger brother of Kunitokotachi-no-Mikoto. This deity was also known by the name Amenoyakutari-no-Mikoto.
From that deity, through successive generations of heirs:
Amenoawase-no-Mikoto, Amenoyawohi-no-Mikoto,
