【Main Text】
Among the five parts of the human body, the nose, mouth, ears, and eyes are all things that are needed quickly. Among these, the most important is the eye. From the moment one wakes in the morning until one sleeps at night, the eyes work without a single moment of rest. If they are overworked, one will suffer from wind-eye disease (fugan) and other eye ailments. Therefore, one must occasionally let the eyes rest and show them something entertaining. As for spending the New Year without giving them this rest — borrowing that saying, "Give the eyes a New Year's holiday" — does it mean merely blindfolding the eyes? Rather, at the turn of the year, people go to see the bean performers (mamezō) at the base of the mountain near the Daishi temple, women stop to visit the lucky-direction shrine and attend the great kagura performances, and the town's children each go to...
