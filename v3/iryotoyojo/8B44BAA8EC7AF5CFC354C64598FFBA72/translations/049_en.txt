　　　　　On the Resonance (Kan'ō) of Qi

People existing within this qi is just like fish living within water. People dwell within qi, yet do not perceive qi. Fish dwell within water, yet do not perceive water. Even though people do not perceive it, from within, qi stretches and fills them, and from without, qi presses upon them. Inner and outer are unified and unbroken; qi circulates back and forth without cease. In sum, all things that exist between Heaven and Earth are wrapped and enveloped by this single qi.
