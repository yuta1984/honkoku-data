　　Yōki-setsu, Volume Two

　　　　　On the Sympathetic Response (Kan'ō) of Qi

　That humans exist within this qi is just like fish dwelling in water. Humans live within qi yet do not perceive qi. Fish live within water yet do not perceive water. Although humans do not perceive it, from within, qi stretches and fills the body, and from without, qi presses upon it. Inner and outer are unified and continuous, circulating to and fro without cease. All things that exist between Heaven and Earth are simply wrapped and enveloped by this single qi.
