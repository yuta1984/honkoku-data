Having grasped with heart and eye—as discussed above—that this *ki* (vital energy/qi), from its constitution in the body, pervades this earthly realm, and with this single *ki* penetrates all the way to the very bottom of the universe beyond heaven and earth, to the uttermost depths of existence (*konrinzai*): after this understanding is achieved, one will come to a further realization in the later volumes—namely, concerning the wondrous workings of this *ki*'s spiritual responsiveness (*kan'nō*), as well as the principle of cultivating oneself and making one's intentions sincere so as to govern the realm and state; that in military arts, archery and horsemanship, and all manner of subsidiary skills, every action of human beings who dwell within this *ki* is, without exception, none other than the operation of this *ki*. After achieving this awakening (*hatsugo*), one may at last illuminate the Great Way of *Ikan* (ease and simplicity), and bring its virtue to fulfillment—but this, in the end, rests solely upon the person of true caliber.
