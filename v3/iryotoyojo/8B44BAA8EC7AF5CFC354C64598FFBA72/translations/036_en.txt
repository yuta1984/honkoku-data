**[H] (Xin)**
Solid

It is only upon reaching the solid that form is truly determined. Among solids, the cube (the "square solid") is taken as the standard. Therefore, the fact that musical tuning (ritsu) emerges from this solid body — whose top, bottom, four sides, and six faces are all equal — was discovered independently by Tomonobu, without reference to any mathematical texts; this too is a remarkable thing.

**Diagram showing that the triangle is the beginning of all mathematical operations:**

Four lines · Four lines (long and short) · Five lines · Six lines (long and short) · Eight lines
*(The way in which multiple lines form figures is varied, as illustrated here.)*

Any figure of four lines or more can always be divided into triangles, but when a triangle is divided, it yields only lines and no longer forms a shape. Therefore, it cannot be divided further. Thus the triangle is a naturally occurring form that inherently contains within it the principles of all shapes. This is the reason why three gives rise to all things. In astronomy, calendar science, solar and lunar eclipses, and all forms of arithmetic, the use of this number three is an immutable principle of heaven and earth, and it is the very reason why the study of musical tuning (ritsuryō) takes heaven and earth as its model.
