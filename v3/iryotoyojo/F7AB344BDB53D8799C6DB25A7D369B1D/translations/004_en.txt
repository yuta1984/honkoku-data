Long ago, in the land of Zhang in China (Morokoshi/Tang Mountain), there lived a renowned physician called Bunchūshi. One day, a person fell ill with a condition known as *ōseichū* (the "echo worm"). This ailment was such that whatever words the afflicted person spoke, an answering voice would respond from within the belly.

Bunchūshi thereupon opened the *Honzō* (the Materia Medica), and had the patient read aloud the names of the medicines listed within. Sure enough, when they reached "ai" (indigo/Isatis) and "raigan" (Quisqualis/Ficus pumila, a medicinal herb), though the patient spoke the words aloud, no answering voice came from within the belly.

Bunchūshi administered ai and raigan to the patient, and the illness was at last cured.
