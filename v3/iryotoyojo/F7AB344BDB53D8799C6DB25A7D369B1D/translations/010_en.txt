## 【Main Text】

*《Box: Continued》*

Letting out a sigh of relief, Chikuan thought to himself, "So this must be what it is like for those people in the old tales who were swallowed by a great serpent." As he pressed on, he came out into a wide open expanse, and there by the side of the road stood a direction post, reading: "To the right: the Pond of the Bladder (*bōkō*). To the left: the Domain of Itami-no-suke, Lord of Colic Pains." Seeing this, Chikuan made his way down the slope to the left, and before him appeared what seemed to be the mansion of Lord Itami-no-suke, with an imposing and dignified gate. He picked his way there with great difficulty, approached the gate, and addressed the gatekeeper:

"I am from Mizuta in Shinshū Province, and, drawn here by the threads of a wondrous mechanical marvel, I have come this far — though I am quite at a loss as to the way, and find myself in quite a fix. I beg you most humbly to grant me shelter for just one night."

The gatekeeper scrutinized him carefully and said, "Well now, this is a strange thing indeed! Whether it's a person, a fish, or a vegetable — there ought to be marks from the knife, and certainly marks from the teeth. However round in the head one may be, to have been swallowed whole, body and all — that's no orthodox treatment, that is! This is quite remarkable. And just what line of work are you in, exactly?"

Here was his chance. Chikuan cleared his throat and declared:

"I am descended from the great physicians of China — Jīvaka (*Giba*) and Biān Què (*Henjaku*) — my most venerable lineage..."

## 【Lower Right Panel】

"...and through the line of Tanba no Masatada, who crossed over to our very own Land of the Rising Sun, I am that living healer — Yabu Chikuan!"

At this, the gatekeeper was greatly startled and cried, "Why, in this realm, doctors and healers are strictly forbidden items — the greatest of contraband! Therefore, to lodge you for a full night is out of the question — not even for half an hour (*hangtoki*) could we permit you to stay!"
