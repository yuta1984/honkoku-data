## 【Main Text】

*《Box: Continued》*

With every intention of reporting the good news to the lord within, the gatekeeper went off smiling into the inner quarters. Left behind, Chikuan mused inwardly: "Well, well — they say that even the judgments of Hell depend on gold, but it seems that inside the belly, things work just the same way!" He was thoroughly impressed.

Thus the gatekeeper wasted no time: proceeding through the Gate of Chest-Tightness (*Munasaki-tsukae-mon*), he reported to the lord of the domain. "If it's a *taikomochi*, then bring him in," came the order, and Chikuan was promptly escorted inside the mansion.

As it happened, the season had been one of persistently gloomy and unsettled weather — an untimely and irregular climate — and for the Domain of Irritability (*Senshaku-ryō*), this was precisely the moment they had been waiting for. In the gatehouse quarters and the samurai rooms alike, quarreling and arguments broke out in a tremendous uproar of shouting and commotion. Observing this, Chikuan thought to himself: "With the interior of the belly in such a state, it stands to reason that people would suffer terribly from irritability (*senshaku*)." Nodding inwardly at this insight, he proceeded to the inner quarters, where he became the lord's companion, dancing and leaping about and drinking wine — when suddenly a cry rang out: "*Gochūshin! Gochūshin!* — Most loyal report! Most loyal report!"

The one who came rushing in with this message was a trusted intimate of the region — Nodo-moto and Tsuba-kichi, gasping for breath, prostrated themselves before the lord and reported: "Of late, ailments afflicting the Domain of Irritability have become especially severe — and now, from somewhere or other, a great dose of medicine has smashed through the Barrier of Swallowing (*Nondo-no-seki*), apparently having dispensed with the Flag of Qi (*ki no hata*) without leave. Flying the banner of chrysanthemum crest and crescent moon, the forces of Matsuya Denhachirō — a druggist (*yakushu-dana*) of Cloud Town (*Kumo-no-machi*), hailing from Ueda in Shinshū Province, as dispatched by his establishment — have advanced their proprietary infusion, the celebrated family-formula decoction, along with a great many other medicinal agents, and they now fill the plains of the Chest Region (*Munasaki-no-hara*) to overflowing! Take heed, take heed!"

Hearing this, Lord Itami-no-suke declared: "So then — it must be the celebrated Senshaku-yu (*Irritability Decoction*), the wondrous remedy of the Tōzandō establishment of Ueda in Shinshū, of which I have long heard tell — it has come to attack us!"
