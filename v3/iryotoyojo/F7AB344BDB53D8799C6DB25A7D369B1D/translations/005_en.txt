**【Kyōka Satirical Poem】**
*Composed on the theme of the fashionable/popular doctor*

The doctor's lot is this:
　the more his spoon goes round and round,
　　the more he goes his rounds—
yet to the patient's house
　he comes
　　ever late.

— *Ekitei Komahito*

---

**【Upper Left of Page — Speech bubble of the Popular Doctor】**

"Oh my, so busy, so busy!
Today I've already walked to fourteen or fifteen houses, and from here I still have to make rounds to another fifty or sixty! Tomorrow I'd like to take a palanquin, but my sandal straps have worn through and I can't keep going like this."

**【Lower Section — Speech bubble of the Popular Geisha】**

"Now then, yesterday I really did get completely drunk, but today it's a visit to a grand residence, so I suppose I'll have to be a little more proper.
Tomorrow and the day after, too, I have appointments, so I'm already at a loss as to how to turn people down."
