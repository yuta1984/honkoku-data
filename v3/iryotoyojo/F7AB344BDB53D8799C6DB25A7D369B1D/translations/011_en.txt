## 【Main Text】

*《Box: Continued》*

"Come now, come now — be off with you quickly!" the gatekeeper urged, trying to send him away. Hearing this, Chikuan said: "How dreadfully rude of me — a thousand apologies! It's simply because my head is so round that things just tumble out of my mouth without thinking, and I said what I said. But in truth, I am no doctor at all. I am one who is hired for the village vigils — the *himachi* and *tsukimachi* gatherings — where I perform *jōruri* recitations, *nagauta* songs, and dancing, adding to the merriment of the drinking party and making my way in the world: I am what is known as a *taikomochi*, a professional jester and entertainer. I thought that if I told you such a trade, you might scold me as a worthless good-for-nothing, and so I claimed to be a doctor — but the truth is exactly as I have now told you. Please, I beg you, do allow me to stay."

With these words, he quietly tossed into the gatekeeper's sleeve a small package of *kodama-gin* — a little silver coin he had received as a gift. The gatekeeper wasted no time in pocketing it, and said: "Why, this is perfectly fortunate timing! Just at this moment, various members of the household — the Lords of Irritability (*kanshaku-sama*) among the relatives — are all in residence here..."

## 【Upper Right Panel — Exchange with Lord Itami-no-suke】

**Lord Itami-no-suke:**
"'Nodo-moto,' 'Tsuba-kichi,' 'Chūshin' — I don't like the sound of this at all. It can't be anything good. Out with it quickly — what is it, what is it?"

**Chikuan:**
"Why, my Lady and your attendants — the finest beautifying preparation in all of Edo is none other than *Senjokō*, from the Inari Shintō shrine precinct at Kyōbashi, and nothing compares to it! When I next make my way here, I shall bring some for you."

---

## 【Upper Left Panel】

"Why indeed! Word of that *Senjokō* has reached even this world of ours, and I have long been wishing to obtain some!"
