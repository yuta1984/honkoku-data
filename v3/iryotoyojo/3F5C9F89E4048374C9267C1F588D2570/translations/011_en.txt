Consolidating the white forces of the Mochi faction, and winning over the renowned and celebrated rice cakes to their cause, they plotted to crush Emperor Manma (Rice), and their preparations were growing ever more urgent — when suddenly the kitchen fell into an uproar. "The rice bowl that was about to be cleared away has appeared!" came the warning from Yaenari (Green Bean), and at this, all were struck with utter astonishment, falling flat on their backsides (*shiri-mochi*) in bewilderment. White Ball Princess (*Shiratama-hime*) came tumbling out, Lady Botamochi (*Botamochi Gozen*) fell from the shelf, weeping, "Surely this must be a dream!" Yet at that moment, Mochiari showed not the slightest agitation — calm and settled as a well-set rice cake — and summoned Tada no Manjū (Plain Steamed Bun),

appointing him as commander to hold back the forces of Rice and Sake, and bestowing upon him the stratagem of the *Tsurukake-masu* (Vine-hanging Measuring Box). In the broad courtyard of Seirō-den (Steam Basket Hall), a rough horse with sugar-dusted coat, named "Yukishiro" (Snow White), was led forward by a groom (*kuchitori*), and a saddle of the never-before-seen "Geko-no-Tameshine" style (a saddle set up by a non-drinker) was placed upon it, all awarded as an on-the-spot reward. Meanwhile, Tada no Manjū, receiving a brewing of fresh Uji tea, took up position as the force to suppress the *jōgo* (heavy drinker) army, entrenching himself in the fortress of the *takatsuki* (tall-stemmed offering cup), and bearing the summons (*kitte*) to muster troops, earned fame in various places...
