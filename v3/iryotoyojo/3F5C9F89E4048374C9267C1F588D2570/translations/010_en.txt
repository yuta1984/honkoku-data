It must be of the utmost importance, they all said — that single word, striking true as the star (*hoshi*) on a sake barrel. Indeed, there was no one who did not feel the heat (*kan*) of that fine wine from the Five Grains faction. Yaenari's face turned pale; he pressed down the boiling fury within his chest and sat there simmering. But now that the bamboo husk he had kept wrapped around himself was laid bare for all to see, he threw caution to the wind with reckless abandon, pulled out the spigot (*nomiguchi*) that Morohaku wore at his hip, and hurled it at him like a throwing star (*shuriken*). Morohaku, however, was prepared — catching it with his left hand on the bow side (*yunde*), a southpaw move — and contrary to Yaenari's hopes of soft bean paste (*an*),
