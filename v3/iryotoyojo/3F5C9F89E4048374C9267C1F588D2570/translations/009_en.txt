【Right page】

〽 Of late, having taken in
　the one who warms the sake (*kanjya*) —
　swallow down the whole situation
　nice and slowly, just like that.
　You with your disloyal heart, O Yaenari —
　do make haste to join
　the side of Mochi!

　　　　　　— Morohaku


【Left page】

〽 To think so little of this Yaenari, and tell me
　to stick myself to the Mochi faction —
　these are words ill-suited to the lips of a heavy drinker (*jōto*).
　Though I know it for the raving
　that comes spilling out when one is drunk,
　still it sits ill with me.
　Is this your true nature showing, then,
　　Master Morohaku?

To become the great man (*chōja*) of a hundred vegetables (*hyakusai*) —
　what a pleasant, drunken feeling.
Let this suffice
　as a pillow-word (*makura*) to rest upon.

　　　　　　Setsuritsuen Chiin
　　　　　　　　　　　　Yaenari (Green Bean)
