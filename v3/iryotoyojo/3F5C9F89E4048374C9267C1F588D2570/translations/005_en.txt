It was the height of a bountiful harvest season — "Ten Thousand Years of Good Harvest" — the middle of the ripening autumn. Word came that some manner of council was to be convened. And so, Lord Azuki Dainagon (Great Counselor Red Bean), Lord Yaenari Shōchū Nagon (Counselor Mung Bean-and-Spirits), and Lord Morohaku Inamura assembled at the paddy-field manor in the valley. There, Katsushika Inabo-no-Kami Wasenari (Early-Ripening, Lord of the Rice Ear of Katsushika), presiding as regent, had donned his ample long-hakama of fresh straw and settled grandly into his seat.

In the seats that followed were: Mugihara Shōshō Hikiwari (Lesser Captain Cracked Barley), Shinmai Ara Jirō (Young Master Rough New Rice), Mominuka Okabo no Rokuyata Hatazumi (Rokuyata Husk-and-Bran of the Upland Field Corner), and beyond these, the assembled great and lesser beans of the countryside — early-ripening and late-ripening alike — their well-laden ears bowed down to the earth, the surface of the paddies calm in the auspicious seasonal breeze, presenting a picture of the most abundant plenty. It was just at this moment that —

[Left Page]

— the attendant on duty announced an arrival: "Lord Nagasaki Jūrō Kowameshi (Jūrō Red Rice of Nagasaki) approaches even now!" And out he stepped. His age had already surpassed the "rice jubilee" (the sixty-first year), and his sideburns were flecked with sesame-and-salt gray. Wearing a folded eboshi cap adorned with noshi decorations and robed in a suō of deep red-bean color, he prostrated himself at a respectful distance before the full ceremonial banquet of two soups and five dishes, and made his report:

"At this time, Koshigaya Daikokujō Mochiāri (Lord Most-Superior-Grade Rice Cake of Koshigaya) has raised treacherous ambitions — plotting to crush His Majesty Emperor Manma (His Majesty Rice), and to seize the throne for himself. To this end, he has ordered Ishiusu Goro Gorō (Five-Road Gorō of the Stone Mortar) to take the New King White Rice captive as a 'prisoner of the mochi,' and, though it is no store set aside against lean years, to scheme and manipulate affairs entirely as he pleases."
