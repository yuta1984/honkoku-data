It was the height of a bountiful harvest — a year of ten-thousand-year plenty — in the midst of the ripening autumn. Word had gone out that some manner of council was to be convened. Lord Azuki Dainagon (Great Counselor Red Bean), Lord Yaenari Shōchū Chūnagon (Middle Counselor Green Bean Spirits), and the rest assembled at the estate grounds of Morohaku-Inamura-ga-yatsu. There, Katsushika-Inabo-no-Kami Wasenari (Lord of the Rice Ear of Katsushika, Early Ripening) — serving as regent — had arrayed himself splendidly in long hakama of new straw and taken his seat.

In the next seats were: Mugihara Shōshō Hikiwari (Minor Captain Cracked Barley), Shinmai Ara-jirō (Second Son New Rough Rice), Mominuka Okabo-no-Rokuyata Hatazumi (Chaff-and-Husk, Upland-Rice Sixth Son of the Field Corner), and beyond these, the various great and small beans of the countryside. Early-ripening and late-ripening alike, their heads heavy with well-filled grain, bowed down to the earth — a scene of serene plenty, like a calm wind over the face of the paddy fields. Into this gathering——

【Left page】

——the duty officer announced: "Nagasaki Jūrō Kowameshi (Long Cape, Tenth Son, Red-Bean Rice) requests to present himself." He was ushered in and came forward. His age had already passed beyond the "Rice Celebration" (the eighty-eighth year), and the hair at his temples was a salt-and-sesame grey. Using a noshi-wrapped bundle in place of a folded eboshi cap, and dressed in a suō robe the color of red beans, he knelt with great reverence before an assembled banquet of two soups and five dishes, and made his report:

"At this time, Koshigaya Daikokujo Mochiari (Supreme-Grade Mochi of Koshigaya) has harbored treasonous ambition. He plots to crush His Majesty Emperor Manma (Rice-and-Meal), and to seize the throne for himself. To this end, he has instructed Ishiusu Goro-gorō (Five-Road Stone Mortar Five) to take the White Rice Prince captive — making him a prisoner of mochi — and, though this is no time of famine, to scheme freely as he wishes."
