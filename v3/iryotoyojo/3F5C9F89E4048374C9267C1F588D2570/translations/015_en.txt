【Kyōka poem inside the decorative frame】

Like a shiratama (rice ball),
　raised up and nurtured
　　　　　by the parental bowl—
　how deep
　　　the blessing,
　how well this child knows it
　　　　　(shiruko-mochi)

Naniwa　Baikōen Morichika
