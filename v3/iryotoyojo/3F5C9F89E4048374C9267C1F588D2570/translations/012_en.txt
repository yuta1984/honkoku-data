〔Kyōka poem〕 Composed by Seiginsha Shujin:

Rather than flowers —
not even crying "sake!"
but crying "dango!" —
oh what a waste, these blossoms
at the teahouse of Pine Seat

〔Within the illustration〕
　Torayā — Izumi-chō district

The offerings upon the *sanpō* (ritual tray) were plundered
by the wild warrior ruffians (*nobushi*) of the heavy drinkers' (*jōgo*) army;
the Abegawa-mizu-mochi (Abe River soft rice cakes) were caught
between the cedar-leaf and plantain (*shō*) forces and held at bay —
and all the while, spouting tasteless puns
as flat and flavourless as cheap sweets (*dagashi*),
they declared: "You shall be seen as monsters (*yōkai*) before us!"
and such like.
