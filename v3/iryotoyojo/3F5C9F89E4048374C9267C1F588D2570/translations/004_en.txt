Kokkei Gokoku Taiheiki (A Comic Chronicle of the Five Grains' Great Peace), Volume One

　　　　　　Composed playfully by Gesaku-sha Kishō of Edo

　　　　Chapter One

In the land of Tang (China), the Divine Farmer, Shennong, fashioned the plow and taught the people the art of cultivation. Here in our land of Japan, in the age of the gods long ago — in the lofty paddy fields of Heaven — rice was first planted, to nourish the people. The sovereign of all grains, the lord of the Five Grains who nurtures mankind, is known as "Emperor Manma (His Majesty Rice)." So profound is His Majesty's benevolence and virtue that all under heaven revere and honor him.
