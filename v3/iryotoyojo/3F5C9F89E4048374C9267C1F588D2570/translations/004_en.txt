Kokkei Gokoku Taiheiki, Volume One
　　　　　　Edo: Playfully compiled by Gesaku-sha Kishō
　　　　Chapter One

In China, the Divine Farmer Shennong fashioned the plow and taught the people the art of cultivation. Here in our land of Japan, in the distant age of the gods — that age of the soaring heavens — rice was first planted in the celestial narrow paddies of Heaven, to nourish the people. The deity who presides over the five grains, the lord of cultivated things, known and revered as His Majesty Emperor Manma (Rice-and-Meal), bestows blessings upon all the people of the realm far and wide. So deep was his benevolence and virtue that all under heaven held him in the highest reverence.
