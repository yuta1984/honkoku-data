　　　　Nagasaki Jūrō
　　　　Report/Petition of Kowameshi (Red-Bean Rice)

*(Kyōka verse:)*

The ancients said: "Disaster issues from the mouth; illness enters through the mouth" —
and so one must be careful about what goes in and out of one's mouth.
If the mouth is the "gate-field" (*kadota*) through which misfortune enters,
then all must be cautious and guard their mouths well.
Fish (*to-to*) and scarecrows (*kakashi*) alike!

　　　　　　　　　　— Bunkaido Shujin (Master of the Literary Assembly Hall)
