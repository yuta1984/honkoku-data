"There are preparations afoot for a great battle of the waters (*Mizu-mizu kassen*). Already, they have been secretly trying to recruit me to their side — but as I am, humble though I may be, the master of but a single layer (*ichijū*), how could I, like a bean paste confection (*anmono*) that has been trampled flat, go flitting from one side to the other? Unless you swiftly dispatch a punitive force and crush and flatten the enemy before their defenses have solidified, there will ultimately be the worry of having to resort to the use of the axe-handle (*onoe*)." So he spoke, his eloquence flowing smoothly like the rinsing of rice, this freshly steamed box of red rice (*kowameshi*), exhaling great breaths of steam like sighs — puff, puff — as he made his report. 　　　　Chapter Two

No sooner had Yaenari (Lord Green Bean) heard this than cold sweat ran down his chest. This was the great crisis of Mochiari's lifetime. Silently praying in his heart, "Namu Kimyō, Shakushi Nyorai (*Ladle Tathāgata*), please save us," he composed his expression to appear as though nothing were amiss, steadied his heart, and — sticky and clinging as a *hie* millet dumpling caught in the teeth — began to speak in a slow, adhesive manner (*nichāri nichāri*)...
