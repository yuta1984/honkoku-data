"(Mizu/Beans) is steadily making preparations for battle. He has already secretly invited me to join his side, but I, humble though I am, as master of my own single layer, cannot possibly go flitting about hither and thither like a crushed sweet bean paste cake. Unless you quickly dispatch an attack force and break through and flatten the enemy before their defenses solidify, there will ultimately be cause to fear the need for the axe handle (i.e., drastic measures)." Thus he spoke, his eloquence flowing smoothly like the washing of rice — the freshly steamed red rice (kowameshi) from the lacquer-box, puffing out great breaths like rising steam. The assembled
