Biwa-no-Yashirō (Loquat Valley Fourth Son), Gosho-no-Gorō Kaki (Imperial Persimmon Fifth Son), Tarunuki-emon Momonoī Aka-no-Sanosuke (Barrel-drawing Peach Well Red Assistant), Kurifusa-emon (Chestnut Grove Left Gate), Uriware Shirō (Melon-splitting Fourth Son), Suika Saburō (Watermelon Third Son), Umetsu Kamon (Plum Ferry Gate) and others — together with the countless warrior-grapes — placed saddles of rough-textured cloth upon their wild chestnut-colored horses from Tamba, and writhing as they wound the reins around their hands, came charging forth, pushing and shoving, to bring reinforcements from the mochi side to their allies, the "Tane no mi" (Seed-fruits / trusted allies).
