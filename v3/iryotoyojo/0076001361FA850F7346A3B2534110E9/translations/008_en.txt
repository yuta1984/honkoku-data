Ina (Striped mullet)　　䰵魚 (Rigyo)
　Sweet and neutral nature. Non-toxic. ○ This fish changes its name as it gradually grows. When small at first, it is called "ina" or "subashiri." When fully grown and large, it is called "bora" or "nayoshi." The smaller ones have a mild flavor and are not harmful to sick people. The larger ones have a rich flavor and should not be given carelessly to sick people.
