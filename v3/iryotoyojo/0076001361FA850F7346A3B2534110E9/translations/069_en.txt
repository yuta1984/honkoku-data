Zabon (Japanese citrus pomelo) - Kōran (Citrus maxima)
Pungent and sour. Non-toxic.

Cherry (sakuranbo) - Ōtō (Prunus avium/pseudocerasus)
Pungent, sweet, and neutral. Non-toxic. It clears the chest and diaphragm region, and counteracts alcohol toxicity.

Sakarame (a type of seaweed)
Sweet, salty, and neutral. Non-toxic. It belongs to the same category as seaweed (wakame).

Sashimi (raw fish) - See also the entry on "namasu" (raw fish/vegetable dressed with vinegar).

Spanish mackerel (sawara) - Mǎjiāoyú (Scomberomorus niphonius)
Sweet and warm. Mildly toxic. It supplements deficiency, boosts qi, and makes a person robust and healthy. Its flavor is particularly rich; however, eating too much will stir up damp-heat...

...and cause skin eruptions and sores. It is not suitable for sick persons.

Shark (same) - Jiāoyú (various shark species)
Sweet and warm. Toxic. Eating a small amount boosts qi and invigorates one's strength.
A note by Genkon (the author): The variety known as hoshi-zame (spotted shark / Mustelus manazo) is non-toxic.

Salmon (sake) - Xīng (Oncorhynchus keta)
Sweet and salty. Non-toxic. It warms the interior of the body and invigorates qi. Eating too much will stir up internal fire (heat) and cause skin eruptions.
○ Salt-cured salmon (sake shiobiki): Raw salmon flesh may stir up illness. When made into salt-cured salmon, its qi and flavor (nature and taste) become considerably milder.
○ Salmon roe (harako): Has the same properties as the flesh.
