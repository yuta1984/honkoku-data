Taro (satoimo) - Colocasia esculenta
Acrid, pungent, neutral, and smooth in nature. Mildly toxic. It relaxes the intestines and stomach. Eating too much will obstruct the qi. Pregnant women should avoid it.

Japanese pepper (sanshō) - Zanthoxylum piperitum
Pungent and warm. Non-toxic. It strengthens the stomach, aids in digesting undigested food that has stagnated, and kills intestinal worms.
○ Japanese pepper berries that have not opened at the seam are toxic and must not be eaten. Also, when one chokes on Japanese pepper, one should lick some salt. Furthermore, if an opened Japanese pepper berry is blown into a person's throat, the discomfort will immediately subside.

Astringency-removed persimmon (sawashigaki) - Rin-gaki
Cool in nature. Non-toxic. It strengthens the spleen and stomach.

Pomegranate (zakuro) - Punica granatum
Sweet, sour, warm, and astringent in nature. Non-toxic. It treats dysentery with lockjaw (kinkoori — a severe form of dysentery accompanied by inability to open the mouth).
