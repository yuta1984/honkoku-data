**Right Page**

**Sugar (砂糖, satō)**
It has a sweet, cold, and neutral nature. Non-toxic. It harmonizes the interior of the body, supports the spleen, removes heat, moistens dryness, dissolves phlegm, relieves thirst, neutralizes alcohol toxicity, promotes urination and bowel movements, and treats vomiting and regurgitation. However, prolonged consumption may cause roundworm infestation.

**Taro (里芋, satoimo)** [Green Taro]
It has an acrid, pungent, neutral, and smooth nature. Slightly toxic. It relaxes the intestines and stomach. Eating too much will obstruct qi. Pregnant women should avoid it.

**Sweet Potato (薩摩芋, satsumaimo)** [Also called Ryūkyū Potato]
It has a sweet and neutral nature. Non-toxic and generally harmless for all ailments. ▲ Kumquat has a harmful food combination with "Kuma no i" (a specific food item).

**March Radish (三月大根, Sangwatsu Daikon)** [Yōka Radish / 楊花蘿蔔]

**Left Page**

It has a pungent and warm nature. Non-toxic and neutralizes the toxicity of all types of noodles and flour-based foods.

**Japanese Pepper (山椒, sanshō)** [Qin Pepper / 秦椒]
It has a pungent and warm nature. Non-toxic. It invigorates the stomach, aids digestion of food that has stagnated in the stomach, and kills parasites.
○ Sansho berries that have not opened are toxic and must not be eaten. If one chokes on sansho, one should lick salt to find relief. Alternatively, opening one's mouth and having someone blow air into one's throat will provide immediate relief.

**Astringency-removed Persimmon (渋抜き柿, sawashigaki)** [Rin Kaki / 醂柿]
It has a cold nature. Non-toxic and invigorates the spleen and stomach.

**Pomegranate (ざくろ, zakuro)** [Pomegranate / 安石榴]
It has a sweet, sour, warm, and astringent nature. Non-toxic and treats "kinkoUri" (dysentery with locked jaw / severe dysentery).
