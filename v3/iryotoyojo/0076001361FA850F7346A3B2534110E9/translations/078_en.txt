**Shishitake** (also known as *kawatake*) [Chinese name: 鹿茸 *Lù Róng*]
Sweet flavor, neutral nature. Non-toxic.

**Shōro (matsutake truffle / sandy truffle)** [Chinese name: 麦蕈 *Mài Xùn*]
Sweet flavor, neutral nature. Non-toxic.

**Shiramo (a type of sea algae)** [Chinese name: 竜鬚菜 *Lóng Xū Cài*]
Sweet flavor, salty flavor, cold nature. Non-toxic. Promotes urination.

**Shōfu (wheat flour)** [Chinese name: 麦粉 *Mài Fěn*]
Sweet flavor, cool nature. Non-toxic.

**Shiinomi (chinquapin/stone oak nuts)** [Chinese name: 柯樹子 *Kē Shù Zǐ*]
Sweet flavor, neutral nature. Non-toxic. Treats water disease (edema/dropsy).

**Shibugaki (astringent persimmon)** [Chinese name: 漆柿 *Qī Shì*]

**Shibaguri (wild chestnut)** [Chinese name: 茅栗 *Máo Lì*]

**Shirauo (Japanese icefish / whitebait)** [Chinese name: 鱠残魚 *Kuài Cán Yú*]
Sweet flavor, neutral nature. Non-toxic. Opens the stomach and promotes appetite. Eating too much can cause heat [in the body]. It is commonly said to "break the blood" (deplete the blood).

**Shihira** [no Chinese name given]
Sweet flavor, sour flavor. Non-toxic.

**Shakunagi (mantis shrimp)** [Chinese name: 蝦姑 *Xiā Gū*]
Sweet flavor, neutral nature. Non-toxic.
