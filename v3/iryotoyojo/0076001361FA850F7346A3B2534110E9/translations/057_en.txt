Goma (sesame)　　胡麻
　Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. Comes in three varieties: black, white, and red. ○Black sesame enters the kidneys, increases vitality, strengthens muscles and bones, and sharpens hearing and vision
　○White sesame enters the lungs, moistens the five organs, promotes circulation in the blood vessels, and aids bowel movements. Both black and white sesame are beneficial to people
Goma no abura (sesame oil)　　胡麻油
　Sweet and warm. Non-toxic. Promotes bowel movements and neutralizes heat toxins and insect toxins
　○Frying or sautéeing various foods in oil stirs up internal fire and produces phlegm. Not suitable for sick people
