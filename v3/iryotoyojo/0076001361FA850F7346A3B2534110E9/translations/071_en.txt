【Right Page】

Millet (kimi) - Also known as "akakimi" (red millet) and "morokoshi."
Sweet and warm. Non-toxic. It tonifies the lungs and increases qi (vital energy). Eating too much generates heat and loosens the sinews and bones.
○ Morokoshi dumplings (morokoshi dango): If prepared to be soft, they are harmless to the ill.

Okara (kirazu) - Also known as "unohana." Xuěhuācài (Snow Flower Vegetable; tofu lees)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It opens the stomach, promotes appetite, and counteracts alcohol toxicity.

Chrysanthemum flower (kiku no hana) - Huángjúhuā (Yellow chrysanthemum)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It relieves headaches and brightens the eyes.

Kyōna (mizuna) - Same as mizuna (water greens).

【Left Page】

Cucumber (kyūri) - Húguā
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It clears heat, quenches thirst, and promotes urination. Children should avoid consuming it.

Kishimeji mushroom (Tricholoma flavovirens) - Huánxùn (Yellow mushroom)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic.

Wood ear mushroom (kikurage) - Mù'ěr (Auricularia auricula-judae)
Sweet and neutral. Mildly toxic. It benefits the five viscera (zang organs), increases qi, and treats hemorrhoids.

Ginkgo nut (ginnan) - Yínxìng
Sweet, bitter, and neutral. Non-toxic. It descends phlegm, kills intestinal parasites, and counteracts alcohol toxicity. It is harmless across a wide range of ailments.
