Luffa / Sponge gourd (hechima)　　Silk gourd
　Sweet and neutral in nature. Non-toxic. Reduces swelling, dissolves phlegm, and kills parasites.

Safflower (benibana)　　Safflower seedling
　Sweet and warm in nature. Non-toxic. Promotes blood circulation and relieves pain.

Red mushroom (benitake)　　Cochineal mushroom
　Those that grow in spring are non-toxic. Those that grow in autumn are toxic.

Red apple (beni ringo)
　Same as ringo (apple).

Tōhoshi (long-grain rice)　　Great Tang rice
　Sweet and neutral in nature. Non-toxic. Beneficial when consumed regularly by those suffering from stroke (chūfū). However, it provides no benefit to those with a weak spleen and stomach, the elderly, or patients.

Corn (tōmorokoshi)　　Also known as nanban kimi　　Maize
　Sweet and neutral in nature. Non-toxic. Warms the interior of the body and tightens the intestines and stomach. Stops霍乱 (kakuran; acute gastrointestinal distress). If eaten in excess, it is difficult to digest. Children and patients should avoid it.

Tofu (tōfu)　　Bean curd
　Sweet, salty, and cold in nature. Non-toxic. Loosens and relaxes the interior of the body, tonifies qi, harmonizes the spleen and stomach, and neutralizes alcohol toxicity. ○ In case of food poisoning from tofu, one should use the pressed juice of daikon radish as a remedy.
