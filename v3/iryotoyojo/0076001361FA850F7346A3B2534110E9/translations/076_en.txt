Shōyu (soy sauce) — Jiàngyóu
Salty, slightly sweet, and cold. Non-toxic. It moderates the toxins in all manner of foods and drinks, fish and meat, and vegetables. It is an item that cannot be absent from the kitchen for even a single day.

Shio (salt) — Shokuén (table salt)
Salty and cold. Non-toxic. It neutralizes all manner of poisons. It is an item that cannot be absent from any household for even a single day.

Shōchū (distilled spirits) — Shāozhū
Pungent, slightly sweet, and greatly hot. Toxic. It lifts depression, opens and clears the chest and diaphragm, disperses phlegm,

Shiso (Japanese perilla; Perilla frutescens) — Zǐsū
Pungent and warm. Non-toxic. It descends qi, dissolves phlegm, and neutralizes all toxins from fish and crab.

Shiro goma (white sesame seeds; Sesamum indicum) — Bái húmá
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It moistens the skin and flesh.
