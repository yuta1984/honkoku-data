Tsumire (fish/wheat dumplings)　　Made from wheat flour
　Sweet and warm. Non-toxic. Closes qi, generates heat, stirs up accumulations, and causes hernia pain. Do not eat in large quantities. Sick people and children should avoid it.
　○When harmed by tsumire, one should use squeezed daikon radish juice. ○Fish tsumire is the same as kamaboko (fish cake).

Tsumami-na (young mustard greens)　　Jimao Cai
　Pungent, sweet, and bitter. Non-toxic.

Tsubana (young cogon grass shoots)　　Maozhen
　Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. Drains excess water from the body. Beneficial for children.

【Left page】
Tsukushi (horsetail shoots)　　Bitoucai
　Sweet, bitter, and neutral. Non-toxic. However, it is not suitable for those with upward-surging qi, accumulated heat, or vomiting.

Tsukuneimo (Chinese yam)　　Fozhangshu
　Taste and properties same as mountain yam (yamaimo).

Zuwai-gani (snow crab)　　Mao Xie
　Salty, sweet, and cold. Slightly toxic. Resolves alcohol toxicity and strengthens sinews and bones.

Tsuru (crane)　　He
