Bōfū (a type of parsley/coastal plant)
　Same as hamaboufuu (coastal bōfū).

Sargassum seaweed (hodawara)　　Horse-tail seaweed
　Sweet, salty, slippery, and cold in nature. Non-toxic. Eliminates alcohol toxicity.

Dried persimmon (hoshigaki)　　Dried persimmon
　Sweet and cold in nature. Non-toxic. Disperses accumulated old blood and treats rectal bleeding. ▲Incompatible with crab (should not be eaten together).

Striped mullet (bora)　　Mugil fish
　Sweet and neutral in nature. Non-toxic. Opens the stomach and benefits the five internal organs. However, as its flavor is rich and its nature heavy, it is not suitable for patients.

Gurnard (hōbō)　　Bamboo-wheat fish
　Sweet and neutral in nature. Non-toxic.

Sea squirt (hoya)　　Sea pineapple
　Salty and cold in nature. Non-toxic. Promotes urination and treats vaginal discharge in women.

Dried abalone (hoshi awabi)　　Dried abalone

Cuckoo (hototogisu)　　Cuckoo
　Sweet and cold in nature. Non-toxic. Removes heat toxins from smallpox and kills parasites.

Japanese bunting / White-eye (hōjiro)
　Sweet and neutral in nature. Non-toxic.
