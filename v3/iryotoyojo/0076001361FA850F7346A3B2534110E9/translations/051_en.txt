Yamaimo (Japanese mountain yam)　　薯蕷
　Sweet and cool. Non-toxic. Nourishes kidney qi, strengthens the spleen and stomach, and dispels evil qi. Continued consumption over a long period clarifies hearing and vision.

Yatsukashira (taro variety)
　Its properties and flavor are the same as tōnoimo (taro).

Yanagitake (willow mushroom)　　柳耳
　Bitter, pungent, and neutral. Non-toxic. Supplements the stomach and promotes the circulation of qi.

Yamamomo (Japanese bayberry)　　楊梅
　Sour, sweet, and warm. Non-toxic. Stops vomiting and quenches thirst. ▲Incompatible with soba noodles and roasted beans.

Yanagi no mushi (willow borer)　　柳蠹虫
　Treats digestive disorders (kan) in children.

Yakara (a type of bird)　　火筩觜
　Sweet and warm. Non-toxic. Relaxes the chest cavity, and treats regurgitation and esophageal obstruction.

Yamame　　Same as ugui (Japanese dace).
