【Right Page】

Shiro-jisa (white lettuce) — Báijù (Lactuca sativa)
Bitter and cold. Non-toxic. It neutralizes alcohol toxicity and promotes both urination and defecation.

Shiro-mame (white beans) — Huáng dàdòu (soybeans)
Sweet and warm. Non-toxic. It relaxes the interior of the abdomen, descends qi, and promotes defecation.

Shiro-azuki (white adzuki beans) — Bái xiǎodòu (white small beans)
Sweet, slightly sour, and neutral. Non-toxic.

Shiro-uri (white melon) — Yuèguā (Oriental pickling melon; Cucumis melo var. conomon)
Sweet and cold. Non-toxic. It promotes urination, dispels vexatious heat (uncomfortable bodily heat/fever), and neutralizes alcohol toxicity.

Shungiku (garland chrysanthemum; Glebionis coronaria) — Tónghāo

【Left Page】

Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It dissolves phlegm and regulates the functions of the intestines and stomach.

Junsai (water shield; Brasenia schreberi) — Chún
Sweet and cool. Non-toxic. It strengthens and thickens the intestines and stomach, and expels excess water from the body.

Shiitake (shiitake mushroom; Lentinula edodes) — Xiāngsùn (fragrant mushroom)
Sweet and slightly warm. Non-toxic. Raw shiitake causes qi to rise upward. Eating too many can cause a state resembling intoxication and may also generate parasites (worms) in the body.

Shimeji-take (shimeji mushroom; Lyophyllum shimeji) — Biāomáoróng
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic.

Shira-shimeji (white shimeji; Lyophyllum connatum) — Yùxùn (jade mushroom)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic.
