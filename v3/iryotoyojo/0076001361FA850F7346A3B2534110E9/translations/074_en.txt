Myōga (Japanese ginger; Zingiber mioga) - Ránghé
Spicy and warm. Non-toxic. It treats malaria/intermittent fevers (okori) and brightens the eyes.

Mebaru (rockfish; Sebastes spp.)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic.

Miso (fermented soybean paste) - Wèizēng
Sweet, salty, and warm. Non-toxic. It tonifies the body's center, increases qi, regulates the spleen and stomach,

strengthens the four limbs, and when used to cook all manner of fish and vegetables, is of great benefit to people. Furthermore, it neutralizes the toxic effects of alcohol and the poisons of birds, beasts, vegetables, and mushrooms. However, those suffering from beriberi, edema (swelling), or poor urination should avoid it.

○ White miso (shiro miso) — Made by combining white soybeans with white kōji (mold culture). It is ready in just over twenty days. Its flavor is sweet, and for that reason, it deteriorates quickly in taste. It is not of particular benefit to people.

○ Kinzanji miso, hishio (a fermented condiment similar to soy sauce paste), and dōko miso (barrel-aged miso)
These belong to the same category as nattō (fermented soybeans). They are safe for sick people.
