Kagami fish (Japanese lookdown/mirror fish)
　Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic.

Rockfish (kasago)
　Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It is a type of the mou fish (pufferfish family).

Kado　　Same as herring (nishin).

Herring roe (kazunoko)　　Blue fish roe
　Sweet, neutral, and astringent. Non-toxic. Should not be eaten in large quantities.

Fish cake (kamaboko)　　Fish meat cake
　Sweet and mildly warm. Non-toxic. Its quality depends on the nature of the fish used as its ingredient. Sick people should select it carefully before use.

【Left page】
Karasumi (dried mullet roe)　　Dried fish roe
　Sweet and salty. Non-toxic. Treats abdominal pain after childbirth. ○ There are three types of karasumi: the roe of mullet (bora) is called "Nomo," and comes from Nagasaki. Its flavor is the finest. The others are made from the roe of sea bass (suzuki) and Spanish mackerel (sawara). Their flavor is greatly inferior.

Kaburahone (whale cartilage)
　Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It is a type of bone from one variety of whale.
