Kenpōnashi (Japanese raisin tree)　　枳椇子
　Sweet, sour, and astringent. Non-toxic. Quenches thirst and neutralizes alcohol toxicity.
Keri (grey-headed lapwing)
　Sweet and slightly warm. Non-toxic. Regulates the spleen and stomach, and treats esophageal obstruction (kakuetsu). Also used in folk medicine prepared as a charred black preparation (kuro-yaki).

[Fu] [Framed as a heading in the upper margin]
Fu (wheat gluten)　　麪筋
　Sweet and cool. Non-toxic. Clears heat, relaxes the center (digestive organs), aids digestion, and stops diarrhea. Note: its nature is soft and rubbery, making it difficult to digest once it enters the stomach.

Fuki (giant butterbur)　　款冬
　Bitter and warm. Non-toxic. Warms the lungs, treats coughing, dissolves phlegm, and brightens the eyes. ○Fuki no tō (butterbur sprout) has the same properties.
Fudansō (perpetual spinach/Swiss chard)　　Same as Tōchisa.
Fudetake (a type of mushroom)　　蘑菰蕈
　Sweet and cold. Toxic.
Bushūkan (a citrus variety)　　Same as Marubushūkan.
Budō (grape)　　葡萄
　Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. Quenches thirst, moistens the chest and lungs, and treats coughing.
