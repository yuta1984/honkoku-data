Yuba (tofu skin) - Dòufupí
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic.

Lily bulb (yurinone) - Bǎihé
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It loosens and relaxes the chest and lungs, relieves liver stagnation (depression of liver qi), tonifies the body's center, promotes the passage of both urine and stool, and is safe for those suffering from a wide range of ailments.

Yugao (bottle gourd; yūkaho) - Húlu — Same as dried gourd strips (kanpyō).

Yusura plum ( yusurame; Prunus tomentosa) - Yīngtáo (cherry)
Sweet, hot, and astringent. Non-toxic. It regulates the body's center and treats diarrhea.

【Left Page】

Yu, also yuzu (Japanese citrus) - Yòu
Sour and cold. Non-toxic. It aids digestion and neutralizes the toxic effects of alcohol, fish, vegetables, and mushrooms.
○ Yuzu miso (citrus miso paste), yubeshi (yuzu-flavored rice cake), and yuzu salt — all non-toxic.

[Me] 【Heading in a box at the top margin】

Cooked rice (meshi) - Fàn
It is the foremost food for nourishing and sustaining the vital essence of life.

Rice water / rice soup (meshiyu) - Mǐyǐn (rice beverage)
Drinking this after a meal rinses and cleanses the teeth and tongue, helps food to descend through the digestive tract, promotes thorough digestion, and allows food to be well transformed and absorbed by the body. In winter, it generates warmth; in summer, it quenches thirst. One should always drink it after meals.
