Halfbeak (sayori) - Zhēnyú (Hemiramphus sajori)
Sweet and cold. Non-toxic.

Mackerel (saba) - Qīnghuāyú (Scomber japonicus)
Sweet, sour, and warm. Mildly toxic. It stops diarrhea caused by deficiency, regulates cold-type dysentery, and has the effect of breaking up blood stagnation (dispelling blood stasis).
○ Salt-pickled mackerel (sashisaba): Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. Its qi and flavor (nature and taste) are superior to that of raw flesh.

Pacific saury (sanma) - Also known as "oki-sayori" (offshore halfbeak).
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic.

Japanese giant salamander (sanshouo) - Níyú (Andrias japonicus)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It treats consumption (tuberculosis-like chronic wasting illness), chronic cough, and childhood convulsive disorders (kan no yami).

【Left Page】

Turban shell (sazae) - Quánluó (Turbo cornutus)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It brightens the eyes, quenches thirst, promotes urination, and counteracts alcohol toxicity. However, its flesh is tough and difficult to digest once it enters the stomach. One should not eat too much of it.

Sarubō (ark clam, Barbatia virescens)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It belongs to the same category as the blood clam (akagai / Scapharca broughtonii).

Heron (sagi) - Lù (Ardea cinerea / various Ardeidae)
Salty and neutral. Non-toxic. It stops spontaneous sweating (jikan) and night sweats (tōkan).
○ There are many varieties of heron, but they are generally all of similar [medicinal] effect.
