Kumquat (kinkan) - Jīnjú
Sweet, sour, and mildly warm. Non-toxic. It quenches thirst and sobers one from alcohol. ▲ Incompatible with sweet potatoes (satsumaimo).

Gigi (Tachysurus nudiceps; a type of catfish) - Huángsǎngyú (Yellow throat fish)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. ▲ Incompatible with Japanese catnip (keigai; Schizonepeta tenuifolia).

Sillago (kisu; Japanese whiting) - Háoyú
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It opens the stomach and promotes appetite. Eating large amounts causes no harm, and it is safe for those with a wide range of ailments. Half-dried specimens are especially recommended.

Pheasant (kiji) - Zhì
Sweet, sour, and warm. Non-toxic. It tonifies the center (the body's core functions) and stops diarrhea. Eating too much causes

Rufous turtle dove (kijibato) - Gēzi (Dove)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic.

Woodpecker (kitsutsuki) - Zhuómùniǎo
Sweet, sour, and neutral. Non-toxic. It treats tooth decay (dental caries).

Goldfish (kingyo) - Jīnyú
Sweet, salty, and neutral. Non-toxic.

Fox (kitsune) - Hú
Sweet and warm. Non-toxic. It is beneficial for those suffering from prolonged, unhealed scabies (hizen-gasa).
