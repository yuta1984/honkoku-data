Kumquat (kinkan) - Jīnjú
Sweet, sour, and mildly warm. Non-toxic. It quenches thirst and sobers one from alcohol. ▲ Incompatible combination with sweet potato.

Gigi catfish (gigi; Tachysurus nudiceps) - Huángsǎngyú (Yellow throat fish)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. ▲ Incompatible combination with Japanese catnip (keigai; Schizonepeta tenuifolia).

Japanese whiting (kisu; Sillago japonica) - Háoyú
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic. It opens the stomach and promotes appetite. Eating it in large quantities causes no harm, and it is safe across a wide range of ailments. Freshly semi-dried specimens are especially recommended.

Pheasant (kiji) - Zhì
Sweet, sour, and warm. Non-toxic. It tonifies the center (spleen and stomach) and stops diarrhea. Eating too much, however,

Japanese turtle dove (kijibato; Streptopelia orientalis) - Gēzi (Pigeon/Dove)
Sweet and neutral. Non-toxic.

Woodpecker (kitsutsuki) - Zhuómùniǎo
Sweet, sour, and neutral. Non-toxic. It treats tooth decay (dental caries).

Goldfish (kingyo) - Jīnyú
Sweet, salty, and neutral. Non-toxic.

Fox (kitsune) - Hú
Sweet and warm. Non-toxic. It is beneficial for persistent, long-unhealed skin scabies (mange).
