Konuka　　　This refers to rice bran
Konbu　　　Kelp
　Sweet, salty, and slightly smooth. Non-toxic. Improves edema, dissolves swollen lymph nodes (ruireki) and lumps (gobu),
　kills parasites, and treats various diseases of the mouth. ▲Incompatible with magnolia bark (koboku)
Koshō　　　Black pepper
　Pungent and greatly warming. Non-toxic. Treats abdominal pain and hernia-like pain (senshaku), dispels heat from summer, and
　detoxifies water toxins. ▲Incompatible with carp and purslane
Kōji　　　　Koji orange (Citrus nobilis)
　Its flavor and medicinal properties are the same as mandarin orange
