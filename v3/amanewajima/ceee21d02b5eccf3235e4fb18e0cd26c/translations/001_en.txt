Dear Sir/Madam,

As the cool days of autumn deepen, we trust that you are in excellent health and good spirits. We write to inform you that the unlawful and unjust conduct of motorized fishing vessel operators has long been a source of deep regret for us, the traditional fishermen. Taking advantage of the imminent enforcement of the regulations governing motorized trawling operations, these parties are seeking to further expand their oppressive conduct. They have already submitted petitions to the relevant authorities as a means of overwhelming us, and are steadily advancing their campaign.

We cannot stand idly by in the face of these developments. On the 11th of November, beginning at 9 o'clock in the morning, we will convene a prefectural assembly of fishermen at the Yamato-za theater in front of Kanazawa Station, at which we intend to determine our unified stance in opposition to their actions. We earnestly invite you to attend and participate.

Respectfully yours,

November 1st, Taishō 10 (1921)

Representative of Fishermen of the Four Districts of Kaga: Matsuda Ichijirō
And twenty others
