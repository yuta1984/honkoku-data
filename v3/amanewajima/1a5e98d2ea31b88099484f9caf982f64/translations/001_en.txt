Letter of Thanks

Your support for the objectives of our association, and your generous donation of twenty yen on behalf of the Kugisu Village Branch, is something for which our association is profoundly grateful. We hereby express our deepest thanks for your kind generosity.

March 6, Taisho 13 (1924)

President, Imperial Reservists' Association
Field Marshal, General of the Army, Junior Second Rank, First Order of Merit, First Class of Military Merit, Viscount Kawamura Kageaki [Seal]
《Inset text: Official Seal of the President of the Imperial Reservists' Association》

To: Taniuchi District Chief
Kuroda Kōzaemon
