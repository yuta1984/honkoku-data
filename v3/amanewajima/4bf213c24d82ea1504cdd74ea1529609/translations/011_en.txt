[Left folio]

　　The foregoing has been duly examined and is as recorded above. Respectfully submitted.

　　The first vessel of the Tokiwa-maru was newly built in the 19th year of Kyōhō [1734], and thereafter,
　　the second new vessel was built in the 12th year of Hōreki [1762],
　　and thereafter, the third new vessel was built in the 4th year of Kansei [1792].
　　From then until the 3rd year of Bunsei, the Year of the Dragon [1820], all three successive vessels made voyages at sea,
　　and throughout this time, not a single arrow-nock [i.e., not even the smallest fitting] was lost,
　　and the vessel was entirely safe at sea without any mishap.
　　Therefore, now in the 4th year of Bunsei, the Year of the Snake [1821], the fourth new vessel has been built,
　　and this record of the construction has been compiled as stated above.
