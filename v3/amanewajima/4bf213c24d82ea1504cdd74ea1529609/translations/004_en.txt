In the first month of the third year of Bunsei (1820), the Year of the Dragon, an order was placed with Genjirō, a shipwright of Kominato in Wakasa Province. Construction began in the second month and was fully completed by early ninth month.
From the fourth year of Bunsei (1821), the Year of the Earth Ox, the vessel set out to sea to commence trading.

　　Supervising carpenters (yokome daiku): Kuroshima, Fujii, and Ihachi
　　Inspected from the 18th day of the second month; confirmed completion at □ residence on the 6th day of the tenth month.
