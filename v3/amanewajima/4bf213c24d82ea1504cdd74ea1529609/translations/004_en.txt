In the first month of Bunsei 3 (Year of the Dragon), an order was placed with Genjirō, a shipwright from Kominato in Wakasa Province. Construction began in the second month and was completed entirely by the ninth month.
From Bunsei 4 (Year of the己/Tsuchinoto-I), the vessel set out to sea and commenced commercial operations.

　　The supervising carpenter for the above was Fujii Ihachi of Kurojima.
　　He departed on the 18th day of the second month and returned home on the 6th day of the tenth month.

The agreed specifications for timber, nails, and clamps (kasugai) for the above vessel are as follows:
