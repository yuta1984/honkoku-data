## Agreed Regulations of Kugasu Village

1. Within the district, there shall be no celebratory visits or banquets held on the occasion of a birth.

2. At wedding ceremonies (such as the bride's arrival), extravagance shall be avoided and the wearing of *uchikake* (formal outer robes) shall be abolished. Furthermore, the bad practice of *nawabari* (territorial/boundary disputes during ceremonies) shall likewise be abolished.

3. At funeral services, artificial flower arrangements shall be limited to no more than five *men* (units/panels); gold and silver paper shall not be used, and items such as flower baskets and elaborate confectionery displays shall be abolished.

4. At funeral banquets (*honzen*), it shall be the basic principle to abolish the serving of wheat gluten dishes (*fu-zara*), confectionery bowls, and sake. However, exceptions may be made in unavoidable circumstances.

5. After a funeral, the practice of returning condolence gifts (*kōden-gaeshi*) shall be abolished within the district.

6. Souvenir gifts and get-well offerings brought back from pilgrimages to Honganji Temple, Zenkoji Temple, or other religious sites and sightseeing trips shall be abolished.

7. At send-offs for men entering military service, celebratory flags and farewell parties shall be abolished.

8. Punctuality in keeping to agreed times shall be strictly observed.

---

The above items were presented in outline form following a joint deliberation held last October among all the town and village heads of this district. In January of this year, the village called together the heads of each district (*ku*) and instructed them that the uneconomical customs and bad practices that had prevailed in each district should be abolished on this occasion. Each district deliberated accordingly, and it was resolved that such uneconomical customs and bad practices would be abolished. Having compiled these resolutions, the village heads were convened again on the fifteenth of this month, and from among the items resolved upon in each district, those common to other districts and those generally practicable were established as the Agreed Regulations of this village. It is our sincere hope that these regulations will without fail be put into practice.

February, Taisho 12 (1923)　　Kugasu Village Office, Fugeshi District
