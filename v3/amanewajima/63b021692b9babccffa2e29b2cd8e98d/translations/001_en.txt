Certificate of Gratitude

We are deeply grateful beyond measure that you have expressed your wholehearted support for the expansion project of Kashihara Shrine and have generously donated the sum of eleven yen and twenty sen. Accordingly, the amount and your esteemed name have been recorded in our register and will be permanently stored in our treasury. We have today performed a ceremony of announcement before the deity, praying for the continued prosperity of your household. We humbly convey our sincerest regards herewith.

October 1st, Taisho 9 (1920)

General Administrator of the Kashihara Shrine Project, Junior Sixth Rank: Uta Shigemaru (Seal)
President of the Same Shrine Project, Junior Fourth Rank, Third Order of Merit: Kidagawa Keigen (Seal)
Advisor to the Same Shrine Project, Senior Third Rank, Second Order of Merit, Viscount: Okabe Nagakura (Seal)
