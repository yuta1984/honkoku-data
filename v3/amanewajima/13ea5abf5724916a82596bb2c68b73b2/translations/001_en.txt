(Marginal note: Received on May 8, Meiji 23 [1890]; Noto-Fushū District Office, Document No. 1)

Arrived: May 11, Meiji 23 [1890]

Ishikawa Prefecture Directive, Police No. 106

To: Representative of Taniuchi, Kugunosu Village, Fushū District
　　　　　　　Hirata Kōsaburō

Regarding the application dated March 10 of this year for the establishment of a new crematorium: the application is hereby approved, and the land in question is to be classified under the second category of privately-owned land (Minyūchi Dai-nishu).
　However, notice must be given to the jurisdictional police station.

May 6, Meiji 23 [1890]
　　　　Ishikawa Prefecture Governor: Iwamura Takafumi　(Seal)
