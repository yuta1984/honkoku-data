**Memorandum**

Rice from Meiwa 6th year (1769)

Item: 75 koku and 2 to of Iwase rice
　　To be shipped via Ōno route in the current year of Tora (Meiwa 7, 1770)
　　　　　For the account of Lord Kikuchi Jirō

Regarding the above rice, which I have purchased from the wholesale middlemen (nakagai) in the town of Kanazawa: I hereby respectfully request that authorization be granted for its export (tsude) from port, as I wish to ship and transport it to Ōno at this time. Furthermore, upon the ship's arrival at that destination (Ōno), arrangements shall be made so that the export certificate (tsude kitte) will be forwarded from the magistrate (bugyō) of that place, as stated herein.

　　　　　　　　　　　Rice owner: Kurojima Village, Noto Province
Meiwa 7th year, 5th month　　　　　　　Matasaburō　(seal)
　　　　　　　　　　　Ship captain:
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Shichiemon　(seal)

　　To: Niikawa District
　　　　Magistrate's Office (Go-bugyōsho)
