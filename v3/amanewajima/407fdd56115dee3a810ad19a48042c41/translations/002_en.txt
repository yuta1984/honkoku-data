[Postscript]
Furthermore, I have inquired about today's rice market price, and it appears there has been no trading whatsoever. I am told it remains at about the same level as yesterday. I believe tomorrow, the 19th, is a market holiday.

[Main body]
I humbly write to you via express messenger. First and foremost, I trust that everyone in your locale continues to enjoy good health, which is most pleasing to hear. Now, this evening at the sixth hour (around 6 o'clock in the evening), a letter arrived from Inuiya Kichibei of Kanazawa, informing us that a shipment of eight thousand koku for export to other provinces has been successfully arranged. Along with this letter came a note urging us to deliver the news to you as quickly as possible. We inquired whether there was a messenger service to Ōno available here, but as no swift courier was available, we consulted with Minatoya Heisuke and arranged for an express messenger to be dispatched. Should you have a reply for Inuiya, please send it back with this messenger. Wishing to convey the above to you, I write hastily under the circumstances.
