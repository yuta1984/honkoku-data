As I have previously stated, we have submitted the petition up to three times, but each time we have been informed that a decision is difficult to render, and we have made various entreaties. We have also repeatedly consulted with Gozaemon-sama in private, and further petitioned other senior officials for their informal consideration. They appeared to be quite understanding and seemed inclined to issue a ruling. However, as time passed and year's end arrived, the collection of revenues (御収納) could not be completed, and the matter was repeatedly postponed. As it proved impossible to obtain a decision before the end of the year, it has been informally communicated to us that, unavoidably, from the 16th day of the New Year onward, a resolution will be issued as soon as the initial settlement is made, and we will be notified accordingly as to whether the matter has been concluded. In connection with this, we have consulted with Gozaemon-sama regarding his remaining in lodgings until around that time.
