As Gozaemon-sama has returned home, I take this opportunity to write to you. Although the lingering cold of winter remains, I trust that you are in ever-increasing good health, for which I am most grateful. I am pleased to inform you that we here are all well, and I humbly ask that you set your mind at ease regarding us.

First, I offer my New Year's greetings, which I have set out in a separate enclosed letter. I am truly grateful for the many kindnesses you have shown us throughout the past year. I humbly ask that you continue to favor us with your unchanged support and assistance going forward.
