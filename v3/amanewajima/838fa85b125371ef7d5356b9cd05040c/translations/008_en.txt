(Continuing from the overlapping section with the previous folio)

I humbly request that you continue to conduct your trade with us in a prosperous manner. There is nothing else of particular note to report, but I wished to convey the above requests, and so I have written as such. I will continue to keep you informed as matters develop. Written with utmost respect.

　5th day of the 1st month　　　Kikuya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Zensaemon (seal)


　To: Moriokaya Matashirō


Postscript: I humbly write to you regarding the matter of the five ryō in gold that was lent to us by Kansuke-sama the year before last. I understand that Gozaemon-sama has expressed his intention to collect this debt, and various courtesies have been exchanged regarding the matter. However, I most humbly request that you grant us an extension, allowing us to repay this amount when your vessel next calls upon us this coming spring.
