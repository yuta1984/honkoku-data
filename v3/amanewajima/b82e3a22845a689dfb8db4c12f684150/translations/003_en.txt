4th Vessel

One vessel: Tokiwamaru – Newly Built Ship
　　　　Keel length (shiki): 3 jō 9 shaku
　　　　Beam (hara): 1 jō 9 shaku
　　　　Depth: 6 shaku

Cargo capacity of this vessel:
　　　　Approximately 460 koku

The above vessel has been in service for thirteen years up to the present Year of the Horse (Tenpō 5, 1834).
Construction began on the 25th day of the 10th month of the Year of the Snake (Bunsei 12, 1829),
and was completed on the 16th day of the 2nd month of the following Year of the Horse (Bunsei 13 / Tenpō 1, 1830).
Therefore, in this thirteenth year of service, a major repair (daisaku) was carried out,
and the vessel set sail on the 17th day of the 2nd month of the Year of the Horse (Tenpō 5, 1834).
