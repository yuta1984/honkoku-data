Memorandum submitted on the 18th day of the 11th month — Addressed to Lord Takigawa Zushо̄

　　　Memorandum

One: From the rice payments (払米) of the current Year of the Rabbit onward, it is hereby agreed that 17,000 koku per year shall be delivered without fail.
　　Stipulation: Although it is to be expected that harvests will vary due to annual conditions and natural disasters, the quantity of rice stipulated in this document shall not be altered. However, should an exceptionally severe crop failure occur, the matter shall be discussed and negotiated at that time; therefore, we ask that appropriate consideration be extended in such circumstances.
