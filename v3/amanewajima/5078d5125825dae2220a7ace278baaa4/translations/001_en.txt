## Petition for Permission to Conduct a Mikoshi Procession

Item 1 — Location of the Mikoshi Procession
From Ichihara Kitashirohiko Shrine to each sub-district (koaza), as shown on the attached map.

Item 2 — Date and Time of the Mikoshi Procession
(Evening Festival) September 15, Showa 11 [1936]: from 8:00 PM to 11:20 PM
(Daytime Festival) September 16, Showa 11 [1936]: from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM

Item 3 — Reason for the Mikoshi Procession
For the festival (sairei) of Ichihara Kitashirohiko Shrine.

Item 4 — Structure of the Hōtō (Votive Lantern Floats)
Seven floats, each measuring 5 ken in height and 6 shaku in width.

Item 5 — Reason for Transporting the Hōtō
For the entertainment programs (yōkō) associated with the festival of Ichihara Kitashirohiko Shrine.

Item 6 — Location for Transporting the Hōtō
The same route as the mikoshi procession, as shown on the attached map.

Item 7 — Date and Time of Transporting the Hōtō
September 15, Showa 11 [1936]: from 8:00 PM to 11:30 PM

As stated above, we respectfully request your permission to conduct the mikoshi procession and transport the hōtō lantern floats.

September 11, Showa 11 [1936]

Taniuchi, Kukusu Village, Fugeshi District
District Head (Kuchō): Ryūtarō Nakayama

To: Sōkichi Satō, Chief of the Wajima Police Station
