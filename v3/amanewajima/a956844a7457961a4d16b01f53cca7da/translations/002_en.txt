Written on the 18th day of the 2nd month, addressed to Itahisa.

I am deeply grateful and humbled to have received your kind letter of the 15th, in which you thoughtfully informed me of the details of the situation. I find it most reasonable that the two individuals have been exerting themselves in various ways on my behalf. I am truly grateful beyond measure for all the care you have extended, both within and without.

In recent days, I dispatched the ship captains for departure, but we are extremely short-handed, and although I have been meaning to send a messenger, there has been no one available to send. In light of this, I humbly request that you continue to extend your kind mediation on my behalf to your household and all those staying there during this time.

As of late, neither the rice loaded from Etchū nor the rice loaded by Kuboya has arrived. Furthermore, the servant here has fallen ill and taken to bed, and both children have contracted smallpox (ōbōsō). We are at a complete loss in every direction. Truly, the worries here at this end are now beyond what words or brush can express, and I find myself at my wit's end.

I humbly ask that you take heed of the above circumstances and mediate as favorably as possible on my behalf. Regarding all matters, I eagerly await your kind assistance upon your return, once your business there is concluded.

I offer this immediate reply to convey the above circumstances.

　18th day of the 2nd month, evening　Kanoya
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Jinsaku
　　　Itahisa-sama
