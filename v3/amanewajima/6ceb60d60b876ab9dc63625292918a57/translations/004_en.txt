【Right Folio】

The vessel (described herein) was ordered and built by Tanakaya Kichisaburō, a ship carpenter of Anamizu Village.

Construction commenced on the 7th day of the 4th month of Bunka 10 (1813), Year of the Rooster, and by the last day of the 9th month, the rudder, mast, ship's tender (hashifune), and all other equipment and fittings were fully completed.

　　　　　Master Carpenter (Tōryō): Kuroshima Kotoya Isoemon
　　　　　Supervising Carpenter (Yokome-tsuke Daiku): Daimonjiya Nihei
