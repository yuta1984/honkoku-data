　One: How many tsubo is this embankment? When asked,
　　　　The answer is 136 tsubo.

[Base] □□□□□□ [Approximately 17 ken in length]
[Upper front face] 2 ken　[Vertical] Height 2 ken　[Lower] □□□□□□
　In the calculation method, when the upper width of 2 ken is added
　to the lower width of 6 ken,
　the total becomes 8 ken. When this is divided by two,
　it becomes 4 ken. When this is multiplied by the height of 2 ken,
　it becomes 8 tsubo. When this is multiplied by
　the length of 17 ken, it is known as 136 tsubo.
