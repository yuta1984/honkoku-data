《No. 34》　On Doubling Day by Day

　※Starting with one poppy seed and doubling it day by day,
　　《in 30 days》　　　Assuming 4,000,000 seeds fit in one *shō* (升):
　　　Total: 1 *koku* (石) 3 *to* (斗) 4 *shō* (升) 2 *gō* (合) 1 *shaku* (勺) 7 *sai* (才) 7 *satsu* (撮) 2 *kei* (圭) 8 *soku* (栗)
　　　　　The above number amounts to
　　　　　　536,870,912 seeds in total

　※Starting with one *mon* (文) coin and doubling it day by day,
　　《in 30 days》　　　Calculating at the rate of 96 *mon* per 100 *mon* (accounting for exchange fees):
　　　Total: 559,240 *kan* (貫) 532 *mon*, including exchange fees (*mesen*)
　　　　　Of this, the exchange fees amount to 22,369 *kan* 620 *mon*
