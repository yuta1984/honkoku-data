【Right page】

　※Starting with one grain of rice and doubling it day by day,
　　《in 30 days》　　　Assuming 60,000 grains fit in one *shō* (升):
　　　Total: 89 *koku* (石) 4 *to* (斗) 7 *shō* (升) 8 *gō* (合) 4 *shaku* (勺) 8 *sai* (才) 5 *satsu* (撮) 3 *kei* (圭) 3 *soku* (栗)
　　　　The above number amounts to
　　　　　536,870,912 grains in total.

　※Starting with one soybean and doubling it day by day,
　　《in 30 days》　　　Assuming 5,000 beans fit in one *shō* (升):
　　　Total: 1,073 *koku* (石) 7 *to* (斗) 4 *shō* (升) 1 *gō* (合) 8 *shaku* (勺) 2 *sai* (才) 4 *satsu* (撮)
　　　　The above number is
　　　　　the same as the number of grains of rice.
