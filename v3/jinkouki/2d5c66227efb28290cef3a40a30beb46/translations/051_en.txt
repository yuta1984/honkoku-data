　　　　　　　　　[Bottom end]　　　　　　　　One

There are thirty children. Fifteen of them are children of the first wife, and the remaining fifteen are children of the current wife. They are arranged in a circle as shown to the right, and every tenth person is removed; then every twentieth is removed, continuing until twenty-nine have been removed, with the last remaining one to inherit the estate. The stepmother arranged them in this manner. When counted, just as the fourteenth child of the first wife was being removed, if counted once more, all the children of the first wife would be eliminated. So the one remaining child of the current wife said: "We are being eliminated one pair at a time, so from here, please begin counting from my position." Unable to refuse, they began counting from the position of the one remaining child of the first wife, with the result that all the children of the current wife remained, and only one child of the first wife survived.
