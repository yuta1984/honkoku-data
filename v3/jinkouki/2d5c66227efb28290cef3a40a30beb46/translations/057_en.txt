《Section 36》　Finding the cube root of 1,000 gold coins

　〇 Length: 6 *sun* 3 *bu* 1 *rin* 1 *mō* 5 *shi*
　〇 Width:  6 *sun* 3 *bu* 1 *rin* 1 *mō* 5 *shi*
　〇 Height: 6 *sun* 3 *bu* 1 *rin* 1 *mō* 5 *shi*
　　　　Assuming 175 *monme* per square *sun* (i.e., per cubic *sun*).

　　The method: 1,000 gold coins weigh 44 *monme* each [in total, 44,000 *monme*]. Dividing this by 175 *monme*, one obtains 251 *tsubo* 4 *bu* 2 *rin* 8 *mō* 5 *shi* 7 *kotsu* 1 [in cubic *sun*]. Applying the cube root method to this gives 6 *sun* 3 *bu* 1 *rin* 1 *mō* 5 *shi*.
