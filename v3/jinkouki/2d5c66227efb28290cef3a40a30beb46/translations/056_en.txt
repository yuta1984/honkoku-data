《Section 35》　On the "Crow Calculation" (*Karasu-zan*)

　※If 999 crows cry from 999 inlets (*ura*),
　　with each crow crying 999 times, and you ask
　　how many cries there are in total:
　　　〇 Total: 9 *gai* (垓) 9 *kei* (京) 7 *chō* (兆) ○○ 2,999 cries


【Left page】

　　The method: place 999 cries for 999 crows and multiply,
　　and it becomes 998,001 cries. Multiply this further
　　by 999 inlets, and you get
　　9 *gai* (垓) 9 *kei* (京) 7 *chō* (兆) ○○ 2,999 cries.

　※Likewise, if 99 crows cry 99 times each at 99
　　inlets, how many cries are there in total?
　　　〇 970,299 cries
　　The method is the same as above.
