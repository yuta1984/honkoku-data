※○1 tan 7 se 3 bu
[Right figure]
[Left side] Length 24 ken [Middle vertical] Length 33 ken [Right side] 24 ken
[Horizontal] Width 18 ken
　By the calculation method, adding 24 ken to the length of 33 ken gives 57 ken.
　Dividing this by two gives 28 ken and a half. Multiplying this by
　18 ken gives 513 tsubo. When dividing this
　by the field method of 3, it should be known as 1 tan 7 se 3 bu.
　　The above is a transcription of the land survey calculations, provided here for reference and convenience.
　　The underlying principles are all the same, but this has been presented
　　in this manner as a teaching aid for beginners.

《Section 27》　On the yield of landholdings (chigyo monari)
　※When there are 2 tan 7 se, and the assessed rice yield rate (tokudai) is 1 koku 5 to per tan,
　　when asked how much the total assessed yield (taka) is for the above,
　　　○The total assessed yield (taka) is 4 koku 5 sho.
　　By the calculation method, place 2 tan 7 se to the right, and multiply by 1 koku 5 to,
　　and the assessed yield (taka) should be known as 4 koku 5 sho.
