《Problem 25》　On Nested-Box Calculation
※Suppose a set of six nested boxes was purchased for 21 monme of silver, with each successive box priced at eight bu more than the next smaller one. When asked what the price of the smallest box is:
　　① 5 monme 5 bu　　② 4 monme 7 bu　　③ 3 monme 9 bu
　　④ 3 monme 1 bu　　⑤ 2 monme 3 bu　　⑥ 1 monme 5 bu

The method: divide 21 monme by six to get 3 monme 5 bu.
