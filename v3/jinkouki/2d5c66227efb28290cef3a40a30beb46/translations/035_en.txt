《Box: No. 30》　On the Matter of Estimating Gold Leaf for a Folding Screen
　※A two-panel folding screen has a height of 5 shaku and a width of 3 shaku per panel. Together,
　　the horizontal measure is 6 shaku. When asked how many
　　sheets of 4-sun gold leaf can fit,


[Left page]
　　　　○187 and a half sheets fit.
[Illustration of a folding screen at lower right]
　As the method, when 5 shaku is multiplied by 6 shaku, the area becomes 3,000 sun-tsubo.
　When this is divided by 16, the area in sun-tsubo of a 4-sun sheet of leaf,
　one knows it to be 187 and a half sheets. To this, add an allowance of
　10% or 20%. This is to be said depending on the quality of the leaf. Generally,
　one should know it to be approximately as described.
