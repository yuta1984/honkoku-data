## Tayasu Family Estate Garden Illustrations — Waka Poetry Collection

---

**The Frost-Withered Hedge (Shimogareha no Magaki)**

At the evening of the frost-withered hedge with its myriad vines,
The fragrance of plum blossoms fills this village heralding spring.

---

**The Bower of Affinity (Yukari no Ya)**

Without asking whether summer has passed or spring still lingers,
Here hang the cascading waves of wisteria, bound by deep affinity.

---

**The Jeweled Weed Pond (Tamamo no Ike)**

The clear waters of the Jeweled Weed Pond
Are like a pattern adorned with drops of dew.

---

**The Jeweled Weed Mountain (Tamamo no Yama)**

What a fitting dwelling this stillness provides —
How magnificent, this "Jeweled Weed Mountain" of the Lord's eternal reign.

---

**The Thousand-Generation Narrow Path (Chiyo no Hosomichi)**

The frost and snow that pile upon the pine trees, coming and going —
How many times shall we sweep thee, O Thousand-Generation Narrow Path?

---

**The Purification Slope (Oharai Saka)**

On the Purification Slope, where today summer and autumn meet,
Come, let us cross it fully and enjoy the cool of evening.

---

**The Early Autumn Grove (Hatsuaki no Mori)**

In the morning breeze blowing through the leaves of the early autumn grove,
The first things to scatter are the mountain dewdrops between the trees.

---

**The Autumn Wind Pond (Akikaze no Ike)**

Take heed, O garment of the night amid the scattering waves —
The autumn wind has risen upon the pond at autumn's end.

---

**The Mishina Rice Fields (Mishina to Da)**

In the Mishina paddies, where one rests during the late-rice harvest of autumn,
Endless waves arise of their own accord.

---
