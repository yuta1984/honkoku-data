**Scattered Waves (Chirichiri-nami)**
O robes of the night, scattered like rippling waves — take heed!
The autumn wind has risen over the pond of Akirase.

**Mishinato-wa (Gate of the Honorable Science/Landing)**
At the harbor rice fields of the autumn moorings, where late-ripening rice is reaped,
The endless waves spread out across the fields.

**Garden of a Thousand Varieties (Chigusa no Sono)**

Among the thousand autumn grasses,　　　　　at each and every flower,
Dew has settled, invisible even through the haze.

**Boat Mountain (Funa-yama)**

On this autumn night, I would ask of you, O Moon Mountain shaped like a boat —
Do you take up the oar of the laurel, or do you not?

**Embankment of Pampas Grass (Obana no Tsutsumi)**

Wandering lost among the pampas grass on the embankment, first parting the stalks —
Even so, it seems the grasses have beckoned people to come.

**Barrier of True Bush Clover (Mahagi ga Seki)**

The autumn wind blows dew across the barrier — how it blows!
Has the barrier of Sahagi permitted the blossoms to pass?

**Willow Bay (Yanagi ga Ura)**

The evening moon over Willow Bay, drifting to the shore's edge —
Even its reflection is thin and hazy in the spring mist.

**Cape of the Torii Gate (Torii no Saki)**

On the pine branches of the sacred torii cape, where the gods dwell,
Has the sea mist draped white snow-like waves?

**Cape of the Maiden (Otome ga Saki)**

The sleeve of a robe in this land seems to have vanished — how cold it is!
The evening wind at Cape Otome, where maidens harvest jeweled seaweed.

**Bay of the Reed Weir (Ashiro ga Ura)**

As if saying "come and see," at Ashiro Bay, a great person
Has layered domain upon domain, and so shall dwell here.
