## Waka Poems of Yokuen-en Garden

**"Chitose no Hama" (Beach of a Thousand Years)**
Familiar and ever-watchful, in the island breeze of a thousand years,
Even that speaks to us — the voice of the pines.

**"Chiyo no Iwahashi" (Stone Bridge of a Thousand Generations)**
Surely this is the path where the immortal sage
Of a thousand ages still treads, leaving traces to this day.

**"Kinugasa Yanagi" (Parasol Willow)**
Taking as an example the flowers that the warbler seems to sew,
How like a spring garment is the dyed, draping willow.

**"Tachiake no Ura" (Bay of the Rising Dawn)**
In the autumn mist of the bay where the late moon lingers in mid-autumn,
The undisturbed reflection — white chrysanthemums along the shore.

**"Iroka no Sono" (Garden of Color and Fragrance)**
In the pale light of spring just beginning to spread,
The garden of hazy color and scent, its plum blossoms in half-bloom.

**"Haru Shiru Sato" (Village that Knows Spring)**
The frost-withered evening falls over the countless garden fences,
And in the village that knows spring, the fragrance of plum blossoms drifts.

**"Yukari no Ya" (Cottage of Connection)**
Is it summer arriving, or spring lingering? — in this twofold moment,
The wisteria waves cast their spell, weaving connections like salt mist.

**"Tamamo no Ike" (Jeweled Weed Pond)**
The water, its graceful aquatic plants, the valley, half the ground spanning several *sun* beyond — *(continues)*
