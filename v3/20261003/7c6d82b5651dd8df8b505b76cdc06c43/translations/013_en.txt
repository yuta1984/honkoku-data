## Tayasu Family Estate Garden Map (No. 5) — Waka Poems for Each Scenic Spot

**Spring Pond (Haru no Ike)**
Even the insects of the spring breeze's domain seem visible—
how serenely the mist drifts over the surface of the water.

---

**Yamabuki Gate (Yamabuki no Seki)**
Even the passing spring, draping itself over the blooming yamabuki flowers,
passes through Yamabuki Gate without announcing its name.

---

**Shinonome Cove (Shinonome no Ura)**
Is it flowers creeping along the pine trees?
At Shinonome Cove, horizontal clouds drift, tinged in purple at dawn.

---

**Flower Shade (Hana no Kage Nameshi)**
Perhaps these crumbling thickets form the path along which spring fades—
as fleeting as flowers blooming in the hearts of people.

---

**Sakura Abyss (Sakuragafuchi)**
Because the wind blows so fiercely, the petals scattered into Sakura Abyss
evoke the very image of waves crashing against the rocks.

---

**Lower Winding Path (Maki no Shitamichi)**
When the mist that was the dwelling of Lady Saho, goddess of spring, clears,
the birds' songs drift hazily along the lower winding path.

---

**Brocade Island (Nishiki no Shima)**
Everyone who sees it laments leaving Brocade Island—
yet why do people hide their oars and steal away?

---

**Colored Voices Mountain Path (Iro-ne no Yamaji)**
As I behold it, the leaves surrendering themselves to the wind—
how I wish I could know the figure traveling the mountain path of colored voices.

---

**Takaoka Mountain (Takaoka Yama)**
The wind blowing through the morning sun on Takaoka Mountain—
could it be luring the voices of birds yet unseen?

---

**Hayama Gate (Hayama no Seki)**
At the gate of Hayama Pass, buried in bamboo leaves,
still the sound of snow-broken branches is questioned and found at fault.

*(Bamboo Field Path)*
