**Otome no Saki (Maiden's Cape)**

The evening breeze at Otome no Saki chills the sleeves of the maiden gathering jeweled seaweed, like the endless flow of a garment of this world.

---

**Ashiroka no Ura (Ashiroka Bay)**

One who comes to Ashiroka Bay, never tiring of gazing upon it, must be destined to spend day after day dwelling here.

---

**Matsu no Kojima (Pine Islet)**

In the helpless quiet of evening, when even the sound of the flowing current seems unruly, there stands the little islet with its one and two lone pines.

---

**Kusuresu no Kishi (Rustling Reed Bank)**

Entrusting its voice to the plum-scented wind, the waves return languorously, brushing against the reeds along the rustling bank.

---

**Momiji no Shitamichi (Path Beneath the Maples)**

Along the shaded path beneath the trees, dyed with morning dew, one hurries as if left behind by the twilight.

---

**Chishire no Fuchi (Thousand-Dyed Deep Pool)**

Into the deep pool dyed a thousandfold by the crimson leaves, one plunges — but can one truly fathom the depth of autumn?

---

**Shirasagi no Hashi (White Heron Bridge)**

Whatever colors spring and autumn may lend to the pools and shallows, the white heron at the bridge remains unknowing, ever still.

---

**Harukaze no Ike (Spring Breeze Pond)**

Along the shore where the spring breeze blows, one can only gaze — the surface of the water drifts gently in a haze.

---

**Yamabuki no Seki (Kerria Rose Gate)**

Even spring, which passes by noting every blooming flower, crosses the Yamabuki Gate without announcing its name.
