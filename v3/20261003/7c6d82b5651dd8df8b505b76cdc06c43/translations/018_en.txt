Though one looks upon it with reverence, the Bay of Ajiro is never wearying to behold — how intriguing to wonder who awaits the night-shallows of the fisherman's torches. If one were to walk the mountain path of the sounds and colors of words, how much more like the nesting-place of flowers and birds one's heart would feel at rest. To behold in one place the scenery of all four seasons in this manner is truly without equal. And so, may flourishing increase without cease through the narrow bamboo path to the very tips of the leaves of the pine islets; taking as precedent the shore of a thousand years that never crumbles, may one continue to bathe in the gracious waves of Your Lordship's benevolence; and wishing that you may long enjoy the moon and blossoms in this floating life of a thousand autumns, I offer my heartfelt congratulations — with that thought, I too wish for nothing more than to become a figure within this painting.

*Masatoshi* [with seal/signature]
