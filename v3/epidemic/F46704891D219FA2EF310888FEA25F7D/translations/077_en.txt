Tokyo Nichinichi Shimbun, No. 917

Minister Kibi — played by Ichikawa Danjūrō
　　　　　Ikkeisai Yoshiiku [seal]

At the Kawarasaki-za theatre in Shiba Shinbori, a new play entitled "Kibi Daijin: A Tale of China" has been put together for performance. In it, our country's Minister Kibi, over a certain matter arising between Japan and Tang China, presses the Tang Emperor Xuanzong and compels him to hand over tribute gold. Hearing that the part of the Minister in this scene is to be taken by the celebrated Ichikawa Danjūrō, one imagines that his eloquent debate with An Lushan and the others will be splendid indeed. And since the author is the famous old master Kawatake (Mokuami), people say there will surely be something of great interest in it.
