Osaka Nishiki-e Shinwa (New Tales in Osaka Brocade Pictures), No. 10 [written horizontally]

It is well known to all the world that Lady Tang, wife of a man named Cui Shannan, when her mother-in-law Lady Zhangsun grew too old to take solid food, constantly gave her own breast milk to her, fulfilling her filial duty to the utmost.

Here now is a story that is the very reverse of that. Otsuru, wife of a man named Shimada Masashichi of No. 7 Kita-Jinbōchō, Tokyo, did not live out the crane's thousand years: around the seventh year of Meiji (1874) she died, leaving behind as her keepsake a baby girl to whom she had given birth. The old grandmother of the house, unable to bear her grief, resolved to raise the grandchild with her own hands, and gave the infant her withered, wrinkled breasts of some seventy years to suck — whereupon, wondrous to relate, milk of late came forth and flowed freely, and the grandchild has grown plump upon it. Now, is that not a rare thing indeed!

The full account appears in the Yomiuri, No. 85.
