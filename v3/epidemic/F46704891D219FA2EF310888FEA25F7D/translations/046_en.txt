Adultery is one and the same thing as the mind of a thief. And here is a curious tale.

▲ It is a story of old folk. The place, they say, is somewhere around Fukushima. A man called Gorōemon, though he was already three or four years past sixty, took up with a woman of the same village named O-Ume — she too, they say, about fifty-two or fifty-three. Now this O-Ume has a lawful husband of her own. By what strange karma was it that her adultery with Gorōemon became the talk of the town? Surely it is what Heaven knows and reveals. At last, unable to endure it any longer, the two slipped away under cover of a dark night and eloped — a sight rather like the flight of Umegawa and Chūbei; but such carryings-on in imitation of that by old people are utterly ridiculous and deserve nothing but contempt.
