Nichinichi Shimbun, No. 16

On the night of the 22nd day of the Second Month last, a steamship on the Tokushima run accidentally caught fire on board off Iwaya in Awa Province (Awaji Island). The flames spread to the cargo and grew fierce; the whole crew strove with all their might to put it out, while the spirits of the passengers aboard the same vessel burned as though ten times the coal had been stoked—so swiftly did the ship run that, with black smoke covering the sky, in but a single instant she reached the shore. That the more than two hundred crew and passengers barely escaped with their lives is indeed cause to rejoice that the *Buji-maru* ("Safety Maru") lived up to her name; and we must admire the captain's quick wit and his manly resolve in the crisis.
