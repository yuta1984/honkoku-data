In the province of Higo [present-day Kumamoto Prefecture], a shining object appeared in the sea every night, so an official of that district went out to look, whereupon a being of the form shown in the picture appeared. It said: "I am a creature dwelling in the sea, called Amabie. For six years from this year there will be abundant harvests throughout the provinces; but at the same time an epidemic will spread. Quickly draw a picture of me and show it to the people." So saying, it went back down into the sea. The picture above is a copy of the one that this official reported to Edo.

Mid-fourth month, Kōka 3, the year of the Horse [1846]
