Nichinichi Shimbun

Such is the blessing of civilization that in each district elementary schools are built to nurture men of talent; and such is the benevolence of enlightenment that in every prefecture hospitals are established to preserve the people's health. Just now, in the Twelfth Ward of Kamigyō in the Western Capital (Kyoto), a plan arose to build a hospital to relieve the sick, and the "sand-carrying" (sunamochi) undertaken for that purpose ran for ten fine-weather days beginning on the fifth day of the fourth month of Meiji 8 (1875). The people of each ward turned out, vying with one another not to be outdone in wealth and display; their splendor was such that, as the old poem has it, "the capital itself is the brocade of spring," so richly were they attired. The decorated floats (dashi) that each town made as its emblem closely resembled the yama and hoko floats of the Gion Festival. Among them, the most magnificent of all were those of the Third and Fourth Wards of Shimogyō. It is further said that the sand-carrying for Toyokuni Shrine will take place in the near future, and that too will surely be a lively affair.
