Nichinichi Shinbun, No. 8
　　　　　　　　Konobu, now the second generation — Sadanobu [artist]
　　　　　　　　　　　　Wata-Masa, blocks
　　　　　　　　　　　　Carver: Kyūichi

Adultery is one and the same thing as the mind of a thief. And here is a curious tale of some old folk. The place, they say, is somewhere around Fukushima. There was a man called Gorōemon, who, though he was three or four years past sixty, took up an illicit relationship with a woman of the same village named O-Ume — she too, they say, about fifty-two or fifty-three. Now this O-Ume has a lawful husband of her own. By what strange karma was it, then, that her adultery with Gorōemon became the talk of the town? Surely it is what Heaven knows and reveals.

At last, unable to endure it any longer, the two slipped away under cover of a dark night and eloped — a scene rather like the flight of Umegawa and Chūbei; but such carryings-on by old people are utterly ridiculous and deserve nothing but contempt.
