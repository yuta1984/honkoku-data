Tokyo Nichinichi Shimbun, No. 964

It is well known to all the world that the shrine of the Goddess Benzaiten at Enoshima in Sōshū (Sagami Province) has, since ancient times, held its public unveiling of the image once every seven years. Now, in this Year of the Boar, it is said that an extraordinary grand festival will be held for fifty days, from the first day of the fourth month through the twentieth day of the fifth month. In connection with this, the inn Ebisuya — a famous member of the "Isshin-kō" lodging association resident on this island — has newly erected a three-storied tower building. Its construction is in the Japanese style, and beginning with the careful attention given to the arrangement of the rooms, every one of its appointments is done in the height of refined taste. Gazing out from the upper floor, one has gathered below one's eyes the scenery of Miura, Kamakura, and Yui-ga-hama; Mount Fuji is white in the far distance, and Hakone is green close at hand. The air is clean and bracing, such as to restore one's health, and the place is especially rich in the fruits of the sea, so that fine delicacies are never lacking. Visitors coming to worship should by all means go up and try it for themselves.

Lately, it is said, in order to hang it in this third-story hall, the actors Bandō Hikosaburō, Nakamura Shikan, (Onoe) Kikugorō, (Ichikawa) Sadanji, (Ichikawa) Danjūrō, and (Iwai) Hanshirō, together with more than ten other actors, as well as playwrights, tomeba (theatre ushers) and others, are having a noren curtain made dyed with their names, and are busily preparing to present it to Ebisuya.

Keisai Yoshiiku (artist)
Ningyō-chō, Gusokuya (publisher); Watanabe Horiei (engraver)
