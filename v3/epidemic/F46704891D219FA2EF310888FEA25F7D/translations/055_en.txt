Yomiuri Shimbun, No. 115
　　　　　　　　　　　　Drawn by Morikawa Chikashige

Yamamoto Tadeo (Taneo?) of Yamaguchi Prefecture, residing at No. 10, Tsukiji 1-chōme, had been carrying on an illicit affair with a woman named Kimura Ofuku of No. 2, Fukagawa Mannen-chō. Recently, however, Ofuku went to live at the house of a certain Fukui in Sakurada Fushimi-chō, and there she repeatedly committed adultery with Fukui as well, and became pregnant. Since it was unclear whose child it was, the parties were in the midst of negotiations when, on the twenty-ninth day of the fifth month, Ofuku spotted Yamamoto passing through Fushimi-chō, beat him with her parasol, and caused a great commotion.
