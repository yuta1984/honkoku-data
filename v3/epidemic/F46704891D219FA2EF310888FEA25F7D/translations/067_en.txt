Osei, the wife of one Ogawa Usaku of No. 6, Mikasa-chō, Honjo, Tokyo, was arrested for thieving. But her husband Usaku was himself a man who, back when this same Osei was still another man's wife, had carried on an adulterous affair with her — snatching her away from her husband — and so entered into an illicit union with her. In lawlessness and iniquity, then, the two make a well-matched pair indeed.

Their bond, close as fish and water yet ill-starred, could not escape the net of Heaven; and so, when both of them end up bound in the ropes of the law, one can only say that they have received the retribution they deserved.
