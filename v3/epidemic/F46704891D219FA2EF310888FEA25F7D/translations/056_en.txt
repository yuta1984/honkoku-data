Yomiuri Shimbun, No. 109 — Drawn by [Morikawa] Chikashige

At a restaurant called "Uehan" in Mukōjima, a retired nobleman (kazoku) — someone styled "Lord Nanigawa," said to live around Imado — came and was drinking sake.

A woman named Osen who waited on him at that gathering was not only beautiful in face, but gentle even in her heart and bearing. So the old gentleman presented her with a coral bead more than an inch across, and putting on airs, made a little pass at her with amorous designs. But as it happened Osen was a woman of upright character, and she said:

"Please spare me such unpleasant talk. I may indeed work as a teahouse girl, but my heart (is my own and clean); I will not curry your favor in any such way."

Being, after all, a woman whose trade is charm, she rebuffed the old gentleman neatly and politely.
