The child wrestler Godairiki Moichi, whose likeness was reproduced the other day in Nishiki-ga Shinbun No. 14, has the vigor of a carp swimming up the rivers of Naniwa (Osaka). Universally praised, his name resounds shrill and brilliant along with the tower drums of the grand fair-weather sumo tournaments. As for the true particulars of the physique of this boy who performed the ring-entering ceremony, they are as follows:

　Osaka Prefecture, stable master (tōdori)
　　　Takasaki Yōzaemon
　　　Disciple:
　　　Godairiki Moichi
　　　Aged seven years and two months
　　Height: 4 shaku 5 sun (about 136 cm)
　　Weight: 18 kanme (about 67.5 kg)

Here we depict him once more, and can only look forward to the day when both his popularity and his strength shall flourish still further.

　　　　　　　(Takarai) Kikaku
