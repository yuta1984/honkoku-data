Meiji 8 (1875)
Nishiki-e Shinbun (Brocade-Picture Newspaper)　　Drawn by Sasaki Yoshitaki

In the village of Ōyazawa, Koma District, Bushū (Musashi Province), there lived a stalwart man whose strength surpassed that of ordinary men. His name was Ōkōchi Seibei; he was no weakling at swordsmanship either, and was of an exceptionally bold and forceful temperament.

One day, as he was returning from a neighboring village, something about the state of his house seemed suspicious. Listening carefully, he realized that robbers had broken in; so he borrowed a wooden sword from the house next door and, with a great shout, rushed in upon them. After a furious, ever-shifting struggle in which he himself was wounded, he at last took the thief alive. When the man was seized, he proved to be one Tajima Sōbei of Ogose village, Iruma District, in the same province.

　When sparks fly from the wooden sword,
　　the robber's cry of "Hand over the gold! Hand over the gold!"
　　comes to nothing — dissolves into water.

　　　　Shōjōdō
