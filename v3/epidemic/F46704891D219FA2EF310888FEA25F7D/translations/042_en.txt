A man named Itakura Kihee, of Shinmachi 2-chōme in Osaka, was staying at an inn in Matsue in Unshū (Izumo Province). Day after day he indulged to the utmost in food, drink, and the company of geisha, spending a thousand pieces of gold as freely as if it were hot water or cold. Yet he never used paper money — only hard coin — and this drew suspicious eyes upon him (his scent, as it were, was caught by watchful eyes). One night at last he was seized and interrogated, whereupon it emerged that the money in question was the loot of that great robbery in which, in the ninth month of Meiji 7 (1874), the strongbox of the Mitsui house in Osaka was broken open and twenty-seven thousand yen carried off. — The full details are given in the Hōchi Shinbun.
