**[Right folio]**

From the fourth month through the ninth month, for those who come to receive inoculation, a suitable gratuity (*kaka-kin*, reward money) is provided to those who allow the vaccine lymph to be collected from them. The reason for establishing this gratuity is that, when the height of summer's sweltering heat arrives, even those who ordinarily hold the method in deep trust will largely hesitate and prove reluctant to come. If there are no recipients to be inoculated, there will be no lymph to collect; and if no lymph can be collected, there will be no means of continuing the vaccine strain. Now, by establishing this gratuity, the children of those who come are already protected in safety, and even the poor receive no small benefit. Thereby, those coming for inoculation arrive in a steady, unceasing stream, and the quality vaccine strain remains continuous and unbroken. Since this practice has now been carried on for a considerable length of time, people in general have come to understand that the method of cowpox vaccination presents no obstacle even during the height of summer.

*(Supplementary note)* Upon careful reflection: even within China, it is not possible to obtain the vaccine material directly from local cattle there. To offer payment so that people come willingly and thus ensure that the vaccine strain continues unbroken — this truly deserves to be called a benevolent heart of universal love.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**The next priority lies in recognizing and identifying skin diseases (*fūshitsu*).**

The love that parents bear for their children is universally the same among all people. In the Guangdong region (*Yuèdōng*), the terrain is low-lying and damp, such that skin diseases (*fūshitsu*) are virtually unavoidable. Among those who come seeking inoculation, it is feared that they may all arrive together in a confused crowd, and that one might mistakenly collect lymph from a diseased individual and transmit it to a healthy child — the harm caused thereby would be no small matter.
