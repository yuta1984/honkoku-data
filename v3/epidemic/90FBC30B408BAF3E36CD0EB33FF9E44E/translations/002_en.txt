Preface to Sanka Shinsho

When the cowpox vaccine was transmitted from the Western seas, physicians in our land also came, one after another, to advocate for it. It is truly a wondrous technique for preserving life. However, many people regarded it with suspicion and harbored doubts. As a result, its spread stagnated and it was not widely practiced, remaining only in two or three domain states...
