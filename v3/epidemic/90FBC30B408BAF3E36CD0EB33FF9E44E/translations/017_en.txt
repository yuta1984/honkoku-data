**[Right folio]**
Let us compare this to catching a thief: if one seizes the opportunity while his wings have not yet fully grown, capturing him becomes exceedingly easy. Or compare it to removing weeds: if one cuts them down before they spread and multiply, removing them becomes exceedingly easy. When a child in a household breaks out with smallpox and encounters a severe case, a doctor is summoned, medicine is administered, and the entire family watches over the child morning and night. What tremendous hardship this is! Prayers are offered to the gods and the Buddha — what tremendous fear and alarm! Even in mild cases, careful nursing from multiple angles is required. Now, with cowpox vaccination, one need only inoculate four or six pustules. The child laughs and plays, eats and drinks entirely as usual, and within ten days or so, success is proclaimed. There is no disaster, no harm. Not only is the child spared from suffering, but those who raise the child are also freed from worry and toil. This is because the method is truly excellent.

**[Left folio]**
Someone asked: "Is there anyone who has died from receiving cowpox vaccination?" The answer: "There is absolutely no reason that it should cause death. When I was young, I had not yet contracted smallpox, and a Western physician inoculated me. I was thirty-two years of age at the time. It has now been thirteen years since then. The children inoculated by my hand number no fewer than tens of thousands. All of them have grown up soundly, going on to take wives and bear children. I cannot count them all. For instance, there was the Bingu of Nanjō, the former Grand Administrator (大中丞), who was near sixty-two years of age and was struggling to obtain an heir. When I previously established a bureau in Guangdong, he had a child, and commanded me to perform the vaccination. It took effect immediately upon application. I was presented with a hanging plaque bearing the four characters 勿薬有喜 ('No medicine needed — there is joy'). Among all the others I have inoculated, there has not been a single failure in ten thousand cases. This is known to all. If…"
