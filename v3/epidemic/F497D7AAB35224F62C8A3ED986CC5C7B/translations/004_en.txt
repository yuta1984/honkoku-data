[Affixed note]
My own child underwent this cowpox vaccination (smallpox inoculation) with ease and playfulness, and has since grown ever more vigorous and healthy — a source of great joy to me. Since the love of one's children is a feeling shared by all, I wished that the people of this world might enjoy the same fortune as my child. With this in mind, I have obtained a copy of Master Kuwata's printed work and am offering it to the world, so that all may join together in celebrating the flourishing of our descendants.
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Shiin (Donor/Sponsor of this printing)
