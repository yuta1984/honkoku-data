　　　Hoshaku Gyūtō Bosatsu
　　　(The Bodhisattva of Cowpox, Protector of Infants)

　　　Xījiāngyuè (West River Moon) — a ci-poem

With great compassion and mercy, a vow is made;
To save and deliver all living beings — this is the heart's purpose.
From ancient times, methods for protecting infants have been as numerous as trees in a forest,
Yet the cowpox method is the most profound of all.

　The thousand grasses
　　　that might have withered
　　　　　in snow and frost —
　from this day forth,
　　still green,
　　　shall they meet the spring.

　　　　　　　　　　　Haruo
