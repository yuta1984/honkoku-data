**[Great Misfortune] Natural Smallpox**

*Duration:*
The length of illness varies according to the severity of the case.

*Cost:*
Most of the various preparations and provisions prove to be of no effect.

*Precautions:*
Throughout the illness and in the recovery period, appropriate medical treatment is required in all respects.

*Treatment:*
(No specific notes.)

*Disfigurement:*
Scars and blemishes form on the skin and face, and in some cases permanent deformity results.

*Residual Ailments:*
It is extremely common for patients to be left with ulcers in various parts of the body, skin diseases, glandular disease (lymph node disorders), blindness, deafness, and other conditions.

---

**[Misfortune] Variolation by Insufflation (Kinegawa method)**

*Duration:*
There is variation in the onset of fever; in some cases fever does not develop and distress appears instead.

*Cost:*
(No specific notes.)

*Precautions:*
Avoid cold clothing, and take measures to prevent the illness from turning into diseases of the brain, eyes, or ears.

*Treatment:*
Medicine and nutritional care (*setsuyo*) are indispensable.

*Disfigurement:*
The substance easily enters the eyes, and in some cases severe eye disease is left behind.

*Residual Ailments:*
Because the toxin enters through the nasal passage, throat disease and eye disease may result.

---

**[Middling] Vesiculation Variolation**

*Duration:*
In early cases the disease appears in six to seven days; in late cases it only gradually appears after eight to nine days.

*Cost:*
(No specific notes.)

*Precautions:*
Attention to diet, nursing, and medicine is required. Newborns, those with general systemic illness, and children in the teething period are contraindicated.

*Treatment:*
In some cases, dribbling urination (*rinreki*; difficulty urinating) may occur; anti-inflammatory medication (*bōkinzai*) should be used.

*Disfigurement:*
One in thirty to forty patients will develop a pockmarked face (*mamen*).

*Residual Ailments:*
The site of inoculation on the arm may become gangrenous and remain unhealed for a long time.

---

**[Fortunate] Human Pox Inoculation by Scarification**

*Duration:*
Compared to previous (natural) pox, the course is very rapid. Costs vary depending on the case.

*Cost:*
(Included with duration entry.)

*Precautions:*
Avoid severe cold and extreme heat; contraindicated for the elderly and pregnant women.

*Treatment:*
It is advisable to use antiparasitic and detoxifying medicines to cleanse the intestines and stomach.

*Disfigurement:*
It is extremely rare for patients to be left with scars across the entire body.

*Residual Ailments:*
It is very uncommon for ailments to develop after the illness has passed.

---

**[Most Auspicious] Cowpox Vaccination**

*Duration:*
The duration is very short. When it becomes widely available to the public in the future, the cost too will likely be nothing.

*Cost:*
(Included with duration entry.)

*Precautions:*
Beyond following ordinary habits of bodily care, there is absolutely nothing special that requires attention.

*Treatment:*
No medicine is needed. However, medicine to prevent other diseases may be used.

*Disfigurement:*
No disfigurement whatsoever results.

*Residual Ailments:*
There are absolutely no residual ailments or complications of any kind.

**Introduction to Cowpox (*Gyūtō Hatsumon*): General Treatise — Written by Kuwata Wa**

○ Cowpox vaccination is a newly invented excellent method from the West. By administering it to infants who have not yet contracted smallpox, it protects them from being disfigured with a pockmarked face or dying as a result of the virulent epidemic smallpox. Just as fields such as astronomy and calendar calculation become increasingly clear and refined with the accumulation of years and months, this excellent method has newly spread across the four great continents of the world, and in Kaei 2 (1849) it was finally brought to our country (Japan) — this is truly a great blessing for the people of the world. However, it is also a natural course of things that any excellent method, at its very beginning, is looked upon with suspicion and doubt by people and is difficult to put into practice.
