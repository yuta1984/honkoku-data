【Right folio】
The suffering of infants and the grief of parents are truly unbearable to witness. For this reason, around the time when the rash has fully emerged and the pustules begin to swell, even when one observes the warning signs and tells the family, "Unless treated quickly, complications will arise," the family, while the child is not yet in severe pain, does not take the matter seriously enough. They are negligent in their nursing care, violate the prohibitions, fail to avoid sources of contamination, and meanwhile, people around them recommend various reputed remedies and prayers. Then the popular physician of the day mismanages the treatment, and before long it becomes too late. The afflicted child dies, and the number of parents who lament and regret is beyond counting.

【Left folio】
Smallpox that could have been cured with diligent care from the very beginning and proper medicinal treatment leads to this untimely death simply because the parents are ignorant of medicine. An ancient teaching says something to the effect that "not knowing medicine leads to a failure of benevolence and filial piety."
Smallpox in particular is a critical gateway between life and death for children. Therefore, I have long wished to teach — even to women — the proper methods of health maintenance, the essentials of nursing care, and dietary prohibitions and restrictions, so that they may thoroughly understand them. When I shared these teachings with a certain person, explaining what they entail, that person clapped their hands in admiration and said, "What a kind and thoughtful teaching this is! If one were to follow this teaching well..."
