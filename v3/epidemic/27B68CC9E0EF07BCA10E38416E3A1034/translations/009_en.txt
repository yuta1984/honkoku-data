One: People in the world believe that Teriaka (Theriaca/Theriac) is made with Hanpi (viper/mamushi snake) and consider it a miraculous medicine for smallpox, using it indiscriminately. This is a mistake. What is brought from the Netherlands these days is all five-ingredient Teriaka, which contains no Hanpi. While it is true that the genuine nine-ingredient formula does contain Hanpi, it is rarely imported. Moreover, Teriaka is a Dutch remedy for colds and coughs. According to Dutch physicians, it was occasionally used in the past to help bring out the smallpox eruption, but is no longer used for that purpose today. Upon examining its formula,

Alas, what can this mean? Smallpox has conditions of deficiency and excess, and people have varying degrees of strength and weakness. Whether to purge or to supplement depends on the symptoms.
