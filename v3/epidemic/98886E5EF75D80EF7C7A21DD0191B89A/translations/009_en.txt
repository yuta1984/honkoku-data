Thus, as the foremost medicines for expelling children's afflictions (kan no mushi), the ancient imperial remedy Shinjugan, the Kasu kan medicine (Kiōgan), Kyūmeigan, yanagi no mushi (willow worm remedy), red frog medicine, and all other miraculous medicines known throughout the world agreed among themselves to protect the bodies of children. Each hastening along their path of efficacy, they rushed forward one after another. However, the kan no mushi (convulsion spirits), hōzuki mushi (ground cherry bug spirits), the plague god, and the cold god all conspired to prevent medicines from entering the children's bodies — making children's fevers burn fiercely so that the children would cry out "I don't want medicine! I don't want it!" Because of this, even the parents could not manage to force open their children's mouths to make them swallow the medicines.
