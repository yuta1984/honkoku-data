《Smallpox Relief》 The Celebration of Sasa-yu

The author of this book hails from the foot of Yunoo Pass in Echizen Province, and is the eldest grandson of a truly "light" (frivolous/carefree) fellow. His wife is a "light woman" from Karuizawa, making them both a thoroughly lighthearted couple. For this reason, he cheerfully accepted the publisher's request without hesitation, and taking up his rouge-brush with the intent to magnificently pile up a mountain of red-covered books, he rose to the task, dashed it off at a quick pace, and auspiciously sets it down here.

　[Written as] a wondrous remedy for smallpox,
　　On a spring day of the Year of the Rabbit, upon hearing the first thunder,
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Both in spirit and in fortune,
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　A "light" author — Shunsui, composed
