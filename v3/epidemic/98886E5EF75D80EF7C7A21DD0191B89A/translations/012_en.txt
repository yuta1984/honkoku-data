• From the month that the mother becomes pregnant, she must not eat pickled plums, vinegared sushi, or anything whatsoever that carries a sour, vinegary quality.

• She must not eat so much as a bite of wild goose, duck, kashiwa (chicken), or any other fowl, nor any fish that is particularly rich in oil.

• While nursing, she must avoid all harmful foods, just as during pregnancy.
— This is the very foundation of raising a child in good health.

• Before a child's smallpox erupts, just as the mother cut out harmful foods, vinegar should be known as the foremost poison.

• Always administer worm-purging medicines and medicines to expel fetal toxins, and even when the child appears healthy, do not be negligent morning or evening.

• In the period before smallpox erupts, it is frequently good to have the child eat rabbit. It is also greatly beneficial to have the child drink rabbit's blood.

• Boil pike conger eel (hamo) in water, and with that water, pour it over the smallpox-anticipated child from head to toe, washing them so that the water penetrates thoroughly to the tips of the hands and feet. If this is done once a month from birth, the child will never suffer smallpox for the rest of their life.
