[Main Text]

[Upper left folio]
Now then — renowned as the greatest warrior in all of Japan, the Great Deity Chinzei Hachirō Tametomo is a divine god who protects people from the severity of smallpox. In households that have preserved and passed down an arrowhead of Tametomo, it is guaranteed that smallpox will be mild. Here, at the Handa shrine in Kasai Karumachi, enshrined as the Tenmeijin (Heavenly Bright Deity), the divine vow is that smallpox shall be mild and measles shall be mild. One day, Tametomo traveled all the way to this shrine in Kasai Karumachi and spoke to the Great Deity Inari, saying: "Of late, the spread of smallpox knows no distinction of spring, summer, autumn, or winter — past or present — and while this may be well enough for the world at large, it brings great suffering to children. Have you, unlike in former times, grown negligent in tending to the matter of smallpox? The smallpox deities have been growing far too bold. I have half a mind to take aim with my arrows and drive every last one of those smallpox gods away — but I came first to consult with you. What say you?" ⨁

[Lower right folio]
⨁Upon hearing this, Lord Inari said: "Indeed, you are entirely right. I shall straightaway instruct my retinue to summon the smallpox gods,

[Lower left folio]
gather them together, and give them a thorough reprimand. Please grant them a brief reprieve for the moment." How very kind of Lord Inari, to plead on behalf of the smallpox gods! After that, the Inari deity of Kasai Karumachi called together the fox messengers in service and sent them running in every direction to summon the smallpox gods. Upon hearing of this matter, the smallpox deities who had been scattered here and there all came rushing, one after another, to gather before the Inari deity of Kasai Karumachi. →[continued on next page]
