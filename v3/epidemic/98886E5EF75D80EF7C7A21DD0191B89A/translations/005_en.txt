→[continued] …Having thus gathered together, Lord Inari came forth to that assembly with unhurried dignity, and turning to face the smallpox gods, said: "Now then, hear me, all of you. On this occasion, the Great Deity Tametomo has gone to the trouble of coming here in person, declaring that if your conduct is not set right, he shall put you to the point of his arrows. None of you would fare well facing Tametomo's arrows. From this day forward, let it be understood by all present that smallpox must be made mild — without fail. ▼▲
