【Right Sheet, Upper Section】

Having watched over and cared for the children, the efficacy of the wondrous medicine proved excellent, and the twelve-day count passed in the blink of an eye. All went well with the world — in every village there were no misfortunes, and the celebratory sasayu (bamboo-grass bath) festivities were held merrily. The joy of countless tens of thousands of children and the relief of their parents were immeasurable; they held the smallpox gods in great esteem, offered them feasts, and sent them on their way. So eager were some households to benefit from a light case of smallpox that they immediately invited the gods in for their next child as well — and so children
