【Right Sheet, Upper Section】
"It is not through any wrongdoing on the part of the smallpox gods that misfortune befalls the children. It is because of the children's chronic ailments, and because of negligence in the parents' way of raising them. The smallpox gods, each and every one, wish to see the smallpox run its proper, successful course in the children, and through that achievement to be revered and honored by the parents, and to joyously receive the celebration of the sasa-yu bath —

【Left Sheet, Upper Section】
— and to be celebrated by all. Such is the heart of the gods. Therefore, we humbly ask that you forgive whatever poor opinion you may have held of the smallpox gods until now, and instead declare to the parents of children that they are lacking in the least measure of care — not even one in a thousand — in the effort to make the smallpox light and manageable. Although it may appear that they are always tending to their children, in their excessive affection they allow the children to eat things that are harmful; they do not take proper heed when the child shows signs of mushi-ke (symptoms of worm-spirits) in ordinary times; they give no medicines, apply no moxa, leave things unattended, and allow chronic disease to accumulate within the viscera — and then, when smallpox takes hold, they suddenly fly into a panic. This, time and again, is the very nature of their negligence. For this reason, we have consulted with the smallpox gods on this occasion and have set down in writing the measures by which smallpox in the world may be managed without mishap. We humbly ask that you by all means…"

【Left Sheet, Lower Section】
"…command the parents to observe and abide by these measures carefully, so that children who are born into this world may be spared from suffering." — And with those words, the written document was produced and presented.
