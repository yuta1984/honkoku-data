→[continued] …What I speak of is, as you well know, that depending on the season at the time, the poison received within the womb comes out [through the smallpox]. Therefore, once the illness has run its course and is done with, there is no danger of that fetal toxin transforming into various other ailments later in life. For this reason, it is fortunate indeed that parents choose to have their children go through smallpox. If we [the smallpox gods] ensure it passes lightly, then the parents and

[Upper left folio]
siblings of the household will also be greatly relieved and grateful. Thus, we are not in the least bit negligent when it comes to carefully protecting the children. However, when the proper care before [and during] the smallpox fails to reach the child due to the parents' own carelessness, to then blame this on our negligence — that is more unreasonable and willful on the part of the parents than on the part of the smallpox child." Just as they were thus making their excuses, the Great Deity Tametomo stepped forward with bold and gallant bearing, fixed the smallpox gods with a stern glare, and declared: "No, no — those excuses shall not be accepted." →[continued on next page]
