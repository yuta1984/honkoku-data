"Shabyō Ruiki" (A Classified Record of Sha Disease)

Compiled by Oku Hirotaka of the I Domain

The ninth month of the year Tsuchinoto-Uma (1858), fifth year of the Ansei era

Item: This current epidemic disease is a type of heat-toxin illness, and treatment with ginseng, aconite, and other warming and tonifying medicines is not appropriate. This can be seen in Chinese medical texts as well as in the book titled "Korera-byō Ron" (Treatise on Cholera Disease), which has been lent out for distribution on this occasion, however...
