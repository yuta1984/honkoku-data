One must not indiscriminately use all manner of pills and powdered medicines. However, bear bile (*kuma no i*) may be used without concern.

The common people of Amakusa District in Hizen Province never contract smallpox. For this reason, they fear smallpox even more than they fear snakes and scorpions. In the rare event that someone among them falls ill, they sever the bonds of parent and child and kinsmen alike, and abandon the sick person in the mountains or fields. Furthermore, when smallpox begins to spread in a nearby district, great numbers of people gather, raise a battle cry, beat gongs and drums, and fire guns to drive it away. If there is smallpox in a neighboring province,

**[Left page]**
they halt ships and cut off all passage. In this way, there are places throughout Japan that avoid smallpox and remain free from it. From this, we can see that smallpox is not necessarily something that cannot be avoided.

In my view, if one avoids the epidemic miasma (*ekiki*) when it spreads, even the latent poison (*idoku*) inherited from one's parents will not be stirred into action. And if the latent poison is resolved in advance, the epidemic miasma will not be able to invade. It should be clearly understood: smallpox is above all a disease that must be prevented beforehand.
