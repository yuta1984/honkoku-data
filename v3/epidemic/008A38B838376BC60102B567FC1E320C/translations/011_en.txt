○ When the pox eruptions have burst and ulcerated, and pus fluid is flowing out without drying, finely powdered azuki beans should be dusted over them. Buckwheat flour is also suitable. ○ Even if something is called a "wondrous medicine or extraordinary remedy" for smallpox, it must not be used recklessly. There are many cases in which Kiō-gan (奇応丸), Kyūmei-gan (救命丸), and common bear bile (kumanoi) have been used and caused harm. Great caution is required.

○ In ordinary households, narwhal horn (ichiкaku / "unicorn horn") and rhinoceros horn (saikaku) have been handed down as wondrous remedies for smallpox, and they are applied indiscriminately without distinguishing cold or heat, deficiency or excess patterns, causing no small amount of harm. Great caution is required.
