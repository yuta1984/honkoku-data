By virtue of the remarkable efficacy of this method in preserving the lives of the people, in the fourth month of the tenth year of the Jiaqing era [1805], a brig transported infants from the Philippines, bringing the cowpox vaccine to Macao (支那/China). Physicians from the home country (Spain) collaborated with physicians in Macao and carried out cultivation and inoculation in accordance with the method. This is identical to what is stated in the preface written by Master Qiu himself, with no discrepancy in year or month whatsoever. As for China, the starting point is the fourth month of the second year of Bunka, the year *Itsuchū* [1805]. This is now forty-six years ago. After another four or five years had passed, merely three copies were brought [to Japan], but the work had still not spread widely into the world. In the same year of *Kōshin* [1844], I, Keichoku, went to study at the academy of Master Hoashi of Hiji in Bungo Province. Prior to this, a fellow student from the same province, Sano Tōshun of Kitsuki in Bungo, had been studying under Master Taki in Edo, and during his time there had made a hand-copied transcription of this work, which he kept as a treasured private possession. He happened to show it to [Master Hoashi], who read it and deemed it highly praiseworthy, and thereupon had Keichoku copy it and read it as well.
