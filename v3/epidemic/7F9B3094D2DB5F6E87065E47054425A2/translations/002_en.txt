Supplement to Sanka Shinsho (New Writings on Scattered Flowers)

Dictated by Master Naniwa Hōsetsu of Bizen Province　Recorded in writing by disciple Hayashi Osamu of Hizen Province

　　　　Questions and Answers

Question: It is said that in ancient times there were no persons who suffered from smallpox. Around what period did it begin to spread in our country and in other nations?

Answer: In our country,
it was transmitted from overseas in the seventh year of the Tenpyō era, the year Kinoto-I (乙亥), during the reign of Emperor Shōmu (which was 1,116 years ago from the time of writing). It first arose in Tsukushi (Kyushu) and then spread throughout the entire country. The Shoku Nihongi states: "In the seventh year, the year Kinoto-I, on the day Hinoe-Uma (丙午) of the eighth month, epidemic sores (smallpox) broke out extensively throughout the provinces under the jurisdiction of the Dazaifu, and the common people were completely bedridden. On the day Mizunoe-Tora (壬寅) of the twelfth month, harvests had not been plentiful throughout the year; from summer through winter, throughout the realm…"
