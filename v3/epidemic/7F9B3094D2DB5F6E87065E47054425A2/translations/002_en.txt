# Supplement to Sanka Shinsho (New Writings on Scattered Blossoms)

Dictated by Master Namba Hōsetsu of Bizen Province; Recorded by disciple Hayashi Osamu of Hizen Province

## Questions and Answers

**Question:** It is said that in ancient times there were no people who suffered from smallpox. From approximately what period did the disease begin to spread in our country and in other nations?

**Answer:** In our country, it was transmitted from overseas in the seventh year of the Tenpyō era (乙亥, the year of the Wood Boar), during the reign of Emperor Shōmu — which is 1,116 years prior to the present day. The disease originated in Tsukushi (Kyushu) and spread throughout the entire land. The *Shoku Nihongi* (Chronicles of Japan, Continued) states: "In the eighth month, day Heigo [丙午], of the seventh year of Tenpyō (乙亥), a great epidemic of pox disease broke out across the provinces under the jurisdiction of the Dazaifu, and the common people all fell ill.
