【Record of Newly Developed Fields under Koyanagawa Shuri's Jurisdiction】
(Esashi District)

Item: 4 kan 115 mon — Notezaki Village
　　Note: In the second month of Kanbun 9 (1669), a request was submitted via written document by the Deiri-shū (administrative officials) during the tenure of the previous incumbent Nakatsukasa-dai, concerning newly cultivated paddy fields of 1 chō 4 tan in wild lowland (field), and 15 chō of upland fields. The land survey (竿入) was conducted in the spring of Enpō 1 (1673).

Item: 6 kan 542 mon — Same Village (Notezaki)
　　This concerns excess land (起過) beyond the acreage recorded in the written document for the same wild lowland area: paddy fields of 9 tan 4 se 4 bu, and upland fields of 31 chō 2 tan 9 se 9 bu. The land survey was conducted in the same year as above.

　Combined total for two entries: 10 kan 657 mon

As the above newly developed field records (起目) have been officially issued, from the year of the formal land survey (御竿入) as recorded in the official register (御本牒), the land (地形) shall be transferred accordingly. Those who have been granted newly developed fields valued at 1 kan mon or more shall be instructed to prepare the official seal documents (御黒印).
