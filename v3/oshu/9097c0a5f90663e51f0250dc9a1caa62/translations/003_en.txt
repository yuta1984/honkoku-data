After deliberation, in this August [the matter was] presented [to the lord] in Edo by Shuri and Furuuchi Zōsuke, and it has been settled before His Lordship that [the assessed value] be added to the stipend (知行高). As this matter has been concluded, we hereby inform you accordingly. That is all.

　　　　　　Kenmotsu
Enpō 3, Year of the Rabbit (1675)
　　November 23rd
　　　　　　Shuri

　　　　　　Nakatsukasa

　　To: Wada Han'nosuke
　　To: Tamura Zuisho
　　To: Kagami Uneme

As stated above, regarding the new field registrations (shinden kitome) of Koyanagawa Shuri, the assessed value (daiko) as determined by the official survey (go-saoiri) shall be added to the stipend holdings (chigyōdaka).
