【Shima no Takoto (Affairs of Shima)】

She has been in service since before the birth of the Lady Mother, and has served diligently for thirty-six years up to the present. While she may fall short in some respects as a senior female attendant (rōjo), she is the only person who has continuously served since the time of the birth. In particular, in recent years, the senior female attendants have not handled the personal funds (o-temoto-kin), and the gift monies (o-rekin) have become considerably insufficient; however, it is largely through her efforts and merits that the current financial management has been maintained.

She has continued her extraordinary dedication and hard work without interruption since the birth of the Lady. In recognition of her exceptional service, it is hoped that, from the eleventh day of the coming New Year's month, even a modest increase in stipend (fuchi) be granted to her, so that she may devote herself with even greater diligence and attend to the reserve funds. That said, in recent years, the staff of the inner chambers has also changed, and various additional burdens have made things increasingly difficult. This matter should be further examined and communicated accordingly.
