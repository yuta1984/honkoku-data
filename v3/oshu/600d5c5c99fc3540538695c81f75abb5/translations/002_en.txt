I have had the honor of reviewing your letter. Regarding the recent announcement of the appointment of an adopted heir, you presented a sword and a gift of money in lieu of a horse as congratulatory offerings through a messenger. Upon my conveying this to the relevant party, the messenger was granted an audience, and the matter proceeded most favorably. I humbly and respectfully write this.

　　　Tōyama Tachiwaki

3rd day of the 3rd month　　　(Monogram/Seal)


To: Koyanagiwa Jōnosuke-dono
