【Left folio】
Regarding Koyanagawa (Nakatsukasa): he has reached the age of thirty-three and has no male heir, but has one daughter aged three. We humbly request that Sanzaburō, the natural younger brother of Arai Gonnosuke of Kasahara Naiki's unit, aged eighteen this year, be received as a son-in-law and adoptive heir to said daughter, and that in due course the stipend of Nakatsukasa—eighty kan of rice land—be granted to said Sanzaburō, and that he likewise be invested with the ranks of御一家 (go-ikka) and 御座敷 (go-zashiki). As for the relationship of said Sanzaburō: he is a cousin two degrees removed on Nakatsukasa's side, and two degrees removed on Sanzaburō's side. Nakatsukasa has been making this request little by little since last winter, but in recent days his condition has been far from favorable; and although he has been taking various restorative measures, his condition has gradually worsened to the point of being a matter of life and death, which is why this petition is being submitted at this time.
