【Right folio】
Regarding the desire to install Sejirō-sama as an adoptive heir (the second son of Ayakai Hyōgo-sama), a message was sent through Ayakai Tarōhei-sama. However, the reply came back that both the younger brother of Takasumi Mokunoijō and the younger brother of Arai Gonnosuke were closer in kinship (to Asaburō-sama) than Sejirō-sama, and that it would not be appropriate to pass over them in favor of my own second son. As Takasumi Mokunoijō-sama was residing in his home domain, consultation with him was not possible. It was therefore decided that Sanzaburō-sama, the younger brother of Arai Gonnosuke-sama, would be installed as the adoptive heir. A request was accordingly sent through Tarōhei-sama for Gonnosuke-sama to attend, and on the morning of the 22nd, Gonnosuke-sama came forth, and the consultation with Itō Nakatsukasa-sama was concluded. From our side (Koyanagawa), a joint petition signed by Izumo-sama and Genba-sama was submitted to the Senior Councilors (Shurō).
