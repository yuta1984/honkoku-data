5th month of Shōtoku 2 (1712)
Records from the beginning of Lord Kyūnosuke's administration

Item: As there is official business to be conveyed, please attend at Shima's residence on the morning of the 15th at the Hour of the Fifth Bell (approximately 8 a.m.). Arai Gonnosuke and Ayu(ka)i Jirōhei are also requested to attend. For those who are ill or residing in the countryside, a representative should be sent in their place.
That is all.
　　14th day of the 5th month

　　Copy of the official pronouncement

Regarding Tade Kyūnosuke: he has been graciously arranged to marry the daughter of Koyanagawa Nakatsukasa and take her as his son-in-law and adopted heir. His stipend (chigyo-daka) shall remain unchanged, and he shall succeed to the family name and heirship. He is to be treated without change as a member of the honorable family [御一家].
