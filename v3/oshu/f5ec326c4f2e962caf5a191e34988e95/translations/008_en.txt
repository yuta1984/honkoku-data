A copy of the notice conveyed at the castle in Genroku 8 (1695), through Lord Tōyama Tatewaki and Lord Tomitsuka Nagato:

　　　Murata Shima
　　　Same [clan]: Ichirō
　　　Same [clan]: Jōnosuke
　　Representative of Koyanagawa Shuri:
　　　　Yamaoka Sōuemon
Regarding Koyanagawa Innosuke, [who was appointed] to succeed Lord Itō Saburō —

[Left folio]
— as heir, and accordingly, Murata Jōnosuke has been appointed as heir to Koyanagawa [Innosuke's former position].
This is the substance of the honorable pronouncement.
