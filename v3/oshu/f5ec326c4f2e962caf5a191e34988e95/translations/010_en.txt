(Regarding the matter of Tade Kyūnosuke:) Although a petition had been submitted requesting that Arai Gonnosuke's younger brother, Sanzaburō, be accepted as son-in-law and adopted heir, said Sanzaburō has no legitimate blood connection to the Koyanagawa family, and furthermore his lineage is too distant. As the Koyanagawa family is of exceptional standing, and given that there had been certain precedents set through the late lord Sukezaburō to establish proper order according to established norms, in order to prevent the continuation of the family line from being severed, the matter has been decided as stated above, taking all circumstances into consideration.
