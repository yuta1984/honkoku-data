Item: Regarding the ceremonial items for the wedding celebration, the portion needed in the immediate term will be arranged by Mino-no-kami. It would also seem appropriate to have arrangements made for the household kitchen and catering utensils, as well as items to be distributed to the ladies-in-waiting, their chambers, and attendants' rooms. Respectfully submitted.


　　3rd day of the 9th month　　Inaba Iori
