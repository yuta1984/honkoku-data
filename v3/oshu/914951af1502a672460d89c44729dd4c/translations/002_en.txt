I have had the honor of reading your letter. Regarding this occasion, in which you have bestowed upon the Koyanagawa house the succession of the family, granted the stipend of eight hundred koku of land along with the entire household without any change, and commanded that a marriage be arranged with the daughter of the late Nakatsukasa — these are most gratifying and welcome tidings, and entirely proper. In recognition of this honor, I have dispatched a messenger to present a ceremonial sword and horse money as a gift of thanks. The contents of the letter have been duly conveyed in full. Furthermore, that the messenger was granted an audience is a matter of especial good fortune. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　Shibata Geki

The 19th day of the 10th month　　　Giri [Monogram/Kaō]

To: Koyanagawa Jūrō
