**Record of Audience with the Shogun (Kubō-sama)**

**[Upper Section]**

Item: On the 27th day of the first month of Enpō 2 (1674), the Year of the Tiger, an order was issued from Lord Inaba Mino-no-kami to Shimada Izumo-no-kami, who in turn sent a letter to the lord (head of the Yanagawa Date family), informing him that Date Aki and Koyanagawa Shuri were commanded to have an audience with the Shogun the following morning.

Item: On the 28th, at the upper part of the Hour of the Rabbit (approximately 6:00 AM), accompanied by Lord Aki, and dressed in a small short sword (kodachi), noshime (a formal patterned robe), and long formal hakama, they proceeded to the castle guided by Miyoshi Sukuzaemon, who was then serving as kikiban (duty officer). The lord also arrived at the castle slightly earlier than his usual attendance. Through the introduction of Lord Oki-no-kami, the formal audience chamber was entered.

The gifts presented to the Shogun consisted of one set of kosode (formal robes) and silver (in lieu of a horse). The audience took place at the lower part of the Hour of the Snake (approximately 10:00 AM). The official who announced the audience was Lord Honda Nagato-no-kami.

After the audience concluded, the lord expressed his gratitude on behalf of both persons for being granted the audience, and then immediately made courtesy calls to the Senior Councillors (Rōjū) and the Junior Councillors (Wakadoshiyori). Shuri also proceeded directly from the castle to call upon Senior Councillor Kawachi-no-kami, Junior Councillor Date Tōtōmi-no-kami, Shimada Izumo-no-kami, Ōi Shinuemon, Lord Shinagawa, and Takagi Chūuemon, before returning directly to the residence and presenting his respects to the lord.

In return, messengers were sent from the Senior Councillors — Lord Utagashira, Lord Mino-no-kami, Lord Yamato-no-kami, Lord Tajima, and Lord Harima — bearing congratulatory replies to the lord.

**[Lower Section]**

From Lord Mino-no-kami, Lord Yamato-no-kami, Lord Tajima, and Lord Harima, congratulatory acknowledgments were also extended directly to Shuri. From Lord Kawachi-no-kami and Lord Noto-no-kami, written letters of congratulation were received.
