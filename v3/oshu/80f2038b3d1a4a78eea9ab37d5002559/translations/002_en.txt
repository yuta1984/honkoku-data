This is a record of grain storage documents (mikura kakitsuke) mediated by Lord Iwabuchi Mohee.

　　　Concerning Lord Koyanagawa Shuri

Item: 17 kan 94 mon — Esashi Notezaki Village
　　Note: On the 10th day of the 2nd month of Manji 3 (1660), following the formal presentation by Lord Moniwa Suō and Lord Tomitsuka Kuranojō, the official document was issued.

Issued as a written entry in the official document granting the designation of newly reclaimed fields (shinden okoshime).

Item: 10 kan 657 mon — Same village
　　Note: On the 23rd day of the 11th month of Enpō 3 (1675), an official document was issued by Lord Nakatsukasa, Lord Shuri, and Lord Kenmotsu.
Same handling as above.

Item: 176 mon — Same village
　　Note: On the 21st day of the 3rd month of Enpō 5 (1677), following the formal presentation by Lord Shuri and Lord Kenmotsu, an official document was issued by Lord Nakatsukasa.
