**Ordinance (Sadame)**

Item: Hanzaburō of Kitame Village, Kurokawa District
For the crime of illicit relations (mitsūtsū), both the man and woman shall be beheaded on the spot.

Item: Kinzō of Fukatani Kita Village, Monou District, and the subordinate Ichizō
These two engaged in illicit relations, and the husband, upon discovering this, rightfully struck (Ichizō). Following the husband's petition, the husband was pardoned (for his act of violence). However, when Ichizō subsequently attempted illicit relations again, the woman refused consent, and he wounded her. This offense is considered graver than the original act of illicit relations. Therefore, while crucifixion (haritsuke) would normally apply in such a case, in consideration of the woman having once consented, the sentence is reduced to execution by beheading and three years of imprisonment.

Item: Kokichi of Yanagida Village, Isawa District
For the crime of horse theft, the sentence is beheading.
Note: As it was determined that three years of imprisonment would be difficult to sustain, and the question arose of whether to apply crucifixion, after deliberation and further consideration, the sentence of beheading was decided. This was not the originally intended judgment.

Item: Mokuzaemon of Shioji, Fukatani, Monou District, and the subordinate Sannosuke
For the crime of murdering Mokuzaemon's female servant, (Sannosuke) shall be beheaded.

The above matters have been deliberated repeatedly, and with the agreement of Shibata Nakatsukasa and Daijō Kenmotsū, the decisions have been finalized. Those who have given their agreement shall carry out the executions accordingly. Thus it is written.

Enpō Year 4 (1676)
　November　(Monogram/Kaō)

　　　　　To Lord Koyanagawa Shuri

Regarding the execution of punishment for the aforementioned individuals: should anyone have even the slightest concern about any matter, they should report it without hesitation.
