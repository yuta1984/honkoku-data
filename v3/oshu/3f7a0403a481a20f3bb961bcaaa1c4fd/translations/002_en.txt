Concerning Tade Inosuke, the blood younger brother of Lord Date Sukesaburō: he has been newly granted a stipend of eighty kanmon of chigyo (enfeoffment), and has been ordered to take the surname "Koyanagawa." Accordingly, he is to be entered into the official register (hondei), and the distribution is to be made from the current land yield (tōmotonari), with the draft of the vermillion seal document (goshuin) to be prepared and duly communicated. However, since Koyanagawa Kinojō had passed away without having submitted a petition for an adopted heir during his lifetime, all associated rights and properties had been subject to forfeiture (torishubushi). Nevertheless, as the Koyanagawa family is of distinguished lineage, and furthermore given the connections and relations with Lord Hizen, Tade Inosuke is to succeed to the Koyanagawa surname and continue the family line...
