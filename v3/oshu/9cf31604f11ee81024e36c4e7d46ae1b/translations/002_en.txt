**[Upper Section]**
Record dated the 22nd day of the 5th month, Genroku 8 (1695)

Concerning the succession (katoku) of Lord Innosuke and Lord Sukesaburō, the following order was issued. A copy of the written notice is recorded below.

"It is hereby ordered, without dispute, that Koyanagawa Innosuke shall succeed to the estate of Lord Date Sukesaburō." This was the lord's (藩主's) expressed will.

Regarding the above matter, Lord Tōyama Tatewaki and Lord Tomizuka Nagato visited Lord Sukesaburō's residence on the 22nd day of the 5th month to convey this order, and it is noted that soup (suimono) and other refreshments were served.

On the same day — the 22nd day of the 5th month of the same year — it was ordered that Lord Murata Jōnosuke would succeed to the position of Lord Innosuke. A copy is recorded below.

　　　　　　　Murata Shima
　　　　　　　(same clan) Ichirō
　　　　　　　(same clan) Jōnosuke

**[Lower Section]**
　　　　Acting on behalf of Koyanagawa Shuri:
　　　　　　　Yamaoka Chūemon

The above four persons received this written notice at the castle.

"As Koyanagawa Innosuke has been appointed to succeed Lord Date Sukesaburō, Murata Jōnosuke is hereby appointed to succeed to the position of Koyanagawa." This was the lord's expressed will.
