Kobayakawa Heizō expressed a strong desire to receive the tomoe crest used on our (household's) curtains. In response, on the 19th day of the 11th month of Enkyō 4, since he bears the same family name (Yanagawa), the left-facing tomoe crest was granted to him. As our household's crest is the right-facing tomoe, the left-facing tomoe was given to him so that the two crests may be distinguished from one another in perpetuity. This is hereby recorded and retained.

　19th day, 11th month, Enkyō 4 　　Moritoki

　　Regarding the messenger dispatched to convey the crest: as the individual has served this household continuously and holds the position of yōnin-yaku (chief administrator/aide), Shiraiva Shinzaemon was sent as the messenger in this matter.
