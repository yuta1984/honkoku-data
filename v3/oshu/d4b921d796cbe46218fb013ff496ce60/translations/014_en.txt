...(Xuanzang) caused the texts of the Tian, Ba, Zang, and the Three Baskets to surge forth like waves upon the sea of words. Thereupon, from all the countries he had traversed, he brought back the essential texts of the Tripiṭaka — in total six hundred and fifty-seven fascicles — translated and disseminated them throughout China, and proclaimed and spread their magnificent teachings. He drew the compassionate clouds from the far West and poured down the rain of the Dharma upon the East. Where the sacred teachings had been incomplete, he made them whole again; the sins of the common people were transformed and returned to blessings.
