...grieving at the (Dharma's) decline, he devoted his mind to the profound gate of Buddhist teaching, lamenting the errors and distortions that had crept into the deep scriptures. He wished to separate the branches and analyze the principles, to spread widely the teachings transmitted by his predecessors, to cut away the false and continue the true, and to open the path for those who would study after him. Thus, he lifted his heart toward the Pure Land (of India), and set forth on a journey to the Western Regions. Braving dangers, he traveled far; staff in hand, he marched on alone. Through accumulated snows he flew forward at dawn; along the way, treacherous terrain threatened to swallow the road beneath him; startling sandstorms rose at dusk; beyond the vault of the sky, even heaven seemed lost in the boundless expanse. Crossing mountains and rivers spanning ten thousand li, he pressed onward through mists and haze, his solitary figure advancing through layer upon layer of cold and heat...
