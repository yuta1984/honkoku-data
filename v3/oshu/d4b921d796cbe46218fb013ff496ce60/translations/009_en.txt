Who, then, could harbor any doubt or confusion about its import? (Surely none.) And yet, the arising of the Great Teaching (Buddhism) was rooted in the western lands (India); it soared across to the Han court, illuminating a wondrous dream with its brilliance, and flowed eastward across the domains of the East, spreading compassion far and wide. In the days of old, when (the Buddha) manifested in different forms and traces across the world, transformation was accomplished even before words could travel. In that age when he constantly appeared and constantly dwelt among the world, the people looked up to his virtue and naturally came to know how to follow it.
