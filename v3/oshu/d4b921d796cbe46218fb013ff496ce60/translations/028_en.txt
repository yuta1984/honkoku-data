...and likewise no exhaustion thereof. And so forth, up to: there is no aging and death, and likewise no exhaustion of aging and death. There is no suffering, no origin of suffering, no cessation of suffering, no path. There is no wisdom, and likewise nothing to be attained. Because there is nothing to be attained, the bodhisattva, relying on the Prajñāpāramitā, has no hindrance in the mind. Because there is no hindrance, there is no fear. Being far removed from inverted views and delusive thinking, one reaches ultimate Nirvāṇa. All Buddhas of the three ages, relying on the Prajñāpāramitā, attain Anuttarā...
