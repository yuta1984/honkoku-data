Its scope is vast and far-reaching; its abstruse meaning is profoundly deep. It encompasses the subtle essence of both Emptiness (śūnyatā) and Existence, and embodies the crucial workings of arising and ceasing (birth and extinction). Its words are rich and its Way is boundless — those who seek it cannot exhaust its source. Though its text appears evident, its meaning is hidden — those who tread its path cannot fathom its ultimate limits.
