...is itself emptiness, and emptiness is itself form. Sensation, perception, mental formations, and consciousness are likewise the same. Śāriputra, all phenomena in their empty nature are neither born nor extinguished, neither defiled nor pure, neither increasing nor decreasing. Therefore, within emptiness, there is no form, no sensation, perception, mental formations, or consciousness. There is no eye, ear, nose, tongue, body, or mind. There is no form, sound, smell, taste, touch, or mental object. There is no realm of the eye, and so forth, up to and including no realm of consciousness. There is no ignorance, and likewise no exhaustion of ignorance.
