We humbly reflect that His Majesty the Emperor, drawing upon the blessings bestowed by High Heaven, governs the eight wildernesses (八荒, hakkō — i.e., the entire realm) while at rest with folded hands; his virtue reaches the common people (黔黎, kenrei), and the ten thousand nations come to pay court with their robes gathered respectfully; his benevolence extends even to the stone chambers of crumbling □ [character unclear], where the texts written on palm leaves (貝葉, baiyō — Sanskrit scriptures) return; his grace reaches down to the smallest insects and creatures, and the golden caskets (金匱, kinki) carry forth the gāthās of the Buddhist teachings.
