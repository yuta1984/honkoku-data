Even the wise and sagacious rarely exhaust [the full extent of] these numbers. Yet Heaven and Earth, though they encompass Yin and Yang, are easily understood—because they possess visible form. Yin and Yang, though dwelling within Heaven and Earth, are difficult to fully comprehend—because they have no form. Thus it is known that what manifests in visible form can be verified, and even the foolish will not be deceived. But what lies hidden without form cannot be perceived, and even the wise will lose their way. How much more so, then, with the Buddhist Way, which venerates the Void, rides upon the mysterious and profound, governs the realm of perfect stillness, broadly saves all the myriad living beings, and presides over the ten directions of the universe...
