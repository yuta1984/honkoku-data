...Samyaksaṃbodhi. Therefore, know that the Prajñāpāramitā is the great divine mantra, the great mantra of illumination, the unsurpassed mantra, the mantra beyond all comparison. It truly removes all suffering and is not false. Therefore, the mantra of the Prajñāpāramitā is proclaimed. The mantra is spoken thus:

　　　　Pāragate
