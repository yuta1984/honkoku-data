Even plants and trees — beings without knowledge or awareness — can, by relying upon what is good, achieve goodness. How much more so, then, should those of human society, possessed of understanding and discernment, seek good fortune by aligning themselves with auspicious conditions rather than seeking it without such reliance? It is our earnest hope that this sutra will be disseminated and put into practice widely, flowing on without end alongside the sun and the moon, and that its blessings will spread to the farthest reaches, growing eternally great together with heaven and earth.

【The following is Taizong's "Edict in Reply to the Letter of Thanks" (勅答謝啓)】
I, your sovereign, am deficient in talent — far short of the virtue of jade and gem — and my words fall short of broad and penetrating learning. As for the Inner Scriptures (Buddhist texts) in particular, they are what I have been least versed in. The preface I composed recently was exceedingly crude and clumsy, and I fear only that it has defiled [the sacred teachings]...
