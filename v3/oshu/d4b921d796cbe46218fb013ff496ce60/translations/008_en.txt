Raise up its awesome spiritual power, and there is nothing above it; press down its divine force, and there is nothing below it. Extend it to the great, and it pervades the entire cosmos; reduce it to the small, and it embraces even the finest hair's-breadth. There is neither extinction nor birth; passing through a thousand kalpas, it does not grow old. Whether hidden or manifest, it carries the hundred blessings and endures forever in the present. The wondrous Way crystallizes in profound mystery — follow it, and none can know its furthest limit. The flow of the Dharma runs deep and still — draw from it, and none can fathom its source. Thus we know — even the teeming masses of ordinary fools, even the petty and the vulgar, if they but commit themselves to its meaning...
