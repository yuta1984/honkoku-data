[The Dharma] opens the guiding principles of the net of the Law, spreads the □teachings of the Six Pāramitās, rescues the multitude of sentient beings from their suffering in the pit of misery, and unlocks the secret gates of the Tripiṭaka. Thus it is that the [Buddha's] Name flies far without wings, and the Way stands firm forever without roots. The joy of the Way and the Name flowing and transmitting [through the ages] has remained tranquil and constant since ancient times without cease; the [Buddha's] body that responds to the call of sentient beings (感応道交, kannō-dōkō) does not decay even through kalpas as numerous as dust-motes.
