...treading through frost and rain, pressing ever forward, he truly made light of great hardships and pursued his profound vows until they were fulfilled. He traveled throughout the Western lands for seventeen years, traversing countless kingdoms and domains, inquiring and seeking after the true teachings. At the Twin Sala Trees and the Eight Waters, he savored the taste of the Way and drank in its winds; at the Deer Park and Vulture Peak, he gazed upon wondrous and extraordinary [sacred sites], received the supreme words from the sages of old, and accepted the true teachings from the worthies of high virtue. He plumbed the depths of the mysterious gate of the Dharma, and exhaustively mastered the profound secrets of its practice. The paths of the One Vehicle and the Five Precepts raced and coursed through his mind...
