【Preface to the Holy Teachings of the Tripitaka of the Great Tang】
Composed by Emperor Taizong Wen (Li Shimin)

Characters collected by the monk Huairen of Hongfu Temple,
written in the calligraphy of Wang Xizhi, General of the Right.

It is said that the Two Principles (Heaven and Earth) possess form,
and through their covering and supporting [all things], they encompass all living beings;
the Four Seasons have no form, yet through cold and heat they transform all things.
Thus, in observing Heaven and examining Earth, even the most foolish and common person can perceive their fundamental principles,
and discern the nature of Yin and Yang...
