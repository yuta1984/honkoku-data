(Finding that Central China lacked the necessary scriptures,) he sought the true texts of India, crossing the Ganges River from afar, with the lifelong aim of obtaining the "full-character" teachings (complete doctrines). He repeatedly climbed the peaks of the snow mountains and further obtained the "half-character" teachings. Traveling back and forth in pursuit of the Dharma for seventeen years in all, he became thoroughly versed in the Buddhist scriptures and devoted his heart entirely to benefiting all beings. On the sixth day of the second month of the nineteenth year of the Zhenguan reign era, acting upon imperial decree, he translated the essential texts of the Holy Teachings at Hongfu Monastery, totaling six hundred and fifty-seven fascicles.
