A new stipend of eighty kan-mon has been granted, and permission has been given to succeed to the Koyanagawa family name and headship. Furthermore, in the village of Notezaki, both the stipend and the residential estate in the countryside are hereby granted. This notification is issued in accordance with the above-stated will. As Minbu is currently in Edo, and Buzen has been temporarily excused from duties due to illness, they are unable to add their seals to this document. That is all.
