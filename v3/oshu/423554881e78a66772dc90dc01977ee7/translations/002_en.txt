To Tade Innosuke:

You are hereby ordered to succeed to the headship of the Koyanagawa family, and a stipend (shintai) of eighty kan-mon is granted to you.

(Reason) Since Koyanagawa Kin'no'jō died of illness without having established an adopted heir, you are being assigned his estate and succession; and while you are by origin a member of a separate family, your meritorious service rendered to Lord Hizen (Hizen-dono) has also been taken into consideration in issuing this order.

The above is hereby declared.

The 14th day of the 3rd month
