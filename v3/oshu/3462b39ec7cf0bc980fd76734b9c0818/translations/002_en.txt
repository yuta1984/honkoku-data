【Recto】

Regarding yourself: it is His Lordship's will that you have been ordered to present your formal greetings (o-rei) for the succession of the family headship on the sixteenth of tomorrow. You are to proceed to the castle at the hour of five-and-a-half (approximately 9:00 a.m.). Respectfully submitted.


　　　The Fifteenth Day of the Seventh Month　　　　Motoatsu [monogram/kaō]
