I humbly write to inform you of the following. The oral communication (kōjōgaki) you brought yesterday was delivered in person to Lord Nanbu Daizen last evening through an intermediary.

In response, Lord Daizen conveyed the following as his reply to Lord Minonokami: "I have received the written document from your party. I will promptly send word to the local area (zaisho) to have additional new burial mounds (tsuka) constructed. However, I am concerned that if only peasants (hyakushō) are dispatched from both sides and they come into dispute on the ground, it would be problematic. I therefore believe it would be advisable for you to send approximately two lower-ranking samurai (karuzamurai). I will instruct them to complete the rear mound (nochi-tsuka) before the inspection party (okuni-mawari-shū) arrives. As the Nanbu domain is quite distant, I will send word promptly. I would also like to arrange a time to privately communicate the details to your side."

As Lord Daizen has communicated all of this to me frankly and without concealment, I relay it to you exactly as told.

As I have been instructed by Lord Minonokami to do so, I present this report accordingly.

　6th day of the 3rd month, Iinuma Niemon
　To Lord Koyanagawa Shuri
