Regarding the rice stipend each year, instructions should be given that it be handed over at the Sendai granary.

However, since Shūri-no-kata (Koyanagawa Shūri) has only recently taken up his post, we [the authors] consulted with Kenmotsu regarding the appropriateness of granting him the official stipend. Kenmotsu then presented this matter to the higher authorities, whereupon all parties agreed that the stipend should indeed be granted as Kenmotsu had reported. In accordance with this decision, the matter was conveyed to both of the御申次 (御申次衆, liaison officers).
