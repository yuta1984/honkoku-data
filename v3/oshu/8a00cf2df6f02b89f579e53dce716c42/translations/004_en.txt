After their late father (Magozaemon) died, the second son Seki Kinnosuke inherited the household, but he was frequently ill and died young. Murata Gemba's son, Nakatsukasa Tsugutake, also died early and left no heirs. The second son of Date Hizen Murao, Hisanosuke, inherited the vacant position (stipend and headship of the family). In the first year of Kyōhō (1716), when Hisanosuke returned to the Date clan, the second son of the late Ōmachi Kazue became head of the household and has served the current lord (Taishu) to this day.
