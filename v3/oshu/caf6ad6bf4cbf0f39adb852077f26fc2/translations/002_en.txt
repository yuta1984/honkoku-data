Regarding the original stipend (honchigyo-daka) of Koyanagawa Shuri:
To his existing stipend of 137 kan 94 mon,
an additional increase (go-kazō) of 162 kan 906 mon
has been granted,
bringing the total to 300 kan.
Accordingly, it is to be ordered that the original stipend register (honchō) be updated directly with the details of the newly granted land for the increase, allocated based on current yields (momonari),
and that the official seals (wari-in) be duly prepared.

However, it should be noted that when Shuri was appointed to the position of Senior Councillor (go-karō) in Kanbun 12, since the stipend associated with the post was insufficient (shōshin), he had been granted, as a salary supplement (go-yakuryō), an annual allowance (go-gōryokukin) of 250 ryō in koban gold coins and 477 koku of unpolished rice (genmai). Now that the aforementioned increase in land has been granted as described above,

it has been declared as such.
This is therefore as stated. Needless to say, the aforementioned allowance of gold coins and unpolished rice shall be withdrawn starting from the current year's portion. That is all.
