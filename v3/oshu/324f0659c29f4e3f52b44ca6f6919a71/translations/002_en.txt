**Memorandum (Oboegaki)**

Regarding the matter of notice boards (*seisatsu*) in the territory of Tamura Ukyō-no-dayū: During the tenure of Oki-no-kami, the boards had originally been erected by Ukyō-no-dayū himself. However, Okuyama Ōi submitted a claim stating that only the Christian prohibition board (*Kirishitan-fuda*) was erected by Ukyō-no-dayū himself, while the remaining boards were erected by the office of Mutsu-no-kami. Upon this occasion of replacing the boards due to a change of tenure, Ukyō-no-dayū consulted Mutsu-no-kami, asking whether it would cause any inconvenience for Mutsu-no-kami's office to erect the said boards. Ukyō-no-dayū replied that he wished to erect them himself, as had been done previously.

Mutsu-no-kami's view was that, as a matter of general policy throughout the domain, it would not be appropriate for only the Christian prohibition board to be erected by Ukyō-no-dayū's side. Furthermore, although there may be no immediate inconvenience, for the sake of the future, the remaining boards should likewise be erected by Mutsu-no-kami's office, and the register of Christian inspections (*Kirishitan aratame-chō*) should also be submitted to the magistrate's office (*bugyōsho*) from Mutsu-no-kami's side.

Having consulted Utagashira-dono regarding the above, the response was that it would be appropriate for Ukyō-no-suke to be instructed to have all boards, including the Christian prohibition board, erected under the authority of Mutsu-no-kami, and for the official register (*chōmen*) to be submitted to the magistrate's office from Mutsu-no-kami's side. The above is hereby noted.

　The 25th day of the 12th month
