## Memorandum (Oboegaki)

Item: Regarding Lord Mutsunokami having successfully received his leave of absence this time — this is a matter of great joy to all of us, and we are deeply grateful.
Above all else, you should take heart and be at ease, and it is His Lordship's (the Shogun's) express wish that you understand it as such — this is truly a matter of extraordinary grace.
From this point forward, it is of the utmost importance that you conduct all affairs with the greatest of care and prudence. Given that the Laws for Military Houses (Buke Shohatto) stipulate that "all matters shall comply with and follow the laws of Edo," the established customs passed down through generations should of course be respected as they are, but for any new matters, it would be advisable to consult with the Rōjū (Senior Councilors).

Item: Regarding the matter of the Christian sect — on the day of the formal audience, it was proclaimed that ever greater diligence should be applied in suppressing and investigating this sect.
