...that a Southern Barbarian padre (Bateren) had emerged from a village [within the domain]. Some ten years ago, when the Lords of Kōfu and Tatebayashi also consulted the Senior Councilors (Rōjū) on this matter, [the response was that] for those of lower status, they should be made to renew their temple certificates (tera tegata) and have their hereditary religious sect affiliation re-examined. As for those persons attached [to the domain] by the Shogunal government (kōgi), the question was raised as to how they should be dealt with, and the collective response was that for those from whom it is difficult to obtain written pledges (shōmon), it would be appropriate for [the domain lord] to directly convey to them the stipulations of the prohibition. This is conveyed for your information. As for the lower people, it is considered appropriate that the religious census (ninbetsu aratame) be carried out as has been done previously.

Item: Since it is entirely possible that [agents] from neighboring domains may be secretly infiltrating [your domain] and gathering intelligence on all manner of affairs, which could then reach Edo, you must not let your guard down.
