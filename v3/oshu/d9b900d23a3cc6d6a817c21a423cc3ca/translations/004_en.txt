One: Regarding the proper conduct and procedures (sahō) of the Lord of Mutsu (Mutsu no Kami-dono), I do not believe there is particular cause for concern. However, since there are matters for which there is no knowledge of prior precedent, as well as matters where customs and practices have changed since ancient times, it is difficult to make a blanket determination. Bearing in mind what would be best for all parties, I offer these thoughts as my considered opinion.

One: Concerning matters that are currently pressing, or matters that will arise at the time of the succession to his lordship — those which have been deferred due to his youth are a separate matter — but for those matters that should not be delayed, it is my view that they should be carefully considered at this time and gradually attended to whenever his lordship has leisure. This point has also been communicated in detail to the Lord of Mutsu.
