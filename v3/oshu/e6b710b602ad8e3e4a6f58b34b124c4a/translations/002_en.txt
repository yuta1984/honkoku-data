We have been informed of your illness and that your condition remains uncertain. His lordship is greatly concerned and worried about your wellbeing. In accordance with his lordship's instruction that a swift letter be sent to inquire after your condition, we write to you as such. With deep respect and reverence.

　　　Sasa Iga
11th day of the 5th month　Sadamasu (monogram/kaō)
　　　Shibata Nakatsukasa
　　　　　　Munei (monogram/kaō)

To: Koyanagawa Shuri-dono
