　　　　　　　　　　To: Yamada Naiki
　　　　　　　　　　To: Mayama Gyōbu

In accordance with the above-written document, the uncultivated wild land, comprising a combined total of 50 chō of paddy and upland fields, has been granted to Koyanagawa Nakatsukasa-no-jō as wasteland newly opened for cultivation (kōya shinden), beginning from this year for a period of four years.
