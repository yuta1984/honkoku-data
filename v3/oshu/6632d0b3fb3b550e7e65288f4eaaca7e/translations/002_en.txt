　　　　Record of Land Allocation (Chigyo-o-wari Mokuroku)

[Inspection stamp on the left:]
　Ministry of Agriculture and Commerce, November 1, Meiji 37 (1904)
　Inspection No. 13687

Item: 80 kan-mon [in assessed value], from within Notezaki Village, Esashi District

　Additionally:
　　Residential estate (i-yashiki): 1 property
　　Samurai residences (samurai yashiki): 36 properties

　In accordance with the august order that the above [lands and properties] are to be granted [to the recipient], this record is duly prepared.

　　　　　　　　　　Tōyama Tachi-no-o (Tatewaki)
　　　　　　　　　　　　　(Seal)
　　Genroku 6, the last day of the 1st month　　Yoshio (Monogram/Kaō)
