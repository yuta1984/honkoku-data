【Statement from Heibei of the Kamitsuda Residence】

Regarding the matter of having Torizō of Futsukamachi lodge at your residence on the morning of the 16th of last month — please provide a truthful account of this:

I acknowledge receipt of your inquiry. The said individual is someone who visits my residence from time to time as well. Moreover, on the 15th, as he appeared to be someone who had gone to worship at the Chōkyō Tennō shrine and was returning late, I had him stay over at my residence. I have no knowledge of any other circumstances. I hereby provide this truthful account. That is all.

　　　　　Kamitsuda Residence
　　　　　　　Heibei (seal)
　June 18th

The above account as stated by Heibei is accurate and without error. That is all.

　　　　　His uncle
　　　　　　　Zensaku (seal)
　Same month, same day
