With great deference, I humbly submit this written petition:

Regarding myself: I have been ordered since previous years to serve as the village messenger/notice distributor (murakobure) under the jurisdiction of the honorable landlord (ojitō-sama), as I have reported in my current duty. As I have stated, I have been privately suffering from extreme poverty and have had no means to sustain myself. In this situation, I have been barely managing, up until now, to serve alone in both capacities — as town messenger/notice distributor (machikobure) and as a single official servant. Now that autumn has arrived and the honorable landlord has returned to his home domain, official duties have increased greatly, and I find myself in an unavoidable and helpless situation, unable to manage both messenger duties simultaneously. Therefore, as I have reported during my current duty period, I humbly petition that I be relieved of the village messenger duty until I am removed from the town messenger duty in the next rotation, and that I be granted exemption from it until next spring. The details are as I petitioned on the 17th of this month, and I earnestly request that my petition be granted as stated. Respectfully submitted.

　　　　　　　　　　　　Futsukamachi Yashiki
　　　　　　　　　　　　　　Ushigorō (seal)

Meiwa 1st Year, 11th Month
　　　　To: Jihimoire
　　　　　　　Nihee-dono
