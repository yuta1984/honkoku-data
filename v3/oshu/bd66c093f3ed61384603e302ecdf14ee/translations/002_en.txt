【Regarding Koyanaigawa Shuri】

To the original stipend (honchigyō) of 137 kan 997 mon, an increase (okashimasu) of 162 kan 906 mon has been granted, bringing the total to 300 kan mon. It should be noted that when Shuri was appointed to the position of karō (senior retainer) in the 12th year of Kanbun (1672), since the post was that of shōshin (a lower-ranking office), he had been granted an allowance (gōryoku) of 250 ryō in koban gold coins and 477 koku of genmai (unpolished rice) annually as office stipend (oyakuryō). However, as the aforementioned increase in stipend has now been granted, this is hereby announced accordingly. Naturally, the aforementioned rice allowance (gōryoku genmai) is to be reclaimed starting from this year.

19th day of the 5th month, Enpō 3 (June 21, 1675)
This document was issued by Lord Kenmotsu.
