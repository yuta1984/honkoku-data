Regarding yourself: on this occasion, as you had wished, your audience (o-mimai) with the Lord (Taishu-sama) was carried out successfully. As a celebratory offering for this occasion, you presented one sword and a gift of money in lieu of a horse. I have duly received the intent of your letter and relayed it to the Lord, who is greatly pleased.

Respectfully submitted,

Takeyama Shuri (with monogram seal)

The 29th day of the 12th month

To: Tade Innosuke
