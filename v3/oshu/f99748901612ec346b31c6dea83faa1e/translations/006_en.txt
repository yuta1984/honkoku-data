[Right folio]

A copy of the written instruction (oboegaki) delivered by Kenmotsu-dono to Innosuke-sama at the gate, where the assembled relatives gathered at the time of departure — as recorded by Mata-emon-dono (Senoue Mata-emon).

　　　　　　　　　　Tade Innosuke

　　　You are hereby ordered to succeed
　　　to the Koyanagawa family headship.
　　　A stipend of eighty kan-mon is granted to you.
　　　　　(Reason:) As Koyanagawa Kin-no-jō passed away
　　　　　from illness without having established an heir,
　　　　　the matter of succession had already been
　　　　　determined; however, taking into account
　　　　　the distinguished nature of this household, and furthermore, your

[Left folio]

　　　　　　longstanding and faithful service to
　　　　　　Hizen-dono (Lord Hizen / Date Hizen),
　　　　　　and these circumstances combined,
　　　　　　this order is hereby issued to you.
　　　　　　　　　　Respectfully,
　　　　　　　Three Month, Fourteenth Day

　　　The above having been duly delivered to Innosuke-sama:
　　　　　　　　　　Ōmachi Bizen
　　　　　　　　　　Yamaoka Sōemon
　　　　　　　　　　Senoue Matabe
　　　　　The matter of succession to the Koyanagawa family name is a matter of special distinction.
