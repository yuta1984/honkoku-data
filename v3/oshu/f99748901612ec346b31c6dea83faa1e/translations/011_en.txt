During the current lord's reign, we are in possession of a black-sealed document (go-kokuin) issued by the inspector (metsuke) on the sixteenth day of the eleventh month of Kanbun 1 (1661). On the twelfth day of the last month (twelfth month) of Kanbun 8 (1668), we petitioned to be granted additional wild meadowland (noyachi) within the granted territory of the same village (Notezaki Village). Our petition was granted as requested, and this was conveyed to us on the fourteenth day of the second month of Kanbun 9 (1669) through Shibata Geki and Harada Kai. In the tenth month of Kanbun 10 (1670), the aforementioned Nakatsukasa was ordered to retire (inkyo), and Nakatsukasa petitioned that both the family estate (atoshiki) and the status as an allied family (go-ichiza), along with the associated privileges (ozashiki), be granted to his legitimate heir, Shuri, of the same clan name. The petition was approved as requested,
