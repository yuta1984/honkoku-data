**[Right Page]**
　　To: Tade Inosuke

(In response to the letter from Daijō Kenmotsu on the previous page,) a messenger was dispatched to Sendai that same night.

**12th Day of the 3rd Month**
Inosuke-sama arrived from his domain residence at the Hour of the Sheep (around noon). He immediately sent Kusakari Saburōzaemon as a messenger, relaying the following message to Kenmotsu-dono based on the received letter: "I have arrived. I am ready to appear before you at your instruction and receive your orders." Kenmotsu-dono replied with news regarding the journey to the capital (Edo), saying: "Understood. Please present yourself at the castle tomorrow at the Hour of the Rabbit (around 10 a.m.)."

**[Left Page]**
**13th Day**
A copy of the letter from Daijō Kenmotsu-dono is as follows:

"...[There is one] who has been waiting for the order to proceed to the castle. Please await further instructions and proceed to the castle at the appropriate time. That is all.

　　　13th Day of the 3rd Month　　　Daijō Kenmotsu
　　　To: Tade Inosuke-dono"

An appropriate reply to the above letter was promptly sent.
