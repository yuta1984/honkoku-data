**[Right Folio]**

To Tade Inosuke, the younger brother of Lord Date Sukesaburō: this time, a new stipend of eighty kanmon in domain revenue (*chigyo*) is hereby granted, and the family name Koyanagawa is conferred upon him. Accordingly, you are hereby directed to attach the official stipend register (*hontō*), allocate the lands from the current land surveys and annual yield assessments (*tō monona*ri), and prepare a draft of the vermillion-seal document (*goshuinjō*).

However, since Koyanagawa Kin'no'jō had passed away without having formally submitted a petition for the adoption of an heir during his lifetime, the matter of succession (*atoshiki*) had already been formally resolved [i.e., the family line was considered extinct]. Nevertheless, the Koyanagawa household is a matter of special circumstances, and furthermore, in consideration of the connection to Lord Hizen, Tade Inosuke is hereby granted succession to the Koyanagawa surname and family line, and the [御一
