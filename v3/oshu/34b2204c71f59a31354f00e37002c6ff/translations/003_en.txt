Regarding the arrangement of Lord Tsunamura's marriage; Appended: matters concerning the betrothal gifts and the origins of the Koyanagawa family.

In the twelfth month of Enpō 1 (1673), the year of Mizunoto-Ushi (癸丑), an agreement was reached for the marriage of the Lord of the Domain (Tsunamura) with the daughter of Inaba Mino-no-kami, the lord of Odawara Castle in Sagami Province. On the morning of the sixteenth day, Lord Tsunamura made courtesy visits to the Senior Councillors (Rōjū) in thanks for their assistance, after which he proceeded to the residence of Mino-no-kami. In the reception room (oi-ma), discussions were held and a formal meal was served on wooden lacquerware (mokugu), consisting of two or three courses in the formal style. The three attendants who accompanied Lord Tsunamura were likewise served with food.
