【Recto】

Concerning the lands in Mutsu Province encompassing the twenty districts (gun) of Monou, Oshika, Tome, Iwai, Motoyoshi, Kesen, Isawa, Kami, Tamatsukuri, Kurihara, Shida, Tōda, Katta, Shibata, Igu, Watari, Natori, Miyagi, Kurokawa, and Esashi; together with nine villages within Uta District; thirteen villages within Shida District of Hitachi Province; three villages within Tsukuba District; Ryūgasaki Village within Kawachi District; Ōsonoki Village within Toyoda District of Shimōsa Province; eighteen villages within Gamō District of Ōmi Province; and two villages within Yasu District — totaling a stipend (taka) of over 620,000 koku (the itemized list is on a separate document):

Of this, 30,000 koku shall be administered by Tamura Sakyō-no-Tayū, and the remaining over 590,000 koku is hereby granted. In accordance with the precedents of successive generations, you are to govern these domains as stated herein.

　Enpō 3rd year [recte: Enkyō 3rd year], 10th month, 11th day — [Signed with] Official Seal

　　　　　　　　　To the Sendai Shōshō
