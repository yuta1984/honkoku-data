## Koyanagawa Family Genealogy Box: Record of Contents

**An inventory of documents stored in the Koyanagawa genealogy box**

One: Ancestral genealogies — three scrolls
One: A document in the hand of Lord Morimuné — one document
　　　(Bundled together in one package, with a label on the outside)
　　　Contents:
　　　One: A written record of the family crest for the curtain — one document
　　　One: Draft writings concerning the ancestors (cut paper, folded horizontally) — two sheets
　　　One: A sheet recording the posthumous Buddhist names (dōgō) of the ancestors — one sheet

One: A folded decorative cord with an attached written note — one set
　　　(In moegi [bright green] and crimson colors)

One: Documents concerning the replacement of the domain territory, exempted residential land (nozoki yashiki), stipends received as official service allowances, and matters treated at the same assessed value (dōkō)
