I write to humbly inform you that my eldest son, Jōnosuke, has been appointed as heir to the Koyanagawa household, and I am most deeply grateful for this fortunate turn of events. I write thus in order to convey my thanks. Furthermore, I humbly request that you continue to extend your gracious favor and intercession on behalf of my lord (go-mae). I remain, with utmost respect,

　　　Murata Ichirō
　　　[Month and Day]

　To: Nagasawa Shimono-sama
