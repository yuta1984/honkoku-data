To: Lord Koyanagawa Nakatsukasa
To: The Caretaker Officials (Rusuishu)
From: Tanabe Ryōsuke

Beginning with your ancestor Koyanagawa Nakatsukasa-tayū Morimune and continuing to the present, please compile and submit a written record — in proper order — of all members of the main lineage as well as collateral lines (brothers and sisters) across all generations, both male and female, covering the following items:

1. Both common name (俗名) and formal personal name (実名)
2. For adopted sons: the name and formal personal name of their biological father
3. Among siblings: those who succeeded other families, as well as daughters who married into other families
4. For the deceased: year and month of death, posthumous Buddhist name (法名), and age at death

As the above information is required, I hereby make this request. Even if the record is not complete in all respects, please submit whatever is known. That is all.

　　Ninth Month
