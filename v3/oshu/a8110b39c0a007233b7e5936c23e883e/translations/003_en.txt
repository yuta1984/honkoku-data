The wild wetland (noyachi) adjacent to the enfeoffed land of Notezaki Village was granted, and a cadastral survey (saoire) was conducted to develop it as new paddy fields. The assessed value of seventeen kan and ninety-four mon was awarded on the tenth day of the second month of Manji 3 (1660), bringing the combined total with the original landholding to one hundred thirty-seven kan and ninety-four mon. In the tenth month of Kanbun 10 (1670), (my late father) Nakatsukasa was ordered into retirement, and it was declared to me on the twenty-sixth day of the tenth month of that same year, in Edo, that the said landholding would be transferred to me without alteration. Furthermore, regarding the appointment of myself to the position of magistrate (bugyō), Amano Magodayū and Aneba Hachirozaemon were dispatched as messengers from Edo (to convey this order).
