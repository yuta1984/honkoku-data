From the time of my ancestor Koyanagawa Nakatsukasa Morimune, up through the time of my great-grandfather Koyanagawa Deihan, I have not received any oral tradition as to from which generation we first received our stipend (chigyō).

Regarding my grandfather of the same clan, Gyōbu: his stipend was eighty kan in value. Upon his death from illness, the inherited estate (atoshiki) was granted without dispute to my late father of the same clan, Nakatsukasa.

Furthermore, during the reign of Lord Gisan (義山様), at the time of the general land survey (sō-kenchi) conducted during the Kan'ei era, an additional forty kan was granted as surplus from the surveying rod measurements (sao-deme), combined with an increase in stipend, bringing the total landholding to one hundred and twenty kan.
