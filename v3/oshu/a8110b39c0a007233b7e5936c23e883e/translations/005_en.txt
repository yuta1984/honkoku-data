(From the 23rd day of the 11th month of Enpō 3,) the land was granted, and on the 21st day of the 3rd month of the 5th year [of Enpō], the wild marshy land (noyachi) adjoining the enfeoffed lands of the same village was granted. Upon the official surveying (saonire) of the new field cultivation area, a plot assessed at 176 mon was bestowed upon me. At present, the total amount of land granted comes to 310 kan 833 mon. The above is a complete account.
