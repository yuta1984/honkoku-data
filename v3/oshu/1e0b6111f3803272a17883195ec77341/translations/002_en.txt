**Formal Statement (Kōjō)**

When I succeeded to the Koyanagawa family headship (myōato), the lower-ranking dependents (shimochū) of Notezaki petitioned to be entrusted with the land holdings (kakaechи), and I granted their request, allowing them to retain custody of the land. However, it has come to pass that annual tribute (nengu) is now being levied upon this land. As many among the shimochū are in financial hardship, I would ask that you continue to oversee and make use of them in your service. As this arrangement may also cause inconvenience to Lord Shuri, I wish to request that the necessary amount be sent from within the aforementioned assessed funds (kakarikin).

Respectfully submitted,

2nd Month, 1st Day — Date Innosuke

To: Koyanagawa Jōnosuke
