　　　　　　　　　　Moriaki

　　Love Poems Linked to Grass

What will become of a person's heart in the end—
Like the dew on the tips of love-grass leaves,
such a shallow, fleeting bond.

How transient—the fern of remembrance
growing on this decaying old house;
shall it thus rot away entirely in the end?

On the late-autumn fields where colors change,
O forgetting-grass, how long must you
be turned toward that cold-hearted person?
