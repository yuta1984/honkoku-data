I have read your letter. Regarding the written statement I brought yesterday, which you then brought to Lord Nanbu Daizen last evening and submitted through his intermediary: Lord Daizen directly conveyed a reply to Lord Nōshū, to the effect that:

"Once you have received the document from our side, please send word to your domain without delay, instructing them to construct and add the new boundary mounds. However, if only peasants are sent from both sides and some dispute arises on-site, it could prove troublesome. Therefore, I believe it would be advisable to send about two lower-ranking samurai as well. Please give instructions for the rear mounds to also be completed before your lord's inspection tour arrives. As the Nanbu domain is quite far away, please send word promptly. I would also ask that when you inform us confidentially, arrangements be made regarding the timing."

I have duly noted that Lord Nōshū's intention was that these matters be conveyed to you in full detail, without any concealment, and that he was grateful for the careful consideration shown. I then informed Lord Mutsu exactly as stated in your letter, and judging from his demeanor, he appeared to regard the matter as one handled with great care. He has instructed me to proceed to meet with him and carry out the matter satisfactorily in accordance with his guidance and directions. That is all.

6th day of the 3rd month — Koyanagawa Shuri
To: Iinuma Niuemon
