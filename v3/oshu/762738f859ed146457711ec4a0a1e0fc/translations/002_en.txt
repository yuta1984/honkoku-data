**[Obverse]**

Within the twenty districts (*gun*) of Mutsu Province — Monou, Oshika, Tome, Iwai, Motoyoshi, Isawa, Kami, Tamatsukuri, Kurihara, Shida, Tōda, Katta, Shibata, Igu, Watari, Natori, Miyagi, Kurokawa, Esashi, and Kesen — together with nine villages within Uta District; thirteen villages within Shida District of Hitachi Province; four villages within Tsukuba District; eighteen villages within Kawachi District; and two villages within Nobuchi District — totaling more than 620,000 *koku* in assessed yield, as detailed in the attached inventory — of which 30,000 *koku* are to be administered by Tamura Ukyō-no-tayū, the remaining 590,000-plus *koku* are hereby granted. Let this domain be governed in full accordance with the above.

Jōkyō 1st year, 5th month, 21st day
　　　　　　　To: Matsuno Shōshō-dono
