### Record of Land Allotment Additions to Koyanagawa Shuri's Stipend Domain

Enpō 2nd Year (1674), Spring Land Survey — Esashi District
One item: 176 mon — Notezaki Village

As stated above, the assessed value of the stipend land additions (kirizoe) has been granted as part of the stipend assessment. Accordingly, from the year the official land survey (saonire) is conducted as recorded in the main cadastral register, the landholding (jigyō) shall be transferred, and one-fifth shall be levied as a redemption payment (shōkin) from the year of the survey onward. As previously deliberated and announced by colleagues of the same office, those who have received land additions of one kanmon or more shall have the draft of the lord's sealed document prepared and subsequently formally notified.
