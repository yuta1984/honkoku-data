In accordance with the above, regarding the grant of the stipend addition (kirizoe) as a formal stipend assessment: from the year of the official cadastral survey (sao-iri) as recorded in the main register, the land allocation (jikata) shall be transferred; one-fifth of the redemption payment shall be collected from the year of the cadastral survey. Those who have been granted a stipend addition of one kan-mon or more shall prepare a draft of the official sealed document. That is all.

　　　　　　Gorōzaemon
