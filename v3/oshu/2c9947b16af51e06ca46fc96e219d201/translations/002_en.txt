　　　　　　Ōmachi Shōgen
　　　　　　Same [clan]: Jūrō
　　　　　　Murata Shuzen

On this occasion, by special decree, Koyanagawa Kyūnosuke's household has been removed from the Koyanagawa family lineage and has been assigned the status of御一門 (go-ichmon; a distinguished family rank within the domain hierarchy). Regarding Jūrō, the second son of Ōmachi Shōgen, due to his distinguished lineage and connections, he has been ordered to succeed to the Koyanagawa family, and his stipend of eight [hundred koku — continued on the next frame]
