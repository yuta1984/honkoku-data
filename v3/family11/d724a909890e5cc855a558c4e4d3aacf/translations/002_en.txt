## Chronicle of Personal Record (Kinen Jiki)

Morikatsu's childhood name was Kikuchiyo-maru; he later changed his name to Kimata Seisaburō, and then further to Seizaemon-no-jō. He was also appointed to the rank of Tosa-no-kami.

One entry: From Morikatsu's father, Kimata Awa-no-kami Moritoki — [whose father was] Moriyuki — down to the present Morikatsu, [the family resided] in Kanbe, Ise Province. At this time, in the seventh year of Tenbun (1538), in Mikawa Province, when Lord Takechiyo was thirteen years of age, he came of age (genpuku) at Kanbe and took the name Tokugawa Jirōsaburō Hirotada. In the ninth year of Tenbun (1540), [Morikatsu's] father Moritoki entered into the service of Lord Hirotada. Later [moving] to Sunpu, Lord Hirotada and Moritoki both [subsequently] passed away.
