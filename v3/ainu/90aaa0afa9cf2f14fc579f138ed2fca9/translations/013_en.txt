○ Section on Plants and Trees (Sōmoku-bu)
　This section covers plants and trees of all kinds, including edible grains, fruits, and vegetables, as well as things that sprout and grow in mountains and fields.

○ Section on Categories and Items (Hinmoku)
　This section covers things classified and enumerated by type, including gradations such as large and small, good and bad, rising and falling, flourishing and declining.

○ Section on Grammatical Particles (Joji)
　This section covers words in the Ainu dialect that function as grammatical particles or auxiliary elements.

○ Section on Set Phrases and Compound Words (Jukugo)
　This section covers established compound expressions and idioms in common everyday use among the people.
