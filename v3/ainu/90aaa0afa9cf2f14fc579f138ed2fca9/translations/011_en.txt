**Table of Contents**

○ Section on Heaven and Earth (Tenchi-bu)
　This section covers the names of all created things (the myriad phenomena) found between heaven and earth, names of mountains and seas, and items relating to climate and weather.

○ Section on People (Jinbutsu-bu)
　This section covers terms for people of various social standings (high and low) and family relationships (such as parent and child) who inhabit one's own and other regions, where the names and designations differ.

○ Section on the Human Body (Shitai-bu)
　This section covers the distinctions between the various internal and external parts of the human body, as well as matters relating to longevity and early death.
