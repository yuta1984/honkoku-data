　　　　○Waka poetry translated into the Ainu language (Ezo kotoba)

【Waka 1】
"Though [I think] how cruel it is, the one who does not realize the measure of this life that passes by without saying so — it is none other than my own tears."
《Ainu: KOMUKOMU SETSUSHITEKUWOMAN. NETOHAKEHE RAMUHAITAANONU BETABANGUNI》

(This is a waka poem expressing that one passes through life's hardships without voicing the pain; ironically, only one's tears reveal the truth of one's suffering.)

【Waka 2】
"How I wish to show you — even the sleeves of the fisher-folk of Okishima, though soaked through and through with wet, have not changed their color."

(The Ainu translation for this second waka appears to be cut off or omitted in the transcription.)
