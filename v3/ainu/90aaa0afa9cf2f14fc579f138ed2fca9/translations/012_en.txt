**Table of Contents (continued)**

○ Section on Worldly Affairs (Seiji-bu)
　This section covers matters that arise from thought and deliberation, things accomplished through the work of the hands and feet, and in general all matters pertaining to everyday life in the world.

○ Section on the Five Senses and the Mind (Mouth, Nose, Ears, Eyes, and Heart)
　From within the Section on Worldly Affairs, those items relating specifically to these five faculties — mouth, nose, ears, eyes, and heart/mind — are extracted and presented separately.

○ Section on Implements and Goods (Kizai-bu)
　This section covers household tools, boats and machinery, and in general all things that serve as implements or constitute material goods.

○ Section on Birds and Beasts (Torikemono-bu)
　This section covers not only birds and beasts, but all living things that swim, fly, or roam about in the course of their existence.
