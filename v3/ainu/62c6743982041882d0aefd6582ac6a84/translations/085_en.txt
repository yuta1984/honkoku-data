(Interpretive translation)
Descending upon that place (iko hofuni), radiating brilliance (nbeve kere), endlessly (tsuima ashiuru), rising aloft (anupune kushiu), from afar (uraike ashiuru), coming toward us (chiko ashiuru kuru), proclaiming and announcing (ane igara [kafu]). ▲Also calling out (ano yane [nefu]) [▲Calling repeatedly (kaki [yofu] kayo)]. To the very ends of this world (yai moshiri shina), just as before (ne akushi [tafu]), this land we inhabit (an koro moshiri), beyond its limits (rehe [dafu]), to the distant far-off lands (urara moshiri), surveying all (iyu rachino). So that it may spread widely (araba shiyakube), proclaiming (ane iku shiu), from afar (urai ani), passing through all (iguru ga shike), gathered and surrounded (chita momare), ever doing so (ane karakari), and in this manner (ne gunberi shiu).
