## Compound Phrases / Example Sentences (熟語)

**Is your home village safe and well?**
(Ainu) Ikoro-kotan-shiri-monoshiri-anna
(Meaning: How is the state of your village? / Is everyone safe?)

**The other day, I went out to sea and obtained a fur seal (ottosei).**
(Ainu) Ofunaki-atsui-katarebau-neu-ishtан
(Meaning: Going out to sea, [I/we] caught fur seals.)

**Send it by boat; I will give you two bales of rice as payment for that.**
(Ainu) Chibu-ani-rurayan-itashiya
　　　　Bunmatsu-tara-atte-nankonna
(Meaning: Please carry it by boat; I will give you two bales of rice as reward.)

**The day before yesterday, herring came in a spawning run (kuki) here.**
　　　*(Note: "kuki/群来" is itself an Ainu loanword)*
(Ainu) Hoshike-onumanitan-kotanta
　　　　Heroki-atswa
(Meaning: The day before yesterday, herring swarmed to this village/shore.)

**Is the river nearby as large as the Kushiro River?**
(Ainu) Shiyamata-domo
　　　　Etokota-ambetsu-newa-nanikushiyuri
　　　　Unbetsu-uhatsuku-noana
(Meaning: Is that river over there comparable in size to the Kushiro River [Ambetsu]?)

**Indeed, it is just as you say.**
