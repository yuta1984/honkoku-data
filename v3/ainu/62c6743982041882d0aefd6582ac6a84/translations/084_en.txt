(Continuing from the previous page) Shike. Itakomare. Tan ruwe shiyani. Aroshi nepuni. Yayaha (shiyatsu)tsu. Iki koro ka iki. Tsuimaganne. Kurui se moshiri. Moshiri bake. Iwani rewa. Iuran kobashite. Tan be kushiu. Iko otan. Iwa (tafu) kashi. Oranche (mutsu)pe. Ainu tsumi. Ane nunuka. Kamui tsumi. Ane tangiga. Tan be kushiu. Iya ashiu kushiyakuno. Kamui tsumi.

(Interpretive translation)
(The goddess) descends and manifests in this world. Crying out loudly (yayaha [shiyatsu]tsu), performing her divine deeds (iki koro ka iki), endlessly (tsuimaganne), traversing this land of humans (kurui se moshiri), to the very edges of the world (moshiri bake), stepping over rocks (iwani rewa), surveying all (iuran kobashite). For this reason (tan be kushiu), here (iko otan), upon the rocks (iwa [tafu] kashi), transcending even the sacred (oranche [mutsu]pe), the name of humans (Ainu tsumi) (ane nunuka), and the name of the divine (kamui tsumi) (ane tangiga). For this reason (tan be kushiu), as if truly proclaiming (iya ashiu kushiyakuno), the name of the deity (kamui tsumi).
