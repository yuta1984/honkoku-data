(Continuing from the previous page) Shike. Itakomare. Tan ruwe shiyani. Aroshi nepuni. Yaya-ha (shiyatsu). Iki koro ka iki. Tsuimaganne. Kurui se moshiri. Moshiri bake. Iwani rewa. Iuran kobashite. Tan be kushiu. Iko otan. Iwa (tafu) kashi. Orranchi (mutsu) pe. Ainu tsumi. Ane nunka. Kamui tsumi. Ane tangiga. Tan be kushiu. Iya ashiu kushiyakuno. Kamui tsumi.

[Interpretive translation:]
In this place (tan ruwe shiyani), bearing our things (aroshi nepuni) upon the head (shiyatsu = head), one proceeds (iki koro ka iki). To a distant place (tsuimaganne), to the rugged mountain land (kurui se moshiri). Reaching the border of that land (moshiri bake), crossing over the rocks (iwani rewa), passing through (iuran kobashite). For this reason (tan be kushiu), arriving at this place (iko otan). Upon the rocks (tafu) above. That which descends (mutsu). I call the name of humans (ainu tsumi, ane nunka). I chant the name of the divine (kamui tsumi, ane tangiga). For this reason (tan be kushiu), as if truly proclaiming (iya ashiu kushiyakuno). The name of the gods (kamui tsumi).
