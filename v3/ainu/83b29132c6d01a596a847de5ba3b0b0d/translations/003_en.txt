**Preface to Moshiogusa**

That the will of those above reaches those below is the foundation of governing a nation. To discern the nature of things and communicate the sentiments of the people is the essential point of conducting governance. Now, upon reviewing this work, *Ezo Hōgen* (Ainu Dialects), one finds that it too pertains to matters of vital importance when dealing with the people [of Ezo]. How could it be regarded as merely a diversion for translators? All the more so at this time, when [we] are spreading the influence of [our] virtuous civilization and opening up great enterprises. This is the reason why this work has been committed to woodblock print.

Written by Hakkōsai, in the first year of Bunka, the Year of the Rat [1804]
