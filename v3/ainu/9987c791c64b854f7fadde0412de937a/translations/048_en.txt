　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　By Mogami Tokunai Tsunenori

　　　On the Matter of Wives and Concubines

1.　In Ezo, wives are universally called *machi* and concubines are called *utsushimachi*. Among the *waki-otona* (sub-headmen) of Kunashiri Island in Eastern Ezo, there is a man named Tsukinoito. He has a total of eighteen wives and concubines, with no distinction made between the principal wife and the concubines; he builds houses for them in various locations and has each of them live there independently. Some are separated by five or six *ri*, others by twenty or thirty *ri*, and yet others reside on islands several tens of *ri* away across the sea. The practice of wealthy and prominent Ezo men keeping numerous wives and concubines is not limited to this Tsukinoito — it is the local custom.
