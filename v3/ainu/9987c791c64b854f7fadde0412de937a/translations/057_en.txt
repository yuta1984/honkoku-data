(Continued from previous page) ...though it is recorded, all of these are what is called *shimokin* (lower-grade gold), which is placer gold mixed within soil and sand. It no longer exists. There is also the site of a former gold mine at a place called Urakawa. It is believed that gold would be found if one were to dig there. There are also deposits in the vicinity of Arimo and on Ratsuyu Island, among other places. There may also be deposits deep in the mountains. As this is a vast and undeveloped land, it is difficult to conduct a thorough survey. The matter should be clarified when the opportunity arises.

**Silver Mines**: There have been no reports of silver mines since ancient times. There are deposits at Mount Kawakun and Mount Yutetsubu. Western Ezo has many deep mountain ranges, which gives them an air of mystery, but as I have not yet reached those areas myself, it is difficult to cite even rumors.

**Copper Mines**: There is one in the deep mountains of Shibetsu in Eastern Ezo. There is also one in the mountains near Hakodate.

**Iron Mines**: There are iron deposits in Omori Village and Ishizaki Village near Hakodate, and many other locations throughout the region.

**[Left Page]**

**Lead Mines**: The Obouyu mountain range near Miichi Village in spring is said to be the finest. In a previous year, when digging was conducted at Esashi Village, approximately three hundred units were produced in a single year. There are also deposits in Akagami Village, Eeda Village, and other places.

**Brass-like Metal (Orichalcum/Natural Brass)**: There is such a metal on an island called Metsuno-i-wo and Sutorofu (Iturup). This metal has not yet been seen in Japan; it is a copper that is golden in color from its natural state and is said to be soft like brass. A man who had crossed the sea to those lands came to me and described it in detail.

**Surplus Provisions (Edible Earth/Clay)**: There is such a substance at a place called Shottochikya on Etorofu Island (Iturup). It is stored and used periodically as food. It is white in color, resembling rice cake (*mochi*), and mild in taste. When I crossed to this island and became separated from the companion vessel, left without rice or miso, I boiled plant roots and added this earth to make a meal, and found it remarkably light and easy to eat.
