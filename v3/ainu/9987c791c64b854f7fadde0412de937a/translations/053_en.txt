*(Continuing from the previous page)* The Ainu words relating to anchorage, hairstyles, women, and similar topics are likewise probably derived from ancient Japanese. The names of most objects can be found in existing records and writings, so I will not reproduce them here. What I have compiled are the everyday Ainu words as spoken, and I record them here solely for the benefit of those who wish to study them.
