**Record of the Ship from Hamanomachi, within Kokubu, Ōsumi Province, Drifting Ashore at Oku-Ezo (Etorofu Island)**

The ship departed on the twenty-third day of the tenth month of Shōtoku 1 (the Year of the Rabbit, 1711), and returned to the lord's residence at Shiba, Edo, in Musashi Province, on the fourth day of the eleventh month of the following year (the Year of the Dragon, 1712).

One ship belonging to ship captain Jirōzaemon of Hamanomachi. ※Note: The ship was relatively new, having been in service for five years, was sturdily built, and carried fifteen-tan sails.

- Chief navigator (hired): Konego, Chōuemon — age 51
- Helmsman: Ōnego-Torii-do no Sangōrō — age 41
- Sailor: Ōnego-Torii-do no Kakubē — age 32
- Sailor: Tahē — age 35
- Sailor: Shōgorō — age 22
- Sailor: Chōuemon — age 19
- Sailor: Jinshichi — age 17
