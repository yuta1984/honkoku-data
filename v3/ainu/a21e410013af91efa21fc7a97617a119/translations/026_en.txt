There is also a great quantity of whale oil, which is submitted as tribute to the lord from Ezo. Toward the end of September, whales come ashore in large numbers. The whales are caught, butchered, placed on stones, and fires are lit beneath them to extract the oil. There are also various great trees. There is a tree about ten ken (approximately 18 meters) in length that resembles bamboo. When you roll it up and release it, it returns to its original straight form. This tree is called "shiuri no ki." It seems to be the same as what is called "shibari no ki."

Item: On the 23rd day, we were told it concerned the town magistrate. Handayū was escorted to the residence of Takahashi Asaemon, where, in a room resembling a kitchen area, a man by the name of Okabe Shirōemon was summoned and was present. The ship captain Chōzaemon and the helmsman Sangōrō also appeared. As we were asked in detail (about our circumstances), we described one by one: how in Etorofu, our belongings had been thoroughly confiscated and we had been stripped bare, left with nothing at all and given no food; the successive beatings we had received; and the matter of Yamakawaya no Chōemon having gone missing.
