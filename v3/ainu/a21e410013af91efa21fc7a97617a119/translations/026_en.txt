I humbly submit this account. Even from this alone, there is an enormous amount of revenue to be gained. There is also a great abundance of whale oil. The whales that are delivered from Ezo to the lord appear in great numbers around the end of the ninth month. After catching the whales, they are butchered and rendered over fires built on stone. There are also various large trees. There is a tree about ten ken (approximately 18 meters) in length that resembles bamboo. Even when coiled up, it straightens back to its original form when released. This is called the "shiuri tree." It also seems to be referred to as the "shibari tree."

One entry: On the 23rd day, as it was under the jurisdiction of the town magistrate, Hantayū was summoned and brought to the residence of Takahashi Asaemon. In a place resembling a kitchen, a man named Okabe Shirōemon was called in, and the ship's captain Chōzaemon and the helmsman Sangōrō, who happened to be present, also appeared. As they were questioned in detail, they each recounted: how in Etorofu their belongings had been thoroughly confiscated leaving them with nothing, how they were given no food, how they had been repeatedly beaten, and how the whereabouts of Yamagawa's Chōemon remained unknown.
