*Item:* On the eleventh day of the tenth month of the Year of the Dragon, we departed Matsumae, boarded two available vessels, and arrived at the place called Aomori in Tsugaru on the morning of the twelfth day. This place has around a thousand households. We set out immediately without lodging the night, and from there passed through the Nambu domain, proceeding along the road. As regards our travel, since we carried letters of passage along with temple-registration certificates (shumon-aratame) issued by our home domain, we passed through every place without hindrance.
