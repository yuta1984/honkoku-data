Then Shirōemon came out (toward the kitchen) and said, "As it is a lengthy account, there may be parts not well remembered, so write it down and submit it." Therefore, we wrote everything down without omission, and had the ship's captain submit it, whereupon he was promptly called in, as Asaemon said he would hear the matter directly. As the ship's captain proceeded to recount everything without leaving out a single detail, Asaemon roused himself from the inner room and said, with evident displeasure, "Etorofu is a remote island, beyond the reach of our governance, and unlike the areas contiguous with Matsumae, but nevertheless, such conduct should not occur."

After this, we were told: "We should send you all the way to Tsugaru from here, but at this time there is no suitable vessel available; however, there happens to be a transport ship available.
