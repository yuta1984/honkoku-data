　　Ezo Soshi: Appendix
　　　　On the Matter of Etorofu Island

One: Etorofu Island is the neighboring island of Kunashiri Island. At two places called Itoiya and Heruhetо, located at the foot of steep, towering mountains along the northwestern shore of the Northern Sea (Sea of Okhotsk), at the crossing point from Kunashiri Island, ships awaiting favorable weather for the voyage to Kunashiri Island would gather. One ri to the north of this location is a place called Moyoro, where there is an Ainu village. The village elder (otona) is called Kurarerу. At this elder's fishing grounds on the beach, there is an old anchor weight, weighing approximately seventy kanme. Upon investigation through the records of the Matsumae Drifting Vessel Registry (Matsumae Hyōryū Funadome), it was found that this was a vessel belonging to Horikawaya Hachiemon of Kosonomura village in Kishū (Kii Province), with a ship's captain (oki sendō) named Tomoemon.
