Vocabulary glossary: Japanese terms with their Ainu language equivalents (current page)

To lose one's temper/become angry → Kimaiba / Ainososeku / Shanbeebare
To hate/detest → Eshishi / Hehoyu
To go one after another/gradually proceed → Ubiubi baian
To be patient/easygoing (lit. "long-tempered") → Ramushirroma
To do nothing/be idle → Toranne
Trouble/annoyance/inconvenience → Iramukanhare tabawashi / Iyoserekeretabawashi
To receive/be given → Unkerai
To give free of charge (give at no cost) → Annono icikore / Anno omante
To be fortunate/lucky (good fortune) → Chikannokaru
To sever a relationship/fall out → Rutsuba / Utsuha
Estrangement/interruption of relations → Ukopuri
To become acquainted/grow familiar → Ukobikini / Uetomobaoushi / Ukoseshikinin / Uomoynu
