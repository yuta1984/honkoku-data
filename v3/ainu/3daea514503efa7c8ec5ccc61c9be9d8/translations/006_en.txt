Implements and Goods Section

Records all things that serve as implements or constitute property, including houses, machinery, boats, and tools.

Birds and Beasts Section

Records not only birds and beasts, but broadly all living creatures that swim, walk, or move about.

Plants and Trees Section

Records plants and trees, as well as edible grains, fruits, vegetables, and all things that sprout and grow in mountains and fields.

Classification Section

Records items that are enumerated by raising categories and establishing entries, such as large/small, good/bad, high/low, and rise/fall.

Grammatical Particles Section

Records Ainu dialect words belonging to the category of grammatical particles.

Set Phrases Section

Records commonly used idiomatic expressions that have become established in everyday life among the general population.
