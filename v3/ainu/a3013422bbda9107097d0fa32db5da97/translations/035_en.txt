**[The Appearance and Clothing of Ezo Women]**

Women, whether in towns or villages, mostly wear their hair long, and many are beautiful, lustrous in complexion, and slender-waisted. As for their clothing, some wear attush, or garments with decorative stitching as shown in the illustration, while others wear white cotton garments. They use a wide-strapped apron cloth in place of an obi sash.
