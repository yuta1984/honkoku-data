**Illustration of the Large Boat**

When the chiefs of Ezo (Ainu lands) travel to the administrative offices, they build boats approximately seven and a half hiro (roughly 13.5 meters) in length, and their arrival at Hakodate and Matsumae is called "Uimamu." This term likely means "meeting for the first time" (a formal audience). Similarly, when chiefs from the areas of Kiitappu and Kunashiri travel to the island of the sea otter (Rakko), they decorate and prepare their boats as shown in this illustration.

【Parts of the Boat and Their Ainu Names】
- Bow (Prow): *Nareta* — also called *Nanoro*
- *Achiura* [possibly *Achiute*?]
- Front section: *Shoinanoke*
- *Namachi* [possibly *Takamachi*? or *Taromachi*?]
- Rudder/Oar: *Ashikai* — *Shanma*
- Gunwale (boat's edge): *Chipu-haruru*
- Stern: also called *Umoro*
- *Rashika*
