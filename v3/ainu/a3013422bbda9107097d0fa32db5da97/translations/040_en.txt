**The Stone Pillar at "Kusarichi" in the Western Part of Kunashiri Island**

In the western part of Kunashiri Island, at a place called "Kusarichi," there stands a pillar-like stone, within which there is a form resembling the armor (*yoroi*) of the Ezo (Ainu) people.

According to tradition, this is said to be the armor left behind by Hangan Yoshitsune, which transformed into stone.

This is an exceedingly wondrous thing, the like of which exists nowhere else in Japan.
