First, *Oagari Kombu* (also known as "Tenka Shirikomabu" or "Shirikomabu of the Realm"):

This kelp is produced along the coastline from Cape Shiokoshi eastward to Shika (Shikabe). It measures approximately thirteen to fourteen *shaku* in length (roughly 4 meters) and five to six *sun* in width (approximately 15–18 cm), and displays red, yellow, and green coloring. After harvesting, a clean, pure location is selected for drying. Fifty fronds are bundled into one sheaf, then wrapped in kelp and bound in sixteen places before being presented as tribute to the Imperial Court. This is considered the finest quality of all kelp.

Second, *Shinori Kombu* is produced in the sea to the east of Hakodate (*Hakodatehigashikai*). It measures over seven *hiro* in length (approximately 13 meters or more) and one *shaku* three to four *sun* in width (approximately 40–42 cm), is green in color, and has a sweet and delicious flavor. This kelp is exported to China (*Tōzan*, i.e., the Chinese continent).

Third, *Kashikombu* (Confectionery Kelp) is dark green in color, approximately one *shaku* in length (about 30 cm), and has an exceptionally sweet and delicious taste. It is produced on the seabed from Cape Shiokoshi to Shika.

Fourth, kelp originating from the Ezo territories of Mitsuishi, Horoizumi, and Shiranutka measures over seven *hiro* in length (approximately 13 meters or more) and three to four *sun* in width (approximately 9–12 cm), and is green in color. Other kelp produced at various other locations throughout the region is classified as miscellaneous grade (*zappin*).
