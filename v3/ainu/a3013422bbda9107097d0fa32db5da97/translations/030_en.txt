**Ito-hirika-nekatsup (with illustration)**

This bird is found throughout the inner Ezo lands. Its size is comparable to that of a domestic duck (*ie-gamo*). "*Ito*" refers to the name for its beak; "*hiruka*" is a word meaning "good" or "fine"; and "*nekatsup*" is the general term for bird. "*Chikopka*" is a place name. When one crosses over to the island chain, there are islands where no trees grow. It is said that on these islands, this bird is caught, its bones are roasted, and its meat is boiled and eaten.
