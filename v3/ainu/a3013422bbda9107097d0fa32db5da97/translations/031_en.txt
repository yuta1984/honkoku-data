**Chishiyukina (with a sketch from life)**

This plant grows abundantly in open plains and vast fields. Upon tasting it, one finds it to be mildly sweet and slightly salty. The Ezo (Ainu) people harvest its roots, chop them finely as one would when making rice *koji* [#1], then steam and ripen them to produce *koji* [#2]. Additionally, they prepare a separate batch by thoroughly boiling another portion of the plant, then combine it with the *koji* to brew a form of sake. The taste is equivalent to Japanese *doburoku* (unrefined sake), and it produces intoxication in exactly the same manner, with no difference whatsoever.
