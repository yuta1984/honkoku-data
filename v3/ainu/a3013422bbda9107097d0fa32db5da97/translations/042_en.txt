**On Uruppu Island and the Sea Otter**

Uruppu Island (also known as Rakko Island) lies twenty *ri* to the northeast of Etorofu Island. In former times, Mogami Tsunenori was the first to cross to this island, where he produced a map. The Ainu people of Etorofu Island, Kunashiri Island, and Kiitappu Island cross to this island from early summer to hunt the *ryōko* (sea otter, *rakko*).

The sea otter measures some six to seven *shaku* in length. Its fur is thick, growing without distinction of direction — neither lengthwise, crosswise, nor up and down — and is of a purplish-black color. It is considered the finest among all pelts.

When sea otters float on the surface of the sea, they float belly-up. It is also said that they climb up onto rocky outcroppings.

*(Note: The characters 猟虎 were devised by people based on the phonetic sound "rakko.")*
