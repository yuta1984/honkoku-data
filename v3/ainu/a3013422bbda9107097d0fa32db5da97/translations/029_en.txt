**Tokari (Mizuhyō / Azarashi – the Seal)**

This refers to the harbor/spotted seal (*azarashi*). It measures about five to six *shaku* in length. Its entire body is black, and those with clear leopard-like white spots are called "*bekko-han*" (tortoiseshell-spotted) and are considered the finest quality. Those of a charcoal-grey color are more commonly found. There is also a variety whose entire body bears a pattern resembling the character for "water" (波紋-like markings). All of these are found along the coastal shores of Teshio (*Teshio*), in the western Ezo territories.

The *Azuma Kagami* states that among the materials listed as offerings by the Buddhist sculptor Unkei for the crafting of Buddhist statues, there appear "sixty hides of water-leopard (*mizuhyō*/seal), measuring seven *ken* in center diameter."

There is also a sea creature called "*Toto*." It exceeds one *jō* in size, its color is dark, and its head is said to resemble that of a rat. When its hide is made into rope, it never breaks or frays. Its oil is used for lamp fuel, and the flavor of its meat is as sweet and delicious as whale meat. As the author has not seen the actual creature, no illustration is provided here.
