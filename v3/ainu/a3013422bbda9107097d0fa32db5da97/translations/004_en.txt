Herashitomikaмoi (colloquially called "Kuwasaki" [hoe-tip] in Matsumae): measuring one shaku five sun in size, the front is covered with leather and decorated with gold and silver ornamental rivets as shown in the diagram, while the back is a hammered metal plate. This is considered the foremost sacred object (divine implement) among the Ainu. It was likely originally a leather implement used in armor in ancient times. In the Yukara (epic songs), it is sung and passed down that the bird-creating deity (Korumoroshiri Kamuy) descended from the heavens. Fearing that keeping it in the home might expose it to impurity, a clean plot of land is chosen and a separate structure is built for its enshrinement. It is also secretly stored in caves found in mountain forests and deep valleys. In the fourth month of the New Year during the Hōreki era, several pieces were excavated from a hill at Yunozaki in Kayabe Bay. The leather portions had rotted away, leaving only the rivets, and these are kept in the possession of the chieftain Otereki.
