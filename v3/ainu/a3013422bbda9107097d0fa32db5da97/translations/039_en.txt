**Kunne-chiro-no-tsup (Illustration)**

This is a fox native to Shikotan Island.
Its coat color is a light black.

**[Quotation from the *Shoku Nihongi*: The Presentation of a Black Fox]**

The *Shoku Nihongi* states: In the seventh month of the fifth year of Wadō (712 CE), the provincial governor of Iga Province, Achi no Kiyo and others, presented a black fox. This was immediately recognized as a felicitous omen and presented before the throne, and the provincial governor and those below him were subsequently awarded promotions in court rank.
