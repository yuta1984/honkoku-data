**On the Customs of Etorofu Island**

Etorofu Island lies more than three hundred *ri* to the northeast of the town of Matsumae. No guardian samurai had been dispatched to the island from the Matsumae domain, but in the summer of the fifth year of Tenmei (1785), a man named Mogami Tsunenori crossed to the island on his own initiative. The island's Ainu people, seeing Japanese people for the very first time, are said to have gathered together in great numbers.

Their customs preserve the ways of antiquity, and the people in appearance are like gods. They weave grass into garments, and use the skins of birds and beasts as fur robes (*kawai-goromo*) to ward off the cold. In winter, the surrounding seas freeze over, and the cold is extreme.
