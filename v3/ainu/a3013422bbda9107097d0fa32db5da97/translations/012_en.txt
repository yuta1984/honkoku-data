## Map of Oshamambe

The chief of Kōratsufu (place name) is called Yashima. Long ago, a deity visited this place and caught a large flatfish (halibut/flounder) while fishing in this sea. The deity taught the Ezo (Ainu people), saying: "This fish is sacred. I shall enshrine it on this mountain. After this, only on this mountain will there remain snow in the shape of a flatfish. When you see this, if you fish in this sea, you shall obtain many flatfish to use as food." Even to this day, things are exactly as the deity foretold. For this reason, what was originally called "Uashishyamanhe" has been corrupted in pronunciation over time and is now called "Oyamanhe." "Uashi" means snow, and "Yamanhe" means flatfish.
