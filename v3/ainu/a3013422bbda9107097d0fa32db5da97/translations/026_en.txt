**Figure of the Ōnetsube (Fur Seal / Ottosei)**

**[Haigoujin (Sea Dog's Kidney / Fur Seal Genitalia)]** — Salty in flavor, greatly warming in nature, non-toxic.

Hou#1 states: Soak in alcohol for one day, wrap in paper and roast until fragrant, then mince and pound; alternatively, ripen with alcohol and meat in a silver vessel and combine with medicine.

Shizhen#2 states: If stored together with ■#3 (pepper) and camphor, it will not deteriorate (i.e., resist decay and infestation).

(Medicinal uses) Treats men's ■#4 ailments, accumulation of qi masses, cold accumulation, fatigue qi, decline and damage of kidney essence, consumptive emaciation caused by excessive sexual activity, and depletion of vital essence.
