Kelp can be harvested from the *doyo* period of the sixth month (midsummer)
until the fifteenth day of the eighth month.

The *Shoku Nihongi* states: On the fifteenth day of the tenth month of the first year of Reiki (715 CE),
Urashimibe no Kimi Usomina and others of the third rank among the Emishi of Mutsu Province,
of the Murashibetsu clan, reported: "Our relatives have died, and several
children and grandchildren remain. We constantly fear being plundered by hostile tribespeople.
We request that a district office (*gunke*) be established in Koa Village so that we may be
registered as regular subjects (*henkomin*) and live in permanent security."
Furthermore, Suga no Kimi Komahiru and others among the Emishi reported:
"Since the time of our ancestors, we have offered kelp as tribute without fail,
relying upon this land year after year without interruption. However, now the road
to the provincial capital is very long, and the round trip takes many weeks, causing us
great hardship. We request that an administrative office be established at He Village,
so that, like ordinary subjects, we may lead our kinsmen and continue
to offer kelp as tribute without ever failing to do so."
Both requests were granted.
