Women have no menstruation, and when giving birth they go into the mountains, give birth there, and then wrap the baby in dog skin, tying the front and back with rope in a tube-like fashion, and carry it on their back as they walk. Even so, the infant makes no sound. The infant has a line of black hair growing along its back. The woman who has given birth immediately goes out to collect firewood, works, and returns. Children's clothing is generally made of dog skin. Even on ships sending goods from the islands to Matsumae, there are women who give birth. They deliver with remarkable ease, leaving the baby in the manner described above.

One: Garments made from stripped (animal) hides are worn by those of high rank. Those of lower status rarely wear even a single garment. Rice is also eaten only by those of high rank.
