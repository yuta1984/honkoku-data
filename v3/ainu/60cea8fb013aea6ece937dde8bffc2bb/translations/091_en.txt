　　　　On the Island of Etorofu

Etorofu Island lies adjacent to Kunashiri Island. At the southwestern coastal approach to the island, beneath towering and precipitous mountains, are two places called "Itoiya" and "Heretatarube," which serve as shelters where ships awaiting favorable weather before crossing to Kunashiri Island take refuge. About one ri to the north of these places is an Ainu village called "Moyoro." The village elder (otona) is called "Woshiru." On the beach within this elder's fishing grounds, there are three old anchors, each weighing approximately seventy kan.
