**[Right folio]**
In total, there are twenty-one islands, all of which are Ezo territory (蝦夷地) and dependent islands forming a continuous chain belonging to Japan. From "Notsuke Village" on the eastern shore of the island where Matsumae is located, at a mere three *ri* across the sea, lies the first island: "Kunashiri" (Kunashir), at a northern latitude of approximately 42–43 degrees, with a circumference of roughly 150-odd *ri*. From this island, only five or six *ri* across the sea, lies "Etorou" (Iturup) Island, at a northern latitude of approximately 43–44 degrees, with a circumference of roughly 300-odd *ri*. From this island, approximately nineteen *ri* across the sea, lies [another island] with a circumference of approximately 150-odd *ri*. Among the other large islands, "Shimoshiri" (Simushir), "Hokomoshiri" (Paramushir), and "Onnekōtan" (Onekotan) — these three islands are said to surpass even "Etorou" in size. When compared to Japan, there are many islands as large as Shikoku or Kyushu.

**[Left folio]**
When called "islands," they sound small; when called "countries," they sound large. Because they are so large, they may also be called "countries." The twenty-one islands combined are several times the size of Japan. The northern latitude of Kunashiri Island is 42–43 degrees. The land of "Kansasuka" (Kamchatka) lies at 54 degrees. The north-south separation between Kunashiri and Kamchatka amounts to over twenty degrees of latitude. Kamchatka should lie in the direction of *Ushi-Tora* (north-northeast) from Kunashiri Island. Since the chain of islands fans out from the southwest (*Kun*, 坤) to the northeast (*Gen*, 艮), it surpasses Japan several times over in extent. As evidence: in Japan, Nagasaki lies at 32 degrees and Nanbu (the southern part of Mutsu Province) at 40 degrees — a difference of eight degrees — this being the north-south latitudinal span.
