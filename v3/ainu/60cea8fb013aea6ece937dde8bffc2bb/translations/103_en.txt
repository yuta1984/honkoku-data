...in that year, one large Russian ship crossed the sea to Uruppu Island (*Iturup* / Urup Island). Of the seventy crew members aboard, "Ishiuyo" and two of his retinue remained behind, and to this day they are staying at "Honmoi" in "Etorou" (Etorofu/Iturup) Island, under the jurisdiction of the village headman (*otona*) "Rurishiijuyo" [lacuna in text]. When this matter became known in Matsumae, a retainer and official ordered the headman "Rurishi" to issue instructions that the Russians were to be sent back to their home country. When the headman "Rurishi" conveyed this order to the Russians, the Russian "Ishiuyo" replied, saying: "I have particular reasons why I cannot return to my home country." He has a crime for which he would face the death penalty upon return, making it impossible for him to go back. Yet if they were to be forcibly expelled this time, he threatened that "the wrongdoings of the natives on Uruppu Island would be

**[Left folio]**
reported in full detail to the sovereign." The wrongdoings of the natives in question are as follows: a Russian government vessel, laden with various products from the home country, drifted ashore on Uruppu Island. There were no people aboard the ship, only the corpse of one Russian, and the cargo was otherwise undisturbed. Taking advantage of the ship being unmanned, a great number of natives from a neighboring island gathered and plundered all the cargo from within the ship, then subsequently set fire to and burned the vessel to the ground. This is what he said he intended to report to the sovereign. Because of this, the natives said it was impossible to expel [the Russians]. In my estimation, the reasoning of the preceding passage is understandable, yet even so it is difficult to determine whether it is truly the case.
