Reflecting carefully on all of this, I find that from the port of "Okhotsk," Russian government vessels set sail across a vast ocean lying several leagues to the east of Japan, beyond which lies what is called North America — a great nation stretching in a broad belt, roughly between directly below the North Pole and directly below the South Pole. "Ishiyoha" told me in detail about the voyage made to that country. First, they set out with three large ships, but perhaps because the sea crossing to that country was so very far, they were delayed in the voyage and returned to "Okhotsk." Thereafter, a royal command was issued again, and nine large vessels were loaded to capacity with domestic goods, with roughly seventy persons boarding each ship, and they set out once more.
