Nevertheless, there are none among them who resemble each other [in variety], and they are said to resemble the Ezo native peoples of Japan. However, in recent years, the Russians went there and subjugated them, renamed the country "Anaaterisukoi." In this country there is a great river called "Anateri," and it is said the country was named after this river. Among the country's products there is a mountain beast called "Oreni." The hide of this beast is truly a fine fur. The lands to the north of this country, though connected by small islands, are said to be lands of extreme cold, with seas frozen year-round throughout all four seasons. Therefore, navigation there is impossible. It is said to extend to approximately sixty-seven degrees of north polar altitude (north latitude). The people of this country are said to make their livelihood by hunting in the mountains.

**[Left folio]**
For this reason they keep many dogs, taking them along during mountain hunts to assist in driving game, and on cold nights when sleeping in the mountains, they lie down together with their dogs. The "Oreni" is about the size of a deer. I had a good look at its hide when I lived together with Russians for approximately ninety days. I received from a Russian one spoon (*saji*) crafted from the horn of that beast, which I still possess to this day. It is something resembling white rhinoceros horn. The Russians, in all things, follow their national customs and consider it an honor to excel greatly in their enterprises. In particular, it appears that for the purpose of opening up new ventures, they stake their very lives and establish the achievement of pioneering and founding new lands.
