## On Local Products (continued)

**Wansei / Bowl-Blue (continued from previous page)**
——and named it accordingly. It is said to be a pigment used for painting patterns on pottery before firing.

**Inkstone**
Found throughout the area of "Mikoi Beach" in Ishizaki Village, beyond Hakodate Village. There are also deposits along the riverbed of a river called "Surui River," in the shadow of this mountain. I had some carved by craftsmen in Edo and keep them in my possession. They are easy to transport to Japan (the mainland).

**Stalactites**
Said to be found in a rock cave where a Jizō (stone guardian deity) is enshrined, at the foot of Ōta Mountain in Western Ezo.

**Coal**
Found in Hetsushafu Village, within the Kushiro (Kusuri) district.

**Sea Lettuce / Miru (Codium)** *(海松)*
Produced all along the coastlines of Matsumae. The color is red and resembles the leaves of a hinoki cypress. Near Matsumae, it is used as a decorative ornament in the tokonoma (display alcove).

**Shiokogori (Sea Coral)** *(汐凝)*
Commonly called "Ezo Coral." It resembles branching coral, is crimson in color, and is an exceptionally beautiful object. It too is used as a decorative ornament.

**Alum** *(明礬)*
Found in abundance in Esashi. The method of processing it is unknown, and therefore the indigenous people (Ainu) leave it as it is, unused.

**Hot Springs** *(温泉)*
Found at: Ōsawa, Katsutami, Otobe, Miichi, Hiratauchi, Yunotai, Shibetsu, Seseki, Shikabe, Tomenosaba, Asashi, Yama-no-yu, Shiriute, Suenokawa — and many other locations throughout the region.

**Black-Flowered Lily** *(黒花百合)*
Found in various places inland from the Akkeshi area and beyond.

**White-Flowered Garland Chrysanthemum** *(白花春菊)*
This type of chrysanthemum is found throughout Ezo.

**Autumn Bush Clover** *(秋萩)*
Found around "Yamokishinai Village" near Monashibe Village. Some specimens have trunks with a circumference of over four sun (approximately 12 cm).

**Dwarf Bamboo** *(篠竹)*
Found in a place called "Shieyatan Village" in a location in Western Ezo known as "Shakotan." The bamboo that grows there has black tiger-stripe markings.
