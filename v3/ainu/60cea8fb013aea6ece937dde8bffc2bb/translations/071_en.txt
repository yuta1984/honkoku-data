## On Local Products (continued)

**Burdock Root (continued from previous page)**
——the flesh is tender and the center is not hollow.

**Narwhal Horn** *(一角)*
There have been instances of obtaining one in the Urakawa district. A retainer of the Matsumae domain, Kitagawa Izaemon, brought one to Edo (the capital), where it fetched an extremely high price.

**Polar Bear** *(白熊)*
Produced from an island called "Metsunoj" (also known as "Ostrov"). The *akabito* (Russians/red-haired people) prize it very highly.

**Black Fox** *(黒狐)*
It is said that one caught by gun in Matsumae was given a burial at Sennenji Temple.

**Silver Mouse / Ermine** *(銀鼠)*
Found in various places throughout Eastern Ezo. It is a creature smaller than a weasel and pure white. Rarely, reddish ones are also found.

**Golden Sea Cucumber** *(金海鼠)*
It was said that the famous variety was harvested from the sea near Kinkasan in Ōshū (northern Honshū), and I had thought it was not found elsewhere — but it also exists in Eastern Ezo, around Shirasuka and Kushiro (Kusuri), and even on Kunashiri Island.

---

**Murikara** *(ムリカラ)* [Ainu term]
A large crab with legs measuring roughly four to five *shaku* (approximately 120–150 cm) in length. The taste is exceptionally delicious.

**Sechikoro** *(セチコロウ)* [Ainu term]
A small creature resembling a type of paste or jelly. The flesh is firm and the taste is good.

**Aichiyuruhe** *(アイチユルヘ)* [Ainu term]
Resembles a red fish (*akauo*), with a horn at the tail. The rough outer layer of this horn is scraped off and applied to arrowheads, with which animals are shot. A single arrow is enough to bring down the prey. The horn resembles the *karasu-i-kaku* (black rhinoceros horn).

**Kachiyuruhe** *(カチユルヘ)* [Ainu term]
Its form is not well known. It has a single horn and is seen creating waves on the water's surface, but since its body cannot be caught or observed, its true nature is unknown. When one approaches it, one becomes intoxicated and overcome by its fragrance.

**Rahanhekoruhe** *(ラハンヘコルヘ)* [Ainu term]
Called "*Shari-gani*" (Shari Crab) in Matsumae. It is a creature with the head of a crab and the tail of a shrimp (*ebi*). There is a pearl in its head. It is the same as what the *Kōmōjin* (Dutch/Europeans) bring, known as "*Kurikankiri*."

**Kakamoyuruhe** *(カヽモユルヘ)* [Ainu term]
Called "*Yutsuyu*" in Matsumae; it is a fish with scales that resembles a *fuku* (pufferfish). Its belly has a pattern resembling a chrysanthemum flower, and it also resembles a fish called "*Oyuse*." It is considered a poisonous fish, and the Ezo people (Ainu) do not eat it.
